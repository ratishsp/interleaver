Week 28: Maya reflects on her settled life in Copenhagen

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

commute reflection
Storyboard: Maya takes her usual morning bus to the software company, enjoying the warm sunlight. She watches the familiar neighborhoods roll by through the window, briefly reflecting on how freezing and completely unfamiliar this exact route felt back in January.
我坐喺巴士嘅窗邊。I sit by the window on the bus.
今日朝早啲陽光好舒服、好溫暖。The morning sun is lovely and warm today.
哥本哈根而家係夏天，成個城市好靚。It is summer in Copenhagen, and the city looks beautiful.
我望住外面熟悉嘅街道。I look out at the familiar streets.
我哋經過一個種滿綠樹嘅大公園。We drive past a large park with green trees.
已經有人喺單車徑上面踩緊單車。People are already cycling on the bike paths.
我朝朝都搭呢架巴士返工。I take this bus to my work every morning.
行車路線同一月嗰陣一模一樣。The route is exactly the same as in January.
但係而家所有嘢都完全唔同晒。But everything is completely different now.
我記得我喺呢個城市嘅第一個禮拜。I remember my first week here in the city.
當時天氣凍冰冰，周圍又好黑。It was freezing cold and very dark.
嗰陣我完全唔熟悉附近嘅環境。I did not know the neighborhood at all back then.
我亦因為軟件公司份新工而覺得好緊張。I was also very nervous about my new job at the software company.
而家我好清楚我哋喺邊度。Now I know exactly where we are.
我認得附近嘅麵包舖同埋廣場。I recognize the local baker and the square.
巴士停站，有更多人上車。The bus stops, and more people get on.
我笑一笑，挨落座位度。I smile and lean back in my seat.
我準備好喺寫字樓過美好嘅一日。I am ready for a good day at the office.
office routine
Storyboard: At the office, Maya sits down with Eva to look at the new layout for their big digital project. They quickly fix a small visual error on the screen together, easily chatting as they work through their comfortable, everyday routine.
我坐喺辦公室自己張寫字枱度。I sit at my desk in the office.
Eva 攞住杯咖啡行過嚟我度。Eva comes over to me with a cup of coffee.
我哋今個禮拜合作得好緊密。We are working closely together this week.
佢問：「你準備好睇下個新版面未呀？」"Are you ready to look at the new layout?" she asks.
我答：「係呀，等我開一開個程式先。」"Yes, I just need to open the program," I answer.
我哋一齊望住個大螢幕。We look at the large screen together.
呢個大型嘅數碼項目終於睇落唔錯。The big digital project finally looks good.
但係 Eva 突然見到角落頭有個小錯誤。But suddenly Eva sees a small error in the corner.
有粒藍色嘅掣有啲太過偏左。A blue button is a bit too far to the left.
佢話：「你睇嗰度，個位置唔啱呀。」"Look there, the position is wrong," she says.
我話：「你講得啱，我要將佢移去右邊。」"You are right, I must move it to the right," I say.
我好快咁寫咗幾行程式碼。I quickly write a few lines of code.
粒掣就移咗去啱嘅位置。The button moves to the right place.
Eva 笑得好親切咁話：「完美，唔該晒你幫手。」"Perfect, thank you for the help," Eva says and smiles warmly.
我哋一齊傾週末嘅計劃，有講有笑。We talk and laugh together about our plans for the weekend.
依家我同辦公室入面嘅每一個人都好熟。Now I know everyone at the office well.
thursday badminton
Storyboard: On Thursday evening, Maya heads to the sports hall for her weekly badminton session. She lunges across the court to return a difficult shot, swings her racket hard to score a tough point, and then pauses by the net to drink water and catch her breath.
依家係星期四夜晚，我喺體育館。It is Thursday evening, and I am at the sports hall.
我每個星期都會嚟呢度打羽毛球。I play badminton here every week.
我同球會嘅朋友 Sara 打緊比賽。I am playing a match against my friend Sara from the club.
場比賽好刺激，比分好接近。The match is very exciting and close.
我哋打緊最後一分。We are playing for the very last point.
佢好用力咁打個羽毛球。She hits the shuttlecock hard.
個波好快咁飛過個網，向住我飛過嚟。It flies fast over the net and over towards me.
我喺場上面跨一大步去接個波。I take a big step across the court to reach it.
我用盡全力揮動球拍。I swing my racket with all my power.
我完美咁打中個羽毛球。I hit the shuttlecock perfectly.
個波啱啱好落喺白線上面。It lands right on the white line.
呢分係我嘅，我贏咗場比賽。The point is mine, and I win the match.
Sara 隔住個網微笑住講：「打得好！」Sara smiles across the net and says, "Good match!"
「好多謝你，嗰分真係好難打，」我答。"Thank you very much, that was a difficult point," I reply.
我喺網邊企咗一陣去唞下氣。I stand by the net for a bit to catch my breath.
我流咗好多汗，需要休息下。I am sweating a lot and need a break.
我喺袋入面搵到我個紅色水樽。I find my red water bottle in the bag.
我飲咗好多凍水。I drink a lot of cold water.
喺辦公室做咗成日嘢之後，可以郁下身體感覺真係好。It is great to use my body after a long day at the office.
quiet errand
Storyboard: Taking a quiet walk on Friday afternoon, Maya drops off her heavy winter coat at the local dry cleaner. She hands over the bulky jacket, pays two hundred and fifty kroner at the counter, and steps back out onto the busy pavement.
星期五下晝，我喺陽光下靜靜哋散步。On Friday afternoon I take a quiet walk in the sun.
我拎住我件厚重嘅冬裝外套。I am carrying my heavy winter coat.
我已經完全唔需要佢喇。I do not need it at all anymore.
我喺附近嘅乾洗店門前停低。I stop in front of the local dry cleaner.
我開門行入去。I open the door and go inside.
呢間細舖頭充滿乾淨衫同番梘嘅氣味。It smells of clean clothes and soap in the little shop.
一個上咗年紀嘅男人企喺櫃枱後面。An older man is standing behind the counter.
「你好，我想乾洗呢件外套，」我話。"Hello, I would like to have this coat cleaned," I say.
我將件大外套遞過櫃枱。I hand the large jacket over the counter.
「當然可以，」男人答，然後檢查啲布料。"Of course," the man answers and examines the fabric.
「星期三就洗好，」佢話。"It will be ready on Wednesday," he says.
「總共係二百五十克朗。」"That will be two hundred and fifty kroner."
我喺袋度攞張扣賬卡出嚟。I take my debit card out of my bag.
我喺收銀台畀錢，攞到一張細細嘅黃色收據。I pay at the register and get a small yellow receipt.
「多謝晒，週末愉快，」我話。"Thank you very much, have a good weekend," I say.
「你都係，」佢微笑住答。"You too," he answers with a smile.
我將張收據放落袋入面。I put the receipt down into my bag.
然後我再次行出去繁忙嘅行人路上。Then I step out onto the busy pavement again.
我好享受喺溫暖嘅午後陽光下散步。I enjoy walking in the warm afternoon sun.
saturday cafe
Storyboard: During her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the neighborhood café, Maya confidently operates the large espresso machine. She wipes down the wooden counter and hands over steaming cups of coffee and warm pastries to the waiting customers.
星期六朝早，我喺附近嘅咖啡吧。It is Saturday morning, and I am at the local café.
我每個週末都會喺度做義工。I volunteer here every weekend.
咖啡吧已經坐滿晒人。The café is already full of people.
我企喺一張大木吧枱後面。I stand behind the large wooden counter.
我用一塊乾淨嘅布抹張吧枱。I wipe down the counter with a clean cloth.
有個後生仔行過嚟吧枱。A young man walks over to the counter.
佢話：「早晨，我想要一杯大咖啡。」"Good morning, I would like a large coffee," he says.
佢補充話：「同埋兩件熱嘅酥皮麵包，唔該。」"And two warm pastries, please," he adds.
我笑住答：「無問題，即刻嚟。」"Of course, coming right up," I answer with a smile.
我行過去嗰部大嘅特濃咖啡機度。I walk over to the large espresso machine.
我而家對呢部機已經非常之熟。I know the machine really well now.
我好快就沖咗一杯熱辣辣嘅咖啡。I quickly make a steaming hot cup of coffee.
新鮮咖啡嘅香味充滿咗呢個細細嘅空間。The smell of fresh coffee fills the small room.
之後，我將兩件熱嘅酥皮麵包裝入一個紙袋。Afterwards, I put two warm pastries in a paper bag.
我隔住吧枱將咖啡同個紙袋遞畀佢。I hand the coffee and the bag over the counter to him.
我話：「總共五十五克朗。」"That will be fifty-five kroner," I say.
佢用卡畀錢，然後接過杯咖啡。He pays with his card and accepts the coffee.
佢好開心咁話：「週末愉快。」"Have a good weekend," he says happily.
我答佢：「多謝，你都係。」"Thanks, you too," I reply.
我揮手示意排緊隊嘅下一位客上前。I wave the next customer in line forward.
cooking preparation
Storyboard: Later that afternoon, Maya stops by the local supermarket to buy fresh tomatoes, beans, and spices. She spends a relaxing hour in her small kitchen, happily chopping ingredients on a wooden board to prepare a large pot of traditional Mexican stew.
下晝晏啲嗰陣，我行落去附近嘅超級市場。Later in the afternoon, I walk down to the local supermarket.
我要去買今晚嘅餸。I need to buy groceries for tonight.
我搵到啲新鮮嘅紅番茄同埋一包豆。I find some fresh, red tomatoes and a bag of beans.
我仲需要一啲濃味嘅香料。I also need some strong spices.
我喺收銀處畀錢，然後行返屋企。I pay at the register and walk home again.
我個廚房好細，不過好溫馨。My kitchen is small, but it is very cozy.
我將啲袋放喺枱面，然後洗手。I put my bags on the table and wash my hands.
我打算整一大煲傳統嘅墨西哥燉菜。I am going to make a large batch of traditional Mexican stew.
呢個係我阿媽其中一個舊食譜。It is one of my mother's old recipes.
我攞塊木砧板同一把利刀出嚟。I take out my wooden board and a sharp knife.
我喺廚房輕輕鬆鬆咁度過咗一個鐘頭。I spend a relaxing hour in the kitchen.
企喺度切菜感覺好好。It is nice to stand and chop vegetables.
我將啲番茄同洋蔥切成細塊。I cut the tomatoes and onions into small pieces.
呢啲香味令我諗起墨西哥嘅屋企。The smells remind me of my home in Mexico.
不過而家哥本哈根呢度都係我嘅屋企。But now it is also my home here in Copenhagen.
我將全部材料放晒入爐頭上面嗰個大煲度。I put it all into a large pot on the stove.
呢味燉菜需要慢慢咁燉。The stew has to stand and simmer slowly.
我攪勻佢，然後用匙羹試吓啲醬汁嘅味。I stir it and taste the sauce with a spoon.
味道直情係完美。It tastes absolutely perfect.
我好期待同 Nina 一齊食晚餐。I am really looking forward to eating dinner with Nina.
evening dinner
Storyboard: Nina arrives for dinner, laughing as she takes off her shoes in the hallway. She hands Maya a beautiful green houseplant as a gift, remarking that the wonderful, spicy smells coming from the kitchen make her absolutely starving.
我企喺廚房嗰陣，門鐘響咗。I am standing in my kitchen when the doorbell rings.
我行出走廊，打開道門。I walk out into the hallway and open the door.
係妮娜，佢面上掛住大大嘅笑容。It is Nina, and she has a big smile on her face.
「哈囉瑪雅，多謝晒你嘅邀請，」佢話。"Hello Maya, thank you so much for the invitation," she says.
佢行入屋，一邊除鞋一邊笑。She steps inside and laughs while she takes off her shoes.
我熱情咁攬咗佢一下。I give her a warm hug.
妮娜手裡捧住一盆好靚嘅綠色盆栽。Nina is holding a beautiful, green houseplant in her hands.
「呢盆係送畀你㗎，」佢一邊講，一邊將盆栽遞畀我。"This is for you," she says and hands me the plant.
「擺喺你個客廳度就啱晒。」"It fits perfectly in your living room."

「嘩，呢度真係好香呀，」佢話。"Wow, it smells absolutely wonderful in here," she says.

「呢陣辛香味搞到我勁肚餓，」妮娜笑住講。"The spicy smell makes me absolutely starving," Nina laughs.
「好彩啲嘢食已經準備好晒，」我微笑住講。"Fortunately, the food is completely ready," I say with a smile.
我哋一齊坐喺餐枱前食飯。We sit down at the table together to eat.
family update
Storyboard: On Sunday morning, Maya sits on her sofa for a long video call with her parents, Sofía, and Diego. With Lola barking excitedly at the screen, Maya looks around her bright living room and happily shares that the city truly feels like home.
星期日下午，我坐喺客廳張梳化度。On Sunday afternoon I sit on the sofa in my living room.
陽光透過窗照入嚟，間屋非常光猛。The sun is shining through the window, and the room is beautifully bright.
我將部電腦放喺面前張檯度，準備傾一個好長嘅視像電話。I have my computer on the table in front of me for a long video call.
我喺墨西哥嘅成家人都喺螢幕度。My whole family in Mexico is on the screen.
我見到我阿爸阿媽、阿妹蘇菲亞，同埋阿哥迪亞哥。I can see my parents, my sister Sofía, and my brother Diego.
突然間，我哋隻狗蘿拉好大聲咁吠。Suddenly our dog, Lola, barks very loudly.
佢好興奮咁喺鏡頭前面跳嚟跳去。She jumps excitedly around in front of the camera.
蘇菲亞笑住話：「蘿拉好掛住你呀。」"Lola misses you," Sofía says and laughs.
「我都好掛住你哋，」我一邊講，一邊同佢哋揮手。"I miss you all too," I say and wave to them.
我阿爸問起我嘅工作同埋呢度嘅日常生活。My father asks about my job and my everyday life here.
我同佢哋分享我嘅工作、打羽毛球嘅事，同埋我個好好人嘅鄰居。I tell them about my work, my badminton, and my sweet neighbor.
我哋一齊傾咗超過一個鐘。We talk together for over an hour.
我望吓我間光猛嘅客廳。I look around my bright living room.
我望住噚日嗰盆好靚嘅綠色植物。I look at the nice green plant from yesterday.
由一月到依家，已經過咗好長嘅時間。A lot of time has passed since January.
我阿媽問：「你喺嗰度住得開唔開心呀？」"Are you happy living there?" my mother asks.
我對住佢笑得好燦爛。I smile broadly at her.
我答：「係呀。」"Yes," I answer.
「我依家真係覺得呢個城市好似屋企一樣。」"I truly feel at home in the city now."
