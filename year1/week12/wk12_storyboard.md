# Week 12 — Storyboard (Maya joins a local badminton club)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Verb + infinitive; *godt kan lide at*; *spille/dyrke*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_finding_a_club | Maya works out at the gym on Wednesdays but wants to meet people, so she looks for a social sport. She loves badminton — she played it a lot back home in Mexico — so she finds a local club online and books a trial session for Thursday evening. |
| 2 | 02_arriving_at_hall | On Thursday evening Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sound of shoes squeaking on the many wooden courts. |
| 3 | 03_renting_the_racket | Maya goes to the small hall kiosk. She tells the clerk she is there to try out the club, and pays forty kroner to rent a racket for the evening. |
| 4 | 04_playing_doubles | In the busy hall, Maya joins a doubles game with two friendly players, Kasper and Sara, and a fourth person. She has played for years, so she smashes the shuttlecock hard across the net. |
| 5 | 05_post_match_chat | The fast match finishes and the tired players walk off the court. Sara says Maya plays really well, and Maya explains that she plays a lot back home in Mexico. |
| 6 | 06_hall_cafeteria | The group sits in the small hall cafeteria to drink water and chat. Kasper asks if Maya plans to come back, and she happily promises to make it a weekly Thursday routine. |
| 7 | 07_walking_to_bus | Maya leaves the sports hall with Sara. They walk together to the bus stop in the cold winter evening, and Maya feels glad to have met fun, active locals. |
