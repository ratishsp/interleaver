# Week 4 — Storyboard (Moving into the new flat)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Prepositions of place with the dative (**in, auf, unter, neben** + *dem/der*); the placement verbs **stehen/liegen/hängen**; the definite articles **der/die/das**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_temporary_room_departure | Maya packs her last few belongings in her temporary room. She places her laptop carefully into her backpack and zips it up. She pulls her heavy suitcases out into the hallway, ready for the short journey through the building to her permanent home. |
| 2 | 02_hallway_help | Maya drags the heavy bags along the corridor. Hearing the noise, her neighbor Sabine Krüger opens her door. Addressing Maya with a polite Sie, Frau Krüger briskly offers a hand and helps carry one of the bulky suitcases to the door of the new flat. |
| 3 | 03_opening_the_door | Maya turns the key and unlocks the door to her new home—the flat. She steps inside, immediately struck by the high ceilings and the tall windows looking out onto a quiet courtyard. Frau Krüger wishes her a successful move and returns to her own flat, leaving Maya to settle in. |
| 4 | 04_hallway_inventory | Maya pulls the bags fully inside and closes the door. She takes stock of the small furnished hallway, noting where things stand and hang. A mirror hangs next to the door (neben der Tür), and a small shoe rack stands under the coat hooks (unter der Garderobe). |
| 5 | 05_setting_up_the_desk | Moving into the main living space, Maya finishes unpacking her work equipment. Now, her laptop stands ready on the wooden table (auf dem Tisch). Her reference books lie neatly next to the computer, making the space finally feel like a proper home office. |
| 6 | 06_bedroom_suitcases | Maya tackles her clothes in the small bedroom. After some organizing, her main suitcase lies open on the bed (liegen), while her shirts hang neatly in the wardrobe (in dem Schrank). She steps back and smiles, seeing her shoes standing in a row under the bed (unter dem Bett). |
| 7 | 07_finding_the_charger | Maya wants to play some music while she works, but her phone has a low battery. She searches for her charger, finally finding it lying hidden in her backpack, tucked under a thick winter sweater. |
| 8 | 08_coffee_break | Needing a quick rest, Maya takes a break in her new kitchen. She pulls a travel thermos out of her bag that she filled before leaving her old room. She pours a warm cup of coffee and stands by the tall window looking out at the bare winter courtyard, feeling relieved to finally be in her own space. |
| 9 | 09_housewarming_plant | A polite knock interrupts the quiet. Frau Krüger stands at the door holding a small potted plant as a housewarming gift. Maya thanks her warmly using Sie, and they agree the plant looks perfect standing on the wide windowsill next to the glass. |
