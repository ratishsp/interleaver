Week 12: Maya joins a badminton club

Serbian with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding a club
Storyboard: Maya works out at the gym on Wednesdays but wants to meet people, so she looks for a social sport. She loves badminton — she played it a lot back home in Mexico — so she finds a local club online and books a trial session for Thursday evening.
Idem u teretanu svake srede uveče.I go to the gym every Wednesday evening.
Volim da treniram.I like to work out.
Ali takođe želim da se bavim sportom.But I also want to play a sport.
Obožavam da igram badminton.I love to play badminton.
Igrala sam ga puno kod kuće u Meksiku.I played it a lot back home in Mexico.
Sada tražim lokalni klub.Now I am looking for a local club.
Sedim na kauču sa svojim kompjuterom.I am sitting on my couch with my computer.
Pretražujem internet.I search online.
Nalazim dobar badminton klub.I find a good badminton club.
Gledam veb-sajt kluba.I look at the club's website.
Imaju mnogo timova.They have many teams.
Igraju u četvrtak uveče.They play on Thursday evening.
Želim da zakažem probni trening.I want to book a trial session.
Nalazim formular na veb-sajtu.I find a form on the website.
Pišem svoje ime i imejl.I write my name and my email.
Biram četvrtak uveče.I choose Thursday evening.
Trening počinje u sedam uveče.The training starts at seven in the evening.
To je savršeno.It is perfect.
Radujem se što ću ponovo igrati.I look forward to playing again.
arriving at hall
Storyboard: On Thursday evening Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sound of shoes squeaking on the many wooden courts.
Četvrtak je uveče i kod kuće sam.It is Thursday evening, and I am at home.
Pakujem svoju sportsku torbu.I pack my sports bag.
Stavljam patike i odeću u torbu.I put my shoes and my clothes in the bag.
Radujem se što ću igrati večeras.I look forward to playing tonight.
Idem autobusom do velike sportske hale.I take the bus to a large sports hall.
Hladno je zimsko veče.It is a cold winter evening.
Sedim u autobusu i gledam napolje.I sit in the bus and look out.
Autobus staje i ja izlazim.The bus stops, and I get out.
Ulazim u veliku sportsku halu.I walk into the large sports hall.
Veoma je svetlo.It is very bright.
Vidim mnogo ljudi u hali.I see many people in the hall.
Oni vole da se bave sportom.They love to play sports.
Čujem glasan zvuk.I hear a loud sound.
Patike škripe na mnogim drvenim terenima.The shoes squeak on the many wooden courts.
Ljudi brzo trče i igraju zajedno.People run fast and play together.
Izgleda veoma zabavno.It looks very fun.
Spremna sam da igram.I am ready to play.
Ali prvo moram da iznajmim reket.But first I need to rent a racket.
renting the racket
Storyboard: Maya goes to the small hall kiosk. She tells the clerk she is there to try out the club, and pays forty kroner to rent a racket for the evening.
Prilazim malom kiosku u sportskoj hali.I walk over to a small kiosk in the sports hall.
Mladi čovek stoji iza pulta.A young man stands behind the counter.
Smeši se i kaže zdravo.He smiles and says hello.
„Zdravo, želim danas da igram badminton“, kažem."Hello, I want to play badminton today," I say.
„Jesi li nova ovde u klubu?“ pita on."Are you new here in the club?" he asks.
„Da, volela bih da probam da se pridružim“, odgovaram."Yes, I would like to try to join in," I answer.
„Dobro došla“, kaže čovek."Welcome," the man says.
„Da li treba da iznajmiš reket?“ pita on."Do you need to rent a racket?" he asks.
„Da, molim te, želim da iznajmim reket za večeras“, kažem."Yes please, I want to rent a racket for the evening," I say.
Pronalazi mi dobar reket.He finds a good racket for me.
„To košta četrdeset kruna“, kaže on."It costs forty kroner," he says.
Vadim svoju platnu karticu.I take out my payment card.
Plaćam četrdeset kruna.I pay the forty kroner.
Daje mi reket preko pulta.He gives me the racket over the counter.
„Mislim da je to zabavan sport“, kažem veselo."I think it is a fun sport," I say happily.
„Želim ti zaista dobru partiju.“"Have a really good game."
Zahvaljujem se i odlazim do terena.I say thank you and walk over to the courts.
playing doubles
Storyboard: In the busy hall, Maya joins a doubles game with two friendly players, Kasper and Sara, and a fourth person. She has played for years, so she smashes the shuttlecock hard across the net.
Prilazim terenu za badminton.I walk over to a badminton court.
Na terenu stoje tri osobe.Three people are standing on the court.
„Zdravo, ja sam Kasper”, kaže jedan muškarac."Hi, I am Kasper," a man says.
„A ja sam Sara”, kaže jedna žena."And I am Sara," a woman says.
Treći igrač se zove Emil.The third player is named Emil.
„Ja sam Maya”, kažem."I am Maya," I say.
„Mi obično igramo dubl”, kaže Kasper."We usually play doubles," Kasper says.
„Da li želiš da igraš sa nama?”, pita on."Do you want to play with us?" he asks.
„Da, vrlo rado”, odgovaram."Yes, I would love to," I reply.
Stvarno volim da igram badminton.I really like to play badminton.
Kasper i ja igramo protiv Sare i Emila.Kasper and I play against Sara and Emil.
Odmah počinjemo da igramo.We start to play immediately.
Sara započinje meč.Sara starts the match.
Loptica leti brzo tamo-vamo.The shuttlecock flies quickly back and forth.
Emil udara lopticu visoko u vazduh.Emil hits the shuttlecock high up in the air.
Obožavam da smečujem.I love to smash.
Skačem i snažno je smečujem preko mreže.I jump and smash it hard over the net.
Pada na njihovu stranu terena.It lands on their side of the court.
„Dobar udarac, Maya!”, viče Kasper."Good shot, Maya!" Kasper shouts.
post match chat
Storyboard: The fast match finishes and the tired players walk off the court. Sara says Maya plays really well, and Maya explains that she plays a lot back home in Mexico.
Brzi meč je završen.The fast match is over.
Zajedno silazimo sa terena.We walk off the court together.
Veoma sam umorna.I am very tired.
Kasper i Sara su takođe umorni.Kasper and Sara are also tired.
Pijemo malo vode.We drink some water.
„Veoma dobro igraš“, kaže Sara."You play very well," Sara says.
„Mnogo hvala“, kažem."Thank you very much," I say.
„Mislim da je to baš zabavno.“"I think it is so much fun."
„Da li često igraš?“ pita Kasper."Do you play often?" Kasper asks.
„Da, igram mnogo kod kuće u Meksiku“, odgovaram."Yes, I play a lot back home in Mexico," I answer.
„Obično igram sa svojom sestrom.“"I usually play with my sister."
„To je zabavan sport“, kaže Sara."It is a fun sport," Sara says.
„Volimo da igramo ovde u klubu.“"We love to play here in the club."
„Želim više da igram ovde u Danskoj“, kažem."I want to play more here in Denmark," I say.
„Definitivno si brza“, smeje se Kasper."You are definitely fast," Kasper laughs.
„Hvala, ali sada želim da sednem“, kažem."Thanks, but now I want to sit down," I say.
„Hoćemo li da idemo u kafeteriju?“ pita Kasper."Shall we go into the cafeteria?" Kasper asks.
„Da, to bih baš volela“, odgovaram."Yes, I would very much like that," I answer.
hall cafeteria
Storyboard: The group sits in the small hall cafeteria to drink water and chat. Kasper asks if Maya plans to come back, and she happily promises to make it a weekly Thursday routine.
Ulazimo u malu kafeteriju u sali.We walk into the small cafeteria in the hall.
Nalazimo sto.We find a table.
Veoma sam žedna.I am very thirsty.
Pijemo hladnu vodu i pričamo.We drink cold water and talk.
„Ovo je stvarno dobar klub”, kažem."This is a really good club," I say.
„To je dobar sport za treniranje”, kaže Kasper."It is a good sport to do," Kasper says.
Kasper me gleda.Kasper looks at me.
„Da li želiš ponovo da nam se pridružiš?”, pita on."Do you want to join again?" he asks.
„Da, vrlo rado”, odgovaram."Yes, very much," I answer.
„Želim da igram ovde svakog četvrtka”, objašnjavam."I want to play here every Thursday," I explain.
„To zvuči divno”, kaže Sara."That sounds lovely," Sara says.
„Mi uvek igramo ovde četvrtkom”, kaže Kasper."We always play here on Thursdays," Kasper says.
„Onda se vidimo sledeće nedelje”, kažem radosno."Then we'll see each other next week," I say happily.
Pijemo ostatak vode.We drink the rest of our water.
Divno je upoznavati nove ljude.It is lovely to meet new people.
walking to bus
Storyboard: Maya leaves the sports hall with Sara. They walk together to the bus stop in the cold winter evening, and Maya feels glad to have met fun, active locals.
Sara i ja izlazimo iz sportske hale.Sara and I walk out of the sports hall.
Kasno je, i hladno je zimsko veče.It is late, and it is a cold winter evening.
Ali meni je i dalje toplo posle meča.But I am still warm after the match.
Zajedno idemo do autobuske stanice.We walk over to the bus stop together.
Vetar duva, a ulica je mračna.The wind is blowing, and the street is dark.
„Da li i ti voliš da igraš badminton vikendom?“ pitam."Do you also like to play badminton at the weekend?" I ask.
„Ne, badminton igram samo četvrtkom,“ odgovara Sara."No, I only play badminton on Thursdays," Sara answers.
„Ali volim da se bavim sportom,“ kaže ona."But I like to do sports," she says.
„Obično trčim u parku,“ nastavlja ona."I usually go for a run in the park," she continues.
Ona je veoma aktivna i zabavna osoba.She is a very active and fun person.
Čekamo autobus na hladnoći.We wait for the bus in the cold.
„Moj autobus stiže za deset minuta,“ kažem."My bus comes in ten minutes," I say.
„Idemo istim autobusom,“ osmehuje se Sara."We take the same bus," Sara smiles.
Veoma mi je drago zbog toga.I am very happy about that.
Volim da razgovaram sa njom.I like to talk with her.
Kasper i Sara su zabavni i dragi ljudi.Kasper and Sara are fun and nice people.
Sada imam dobru rutinu četvrtkom.Now I have a good routine on Thursdays.
Umorna sam, ali sam dobro raspoložena.I am tired, but I am in a good mood.
Autobus konačno stiže.The bus finally arrives.
Već se radujem sledećem četvrtku.I am already looking forward to next Thursday.
