Week 7: Maya plans a busy week

planning the week
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya makes a cup of tea and opens her calendar at the kitchen table — she always plans the week here. She works through it: the bike goes to the repair shop, the salon needs a phone call, the gym is on Wednesday. She writes each one in, and February fills up.
Ir pirmdienas rīts.It is Monday morning.
Es pagatavoju tasi karstas tējas.I make a cup of hot tea.
Es sēžu pie galda virtuvē.I sit at the table in the kitchen.
Es atveru savu kalendāru.I open my calendar.
Es vienmēr plānoju savu nedēļu šeit.I always plan my week here.
Tagad ir februāris.It is February now.
Mēnesis jau ir ļoti pilns.The month is already very full.
Rīt ir otrdiena.Tomorrow is Tuesday.
Mans velosipēds diemžēl ir salūzis.My bicycle is unfortunately broken.
Rīt no rīta es iešu pie velosipēdu mehāniķa.I walk to the bicycle mechanic tomorrow morning.
Viņš atver pulksten astoņos.He opens at eight o'clock.
Es to ierakstu kalendārā.I write it in the calendar.
Šodien es arī zvanīšu frizierim.I also call the hairdresser today.
Mani mati ir krietni par garu.My hair is way too long.
Trešdien es iešu uz sporta zāli.Wednesday I go to the gym.
Es bieži trenējos trešdienās.I often train on Wednesdays.
Parasti tas ir pulksten septiņos vakarā.It is usually at seven o'clock in the evening.
Es atkal paskatos uz savu plānu.I look at my plan again.
Es dzeru savu tēju.I drink my tea.
Tagad esmu gatava jaunai nedēļai.Now I am ready for a new week.
dropping off the bike
Storyboard: On Tuesday morning at a quarter past eight, Maya wheels her bicycle to the local repair shop. She hands the bike to the mechanic, leaves the shop, and walks the rest of the way to the office.

Darbnīca ir pilna ar velosipēdiem.The workshop is full of bicycles.
Velosipēdu meistars paceļ skatienu.The bicycle mechanic looks up.
"Labrīt," viņš saka."Good morning," he says.

Viņš paņem manu velosipēdu.He takes my bicycle.

Pārsvarā es braucu ar velosipēdu nedēļas nogalē.I mostly cycle at the weekend.
Drīz pienāk mans autobuss.Soon my bus comes.
Ir skaists rīts februārī.It is a beautiful morning in February.
Es esmu darbā bez ceturkšņa deviņos.I am at work at a quarter to nine.
booking a haircut
Storyboard: During her lunch break, Maya calls a hair salon on her mobile phone. She speaks to the hairdresser and books an appointment for Thursday at half past four.
Šis ir mans pusdienu pārtraukums.It is my lunch break.
Es sēžu ēdnīcā savā darbā.I am sitting in the canteen at my work.
Vakar es biju aizņemta.I was busy yesterday.
Tāpēc es tagad zvanu frizierim.Therefore I am calling the hairdresser now.
Es paņemu savu mobilo telefonu.I take my mobile phone.
Es zvanu uz frizētavu pilsētā.I call a hair salon in the city.
Kāda sieviete atbild uz zvanu.A woman answers the phone.
"Labdien, šeit frizētava," saka sieviete."Hello, this is the hair salon," says the woman.
"Labdien, mani sauc Maya," es saku."Hello, my name is Maya," I say.
"Es gribētu pieteikt vizīti ceturtdien.""I would like to book an appointment on Thursday."
"Vienu mirkli," saka friziere."One moment," says the hairdresser.
"Vai jums ir kāds brīvs laiks pēcpusdienā?" es jautāju."Do you have an appointment in the afternoon?" I ask.
"Jā, mums ir brīvs laiks puspiecos," viņa saka."Yes, we have an available appointment at half past four," she says.
"Tas man ļoti labi der," es saku."That suits me very well," I say.
"Kāds ir jūsu telefona numurs?" jautā friziere."What is your phone number?" asks the hairdresser.
"Mans numurs ir divpadsmit, trīsdesmit četri, piecdesmit seši, septiņdesmit astoņi," es saku."My number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
"Liels paldies, tiksimies ceturtdien puspiecos," viņa saka."Thank you very much, see you on Thursday at half past four," she says.
"Tiksimies," es atbildu."See you," I reply.

Tagad es beidzot ēdu savu ēdienu.Now I finally eat my food.
evening gym

Es bieži skrienu ātri.I often run fast.
Es vienmēr klausos mūziku.I always listen to music.

hallway chat

scheduling conflict
