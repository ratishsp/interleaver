# Week 11 — Storyboard (Maya experiences Berlin weather)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Weather expressions **es ist kalt / es schneit / es regnet / es ist sonnig**; the future with **werden** for the forecast (*es wird regnen*)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_dark_morning | Maya wakes up in her Wohnung. Outside the window, the sky is pitch black and windy. She picks up her Handy from the bedside table and checks the weather app. The forecast is uninviting: it will rain today, and it will be very cold. She sighs and stays in her warm bed for five more minutes. |
| 2 | 02_dressing_for_cold | Maya gets ready for her commute to the office. She trusts the bad forecast, so she prepares carefully. She puts on her new thick, grey sweatshirt, her heavy dark green coat, and her warm yellow hat. She grabs a sturdy umbrella by the door and leaves the flat. |
| 3 | 03_sleety_commute | Maya walks to the U-Bahn station. The sky is a heavy, dark grey. A freezing wind blows down the street, and suddenly it snows a little. Quickly, the snow turns to icy rain. She is cold, but she opens her umbrella, wraps her coat tighter, and hurries down the stairs to the dry platform. |
| 4 | 04_office_forecast | Maya is at the software company, typing on her Laptop. It is Friday, so the office is relatively quiet. Her colleague Jonas sits opposite her. Outside the large office window, the grey rain continues to fall. Jonas checks the news online and announces a much better forecast for the afternoon: the clouds will leave, and it will be sunny. |
| 5 | 05_sky_clears | Maya finishes her Friday workday early at two o'clock. She steps out of the office building and opens her umbrella, expecting freezing rain. But right at that moment, the heavy clouds break apart, revealing patches of bright blue sky. Jonas and the forecast are right: the sun comes out, and the light is dazzling. Tempted by the beautiful weather, she decides to walk toward a nearby canal instead of taking the nearest U-Bahn. |
| 6 | 06_canal_walk | Maya closes her umbrella and decides to enjoy the sudden good weather. She stays by the canal instead of going straight down into the U-Bahn. The first real sunshine of the year transforms the whole city. People are suddenly everywhere, walking their dogs along the water and chatting on the public benches. |
| 7 | 07_afternoon_cake | Maya feels cheerful in the bright light and stops at a small bakery near the water. She orders a Kaffee and a slice of cake. The shop assistant tells her the price: five Euro and eighty Cents. She pays, declines the Kassenbon, and carries her afternoon snack outside. |
| 8 | 08_steps_in_the_sun | Maya finds a free spot on some wide stone steps facing the canal. She sits down, drinks her warm Kaffee, and eats her cake. It is beautifully sunny and calm. The sunshine feels surprisingly warm on her face, so she smiles, unbuttons her green coat, and pulls off her yellow hat. |
| 9 | 09_late_light | It is half past four, and Maya is back in her Wohnung. The short late-winter day is ending, and the sun is setting, but the sky is still clear and bright. She stands by the open window and watches the long, golden light stretch across the Berlin rooftops. Tomorrow it will be sunny again, and she looks forward to it. |
