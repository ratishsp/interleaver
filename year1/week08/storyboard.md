# Week 8 — Storyboard (Maya explores the city)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Imperatives (gå, drej, tag, kig); direction prepositions (til venstre,
til højre, ligeud, forbi, over, gennem); present tense review · **Target:** ~30 min

A Saturday out exploring the city. The grammar rides on action: Nina's send-off, a stranger's
directions, a café stop, a museum guard, and harbour signs. Settings stay **generic** (a big square,
the old streets, a café, a harbour, a museum) for clean n-way translation, with **ONE deliberate
landmark — the Little Mermaid statue** — as the day's highlight (a subject-earned anchor, like wk5's
smørrebrød). Maya goes out **on her own**; the recurring cast is a single light touch (Nina, sc1),
and there is **no family call** this week. Season: still winter — she dresses for the cold, but the
day is about the sights, not the weather.

| # | stem | beat |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_saturday_recommendation | Nina drops by on Saturday morning and suggests a day out exploring — the old streets and the harbour. She gives Maya the first directions: "Take bus number forty-two, and get off at the big square." (Imperatives *tag, stå af*; *ved havnen*; number *bus nummer toogfyrre* as words; the week's one cast touch) |
| 2 | 02_asking_for_directions | Maya gets off the bus at the busy square and asks a friendly older woman the way to the harbour. The woman smiles, points, and routes her through the old streets: "Walk straight ahead, and turn left at the bakery." (Imperatives *gå, drej*; directions *ligeud, til venstre*; sends her via the old streets → sc3) |
| 3 | 03_the_old_street | Maya follows the directions down a charming old street toward the water, narrating her route as she walks: past a tall church, over a small bridge, and through a little park. (Direction prepositions *ned ad, forbi, over, gennem*; solo) |
| 4 | 04_coffee_break | After a lot of walking, Maya stops at a small café for a break. She orders at the counter: "I would like a coffee and a cinnamon roll." The man points to a free table: "Sit by the window." She bites into the warm *kanelsnegl* and watches the street outside. (a pacing break; review *jeg vil gerne have*; a light imperative *sæt dig*; *kanelsnegl*) |
| 5 | 05_the_harbour_museum | Maya reaches the harbour and goes into the museum, buying one ticket. The guard shows her the layout — "Go down the stairs, and turn right" — and points her onward afterwards: walk along the water to see the Little Mermaid. (Imperatives *gå ned, drej*; direction *til højre*; the guard bridges to sc6) |
| 6 | 06_the_little_mermaid | Maya walks along the harbour, following the signs, to the famous Little Mermaid — the day's highlight. She finds the small bronze figure on her rock by the water, among the other visitors, takes a photo, and walks home at the end of a full day. (the one deliberate landmark; directions *langs, forbi*; a concrete close, no "happy" tag) |
