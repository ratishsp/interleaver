Week 27: Maya handles her digital admin

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

tuesday admin
Storyboard: Maya clears her small kitchen table on a quiet Tuesday evening. She realizes she needs to update her payment details on her electricity provider's website, a boring digital chore she usually puts off until the last minute.
星期二夜晚，我間公寓入面好平靜。It is a quiet Tuesday evening in my apartment.
我執好廚房嗰張細枱。I clear my small kitchen table.
啲碟洗乾淨晒，張枱亦都抹好。The plates are washed, and the table is wiped down.
喺辦公室做咗成日嘢之後，我享受住呢一份寧靜。I enjoy the quiet after a long day at the office.
但係我突然諗起一件重要嘅事。But suddenly I remember an important task.
我需要喺電力公司嘅網站上面更新我嘅聯絡資料。I need to update my contact details on my electricity provider's website.
我收到一封佢哋寄嚟嘅提醒電郵。I have received an email with a reminder from them.
訊息入面寫住：「你嘅資料必須喺星期三之前更新。」In the message it says: "Your details must be updated before Wednesday."
咁已經係聽日啦！That is already tomorrow!
呢件真係一件好悶嘅電子差事。It is a really boring digital chore.
我通常都會將呢類嘢拖到最後一刻。I usually put off that kind of thing until the last minute.
事實上，我成日都會完全唔記得咗，直到收到警告為止。Actually, it often gets completely forgotten until I get a warning.
但係今晚我想提前搞掂。But tonight I want to be ahead of things.
我望住張吉枱一陣。I look at the empty table for a moment.
我部電腦喺走廊出面個返工袋入面。My computer is in my work bag out in the hallway.
我而家完全冇心機去攞佢出嚟開機。I do not have the energy to fetch and start it now.
好彩，我通常都係用手機嚟處理呢啲小任務。Fortunately, I usually just use my phone for the small tasks.
我嘆咗一聲大氣，由褲袋度攞部手機出嚟。I take the phone out of my pocket with a heavy sigh.
而家只係需要快啲搞掂佢。Now it just needs to be over and done with.
digital form
Storyboard: Settling on the sofa, she opens the utility portal on her phone. She reads the on-screen instructions stating that all required fields must be completed, then carefully types her phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight.
我攞住部手機坐喺梳化度。I sit down on the sofa with my phone.
我通常會喺夜晚處理呢啲嘢。I usually take care of that sort of thing in the evening.
我打開電力公司嘅網站。I open the website for my electricity provider.
希望唔會搞太耐。Hopefully it will not take too long.
首先，我要登入我嘅戶口。First, I must log in to my account.
我撳個選單，揀「更新個人資料」。I click on the menu and choose "update profile".
螢幕顯示咗一張電子表格。A digital form is shown on the screen.
我仔細噉睇吓啲指示。I read the instructions carefully.
最頂寫住：「必須填寫所有必填欄位。」"All required fields must be filled out," it says at the top.
段字繼續寫住：「更改會自動儲存。」"Changes are saved automatically," the text continues.
我拉落去聯絡資料嗰一欄。I scroll down to the field for contact information.
我打入我新嘅丹麥號碼。I type in my new Danish number.
我自己大聲讀出啲數字，確保無錯。I say the numbers out loud to myself to be sure.
「十二、三十四、五十六、七十八。」"Twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight."
我再檢查多一次螢幕上面嘅數字。I check the numbers on the screen one more time.
睇落全部都啱，所以我撳「確認」。Everything looks correct, so I press approve.
delayed code
Storyboard: A system prompt states that a one-time security code will be sent via text message to verify her identity. Maya watches her blank screen, slightly annoyed because these automated codes usually arrive instantly.
我部手機螢幕上面彈出一個新訊息。A new message appears on my phone screen.
上面寫住要驗證我嘅身分。It says that my identity must be verified.
「一次性驗證碼會透過短訊發送，」系統寫住。"A one-time code will be sent via text message," the system writes.
我睇住螢幕底部嘅指示。I read the instructions at the bottom of the screen.
「請稍候。」"Please wait."
「請勿關閉頁面。」"Do not close the page."
我望住部手機喺度等。I look at my phone and wait.
個螢幕依然空白，咩都冇發生。The screen remains blank, and nothing happens.
過咗十秒。Ten seconds pass.
過咗二十秒。Twenty seconds pass.
我自己靜靜哋嘆咗口氣。I sigh quietly to myself.
我開始覺得有啲忟。I am starting to get a little annoyed.
呢啲自動驗證碼通常即刻就會收到。These automated codes usually arrive immediately.
正常嚟講只需要一秒。Normally it only takes a second.
但係我個收件箱依然係空嘅。But my inbox is still empty.
我睇睇手機上面嘅時間。I check the clock on the phone.
可能今晚個系統慢啲啩？Maybe the system is just slow this evening?
我決定等多一分鐘。I decide to wait one more minute.
如果個驗證碼再唔發送過嚟，我就要再試一次。If the code is not sent soon, I will have to try again.
successful refresh
Storyboard: Following the troubleshooting instructions, she taps the link to request a new code, and her phone finally buzzes. She inputs the digits and smiles when a green banner appears, declaring that her profile is successfully updated.
我再望住個螢幕。I look at the screen again.
個驗證碼仲未有。The code is still missing.
我平時用個系統都冇問題。I do not usually have problems with the system.
我睇吓網站上面嘅指示。I read the instructions on the website.
上面寫住：「如果收唔到驗證碼，可以索取新嘅驗證碼。」It says: "If the code is not received, a new code can be requested."
我撳網頁最底嗰條連結。I press the link at the bottom of the page.
嗰度有一段簡短嘅訊息：「請稍候。」There is a short message: "Please wait."
過咗一陣，我部手機喺枱面震咗一下。A moment later, my phone vibrates on the table.
終於有一條新訊息傳咗過嚟。A new message has finally been sent.
我打開個訊息去睇我嘅新驗證碼。I open the message to read my new code.
係四、九、二、七。It is four, nine, two, seven.
我好快咁將啲數字打入個空白欄度。I quickly type the numbers into the empty field.
之後，我撳「確認」掣。Afterwards, I press the button "Approve".
個網頁即刻更新咗。The page is updated immediately.
螢幕上面顯示咗一條好大嘅綠色橫額。A large green banner is shown on the screen.
啲字寫住：「你嘅個人檔案已經更新。」The text says: "Your profile is now updated."
我笑咗笑，然後閂咗個網頁。I smile and close the website down.
搞掂呢啲實際嘅事情，感覺總係好好。It is always nice when those kinds of practical things are sorted.
nina delivery
Storyboard: A quick knock interrupts her thoughts; she opens the door to find Nina holding a small cardboard box. Nina explains that the mail carrier accidentally delivered Maya's new coffee beans to her apartment earlier in the afternoon.
我將部電話放喺枱面，然後放鬆下。I put my phone on the table and relax.
突然間，有人急促咁敲我道門。Suddenly there is a quick knock on my door.
咁樣打斷咗我嘅思緒。It interrupts my thoughts.
我喺梳化度起身，行出去玄關。I get up from the sofa and walk out into the hallway.
當我打開門，妮娜就企咗喺出面走廊。When I open the door, Nina is standing out in the hall.
佢手上攞住一個細嘅紙皮箱。She has a small cardboard box in her hands.
「嗨，瑪雅，我有件包裹畀你，」佢笑住講。"Hi Maya, I have a package for you," she says and smiles.
佢將個紙箱交畀我。She hands me the box.
「呢件包裹係今日下晝早啲時候送到我嗰度嘅，」妮娜解釋。"It was delivered to my place earlier this afternoon," Nina explains.
「郵差應該係唔小心搞錯咗我哋兩戶。」"The mail carrier must have accidentally mixed up our doors."
我望下個紙箱，見到上面寫住我個名。I look at the box and see my name on it.
「哦！」"Oh!"
「係我新買嘅咖啡豆啊！」"It is my new coffee beans!"
「我通常都係喺網上買嘅，」我同佢講。"I usually buy them online," I tell her.
「平時啲包裹都係放喺樓下大堂嘅，」妮娜話。"Normally packages are left down in the lobby," Nina says.
「係啊，通常都會放喺嗰度，」我答。"Yes, they usually get put there," I reply.
我帶住燦爛嘅笑容接過個紙皮箱。I accept the cardboard box with a big smile.
佢已經散發出一陣淡淡嘅烤咖啡香味。It already smells faintly of roasted coffee.
「非常多謝你攞過嚟畀我，」我話。"Thank you so much for bringing it over," I say.
practical advice
Storyboard: Maya takes the box with a smile and mentions she just spent the last twenty minutes fighting with the utility website. Nina laughs and advises her to make sure her confirmation receipt is downloaded, noting that the provider's portal is infamous for deleting old messages.
我將個箱放喺地下，然後再次多謝佢。I put the box on the floor and thank her again.
「唔使客氣，」Nina 答。"You are welcome," Nina replies.
我同佢講，今晚個平台有啲慢。I tell her that the portal was a bit slow tonight.
Nina 笑住話：「係呀，通常都好慢。」"Yes, it usually is very slow," Nina laughs.
佢問我有冇儲存到份確認信。She asks if my confirmation has been saved.
佢建議話：「記住一定要下載你份確認信。」"Always remember to download your confirmation," she advises.
我問佢點解咁重要。I ask her why that is important.
Nina 解釋話佢哋網站經常會刪除舊嘅訊息。Nina explains that old messages are often deleted on their website.
其實過一段短時間之後，系統就會自動刪除佢哋。The system actually deletes them automatically after a short time.
我點點頭，再次喺手機打開個平台。I nod and open the portal on my phone again.
我搵到個檔案，然後即刻下載落嚟。I find the file, and it is immediately downloaded.
我鬆一口氣咁話：「咁份文件就安全儲存好喇。」"Then the document is safely saved," I say relieved.
Nina 開心咁笑，祝我有個愉快嘅夜晚。Nina smiles happily and wishes me a good evening.
我都一樣咁祝佢。I wish her the same.
佢沿住走廊行，而我就閂埋門。She walks down the hallway, and I close my door.
有個咁聰明又樂於助人嘅鄰居真係好。It is really lovely to have a smart and helpful neighbor.
gym prep

佢平時會擺喺梳化，但而家要充電。It usually lies on the sofa, but now it needs to charge.
