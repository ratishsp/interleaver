Week 2: First Day & Registration

Estonian with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning commute
Storyboard: Maya walks briskly through the chilly morning air, looking for her new workplace. She checks the large numbers on the buildings along the street until she spots the software company's logo next to number forty-four.
Kopenhaagenis on hommik.It is morning in Copenhagen.
On jaanuar ja on väga külm.It is January, and it is very cold.
Ma olen veidi närvis.I am a little nervous.
Täna on suur päev.Today is a big day.
See on minu jaoks uus algus.It is a new start for me.
See on mu esimene päev uuel töökohal.It is my first day at my new job.
Ma sõidan tööle bussiga.I take the bus to work.
Ma väljun õiges peatuses.I get off at the right stop.
Nüüd kõnnin viimase lõigu.Now I walk the last part.
Kus on kontor?Where is the office?
Ma vaatan suuri hooneid.I look at the large buildings.
Igal hoonel on number.Each building has a number.
Siin on number kolmkümmend kaheksa.Here is number thirty-eight.
Ja siin on number nelikümmend.And here is number forty.
Ma kõnnin natuke edasi.I walk a little further.
Siin on number nelikümmend kaks.Here is number forty-two.
Ja seal see ongi!And there it is!
Siin on number nelikümmend neli.Here is number forty-four.
Ma näen suurt logo.I see a large logo.
Minu uus tarkvarafirma on siinsamas.My new software company is right here.
Mul on hea tunne.I have a good feeling.
Ma olen täiesti valmis.I am completely ready.
meeting peter
Storyboard: Inside the bright office, a smiling colleague introduces himself as Peter. He shakes her hand, hands her a new laptop, and leads her through the open-plan workspace to her assigned desk.
Ma olen nüüd uues kontoris.I am now inside the new office.
See on suur ja valgusküllane ruum.It is a large and bright room.
Sõbralik mees seisab ukse juures.A friendly man stands by the door.
Ta naeratab mulle.He smiles at me.
"Tere tulemast!" ütleb ta."Welcome!" he says.
"Minu nimi on Peter.""My name is Peter."
Ma surun ta kätt.I shake his hand.
"Tere, Peter, minu nimi on Maya," ütlen ma."Hi Peter, my name is Maya," I say.
Peter on kolleeg.Peter is a colleague.
Me kõnnime koos läbi kontori.We walk together through the office.
"Sul on siin kirjutuslaud," ütleb Peter."You have a desk here," says Peter.
Kirjutuslaual on number kuusteist.The desk has number sixteen.
"Ja sul on arvuti," ütleb ta."And you have a computer," he says.
Ta annab mulle uue sülearvuti.He gives me a new laptop.
Ma vaatan oma uut kohta.I look at my new spot.
Mul on tool ja suur ekraan.I have a chair and a large monitor.
"Suur tänu, Peter," ütlen ma."Thank you very much, Peter," I say.
Ma olen valmis oma esimeseks tööpäevaks.I am ready for my first workday.
the phone shop
Storyboard: On her lunch break, Maya ducks into a telecom shop to buy a local SIM card. She hands the clerk her phone, and he activates it, reading her brand-new local number aloud as she saves it.
Mul on lõunapaus.It is my lunch break.
Ma olen telefonipoes.I am in a phone shop.
Mul on telefon Mehhikost.I have a phone from Mexico.
Müüja on noor mees.The clerk is a young man.
"Kas teil on Taani SIM-kaart?" küsin ma."Do you have a Danish SIM card?" I ask.
"Jah, muidugi," vastab ta."Yes, of course," he answers.
Ma annan talle oma telefoni.I give him my phone.
Ta paneb SIM-kaardi telefoni.He puts the SIM card in the phone.
Telefon on nüüd valmis.The phone is ready now.
"Teie uus number on kaksteist, kolmkümmend neli, viiskümmend kuus, seitsekümmend kaheksa," ütleb ta."Your new number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," he says.
Mul on kotis märkmik.I have a notebook in my bag.
Ma kirjutan numbri üles.I write the number.
"Kaksteist, kolmkümmend neli, viiskümmend kuus, seitsekümmend kaheksa," kordan ma."Twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I repeat.
"Jah, täpselt," ütleb müüja."Yes, exactly," says the clerk.
"Aitäh abi eest," ütlen ma."Thank you for the help," I say.
Mul on nüüd Taani number!I have a Danish number now!
Ma panen telefoni taskusse.I put the phone in my pocket.
queue ticket
Storyboard: The next morning, Maya enters the crowded public registration office. She presses a button on a red terminal, pulls a paper ticket, and checks her high number before finding a seat to wait.
On järgmine hommik.It is the next morning.
Ma olen täna suures registreerimisbüroos.I am at the large registration office today.
Siin on palju inimesi.There are many people in here.
Mul on pass ja paberid kotis.I have my passport and my papers in my bag.
Kõik on minu registreerimiseks valmis.Everything is ready for my registration.
Ma seisan punase masina juures.I stand by a red machine.
Ma vajutan nuppu.I press a button.
Masin annab mulle väikese numbripileti.The machine gives me a small number slip.
Mis on minu number?What is my number?
Ma vaatan paberit.I look at the paper.
Mul on number viiskümmend üheksa.I have number fifty-nine.
See on üsna suur number.It is a rather high number.
Suur ekraan seinal näitab numbrit.A large screen on the wall shows a number.
Ekraan näitab ainult numbrit kakskümmend üks.The screen only shows number twenty-one.
Mul on palju aega.I have plenty of time.
Ma leian vaba tooli.I find an empty chair.
Ma istun oma paberitega maha.I sit down with my papers.
Nüüd ma ootan oma korda.Now I wait for my turn.
registration
Storyboard: The screen chimes with her number, and Maya steps up to the counter. She volunteers her name and age to the clerk, hands over her passport, and he processes the paperwork for her private national ID number.
Ekraan näitab minu numbrit.A screen shows my number.
Number on viiskümmend üheksa.The number is fifty-nine.
Ma lähen leti juurde.I walk up to the counter.
Leti taga istub mees.A man sits behind the counter.
"Tere hommikust," ütleb ta."Good morning," he says.
"Tere hommikust," vastan ma."Good morning," I reply.
"Minu nimi on Maya ja ma olen kolmkümmend üks aastat vana," ütlen ma."My name is Maya, and I am thirty-one years old," I say.
"Tere tulemast, Maya," ütleb ta."Welcome, Maya," he says.
"Kas teil on pass kaasas?" küsib ta."Do you have your passport?" he asks.
"Jah, mul on pass ja paberid kaasas," vastan ma."Yes, I have my passport and my papers," I answer.
"Palun," ütlen ma."Here you go," I say.
Ta vaatab pabereid.He looks at the papers.
Ta trükib oma arvutis.He types on his computer.
Ma saan Taani isikukoodi.I get a Danish personal identification number.
Number on privaatne ja väga tähtis.The number is private and very important.
Ta annab mulle uue kaardi.He gives me a new card.
"Palun, siin on teie kaart," ütleb ta."Here you go, here is your card," he says.
"Nüüd olete Taanis registreeritud.""Now you are registered in Denmark."
Ma naeratan ja võtan kaardi.I smile and take the card.
Nüüd olen ma osa Taanist.Now I am a part of Denmark.
bakery celebration
Storyboard: Heading home from the registration office, Maya stops at a neighbourhood bakery to celebrate being officially registered at last. She points to a cinnamon pastry in the glass display, asks the price, and taps her card to pay.
Ma olen jälle tänaval.I am out on the street again.
Kõik on lõpuks paigas.Everything is finally in place.
On aeg väikeseks tähistamiseks.It is time for a little celebration.
Ma näen nurgal väikest pagariäri.I see a little bakery on the corner.
Ma lähen sisse.I go inside.
Lõhnab imeliselt.It smells lovely.
Pagar seisab leti taga.A baker stands behind the counter.
"Tere," ütleb pagar."Hello," the baker says.
"Tere," vastan ma."Hello," I reply.
Ma osutan klaasi taga olevale küpsetisele.I point to a pastry behind the glass.
"Ma soovin ühte kaneelirulli," ütlen ma."I would like a cinnamon pastry," I say.
"Jah, muidugi," ütleb ta."Yes, of course," he says.
"Kui palju see maksab?" küsin ma."What does it cost?" I ask.
"See maksab kakskümmend viis krooni," vastab pagar."It costs twenty-five kroner," the baker replies.
Minu rahakott on mu kotis.I have my wallet in my bag.
Ma võtan välja oma pangakaardi.I take out my debit card.
Ma maksan oma kaneelirulli eest.I pay for my cinnamon pastry.
Pagar annab mulle väikese koti.The baker gives me a small bag.
Nüüd on mul kaneelirull oma väikeseks tähistamiseks.Now I have a cinnamon pastry for my little celebration.
swapping numbers
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her apartment building with her pastry bag and bumps into her neighbour Nina by the front door. They chat warmly and swap phone numbers so they can stay in touch; Nina reads out hers for Maya to save.
Ma seisan oma korteri juures.I am standing by my apartment.
Mul on kott kaneelirulliga.I have a bag with a cinnamon pastry.
Minu naaber Nina on ukse juures.My neighbour Nina is at the door.
Ta naeratab laialt ja ütleb tere.She smiles broadly and says hello.
"Tere, Maya, kas sul on nüüd Taani number?" küsib Nina."Hello Maya, do you have a Danish number now?" Nina asks.
"Jah, mul on uus telefoninumber," vastan ma."Yes, I have a new phone number," I reply.
"See on hea," ütleb ta."That is good," she says.
Mul on telefon käes.I have my phone in my hand.
"Mis sinu number on?" küsin ma."What is your number?" I ask.
"Minu number on kaheksakümmend seitse, kuuskümmend viis, nelikümmend kolm, kakskümmend üks," ütleb Nina."My number is eighty-seven, sixty-five, forty-three, twenty-one," Nina says.
Ma kirjutan tema numbri oma telefoni.I write her number on my phone.
"Ja minu number on kaksteist, kolmkümmend neli, viiskümmend kuus, seitsekümmend kaheksa," ütlen ma."And my number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
Nina salvestab minu numbri oma telefoni.Nina saves my number on her phone.
Nüüd on meil kontakt.Now we are in touch.
Me läheme koos sisse.We go inside together.
Ma olen oma uue sõbra üle väga rõõmus.I am very happy about my new friend.
