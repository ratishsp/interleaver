Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
星期五晚上，妮娜敲了我的门。It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
她带着她的狗，路德维希。She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.
这个周末他要住在我家。He is going to stay with me for the weekend.
妮娜微笑着把狗绳交给我。Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
“太感谢你的帮忙了，玛雅，”她说。"Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
“完全没问题，”我高兴地回答。"It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
她向我交代了一些关于他的事情。She explains a few things to me about him.
“早上七点和晚上六点喂他吃东西，”妮娜说。"Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
“还有记住，不能给他吃人类的食物。”"And remember, he must not have human food."
我笑了，低头看着这只小狗。I laugh and look down at the little dog.
“不行，路德维希，我的晚饭不是给你的，”我对他说道。"No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
妮娜也笑了，指着门。Nina laughs too and points at the door.
“靠近大路时，一定要一直用狗绳牵着他，”她说。"Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
“在公园里他可以自由奔跑。”"In the park he is allowed to run freely."
我点点头。I nod.
“别担心，我会好好照顾他的。”"Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
妮娜拥抱了我，向我们道别。Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
她关上门，只剩下我和路德维希站在那里。She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
他抬头看着我，摇着尾巴。He looks up at me and wags his tail.
我已经非常喜欢他了。I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll
Storyboard: Before bed, Maya takes Ludwig for a short evening walk around the neighborhood. When he suddenly sniffs at a discarded food wrapper on the pavement, she gently commands him to leave it and pulls him away, reminding him that he must not eat trash.
现在是星期五深夜。It is late Friday evening.
睡觉前，我带路德维希出去。Before we go to bed, I take Ludwig out.
我们在附近简单散散步。We go for a short walk in the neighborhood.
现在的街道非常安静。The streets are very quiet now.
路德维希乖乖地走在我旁边。Ludwig walks nicely next to me.
突然它停了下来。Suddenly he stops.
它在人行道上急切地嗅着什么东西。He sniffs eagerly at something on the pavement.
那是一个装着食物残渣的脏袋子。It is a dirty bag with food scraps.
它张开嘴想去叼它。He opens his mouth to take it.
“不行，放下！”我坚决地对它说。"No, drop it!" I say firmly to him.
我轻轻地把它从袋子旁拉开。I gently pull him away from the bag.
“垃圾不是食物，路德维希，”我解释道。"The trash is not food, Ludwig," I explain.
“过来，路德维希，”我叫它。"Come here, Ludwig," I call to him.
它放下袋子，抬头看着我。He drops the bag and looks up at me.
它很快开始摇尾巴。He quickly begins to wag his tail.
“乖狗狗，”我说着，摸了摸它。"Good boy," I say and pet him.
我们继续沿着街道散步。We continue our walk down the street.
现在我们只需要回家睡觉了。Now we just need to go home and sleep.
saturday breakfast
Storyboard: Early Saturday morning, Ludwig nudges Maya awake. Maya prepares his breakfast, gently telling him to sit and wait while she fills the bowl, before finally petting him and telling him to eat it.
现在是星期六早上七点。It is Saturday morning at seven o'clock.
我还在熟睡。I am still sleeping heavily.
突然，我感觉手臂上有个冰凉的东西。Suddenly I feel something cold on my arm.
我睁开眼睛，看到了路德维格。I open my eyes and see Ludwig.
他用鼻子蹭了蹭我。He nudges me with his snout.
“早安，路德维格，”我说着起了床。"Good morning, Ludwig," I say and get up.
我们一起走到厨房。We go out into the kitchen together.
他非常饿，跳起来扑到我身上。He is very hungry and jumps up on me.
“别急，路德维格，”我对他说。"Easy now, Ludwig," I say to him.
“坐下，”我平静地说。"Sit," I say calmly.
他看着我，在地板上坐了下来。He looks at me and sits down on the floor.
我拿起狗粮袋并把它打开。I take the bag of dog food and open it.
我把狗粮倒进他的碗里。I pour the food into his bowl.
他站了起来，因为他想马上开吃。He gets up because he wants it immediately.
“不行，等一下，”我说。"No, wait," I say.
他又坐了下来，乖乖地等着。He sits down again and waits nicely.
我把碗放在他面前。I put the bowl down in front of him.
我轻轻地摸了摸他的头。I gently pet him on the head.
“来，吃吧，”我微笑着说。"Here you go, eat," I say with a smile.
他摇着尾巴，开始吃了起来。He wags his tail and begins to eat.
a morning together
Storyboard: Saturday morning, with the café shift she arranged off in advance so Ludwig is not left alone, Maya has the morning free. she and Ludwig enjoy it together — she throws his ball and calls him back, gives him a good fuss — happy she chose to stay with him.
吃完早饭后，我有很多时间。After breakfast I have plenty of time.
星期六我通常会在一家咖啡馆做义工。I normally volunteer at a café on Saturdays.
但是路德维希不能单独待着。But Ludwig should not be alone.
幸好今天我在咖啡馆休息。Fortunately I have the day off from the café today.
上周我就跟他们说好了。I arranged it with them last week.
现在，路德维希和我可以一起度过整个上午。Now Ludwig and I have the whole morning together.
我们走到我住的大楼后面的院子里。We go out into the courtyard behind my building.
我拿出他的小球。I take out his little ball.
“看这个球，路德维希！”我对他说。"Look at the ball, Ludwig!" I say to him.
我把它扔到草地上。I throw it across the grass.
“去捡回来！”我喊道。"Fetch it!" I shout.
他跑得很快，接住了球。He runs fast and catches the ball.
“过来！”我叫道。"Come here!" I call.
他开心地跑回我身边。He runs happily back to me.
“把它给我，”我温柔地说。"Give it to me," I say gently.
他松开球，我摸了摸他。He lets go of the ball, and I pet him.
“你真是只乖狗狗，”我笑着说。"You are a good dog," I say and smile.
我又扔了一次，他又把它捡了回来。I throw it again, and he fetches it.
整个周末我都必须好好照顾他。I must take good care of him all weekend.
我很高兴我留在家里陪他。I am very happy that I stayed at home with him.
the hundeskov
Storyboard: Saturday afternoon, Maya walks with Ludwig to a local hundeskov (dog forest). Once inside the safe, gated area, she unclips the lead, happily telling him that he may run free now, and throws a stick for him to fetch.
路德维希在我旁边走得很快。Ludwig walks quickly next to me.
我们正在去当地的一个狗狗森林。We are on our way to a local dog forest.
今天下午的阳光很好。The sun is shining nicely this afternoon.
他有些急切地拽着牵引绳。He pulls a little eagerly on the leash.
“等一下，”我坚定地对他说。"Wait," I say firmly to him.
“来，我们好好走。”"Come, let us walk nicely."
我们终于到了大门前。We finally arrive at the large gate.
狗狗森林有围栏，非常安全。The dog forest is fenced and completely safe.
我们走进去，然后我关上了身后的门。We go in, and I close the gate behind us.
我蹲下来看着他。I crouch down and look at him.
我慢慢地从他的项圈上解下牵引绳。I slowly unclip the leash from his collar.
“现在你可以自由奔跑了，”我微笑着说。"Now you may run freely," I say with a smile.
他高兴地看着我，然后飞快地跑开了。He looks happily at me and runs off quickly.
我在地上找到了一根不错的树枝。I find a good stick on the ground.
“看这里，路德维希！”我大声喊道，并把它拿给他看。"Look here, Ludwig!" I shout and show it to him.
我把树枝远远地扔进了树丛中。I throw the stick far away in among the trees.
“把它捡回来！”我大声说。"Fetch it!" I say loudly.
他跑到树枝那里，把它叼在嘴里。He runs over to the stick and takes it in his mouth.
“回来，”我一边呼唤，一边拍手。"Come back," I call and clap my hands.
他真是一只乖狗狗。He is really a good dog.
gentle recall
Storyboard: Ludwig spots a bird and wanders off toward the trees, completely ignoring Maya's loud commands to stop. Realizing yelling does not work, Maya crouches down with a treat, softly calling him over; he quickly runs to her, and she tells him to sit before clipping the leash back on him.

family video call
