Week 18: Maya gets a spring allergy

waking up unwell
Storyboard: On Thursday morning, Maya wakes up and feels terrible. Her head is heavy, her eyes itch, and her throat is dry. She decides she ought to stay home from work.

Vlastne to trochu bolí, keď žmurkám.It actually hurts a little when I blink.
Skúšam sa napiť trochu vody z pohára na mojom stole.I try to drink some water from the glass on my table.
Ale hrdlo mám úplne suché.But my throat is completely dry.
Keď prehĺtam, bolí ma v hrdle.It hurts in my throat when I swallow.

Ale dnes nemám vôbec žiadnu energiu.But I have no energy at all today.
Necítim sa len unavená, ale naozaj chorá.I do not just feel tired, but really sick.
Určite by som dnes mala zostať doma a neísť do práce.I definitely ought to stay home from work today.

Vysvetľujem, že som chorá a neprídem do kancelárie.I explain that I am sick and will not be coming into the office.

calling the clinic

„Lekárska klinika, hovoríte s Hannou,“ hovorí."Medical clinic, you are speaking with Hanna," she says.

scanning health card

Vonku svieti slnko.The sun is shining outside.
Ale mala by som mať slnečné okuliare, pretože ma z toho svetla bolia oči.But I ought to wear sunglasses, because the light hurts my eyes.

waiting room boy

Sadnem si na stoličku v čakárni.I sit down on a chair in the waiting room.

explaining the pain

Priateľský lekár otvorí dvere a zavolá moje meno.A friendly doctor opens the door and calls my name.
Vojdem do jeho svetlej ordinácie.I go into his bright office.

„Ako sa dnes cítite?“ pýta sa."How are you feeling today?" he asks.

Lekár sa na mňa pozrie.The doctor looks at me.
„Kde vás to bolí?“ pýta sa milo."Where does it hurt?" he asks kindly.

„Je veľmi suché.“"It is very dry."
Lekár prikývne a pozorne počúva.The doctor nods and listens attentively.
„Moja hlava je tiež veľmi ťažká,“ pokračujem."My head is also very heavy," I continue.

„A moje oči sú úplne červené.“"And my eyes are completely red."
Stále ma svrbia.They itch all the time.
„Rozhodne by ste si mali oddýchnuť,“ hovorí."You absolutely ought to rest," he says.
Zapne malú baterku.He turns on a small flashlight.
„Dovoľte mi pozrieť sa na vás bližšie,“ hovorí."Let me take a closer look at you," he says.
spring allergy diagnosis

„Povedzte á,“ hovorí s úsmevom."Say ah," he says with a smile.
