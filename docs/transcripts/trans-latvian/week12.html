Week 12: Maya joins a badminton club

Latvian with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding a club
Storyboard: Maya works out at the gym on Wednesdays but wants to meet people, so she looks for a social sport. She loves badminton — she played it a lot back home in Mexico — so she finds a local club online and books a trial session for Thursday evening.
Es eju uz sporta zāli katru trešdienas vakaru.I go to the gym every Wednesday evening.
Man patīk trenēties.I like to work out.
Bet es arī gribu nodarboties ar sportu.But I also want to play a sport.
Man ļoti patīk spēlēt badmintonu.I love to play badminton.
Es to daudz spēlēju mājās Meksikā.I played it a lot back home in Mexico.
Tagad es meklēju vietējo klubu.Now I am looking for a local club.
Es sēžu uz sava dīvāna ar savu datoru.I am sitting on my couch with my computer.
Es meklēju internetā.I search online.
Es atrodu labu badmintona klubu.I find a good badminton club.
Es apskatu kluba mājaslapu.I look at the club's website.
Viņiem ir daudz komandu.They have many teams.
Viņi spēlē ceturtdienas vakarā.They play on Thursday evening.
Es gribu rezervēt izmēģinājuma treniņu.I want to book a trial session.
Es atrodu veidlapu mājaslapā.I find a form on the website.
Es uzrakstu savu vārdu un savu e-pastu.I write my name and my email.
Es izvēlos ceturtdienas vakaru.I choose Thursday evening.
Treniņš sākas pulksten septiņos vakarā.The training starts at seven in the evening.
Tas ir perfekti.It is perfect.
Es ar nepacietību gaidu, kad atkal spēlēšu.I look forward to playing again.
arriving at hall
Storyboard: On Thursday evening Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sound of shoes squeaking on the many wooden courts.
Ir ceturtdienas vakars, un es esmu mājās.It is Thursday evening, and I am at home.
Es krāmēju savu sporta somu.I pack my sports bag.
Es lieku savus apavus un drēbes somā.I put my shoes and my clothes in the bag.
Es ar nepacietību gaidu, kad šovakar varēšu spēlēt.I look forward to playing tonight.
Es braucu ar autobusu uz lielu sporta halli.I take the bus to a large sports hall.
Ir auksts ziemas vakars.It is a cold winter evening.
Es sēžu autobusā un skatos ārā.I sit in the bus and look out.
Autobuss apstājas, un es izkāpju.The bus stops, and I get out.
Es ieeju lielajā sporta hallē.I walk into the large sports hall.
Tā ir ļoti gaiša.It is very bright.
Es hallē redzu daudz cilvēku.I see many people in the hall.
Viņi mīl sportot.They love to play sports.
Es dzirdu skaļu troksni.I hear a loud sound.
Apavi čīkst uz daudzajiem koka laukumiem.The shoes squeak on the many wooden courts.
Cilvēki ātri skrien un spēlē kopā.People run fast and play together.
Tas izskatās ļoti jautri.It looks very fun.
Es esmu gatava spēlēt.I am ready to play.
Bet vispirms man jāiznomā rakete.But first I need to rent a racket.
renting the racket
Storyboard: Maya goes to the small hall kiosk. She tells the clerk she is there to try out the club, and pays forty kroner to rent a racket for the evening.
Es pieeju pie neliela kioska sporta hallē.I walk over to a small kiosk in the sports hall.
Jauns vīrietis stāv aiz letes.A young man stands behind the counter.
Viņš smaida un saka sveiki.He smiles and says hello.
"Sveiki, es šodien vēlos spēlēt badmintonu," es saku."Hello, I want to play badminton today," I say.
"Vai tu šeit klubā esi jauniņā?" viņš jautā."Are you new here in the club?" he asks.
"Jā, es gribētu pamēģināt piedalīties," es atbildu."Yes, I would like to try to join in," I answer.
"Laipni lūgta," saka vīrietis."Welcome," the man says.
"Vai tev vajag iznomāt raketi?" viņš jautā."Do you need to rent a racket?" he asks.
"Jā, lūdzu, es vēlos nomāt raketi šim vakaram," es saku."Yes please, I want to rent a racket for the evening," I say.
Viņš man atrod labu raketi.He finds a good racket for me.
"Tas maksā četrdesmit kronas," viņš saka."It costs forty kroner," he says.
Es izņemu savu maksājumu karti.I take out my payment card.
Es samaksāju četrdesmit kronas.I pay the forty kroner.
Viņš man iedod raketi pāri letei.He gives me the racket over the counter.
"Man šķiet, ka tas ir jautrs sporta veids," es priecīgi saku."I think it is a fun sport," I say happily.
"Lielisku spēli!""Have a really good game."
Es pasaku paldies un dodos pie laukumiem.I say thank you and walk over to the courts.
playing doubles
Storyboard: In the busy hall, Maya joins a doubles game with two friendly players, Kasper and Sara, and a fourth person. She has played for years, so she smashes the shuttlecock hard across the net.
Es pieeju pie badmintona laukuma.I walk over to a badminton court.
Laukumā stāv trīs cilvēki.Three people are standing on the court.
"Sveiki, mani sauc Kaspers," saka kāds vīrietis."Hi, I am Kasper," a man says.
"Un mani sauc Sāra," saka kāda sieviete."And I am Sara," a woman says.
Trešo spēlētāju sauc Emīls.The third player is named Emil.
"Mani sauc Maija," es saku."I am Maya," I say.
"Mēs parasti spēlējam dubultspēles," saka Kaspers."We usually play doubles," Kasper says.
"Vai tu gribi spēlēt ar mums?" viņš jautā."Do you want to play with us?" he asks.
"Jā, ļoti labprāt," es atbildu."Yes, I would love to," I reply.
Man ļoti patīk spēlēt badmintonu.I really like to play badminton.
Kaspers un es spēlējam pret Sāru un Emīlu.Kasper and I play against Sara and Emil.
Mēs uzreiz sākam spēlēt.We start to play immediately.
Sāra sāk spēli.Sara starts the match.
Volāns ātri lido šurpu turpu.The shuttlecock flies quickly back and forth.
Emīls sit volānu augstu gaisā.Emil hits the shuttlecock high up in the air.
Es dievinu gremdēt.I love to smash.
Es palecos un spēcīgi to gremdēju pāri tīklam.I jump and smash it hard over the net.
Tas nolaižas viņu laukuma pusē.It lands on their side of the court.
"Labs sitiens, Maija!" sauc Kaspers."Good shot, Maya!" Kasper shouts.
post match chat
Storyboard: The fast match finishes and the tired players walk off the court. Sara says Maya plays really well, and Maya explains that she plays a lot back home in Mexico.
Ātrā spēle ir beigusies.The fast match is over.
Mēs kopā noejam no laukuma.We walk off the court together.
Es esmu ļoti nogurusi.I am very tired.
Kaspers un Sāra arī ir noguruši.Kasper and Sara are also tired.
Mēs iedzeram nedaudz ūdens.We drink some water.
"Tu spēlē ļoti labi," saka Sāra."You play very well," Sara says.
"Liels paldies," es saku."Thank you very much," I say.
"Man tas šķiet tik jautri.""I think it is so much fun."
"Vai tu bieži spēlē?" jautā Kaspers."Do you play often?" Kasper asks.
"Jā, es daudz spēlēju mājās Meksikā," es atbildu."Yes, I play a lot back home in Mexico," I answer.
"Parasti es spēlēju ar savu māsu.""I usually play with my sister."
"Tas ir jautrs sporta veids," saka Sāra."It is a fun sport," Sara says.
"Mums ļoti patīk spēlēt šeit klubā.""We love to play here in the club."
"Es vēlos vairāk spēlēt šeit Dānijā," es saku."I want to play more here in Denmark," I say.
"Tu noteikti esi ātra," smejas Kaspers."You are definitely fast," Kasper laughs.
"Paldies, bet tagad es vēlos apsēsties," es saku."Thanks, but now I want to sit down," I say.
"Vai iesim iekšā kafejnīcā?" jautā Kaspers."Shall we go into the cafeteria?" Kasper asks.
"Jā, es to ļoti vēlētos," es atbildu."Yes, I would very much like that," I answer.
hall cafeteria
Storyboard: The group sits in the small hall cafeteria to drink water and chat. Kasper asks if Maya plans to come back, and she happily promises to make it a weekly Thursday routine.
Mēs ieejam mazajā kafejnīcā hallē.We walk into the small cafeteria in the hall.
Mēs atrodam galdiņu.We find a table.
Es esmu ļoti izslāpusi.I am very thirsty.
Mēs dzeram aukstu ūdeni un sarunājamies.We drink cold water and talk.
"Šis ir patiešām labs klubs," es saku."This is a really good club," I say.
"Tas ir labs sporta veids, ar ko nodarboties," saka Kaspers."It is a good sport to do," Kasper says.
Kaspers skatās uz mani.Kasper looks at me.
"Vai tu vēlies atkal pievienoties?" viņš jautā."Do you want to join again?" he asks.
"Jā, ļoti labprāt," es atbildu."Yes, very much," I answer.
"Es vēlos šeit spēlēt katru ceturtdienu," es paskaidroju."I want to play here every Thursday," I explain.
"Tas izklausās jauki," saka Sāra."That sounds lovely," Sara says.
"Mēs šeit vienmēr spēlējam ceturtdienās," saka Kaspers."We always play here on Thursdays," Kasper says.
"Tad tiksimies nākamnedēļ," es priecīgi saku."Then we'll see each other next week," I say happily.
Mēs izdzeram savu atlikušo ūdeni.We drink the rest of our water.
Ir jauki satikt jaunus cilvēkus.It is lovely to meet new people.
walking to bus
Storyboard: Maya leaves the sports hall with Sara. They walk together to the bus stop in the cold winter evening, and Maya feels glad to have met fun, active locals.
Sāra un es izejam no sporta zāles.Sara and I walk out of the sports hall.
Ir vēls, un ir auksts ziemas vakars.It is late, and it is a cold winter evening.
Bet man joprojām ir silti pēc spēles.But I am still warm after the match.
Mēs kopā ejam uz autobusa pieturu.We walk over to the bus stop together.
Pūš vējš, un iela ir tumša.The wind is blowing, and the street is dark.
"Vai tev arī patīk spēlēt badmintonu nedēļas nogalē?" es jautāju."Do you also like to play badminton at the weekend?" I ask.
"Nē, es spēlēju badmintonu tikai ceturtdienās," atbild Sāra."No, I only play badminton on Thursdays," Sara answers.
"Bet man patīk nodarboties ar sportu," viņa saka."But I like to do sports," she says.
"Parasti es dodos paskriet parkā," viņa turpina."I usually go for a run in the park," she continues.
Viņa ir ļoti aktīvs un jautrs cilvēks.She is a very active and fun person.
Mēs gaidām autobusu aukstumā.We wait for the bus in the cold.
"Mans autobuss atbrauks pēc desmit minūtēm," es saku."My bus comes in ten minutes," I say.
"Mēs braucam ar to pašu autobusu," Sāra pasmaida."We take the same bus," Sara smiles.
Es esmu ļoti priecīga par to.I am very happy about that.
Man patīk ar viņu sarunāties.I like to talk with her.
Kaspers un Sāra ir jautri un jauki cilvēki.Kasper and Sara are fun and nice people.
Tagad man ir laba rutīna ceturtdienās.Now I have a good routine on Thursdays.
Es esmu nogurusi, bet man ir labs garastāvoklis.I am tired, but I am in a good mood.
Autobuss beidzot ierodas.The bus finally arrives.
Es jau ar nepacietību gaidu nākamo ceturtdienu.I am already looking forward to next Thursday.
