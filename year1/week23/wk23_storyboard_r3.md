# Week 23 — Storyboard (Maya visits Eva for dinner and makes future plans)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Future *skal/vil*; *har tænkt mig at*; *synes/tror/føler at* + subordinate *at*-clause word order (needs a sentence adverb — *ikke/altid/nok* — in the clause to show)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_office_invitation | At the software company, Eva invites Maya over for dinner on Friday evening. Maya quickly checks her schedule app, gladly accepts the invitation, and notes that she intends to bring some dessert from a local bakery. |
| 2 | 02_flowers_and_bus | On Friday afternoon, Maya buys a bright bouquet of yellow tulips instead of dessert. She takes the bus to Eva's neighborhood, carefully following the map on her phone because she thinks that she will probably get lost otherwise. |
| 3 | 03_arriving_at_evas | Maya rings the doorbell, and Eva welcomes her into an apartment filled with colorful art prints, hanging spider plants, and a vintage yellow lamp. Maya cheerfully hands over the tulips, saying she hopes that Eva actually likes flowers. |
| 4 | 04_mushroom_tart_dinner | They sit down at a wooden table while Eva serves a warm mushroom tart and Maya pours sparkling water into their glasses. Tasting the first bite, Maya smiles broadly and tells her host that she thinks that the food is simply wonderful. |
| 5 | 05_kitchen_cleanup | After the meal, they carry the empty plates to the kitchen together. Leaning against the counter, Maya explains that she feels that she finally has a good daily rhythm now, and she intends to stay in the city for a long time. |
| 6 | 06_sister_visiting | They move to the comfortable sofa, where Maya opens her phone to show Eva pictures of her family dog, Lola, and her siblings. She admits she misses them, but says she believes that her sister Sofía will likely visit Europe next summer. |
| 7 | 07_art_museum_plan | Eva brings out two cups of coffee and suggests a weekend trip to a modern art museum next month. Maya pulls up her calendar app and agrees enthusiastically, saying she feels that it definitely sounds like a fun idea. |
| 8 | 08_heading_home | Standing in the hallway, Maya puts on her coat and thanks Eva for a great evening. They hug goodbye, and Maya promises that she will text Eva tomorrow so they can buy the museum tickets. |
