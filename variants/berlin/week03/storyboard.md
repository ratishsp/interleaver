# Week 3 — Storyboard (A Sunday call to Mexico)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Possessives **mein/meine, Ihr/Ihre** in the nominative; the indefinite article **ein/eine**; **wer** questions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_sunday_morning | Maya wakes up late in her flat. Her street is completely quiet. She eats a late breakfast and checks das Handy to see the time in Mexico. It is still night there, so she has a few hours to prepare for the family call. |
| 2 | 02_the_spaeti_errand | Maya puts on her warm coat and steps out into the cold January air. Because it is Sunday, the ordinary shops and supermarkets are shut. She walks down the street to a Späti—the local corner kiosk—to buy a small German treat to show her family later. |
| 3 | 03_buying_the_treat | Inside the brightly lit Späti, Maya picks out a bar of German chocolate. She goes to the counter, pays a few Euro, and turns to leave. She accidentally leaves her bag on the counter. The cashier calls out to her, using Sie to politely ask if it is her bag (Ihre Tasche). Maya thanks him, takes it, and heads back home. |
| 4 | 04_laptop_setup | Back in die Wohnung, the time for the call approaches. Maya sits at her table, opens der Laptop, and sets the bar of chocolate next to it. She checks her camera and initiates the video call. |
| 5 | 05_parents_on_screen | The screen lights up and connects to Mexico, where it is morning. Her mother and father appear and greet her warmly. Maya uses mein and meine to introduce her new desk setup, and they ask her how she is settling into the new city. |
| 6 | 06_who_is_that | A blurry figure walks behind her parents in the background. Maya asks a wer question: 'Who is that?' It is her older brother, Diego. He leans in to say good morning, and moments later, her younger sister, Sofia, joins the frame too. |
| 7 | 07_lola_the_dog | A loud bark interrupts the conversation. Her parents lift Lola, the family dog, up to the camera. Maya laughs and turns her laptop around to show Lola her flat, pointing out the space and saying, 'This is my flat' (meine Wohnung). |
| 8 | 08_the_chocolate_reveal | Maya holds up the chocolate she bought at the Späti to the camera. She explains that it is a German brand. Her family asks if she has a big supermarket nearby, and Maya uses ein and eine to describe the little kiosk just around the corner where she found the treat. |
| 9 | 09_goodbye_and_walk | They finally say their goodbyes, and Maya closes der Laptop. The flat suddenly feels very quiet. She puts her coat back on and goes for a walk along her street, feeling a touch homesick but carrying the warm voices of her family with her in the Sunday calm. |
