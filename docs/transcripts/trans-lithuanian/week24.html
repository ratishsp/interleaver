Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Lithuanian with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
Penktadienio vakaras, ir Nina pasibeldžia į mano duris.It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
Ji atsivedė savo šunį Liudvigą.She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.
Jis pasiliks pas mane savaitgaliui.He is going to stay with me for the weekend.
Nina nusišypso ir paduoda man pavadėlį.Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
„Labai ačiū už pagalbą, Maja,“ – sako ji."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
„Viskas gerai,“ – linksmai atsakau."It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
Ji man paaiškina kelis dalykus apie jį.She explains a few things to me about him.
„Duok jam maisto septintą valandą ryto ir šeštą valandą vakaro,“ – sako Nina."Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
„Ir atsimink, jam negalima duoti žmonių maisto.“"And remember, he must not have human food."
Aš nusijuokiu ir pažvelgiu žemyn į mažą šunį.I laugh and look down at the little dog.
„Ne, Liudvigai, mano vakarienė ne tau,“ – pasakau jam."No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
Nina taip pat nusijuokia ir rodo į duris.Nina laughs too and points at the door.
„Visada laikyk jį už pavadėlio, kai einate arti didelio kelio,“ – sako ji."Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
„Parke jam galima lakstyti laisvai.“"In the park he is allowed to run freely."
Aš linkteliu.I nod.
„Nesijaudink, aš juo gerai pasirūpinsiu.“"Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
Nina mane apkabina ir atsisveikina su mumis.Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
Ji uždaro duris, ir aš lieku stovėti viena su Liudvigu.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
Jis pažvelgia aukštyn į mane ir vizgina uodegą.He looks up at me and wags his tail.
Aš jau dabar jį labai pamėgau.I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll

saturday breakfast

a morning together

the hundeskov
