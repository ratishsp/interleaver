Week 5: Maya stocks her new kitchen

Estonian with English gloss, line-aligned.

empty cupboards
Storyboard: Maya stands in the kitchen, noticing that the fridge and the cupboard are completely empty. She grabs a bag and heads out, ready to buy food.
Kell on kuus.It is six o'clock.
Ma seisan köögis.I am standing in the kitchen.
Ma olen näljane.I am hungry.
Ma avan külmkapi.I open the fridge.
See on täiesti tühi.It is completely empty.
Ma vaatan kappi.I look in the cupboard.
Kapis ei ole toitu.There is no food in the cupboard.
Mul ei ole toitu.I have no food.
Mul on ainult natuke vett.I only have a little water.
Ma tahaksin nüüd toitu.I would like to have food now.
Mulle meeldivad köögiviljad.I like vegetables.
Mulle meeldib ka leib.I also like bread.
Aga mul ei ole siin midagi.But I have nothing here.
Ma leian koti.I find a bag.
Kott on punane.The bag is red.
See on ostlemiseks hea.It is good for shopping.
Ma lähen korterist välja.I walk out of the flat.
Ma sulgen ukse.I close the door.
Ma olen valmis.I am ready.
Nüüd ma lähen supermarketisse.Now I am walking to the supermarket.
produce section
Storyboard: Maya enters a supermarket and pulls a basket from a stack by the door. Because she likes carrots, she grabs a large bunch from a green crate and drops it into the basket.
Ma seisan supermarketi ees.I stand in front of the supermarket.
Supermarket on suur ja valgusküllane.The supermarket is large and bright.
Uks avaneb automaatselt.The door opens automatically.
Ma lähen poodi sisse.I go into the shop.
Poes on palju kaupu.The shop has many goods.
Sissepääsu juures on korvivirnad.There are stacks of baskets by the entrance.
Ma võtan korvi.I take a basket.
Korv on punane ja kerge.The basket is red and light.
Ma otsin köögivilju.I look for vegetables.
Osakond on kohe siin.The section is right here.
Ma näen põrandal kasti.I see a crate on the floor.
Kast on roheline.The crate is green.
Kastis on palju porgandeid.There are many carrots in the crate.
Mulle meeldivad porgandid.I like carrots.
Ma valin suure kimbu.I choose a large bunch.
Kimp näeb väga värske välja.The bunch looks very fresh.
Ma panen kimbu korvi.I put the bunch down into the basket.
Nüüd ei ole korv tühi.Now the basket is not empty.
Ma kõnnin poes edasi.I walk further into the shop.
the parsnip
Storyboard: Looking around, Maya spots a pale, unusual root. She reads a sign and learns it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she decides to buy it anyway out of curiosity.
Ma näen veidrat köögivilja.I see a strange vegetable.
Köögivili on pikk juurikas.The vegetable is a long root.
Juurikas on väga hele.The root is very pale.
See näeb välja nagu valge porgand.It looks like a white carrot.
Ma vaatan alla sildile.I look down at the sign.
Sildil on kirjas "pastinaak".It says "parsnip" on the sign.
Ma ei tunne seda köögivilja.I do not know the vegetable.
Kas see on Taani köögivili?Is it a Danish vegetable?
Mulle meeldivad köögiviljad.I like vegetables.
Mulle meeldivad porgandid.I like carrots.
Võib-olla maitseb pastinaak hästi.Maybe the parsnip tastes good.
Ma võtan pastinaagi kätte.I take the parsnip in my hand.
Juurikas on üsna suur.The root is quite big.
Ma tahaksin seda.I would like to have it.
Ma vaatan korvi.I look at the basket.
Ma panen juurika korvi.I put the root in the basket.
Ma tahaksin proovida midagi uut.I would like to try something new.
the rye bread
Storyboard: At a shelf with baked goods, Maya looks for a local staple. She lifts a loaf of rye bread, surprised by how heavy it feels, and lays the bread carefully in the basket.
Ma kõnnin leivariiuli juurde.I walk over to a shelf with bread.
Ma otsin kohalikku klassikat.I am looking for a local classic.
Ma tahaksin üht Taani rukkileiba.I would like to have a Danish rye bread.
Ma vaatan riiulit.I look at the shelf.
Ma leian ühe rukkileiva.I find a rye bread.
See on kotis.It is in a bag.
Ma vaatan kotti.I look at the bag.
Ma võtan rukkileiva.I take the rye bread.
Ma tõstan leiba.I lift the bread.
Ma olen üllatunud.I am surprised.
Leib on väga raske.The bread is very heavy.
See tundub nagu kivi!It feels like a stone!
Mulle meeldib hele leib.I like light bread.
Aga rukkileib on tume ja raske.But the rye bread is dark and heavy.
Ma asetan leiva ettevaatlikult korvi.I carefully lay the bread in the basket.
Korv on nüüd ka raske.The basket is also heavy now.
bakery counter
Storyboard: The bakery counter has a member of staff waiting. Maya catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry.
Ma lähen pagarileti juurde.I walk over to the bakery counter.
Lõhn on väga hea.The smell is very good.
Mulle meeldivad koogid.I like cakes.
Ja mulle väga meeldivad kaneelirullid.And I really like cinnamon pastries.
Pagar seisab leti taga.A baker stands behind the counter.
Pagar vaatab mind.The baker looks at me.
Ta naeratab.He smiles.
"Mida te soovite?" küsib pagar."What would you like to have?" the baker asks.
Ma osutan kaneelirullile.I point at a cinnamon pastry.
Saiake näeb maitsev välja.The pastry looks delicious.
"Ma soovin ühte kaneelirulli, palun," ütlen ma."I would like to have a cinnamon pastry, please," I say.
"Jah, muidugi," ütleb pagar."Yes, of course," the baker says.
Ta võtab saiakese.He takes the pastry.
Ta paneb saiakese kotti.He puts the pastry in a bag.
Ta ulatab koti üle leti.He hands the bag over the counter.
"Palun," ütleb ta."Here you go," he says.
Ma võtan koti.I take the bag.
"Suur tänu," ütlen ma."Thank you very much," I say.
Ma panen koti korvi.I put the bag down into the basket.
finding the milk
Storyboard: Maya searches for the milk without luck. She stops an employee to ask for help, and he points her toward the dairy section at the back.
Nüüd on mul piima vaja.Now I need milk.
Ma kõnnin mööda pikka vahekäiku.I walk down a long aisle.
Ma vaatan riiulit.I look at the shelf.
Ma näen vett ja mahla.I can see the water and the juice.
Aga ma ei leia piima.But I cannot find the milk.
Ma otsin igalt poolt.I look everywhere.
Ma näen üht meest.I see a man.
Ta töötab supermarketis.He works in the supermarket.
Ma peatan töötaja ja naeratan.I stop the employee and smile.
"Vabandage," ütlen ma."Excuse me," I say.
"Ma sooviksin piima.""I would like to have milk."
"Kus on piim?""Where is the milk?"
Mees osutab poe tagaosa poole.The man points to the back of the shop.
"Piimaosakond on seal," ütleb ta."The dairy section is down there," he says.
"Suur tänu," vastan ma."Thank you very much," I reply.
Ma lähen sinna.I walk down there.
Lõpuks leian külmkapi ja piima.I finally find the fridge and the milk.
Ma võtan paki ja panen selle korvi.I take a carton and lay it in the basket.
taste of home
Storyboard: Exploring another section, Maya hunts for a taste of Mexico because she really likes beans. She recognizes a bright can of black beans on a low shelf and reaches all the way to the back to pull it out.
Lähen edasi teise riiuli juurde.I walk on to another set of shelves.
Vaatan toitu.I look at the food.
Supermarketis on palju asju teistest riikidest.The supermarket has many things from other countries.
Otsin Mehhiko maitset.I look for a taste of Mexico.
Igatsen kodust toitu.I miss the food from home.
Mulle väga meeldivad oad.I really like beans.
Söön tihti musti ube.I often eat black beans.
Vaatan madalat riiulit.I look at a low shelf.
Riiul on veidi hämar.The shelf is a bit dark.
Aga ma näen värvilist purki.But I see a colourful can.
Purk seisab täiesti taga.The can stands all the way at the back.
See on mustade ubade purk.It is a can of black beans.
Naeratan.I smile.
Sirutan käe täiesti sisse.I reach my arm all the way in.
Tõmban purgi välja.I pull the can out.
Loen purgi pealt silti.I read the label on the can.
See on mu õhtusöögiks täiuslik.It is perfect for my dinner.
Panen purgi korvi.I put the can in the basket.
Mul on piisavalt toitu.I have enough food.
the checkout
Storyboard: Maya brings the basket to the checkout. She places the food on the belt, holds her card over the machine, and packs the bag quickly. No price is spoken or shown.
Ma lähen kassa juurde.I walk up to the checkout.
Mul on korv käes.I have the basket in my hand.
Ma panen toidu lindile.I put the food on the belt.
Kõigepealt võtan ma korvist rukkileiva.First I take the rye bread from the basket.
Siis võtan piima ja pastinaagi.Then I take the milk and the parsnip.
Ma võtan ka porgandeid ja musti ube.I also take carrots and black beans.
Lõpuks panen saiakese lindile.Finally I put the pastry on the belt.
Kassapidaja istub kassa taga.The cashier sits behind the checkout.
Ta naeratab mulle.He smiles at me.
"Tere," ütleb ta."Hello," he says.
"Tere," vastan ma."Hello," I reply.
Ta skannib toidu kiiresti.He scans the food quickly.
Ma võtan oma kaardi välja.I take my card out.
Ma hoian kaarti masina kohal.I hold the card over the machine.
Masin piiksub.The machine beeps.
"Kas te soovite tšekki?" küsib ta."Would you like to have the receipt?" he asks.
"Jah, palun, ma soovin tšekki," ütlen ma."Yes please, I would like to have the receipt," I say.
Ma avan oma koti.I open my bag.
Ma pakin koti kiiresti.I pack the bag quickly.
"Head päeva," ütleb ta."Have a good day," he says.
putting away

first home cooked meal
Storyboard: Ready to eat, Maya chops some vegetables and opens the can. She cooks dinner on the stove, finally making a proper meal in the flat — and afterwards she has the pastry from the bakery counter for dessert.

Aga pliit on hea.But the stove is good.
Ma võtan pastinaagi ja ühe porgandi.I take the parsnip and a carrot.

Ma panen toidu potti.I put the food in a pot.
Pott on pliidil.The pot is on the stove.
Nüüd on toit soe.Now the food is warm.
See lõhnab imeliselt.It smells lovely.
Ma võtan rukkileiva.I take the rye bread.
