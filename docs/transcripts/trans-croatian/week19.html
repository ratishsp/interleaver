Week 19: Maya starts a new project with the team

monday bus excitement
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya takes the bus to work. She feels excited and a little nervous because a large new software project starts today, and she will finally work closely with the entire team.
Ponedjeljak je ujutro i sjedim u autobusu.It is Monday morning, and I am sitting on the bus.
Na putu sam na posao.I am on my way to work.

Ali se također jako veselim tome.But I am also looking forward to it a lot.
Danas počinje veliki, novi softverski projekt.A large, new software project starts today.
Radim kao softverska inženjerka u tvrtki od siječnja.I have worked as a software engineer at the company since January.

Ali sada moram blisko surađivati s cijelim timom.But now I must work closely with the whole team.

Oni već dugo rade u tvrtki.They have worked at the company for a long time.
Autobus staje i još ljudi ulazi.The bus stops, and more people get on.
Gledam na sat.I look at my watch.
Osam je sati.It is eight o'clock.
Jutarnji sastanak počinje za pola sata.The morning meeting begins in half an hour.
Duboko udahnem.I take a deep breath.
Spremna sam za novi projekt.I am ready for the new project.
first morning presentation

boss project manager
Storyboard: After the morning meeting, her boss Thomas stops by Maya's desk with a part of the new project for her. He is warm and welcoming: he compliments her on her presentation and says the whole team has looked forward to having her on board. Maya feels genuinely welcomed onto the team.
Nakon jutarnjeg sastanka sjedim za svojim radnim stolom.After the morning meeting, I sit at my desk.

Ovdje radi već mnogo godina.He has worked here for many years.
"Dobro jutro, Maya", kaže s osmijehom."Good morning, Maya," he says with a smile.

Gledam u ekran svog računala.I look at my computer screen.
Osjećam se zaista dobrodošlom u timu.I feel genuinely welcome on the team.
peter software engineer
Storyboard: Maya sits down with Peter to write code. As software engineers, they solve complex problems together, and Peter jokes that he has worked on one specific error for three hours until Maya easily spots his mistake.
Sjedam za Peterov radni stol.I sit down at Peter's desk.
Gledamo u njegov veliki ekran.We look at his large screen.
Peter je također softverski inženjer.Peter is also a software engineer.
On je dobar kolega i zajedno pišemo puno koda.He is a good colleague, and we write a lot of code together.
Danas izgleda jako umorno.Today he looks very tired.
"Što radiš, Peter?" pitam."What are you doing, Peter?" I ask.
"Imam veliki problem," odgovara on."I have a big problem," he answers.
"Radim na ovoj grešci tri sata.""I have worked on this error for three hours."
Smiješim se i pažljivo gledam kod.I smile and look closely at the code.
Na poslu često zajedno rješavamo složene probleme.We often solve complex problems together in our work.
Odjednom vidim nešto čudno na ekranu.Suddenly I see something strange on the screen.
To je jako mala greška.It is a very small mistake.
"Probaj pogledati ovdje," kažem i pokazujem na ekran."Try to look here," I say and point at the screen.
"Ovdje si napravio malu grešku u tipkanju.""You have made a small typo here."
Peter dvije sekunde tiho gleda u ekran.Peter looks quietly at the screen for two seconds.
Zatim se glasno nasmije.Then he laughs loudly.
"Potpuno si u pravu," kaže Peter."You are absolutely right," Peter says.
"Radio sam tri sata, i onda je to samo jedna mala greška!""I have worked for three hours, and then it is just one small error!"
I ja se smijem, jer svi softverski inženjeri poznaju tu situaciju.I laugh too, because all software engineers know that situation.
Ispravljamo grešku i program napokon radi savršeno.We fix the error, and the program finally works perfectly.
eva digital designer

meeting the tester
