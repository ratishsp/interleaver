Week 14: Maya takes public transport across the city

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

checking the route
Storyboard: Maya looks at a travel app on her phone. She needs to go across the city to pick up a package, so she plans a route with three different types of public transport.
我今日要去攞一件包裹。I need to pick up a package today.
佢喺城市嘅另一邊。It is on the other side of the city.
我望住我部手機。I look at my phone.
我打開一個公共交通 app。I open a public transport app.
我計劃我嘅路線。I plan my route.
條路線好長。The route is long.
佢有三個部分。It has three parts.
首先，我要搭巴士。First, I need to take a bus.
架巴士會去到地鐵站。The bus drives to the metro.
然後我要搭地鐵。Then I need to ride the metro.
最後，我要去海港。Finally, I need to go to the harbour.
喺呢度，我要搭水上巴士。Here, I need to take the harbour bus.
水上巴士係一隻大船。The harbour bus is a large boat.
佢喺水上面航行。It sails on the water.
件包裹喺一間細舖頭入面。The package is in a small shop.
間舖頭好近水邊。The shop is located close to the water.
呢個計劃好好。It is a good plan.
我攞起我張藍色交通卡。I take my blue travel card.
我準備好出發喇。I am ready for the trip.
topping up the card
Storyboard: She walks to a nearby ticket machine. She taps her blue travel card on the screen and pays two hundred kroner to top up her balance for the long trip.
我行埋去街邊嘅一部售票機度。I walk over to a ticket machine on the street.
首先，我張交通卡要入多啲錢。First my travel card needs more money.
我由個袋度攞張交通卡出嚟。I take my travel card out of my bag.
佢係一張藍色嘅細卡。It is a small, blue card.
我將張卡擺喺部機嘅熒幕上面。I place the card on the machine's screen.
熒幕顯示我嘅餘額。The screen shows my balance.
餘額太低，唔夠搭長途車。The balance is too low for a long trip.
我㩒個熒幕。I tap on the screen.
我揀一個金額。I choose an amount.
我用銀行卡畀兩百克朗。I pay two hundred kroner with my payment card.
部機喺熒幕上面寫住「已批准」。The machine writes "Approved" on the screen.
依家我張卡入面有錢喇。Now I have money on the card.
我將張卡擺返入個袋度。I put the card back in the bag.
我準備好搭長途車喇。I am ready for the long trip.
依家我要去搭巴士。Now I need to take the bus.
我行過去巴士站度。I walk over to the bus stop.
taking the bus
Storyboard: Maya waits at the bus stop until a long yellow bus arrives. She steps inside, scans her card at the door, and finds an empty seat by the window.
我企喺巴士站。I stand at the bus stop.
好快我就會搭巴士。Soon I travel by bus.
我順住條街望過去。I look down the street.
五分鐘之後，巴士就到。After five minutes, the bus arrives.
呢架係一架又長又黃嘅巴士。It is a long and yellow bus.
巴士正正停喺我面前。The bus stops right in front of me.
車門打開。The doors open.
我行上巴士。I step up into the bus.
我手拎住張交通卡。I have my travel card in my hand.
我喺車門旁邊拍卡。I scan the card by the door.
今日有好多乘客。There are many passengers today.
我搵下有冇空位。I look for an empty seat.
我搵到個靠窗嘅好位。I find a good seat by the window.
我坐低。I sit down.
我望向外面嘅城市。I look out at the city.
依家我搭巴士去地鐵站。Now I take the bus to the metro station.
asking a passenger
Storyboard: She turns to an older woman sitting nearby. Maya politely asks if she travels with this bus to the metro station, and the woman nods with a friendly smile.
我坐喺巴士度。I am sitting in the bus.
我望出窗外。I look out the window.
我唔係好肯定條路線。I am not completely sure about the route.
有個上咗年紀嘅女士坐喺我附近。An older woman is sitting close to me.
我望向佢。I look over at her.
「唔好意思，」我好有禮貌咁講。"Excuse me," I say politely.
「請問呢架巴士去唔去地鐵站㗎？」"Does this bus go to the metro station?"
個女士望住我。The woman looks at me.
佢帶住友善嘅笑容點點頭。She nods with a friendly smile.
「係呀，架車去地鐵站嘅，」佢答。"Yes, it goes to the metro station," she replies.
「你係咪要搭地鐵呀？」個女士問。"Are you taking the metro?" the woman asks.
「係呀，我要搭地鐵，」我講。"Yes, I am taking the metro," I say.
「我成日搭呢架巴士，」個女士講。"I often ride with this bus," the woman says.
「呢條路線好簡單，」佢補充。"It is an easy route," she adds.
「多謝你幫手，」我講。"Thank you very much for the help," I say.
「我係第一次搭呢條線。」"It is my first trip on this route."
個女士又點點頭。The woman nods again.
我喺座位度放鬆落嚟。I relax in my seat.
我哋繼續坐車穿過市區。We drive on through the city.
transferring to metro
Storyboard: The bus stops, and Maya gets off at a busy square. She walks down the wide stairs into a bright, underground metro station and taps her card again.
巴士停喺一個好繁忙嘅廣場。The bus stops at a busy square.
我落巴士。I get off the bus.
我同嗰位婆婆講拜拜。I say goodbye to the older woman.
依家我要去搭地鐵。Now I am going to the metro.
我穿過廣場。I walk across the square.
呢度有好多人。There are many people here.
我行落嗰條好闊嘅樓梯。I walk down the wide stairs.
車站喺地底。The station is underground.
個站好大，又好光猛。It is very big and bright.
我要再拍一次卡。I have to check in again.
我攞我張藍色卡出嚟。I take out my blue card.
我喺部機度拍張卡。I scan the card on the machine.
部機發出好大嘅「嗶」聲。The machine makes a loud beep.
依家我可以搭地鐵喇。Now I can ride with the metro.
我行落去月台。I walk down to the platform.
我會搭下一班車。I am taking the next train.
架車會直接開去海港。It goes straight towards the harbour.
架車三分鐘之後就到。The train arrives in three minutes.
riding the metro
Storyboard: Maya stands right at the front of the driverless train. She rides with the metro through the dark tunnels and watches the lights blur past as they speed forward.
地鐵到站，車門打開。The metro arrives, and the doors open.
我行入車廂。I go into the train.
架列車冇司機駕駛。The train drives without a driver.
我一直行到最前。I walk all the way to the front.
我企喺大窗旁邊。I stand by the large window.
我搭住地鐵穿過黑暗。I ride with the metro through the dark.
我哋喺一條長隧道裡面。We are in a long tunnel.
架車行得好快。We drive very fast.
啲光喺窗外面飛過。The light flies past the window.
睇落好靚。It looks fantastic.
有個小男孩企喺我隔籬。A little boy stands next to me.
佢都望向外面嘅黑暗。He also looks out into the dark.
我成日搭地鐵。I often take the metro.
呢程車又快又方便。It is a fast and easy trip.
依家就快到我個站喇。Now we are approaching my station.
我要去海港。I am going to the harbour.
我下一程會搭海港巴士。My next trip is with the harbour bus.
我好期待睇到海。I am looking forward to seeing the water.
finding the pier
Storyboard: She takes the escalator up to the street level and walks toward the water. She finds a small wooden pier by the edge of the canal where the harbour bus stops.
地鐵車程完喇。The trip with the metro is over.
而家我要上返去街度。Now I am going up to the street.
我搭扶手電梯。I take the escalator.
上面條街出緊太陽。The sun is shining up on the street.
而家我要去海邊。Now I am going to the water.
我要搭水上巴士。I am going by the harbour bus.
我沿住一條好闊嘅街行。I walk down a wide street.
然後我見到條運河。Then I see the canal.
啲水好藍。The water is very blue.
我搵緊我個站。I look for my stop.
架船會喺邊度停㗎？Where does the boat stop?
我行近啲岸邊。I walk closer to the edge.
然後我見到一個細嘅木碼頭。Then I see a small wooden pier.
佢就喺正水邊。It is located right down by the water.
碼頭旁邊有個黃色嘅牌。There is a yellow sign by the pier.
牌上面寫住「水上巴士」。It says "Harbour bus" on the sign.
呢度就係啱嘅地方喇。This is the right place.
我行出去碼頭。I walk out onto the pier.
而家我淨係喺度等架船。Now I am just waiting for the boat.
the harbour bus
Storyboard: A wide boat arrives, and Maya boards the harbour bus. She stands on the open deck and enjoys the fresh air as she sails across the blue water.
一隻闊身嘅船喺水面上駛過嚟。A wide boat comes across the water.
呢架係海港巴士。It is the harbour bus.
隻船駛埋去木碼頭。The boat sails in to the wooden pier.
佢停喺岸邊。It stops at the edge.
我而家要搭海港巴士喇。I am going with the harbour bus now.
我行上船。I step on board the ship.
我再拍一次我張藍色交通卡。I scan my blue travel card again.
裏面坐咗好多乘客。Many passengers sit inside.
但係我行出去露天甲板。But I walk out onto the open deck.
我企喺最前面。I stand at the very front.
我哋坐船駛過藍色嘅水面。We sail with the boat across the blue water.
從水面上睇，呢個城市好靚。The city looks beautiful from the water.
陽光照落我哋度。The sun shines down on us.
我深呼吸一下。I take a deep breath.
空氣好清新，又好凍。The air is very fresh and cold.
我好享受呢程短暫嘅旅程。I enjoy the short trip.
我哋好快就去到對面岸。We are soon over at the other side.
arriving for the errand
Storyboard: The boat docks at the opposite shore. Maya steps off the vessel and walks toward a small shop on the corner to finally collect her package.
隻船駛到對岸。The boat arrives at the other side of the water.
水上巴士停喺一個細碼頭度。The harbour bus stops at a small pier.
我落船。I get off the boat.
船程而家完咗。The trip by boat is now over.
我睇睇手機上面嘅地圖。I check the map on my phone.
我要去一間細士多。I am going to a small kiosk.
間士多就喺轉角位。The kiosk is located over on a corner.
我沿住條街行上去。I walk up the street.
我見到條路盡頭嗰間舖頭。I see the shop at the end of the road.
我打開門行入去。I open the door and walk in.
有個後生仔企喺收銀台後面。A young man stands behind the register.
「你好，」佢好親切咁講。"Hi," he says kindly.
「你好，我嚟攞件包裹，」我話。"Hi, I am going to pick up a package," I say.
個男人點頭微笑。The man nods and smiles.
佢問我叫咩名。He asks for my name.
「我叫 Maya，」我答。"My name is Maya," I answer.
佢喺貨架度搵到一個大紙箱。He finds a large box on a shelf.
佢將件包裹交畀我。He gives the package to me.
「唔該晒你幫手，」我話。"Thank you very much for the help," I say.
而家我帶住件包裹，又要展開漫長嘅路程返屋企。Now I take the long trip home again with my package.
