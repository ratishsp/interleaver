Week 7: Maya plans a busy week

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

planning the week
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya makes a cup of tea and opens her calendar at the kitchen table — she always plans the week here. She works through it: the bike goes to the repair shop, the salon needs a phone call, the gym is on Wednesday. She writes each one in, and February fills up.
Ni Jumatatu asubuhi.It is Monday morning.
Ninatengeneza kikombe cha chai ya moto.I make a cup of hot tea.
Ninakaa mezani jikoni.I sit at the table in the kitchen.
Ninafungua kalenda yangu.I open my calendar.
Siku zote ninapanga wiki yangu hapa.I always plan my week here.
Ni Februari sasa.It is February now.
Mwezi tayari umejaa sana.The month is already very full.
Kesho ni Jumanne.Tomorrow is Tuesday.
Kwa bahati mbaya baiskeli yangu imeharibika.My bicycle is unfortunately broken.
Ninatembea hadi kwa fundi wa baiskeli kesho asubuhi.I walk to the bicycle mechanic tomorrow morning.
Anafungua saa mbili asubuhi.He opens at eight o'clock.
Ninaandika hili kwenye kalenda.I write it in the calendar.
Pia ninampigia simu mtengeneza nywele leo.I also call the hairdresser today.
Nywele zangu ni ndefu mno.My hair is way too long.
Jumatano ninaenda kwenye ukumbi wa mazoezi.Wednesday I go to the gym.
Mara nyingi mimi hufanya mazoezi siku za Jumatano.I often train on Wednesdays.
Kwa kawaida ni saa moja jioni.It is usually at seven o'clock in the evening.
Ninauangalia mpango wangu tena.I look at my plan again.
Ninakunywa chai yangu.I drink my tea.
Sasa niko tayari kwa wiki mpya.Now I am ready for a new week.
dropping off the bike
Storyboard: On Tuesday morning at a quarter past eight, Maya wheels her bicycle to the local repair shop. She hands the bike to the mechanic, leaves the shop, and walks the rest of the way to the office.
Ni Jumanne asubuhi.It is Tuesday morning.
Ni saa mbili na robo.The time is a quarter past eight.
Ninasukuma baiskeli yangu.I wheel my bicycle.
Imeharibika.It is broken.
Ninaingia kwa fundi wa baiskeli.I go in to the bicycle mechanic.
Mara nyingi yeye hufanya kazi mapema.He often works early.
Karakana imejaa baiskeli.The workshop is full of bicycles.
Fundi wa baiskeli anatazama juu.The bicycle mechanic looks up.
"Habari ya asubuhi," anasema."Good morning," he says.
"Habari ya asubuhi," ninasema."Good morning," I say.
"Baiskeli yangu imeharibika.""My bicycle is broken."
Anachukua baiskeli yangu.He takes my bicycle.
"Ninaitengeneza leo," anasema."I am repairing it today," he says.
"Asante sana," ninasema."Thank you very much," I say.
Ninatoka nje ya karakana.I walk out of the workshop.
Sasa ninatembea hadi kituo cha basi.Now I walk to the bus stop.
Kwa kawaida mimi hupanda basi kwenda kazini.I normally take the bus to work.
Mimi huendesha baiskeli zaidi wakati wa wikendi.I mostly cycle at the weekend.
Hivi karibuni basi langu linakuja.Soon my bus comes.
Ni asubuhi nzuri ya Februari.It is a beautiful morning in February.
Nipo kazini saa tatu kasorobo.I am at work at a quarter to nine.
booking a haircut
Storyboard: During her lunch break, Maya calls a hair salon on her mobile phone. She speaks to the hairdresser and books an appointment for Thursday at half past four.
Ni mapumziko yangu ya chakula cha mchana.It is my lunch break.
Nimekaa kwenye kantini kazini kwangu.I am sitting in the canteen at my work.
Nilikuwa na shughuli nyingi jana.I was busy yesterday.
Kwa hiyo ninampigia simu mtengeneza nywele sasa.Therefore I am calling the hairdresser now.
Ninachukua simu yangu ya mkononi.I take my mobile phone.
Ninapiga simu kwenye saluni ya nywele mjini.I call a hair salon in the city.
Mwanamke anapokea simu.A woman answers the phone.
"Habari, hapa ni saluni ya nywele," anasema mwanamke."Hello, this is the hair salon," says the woman.
"Habari, jina langu ni Maya," ninasema."Hello, my name is Maya," I say.
"Ningependa kuweka miadi siku ya Alhamisi.""I would like to book an appointment on Thursday."
"Ngoja kidogo," anasema mtengeneza nywele."One moment," says the hairdresser.
"Mna nafasi ya miadi alasiri?" ninauliza."Do you have an appointment in the afternoon?" I ask.
"Ndiyo, tuna miadi wazi saa kumi na nusu," anasema."Yes, we have an available appointment at half past four," she says.
"Hiyo inanifaa sana," ninasema."That suits me very well," I say.
"Namba yako ya simu ni ipi?" anauliza mtengeneza nywele."What is your phone number?" asks the hairdresser.
"Namba yangu ni kumi na mbili, thelathini na nne, hamsini na sita, sabini na nane," ninasema."My number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
"Asante sana, tutaonana Alhamisi saa kumi na nusu," anasema."Thank you very much, see you on Thursday at half past four," she says.
"Tutaonana," ninajibu."See you," I reply.
Ninaweka simu mezani.I put the phone on the table.
Sasa hatimaye ninakula chakula changu.Now I finally eat my food.
evening gym
Storyboard: On Wednesday evening, Maya visits the local gym. She usually trains there twice a week, so she lifts weights and runs on the treadmill for an hour before heading home.
Ni Jumatano jioni.It is Wednesday evening.
Ni saa moja.It is seven o'clock.
Niko kwenye ukumbi wa mazoezi wa mtaa.I am at the local gym.
Ninapenda sana kufanya mazoezi.I really like to work out.
Siku zote niko hapa Jumatano.I am always here on Wednesdays.
Ninabadili nguo.I change clothes.
Kwanza, ninainua vyuma.First, I lift weights.
Vyuma ni vizito sana.The weights are very heavy.
Ninapumzika kidogo.I take a short break.
Baadaye, ninakimbia kwenye mashine ya kukimbia.Afterwards, I run on the treadmill.
Mara nyingi ninakimbia kwa kasi.I often run fast.
Siku zote ninasikiliza muziki.I always listen to music.
Ninafanya mazoezi kwa saa moja kwa jumla.I train for one hour in total.
Sasa ni saa mbili.Now it is eight o'clock.
Nimechoka, lakini nina furaha.I am tired, but I am happy.
Ninachukua begi langu.I take my bag.
Ninatembea kwenda nyumbani.I walk home.
hallway chat
Storyboard: After the gym, Maya bumps into Nina in the stairwell. They stand and chat cheerfully, and they agree to drink coffee together on Thursday afternoon at half past four.
Ninapanda ngazi.I walk up the stairs.
Nimechoka baada ya mazoezi.I am tired after the gym.
Kwenye ghorofa ya pili, ninakutana na Nina.On the second floor, I meet Nina.
Ananitabasamulia.She smiles at me.
"Hujambo Maya," anasema."Hi Maya," she says.
"Hujambo Nina, mambo vipi?" ninauliza."Hi Nina, how is it going?" I ask.
"Inaendelea vizuri sana," Nina anajibu."It is going very well," Nina answers.
"Unaonekana umechoka kidogo.""You look a little tired."
"Ndiyo, mimi hufanya mazoezi kila siku ya Jumatano," ninaeleza."Yes, I always work out on Wednesdays," I explain.
"Siku hizi ni nadra sana sisi kunywa kahawa pamoja," Nina anasema."We rarely drink coffee together lately," Nina says.
"Una muda kesho?" anauliza."Do you have time tomorrow?" she asks.
Ninafikiria nitafanya nini siku ya Alhamisi.I think about what I am doing on Thursday.
"Ndiyo, Alhamisi ni siku nzuri sana," ninasema."Yes, Thursday is a really good day," I say.
"Saa ngapi?" ninauliza."What time?" I ask.
"Labda saa kumi na nusu?""Maybe at half past four?"
"Saa kumi na nusu inasikika kuwa sawa kabisa," ninajibu kwa furaha."Half past four sounds perfect," I answer happily.
"Tutaonana kesho alasiri," Nina anasema."See you tomorrow afternoon," Nina says.
Tunaagana nje kwenye eneo la ngazi.We say goodbye out in the stairwell.
Ninaingia kwenye nyumba yangu.I go into my apartment.
Ninasubiri kwa hamu miadi yetu ya kahawa.I am looking forward to our coffee date.
scheduling conflict
Storyboard: Inside her apartment, Maya opens her calendar to add the coffee date and suddenly sees a problem: the haircut and the coffee are both scheduled at half past four. She immediately sends Nina a text message to move the coffee to six o'clock, and Nina happily agrees.
Ninachukua simu yangu.I take my phone.
Ninafungua kalenda yangu.I open my calendar.
Ninaangalia Alhamisi.I look at Thursday.
Ghafla ninaona tatizo.I suddenly see a problem.
Nina miadi kwa mtengeneza nywele saa kumi na nusu.I have an appointment at the hairdresser at half past four.
Kahawa yangu na Nina pia ni saa kumi na nusu!My coffee with Nina is also at half past four!
Ninamtumia Nina ujumbe mfupi mara moja.I immediately send a text message to Nina.
Ninaandika: "Hujambo Nina, samahani."I write: "Hi Nina, sorry."
"Nina miadi kwa mtengeneza nywele siku ya Alhamisi saa kumi na nusu.""I have a hairdresser appointment on Thursday at half past four."
"Je, una muda saa kumi na mbili badala yake?""Do you have time at six o'clock instead?"
Nina anajibu haraka sana.Nina replies very quickly.
Anaandika: "Hakuna shida!"She writes: "No problem!"
"Tutaonana saa kumi na mbili.""See you at six o'clock."
Ninatabasamu na kubadilisha muda kwenye kalenda.I smile and change the time in the calendar.
Sasa Alhamisi yangu inafaa kikamilifu.Now my Thursday fits perfectly.

appointments kept
