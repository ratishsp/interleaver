Week 12: Maya joins a badminton club

Norwegian with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding a club
Storyboard: Maya works out at the gym on Wednesdays but wants to meet people, so she looks for a social sport. She loves badminton — she played it a lot back home in Mexico — so she finds a local club online and books a trial session for Thursday evening.
Jeg drar på treningssenter hver onsdag kveld.I go to the gym every Wednesday evening.
Jeg liker å trene.I like to work out.
Men jeg vil også gjerne drive med en sport.But I also want to play a sport.
Jeg elsker å spille badminton.I love to play badminton.
Jeg spilte det mye hjemme i Mexico.I played it a lot back home in Mexico.
Nå leter jeg etter en lokal klubb.Now I am looking for a local club.
Jeg sitter i sofaen min med datamaskinen min.I am sitting on my couch with my computer.
Jeg søker på nettet.I search online.
Jeg finner en god badmintonklubb.I find a good badminton club.
Jeg ser på klubbens hjemmeside.I look at the club's website.
De har mange lag.They have many teams.
De spiller torsdag kveld.They play on Thursday evening.
Jeg vil gjerne booke en prøvetrening.I want to book a trial session.
Jeg finner et skjema på hjemmesiden.I find a form on the website.
Jeg skriver navnet mitt og e-posten min.I write my name and my email.
Jeg velger torsdag kveld.I choose Thursday evening.
Treningen starter klokken syv om kvelden.The training starts at seven in the evening.
Det er perfekt.It is perfect.
Jeg gleder meg til å spille igjen.I look forward to playing again.
arriving at hall
Storyboard: On Thursday evening Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sound of shoes squeaking on the many wooden courts.
Det er torsdag kveld, og jeg er hjemme.It is Thursday evening, and I am at home.
Jeg pakker sportsbagen min.I pack my sports bag.
Jeg legger skoene og klærne mine i bagen.I put my shoes and my clothes in the bag.
Jeg gleder meg til å spille i kveld.I look forward to playing tonight.
Jeg tar bussen til en stor idrettshall.I take the bus to a large sports hall.
Det er en kald vinterkveld.It is a cold winter evening.
Jeg sitter på bussen og ser ut.I sit in the bus and look out.
Bussen stopper, og jeg går av.The bus stops, and I get out.
Jeg går inn i den store idrettshallen.I walk into the large sports hall.
Det er veldig lyst.It is very bright.
Jeg ser mange mennesker i hallen.I see many people in the hall.
De elsker å drive med sport.They love to play sports.
Jeg hører en høy lyd.I hear a loud sound.
Skoene piper på de mange trebanene.The shoes squeak on the many wooden courts.
Folk løper fort og spiller sammen.People run fast and play together.
Det ser veldig gøy ut.It looks very fun.
Jeg er klar til å spille.I am ready to play.
Men først må jeg leie en racket.But first I need to rent a racket.
renting the racket
Storyboard: Maya goes to the small hall kiosk. She tells the clerk she is there to try out the club, and pays forty kroner to rent a racket for the evening.
Jeg går bort til en liten kiosk i sportshallen.I walk over to a small kiosk in the sports hall.
En ung mann står bak disken.A young man stands behind the counter.
Han smiler og sier hei.He smiles and says hello.
"Hei, jeg vil gjerne spille badminton i dag," sier jeg."Hello, I want to play badminton today," I say.
"Er du ny her i klubben?" spør han."Are you new here in the club?" he asks.
"Ja, jeg vil gjerne prøve å være med," svarer jeg."Yes, I would like to try to join in," I answer.
"Velkommen," sier mannen."Welcome," the man says.
"Skal du leie en racket?" spør han."Do you need to rent a racket?" he asks.
"Ja takk, jeg vil gjerne leie en racket til i kveld," sier jeg."Yes please, I want to rent a racket for the evening," I say.
Han finner frem en god racket til meg.He finds a good racket for me.
"Det koster førti kroner," sier han."It costs forty kroner," he says.
Jeg tar frem betalingskortet mitt.I take out my payment card.
Jeg betaler de førti kronene.I pay the forty kroner.
Han gir meg racketen over disken.He gives me the racket over the counter.
"Jeg synes det er en morsom sport," sier jeg glad."I think it is a fun sport," I say happily.
"Riktig god kamp!""Have a really good game."
Jeg sier takk og går over til banene.I say thank you and walk over to the courts.
playing doubles
Storyboard: In the busy hall, Maya joins a doubles game with two friendly players, Kasper and Sara, and a fourth person. She has played for years, so she smashes the shuttlecock hard across the net.
Jeg går bort til en badmintonbane.I walk over to a badminton court.
Det står tre personer på banen.Three people are standing on the court.
"Hei, jeg heter Kasper," sier en mann."Hi, I am Kasper," a man says.
"Og jeg heter Sara," sier en kvinne."And I am Sara," a woman says.
Den tredje spilleren heter Emil.The third player is named Emil.
"Jeg heter Maya," sier jeg."I am Maya," I say.
"Vi pleier å spille double," sier Kasper."We usually play doubles," Kasper says.
"Vil du spille med oss?" spør han."Do you want to play with us?" he asks.
"Ja, veldig gjerne," svarer jeg."Yes, I would love to," I reply.
Jeg liker veldig godt å spille badminton.I really like to play badminton.
Kasper og jeg spiller mot Sara og Emil.Kasper and I play against Sara and Emil.
Vi begynner å spille med en gang.We start to play immediately.
Sara starter kampen.Sara starts the match.
Fjærballen flyr raskt frem og tilbake.The shuttlecock flies quickly back and forth.
Emil slår fjærballen høyt opp i luften.Emil hits the shuttlecock high up in the air.
Jeg elsker å smashe.I love to smash.
Jeg hopper og smasher den hardt over nettet.I jump and smash it hard over the net.
Den lander på deres side av banen.It lands on their side of the court.
"Godt slag, Maya!" roper Kasper."Good shot, Maya!" Kasper shouts.
post match chat
Storyboard: The fast match finishes and the tired players walk off the court. Sara says Maya plays really well, and Maya explains that she plays a lot back home in Mexico.
Den raske kampen er over.The fast match is over.
Vi går av banen sammen.We walk off the court together.
Jeg er veldig sliten.I am very tired.
Kasper og Sara er også slitne.Kasper and Sara are also tired.
Vi drikker litt vann.We drink some water.
"Du spiller veldig bra," sier Sara."You play very well," Sara says.
"Tusen takk," sier jeg."Thank you very much," I say.
"Jeg synes det er så gøy.""I think it is so much fun."
"Spiller du ofte?" spør Kasper."Do you play often?" Kasper asks.
"Ja, jeg spiller mye hjemme i Mexico," svarer jeg."Yes, I play a lot back home in Mexico," I answer.
"Jeg pleier å spille med søsteren min.""I usually play with my sister."
"Det er en morsom sport," sier Sara."It is a fun sport," Sara says.
"Vi elsker å spille her i klubben.""We love to play here in the club."
"Jeg vil gjerne spille mer her i Danmark," sier jeg."I want to play more here in Denmark," I say.
"Du er absolutt rask," ler Kasper."You are definitely fast," Kasper laughs.
"Takk, men nå vil jeg gjerne sette meg ned," sier jeg."Thanks, but now I want to sit down," I say.
"Skal vi gå inn i kafeteriaen?" spør Kasper."Shall we go into the cafeteria?" Kasper asks.
"Ja, det vil jeg veldig gjerne," svarer jeg."Yes, I would very much like that," I answer.
hall cafeteria
Storyboard: The group sits in the small hall cafeteria to drink water and chat. Kasper asks if Maya plans to come back, and she happily promises to make it a weekly Thursday routine.
Vi går inn i det lille kafeteriaet i hallen.We walk into the small cafeteria in the hall.
Vi finner et bord.We find a table.
Jeg er veldig tørst.I am very thirsty.
Vi drikker kaldt vann og snakker.We drink cold water and talk.
"Dette er en veldig god klubb," sier jeg."This is a really good club," I say.
"Det er en god sport å drive med," sier Kasper."It is a good sport to do," Kasper says.
Kasper ser på meg.Kasper looks at me.
"Vil du være med igjen?" spør han."Do you want to join again?" he asks.
"Ja, veldig gjerne," svarer jeg."Yes, very much," I answer.
"Jeg vil gjerne spille her hver torsdag," forklarer jeg."I want to play here every Thursday," I explain.
"Det høres hyggelig ut," sier Sara."That sounds lovely," Sara says.
"Vi spiller alltid her på torsdager," sier Kasper."We always play here on Thursdays," Kasper says.
"Da ses vi neste uke," sier jeg glad."Then we'll see each other next week," I say happily.
Vi drikker resten av vannet vårt.We drink the rest of our water.
Det er hyggelig å møte nye mennesker.It is lovely to meet new people.
walking to bus
Storyboard: Maya leaves the sports hall with Sara. They walk together to the bus stop in the cold winter evening, and Maya feels glad to have met fun, active locals.
Sara og jeg går ut av idrettshallen.Sara and I walk out of the sports hall.
Det er sent, og det er en kald vinterkveld.It is late, and it is a cold winter evening.
Men jeg er fortsatt varm etter kampen.But I am still warm after the match.
Vi går sammen bort til busstoppet.We walk over to the bus stop together.
Vinden blåser, og gaten er mørk.The wind is blowing, and the street is dark.
"Liker du også å spille badminton i helgen?" spør jeg."Do you also like to play badminton at the weekend?" I ask.
"Nei, jeg spiller bare badminton på torsdager," svarer Sara."No, I only play badminton on Thursdays," Sara answers.
"Men jeg liker å drive med sport," sier hun."But I like to do sports," she says.
"Jeg pleier å løpe en tur i parken," fortsetter hun."I usually go for a run in the park," she continues.
Hun er en veldig aktiv og morsom person.She is a very active and fun person.
Vi venter på bussen i kulden.We wait for the bus in the cold.
"Bussen min kommer om ti minutter," sier jeg."My bus comes in ten minutes," I say.
"Vi tar den samme bussen," smiler Sara."We take the same bus," Sara smiles.
Det er jeg veldig glad for.I am very happy about that.
Jeg liker å snakke med henne.I like to talk with her.
Kasper og Sara er morsomme og hyggelige mennesker.Kasper and Sara are fun and nice people.
Nå har jeg en god rutine på torsdager.Now I have a good routine on Thursdays.
Jeg er sliten, men jeg er i godt humør.I am tired, but I am in a good mood.
Bussen kommer endelig.The bus finally arrives.
Jeg gleder meg allerede til neste torsdag.I am already looking forward to next Thursday.
