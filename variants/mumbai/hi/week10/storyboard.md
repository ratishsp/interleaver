# Week 10 — Storyboard (Maya buys cotton clothes for the summer)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Adjectives as a system — **अच्छा/बड़ा/छोटा** agreeing in gender and number (and the invariable ones like **लाल**); **पहनना** (wear)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_march_heat | Maya feels the March heat arriving in Mumbai. She puts on her usual thick jeans for the office, but she immediately feels too hot. She realizes she needs light cotton clothes to wear for the coming summer. |
| 2 | 02_cotton_shop | After work, Maya visits a bright neighbourhood clothing store. She greets the young salesman as bhaiya and explains that she wants light cotton clothes to wear in the Mumbai heat. |
| 3 | 03_blue_kurta | Maya browses the racks, gathers a few items, and goes into the fitting room. First, she picks up a beautiful blue kurta from her pile, tries on the large size, checks the affordable price tag, and decides to buy it. |
| 4 | 04_yellow_skirt | Next from her pile, Maya tries on a bright yellow cotton skirt and likes how it looks on her. But the price on the tag is far too high — too expensive for her — so with a little regret she sets it aside and moves on to the next thing. |
| 5 | 05_white_shirt | Still in the fitting room, Maya picks up a classic white cotton shirt. She checks the price tag, tries the shirt on, and sees the fit is perfectly professional for the software office. She happily decides to buy it. |
| 6 | 06_red_scarf | Maya steps out of the fitting room and spots a striking red dupatta near a large mirror. She looks at the low price, touches the soft fabric, drapes it over her shoulder to try the look, and tells the bhaiya she wants it. |
| 7 | 07_billing_counter | Maya brings the big blue kurta, the white shirt, and the red scarf to the counter. She asks the bhaiya for the total amount, pays in rupees, and happily packs the new clothes into her bag. |
| 8 | 08_cool_commute | The next morning, Maya wears her new blue cotton kurta to the office. She walks to the BEST bus stop in the bright sunshine, feeling fresh, cool, and ready for the warm weather. |
