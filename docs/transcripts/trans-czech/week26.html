Week 26: Maya's first Danish summer and thirty-second birthday

birthday messages
Storyboard: Maya wakes up on the morning she turns thirty-two, feeling a bit nostalgic as it is her first birthday away from Mexico. She smiles as she reads the flood of loving messages that her family has sent overnight while she slept.

Dnes mám totiž narozeniny.Today is actually my birthday.
Dnes je mi třicet dva let.I turn thirty-two years old today.
Jsou to moje první narozeniny daleko od rodiny.It is my first birthday away from my family.
Cítila jsem se kvůli tomu trochu nostalgicky.I felt a little nostalgic about it.

Moje rodina mi poslala mnoho láskyplných zpráv.My family has sent many loving messages.
Napsali je v noci, zatímco jsem spala.They wrote them last night while I slept.

Čtu si ty zprávy znovu a znovu.I read the messages again and again.
Je to fantastický začátek mých narozenin.It is a fantastic start to my birthday.
office pastry
Storyboard: At the software company, Maya is surprised when Peter and Eva place a small cake with a single candle on her desk. They wish her a happy birthday.

family call

Diego zvedá Lolu ke kameře.Diego lifts Lola up to the camera.
„Lola taky snědla malý dortík,“ směje se."Lola has also eaten a little cake," he laughs.
Hlasitě se tomu malému psovi směju.I laugh loudly at the little dog.
Jsou to moje první narozeniny daleko od domova, ale připadají mi velmi blízko.It is my first birthday away from home, but they feel very close.
cafe summer

Ten den jsme měli celý den hodně napilno.That day we were very busy all day.

Eva odpovídá téměř okamžitě.Eva replies almost immediately.

Usmívám se a beru si tašku.I smile and take my bag.

bus stop contrast

beach bonfire
