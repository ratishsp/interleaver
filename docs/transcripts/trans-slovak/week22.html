Week 22: Maya cooks a local dinner for her friends

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

planning the menu
Storyboard: Maya decides to host her Thursday badminton friends, Kasper and Sara, for dinner on Friday evening. She wants to cook a traditional local dish, so she finds a recipe for Danish meatballs with potatoes and writes down a shopping list with the exact quantities.
Každý štvrtok večer hrám bedminton.I play badminton every Thursday evening.
Kasper a Sara hrajú v rovnakom tíme ako ja.Kasper and Sara play on the same team as me.
Zajtra je piatok a pozvala som ich na večeru.Tomorrow is Friday, and I have invited them over for dinner.
Chcem uvariť tradičné dánske jedlo.I want to cook a traditional Danish dish.
Tak sa rozhodnem pripraviť fašírky so zemiakmi.So I decide to make meatballs with potatoes.
Nájdem dobrý recept na internete.I find a good recipe on the internet.
Teraz musím napísať nákupný zoznam.Now I need to write a shopping list.
Najprv potrebujem mäso na fašírky.First, I need meat for the meatballs.
V recepte sa píše päťsto gramov mletého bravčového mäsa.The recipe says five hundred grams of minced pork.
Potom potrebujem aj dve veľké žlté cibule.Then I also need two large yellow onions.
Poznačím si aj trochu mlieka a jedno vajce.I also note down a little milk and an egg.
Všetko si to zapíšem do poznámkového bloku.I write it all down on my notepad.
Potom sa pozriem na množstvo zemiakov.Afterwards, I look at the amount of potatoes.
Musím kúpiť presne jedno kilo malých zemiakov.I must buy exactly one kilo of small potatoes.
Nakoniec do zoznamu pridám maslo a soľ.Finally, I add butter and salt to the list.
Na opekanie mäsa treba vždy použiť dostatok masla.You must always use plenty of butter to fry the meat.
Zoznam je konečne hotový a ja som pripravená.The list is finally complete, and I am ready.
Zajtra po práci pôjdem priamo do supermarketu.Tomorrow after work, I will go straight to the supermarket.
shopping for amounts
Storyboard: At the supermarket after work, Maya carefully gathers her ingredients. She weighs out exactly one kilo of small potatoes, picks up five hundred grams of minced pork, and grabs two large yellow onions.
Stojím v supermarkete po práci.I am standing in the supermarket after work.
Dnes je v obchode veľa ľudí.There are many people in the store today.
V ruke mám svoj nákupný zoznam.I have my shopping list in my hand.
Na zajtrajšiu večeru potrebujem presné množstvá.I need exact amounts for my dinner tomorrow.
Najprv idem k zelenine.First, I go over to the vegetables.
Nájdem nejaké malé, dobré zemiaky.I find some small, good potatoes.
Dám ich do vrecka a odvážim ich.I put them in a bag and weigh them.
Váha ukazuje presne jedno kilo.The scale shows exactly one kilo.
To je to správne množstvo do receptu.That is the right amount for the recipe.
Potom vezmem dve veľké, žlté cibule.Then, I take two large, yellow onions.
Následne prejdem k mäsu.After that, I walk over to the meat.
Potrebujem mleté bravčové mäso na fašírky.I need minced pork for the meatballs.
Pozerám sa na rôzne balenia v chladiacom boxe.I look at the different packages in the cooler.
Vyberiem si balenie s päťsto gramami.I choose a package with five hundred grams.
Nakoniec si svoj zoznam skontrolujem ešte raz.Finally, I check my list one extra time.
Zoberiem si aj balenie masla a trochu mlieka.I also take a package of butter and a little milk.
Všetky veci vložím do košíka.I put all the things down into my basket.
Teraz som pripravená variť pre Kaspera a Saru.Now I am ready to cook for Kasper and Sara.
first prep step
Storyboard: Back in her apartment kitchen, Maya puts on an apron and reads the recipe's first instructions. The recipe states clearly: first, finely chop the onions, and mix them thoroughly with the meat, an egg, and a little flour.
Som doma vo svojej kuchyni.I am home in my kitchen.
Dávam si zásteru.I put on my apron.
Čítam si recept na telefóne.I read the recipe on my phone.
Recept je veľmi zrozumiteľný.The recipe is very clear.
Píše sa tam: Najprv nakrájajte cibuľu veľmi najemno.It says: First, chop the onions very finely.
Potom dajte mäso do veľkej misy.Then, put the meat in a large bowl.
Následne pridajte vajce, trochu múky a mlieka.Afterwards, add an egg, a little flour, and milk.
Nakoniec to všetko dôkladne zmiešajte.Finally, mix it all thoroughly together.
Nájdem si dosku na krájanie a ostrý nôž.I find a cutting board and a sharp knife.
Nakrájam tie dve žlté cibule na malé kúsky.I cut the two yellow onions into small pieces.
Trochu mi slzia oči.My eyes water a little.
Otvorím balenie s päťsto gramami mletého bravčového mäsa.I open the package with the five hundred grams of minced pork.
Dám mäso do svojej veľkej modrej misy.I put the meat into my large, blue bowl.
Potom dám cibuľu k mäsu.Then I put the onions down with the meat.
Rozbijem vajce do misy.I crack the egg into the bowl.
Následne pridám trochu múky a mlieka.Afterwards, I add a little flour and milk.
Vezmem veľkú lyžicu a všetko to spolu zamiešam.I take a large spoon and stir it all together.
Mäsová zmes vyzerá naozaj dobre.The meat mixture looks really good.
Prvý krok bol veľmi jednoduchý.The first step was very easy.
then the potatoes
Storyboard: Next, Maya prepares the potatoes. She follows the next sequence of commands: scrub the potatoes clean, place them in a pot of water, and then add a large spoonful of coarse salt before turning on the stove.
Teraz musím uvariť zemiaky.Now I have to boil the potatoes.
Znova sa pozriem na svoj recept.I look at my recipe again.
Má nejaké veľmi krátke pokyny.It has some very short instructions.
Najprv sa tam píše: „Vydrhnite malé zemiaky dočista.“First it says: "Scrub the small potatoes clean."
Robím to malou kefkou pod studenou vodou.I do that with a small brush under cold water.
Potom si prečítam ďalší riadok.Then I read the next line.
Ďalej sa tam píše: „Vložte zemiaky do hrnca s vodou.“Afterwards it says: "Place the potatoes in a pot of water."
Vyberiem zo skrinky svoj veľký hrniec.I get out my large pot from the cupboard.
Vložím celé kilo do hrnca.I put the whole kilo down into the pot.
Hrniec je takmer úplne plný.The pot is almost completely full.
Aký je ďalší krok?What is the next step?
V recepte sa píše: „Pridajte do vody veľkú lyžicu hrubej soli.“The recipe says: "Add a large spoonful of coarse salt to the water."
Vezmem si soľ a lyžicu.I take out the salt and a spoon.

Pridám tú veľkú lyžicu k zemiakom.I add the large spoonful down to the potatoes.
Nakoniec sa tam píše: „Zapnite sporák.“Finally it says: "Turn on the stove."
Položím hrniec na sporák a zapnem ho.I put the pot on the stove and turn it on.
Teraz sa zemiaky musia variť už len dvadsať minút.Now the potatoes just have to boil for twenty minutes.
afterwards frying
Storyboard: With the potatoes boiling, Maya heats a large frying pan. The instructions tell her to melt a thick slice of butter, and afterwards, form the meat mixture into round balls using a spoon and fry them until they are crisp and brown.
Zemiaky sa teraz varia v hrnci.The potatoes are now boiling in the pot.
V kuchyni to už krásne vonia.It already smells lovely in the kitchen.
Vyberiem veľkú panvicu.I take out a large frying pan.
Čítam si ďalšie pokyny v recepte.I read the next instructions in the recipe.
Ďalšia časť je dôležitá.The next part is important.
Píše sa tam: "Najprv na panvici roztopte hrubý kúsok masla."It says: "First melt a thick piece of butter in the pan."
Odkrojím trochu masla a dám ho na panvicu.I cut some butter and put it in the pan.
Maslo rýchlo zasyčí na horúcom kove.The butter quickly sizzles on the hot metal.
Čítam ďalej: "Potom z mäsa vytvarujte okrúhle guľky."I read on: "Afterwards form the meat into round balls."
Na tvarovanie mäsa používam lyžicu.I use a spoon to form the meat.
Je to trochu ťažké, ale sú pekné a okrúhle.It is a bit difficult, but they turn out nice and round.
Opatrne ich ukladám na horúcu panvicu.I carefully place them in the hot pan.
Recept pokračuje: "Potom ich opekajte, kým nebudú chrumkavé a hnedé."The recipe continues: "Then fry them until they are crisp and brown."
Trochu znížim teplotu.I turn down the heat a little.
Musia sa opekať asi pätnásť minút.They need to fry for about fifteen minutes.
Často ich otáčam, aby neprihoreli.I turn them often so they do not burn.
Fantasticky voňajú a dostávajú dobrú farbu.They smell fantastic and get a good color.
Teraz sú mäsové guľky a zemiaky takmer hotové.Now the meatballs and the potatoes are almost ready.
Teším sa, keď prídu Kasper a Sara.I am looking forward to Kasper and Sara arriving.
Je to úplne perfektné načasovanie.It is absolutely perfect timing.

guests arrive
