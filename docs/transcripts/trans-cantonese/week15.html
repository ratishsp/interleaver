Week 15: Maya's everyday life in the city

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning weather
Storyboard: Maya wakes up early and looks out the window. The sun shines, but the wind is cold, so she puts on a warm sweater for the day.
我好早就醒咗。I wake up early.
依家先至六點。It is only six o'clock.
係新嘅一日。It is a new day.
我慢慢起身。I get up slowly.
我行埋去窗邊。I walk over to the window.
我望出去睇吓呢個城市。I look out at the city.
出面仲係好黑、好凍。It is still dark and cold outside.
冬天依然仲喺度。Winter is still here.
我行入浴室。I go into the bathroom.
我洗面。I wash my face.
我刷牙。I brush my teeth.
然後我就着衫。Then I get dressed.
我着上一件又厚又暖嘅冷衫。I put on a thick, warm sweater.
凍冰冰嘅日子着就最完美啦。It is perfect for a cold day.
我行入廚房。I go into the kitchen.
我沖一杯熱咖啡。I make a cup of warm coffee.
我食一塊麵包加芝士。I eat a slice of bread with cheese.
依家我完全清醒晒啦。Now I am completely awake.
我準備好迎接新嘅一日。I am ready for the new day.
the bus commute
Storyboard: She walks to the bus stop and waits for her daily transport. The yellow bus arrives, she steps inside, scans her travel card, and rides through the busy streets.
我行出去條街度。I walk out onto the street.
今日係一個普通嘅朝早。It is a normal morning.
我行去巴士站。I walk down to the bus stop.
嗰度企咗好多人。Many people are standing there.
佢哋都喺度等巴士。They are also waiting for the bus.
我順住條路望過去。I look down the road.
一架紅色嘅大巴士到咗。A big red bus arrives.
係五號巴士。It is bus number five.
巴士喺我面前停低。The bus stops in front of me.
車門打開。The doors open.
我行上巴士。I step inside the bus.
我喺袋度搵張交通卡出嚟。I find my travel card in my bag.
我喺部機度拍嗰張藍色卡。I scan the blue card on the machine.
部機「嘟」咗一聲。The machine beeps.
我行去巴士嘅車尾。I walk to the back of the bus.
我搵到一個靠窗嘅空位。I find an empty seat by the window.
我坐低。I sit down.
我望住街上嘅人。I look at the people in the street.
我哋駛過繁忙嘅街道。We drive through the busy streets.
我準備好開始新一日嘅工作。I am ready for a new workday.
working with peter
Storyboard: Maya arrives at the software company and says good morning to her friendly colleague Peter. She sits at her desk, drinks black coffee, and types code on her computer.
我去到辦公室。I arrive at the office.
呢間係一間大型軟件公司。It is a large software company.
我行入去。I walk inside.
我同事 Peter 已經喺度。My colleague Peter is already there.
佢好好人。He is very friendly.
Peter 話：「早晨，Maya。」"Good morning, Maya," Peter says.
我話：「早晨，Peter。」"Good morning, Peter," I say.
我行過去我個位。I walk over to my space.
我坐喺我張寫字檯前。I sit at my desk.
我開電腦。I turn on my computer.
我有一杯咖啡。I have a cup of coffee.
係一杯熱嘅黑咖啡。It is black and hot.
我飲咖啡。I drink the coffee.
我做軟件工程師。I work as a software engineer.
我望住個螢幕。I look at the screen.
我喺電腦寫程式。I type code on my computer.
呢份工作好有趣。The work is very exciting.
我做呢份工好開心。I am happy with my job.
canteen lunch
Storyboard: At noon, Maya and Peter go down to the bright canteen together. She chooses a classic open sandwich with fish and pays forty kroner at the register.
依家係十二點。It is twelve o'clock.
係食晏時間。It is lunch time.
彼得同我落去飯堂。Peter and I go down to the canteen.
飯堂好大好光猛。The canteen is large and bright.
我哋好肚餓。We are very hungry.
我哋望住啲好嘢食。We look at the good food.
今日有好多選擇。There are many options today.
我揀咗一份經典嘅開放式三文治。I choose a classic open sandwich.
係魚配檸檬嘅。It is with fish and lemon.
彼得要咗一份大沙律。Peter takes a large salad.
我哋行過去收銀處。We walk over to the register.
有個好友善嘅男人企喺收銀處。A friendly man stands at the register.
「總共四十克朗，」佢同我講。"That will be forty kroner," he says to me.
我用卡畀錢。I pay with my card.
「畀你，」我話。"Here you go," I say.
「多謝，慢慢食，」佢答。"Thank you, and enjoy your meal," he answers.
我哋喺角落頭搵到張空枱。We find a free table in the corner.
我哋坐低。We sit down.
啲嘢食真係好好味。The food tastes really good.
我哋傾吓今日過得點。We talk about our day.
giving directions
Storyboard: After work, Maya steps out into a large square. A tourist asks for the way to a local museum, and she politely explains that they must walk straight ahead and then turn left.
我放工喇。I am off from work.
我行出一個大廣場。I walk out onto a large square.
今日市中心有好多人。There are many people in the city today.
有個男人行埋嚟我度。A man walks up to me.
佢有一部大相機。He has a large camera.
佢係遊客。He is a tourist.
佢笑住講嗨。He smiles and says hi.
「博物館喺邊度呀？」個遊客問。"Where is the museum?" the tourist asks.
我好熟嗰間博物館。I know the museum well.
離呢度唔係好遠。It is not far away.
我指住前面條街。I point down the street.
「你一直向前行，」我解釋。"You walk straight ahead," I explain.
「然後轉左。」"Then you turn to the left."
「博物館就喺右邊。」"The museum is on the right side."
「非常多謝你幫手，」個男人話。"Thank you very much for the help," the man says.
「唔使客氣，」我好開心咁答。"You are welcome," I answer happily.
佢沿住條街行過去。He walks down the street.
我繼續行去麵包舖。I walk on towards the bakery.
buying a cake
Storyboard: On the way home, she stops at a small bakery on the corner. She orders a chocolate cake for the evening and pays sixty-five kroner to the smiling baker.
我嚟到轉角嗰間細麵包舖。I arrive at the small bakery on the corner.
我行入去嗰間好暖嘅舖頭。I go into the warm shop.
聞到好香嘅咖啡同蛋糕味。It smells lovely of coffee and cake.
有個著住白圍裙嘅麵包師傅企喺櫃檯後面。A baker in a white apron stands behind the counter.
麵包師傅話：「午安。」"Good afternoon," the baker says.
佢問：「有咩幫到你呀？」"How can I help?" he asks.
我望住咁多款蛋糕。I look at the many cakes.
我要買個蛋糕今晚食。I am buying a cake for tonight.
我指住一個好靚嘅朱古力蛋糕。I point at a nice chocolate cake.
我話：「唔該，我想要呢個朱古力蛋糕。」"I would like the chocolate cake, please," I say.
麵包師傅答：「冇問題。」"Of course," the baker answers.
佢將個蛋糕放入一個白色盒入面。He puts the cake in a white box.
佢話：「總共六十五克朗。」"That will be sixty-five kroner," he says.
我喺袋度攞個銀包出嚟。I take my wallet out of my bag.
我畀咗六十五克朗。I pay the sixty-five kroner.
麵包師傅話：「多謝晒，祝你有個愉快嘅夜晚。」"Thank you very much, and have a good evening," the baker says.
我答：「多謝，你都係。」"Thank you, you too," I answer.
我攞起個白色盒。I take the white box.
我拎住個蛋糕，小心噉行出舖頭。I walk carefully out of the shop with the cake.
arriving home
Storyboard: Maya takes the bus back to her familiar street. She unlocks the front door of her building and feels happy and completely at home in her new neighborhood.
我拎住我件蛋糕企喺巴士站。I stand at the bus stop with my cake.
架紅色巴士好快就到。The red bus comes quickly.
我上咗車，搵到個位坐。I get on and find a seat.
巴士穿過城市。The bus drives through the city.
車程唔長。The trip is not long.
好快我哋就去到我嗰條街。Soon we are at my street.
我落巴士。I get off the bus.
依家夜晚嘅空氣有啲凍。The evening air is a little cold now.
但係我著住件好暖嘅冷衫。But I have my warm sweater on.
我沿住條街行。I walk down the street.
我依家對呢啲大廈已經好熟悉。I know the buildings well now.
呢度就係我棟大廈。Here is my building.
我喺袋度搵我啲鎖匙。I find my keys in my bag.
我用鎖匙打開嗰道好重嘅大門。I unlock the heavy front door.
我行入樓梯間。I walk into the stairwell.
呢度好安靜，好平靜。It is quiet and peaceful here.
我用鎖匙開自己道門，然後行入去。I unlock my own door and go in.
我將個袋放喺玄關。I put my bag in the hallway.
依家我喺自己嘅城市，返到屋企喇。Now I am home in my own city.
evening with nina
Storyboard: She knocks on her neighbor Nina's door and holds up the sweet cake. They sit in the cozy living room, drink tea, and chat about their day in the city.
我行過去妮娜嘅門口。I walk over to Nina's door.
我敲門。I knock on the door.
妮娜打開門，對住我笑。Nina opens and smiles.
我俾個朱古力蛋糕佢睇。I show her the chocolate cake.
「嗨，妮娜，」我話。"Hi Nina," I say.
「我帶咗個喺麵包舖買嘅蛋糕嚟。」"I have a cake from the bakery."
「嗨，瑪雅，真係太好啦！」妮娜話。"Hi Maya, how lovely!" Nina says.
我哋行入佢嘅客廳。We go into her living room.
客廳好溫暖，好溫馨。The living room is very warm and cozy.
我哋坐喺柔軟嘅梳化上面。We sit on the soft sofa.
妮娜沖咗啲熱茶俾我哋。Nina makes hot tea for us.
我哋一齊飲茶，食甜甜嘅蛋糕。We drink tea and eat the sweet cake.
個朱古力蛋糕真係好好味。The chocolate cake tastes really good.
我哋傾下大家今日喺市區過得點。We talk about our day in the city.
妮娜問起我嘅工作。Nina asks about my work.
我同佢講辦公室同埋我同事彼得嘅事。I talk about the office and my colleague Peter.
妮娜亦都分享咗佢今日係點過嘅。Nina also talks about her day.
我哋成晚都有傾有笑。We laugh and talk all evening.
呢個夜晚真係非常愉快。It is a really good evening.
喺我呢個新城市呢度，我覺得好有屋企嘅感覺。I feel at home here in my new city.
