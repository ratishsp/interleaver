Week 11: Maya experiences the Danish winter weather

dark winter morning
Storyboard: Maya wakes up to the sound of her alarm. She looks towards the window, but it is completely pitch-black outside. It is midwinter in Denmark, and she realizes the mornings are incredibly dark.

现在是早晨，但天还是很黑。It is morning, but it is still dark.

我朝窗户望去。I look out toward the window.

丹麦现在还是冬天。It is still winter in Denmark.
这里的早晨异常地黑。It is incredibly dark in the morning here.
天亮得很晚。It becomes light very late.
我在床上慢慢坐起来。I slowly sit up in bed.

窗外的天空依然完全是黑的。Outside the window, the sky is still completely black.

但在这里，太阳还在睡觉。But here the sun is sleeping.

我看了看钟。I look at the clock.
我下了床。I get out of bed.
一天现在开始了。The day begins now.
checking the forecast

天空不再是黑色的了。The sky is not black anymore.

只有两度。It is only two degrees.
但外面还是干的。But it is still dry outside.
稍后会变得非常潮湿。Later it becomes very wet.
很快就会下雨。It will rain soon.
也会起风。It also becomes windy.
今天晚些时候风会很大。It blows hard later in the day.
这会是一个真正的冬日。It becomes a real winter day.
我把手机放在桌子上。I put the phone on the table.
我又看了一眼天空。I look at the sky one more time.
bundling up

今天很冷。It is cold today.
现在是冬天，早晨天很暗。It is winter, and the morning is dark.
我站在房间里做准备。I am standing in my room getting ready.
我找出保暖的冬装。I find my warm winter clothes.
首先，我穿上一件厚毛衣。First, I put on a thick sweater.
我把它套过头顶。I pull it over my head.
这件毛衣很舒服，又暖和又柔软。The sweater is nice and warm and soft.
我还拿了一条长围巾。I also take a long scarf.
我把它紧紧地裹在脖子上。I wrap it tightly around my neck.
之后，我走到门厅。After that, I walk out into my hallway.
楼梯间吹来一阵冷风。A cold draft comes from the stairwell.
我拿起我厚重的冬季外套。I take my heavy winter jacket.
我把外套的拉链一直拉到顶。I zip the jacket all the way up.
它挡住了冷空气。It stops the cold air.
今天保暖很重要。It is important to stay warm today.
这样我就不会在寒冷中挨冻了。Then I will not freeze in the cold.
我也带上了我的雨伞。I also bring my umbrella with me.
我终于准备好去乘公交车了。I am finally ready to take my bus.
我打开大门，走进寒冷中。I open the front door and step out into the cold.
heavy rain

我打开通往街道的门。I open the door to the street.
我走到外面。I step outside.
雨下得很大。It is raining a lot.
今天天气很糟糕。It is bad weather today.
今天天气不会好转了。It is not getting better today.
我手里拿着一把雨伞。I have an umbrella in my hand.

这是一个非常潮湿的早晨。It is a very wet morning.
我走向公交车站。I walk over to the bus stop.

在这外面的雨中很冷。It is cold out here in the rain.
幸好公交车里面会变暖和。Fortunately, it gets warm inside the bus.
strong wind

我紧紧抓住我的围巾。I hold on to my scarf.

我沿着街道走。I walk down the street.

这很快成了一个问题。It quickly becomes a problem.
雨伞左右乱飞。The umbrella flies from side to side.

我终于收起了雨伞。I finally close the umbrella.

我走进大楼。I step into the building.
幸好，这里面温暖又安静。Fortunately, it is warm and quiet in here.
