Week 18: Maya gets a spring allergy

calling the clinic

Zdaj se moram samo malo sprostiti, preden grem tja.Now I just need to relax a bit before I walk over there.
scanning health card
Storyboard: Maya walks slowly to the clinic. In the reception area, she scans the yellow health card she received when she moved to Denmark to check in for her appointment.

waiting room boy

explaining the pain

Ves čas me srbijo.They itch all the time.
"Vsekakor bi morali počivati," reče."You absolutely ought to rest," he says.

spring allergy diagnosis
Storyboard: The doctor listens to her breathing and looks at her throat. He explains it is a common spring allergy she has never experienced before, and says she ought to buy some mild medicine.
Zdravnik me prosi, naj odprem usta.The doctor asks me to open my mouth.

"Potem se boste spet dobro počutili," doda."Then you will feel well again," he adds.
