Week 18: Maya gets a spring allergy

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

waking up unwell
Storyboard: On Thursday morning, Maya wakes up and feels terrible. Her head is heavy, her eyes itch, and her throat is dry. She decides she ought to stay home from work.
星期四朝早，我個鬧鐘響。It is Thursday morning, and my alarm rings.
我醒咗，覺得非常之唔舒服。I wake up and feel really terrible.
我個頭好重，個人好攰。My head is heavy, and I am very tired.
我擘大雙眼，但係對眼好痕。I open my eyes, but they itch a lot.
眨眼嗰陣其實有少少痛。It actually hurts a little when I blink.
我試吓飲少少我張枱上面嗰杯水。I try to drink some water from the glass on my table.
但係我個喉嚨完全乾晒。But my throat is completely dry.
吞嘢嗰陣我喉嚨好痛。It hurts in my throat when I swallow.
我望吓部手機，睇吓幾點。I look at my phone to see what time it is.
依家係七點半，平時呢個時候我會搭巴士。It is half past seven, and I normally take the bus at this time.
但係今日我完全冇力氣。But I have no energy at all today.
我唔單止覺得攰，仲覺得真係病咗。I do not just feel tired, but really sick.
我今日肯定要留喺屋企唔返工。I definitely ought to stay home from work today.
我打咗個短訊畀同事Peter。I write a short message to my colleague Peter.
我解釋話我病咗，唔會返辦公室。I explain that I am sick and will not be coming into the office.
佢好快覆我，叫我放鬆啲，等身體好返。He replies quickly that I should just relax and get well.
我應該等診所開門就打電話過去。I ought to call the medical clinic when they open.
但係依家我只係想合埋雙眼，畀我沉重嘅頭休息吓。But right now I just want to close my eyes and rest my heavy head.
calling the clinic
Storyboard: She calls her local medical clinic. She explains her unfamiliar symptoms to the receptionist, who agrees she ought to see a doctor today and gives her an appointment for the early afternoon.
我搵到附近診所嘅電話號碼。I find the phone number for my local medical clinic.
號碼係三十二、四十五、六十七、八十九。The number is thirty-two, forty-five, sixty-seven, eighty-nine.
我打畀佢哋，等咗幾分鐘。I call them and wait a few minutes.
一個好友善嘅接待員聽電話。A friendly receptionist answers the phone.
「呢度係診所，我係 Hanna，」佢話。"Medical clinic, you are speaking with Hanna," she says.
我解釋話我今日病得好犀利。I explain that I have become really sick today.
「我個頭好重，對眼又好痕，」我話。"My head is heavy, and my eyes itch," I say.
我仲同佢講我喉嚨痛。I also tell her that my throat hurts.
佢問我有冇發燒。She asks if I have a fever.
我答話應該冇，不過我好攰。I answer that I do not think so, but I am very tired.
「你今日應該睇吓醫生，」接待員話。"You ought to speak with a doctor today," the receptionist says.
佢睇吓個時間表幫我搵個期。She looks in the calendar to find an appointment.
「你可唔可以一點鐘過嚟？」佢問。"Can you come at one o'clock?" she asks.
「可以，呢個時間啱晒，」我答。"Yes, that fits perfectly," I reply.
「記住帶埋你張黃色健康卡，」佢話。"Remember to bring your yellow health card," she says.
我多謝佢然後收線。I thank her and hang up.
而家我要喺行過去之前放鬆一陣先。Now I just need to relax a bit before I walk over there.
scanning health card
Storyboard: Maya walks slowly to the clinic. In the reception area, she scans the yellow health card she received when she moved to Denmark to check in for her appointment.
依家係一點過咗少少。It is a little past one o'clock.
我慢慢行過去診所。I walk slowly over to the medical clinic.
雖然路程好短，但我依然覺得好痠痛同埋好攰。It is a short trip, but I am still very sore and tired.
我攞張黃色嘅醫療卡出嚟。I take out my yellow health card.
出面出緊太陽。The sun is shining outside.
但我應該戴太陽眼鏡，因為啲光令到我對眼好痛。But I ought to wear sunglasses, because the light hurts my eyes.
我行入診所嘅接待處。I enter the clinic's reception.
牆上面掛住一部細細嘅機。A small machine hangs on the wall.
喺度每次都要掃描張黃色醫療卡。You always have to scan your yellow health card here.
我搬嚟丹麥嗰陣，張卡係寄過嚟畀我嘅。I got the card in the mail when I moved to Denmark.
我喺部機度掃描張黃卡。I scan the yellow card on the machine.
部機發出一吓微細嘅聲，然後閃綠燈。It makes a little sound and flashes green.
咁即係代表醫生知道我已經到咗。That means that the doctor knows I am here.
我行入去候診室入面。I walk into the actual waiting room.
我坐低喺一張吉凳度等。I sit down on an empty chair and wait.
waiting room boy
Storyboard: She sits in the quiet waiting room near a little boy. The boy complains loudly that his ear hurts, while his mother gently tries to comfort him.
我坐喺等候室嘅一張凳度。I sit down on a chair in the waiting room.
間房幾乎完全冇聲。The room is almost completely quiet.
我好有耐性咁等。I wait patiently.
我個頭仲係好重。My head is still very heavy.
一個媽媽同一個男仔坐喺我對面。A mother and a little boy sit opposite me.
個男仔大約五歲。The boy is maybe five years old.
佢一隻手㩒住右邊耳仔。He holds a hand on his right ear.
「哎呀，我隻耳仔好痛呀！」佢好大聲咁講。"Ouch, my ear hurts!" he says loudly.
佢媽媽望住佢。His mother looks at him.
「我知呀，寶貝，」佢溫柔咁講。"I know, sweetie," she says gently.
「但係真係好痛呀！」個男仔埋怨。"But it hurts a lot!" the boy complains.
「醫生應該好快會叫我哋㗎喇，」媽媽講。"The doctor ought to call us soon," the mother says.
佢一隻手搭住佢膊頭。She puts an arm around his shoulders.
個男仔好大聲咁嘆咗口氣。The boy sighs loudly.
佢個樣睇落好攰。He looks very tired.
我好明白佢。I understand him well.
我自己個喉嚨都好痛。My own throat really hurts too.
我淨係想個人舒服返。I just want to feel well again.
explaining the pain
Storyboard: The friendly doctor calls her in. Maya sits in his bright office and explains exactly where it hurts, pointing to her scratchy throat, her heavy head, and her red eyes.
一位友善嘅醫生打開門，叫我個名。A friendly doctor opens the door and calls my name.
我行入去佢明亮嘅辦公室。I go into his bright office.
我哋坐喺佢張辦公枱前。We sit down at his desk.
「你今日覺得點呀？」佢問。"How are you feeling today?" he asks.
「我覺得好唔舒服，」我答。"I feel really bad," I answer.
醫生望住我。The doctor looks at me.
「邊度痛呀？」佢溫和噉問。"Where does it hurt?" he asks kindly.
我指住我喉嚨。I point to my throat.
「我喉嚨好痛，」我解釋。"My throat hurts a lot," I explain.
「好乾。」"It is very dry."
醫生點點頭，好留心噉聽。The doctor nods and listens attentively.
「我個頭都好重，」我繼續講。"My head is also very heavy," I continue.
我摸吓我塊面。I touch my face.
「同埋對眼紅晒。」"And my eyes are completely red."
一直都好痕。They itch all the time.
「你一定要好好休息，」佢話。"You absolutely ought to rest," he says.
佢開咗支細電筒。He turns on a small flashlight.
「等我幫你睇清楚啲，」佢話。"Let me take a closer look at you," he says.
spring allergy diagnosis
Storyboard: The doctor listens to her breathing and looks at her throat. He explains it is a common spring allergy she has never experienced before, and says she ought to buy some mild medicine.
醫生叫我張開口。The doctor asks me to open my mouth.
「講啊，」佢微笑住講。"Say ah," he says with a smile.
佢好仔細噉睇我個喉嚨。He looks carefully at my throat.
之後，佢聽吓我嘅呼吸。Afterward, he listens to my breathing.
佢問我有冇其他地方覺得唔舒服。He asks if I feel unwell anywhere else.
我話畀佢知，我個頭都痛。I tell him that my head also hurts.
「呢個唔係感冒，」佢話。"It is not a cold," he says.
「你有普通嘅春季敏感，」佢解釋。"You have a common spring allergy," he explains.
我有少少驚訝。I am a little surprised.
我以前從來未試過敏感。I have never had allergies before.
佢同我講呢個係完全正常嘅。He tells me that it is completely normal.
呢度嘅樹木同花朵對我嘅身體嚟講係新嘅。The trees and flowers here are new to my body.
「你應該去藥房買啲溫和嘅藥，」醫生話。"You ought to buy some mild medicine at the pharmacy," the doctor says.
佢保證好快就會有幫助。He promises that it will help quickly.
「咁你就會舒服返，」佢補充。"Then you will feel well again," he adds.
我鬆咗口氣。I am very relieved.
我個喉嚨痛，但好彩唔嚴重。My throat hurts, but fortunately it is not serious.
pharmacy visit
Storyboard: Maya stops at the pharmacy on her way back. The pharmacist hands her a small box of allergy pills and suggests some sweet drops because her throat still hurts.
睇完醫生返屋企嘅途中，我入咗去附近嘅藥房。On my way home from the doctor, I go into the local pharmacy.
我喺部機度攞咗張籌，然後等咗一陣。I pull a number from the machine and wait a little.
一個友善嘅藥劑師叫三十五號。A friendly pharmacist calls number thirty-five.
我行埋去櫃檯，出示我張黃色健康卡。I walk over to the counter and show my yellow health card.
佢喺電腦度搵到我嘅處方。She finds my prescription on the computer.
藥劑師畀咗一細盒敏感藥丸我。The pharmacist gives me a small box of allergy pills.
佢話：「你應該每日朝早食一粒藥。」"You ought to take one pill every morning," she says.
我問佢有冇啲咩可以紓緩喉嚨。I ask her if she has something for my throat.
我解釋：「我喉嚨仲係好痛。」"My throat still hurts," I explain.
佢點點頭，提議我買啲甜嘅喉糖。She nods and suggests some sweet throat drops.
佢話：「喉嚨痛嗰陣食會有幫助。」"They help when your throat hurts," she says.
「同埋你今日都應該飲多啲水。」"And you ought to drink a lot of water today as well."
我多謝佢，然後買咗啲藥丸同喉糖。I thank her and buy the pills and the drops.
總共八十五克朗。It costs eighty-five kroner in total.
我依然覺得好攰同痠痛，但係好開心已經攞到藥。I am still tired and sore, but I am glad to have the medicine.
依家我淨係想返屋企，坐喺自己張梳化度。Now I just want to go home to my own sofa.
skipping badminton
Storyboard: That evening, Maya has to cancel her usual Thursday badminton game. She rests on her sofa, takes her new medicine, and drinks a large cup of hot tea with honey.
星期四夜晚，我通常會打羽毛球。It is Thursday evening, and I usually play badminton.
不過我仲係病緊，所以要取消。But I am still sick, so I have to cancel.
我傳短訊畀我個羽毛球拍檔。I write a message to my badminton partner.
我解釋話我病咗，仲有頭痛。I explain that I am sick and that my head hurts.
我喉嚨都仲係好痛。My throat also still hurts a lot.
我今晚應該淨係喺梳化度休息下。I ought to just relax on the sofa tonight.
我拉一張暖毛氈冚住自己。I pull a warm blanket over myself.
我食喺藥房買嘅新藥。I take my new medicine from the pharmacy.
之後，我沖咗一大杯熱茶。Afterwards, I make a large cup of hot tea.
我加咗一大匙羹蜜糖落杯茶度。I put a large spoonful of honey in the tea.
飲落去令我痛緊嘅喉嚨覺得好舒服。It feels really good in my sore throat.
我慢慢咁飲杯熱茶。I drink the hot tea very slowly.
好快我就覺得好返啲。Soon I feel a little better.
我合埋雙眼，聽平靜嘅音樂。I close my eyes and listen to calm music.
我仲未完全好返。I do not feel completely well yet.
不過好彩我已經覺得好咗啲。But fortunately I already feel a little better.
我應該早啲上床瞓覺。I ought to go to bed early and sleep.
healthy saturday morning
Storyboard: By Saturday morning, the mild medicine has worked perfectly. Maya feels completely well again — clear eyes, no headache. She opens the window to the mild spring air, makes a proper breakfast, and takes a slow walk in the park, glad to have her energy back.
星期六朝早。It is Saturday morning.
我好早就醒咗，覺得個人又精神返晒。I wake up early, and I feel really well again.
星期五我全日都喺屋企休息。I rested at home all day Friday.
嗰啲溫和嘅藥非常有效。The mild medicine has worked perfectly.
我雙眼好清爽，而且完全唔覺得痕。My eyes are clear, and they do not itch at all.
我無頭痛，喉嚨亦都唔再痛喇。I do not have a headache, and my throat does not hurt anymore.
我落床打開窗，呼吸溫和嘅春天空氣。I get out of bed and open the window to the mild spring air.
能夠順暢呼吸真係好。It is lovely to be able to breathe without problems.
我行出廚房，準備整一份正正經經嘅早餐。I go out into the kitchen to make a proper breakfast.
我食咗麵包配芝士，仲飲咗一大杯熱咖啡。I eat bread with cheese and drink a large cup of hot coffee.
陽光普照，所以我諗我應該去公園散下步。The sun is shining, so I think that I ought to go for a walk in the park.
我著起件春裝外套，然後落街。I put on my spring jacket and go down to the street.
啲樹依家已經綠油油，好多人都喺度享受緊呢個好天氣。The trees are completely green now, and many people are enjoying the weather.
終於等到春天，但係身體唔舒服真係好無癮。It is really boring to feel unwell when it is finally spring.
不過今日我覺得雙腳好有力，而且回復晒精力。But today my legs feel strong, and I have my energy back.
我圍繞住公園個小湖慢慢散步。I take a slow walk around the small lake in the park.
好彩我依家已經完全好返，準備好享受週末喇。Fortunately I am completely healthy now, and I am ready to enjoy the weekend.
