Week 13: Maya goes to a café

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.
Ni majira ya baridi Kopenhagen.It is winter in Copenhagen.
Ninatembea barabarani.I am walking down the street.
Upepo ni baridi sana.The wind is very cold.
Unavuma kwa nguvu leo.It is blowing hard today.
Ninaegemea mbele dhidi ya upepo wa baridi.I lean against the cold wind.
Ninavuta skafu yangu juu.I pull my scarf up.
Ninasikia baridi sana usoni.My face is freezing.
Ninatazama huku na huku.I look around.
Ningependa kuingia mahali penye joto.I would like to get into the warmth.
Ninaona mkahawa mdogo kwenye kona.I see a small café on the corner.
Madirisha yana ukungu.The windows are fogged up.
Kunaonekana kuwa na joto huko ndani.It looks warm in there.
Ninatembea kuelekea kwenye mkahawa.I walk over to the café.
Ninafungua mlango mzito.I open the heavy door.
Ninaingia ndani.I step inside.
Hapa pananukia kahawa.It smells like coffee here.
Hewa ni nzuri na ina joto.The air is nice and warm.
Ninafunga mlango nyuma yangu.I close the door behind me.
Upepo wa baridi unabaki nje.The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
Niko ndani ya mkahawa sasa.I am inside the café now.
Hapa kuna joto sana.It is very warm here.
Ninavua koti langu nene la baridi.I take off my thick winter coat.
Ni zito.It is heavy.
Ninapata kigingi ukutani.I find a hook on the wall.
Ninatundika koti langu kwenye kigingi.I hang my coat on the hook.
Pia ninavua skafu yangu na glavu zangu.I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
Mkahawa huu ni mdogo na wa kustarehesha.The café is small and cozy.
Ninatafuta sehemu nzuri ya kukaa.I look for a good seat.
Kuna meza ndogo kando ya dirisha.There is a small table by the window.
Meza hiyo ipo karibu na hita.The table is next to a radiator.
Ninaelekea kwenye meza hiyo.I walk over to the table.
Ninaketi kwenye kiti.I sit down on the chair.
Mikono yangu bado ina baridi.My hands are still cold.
Ninaisugua pamoja.I rub them together.
Mikono yangu inazidi kupata joto.My hands get warmer.
Ni hisia nzuri sana.It feels fantastic.
Sasa ninakaribia kupata joto kabisa.Now I am almost warm.
Nina njaa na kiu.I am hungry and thirsty.
Ningependa kuagiza kitu sasa.I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
Natembea kuelekea kwenye kaunta ya kioo.I walk over to the glass counter.
Imejaa keki na mikate.It is full of cakes and bread.
Ninaangalia vitu hivyo vingi.I look at the many things.
Vinaonekana vitamu sana.They look very delicious.
Kuna vibao vidogo kando ya keki.There are small signs next to the cakes.
Vibao hivyo vimeandikwa kwa mkono.The signs are handwritten.
Ninasoma majina kwenye vibao hivyo.I read the names on the signs.
Kuna aina nyingi za vitafunwa.There are many kinds of pastry.
Ni vigumu kuchagua.It is hard to choose.
Kuna keki kubwa ya chokoleti.There is a large chocolate cake.
Lakini ninaangalia kitafunwa kingine.But I look at another piece of pastry.
Ni rolu kubwa ya mdalasini.It is a large cinnamon roll.
Ina sukari nyingi kwa juu.It has a lot of sugar on top.
Ninapenda ladha ya mdalasini.I love the taste of cinnamon.
Inafaa kabisa kwa siku baridi ya msimu wa baridi.It is perfect for a cold winter day.
Ningependa sana kupata rolu hiyo ya mdalasini.I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
Sasa niko tayari kuagiza.Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
Ninaenda kwenye sehemu ya kulipia.I walk over to the register.
Kijana mmoja amesimama nyuma ya kaunta.A young man stands behind the counter.
Yeye ni barista kwenye mkahawa.He is a barista in the café.
Ninamtabasamu.I smile at him.
Anaitikia kwa kichwa kwa uchangamfu.He nods warmly.
"Habari," anasema."Hi," he says.
"Ungependa nini?" anauliza."What would you like?" he asks.
"Habari," ninajibu."Hi," I answer.
"Ningependa kahawa nyeusi, tafadhali.""I would like a black coffee, please."
"Na ninaweza pia kuomba mkate wa mdalasini?""And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
"Bila shaka," anasema."Of course," he says.
"Kahawa nyeusi na mkate wa mdalasini.""A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
"Ungependa kukaa hapa?" anauliza."Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
"Ndiyo tafadhali, nitakaa pale," ninasema."Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
"Sawa," barista anasema."Alright," the barista says.
"Nitatengeneza kahawa yako sasa.""I will make your coffee now."
"Asante sana," ninasema."Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
Ninasubiri kwenye kaunta.I wait at the counter.
Anatengeneza kahawa yangu.He makes my coffee.
Anaweka kikombe cha moto cha kahawa kwenye sinia la mbao.He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
Pia anaweka sahani ndogo kwenye sinia.He also puts a small plate on the tray.
Roli yangu ya mdalasini ipo kwenye sahani.My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
Inanukia vizuri.It smells lovely.
"Hii hapa, oda yako," anasema."Here you go, your order," he says.
"Asante sana," ninajibu."Thank you very much," I reply.
"Asante kwa huduma nzuri.""Thank you for the good service."
"Karibu," anasema kwa upole."You are welcome," he says kindly.
Ninachukua sinia kwa mikono yote miwili.I take the tray with both hands.
Ninatembea kwa uangalifu sana kwenye sakafu.I walk very carefully across the floor.
Ninarudi kwenye meza yangu kando ya dirisha.I walk back to my table by the window.
Ninaweka sinia chini kwenye meza.I put the tray down on the table.
Sasa ningependa kukaa chini.Now I would like to sit down.
Nipo tayari kwa mapumziko mazuri.I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
Sasa nimekaa kwenye meza yangu ndogo.I now sit at my small table.
Hapa ni pazuri na pana joto.It is lovely and warm here.
Ninatazama keki yangu ya mdalasini.I look at my cinnamon roll.
Inanukia vizuri na kwa utamu.It smells sweet and good.
Ninauma kidogo.I take a small bite.
Keki hiyo ni mpya kabisa na ina joto.The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
Ina ladha nzuri sana.It tastes really good.
Ninakunywa kahawa yangu nyeusi kidogo.I drink a little of my black coffee.
Hatimaye, vidole vyangu vina joto tena.Finally, my fingers are warm again.
Ninapumzika kwenye kiti kilaini.I relax in the soft chair.
Mkahawa unakaribia kujaa watu.The café is almost full of people.
Watu wamekaa katika makundi madogo.People sit in small groups.
Wanazungumza na kucheka pamoja.They talk and laugh together.
Ninasikiliza sauti za chini ndani ya chumba.I listen to the low voices in the room.
Sauti hiyo inastarehesha sana.The sound is very cozy.
Ninatazama nje kupitia dirisha kubwa.I look out of the large window.
Hali ya hewa nje ni ya kijivu na yenye upepo.The weather outside is gray and windy.
Sasa theluji inanyesha tena kwa kweli.Now it is actually snowing again.
Chembe nyeupe za theluji zinaanguka polepole barabarani.White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
Ninafuraha tu kuwa ndani.I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
Ninakula kipande cha mwisho cha keki yangu ya mdalasini.I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
Ina ladha nzuri sana.It tastes really good.
Lakini pia ni tamu sana.But it is also very sweet.
Kikombe changu cha kahawa ni kitupu kabisa.My coffee cup is completely empty.
Ghafla ninasikia kiu sana.I am suddenly very thirsty.
Ningependa kupata maji baridi kidogo.I would like to have some cold water.
Mwanamume kijana anapita kando ya meza yangu.A young man walks past my table.
Anafanya kazi hapa mkahawani.He works here in the café.
Anakusanya vikombe vitupu na sahani tupu.He collects empty cups and plates.
Macho yangu yanakutana na yake.I catch his eye.
Anasimama kwenye meza yangu na kutabasamu.He stops at my table and smiles.
"Samahani," ninasema kwa heshima."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Naam?" anajibu yule mwanamume kijana."Yes?" answers the young man.
"Naweza kuomba glasi ya maji ya bomba?" ninauliza."May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
"Ndiyo, bila shaka," anasema."Yes, of course," he says.
"Asante sana," ninasema."Thank you very much," I say.
Anachukua sahani na kikombe changu kitupu.He takes my empty plate and cup.
Anarudi kwenye kaunta.He walks back to the counter.
Muda mfupi baadaye anakuja na glasi ya maji baridi.A little later he comes with a cold glass of water.
asking for the bill
Storyboard: Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab.
Ninakunywa maji yangu.I drink my water.
Maji ni baridi na mazuri.The water is cold and nice.
Ninachukua begi langu kutoka sakafuni.I take my bag from the floor.
Nimemaliza mapumziko yangu.I am finished with my break.
Ninatembea hadi kwenye kaunta.I walk up to the counter.
Yule barista mkarimu bado amesimama pale.The friendly barista is still standing there.
Ananiangalia kwa tabasamu.He looks at me with a smile.
"Ungependa kitu kingine chochote?" anauliza."Would you like anything else?" he asks.
"Hapana asante," ninajibu."No thank you," I answer.
"Ningependa kulipa.""I would like to pay."
"Ndio, bila shaka," anasema."Yes, of course," he says.
Anaangalia skrini yake ndogo.He looks at his little screen.
"Kahawa moja nyeusi na roli moja ya mdalasini," anasema."One black coffee and one cinnamon roll," he says.
"Ndio, ni sahihi," ninasema."Yes, that is correct," I say.
"Unataka kulipa kwa kadi au pesa taslimu?" anauliza."Do you want to pay with card or cash?" he asks.
"Ningependa kulipa kwa kadi," ninajibu."I would like to pay with a card," I answer.
"Sawa.""Fine."
"Itakuwa kroner themanini na tano," anasema."That will be eighty-five kroner," he says.
Ninatoa kadi yangu ya malipo.I take out my payment card.
paying and leaving
Storyboard: Maya taps her card on the terminal to pay eighty-five kroner. She puts on her heavy coat, thanks the staff for a nice time, and pushes the front door open to face the cold again.
Barista ananitabasamia.The barista smiles at me.
Ninashikilia kadi yangu juu ya mashine ya malipo.I hold my card over the terminal.
Mashine inatoa mlio.The machine beeps.
"Unataka risiti?" anauliza."Do you want a receipt?" he asks.
"Hapana asante," ninajibu."No thank you," I answer.
Ninatembea kuelekea ukutani.I walk over to the wall.
Ninachukua koti langu nene la msimu wa baridi kutoka kwenye kitundikio.I take my thick winter coat from a hook.
Ninavaa koti hilo.I put the coat on.
Ninavaa skafu yangu pia.I also put my scarf on.
Ninavaa glavu zangu pia.I also put my gloves on.
Sasa niko tayari kwa baridi.Now I am ready for the cold.
Ninamwangalia barista tena.I look at the barista again.
"Asante sana kwa kahawa," ninasema."Thank you very much for the coffee," I say.
"Karibu," anasema kwa tabasamu."You are welcome," he says with a smile.
"Uwe na siku njema.""Have a good day."
"Nawe pia," ninasema."Likewise," I say.
Ninatembea kuelekea mlango mkuu.I walk over to the front door.
Ninasukuma mlango mzito ufunguke.I push the heavy door open.
Upepo ni wa baridi, lakini ninajisikia vizuri.The wind is cold, but I feel good.
