# Week 11 — Storyboard (Maya experiences Kochi's humid heat and her first thundershower)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Weather expressions **ചൂടാണ് / വെയിലാണ് / മഴ പെയ്യുന്നു**; simple future **-ും** for the forecast (മഴ പെയ്യും)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_forecast | Maya wakes up to a bright, hot morning. She checks the weather app on her phone, which shows that it will rain in the evening. She puts on her light cotton clothes and sensibly packs an umbrella in her bag. |
| 2 | 02_humid_walk | Maya walks down the street to her software office. The sun is intense, and the coastal Kochi air feels incredibly hot and humid. She deliberately walks in the shade to escape the harsh heat. |
| 3 | 03_office_ac | Maya enters the cool air-conditioning of the software company and greets Prakash. Prakash complains that it is extremely hot outside today. Maya agrees and tells him that the app says it will rain later. |
| 4 | 04_lime_juice_break | During the lunch break, Maya steps outside to a nearby juice shop. The midday sun is still very harsh on the street. She buys a cold, fresh lime juice to cool down from the persistent heat. |
| 5 | 05_afternoon_clouds | Late in the afternoon, Maya types code at her desk. The office suddenly gets dark. She looks out the window and sees that the bright sun is completely gone, replaced by thick black clouds rolling over the city. |
| 6 | 06_sudden_downpour | Maya leaves the office building just as the rain starts. Large drops fall, quickly turning into a heavy thundershower. She opens her umbrella, but the blowing wind and heavy water make walking very difficult. |
| 7 | 07_auto_commute | Realizing the umbrella is not enough for the stormy weather, Maya hails a passing auto. She sits safely inside the auto-rickshaw, watching the heavy rain flood the streets during the commute home. |
| 8 | 08_lobby_arrival | Maya arrives at her apartment building and steps into the dry lobby. Her neighbor Anitha is standing nearby. Anitha smiles at the storm and tells Maya that this heavy rain brings welcome relief to the hot city. |
| 9 | 09_cool_breeze | Maya enters her flat, leaves her wet umbrella by the door, and opens the living room window. The sticky heat is completely gone. She stands comfortably at the window, enjoying the cool evening breeze and her very first Kochi rain. |
