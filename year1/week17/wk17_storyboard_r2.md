# Week 17 — Storyboard (Maya explores Odense in the light spring)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Past-tense narration; *var/havde/tog*; 3rd-person possessive *sin* vs *hans/hendes*; time expressions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_spring_breeze_diary | Maya opens her living room window to feel the warm spring breeze. She sits at her table in the bright evening sunlight and begins to write in her travel diary about her weekend trip. |
| 2 | 02_sunday_train_trip | She writes about her Sunday morning excursion. She packed a backpack, bought a warm coffee, and took the fast train out of Copenhagen to visit the city of Odense. |
| 3 | 03_long_sea_bridge | She describes the spectacular journey as the train crossed the very long bridge over the sea. She looked out the large window and admired the blue water and the small ships. |
| 4 | 04_stressed_father_train | She watched a stressed father sitting across the aisle. He desperately tried to read his book, but his two young children constantly threw their toys on the floor. |
| 5 | 05_naughty_little_dog | The family also brought a little dog. The dog suddenly jumped out of its basket and ran away with the father's left shoe, making the children laugh loudly at him. |
| 6 | 06_crooked_colorful_houses | Maya arrived in Odense and walked straight into the old quarter. She loved the historic atmosphere and took pictures of the small, crooked houses painted in bright yellow and red. |
| 7 | 07_painting_the_house | She stopped to watch an older local couple. The man stood in the garden and painted his house, while his wife sat on a wooden bench and drank her warm tea. |
| 8 | 08_fairy_tale_museum | She visited the museum dedicated to the famous fairy tale writer, H.C. Andersen. She saw his old top hat and read about the man who invented the little mermaid she met back in February. |
| 9 | 09_river_boat_trip | In the late afternoon, she bought a ticket for a small wooden boat down the local river. She relaxed on the deck and enjoyed the long, light evening under the green trees. |
| 10 | 10_diary_closed | On the evening train back home, Maya finishes her story. She closes her diary, puts the pen back in her bag, and smiles about a perfect spring weekend. |
