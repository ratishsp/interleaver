Week 10: Maya buys winter clothes

Indonesian with English gloss, line-aligned.

a cold morning
Storyboard: It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to the bus stop on her way to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter.
Ini pagi hari di Kopenhagen.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Saya berjalan ke halte bus.I walk to the bus stop.
Cuaca sangat dingin hari ini.The weather is very cold today.
Cuacanya berangin.It is windy.
Anginnya sedingin es.The wind is ice-cold.
Saya kedinginan.I am freezing.
Saya memakai jaket tipis.I am wearing a thin jacket.
Jaket saya sama sekali tidak hangat.My jacket is not warm at all.
Saya juga memakai sepatu tipis.I am also wearing thin shoes.
Saya tidak memakai syal yang hangat.I am not wearing a warm scarf.
Tangan saya sangat dingin.My hands are very cold.
Saya menggigil.I am shivering.
Musim dingin di Denmark itu berat.The winter in Denmark is hard.
Saya butuh pakaian hangat.I need warm clothes.
Saya butuh sweter tebal.I need a thick sweater.
Saya juga butuh syal besar.I also need a large scarf.
Dan saya butuh sarung tangan hangat.And I need warm gloves.
Saya berjalan sangat cepat.I walk very fast.
Saya akan membeli pakaian musim dingin setelah kerja.I am buying winter clothes after work.
entering the shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things.
Saya sudah selesai bekerja.I am finished at work.
Saya berjalan menyusuri jalan khusus pejalan kaki.I walk down the pedestrian street.
Anginnya masih sangat dingin.The wind is still very cold.
Saya melihat sebuah toko pakaian yang besar.I see a large clothing shop.
Toko itu sangat terang.The shop is very bright.
Saya masuk ke dalam toko itu.I go into the shop.
Di dalam sini terasa hangat dan nyaman.It is wonderfully warm in here.
Seorang pelayan toko berdiri di belakang mesin kasir.A shop assistant stands behind the register.
Dia melihat saya dan tersenyum.She looks at me and smiles.
"Halo," katanya."Hello," she says.
"Halo," jawab saya."Hello," I answer.
"Saya sangat kedinginan," kata saya."I am freezing a lot," I say.
"Saya hanya memakai jaket tipis.""I only have a thin jacket on."
"Saya ingin mencari beberapa pakaian hangat.""I would like to find some warm things."
Pelayan toko itu tersenyum lagi.The shop assistant smiles again.
"Hari ini juga sangat dingin," katanya."It is also very cold today," she says.
"Kami punya banyak sweter tebal.""We have many thick sweaters."
"Kami juga punya pakaian musim dingin yang hangat.""We also have warm winter clothes."
"Silakan lihat-lihat saja," katanya dengan ramah."You just take a look," she says kindly.
"Terima kasih banyak," jawab saya."Many thanks," I answer.
the red sweater
Storyboard: Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it.
Saya melihat pakaian di rak.I look at the clothes on the shelves.
Saya melihat sebuah sweter tebal.I see a thick sweater.
Warnanya merah.It is red.
Saya butuh sweter hangat.I need a warm sweater.
Toko itu memiliki banyak ukuran.The shop has many sizes.
Saya menemukan ukuran medium.I find a size medium.
Saya masuk ke kamar pas.I walk into the fitting room.
Saya memakai sweter merah itu.I put the red sweater on.
Saya memakainya dan melihat ke cermin besar.I have it on and look in the big mirror.
Sweter itu pas sekali.The sweater fits perfectly.
Ini luar biasa hangat.It is incredibly warm.
Saya sangat menyukai warna merahnya.I love the red colour.
Saya melihat label harganya.I look at the price tag.
Harga sweter itu tiga ratus kroner.The sweater costs three hundred kroner.
Itu harga yang bagus untuk sweter tebal.It is a good price for a thick sweater.
Saya melepas sweter itu lagi.I take the sweater off again.
Saya membelinya.I buy it.
the tight green coat
Storyboard: Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a larger size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. The assistant brings her a warm dark-blue winter jacket in her size instead — it fits perfectly, and Maya decides to take it.
Saya juga butuh jaket musim dingin.I also need a winter jacket.
Saya melihat-lihat lebih banyak pakaian di toko.I look at more clothes in the shop.
Saya menemukan mantel hijau yang indah.I find a beautiful, green coat.
Itu adalah desain yang sangat indah.It is a very beautiful design.
Saya memakai mantel hijau itu.I put the green coat on.
Tapi itu terlalu kecil.But it is too small.
Itu sangat ketat di bagian bahu.It is very tight across the shoulders.
"Apakah Anda punya ini dalam ukuran lain?" tanyaku."Do you have it in another size?" I ask.
Pramuniaga itu mencarinya.The shop assistant looks.
"Sayangnya, tidak ada," jawabnya."No, unfortunately," she answers.
Jadi saya menggantungkan kembali mantel hijau itu.So I hang the green coat back.
Pramuniaga itu menemukan jaket lain untuk saya.The shop assistant finds another jacket for me.
Itu adalah jaket musim dingin biru tua yang hangat.It is a warm, dark-blue winter jacket.
Saya memakai jaket biru tua itu.I put the dark-blue jacket on.
Ukurannya pas sekali.It fits perfectly.
Ukurannya bagus, besar, dan tidak ketat.It is nice and big and not tight.
Saya melihat ke cermin besar.I look in a large mirror.
Saya menyukainya.I like it.
"Saya ambil ini," kataku."I will take it," I say.
the blue scarf
Storyboard: Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases.
Saya berjalan ke sebuah meja di toko itu.I walk over to a table in the shop.
Ada banyak barang di atas meja itu.There are many things on the table.
Saya tidak memakai syal hari ini.I am not wearing a scarf today.
Leher saya terasa sangat dingin.I am very cold around my neck.
Saya melihat sebuah syal biru.I see a blue scarf.
Itu adalah syal yang sangat panjang.It is a very long scarf.
Itu terbuat dari wol.It is made of wool.
Saya mengambil syal biru itu.I take the blue scarf.
Saya mengalungkannya di leher saya.I put it around my neck.
Terasa luar biasa hangat.It feels wonderfully warm.
"Warnanya bagus," kata pelayan toko itu."It is a nice colour," the shop assistant says.
"Ya, dan ini sangat lembut," kata saya."Yes, and it is very soft," I say.
Saya melihat label harganya.I look at the price tag.
Harganya seratus lima puluh kroner.It costs one hundred and fifty kroner.
Itu harga yang bagus untuk sebuah syal yang hangat.It is a good price for a warm scarf.
Saya melepaskannya lagi.I take it off again.
"Saya juga membelinya," kata saya kepadanya."I am buying it too," I say to her.
Saya meletakkan syal biru itu bersama dengan sweter merah itu.I put the blue scarf together with the red sweater.
the black gloves
Storyboard: Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well.
Saya masih memakai jaket tipis saya.I am still wearing my thin jacket.
Saya memikirkan tangan saya yang dingin dari tadi pagi.I think about my cold hands from this morning.
Saya butuh sarung tangan tebal.I need thick gloves.
Saya melihat-lihat ke sekeliling toko yang terang itu.I look around the bright shop.
Saya melihat sebuah keranjang besar.I see a large basket.
Keranjang itu penuh dengan sarung tangan.The basket is full of gloves.
Saya menemukan sepasang sarung tangan musim dingin berwarna hitam.I find a pair of black winter gloves.
Sarung tangan itu sangat tebal dan lembut.They are very thick and soft.
Saya memakai sarung tangan itu.I put the gloves on.
Ukurannya pas sekali untuk saya.They fit me perfectly.
Saya bertanya kepada pramuniaga yang tersenyum itu.I ask the smiling shop assistant.
"Berapa harga sarung tangan hitam itu?" tanya saya."What do the black gloves cost?" I ask.
"Harganya seratus dua puluh kroner," jawabnya."They cost one hundred and twenty kroner," she answers.
Seratus dua puluh kroner adalah harga yang bagus.One hundred and twenty kroner is a good price.
Tangan saya sudah terasa hangat.My hands are already warm.
"Saya ambil ini," kata saya dengan senang."I will take them," I say happily.
Saya melepaskan sarung tangan itu lagi.I take the gloves off again.
Saya meletakkan sarung tangan hitam itu bersama dengan sweter merah dan syal biru saya.I put the black gloves together with my red sweater and my blue scarf.
Sekarang saya siap untuk membayar.Now I am ready to pay.
paying for the clothes
Storyboard: Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the dark-blue winter jacket, the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is one thousand five hundred kroner, and Maya pays with her card.
Saya berjalan ke kasir dengan pakaian baru saya.I walk over to the counter with my new clothes.
Pramuniaga itu tersenyum ramah kepada saya.The shop assistant smiles kindly at me.
"Apakah itu semuanya?" tanyanya."Is that everything?" she asks.
"Ya, itu semuanya," jawab saya."Yes, that is everything," I answer.
Dia memindai semua barang baru saya.She scans all my new things.
Ada sweter, jaket, syal, dan sarung tangan.There is a sweater, a jacket, a scarf and gloves.
"Sekarang Anda tidak akan kedinginan," katanya dengan gembira."Now you will not freeze," she says happily.
"Totalnya seribu lima ratus kroner.""That will be one thousand five hundred kroner, please."
Pakaian itu mahal, tetapi sangat hangat.The clothes are expensive, but they are very warm.
Saya mengeluarkan kartu pembayaran saya.I take out my payment card.
"Saya membayar dengan kartu," kata saya."I am paying by card," I say.
"Anda bisa langsung membayar di sini," katanya."You can just pay here," she says.
Dia menunjuk ke sebuah mesin kecil berwarna hitam.She points at a small, black machine.
Saya memegang kartu saya di atas mesin.I hold my card over the machine.
Terdengar bunyi bip, dan pembayaran disetujui.It beeps, and the payment is approved.
"Apakah Anda mau struknya?" tanya pramuniaga itu."Do you want the receipt?" the shop assistant asks.
"Tidak, terima kasih," jawab saya."No thank you," I answer.
Sekarang saya memiliki banyak pakaian musim dingin yang bagus.Now I own a lot of nice winter clothes.
stepping into the cold
Storyboard: Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She pulls on the red sweater, zips her new dark-blue winter jacket over it, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm.
"Mau kantong?" tanya pramuniaga itu."A bag?" the shop assistant asks.
"Tidak, terima kasih," kataku."No thank you," I say.
Aku tidak butuh kantong.I do not need a bag.
Aku langsung memakai pakaian baru itu.I put the new clothes on right away.
Pertama, aku memakai sweter merah yang tebal itu.First, I put on the thick, red sweater.
Kemudian, aku memakai jaket musim dingin biru tua itu.Then, I put on the dark-blue winter jacket.
Aku menaikkan resleting jaket hangat itu.I zip the warm jacket.
Setelah itu, aku mengambil syal biru yang panjang itu.Afterwards, I take the long, blue scarf.
Aku melilitkannya di leherku.I wrap it around my neck.
Terakhir, aku memakai sarung tangan hitam yang baru itu.Finally, I put on the new, black gloves.
Sekarang aku sudah memakai semua pakaian hangatku.Now I have all my warm clothes on.
Aku tersenyum dan berpamitan.I smile and say goodbye.
Aku berjalan keluar dari toko.I walk out of the shop.
Aku kembali berdiri di jalan yang dingin.I stand out on the cold street again.
Sangat dingin membeku.It is freezing cold.
Angin bertiup kencang.The wind blows hard.
Tapi aku tidak kedinginan sama sekali.But I am not freezing at all.
Sekarang aku siap menghadapi musim dingin.Now I am ready for winter.
