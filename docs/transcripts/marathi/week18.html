waking up sick

calling the clinic
Storyboard: Maya finds the contact number for a nearby neighborhood clinic and makes a phone call. She speaks politely to the receptionist to explain her situation and successfully books a morning appointment to see the doctor.

"हो, मी साडेदहा वाजता तिथे येईन.""Yes, I will come there at half past ten."
"खूप धन्यवाद," मी म्हणते आणि फोन ठेवते."Thank you very much," I say and hang up the phone.
आता मी हळूहळू बाहेर जाण्यासाठी तयार होते.Now I slowly get ready to go out.

explaining the symptoms

the doctors advice
Storyboard: After a quick examination, the doctor reassures her that it is just a seasonal viral infection and writes a prescription. He advises her that she should drink plenty of warm water, eat light food, and rest as much as possible.

मी थोडी शांत बसते.I sit calmly for a moment.

"हा फक्त ऋतू बदलल्यामुळे झालेला व्हायरल संसर्ग आहे.""This is just a viral infection caused by the changing season."
