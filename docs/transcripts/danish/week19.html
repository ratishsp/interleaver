Week 19: Maya starts a new project with the team

monday bus excitement
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya takes the bus to work. She feels excited and a little nervous because a large new software project starts today, and she will finally work closely with the entire team.

Jeg er lidt nervøs i dag.I am a little nervous today.
Men jeg glæder mig også meget.But I am also looking forward to it a lot.
Et stort, nyt softwareprojekt starter i dag.A large, new software project starts today.
Jeg har arbejdet som softwareingeniør i firmaet siden januar.I have worked as a software engineer at the company since January.
Jeg kender min kollega Peter godt.I know my colleague Peter well.

Bussen stopper, og flere mennesker stiger på.The bus stops, and more people get on.
Jeg kigger på mit ur.I look at my watch.
Klokken er otte.It is eight o'clock.
Morgenmødet begynder om en halv time.The morning meeting begins in half an hour.
Jeg tager en dyb indånding.I take a deep breath.
Jeg er klar til det nye projekt.I am ready for the new project.
first morning presentation
Storyboard: At the short morning meeting, Maya stands in front of her colleagues. She presents her part of the technical plan for the first time, explaining what she has worked on to prepare for the week's tasks.
Vi sidder i mødelokalet.We are sitting in the meeting room.
Det er tid til vores korte morgenmøde.It is time for our short morning meeting.
Jeg kigger rundt på mine kolleger.I look around at my colleagues.

Mine hænder ryster lidt.My hands are shaking a little.
Men jeg har forberedt mig godt.But I have prepared well.
"Godmorgen," siger jeg højt."Good morning," I say loudly.
"Jeg har arbejdet på den tekniske plan," fortæller jeg dem."I have worked on the technical plan," I tell them.
"Jeg har læst alle dokumenterne.""I have read all the documents."
"Og jeg har skrevet notater til ugens opgaver.""And I have written notes for the week's tasks."
Jeg forklarer planen for hele holdet.I explain the plan to the whole team.

Peter sidder over for mig.Peter is sitting opposite me.
Jeg mærker, at jeg slapper mere af.I feel myself relaxing more.
Han lytter og nikker.He listens and nods.
Jeg slutter min præsentation, og de andre smiler.I finish my presentation, and the others smile.
Min første præsentation på arbejdet er gået godt.My first presentation at work has gone well.
boss project manager
Storyboard: After the morning meeting, her boss Thomas stops by Maya's desk with a part of the new project for her. He is warm and welcoming: he compliments her on her presentation and says the whole team has looked forward to having her on board. Maya feels genuinely welcomed onto the team.
Efter morgenmødet sidder jeg ved mit skrivebord.After the morning meeting, I sit at my desk.
Jeg kigger på mine noter fra præsentationen.I look at my notes from the presentation.
Min chef, Thomas, kommer over til mig.My boss, Thomas, comes over to me.
Han er vores projektleder.He is our project manager.
Han har arbejdet her i mange år.He has worked here for many years.
"Godmorgen, Maya," siger han med et smil."Good morning, Maya," he says with a smile.
"Det var en rigtig god præsentation.""That was a really good presentation."
"Tak, Thomas," svarer jeg."Thank you, Thomas," I answer.
"Vi er meget glade for at have dig på holdet," fortsætter Thomas."We are very happy to have you on the team," Thomas continues.

Thomas smiler igen og går tilbage til sit kontor.Thomas smiles again and walks back to his office.
Jeg kigger på min computerskærm.I look at my computer screen.
Jeg føler mig rigtig velkommen på holdet.I feel genuinely welcome on the team.
peter software engineer

Jeg sætter mig ved Peters skrivebord.I sit down at Peter's desk.
Vi kigger på hans store skærm.We look at his large screen.
Peter er også softwareingeniør.Peter is also a software engineer.
Han er en god kollega, og vi skriver meget kode sammen.He is a good colleague, and we write a lot of code together.
I dag ser han meget træt ud.Today he looks very tired.
"Hvad laver du, Peter?" spørger jeg."What are you doing, Peter?" I ask.
"Jeg har et stort problem," svarer han."I have a big problem," he answers.
"Jeg har arbejdet med denne fejl i tre timer.""I have worked on this error for three hours."
Jeg smiler og kigger godt på koden.I smile and look closely at the code.
Vi løser ofte komplekse problemer sammen i vores arbejde.We often solve complex problems together in our work.
Pludselig ser jeg noget mærkeligt på skærmen.Suddenly I see something strange on the screen.
Det er en meget lille fejl.It is a very small mistake.
"Prøv at kigge her," siger jeg og peger på skærmen."Try to look here," I say and point at the screen.
"Du har lavet en lille tastefejl her.""You have made a small typo here."
Peter kigger stille på skærmen i to sekunder.Peter looks quietly at the screen for two seconds.
Så griner han højt.Then he laughs loudly.
"Du har helt ret," siger Peter."You are absolutely right," Peter says.
"Jeg har arbejdet i tre timer, og så er det bare én lille fejl!""I have worked for three hours, and then it is just one small error!"
Jeg griner også, for den situation kender alle softwareingeniører.I laugh too, because all software engineers know that situation.
Vi retter fejlen, og programmet virker endelig perfekt.We fix the error, and the program finally works perfectly.
eva digital designer

meeting the tester
