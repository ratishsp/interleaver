meeting peter
Storyboard: Inside the bright office, a smiling colleague introduces himself as Peter. He shakes her hand, hands her a new laptop, and leads her through the open-plan workspace to her assigned desk.

the phone shop
Storyboard: On her lunch break, Maya ducks into a telecom shop to buy a local SIM card. She hands the clerk her phone, and he activates it, reading her brand-new local number aloud as she saves it.
Es mi hora de almuerzo.It is my lunch break.

Tengo un teléfono de México.I have a phone from Mexico.

"Sí, por supuesto", responde."Yes, of course," he answers.
Le doy mi teléfono.I give him my phone.
Pone la tarjeta SIM en el teléfono.He puts the SIM card in the phone.

"Doce, treinta y cuatro, cincuenta y seis, setenta y ocho", repito."Twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I repeat.
"Sí, exactamente", dice el empleado."Yes, exactly," says the clerk.

Guardo el teléfono en mi bolsillo.I put the phone in my pocket.
queue ticket

registration
Storyboard: The screen chimes with her number, and Maya steps up to the counter. She volunteers her name and age to the clerk, hands over her passport, and he processes the paperwork for her private national ID number.
Una pantalla muestra mi número.A screen shows my number.
El número es cincuenta y nueve.The number is fifty-nine.
Me acerco al mostrador.I walk up to the counter.

Escribe en su computadora.He types on his computer.

El número es privado y muy importante.The number is private and very important.

Ahora soy parte de Dinamarca.Now I am a part of Denmark.
