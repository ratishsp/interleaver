Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
Lietadlo je teraz v Kodani.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
Je január.It is January.
Som veľmi nadšená.I am very excited.
Balím si knihu do tašky.I pack my book into my bag.
Vedľa mňa je cestujúci.There is a passenger next to me.
Pozerá sa na mňa a usmieva sa.He looks at me and smiles.
„Ahoj,“ hovorí."Hello," he says.
„Ahoj,“ hovorím."Hello," I say.
„Ako sa voláš?“ pýta sa."What is your name?" he asks.
„Volám sa Maya,“ hovorím."My name is Maya," I say.
„Odkiaľ pochádzaš, Maya?“ pýta sa."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
Radostne sa usmievam.I smile happily.
„Pochádzam z Mexika,“ odpovedám."I come from Mexico," I answer.
„Si z Dánska?“ pýtam sa."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
„Áno, som z Dánska,“ hovorí."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
„Vitaj v Dánsku,“ hovorí."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem,“ hovorím."Thank you very much," I say.
Teraz sme tu.Now we are here.
Som pripravená.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
Teraz som vonku.I am outside now.
Môj kufor je veľký a ťažký.My suitcase is big and heavy.
Tam je taxík.There is a taxi.
Dvíham svoj kufor do taxíka.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
Vodič je priateľský muž.The driver is a friendly man.
„Aká je adresa?“ pýta sa."What is the address?" he asks.
„Je to Parkvej číslo dvanásť,“ hovorím."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
Prikývne a ide.He nods and drives.
„Ste turistka?“ pýta sa vodič."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
„Nie, nie som turistka,“ odpovedám."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
„Odkiaľ pochádzate?“ pýta sa."Where do you come from?" he asks.
„Pochádzam z Mexika,“ hovorím."I come from Mexico," I say.
Pozerám sa z okna.I look out the window.
Som veľmi hrdá.I am very proud.
„Som softvérová inžinierka,“ hovorím."I am a software engineer," I say.
„Moja nová práca je tu v Kodani.“"My new job is here in Copenhagen."
Usmeje sa.He smiles.
„Aké vzrušujúce.“"How exciting."
„Veľa šťastia v Dánsku,“ hovorí."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem,“ hovorím."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
Dvere sú otvorené.The door is open.
Prah je vysoký.The threshold is high.
Kufor je veľmi ťažký!The suitcase is very heavy!
Ale teraz je kufor na chodbe.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
Je tu úplná tma.It is completely dark here.
Kde je vypínač?Where is the switch?
Stena je studená.The wall is cold.
Je vypínač tu?Is the switch here?
Áno, tam je vypínač!Yes, there is the switch!
Teraz je tu svetlo.Now there is light.
Som v byte.I am in the apartment.
Je malý, ale je pekný.It is small, but it is nice.
Tu je obývačka.Here is a living room.
Je tam pohovka a stôl.There is a sofa and a table.
Je to zariadený domov.It is a furnished home.
Kufor je na podlahe.The suitcase is on the floor.
Som veľmi unavená.I am very tired.
Ale som aj šťastná.But I am also happy.
Som v Kodani!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
Stojím pri okne.I am standing by the window.
Sklo je studené.The glass is cold.
Je január.It is January.
Pozerám sa von.I look out.
Kodaň je dnes sivá.Copenhagen is grey today.
Vonku je zima.It is cold outside.
Na ulici je sneh.There is snow on the street.
Ale tu je teplo.But it is warm here.
Byt je dobrý.The apartment is good.
Myslím na Mexiko.I think about Mexico.
V Mexiku je slnko.In Mexico, there is sun.
Aj tam je teplo.It is also warm there.
Trochu mi chýba slnko.I miss the sun a little.
Ale usmievam sa.But I smile.
Je to nové mesto.It is a new city.
Teraz som v Dánsku.I am in Denmark now.
Je to dobrý začiatok.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
Teraz som v obývačke.I am in the living room now.
Je tu malá pohovka.There is a small sofa.
Volám mame do Mexika.I call my mother in Mexico.
„Ahoj, mami,“ hovorím."Hi Mom," I say.
„Ahoj, Maya,“ hovorí moja mama."Hi Maya," my mother says.
„Kde si?“ pýta sa."Where are you?" she asks.
„Som v Kodani,“ odpovedám."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
„Teraz som v byte.“"I am in the apartment now."
„To je veľmi dobre,“ hovorí moja mama."That is so good," my mother says.
„Je veľký?“ pýta sa."Is it big?" she asks.
„Nie, je malý,“ odpovedám."No, it is small," I answer.
„Ale je útulný a som šťastná.“"But it is cozy, and I am happy."
„Je tam zima?“ pýta sa."Is it cold?" she asks.
„Áno, vonku je zima,“ hovorím."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
„Ale tu je teplo.“"But it is warm in here."
„Som v bezpečí.“"I am safe."
„To je dobre,“ hovorí moja mama."That is good," my mother says.
Usmievam sa.I smile.
Dánsko je môj nový domov.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
Otváram svoje dvere.I open my door.
Stojím na schodisku.I am standing in the stairwell.
Je tu ticho.It is quiet.
Otvárajú sa dvere.A door opens.
Nejaká žena sa na mňa usmieva.A woman smiles at me.
„Ahoj,“ hovorí."Hi," she says.
„Ako sa voláš?“ pýta sa."What is your name?" she asks.
„Volám sa Maya,“ odpovedám."My name is Maya," I answer.
„Odkiaľ pochádzaš?“ pýta sa."Where do you come from?" she asks.
„Pochádzam z Mexika,“ hovorím."I come from Mexico," I say.
„Vitaj v dome,“ hovorí žena."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
„Volám sa Nina,“ hovorí."My name is Nina," she says.
„Som tvoja susedka.“"I am your neighbour."
Nina je veľmi priateľská.Nina is very friendly.
Hovorí pomaly a zreteľne.She speaks slowly and clearly.
„Tu sú moje dvere,“ hovorí."Here is my door," she says.
„Rada pomôžem.“"I am happy to help."
„Stačí len zaklopať,“ hovorí Nina."You just knock," Nina says.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem, Nina,“ odpovedám."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
Som veľmi šťastná.I am very happy.
Teraz mám v Kodani kamarátku.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
Teraz je noc.Now it is night.
Je to moja prvá noc v Kodani.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
Som v kúpeľni.I am in the bathroom.
Je veľmi malá.It is very small.
Je tam umývadlo.There is a sink.
Moja zubná kefka je červená.My toothbrush is red.
Dávam si zubnú pastu na kefku.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
Čistím si zuby.I brush my teeth.
Voda je studená.The water is cold.
Umývam si tvár.I wash my face.
Utieram si tvár.I dry my face.
Idem k svojej posteli.I go over to my bed.
Posteľ je úzka.The bed is narrow.
Ale je teplá a mäkká.But it is warm and soft.
Teraz ležím pod paplónom.I am lying under the duvet now.
Môj telefón leží na stole.My phone is lying on the table.
Nastavujem si budík.I set my alarm.
Zazvoní zajtra o ôsmej.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
Som unavená, ale som pripravená.I am tired, but I am ready.
Dobrú noc, Kodaň.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
