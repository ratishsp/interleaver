# Week 26 — Storyboard (Summer Light and New Traditions)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Perfect vs preteritum contrast

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_birthday_morning | Maya wakes up to brilliant sunlight on the morning she turns thirty-two. Pouring her coffee, she reflects that she moved to Denmark during a dark, freezing January, but she has thoroughly enjoyed these newly bright and endless summer mornings. |
| 2 | 02_office_pastry | At the software company, Maya is surprised when Peter and Eva place a small pastry with a candle on her desk. Peter explains that they bought the treat from the corner bakery earlier because she has worked incredibly hard on their latest project. |
| 3 | 03_family_gift | That evening, Maya has a lively video call with her parents, Sofía, and Diego. Sofía eagerly asks if Maya received the package they have sent from Mexico, and Maya happily holds up the colorful birthday scarf that arrived in the mail yesterday. |
| 4 | 04_cafe_anticipation | On Saturday morning, just after she finished her regular volunteer shift at the café, Maya realizes she has never experienced a traditional Danish Midsummer. She quickly texts Nina to confirm their plans for the evening bonfire. |
| 5 | 05_packing_sweaters | Later that evening, Nina knocks on Maya's door so they can head down to the waterfront. Nina asks if Maya has packed a jacket for the night; Maya nods, explaining she grabbed her thickest sweater because she noticed the wind picked up earlier. |
| 6 | 06_biking_light | Riding their bicycles toward the beach, Maya marvels at the vibrant evening sky. She tells Nina that she biked home in pitch black at four o'clock back in February, but tonight the sun has not even started to set. |
| 7 | 07_midsummer_bonfire | They meet Eva and Peter on the sand just as a large crowd lights the towering Midsummer bonfire. Eva shares fresh strawberries she bought at the market, noting that the glowing fire has already chased away the coastal chill. |
| 8 | 08_midnight_blue | Walking their bikes back to the apartment near midnight under a sky that is still soft blue, Maya feels a deep sense of peace. She realizes she has built a wonderful life with good friends since she stepped off the plane six months ago. |
