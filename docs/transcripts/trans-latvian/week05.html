Week 5: Maya stocks her new kitchen

empty cupboards
Storyboard: Maya stands in the kitchen, noticing that the fridge and the cupboard are completely empty. She grabs a bag and heads out, ready to buy food.

Es stāvu virtuvē.I am standing in the kitchen.

Tas ir pilnīgi tukšs.It is completely empty.
Es ieskatos skapī.I look in the cupboard.
Skapī nav ēdiena.There is no food in the cupboard.
Man nav ēdiena.I have no food.
Man ir tikai nedaudz ūdens.I only have a little water.
Es tagad gribētu paēst.I would like to have food now.
Man garšo dārzeņi.I like vegetables.
Man garšo arī maize.I also like bread.
Bet man šeit nekā nav.But I have nothing here.

Maisiņš ir sarkans.The bag is red.
Tas ir labs iepirkumiem.It is good for shopping.
Es izeju no dzīvokļa.I walk out of the flat.
Es aizveru durvis.I close the door.
Es esmu gatava.I am ready.
Tagad es eju uz lielveikalu.Now I am walking to the supermarket.
produce section

Nodaļa ir tieši šeit.The section is right here.

the parsnip
Storyboard: Looking around, Maya spots a pale, unusual root. She reads a sign and learns it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she decides to buy it anyway out of curiosity.
Es redzu dīvainu dārzeni.I see a strange vegetable.
Dārzenis ir gara sakne.The vegetable is a long root.
Sakne ir ļoti gaiša.The root is very pale.
Tā izskatās pēc balta burkāna.It looks like a white carrot.
Es paskatos uz leju uz zīmīti.I look down at the sign.
Uz zīmītes rakstīts "pastinaks".It says "parsnip" on the sign.
Es nepazīstu šo dārzeni.I do not know the vegetable.
Vai tas ir dāņu dārzenis?Is it a Danish vegetable?
Man garšo dārzeņi.I like vegetables.
Man garšo burkāni.I like carrots.
Varbūt pastinaks garšo labi.Maybe the parsnip tastes good.
Es paņemu pastinaku rokā.I take the parsnip in my hand.
Sakne ir diezgan liela.The root is quite big.
Es gribētu to.I would like to have it.

Es gribētu pamēģināt kaut ko jaunu.I would like to try something new.
the rye bread
Storyboard: At a shelf with baked goods, Maya looks for a local staple. She lifts a loaf of rye bread, surprised by how heavy it feels, and lays the bread carefully in the basket.
Es pieeju pie plaukta ar maizi.I walk over to a shelf with bread.
Es meklēju vietējo klasiku.I am looking for a local classic.
Es gribētu dāņu rudzu maizi.I would like to have a Danish rye bread.
Es skatos uz plauktu.I look at the shelf.
Es atrodu rudzu maizi.I find a rye bread.
Tā ir maisiņā.It is in a bag.
Es skatos uz maisiņu.I look at the bag.
Es paņemu rudzu maizi.I take the rye bread.
Es paceļu maizi.I lift the bread.
Es esmu pārsteigta.I am surprised.
Maize ir ļoti smaga.The bread is very heavy.
Tā šķiet kā akmens!It feels like a stone!

bakery counter
Storyboard: The bakery counter has a member of staff waiting. Maya catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry.
Es pieeju pie maizesnīcas letes.I walk over to the bakery counter.
Smarža ir ļoti laba.The smell is very good.

Un man ļoti patīk kanēļmaizītes.And I really like cinnamon pastries.

Maiznieks skatās uz mani.The baker looks at me.
Viņš smaida.He smiles.
"Ko jūs vēlētos?" jautā maiznieks."What would you like to have?" the baker asks.
Es norādu uz kanēļmaizīti.I point at a cinnamon pastry.
Maizīte izskatās garda.The pastry looks delicious.
"Es gribētu kanēļmaizīti, lūdzu," es saku."I would like to have a cinnamon pastry, please," I say.
"Jā, protams," saka maiznieks."Yes, of course," the baker says.
Viņš paņem maizīti.He takes the pastry.
Viņš ieliek maizīti maisiņā.He puts the pastry in a bag.
Viņš pasniedz maisiņu pāri letei.He hands the bag over the counter.
"Lūdzu," viņš saka."Here you go," he says.
Es paņemu maisiņu.I take the bag.
"Liels paldies," es saku."Thank you very much," I say.
Es ielieku maisiņu grozā.I put the bag down into the basket.
