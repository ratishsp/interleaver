# Week 20 — Storyboard (Maya starts volunteering at the community café)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Past + perfect mixed; **പഠിക്കുക + -ാൻ** (learning to); ordinals **ഒന്നാമത്തെ/രണ്ടാമത്തെ**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_saturday_morning_walk | Maya walks to the little NGO-run community café she has frequented for months, carrying her umbrella through the light monsoon drizzle. She reflects on her decision to spend her Saturday mornings volunteering behind the counter: she wanted to meet new people and feel even more rooted in the city. |
| 2 | 02_greeting_jessy | Inside the community space, Maya is greeted by Jessy, the experienced café worker she knows from her earlier visits. Jessy hands Maya a clean apron and welcomes her to her first official shift; knowing Maya is a software engineer, Jessy asks if she has ever volunteered in hospitality or worked in a kitchen before. |
| 3 | 03_the_espresso_machine | Before the doors open, Jessy introduces Maya to the large, gleaming espresso machine. Maya watches closely as Jessy demonstrates the dials and the steam wand, explaining that learning to operate it properly takes patience and practice. |
| 4 | 04_first_coffee_attempt | Under Jessy's watchful eye, Maya tries to make her very first coffee of the day. She struggles to angle the jug while frothing the milk, and the drink comes out completely wrong—flat and far too hot—making her laugh nervously as she apologizes. |
| 5 | 05_second_coffee_success | Taking a deep breath, Maya prepares her second coffee. This time, she focuses on what she has just learned to do with the steam wand, and the milk comes out smooth and frothy; Jessy nods in approval, and Maya feels a genuine rush of pride. |
| 6 | 06_the_first_customer | The community café opens its doors, and Maya stands nervously at the counter to serve her first customer. Having practiced the mechanics, she now focuses entirely on the interaction—greeting the patron, carefully taking the order, and cleanly handing over the finished cup without keeping anyone waiting. |
| 7 | 07_mid_morning_rush | As the morning rush peaks, the pace becomes much too fast for a beginner on the espresso machine. Jessy smoothly takes over the coffee making, while Maya learns to manage the pastry case and the register, counting out change carefully as the queue moves. |
| 8 | 08_break_with_jessy | During a short lull before noon, Maya and Jessy share a quick break by the back counter—Jessy with a coffee, and Maya with a cup of local tea, as she prefers to brew her Mexican coffee stash at home. They chat about how the shift has gone, and about the small things Maya has slowly learned to love in Kochi. |
| 9 | 09_video_call_home | Back in her flat that evening, Maya video calls her sister Sofia in Mexico. She excitedly recounts the events of her first shift, detailing the mistakes she made early on and proudly sharing how she has learned to steam milk by her second try. |
