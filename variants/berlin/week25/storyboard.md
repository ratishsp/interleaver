# Week 25 — Storyboard (A slow Sunday and a sudden shower)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Demonstratives **dieser/diese/dieses** against plain **der/die/das**; **vor** for "ago" (*vor zwei Monaten*); time adverbs

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_sunday_morning_reflection | A quiet Sunday morning in June. Maya wakes up late in the flat and makes herself coffee. With ordinary shops shut for the day — said here once, and not re-explained later in the week — she has no errands to run and nowhere to be. Looking out the window at the bright morning, she thinks about the timeline of her life here. She reflects that she arrived exactly five months ago, when it was freezing and dark, and marvels at how much her life has changed since then. |
| 2 | 02_the_flea_market | Late morning. Maya grabs her backpack and walks to a large flea market in a local park. Flea markets are always bustling on Berlin Sundays. She wanders slowly between the stalls, using time adverbs to note that she 'often' or 'sometimes' comes here just to look. At one clothing stall, the vendor (using the formal 'Sie') points out a jacket, but Maya politely corrects him, pointing to another one: she does not want that jacket, she wants to look at *this* jacket ('diese Jacke'). |
| 3 | 03_antique_camera | Continuing her stroll, Maya stops at a table covered in vintage electronics. She asks the stallholder ('Sie' register) about the price difference between two items, asking how much 'this camera' ('diese Kamera') costs compared to 'that camera'. They chat briefly, and Maya mentions that she bought her own digital camera many years ago ('vor vielen Jahren'). She decides to leave it on the table, enjoying the conversation more than the shopping. |
| 4 | 04_sudden_summer_rain | Early afternoon. The warm June day is suddenly interrupted by a heavy summer rain shower. Time adverbs drive the action. Vendors scramble to throw plastic sheets over their tables. Standing under a tree, Maya remembers a freezing, miserable rainstorm from three months ago ('vor drei Monaten'), but notes that this warm summer rain feels completely different. |
| 5 | 05_museum_shelter | To escape the downpour, Maya hurries into a small local history museum bordering the park, knowing museums are reliably open on Sundays. At the ticket desk, the attendant sells her an entry ticket and hands her the receipt. Maya wipes the rain from her face, checks her backpack into the cloakroom as is strictly required by the museum, and steps into the quiet, dry exhibition halls. |
| 6 | 06_comparing_paintings | Maya wanders through a gallery of local Berlin landscape paintings. She stands in front of two pieces side by side, comparing them in her head: she decides she likes *this* picture ('dieses Bild') much more than *that* picture. She pulls out her phone to send a quick photo of the artwork to her colleague Jonas, reminding him of a conversation they had about art a few weeks ago ('vor ein paar Wochen'). |
| 7 | 07_museum_cafe | Late afternoon. The rain is still falling steadily, so Maya heads to the museum's café to wait it out. She buys a tea and a Brötchen, taking a seat by the large glass windows. Watching the rain, she falls into another reflection, using 'vor' to think about how overwhelmed she felt half a year ago ('vor einem halben Jahr') when she was still packing her life into boxes in Mexico. |
| 8 | 08_cleared_skies | Early evening. The rain finally stops and the clouds part, leaving the city smelling fresh and cool. Maya collects her Rucksack from the cloakroom and steps out of the museum. Instead of taking the U-Bahn, she decides to walk all the way back to the flat. Do not state again that the shops are shut — scenes 1 and 2 have already established it, and a third telling flattens the ending. Walking home through the quiet streets, she feels a deep sense of calm and belonging, quietly looking forward to the rest of the summer and turning 32 next week. |
