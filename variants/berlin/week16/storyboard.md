# Week 16 — Storyboard (Maya tells the story of how she came to Berlin)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** The **Perfekt** with **haben** (*ich habe … gemacht, gesehen, gekauft*); time phrases **gestern / letzte Woche / im Januar** (the Perfekt with *sein* is out of scope — week 17 owns it)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_thursday_badminton | It is Thursday evening at half past five. Maya is at the badminton club. She rents a racket and plays a lively doubles match with Timo, Lena and Max. They cheer each other on, firmly using their familiar du. Afterwards Max says goodbye — he has an early start tomorrow — and the other three stay behind. |
| 2 | 02_the_berlin_question | After the game they sit down to rest. Timo and Lena tell her about a tournament they played letzte Woche, then ask Maya why she chose Berlin and how she found her job. Maya begins her story: she found the advertisement im Oktober and applied straight away. Then she waited a long time — papers, forms, an appointment at the embassy. Im Januar she finally packed her suitcase. |
| 3 | 03_taxi_and_stairs | Maya carries the story on. At the airport she paid for a taxi and gave the driver her new address. She dragged her heavy suitcase up the stairs, and on that very first day she met her neighbour Sabine and the dog Bruno. Timo and Lena laugh at the picture of it. |
| 4 | 04_first_office_day_recap | Lena asks about the job. Maya describes her first day: she met Jonas, unpacked her laptop from her Rucksack, and they drank coffee together while she set up her desk. Then Lena asks what she is working on now, and Maya says that she and Jonas finished a big project gestern. |
| 5 | 05_calling_home | Later that Thursday evening Maya relaxes in the flat. She picks up her phone and starts a video call with her family in Mexico. Her mother answers. Maya mentions that she told Timo and Lena the whole story this evening — the job, the papers, the first weeks here. |
| 6 | 06_the_airport_morning | Her mother was there for the goodbye, but she has never heard Maya's own version of that morning, and she asks for it now. So Maya tells her the part she could not see: how she counted the people in the queue to keep herself calm, how she has never told anyone that she cried at the gate, and how she watched the family through the glass for as long as she could. |
| 7 | 07_her_mothers_side | Then her mother tells Maya what she remembers of the same morning. Diego bought Maya far too much chocolate for the flight. Sofia took a photo of them all at the café. Afterwards they stood at the big window and watched the planes, and on the way home Diego said nothing at all. Maya has not talked about that morning for months. She sits with her phone in her hand and feels very close to them. |
