# Week 7 — Storyboard (Maya plans a busy week)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Telling the clock — *klokken*; weekdays/months; frequency adverbs

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_february_calendar | On Monday morning, Maya clears her breakfast dishes, sits at the kitchen table, and turns her calendar page to February. She finds a pen and carefully writes her regular Monday and Wednesday gym classes on the fresh page. |
| 2 | 02_broken_bicycle | On Tuesday morning at eight o'clock, Maya rides her bicycle towards the office, but the chain suddenly makes a loud noise. She stops at a small repair shop, leaves the bike with a mechanic, and walks the rest of the way to work. |
| 3 | 03_booking_haircut | During a quiet break on Wednesday, Maya looks at the clock on her computer. It is a quarter past two, so she calls a local hair salon and successfully books an appointment for Thursday afternoon at half past four. |
| 4 | 04_evening_workout | Later that evening, Maya arrives at the local gym at seven o'clock. She usually trains twice a week, so she stretches her arms, joins a lively fitness class, and finally walks home feeling tired but happy. |
| 5 | 05_morning_mail | On Thursday morning, Maya checks her mailbox in the hallway and unexpectedly meets Nina. They stand and chat for a few minutes, and they happily agree to drink coffee together later today at half past four. |
| 6 | 06_scheduling_conflict | On the bus to work, Maya opens her calendar app to add the coffee date and notices a big problem. Both the haircut and the coffee are at half past four, so she immediately sends Nina a message to move the cafe trip to six o'clock. |
| 7 | 07_afternoon_errands | In the afternoon, Maya leaves work at a quarter to four and walks back to the mechanic. She collects her perfectly fixed bicycle, cycles to the salon, and sits back in a chair for a fresh haircut. |
| 8 | 08_cafe_with_nina | At exactly six o'clock, Maya meets Nina at a cozy neighborhood cafe. They order warm drinks, find a small table by the window, and enjoy a long, relaxing evening together away from their busy schedules. |
