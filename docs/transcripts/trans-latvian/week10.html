Week 10: Maya buys winter clothes

Latvian with English gloss, line-aligned.

a cold morning
Storyboard: It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to the bus stop on her way to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter.
Kopenhāgenā ir rīts.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Es eju uz autobusa pieturu.I walk to the bus stop.
Laiks šodien ir ļoti auksts.The weather is very cold today.
Ir vējains.It is windy.
Vējš ir ledusauksts.The wind is ice-cold.
Es salstu.I am freezing.
Man mugurā ir plāna jaka.I am wearing a thin jacket.
Mana jaka nemaz nav silta.My jacket is not warm at all.
Man kājās ir arī plāni apavi.I am also wearing thin shoes.
Man nav uzlikta silta šalle.I am not wearing a warm scarf.
Manas rokas ir ļoti aukstas.My hands are very cold.
Es drebu.I am shivering.
Ziema Dānijā ir barga.The winter in Denmark is hard.
Man vajag siltas drēbes.I need warm clothes.
Man vajag biezu džemperi.I need a thick sweater.
Man vajag arī lielu šalli.I also need a large scarf.
Un man vajag siltus cimdus.And I need warm gloves.
Es eju ļoti ātri.I walk very fast.
Es pirkšu ziemas drēbes pēc darba.I am buying winter clothes after work.
entering the shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things.
Esmu beigusi darbu.I am finished at work.
Es eju pa gājēju ielu.I walk down the pedestrian street.
Vējš joprojām ir ļoti auksts.The wind is still very cold.
Es redzu lielu apģērbu veikalu.I see a large clothing shop.
Veikals ir ļoti gaišs.The shop is very bright.
Es ieeju veikalā.I go into the shop.
Šeit iekšā ir patīkami silti.It is wonderfully warm in here.
Aiz kases stāv pārdevēja.A shop assistant stands behind the register.
Viņa skatās uz mani un smaida.She looks at me and smiles.
"Sveiki," viņa saka."Hello," she says.
"Sveiki," es atbildu."Hello," I answer.
"Es ļoti salstu," es saku."I am freezing a lot," I say.
"Man mugurā ir tikai plāna jaka.""I only have a thin jacket on."
"Es gribētu atrast kādas siltas drēbes.""I would like to find some warm things."
Pārdevēja atkal pasmaida.The shop assistant smiles again.
"Šodien arī ir ļoti auksts," viņa saka."It is also very cold today," she says.
"Mums ir daudz biezu džemperu.""We have many thick sweaters."
"Mums ir arī silts ziemas apģērbs.""We also have warm winter clothes."
"Droši apskatieties," viņa laipni saka."You just take a look," she says kindly.
"Liels paldies," es atbildu."Many thanks," I answer.
the red sweater
Storyboard: Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it.
Es skatos uz drēbēm plauktos.I look at the clothes on the shelves.
Es redzu biezu džemperi.I see a thick sweater.
Tas ir sarkans.It is red.
Man vajag siltu džemperi.I need a warm sweater.
Veikalā ir daudz izmēru.The shop has many sizes.
Es atrodu M izmēru.I find a size medium.
Es ieeju pielaikošanas kabīnē.I walk into the fitting room.
Es uzvelku sarkano džemperi.I put the red sweater on.
Man tas ir mugurā, un es skatos lielajā spogulī.I have it on and look in the big mirror.
Džemperis der ideāli.The sweater fits perfectly.
Tas ir neticami silts.It is incredibly warm.
Man ļoti patīk sarkanā krāsa.I love the red colour.
Es skatos uz cenu zīmi.I look at the price tag.
Džemperis maksā trīssimt kronas.The sweater costs three hundred kroner.
Tā ir laba cena par biezu džemperi.It is a good price for a thick sweater.
Es atkal novelku džemperi.I take the sweater off again.
Es to nopērku.I buy it.
the tight green coat
Storyboard: Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a larger size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. The assistant brings her a warm dark-blue winter jacket in her size instead — it fits perfectly, and Maya decides to take it.
Man arī vajag ziemas jaku.I also need a winter jacket.
Es apskatu vēl citas drēbes veikalā.I look at more clothes in the shop.
Es atrodu skaistu, zaļu mēteli.I find a beautiful, green coat.
Tas ir ļoti skaists dizains.It is a very beautiful design.
Es uzvelku zaļo mēteli.I put the green coat on.
Bet tas ir pārāk mazs.But it is too small.
Tas ir ļoti ciešs plecos.It is very tight across the shoulders.
"Vai jums tas ir citā izmērā?" es jautāju."Do you have it in another size?" I ask.
Pārdevēja paskatās.The shop assistant looks.
"Nē, diemžēl," viņa atbild."No, unfortunately," she answers.
Tāpēc es pakaru zaļo mēteli atpakaļ.So I hang the green coat back.
Pārdevēja man atrod citu jaku.The shop assistant finds another jacket for me.
Tā ir silta, tumši zila ziemas jaka.It is a warm, dark-blue winter jacket.
Es uzvelku tumši zilo jaku.I put the dark-blue jacket on.
Tā der perfekti.It fits perfectly.
Tā ir patīkami liela un nav cieša.It is nice and big and not tight.
Es ieskatos lielā spogulī.I look in a large mirror.
Man tā patīk.I like it.
"Es to ņemšu," es saku."I will take it," I say.
the blue scarf
Storyboard: Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases.
Es pieeju pie galda veikalā.I walk over to a table in the shop.
Uz galda ir daudz lietu.There are many things on the table.
Šodien es neesmu aplikusi šalli.I am not wearing a scarf today.
Man ļoti salst kakls.I am very cold around my neck.
Es redzu zilu šalli.I see a blue scarf.
Tā ir ļoti gara šalle.It is a very long scarf.
Tā ir izgatavota no vilnas.It is made of wool.
Es paņemu zilo šalli.I take the blue scarf.
Es to aplieku ap kaklu.I put it around my neck.
Tā šķiet brīnišķīgi silta.It feels wonderfully warm.
"Tā ir skaista krāsa," saka pārdevēja."It is a nice colour," the shop assistant says.
"Jā, un tā ir ļoti mīksta," es saku."Yes, and it is very soft," I say.
Es paskatos uz cenu zīmi.I look at the price tag.
Tā maksā simt piecdesmit kronu.It costs one hundred and fifty kroner.
Tā ir laba cena par siltu šalli.It is a good price for a warm scarf.
Es to atkal noņemu.I take it off again.
"Es pērku arī to," es viņai saku."I am buying it too," I say to her.
Es nolieku zilo šalli kopā ar sarkano džemperi.I put the blue scarf together with the red sweater.
the black gloves
Storyboard: Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well.
Man joprojām ir mugurā mana plānā jaka.I am still wearing my thin jacket.
Es domāju par savām aukstajām rokām no šī rīta.I think about my cold hands from this morning.
Man vajag biezus cimdus.I need thick gloves.
Es skatos apkārt gaišajā veikalā.I look around the bright shop.
Es redzu lielu grozu.I see a large basket.
Grozs ir pilns ar cimdiem.The basket is full of gloves.
Es atrodu melnu ziemas cimdu pāri.I find a pair of black winter gloves.
Tie ir ļoti biezi un mīksti.They are very thick and soft.
Es uzvelku cimdus.I put the gloves on.
Tie man der perfekti.They fit me perfectly.
Es jautāju smaidīgajai pārdevējai.I ask the smiling shop assistant.
"Cik maksā melnie cimdi?" es jautāju."What do the black gloves cost?" I ask.
"Tie maksā simt divdesmit kronas," viņa atbild."They cost one hundred and twenty kroner," she answers.
Simt divdesmit kronas ir laba cena.One hundred and twenty kroner is a good price.
Manas rokas jau ir siltas.My hands are already warm.
"Es tos ņemšu," es priecīgi saku."I will take them," I say happily.
Es atkal novelku cimdus.I take the gloves off again.
Es nolieku melnos cimdus kopā ar savu sarkano džemperi un zilo šalli.I put the black gloves together with my red sweater and my blue scarf.
Tagad esmu gatava maksāt.Now I am ready to pay.
paying for the clothes
Storyboard: Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the dark-blue winter jacket, the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is one thousand five hundred kroner, and Maya pays with her card.
Es pieeju pie kases ar savām jaunajām drēbēm.I walk over to the counter with my new clothes.
Pārdevēja man laipni uzsmaida.The shop assistant smiles kindly at me.
"Vai tas ir viss?" viņa jautā."Is that everything?" she asks.
"Jā, tas ir viss," es atbildu."Yes, that is everything," I answer.
Viņa noskenē visas manas jaunās lietas.She scans all my new things.
Tur ir džemperis, jaka, šalle un cimdi.There is a sweater, a jacket, a scarf and gloves.
"Tagad jums nesals," viņa priecīgi saka."Now you will not freeze," she says happily.
"Tas būs viens tūkstotis pieci simti kronu, lūdzu.""That will be one thousand five hundred kroner, please."
Drēbes ir dārgas, bet tās ir ļoti siltas.The clothes are expensive, but they are very warm.
Es izņemu savu maksājumu karti.I take out my payment card.
"Es maksāju ar karti," es saku."I am paying by card," I say.
"Jūs varat vienkārši samaksāt šeit," viņa saka."You can just pay here," she says.
Viņa norāda uz mazu, melnu ierīci.She points at a small, black machine.
Es turu savu karti virs ierīces.I hold my card over the machine.
Tā nopīkst, un maksājums ir apstiprināts.It beeps, and the payment is approved.
"Vai vēlaties čeku?" pārdevēja jautā."Do you want the receipt?" the shop assistant asks.
"Nē, paldies," es atbildu."No thank you," I answer.
Tagad man pieder daudz jauku ziemas drēbju.Now I own a lot of nice winter clothes.
stepping into the cold
Storyboard: Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She pulls on the red sweater, zips her new dark-blue winter jacket over it, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm.
"Maisiņu?" jautā pārdevējs."A bag?" the shop assistant asks.
"Nē, paldies," es saku."No thank you," I say.
Man nevajag maisiņu.I do not need a bag.
Es uzreiz uzvelku jaunās drēbes.I put the new clothes on right away.
Vispirms es uzvelku biezo, sarkano džemperi.First, I put on the thick, red sweater.
Tad es uzvelku tumši zilo ziemas jaku.Then, I put on the dark-blue winter jacket.
Es aizvelku silto jaku.I zip the warm jacket.
Pēc tam es paņemu garo, zilo šalli.Afterwards, I take the long, blue scarf.
Es to aptinu ap kaklu.I wrap it around my neck.
Visbeidzot es uzvelku jaunos, melnos cimdus.Finally, I put on the new, black gloves.
Tagad man ir mugurā visas manas siltās drēbes.Now I have all my warm clothes on.
Es pasmaidu un saku uz redzēšanos.I smile and say goodbye.
Es izeju no veikala.I walk out of the shop.
Es atkal stāvu ārā uz aukstās ielas.I stand out on the cold street again.
Ir stindzinoši auksts.It is freezing cold.
Vējš stipri pūš.The wind blows hard.
Bet man nemaz nesalst.But I am not freezing at all.
Tagad es esmu gatava ziemai.Now I am ready for winter.
