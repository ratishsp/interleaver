Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
现在是一月，天气非常冷。It is January, and it is very cold.
我正从哥本哈根的公交车站走回家。I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
我看到街上有一家花店。I see a flower shop on the street.
里面又明亮又温暖。It is bright and warm inside.
我走进了这家温暖的店。I go into the warm shop.
我在看一盆绿色的小植物。I look at a small, green plant.
一位店员对着我微笑。A clerk smiles at me.
“你好，”他说。"Hello," he says.
“你好，”我说。"Hello," I say.
“我想找一盆植物。”"I am looking for a plant."
“这是一份礼物吗？”他问。"Is it a gift?" he asks.
“不是，是放我房间里的，”我回答。"No, it is for my room," I answer.
“这是一盆非常好的植物，”他说。"It is a really good plant," he says.
“这个多少钱？”我问。"How much does it cost?" I ask.
“要五十五克朗，”他回答。"It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
我为我的新植物付了钱。I pay for my new plant.
他把植物放进了一个纸袋里。He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
“给你，这是你的袋子，”他说。"Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
“非常感谢，”我开心地说。"Thank you very much," I say happily.
我走进寒冷的夜色中。I go out into the cold evening.
我的小植物让我非常开心。My little plant makes me very happy.
evening prep
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her temporary room and sets the new plant on a small desk. With it already being pitch black outside, she turns on a warm lamp to reflect against the dark window, makes a cup of tea, and flips open her laptop to dial home.
我现在在家。I am home now.
这里是我的房间。Here is my room.
这是一个小房间。It is a small room.
我有一盆新植物。I have a new plant.
它摆在我的书桌上。It stands on my desk.
现在是晚上，而且是一月。It is evening, and it is January.
我的窗户非常暗。My window is very dark.
外面一片漆黑。It is completely black outside.
我有一盏台灯和一杯茶。I have a lamp and a cup of tea.
我打开我的台灯。I turn on my lamp.
现在我的房间温暖又明亮。Now my room is warm and bright.
我的茶也是热的。My tea is also warm.
我坐在书桌前。I sit at my desk.
我的电脑在这里。I have my computer here.
我打开我的电脑。I open my computer.
现在我给在墨西哥的家人打电话。Now I call my family in Mexico.
我很期待见到他们。I look forward to seeing them.
parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
电话响了几声。It rings a couple of times.
屏幕亮了起来。The screen lights up.
我看到一个客厅。I see a living room.
阳光很好，客厅里非常明亮。There is sun, and the living room is very bright.
我看到两个人。I see two people.
是我的妈妈和爸爸。It is my mother and my father.
他们笑得很开心，向我挥手。They smile a lot and wave to me.
“嗨，妈妈，爸爸！”我大声说。"Hello mom and dad!" I say loudly.
“你好，玛雅，”我爸爸说。"Hello, Maya," my father says.
“最近怎么样？”"How is it going?"
“挺好的，”我高兴地回答。"It is going well," I answer happily.
突然，我听到一个声音。Suddenly I hear a sound.
是一声轻轻的狗叫。It is a little bark.
“哦，我听到狗叫了！”我笑着说。"Oh, I hear a dog!" I say and laugh.
一只小狗跳到了沙发上。A little dog jumps up onto a sofa.
它看着镜头。It looks into the camera.
“那是我的狗！”"There is my dog!"
“嗨，萝拉，”我说。"Hello, Lola," I say.
萝拉摇了摇尾巴。Lola wags her tail.
“对，是你的狗，”我妈妈微笑着说。"Yes, it is your dog," my mother says with a smile.
我爱我的家人。I love my family.
能看到他们大家真是太好了。It is lovely to see them all.
the siblings
Storyboard: Her younger sister Sofía and older brother Diego squeeze into the frame, playfully shoving each other. Sofía proudly holds up a thick university textbook, while Diego jokingly asks Maya if she is surviving the freezing Danish winter.
现在我的屏幕上出现了两张新面孔。Now there are two new faces on my screen.
是我的妹妹索菲亚和我的哥哥迭戈。It is my little sister, Sofía, and my big brother, Diego.
他们笑着互相推搡了一下。They laugh and push each other a little.
“妹妹、哥哥，你们好！”我开心地说。"Hello little sister and big brother!" I say happily.
“嗨，玛雅，”迭戈说。"Hi Maya," says Diego.
我哥哥脸上挂着大大的笑容。My brother has a big smile.
“丹麦冷吗？”他问。"Is it cold in Denmark?" he asks.
“冬天很冷吗？”"Is it a cold winter?"
“是的，这是一个非常寒冷的冬天，”我笑着说。"Yes, it is a very cold winter," I laugh.
“但我有一个温暖的房间，还有一杯茶。”"But I have a warm room and a cup of tea."
这时，索菲亚在屏幕前举起了一个大东西。Now Sofía lifts a big thing up in front of the screen.
那是一本很大的书。It is a large book.
“你拿的是一本新书吗，索菲亚？”我问。"Do you have a new book there, Sofía?" I ask.
“是的，这是我的新书，”妹妹自豪地说。"Yes, it is my new book," my sister says proudly.
“这是我大学里的书。”"It is a book from my university."
“这是一本非常厚的书！”"It is a very thick book!"
我对着哥哥和妹妹微笑。I smile at my brother and my sister.
我想念我在墨西哥的家人。I miss my family in Mexico.
但他们现在就在我小小的屏幕上。But they are right here on my little screen.
temporary room tour
Storyboard: Her mother asks for a tour of her current living space. Maya pans the camera around to show her new plant and the small bed, explaining that it is just a temporary room and pointing out the black, sunless window.
我妈妈看着屏幕。My mother looks at the screen.
“那是你的房间吗，玛雅？”她问。"Is that your room, Maya?" she asks.
“是的，这是我的房间，”我回答。"Yes, it is my room," I answer.
“但这是一个临时的房间。”"But it is a temporary room."
我举起我的电脑。I lift my computer.
我向家人展示我的小房间。I show my small room to my family.
“这里是一张床，”我说。"Here is a bed," I say.
“这里是我的新植物。”"And here is my new plant."
“这是一盆非常漂亮的植物，”我妈妈说。"It is a very nice plant," my mother says.
我转动我的电脑。I turn my computer.
“还有这里是一扇窗户，”我说。"And here is a window," I say.
我妹妹索菲亚看着我的窗户。My sister Sofía looks at my window.
“但是很暗，”她说。"But it is very dark," she says.
“是的，外面一片漆黑，”我回答。"Yes, it is completely black out there," I answer.
“但夏天会有很多阳光。”"But in summer there is plenty of sun."
现在是晚上，天气很冷。It is evening, and it is cold.
“但我有我的台灯，”我笑着说。"But I have my lamp," I smile.
“所以这是个好房间。”"So it is a good room."
mexico garden
Storyboard: Feeling a bit homesick, Maya asks her father to take the phone outside. He walks out to their sunny garden, showing a large, familiar leafy tree. Maya smiles deeply, feeling warm and comforted by the glimpse of home before they all wave goodbye.
我看着我的电脑。I look at my computer.
我有点想念我在墨西哥的家。I miss my home in Mexico a little bit.
“爸爸，你的花园怎么样了？”我说。"Dad, how is your garden?" I say.
我爸爸对着我微笑。My dad smiles at me.
他拿着手机走到了外面。He goes outside with the phone.
那里有一个很大的花园。There is a large garden.
那里阳光非常灿烂。The sun is shining a lot there.
“看，玛雅，”我爸爸说。"Look, Maya," my dad says.
“这是花园和一棵树。”"Here is the garden and a tree."
这是一棵非常大的树。It is a very big tree.
这是一棵很漂亮的树。It is a beautiful tree.
我对着屏幕微笑。I smile at my screen.
我感觉心里暖暖的，很舒服。I feel good and warm inside.
我妈妈、我姐姐和我弟弟也来到了外面。My mother, my sister, and my brother come outside too.
我的狗在树下跑。My dog runs under the tree.
我的全家人都在向我挥手。My whole family waves at me.
“大家再见！”我说。"Goodbye, everyone!" I say.
“再见，玛雅，”他们说。"Goodbye, Maya," they say.
我合上了我的电脑。I close my computer.
