Week 13: Maya goes to a café

Norwegian with English gloss, line-aligned.

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.
Det er vinter i København.It is winter in Copenhagen.
Jeg går nedover gaten.I am walking down the street.
Vinden er veldig kald.The wind is very cold.
Det blåser hardt i dag.It is blowing hard today.
Jeg lener meg mot den kalde vinden.I lean against the cold wind.
Jeg trekker opp skjerfet mitt.I pull my scarf up.
Jeg fryser i ansiktet.My face is freezing.
Jeg ser meg omkring.I look around.
Jeg vil gjerne inn i varmen.I would like to get into the warmth.
Jeg ser en liten kafé på hjørnet.I see a small café on the corner.
Vinduene er duggete.The windows are fogged up.
Det ser varmt ut der inne.It looks warm in there.
Jeg går bort til kafeen.I walk over to the café.
Jeg åpner den tunge døren.I open the heavy door.
Jeg går inn.I step inside.
Her lukter det kaffe.It smells like coffee here.
Luften er deilig varm.The air is nice and warm.
Jeg lukker døren bak meg.I close the door behind me.
Den kalde vinden blir utenfor.The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
Jeg er inne i kafeen nå.I am inside the café now.
Det er veldig varmt her.It is very warm here.
Jeg tar av meg den tykke vinterjakken min.I take off my thick winter coat.
Den er tung.It is heavy.
Jeg finner en knagg på veggen.I find a hook on the wall.
Jeg henger jakken min på knaggen.I hang my coat on the hook.
Jeg tar også av meg skjerfet mitt og hanskene mine.I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
Kafeen er liten og koselig.The café is small and cozy.
Jeg ser etter en god plass.I look for a good seat.
Det er et lite bord ved vinduet.There is a small table by the window.
Bordet står ved siden av en radiator.The table is next to a radiator.
Jeg går over til bordet.I walk over to the table.
Jeg setter meg på stolen.I sit down on the chair.
Hendene mine er fortsatt kalde.My hands are still cold.
Jeg gnir dem mot hverandre.I rub them together.
Hendene mine blir varmere.My hands get warmer.
Det føles fantastisk.It feels fantastic.
Nå er jeg nesten varm.Now I am almost warm.
Jeg er sulten og tørst.I am hungry and thirsty.
Jeg vil gjerne bestille noe nå.I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
Jeg går over til glassdisken.I walk over to the glass counter.
Den er full av kaker og brød.It is full of cakes and bread.
Jeg ser på de mange tingene.I look at the many things.
De ser veldig deilige ut.They look very delicious.
Det er små skilt ved siden av kakene.There are small signs next to the cakes.
Skiltene er håndskrevne.The signs are handwritten.
Jeg leser navnene på skiltene.I read the names on the signs.
Det er mange slags bakverk.There are many kinds of pastry.
Det er vanskelig å velge.It is hard to choose.
Det er en stor sjokoladekake.There is a large chocolate cake.
Men jeg ser på et annet stykke bakverk.But I look at another piece of pastry.
Det er en stor kanelbolle.It is a large cinnamon roll.
Den har mye sukker på toppen.It has a lot of sugar on top.
Jeg elsker smaken av kanel.I love the taste of cinnamon.
Det er perfekt til en kald vinterdag.It is perfect for a cold winter day.
Jeg vil veldig gjerne ha kanelbollen.I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
Nå er jeg klar til å bestille.Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
Jeg går bort til kassen.I walk over to the register.
En ung mann står bak disken.A young man stands behind the counter.
Han er barista i kafeen.He is a barista in the café.
Jeg smiler til ham.I smile at him.
Han nikker vennlig.He nods warmly.
"Hei," sier han."Hi," he says.
"Hva vil du gjerne ha?" spør han."What would you like?" he asks.
"Hei," svarer jeg."Hi," I answer.
"Jeg vil gjerne ha en svart kaffe, takk.""I would like a black coffee, please."
"Og kan jeg også be om en kanelbolle?""And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
"Selvfølgelig," sier han."Of course," he says.
"En svart kaffe og en kanelbolle.""A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
"Vil du sitte her?" spør han."Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
"Ja takk, jeg setter meg der borte," sier jeg."Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
"Helt i orden," sier baristaen."Alright," the barista says.
"Jeg lager kaffen din nå.""I will make your coffee now."
"Tusen takk," sier jeg."Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
Jeg venter ved disken.I wait at the counter.
Han lager kaffen min.He makes my coffee.
Han setter en varm kopp kaffe på et trebrett.He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
Han setter også en liten tallerken på brettet.He also puts a small plate on the tray.
Kanelbollen min ligger på tallerkenen.My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
Den dufter deilig.It smells lovely.
"Vær så god, bestillingen din," sier han."Here you go, your order," he says.
"Mange takk," svarer jeg."Thank you very much," I reply.
"Takk for den gode servicen.""Thank you for the good service."
"Bare hyggelig," sier han vennlig."You are welcome," he says kindly.
Jeg tar brettet med begge hender.I take the tray with both hands.
Jeg går veldig forsiktig over gulvet.I walk very carefully across the floor.
Jeg går tilbake til bordet mitt ved vinduet.I walk back to my table by the window.
Jeg setter brettet ned på bordet.I put the tray down on the table.
Nå vil jeg gjerne sette meg ned.Now I would like to sit down.
Jeg er klar for en god pause.I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
Jeg sitter nå ved det lille bordet mitt.I now sit at my small table.
Det er deilig og varmt her.It is lovely and warm here.
Jeg ser på kanelbollen min.I look at my cinnamon roll.
Den lukter søtt og godt.It smells sweet and good.
Jeg tar en liten bit.I take a small bite.
Baksten er helt fersk og varm.The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
Den smaker veldig godt.It tastes really good.
Jeg drikker litt av den svarte kaffen min.I drink a little of my black coffee.
Endelig er fingrene mine varme igjen.Finally, my fingers are warm again.
Jeg slapper av i den myke stolen.I relax in the soft chair.
Kafeen er nesten full av mennesker.The café is almost full of people.
Folk sitter i små grupper.People sit in small groups.
De snakker og ler sammen.They talk and laugh together.
Jeg lytter til de lave stemmene i rommet.I listen to the low voices in the room.
Lyden er veldig koselig.The sound is very cozy.
Jeg ser ut av det store vinduet.I look out of the large window.
Været utenfor er grått og vindfullt.The weather outside is gray and windy.
Nå snør det faktisk igjen.Now it is actually snowing again.
Hvite snøfnugg faller sakte ned på gaten.White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
Jeg er bare glad for å være inne.I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
Jeg spiser det siste stykket av kanelbollen min.I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
Den smaker veldig godt.It tastes really good.
Men den er også veldig søt.But it is also very sweet.
Kaffekoppen min er helt tom.My coffee cup is completely empty.
Jeg er plutselig veldig tørst.I am suddenly very thirsty.
Jeg vil gjerne ha litt kaldt vann.I would like to have some cold water.
En ung mann går forbi bordet mitt.A young man walks past my table.
Han jobber her på kafeen.He works here in the café.
Han samler inn tomme kopper og tallerkener.He collects empty cups and plates.
Jeg fanger blikket hans.I catch his eye.
Han stopper ved bordet mitt og smiler.He stops at my table and smiles.
"Unnskyld," sier jeg høflig."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Ja?" svarer den unge mannen."Yes?" answers the young man.
"Kan jeg be om et glass kranvann?" spør jeg."May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
"Ja, selvfølgelig," sier han."Yes, of course," he says.
"Tusen takk," sier jeg."Thank you very much," I say.
Han tar den tomme tallerkenen og koppen min.He takes my empty plate and cup.
Han går tilbake til disken.He walks back to the counter.
Litt etter kommer han med et kaldt glass vann.A little later he comes with a cold glass of water.
asking for the bill
Storyboard: Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab.
Jeg drikker vannet mitt.I drink my water.
Vannet er kaldt og deilig.The water is cold and nice.
Jeg tar vesken min fra gulvet.I take my bag from the floor.
Jeg er ferdig med pausen min.I am finished with my break.
Jeg går opp til disken.I walk up to the counter.
Den vennlige baristaen står der ennå.The friendly barista is still standing there.
Han ser på meg med et smil.He looks at me with a smile.
"Vil du ha noe annet?" spør han."Would you like anything else?" he asks.
"Nei takk," svarer jeg."No thank you," I answer.
"Jeg vil gjerne betale.""I would like to pay."
"Ja, selvfølgelig," sier han."Yes, of course," he says.

paying and leaving
