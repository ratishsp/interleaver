Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
Je január a je veľmi chladno.It is January, and it is very cold.
Kráčam domov z autobusovej zastávky v Kodani.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Na ulici vidím kvetinárstvo.I see a flower shop on the street.
Vnútri je svetlo a teplo.It is bright and warm inside.
Vchádzam do teplého obchodu.I go into the warm shop.
Pozerám sa na malú zelenú rastlinu.I look at a small, green plant.
Predavač sa na mňa usmieva.A clerk smiles at me.
"Dobrý deň," hovorí."Hello," he says.
"Dobrý deň," hovorím."Hello," I say.
"Hľadám nejakú rastlinu.""I am looking for a plant."
"Je to darček?" pýta sa."Is it a gift?" he asks.
"Nie, je to do mojej izby," odpovedám."No, it is for my room," I answer.
"Je to naozaj dobrá rastlina," hovorí."It is a really good plant," he says.
"Koľko stojí?" pýtam sa."How much does it cost?" I ask.
"Stojí päťdesiatpäť korún," odpovedá."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
Platím za svoju novú rastlinu.I pay for my new plant.
Vkladá rastlinu do papierovej tašky.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
"Nech sa páči, tu je vaša taška," hovorí."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
"Veľmi pekne ďakujem," hovorím s radosťou."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Vychádzam do chladného večera.I go out into the cold evening.
Moja malá rastlina ma veľmi teší.My little plant makes me very happy.
evening prep
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her temporary room and sets the new plant on a small desk. With it already being pitch black outside, she turns on a warm lamp to reflect against the dark window, makes a cup of tea, and flips open her laptop to dial home.
Teraz som doma.I am home now.
Tu je moja izba.Here is my room.
Je to malá izba.It is a small room.
Mám novú rastlinu.I have a new plant.
Stojí na mojom písacom stole.It stands on my desk.
Je večer a je január.It is evening, and it is January.
Moje okno je veľmi tmavé.My window is very dark.
Vonku je úplná tma.It is completely black outside.
Mám lampu a šálku čaju.I have a lamp and a cup of tea.
Zapínam svoju lampu.I turn on my lamp.
Teraz je moja izba teplá a svetlá.Now my room is warm and bright.
Môj čaj je tiež teplý.My tea is also warm.
Sedím za svojím písacím stolom.I sit at my desk.
Mám tu svoj počítač.I have my computer here.
Otváram svoj počítač.I open my computer.
Teraz volám svojej rodine do Mexika.Now I call my family in Mexico.
Teším sa, že ich uvidím.I look forward to seeing them.
parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
Párkrát to zazvoní.It rings a couple of times.
Obrazovka sa rozsvieti.The screen lights up.
Vidím obývačku.I see a living room.
Svieti slnko a obývačka je veľmi svetlá.There is sun, and the living room is very bright.
Vidím dvoch ľudí.I see two people.
Je to moja mama a môj otec.It is my mother and my father.
Veľmi sa usmievajú a mávajú mi.They smile a lot and wave to me.
„Ahoj, mami a oci!“ hovorím nahlas."Hello mom and dad!" I say loudly.
„Ahoj, Maya,“ hovorí môj otec."Hello, Maya," my father says.
„Ako sa máš?“"How is it going?"
„Mám sa dobre,“ odpovedám radostne."It is going well," I answer happily.
Zrazu začujem zvuk.Suddenly I hear a sound.
Je to malé zaštekanie.It is a little bark.
„Ach, počujem psa!“ hovorím a smejem sa."Oh, I hear a dog!" I say and laugh.
Malý pes vyskočí na pohovku.A little dog jumps up onto a sofa.
Pozrie sa do kamery.It looks into the camera.
„Tam je môj pes!“"There is my dog!"
„Ahoj, Lola,“ hovorím."Hello, Lola," I say.
Lola vrtí chvostom.Lola wags her tail.
„Áno, je to tvoj pes,“ hovorí moja mama s úsmevom."Yes, it is your dog," my mother says with a smile.
Milujem svoju rodinu.I love my family.
Je krásne vidieť ich všetkých.It is lovely to see them all.
the siblings
Storyboard: Her younger sister Sofía and older brother Diego squeeze into the frame, playfully shoving each other. Sofía proudly holds up a thick university textbook, while Diego jokingly asks Maya if she is surviving the freezing Danish winter.
Teraz sú na mojej obrazovke dve nové tváre.Now there are two new faces on my screen.
Je to moja mladšia sestra Sofía a môj starší brat Diego.It is my little sister, Sofía, and my big brother, Diego.
Smejú sa a trochu do seba strkajú.They laugh and push each other a little.
"Ahojte, sestrička a braček!" hovorím radostne."Hello little sister and big brother!" I say happily.
"Ahoj, Maya," hovorí Diego."Hi Maya," says Diego.
Môj brat má široký úsmev.My brother has a big smile.
"Je v Dánsku zima?" pýta sa."Is it cold in Denmark?" he asks.
"Je to chladná zima?""Is it a cold winter?"
"Áno, je to veľmi chladná zima," smejem sa."Yes, it is a very cold winter," I laugh.
"Ale mám teplú izbu a šálku čaju.""But I have a warm room and a cup of tea."
Teraz Sofía dvíha pred obrazovku veľkú vec.Now Sofía lifts a big thing up in front of the screen.
Je to veľká kniha.It is a large book.
"Máš tam novú knihu, Sofía?" pýtam sa."Do you have a new book there, Sofía?" I ask.
"Áno, je to moja nová kniha," hovorí moja sestra hrdo."Yes, it is my new book," my sister says proudly.
"Je to kniha z mojej univerzity.""It is a book from my university."
"Je to veľmi hrubá kniha!""It is a very thick book!"
Usmievam sa na svojho brata a svoju sestru.I smile at my brother and my sister.
Chýba mi moja rodina v Mexiku.I miss my family in Mexico.
Ale sú priamo tu na mojej malej obrazovke.But they are right here on my little screen.
temporary room tour
Storyboard: Her mother asks for a tour of her current living space. Maya pans the camera around to show her new plant and the small bed, explaining that it is just a temporary room and pointing out the black, sunless window.
Moja mama sa pozerá na obrazovku.My mother looks at the screen.
„Je to tvoja izba, Maya?“ pýta sa."Is that your room, Maya?" she asks.
„Áno, to je moja izba,“ odpovedám."Yes, it is my room," I answer.
„Ale je to dočasná izba.“"But it is a temporary room."
Zdvihnem svoj počítač.I lift my computer.
Ukazujem svoju malú izbu rodine.I show my small room to my family.
„Tu je posteľ,“ hovorím."Here is a bed," I say.
„A tu je moja nová rastlina.“"And here is my new plant."
„Je to veľmi pekná rastlina,“ hovorí moja mama."It is a very nice plant," my mother says.
Otočím svoj počítač.I turn my computer.
„A tu je okno,“ hovorím."And here is a window," I say.
Moja sestra Sofía sa pozerá na moje okno.My sister Sofía looks at my window.
„Ale je tam veľká tma,“ hovorí."But it is very dark," she says.
„Áno, vonku je úplne čierno,“ odpovedám."Yes, it is completely black out there," I answer.
„Ale v lete je tu veľa slnka.“"But in summer there is plenty of sun."
Je večer a je chladno.It is evening, and it is cold.
„Ale mám svoju lampu,“ usmejem sa."But I have my lamp," I smile.
„Takže je to dobrá izba.“"So it is a good room."
mexico garden
Storyboard: Feeling a bit homesick, Maya asks her father to take the phone outside. He walks out to their sunny garden, showing a large, familiar leafy tree. Maya smiles deeply, feeling warm and comforted by the glimpse of home before they all wave goodbye.
Pozerám sa na svoj počítač.I look at my computer.
Trochu mi chýba môj domov v Mexiku.I miss my home in Mexico a little bit.

Môj otec sa na mňa usmieva.My dad smiles at me.

Je to krásny strom.It is a beautiful tree.
