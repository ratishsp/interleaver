Week 18: Maya gets a spring allergy

waking up unwell

我看了一眼手机，想知道现在几点了。I look at my phone to see what time it is.
现在是七点半，平时这个时候我都会去坐公交车。It is half past seven, and I normally take the bus at this time.
但我今天一点力气都没有。But I have no energy at all today.
我不只是觉得累，而是真的病了。I do not just feel tired, but really sick.

calling the clinic

她查看日历，想找个预约时间。She looks in the calendar to find an appointment.

scanning health card
Storyboard: Maya walks slowly to the clinic. In the reception area, she scans the yellow health card she received when she moved to Denmark to check in for her appointment.
现在刚过一点。It is a little past one o'clock.
我慢慢走向诊所。I walk slowly over to the medical clinic.
虽然路程很短，但我还是觉得浑身酸痛，非常疲惫。It is a short trip, but I am still very sore and tired.
我拿出我的黄色医疗卡。I take out my yellow health card.
外面阳光明媚。The sun is shining outside.
但我应该戴上墨镜的，因为光线刺得我眼睛疼。But I ought to wear sunglasses, because the light hurts my eyes.

waiting room boy

我在候诊室的一把椅子上坐下。I sit down on a chair in the waiting room.
房间里几乎完全安静。The room is almost completely quiet.
我耐心地等待着。I wait patiently.
我的头依然很沉。My head is still very heavy.
一位母亲和一个小男孩坐在我对面。A mother and a little boy sit opposite me.
男孩大概五岁。The boy is maybe five years old.
他一只手捂着右耳。He holds a hand on his right ear.
“哎哟，我的耳朵好痛！”他大声说道。"Ouch, my ear hurts!" he says loudly.

“我知道，宝贝，”她温柔地说。"I know, sweetie," she says gently.
“但是非常痛啊！”男孩抱怨道。"But it hurts a lot!" the boy complains.
“医生应该很快就会叫我们了，”妈妈说。"The doctor ought to call us soon," the mother says.
她用一只手臂搂住他的肩膀。She puts an arm around his shoulders.
男孩大声叹了口气。The boy sighs loudly.
他看起来非常疲惫。He looks very tired.

我自己的嗓子也非常痛。My own throat really hurts too.

explaining the pain

一位和蔼的医生打开门，叫了我的名字。A friendly doctor opens the door and calls my name.
我走进他明亮的办公室。I go into his bright office.
我们在他的办公桌旁坐下。We sit down at his desk.
“你今天感觉怎么样？”他问。"How are you feeling today?" he asks.
“我感觉很糟糕，”我回答。"I feel really bad," I answer.
医生看着我。The doctor looks at me.
“哪里痛？”他亲切地问。"Where does it hurt?" he asks kindly.
我指了指我的喉咙。I point to my throat.
“我的喉咙很痛，”我解释说。"My throat hurts a lot," I explain.
“非常干。”"It is very dry."
医生点点头，专注地听着。The doctor nods and listens attentively.
“我的头也很沉，”我继续说道。"My head is also very heavy," I continue.
我摸了摸我的脸。I touch my face.
“而且我的眼睛全红了。”"And my eyes are completely red."
它们一直很痒。They itch all the time.
“你绝对应该休息，”他说。"You absolutely ought to rest," he says.
他打开了一个小手电筒。He turns on a small flashlight.
“让我仔细看看你，”他说。"Let me take a closer look at you," he says.
spring allergy diagnosis
Storyboard: The doctor listens to her breathing and looks at her throat. He explains it is a common spring allergy she has never experienced before, and says she ought to buy some mild medicine.
医生让我张开嘴。The doctor asks me to open my mouth.
“说‘啊’，”他微笑着说。"Say ah," he says with a smile.
他仔细看了看我的喉咙。He looks carefully at my throat.
之后，他听了听我的呼吸。Afterward, he listens to my breathing.
他问我还有没有其他地方不舒服。He asks if I feel unwell anywhere else.
我告诉他我的头也疼。I tell him that my head also hurts.
“这不是感冒，”他说。"It is not a cold," he says.
“你这是常见的春季过敏，”他解释道。"You have a common spring allergy," he explains.
我有点惊讶。I am a little surprised.
我以前从来没有过敏过。I have never had allergies before.
他告诉我这完全正常。He tells me that it is completely normal.
这里的树木和花朵对我的身体来说是全新的。The trees and flowers here are new to my body.
“你应该去药店买点温和的药，”医生说。"You ought to buy some mild medicine at the pharmacy," the doctor says.
他保证这很快就会见效。He promises that it will help quickly.
“这样你就会好起来了，”他补充道。"Then you will feel well again," he adds.
我松了一大口气。I am very relieved.
我的喉咙很痛，但幸运的是并不严重。My throat hurts, but fortunately it is not serious.
pharmacy visit
