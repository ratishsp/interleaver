Week 7: Maya plans a busy week

Lithuanian with English gloss, line-aligned.

planning the week
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya makes a cup of tea and opens her calendar at the kitchen table — she always plans the week here. She works through it: the bike goes to the repair shop, the salon needs a phone call, the gym is on Wednesday. She writes each one in, and February fills up.
Pirmadienio rytas.It is Monday morning.
Pasidarau puodelį karštos arbatos.I make a cup of hot tea.
Sėdžiu prie stalo virtuvėje.I sit at the table in the kitchen.
Atsidarau savo kalendorių.I open my calendar.
Čia visada planuoju savo savaitę.I always plan my week here.
Dabar vasaris.It is February now.
Mėnuo jau labai užimtas.The month is already very full.
Rytoj antradienis.Tomorrow is Tuesday.
Mano dviratis, deja, sugedęs.My bicycle is unfortunately broken.
Rytoj ryte einu pas dviračių meistrą.I walk to the bicycle mechanic tomorrow morning.
Jis pradeda dirbti aštuntą valandą.He opens at eight o'clock.
Įsirašau tai į kalendorių.I write it in the calendar.
Šiandien taip pat skambinu kirpėjai.I also call the hairdresser today.
Mano plaukai gerokai per ilgi.My hair is way too long.
Trečiadienį einu į sporto klubą.Wednesday I go to the gym.
Dažnai treniruojuosi trečiadieniais.I often train on Wednesdays.
Paprastai tai būna septintą valandą vakaro.It is usually at seven o'clock in the evening.
Vėl pažiūriu į savo planą.I look at my plan again.
Geriu savo arbatą.I drink my tea.
Dabar esu pasiruošusi naujai savaitei.Now I am ready for a new week.
dropping off the bike
Storyboard: On Tuesday morning at a quarter past eight, Maya wheels her bicycle to the local repair shop. She hands the bike to the mechanic, leaves the shop, and walks the rest of the way to the office.
Antradienio rytas.It is Tuesday morning.

Aš veduosi savo dviratį.I wheel my bicycle.
Jis sugedęs.It is broken.

Jis dažnai dirba anksti.He often works early.
Dirbtuvės pilnos dviračių.The workshop is full of bicycles.

„Labas rytas,“ sako jis."Good morning," he says.
„Labas rytas,“ sakau aš."Good morning," I say.
„Mano dviratis sugedęs.“"My bicycle is broken."
Jis paima mano dviratį.He takes my bicycle.
„Sutaisysiu jį šiandien,“ sako jis."I am repairing it today," he says.
„Labai ačiū,“ sakau aš."Thank you very much," I say.
Išeinu iš dirbtuvių.I walk out of the workshop.
Dabar einu į autobusų stotelę.Now I walk to the bus stop.
Paprastai į darbą važiuoju autobusu.I normally take the bus to work.
Dviračiu dažniausiai važinėju savaitgaliais.I mostly cycle at the weekend.
Netrukus atvažiuoja mano autobusas.Soon my bus comes.
Tai gražus vasario rytas.It is a beautiful morning in February.
Darbe esu be penkiolikos devynios.I am at work at a quarter to nine.
booking a haircut
Storyboard: During her lunch break, Maya calls a hair salon on her mobile phone. She speaks to the hairdresser and books an appointment for Thursday at half past four.
Tai mano pietų pertrauka.It is my lunch break.
Sėdiu valgykloje savo darbe.I am sitting in the canteen at my work.
Vakar buvau užsiėmusi.I was busy yesterday.
Todėl dabar skambinu kirpėjai.Therefore I am calling the hairdresser now.
Paimu savo mobilųjį telefoną.I take my mobile phone.
Skambinu į kirpyklą mieste.I call a hair salon in the city.
Telefonu atsiliepia moteris.A woman answers the phone.
„Laba diena, čia kirpykla“, – sako moteris."Hello, this is the hair salon," says the woman.
„Laba diena, mano vardas Maya“, – sakau aš."Hello, my name is Maya," I say.
„Norėčiau užsiregistruoti ketvirtadieniui.“"I would like to book an appointment on Thursday."
„Vieną akimirką“, – sako kirpėja."One moment," says the hairdresser.
„Ar turite laisvą laiką po pietų?“ – klausiu aš."Do you have an appointment in the afternoon?" I ask.
„Taip, turime laisvą laiką pusę penkių“, – sako ji."Yes, we have an available appointment at half past four," she says.
„Man tai labai tinka“, – sakau aš."That suits me very well," I say.
„Koks jūsų telefono numeris?“ – klausia kirpėja."What is your phone number?" asks the hairdresser.
„Mano numeris – dvylika, trisdešimt keturi, penkiasdešimt šeši, septyniasdešimt aštuoni“, – sakau aš."My number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
„Labai ačiū, pasimatysime ketvirtadienį, pusę penkių“, – sako ji."Thank you very much, see you on Thursday at half past four," she says.
„Iki pasimatymo“, – atsakau aš."See you," I reply.
Padedu telefoną ant stalo.I put the phone on the table.
Dabar pagaliau valgau savo maistą.Now I finally eat my food.
evening gym

Aš persirengiu.I change clothes.
Pirmiausia kilnoju svarmenis.First, I lift weights.

Po to bėgu ant bėgimo takelio.Afterwards, I run on the treadmill.
Aš dažnai bėgu greitai.I often run fast.
Aš visada klausausi muzikos.I always listen to music.
Iš viso sportuoju vieną valandą.I train for one hour in total.
Dabar aštunta valanda.Now it is eight o'clock.
Esu pavargusi, bet laiminga.I am tired, but I am happy.

Einu namo.I walk home.
hallway chat

Įeinu į savo butą.I go into my apartment.
Nekantriai laukiu mūsų susitikimo prie kavos.I am looking forward to our coffee date.
scheduling conflict
