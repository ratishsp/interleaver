# Week 7 — Storyboard (Maya plans her weekly schedule)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Clock time **മണി** (എത്ര മണിക്ക്?); weekdays & months; frequency adverbs **എപ്പോഴും/ചിലപ്പോൾ/എല്ലാ ദിവസവും**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_sunday_planning | On a quiet Sunday morning in February, Maya looks at her calendar and plans her schedule. She goes to the office every day from Monday to Friday, so she carefully organizes her personal tasks for the evenings. |
| 2 | 02_evening_fitness | Maya has a regular evening fitness routine. Sometimes she runs outside in the pleasant weather, but on Tuesdays and Thursdays she always goes to the local gym at exactly half past six. |
| 3 | 03_booking_a_haircut | Maya needs a haircut, so she calls a salon in Panampilly Nagar. She speaks to the receptionist on the phone and books an appointment for Wednesday evening at five-thirty. |
| 4 | 04_dropping_the_bicycle | On Monday evening after work, Maya walks her bicycle to a local roadside repair shop. She leaves it for a general service and tells the mechanic that she is coming back for it on Wednesday at seven o'clock. |
| 5 | 05_anithas_invitation | On Tuesday, Maya meets her neighbour Anitha in the apartment hallway. Anitha invites Maya to coffee at a cafe the following evening at half past five, to celebrate that Maya has now been in Kochi for one month. Maya checks her phone calendar and sees the hour clashes with her salon appointment; she tells Anitha she will simply move the haircut, and the coffee plan is settled on the spot. |
| 6 | 06_rescheduling_haircut | Maya calls the salon again and changes her haircut to Friday evening at a quarter past six, safely avoiding her gym days. |
| 7 | 07_wednesday_coffee | When Wednesday arrives, Maya and Anitha sit in a nearby Kochi cafe. They drink hot coffee together and enjoy a relaxing midweek break. |
| 8 | 08_collecting_the_bicycle | Later that same evening, Maya walks to the cycle repair shop to collect her bicycle. The mechanic finishes the work, and she happily rides it back to her apartment building. |
