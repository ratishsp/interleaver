Week 18: Maya gets a spring allergy

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

waking up unwell
Storyboard: On Thursday morning, Maya wakes up and feels terrible. Her head is heavy, her eyes itch, and her throat is dry. She decides she ought to stay home from work.
Ni Alhamisi asubuhi, na saa yangu ya kengele inalia.It is Thursday morning, and my alarm rings.
Ninaamka na kujisikia vibaya sana.I wake up and feel really terrible.
Kichwa changu ni kizito, na nimechoka sana.My head is heavy, and I am very tired.
Ninafumbua macho yangu, lakini yanawasha sana.I open my eyes, but they itch a lot.
Kwa kweli inauma kidogo ninapopepesa macho.It actually hurts a little when I blink.
Ninajaribu kunywa maji kidogo kutoka kwenye glasi iliyoko mezani pangu.I try to drink some water from the glass on my table.
Lakini koo langu limekauka kabisa.But my throat is completely dry.
Inauma kooni ninapomeza.It hurts in my throat when I swallow.
Ninaangalia simu yangu ili kuona ni saa ngapi.I look at my phone to see what time it is.
Ni saa moja na nusu, na kwa kawaida mimi hupanda basi wakati huu.It is half past seven, and I normally take the bus at this time.
Lakini sina nguvu kabisa leo.But I have no energy at all today.
Sijisikii tu kuchoka, bali ninaumwa kweli.I do not just feel tired, but really sick.
Bila shaka ninapaswa kubaki nyumbani na kutokwenda kazini leo.I definitely ought to stay home from work today.
Ninamwandikia ujumbe mfupi mfanyakazi mwenzangu Peter.I write a short message to my colleague Peter.
Ninaeleza kwamba mimi ni mgonjwa na sitakuja ofisini.I explain that I am sick and will not be coming into the office.
Anajibu haraka kwamba ninapaswa tu kupumzika na kupata nafuu.He replies quickly that I should just relax and get well.
Ninapaswa kupiga simu kwenye kliniki ya matibabu watakapofungua.I ought to call the medical clinic when they open.
Lakini sasa hivi ninataka tu kufumba macho yangu na kupumzisha kichwa changu kizito.But right now I just want to close my eyes and rest my heavy head.
calling the clinic
Storyboard: She calls her local medical clinic. She explains her unfamiliar symptoms to the receptionist, who agrees she ought to see a doctor today and gives her an appointment for the early afternoon.
Ninapata nambari ya simu ya kliniki ya matibabu ya eneo langu.I find the phone number for my local medical clinic.
Nambari ni thelathini na mbili, arobaini na tano, sitini na saba, themanini na tisa.The number is thirty-two, forty-five, sixty-seven, eighty-nine.
Ninawapigia simu na kusubiri kwa dakika chache.I call them and wait a few minutes.
Mhudumu mkarimu wa mapokezi anapokea simu.A friendly receptionist answers the phone.
"Kliniki ya matibabu, unazungumza na Hanna," anasema."Medical clinic, you are speaking with Hanna," she says.
Ninaeleza kwamba nimekuwa mgonjwa sana leo.I explain that I have become really sick today.
"Kichwa changu ni kizito, na macho yangu yanawasha," ninasema."My head is heavy, and my eyes itch," I say.
Pia ninamwambia kwamba koo langu linauma.I also tell her that my throat hurts.
Anauliza kama nina homa.She asks if I have a fever.
Ninajibu kwamba sidhani hivyo, lakini nimechoka sana.I answer that I do not think so, but I am very tired.
"Unapaswa kuzungumza na daktari leo," mhudumu wa mapokezi anasema."You ought to speak with a doctor today," the receptionist says.
Anaangalia kwenye kalenda ili kutafuta miadi.She looks in the calendar to find an appointment.
"Unaweza kuja saa saba?" anauliza."Can you come at one o'clock?" she asks.
"Ndiyo, hiyo inafaa kabisa," ninajibu."Yes, that fits perfectly," I reply.
"Kumbuka kuleta kadi yako ya njano ya afya," anasema."Remember to bring your yellow health card," she says.
Ninamshukuru na kukata simu.I thank her and hang up.
Sasa ninahitaji tu kupumzika kidogo kabla ya kutembea kwenda huko.Now I just need to relax a bit before I walk over there.
scanning health card
Storyboard: Maya walks slowly to the clinic. In the reception area, she scans the yellow health card she received when she moved to Denmark to check in for her appointment.
Ni saa saba na dakika chache.It is a little past one o'clock.
Ninatembea polepole kuelekea kwenye kliniki ya matibabu.I walk slowly over to the medical clinic.
Ni safari fupi, lakini bado mwili unaniuma na nimechoka sana.It is a short trip, but I am still very sore and tired.
Ninatoa kadi yangu ya manjano ya afya.I take out my yellow health card.
Jua linawaka nje.The sun is shining outside.
Lakini ninapaswa kuvaa miwani ya jua, kwa sababu mwanga unaumiza macho yangu.But I ought to wear sunglasses, because the light hurts my eyes.
Ninaingia kwenye mapokezi ya kliniki.I enter the clinic's reception.
Mashine ndogo inaning'inia ukutani.A small machine hangs on the wall.
Unapaswa kila mara kuskani kadi yako ya manjano ya afya hapa.You always have to scan your yellow health card here.
Nilipata kadi hiyo kwa njia ya posta nilipohamia Denmark.I got the card in the mail when I moved to Denmark.
Ninaskani kadi ya manjano kwenye mashine.I scan the yellow card on the machine.
Inatoa sauti ndogo na kumulika rangi ya kijani.It makes a little sound and flashes green.
Hiyo inamaanisha kuwa daktari anajua nipo hapa.That means that the doctor knows I am here.
Ninaingia kwenye chumba chenyewe cha kusubiria.I walk into the actual waiting room.
Ninakaa kwenye kiti kitupu na kusubiri.I sit down on an empty chair and wait.
waiting room boy
Storyboard: She sits in the quiet waiting room near a little boy. The boy complains loudly that his ear hurts, while his mother gently tries to comfort him.
Ninakaa kwenye kiti katika chumba cha kusubiria.I sit down on a chair in the waiting room.
Chumba kiko kimya karibu kabisa.The room is almost completely quiet.
Ninasubiri kwa uvumilivu.I wait patiently.
Kichwa changu bado ni kizito sana.My head is still very heavy.
Mama na mvulana mdogo wamekaa mkabala na mimi.A mother and a little boy sit opposite me.
Mvulana huyo labda ana umri wa miaka mitano.The boy is maybe five years old.
Ameweka mkono kwenye sikio lake la kulia.He holds a hand on his right ear.
"Au, sikio langu linauma!" anasema kwa sauti kubwa."Ouch, my ear hurts!" he says loudly.
Mama yake anamtazama.His mother looks at him.
"Ninajua, mpenzi," anasema kwa upole."I know, sweetie," she says gently.
"Lakini inauma sana!" mvulana analalamika."But it hurts a lot!" the boy complains.
"Daktari anapaswa kutuita hivi karibuni," mama anasema."The doctor ought to call us soon," the mother says.
Anaweka mkono wake kuzunguka mabega yake.She puts an arm around his shoulders.
Mvulana anashusha pumzi nzito kwa sauti kubwa.The boy sighs loudly.
Anaonekana mchovu sana.He looks very tired.
Ninamwelewa vizuri.I understand him well.
Koo langu mwenyewe linauma sana pia.My own throat really hurts too.
Ninataka tu kujisikia vizuri tena.I just want to feel well again.
explaining the pain
Storyboard: The friendly doctor calls her in. Maya sits in his bright office and explains exactly where it hurts, pointing to her scratchy throat, her heavy head, and her red eyes.
Daktari mwenye urafiki anafungua mlango na kuita jina langu.A friendly doctor opens the door and calls my name.
Ninaingia kwenye ofisi yake angavu.I go into his bright office.
Tunaketi kwenye dawati lake.We sit down at his desk.
"Unajisikiaje leo?" anauliza."How are you feeling today?" he asks.
"Ninajisikia vibaya sana," ninajibu."I feel really bad," I answer.
Daktari ananiangalia.The doctor looks at me.
"Inauma wapi?" anauliza kwa upole."Where does it hurt?" he asks kindly.
Ninaonyesha koo langu.I point to my throat.
"Koo langu linauma sana," ninaeleza."My throat hurts a lot," I explain.
"Limekauka sana.""It is very dry."
Daktari anaitikia kwa kichwa na kusikiliza kwa makini.The doctor nods and listens attentively.
"Kichwa changu pia ni kizito sana," ninaendelea."My head is also very heavy," I continue.
Ninagusa uso wangu.I touch my face.
"Na macho yangu ni mekundu kabisa.""And my eyes are completely red."
Yanawasha muda wote.They itch all the time.
"Unapaswa kupumzika kabisa," anasema."You absolutely ought to rest," he says.
Anawasha tochi ndogo.He turns on a small flashlight.
"Hebu nikuangalie kwa karibu zaidi," anasema."Let me take a closer look at you," he says.
spring allergy diagnosis
Storyboard: The doctor listens to her breathing and looks at her throat. He explains it is a common spring allergy she has never experienced before, and says she ought to buy some mild medicine.
Daktari ananiomba nifungue mdomo wangu.The doctor asks me to open my mouth.
"Sema aah," anasema kwa tabasamu."Say ah," he says with a smile.
Anaangalia koo langu kwa makini.He looks carefully at my throat.
Baadaye, anasikiliza kupumua kwangu.Afterward, he listens to my breathing.
Anauliza kama ninajisikia vibaya sehemu nyingine yoyote.He asks if I feel unwell anywhere else.
Ninamwambia kuwa kichwa changu pia kinauma.I tell him that my head also hurts.
"Sio mafua," anasema."It is not a cold," he says.
"Una mzio wa kawaida wa majira ya kuchipuka," anaeleza."You have a common spring allergy," he explains.
Ninashangaa kidogo.I am a little surprised.
Sijawahi kuwa na mzio hapo awali.I have never had allergies before.
Ananiambia kuwa ni jambo la kawaida kabisa.He tells me that it is completely normal.
Miti na maua ya hapa ni mapya kwa mwili wangu.The trees and flowers here are new to my body.
"Unapaswa kununua dawa isiyo kali kwenye duka la dawa," daktari anasema."You ought to buy some mild medicine at the pharmacy," the doctor says.
Anaahidi kwamba itasaidia haraka.He promises that it will help quickly.
"Kisha utajisikia vizuri tena," anaongeza."Then you will feel well again," he adds.
Nimefarijika sana.I am very relieved.
Koo langu linauma, lakini kwa bahati nzuri siyo hatari.My throat hurts, but fortunately it is not serious.
pharmacy visit
Storyboard: Maya stops at the pharmacy on her way back. The pharmacist hands her a small box of allergy pills and suggests some sweet drops because her throat still hurts.
Nikiwa njiani kurudi nyumbani kutoka kwa daktari, ninaingia katika duka la dawa la mtaani.On my way home from the doctor, I go into the local pharmacy.
Ninachukua namba kutoka kwenye mashine na kusubiri kidogo.I pull a number from the machine and wait a little.
Mfamasia mkarimu anaita namba thelathini na tano.A friendly pharmacist calls number thirty-five.
Ninaenda kwenye kaunta na kuonyesha kadi yangu ya njano ya afya.I walk over to the counter and show my yellow health card.
Anapata cheti changu cha dawa kwenye kompyuta.She finds my prescription on the computer.
Mfamasia ananipa kisanduku kidogo cha vidonge vya mzio.The pharmacist gives me a small box of allergy pills.
"Unapaswa kumeza kidonge kimoja kila asubuhi," anasema."You ought to take one pill every morning," she says.
Ninamuuliza kama ana kitu kwa ajili ya koo langu.I ask her if she has something for my throat.
"Koo langu bado linauma," ninaeleza."My throat still hurts," I explain.
Anaitikia kwa kichwa na kupendekeza pipi tamu za koo.She nods and suggests some sweet throat drops.
"Zinasaidia wakati koo linauma," anasema."They help when your throat hurts," she says.
"Na pia unapaswa kunywa maji mengi leo.""And you ought to drink a lot of water today as well."
Ninamshukuru na kununua vidonge na pipi hizo.I thank her and buy the pills and the drops.
Inagharimu kroner themanini na tano kwa jumla.It costs eighty-five kroner in total.
Bado nimechoka na nina maumivu, lakini ninafurahi kuwa na dawa.I am still tired and sore, but I am glad to have the medicine.
Sasa ninataka tu kurudi nyumbani kwenye sofa langu mwenyewe.Now I just want to go home to my own sofa.
skipping badminton
Storyboard: That evening, Maya has to cancel her usual Thursday badminton game. She rests on her sofa, takes her new medicine, and drinks a large cup of hot tea with honey.
Ni Alhamisi jioni, na kwa kawaida mimi hucheza badminton.It is Thursday evening, and I usually play badminton.
Lakini bado mimi ni mgonjwa, kwa hivyo inabidi nighairi.But I am still sick, so I have to cancel.
Ninaandika ujumbe kwa mwenzangu wa badminton.I write a message to my badminton partner.
Ninaeleza kwamba mimi ni mgonjwa na kwamba kichwa changu kinauma.I explain that I am sick and that my head hurts.
Koo langu pia bado linauma sana.My throat also still hurts a lot.
Ninapaswa kupumzika tu kwenye sofa usiku wa leo.I ought to just relax on the sofa tonight.
Ninajifunika kwa blanketi la joto.I pull a warm blanket over myself.
Ninakunywa dawa yangu mpya kutoka kwenye duka la dawa.I take my new medicine from the pharmacy.
Baadaye, ninatengeneza kikombe kikubwa cha chai ya moto.Afterwards, I make a large cup of hot tea.
Ninaweka kijiko kikubwa cha asali kwenye chai.I put a large spoonful of honey in the tea.
Inaleta hisia nzuri sana kwenye koo langu linalouma.It feels really good in my sore throat.
Ninakunywa chai ya moto polepole sana.I drink the hot tea very slowly.
Punde, ninapata nafuu kidogo.Soon I feel a little better.
Ninafunga macho yangu na kusikiliza muziki mtulivu.I close my eyes and listen to calm music.
Bado sijisikii vizuri kabisa.I do not feel completely well yet.
Lakini kwa bahati nzuri tayari ninajisikia nafuu kidogo.But fortunately I already feel a little better.
Ninapaswa kwenda kitandani mapema na kulala.I ought to go to bed early and sleep.
healthy saturday morning
Storyboard: By Saturday morning, the mild medicine has worked perfectly. Maya feels completely well again — clear eyes, no headache. She opens the window to the mild spring air, makes a proper breakfast, and takes a slow walk in the park, glad to have her energy back.
Ni asubuhi ya Jumamosi.It is Saturday morning.
Ninaamka mapema, na ninajisikia vizuri sana tena.I wake up early, and I feel really well again.
Nilipumzika nyumbani siku nzima ya Ijumaa.I rested at home all day Friday.
Dawa isiyo kali imefanya kazi kikamilifu.The mild medicine has worked perfectly.
Macho yangu yapo safi, na hayawashi hata kidogo.My eyes are clear, and they do not itch at all.
Sina maumivu ya kichwa, na koo langu haliumi tena.I do not have a headache, and my throat does not hurt anymore.
Ninatoka kitandani na kufungua dirisha ili kupokea hewa nyororo ya majira ya kuchipua.I get out of bed and open the window to the mild spring air.
Inapendeza sana kuweza kupumua bila matatizo.It is lovely to be able to breathe without problems.
Ninaenda jikoni kuandaa kifungua kinywa kamili.I go out into the kitchen to make a proper breakfast.
Ninakula mkate na jibini na kunywa kikombe kikubwa cha kahawa ya moto.I eat bread with cheese and drink a large cup of hot coffee.
Jua linawaka, kwa hivyo ninafikiri kwamba ninapaswa kwenda kutembea bustanini.The sun is shining, so I think that I ought to go for a walk in the park.
Ninavaa jaketi langu la majira ya kuchipua na kushuka barabarani.I put on my spring jacket and go down to the street.
Miti ni ya kijani kibichi kabisa sasa, na watu wengi wanafurahia hali ya hewa.The trees are completely green now, and many people are enjoying the weather.
Inachosha sana kujisikia vibaya wakati ambapo hatimaye ni majira ya kuchipua.It is really boring to feel unwell when it is finally spring.
Lakini leo miguu yangu inahisi imara, na nishati yangu imerudi.But today my legs feel strong, and I have my energy back.
Ninatembea polepole kuzunguka ziwa dogo lililoko bustanini.I take a slow walk around the small lake in the park.
Kwa bahati nzuri mimi ni mzima kabisa sasa, na niko tayari kufurahia wikendi.Fortunately I am completely healthy now, and I am ready to enjoy the weekend.
