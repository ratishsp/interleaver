# Week 1 — Storyboard (Maya arrives in Copenhagen)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Present of *være/hedde/komme fra*; existential *der er*; subject pronouns; *hvad/hvor* questions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_airplane_arrival | Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile. |
| 2 | 02_the_taxi | Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job. |
| 3 | 03_suitcase_struggle | Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat. |
| 4 | 04_window_and_home | Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico. |
| 5 | 05_calling_mom | Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy. |
| 6 | 06_meeting_nina | Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2. |
| 7 | 07_bedtime_routine | Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning. |
