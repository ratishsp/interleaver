Week 21: Maya buys spring clothes and makes an exchange

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

spring afternoon shopping
Storyboard: After finishing her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the café, Maya enjoys the mild spring weather. She walks down the bustling main street to look for lighter clothes for the changing season.
依家十二點，我喺咖啡店嘅工作做完喇。It is twelve o'clock, and I am done at the café.
我星期六朝早嘅義工更通常都好忙，但係好溫馨。My volunteer shift on Saturday morning is always busy, but cozy.
依家我收工喇。Now I am off work.
今日天氣好好。The weather is lovely today.
天氣變得好和暖、好舒服。It has become really mild and lovely.
陽光普照，而且比上個禮拜暖好多。The sun is shining, and it is much milder than last week.
我想獎勵下自己。I feel like treating myself a little.
我沿住嗰條大步行街行。I walk down the big pedestrian street.
今日市區好多人。There are a lot of people in the city today.
大家面帶笑容，享受緊咁好嘅天氣。People are smiling and enjoying the good weather.
我想買啲新衫。I would like to buy some new clothes.
我搵緊啲薄啲嘅春裝。I am looking for some lighter spring clothes.
可能係一件靚嘅新春裝外套同一件恤衫。Maybe a nice new spring jacket and a shirt.
我透過櫥窗望入去一間大型服裝店。I look through the window of a large clothing store.
佢哋啲衫有好多好靚嘅春天顏色。They have clothes in many beautiful spring colors.
我決定行入去。I decide to go in there.
我好期待試著下其中幾件。I am looking forward to trying some of the things on.
trying spring jacket
Storyboard: Inside a large clothing store, she finds a light green spring jacket. She decides to try it on in front of a mirror on the shop floor, and since it fits perfectly, she decides to buy it.
我行入一間好大嘅服裝店。I walk into a large clothing store.
我望吓啲新款春裝。I look at the new spring clothes.
嗰度掛住好多薄外套同恤衫。There are many light jackets and shirts hanging.
我喺舖頭入面慢慢四圍望。I look slowly around the store.
所有嘢睇落都好清新，好有春天氣息。Everything looks fresh and spring-like.
突然間，我見到一件好靚嘅外套。Suddenly I see a very nice jacket.
佢係淺綠色嘅。It is light green.
我喺衣架度攞佢落嚟。I take it down from the hanger.
我想試着吓。I would like to try it on.
地下擺咗一塊大鏡。There is a large mirror standing on the floor.
所以我唔需要試身室。So I do not need a fitting room.
我着上呢件淺綠色嘅外套。I put the light green jacket on.
我望住鏡入面嘅自己。I look at myself in the mirror.
件外套非常啱身。The jacket fits really well.
唔會太大，又唔會太細。It is not too big and not too small.
其實大小啱啱好。It actually fits perfectly.
呢隻顏色着喺我身上都好靚。The color is also pretty on me.
我對住塊鏡笑。I smile at the mirror.
我除返件外套落嚟。I take the jacket off again.
我決定買佢。I decide to buy it.
grabbing a shirt
Storyboard: On her way to the cash register, Maya spots a nice blue cotton shirt. The line for the fitting rooms is very long, so she simply picks her usual size and decides not to try it on first.
我攞住件新外套行過去收銀台。I walk over towards the cash register with my new jacket.
喺途中，我見到一件好靚嘅恤衫。On the way, I see a really nice shirt.
佢係淺藍色嘅，用柔軟嘅棉布做。It is light blue and made of soft cotton.
其實我真係需要一件新恤衫返工著。I actually need a new shirt for the office.
我喺衣架上面攞件恤衫落嚟。I take the shirt down from the hanger.
我望向試身室嗰邊。I look over at the fitting rooms.
今日舖頭入面有好多人。There are a lot of people in the store today.
試身室條隊排到好長。The line for the fitting rooms is very long.
我唔想等咁耐。I do not want to wait for a long time.
所以，我決定唔試住。Therefore, I decide that I will not try it on first.
我平時著開中碼。I usually wear size medium.
我睇吓件恤衫嘅尺碼。I check the shirt's size.
不過，我覺得佢望落去有啲細件。However, I think that it looks a little small.
但係大一個碼可能又會太大。But a bigger size is probably too big.
而細一個碼就一定會太緊。And a smaller size will definitely be too tight.
所以我都係攞返平時著開嘅碼。So I just take my normal size.
我將件恤衫同件外套一齊搭喺手臂度。I put the shirt over my arm together with the jacket.
我希望返到屋企著會啱身。I hope the shirt fits at home.
keeping the receipt
Storyboard: At the checkout, she pays for both the jacket and the shirt. The friendly clerk hands her the paper receipt, and Maya folds it into her wallet.
我攞住啲新嘢行埋去收銀處。I walk over to the register with my new things.
售貨員抬起頭同我打招呼。The clerk looks up and says hi.
「你好，」佢話。"Hi," she says.
「你好，」我答。"Hi," I reply.
我將件淺綠色外套同埋件藍色恤衫擺喺櫃檯上面。I put the light green jacket and the blue shirt on the counter.
佢嘟咗呢兩件嘢。She scans both things in.
「總共六百五十克朗，」佢話。"That will be six hundred and fifty kroner," she says.
我用卡畀錢。I pay with my card.
「你要唔要收據啊？」售貨員問。"Do you want the receipt?" the clerk asks.
「要，唔該，」我好快噉答。"Yes please," I answer quickly.
「我未試過件恤衫，」我解釋。"I have not tried the shirt on," I explain.
「希望我唔使要件大啲嘅啦。」"I hope I won't need a bigger one."
「你隨時都可以攞嚟換，」售貨員親切噉講。"You can always exchange it," the clerk says kindly.
佢印張收據出嚟遞畀我。She prints the receipt out and gives it to me.
「記住收好啊，」佢話。"Remember to save it," she says.
我接過張收據。I take the receipt.
我摺好呢張細紙仔。I fold the small piece of paper.
然後穩陣噉擺入我個銀包度。Then I put it safely down into my wallet.
「週末愉快，」我同佢講。"Have a good weekend," I say to her.
「你都係，」佢答。"You too," she replies.
shirt too small
Storyboard: Later that afternoon, back in her apartment, Maya unpacks her shopping bags. She puts on the new blue shirt, but quickly realizes it is much too small across the shoulders.
晏晝晏啲嘅時候，我返到公寓。Later in the afternoon, I am back in my apartment.
買完嘢之後，我有啲攰。I am a little tired after my shopping trip.
我坐喺床上面，打開我啲購物袋。I sit on the bed and unpack my bags.
首先，我攞出我件新嘅淺綠色春裝外套。First, I take out my new, light green spring jacket.
然後，我攞埋件藍色棉質恤衫出嚟。Then I take out the blue cotton shirt.
我好期待試着。I am looking forward to trying it on.
我除低件舊冷衫，着上件恤衫。I take off my old sweater and put the shirt on.
但我即刻就覺得有問題。But I can immediately feel a problem.
件恤衫實在太細喇。The shirt is way too small.
膊頭位好緊。It is very tight across the shoulders.
我直頭幾乎舉唔起對手。I actually can barely lift my arms.
喺鏡入面睇，件恤衫真係太緊。The shirt looks far too tight in the mirror.
好明顯我需要大啲嘅碼。I obviously need a bigger size.
呢個款比我平時着開嘅碼細好多。This model is much smaller than my normal size.
喺舖頭嗰陣冇試着到，真係有啲可惜。It is a bit of a pity that I did not try it on in the store.
不過試身室條隊實在太長喇。But the line for the fitting rooms was simply too long.
我將嗰件細恤衫除返落嚟。I take the small shirt off again.
我將佢摺好，擺返入個袋度。I fold it neatly and put it back in the bag.
好彩我仲有張單。Fortunately, I still have my receipt.
星期一放工嗰陣，我可以將件恤衫換件大啲嘅。I can exchange the shirt for a bigger one when I get off work on Monday.
returning with receipt
Storyboard: On Monday afternoon, after taking the bus home from the software company, Maya stops by the same clothing store. She walks directly to the front counter holding the unworn shirt and her receipt.
星期一下晝，我收咗工。It is Monday afternoon, and I am off from work.
我由軟件公司搭巴士走。I take the bus from the software company.
但係我冇直接返屋企。But I do not go directly home.
我喺嗰條好大嘅行人專用區落巴士。I get off the bus at the large pedestrian street.
我行去星期六去過嗰間服裝店。I walk over to the clothing store from Saturday.
我個袋度有嗰件藍色棉質恤衫。In my bag, I have the blue cotton shirt.
件衫仲係全新，未著過嘅。It is still completely new and unworn.
件衫太細件喇，所以我想試件大啲嘅。The shirt is too small, so I would like to try a bigger one.
我希望佢哋有第二個碼。I hope that they have it in another size.
我行入去嗰間光猛嘅舖頭。I walk into the bright store.
我直接行埋去收銀台。I walk directly up to the cash register.
我打開銀包，搵張收據出嚟。I open my wallet and find my receipt.
我將佢攞定喺手度。I hold it ready in my hand.
然後我喺個袋度攞件衫出嚟。Then I take the shirt out of my bag.
有個友善嘅店員企喺櫃檯後面。A friendly clerk stands behind the counter.
佢抬起頭，對住我笑。She looks up and smiles at me.
我都笑住望返佢。I smile back.
asking for exchange
Storyboard: She explains to a helpful shop assistant that the shirt is too small. She asks if she can exchange it for a bigger size, and the assistant kindly hands her the next size up to try.
我將件恤衫同張收據放喺櫃枱上面。I place the shirt and my receipt on the counter.
店員準備好幫我。The clerk is ready to help me.
「你好，我星期六買咗呢件恤衫，」我話。"Hi, I bought this shirt on Saturday," I say.
「但係好可惜，佢太細件喇。」"But unfortunately, it is too small."
「可唔可以換件大啲嘅碼？」"Can I exchange it for a larger size?"
「可以，當然可以啦，」店員答。"Yes, of course you can," the assistant answers.
「你需要咩碼？」"Which size do you need?"
「我需要大碼，唔要中碼，」我解釋。"I need a large instead of a medium," I explain.
「等一陣，」店員話。"One moment," the assistant says.
佢行過去擺藍色恤衫嗰個貨架。She walks over to the shelves with the blue shirts.
佢好快搵到啱嘅碼，然後行返過嚟。She quickly finds the right size and comes back.
「呢件係大碼，」佢笑住話。"Here is a large," she says with a smile.
「你想唔想試吓先？」"Do you want to try it on first?"
「好呀，唔該，」我好開心咁答。"Yes please," I reply happily.
「試身室就喺右邊嗰度，」佢同我講。"The fitting rooms are right over there to the right," she tells me.
「好唔該晒你幫手，」我話，然後接過件新恤衫。"Thank you very much for the help," I say and take the new shirt.
依家我可以睇吓件大啲嘅恤衫係咪好啲。Now I can see if the larger shirt is better.
fitting room comparison
Storyboard: Maya takes the shirt into the fitting room so she can try it. Looking in the mirror, she sees that the bigger size is much more comfortable and fits better than the smaller one she bought earlier.
我攞住件新恤衫行入試身室。I walk into the fitting room with the new shirt.
我想即刻試下。I want to try it right away.
間房好細，但係有塊幾好嘅鏡。The cabin is small, but there is a good mirror.
我除低我件外套同冷衫。I take off my jacket and my sweater.
然後我著起件藍色恤衫。Then I put on the blue shirt.
我照下鏡，睇下啱唔啱身。I check the fit in the mirror.
大啲嘅尺碼好好多。The bigger size is much better.
膊頭位唔會太緊。It does not fit too tightly across the shoulders.
我郁下對手臂。I move my arms a bit.
呢件恤衫比另一件舒服好多。The shirt is much more comfortable than the other one.
星期六嗰件細啲嘅恤衫真係太細喇。The smaller shirt from Saturday was simply too small.
呢件恤衫非常啱身。This shirt fits perfectly.
長度亦都啱啱好。The length is also exactly right.
顏色依然係咁靚。The color is still just as nice.
我好滿意咁點點頭。I nod with satisfaction.
我再除低件恤衫。I take the shirt off again.
之後，我著返自己嘅衫。After that, I put on my own clothes.
我帶埋件新恤衫行出試身室。I take the new shirt with me out of the fitting room.
我一定會要呢件。I definitely want to keep it.
而家我淨係要再同店員講聲。Now I just need to speak with the clerk again.
successful exchange
Storyboard: She confirms that she wants to keep the larger size. The clerk scans the old receipt to complete the exchange, and Maya leaves the store satisfied.
我攞住件藍色恤衫行出試身室。I walk out of the fitting room with the blue shirt.
大啲嗰個碼啱啱好。The bigger size fits perfectly.
售貨員仲企喺收銀處度。The clerk is still standing at the register.
「呢件係咪好啲？」佢微笑住問。"Is it better?" she asks with a smile.
「係，好咗好多，」我答。"Yes, it is much better," I answer.
「細啲嗰件恤衫太緊，」我解釋。"The smaller shirt was too tight," I explain.
「我想換呢件。」"I would like to exchange for this one."
「絕對無問題，」佢話。"That is completely fine," she says.
「可唔可以畀張收據我睇多次？」佢問。"May I see your receipt again?" she asks.
我將張細嘅紙收據畀佢。I give her the small paper receipt.
佢掃描個條碼。She scans the barcode.
「搞掂，而家換好喇，」佢話。"There, now it is exchanged," she says.
「唔使加錢。」"It does not cost extra."
「多謝你幫手，」我話。"Thank you very much for the help," I say.
「唔使客氣，祝你有美好嘅一日，」佢答。"You are welcome, and have a good day," she answers.
我將件恤衫放落我個袋度。I put the shirt down into my bag.
而家我終於有啲啱春天著嘅靚衫喇。Now I finally have some good clothes for the spring.
我行出舖頭。I walk out of the store.
行返屋企嗰陣，我非常滿意。I am very satisfied as I walk home.
