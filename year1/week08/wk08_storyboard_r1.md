# Week 8 — Storyboard (Maya explores the city)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Imperatives *gå/drej*; direction prepositions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_nina_gives_directions | On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina suggests a route to the harbour. Nina points down the road and tells Maya to go straight ahead and then turn right at the park. |
| 2 | 02_the_first_leg | Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns right exactly where the park begins. She enjoys the fresh air and looks at the beautiful architecture. |
| 3 | 03_asking_an_older_man | Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He politely tells her to walk past the local museum and then turn left. |
| 4 | 04_getting_lost | Maya walks past a large building, but she accidentally turns left too early. She suddenly stands in a quiet residential street with no shops and no people. She looks around and realizes she is completely lost. |
| 5 | 05_the_helpful_dog_walker | Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog on the pavement. Maya approaches her and asks for the correct way to the harbour. The woman smiles and tells her to go back to the square, cross the street, and go straight ahead. |
| 6 | 06_arriving_at_the_harbour | Maya carefully follows the woman's clear instructions. She walks back to the square, crosses the wide street, and finally spots the blue water. She arrives at the lively harbour and watches the boats. |
| 7 | 07_the_little_mermaid | Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico. |
| 8 | 08_walking_home | Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. She does not ask anyone for directions this time. She confidently goes straight, turns the right corners, and feels happy that she can finally picture the city map in her head. |
