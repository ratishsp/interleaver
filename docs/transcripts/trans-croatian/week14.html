Week 14: Maya takes public transport across the city

Croatian with English gloss, line-aligned.

checking the route
Storyboard: Maya looks at a travel app on her phone. She needs to go across the city to pick up a package, so she plans a route with three different types of public transport.
Danas moram pokupiti paket.I need to pick up a package today.
Na drugoj je strani grada.It is on the other side of the city.
Gledam u svoj mobitel.I look at my phone.
Otvaram aplikaciju za javni prijevoz.I open a public transport app.
Planiram svoju rutu.I plan my route.
Ruta je duga.The route is long.
Ima tri dijela.It has three parts.
Prvo moram ići autobusom.First, I need to take a bus.
Autobus vozi do metroa.The bus drives to the metro.
Zatim se moram voziti metroom.Then I need to ride the metro.
Na kraju moram ići do luke.Finally, I need to go to the harbour.
Ovdje moram ići lučkim autobusom.Here, I need to take the harbour bus.
Lučki autobus je veliki brod.The harbour bus is a large boat.
Plovi po vodi.It sails on the water.
Paket je u maloj trgovini.The package is in a small shop.
Trgovina se nalazi blizu vode.The shop is located close to the water.
To je dobar plan.It is a good plan.
Uzimam svoju plavu putnu kartu.I take my blue travel card.
Spremna sam za put.I am ready for the trip.
topping up the card
Storyboard: She walks to a nearby ticket machine. She taps her blue travel card on the screen and pays two hundred kroner to top up her balance for the long trip.
Idem do aparata za karte na ulici.I walk over to a ticket machine on the street.
Prvo mi na putnoj karti treba još novca.First my travel card needs more money.
Vadim svoju putnu kartu iz torbe.I take my travel card out of my bag.
To je mala, plava karta.It is a small, blue card.
Stavljam kartu na zaslon aparata.I place the card on the machine's screen.
Zaslon prikazuje moje stanje.The screen shows my balance.
Stanje je prenisko za dugo putovanje.The balance is too low for a long trip.
Pritišćem zaslon.I tap on the screen.
Biram iznos.I choose an amount.
Plaćam dvjesto kruna svojom platnom karticom.I pay two hundred kroner with my payment card.
Aparat ispisuje "Odobreno" na zaslonu.The machine writes "Approved" on the screen.
Sada imam novca na karti.Now I have money on the card.
Stavljam kartu natrag u torbu.I put the card back in the bag.
Spremna sam za dugo putovanje.I am ready for the long trip.
Sada moram ići autobusom.Now I need to take the bus.
Idem do autobusne stanice.I walk over to the bus stop.
taking the bus
Storyboard: Maya waits at the bus stop until a long yellow bus arrives. She steps inside, scans her card at the door, and finds an empty seat by the window.
Stojim na autobusnoj stanici.I stand at the bus stop.
Uskoro se vozim autobusom.Soon I travel by bus.
Gledam niz ulicu.I look down the street.
Nakon pet minuta, stiže autobus.After five minutes, the bus arrives.
To je dug i žut autobus.It is a long and yellow bus.
Autobus staje točno ispred mene.The bus stops right in front of me.
Vrata se otvaraju.The doors open.
Penjem se u autobus.I step up into the bus.
Imam putnu kartu u ruci.I have my travel card in my hand.
Skeniram kartu kod vrata.I scan the card by the door.
Danas ima mnogo putnika.There are many passengers today.
Tražim slobodno mjesto.I look for an empty seat.
Nalazim dobro mjesto uz prozor.I find a good seat by the window.
Sjedam.I sit down.
Gledam van na grad.I look out at the city.
Sada idem autobusom do stanice metroa.Now I take the bus to the metro station.
asking a passenger
Storyboard: She turns to an older woman sitting nearby. Maya politely asks if she travels with this bus to the metro station, and the woman nods with a friendly smile.
Sjedim u autobusu.I am sitting in the bus.
Gledam kroz prozor.I look out the window.
Nisam posve sigurna u rutu.I am not completely sure about the route.
Starija žena sjedi blizu mene.An older woman is sitting close to me.
Pogledam prema njoj.I look over at her.
"Oprostite," kažem ljubazno."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Vozi li ovaj autobus do stanice metroa?""Does this bus go to the metro station?"
Žena me pogleda.The woman looks at me.
Ona kimne s prijateljskim osmijehom.She nods with a friendly smile.
"Da, vozi do stanice metroa," odgovara ona."Yes, it goes to the metro station," she replies.
"Idete li metroom?" pita žena."Are you taking the metro?" the woman asks.
"Da, idem metroom," kažem."Yes, I am taking the metro," I say.
"Često se vozim ovim autobusom," kaže žena."I often ride with this bus," the woman says.
"To je jednostavna ruta," dodaje ona."It is an easy route," she adds.
"Puno hvala na pomoći," kažem."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Ovo mi je prva vožnja ovom rutom.""It is my first trip on this route."
Žena ponovno kimne.The woman nods again.
Opuštam se na sjedalu.I relax in my seat.
Vozimo se dalje kroz grad.We drive on through the city.
transferring to metro
Storyboard: The bus stops, and Maya gets off at a busy square. She walks down the wide stairs into a bright, underground metro station and taps her card again.
Autobus staje na prometnom trgu.The bus stops at a busy square.
Izlazim iz autobusa.I get off the bus.
Kažem doviđenja starijoj gospođi.I say goodbye to the older woman.
Sada idem do metroa.Now I am going to the metro.
Hodam preko trga.I walk across the square.
Ovdje ima mnogo ljudi.There are many people here.
Silazim niz široke stepenice.I walk down the wide stairs.
Stanica je pod zemljom.The station is underground.
Vrlo je velika i svijetla.It is very big and bright.
Moram se ponovno prijaviti.I have to check in again.
Vadim svoju plavu karticu.I take out my blue card.
Skeniram karticu na aparatu.I scan the card on the machine.
Aparat se oglasi glasnim bipom.The machine makes a loud beep.
Sada se mogu voziti metroom.Now I can ride with the metro.
Silazim do perona.I walk down to the platform.
Idem sljedećim vlakom.I am taking the next train.
Vozi izravno prema luci.It goes straight towards the harbour.
Vlak stiže za tri minute.The train arrives in three minutes.
riding the metro
Storyboard: Maya stands right at the front of the driverless train. She rides with the metro through the dark tunnels and watches the lights blur past as they speed forward.
Metro stiže i vrata se otvaraju.The metro arrives, and the doors open.
Ulazim u vlak.I go into the train.
Vlak vozi bez vozača.The train drives without a driver.
Idem skroz naprijed.I walk all the way to the front.
Stojim pored velikog prozora.I stand by the large window.
Vozim se metroom kroz mrak.I ride with the metro through the dark.
U dugom smo tunelu.We are in a long tunnel.
Vozimo se jako brzo.We drive very fast.
Svjetlo leti pokraj prozora.The light flies past the window.
Izgleda fantastično.It looks fantastic.
Mali dječak stoji pored mene.A little boy stands next to me.
I on gleda van u mrak.He also looks out into the dark.
Često se vozim metroom.I often take the metro.
To je brza i jednostavna vožnja.It is a fast and easy trip.
Sada se približavamo mojoj stanici.Now we are approaching my station.
Idem u luku.I am going to the harbour.
Moja sljedeća vožnja je lučkim autobusom.My next trip is with the harbour bus.
Veselim se što ću vidjeti vodu.I am looking forward to seeing the water.
finding the pier
Storyboard: She takes the escalator up to the street level and walks toward the water. She finds a small wooden pier by the edge of the canal where the harbour bus stops.
Vožnja metroom je gotova.The trip with the metro is over.
Sada idem gore na ulicu.Now I am going up to the street.
Idem pokretnim stepenicama.I take the escalator.
Sunce sja gore na ulici.The sun is shining up on the street.
Sada idem do vode.Now I am going to the water.
Idem lučkim autobusom.I am going by the harbour bus.
Hodam niz široku ulicu.I walk down a wide street.
Zatim ugledam kanal.Then I see the canal.
Voda je vrlo plava.The water is very blue.
Tražim svoje stajalište.I look for my stop.
Gdje staje brod?Where does the boat stop?
Prilazim bliže rubu.I walk closer to the edge.
Zatim ugledam mali drveni mol.Then I see a small wooden pier.
Nalazi se skroz dolje uz vodu.It is located right down by the water.
Pored mola nalazi se žuti znak.There is a yellow sign by the pier.
Na znaku piše "Lučki autobus".It says "Harbour bus" on the sign.
To je pravo mjesto.This is the right place.
Izlazim na mol.I walk out onto the pier.
Sada samo čekam brod.Now I am just waiting for the boat.
the harbour bus
Storyboard: A wide boat arrives, and Maya boards the harbour bus. She stands on the open deck and enjoys the fresh air as she sails across the blue water.
Širok brod dolazi preko vode.A wide boat comes across the water.
To je lučki autobus.It is the harbour bus.
Brod plovi do drvenog mola.The boat sails in to the wooden pier.
Zaustavlja se na rubu.It stops at the edge.
Sada idem lučkim autobusom.I am going with the harbour bus now.
Ukrcavam se na brod.I step on board the ship.
Ponovno skeniram svoju plavu putnu kartu.I scan my blue travel card again.
Unutra sjedi mnogo putnika.Many passengers sit inside.
Ali ja izlazim na otvorenu palubu.But I walk out onto the open deck.
Stojim skroz naprijed.I stand at the very front.
Plovimo brodom preko plave vode.We sail with the boat across the blue water.
Grad izgleda predivno s vode.The city looks beautiful from the water.
Sunce sja po nama.The sun shines down on us.
Duboko udahnem.I take a deep breath.
Zrak je vrlo svjež i hladan.The air is very fresh and cold.
Uživam u kratkom putovanju.I enjoy the short trip.
Uskoro smo na drugoj strani.We are soon over at the other side.
arriving for the errand
Storyboard: The boat docks at the opposite shore. Maya steps off the vessel and walks toward a small shop on the corner to finally collect her package.
Brod stiže na drugu stranu vode.The boat arrives at the other side of the water.
Lučki autobus staje na malom pristaništu.The harbour bus stops at a small pier.
Silazim s broda.I get off the boat.
Vožnja brodom je sada gotova.The trip by boat is now over.
Provjeravam kartu na svom mobitelu.I check the map on my phone.
Idem do malog kioska.I am going to a small kiosk.
Kiosk se nalazi tamo na uglu.The kiosk is located over on a corner.
Hodam uz ulicu.I walk up the street.
Vidim trgovinu na kraju ceste.I see the shop at the end of the road.
Otvaram vrata i ulazim.I open the door and walk in.
Mladić stoji iza blagajne.A young man stands behind the register.
"Bok", kaže on ljubazno."Hi," he says kindly.
"Bok, trebam preuzeti paket", kažem."Hi, I am going to pick up a package," I say.
Muškarac kima glavom i smiješi se.The man nods and smiles.
Pita za moje ime.He asks for my name.
"Moje ime je Maya", odgovaram."My name is Maya," I answer.
Pronalazi veliku kutiju na polici.He finds a large box on a shelf.
Daje mi paket.He gives the package to me.
"Puno hvala na pomoći", kažem."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
Sada ponovno krećem na dugi put kući sa svojim paketom.Now I take the long trip home again with my package.
