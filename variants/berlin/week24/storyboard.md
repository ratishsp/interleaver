# Week 24 — Storyboard (Maya looks after Sabine's dog)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Imperatives + accusative pronouns (**ihn/sie/es**); rules with **müssen** and **nicht dürfen**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_sabines_instructions | Friday evening. Only some scenes should open by naming the day or the time — most should start in the middle of an action instead, or the week reads as a robotic list. Sabine arrives at Maya's flat with her small dog, Bruno, his basket, and a bag containing his food, lead and favourite toy. Sabine is going away for the weekend. Standing in the hallway, Sabine quickly lists the rules for Bruno's care. She emphasizes what Maya must do and what Bruno is not allowed to do, such as sleeping on the human bed. Maya takes the bag and reassures her friend, using the informal 'du', that she will take great care of the dog. She smiles and says, 'I will feed him'. Sabine says a warm goodbye and leaves for her trip. |
| 2 | 02_early_routine | Saturday morning. Maya wakes up early before her weekly café volunteer shift. Knowing Bruno cannot be left alone all morning without a bathroom break, she first takes him for a quick walk around the block, keeping him strictly on the lead as Sabine instructed. Back inside, Bruno is whining for breakfast. Maya opens his dog food. As Bruno jumps up excitedly, Maya uses an imperative to calm him down: 'Sit!' ('Sitz!'). Once he sits, she places the bowl on the floor for him. |
| 3 | 03_afternoon_walk | Saturday afternoon. Maya returns from her café shift to a warm, bright late-spring afternoon. She decides it is the perfect time for a longer walk. She picks up the lead and calls out to the dog with an imperative: 'Come here, Bruno!' ('Komm her!'). Bruno trots over, and she happily clips the lead onto his collar to take him outside. |
| 4 | 04_park_distractions | They walk to a nearby park, where there are many people and other dogs about. Do not restate the weather — the previous scene has already set it — and do not restate the lead rule, which has been said twice already; here it is simply obeyed. Suddenly, Bruno spots a squirrel and tries to dart into the bushes. Maya quickly pulls the lead back and uses a sharp imperative: 'Stop! Stay here!' ('Halt! Bleib hier!'). She firmly reminds herself of Sabine's rule: Bruno must stay on the lead. |
| 5 | 05_dinner_begging | Saturday evening back in the flat. Maya is eating her own dinner at the table. Bruno sits right beside her chair, looking up with big, pathetic eyes. Maya laughs but stays firm, using an imperative: 'Go to your basket!'. She tells him that dogs are not allowed to eat human food. |
| 6 | 06_sunday_playtime | Sunday morning. Maya and Bruno are having a relaxed morning in the living room. Maya takes Bruno's favorite toy from Sabine's bag. She throws it across the room and tells Bruno to fetch it (Imperative: 'Hol es!'). Bruno happily retrieves the toy, and Maya praises him with a little dog treat. |
| 7 | 07_family_video_call | Sunday afternoon. Maya sits on the sofa with her phone for her weekly video call with her family in Mexico. She points the camera at the sleeping dog to show her parents and her sister Sofia. Sofia thinks Bruno is adorable and asks Maya if she can wake him up so they can see him play, but Maya says no, letting the tired dog rest. |
| 8 | 08_sabine_returns | Sunday evening. Sabine knocks on the door to collect her dog. Bruno wakes up, hears her voice, and runs enthusiastically to greet her ('sie' — accusative pronoun). Maya tells Sabine that they had a wonderful weekend. Sabine thanks Maya for her help, scoops up the small dog, and takes him back home. |
