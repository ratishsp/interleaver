# Week 6 — Storyboard (Maya settles into a daily routine)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Present-tense regular verbs; reflexive *vasker mig*; V2 review — the verb comes before the subject whenever a time or place phrase opens the sentence

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_routine | In the morning, Maya wakes up and gets out of bed. She steps into the bathroom, where she takes a warm shower and washes herself to wake up fully. |
| 2 | 02_getting_ready | Back in the bedroom, she chooses her clothes and gets dressed for work. In the kitchen, she pours a cup of coffee and eats a quick breakfast before leaving the flat. |
| 3 | 03_the_bus_ride | Out on the street, Maya waits at the stop for her bus. She steps on board, finds a seat by the window, and watches the city pass by on her way to work. |
| 4 | 04_at_the_desk | At the software company, Maya sits down at her desk. She turns on her computer, reads her messages, and begins to write code for her project. |
| 5 | 05_lunch_with_peter | At midday, Maya walks down to the bright canteen. She meets her friendly colleague Peter by the buffet, and they sit together to eat and chat about their work. |
| 6 | 06_finishing_work | Later in the afternoon, Maya completes her final coding task. She closes her laptop, packs her bag, and says goodbye to Peter before heading out of the building. |
| 7 | 07_cooking_dinner | At home in the kitchen, Maya prepares a simple dinner. She chops fresh vegetables, cooks the food in a pan on the stove, and enjoys a quiet meal. |
| 8 | 08_relaxing_evening | After dinner, Maya moves into the living room. She sits comfortably on the sofa, opens a good book, and relaxes completely in the warm apartment. |
| 9 | 09_bedtime | In the evening, Maya gets ready for the night. She brushes her teeth, turns off the lights, and goes to bed to sleep, ready for another day. |
