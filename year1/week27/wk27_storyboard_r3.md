# Week 27 — Storyboard (Maya handles her digital admin)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** *pleje at*; instructions; passive *-s* + *blive* + participle

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_tuesday_admin | Maya clears her small kitchen table on a quiet Tuesday evening. She realizes she needs to update her payment details on her electricity provider's website, a boring digital chore she usually puts off until the last minute. |
| 2 | 02_digital_form | Settling on the sofa, she opens the utility portal on her phone. She reads the on-screen instructions stating that all required fields must be completed, then carefully types her phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight. |
| 3 | 03_delayed_code | A system prompt states that a one-time security code will be sent via text message to verify her identity. Maya watches her blank screen, slightly annoyed because these automated codes usually arrive instantly. |
| 4 | 04_successful_refresh | Following the troubleshooting instructions, she taps the link to request a new code, and her phone finally buzzes. She inputs the digits and smiles when a green banner appears, declaring that her profile is successfully updated. |
| 5 | 05_nina_delivery | A quick knock interrupts her thoughts; she opens the door to find Nina holding a small cardboard box. Nina explains that the mail carrier accidentally delivered Maya's new coffee beans to her apartment earlier in the afternoon. |
| 6 | 06_practical_advice | Maya takes the box with a smile and mentions she just spent the last twenty minutes fighting with the utility website. Nina laughs and advises her to make sure her confirmation receipt is downloaded, noting that the provider's portal is infamous for deleting old messages. |
| 7 | 07_gym_prep | Closing the door, Maya decides she is completely finished with screens for the night and plugs her phone into its charger. She pulls her sports bag from the closet, packing her shoes and water bottle for her regular Wednesday evening gym session tomorrow. |
| 8 | 08_offline_reading | Relieved that her tedious administration is finally handled and her gear is ready, Maya turns on a warm reading lamp. She settles into her armchair with a new paperback, looking forward to a quiet, completely offline end to the day. |
