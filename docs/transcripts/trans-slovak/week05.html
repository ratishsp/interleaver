Week 5: Maya stocks her new kitchen

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

empty cupboards
Storyboard: Maya stands in the kitchen, noticing that the fridge and the cupboard are completely empty. She grabs a bag and heads out, ready to buy food.
Je šesť hodín.It is six o'clock.
Stojím v kuchyni.I am standing in the kitchen.
Som hladná.I am hungry.
Otváram chladničku.I open the fridge.
Je úplne prázdna.It is completely empty.
Pozerám sa do skrinky.I look in the cupboard.
V skrinke nie je žiadne jedlo.There is no food in the cupboard.
Nemám žiadne jedlo.I have no food.
Mám len trochu vody.I only have a little water.
Chcela by som teraz jedlo.I would like to have food now.
Mám rada zeleninu.I like vegetables.
Tiež mám rada chlieb.I also like bread.
Ale tu nič nemám.But I have nothing here.
Nájdem tašku.I find a bag.
Taška je červená.The bag is red.
Je dobrá na nákupy.It is good for shopping.
Vychádzam z bytu.I walk out of the flat.
Zatváram dvere.I close the door.
Som pripravená.I am ready.
Teraz idem do supermarketu.Now I am walking to the supermarket.
produce section
Storyboard: Maya enters a supermarket and pulls a basket from a stack by the door. Because she likes carrots, she grabs a large bunch from a green crate and drops it into the basket.
Stojím pred supermarketom.I stand in front of the supermarket.
Supermarket je veľký a svetlý.The supermarket is large and bright.
Dvere sa otvárajú automaticky.The door opens automatically.
Idem do obchodu.I go into the shop.
Obchod má veľa tovaru.The shop has many goods.
Pri vchode sú kopy košíkov.There are stacks of baskets by the entrance.
Beriem si košík.I take a basket.
Košík je červený a ľahký.The basket is red and light.
Hľadám zeleninu.I look for vegetables.
Oddelenie je hneď tu.The section is right here.
Vidím debničku na zemi.I see a crate on the floor.
Debnička je zelená.The crate is green.
V debničke je veľa mrkiev.There are many carrots in the crate.
Mám rada mrkvu.I like carrots.
Vyberám si veľký zväzok.I choose a large bunch.
Zväzok vyzerá veľmi čerstvý.The bunch looks very fresh.
Vkladám zväzok do košíka.I put the bunch down into the basket.
Teraz košík nie je prázdny.Now the basket is not empty.
Idem ďalej do obchodu.I walk further into the shop.
the parsnip
Storyboard: Looking around, Maya spots a pale, unusual root. She reads a sign and learns it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she decides to buy it anyway out of curiosity.
Vidím zvláštnu zeleninu.I see a strange vegetable.
Tá zelenina je dlhý koreň.The vegetable is a long root.
Ten koreň je veľmi bledý.The root is very pale.
Vyzerá ako biela mrkva.It looks like a white carrot.
Pozriem sa dole na ceduľku.I look down at the sign.
Na ceduľke je napísané „paštrnák“.It says "parsnip" on the sign.
Nepoznám túto zeleninu.I do not know the vegetable.
Je to dánska zelenina?Is it a Danish vegetable?
Mám rada zeleninu.I like vegetables.
Mám rada mrkvu.I like carrots.
Možno paštrnák chutí dobre.Maybe the parsnip tastes good.
Vezmem paštrnák do ruky.I take the parsnip in my hand.
Ten koreň je celkom veľký.The root is quite big.
Chcela by som ho.I would like to have it.
Pozriem sa na košík.I look at the basket.
Vložím koreň do košíka.I put the root in the basket.
Rada by som vyskúšala niečo nové.I would like to try something new.
the rye bread
Storyboard: At a shelf with baked goods, Maya looks for a local staple. She lifts a loaf of rye bread, surprised by how heavy it feels, and lays the bread carefully in the basket.
Podídem k regálu s chlebom.I walk over to a shelf with bread.
Hľadám miestnu klasiku.I am looking for a local classic.
Chcela by som dánsky ražný chlieb.I would like to have a Danish rye bread.
Pozerám sa na regál.I look at the shelf.
Nájdem ražný chlieb.I find a rye bread.
Je vo vrecku.It is in a bag.
Pozerám sa na vrecko.I look at the bag.
Zoberiem ražný chlieb.I take the rye bread.
Zodvihnem chlieb.I lift the bread.
Som prekvapená.I am surprised.
Chlieb je veľmi ťažký.The bread is very heavy.
Pripadá mi ako kameň!It feels like a stone!
Mám rada svetlý chlieb.I like light bread.
Ale ražný chlieb je tmavý a ťažký.But the rye bread is dark and heavy.
Opatrne vložím chlieb do košíka.I carefully lay the bread in the basket.
Košík je teraz tiež ťažký.The basket is also heavy now.
bakery counter
Storyboard: The bakery counter has a member of staff waiting. Maya catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry.
Idem k pultu v pekárni.I walk over to the bakery counter.
Tá vôňa je veľmi dobrá.The smell is very good.
Mám rada koláče.I like cakes.
A naozaj mám rada škoricové hniezda.And I really like cinnamon pastries.
Za pultom stojí pekár.A baker stands behind the counter.
Pekár sa na mňa pozerá.The baker looks at me.
Usmieva sa.He smiles.
„Čo si prosíte?“ pýta sa pekár."What would you like to have?" the baker asks.
Ukážem na škoricové hniezdo.I point at a cinnamon pastry.
Ten koláč vyzerá lahodne.The pastry looks delicious.
„Dala by som si jedno škoricové hniezdo, prosím,“ hovorím."I would like to have a cinnamon pastry, please," I say.
„Áno, samozrejme,“ hovorí pekár."Yes, of course," the baker says.
Vezme koláč.He takes the pastry.
Vloží koláč do vrecka.He puts the pastry in a bag.
Podá vrecko cez pult.He hands the bag over the counter.
„Nech sa páči,“ hovorí."Here you go," he says.
Vezmem si vrecko.I take the bag.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem,“ hovorím."Thank you very much," I say.
Vložím vrecko do košíka.I put the bag down into the basket.
finding the milk
Storyboard: Maya searches for the milk without luck. She stops an employee to ask for help, and he points her toward the dairy section at the back.
Teraz potrebujem mlieko.Now I need milk.
Kráčam dlhou uličkou.I walk down a long aisle.
Pozerám sa na regál.I look at the shelf.
Vidím vodu a džús.I can see the water and the juice.
Ale neviem nájsť mlieko.But I cannot find the milk.
Pozerám sa všade.I look everywhere.
Vidím muža.I see a man.
Pracuje v supermarkete.He works in the supermarket.
Zastavím zamestnanca a usmejem sa.I stop the employee and smile.
„Prepáčte,“ hovorím."Excuse me," I say.
„Chcela by som mlieko.“"I would like to have milk."
„Kde je mlieko?“"Where is the milk?"
Muž ukáže do zadnej časti obchodu.The man points to the back of the shop.
„Oddelenie mliečnych výrobkov je tam dole,“ hovorí."The dairy section is down there," he says.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem,“ odpovedám."Thank you very much," I reply.
Idem tam dole.I walk down there.
Konečne nachádzam chladničku a mlieko.I finally find the fridge and the milk.
Vezmem si kartón a položím ho do košíka.I take a carton and lay it in the basket.
taste of home
Storyboard: Exploring another section, Maya hunts for a taste of Mexico because she really likes beans. She recognizes a bright can of black beans on a low shelf and reaches all the way to the back to pull it out.
Idem ďalej k inému regálu.I walk on to another set of shelves.
Pozerám sa na jedlo.I look at the food.
Supermarket má veľa vecí z iných krajín.The supermarket has many things from other countries.
Hľadám chuť Mexika.I look for a taste of Mexico.
Chýba mi jedlo z domova.I miss the food from home.
Mám veľmi rada fazuľu.I really like beans.
Často jem čiernu fazuľu.I often eat black beans.
Pozerám sa na nízku policu.I look at a low shelf.
Polica je trochu tmavá.The shelf is a bit dark.
Ale vidím farebnú konzervu.But I see a colourful can.
Konzerva stojí úplne vzadu.The can stands all the way at the back.
Je to konzerva s čiernou fazuľou.It is a can of black beans.
Usmievam sa.I smile.
Načiahnem ruku úplne dozadu.I reach my arm all the way in.
Vytiahnem konzervu.I pull the can out.
Čítam si etiketu na konzerve.I read the label on the can.
Je perfektná na moju večeru.It is perfect for my dinner.
Dám konzervu do košíka.I put the can in the basket.
Mám dosť jedla.I have enough food.
the checkout
Storyboard: Maya brings the basket to the checkout. She places the food on the belt, holds her card over the machine, and packs the bag quickly. No price is spoken or shown.
Idem k pokladni.I walk up to the checkout.
V ruke mám košík.I have the basket in my hand.
Kladiem jedlo na pás.I put the food on the belt.
Najprv vyberiem z košíka ražný chlieb.First I take the rye bread from the basket.
Potom vezmem mlieko a paštrnák.Then I take the milk and the parsnip.
Vezmem aj mrkvu a čiernu fazuľu.I also take carrots and black beans.
Nakoniec položím na pás koláč.Finally I put the pastry on the belt.
Pokladník sedí za pokladňou.The cashier sits behind the checkout.
Usmieva sa na mňa.He smiles at me.
„Dobrý deň,“ hovorí."Hello," he says.
„Dobrý deň,“ odpovedám."Hello," I reply.
Rýchlo skenuje jedlo.He scans the food quickly.
Vytiahnem svoju kartu.I take my card out.
Podržím kartu nad prístrojom.I hold the card over the machine.
Prístroj pípne.The machine beeps.
„Chceli by ste bloček?“ pýta sa."Would you like to have the receipt?" he asks.
„Áno, prosím, chcela by som bloček,“ hovorím."Yes please, I would like to have the receipt," I say.
Otvorím si tašku.I open my bag.
Rýchlo si zbalím tašku.I pack the bag quickly.
„Pekný deň,“ hovorí."Have a good day," he says.
putting away
Storyboard: Back in the flat, Maya empties the bag on the counter. She puts the milk and the parsnip in the fridge, and she places the bread and the can in the cupboard.
Som opäť doma v byte.I am home in the flat again.
Stojím v kuchyni.I am standing in the kitchen.
Vyprázdňujem tašku na kuchynskú linku.I empty the bag on the kitchen counter.
Vybaľujem jedlo.I unpack the food.
Beriem mlieko.I take the milk.
Dávam mlieko do chladničky.I put the milk in the fridge.
Potom sa pozerám na paštrnák.Then I look at the parsnip.
Je to smiešny koreň.It is a funny root.
Použijem ho do mojej polievky.I will use it in my soup.
Dávam paštrnák do chladničky.I put the parsnip in the fridge.
Teraz chladnička nie je prázdna.Now the fridge is not empty.
Beriem chlieb.I take the bread.
Ražný chlieb je veľmi ťažký.The rye bread is very heavy.
Dávam chlieb do skrinky.I put the bread in the cupboard.
Potom beriem konzervu s fazuľou.Then I take the can of beans.
Fazuľa vydrží dlho.Beans last a long time.
Dávam konzervu do skrinky.I put the can in the cupboard.
Teraz je v skrinke jedlo.Now there is food in the cupboard.
Som celkom hladná.I am quite hungry.
Rada by som si teraz dala večeru.I would like to have dinner now.
first home cooked meal
Storyboard: Ready to eat, Maya chops some vegetables and opens the can. She cooks dinner on the stove, finally making a proper meal in the flat — and afterwards she has the pastry from the bakery counter for dessert.
Kuchyňa je malá.The kitchen is small.
Ale sporák je dobrý.But the stove is good.
Beriem paštrnák a mrkvu.I take the parsnip and a carrot.
Beriem aj nôž.I also take a knife.
Nôž je ostrý.The knife is sharp.
Krájam paštrnák a mrkvu.I cut the parsnip and the carrot.
Otváram konzervu.I open the can.
Dávam jedlo do hrnca.I put the food in a pot.
Hrniec je na sporáku.The pot is on the stove.
Teraz je jedlo teplé.Now the food is warm.
Krásne to vonia.It smells lovely.
Beriem ražný chlieb.I take the rye bread.
Odkrajujem si krajec.I cut a slice.
Tanier je na stole.The plate is on the table.
Konečne jem v byte poriadne jedlo.Finally, I am eating a proper meal in the flat.
Potom jem škoricové pečivo.Afterwards I eat the cinnamon pastry.
Chutí sladko.It tastes sweet.
