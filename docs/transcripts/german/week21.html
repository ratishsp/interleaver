Week 21: Maya buys summer clothes and makes an exchange

German with English gloss, line-aligned.

warm may afternoon
Storyboard: On a beautifully warm Saturday afternoon in May, Maya finishes her volunteer shift at the café and returns to the flat. With the weather warming up and her thick winter coat officially packed away, she realizes her wardrobe is unprepared for the coming season. She decides it is the perfect afternoon to shop for a light summer jacket and a new shirt.

„Tschüss, Elif!“, sage ich."Bye, Elif!" I say.
„Bis nächste Woche, Maya!“, antwortet sie."See you next week, Maya!" she answers.

Mein dicker Wintermantel ist schon im Schrank.My thick winter coat is already in the wardrobe.
Ich brauche ihn nicht mehr.I do not need it anymore.

Ich brauche unbedingt eine leichte Sommerjacke.I really need a light summer jacket.
Und ich möchte auch ein neues T-Shirt kaufen.And I also want to buy a new shirt.

Ich nehme meinen Rucksack und gehe wieder nach draußen.I take my backpack and go back outside.

comparing jackets

the checkout

too small at home
