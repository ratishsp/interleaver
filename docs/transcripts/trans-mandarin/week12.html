Week 12: Maya joins a badminton club

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding a club
Storyboard: Maya works out at the gym on Wednesdays but wants to meet people, so she looks for a social sport. She loves badminton — she played it a lot back home in Mexico — so she finds a local club online and books a trial session for Thursday evening.
我每个星期三晚上去健身房。I go to the gym every Wednesday evening.
我很喜欢锻炼。I like to work out.
但我也想参加一项体育运动。But I also want to play a sport.
我热爱打羽毛球。I love to play badminton.
我在墨西哥老家的时候经常打。I played it a lot back home in Mexico.
现在我在找一家当地的俱乐部。Now I am looking for a local club.
我拿着电脑坐在沙发上。I am sitting on my couch with my computer.
我在网上搜索。I search online.
我找到了一家不错的羽毛球俱乐部。I find a good badminton club.
我看了俱乐部的网站。I look at the club's website.
他们有很多支队伍。They have many teams.
他们在星期四晚上打球。They play on Thursday evening.
我想预约一次体验训练。I want to book a trial session.
我在网站上找到一份表格。I find a form on the website.
我填写了我的名字和电子邮箱。I write my name and my email.
我选择了星期四晚上。I choose Thursday evening.
训练在晚上七点开始。The training starts at seven in the evening.
这太完美了。It is perfect.
我很期待再次打球。I look forward to playing again.
arriving at hall
Storyboard: On Thursday evening Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sound of shoes squeaking on the many wooden courts.
现在是星期四晚上，我在家里。It is Thursday evening, and I am at home.
我在收拾我的运动包。I pack my sports bag.
我把我的鞋子和衣服放进包里。I put my shoes and my clothes in the bag.
我很期待今晚去打球。I look forward to playing tonight.
我坐公交车去一个很大的体育馆。I take the bus to a large sports hall.
这是一个寒冷的冬夜。It is a cold winter evening.
我坐在公交车里向外看。I sit in the bus and look out.
公交车停了，我下了车。The bus stops, and I get out.
我走进那个大体育馆。I walk into the large sports hall.
里面非常明亮。It is very bright.
我看到体育馆里有很多人。I see many people in the hall.
他们热爱运动。They love to play sports.
我听到了很大的声音。I hear a loud sound.
鞋子在许多木制球场上摩擦出吱吱的声音。The shoes squeak on the many wooden courts.
人们跑得很快，一起打球。People run fast and play together.
看起来非常有趣。It looks very fun.
我准备好去打球了。I am ready to play.
但首先我需要租一把球拍。But first I need to rent a racket.
renting the racket
Storyboard: Maya goes to the small hall kiosk. She tells the clerk she is there to try out the club, and pays forty kroner to rent a racket for the evening.
我走到体育馆里的一个小售货亭。I walk over to a small kiosk in the sports hall.
一个年轻男子站在柜台后面。A young man stands behind the counter.
他微笑着说你好。He smiles and says hello.
“你好，我今天想打羽毛球，”我说。"Hello, I want to play badminton today," I say.
“你是俱乐部的新人吗？”他问。"Are you new here in the club?" he asks.
“是的，我想试着参加，”我回答。"Yes, I would like to try to join in," I answer.
“欢迎你，”男人说。"Welcome," the man says.
“你需要租球拍吗？”他问。"Do you need to rent a racket?" he asks.
“是的，谢谢，我想租一把今晚用的球拍，”我说。"Yes please, I want to rent a racket for the evening," I say.
他给我找了一把好球拍。He finds a good racket for me.
“一共四十克朗，”他说。"It costs forty kroner," he says.
我拿出我的银行卡。I take out my payment card.
我付了那四十克朗。I pay the forty kroner.
他隔着柜台把球拍递给我。He gives me the racket over the counter.
“我觉得这是一项很有趣的运动，”我开心地说。"I think it is a fun sport," I say happily.
“祝你打得愉快。”"Have a really good game."
我道了谢，然后向球场走去。I say thank you and walk over to the courts.
playing doubles
Storyboard: In the busy hall, Maya joins a doubles game with two friendly players, Kasper and Sara, and a fourth person. She has played for years, so she smashes the shuttlecock hard across the net.
我走向一个羽毛球场。I walk over to a badminton court.
场上站着三个人。Three people are standing on the court.
“嗨，我叫卡斯帕，”一个男人说。"Hi, I am Kasper," a man says.
“我叫莎拉，”一个女人说。"And I am Sara," a woman says.
第三名球员叫埃米尔。The third player is named Emil.
“我叫玛雅，”我说。"I am Maya," I say.
“我们通常打双打，”卡斯帕说。"We usually play doubles," Kasper says.
“你想和我们一起打吗？”他问。"Do you want to play with us?" he asks.
“好的，我很乐意，”我回答。"Yes, I would love to," I reply.
我很喜欢打羽毛球。I really like to play badminton.
卡斯帕和我对阵莎拉和埃米尔。Kasper and I play against Sara and Emil.
我们立刻开始打球。We start to play immediately.
莎拉开始比赛。Sara starts the match.
羽毛球快速地飞来飞去。The shuttlecock flies quickly back and forth.
埃米尔将羽毛球高高击向空中。Emil hits the shuttlecock high up in the air.
我喜欢扣杀。I love to smash.
我跳起来，用力把它扣过网。I jump and smash it hard over the net.
它落在了他们那边的场地上。It lands on their side of the court.
“好球，玛雅！”卡斯帕喊道。"Good shot, Maya!" Kasper shouts.
post match chat
Storyboard: The fast match finishes and the tired players walk off the court. Sara says Maya plays really well, and Maya explains that she plays a lot back home in Mexico.
这场节奏很快的比赛结束了。The fast match is over.
我们一起走下球场。We walk off the court together.
我很累。I am very tired.
卡斯佩和莎拉也很累。Kasper and Sara are also tired.
我们喝了点水。We drink some water.
“你打得很好，”莎拉说。"You play very well," Sara says.
“非常感谢，”我说。"Thank you very much," I say.
“我觉得太好玩了。”"I think it is so much fun."
“你经常打吗？”卡斯佩问。"Do you play often?" Kasper asks.
“是的，我在墨西哥老家经常打，”我回答。"Yes, I play a lot back home in Mexico," I answer.
“我通常和我妹妹一起打。”"I usually play with my sister."
“这是一项有趣的运动，”莎拉说。"It is a fun sport," Sara says.
“我们很喜欢在俱乐部这里打球。”"We love to play here in the club."
“我想在丹麦多打打球，”我说。"I want to play more here in Denmark," I say.
“你的确很快，”卡斯佩笑着说。"You are definitely fast," Kasper laughs.
“谢谢，但现在我想坐下来，”我说。"Thanks, but now I want to sit down," I say.
“我们要不要去餐厅？”卡斯佩问。"Shall we go into the cafeteria?" Kasper asks.
“是的，我很乐意，”我回答。"Yes, I would very much like that," I answer.
hall cafeteria
Storyboard: The group sits in the small hall cafeteria to drink water and chat. Kasper asks if Maya plans to come back, and she happily promises to make it a weekly Thursday routine.
我们走进体育馆里的小餐厅。We walk into the small cafeteria in the hall.
我们找了一张桌子。We find a table.
我非常渴。I am very thirsty.
我们喝着冷水聊天。We drink cold water and talk.
“这个俱乐部真不错，”我说。"This is a really good club," I say.
“这是一项很好的运动，”卡斯帕说。"It is a good sport to do," Kasper says.
卡斯帕看着我。Kasper looks at me.
“你想再来参加吗？”他问。"Do you want to join again?" he asks.
“是的，非常想，”我回答。"Yes, very much," I answer.
“我想每个星期四都在这里打球，”我解释道。"I want to play here every Thursday," I explain.
“听起来很棒，”莎拉说。"That sounds lovely," Sara says.
“我们总是星期四在这里打球，”卡斯帕说。"We always play here on Thursdays," Kasper says.
“那我们下周见，”我高兴地说。"Then we'll see each other next week," I say happily.
我们喝完了剩下的水。We drink the rest of our water.
结识新朋友真好。It is lovely to meet new people.
walking to bus
Storyboard: Maya leaves the sports hall with Sara. They walk together to the bus stop in the cold winter evening, and Maya feels glad to have met fun, active locals.
萨拉和我走出体育馆。Sara and I walk out of the sports hall.
天很晚了，这是一个寒冷的冬夜。It is late, and it is a cold winter evening.
但比赛后我还是觉得很暖和。But I am still warm after the match.
我们一起走到公交车站。We walk over to the bus stop together.
风在吹，街道很暗。The wind is blowing, and the street is dark.
“你周末也喜欢打羽毛球吗？”我问。"Do you also like to play badminton at the weekend?" I ask.
“不，我只在星期四打羽毛球，”萨拉回答。"No, I only play badminton on Thursdays," Sara answers.
“但我喜欢做运动，”她说。"But I like to do sports," she says.
“我通常会去公园跑步，”她接着说。"I usually go for a run in the park," she continues.
她是一个非常活跃又有趣的人。She is a very active and fun person.
我们在寒冷中等公交车。We wait for the bus in the cold.
“我的公交车十分钟后到，”我说。"My bus comes in ten minutes," I say.
“我们坐同一辆公交车，”萨拉笑着说。"We take the same bus," Sara smiles.
这让我很高兴。I am very happy about that.
我喜欢和她聊天。I like to talk with her.
卡斯帕和萨拉都是有趣又友善的人。Kasper and Sara are fun and nice people.
现在我在星期四有了一个很好的固定安排。Now I have a good routine on Thursdays.
我很累，但心情很好。I am tired, but I am in a good mood.
公交车终于来了。The bus finally arrives.
我已经开始期待下个星期四了。I am already looking forward to next Thursday.
