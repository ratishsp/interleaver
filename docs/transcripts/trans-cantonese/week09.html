Week 9: Maya visits the winter market

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

arriving at the market
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a busy outdoor market in a large square. The winter morning is cold, but the stalls look inviting. She carries a large empty bag and looks for fresh food for the weekend.
今日係星期六朝早。It is Saturday morning.
丹麥而家係冬天。It is winter in Denmark.
今日天氣好凍。The weather is very cold today.
我企喺城市裡面一個大廣場度。I am standing in a large square in the city.
呢度有個露天市場。There is an outdoor market here.
我望住咁多個攤檔。I look at the many stalls.
佢哋全部睇落都好吸引。They all look inviting.
廣場上面有好多人。There are many people in the square.
啲人著住保暖嘅冬天衫。People are wearing warm winter clothes.
我淨係著住一件薄外套。I only have a thin jacket on.
我帶住一個大袋。I have a large bag with me.
個袋而家完全係空嘅。The bag is completely empty right now.
我搵緊週末食嘅新鮮食物。I am looking for fresh food for the weekend.
市場有好多新鮮貨品。The market has many fresh goods.
我見到蔬菜、生果同埋麵包。I see vegetables, fruit, and bread.
我諗緊啲價錢。I think about the prices.
呢度啲嘢要幾多錢呢？How much do things cost here?
我行過去第一個攤檔。I walk over to the first stall.
carrots for soup
Storyboard: Maya stops at a vegetable stall to buy ingredients for a hot soup. She points at some winter carrots and asks how much they cost. The vendor says they cost fifteen kroner per kilo. Maya asks for one kilo and pays.
我行去一個菜檔。I walk over to a vegetable stall.
我今日煲熱湯。I am making a hot soup today.
我望住枱面上好多嘅蔬菜。I look at the many vegetables on the table.
我指住一啲大條嘅冬季紅蘿蔔。I point at some large winter carrots.
我煲湯需要紅蘿蔔。I need carrots for my soup.
有個小販企喺張枱後面。There is a vendor standing behind the table.
「你好，」佢話。"Hello," he says.
「你好，」我答。"Hello," I reply.
「啲紅蘿蔔幾多錢？」我問。"How much do the carrots cost?" I ask.
「十五克朗一公斤，」小販答。"They cost fifteen kroner per kilo," the vendor answers.
十五克朗係個好價錢。Fifteen kroner is a good price.
「我要一公斤，唔該，」我話。"I will take one kilo, please," I say.
小販幫啲紅蘿蔔秤重。The vendor weighs the carrots.
佢將啲紅蘿蔔放入一個紙袋度。He puts them in a paper bag.
佢畀個袋我。He gives me the bag.
我畀咗嗰十五克朗。I pay the fifteen kroner.
我將個袋放入我個大袋度。I put the bag down in my large bag.
我好期待碗熱湯。I look forward to the hot soup.
a taste of cheese
Storyboard: At the next stall, a friendly vendor sells large cheeses. He offers Maya a small piece on a wooden stick to taste. She eats it, likes it, and asks for the price of a small block. It costs fifty-five kroner, so she buys it.
我前面有一個好大嘅芝士檔。In front of me is a large cheese stall.
檔口有好多大嚿嘅芝士。The stall has many large cheeses.
一個友善嘅檔主對住我笑。A friendly vendor smiles at me.
佢有條木籤，上面拮住一細嚿芝士。He has a small piece of cheese on a wooden stick.
佢話：「畀你㗎，試少少吖。」"Here you go, a small taste," he says.
佢將條木籤遞畀我。He gives the stick to me.
我食嗰嚿黃色嘅芝士。I eat the yellow cheese.
味道真係好好。It tastes really good.
我望住一細嚿芝士。I look at a small cheese.
我問：「呢嚿芝士幾多錢？」"How much does this cheese cost?" I ask.
檔主答：「五十五克朗。」"It costs fifty-five kroner," the vendor answers.
呢個價錢好抵。That is a good price.
我指住嗰嚿細芝士。I point at the small cheese.
我話：「我要買呢嚿。」"I will buy it," I say.
佢用紙將嚿芝士包好。He wraps the cheese in paper.
我畀錢佢。I give him the money.
佢話：「多謝晒。」"Thank you very much," he says.
嚿芝士聞落又濃又香。The cheese smells strong and good.
the expensive gloves
Storyboard: The wind blows cold, so Maya looks at a stall with warm clothes. She picks up a pair of thick wool gloves and asks how much the gloves cost. The vendor answers that the price is two hundred kroner. Maya thinks that is too dear, so she politely says no thank you and walks on.
今日啲風吹得好凍。The wind blows cold today.
我對手好凍。My hands are very cold.
我見到有個賣保暖衫嘅大檔口。I see a large stall with warm clothes.
我行過去檔主嗰度。I walk over to the vendor.
佢張枱上面有好多保暖嘅嘢。He has many warm things on the table.
我拎起一對厚嘅羊毛手套。I pick up a pair of thick wool gloves.
質地好軟熟。They are very soft.
我問：「呢對手套幾多錢呀？」"How much do the gloves cost?" I ask.
檔主答：「兩百克朗。」"They cost two hundred kroner," the vendor answers.
我再望下對手套。I look at the gloves again.
兩百克朗呢個價錢好高。Two hundred kroner is a high price.
對我嚟講太貴喇。It is too expensive for me.
我將對手套擺返喺張枱度。I put the gloves back on the table.
我好有禮貌咁講：「唔使喇，唔該。」"No thank you," I say politely.
「祝你有愉快嘅一日。」"Have a good day."
檔主點點頭微笑。The vendor nods and smiles.
佢話：「你都係。」"You too," he says.
我繼續喺市集周圍行。I walk on around the market.
buying winter apples
Storyboard: Maya finds a fruit stall with baskets of dark red winter apples. She asks the vendor how much a small bag costs. The vendor smiles and says the price is twenty kroner. Maya hands him a paper note for fifty kroner. The vendor hands her the bag of apples and gives her exactly thirty kroner back in coins. Maya puts the heavy coins into her coat pocket.
今日街市有好多人。There are many people at the market today.
我搵到一間細嘅生果檔。I find a small fruit stall.
檔口入面有好多個籃。There are many baskets in the stall.
啲籃裝滿晒深紅色嘅冬蘋果。The baskets are full of dark red winter apples.
我望住一細袋蘋果。I look at a small bag of apples.
「一細袋幾多錢呀？」我問檔主。"How much does a small bag cost?" I ask the vendor.
佢望吓個籃入面。He looks in the basket.
佢答：「價錢係二十克朗。」"The price is twenty kroner," he answers.
我喺袋入面搵錢。I look in my bag for money.
我畀一張五十克朗嘅銀紙佢。I give him a fifty-krone note.
檔主將嗰袋蘋果遞畀我。The vendor hands me the bag of apples.
然後佢找錢畀我。Then he gives me money back.
我啱啱好找返三十克朗嘅銀仔。I get exactly thirty kroner back in coins.
啲銀仔又厚又重。The coins are thick and heavy.
我將啲好重嘅銀仔放落大褸袋。I put the heavy coins down into my coat pocket.
啲蘋果聞落好新鮮。The apples smell very fresh.
我將嗰袋蘋果放落我個大購物袋入面。I put the bag down into my large shopping bag.
fresh rye bread
Storyboard: Maya smells baked goods and walks over to a baker's stall. She points to a fresh, dark rye bread and asks how much it costs. The baker tells her it costs thirty-five kroner. She pays with her card and places the warm bread in her bag.
我經過一個細攤檔。I walk past a small stall.
嗰度有陣新鮮麵包嘅香味。It smells of fresh bread.
我行過去個攤檔度。I walk over to the stall.
麵包師傅企喺一張大枱後面。The baker is standing behind a large table.
張枱擺滿晒烘焙食品。The table is full of baked goods.
我見到一個深色嘅黑麥包。I see a dark rye bread.
佢望落好新鮮。It looks very fresh.
我指住個黑麥包。I point at the rye bread.
我問：「呢個深色黑麥包幾多錢呀？」"How much does the dark rye bread cost?" I ask.
麵包師傅抹抹對手。The baker wipes his hands.
佢答：「三十五克朗。」"It costs thirty-five kroner," he replies.
我話：「我買呢個。」"I am buying it," I say.
我攞張付款卡出嚟。I take out my payment card.
我用卡畀錢。I pay with the card.
麵包師傅用紙袋裝住個麵包畀我。The baker gives me the bread in a paper bag.
我摸吓個袋。I touch the bag.
個麵包仲係暖嘅。The bread is still warm.
我好期待食呢個包。I look forward to eating it.
exact change for coffee
Storyboard: Maya feels cold and spots a small coffee cart. She approaches and asks the barista how much a hot black coffee costs. The barista replies that it costs thirty kroner. Maya takes the new coins from her coat pocket and pays for the coffee with exact change.
我而家好凍。I am very cold now.
我對手好凍。My hands are cold.
我喺市集度四圍望。I look around the market.
我見到一架細嘅咖啡車。I see a small coffee cart.
一個後生仔企喺架車後面。A young man stands behind the cart.
我行過去佢度。I walk over to him.
我同咖啡師打招呼。I say hello to the barista.
我問：「你好，一杯黑咖啡幾多錢呀？」"Hello, how much does a black coffee cost?" I ask.
咖啡師微笑。The barista smiles.
佢答：「一杯熱嘅黑咖啡三十克朗。」"A hot, black coffee costs thirty kroner," he answers.
我大褸個袋入面有啲銀仔。I have coins in my coat pocket.
呢啲係買蘋果找嘅錢。It is my change from the apples.
我攞啲銀仔出嚟。I take the coins out.
我數一數。I count them.
我啱啱好有三十克朗。I have exactly thirty kroner.
我話：「畀你，呢度三十克朗。」"Here you go, here are thirty kroner," I say.
佢接過啲錢。He takes the money.
佢畀杯熱咖啡我。He gives me the hot coffee.
杯咖啡聞落好香。The coffee smells wonderful.
我捧住隻杯嚟暖手。I warm my hands on the cup.
winter flowers
Storyboard: Maya walks toward the market exit and stops at a flower stall. She points at a bundle of bright winter tulips and asks for the price. The vendor says they cost forty kroner. Maya buys the flowers and walks back to her apartment, drinking her hot coffee and holding the colorful tulips.
我向住市場嘅出口行。I walk towards the exit of the market.
我見到一個好大嘅花檔。I see a large flower stall.
檔口有好多好靚嘅花。The stall has many beautiful flowers.
我喺檔口前面停低。I stop in front of the stall.
我望住一束冬天嘅鬱金香。I look at a bundle of winter tulips.
啲花色彩好繽紛。They are very colourful.
我指住啲鬱金香。I point at the tulips.
「啲鬱金香幾多錢呀？」我問檔主。"How much do the tulips cost?" I ask the vendor.
檔主拎起啲鬱金香。The vendor holds up the tulips.
「四十克朗，」佢答。"They cost forty kroner," he answers.
「我買啦，」我話。"I will buy them," I say.
我畀錢買花。I pay for the flowers.
檔主將啲鬱金香畀我。The vendor gives me the tulips.
而家我行完市場喇。Now I am finished at the market.
我行返去我嘅公寓。I walk back to my apartment.
我隻手拎住色彩繽紛嘅鬱金香。I hold the colourful tulips in my hand.
我飲我杯熱咖啡。I drink my hot coffee.
陽光照耀住繁忙嘅市場。The sun shines over the busy market.
