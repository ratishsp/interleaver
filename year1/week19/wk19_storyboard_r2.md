# Week 19 — Storyboard (Maya starts a new project with the team)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Present perfect *har arbejdet*; professions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_monday_bus_nerves | On Monday morning, Maya takes the bus to the office. She is a software engineer and has worked at the company since January, but today she feels slightly nervous because her first major project finally begins. |
| 2 | 02_morning_meeting_boss | The boss gathers everyone in the bright meeting room. As the project manager, he explains the tight schedule and mentions that the client has worked with them before, so expectations are high. |
| 3 | 03_maya_presents | Maya stands up in front of the group for the first time. She presents her technical plan and clearly explains the new database solutions she has worked on over the past few days. |
| 4 | 04_eva_the_designer | Later, Maya visits Eva, the team's designer. Eva points to a screen full of colorful drawings and explains how hard she has worked on the visual design to make it simple and beautiful for the users. |
| 5 | 05_coding_with_peter | In the afternoon, Maya sits with Peter to write the actual code. As fellow software engineers, they solve problems well together, though Peter laughs that they have worked on one tiny error for two whole hours. |
| 6 | 06_the_software_tester | The next day, Maya watches the team's tester click rapidly through her finished screens. He quickly breaks her code, smiling as he adds that he has tested software for five years and knows exactly where the mistakes hide. |
| 7 | 07_wednesday_gym_exhaustion | By Wednesday evening, Maya is sweating at her weekly gym class. The team has worked incredibly fast over the last three days, and she enjoys lifting heavy weights to give her tired brain a much-needed rest. |
| 8 | 08_friday_canteen_celebration | The busy week ends with a cheerful Friday lunch in the canteen. Maya sits with Peter, Eva, and the tester, eating warm food and feeling genuinely proud of what they have worked on together. |
