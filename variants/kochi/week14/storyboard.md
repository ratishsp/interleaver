# Week 14 — Storyboard (Maya crosses the city by public transport)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** **കയറുക/ഇറങ്ങുക** (board/alight); **-ഇൽ/-ലേക്ക്** with vehicles; **പോകണം** (need to get to)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_waiting_for_the_bus | The morning sun feels warm, and the busy street is full of traffic. Maya stands at the bus stop near her home, knowing she needs to get to the main metro station for her journey across the city. |
| 2 | 02_boarding_and_asking | A bright city bus arrives. As she boards, Maya politely asks a friendly female passenger seated near the door if this bus goes to the metro station. The passenger says yes, and Maya sits down beside her. |
| 3 | 03_alighting_at_the_station | Soon the bus reaches the bustling junction. Maya steps off the bus carefully and walks away from the noisy road into the cool, quiet Kochi Metro station. |
| 4 | 04_buying_the_metro_card | Maya goes to the staffed ticket counter and politely asks for the travel card that works on both the train and the boat. She shows her ID card, pays, and the worker hands her the new card. |
| 5 | 05_riding_the_train | Maya scans her new card at the gates and boards the air-conditioned train. She sits comfortably by the window, watching the city buildings pass by below as she travels toward the waterfront terminal. |
| 6 | 06_finding_the_route | At the right stop, Maya alights from the train, leaves the station, and walks a short way down the road to the modern water metro terminal. She pauses in front of a large map on the wall and confirms her stop for Fort Kochi. |
| 7 | 07_boarding_the_water_metro | Maya taps her card at the entrance and walks down the ramp to the dock. She boards the sleek electric boat, and notices how quiet it is as it glides out onto the water. |
| 8 | 08_crossing_the_harbour | The boat moves smoothly and quietly across the wide blue water of the harbour. Maya looks out the window and admires the famous, giant wooden fishing nets lining the shore as the boat approaches the dock. |
| 9 | 09_stepping_off_for_the_errand | Maya steps off the boat at Fort Kochi and walks directly to a small spice shop nearby. She warmly greets the shop worker and asks for a packet of fresh black pepper to send to her family in Mexico. |
