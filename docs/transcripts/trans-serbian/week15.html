Week 15: Maya's everyday life in the city

morning weather
Storyboard: Maya wakes up early and looks out the window. The sun shines, but the wind is cold, so she puts on a warm sweater for the day.
Budim se rano.I wake up early.
Tek je šest sati.It is only six o'clock.
Novi je dan.It is a new day.
Polako ustajem.I get up slowly.
Idem do prozora.I walk over to the window.
Gledam napolje na grad.I look out at the city.
Napolju je još uvek mračno i hladno.It is still dark and cold outside.
Zima je još uvek tu.Winter is still here.
Idem u kupatilo.I go into the bathroom.
Umivam se.I wash my face.
Perem zube.I brush my teeth.
Zatim se oblačim.Then I get dressed.
Oblačim debeo, topao džemper.I put on a thick, warm sweater.
Savršen je za hladan dan.It is perfect for a cold day.
Idem u kuhinju.I go into the kitchen.
Pravim šolju tople kafe.I make a cup of warm coffee.
Jedem parče hleba sa sirom.I eat a slice of bread with cheese.
Sada sam potpuno budna.Now I am completely awake.
Spremna sam za novi dan.I am ready for the new day.
the bus commute
Storyboard: She walks to the bus stop and waits for her daily transport. The yellow bus arrives, she steps inside, scans her travel card, and rides through the busy streets.

To je autobus broj pet.It is bus number five.

Vrata se otvaraju.The doors open.

Nalazim svoju karticu za prevoz u torbi.I find my travel card in my bag.

Aparat zapišti.The machine beeps.
Idem u zadnji deo autobusa.I walk to the back of the bus.
Nalazim slobodno mesto pored prozora.I find an empty seat by the window.

Gledam ljude na ulici.I look at the people in the street.

working with peter

On je veoma ljubazan.He is very friendly.
"Dobro jutro, Majo", kaže Peter."Good morning, Maya," Peter says.

Imam šolju kafe.I have a cup of coffee.

Posao je veoma uzbudljiv.The work is very exciting.
Zadovoljna sam svojim poslom.I am happy with my job.
canteen lunch
Storyboard: At noon, Maya and Peter go down to the bright canteen together. She chooses a classic open sandwich with fish and pays forty kroner at the register.
Dvanaest je sati.It is twelve o'clock.
Vreme je za ručak.It is lunch time.
Peter i ja silazimo u kantinu.Peter and I go down to the canteen.

Veoma smo gladni.We are very hungry.
Gledamo dobru hranu.We look at the good food.

giving directions
Storyboard: After work, Maya steps out into a large square. A tourist asks for the way to a local museum, and she politely explains that they must walk straight ahead and then turn left.
Slobodna sam od posla.I am off from work.
Izlazim na jedan veliki trg.I walk out onto a large square.
Danas u gradu ima mnogo ljudi.There are many people in the city today.
Jedan čovek mi prilazi.A man walks up to me.
On ima veliki fotoaparat.He has a large camera.
On je turista.He is a tourist.
Osmehuje se i kaže zdravo.He smiles and says hi.
"Gde je muzej?", pita turista."Where is the museum?" the tourist asks.
Dobro poznajem taj muzej.I know the museum well.
Nije daleko.It is not far away.
Pokazujem niz ulicu.I point down the street.
"Idete pravo", objašnjavam."You walk straight ahead," I explain.
"Onda skrenete levo.""Then you turn to the left."
"Muzej je sa desne strane.""The museum is on the right side."
"Mnogo vam hvala na pomoći", kaže čovek."Thank you very much for the help," the man says.
"Nema na čemu", odgovaram veselo."You are welcome," I answer happily.
On ide niz ulicu.He walks down the street.
Ja nastavljam ka pekari.I walk on towards the bakery.
buying a cake
Storyboard: On the way home, she stops at a small bakery on the corner. She orders a chocolate cake for the evening and pays sixty-five kroner to the smiling baker.
Stižem u malu pekaru na uglu.I arrive at the small bakery on the corner.
Ulazim u toplu radnju.I go into the warm shop.
Divno miriše na kafu i kolače.It smells lovely of coffee and cake.
Pekar u beloj kecelji stoji iza pulta.A baker in a white apron stands behind the counter.
„Dobar dan”, kaže pekar."Good afternoon," the baker says.
„Kako mogu da pomognem?”, pita on."How can I help?" he asks.
Gledam brojne kolače.I look at the many cakes.

„Želela bih čokoladni kolač, molim vas”, kažem."I would like the chocolate cake, please," I say.
„Naravno”, odgovara pekar."Of course," the baker answers.

Vadim novčanik iz torbe.I take my wallet out of my bag.
Plaćam šezdeset pet kruna.I pay the sixty-five kroner.
„Hvala lepo, i prijatno veče”, kaže pekar."Thank you very much, and have a good evening," the baker says.
„Hvala, takođe”, odgovaram."Thank you, you too," I answer.
Uzimam belu kutiju.I take the white box.
Pažljivo izlazim iz radnje sa kolačem.I walk carefully out of the shop with the cake.
