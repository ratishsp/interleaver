Week 7: Maya plans a busy week

planning the week
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya makes a cup of tea and opens her calendar at the kitchen table — she always plans the week here. She works through it: the bike goes to the repair shop, the salon needs a phone call, the gym is on Wednesday. She writes each one in, and February fills up.
Hétfő reggel van.It is Monday morning.
Készítek egy csésze forró teát.I make a cup of hot tea.
Az asztalnál ülök a konyhában.I sit at the table in the kitchen.
Kinyitom a naptáramat.I open my calendar.
Mindig itt tervezem meg a hetemet.I always plan my week here.
Most február van.It is February now.
A hónap már most nagyon tele van.The month is already very full.
Holnap kedd lesz.Tomorrow is Tuesday.
A biciklim sajnos elromlott.My bicycle is unfortunately broken.
Holnap reggel elsétálok a bicikliszerelőhöz.I walk to the bicycle mechanic tomorrow morning.
Nyolc órakor nyit.He opens at eight o'clock.
Beírom a naptárba.I write it in the calendar.
Ma a fodrászt is felhívom.I also call the hairdresser today.
A hajam már túlságosan hosszú.My hair is way too long.
Szerdán elmegyek az edzőterembe.Wednesday I go to the gym.
Szerdánként gyakran edzek.I often train on Wednesdays.
Ez általában este hét órakor van.It is usually at seven o'clock in the evening.
Újra ránézek a tervemre.I look at my plan again.
Iszom a teámat.I drink my tea.
Most már készen állok egy új hétre.Now I am ready for a new week.
dropping off the bike

booking a haircut
Storyboard: During her lunch break, Maya calls a hair salon on her mobile phone. She speaks to the hairdresser and books an appointment for Thursday at half past four.
Ebédszünetem van.It is my lunch break.
A munkahelyem étkezdéjében ülök.I am sitting in the canteen at my work.
Tegnap sok dolgom volt.I was busy yesterday.
Ezért most hívom a fodrászt.Therefore I am calling the hairdresser now.
Fogom a mobiltelefonomat.I take my mobile phone.
Felhívok egy fodrászatot a városban.I call a hair salon in the city.
Egy nő veszi fel a telefont.A woman answers the phone.
"Jó napot, itt a fodrászat," mondja a nő."Hello, this is the hair salon," says the woman.
"Jó napot, a nevem Maya," mondom."Hello, my name is Maya," I say.
"Szeretnék időpontot foglalni csütörtökre.""I would like to book an appointment on Thursday."
"Egy pillanat," mondja a fodrász."One moment," says the hairdresser.
"Van időpontjuk délutánra?" kérdezem."Do you have an appointment in the afternoon?" I ask.
"Igen, van egy szabad időpontunk fél ötkor," mondja."Yes, we have an available appointment at half past four," she says.
"Az nagyon megfelel nekem," mondom."That suits me very well," I say.
"Mi a telefonszáma?" kérdezi a fodrász."What is your phone number?" asks the hairdresser.
"A számom tizenkettő, harmincnégy, ötvenhat, hetvennyolc," mondom."My number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
"Nagyon köszönöm, találkozunk csütörtökön fél ötkor," mondja."Thank you very much, see you on Thursday at half past four," she says.
"Viszlát," válaszolom."See you," I reply.
Leteszem a telefont az asztalra.I put the phone on the table.
Most végre megeszem az ételemet.Now I finally eat my food.
evening gym

hallway chat

scheduling conflict
