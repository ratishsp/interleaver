Week 8: Maya explores the city

Latvian with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
Ir sestdienas rīts.It is Saturday morning.
Saule spīd pār pilsētu.The sun is shining over the city.
Es eju lejā uz ielas.I go down to the street.
Mana kaimiņiene Nina stāv ārā pie mūsu ēkas.My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
"Labrīt, Maija," viņa saka un pasmaida."Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
"Labrīt, Nina," es atbildu."Good morning, Nina," I answer.
"Man šodien ir brīva diena," es viņai saku."I have the day off today," I tell her.
"Es dodos uz pilsētu.""I am heading out into the city."
"Kurp tu dodies?" jautā Nina."Where are you going?" Nina asks.
"Es eju lejā pie ūdens," es saku."I am walking down to the water," I say.
"Bet es nezinu ceļu.""But I do not know the way."
Nina norāda lejup pa ielu.Nina points down the street.
"Par laimi, tas ir ļoti vienkārši," viņa saka."Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
"Šeit ej taisni uz priekšu.""Go straight ahead here."
"Un pie parka nogriezies pa kreisi.""And turn left at the park."
"Tad tu nonāksi tieši pie ostas.""Then you come right down to the harbour."
"Liels paldies par palīdzību, Nina," es saku."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
"Nav par ko, un jauku pastaigu," viņa atbild."You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
Es viņai pamāju.I wave to her.
Es sāku savu pastaigu uz ūdens pusi.I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
Es sekoju Nīnas maršrutam.I follow Nina's route.
Gaiss ir svaigs un auksts.The air is fresh and cold.
Tagad es eju pa galveno ielu.Now I walk down the main street.
Vispirms es vienkārši eju taisni uz priekšu.First I just walk straight ahead.
Uz ielas ir liela satiksme.There is a lot of traffic on the street.
Pa ceļam es redzu trīs dzeltenus velosipēdus.On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
Velosipēdi ātri pabrauc man garām.The bicycles ride quickly past me.
Pēc tam es eju tālāk uz priekšu.After that I walk further ahead.
Tad es nonāku pie liela parka.Then I come to a large park.
Tagad es atceros Nīnas vārdus.Now I remember Nina's words.
"Nogriezies pa kreisi pie parka," viņa saka."Turn left at the park," she says.
Šeit es nogriežos pa kreisi.Here I turn left.
Nedaudz vēlāk es redzu atvērtas durvis.A little later I see an open door.
Tā ir maza maiznīca.It is a small bakery.
Ir patīkama svaigas kafijas smarža.There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
Es dievinu siltu maizi.I love warm bread.
Šodien pilsēta ir jauka.Today the city is lovely.
Pēc tam es eju tālāk uz ūdens pusi.Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
Es nonāku lielā laukumā.I arrive at a large square.
Tur ir daudz cilvēku.There are many people.
Bet es neredzu ūdeni.But I do not see the water.
Es meklēju ostu.I am looking for the harbour.
Es redzu vecāku vīrieti uz ietves.I see an older man on the pavement.
Es viņu apstādinu.I stop him.
"Atvainojiet," es saku."Excuse me," I say.
"Kur ir osta?""Where is the harbour?"
Vecākais vīrietis pamāj ar galvu.The older man nods.
Viņš norāda uz priekšu.He points ahead.
"Ejiet taisni pāri laukumam," viņš saka."Walk straight across the square," he says.
"Ejiet garām muzejam.""Go past the museum."
"Un pagriezieties pa labi.""And turn right."
Es skatos pāri laukumam.I look across the square.
Es redzu lielu ēku.I see a large building.
Tas ir muzejs.It is the museum.
"Liels paldies," es saku."Thank you very much," I say.
"Lūdzu," viņš atbild."You are welcome," he replies.
Es eju tālāk.I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
Es eju pāri lielajam laukumam.I walk across the large square.
Pēkšņi es dzirdu mūziku.Suddenly, I hear music.
Uz stūra stāv ielu muzikants.On the corner stands a street musician.
Viņš spēlē ģitāru.He plays the guitar.
Es klausos labo mūziku.I listen to the good music.
Es ilgi skatos uz viņu.I look at him for a long time.
Tad es eju tālāk.Then I walk on.
Es aizmirstu par muzeju.I forget the museum.
Es aizmirstu arī savu maršrutu.I also forget my route.
Tā vietā es eju pa šauru sānielu.Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
Šeit ir ļoti kluss.It is very quiet here.
Visapkārt es redzu tikai dažas mazas mājas.All around I only see some small houses.
Tad es apstājos.Then I stop.
Kur ir muzejs?Where is the museum?
Tagad es skatos apkārt.Now I look around.
Es to vairs nevaru redzēt.I cannot see it anymore.
Es ātri paskatos atpakaļ pa ielu.I quickly look back up the street.
Es nevaru redzēt arī laukumu.I cannot see the square either.
Es esmu pilnībā apmaldījusies.I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
Es stāvu uz klusas ielas.I am standing on a quiet street.
Esmu apmaldījusies.I am lost.
Sev priekšā es redzu jaunu sievieti.Ahead of me I see a young woman.
Viņa ved pastaigā mazu suni.She is walking a small dog.
Suns izskatās kā manas ģimenes suns Lola.The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
Es pieeju klāt sievietei.I walk over to the woman.
"Atvainojiet," es saku."Excuse me," I say.
"Es meklēju ostu.""I am looking for the harbour."
Sieviete apstājas un klausās.The woman stops and listens.
Viņa norāda atpakaļ pa ielu.She points back up the street.
"Ejiet taisni atpakaļ uz laukumu," viņa saka."Go straight back to the square," she says.
"Un pagriezieties pa labi pie muzeja.""And turn right at the museum."
"Liels paldies par palīdzību," es saku."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Nav par ko," atbild sieviete."You are welcome," the woman replies.
Es paskatos lejup uz mazo suni.I look down at the little dog.
Tas skatās uz mani.It looks at me.
Es eju atpakaļ laukuma virzienā.I walk back toward the square.
Tagad es zinu, kur es atrodos.Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
Es eju atpakaļ pa kluso ielu.I walk back along the quiet street.
Es eju taisni uz priekšu.I walk straight ahead.
Drīz es atkal redzu lielo laukumu.Soon I see the large square again.
Tad es eju pāri laukumam.Then I walk across the square.
Tagad es stāvu pie muzeja.Now I am standing by the museum.
Es atceros sievietes vārdus: "Nogriezieties pa labi."I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
Pie muzeja es nogriežos pa labi.At the museum I turn right.
Es eju pa platu ielu.I walk down a wide street.
Es skatos uz priekšu.I look ahead.
Beidzot es redzu kaut ko zilu.Finally I see something blue.
Tas ir ūdens!It is the water!
Pa ceļam es eju garām mazai kafejnīcai.On the way I walk past a little café.
Visbeidzot es aizeju līdz pat ūdenim.At last I walk all the way down to the water.
Tagad es esmu ostā.Now I am at the harbour.
Osta ir ļoti liela.The harbour is very large.
Vējš ir auksts un svaigs.The wind is cold and fresh.
Uz ūdens ir daudz laivu.There are many boats on the water.
Šeit es apstājos.Here I stop.
Es stāvu un skatos uz laivām.I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
Es eju taisni uz priekšu gar ostu.I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
Es eju garām mazajām laivām.I walk past the small boats.
Pēkšņi es redzu daudz cilvēku.Suddenly I see many people.
Viņi stāv lejā pie ūdens.They stand down by the water.
Es dodos turp.I walk over there.
Beidzot es to redzu.Finally, I see it.
Mazā nāriņa sēž uz akmens ūdenī.The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
Tā ir ļoti slavena statuja Kopenhāgenā.She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.
Figūra patiesībā nav ļoti liela.The figure is actually not very big.
Bet bronzas figūra saulē ir skaista.But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
Es stāvu starp citiem apmeklētājiem.I stand among the other visitors.
Daudzi tūristi fotografē statuju.Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
Es arī izņemu savu telefonu.I also take out my phone.
Es uzņemu skaistu nāriņas bildi.I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
Es nosūtu bildi savai ģimenei Meksikā.I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
Mana māsa, Sofía, ātri atbild.My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
"Viņa ir ļoti glīta," viņa raksta."She is very pretty," she writes.
Es atkal skatos uz ūdeni.I look at the water again.
walking home
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.
Ir vēla pēcpusdiena.It is late in the afternoon.
Tagad es eju mājās uz savu dzīvokli.Now I walk home to my apartment.
Es eju pa vieglu ceļu.I walk an easy way.
Es vienkārši sekoju garajam ceļam.I just follow the long road.
Ceļš ir pilnīgi taisns.The road is completely straight.
Es eju gar ūdeni.I walk along the water.
Visbeidzot es nogriežos prom no ūdens.Finally I turn away from the water.
Tagad es atkal eju iekšā pilsētā.Now I walk into the city again.
Tad es eju garām maiznīcai.Then I walk past the bakery.
Joprojām smaržo pēc kafijas.It still smells of coffee.
Nedaudz vēlāk es redzu parku.A little later I see the park.
Pie parka es nogriežos pa labi.At the park I turn right.
Es nevienam nejautāju ceļu.I do not ask anyone for directions.
Es atrodu ceļu pilnīgi viena pati.I find the way all by myself.
Nedaudz vēlāk es redzu savu ielu.A little later I see my own street.
Visbeidzot es stāvu pie savām ārdurvīm.Finally I stand at my front door.
No durvīm es atskatos lejup pa ielu.From the door I look back down the street.
Tagad es zinu ceļu uz mājām.Now I know my way home.
