Week 22: Maya cooks a local dinner for her friends

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

planning the menu
Storyboard: Maya decides to host her Thursday badminton friends, Kasper and Sara, for dinner on Friday evening. She wants to cook a traditional local dish, so she finds a recipe for Danish meatballs with potatoes and writes down a shopping list with the exact quantities.
我每逢星期四晚都會打羽毛球。I play badminton every Thursday evening.
卡斯柏同莎拉同我喺同一個球隊。Kasper and Sara play on the same team as me.
聽日係星期五，我請咗佢哋嚟食晚飯。Tomorrow is Friday, and I have invited them over for dinner.
我想煮一味傳統嘅丹麥菜。I want to cook a traditional Danish dish.
所以我決定整肉丸配薯仔。So I decide to make meatballs with potatoes.
我上網搵到個好食譜。I find a good recipe on the internet.
依家我要寫張購物清單。Now I need to write a shopping list.
首先，我需要整肉丸用嘅肉。First, I need meat for the meatballs.
食譜上面寫住五百克免治豬肉。The recipe says five hundred grams of minced pork.
然後我仲需要兩個大黃洋蔥。Then I also need two large yellow onions.
我仲寫低少少牛奶同一隻雞蛋。I also note down a little milk and an egg.
我將全部嘢寫低落記事簿度。I write it all down on my notepad.
之後，我睇吓薯仔嘅份量。Afterwards, I look at the amount of potatoes.
我要買啱啱好一公斤嘅細薯仔。I must buy exactly one kilo of small potatoes.
最後，我喺清單度加埋牛油同鹽。Finally, I add butter and salt to the list.
煎肉永遠都要用大量嘅牛油。You must always use plenty of butter to fry the meat.
張清單終於寫好，我都準備好喇。The list is finally complete, and I am ready.
聽日放工之後，我會直接去超級市場。Tomorrow after work, I will go straight to the supermarket.
shopping for amounts
Storyboard: At the supermarket after work, Maya carefully gathers her ingredients. She weighs out exactly one kilo of small potatoes, picks up five hundred grams of minced pork, and grabs two large yellow onions.
放工之後，我企喺超級市場度。I am standing in the supermarket after work.
今日舖頭好多人。There are many people in the store today.
我手上面攞住張購物清單。I have my shopping list in my hand.
我聽日嘅晚餐需要好準確嘅份量。I need exact amounts for my dinner tomorrow.
首先，我行過去蔬菜區。First, I go over to the vegetables.
我搵到啲又細又靚嘅薯仔。I find some small, good potatoes.
我將佢哋放入一個袋度，然後磅下。I put them in a bag and weigh them.
個磅顯示啱啱好一公斤。The scale shows exactly one kilo.
呢個正正就係食譜需要嘅份量。That is the right amount for the recipe.
跟住，我攞咗兩個大嘅黃洋蔥。Then, I take two large, yellow onions.
之後，我行過去肉類區。After that, I walk over to the meat.
我要買免治豬肉嚟整肉丸。I need minced pork for the meatballs.
我望下凍櫃入面唔同嘅包裝。I look at the different packages in the cooler.
我揀咗一包五百克嘅。I choose a package with five hundred grams.
最後，我再檢查多一次張清單。Finally, I check my list one extra time.
我仲攞咗一包牛油同一啲牛奶。I also take a package of butter and a little milk.
我將所有嘢放曬入個購物籃度。I put all the things down into my basket.
依家我準備好煮飯畀卡斯柏同莎拉食喇。Now I am ready to cook for Kasper and Sara.
first prep step
Storyboard: Back in her apartment kitchen, Maya puts on an apron and reads the recipe's first instructions. The recipe states clearly: first, finely chop the onions, and mix them thoroughly with the meat, an egg, and a little flour.
我喺屋企嘅廚房。I am home in my kitchen.
我著上圍裙。I put on my apron.
我喺電話度睇食譜。I read the recipe on my phone.
個食譜寫得好清楚。The recipe is very clear.
上面寫住：首先，將洋蔥切到好碎。It says: First, chop the onions very finely.
然後，將啲肉放入一個大碗度。Then, put the meat in a large bowl.
跟住，加一隻雞蛋、少少麵粉同牛奶。Afterwards, add an egg, a little flour, and milk.
最後，將所有嘢徹底攪勻。Finally, mix it all thoroughly together.
我搵咗塊砧板同一把利刀。I find a cutting board and a sharp knife.
我將兩個黃洋蔥切成細粒。I cut the two yellow onions into small pieces.
我對眼有啲標眼水。My eyes water a little.
我打開嗰包五百克嘅免治豬肉。I open the package with the five hundred grams of minced pork.
我將啲肉放落我個藍色大碗度。I put the meat into my large, blue bowl.
然後我將啲洋蔥放埋落啲肉度。Then I put the onions down with the meat.
我將隻蛋打落隻碗度。I crack the egg into the bowl.
跟住，我加少少麵粉同牛奶。Afterwards, I add a little flour and milk.
我攞一隻大匙羹將所有嘢攪勻。I take a large spoon and stir it all together.
啲肉餡望落好靚。The meat mixture looks really good.
第一步好簡單。The first step was very easy.
then the potatoes
Storyboard: Next, Maya prepares the potatoes. She follows the next sequence of commands: scrub the potatoes clean, place them in a pot of water, and then add a large spoonful of coarse salt before turning on the stove.
依家我要烚熟啲薯仔。Now I have to boil the potatoes.
我再睇睇張食譜。I look at my recipe again.
裏面有啲好短嘅指示。It has some very short instructions.
首先寫住：「將啲細薯仔刷乾淨。」First it says: "Scrub the small potatoes clean."
我就係用一把細刷喺凍水下面刷。I do that with a small brush under cold water.
然後我睇下一行。Then I read the next line.
之後寫住：「將啲薯仔放入裝咗水嘅煲度。」Afterwards it says: "Place the potatoes in a pot of water."
我喺櫥櫃攞個大煲出嚟。I get out my large pot from the cupboard.
我將足足一公斤放入煲度。I put the whole kilo down into the pot.
個煲就快滿晒。The pot is almost completely full.
下一步係乜嘢？What is the next step?
食譜寫住：「加一大匙羹粗鹽落水度。」The recipe says: "Add a large spoonful of coarse salt to the water."
我攞啲鹽同一隻匙羹出嚟。I take out the salt and a spoon.
我將啲鹽倒落匙羹度。I pour the salt onto the spoon.
我將嗰一大匙羹鹽加落啲薯仔度。I add the large spoonful down to the potatoes.
最後寫住：「開爐。」Finally it says: "Turn on the stove."
我將個煲擺上爐頭，然後開火。I put the pot on the stove and turn it on.
依家啲薯仔只需要烚二十分鐘就得喇。Now the potatoes just have to boil for twenty minutes.
afterwards frying
Storyboard: With the potatoes boiling, Maya heats a large frying pan. The instructions tell her to melt a thick slice of butter, and afterwards, form the meat mixture into round balls using a spoon and fry them until they are crisp and brown.
啲薯仔而家喺個煲度滾緊。The potatoes are now boiling in the pot.
廚房已經聞到好香。It already smells lovely in the kitchen.
我攞一個大煎鑊出嚟。I take out a large frying pan.
我睇食譜下一步嘅指示。I read the next instructions in the recipe.
下一部份好重要。The next part is important.
上面寫住：「首先喺鑊度煮溶一大塊牛油。」It says: "First melt a thick piece of butter in the pan."
我切咗啲牛油放落個鑊度。I cut some butter and put it in the pan.
牛油好快喺熱辣辣嘅金屬面上發出滋滋聲。The butter quickly sizzles on the hot metal.
我繼續睇：「然後將啲肉搓成圓球。」I read on: "Afterwards form the meat into round balls."
我用一隻匙羹將啲肉整成形。I use a spoon to form the meat.
雖然有啲難，但係佢哋整出嚟好靚好圓。It is a bit difficult, but they turn out nice and round.
我小心噉將佢哋放落熱辣辣嘅鑊度。I carefully place them in the hot pan.
食譜繼續寫住：「然後將佢哋煎到脆卜卜同埋變啡色。」The recipe continues: "Then fry them until they are crisp and brown."
我將個火較細少少。I turn down the heat a little.
佢哋需要煎大約十五分鐘。They need to fry for about fifteen minutes.
我經常反轉佢哋，等佢哋唔會燶。I turn them often so they do not burn.
佢哋聞落好香，而且顏色好靚。They smell fantastic and get a good color.
而家啲肉丸同薯仔差唔多得喇。Now the meatballs and the potatoes are almost ready.
我好期待 Kasper 同 Sara 嚟到。I am looking forward to Kasper and Sara arriving.
時間夾得啱啱好。It is absolutely perfect timing.
setting the table
Storyboard: While the meatballs finish cooking, Maya prepares her small dining area. She arranges three plates, sets out the forks and knives, and folds some paper napkins to make the table look inviting.
趁啲肉就快煎好，我喺客廳擺好張餐枱。While the meat finishes frying, I set the table in the living room.
我食飯嗰個位幾細吓。My dining area is quite small.
但係都夠晒位畀 Kasper、Sara 同埋我。But there is plenty of room for Kasper, Sara, and me.
我喺櫥櫃度攞三隻碟出嚟。I take three plates out of the cupboard.
我將佢哋擺喺張細枱上面。I place them on the small table.
然後，我打開裝餐具嗰個櫃桶。Afterwards, I open the drawer with cutlery.
我攞啲叉同埋刀出嚟。I take out forks and knives.
我喺每隻碟旁邊擺一隻叉同一把刀。I lay a fork and a knife by each plate.
我哋仲需要三隻水杯。We will also need three glasses for water.
我去廚房度攞。I fetch those out in the kitchen.
最後，我喺包裝袋度攞幾張淺藍色嘅紙餐巾出嚟。Finally, I take some light blue paper napkins from a package.
我將佢哋摺得好靚。I fold them nicely.
然後我擺一張餐巾喺每隻碟上面。Then I put a napkin on each plate.
依家張枱已經擺好，準備好迎接客人啦。Now the table is set and ready for guests.
我行返去煮食爐嗰度。I walk back to the stove.
啲肉丸喺熱辣辣嘅平底鑊入面仲發出滋滋聲。The meatballs are still sizzling in the hot pan.
我將平底鑊移開個爐。I take the pan off the heat.
我已經完全準備好迎接客人啦。I am completely ready to have guests.
guests arrive
Storyboard: The doorbell rings, and Kasper and Sara arrive in high spirits. Maya welcomes them inside, takes their coats, and gratefully accepts some cold bottles of local beer they brought to share with the meal.
門鐘響起。The doorbell rings.
我笑住開門。I open the door with a smile.
Kasper 同 Sara 企喺出面。Kasper and Sara are standing outside.
佢哋笑住向我揮手。They smile and wave at me.
「嗨 Maya，好多謝你嘅邀請呀！」Kasper 開心咁講。"Hi Maya, and thank you very much for the invitation!" Kasper says happily.
「嗨！」"Hi!"
「入嚟啦，」我答。"Come inside," I answer.
「已經聞到好香喇，」Sara 話。"It smells amazing already," Sara says.
「除低件外褸先啦，」我同佢哋講。"Take your jackets off first," I tell them.
我接過佢哋嘅外褸，掛喺走廊。I take their jackets and hang them up in the hallway.
之後，Sara 畀咗個袋我。Afterwards, Sara gives me a bag.
「我哋帶咗啲凍啤酒嚟，」佢解釋。"We brought some cold beers," she explains.
「係丹麥本地出嘅啤酒，」Kasper 補充。"It is a local Danish beer," Kasper adds.
我望下個袋入面。I look in the bag.
入面有六樽。There are six bottles in it.
「唔該晒，真係啱晒，」我講。"Thank you so much, that is absolutely perfect," I say.
「等我擺佢哋入雪櫃先。」"I will just put them in the fridge."
「好主意，咁我哋就準備好開餐喇，」Kasper 笑住話。"Good idea, and then we are ready to eat," Kasper laughs.
我攞住啲酒樽行入廚房。I walk out into the kitchen with the bottles.
eating together
Storyboard: The three friends squeeze around the cozy table and begin their meal. Kasper and Sara are highly impressed with the food, praising the authentic flavors as they eagerly pour the beer and take second helpings.
我哋喺我張細檯坐低。We sit down at my small table.
空間有啲窄，但我哋坐得好埋，好舒服。It is a bit tight, but we sit close and comfortable.
首先，我將裝住食物嘅碗放上檯。First, I put the bowls of food on the table.
之後，Kasper 同 Sara 攞咗一大份。Afterward, Kasper and Sara take a large portion.
佢哋試食啲肉丸同薯仔。They taste the meatballs and the potatoes.
Sara 話：「真係好好味呀，Maya！」"This tastes really delicious, Maya!" Sara says.
Kasper 話：「係呀，真係好正。」"Yes, they are really good," Kasper says.
佢好快又食多一啖。He quickly takes another bite.
Kasper 仲開埋啲凍啤酒。Kasper also opens the cold beers.
然後佢將啤酒斟入我哋隻杯度。Then he pours them into our glasses.
「飲杯！」我哋三個一齊講，然後飲酒。"Cheers!" all three of us say and drink.
Kasper 喺碟度夾多啲肉丸。Kasper takes more meatballs from the dish.
Sara 亦都夾多份薯仔。Sara also takes an extra portion of potatoes.
我話：「我好開心你哋鍾意。」"I am so glad that you like it," I say.
我微笑住講：「仲有好多，夠晒大家食。」"There is plenty for everyone," I smile.
佢哋讚啲味道夠晒正宗。They praise the authentic flavor.
我哋圍住張檯有講有笑。We talk and laugh around the table.
我哋一邊食一邊傾咗好耐。We eat and talk for a long time.
sharing the recipe
Storyboard: When Sara asks how to make the dish, Maya promises to send her a link to the written recipe. The friends clear the empty plates and cheerfully agree that it will be Kasper's turn to cook next time.
我哋三個而家都好飽。We are all three very full now.
枱上面已經冇嘢食剩喇。There is no more food left on the table.
「Maya，呢餐晚飯真係好正，」Sara 好開心咁講。"That was a fantastic dinner, Maya," Sara says happily.
「呢啲肉丸究竟係點整㗎？」佢跟住問。"How exactly do you make these meatballs?" she then asks.
「係咪好難㗎？」"Is it very difficult?"
「唔係，其實好容易，」我答。"No, it is actually quite easy," I answer.
「首先切碎啲洋蔥，然後再撈勻啲肉，」我解釋。"First you chop onions, and afterwards you mix the meat," I explain.
「我電話入面有個好正嘅食譜。」"I have a really good recipe on my phone."
「我今晚傳條連結畀你啦。」"I will send a link to you tonight."
「唔該晒，我想試吓整，」Sara 話。"Thank you very much, I would like to try that," Sara says.
我哋喺啲細凳度企起身。We stand up from the small chairs.
Kasper 開始執啲空碟。Kasper begins to gather the empty plates.
「我哋而家係咪攞啲碟入去廚房呀？」佢問。"Should we take the plates out into the kitchen now?" he asks.
「好呀，唔該。」"Yes please."
「就咁擺喺廚房枱面得喇，」我講。"Just put them on the kitchen counter," I say.
我哋一齊好快咁執好張枱。We quickly clear the table together.
「不過喂，下次輪到我喇，」Kasper 突然間講。"But hey, next time it is my turn," Kasper says suddenly.
「係呀。」"Yes."
「下次你要煮飯畀我哋食呀！」"Next time you must cook for us!"
Sara 笑咗。Sara laughs.
「無錯。」"Exactly."
「而且我保證一定好好食，」佢話。"And I promise that it will be good," he says.
我哋笑住咁將其餘嘅嘢攞入廚房。We laugh and carry the rest of the things out into the kitchen.
