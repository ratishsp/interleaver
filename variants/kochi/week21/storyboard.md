# Week 21 — Storyboard (Maya shops for the monsoon and exchanges a shirt)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Comparative **-നെക്കാൾ** (than); **വലുത്/ചെറുത്**; trying on and exchanging

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_after_the_cafe_shift | After finishing her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the community café, Maya steps out into the steady monsoon drizzle. Three weeks of daily rain have shown her that an umbrella is not enough on the bicycle, so she decides to stop at a large local clothing store in town for a proper raincoat — and a fresh shirt. |
| 2 | 02_entering_the_clothing_store | Maya arrives at the clothing store, deeply relieved as the blast of cold air conditioning hits her. She navigates past the crowded displays, looking first for the seasonal rainwear section. |
| 3 | 03_comparing_raincoats | In the rainwear aisle, Maya examines two raincoats: a heavy dark blue one and a lightweight, bright yellow one. She decides the yellow one is much better than the blue one for visibility when riding her bicycle, and it looks large enough to cover her backpack. |
| 4 | 04_buying_without_trying | Next, she browses the casual wear and picks out a nice green cotton shirt. Assuming her standard size will fit perfectly, she decides to skip the long line for the fitting rooms, pays for both items at the register, and carefully pockets the receipt. |
| 5 | 05_too_small_at_home | Back in her flat later that afternoon, Maya tries on the new green shirt in front of her bedroom mirror. She realizes immediately that the fit is wrong; the cut of this brand is much smaller than her usual shirts, making it uncomfortably tight across her shoulders. She decides to take it back tomorrow morning. |
| 6 | 06_taking_the_shirt_back | The next morning — a quiet Sunday — Maya sets out for the clothing store with the folded shirt in its shopping bag, wearing her new yellow raincoat against the steady rain. |
| 7 | 07_asking_for_a_bigger_size | Maya approaches the store's customer service counter, shows the receipt to the clerk, and explains the issue. She politely asks if they have the exact same green shirt in stock, but in a size bigger than the one she brought back. |
| 8 | 08_the_fitting_room | The clerk kindly brings her the larger size, and this time Maya goes straight to the fitting rooms to be entirely sure. She tries it on and checks the mirror; this one is noticeably bigger than the first shirt and fits her comfortably. |
| 9 | 09_successful_exchange | Maya completes the exchange at the counter without any hassle and thanks the helpful clerk. She walks back out into the steady midday rain, feeling relieved and fully prepared for the rest of the monsoon. |
