# Week 1 — Storyboard (Maya arrives in Berlin)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** **sein** (ich bin / Sie sind), **heißen**, **kommen aus**; **es gibt**; subject pronouns **ich/Sie/er/sie**; **wie/wo/was** questions and yes-no questions with the verb first (*Sind Sie …?*)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_airport_arrival | Maya pulls her suitcase through the sliding doors of the airport into the dark January evening. She asks a passing airport worker where the taxi rank is. |
| 2 | 02_the_taxi_ride | Maya climbs into the back of a warm taxi and gives the driver the address of her new building. The driver asks where she is from and what she does, so she introduces herself politely, explaining that she is a software engineer from Mexico. |
| 3 | 03_finding_the_building | The taxi drops Maya on a quiet street in front of an old building. She reads the names on the bell panel by the front door, finds the right one, and works out which staircase and which floor she needs. |
| 4 | 04_meeting_frau_krueger | Hauling her suitcase up the stairs, Maya meets a brisk, friendly woman coming down with a small dog on a lead. The woman introduces herself as her neighbour **Sabine Krüger** and the dog as **Bruno**. Sabine asks whether Maya is the new tenant and where she is from. |
| 5 | 05_entering_the_flat | Maya turns the key to her temporary room, flicks on the light, and drops the suitcase and her backpack with relief. She looks around at what is there: a bed, a table, a chair, a lamp, and a tall window. |
| 6 | 06_the_window_view | Maya walks to the window and presses her hand against the cold glass, looking out over the unfamiliar, icy city. The winter darkness hits her, and she feels a sudden, sharp pang of homesickness for the warmth of Mexico. |
| 7 | 07_calling_home | Needing a familiar voice, Maya sits on the edge of the bed, takes out her phone, and makes a quick audio call to her parents. She cheerfully reassures them that she is safe, the room is warm, and she is doing fine. |
| 8 | 08_settling_in | Maya unzips her suitcase and unpacks what she needs for the night. She puts her laptop on the small table, hangs up the jacket she travelled in, and sets an alarm for the morning. |
