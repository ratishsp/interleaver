Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.

"Muito obrigada pela ajuda, Maya", diz ela."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
"Não tem problema nenhum", respondo feliz."It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.

"E lembre-se, ele não pode comer comida de humano.""And remember, he must not have human food."

Ela fecha a porta, e eu fico sozinha com o Ludwig.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.

Eu já gosto muito dele.I am already very fond of him.

a morning together
