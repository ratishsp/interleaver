# Week 20 — Storyboard (Maya starts volunteering at the café)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Past + perfect mixed; *lære at*; ordinals

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_first_shift_arrival | On her very first Saturday shift, Maya arrived early at the little café. She tied her new apron in the back room and carried the heavy iron chairs out to the pavement to prepare for opening. |
| 2 | 02_learning_the_machine | Before the doors opened, an experienced volunteer brought Maya over to the large espresso machine. Maya watched the valves closely, knowing she needed to learn to steam the milk without making a mess. |
| 3 | 03_the_first_coffee | When the morning rush hit, Maya made a cappuccino for a waiting customer. She had steamed the milk a bit too long, so it lacked thick foam, but she handed her first coffee over with a polite smile. |
| 4 | 04_bun_with_cheese | A moment later, she sliced a warm sourdough bun and added a thick piece of cheese. She placed it on a small plate and navigated through the standing customers to deliver it safely to the fourth table. |
| 5 | 05_the_ginger_beer | During her second Saturday shift, a woman sitting at the fifth table asked for a ginger beer. Maya had not served one before, but she crouched down and dug through the glass fridge until she found a cold bottle. |
| 6 | 06_second_coffee_progress | Later that morning, Maya tried foaming milk again for a large latte. This second attempt turned out much better, and she felt proud that she had finally learned to control the pressure of the hot steam. |
| 7 | 07_third_saturday_regular | By her third Saturday, Maya spotted a familiar older man walking towards the front door. She had memorized his exact morning routine, so she poured his black coffee before he even reached the counter. |
| 8 | 08_ending_the_shift | After the busy morning shift ended, Maya scrubbed the wooden counter clean. She hung her apron on the hook in the staff room, said goodbye to the other volunteers, and stepped out into the bright street, happy with everything she has learned so far. |
