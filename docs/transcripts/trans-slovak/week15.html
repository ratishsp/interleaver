Week 15: Maya's everyday life in the city

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning weather
Storyboard: Maya wakes up early and looks out the window. The sun shines, but the wind is cold, so she puts on a warm sweater for the day.
Budím sa skoro.I wake up early.
Je len šesť hodín.It is only six o'clock.
Je nový deň.It is a new day.
Pomaly vstávam.I get up slowly.
Idem k oknu.I walk over to the window.
Pozerám sa von na mesto.I look out at the city.
Vonku je stále tma a chladno.It is still dark and cold outside.
Zima je stále tu.Winter is still here.
Idem do kúpeľne.I go into the bathroom.
Umývam si tvár.I wash my face.
Umývam si zuby.I brush my teeth.
Potom sa obliekam.Then I get dressed.
Obliekam si hrubý, teplý sveter.I put on a thick, warm sweater.
Je perfektný na chladný deň.It is perfect for a cold day.
Idem do kuchyne.I go into the kitchen.
Robím si šálku teplej kávy.I make a cup of warm coffee.
Jem krajec chleba so syrom.I eat a slice of bread with cheese.
Teraz som úplne prebudená.Now I am completely awake.
Som pripravená na nový deň.I am ready for the new day.
the bus commute
Storyboard: She walks to the bus stop and waits for her daily transport. The yellow bus arrives, she steps inside, scans her travel card, and rides through the busy streets.
Vychádzam na ulicu.I walk out onto the street.
Je to bežné ráno.It is a normal morning.
Idem dole k zastávke.I walk down to the bus stop.
Stojí tam veľa ľudí.Many people are standing there.
Tiež čakajú na autobus.They are also waiting for the bus.
Pozriem sa dole cestou.I look down the road.
Prichádza veľký červený autobus.A big red bus arrives.
Je to autobus číslo päť.It is bus number five.
Autobus predo mnou zastaví.The bus stops in front of me.
Dvere sa otvoria.The doors open.
Nastúpim do autobusu.I step inside the bus.
V taške si nájdem cestovnú kartu.I find my travel card in my bag.
Naskenujem modrú kartu na prístroji.I scan the blue card on the machine.
Prístroj pípne.The machine beeps.
Prejdem do zadnej časti autobusu.I walk to the back of the bus.
Nájdem si voľné miesto pri okne.I find an empty seat by the window.
Sadnem si.I sit down.
Pozerám sa na ľudí na ulici.I look at the people in the street.
Prechádzame rušnými ulicami.We drive through the busy streets.
Som pripravená na nový pracovný deň.I am ready for a new workday.
working with peter
Storyboard: Maya arrives at the software company and says good morning to her friendly colleague Peter. She sits at her desk, drinks black coffee, and types code on her computer.
Prichádzam do kancelárie.I arrive at the office.
Je to veľká softvérová firma.It is a large software company.
Vchádzam dnu.I walk inside.
Môj kolega Peter je už tam.My colleague Peter is already there.
Je veľmi priateľský.He is very friendly.
„Dobré ráno, Maya,“ hovorí Peter."Good morning, Maya," Peter says.
„Dobré ráno, Peter,“ hovorím."Good morning, Peter," I say.
Idem na svoje miesto.I walk over to my space.
Sedím za svojím stolom.I sit at my desk.
Zapínam svoj počítač.I turn on my computer.
Mám šálku kávy.I have a cup of coffee.
Je čierna a horúca.It is black and hot.
Pijem kávu.I drink the coffee.
Pracujem ako softvérová inžinierka.I work as a software engineer.
Pozerám sa na obrazovku.I look at the screen.
Píšem kód na svojom počítači.I type code on my computer.
Práca je veľmi zaujímavá.The work is very exciting.
Som spokojná so svojou prácou.I am happy with my job.
canteen lunch
Storyboard: At noon, Maya and Peter go down to the bright canteen together. She chooses a classic open sandwich with fish and pays forty kroner at the register.
Je dvanásť hodín.It is twelve o'clock.
Je čas na obed.It is lunch time.
Peter a ja ideme dole do jedálne.Peter and I go down to the canteen.
Jedáleň je veľká a svetlá.The canteen is large and bright.
Sme veľmi hladní.We are very hungry.
Pozeráme sa na dobré jedlo.We look at the good food.
Dnes je veľa možností.There are many options today.
Vyberám si klasický obložený chlebíček.I choose a classic open sandwich.
Je s rybou a citrónom.It is with fish and lemon.
Peter si berie veľký šalát.Peter takes a large salad.
Ideme k pokladni.We walk over to the register.
Pri pokladni stojí milý muž.A friendly man stands at the register.
„Bude to štyridsať korún,“ hovorí mi."That will be forty kroner," he says to me.
Platím kartou.I pay with my card.
„Nech sa páči,“ hovorím."Here you go," I say.
„Ďakujem a dobrú chuť,“ odpovedá."Thank you, and enjoy your meal," he answers.
Nachádzame voľný stôl v rohu.We find a free table in the corner.
Sadáme si.We sit down.
Jedlo chutí naozaj dobre.The food tastes really good.
Rozprávame sa o našom dni.We talk about our day.
giving directions
Storyboard: After work, Maya steps out into a large square. A tourist asks for the way to a local museum, and she politely explains that they must walk straight ahead and then turn left.
Skončila som v práci.I am off from work.
Vychádzam na veľké námestie.I walk out onto a large square.
V meste je dnes veľa ľudí.There are many people in the city today.
Pristúpi ku mne jeden muž.A man walks up to me.
Má veľký fotoaparát.He has a large camera.
Je to turista.He is a tourist.
Usmeje sa a povie ahoj.He smiles and says hi.
„Kde je múzeum?“ pýta sa turista."Where is the museum?" the tourist asks.
To múzeum dobre poznám.I know the museum well.
Nie je to ďaleko.It is not far away.
Ukážem dole ulicou.I point down the street.
„Pôjdete rovno,“ vysvetľujem."You walk straight ahead," I explain.
„Potom odbočíte doľava.“"Then you turn to the left."
„Múzeum je na pravej strane.“"The museum is on the right side."
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem za pomoc,“ hovorí muž."Thank you very much for the help," the man says.
„Niet za čo,“ odpovedám veselo."You are welcome," I answer happily.
Kráča dole ulicou.He walks down the street.
Kráčam ďalej smerom k pekárni.I walk on towards the bakery.
buying a cake
Storyboard: On the way home, she stops at a small bakery on the corner. She orders a chocolate cake for the evening and pays sixty-five kroner to the smiling baker.
Prichádzam k malej pekárni na rohu.I arrive at the small bakery on the corner.
Vchádzam do teplého obchodu.I go into the warm shop.
Krásne to tu vonia po káve a koláčoch.It smells lovely of coffee and cake.
Za pultom stojí pekár v bielej zástere.A baker in a white apron stands behind the counter.
„Dobré popoludnie,“ hovorí pekár."Good afternoon," the baker says.
„Ako vám môžem pomôcť?“ pýta sa."How can I help?" he asks.
Pozerám sa na množstvo koláčov.I look at the many cakes.
Kupujem koláč na dnešný večer.I am buying a cake for tonight.
Ukazujem na pekný čokoládový koláč.I point at a nice chocolate cake.
„Prosila by som si ten čokoládový koláč,“ hovorím."I would like the chocolate cake, please," I say.
„Samozrejme,“ odpovedá pekár."Of course," the baker answers.
Vkladá koláč do bielej škatule.He puts the cake in a white box.
„Bude to šesťdesiatpäť korún,“ hovorí."That will be sixty-five kroner," he says.
Vyberám peňaženku z tašky.I take my wallet out of my bag.
Platím šesťdesiatpäť korún.I pay the sixty-five kroner.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem a prajem pekný večer,“ hovorí pekár."Thank you very much, and have a good evening," the baker says.
„Ďakujem, aj vám,“ odpovedám."Thank you, you too," I answer.
Beriem si bielu škatuľu.I take the white box.
Opatrne vychádzam z obchodu s koláčom.I walk carefully out of the shop with the cake.
arriving home
Storyboard: Maya takes the bus back to her familiar street. She unlocks the front door of her building and feels happy and completely at home in her new neighborhood.
Stojím na zastávke autobusu so svojím koláčom.I stand at the bus stop with my cake.
Červený autobus rýchlo prichádza.The red bus comes quickly.
Nastupujem a nachádzam si miesto.I get on and find a seat.
Autobus prechádza mestom.The bus drives through the city.
Cesta nie je dlhá.The trip is not long.
Čoskoro sme na mojej ulici.Soon we are at my street.
Vystupujem z autobusu.I get off the bus.
Večerný vzduch je teraz trochu chladný.The evening air is a little cold now.
Ale mám na sebe svoj teplý sveter.But I have my warm sweater on.
Kráčam po ulici.I walk down the street.
Budovy už teraz dobre poznám.I know the buildings well now.
Tu je moja budova.Here is my building.
Vo svojej taške nachádzam kľúče.I find my keys in my bag.
Odomykám ťažké vchodové dvere.I unlock the heavy front door.
Vchádzam na schodisko.I walk into the stairwell.
Je tu ticho a pokoj.It is quiet and peaceful here.
Odomykám svoje vlastné dvere a vchádzam dnu.I unlock my own door and go in.
Odkladám si tašku do predsiene.I put my bag in the hallway.
Teraz som doma vo svojom vlastnom meste.Now I am home in my own city.
evening with nina

Ukážem jej čokoládový koláč.I show her the chocolate cake.
„Ahoj, Nina,“ poviem."Hi Nina," I say.
„Mám koláč z pekárne.“"I have a cake from the bakery."
„Ahoj, Maya, to je skvelé!“ povie Nina."Hi Maya, how lovely!" Nina says.
Ideme do jej obývačky.We go into her living room.
Obývačka je veľmi teplá a útulná.The living room is very warm and cozy.
Sedíme na mäkkej pohovke.We sit on the soft sofa.
Nina nám uvarí horúci čaj.Nina makes hot tea for us.
Pijeme čaj a jeme ten sladký koláč.We drink tea and eat the sweet cake.
Čokoládový koláč chutí naozaj dobre.The chocolate cake tastes really good.
Rozprávame sa o našom dni v meste.We talk about our day in the city.
Nina sa pýta na moju prácu.Nina asks about my work.
Rozprávam o kancelárii a o svojom kolegovi Petrovi.I talk about the office and my colleague Peter.
Nina tiež rozpráva o svojom dni.Nina also talks about her day.
Smejeme sa a rozprávame sa celý večer.We laugh and talk all evening.

Cítim sa tu v mojom novom meste ako doma.I feel at home here in my new city.
