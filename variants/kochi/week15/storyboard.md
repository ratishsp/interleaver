# Week 15 — Storyboard (A Day in the Life)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Mixed present-tense narration; consolidation

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_routine | Maya wakes up early as the warm sunlight fills her bedroom. She prepares a quick breakfast, drinks her morning coffee, and packs her laptop bag for the office. |
| 2 | 02_commuting_to_infopark | Maya walks to the nearby bus stop and boards the bus to Infopark. She pays the conductor for her ticket, finds a seat by the window, and watches the busy city traffic pass by. |
| 3 | 03_working_with_prakash | Inside the air-conditioned software office, Maya sits at her desk typing on her computer. Her colleague Prakash walks over, and they look at the screen together to discuss a new software project. |
| 4 | 04_office_lunch_break | At lunchtime, Maya and Prakash go to the office cafeteria. They sit at their usual table, eat their meals, and chat easily — Prakash jokes about the office coffee, and Maya tells him about last Thursday's badminton game. |
| 5 | 05_the_afternoon_return | When the workday finishes, Maya boards a bus back towards the city center. She steps off at a busy junction near her neighbourhood, ready to run a few errands. |
| 6 | 06_helping_a_tourist | As Maya walks away from the bus stop, a tourist approaches her and asks for directions to the nearest metro station. Maya politely points down the road and explains the correct route. |
| 7 | 07_buying_fresh_vegetables | Maya continues down the street and stops at a small local shop. She greets the vendor, asks for fresh tomatoes and onions, pays in rupees, and carefully puts the items into her bag. |
| 8 | 08_arriving_at_the_flat | Maya reaches her apartment building and walks up the stairs to her floor. She unlocks her door, walks into her quiet flat, and sets down her laptop bag and the vegetables she picked up on the way. |
| 9 | 09_tea_with_anitha | In the evening, Maya knocks on the door of her neighbour, Anitha. Anitha warmly invites her inside, and the two friends sit together drinking tea and happily discussing their day. |
