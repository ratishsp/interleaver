# Week 12 — Storyboard (Maya joins a badminton club)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Verb + infinitive; *godt kan lide at*; *spille/dyrke*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_online_trial_booking | Maya sits in her apartment and looks at her laptop. She really likes to play badminton and wants to find a local club. She visits a community sports website and writes a short message to arrange a trial session. |
| 2 | 02_sports_hall_arrival | Maya takes the bus to a large community sports hall. She walks straight into the building and listens to the loud echoes. She hears people shouting and hitting shuttlecocks across the busy wooden courts. |
| 3 | 03_borrowing_a_racket | Maya finds court number three and greets Kasper, the club organizer. She explains that she does not own a racket yet. Kasper smiles, opens a large canvas bag, and lets her borrow a spare club racket for the evening. |
| 4 | 04_warming_up | Maya walks over to the net and introduces herself to Sara, another club member. They begin to stretch their arms and legs to warm up. Sara asks if Maya prefers to play singles or doubles, and they agree to play a quick match together. |
| 5 | 05_the_badminton_match | Maya steps onto the floor and begins to play. She has to run very fast from side to side to hit the small shuttlecock. She swings the racket hard and laughs out loud when she completely misses a high shot. |
| 6 | 06_water_break_chat | The players finish the game and sit on a wooden bench to drink water. Maya chats with Kasper and Sara, explaining that she works as a software engineer. She tells them that she really likes to live in the city and meet new people. |
| 7 | 07_planning_to_return | Kasper asks Maya if she wants to join the club officially. She nods enthusiastically and promises to return next week to play again. She packs her sweaty sports clothes into her bag and waves goodbye to her new acquaintances. |
| 8 | 08_waiting_for_the_bus | Maya walks out into the dark, freezing evening to catch the bus. She stands under the street lamp and rubs her tired shoulders. Her muscles ache, but she feels proud to participate in a real Danish sports community. |
