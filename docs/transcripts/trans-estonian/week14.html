Week 14: Maya takes public transport across the city

Estonian with English gloss, line-aligned.

checking the route
Storyboard: Maya looks at a travel app on her phone. She needs to go across the city to pick up a package, so she plans a route with three different types of public transport.
Ma pean täna pakile järele minema.I need to pick up a package today.
See on teisel pool linna.It is on the other side of the city.
Ma vaatan oma telefoni.I look at my phone.
Ma avan ühistranspordi äpi.I open a public transport app.
Ma planeerin oma marsruuti.I plan my route.
Marsruut on pikk.The route is long.
Sellel on kolm osa.It has three parts.
Kõigepealt pean ma minema bussiga.First, I need to take a bus.
Buss sõidab metrooni.The bus drives to the metro.
Siis pean ma sõitma metrooga.Then I need to ride the metro.
Lõpuks pean ma minema sadamasse.Finally, I need to go to the harbour.
Siin pean ma sõitma sadamabussiga.Here, I need to take the harbour bus.
Sadamabuss on suur paat.The harbour bus is a large boat.
See sõidab vee peal.It sails on the water.
Pakk on väikeses poes.The package is in a small shop.
Pood asub vee lähedal.The shop is located close to the water.
See on hea plaan.It is a good plan.
Ma võtan oma sinise sõidukaardi.I take my blue travel card.
Ma olen sõiduks valmis.I am ready for the trip.
topping up the card
Storyboard: She walks to a nearby ticket machine. She taps her blue travel card on the screen and pays two hundred kroner to top up her balance for the long trip.
Ma kõnnin tänaval piletiautomaadi juurde.I walk over to a ticket machine on the street.
Kõigepealt on mu sõidukaardile rohkem raha vaja.First my travel card needs more money.
Ma võtan oma sõidukaardi kotist välja.I take my travel card out of my bag.
See on väike sinine kaart.It is a small, blue card.
Ma asetan kaardi automaadi ekraanile.I place the card on the machine's screen.
Ekraan näitab mu saldot.The screen shows my balance.
Saldo on pika sõidu jaoks liiga väike.The balance is too low for a long trip.
Ma vajutan ekraanile.I tap on the screen.
Ma valin summa.I choose an amount.
Ma maksan oma maksekaardiga kakssada krooni.I pay two hundred kroner with my payment card.
Automaat kirjutab ekraanile "Kinnitatud".The machine writes "Approved" on the screen.
Nüüd on mul kaardil raha.Now I have money on the card.
Ma panen kaardi kotti tagasi.I put the card back in the bag.
Ma olen pikaks sõiduks valmis.I am ready for the long trip.
Nüüd pean ma bussi peale minema.Now I need to take the bus.
Ma kõnnin bussipeatuse juurde.I walk over to the bus stop.
taking the bus
Storyboard: Maya waits at the bus stop until a long yellow bus arrives. She steps inside, scans her card at the door, and finds an empty seat by the window.
Ma seisan bussipeatuses.I stand at the bus stop.
Varsti sõidan ma bussiga.Soon I travel by bus.
Ma vaatan pikki tänavat.I look down the street.
Viie minuti pärast tuleb buss.After five minutes, the bus arrives.
See on pikk ja kollane buss.It is a long and yellow bus.
Buss peatub otse minu ees.The bus stops right in front of me.
Uksed avanevad.The doors open.
Ma astun bussi.I step up into the bus.
Mul on sõidukaart käes.I have my travel card in my hand.
Ma skannin kaarti ukse juures.I scan the card by the door.
Täna on palju reisijaid.There are many passengers today.
Ma otsin vaba kohta.I look for an empty seat.
Ma leian hea koha akna juures.I find a good seat by the window.
Ma istun maha.I sit down.
Ma vaatan välja linna peale.I look out at the city.
Nüüd sõidan ma bussiga metroojaama.Now I take the bus to the metro station.
asking a passenger
Storyboard: She turns to an older woman sitting nearby. Maya politely asks if she travels with this bus to the metro station, and the woman nods with a friendly smile.
Ma istun bussis.I am sitting in the bus.
Ma vaatan aknast välja.I look out the window.
Ma ei ole marsruudis päris kindel.I am not completely sure about the route.
Vanem naine istub minu lähedal.An older woman is sitting close to me.
Ma vaatan tema poole.I look over at her.
"Vabandage," ütlen ma viisakalt."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Kas see buss sõidab metroojaama?""Does this bus go to the metro station?"
Naine vaatab mind.The woman looks at me.
Ta noogutab sõbraliku naeratusega.She nods with a friendly smile.
"Jah, see sõidab metroojaama," vastab ta."Yes, it goes to the metro station," she replies.
"Kas sa lähed metrooga?" küsib naine."Are you taking the metro?" the woman asks.
"Jah, ma lähen metrooga," ütlen ma."Yes, I am taking the metro," I say.
"Ma sõidan tihti selle bussiga," ütleb naine."I often ride with this bus," the woman says.
"See on lihtne marsruut," lisab ta."It is an easy route," she adds.
"Suur aitäh abi eest," ütlen ma."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"See on minu esimene sõit sellel marsruudil.""It is my first trip on this route."
Naine noogutab uuesti.The woman nods again.
Ma lõõgastun oma istmel.I relax in my seat.
Me sõidame edasi läbi linna.We drive on through the city.
transferring to metro
Storyboard: The bus stops, and Maya gets off at a busy square. She walks down the wide stairs into a bright, underground metro station and taps her card again.
Buss peatub rahvarohkel väljakul.The bus stops at a busy square.
Ma väljun bussist.I get off the bus.
Ma jätan vanema prouaga hüvasti.I say goodbye to the older woman.
Nüüd lähen ma metroosse.Now I am going to the metro.
Ma kõnnin üle väljaku.I walk across the square.
Siin on palju inimesi.There are many people here.
Ma kõnnin laiadest treppidest alla.I walk down the wide stairs.
Jaam on maa all.The station is underground.
See on väga suur ja valgusküllane.It is very big and bright.
Ma pean uuesti sisse registreerima.I have to check in again.
Ma võtan oma sinise kaardi välja.I take out my blue card.
Ma skannin kaarti masinas.I scan the card on the machine.
Masin teeb valju piiksu.The machine makes a loud beep.
Nüüd saan ma metrooga sõita.Now I can ride with the metro.
Ma kõnnin alla perroonile.I walk down to the platform.
Ma lähen järgmise rongiga.I am taking the next train.
See sõidab otse sadama poole.It goes straight towards the harbour.
Rong tuleb kolme minuti pärast.The train arrives in three minutes.
riding the metro
Storyboard: Maya stands right at the front of the driverless train. She rides with the metro through the dark tunnels and watches the lights blur past as they speed forward.
Metroo tuleb ja uksed avanevad.The metro arrives, and the doors open.
Ma astun rongi.I go into the train.
Rong sõidab ilma juhita.The train drives without a driver.
Ma lähen päris etteotsa.I walk all the way to the front.
Ma seisan suure akna juures.I stand by the large window.
Ma sõidan metrooga läbi pimeduse.I ride with the metro through the dark.
Me oleme pikas tunnelis.We are in a long tunnel.
Me sõidame väga kiiresti.We drive very fast.
Valgus lendab aknast mööda.The light flies past the window.
See näeb fantastiline välja.It looks fantastic.
Väike poiss seisab mu kõrval.A little boy stands next to me.
Ta vaatab samuti välja pimedusse.He also looks out into the dark.
Ma sõidan tihti metrooga.I often take the metro.
See on kiire ja lihtne sõit.It is a fast and easy trip.
Nüüd läheneme minu jaamale.Now we are approaching my station.
Ma lähen sadamasse.I am going to the harbour.
Minu järgmine sõit on sadamabussiga.My next trip is with the harbour bus.
Ma ootan põnevusega vee nägemist.I am looking forward to seeing the water.
finding the pier
Storyboard: She takes the escalator up to the street level and walks toward the water. She finds a small wooden pier by the edge of the canal where the harbour bus stops.
Sõit metrooga on läbi.The trip with the metro is over.
Nüüd lähen ma üles tänavale.Now I am going up to the street.
Ma lähen eskalaatoriga.I take the escalator.
Päike paistab üleval tänaval.The sun is shining up on the street.
Nüüd lähen ma vee äärde.Now I am going to the water.
Ma sõidan sadamabussiga.I am going by the harbour bus.
Ma kõnnin mööda laia tänavat alla.I walk down a wide street.
Siis ma näen kanalit.Then I see the canal.
Vesi on väga sinine.The water is very blue.
Ma otsin oma peatust.I look for my stop.
Kus paat peatub?Where does the boat stop?
Ma kõnnin äärele lähemale.I walk closer to the edge.
Siis ma näen väikest puidust paadisilda.Then I see a small wooden pier.
See asub kohe all vee ääres.It is located right down by the water.
Paadisilla juures on kollane silt.There is a yellow sign by the pier.
Sildil on kirjas "Sadamabuss".It says "Harbour bus" on the sign.
See on õige koht.This is the right place.
Ma kõnnin paadisillale.I walk out onto the pier.
Nüüd ma lihtsalt ootan paati.Now I am just waiting for the boat.
the harbour bus
Storyboard: A wide boat arrives, and Maya boards the harbour bus. She stands on the open deck and enjoys the fresh air as she sails across the blue water.
Lai paat tuleb üle vee.A wide boat comes across the water.
See on sadamabuss.It is the harbour bus.
Paat sõidab puidust kai juurde.The boat sails in to the wooden pier.
See peatub servas.It stops at the edge.
Ma lähen nüüd sadamabussiga.I am going with the harbour bus now.
Ma astun laeva pardale.I step on board the ship.
Ma skannin uuesti oma sinist sõidukaarti.I scan my blue travel card again.
Sees istub palju reisijaid.Many passengers sit inside.
Aga ma lähen välja avatud tekile.But I walk out onto the open deck.
Ma seisan päris ees.I stand at the very front.
Me sõidame paadiga üle sinise vee.We sail with the boat across the blue water.
Linn näeb vee pealt ilus välja.The city looks beautiful from the water.
Päike paistab meie peale.The sun shines down on us.
Ma hingan sügavalt sisse.I take a deep breath.
Õhk on väga värske ja külm.The air is very fresh and cold.
Ma naudin lühikest sõitu.I enjoy the short trip.
Me oleme varsti teisel pool.We are soon over at the other side.
arriving for the errand
Storyboard: The boat docks at the opposite shore. Maya steps off the vessel and walks toward a small shop on the corner to finally collect her package.
Paat jõuab teisele poole vett.The boat arrives at the other side of the water.
Sadamabuss peatub väikese kai juures.The harbour bus stops at a small pier.
Ma lähen paadist maha.I get off the boat.
Paadisõit on nüüd läbi.The trip by boat is now over.
Ma vaatan oma telefonist kaarti.I check the map on my phone.
Ma lähen väikesesse kioskisse.I am going to a small kiosk.
Kiosk asub seal nurga peal.The kiosk is located over on a corner.
Ma kõnnin mööda tänavat üles.I walk up the street.
Ma näen poodi tee lõpus.I see the shop at the end of the road.
Ma avan ukse ja astun sisse.I open the door and walk in.
Üks noor mees seisab kassa taga.A young man stands behind the register.
"Tere," ütleb ta lahkelt."Hi," he says kindly.
"Tere, ma tulin pakile järele," ütlen ma."Hi, I am going to pick up a package," I say.
Mees noogutab ja naeratab.The man nods and smiles.
Ta küsib minu nime.He asks for my name.
"Minu nimi on Maya," vastan ma."My name is Maya," I answer.
Ta leiab riiulilt suure kasti.He finds a large box on a shelf.
Ta annab paki mulle.He gives the package to me.
"Suur tänu abi eest," ütlen ma."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
Nüüd võtan ma oma pakiga uuesti ette pika teekonna koju.Now I take the long trip home again with my package.
