# Week 17 — Storyboard (Maya takes a day trip to Lonavala)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Past-tense narration; **स्वतःचा** vs **त्याचा/तिचा** (one's own vs another's); time expressions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_a_hot_mumbai_morning | Maya woke up early yesterday morning to the intense, heavy heat of the city. She packed her own backpack with a water bottle and an umbrella, determined to escape to the hills for a day trip. |
| 2 | 02_boarding_the_intercity_train | Maya arrived at the busy railway station. She found her platform, boarded the outstation train bound for Lonavala, and settled comfortably into her reserved window seat before departure. |
| 3 | 03_train_people_watching | As the journey began, Maya observed the passengers around her. A man frantically searched his own pockets for his ticket, while a young girl across the aisle tried to grab her older brother's snack. |
| 4 | 04_climbing_the_ghats | The scenery shifted dramatically as the train began its steep ascent into the ghats. Maya looked out of her window at the dry, sun-scorched brown valleys and rocky hillsides characteristic of the peak summer season. |
| 5 | 05_arriving_in_the_hills | Stepping off the train in Lonavala, Maya felt an immediate, refreshing drop in temperature. She walked up to a platform vendor, called him 'bhaiya', and bought a packet of the town's famous peanut chikki. |
| 6 | 06_the_slow_walk_up | Leaving the bustling town center, Maya took a leisurely afternoon walk. She strolled slowly up the winding roads toward a well-known viewpoint, enjoying the quiet contrast to her busy Mumbai routine. |
| 7 | 07_the_mischievous_monkey | Along the scenic path, Maya paused to watch a sudden commotion. A mischievous monkey snatched a distracted tourist's hat, prompting Maya to securely grip her own belongings. |
| 8 | 08_the_valley_viewpoint | Reaching the top of the hill, Maya stood at the viewpoint looking down into the deep, expansive valleys. She took out her own camera to photograph the dramatic landscape, completely losing track of time. |
| 9 | 09_a_tired_but_happy_return | As evening fell, Maya boarded the train ride back down the mountains. She returned to her quiet apartment late at night, her legs tired from the walking but her mind completely refreshed. |
