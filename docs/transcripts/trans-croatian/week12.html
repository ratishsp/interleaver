Week 12: Maya joins a badminton club

Croatian with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding a club
Storyboard: Maya works out at the gym on Wednesdays but wants to meet people, so she looks for a social sport. She loves badminton — she played it a lot back home in Mexico — so she finds a local club online and books a trial session for Thursday evening.
Svake srijede navečer idem u teretanu.I go to the gym every Wednesday evening.
Volim vježbati.I like to work out.
Ali želim se baviti i nekim sportom.But I also want to play a sport.
Obožavam igrati badminton.I love to play badminton.
Puno sam ga igrala kod kuće u Meksiku.I played it a lot back home in Mexico.
Sada tražim lokalni klub.Now I am looking for a local club.
Sjedim na kauču sa svojim računalom.I am sitting on my couch with my computer.
Pretražujem internet.I search online.
Nalazim dobar klub za badminton.I find a good badminton club.
Gledam web stranicu kluba.I look at the club's website.
Imaju puno ekipa.They have many teams.
Igraju u četvrtak navečer.They play on Thursday evening.
Želim rezervirati probni trening.I want to book a trial session.
Nalazim obrazac na web stranici.I find a form on the website.
Upisujem svoje ime i svoj e-mail.I write my name and my email.
Odabirem četvrtak navečer.I choose Thursday evening.
Trening počinje u sedam navečer.The training starts at seven in the evening.
To je savršeno.It is perfect.
Veselim se što ću ponovno igrati.I look forward to playing again.
arriving at hall
Storyboard: On Thursday evening Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sound of shoes squeaking on the many wooden courts.
Četvrtak je navečer i kod kuće sam.It is Thursday evening, and I am at home.
Pakiram svoju sportsku torbu.I pack my sports bag.
Stavljam tenisice i odjeću u torbu.I put my shoes and my clothes in the bag.
Veselim se što ću večeras igrati.I look forward to playing tonight.
Idem autobusom do velike sportske dvorane.I take the bus to a large sports hall.
Hladna je zimska večer.It is a cold winter evening.
Sjedim u autobusu i gledam van.I sit in the bus and look out.
Autobus staje i ja izlazim.The bus stops, and I get out.
Ulazim u veliku sportsku dvoranu.I walk into the large sports hall.
Jako je svijetla.It is very bright.
Vidim mnogo ljudi u dvorani.I see many people in the hall.
Vole se baviti sportom.They love to play sports.
Čujem glasan zvuk.I hear a loud sound.
Tenisice škripe na mnogim drvenim terenima.The shoes squeak on the many wooden courts.
Ljudi brzo trče i igraju zajedno.People run fast and play together.
Izgleda jako zabavno.It looks very fun.
Spremna sam igrati.I am ready to play.
Ali prvo moram unajmiti reket.But first I need to rent a racket.
renting the racket
Storyboard: Maya goes to the small hall kiosk. She tells the clerk she is there to try out the club, and pays forty kroner to rent a racket for the evening.
Prilazim malom kiosku u sportskoj dvorani.I walk over to a small kiosk in the sports hall.
Mladić stoji iza pulta.A young man stands behind the counter.
Smiješi se i kaže bok.He smiles and says hello.
"Bok, danas želim igrati badminton", kažem."Hello, I want to play badminton today," I say.
"Jesi li nova ovdje u klubu?", pita on."Are you new here in the club?" he asks.
"Da, željela bih se probati pridružiti", odgovaram."Yes, I would like to try to join in," I answer.
"Dobrodošla", kaže muškarac."Welcome," the man says.
"Trebaš li unajmiti reket?", pita on."Do you need to rent a racket?" he asks.
"Da, molim, želim unajmiti reket za večeras", kažem."Yes please, I want to rent a racket for the evening," I say.
On mi pronalazi dobar reket.He finds a good racket for me.
"To košta četrdeset kruna", kaže on."It costs forty kroner," he says.
Vadim svoju karticu za plaćanje.I take out my payment card.
Plaćam tih četrdeset kruna.I pay the forty kroner.
Daje mi reket preko pulta.He gives me the racket over the counter.
"Mislim da je to zabavan sport", kažem veselo."I think it is a fun sport," I say happily.
"Želim ti jako dobru igru.""Have a really good game."
Kažem hvala i odlazim do terena.I say thank you and walk over to the courts.
playing doubles
Storyboard: In the busy hall, Maya joins a doubles game with two friendly players, Kasper and Sara, and a fourth person. She has played for years, so she smashes the shuttlecock hard across the net.
Prilazim terenu za badminton.I walk over to a badminton court.
Na terenu stoje tri osobe.Three people are standing on the court.
"Bok, ja sam Kasper", kaže jedan muškarac."Hi, I am Kasper," a man says.
"A ja sam Sara", kaže jedna žena."And I am Sara," a woman says.
Treći igrač zove se Emil.The third player is named Emil.
"Ja sam Maya", kažem."I am Maya," I say.
"Obično igramo u parovima", kaže Kasper."We usually play doubles," Kasper says.
"Želiš li igrati s nama?", pita on."Do you want to play with us?" he asks.
"Da, vrlo rado", odgovaram."Yes, I would love to," I reply.
Jako volim igrati badminton.I really like to play badminton.
Kasper i ja igramo protiv Sare i Emila.Kasper and I play against Sara and Emil.
Odmah počinjemo igrati.We start to play immediately.
Sara započinje meč.Sara starts the match.
Loptica brzo leti naprijed-natrag.The shuttlecock flies quickly back and forth.
Emil udara lopticu visoko u zrak.Emil hits the shuttlecock high up in the air.
Obožavam smečirati.I love to smash.
Skačem i snažno je smečiram preko mreže.I jump and smash it hard over the net.
Pada na njihovu stranu terena.It lands on their side of the court.
"Dobar udarac, Maya!" viče Kasper."Good shot, Maya!" Kasper shouts.
post match chat
Storyboard: The fast match finishes and the tired players walk off the court. Sara says Maya plays really well, and Maya explains that she plays a lot back home in Mexico.
Brzi meč je gotov.The fast match is over.
Zajedno silazimo s terena.We walk off the court together.
Jako sam umorna.I am very tired.
Kasper i Sara su također umorni.Kasper and Sara are also tired.
Pijemo malo vode.We drink some water.
"Jako dobro igraš", kaže Sara."You play very well," Sara says.
"Puno hvala", kažem."Thank you very much," I say.
"Mislim da je to jako zabavno.""I think it is so much fun."
"Igraš li često?" pita Kasper."Do you play often?" Kasper asks.
"Da, puno igram kod kuće u Meksiku", odgovaram."Yes, I play a lot back home in Mexico," I answer.
"Obično igram sa svojom sestrom.""I usually play with my sister."
"To je zabavan sport", kaže Sara."It is a fun sport," Sara says.
"Volimo igrati ovdje u klubu.""We love to play here in the club."
"Želim više igrati ovdje u Danskoj", kažem."I want to play more here in Denmark," I say.
"Definitivno si brza", smije se Kasper."You are definitely fast," Kasper laughs.
"Hvala, ali sada želim sjesti", kažem."Thanks, but now I want to sit down," I say.
"Hoćemo li ući u kafeteriju?" pita Kasper."Shall we go into the cafeteria?" Kasper asks.
"Da, to bih jako voljela", odgovaram."Yes, I would very much like that," I answer.
hall cafeteria
Storyboard: The group sits in the small hall cafeteria to drink water and chat. Kasper asks if Maya plans to come back, and she happily promises to make it a weekly Thursday routine.
Ulazimo u malu kafeteriju u dvorani.We walk into the small cafeteria in the hall.
Pronalazimo stol.We find a table.
Jako sam žedna.I am very thirsty.
Pijemo hladnu vodu i razgovaramo.We drink cold water and talk.
"Ovo je stvarno dobar klub", kažem."This is a really good club," I say.
"To je dobar sport za igranje", kaže Kasper."It is a good sport to do," Kasper says.
Kasper me gleda.Kasper looks at me.
"Želiš li nam se opet pridružiti?" pita on."Do you want to join again?" he asks.
"Da, vrlo rado", odgovaram."Yes, very much," I answer.
"Želim igrati ovdje svaki četvrtak", objašnjavam."I want to play here every Thursday," I explain.
"To zvuči divno", kaže Sara."That sounds lovely," Sara says.
"Mi uvijek igramo ovdje četvrtkom", kaže Kasper."We always play here on Thursdays," Kasper says.
"Onda se vidimo sljedeći tjedan", kažem veselo."Then we'll see each other next week," I say happily.
Pijemo ostatak svoje vode.We drink the rest of our water.
Lijepo je upoznati nove ljude.It is lovely to meet new people.
walking to bus
Storyboard: Maya leaves the sports hall with Sara. They walk together to the bus stop in the cold winter evening, and Maya feels glad to have met fun, active locals.
Sara i ja izlazimo iz sportske dvorane.Sara and I walk out of the sports hall.
Kasno je i hladna je zimska večer.It is late, and it is a cold winter evening.
Ali još uvijek mi je toplo nakon meča.But I am still warm after the match.
Zajedno hodamo do autobusne stanice.We walk over to the bus stop together.
Vjetar puše i ulica je mračna.The wind is blowing, and the street is dark.
"Voliš li i ti igrati badminton vikendom?" pitam."Do you also like to play badminton at the weekend?" I ask.
"Ne, igram badminton samo četvrtkom," odgovara Sara."No, I only play badminton on Thursdays," Sara answers.
"Ali volim se baviti sportom," kaže ona."But I like to do sports," she says.
"Obično trčim u parku," nastavlja ona."I usually go for a run in the park," she continues.
Ona je vrlo aktivna i zabavna osoba.She is a very active and fun person.
Čekamo autobus na hladnoći.We wait for the bus in the cold.
"Moj autobus dolazi za deset minuta," kažem."My bus comes in ten minutes," I say.
"Idemo istim autobusom," nasmiješi se Sara."We take the same bus," Sara smiles.
Jako sam sretna zbog toga.I am very happy about that.
Volim razgovarati s njom.I like to talk with her.
Kasper i Sara su zabavni i dragi ljudi.Kasper and Sara are fun and nice people.
Sada imam dobru rutinu četvrtkom.Now I have a good routine on Thursdays.
Umorna sam, ali sam dobro raspoložena.I am tired, but I am in a good mood.
Autobus napokon stiže.The bus finally arrives.
Već se veselim sljedećem četvrtku.I am already looking forward to next Thursday.
