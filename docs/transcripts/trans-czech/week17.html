Week 17: Maya explores Odense in the light spring

spring breeze diary

Miluji psát o svých zážitcích v Dánsku.I love to write about my experiences in Denmark.
Otevřu knihu na prázdné stránce.I open to a blank page in the book.

Včera jsem byla na dlouhém výletě.I was on a long trip yesterday.
Cestovala jsem úplně sama do Odense.I travelled all alone to Odense.

sunday train trip

Pila jsem svou teplou kávu a těšila se na výlet.I drank my warm coffee and looked forward to the excursion.
long sea bridge
Storyboard: She describes the spectacular journey as the train crossed the very long bridge over the sea. She looked out the large window and admired the blue water and the small ships.

Seděla jsem tiše na svém místě.I sat quietly in my seat.
Měla jsem opravdu dobré místo u okna.I had a really good window seat.

stressed father train
Storyboard: She watched a stressed father sitting across the aisle. He desperately tried to read his book, but his two young children constantly threw their toys on the floor.

Měl s sebou své dvě malé děti.He brought his two small children.
Otec měl velkou knihu.The father had a big book.
Opravdu si chtěl číst svou knihu.He really wanted to read his book.

Jeho dcera měla červený míč.His daughter had a red ball.

Děti vůbec neseděly v klidu.The children did not sit still at all.
Neustále házely své hračky na zem.They constantly threw their toys on the floor.
Otec si hlasitě povzdechl a zavřel knihu.The father sighed loudly and closed his book.

Znovu otevřel svou knihu.He opened his book again.
O dvě minuty později spadl míč na zem.Two minutes later, the ball fell on the floor.

Muž ten míč znovu zvedl.The man picked up the ball again.
Usmála jsem se na unaveného otce.I smiled at the tired father.
Jen zavrtěl hlavou a podíval se dolů do své knihy.He just shook his head and looked down into his book.
naughty little dog

crooked colorful houses
