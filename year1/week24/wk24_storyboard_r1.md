# Week 24 — Storyboard (Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Imperatives + object pronouns *den/ham/hende*; *skal/må* (rules)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_ninas_instructions | On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig to Maya's apartment for the weekend. Nina explains the house rules, stating that Maya must feed him his dry food and that he definitely may not eat any human snacks. |
| 2 | 02_leash_rules | Before leaving, Nina hands over the leash and explains the outdoor rules. She says that Maya must keep him on the lead near the roads, but she may let him run free when they reach the large park. |
| 3 | 03_saturday_breakfast | Early Saturday morning, Ludwig nudges Maya awake. Maya prepares his breakfast, firmly telling him to sit and wait while she fills the bowl, before finally telling him to eat. |
| 4 | 04_cafe_shift_departure | Getting ready for her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the café, Maya settles Ludwig in his basket. She commands him to stay and be a good boy, knowing he must rest safely indoors while she is away. |
| 5 | 05_afternoon_park_walk | Saturday afternoon, Maya takes Ludwig to the local park and unclips the lead from him. She throws a stick across the grass, happily shouting at him to fetch it and bring it back to her. |
| 6 | 06_calling_ludwig_back | Suddenly, Ludwig spots a bird and wanders off toward the trees. Maya calls him loudly, commanding him to stop and leave it, but he completely ignores her. |
| 7 | 07_gentle_approach | Realizing that yelling does not work, Maya tries a gentler approach. She crouches down with a treat, softly calling him over; he quickly runs to her, and she tells him to sit before clipping the leash back on him. |
| 8 | 08_family_video_call | That evening, Maya video calls her family in Mexico to show them her furry guest. She points the camera at Ludwig, telling Sofía and Diego to look at him, noting that he is just as calm as their dog Lola. |
| 9 | 09_ninas_return | On Sunday evening, Nina returns from her trip and knocks on the door. Ludwig greets her joyfully, and Maya warmly tells Nina that she must definitely let her look after him again. |
