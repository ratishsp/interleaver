Week 11: Maya experiences the Danish winter weather

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

dark winter morning
Storyboard: Maya wakes up to the sound of her alarm. She looks towards the window, but it is completely pitch-black outside. It is midwinter in Denmark, and she realizes the mornings are incredibly dark.
我個鬧鐘響得好大聲。My alarm rings loudly.
而家係朝早，但仲係好暗。It is morning, but it is still dark.
我擘大眼。I open my eyes.
我望向隻窗。I look out toward the window.
出面完全黑晒。It is completely black outside.
太陽完全未出。The sun is not up at all.
丹麥仲係冬天。It is still winter in Denmark.
呢度朝早真係好暗。It is incredibly dark in the morning here.
天好遲先光。It becomes light very late.
我喺床上慢慢坐起身。I slowly sit up in bed.
張被出面好凍。It is cold outside the duvet.
我開咗枱面盞燈。I turn on the lamp on the table.
有少少光即刻舒服好多。It immediately becomes nice with a little light.
窗外個天仲係完全黑晒。Outside the window, the sky is still completely black.
喺墨西哥，而家通常都有好多陽光。In Mexico there is often a lot of sunlight now.
但係呢度，太陽仲瞓緊覺。But here the sun is sleeping.
喺黑暗中好難醒。It is hard to wake up in the dark.
我望吓個鐘。I look at the clock.
我落床。I get out of bed.
新嘅一日而家開始。The day begins now.
checking the forecast
Storyboard: Maya stands by the window and checks the weather app on her phone. The sky slowly turns gray. The app shows that it is cold now, and that it becomes very wet and windy later in the day.
我企喺客廳嘅窗邊。I stand by the window in the living room.
我望出去個城市。I look out at the city.
個天唔再係黑色。The sky is not black anymore.
慢慢變成灰色。It slowly becomes gray.
太陽好遲先升起。The sun rises late.
我拎起我部電話。I take my phone.
我睇今日嘅天氣預報。I read the weather forecast for today.
而家好凍。Right now it is very cold.
只有兩度。It is only two degrees.
但係出面仲係乾嘅。But it is still dry outside.
晏啲會變得好濕。Later it becomes very wet.
好快就會落雨。It will rain soon.
亦都會好大風。It also becomes windy.
今日晏啲會吹大風。It blows hard later in the day.
會變成一個真正嘅冬日。It becomes a real winter day.
我將部電話放喺枱面。I put the phone on the table.
我再望多次個天。I look at the sky one more time.
bundling up
Storyboard: Maya prepares to face the chilly morning. She pulls her thick sweater over her head and wraps a long winter scarf tightly around her neck. She zips her coat all the way up to block out the cold hallway draft.
今日好凍。It is cold today.
依家係冬天，朝早天色好暗。It is winter, and the morning is dark.
我企喺房入面準備緊。I am standing in my room getting ready.
我搵啲保暖嘅冬天衫出嚟。I find my warm winter clothes.
首先，我著一件厚冷衫。First, I put on a thick sweater.
我將佢笠過個頭。I pull it over my head.
件冷衫又暖又軟熟，好舒服。The sweater is nice and warm and soft.
我仲攞咗條長頸巾。I also take a long scarf.
我將佢緊緊噉圍住條頸。I wrap it tightly around my neck.
之後，我行出去走廊。After that, I walk out into my hallway.
樓梯間吹嚟一陣冷風。A cold draft comes from the stairwell.
我攞起我件厚重嘅冬天外套。I take my heavy winter jacket.
我將件外套條拉鍊拉到上最頂。I zip the jacket all the way up.
佢可以擋住啲冷空氣。It stops the cold air.
今日保暖好重要。It is important to stay warm today.
噉我就唔會喺咁凍嘅天氣度凍僵。Then I will not freeze in the cold.
我仲帶埋把遮。I also bring my umbrella with me.
我終於準備好去搭巴士。I am finally ready to take my bus.
我打開大門，行出去凍冰冰嘅外面。I open the front door and step out into the cold.
heavy rain
Storyboard: Maya steps out of her building to commute to work. It is raining heavily now. She opens her umbrella and navigates around large puddles on the sidewalk, listening to the rain splash against her boots as she walks to the bus stop.
我打開通往街外嘅門。I open the door to the street.
我行出去。I step outside.
落緊好大雨。It is raining a lot.
今日天氣好差。It is bad weather today.
今日嘅天氣唔會好轉。It is not getting better today.
我手拎住一把遮。I have an umbrella in my hand.
我打開把遮。I open the umbrella.
行人路上有好大嘅水氹。There are large puddles on the sidewalk.
我兜過嗰啲大水氹。I walk around the large puddles.
我著住我對冬天靴。I have my winter boots on.
啲雨水彈落對靴度。The rain splashes down onto the boots.
呢個朝早非常潮濕。It is a very wet morning.
我行過去巴士站。I walk over to the bus stop.
我通常都搭巴士返工。I usually take the bus to work.
喺出面落住雨好凍。It is cold out here in the rain.
好彩巴士入面會變返暖。Fortunately, it gets warm inside the bus.
strong wind
Storyboard: Maya gets off the bus near her software company. Suddenly, a hard wind blows through the street. It becomes impossible to hold the umbrella straight, so she wrestles to close it and hurries through the fierce gusts to the office entrance.
我喺公司附近落巴士。I get off the bus near my work.
我捉實條頸巾。I hold on to my scarf.
今日天氣好惡劣。The weather is very wild today.
我沿住條街行。I walk down the street.
突然間變得好大風。Suddenly it becomes very windy.
一陣強風迎面吹埋嚟。A strong wind blows against me.
我手拎住把開咗嘅遮。I have my opened umbrella in my hand.
但係啲風太勁喇。But the wind is too strong.
咁樣好快就變咗個問題。It quickly becomes a problem.
把遮畀風吹到左搖右擺。The umbrella flies from side to side.
依家根本冇可能撐得住。It is impossible now.
我同把遮喺度搏鬥緊。I struggle with it.
我最後都係收埋把遮。I finally close the umbrella.
冇咗把遮真係好凍。It is very cold without it.
啲雨打落我塊面度。The rain hits my face.
我頂住啲風行快兩步。I hurry through the wind.
寫字樓門口就喺前面。The office entrance is right ahead.
我行入大廈。I step into the building.
好彩入面又暖又靜。Fortunately, it is warm and quiet in here.
gray afternoon
Storyboard: Maya sits at her desk and writes code. She glances out the office window at the stormy weather. The sky is dark gray, and it rains steadily against the glass. It becomes completely gloomy, marking a typical winter afternoon.
我坐喺辦公室嘅辦公枱前。I sit at my desk in the office.
我用電腦寫程式。I write code on my computer.
我望出窗外。I look out the window.
今日天氣好差。It is very bad weather today.
天空又黑又沉。The sky is dark and heavy.
出面灰濛濛一片。It is completely gray outside.
啲雨不斷打落玻璃度。It rains constantly against the glass.
仲刮緊大風。It is also blowing a lot.
我望下牆上面個鐘。I look at the clock on the wall.
依家只係下晝頭。It is only early afternoon.
但係好快就變得好陰沉。But it becomes gloomy very quickly.
光線消失喺灰色嘅天空入面。The light disappears into the gray sky.
呢個季節天黑得好早。It becomes dark early in this season.
呢個係丹麥典型嘅冬天晏晝。It is a typical winter afternoon in Denmark.
出面大風好凍。It is very cold out there in the wind.
但係呢度入面就好舒服好暖。But it is nicely warm in here.
我飲咗幾啖杯入面嘅熱咖啡。I drink some hot coffee from my cup.
我聽咗一分鐘雨聲。I listen to the rain for a minute.
然後我再望向我個電腦螢幕。Then I look at my computer screen again.
我繼續寫我嘅程式。I continue with my code.
early dusk
Storyboard: Maya finishes her work and steps outside the office building. She is surprised to see that dusk is already falling and the streetlights are on. It becomes dark very early in this season, and she walks back to the bus in the fading light.
我今日做完嘢喇。I am finished with my work for today.
我執好我個袋。I pack my bag.
我喺辦公室同彼得講拜拜。I say goodbye to Peter at the office.
我行出辦公大樓。I walk out of the office building.
我喺街上停低，望住個天。I stop on the street and look at the sky.
我依家已經習慣咗。I am used to it now.
依家只係下晝。It is only afternoon.
但係天已經黑晒。But it is already completely dark.
周圍嘅街燈都著晒。The streetlights are on everywhere.

呢度同墨西哥好唔同。It is very different from Mexico.
風依然吹得好大。The wind is still blowing hard.
依家天氣好快就變凍。It quickly gets colder now.
我拉高我條厚頸巾。I pull my thick scarf up.
我喺微弱嘅光線下沿住條街行。I walk down the street in the fading light.

wet layers

我打開門鎖。I unlock the door.
我終於返到我嘅公寓。I am finally home in my apartment.
外面又濕又大風。It is wet and windy outside.
但係入面好暖。But it is warm in here.

我除低件外套。I take the jacket off.
我把遮都滴緊水。My umbrella also drips with water.
我行去洗手間。I walk out into the bathroom.
我將把遮擺喺地下。I put the umbrella on the floor.
啲水滴落去瓷磚度。The water drips down onto the tiles.
然後我行入客廳。Then I walk into the living room.
呢度入面完全黑晒。It is completely dark in here.
我幾乎咩都睇唔到。I can barely see anything.

steaming tea
