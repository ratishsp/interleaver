| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_monday_bus_ticket | On Monday morning, Maya boards the bus and accidentally drops her ticket. A kind passenger hands it back to her. She finds a window seat and reads through her handwritten notes, preparing for the big office project that begins today. |
| 2 | 02_morning_meeting_boss | The boss stands by the whiteboard in the bright meeting room. He outlines the new client project, explaining that he has led software teams for ten years and believes they can easily meet the tight deadline. |
