Week 6: Maya settles into a daily routine

morning routine
Storyboard: In the morning, Maya wakes up and gets out of bed. She steps into the bathroom, where she takes a warm shower and washes herself to wake up fully.
出面仲係好黑。It is still dark outside.
朝早我慢慢醒過來。In the morning, I wake up slowly.
我喺床上面攤一陣。I lie in bed for a bit.
今日係一個好普通嘅平日。Today is a completely ordinary weekday.
而家我起身落床。Now I get out of bed.
腳下嘅地板有啲凍。The floor is a little cold under my feet.
我直接行去浴室。I walk straight out to the bathroom.
喺浴室，我開著啲水。In the bathroom, I turn on the water.
啲水又暖又舒服。The water is warm and nice.
我行入企缸。I step into the shower cubicle.
喺度我沖一個好舒服嘅暖水涼。Here I take a lovely warm shower.
首先，我用洗頭水洗頭。First I wash my hair with shampoo.
之後，我用番梘洗身。Afterward I wash myself with soap.
嚿番梘有陣清新嘅柑橘香味。The soap smells fresh of citrus.
啲暖水令我個人醒晒。The warm water wakes me up.
最後，我閂咗個花灑。Finally, I turn off the shower.
我攞一條柔軟嘅毛巾。I take a soft towel.
而家我將個身徹底抹乾。Now I dry myself thoroughly.
我已經完全清醒，準備好開始呢一日。I am completely awake and ready for the day.
getting ready
Storyboard: Back in the bedroom, she chooses her clothes and gets dressed for work. In the kitchen, she pours a cup of coffee and eats a quick breakfast before leaving the flat.
喺睡房，我打開我個衣櫃。In the bedroom, I open my closet.
我望吓我啲衫。I look at my clothes.
我揀咗件藍色恤衫同黑色褲。I choose a blue shirt and black trousers.
然後我著衫。Then I get dressed.
我喺塊鏡前面梳頭。I comb my hair in front of the mirror.
依家我行出去廚房。Now I go out into the kitchen.
我肚餓。I am hungry.
我搵咗隻碟同隻杯。I find a plate and a cup.
我快手整份早餐。I make a quick breakfast.
今日我食麵包加芝士。Today I eat bread with cheese.
我倒咖啡落隻杯度。I pour coffee into the cup.
啲咖啡又黑又濃。The coffee is black and strong.
我坐喺張枱度。I sit down at the table.
我喺呢度食嘢。Here I eat my food.
好好味。It tastes good.
我飲我杯咖啡。I drink my coffee.
最後，我攞我個袋同件外套。Finally, I take my bag and my jacket.
我鎖門，然後行出去。I lock the door and go out.
the bus ride
Storyboard: Out on the street, Maya waits at the stop for her bus. She steps on board, finds a seat by the window, and watches the city pass by on her way to work.
喺街上面，我等緊我架巴士。Out on the street, I wait for my bus.
今日好凍。It is cold today.
好多人企喺巴士站度。Many people stand at the bus stop.
有架紅色巴士嚟到。A red bus arrives.
車門打開。The doors open.
我上巴士。I get on the bus.
我喺度嘟我張交通卡。Here I scan my travel card.
巴士司機望住我。The bus driver looks at me.
「早晨，」司機話。"Good morning," says the driver.
「早晨，」我答。"Good morning," I answer.
我向車廂後面行。I walk down through the bus.
我搵到一個近窗嘅空位。I find an empty seat by the window.
我坐低。I sit down.
巴士穿過城市。The bus drives through the city.
喺車窗外面，我見到好多人。Outside the window, I see many people.
佢哋喺單車徑踩得好快。They cycle fast on the bike lane.
我哋駛過一個好靚嘅公園。We drive past a beautiful park.
喺公園入面，啲人喺度散步。In the park, people take a walk.
依家巴士停喺我返工嘅地方。Now the bus stops at my workplace.
我落車，然後行入辦公室。I get off and walk into the office.
at the desk
Storyboard: At the software company, Maya sits down at her desk. She turns on her computer, reads her messages, and begins to write code for her project.
我返到公司。I arrive at my work.
我喺一間軟件公司做嘢。I work in a software company.
我行入去嗰個大房度。I walk into the large room.
我張寫字枱喺窗邊。My desk stands by the window.
喺呢度我坐落我張凳度。Here I sit down on my chair.
我開我部電腦。I turn on my computer.
個螢幕著咗。The screen lights up.
首先我睇下我啲訊息。First I read my messages.
我有封經理寄嚟嘅電郵。I have an email from my manager.
封電郵係關於我個新項目嘅。The email is about my new project.
我寫咗個簡短嘅回覆。I write a short reply.
跟住我打開做嘢用嘅程式。Next I open my work program.
而家我幫個項目寫程式碼。Now I write code for the project.
啲程式碼填滿晒成個螢幕。The code fills the whole screen.
我喺鍵盤度好快咁打字。I type quickly on my keyboard.
我好專注喺啲程式碼上面。I concentrate closely on the code.
呢份工作好有趣。The work is exciting.
我飲咗少少水樽入面嘅水。I drink a little water from my bottle.
我好開心可以喺哥本哈根做呢份新工。I am very happy with my new job in Copenhagen.
lunch with peter
Storyboard: At midday, Maya walks down to the bright canteen. She meets her friendly colleague Peter by the buffet, and they sit together to eat and chat about their work.
中午嘅時候，我行落去飯堂。In the middle of the day, I walk down to the canteen.
入面好光猛，感覺好舒服。The room is bright and nice.
好多人企喺自助餐區。Many people are standing by the buffet.
我見到我同事 Peter。I see my colleague Peter.
佢攞咗一碟嘢食。He takes a plate with food.
佢笑住咁話：「哈囉，Maya。」"Hello Maya," he says with a smile.
我回答：「哈囉，Peter。」"Hello Peter," I reply.
我哋攞咗啲麵包同沙律。We take some bread and salad.
我哋一齊搵到窗邊嘅九號枱。Together we find table number nine by the window.
我哋坐喺張凳度。We sit down on the chairs.
啲嘢食好好味。The food tastes really good.
Peter 講起佢嘅項目。Peter talks about his project.
佢做緊新嘅軟件。He works with new software.
我邊聽邊點頭。I listen and nod.
最後，我都講起我自己寫嘅程式碼。Finally I talk about my own code.
我哋飲咗杯水。We drink a glass of water.
呢個小休時間我過得好開心。I enjoy myself a lot during the break.
好快，我哋就行返去辦公室。Soon we walk back to the office.
finishing work

我望住個螢幕。I look at my screen.

出到走廊，我搭𨋢落樓。Out in the hallway, I take the elevator down.
最後，我行出大廈。Finally, I walk out of the building.

cooking dinner
