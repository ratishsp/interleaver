# Week 20 — Storyboard (Maya starts volunteering at the café)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Past + perfect mixed; *lære at*; ordinals

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_finding_the_volunteering | Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. One evening she browses a volunteering website, reads a few listings, and finds the little café she already knows, which needs weekend helpers, so she fills in the short sign-up form. |
| 2 | 02_first_shift_arrival | On her very first Saturday shift, Maya arrives early at the little café. She ties her new apron in the back room and carries the heavy iron chairs out to the pavement to prepare for opening. |
| 3 | 03_learning_the_machine | Before the doors open, an experienced volunteer brings Maya over to the large espresso machine. Maya watches the valves closely, knowing she must learn to steam the milk without making a mess. |
| 4 | 04_the_first_coffee | When the morning rush hits, Maya makes a cappuccino for a waiting customer. She has steamed the milk a bit too long, so it lacks thick foam, but it still looks acceptable, so she hands it over with a polite smile. |
| 5 | 05_bun_with_cheese | A moment later, she slices a warm sourdough bun and adds a thick piece of cheese. She places it on a small plate and navigates through the standing customers to deliver it safely to a corner booth. |
| 6 | 06_the_ginger_beer | During her second Saturday shift, a woman sitting at the fifth table asks for a ginger beer. Maya has not served one before, but she crouches down and digs through the bottom of the glass fridge until she finds a cold bottle. |
| 7 | 07_second_coffee_progress | Later that morning, Maya tries foaming milk again for a large latte. This second attempt turns out much better, and she realizes that she has finally learned to control the pressure of the hot steam. |
| 8 | 08_third_saturday_regular | By her third Saturday, Maya spots a familiar older man walking towards the front door. She has memorized his exact morning routine, so she pours his black coffee before he even reaches the counter. |
| 9 | 09_hanging_up_apron | After the busy shift ends, Maya scrubs the wooden counter clean. She hangs her apron on the hook in the staff room, locks the front door, and steps out into the bright afternoon street, happy with what she has learned so far. |
