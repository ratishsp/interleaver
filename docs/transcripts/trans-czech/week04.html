final sweep
Storyboard: Maya stands in her temporary room in the building. She zips up her large suitcases and checks the space one last time, making sure nothing lies under the small bed or stands forgotten in the closet.

unlocking the door

ninas help
Storyboard: Maya steps back out to the shared stairwell to grab her heaviest suitcase. Nina comes up the stairs, spots her struggling, and cheerfully helps carry the bag inside, setting it carefully on the floor by the large living room window.

Můj největší kufr stojí na podlaze.My biggest suitcase stands on the floor.
Je velmi těžký.It is very heavy.
Zatáhnu za kufr.I pull on the suitcase.

Vřele se na mě usmívá.She smiles warmly at me.
„Dnes se stěhuješ, Mayo?“ ptá se."Are you moving in today, Maya?" she asks.
„Ano, konečně jsem ve svém novém bytě,“ říkám."Yes, I am finally in my new apartment," I say.
„Pomůžu ti,“ říká Nina laskavě."I will help you," Nina says kindly.
Chytne kufr.She grabs hold of the suitcase.
Společně neseme kufr do obýváku.Together we carry the suitcase into the living room.
Nina ho opatrně postaví na podlahu.Nina sets it carefully on the floor.
Teď kufr stojí u velkého okna.Now the suitcase stands by the large window.
Pod oknem už leží taška.There already lies a bag under the window.
„Moc děkuji za pomoc, Nino,“ říkám radostně."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say happily.
„Není zač,“ usmívá se."You are welcome," she smiles.
Nina odchází na chodbu.Nina walks out into the stairwell.
Zavírám dveře a dívám se na kufr u okna.I close the door and look at the suitcase by the window.
