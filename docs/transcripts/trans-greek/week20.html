Week 20: Maya starts volunteering at the café

finding the volunteering
Storyboard: Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. One evening she browses a volunteering website, reads a few listings, and finds the little café she already knows, which needs weekend helpers, so she fills in the short sign-up form.

Ένα βράδυ, κάθομαι με τον υπολογιστή μου.One evening, I am sitting with my computer.

Διαβάζω τις τρεις πρώτες αγγελίες στη σελίδα.I read the first three listings on the site.

Χρειάζονται άτομα για τα πρωινά του Σαββάτου.They need people for Saturday mornings.
Αυτό με βολεύει απόλυτα.That suits me absolutely perfectly.

Όταν έφτασα σήμερα το πρωί, ήμουν πολύ αγχωμένη.When I arrived this morning, I was very nervous.
Αλλά το εξήγησε καλά, οπότε έχω ήδη καταλάβει τα περισσότερα.But he explained it well, so I have already understood most of it.
the first coffee

the ginger beer
