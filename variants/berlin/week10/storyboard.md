# Week 10 — Storyboard (Maya shops for winter clothes)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Systematising adjective endings after the definite and the indefinite article (nominative and accusative, singular) — learners have been *hearing* these since week 1, and this week is where the pattern is laid out and drilled rather than met for the first time; **anziehen/tragen**; joining clauses with **und / oder / aber / denn** (*schön, aber zu teuer*)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_cold_morning | Maya walks to the U-Bahn for her commute. March in Berlin is still very cold. She wears her thin jacket from Mexico with a thick sweater underneath, aber it is still not warm enough. She is freezing, denn an icy wind is blowing. She has had enough: she needs a real winter coat. |
| 2 | 02_sabine_recommendation | After work, Maya meets Sabine and Bruno in the hallway. Maya asks Sabine for advice about winter clothes. Sabine recommends a large clothes shop in the city centre, denn it sells excellent winterwear. |
| 3 | 03_the_green_coat | It is Saturday. Maya visits the large shop. Inside it is warm, so she takes off her thin jacket and puts it into her shopping bag. She browses the outerwear rails and finds a heavy, dark green coat in size thirty-eight. She checks the tag: it costs one hundred and twenty Euro. She puts it on right there in the aisle. It is long und very warm, so she decides to buy it. She takes the coat off again and carries it over her arm. |
| 4 | 04_expensive_jacket | Maya walks past the leather jackets. One of them is beautiful — soft, dark brown, exactly her style, und in size thirty-eight. She puts it on and looks at herself in the mirror. The jacket suits her perfectly. Then she looks at the price tag: two hundred and fifty Euro. The jacket is wonderful, aber it is much too expensive. She hangs it back on the rail. |
| 5 | 05_warm_sweatshirt | Maya looks for a warm top instead. She spots a thick, grey sweatshirt on a nearby table und finds size M in the stack. She takes it to the fitting room and tries it on there. It costs forty-five Euro und feels extremely comfortable. |
| 6 | 06_impulse_hat | On her way across the shop floor, Maya passes a bright accessory display. She wants something for her ears against the biting wind. Two thick wool hats catch her eye: the yellow one oder the grey one. She pulls the yellow hat onto her head. It costs only fifteen Euro und looks good with the dark green coat, so she keeps it. |
| 7 | 07_checkout_desk | Maya carries the coat, the sweatshirt, and the hat to the till. The shop assistant scans the items and names the total: one hundred and eighty Euro. Maya pays with her card, takes the Kassenbon, and packs the sweatshirt into her large shopping bag. |
| 8 | 08_warm_at_last | She puts the green coat und the warm yellow hat on right away, there in the shop. She walks out of the shop into the freezing March weather. She smiles, denn for the first time in Berlin she feels truly warm. |
