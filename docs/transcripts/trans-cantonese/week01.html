Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
飛機而家喺哥本哈根。The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
而家係一月。It is January.
我好興奮。I am very excited.
我將本書放落個袋度。I pack my book into my bag.
我旁邊有一個乘客。There is a passenger next to me.
佢望住我微笑。He looks at me and smiles.
「你好，」佢話。"Hello," he says.
「你好，」我話。"Hello," I say.
「你叫咩名呀？」佢問。"What is your name?" he asks.
「我叫瑪雅，」我話。"My name is Maya," I say.
「你來自邊度呀，瑪雅？」佢問。"Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
我好開心噉微笑。I smile happily.
「我來自墨西哥，」我答。"I come from Mexico," I answer.
「你係咪來自丹麥㗎？」我問。"Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
「係呀，我來自丹麥，」佢話。"Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
「歡迎嚟到丹麥，」佢話。"Welcome to Denmark," he says.
「多謝晒，」我話。"Thank you very much," I say.
而家我哋到喇。Now we are here.
我準備好喇。I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
我而家喺出面。I am outside now.
我個篋又大又重。My suitcase is big and heavy.
嗰度有架的士。There is a taxi.
我將個篋搬上的士。I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
司機係個友善嘅男人。The driver is a friendly man.
「地址係咩？」佢問。"What is the address?" he asks.
「帕克路十二號，」我話。"It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
佢點點頭，然後開車。He nods and drives.
「你係咪遊客？」司機問。"Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
「唔係，我唔係遊客，」我答。"No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
「你喺邊度嚟㗎？」佢問。"Where do you come from?" he asks.
「我嚟自墨西哥，」我話。"I come from Mexico," I say.
我望向窗外。I look out the window.
我好自豪。I am very proud.
「我係軟件工程師，」我話。"I am a software engineer," I say.
「我嘅新工喺哥本哈根呢度。」"My new job is here in Copenhagen."
佢微笑。He smiles.
「真係令人興奮。」"How exciting."
「祝你喺丹麥好運，」佢話。"Good luck in Denmark," he says.
「多謝晒，」我話。"Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
道門開咗。The door is open.
個門檻好高。The threshold is high.
個喼好重呀！The suitcase is very heavy!
但係而家個喼喺走廊度。But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
呢度完全黑晒。It is completely dark here.
電燈掣喺邊？Where is the switch?
幅牆好凍。The wall is cold.
個掣係咪喺呢度？Is the switch here?
係喎，個掣喺度！Yes, there is the switch!
而家有光啦。Now there is light.
我喺間公寓度。I am in the apartment.
間屋好細，但係好靚。It is small, but it is nice.
呢度有個客廳。Here is a living room.
有一張梳化同一張枱。There is a sofa and a table.
呢度係一間有齊傢俬嘅屋。It is a furnished home.
個喼喺地下。The suitcase is on the floor.
我好攰。I am very tired.
但係我都好開心。But I am also happy.
我喺哥本哈根呀！I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
我企喺窗邊。I am standing by the window.
啲玻璃好凍。The glass is cold.
依家係一月。It is January.
我望出去。I look out.
今日哥本哈根灰濛濛。Copenhagen is grey today.
出面好凍。It is cold outside.
街上面有雪。There is snow on the street.
不過呢度好暖。But it is warm here.
間公寓幾好。The apartment is good.
我諗起墨西哥。I think about Mexico.
墨西哥有陽光。In Mexico, there is sun.
嗰度都好暖。It is also warm there.
我有啲掛住啲陽光。I miss the sun a little.
不過我笑咗。But I smile.
呢度係一個新城市。It is a new city.
我依家喺丹麥。I am in Denmark now.
呢個係一個好開始。It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
我依家喺客廳。I am in the living room now.
嗰度有一張細梳化。There is a small sofa.
我打電話畀喺墨西哥嘅媽咪。I call my mother in Mexico.
我話：「嗨，媽咪。」"Hi Mom," I say.
媽咪話：「嗨，瑪雅。」"Hi Maya," my mother says.
佢問：「你喺邊度呀？」"Where are you?" she asks.
我答：「我喺哥本哈根。」"I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
「我依家喺公寓入面。」"I am in the apartment now."
媽咪話：「太好喇。」"That is so good," my mother says.
佢問：「大唔大間呀？」"Is it big?" she asks.
我答：「唔大，好細間。」"No, it is small," I answer.
「不過好溫馨，我好開心。」"But it is cozy, and I am happy."
佢問：「凍唔凍呀？」"Is it cold?" she asks.
我話：「係呀，出面好凍。」"Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
「不過呢度好暖。」"But it is warm in here."
「我好安全。」"I am safe."
媽咪話：「咁就好喇。」"That is good," my mother says.
我笑一笑。I smile.
丹麥係我嘅新屋企。Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
我打開道門。I open my door.
我企喺樓梯間。I am standing in the stairwell.
周圍好靜。It is quiet.
有道門打開咗。A door opens.
有個女人對住我笑。A woman smiles at me.
佢話：「你好。」"Hi," she says.
佢問：「你叫咩名？」"What is your name?" she asks.
我答：「我叫瑪雅。」"My name is Maya," I answer.
佢問：「你喺邊度嚟㗎？」"Where do you come from?" she asks.
我話：「我喺墨西哥嚟。」"I come from Mexico," I say.
個女人話：「歡迎嚟到呢棟樓。」"Welcome to the building," the woman says.
佢話：「我叫妮娜。」"My name is Nina," she says.
「我係你鄰居。」"I am your neighbour."
妮娜好友善。Nina is very friendly.
佢講嘢好慢，好清楚。She speaks slowly and clearly.
佢話：「呢度就係我門口。」"Here is my door," she says.
「我好樂意幫手。」"I am happy to help."
妮娜話：「你直接敲門就得喇。」"You just knock," Nina says.
我答：「多謝晒你，妮娜。」"Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
我好開心。I am very happy.
依家我喺哥本哈根有個朋友喇。Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
依家係夜晚。Now it is night.
呢晚係我喺哥本哈根嘅第一晚。It is my first night in Copenhagen.
我喺浴室裡面。I am in the bathroom.
呢度好細。It is very small.
呢度有個洗手盆。There is a sink.
我把牙刷係紅色嘅。My toothbrush is red.
我唧啲牙膏落牙刷度。I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
我刷牙。I brush my teeth.
啲水好凍。The water is cold.
我洗面。I wash my face.
我抹乾塊面。I dry my face.
我行埋張床度。I go over to my bed.
張床好窄。The bed is narrow.
但係又暖又軟熟。But it is warm and soft.
依家我瞓咗喺張被入面。I am lying under the duvet now.
我部電話擺咗喺檯面。My phone is lying on the table.
我校好鬧鐘。I set my alarm.
聽朝八點鐘會響。It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
我好攰，不過我已經準備好喇。I am tired, but I am ready.
早唞喇，哥本哈根。Goodnight, Copenhagen.
