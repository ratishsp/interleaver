# Week 10 — Storyboard (Maya buys winter clothes)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Adjective agreement *en/et/*plural; *har på*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_the_cold_wind | Maya walks to the office in the morning. A freezing wind blows through the streets. She is wearing a thin black jacket from Mexico and feels incredibly cold. She decides she must buy proper winter clothes today. |
| 2 | 02_entering_the_shop | After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop to escape the cold. She spots a friendly shop assistant and asks for help. The assistant points to a rack of heavy coats and warm outerwear at the back of the store. |
| 3 | 03_the_small_coat | Maya takes a heavy blue coat into the fitting room and tries it on. The material is warm, but the sleeves are too short and it is terribly tight across the shoulders. It does not fit her, so she takes it off and leaves it behind in the cubicle. |
| 4 | 04_the_perfect_jacket | Maya puts her own clothes back on, grabs her bag, and returns to the shop floor. She spots a large green winter jacket on a mannequin. She tries a similar one on in front of a mirror, and she loves how it fits comfortably over her shoulders. |
| 5 | 05_paying_for_the_clothes | Maya takes the green jacket and grabs a pair of thick wool gloves on her way to the register. The assistant rings up the items and says the total is nine hundred kroner. Maya gladly pays with her card and packs her new warm clothes into a bag. |
| 6 | 06_dressing_for_the_weekend | It is Saturday morning and frost covers the apartment windows. Maya prepares for a weekend walk. She puts on her new green jacket and the thick gloves, finally dressing correctly for the harsh midwinter. |
| 7 | 07_the_frozen_lake | Maya walks briskly along a large lake in the park. The water is partially frozen at the edges. She watches other people walking by wearing huge scarves and hats, and she smiles because she no longer feels the biting wind. |
| 8 | 08_showing_the_family | Maya sits on a cold park bench and starts a video call with her family in Mexico. She proudly shows her sister Sofía and brother Diego her new winter outfit. Diego laughs at the giant green jacket, but Maya insists it is perfect. |
