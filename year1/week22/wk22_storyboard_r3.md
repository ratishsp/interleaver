# Week 22 — Storyboard (Maya cooks a local dinner for her friends)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Imperative sequences; quantities; *først/så/derefter*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_planning_the_menu | Maya decides to host her Thursday badminton friends, Kasper and Sara, for dinner on Friday evening. She wants to cook a traditional local dish, so she finds a recipe for Danish meatballs with potatoes and writes down a shopping list with the exact quantities. |
| 2 | 02_shopping_for_amounts | At the supermarket after work, Maya carefully gathers her ingredients. She weighs out exactly one kilo of small potatoes, picks up five hundred grams of minced pork, and grabs two large yellow onions. |
| 3 | 03_first_prep_step | Back in her apartment kitchen, Maya puts on an apron and reads the recipe's first instructions. The recipe states clearly: first, finely chop the onions, and mix them thoroughly with the meat, an egg, and a little flour. |
| 4 | 04_then_the_potatoes | Next, Maya prepares the potatoes. She follows the next sequence of commands: scrub the potatoes clean, place them in a pot of water, and then add a large spoonful of coarse salt before turning on the stove. |
| 5 | 05_afterwards_frying | With the potatoes boiling, Maya heats a large frying pan. The instructions tell her to melt a thick slice of butter, and afterwards, form the meat mixture into round balls using a spoon and fry them until they are crisp and brown. |
| 6 | 06_setting_the_table | While the meatballs finish cooking, Maya prepares her small dining area. She arranges three plates, sets out the forks and knives, and folds some paper napkins to make the table look inviting. |
| 7 | 07_guests_arrive | The doorbell rings, and Kasper and Sara arrive in high spirits. Maya welcomes them inside, takes their coats, and gratefully accepts some cold bottles of local beer they brought to share with the meal. |
| 8 | 08_eating_together | The three friends squeeze around the cozy table and begin their meal. Kasper and Sara are highly impressed with the food, praising the authentic flavors as they eagerly pour the beer and take second helpings. |
| 9 | 09_sharing_the_recipe | When Sara asks how to make the dish, Maya promises to send her a link to the written recipe. The friends clear the empty plates and cheerfully agree that it will be Kasper's turn to cook next time. |
