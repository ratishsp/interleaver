Week 14: Maya takes public transport across the city

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

checking the route
Storyboard: Maya looks at a travel app on her phone. She needs to go across the city to pick up a package, so she plans a route with three different types of public transport.
Danes moram prevzeti paket.I need to pick up a package today.
Je na drugi strani mesta.It is on the other side of the city.
Pogledam svoj telefon.I look at my phone.
Odprem aplikacijo za javni prevoz.I open a public transport app.
Načrtujem svojo pot.I plan my route.
Pot je dolga.The route is long.
Ima tri dele.It has three parts.
Najprej moram iti z avtobusom.First, I need to take a bus.
Avtobus pelje do metroja.The bus drives to the metro.
Potem se moram peljati z metrojem.Then I need to ride the metro.
Na koncu moram iti do pristanišča.Finally, I need to go to the harbour.
Tukaj moram na pristaniški avtobus.Here, I need to take the harbour bus.
Pristaniški avtobus je velika ladja.The harbour bus is a large boat.
Pluje po vodi.It sails on the water.
Paket je v majhni trgovini.The package is in a small shop.
Trgovina se nahaja blizu vode.The shop is located close to the water.
To je dober načrt.It is a good plan.
Vzamem svojo modro potovalno kartico.I take my blue travel card.
Pripravljena sem na pot.I am ready for the trip.
topping up the card
Storyboard: She walks to a nearby ticket machine. She taps her blue travel card on the screen and pays two hundred kroner to top up her balance for the long trip.
Grem do avtomata za vozovnice na ulici.I walk over to a ticket machine on the street.
Najprej moja potovalna kartica potrebuje več denarja.First my travel card needs more money.
Iz torbe vzamem svojo potovalno kartico.I take my travel card out of my bag.
To je majhna, modra kartica.It is a small, blue card.
Kartico položim na zaslon avtomata.I place the card on the machine's screen.
Zaslon prikaže moje stanje.The screen shows my balance.
Stanje je prenizko za dolgo pot.The balance is too low for a long trip.
Pritisnem na zaslon.I tap on the screen.
Izberem znesek.I choose an amount.
S plačilno kartico plačam dvesto kron.I pay two hundred kroner with my payment card.
Avtomat na zaslonu izpiše "Odobreno".The machine writes "Approved" on the screen.
Zdaj imam denar na kartici.Now I have money on the card.
Kartico pospravim nazaj v torbo.I put the card back in the bag.
Pripravljena sem na dolgo pot.I am ready for the long trip.
Zdaj moram iti z avtobusom.Now I need to take the bus.
Stopim do avtobusnega postajališča.I walk over to the bus stop.
taking the bus
Storyboard: Maya waits at the bus stop until a long yellow bus arrives. She steps inside, scans her card at the door, and finds an empty seat by the window.
Stojim na avtobusni postaji.I stand at the bus stop.
Kmalu se peljem z avtobusom.Soon I travel by bus.
Gledam po ulici navzdol.I look down the street.
Po petih minutah pride avtobus.After five minutes, the bus arrives.
To je dolg in rumen avtobus.It is a long and yellow bus.
Avtobus se ustavi točno pred mano.The bus stops right in front of me.
Vrata se odprejo.The doors open.
Stopim na avtobus.I step up into the bus.
V roki imam vozno kartico.I have my travel card in my hand.
Skeniram kartico pri vratih.I scan the card by the door.
Danes je veliko potnikov.There are many passengers today.
Iščem prost sedež.I look for an empty seat.
Najdem dober sedež ob oknu.I find a good seat by the window.
Usedem se.I sit down.
Gledam ven na mesto.I look out at the city.
Zdaj grem z avtobusom do postaje podzemne železnice.Now I take the bus to the metro station.
asking a passenger
Storyboard: She turns to an older woman sitting nearby. Maya politely asks if she travels with this bus to the metro station, and the woman nods with a friendly smile.
Sedim na avtobusu.I am sitting in the bus.
Gledam skozi okno.I look out the window.
Nisem povsem prepričana glede proge.I am not completely sure about the route.
Blizu mene sedi starejša ženska.An older woman is sitting close to me.
Pogledam proti njej.I look over at her.
"Oprostite," rečem vljudno."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Ali ta avtobus vozi do postaje metroja?""Does this bus go to the metro station?"
Ženska me pogleda.The woman looks at me.
Prikima s prijaznim nasmehom.She nods with a friendly smile.
"Da, vozi do postaje metroja," odgovori."Yes, it goes to the metro station," she replies.
"Greste z metrojem?" vpraša ženska."Are you taking the metro?" the woman asks.
"Da, grem z metrojem," rečem."Yes, I am taking the metro," I say.
"Pogosto se vozim s tem avtobusom," reče ženska."I often ride with this bus," the woman says.
"To je enostavna proga," doda."It is an easy route," she adds.
"Najlepša hvala za pomoč," rečem."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"To je moja prva vožnja na tej progi.""It is my first trip on this route."
Ženska spet prikima.The woman nods again.
Sprostim se na sedežu.I relax in my seat.
Peljemo se naprej skozi mesto.We drive on through the city.
transferring to metro
Storyboard: The bus stops, and Maya gets off at a busy square. She walks down the wide stairs into a bright, underground metro station and taps her card again.
Avtobus se ustavi na živahnem trgu.The bus stops at a busy square.
Izstopim iz avtobusa.I get off the bus.
Poslovim se od starejše gospe.I say goodbye to the older woman.
Zdaj grem na metro.Now I am going to the metro.
Grem čez trg.I walk across the square.
Tukaj je veliko ljudi.There are many people here.
Grem po širokih stopnicah navzdol.I walk down the wide stairs.
Postaja je pod zemljo.The station is underground.
Je zelo velika in svetla.It is very big and bright.
Ponovno se moram prijaviti.I have to check in again.
Izvlečem svojo modro kartico.I take out my blue card.
Skeniram kartico na avtomatu.I scan the card on the machine.
Avtomat odda glasen pisk.The machine makes a loud beep.
Zdaj se lahko peljem z metrojem.Now I can ride with the metro.
Grem dol do perona.I walk down to the platform.
Grem z naslednjim vlakom.I am taking the next train.
Pelje naravnost proti pristanišču.It goes straight towards the harbour.
Vlak pride čez tri minute.The train arrives in three minutes.
riding the metro
Storyboard: Maya stands right at the front of the driverless train. She rides with the metro through the dark tunnels and watches the lights blur past as they speed forward.
Metro prispe in vrata se odprejo.The metro arrives, and the doors open.
Vstopim v vlak.I go into the train.
Vlak vozi brez voznika.The train drives without a driver.
Grem čisto naprej.I walk all the way to the front.
Stojim pri velikem oknu.I stand by the large window.
Z metrojem se peljem skozi temo.I ride with the metro through the dark.
Smo v dolgem predoru.We are in a long tunnel.
Vozimo zelo hitro.We drive very fast.
Svetloba leti mimo okna.The light flies past the window.
Videti je fantastično.It looks fantastic.
Zraven mene stoji majhen deček.A little boy stands next to me.
Tudi on gleda ven v temo.He also looks out into the dark.
Pogosto se vozim z metrojem.I often take the metro.
To je hitra in enostavna vožnja.It is a fast and easy trip.
Zdaj se približujemo moji postaji.Now we are approaching my station.
Grem v pristanišče.I am going to the harbour.
Moja naslednja vožnja je s pristaniškim avtobusom.My next trip is with the harbour bus.
Veselim se, da bom videla vodo.I am looking forward to seeing the water.
finding the pier
Storyboard: She takes the escalator up to the street level and walks toward the water. She finds a small wooden pier by the edge of the canal where the harbour bus stops.
Vožnja z metrojem je končana.The trip with the metro is over.
Zdaj grem gor na ulico.Now I am going up to the street.
Grem po tekočih stopnicah.I take the escalator.
Zgoraj na ulici sije sonce.The sun is shining up on the street.
Zdaj grem k vodi.Now I am going to the water.
Grem s pristaniškim avtobusom.I am going by the harbour bus.
Hodim po široki ulici.I walk down a wide street.
Nato zagledam kanal.Then I see the canal.
Voda je zelo modra.The water is very blue.
Iščem svoje postajališče.I look for my stop.
Kje se ustavi ladja?Where does the boat stop?
Grem bližje robu.I walk closer to the edge.
Nato zagledam majhen lesen pomol.Then I see a small wooden pier.
Nahaja se čisto spodaj ob vodi.It is located right down by the water.
Ob pomolu je rumena tabla.There is a yellow sign by the pier.
Na tabli piše "Pristaniški avtobus".It says "Harbour bus" on the sign.
To je pravo mesto.This is the right place.
Grem na pomol.I walk out onto the pier.
Zdaj samo čakam na ladjo.Now I am just waiting for the boat.
the harbour bus
Storyboard: A wide boat arrives, and Maya boards the harbour bus. She stands on the open deck and enjoys the fresh air as she sails across the blue water.
Široka ladjica prihaja čez vodo.A wide boat comes across the water.
To je pristaniški avtobus.It is the harbour bus.
Ladjica pripluje do lesenega pomola.The boat sails in to the wooden pier.
Ustavi se ob robu.It stops at the edge.
Zdaj grem s pristaniškim avtobusom.I am going with the harbour bus now.
Stopim na krov ladje.I step on board the ship.
Znova skeniram svojo modro potovalno kartico.I scan my blue travel card again.
Notri sedi veliko potnikov.Many passengers sit inside.
Ampak jaz grem ven na odprto palubo.But I walk out onto the open deck.
Stojim povsem spredaj.I stand at the very front.
Z ladjico plujemo čez modro vodo.We sail with the boat across the blue water.
Mesto je z vode videti čudovito.The city looks beautiful from the water.
Sonce sije na nas.The sun shines down on us.
Globoko vdihnem.I take a deep breath.
Zrak je zelo svež in hladen.The air is very fresh and cold.
Uživam v kratki vožnji.I enjoy the short trip.
Kmalu smo na drugi strani.We are soon over at the other side.
arriving for the errand
Storyboard: The boat docks at the opposite shore. Maya steps off the vessel and walks toward a small shop on the corner to finally collect her package.
Čoln prispe na drugo stran vode.The boat arrives at the other side of the water.
Pristaniški avtobus se ustavi pri majhnem pomolu.The harbour bus stops at a small pier.
Izstopim s čolna.I get off the boat.
Vožnja s čolnom je zdaj končana.The trip by boat is now over.
Na telefonu preverim zemljevid.I check the map on my phone.
Grem v majhen kiosk.I am going to a small kiosk.
Kiosk se nahaja tam na vogalu.The kiosk is located over on a corner.
Grem po ulici navzgor.I walk up the street.
Na koncu ceste vidim trgovino.I see the shop at the end of the road.
Odprem vrata in vstopim.I open the door and walk in.
Za blagajno stoji mlad moški.A young man stands behind the register.
"Živijo," reče prijazno."Hi," he says kindly.
"Živijo, prišla sem iskat paket," rečem."Hi, I am going to pick up a package," I say.
Moški pokima in se nasmehne.The man nods and smiles.
Vpraša me za ime.He asks for my name.
"Ime mi je Maya," odgovorim."My name is Maya," I answer.
Na polici najde veliko škatlo.He finds a large box on a shelf.
Poda mi paket.He gives the package to me.
"Najlepša hvala za pomoč," rečem."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
Zdaj se s paketom znova odpravim na dolgo pot domov.Now I take the long trip home again with my package.
