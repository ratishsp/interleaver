Week 28: Maya reflects on her settled life in Copenhagen

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

commute reflection
Storyboard: Maya takes her usual morning bus to the software company, enjoying the warm sunlight. She watches the familiar neighborhoods roll by through the window, briefly reflecting on how freezing and completely unfamiliar this exact route felt back in January.
Nimekaa kando ya dirisha kwenye basi.I sit by the window on the bus.
Jua la asubuhi ni zuri na lenye joto leo.The morning sun is lovely and warm today.
Ni majira ya joto Copenhagen, na jiji linaonekana zuri.It is summer in Copenhagen, and the city looks beautiful.
Ninaangalia nje kwenye mitaa ninayoifahamu.I look out at the familiar streets.
Tunapita bustani kubwa yenye miti ya kijani.We drive past a large park with green trees.
Watu tayari wanaendesha baiskeli kwenye njia za baiskeli.People are already cycling on the bike paths.
Mimi hupanda basi hili kwenda kazini kwangu kila asubuhi.I take this bus to my work every morning.
Njia ni sawa kabisa na ya mwezi wa Januari.The route is exactly the same as in January.
Lakini kila kitu ni tofauti kabisa sasa.But everything is completely different now.
Ninakumbuka wiki yangu ya kwanza hapa jijini.I remember my first week here in the city.
Kulikuwa na baridi kali na giza sana.It was freezing cold and very dark.
Sikuujua mtaa kabisa wakati huo.I did not know the neighborhood at all back then.
Pia nilikuwa na wasiwasi sana kuhusu kazi yangu mpya katika kampuni ya programu.I was also very nervous about my new job at the software company.
Sasa ninajua hasa mahali tulipo.Now I know exactly where we are.
Ninatambua duka la mikate la mtaani na uwanja.I recognize the local baker and the square.
Basi linasimama, na watu zaidi wanapanda.The bus stops, and more people get on.
Ninatabasamu na kuegemea nyuma kwenye kiti changu.I smile and lean back in my seat.
Niko tayari kwa siku nzuri ofisini.I am ready for a good day at the office.
office routine
Storyboard: At the office, Maya sits down with Eva to look at the new layout for their big digital project. They quickly fix a small visual error on the screen together, easily chatting as they work through their comfortable, everyday routine.
Ninakaa kwenye dawati langu ofisini.I sit at my desk in the office.
Eva anakuja kwangu na kikombe cha kahawa.Eva comes over to me with a cup of coffee.
Tunafanya kazi kwa karibu pamoja wiki hii.We are working closely together this week.
"Uko tayari kuangalia mpangilio mpya?" anauliza."Are you ready to look at the new layout?" she asks.
"Ndiyo, ninahitaji tu kufungua programu," ninajibu."Yes, I just need to open the program," I answer.
Tunaangalia skrini kubwa pamoja.We look at the large screen together.
Mradi mkubwa wa kidijitali hatimaye unaonekana vizuri.The big digital project finally looks good.
Lakini ghafla Eva anaona kosa dogo kwenye kona.But suddenly Eva sees a small error in the corner.
Kitufe cha bluu kiko mbali kidogo upande wa kushoto.A blue button is a bit too far to the left.
"Tazama pale, nafasi si sahihi," anasema."Look there, the position is wrong," she says.
"Uko sahihi, lazima nikisogeze upande wa kulia," ninasema."You are right, I must move it to the right," I say.
Ninaandika kwa haraka mistari michache ya msimbo.I quickly write a few lines of code.
Kitufe kinasogea mahali sahihi.The button moves to the right place.
"Kamili, asante kwa msaada," Eva anasema na kutabasamu kwa uchangamfu."Perfect, thank you for the help," Eva says and smiles warmly.
Tunazungumza na kucheka pamoja kuhusu mipango yetu ya wikendi.We talk and laugh together about our plans for the weekend.
Sasa ninamjua kila mtu ofisini vizuri.Now I know everyone at the office well.
thursday badminton
Storyboard: On Thursday evening, Maya heads to the sports hall for her weekly badminton session. She lunges across the court to return a difficult shot, swings her racket hard to score a tough point, and then pauses by the net to drink water and catch her breath.

Mechi inasisimua sana na ushindani ni mkali.The match is very exciting and close.
Tunachezea pointi ya mwisho kabisa.We are playing for the very last point.
Anaupiga mpira wa badminton kwa nguvu.She hits the shuttlecock hard.
Unaruka kwa kasi juu ya wavu na kuelekea kwangu.It flies fast over the net and over towards me.
Ninapiga hatua kubwa kwenye uwanja ili kuufikia.I take a big step across the court to reach it.
Ninazungusha raketi yangu kwa nguvu zangu zote.I swing my racket with all my power.
Ninaupiga mpira wa badminton vizuri kabisa.I hit the shuttlecock perfectly.
Unatua moja kwa moja kwenye mstari mweupe.It lands right on the white line.
Pointi ni yangu, na ninashinda mechi.The point is mine, and I win the match.
Sara anatabasamu kutoka upande wa pili wa wavu na kusema, "Mechi nzuri!"Sara smiles across the net and says, "Good match!"
"Asante sana, hiyo ilikuwa pointi ngumu," ninajibu."Thank you very much, that was a difficult point," I reply.
Ninasimama karibu na wavu kwa muda kidogo ili kuvuta pumzi.I stand by the net for a bit to catch my breath.
Ninatokwa na jasho sana na ninahitaji mapumziko.I am sweating a lot and need a break.
Ninaipata chupa yangu nyekundu ya maji kwenye begi.I find my red water bottle in the bag.
Ninakunywa maji baridi mengi.I drink a lot of cold water.
Ni vizuri sana kuutumia mwili wangu baada ya siku ndefu ofisini.It is great to use my body after a long day at the office.
quiet errand
Storyboard: Taking a quiet walk on Friday afternoon, Maya drops off her heavy winter coat at the local dry cleaner. She hands over the bulky jacket, pays two hundred and fifty kroner at the counter, and steps back out onto the busy pavement.
Ijumaa alasiri ninafanya matembezi tulivu kwenye jua.On Friday afternoon I take a quiet walk in the sun.
Nimebeba koti langu zito la baridi.I am carrying my heavy winter coat.
Silihitaji tena hata kidogo.I do not need it at all anymore.
Ninasimama mbele ya duka la dobi la hapa karibu.I stop in front of the local dry cleaner.
Ninafungua mlango na kuingia ndani.I open the door and go inside.
Kunanukia nguo safi na sabuni kwenye duka hilo dogo.It smells of clean clothes and soap in the little shop.
Mwanamume mzee amesimama nyuma ya kaunta.An older man is standing behind the counter.
"Hujambo, ningependa koti hili lisafishwe," ninasema."Hello, I would like to have this coat cleaned," I say.
Ninakabidhi koti hilo kubwa juu ya kaunta.I hand the large jacket over the counter.
"Bila shaka," mwanamume huyo anajibu na kuchunguza kitambaa."Of course," the man answers and examines the fabric.
"Litakuwa tayari siku ya Jumatano," anasema."It will be ready on Wednesday," he says.
"Itakuwa kroner mia mbili na hamsini.""That will be two hundred and fifty kroner."
Ninatoa kadi yangu ya malipo kutoka kwenye mkoba wangu.I take my debit card out of my bag.
Ninalipa kwa keshia na kupata risiti ndogo ya njano.I pay at the register and get a small yellow receipt.
"Asante sana, uwe na wikendi njema," ninasema."Thank you very much, have a good weekend," I say.
"Na wewe pia," anajibu kwa tabasamu."You too," he answers with a smile.
Ninaweka risiti ndani ya mkoba wangu.I put the receipt down into my bag.
Kisha ninatoka nje tena kwenye njia ya watembea kwa miguu yenye shughuli nyingi.Then I step out onto the busy pavement again.
Ninafurahia kutembea kwenye jua la joto la alasiri.I enjoy walking in the warm afternoon sun.
saturday cafe
Storyboard: During her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the neighborhood café, Maya confidently operates the large espresso machine. She wipes down the wooden counter and hands over steaming cups of coffee and warm pastries to the waiting customers.
Ni asubuhi ya Jumamosi, na niko kwenye mkahawa wa mtaani.It is Saturday morning, and I am at the local café.
Ninafanya kazi ya kujitolea hapa kila wikendi.I volunteer here every weekend.
Mkahawa tayari umejaa watu.The café is already full of people.
Nimesimama nyuma ya kaunta kubwa ya mbao.I stand behind the large wooden counter.
Ninaifuta kaunta kwa kitambaa safi.I wipe down the counter with a clean cloth.
Kijana mmoja anatembea kuja kwenye kaunta.A young man walks over to the counter.
"Habari ya asubuhi, ningependa kahawa kubwa," anasema."Good morning, I would like a large coffee," he says.
"Na vitafunwa viwili vya moto, tafadhali," anaongeza."And two warm pastries, please," he adds.
"Bila shaka, inakuja sasa hivi," ninajibu kwa tabasamu."Of course, coming right up," I answer with a smile.
Ninatembea kuelekea kwenye mashine kubwa ya espresso.I walk over to the large espresso machine.
Ninaifahamu mashine hiyo vizuri sana sasa.I know the machine really well now.
Ninatengeneza haraka kikombe cha kahawa ya moto inayofuka moshi.I quickly make a steaming hot cup of coffee.
Harufu ya kahawa mpya inakijaza chumba hicho kidogo.The smell of fresh coffee fills the small room.
Baadaye, ninaweka vitafunwa viwili vya moto kwenye mfuko wa karatasi.Afterwards, I put two warm pastries in a paper bag.
Ninampatia kahawa na mfuko huo kwa juu ya kaunta.I hand the coffee and the bag over the counter to him.
"Zitakuwa kroner hamsini na tano," ninasema."That will be fifty-five kroner," I say.
Analipa kwa kadi yake na kuipokea kahawa.He pays with his card and accepts the coffee.
"Uwe na wikendi njema," anasema kwa furaha."Have a good weekend," he says happily.
"Asante, na wewe pia," ninajibu."Thanks, you too," I reply.
Ninampungia mkono mteja anayefuata kwenye foleni asogee mbele.I wave the next customer in line forward.
cooking preparation

family update
