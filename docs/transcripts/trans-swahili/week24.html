Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
Ni Ijumaa jioni, na Nina anagonga mlango wangu.It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
Yuko pamoja na mbwa wake, Ludwig.She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.
Atakaa nami kwa wikendi.He is going to stay with me for the weekend.
Nina anatabasamu na kunipa kamba.Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
"Asante sana kwa msaada, Maya," anasema."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
"Hakuna shida kabisa," ninajibu kwa furaha."It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
Ananieleza mambo machache kumhusu.She explains a few things to me about him.
"Mpe chakula saa moja asubuhi na saa kumi na mbili jioni," Nina anasema."Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
"Na kumbuka, hapaswi kula chakula cha binadamu.""And remember, he must not have human food."
Ninacheka na kumtazama yule mbwa mdogo chini.I laugh and look down at the little dog.
"Hapana, Ludwig, chakula changu cha jioni si chako," ninamwambia."No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
Nina anacheka pia na kuonyesha mlangoni.Nina laughs too and points at the door.
"Daima mshikilie kwenye kamba mnapotembea karibu na barabara kubwa," anasema."Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
"Kwenye bustani anaruhusiwa kukimbia kwa uhuru.""In the park he is allowed to run freely."
Ninakubali kwa kichwa.I nod.
"Usijali, nitamtunza vizuri.""Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
Nina ananikumbatia na kutuaga.Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
Anafunga mlango, na ninasimama peke yangu na Ludwig.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
Ananiangalia juu na kutikisa mkia wake.He looks up at me and wags his tail.
Tayari ninampenda sana.I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll
Storyboard: Before bed, Maya takes Ludwig for a short evening walk around the neighborhood. When he suddenly sniffs at a discarded food wrapper on the pavement, she gently commands him to leave it and pulls him away, reminding him that he must not eat trash.
Ni Ijumaa jioni sana.It is late Friday evening.
Kabla hatujaenda kulala, namtoa Ludwig nje.Before we go to bed, I take Ludwig out.
Tunafanya matembezi mafupi mtaani.We go for a short walk in the neighborhood.
Mitaa ni tulivu sana sasa.The streets are very quiet now.
Ludwig anatembea vizuri kando yangu.Ludwig walks nicely next to me.
Ghafla anasimama.Suddenly he stops.
Ananusa kwa hamu kitu kwenye njia ya watembea kwa miguu.He sniffs eagerly at something on the pavement.
Ni mfuko mchafu wenye mabaki ya chakula.It is a dirty bag with food scraps.
Anafungua mdomo wake kuuchukua.He opens his mouth to take it.
"Hapana, uache!" namwambia kwa msisitizo."No, drop it!" I say firmly to him.
Namvuta polepole mbali na mfuko huo.I gently pull him away from the bag.
"Taka sio chakula, Ludwig," ninaeleza."The trash is not food, Ludwig," I explain.
"Njoo hapa, Ludwig," namwita."Come here, Ludwig," I call to him.
Anauacha mfuko na kunitazama.He drops the bag and looks up at me.
Anaanza haraka kutikisa mkia wake.He quickly begins to wag his tail.
"Mbwa mzuri," nasema na kumpapasa."Good boy," I say and pet him.
Tunaendelea na matembezi yetu mtaani.We continue our walk down the street.
Sasa tunahitaji tu kwenda nyumbani na kulala.Now we just need to go home and sleep.
saturday breakfast
Storyboard: Early Saturday morning, Ludwig nudges Maya awake. Maya prepares his breakfast, gently telling him to sit and wait while she fills the bowl, before finally petting him and telling him to eat it.
Ni Jumamosi asubuhi saa moja.It is Saturday morning at seven o'clock.
Bado nimelala fofofo.I am still sleeping heavily.
Ghafla nahisi kitu baridi kwenye mkono wangu.Suddenly I feel something cold on my arm.
Nafumbua macho na kumwona Ludwig.I open my eyes and see Ludwig.
Ananisukuma kwa pua yake.He nudges me with his snout.
"Habari ya asubuhi, Ludwig," nasema na kuamka."Good morning, Ludwig," I say and get up.
Tunaenda jikoni pamoja.We go out into the kitchen together.
Ana njaa sana na ananirukia.He is very hungry and jumps up on me.
"Tulia sasa, Ludwig," namwambia."Easy now, Ludwig," I say to him.
"Kaa," nasema kwa utulivu."Sit," I say calmly.
Ananiangalia na kukaa chini sakafuni.He looks at me and sits down on the floor.
Nachukua mfuko wa chakula cha mbwa na kuufungua.I take the bag of dog food and open it.
Namimina chakula kwenye bakuli lake.I pour the food into his bowl.
Anainuka kwa sababu anakitaka mara moja.He gets up because he wants it immediately.
"Hapana, subiri," nasema."No, wait," I say.
Anakaa chini tena na kusubiri vizuri.He sits down again and waits nicely.
Naweka bakuli chini mbele yake.I put the bowl down in front of him.
Nampapasa kichwani kwa upole.I gently pet him on the head.
"Haya, kula," nasema na tabasamu."Here you go, eat," I say with a smile.
Anatikisa mkia wake na kuanza kula.He wags his tail and begins to eat.
a morning together
Storyboard: Saturday morning, with the café shift she arranged off in advance so Ludwig is not left alone, Maya has the morning free. she and Ludwig enjoy it together — she throws his ball and calls him back, gives him a good fuss — happy she chose to stay with him.
Baada ya kifungua kinywa nina muda wa kutosha.After breakfast I have plenty of time.
Kwa kawaida mimi hujitolea kwenye mkahawa siku za Jumamosi.I normally volunteer at a café on Saturdays.
Lakini Ludwig hapaswi kuwa peke yake.But Ludwig should not be alone.
Kwa bahati nzuri, leo nina siku ya mapumziko kutoka mkahawani.Fortunately I have the day off from the café today.
Nilikubaliana nao wiki iliyopita.I arranged it with them last week.
Sasa mimi na Ludwig tuna asubuhi yote pamoja.Now Ludwig and I have the whole morning together.
Tunatoka nje kwenye uwanja ulioko nyuma ya jengo langu.We go out into the courtyard behind my building.
Ninatoa mpira wake mdogo.I take out his little ball.
"Angalia mpira, Ludwig!" ninamwambia."Look at the ball, Ludwig!" I say to him.
Ninaurusha kwenye nyasi.I throw it across the grass.
"Kaulete!" ninapaza sauti."Fetch it!" I shout.
Anakimbia haraka na kudaka mpira.He runs fast and catches the ball.
"Njoo hapa!" ninaita."Come here!" I call.
Anakimbia kwa furaha kurudi kwangu.He runs happily back to me.
"Nipe," ninasema kwa upole."Give it to me," I say gently.
Anauachia mpira, na ninampapasa.He lets go of the ball, and I pet him.
"Wewe ni mbwa mzuri," ninasema na kutabasamu."You are a good dog," I say and smile.
Ninaurusha tena, naye anauleta.I throw it again, and he fetches it.
Lazima nimtunze vizuri wikendi nzima.I must take good care of him all weekend.
Nina furaha sana kwamba nilibaki nyumbani pamoja naye.I am very happy that I stayed at home with him.
the hundeskov
Storyboard: Saturday afternoon, Maya walks with Ludwig to a local hundeskov (dog forest). Once inside the safe, gated area, she unclips the lead, happily telling him that he may run free now, and throws a stick for him to fetch.
Ludwig anatembea haraka kando yangu.Ludwig walks quickly next to me.
Tuko njiani kuelekea kwenye msitu wa mbwa wa hapa karibu.We are on our way to a local dog forest.
Jua linang'aa vizuri alasiri hii.The sun is shining nicely this afternoon.
Anavuta kamba kwa hamu kidogo.He pulls a little eagerly on the leash.
"Ngoja," ninamwambia kwa uthabiti."Wait," I say firmly to him.
"Njoo, tutembee vizuri.""Come, let us walk nicely."
Hatimaye tunafika kwenye lango kubwa.We finally arrive at the large gate.
Msitu wa mbwa umezungushiwa uzio na ni salama kabisa.The dog forest is fenced and completely safe.
Tunaingia ndani, na ninafunga lango nyuma yetu.We go in, and I close the gate behind us.
Ninachuchumaa na kumtazama.I crouch down and look at him.
Ninafungua kamba polepole kutoka kwenye kola yake.I slowly unclip the leash from his collar.
"Sasa unaweza kukimbia kwa uhuru," ninasema kwa tabasamu."Now you may run freely," I say with a smile.
Ananitazama kwa furaha na kukimbia haraka.He looks happily at me and runs off quickly.
Ninapata kijiti kizuri chini.I find a good stick on the ground.
"Tazama hapa, Ludwig!" ninapaaza sauti na kumwonyesha."Look here, Ludwig!" I shout and show it to him.
Ninatupa kijiti mbali sana katikati ya miti.I throw the stick far away in among the trees.
"Kilete!" ninasema kwa sauti kubwa."Fetch it!" I say loudly.
Anakimbia hadi kwenye kijiti na kukichukua mdomoni.He runs over to the stick and takes it in his mouth.
"Rudi," ninamuita na kupiga makofi."Come back," I call and clap my hands.
Yeye ni mbwa mzuri kweli.He is really a good dog.
gentle recall
Storyboard: Ludwig spots a bird and wanders off toward the trees, completely ignoring Maya's loud commands to stop. Realizing yelling does not work, Maya crouches down with a treat, softly calling him over; he quickly runs to her, and she tells him to sit before clipping the leash back on him.
Ludwig anakimbia kwa furaha huku na huko msituni.Ludwig runs happily around in the forest.
Ghafla anaona ndege mkubwa.Suddenly he sees a big bird.
Anakimbia haraka kumfuata.He runs quickly after it.
Anakimbia moja kwa moja kuelekea kwenye miti.He runs all the way over towards the trees.

"Njoo hapa!""Come over here!"

Anakimbia haraka kurudi kwangu.He runs quickly back to me.
"Kaa," ninasema kwa utulivu."Sit," I say calmly.

family video call
Storyboard: That evening, Maya video calls her family in Mexico to show them her furry guest. She points the camera at Ludwig, telling Sofía and Diego to look at him, noting that he is just as calm as their dog Lola back home.

"Lala chini tena tu, rafiki yangu," ninasema kwa upole na kumpapasa."Just lie down again, my friend," I say gently and pet him.
Ninaitakia familia yangu usiku mwema ili aweze kupata utulivu.I say goodnight to my family so he can get some peace.

ninas return
