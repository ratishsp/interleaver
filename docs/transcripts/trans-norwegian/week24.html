Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Norwegian with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
Det er fredag kveld, og Nina banker på døren min.It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
Hun har med seg hunden sin, Ludwig.She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.

Nina smiler og gir meg båndet.Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
"Tusen takk for hjelpen, Maya," sier hun."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
"Det er helt i orden," svarer jeg glad."It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
Hun forklarer meg et par ting om ham.She explains a few things to me about him.
"Gi ham mat klokken syv om morgenen og klokken seks om kvelden," sier Nina."Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
"Og husk, han må ikke få menneskemat.""And remember, he must not have human food."
Jeg ler og ser ned på den lille hunden.I laugh and look down at the little dog.
"Nei, Ludwig, middagen min er ikke til deg," sier jeg til ham."No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
Nina ler også og peker på døren.Nina laughs too and points at the door.
"Hold ham alltid i bånd når dere går nær den store veien," sier hun."Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
"I parken kan han løpe fritt.""In the park he is allowed to run freely."

"Ta det med ro, jeg skal passe godt på ham.""Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
Nina klemmer meg og sier farvel til oss.Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
Hun lukker døren, og jeg står alene med Ludwig.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
Han ser opp på meg og logrer med halen.He looks up at me and wags his tail.
Jeg er allerede veldig glad i ham.I am already very fond of him.

a morning together
