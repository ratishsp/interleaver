# Week 13 — Storyboard (Maya visits an Irani café)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Polite requests — **ददातु / लभ्यते किम्? / कृपया**; thanking **धन्यवादः**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_entering_the_cafe | Maya walks into an old, bustling Irani café in Mumbai. She looks around the room and notices the vintage atmosphere, from the dark wooden chairs and checkered tablecloths to the large, slow ceiling fans turning above. |
| 2 | 02_greeting_the_cashier | Maya walks up to the grand wooden counter near the entrance. She politely greets the elderly man sitting behind the cash register, addressing him respectfully as chacha, and looks at the handwritten menu displayed under the glass. |
| 3 | 03_ordering_classics | Maya orders her food, politely asking the cashier if a cutting chai and a classic bun maska are available. The man nods warmly, addresses her as madam, and tells her to find a table and wait. |
| 4 | 04_finding_a_seat | Keeping her bag securely with her, Maya scans the busy room and finds a small empty table near the window. She takes a seat under the slow ceiling fans and takes a moment to appreciate the lively, nostalgic charm of the heritage space. |
| 5 | 05_the_waiter_arrives | A young waiter soon approaches her table carrying a tray. Maya clears a space, guides him to set the chai and bun maska down, and sincerely thanks him for the service. |
| 6 | 06_enjoying_the_flavors | Maya takes a sip of the sweet, strong tea and dips the deeply buttered bun into the cup. She watches the vibrant street life outside the open doors, thoroughly enjoying the authentic Mumbai culinary experience. |
| 7 | 07_ordering_sweet_treat | Noticing a glass jar of baked goods on a nearby counter, Maya decides she wants something sweet. She catches the waiter's attention, calls him bhaiya, and politely requests him to bring her a slice of fresh mawa cake. |
| 8 | 08_asking_for_bill | After finishing the rich cake and the last drop of tea, Maya signals the waiter one more time. She politely explains that she is ready to go and asks him to bring the bill to her table. |
| 9 | 09_paying_and_leaving | Maya takes the paper slip back to the front counter. She hands the correct amount of rupees to the elderly cashier, thanks him graciously for the wonderful food, and steps back out into the bustling city evening. |
