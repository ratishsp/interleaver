# Week 9 — Storyboard (Maya visits the bustling outdoor market)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Prices — **വില എത്ര? / എത്ര രൂപ ആയി?**; amounts as words; **തരൂ/വേണം** at the stall

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_buying_water_at_entrance | Maya arrives at the Ernakulam market. The morning is already warm, so she walks up to a small kiosk near the entrance. She asks the vendor for a bottle of water and hands him a twenty-rupee note before entering the main aisles. |
| 2 | 02_pineapple_taste | Maya walks into the fresh fruit section. A friendly vendor cuts a slice of pineapple and offers it to her on the tip of his knife. She tastes it, and the vendor immediately states the price for a whole pineapple without her asking. She tells him she wants one and pays. |
| 3 | 03_tomato_price_per_kilo | Maya stops at a large vegetable stall and points to a pile of fresh tomatoes. She asks the vendor the price per kilo before picking any up. The vendor states the price, so Maya nods, selects a few tomatoes, and asks him to weigh them. |
| 4 | 04_expensive_woven_basket | Near the household goods section, Maya spots a beautiful woven basket. She asks the seller how much it costs. The seller states a high price in rupees. Maya smiles politely, decides it is too expensive for now, and walks away without buying it. |
| 5 | 05_spices_and_change | Maya finds a spice vendor who displays his prices on a small cardboard sign. She reads the price for cardamom and asks the vendor to give her a small packet. She hands over a five-hundred-rupee note, and the vendor carefully counts out her change. |
| 6 | 06_tea_and_pazham_pori | Feeling a little tired from the market crowds, Maya stops at a small tea cart. She drinks a hot black tea and eats a sweet, crispy Pazham Pori. When she finishes, she asks the tea seller how much the total is and hands him the exact coins. |
| 7 | 07_buying_jasmine_strings | Near the market exit, Maya smells fresh jasmine. She sees a woman sitting on a mat, weaving flower strings. Maya asks for a small string to take to her apartment, and the woman hands it to her. The woman names the price, and Maya gladly pays. |
| 8 | 08_auto_rickshaw_fare | Carrying her heavy cloth bags, Maya steps out of the market to the main road and hails an auto-rickshaw. She asks the driver how much it costs to return to her neighborhood. He tells her the fare in rupees, and she gets in for the ride home. |
