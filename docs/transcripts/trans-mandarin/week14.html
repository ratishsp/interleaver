Week 14: Maya takes public transport across the city

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

checking the route
Storyboard: Maya looks at a travel app on her phone. She needs to go across the city to pick up a package, so she plans a route with three different types of public transport.
我今天需要去取一个包裹。I need to pick up a package today.

我打开一个公共交通应用。I open a public transport app.
我规划我的路线。I plan my route.
路线很长。The route is long.
它有三个部分。It has three parts.
首先，我需要坐公交车。First, I need to take a bus.
公交车开到地铁站。The bus drives to the metro.
然后我需要坐地铁。Then I need to ride the metro.
最后，我需要去港口。Finally, I need to go to the harbour.
在这里，我需要坐港口巴士。Here, I need to take the harbour bus.
港口巴士是一艘大船。The harbour bus is a large boat.
它在水上航行。It sails on the water.
包裹在一家小商店里。The package is in a small shop.
商店离水边很近。The shop is located close to the water.
这是一个好计划。It is a good plan.
我拿上我蓝色的交通卡。I take my blue travel card.
我准备好开始这趟行程了。I am ready for the trip.
topping up the card
Storyboard: She walks to a nearby ticket machine. She taps her blue travel card on the screen and pays two hundred kroner to top up her balance for the long trip.
我走到街上的售票机前。I walk over to a ticket machine on the street.

我从包里拿出交通卡。I take my travel card out of my bag.
它是一张蓝色的小卡片。It is a small, blue card.
我把卡放在机器的屏幕上。I place the card on the machine's screen.
屏幕上显示了我的余额。The screen shows my balance.
余额太低，不够一趟长途车程。The balance is too low for a long trip.
我点击屏幕。I tap on the screen.
我选择了一个金额。I choose an amount.
我用银行卡支付了两百克朗。I pay two hundred kroner with my payment card.
机器在屏幕上显示“已批准”。The machine writes "Approved" on the screen.
现在我的卡里有钱了。Now I have money on the card.
我把卡放回包里。I put the card back in the bag.
我为这趟长途车程做好了准备。I am ready for the long trip.
现在我得去坐公交车了。Now I need to take the bus.
我走到公交车站。I walk over to the bus stop.
taking the bus

我站在公交车站。I stand at the bus stop.
很快我就要乘公交车了。Soon I travel by bus.
我顺着街道望去。I look down the street.
五分钟后，公交车来了。After five minutes, the bus arrives.
那是一辆长长的黄色公交车。It is a long and yellow bus.
公交车正好停在我面前。The bus stops right in front of me.
车门打开了。The doors open.
我走上公交车。I step up into the bus.
我手里拿着交通卡。I have my travel card in my hand.
我在车门旁刷卡。I scan the card by the door.
今天有很多乘客。There are many passengers today.
我在找一个空座位。I look for an empty seat.

asking a passenger
Storyboard: She turns to an older woman sitting nearby. Maya politely asks if she travels with this bus to the metro station, and the woman nods with a friendly smile.
我正坐在公交车上。I am sitting in the bus.
我看向窗外。I look out the window.
我对这条路线不完全确定。I am not completely sure about the route.
一位年长的女士坐在我附近。An older woman is sitting close to me.
我转头看向她。I look over at her.
“打扰一下，”我礼貌地说。"Excuse me," I say politely.
“这辆公交车到地铁站吗？”"Does this bus go to the metro station?"
那位女士看着我。The woman looks at me.
她带着友善的微笑点了点头。She nods with a friendly smile.
“是的，这辆车到地铁站，”她回答道。"Yes, it goes to the metro station," she replies.
“你要去坐地铁吗？”那位女士问。"Are you taking the metro?" the woman asks.
“是的，我要去坐地铁，”我说。"Yes, I am taking the metro," I say.
“我经常坐这辆公交车，”那位女士说。"I often ride with this bus," the woman says.
“这条路线很简单，”她补充道。"It is an easy route," she adds.
“非常感谢您的帮助，”我说。"Thank you very much for the help," I say.
“这是我第一次坐这条路线。”"It is my first trip on this route."
那位女士又点了点头。The woman nods again.
我在座位上放松下来。I relax in my seat.
我们继续在城市中穿行。We drive on through the city.

车站位于地下。The station is underground.

我拿出我的蓝卡。I take out my blue card.

他也望着外面的黑暗。He also looks out into the dark.

the harbour bus
