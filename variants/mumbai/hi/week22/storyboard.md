# Week 22 — Storyboard (Maya cooks a Maharashtrian meal for Neha and Amit)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Imperative sequences; quantities (**आधा किलो, दो चम्मच**); **पहले/फिर/उसके बाद**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_finding_the_recipe | Maya decides to invite her badminton friends, Neha and Amit, over for a weekend meal. She searches online and finds a cooking video for traditional Kande Pohe, paying close attention to the chef's imperative instructions and the sequence of steps. |
| 2 | 02_writing_the_shopping_list | Listening carefully to the video, Maya writes down the exact quantities needed: half a kilo of thick poha, a quarter kilo of onions, some peanuts, and a few spoons of basic spices. |
| 3 | 03_buying_dry_groceries | Maya walks down to the neighbourhood kirana store. She politely addresses the shopkeeper as 'bhaiya' and asks him to weigh out half a kilo of poha and a small packet of raw peanuts. |
| 4 | 04_buying_fresh_vegetables | Next, she stops at a nearby vegetable cart. She asks the vendor for onions, fresh green chilies, a lemon, and a generous bunch of green coriander, mentally ticking off the rest of her list. |
| 5 | 05_kitchen_prep_work | Back in her flat, Maya begins the prep work. Following the recipe's initial instructions, she first rinses the poha in a colander, leaves it aside to soften, and then carefully chops the onions and chilies. |
| 6 | 06_step_by_step_cooking | Maya turns on the stove and starts cooking, reciting the video's sequence to herself: first, she heats two spoons of oil in a pan; then, she fries the peanuts; and after that, she adds mustard seeds and the chopped onions. |
| 7 | 07_finishing_the_dish | Once the onions are soft, Maya mixes in a pinch of turmeric and the softened poha. She covers the pan to let it steam, finally garnishing the bright yellow dish with fresh coriander and a squeeze of lemon juice. |
| 8 | 08_friends_arrive | The doorbell rings, and Maya warmly welcomes Neha and Amit into her flat. She invites them to sit at her small dining table and brings out plates, glasses, and a jug of water. |
| 9 | 09_sharing_the_meal | Maya serves the steaming Kande Pohe to her friends. Amit and Neha eagerly try it, complimenting her on the perfect Maharashtrian flavors, and the three friends enjoy a relaxed, joyful meal together. |
