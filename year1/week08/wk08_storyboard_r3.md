# Week 8 — Storyboard (Maya explores the city)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Imperatives *gå/drej*; direction prepositions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_nina_gives_directions | On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park." |
| 2 | 02_the_first_leg | Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door. |
| 3 | 03_asking_an_older_man | Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right." |
| 4 | 04_getting_lost | Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore. |
| 5 | 05_the_helpful_dog_walker | Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman smiles, points back up the street, and shows her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn left by the museum." |
| 6 | 06_arriving_at_the_harbour | Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats. |
| 7 | 07_the_little_mermaid | Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico. |
| 8 | 08_walking_home | Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She easily finds her way home without asking for directions, feeling happy that she can finally picture the city map in her head. |
