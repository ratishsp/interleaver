Week 20: Maya starts volunteering at the café

Indonesian with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding the volunteering
Storyboard: Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. One evening she browses a volunteering website, reads a few listings, and finds the little café she already knows, which needs weekend helpers, so she fills in the short sign-up form.
Saya pernah mendengar bahwa banyak orang Denmark menjadi sukarelawan di waktu luang mereka.I have heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time.
Saya suka ide itu.I like the idea.
Saya ingin belajar berbicara bahasa Denmark dengan lebih baik dan bertemu orang-orang baru.I want to learn to speak better Danish and meet new people.
Suatu malam, saya sedang duduk di depan komputer saya.One evening, I am sitting with my computer.
Saya menemukan situs web untuk pekerjaan sukarela.I find a website for voluntary work.
Saya membaca tiga lowongan pertama di situs itu.I read the first three listings on the site.
Lowongan pertama berasal dari klub olahraga.The first listing is from a sports club.
Tapi mereka butuh bantuan pada Kamis malam, dan saat itu saya bermain bulu tangkis.But they need help on Thursday evenings, and I play badminton then.
Lowongan kedua berasal dari sebuah museum.The second listing is from a museum.
Mereka membutuhkan seseorang pada Rabu malam, tapi saat itu saya berolahraga.They need a person on Wednesday evenings, but I work out then.
Kemudian saya melihat lowongan ketiga.Then I look at the third listing.
Itu dari kafe kecil yang sudah saya kenal.It is from the little café that I already know.
Mereka butuh orang untuk Sabtu pagi.They need people for Saturday mornings.
Itu benar-benar cocok untuk saya.That suits me absolutely perfectly.
Saya mengisi formulir singkat dengan nama dan alamat email saya.I fill in a short form with my name and my email address.
Saya juga menulis nomor telepon saya: dua belas, tiga puluh empat, lima puluh enam, tujuh puluh delapan.I also write my phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight.
Terakhir, saya menekan tombol kirim.Finally, I press send.
Saya belum pernah bekerja di kafe sebelumnya, tetapi saya siap untuk mempelajarinya.I have never worked in a café before, but I am ready to learn it.
first shift arrival
Storyboard: On her very first Saturday shift, Maya arrives early at the little café. She ties her new apron in the back room and carries the chairs out to the pavement to prepare for opening.
Ini adalah sif Sabtu pertamaku di kafe kecil itu.It is my first Saturday shift at the little café.
Aku tiba lebih awal.I have arrived early.
Matahari bersinar di jalanan Kopenhagen.The sun is shining on the street in Copenhagen.
Aku menantikannya, tapi aku juga sedikit gugup.I am looking forward to it, but I am also a little nervous.
Ini adalah pertama kalinya aku bekerja di sebuah kafe.It is the first time I am working in a café.
Aku masuk ke ruang belakang untuk bersiap-siap.I went into the back room to get ready.
Di atas meja, ada sebuah celemek baru untukku.On a table lay a new apron for me.
Aku memakainya dan mengikatnya di belakang.I put it on and tied it in the back.
Terasa nyaman saat memakainya.It felt good to wear it.
Sekarang aku siap untuk memulai.Now I am ready to begin.
Aku harus belajar cara membuka kafe dengan benar.I have to learn to open the café correctly.
Pertama, manajer membuka kunci pintu depan yang berat.First, the manager unlocked the heavy front door.
Setelah itu, aku keluar ke trotoar untuk bersiap-siap.Afterwards, I went out onto the pavement to get ready.
Aku membawa empat kursi ke luar.I carried four chairs out.
Aku telah menempatkannya di sekeliling sebuah meja kecil.I have placed them around a little table.
Sebentar lagi, para relawan lainnya akan tiba.In a little while, the other volunteers will arrive.
Aku sangat antusias dengan hari pertamaku.I am very excited about my first day.
learning the machine
Storyboard: Before the doors open, an experienced volunteer brings Maya over to the large espresso machine. The volunteer shows her how to grind the coffee beans and how to steam the milk. Maya watches every step closely, because it is her first time behind the counter.
Ini pertama kalinya saya berada di belakang konter.It is my first time behind the counter.
Kafenya belum buka.The café is not open yet.
Salah satu relawan berpengalaman memanggil saya.One of the experienced volunteers calls for me.
Kami berdiri di depan mesin espreso yang besar.We stand in front of the large espresso machine.
"Hari ini kamu harus belajar membuat kopi," katanya."Today you must learn to make coffee," he says.
Saya belum pernah membuat kopi dengan mesin seperti ini sebelumnya.I have never made coffee on such a machine before.
Langkah pertama adalah menggiling biji kopi.The first step is to grind the coffee beans.
Dia menekan sebuah tombol kecil.He presses a small button.
Mesin itu menggiling biji-biji berwarna cokelat tersebut dengan suara keras.The machine grinds the brown beans with a loud sound.
Langkah kedua adalah memanaskan susu dengan uap.The second step is to steam the milk.
Dia menuangkan susu dingin ke dalam teko logam.He pours cold milk into a metal jug.
Setelah itu, dia menyalakan uap panasnya.Afterwards he turns on the hot steam.
Sementara itu, saya memperhatikan tangannya dengan saksama.I look closely at his hands meanwhile.
Susu di dalam teko dengan cepat menjadi hangat dan kental.The milk in the jug quickly becomes warm and thick.
Dia jelas sudah sering membuat kopi sebelumnya.He has clearly made coffee many times before.
Ketika saya tiba pagi ini, saya sangat gugup.When I arrived this morning, I was very nervous.
Namun dia menjelaskannya dengan baik, jadi saya sudah memahami sebagian besarnya.But he explained it well, so I have already understood most of it.
the first coffee
Storyboard: When the morning rush hits, Maya makes a cappuccino for a waiting customer. She has held the steam wand a bit too deep in the milk, so it does not get a thick foam. It still looks acceptable, though, so she pours the milk over the coffee and hands it over with a polite smile.
Pagi ini kami luar biasa sibuk.This morning we have been incredibly busy.
Saya baru saja membuat cappuccino pertama saya.I have just made my very first cappuccino.
Menjelang pukul sepuluh, seorang pria menghampiri konter.A little before ten, a man came over to the counter.
"Satu cappuccino, tolong," katanya."A cappuccino, please," he said.
Saya baru saja belajar menggunakan mesin espreso.I have just learned to use the espresso machine.
Jadi saya langsung menghampiri mesin itu.So I went over to the machine right away.
Saya menyeduh kopi dan memasukkan pipa uap ke dalam susu.I brewed the coffee and put the steam wand down into the milk.
Tapi saya memegangnya sedikit terlalu dalam.But I held it a little too deep down.
Saya mungkin agak terlalu gugup.I have probably been a little too nervous.
Sayangnya, susu tersebut tidak menghasilkan busa tebal.Unfortunately, the milk did not get any thick foam.
Saya melihat ke dalam teko dengan hati-hati.I looked carefully down into the pitcher.
Busanya tidak sepenuhnya sempurna.The foam was not completely perfect.
Tapi kelihatannya masih bisa diterima.But it still looked acceptable.
Saya menuangkan susu panas ke atas kopi di dalam cangkir.I poured the hot milk over the coffee in the cup.
Kemudian saya memberikan kopi tersebut kepada pria itu dengan senyuman sopan.Then I gave the coffee to the man with a polite smile.
"Silakan, harganya lima puluh lima kroner," kata saya."Here you go, that will be fifty-five kroner," I said.
Dia mengambil cangkir itu dan duduk di meja pertama.He took the cup and sat down at the first table.
bun with cheese
Storyboard: A moment later, she slices a warm sourdough bun and adds a thick piece of cheese. She places it on a small plate and navigates through the standing customers to deliver it safely to a corner booth.
Sesaat kemudian, pesanan berikutnya datang.Shortly after, the next order came.
Saya sudah membuat kopi pertama saya hari ini.I have already made my first coffee today.
Namun, pelanggan ini hanya menginginkan makanan.But this customer just wanted food.
Dia memesan roti bundar dengan keju.He ordered a bun with cheese.
Saya mengambil roti bundar sourdough hangat dari keranjang.I took a warm sourdough bun from the basket.
Roti sourdough sering kali memiliki kulit yang keras.Sourdough bread often has a hard crust.
Namun, saya sudah belajar memotong roti itu menjadi dua dengan cepat.But I have learned to cut the bread in half quickly.
Setelah itu, saya meletakkan sepotong keju tebal di atas roti bundar tersebut.Afterwards, I put a thick slice of cheese on the bun.
Saya menata makanan itu dengan rapi di atas piring kecil.I arranged the food nicely on a small plate.
Sekarang saya harus mengantarkannya kepada pelanggan itu.Now I had to deliver it to the customer.
Ada banyak orang yang berdiri di tengah kafe.There were many people standing in the middle of the café.
Saya menyeimbangkan piring itu dan berjalan maju dengan hati-hati.I balanced the plate and walked carefully forward.
Saya berjalan perlahan melewati tamu-tamu lainnya.I walked slowly past the other guests.
Untungnya, pelanggan itu duduk tepat di pojok.Fortunately, the customer was sitting right down in the corner.
Dia menunggu di meja keempat.He was waiting at the fourth table.
Saya meletakkan piring itu di meja di depannya.I placed the plate on the table in front of him.
"Silakan, roti bundar hangat dengan keju," kata saya."Here you go, a warm bun with cheese," I said.
"Terima kasih banyak," jawab pria itu."Thank you very much," the man replied.
Senang rasanya melihat tamu yang puas.It is lovely to see a satisfied guest.
the ginger beer
Storyboard: During her second Saturday shift, a woman asks for a ginger beer. Maya finds the ginger beer in the glass fridge, inverts it so that the ginger mixes. Then she hands it to the customer.
Itu adalah sif Sabtu keduaku di kafe.It was my second Saturday shift in the café.
Aku sudah belajar melakukan banyak hal dalam pekerjaan ini.I have already learned to do a lot of the work.
Pagi itu di luar terasa hangat dan menyenangkan.That morning it was wonderfully warm outside.
Seorang wanita datang ke konter.A woman came over to the counter.
"Saya ingin memesan bir jahe," katanya."I would like to ask for a ginger beer," she said.
"Tentu saja, sebentar," jawabku."Of course, just a moment," I answered.
Aku menghampiri kulkas kaca besar di belakang konter.I went over to the large glass fridge behind the counter.
Aku melihat ke rak ketiga.I looked on the third shelf.
Botol-botol bir jahe yang dingin ada di sana.The cold bottles of ginger beer were standing there.
Aku menemukan satu botol dan mengeluarkannya.I found a bottle and took it out.
Jahenya mengendap seperti awan tebal di bagian bawah.The ginger lay like a thick cloud down at the bottom.
Aku sudah belajar untuk mencampurnya sebelum aku membuka botolnya.I have learned to mix it before I open the bottle.
Jadi aku membalikkan botol itu dengan sangat hati-hati.So I turned the bottle very carefully upside down.
Aku menahannya seperti itu selama beberapa detik.I held it like that for a couple of seconds.
Jahenya bercampur perlahan dengan sisa cairan tersebut.The ginger mixed slowly with the rest of the liquid.
Sekarang minuman itu terlihat pas.Now the drink looked exactly right.
Aku membalikkan botol itu kembali dan meletakkannya di atas konter.I turned the bottle around again and placed it on the counter.
"Ini dia, semuanya tiga puluh lima kroner," kataku kepadanya."Here you go, that will be thirty-five kroner," I said to her.
Dia membayar, dan dia mungkin telah menikmati minuman dinginnya.She paid, and she has probably enjoyed her cold drink.
second coffee progress
Storyboard: Later that morning, Maya tries foaming milk again for a large latte. This second attempt turns out much better, and she realizes that she has finally learned to control the pressure of the hot steam.
Kemudian di hari Sabtu yang sama, kami menjadi sangat sibuk lagi.Later the same Saturday, we got very busy again.
Seorang wanita muda datang ke konter dan memesan secangkir cappuccino ukuran besar.A young woman came up to the counter and ordered a large cappuccino.
Cangkir pertama saya tidak sepenuhnya sempurna.My first cup was not quite perfect.
Tapi kali ini saya sudah siap.But this time I was ready.
Saya mengambil teko logam dan menuangkan susu dingin ke dalamnya.I took the metal pitcher and poured cold milk into it.
Saya meletakkan teko itu di bawah mesin dan menyalakan uap panas.I placed the pitcher under the machine and turned on the hot steam.
Saya tidak langsung menaikkan tekanannya.I did not turn up the pressure immediately.
Saya sebenarnya telah belajar untuk mendengarkan mesin itu.I have actually learned to listen to the machine.
Suara keras dari uap itu sangat pas kali ini.The loud sound from the steam was exactly right this time.
Susu itu perlahan menjadi hangat.The milk slowly became warm.
Dan busa di atasnya menjadi sangat tebal dan lembut.And the foam on top became very thick and soft.
Itu adalah sebuah kemajuan besar.It was a big step forward.
Saya dengan hati-hati menuangkan susu tersebut ke atas kopi di dalam cangkir besar.I carefully poured the milk over the coffee in a large cup.
Itu benar-benar terlihat seperti cappuccino profesional sungguhan.It actually looked like a real professional cappuccino.
"Silakan," kata saya dan meletakkan cangkir itu di atas konter."Here you go," I said and placed the cup on the counter.
Wanita itu tersenyum dan mengambil kopinya.The woman smiled and took her coffee.
Saya akhirnya belajar untuk mengendalikan tekanannya.I have finally learned to control the pressure.
third saturday regular
Storyboard: By her third Saturday, Maya spots a familiar older man walking towards the front door. She has memorized his usual order, so when he reaches the counter she greets him and offers to make his usual cortado.
Pada hari Sabtu ketiga saya, saya berdiri siap di belakang konter.On my third Saturday, I stood ready behind the counter.
Saya akhirnya belajar untuk sedikit bersantai di kafe.I have finally learned to relax a bit in the café.
Dari balik jendela, saya melihat seorang pria tua di jalan.Through the window, I saw an older man on the street.
Dia berjalan perlahan menuju pintu depan.He walked slowly towards the front door.
Dia juga ada di sini pada sif pertama dan kedua saya.He was also here on my first and my second shift.

Saya menatapnya dan tersenyum.I looked at him and smiled.

"Mau saya buatkan cortado seperti biasanya?""Should I make your usual cortado?"

Lalu dia tersenyum senang.Then he smiled happily.
"Ya, tolong, saya akan sangat senang," jawabnya."Yes please, I would very much like that," he answered.
Saya menghampiri mesin espreso.I went over to the espresso machine.
Saya sudah berkali-kali membuat kopi sekarang, jadi prosesnya cepat.I have made coffee many times now, so it went quickly.

hanging up apron
