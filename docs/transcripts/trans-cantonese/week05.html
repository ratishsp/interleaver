Week 5: Maya stocks her new kitchen

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

empty cupboards
Storyboard: Maya stands in the kitchen, noticing that the fridge and the cupboard are completely empty. She grabs a bag and heads out, ready to buy food.
而家六點。It is six o'clock.
我企喺廚房入面。I am standing in the kitchen.
我好肚餓。I am hungry.
我打開雪櫃。I open the fridge.
入面完全係空嘅。It is completely empty.
我望下個廚櫃。I look in the cupboard.
廚櫃入面冇嘢食。There is no food in the cupboard.
我冇嘢食。I have no food.
我淨係得少少水。I only have a little water.
我而家想食嘢。I would like to have food now.
我鍾意食蔬菜。I like vegetables.
我都鍾意食麵包。I also like bread.
但係我呢度咩都冇。But I have nothing here.
我搵到個袋。I find a bag.
個袋係紅色嘅。The bag is red.
用嚟買嘢就最啱。It is good for shopping.
我行出單位。I walk out of the flat.
我閂門。I close the door.
我準備好喇。I am ready.
而家我行去超級市場。Now I am walking to the supermarket.
produce section
Storyboard: Maya enters a supermarket and pulls a basket from a stack by the door. Because she likes carrots, she grabs a large bunch from a green crate and drops it into the basket.
我企喺超級市場前面。I stand in front of the supermarket.
間超級市場又大又光猛。The supermarket is large and bright.
道門自動打開。The door opens automatically.
我行入間舖頭。I go into the shop.
間舖頭有好多貨品。The shop has many goods.
入口旁邊有幾疊籃。There are stacks of baskets by the entrance.
我攞一個籃。I take a basket.
個籃係紅色嘅，好輕。The basket is red and light.
我搵吓蔬菜。I look for vegetables.
呢區就喺呢度。The section is right here.
我見到地下有個箱。I see a crate on the floor.
個箱係綠色嘅。The crate is green.
箱入面有好多紅蘿蔔。There are many carrots in the crate.
我鍾意紅蘿蔔。I like carrots.
我揀一大紮。I choose a large bunch.
呢紮睇落好新鮮。The bunch looks very fresh.
我將呢紮放落籃度。I put the bunch down into the basket.
依家個籃唔係空嘅。Now the basket is not empty.
我繼續向舖頭入面行。I walk further into the shop.
the parsnip
Storyboard: Looking around, Maya spots a pale, unusual root. She reads a sign and learns it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she decides to buy it anyway out of curiosity.
我見到一種好奇怪嘅蔬菜。I see a strange vegetable.
呢種蔬菜係一條好長嘅根。The vegetable is a long root.
條根嘅顏色好淺。The root is very pale.
佢個樣好似一條白色嘅紅蘿蔔。It looks like a white carrot.
我向下望住個牌。I look down at the sign.
個牌上面寫住「防風根」。It says "parsnip" on the sign.
我唔識呢種蔬菜。I do not know the vegetable.
佢係咪丹麥蔬菜嚟㗎？Is it a Danish vegetable?
我好鍾意食蔬菜。I like vegetables.
我好鍾意食紅蘿蔔。I like carrots.
可能防風根會好好味呢。Maybe the parsnip tastes good.
我將條防風根攞喺手度。I take the parsnip in my hand.
條根都幾大。The root is quite big.
我想要佢。I would like to have it.
我望住個籃。I look at the basket.
我將條根放落個籃度。I put the root in the basket.
我想試下啲新嘢。I would like to try something new.
the rye bread
Storyboard: At a shelf with baked goods, Maya looks for a local staple. She lifts a loaf of rye bread, surprised by how heavy it feels, and lays the bread carefully in the basket.
我行埋去一個麵包貨架度。I walk over to a shelf with bread.
我喺度搵緊一款本地經典。I am looking for a local classic.
我想要一個丹麥黑麥包。I would like to have a Danish rye bread.
我望住個貨架。I look at the shelf.
我搵到一個黑麥包。I find a rye bread.
佢裝喺一個袋入面。It is in a bag.
我望下個袋。I look at the bag.
我攞起個黑麥包。I take the rye bread.
我拎高個麵包。I lift the bread.
我好驚訝。I am surprised.
個麵包好重。The bread is very heavy.
感覺就好似一舊石頭咁！It feels like a stone!
我鍾意淺色嘅麵包。I like light bread.
但係黑麥包又深色又重。But the rye bread is dark and heavy.
我小心咁將個麵包放落個籃度。I carefully lay the bread in the basket.
依家個籃都好重。The basket is also heavy now.
bakery counter
Storyboard: The bakery counter has a member of staff waiting. Maya catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry.
我行埋去麵包櫃枱度。I walk over to the bakery counter.
陣味好香。The smell is very good.
我鍾意食蛋糕。I like cakes.
而且我真係好鍾意食肉桂卷。And I really like cinnamon pastries.
有個麵包師傅企喺櫃枱後面。A baker stands behind the counter.
麵包師傅望住我。The baker looks at me.
佢微笑。He smiles.
「你想要啲咩啊？」麵包師傅問。"What would you like to have?" the baker asks.
我指住一個肉桂卷。I point at a cinnamon pastry.
件糕點睇落好好食。The pastry looks delicious.
「我想要一個肉桂卷，唔該，」我話。"I would like to have a cinnamon pastry, please," I say.
「好，當然可以，」麵包師傅話。"Yes, of course," the baker says.
佢攞起件糕點。He takes the pastry.
佢將件糕點放入一個袋度。He puts the pastry in a bag.
佢隔住個櫃枱遞個袋過嚟。He hands the bag over the counter.
「畀你，」佢話。"Here you go," he says.
我接過個袋。I take the bag.
「唔該晒，」我話。"Thank you very much," I say.
我將個袋放入個籃度。I put the bag down into the basket.
finding the milk
Storyboard: Maya searches for the milk without luck. She stops an employee to ask for help, and he points her toward the dairy section at the back.
依家我需要牛奶。Now I need milk.
我行過一條好長嘅通道。I walk down a long aisle.
我望住個貨架。I look at the shelf.
我見到水同果汁。I can see the water and the juice.
但係我搵唔到牛奶。But I cannot find the milk.
我周圍都望過。I look everywhere.
我見到一個男人。I see a man.
佢喺超級市場度做嘢。He works in the supermarket.
我截停個職員，然後笑一笑。I stop the employee and smile.
「唔好意思，」我話。"Excuse me," I say.
「我想要牛奶。」"I would like to have milk."
「牛奶喺邊度呀？」"Where is the milk?"
個男人指住舖頭後面。The man points to the back of the shop.
「奶製品區喺嗰邊，」佢話。"The dairy section is down there," he says.
「唔該晒，」我答。"Thank you very much," I reply.
我行過去。I walk down there.
我終於搵到個雪櫃同埋啲牛奶。I finally find the fridge and the milk.
我攞起一盒，放落個籃度。I take a carton and lay it in the basket.
taste of home
Storyboard: Exploring another section, Maya hunts for a taste of Mexico because she really likes beans. She recognizes a bright can of black beans on a low shelf and reaches all the way to the back to pull it out.
我繼續行去另一排貨架。I walk on to another set of shelves.
我望下啲嘢食。I look at the food.
超級市場有好多嚟自其他國家嘅嘢。The supermarket has many things from other countries.
我喺度搵墨西哥嘅味道。I look for a taste of Mexico.
我好掛住家鄉嘅嘢食。I miss the food from home.
我好鍾意食豆。I really like beans.
我成日食黑豆。I often eat black beans.
我望向一個較低嘅貨架。I look at a low shelf.
個貨架有啲暗。The shelf is a bit dark.
不過我見到一個色彩繽紛嘅罐頭。But I see a colourful can.
個罐頭擺喺最入面。The can stands all the way at the back.
係一罐黑豆。It is a can of black beans.
我笑一笑。I smile.
我將隻手伸到最入面。I reach my arm all the way in.
我將個罐頭攞出嚟。I pull the can out.
我睇下罐頭上面嘅標籤。I read the label on the can.
佢做我嘅晚餐就最啱喇。It is perfect for my dinner.
我將個罐頭擺入籃度。I put the can in the basket.
我有足夠嘅嘢食喇。I have enough food.
the checkout
Storyboard: Maya brings the basket to the checkout. She places the food on the belt, holds her card over the machine, and packs the bag quickly. No price is spoken or shown.
我行埋去收銀台。I walk up to the checkout.
我手上攞住個購物籃。I have the basket in my hand.
我將啲嘢食擺上輸送帶。I put the food on the belt.
首先，我由個籃度攞出黑麥包。First I take the rye bread from the basket.
跟住我攞出牛奶同防風根。Then I take the milk and the parsnip.
我仲攞埋紅蘿蔔同黑豆。I also take carrots and black beans.
最後，我將件糕點擺上輸送帶。Finally I put the pastry on the belt.
收銀員坐喺收銀台後面。The cashier sits behind the checkout.
佢對住我微笑。He smiles at me.
佢話：「你好。」"Hello," he says.
我答：「你好。」"Hello," I reply.
佢好快咁掃描啲嘢食。He scans the food quickly.
我攞張卡出嚟。I take my card out.
我將張卡放喺部機上面。I hold the card over the machine.
部機嘟咗一聲。The machine beeps.
佢問：「你要唔要收據呀？」"Would you like to have the receipt?" he asks.
我話：「要呀，唔該，我想要收據。」"Yes please, I would like to have the receipt," I say.
我打開我個袋。I open my bag.
我好快咁將啲嘢裝落袋。I pack the bag quickly.
佢話：「祝你有美好嘅一日。」"Have a good day," he says.
putting away
Storyboard: Back in the flat, Maya empties the bag on the counter. She puts the milk and the parsnip in the fridge, and she places the bread and the can in the cupboard.
我又返到公寓喇。I am home in the flat again.
我企喺廚房度。I am standing in the kitchen.
我將個袋入面嘅嘢全部清落廚房枱面。I empty the bag on the kitchen counter.
我將啲嘢食攞出嚟。I unpack the food.
我攞起盒牛奶。I take the milk.
我將盒牛奶放入雪櫃。I put the milk in the fridge.
然後我望住條防風根。Then I look at the parsnip.
呢條根好得意。It is a funny root.
我會用佢嚟煲湯。I will use it in my soup.
我將條防風根放入雪櫃。I put the parsnip in the fridge.
依家個雪櫃唔係空嘅喇。Now the fridge is not empty.
我攞起嚿麵包。I take the bread.
嚿黑麥包好重。The rye bread is very heavy.
我將嚿麵包放入廚櫃。I put the bread in the cupboard.
然後我攞起罐豆。Then I take the can of beans.
豆可以擺好耐。Beans last a long time.
我將個罐頭放入廚櫃。I put the can in the cupboard.
依家廚櫃入面有嘢食喇。Now there is food in the cupboard.
我幾肚餓。I am quite hungry.
我依家想食晚飯喇。I would like to have dinner now.
first home cooked meal
Storyboard: Ready to eat, Maya chops some vegetables and opens the can. She cooks dinner on the stove, finally making a proper meal in the flat — and afterwards she has the pastry from the bakery counter for dessert.
廚房好細。The kitchen is small.
但係個爐好好。But the stove is good.
我攞起防風根同一條紅蘿蔔。I take the parsnip and a carrot.
我仲攞咗把刀。I also take a knife.
把刀好利。The knife is sharp.
我切開防風根同紅蘿蔔。I cut the parsnip and the carrot.
我打開個罐頭。I open the can.
我將啲嘢食放落個煲度。I put the food in a pot.
個煲喺個爐上面。The pot is on the stove.
依家啲嘢食熱啦。Now the food is warm.
聞落好香。It smells lovely.
我攞起個黑麥包。I take the rye bread.
我切咗一塊出嚟。I cut a slice.
隻碟喺張枱上面。The plate is on the table.
終於，我喺間屋度食到一餐正正經經嘅飯。Finally, I am eating a proper meal in the flat.
之後我食個肉桂卷。Afterwards I eat the cinnamon pastry.
食落好甜。It tastes sweet.
