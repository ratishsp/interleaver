Week 4: Maya moves into her own flat

final sweep
Storyboard: Maya stands in her temporary room in the building. She zips up her large suitcases and checks the space one last time, making sure nothing lies under the small bed or stands forgotten in the closet.
Ni mwezi wa Januari jijini Copenhagen.It is January in Copenhagen.
Nimesimama kwenye chumba changu cha muda.I am standing in my temporary room.
Leo ninahamia kwenye fleti mpya.Today I am moving to a new apartment.
Ipo kwenye jengo lilelile.It is in the same building.
Sanduku langu kubwa lipo sakafuni.My large suitcase stands on the floor.
Ninalifunga.I close it.
Ninatazama kote chumbani kwa mara ya mwisho.I look around the room one last time.
Ninatazama chini ya kitanda kidogo.I look under the small bed.
Kuna kitabu kipo chini ya kitanda.There is a book lying under the bed.
Kimetoka Mexico.It is from Mexico.
Ninaweka kitabu kwenye begi langu.I put the book in my bag.
Ninafungua kabati.I open the closet.
Kuna jozi ya viatu ipo ndani ya kabati.There is a pair of shoes standing in the closet.
Ninachukua viatu.I take the shoes.
Sasa hakuna chochote ndani ya kabati.Now there is nothing in the closet.
Ninaenda kwenye dawati karibu na dirisha.I walk over to the desk by the window.
Kuna kebo ipo kwenye dawati.There is a cable lying on the desk.
Ninachukua kebo.I take the cable.
Ninaweka kebo kwenye begi langu.I put the cable in my bag.
Ninachukua mabegi yangu mawili.I take my two bags.
Ninazima taa na kufunga mlango.I turn off the light and close the door.
unlocking the door
Storyboard: Carrying her first few bags up the communal stairs, Maya arrives at the door of her new, permanent flat. She turns the key, pushes the door open, and steps inside, thrilled to finally see the small furnished space waiting for her.
Ninapanda ngazi.I walk up the stairs.
Nina mabegi mawili mikononi.I have two bags in my hands.
Hatimaye niko kwenye mlango wangu mpya.I am finally at my new door.
Nina ufunguo mfukoni mwangu.I have a key in my pocket.
Ninaweka mabegi chini sakafuni.I put the bags down on the floor.
Ninatoa ufunguo.I take out the key.
Ninafungua mlango kwa ufunguo.I unlock the door.
Ninasukuma mlango wazi na kuingia ndani.I push the door open and go inside.
Fleti ni ndogo, lakini ni kamilifu.The apartment is small, but it is perfect.
Ina samani nzuri, zenye rangi nyepesi.It has nice, light furniture.
Ninatazama ndani ya sebule.I look into the living room.
Kuna sofa karibu na dirisha.There is a sofa standing by the window.
Kuna zulia chini ya sofa.There is a rug under the sofa.
Kuna mto mdogo kwenye sofa.There is a small cushion lying on the sofa.
Kuna taa kwenye kona.There is a lamp standing in the corner.
Ninafunga mlango nyuma yangu.I close the door behind me.
Ninaweka ufunguo wangu mezani.I put my key on the table.
ninas help
Storyboard: Maya steps back out to the shared stairwell to grab her heaviest suitcase. Nina comes up the stairs, spots her struggling, and cheerfully helps carry the bag inside, setting it carefully on the floor by the large living room window.
Ninatoka nje kwenye ngazi tena.I walk out into the stairwell again.
Sanduku langu kubwa zaidi liko sakafuni.My biggest suitcase stands on the floor.
Ni zito sana.It is very heavy.
Ninalivuta sanduku.I pull on the suitcase.
Ghafla ninasikia hatua kwenye ngazi.Suddenly I hear footsteps on the stairs.
Ni jirani yangu wa Kidenmaki, Nina.It is my Danish neighbor, Nina.
Ananitabasamia kwa uchangamfu.She smiles warmly at me.
"Unahamia leo, Maya?" anauliza."Are you moving in today, Maya?" she asks.
"Ndiyo, hatimaye niko kwenye fleti yangu mpya," ninasema."Yes, I am finally in my new apartment," I say.
"Nitakusaidia," Nina anasema kwa wema."I will help you," Nina says kindly.
Analishika sanduku.She grabs hold of the suitcase.
Kwa pamoja tunalibeba sanduku kuingia sebuleni.Together we carry the suitcase into the living room.
Nina analiweka kwa uangalifu sakafuni.Nina sets it carefully on the floor.
Sasa sanduku liko kando ya dirisha kubwa.Now the suitcase stands by the large window.
Tayari kuna begi chini ya dirisha.There already lies a bag under the window.
"Asante sana kwa msaada, Nina," ninasema kwa furaha."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say happily.
"Karibu," anatabasamu."You are welcome," she smiles.
Nina anatoka nje kwenye ngazi.Nina walks out into the stairwell.
Ninafunga mlango na kuliangalia sanduku kando ya dirisha.I close the door and look at the suitcase by the window.
kitchen unpacking
Storyboard: Maya opens a travel bag in the small kitchen and begins organizing. She stacks her favorite mugs in the cupboard and makes sure her red kettle stands on the counter, right by the sink.
Ninatoka na kuingia kwenye jiko dogo.I walk out into the small kitchen.
Begi langu la kusafiria liko sakafuni.My travel bag stands on the floor.
Ninafungua begi.I open the bag.
Kuna vitu vingi ndani ya begi.There are many things in the bag.
Vikombe vyangu nivipendavyo viko ndani ya begi.My favorite mugs lie in the bag.
Ninavitoa vikombe.I take the mugs out.
Kuna kabati juu ya kaunta.There is a cupboard above the counter.
Ninaviweka vikombe ndani ya kabati.I put the mugs in the cupboard.
Sasa viko kwenye rafu.Now they stand on the shelf.
Pia nina birika jekundu la umeme.I also have a red electric kettle.
Liko ndani ya begi chini ya sweta nene.It lies in the bag under a thick sweater.
Ninalitoa birika la umeme.I take the electric kettle out.
Ninaliweka kwenye kaunta ya jikoni.I put it on the kitchen counter.
Sasa kuna birika jekundu la umeme kando kabisa ya sinki.Now there stands a red electric kettle right by the sink.
Pia kuna boksi la chai ndani ya begi.There also lies a box of tea in the bag.
Ninalichukua boksi na kuliweka karibu na birika la umeme.I take the box and place it by the electric kettle.
Sasa jiko liko karibu tayari.Now the kitchen is almost ready.
Nasubiri kwa hamu kikombe cha chai baadaye.I look forward to a cup of tea later.
the workspace

Ina mwanga wa joto.It has a warm light.
Pia kuna kitabu kidogo kipo mezani.There is also a small book lying on the table.
Nina begi dogo jeusi lenye nyaya.I have a small black bag with cables.
Liko chini ya dawati.It lies under the desk.
Hivyo, begi haliko njiani.That way, the bag is not in the way.

Ninafungua kompyuta na kuanza kuandika.I open the computer and begin to type.

the plant

celebratory pizza
