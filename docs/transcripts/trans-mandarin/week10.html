Week 10: Maya buys winter clothes

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

a cold morning
Storyboard: It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to the bus stop on her way to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter.
现在是哥本哈根的早晨。It is morning in Copenhagen.
我走向公交车站。I walk to the bus stop.
今天天气非常冷。The weather is very cold today.
刮风了。It is windy.
风冰冷刺骨。The wind is ice-cold.
我快冻僵了。I am freezing.
我穿着一件薄外套。I am wearing a thin jacket.
我的外套一点也不保暖。My jacket is not warm at all.
我也穿着单薄的鞋子。I am also wearing thin shoes.
我没有戴保暖的围巾。I am not wearing a warm scarf.
我的手非常冷。My hands are very cold.
我在发抖。I am shivering.
丹麦的冬天很难熬。The winter in Denmark is hard.
我需要保暖的衣服。I need warm clothes.
我需要一件厚毛衣。I need a thick sweater.
我还需要一条大围巾。I also need a large scarf.
而且我还需要保暖的手套。And I need warm gloves.
我走得非常快。I walk very fast.
我下班后要去买冬装。I am buying winter clothes after work.
entering the shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things.
我下班了。I am finished at work.
我沿着步行街走。I walk down the pedestrian street.
风还是很冷。The wind is still very cold.
我看到一家很大的服装店。I see a large clothing shop.
店里非常明亮。The shop is very bright.
我走进店里。I go into the shop.
这里面暖和极了。It is wonderfully warm in here.
一位店员站在收银台后面。A shop assistant stands behind the register.
她看着我笑了。She looks at me and smiles.
“你好，”她说。"Hello," she says.
“你好，”我回答。"Hello," I answer.
“我冷极了，”我说。"I am freezing a lot," I say.
“我只穿了一件薄外套。”"I only have a thin jacket on."
“我想找些保暖的东西。”"I would like to find some warm things."
店员又笑了。The shop assistant smiles again.
“今天确实也很冷，”她说。"It is also very cold today," she says.
“我们有很多厚毛衣。”"We have many thick sweaters."
“我们也有保暖的冬装。”"We also have warm winter clothes."
“您随便看看，”她亲切地说。"You just take a look," she says kindly.
“非常感谢，”我回答。"Many thanks," I answer.
the red sweater
Storyboard: Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it.
我看着架子上的衣服。I look at the clothes on the shelves.
我看到一件厚毛衣。I see a thick sweater.
它是红色的。It is red.
我需要一件保暖的毛衣。I need a warm sweater.
这家店有很多尺码。The shop has many sizes.
我找到一件中号的。I find a size medium.
我走进试衣间。I walk into the fitting room.
我穿上这件红色的毛衣。I put the red sweater on.
我穿着它，照了照那面大镜子。I have it on and look in the big mirror.
这件毛衣非常合身。The sweater fits perfectly.
它非常暖和。It is incredibly warm.
我很喜欢这种红色。I love the red colour.
我看了看价签。I look at the price tag.
这件毛衣要三百克朗。The sweater costs three hundred kroner.
对于一件厚毛衣来说，这个价格很划算。It is a good price for a thick sweater.
我又把毛衣脱了下来。I take the sweater off again.
我买下它了。I buy it.
the tight green coat
Storyboard: Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a larger size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. The assistant brings her a warm dark-blue winter jacket in her size instead — it fits perfectly, and Maya decides to take it.
我还需要一件冬装外套。I also need a winter jacket.
我在店里看更多的衣服。I look at more clothes in the shop.
我找到了一件漂亮的绿色大衣。I find a beautiful, green coat.
它的设计非常漂亮。It is a very beautiful design.
我穿上那件绿色大衣。I put the green coat on.
但它太小了。But it is too small.
肩膀部位非常紧。It is very tight across the shoulders.
“你们有别的尺码吗？”我问。"Do you have it in another size?" I ask.
店员看了看。The shop assistant looks.
“很遗憾，没有了，”她回答。"No, unfortunately," she answers.
于是我把那件绿色大衣挂了回去。So I hang the green coat back.
店员给我找了另一件外套。The shop assistant finds another jacket for me.
这是一件保暖的深蓝色冬装外套。It is a warm, dark-blue winter jacket.
我穿上那件深蓝色外套。I put the dark-blue jacket on.
它非常合身。It fits perfectly.
它又大又舒服，一点也不紧。It is nice and big and not tight.
我照着一面大镜子。I look in a large mirror.
我很喜欢它。I like it.
“我要这件了，”我说。"I will take it," I say.
the blue scarf
Storyboard: Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases.
我走到店里的一张桌子旁。I walk over to a table in the shop.
桌子上有很多东西。There are many things on the table.
我今天没有戴围巾。I am not wearing a scarf today.
我觉得脖子很冷。I am very cold around my neck.
我看到一条蓝色的围巾。I see a blue scarf.
它是一条很长的围巾。It is a very long scarf.
它是羊毛做的。It is made of wool.
我拿起那条蓝色的围巾。I take the blue scarf.
我把它围在脖子上。I put it around my neck.
感觉非常暖和。It feels wonderfully warm.
“这个颜色很好看，”店员说。"It is a nice colour," the shop assistant says.
“是的，而且非常柔软，”我说。"Yes, and it is very soft," I say.
我看了看价格标签。I look at the price tag.
它要一百五十克朗。It costs one hundred and fifty kroner.
对于一条保暖的围巾来说，这个价格不错。It is a good price for a warm scarf.
我又把它摘了下来。I take it off again.
“这个我也买，”我对她说。"I am buying it too," I say to her.
我把蓝色围巾和红色毛衣放在了一起。I put the blue scarf together with the red sweater.
the black gloves
Storyboard: Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well.
我还穿着我的薄外套。I am still wearing my thin jacket.
我想起了今天早上我冰冷的双手。I think about my cold hands from this morning.
我需要厚实的手套。I need thick gloves.
我环顾这家明亮的商店。I look around the bright shop.
我看到一个大篮子。I see a large basket.
篮子里装满了手套。The basket is full of gloves.
我找到了一副黑色的冬季手套。I find a pair of black winter gloves.
它们非常厚实柔软。They are very thick and soft.
我戴上手套。I put the gloves on.
我戴着正合适。They fit me perfectly.
我问那位面带微笑的店员。I ask the smiling shop assistant.
“这副黑手套多少钱？”我问道。"What do the black gloves cost?" I ask.
“一百二十克朗，”她回答。"They cost one hundred and twenty kroner," she answers.
一百二十克朗是个好价钱。One hundred and twenty kroner is a good price.
我的手已经暖和起来了。My hands are already warm.
“我要了，”我高兴地说。"I will take them," I say happily.
我又把手套摘了下来。I take the gloves off again.
我把黑手套跟我的红毛衣和蓝围巾放在一起。I put the black gloves together with my red sweater and my blue scarf.
现在我准备好付款了。Now I am ready to pay.
paying for the clothes
Storyboard: Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the dark-blue winter jacket, the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is one thousand five hundred kroner, and Maya pays with her card.
我带着我的新衣服走到收银台前。I walk over to the counter with my new clothes.
店员友好地对我微笑。The shop assistant smiles kindly at me.
“就这些吗？”她问。"Is that everything?" she asks.
“是的，就这些，”我回答。"Yes, that is everything," I answer.
她扫描了我所有的新东西。She scans all my new things.
有一件毛衣、一件外套、一条围巾和一副手套。There is a sweater, a jacket, a scarf and gloves.
“现在你不会挨冻了，”她高兴地说。"Now you will not freeze," she says happily.
“一共是一千五百克朗，谢谢。”"That will be one thousand five hundred kroner, please."
衣服很贵，但是很保暖。The clothes are expensive, but they are very warm.
我拿出我的银行卡。I take out my payment card.
“我用卡支付，”我说。"I am paying by card," I say.
“你直接在这里付款就行，”她说。"You can just pay here," she says.
她指着一台黑色的小机器。She points at a small, black machine.
我把卡放在机器上方。I hold my card over the machine.
它“滴”了一声，付款通过了。It beeps, and the payment is approved.
“你需要小票吗？”店员问。"Do you want the receipt?" the shop assistant asks.
“不用了，谢谢，”我回答。"No thank you," I answer.
现在我拥有了许多很棒的冬装。Now I own a lot of nice winter clothes.
stepping into the cold
Storyboard: Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She pulls on the red sweater, zips her new dark-blue winter jacket over it, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm.
“需要袋子吗？”店员问。"A bag?" the shop assistant asks.
“不用了，谢谢，”我说。"No thank you," I say.
我不需要袋子。I do not need a bag.
我立刻穿上了新衣服。I put the new clothes on right away.
首先，我穿上那件厚厚的红色毛衣。First, I put on the thick, red sweater.
然后，我穿上那件深蓝色的冬季外套。Then, I put on the dark-blue winter jacket.
我拉上这件保暖外套的拉链。I zip the warm jacket.
接着，我拿起那条长长的蓝色围巾。Afterwards, I take the long, blue scarf.
我把它绕在脖子上。I wrap it around my neck.
最后，我戴上那副新的黑色手套。Finally, I put on the new, black gloves.
现在，我穿上了所有保暖的衣服。Now I have all my warm clothes on.
我微笑着说再见。I smile and say goodbye.
我走出商店。I walk out of the shop.
我再次站在寒冷的街道上。I stand out on the cold street again.
天气冰冷刺骨。It is freezing cold.
风刮得很大。The wind blows hard.
但我一点也不觉得冷。But I am not freezing at all.
现在我准备好迎接冬天了。Now I am ready for winter.
