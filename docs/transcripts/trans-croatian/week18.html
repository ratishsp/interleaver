Week 18: Maya gets a spring allergy

waking up unwell
Storyboard: On Thursday morning, Maya wakes up and feels terrible. Her head is heavy, her eyes itch, and her throat is dry. She decides she ought to stay home from work.

Zapravo me malo boli kad trepćem.It actually hurts a little when I blink.
Pokušavam popiti malo vode iz čaše na svom stolu.I try to drink some water from the glass on my table.
Ali grlo mi je potpuno suho.But my throat is completely dry.
Boli me grlo kad gutam.It hurts in my throat when I swallow.
Gledam na mobitel da vidim koliko je sati.I look at my phone to see what time it is.
Pola je osam i obično u ovo vrijeme idem autobusom.It is half past seven, and I normally take the bus at this time.
Ali danas uopće nemam energije.But I have no energy at all today.
Ne osjećam se samo umorno, već i stvarno bolesno.I do not just feel tired, but really sick.
Danas bih definitivno trebala ostati kod kuće i ne ići na posao.I definitely ought to stay home from work today.
Pišem kratku poruku svom kolegi Peteru.I write a short message to my colleague Peter.
Objašnjavam da sam bolesna i da neću doći u ured.I explain that I am sick and will not be coming into the office.
Brzo odgovara da se samo opustim i ozdravim.He replies quickly that I should just relax and get well.
Trebala bih nazvati ambulantu kada otvore.I ought to call the medical clinic when they open.
Ali upravo sada želim samo zatvoriti oči i odmoriti svoju tešku glavu.But right now I just want to close my eyes and rest my heavy head.
calling the clinic

Pronalazim broj telefona svoje lokalne ambulante.I find the phone number for my local medical clinic.
Broj je trideset dva, četrdeset pet, šezdeset sedam, osamdeset devet.The number is thirty-two, forty-five, sixty-seven, eighty-nine.
Zovem ih i čekam nekoliko minuta.I call them and wait a few minutes.
Ljubazna recepcionarka javlja se na telefon.A friendly receptionist answers the phone.
"Ambulanta, razgovarate s Hannom", kaže ona."Medical clinic, you are speaking with Hanna," she says.
Objašnjavam da sam se danas jako razboljela.I explain that I have become really sick today.
"Glava mi je teška i svrbe me oči", kažem."My head is heavy, and my eyes itch," I say.
Također joj govorim da me boli grlo.I also tell her that my throat hurts.
Pita me imam li temperaturu.She asks if I have a fever.
Odgovaram da mislim da nemam, ali sam jako umorna.I answer that I do not think so, but I am very tired.
"Trebali biste danas razgovarati s liječnikom", kaže recepcionarka."You ought to speak with a doctor today," the receptionist says.
Gleda u kalendar kako bi pronašla termin.She looks in the calendar to find an appointment.
"Možete li doći u jedan sat?" pita ona."Can you come at one o'clock?" she asks.
"Da, to mi savršeno odgovara", odgovaram."Yes, that fits perfectly," I reply.
"Ne zaboravite ponijeti svoju žutu zdravstvenu iskaznicu", kaže ona."Remember to bring your yellow health card," she says.
Zahvaljujem joj i prekidam vezu.I thank her and hang up.
Sada se samo trebam malo opustiti prije nego što odem pješice do tamo.Now I just need to relax a bit before I walk over there.
scanning health card
Storyboard: Maya walks slowly to the clinic. In the reception area, she scans the yellow health card she received when she moved to Denmark to check in for her appointment.
Malo je prošlo jedan sat.It is a little past one o'clock.
Polako hodam do klinike.I walk slowly over to the medical clinic.
To je kratak put, ali sam još uvijek jako bolna i umorna.It is a short trip, but I am still very sore and tired.
Vadim svoju žutu zdravstvenu iskaznicu.I take out my yellow health card.
Vani sja sunce.The sun is shining outside.
Ali trebala bih nositi sunčane naočale, jer me od svjetlosti bole oči.But I ought to wear sunglasses, because the light hurts my eyes.
Ulazim na recepciju klinike.I enter the clinic's reception.
Na zidu visi mali aparat.A small machine hangs on the wall.
Ovdje se uvijek mora skenirati žuta zdravstvena iskaznica.You always have to scan your yellow health card here.
Dobila sam iskaznicu poštom kada sam se preselila u Dansku.I got the card in the mail when I moved to Denmark.
Skeniram žutu iskaznicu na aparatu.I scan the yellow card on the machine.
Ispušta tihi zvuk i zatreperi zeleno.It makes a little sound and flashes green.
To znači da liječnik zna da sam ovdje.That means that the doctor knows I am here.
Ulazim u samu čekaonicu.I walk into the actual waiting room.
Sjedam na slobodan stolac i čekam.I sit down on an empty chair and wait.
waiting room boy
Storyboard: She sits in the quiet waiting room near a little boy. The boy complains loudly that his ear hurts, while his mother gently tries to comfort him.
Sjedam na stolicu u čekaonici.I sit down on a chair in the waiting room.
Prostorija je gotovo potpuno tiha.The room is almost completely quiet.
Strpljivo čekam.I wait patiently.
Glava mi je i dalje jako teška.My head is still very heavy.
Majka i mali dječak sjede preko puta mene.A mother and a little boy sit opposite me.
Dječak ima možda pet godina.The boy is maybe five years old.
Drži ruku na desnom uhu.He holds a hand on his right ear.
"Jao, boli me uho!" kaže on glasno."Ouch, my ear hurts!" he says loudly.
Njegova majka ga gleda.His mother looks at him.
"Znam, dušo," kaže ona nježno."I know, sweetie," she says gently.
"Ali jako boli!" žali se dječak."But it hurts a lot!" the boy complains.
"Liječnik bi nas trebao uskoro pozvati," kaže majka."The doctor ought to call us soon," the mother says.
Ona mu stavlja ruku oko ramena.She puts an arm around his shoulders.
Dječak glasno uzdahne.The boy sighs loudly.
Izgleda jako umorno.He looks very tired.
Dobro ga razumijem.I understand him well.
I moje grlo također jako boli.My own throat really hurts too.
Samo se želim opet osjećati dobro.I just want to feel well again.
explaining the pain
Storyboard: The friendly doctor calls her in. Maya sits in his bright office and explains exactly where it hurts, pointing to her scratchy throat, her heavy head, and her red eyes.
Ljubazni doktor otvara vrata i zove moje ime.A friendly doctor opens the door and calls my name.
Ulazim u njegovu svijetlu ordinaciju.I go into his bright office.
Sjedamo za njegov radni stol.We sit down at his desk.
"Kako se osjećate danas?" pita on."How are you feeling today?" he asks.
"Osjećam se jako loše," odgovaram."I feel really bad," I answer.
Doktor me gleda.The doctor looks at me.
"Gdje vas boli?" pita on ljubazno."Where does it hurt?" he asks kindly.
Pokazujem na grlo.I point to my throat.
"Jako me boli grlo," objašnjavam."My throat hurts a lot," I explain.
"Jako je suho.""It is very dry."
Doktor kima glavom i pažljivo sluša.The doctor nods and listens attentively.
"Glava mi je također jako teška," nastavljam."My head is also very heavy," I continue.
Dodirujem lice.I touch my face.
"I oči su mi potpuno crvene.""And my eyes are completely red."
Stalno me svrbe.They itch all the time.
"Svakako biste se trebali odmarati," kaže on."You absolutely ought to rest," he says.
Pali malu svjetiljku.He turns on a small flashlight.
"Dopustite da vas malo bolje pogledam," kaže on."Let me take a closer look at you," he says.
spring allergy diagnosis
Storyboard: The doctor listens to her breathing and looks at her throat. He explains it is a common spring allergy she has never experienced before, and says she ought to buy some mild medicine.
Liječnik me moli da otvorim usta.The doctor asks me to open my mouth.
"Recite a", kaže on uz osmijeh."Say ah," he says with a smile.
Pažljivo mi pregledava grlo.He looks carefully at my throat.
Nakon toga sluša moje disanje.Afterward, he listens to my breathing.
Pita me osjećam li se loše negdje drugdje.He asks if I feel unwell anywhere else.
Kažem mu da me boli i glava.I tell him that my head also hurts.
"To nije prehlada", kaže on."It is not a cold," he says.

Malo sam iznenađena.I am a little surprised.
Nikada prije nisam imala alergije.I have never had allergies before.
Kaže mi da je to potpuno normalno.He tells me that it is completely normal.
Drveće i cvijeće ovdje su novi za moje tijelo.The trees and flowers here are new to my body.
"Trebali biste kupiti neki blagi lijek u ljekarni", kaže liječnik."You ought to buy some mild medicine at the pharmacy," the doctor says.
Obećava da će brzo pomoći.He promises that it will help quickly.
"Tada ćete se opet osjećati dobro", dodaje on."Then you will feel well again," he adds.
Jako mi je laknulo.I am very relieved.
Boli me grlo, ali srećom nije ozbiljno.My throat hurts, but fortunately it is not serious.
pharmacy visit

skipping badminton

healthy saturday morning
