# Week 21 — Storyboard (Maya buys summer clothes and makes an exchange)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Comparatives (**größer als, kleiner als**); **umtauschen** (exchanging)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_warm_may_afternoon | On a beautifully warm Saturday afternoon in May, Maya finishes her volunteer shift at the café and returns to the flat. With the weather warming up and her thick winter coat officially packed away, she realizes her wardrobe is unprepared for the coming season. She decides it is the perfect afternoon to shop for a light summer jacket and a new shirt. |
| 2 | 02_cycling_to_town | Maya takes her bicycle and rides into a lively shopping district in the city center. The streets are bustling. She locks her bike to a rack and heads into a large, well-lit clothing boutique that caught her eye. |
| 3 | 03_comparing_jackets | Inside, Maya browses a rack of light jackets. She pulls out a blue one and a green one to compare them. Noting the fit and style, she sees that the green jacket is a bit longer and much lighter than the blue one. Delighted with the green option, she drapes it over her arm. |
| 4 | 04_picking_a_shirt | Next, she searches for a T-shirt for everyday wear. She spots a cheerful yellow T-shirt on a display table. The queue for the fitting rooms is longer than the queue at the till, Maya decides to skip trying it on, assuming her usual size will fit just fine, and heads toward the cash register. |
| 5 | 05_the_checkout | At the till, Maya pays for the jacket and the T-shirt in Euro, carefully ensuring she receives her receipt just in case. She packs her new clothes neatly into her backpack and heads home, eager to try them on properly. |
| 6 | 06_too_small_at_home | Back at the flat, Maya tries on her purchases. The green jacket is perfect, but as she pulls the yellow T-shirt over her head, she realizes it is far too tight across the shoulders. It is noticeably smaller than her other shirts. She knows she will have to go back and exchange it (umtauschen). |
| 7 | 07_returning_to_the_shop | Later that afternoon, Maya takes her bicycle back to the boutique. She approaches the customer service desk, greets the assistant — a woman, the same one through scenes 7 to 10 — and explains that she bought the shirt earlier today but unfortunately needs to exchange it (umtauschen). She places the shirt and the receipt on the counter. |
| 8 | 08_asking_for_bigger | The same assistant checks the receipt and asks only for the size: Maya bought M and needs one number larger, so L. The assistant goes to look. The assistant smiles, takes the small shirt, and heads into the stockroom to find the correct size. |
| 9 | 09_the_second_fitting | The same assistant returns with the larger size. This time, Maya does not skip the fitting room. She goes into a cubicle and tries on the new yellow T-shirt. She looks in the mirror and is relieved to see that it fits perfectly—it is so much better than the first one (besser als). |
| 10 | 10_successful_exchange | Maya returns to the desk and confirms to the same assistant that the new size is exactly right. The assistant processes the exchange quickly in the system and wishes her a pleasant weekend. Maya tucks the new T-shirt into her backpack and steps back out into the warm May afternoon, finally ready for the Berlin summer. |
