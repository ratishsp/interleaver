Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
Januar je in zelo je mrzlo.It is January, and it is very cold.
Hodim domov z avtobusne postaje v Københavnu.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Na ulici vidim cvetličarno.I see a flower shop on the street.
Notri je svetlo in toplo.It is bright and warm inside.
Vstopim v toplo trgovino.I go into the warm shop.
Gledam majhno zeleno rastlino.I look at a small, green plant.
Prodajalec se mi nasmehne.A clerk smiles at me.
"Zdravo," reče."Hello," he says.
"Zdravo," rečem."Hello," I say.
"Iščem rastlino.""I am looking for a plant."
"Je to darilo?" vpraša."Is it a gift?" he asks.
"Ne, za mojo sobo je," odgovorim."No, it is for my room," I answer.
"To je res dobra rastlina," reče."It is a really good plant," he says.
"Koliko stane?" vprašam."How much does it cost?" I ask.
"Stane petinpetdeset kron," odgovori."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
Plačam svojo novo rastlino.I pay for my new plant.
Rastlino položi v papirnato vrečko.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
"Izvoli, tu je tvoja vrečka," reče."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
"Najlepša hvala," veselo rečem."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Grem ven v mrzel večer.I go out into the cold evening.
Moja majhna rastlina me zelo razveseli.My little plant makes me very happy.
evening prep
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her temporary room and sets the new plant on a small desk. With it already being pitch black outside, she turns on a warm lamp to reflect against the dark window, makes a cup of tea, and flips open her laptop to dial home.
Zdaj sem doma.I am home now.
Tukaj je moja soba.Here is my room.
To je majhna soba.It is a small room.
Imam novo rastlino.I have a new plant.
Stoji na moji pisalni mizi.It stands on my desk.
Večer je in je januar.It is evening, and it is January.
Moje okno je zelo temno.My window is very dark.
Zunaj je povsem črno.It is completely black outside.
Imam svetilko in skodelico čaja.I have a lamp and a cup of tea.
Prižgem svojo svetilko.I turn on my lamp.
Zdaj je moja soba topla in svetla.Now my room is warm and bright.
Tudi moj čaj je topel.My tea is also warm.
Sedim za svojo pisalno mizo.I sit at my desk.
Tukaj imam svoj računalnik.I have my computer here.
Odprem svoj računalnik.I open my computer.
Zdaj pokličem svojo družino v Mehiki.Now I call my family in Mexico.
Veselim se, da jih bom videla.I look forward to seeing them.
parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
Nekajkrat zazvoni.It rings a couple of times.
Zaslon zasveti.The screen lights up.
Vidim dnevno sobo.I see a living room.
Sije sonce in dnevna soba je zelo svetla.There is sun, and the living room is very bright.
Vidim dve osebi.I see two people.
To sta moja mama in moj oče.It is my mother and my father.
Zelo se nasmihata in mi mahata.They smile a lot and wave to me.
"Živijo, mami in oči!" rečem na glas."Hello mom and dad!" I say loudly.
"Živijo, Maya," reče moj oče."Hello, Maya," my father says.
"Kako gre?""How is it going?"
"Dobro gre," veselo odgovorim."It is going well," I answer happily.
Nenadoma zaslišim zvok.Suddenly I hear a sound.
To je droben lajež.It is a little bark.
"O, slišim psa!" rečem in se zasmejem."Oh, I hear a dog!" I say and laugh.
Majhen pes skoči na kavč.A little dog jumps up onto a sofa.
Pogleda v kamero.It looks into the camera.
"Tam je moj pes!""There is my dog!"
"Živijo, Lola," rečem."Hello, Lola," I say.
Lola maha z repom.Lola wags her tail.
"Ja, to je tvoj pes," reče moja mama z nasmehom."Yes, it is your dog," my mother says with a smile.
Rada imam svojo družino.I love my family.
Lepo jih je vse videti.It is lovely to see them all.
the siblings

"To je knjiga z moje univerze.""It is a book from my university."

mexico garden
