Week 20: Maya starts volunteering at the café

Croatian with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding the volunteering
Storyboard: Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. One evening she browses a volunteering website, reads a few listings, and finds the little café she already knows, which needs weekend helpers, so she fills in the short sign-up form.
Čula sam da mnogi Danci volontiraju u slobodno vrijeme.I have heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time.
Sviđa mi se ta ideja.I like the idea.
Želim naučiti bolje govoriti danski i upoznati nove ljude.I want to learn to speak better Danish and meet new people.
Jedne večeri sjedim za svojim računalom.One evening, I am sitting with my computer.
Pronalazim internetsku stranicu za volonterski rad.I find a website for voluntary work.
Čitam prva tri oglasa na stranici.I read the first three listings on the site.
Prvi oglas je iz sportskog kluba.The first listing is from a sports club.
Ali trebaju pomoć u četvrtak navečer, a tada igram badminton.But they need help on Thursday evenings, and I play badminton then.
Drugi oglas je iz muzeja.The second listing is from a museum.
Trebaju osobu u srijedu navečer, ali tada treniram.They need a person on Wednesday evenings, but I work out then.
Zatim gledam treći oglas.Then I look at the third listing.
On je iz malog kafića koji već poznajem.It is from the little café that I already know.
Trebaju ljude za subotu ujutro.They need people for Saturday mornings.
To mi apsolutno savršeno odgovara.That suits me absolutely perfectly.
Ispunjavam kratki obrazac sa svojim imenom i adresom e-pošte.I fill in a short form with my name and my email address.
Također upisujem svoj broj telefona: dvanaest, trideset četiri, pedeset šest, sedamdeset osam.I also write my phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight.
Na kraju pritisnem pošalji.Finally, I press send.
Nikada prije nisam radila u kafiću, ali spremna sam to naučiti.I have never worked in a café before, but I am ready to learn it.
first shift arrival
Storyboard: On her very first Saturday shift, Maya arrives early at the little café. She ties her new apron in the back room and carries the chairs out to the pavement to prepare for opening.
To je moja prva subotnja smjena u malom kafiću.It is my first Saturday shift at the little café.
Stigla sam rano.I have arrived early.

Veselim se tome, ali sam i malo nervozna.I am looking forward to it, but I am also a little nervous.
Ovo je prvi put da radim u kafiću.It is the first time I am working in a café.
Ušla sam u stražnju prostoriju da se pripremim.I went into the back room to get ready.
Na stolu je ležala nova pregača za mene.On a table lay a new apron for me.
Obukla sam je i zavezala na leđima.I put it on and tied it in the back.
Bio je dobar osjećaj nositi je.It felt good to wear it.
Sada sam spremna početi.Now I am ready to begin.
Moram naučiti kako ispravno otvoriti kafić.I have to learn to open the café correctly.

Nakon toga sam izašla na pločnik kako bih sve pripremila.Afterwards, I went out onto the pavement to get ready.
Iznijela sam četiri stolice.I carried four chairs out.
Postavila sam ih oko malog stola.I have placed them around a little table.
Uskoro će stići ostali volonteri.In a little while, the other volunteers will arrive.
Jako sam uzbuđena zbog svog prvog dana.I am very excited about my first day.
learning the machine
Storyboard: Before the doors open, an experienced volunteer brings Maya over to the large espresso machine. The volunteer shows her how to grind the coffee beans and how to steam the milk. Maya watches every step closely, because it is her first time behind the counter.
Ovo mi je prvi put iza pulta.It is my first time behind the counter.
Kafić još nije otvoren.The café is not open yet.
Jedan od iskusnih volontera me zove.One of the experienced volunteers calls for me.
Stojimo ispred velikog aparata za espresso.We stand in front of the large espresso machine.
"Danas moraš naučiti praviti kavu", kaže on."Today you must learn to make coffee," he says.
Nikada prije nisam pravila kavu na takvom aparatu.I have never made coffee on such a machine before.
Prvi korak je samljeti zrna kave.The first step is to grind the coffee beans.
Pritišće mali gumb.He presses a small button.
Aparat melje smeđa zrna uz glasan zvuk.The machine grinds the brown beans with a loud sound.
Drugi korak je zapjeniti mlijeko.The second step is to steam the milk.
Ulijeva hladno mlijeko u metalni vrč.He pours cold milk into a metal jug.
Nakon toga uključuje vruću paru.Afterwards he turns on the hot steam.
Za to vrijeme pozorno gledam njegove ruke.I look closely at his hands meanwhile.
Mlijeko u vrču brzo postaje toplo i gusto.The milk in the jug quickly becomes warm and thick.
Očito je već mnogo puta prije pravio kavu.He has clearly made coffee many times before.
Kad sam jutros stigla, bila sam jako nervozna.When I arrived this morning, I was very nervous.
Ali on je to dobro objasnio, pa sam većinu toga već razumjela.But he explained it well, so I have already understood most of it.
the first coffee
Storyboard: When the morning rush hits, Maya makes a cappuccino for a waiting customer. She has held the steam wand a bit too deep in the milk, so it does not get a thick foam. It still looks acceptable, though, so she pours the milk over the coffee and hands it over with a polite smile.
Jutros smo imali nevjerojatno puno posla.This morning we have been incredibly busy.
Upravo sam napravila svoj prvi cappuccino.I have just made my very first cappuccino.
Malo prije deset, jedan je muškarac prišao pultu.A little before ten, a man came over to the counter.
"Jedan cappuccino, molim," rekao je."A cappuccino, please," he said.
Tek sam naučila koristiti aparat za espresso.I have just learned to use the espresso machine.
Tako da sam odmah otišla do aparata.So I went over to the machine right away.
Skuhala sam kavu i stavila cijev za paru u mlijeko.I brewed the coffee and put the steam wand down into the milk.
Ali držala sam je malo preduboko.But I held it a little too deep down.
Vjerojatno sam bila malo previše nervozna.I have probably been a little too nervous.
Nažalost, mlijeko nije dobilo gustu pjenu.Unfortunately, the milk did not get any thick foam.
Oprezno sam pogledala u vrč.I looked carefully down into the pitcher.
Pjena nije bila sasvim savršena.The foam was not completely perfect.
Ali je još uvijek izgledala prihvatljivo.But it still looked acceptable.
Prelila sam vruće mlijeko preko kave u šalici.I poured the hot milk over the coffee in the cup.
Zatim sam s uljudnim osmijehom dala kavu muškarcu.Then I gave the coffee to the man with a polite smile.
"Izvolite, to će biti pedeset pet kruna," rekla sam."Here you go, that will be fifty-five kroner," I said.
Uzeo je šalicu i sjeo za prvi stol.He took the cup and sat down at the first table.
bun with cheese
Storyboard: A moment later, she slices a warm sourdough bun and adds a thick piece of cheese. She places it on a small plate and navigates through the standing customers to deliver it safely to a corner booth.
Ubrzo nakon toga, stigla je sljedeća narudžba.Shortly after, the next order came.
Danas sam već napravila svoju prvu kavu.I have already made my first coffee today.
Ali ovaj je kupac htio samo hranu.But this customer just wanted food.
Naručio je pecivo sa sirom.He ordered a bun with cheese.
Uzela sam toplo pecivo od kiselog tijesta iz košare.I took a warm sourdough bun from the basket.
Kruh od kiselog tijesta često ima tvrdu koru.Sourdough bread often has a hard crust.
Ali naučila sam brzo prerezati kruh na pola.But I have learned to cut the bread in half quickly.
Nakon toga stavila sam debelu krišku sira na pecivo.Afterwards, I put a thick slice of cheese on the bun.
Lijepo sam složila hranu na mali tanjur.I arranged the food nicely on a small plate.
Sada sam to morala odnijeti kupcu.Now I had to deliver it to the customer.
Mnogo je ljudi stajalo usred kafića.There were many people standing in the middle of the café.
Balansirala sam tanjurom i oprezno hodala naprijed.I balanced the plate and walked carefully forward.
Polako sam prošla pored ostalih gostiju.I walked slowly past the other guests.
Srećom, kupac je sjedio skroz u kutu.Fortunately, the customer was sitting right down in the corner.

Stavila sam tanjur na stol ispred njega.I placed the plate on the table in front of him.
"Izvolite, toplo pecivo sa sirom", rekla sam."Here you go, a warm bun with cheese," I said.
"Puno hvala", odgovorio je muškarac."Thank you very much," the man replied.

the ginger beer
Storyboard: During her second Saturday shift, a woman asks for a ginger beer. Maya finds the ginger beer in the glass fridge, inverts it so that the ginger mixes. Then she hands it to the customer.
Bila je to moja druga subotnja smjena u kafiću.It was my second Saturday shift in the café.
Već sam naučila raditi velik dio posla.I have already learned to do a lot of the work.
Tog je jutra vani bilo predivno toplo.That morning it was wonderfully warm outside.
Jedna je žena prišla pultu.A woman came over to the counter.
"Molila bih jedno pivo od đumbira", rekla je."I would like to ask for a ginger beer," she said.
"Naravno, samo trenutak", odgovorila sam."Of course, just a moment," I answered.
Otišla sam do velikog staklenog hladnjaka iza pulta.I went over to the large glass fridge behind the counter.
Pogledala sam na treću policu.I looked on the third shelf.

second coffee progress
Storyboard: Later that morning, Maya tries foaming milk again for a large latte. This second attempt turns out much better, and she realizes that she has finally learned to control the pressure of the hot steam.

third saturday regular
