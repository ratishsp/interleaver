# Week 26 — Storyboard (Maya's first Danish summer and thirty-second birthday)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Perfect vs preteritum contrast

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_birthday_messages | Maya wakes up on the morning she turns thirty-two, feeling a bit nostalgic as it is her first birthday away from Mexico. She smiles as she reads the flood of loving messages that her family has sent overnight while she slept. |
| 2 | 02_office_pastry | At the software company, Maya is surprised when Peter and Eva place a small cake with a single candle on her desk. They wish her a happy birthday. |
| 3 | 03_family_call | That evening, Maya has a lively video call with her parents, Sofía, Diego, and Lola the dog. Sofía eagerly asks if Maya has opened the package they sent, and Maya happily holds up the colorful scarf that arrived in the mail yesterday. |
| 4 | 04_cafe_summer | A few days after her birthday, Maya finishes her Saturday morning café shift, unties her apron and wipes down the last counter. Eager for the big summer bonfire by the sea that evening, she texts Eva to confirm they are still meeting, noting she got off early today unlike last weekend when she stayed late. |
| 5 | 05_waterfront | Back home that evening, Maya gets ready to head down to the waterfront. |
| 6 | 06_bus_stop_contrast | Walking down toward the beach alone, Maya marvels at how bright the evening sky still is. She thinks back to January, when she stood at the bus stop after work in pitch darkness at four o'clock, freezing — a sharp contrast with tonight, when the sun has not even started to set. |
| 7 | 07_beach_bonfire | At the beach Maya meets Eva and Peter just as the crowd lights the towering summer bonfire. Eva shares fresh strawberries she bought at the market, and the glowing fire chases away the coastal chill as they watch the flames together. |
| 8 | 08_midnight_blue | Strolling back near midnight under a sky that stays soft blue, Maya feels a deep peace. Walking with Eva and Peter, she looks at her friends and realizes how much has changed — she has found real friends and built a life she loves in her new city. |
