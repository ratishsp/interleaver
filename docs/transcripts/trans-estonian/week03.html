Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

Estonian with English gloss, line-aligned.

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
On jaanuar ja on väga külm.It is January, and it is very cold.
Ma kõnnin Kopenhaagenis bussipeatusest koju.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Ma näen tänaval lillepoodi.I see a flower shop on the street.
Sees on valge ja soe.It is bright and warm inside.
Ma lähen sooja poodi sisse.I go into the warm shop.
Ma vaatan väikest rohelist taime.I look at a small, green plant.
Müüja naeratab mulle.A clerk smiles at me.
"Tere," ütleb ta."Hello," he says.
"Tere," ütlen ma."Hello," I say.
"Ma otsin taime.""I am looking for a plant."
"Kas see on kingitus?" küsib ta."Is it a gift?" he asks.
"Ei, see on minu tuppa," vastan ma."No, it is for my room," I answer.
"See on tõesti hea taim," ütleb ta."It is a really good plant," he says.
"Kui palju see maksab?" küsin ma."How much does it cost?" I ask.
"See maksab viiskümmend viis krooni," vastab ta."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
Ma maksan oma uue taime eest.I pay for my new plant.
Ta paneb taime paberkotti.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
"Palun, siin on teie kott," ütleb ta."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
"Suur tänu," ütlen ma rõõmsalt."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Ma lähen välja külma õhtusse.I go out into the cold evening.
Minu väike taim teeb mind väga rõõmsaks.My little plant makes me very happy.
evening prep
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her temporary room and sets the new plant on a small desk. With it already being pitch black outside, she turns on a warm lamp to reflect against the dark window, makes a cup of tea, and flips open her laptop to dial home.
Ma olen nüüd kodus.I am home now.
Siin on minu tuba.Here is my room.
See on väike tuba.It is a small room.
Mul on uus taim.I have a new plant.
See seisab minu kirjutuslaual.It stands on my desk.
On õhtu ja on jaanuar.It is evening, and it is January.
Minu aken on väga pime.My window is very dark.
Väljas on täiesti must.It is completely black outside.
Mul on lamp ja tass teed.I have a lamp and a cup of tea.
Ma lülitan oma lambi sisse.I turn on my lamp.
Nüüd on minu tuba soe ja valge.Now my room is warm and bright.
Minu tee on ka soe.My tea is also warm.
Ma istun oma kirjutuslaua taga.I sit at my desk.
Mul on siin minu arvuti.I have my computer here.
Ma avan oma arvuti.I open my computer.
Nüüd ma helistan oma perele Mehhikosse.Now I call my family in Mexico.
Ma ootan väga nende nägemist.I look forward to seeing them.
parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
See heliseb paar korda.It rings a couple of times.
Ekraan süttib.The screen lights up.
Ma näen elutuba.I see a living room.
Päike paistab ja elutuba on väga valgusküllane.There is sun, and the living room is very bright.
Ma näen kahte inimest.I see two people.
Need on minu ema ja minu isa.It is my mother and my father.
Nad naeratavad palju ja lehvitavad mulle.They smile a lot and wave to me.
"Tere, ema ja isa!" ütlen ma valjusti."Hello mom and dad!" I say loudly.
"Tere, Maya," ütleb minu isa."Hello, Maya," my father says.
"Kuidas läheb?""How is it going?"
"Hästi läheb," vastan ma rõõmsalt."It is going well," I answer happily.
Järsku kuulen ma üht heli.Suddenly I hear a sound.
See on väike haugatus.It is a little bark.
"Oi, ma kuulen koera!" ütlen ma ja naeran."Oh, I hear a dog!" I say and laugh.
Üks väike koer hüppab diivanile.A little dog jumps up onto a sofa.
Ta vaatab kaamerasse.It looks into the camera.
"Seal on minu koer!""There is my dog!"
"Tere, Lola," ütlen ma."Hello, Lola," I say.
Lola liputab saba.Lola wags her tail.
"Jah, see on sinu koer," ütleb minu ema naeratades."Yes, it is your dog," my mother says with a smile.
Ma armastan oma perekonda.I love my family.
On tore neid kõiki näha.It is lovely to see them all.
the siblings
Storyboard: Her younger sister Sofía and older brother Diego squeeze into the frame, playfully shoving each other. Sofía proudly holds up a thick university textbook, while Diego jokingly asks Maya if she is surviving the freezing Danish winter.
Nüüd on minu ekraanil kaks uut nägu.Now there are two new faces on my screen.
See on minu väikeõde Sofía ja suur vend Diego.It is my little sister, Sofía, and my big brother, Diego.
Nad naeravad ja nügivad teineteist natuke.They laugh and push each other a little.
"Tere, väikeõde ja suur vend!" ütlen ma rõõmsalt."Hello little sister and big brother!" I say happily.
"Tere, Maya," ütleb Diego."Hi Maya," says Diego.
Mu vennal on lai naeratus.My brother has a big smile.
"Kas Taanis on külm?" küsib ta."Is it cold in Denmark?" he asks.
"Kas on külm talv?""Is it a cold winter?"
"Jah, see on väga külm talv," naeran ma."Yes, it is a very cold winter," I laugh.
"Aga mul on soe tuba ja tass teed.""But I have a warm room and a cup of tea."
Nüüd tõstab Sofía ekraani ette suure asja.Now Sofía lifts a big thing up in front of the screen.
See on suur raamat.It is a large book.
"Kas sul on seal uus raamat, Sofía?" küsin ma."Do you have a new book there, Sofía?" I ask.
"Jah, see on minu uus raamat," ütleb mu õde uhkelt."Yes, it is my new book," my sister says proudly.
"See on raamat minu ülikoolist.""It is a book from my university."
"See on väga paks raamat!""It is a very thick book!"
Ma naeratan oma vennale ja õele.I smile at my brother and my sister.
Ma igatsen oma peret Mehhikos.I miss my family in Mexico.
Aga nad on siinsamas minu väikesel ekraanil.But they are right here on my little screen.
temporary room tour
Storyboard: Her mother asks for a tour of her current living space. Maya pans the camera around to show her new plant and the small bed, explaining that it is just a temporary room and pointing out the black, sunless window.
Minu ema vaatab ekraani.My mother looks at the screen.
"Kas see on sinu tuba, Maya?" küsib ta."Is that your room, Maya?" she asks.
"Jah, see on minu tuba," vastan ma."Yes, it is my room," I answer.
"Aga see on ajutine tuba.""But it is a temporary room."
Ma tõstan oma arvutit.I lift my computer.
Ma näitan oma väikest tuba oma perele.I show my small room to my family.
"Siin on voodi," ütlen ma."Here is a bed," I say.
"Ja siin on minu uus taim.""And here is my new plant."
"See on väga ilus taim," ütleb minu ema."It is a very nice plant," my mother says.
Ma pööran oma arvutit.I turn my computer.
"Ja siin on aken," ütlen ma."And here is a window," I say.
Minu õde Sofía vaatab minu akent.My sister Sofía looks at my window.
"Aga seal on väga pime," ütleb ta."But it is very dark," she says.
"Jah, seal väljas on täiesti must," vastan ma."Yes, it is completely black out there," I answer.
"Aga suvel on palju päikest.""But in summer there is plenty of sun."
On õhtu ja on külm.It is evening, and it is cold.
"Aga mul on minu lamp," naeratan ma."But I have my lamp," I smile.
"Nii et see on hea tuba.""So it is a good room."
mexico garden
Storyboard: Feeling a bit homesick, Maya asks her father to take the phone outside. He walks out to their sunny garden, showing a large, familiar leafy tree. Maya smiles deeply, feeling warm and comforted by the glimpse of home before they all wave goodbye.
Ma vaatan oma arvutit.I look at my computer.
Ma igatsen natuke oma kodu Mehhikos.I miss my home in Mexico a little bit.
"Isa, milline sinu aed on?" ütlen ma."Dad, how is your garden?" I say.
Mu isa naeratab mulle.My dad smiles at me.
Ta läheb telefoniga õue.He goes outside with the phone.
Seal on suur aed.There is a large garden.
Päike paistab seal palju.The sun is shining a lot there.
"Vaata, Maya," ütleb mu isa."Look, Maya," my dad says.
"Siin on aed ja puu.""Here is the garden and a tree."
See on väga suur puu.It is a very big tree.
See on ilus puu.It is a beautiful tree.
Ma naeratan oma ekraanile.I smile at my screen.
Mul on sees hea ja soe.I feel good and warm inside.
Minu ema, minu õde ja minu vend tulevad samuti õue.My mother, my sister, and my brother come outside too.
Minu koer jookseb puu all.My dog runs under the tree.
Kogu mu pere lehvitab mulle.My whole family waves at me.
"Head aega kõigile!" ütlen ma."Goodbye, everyone!" I say.
"Head aega, Maya," ütlevad nad."Goodbye, Maya," they say.
Ma sulgen oma arvuti.I close my computer.
