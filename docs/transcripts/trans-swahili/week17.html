Week 17: Maya explores Odense in the light spring

spring breeze diary
Storyboard: Maya opens her living room window to feel the warm spring breeze. She sits at her table in the bright evening sunlight and begins to write in her travel diary about her weekend trip.

Jua bado linamulika kwenye meza yangu.The sun is still shining onto my table.
Ninakaa kwenye kiti changu.I sit down on my chair.

Ninapenda kuandika kuhusu mambo niliyopitia nchini Denmaki.I love to write about my experiences in Denmark.
Ninafungua ukurasa mtupu katika kitabu.I open to a blank page in the book.
Ninafikiria kuhusu wikendi yangu ya kupendeza.I think about my fantastic weekend.
Nilikuwa katika safari ndefu jana.I was on a long trip yesterday.
Nilisafiri peke yangu kabisa hadi Odense.I travelled all alone to Odense.
Ilikuwa safari yangu ya kwanza nje ya Copenhagen.It was my first trip outside Copenhagen.
Ninatabasamu, kwa sababu nilikuwa na siku nzuri sana.I smile, because I had a really good day.
Sasa ninataka kuyaandika yote.Now I want to write it all down.
Ninaweka kalamu kwenye karatasi na kuanza hadithi yangu.I put the pen on the paper and begin my story.
sunday train trip
Storyboard: She writes about her Saturday morning excursion. She packed a backpack, bought a warm coffee, and took the fast train out of Copenhagen to visit the city of Odense.
Niliandika katika shajara yangu kuhusu safari yangu ya kwenda Odense.I wrote in my diary about my trip to Odense.
Ilikuwa asubuhi nzuri ya Jumamosi.It was a lovely Saturday morning.
Niliamka mapema saa moja asubuhi.I woke up early at seven o'clock.
Jua lilikuwa likiwaka, na anga lilikuwa la bluu.The sun was shining, and the sky was blue.

Pia nilibeba sweta nene.I also brought a thick sweater.
Hali ya hewa ya msimu wa kuchipua nchini Denmaki inaweza kuwa na baridi kidogo.The spring weather in Denmark can be a bit cold.

Kulikuwa na watu wengi wenye shughuli nyingi ndani.There were many busy people inside.
Nilitembea hadi kwenye kioski kidogo.I walked over to a small kiosk.
Nilinunua kahawa kubwa na ya moto kwa ajili ya safari.I bought a large, warm coffee for the trip.

Niliangalia juu kwenye skrini kubwa.I looked up at the large screen.
Nilipata treni yangu haraka kwenye skrini.I quickly found my train on the screen.
Nilikuwa nikisafiri na treni ya haraka kwenda Odense.I was taking the fast train to Odense.
Treni iliwasili kwenye platifomu saa mbili kamili.The train arrived at the platform exactly at eight o'clock.
Niliingia ndani ya treni na kupata kiti changu kando ya dirisha kubwa.I went into the train and found my seat by a large window.
Nilikunywa kahawa yangu ya moto na kutarajia kwa hamu matembezi hayo.I drank my warm coffee and looked forward to the excursion.
long sea bridge
Storyboard: She describes the spectacular journey as the train crossed the very long bridge over the sea. She looked out the large window and admired the blue water and the small ships.
Treni ilisafiri kwa kasi kupitia mandhari.The train drove quickly through the landscape.
Niliketi kimya kwenye kiti changu.I sat quietly in my seat.
Nilikuwa na kiti kizuri sana cha dirishani.I had a really good window seat.
Baada ya muda mfupi, hatimaye tulifika pwani.After a little while, we finally reached the coast.
Treni iliingia kwenye daraja refu sana juu ya bahari.The train drove out onto a very long bridge over the sea.
Ghafla kulikuwa na maji tu pande zetu zote.Suddenly there was only water all around us.
Daraja lilikuwa juu sana ya maji.The bridge lay high above the water.
Nilitazama nje kupitia dirisha kubwa.I looked out of the large window.
Maji chini yetu yalikuwa ya bluu sana.The water below us was very blue.
Nilistaajabia mandhari nzuri na wazi.I admired the beautiful, open view.
Mbali kule chini, niliona meli ndogo kadhaa.Far below, I saw some small ships.
Zilionekana karibu kama vitu vya kuchezea.They almost looked like toys.

Nilitoa kamera yangu kutoka kwenye begi langu.I took my camera out of my bag.
Nilipiga picha kadhaa za zile meli ndogo.I took a couple of pictures of the small ships.

stressed father train
Storyboard: She watched a stressed father sitting across the aisle. He desperately tried to read his book, but his two young children constantly threw their toys on the floor.
Nilikaa kwenye kiti changu kwenye treni na kutazama huku na huku.I sat in my seat on the train and looked around.
Baba mmoja alikaa mkabala nami.A father sat opposite me.
Aliwaleta watoto wake wawili wadogo.He brought his two small children.
Baba huyo alikuwa na kitabu kikubwa.The father had a big book.
Alitaka sana kusoma kitabu chake.He really wanted to read his book.
Lakini hakupata utulivu sana.But he did not get much peace.
Binti yake alikuwa na mpira mwekundu.His daughter had a red ball.
Mtoto wake wa kiume alikuwa na gari la kuchezea la kijani.His son had a green toy car.
Watoto hawakukaa kwa utulivu hata kidogo.The children did not sit still at all.
Walitupa vitu vyao vya kuchezea sakafuni kila wakati.They constantly threw their toys on the floor.
Baba huyo alishusha pumzi kwa sauti na kufunga kitabu chake.The father sighed loudly and closed his book.
Aliokota vitu hivyo kutoka sakafuni.He picked the things up from the floor.
Aliwarudishia watoto vitu vyao vya kuchezea.He gave the children their toys back.
"Mnapaswa kukaa kwa utulivu," alisema kwa uchovu."You must sit quietly," he said tiredly.
Alifungua kitabu chake tena.He opened his book again.
Dakika mbili baadaye, mpira ulianguka sakafuni.Two minutes later, the ball fell on the floor.
Uligonga kiatu cha kushoto cha baba huyo.It hit the father's left shoe.
Mwanamume huyo aliokota mpira huo tena.The man picked up the ball again.
Nilimtabasamu baba huyo aliyechoka.I smiled at the tired father.
Alitikisa kichwa chake tu na kutazama chini kwenye kitabu chake.He just shook his head and looked down into his book.
naughty little dog
Storyboard: The family also brought a little dog. The dog suddenly jumped out of its basket and ran away with the father's left shoe, making the children laugh loudly at him.

Watoto wawili walimnyooshea kidole.The two children pointed at him.
Waliangaliana na kucheka kwa sauti kubwa.They looked at each other and laughed loudly.
Niliwaangalia kutoka kwenye kiti changu.I looked at them from my seat.
Mimi pia nilicheka kidogo nyuma ya kitabu changu.I also laughed a little behind my book.
Kwa bahati nzuri, hatimaye alimkamata mbwa wake.Fortunately, he finally caught his dog.

crooked colorful houses

Kuta zenye rangi nyingi ziling'aa kwenye jua la majira ya kuchipua.The colorful walls lit up in the spring sun.

Nilisimama na kutazama nyumba hizo zilizopinda kwa muda mrefu.I stood and looked at the crooked houses for a long time.
Kila nyumba ilikuwa na rangi yake yenyewe ya kufurahisha.Each house had its own cheerful colour.
