Week 28: Maya reflects on her settled life in Copenhagen

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

commute reflection
Storyboard: Maya takes her usual morning bus to the software company, enjoying the warm sunlight. She watches the familiar neighborhoods roll by through the window, briefly reflecting on how freezing and completely unfamiliar this exact route felt back in January.
我坐在公交车的窗边。I sit by the window on the bus.
今天的晨光温暖宜人。The morning sun is lovely and warm today.
哥本哈根现在是夏天，这座城市看起来很美。It is summer in Copenhagen, and the city looks beautiful.
我望着窗外熟悉的街道。I look out at the familiar streets.
我们驶过一个绿树成荫的大公园。We drive past a large park with green trees.
人们已经在自行车道上骑车了。People are already cycling on the bike paths.
我每天早上都坐这趟公交车去上班。I take this bus to my work every morning.
路线和一月份时一模一样。The route is exactly the same as in January.
但现在一切都完全不同了。But everything is completely different now.
我记得我来到这座城市的第一个星期。I remember my first week here in the city.
那时天气冰冷刺骨，天色也很暗。It was freezing cold and very dark.
那时我对这个街区一点也不熟悉。I did not know the neighborhood at all back then.
我也对软件公司的新工作感到非常紧张。I was also very nervous about my new job at the software company.
现在我确切地知道我们在哪里。Now I know exactly where we are.
我认出了当地的面包店和广场。I recognize the local baker and the square.
公交车停下来，更多的人上了车。The bus stops, and more people get on.
我微笑着，向后靠在座位上。I smile and lean back in my seat.
我已经准备好迎接在办公室的美好一天。I am ready for a good day at the office.

“看那里，位置不对，”她说。"Look there, the position is wrong," she says.
“你说得对，我得把它往右移，”我说。"You are right, I must move it to the right," I say.
我迅速写了几行代码。I quickly write a few lines of code.
按钮移到了正确的位置。The button moves to the right place.
“太完美了，谢谢你的帮忙，”伊娃温暖地微笑着说。"Perfect, thank you for the help," Eva says and smiles warmly.

thursday badminton

我用尽全力挥动球拍。I swing my racket with all my power.

我在网前站了一会儿喘口气。I stand by the net for a bit to catch my breath.
我流了很多汗，需要休息一下。I am sweating a lot and need a break.
我在包里找到了我的红色水壶。I find my red water bottle in the bag.

在办公室度过了漫长的一天后，活动一下身体感觉棒极了。It is great to use my body after a long day at the office.
quiet errand

“星期三就可以洗好，”他说。"It will be ready on Wednesday," he says.
“一共是两百五十克朗。”"That will be two hundred and fifty kroner."

我把收据放进包里。I put the receipt down into my bag.

我很享受在温暖的午后阳光中漫步。I enjoy walking in the warm afternoon sun.
saturday cafe

我隔着柜台把咖啡和纸袋递给他。I hand the coffee and the bag over the counter to him.
