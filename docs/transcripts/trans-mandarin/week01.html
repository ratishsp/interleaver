Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
飞机现在在哥本哈根。The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
现在是一月。It is January.
我非常激动。I am very excited.
我把书装进包里。I pack my book into my bag.
我旁边有一位乘客。There is a passenger next to me.
他看着我微笑。He looks at me and smiles.
“你好，”他说。"Hello," he says.
“你好，”我说。"Hello," I say.
“你叫什么名字？”他问。"What is your name?" he asks.
“我叫玛雅，”我说。"My name is Maya," I say.
“你来自哪里，玛雅？”他问。"Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
我开心地笑了。I smile happily.
“我来自墨西哥，”我回答。"I come from Mexico," I answer.
“你来自丹麦吗？”我问。"Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
“是的，我来自丹麦，”他说。"Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
“欢迎来到丹麦，”他说。"Welcome to Denmark," he says.
“非常感谢，”我说。"Thank you very much," I say.
现在我们到了。Now we are here.
我准备好了。I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
我现在在外面。I am outside now.
我的行李箱又大又重。My suitcase is big and heavy.
那儿有一辆出租车。There is a taxi.
我把行李箱搬进出租车里。I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
司机是一位友善的男士。The driver is a friendly man.
“地址是哪里？”他问。"What is the address?" he asks.
“公园路十二号，”我说。"It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
他点点头，开车上路。He nods and drives.
“你是游客吗？”司机问。"Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
“不，我不是游客，”我回答。"No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
“你来自哪里？”他问。"Where do you come from?" he asks.
“我来自墨西哥，”我说。"I come from Mexico," I say.
我看着窗外。I look out the window.
我非常自豪。I am very proud.
“我是一名软件工程师，”我说。"I am a software engineer," I say.
“我的新工作就在哥本哈根这里。”"My new job is here in Copenhagen."
他笑了。He smiles.
“真令人兴奋。”"How exciting."
“祝你在丹麦好运，”他说。"Good luck in Denmark," he says.
“非常感谢，”我说。"Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
门开着。The door is open.
门槛很高。The threshold is high.
行李箱非常重！The suitcase is very heavy!
但现在行李箱在走廊里了。But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
这里一片漆黑。It is completely dark here.
开关在哪里？Where is the switch?
墙壁很凉。The wall is cold.
开关在这里吗？Is the switch here?
对，开关在那里！Yes, there is the switch!
现在有光了。Now there is light.
我在公寓里。I am in the apartment.
它很小，但很不错。It is small, but it is nice.
这里有一间客厅。Here is a living room.
那里有一张沙发和一张桌子。There is a sofa and a table.
这是一个带家具的家。It is a furnished home.
行李箱在地板上。The suitcase is on the floor.
我非常累。I am very tired.
但我也很高兴。But I am also happy.
我在哥本哈根！I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
我站在窗边。I am standing by the window.
玻璃很凉。The glass is cold.
现在是一月。It is January.
我向外看。I look out.
哥本哈根今天灰蒙蒙的。Copenhagen is grey today.
外面很冷。It is cold outside.
街上有雪。There is snow on the street.
但这里很暖和。But it is warm here.
这间公寓很好。The apartment is good.
我想到了墨西哥。I think about Mexico.
在墨西哥有阳光。In Mexico, there is sun.
那里也很暖和。It is also warm there.
我有点想念阳光。I miss the sun a little.
但我笑了。But I smile.
这是一座新城市。It is a new city.
我现在在丹麦。I am in Denmark now.
这是一个好的开始。It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
我现在在客厅。I am in the living room now.
这里有一张小沙发。There is a small sofa.
我给在墨西哥的妈妈打电话。I call my mother in Mexico.
“嗨，妈妈，”我说。"Hi Mom," I say.
“嗨，玛雅，”我妈妈说。"Hi Maya," my mother says.
“你在哪里？”她问。"Where are you?" she asks.
“我在哥本哈根，”我回答。"I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
“我现在在公寓里。”"I am in the apartment now."
“那太好了，”我妈妈说。"That is so good," my mother says.
“大吗？”她问。"Is it big?" she asks.
“不，很小，”我回答。"No, it is small," I answer.
“但是很温馨，我很开心。”"But it is cozy, and I am happy."
“冷吗？”她问。"Is it cold?" she asks.
“是的，外面很冷，”我说。"Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
“但这里面很暖和。”"But it is warm in here."
“我很安全。”"I am safe."
“那就好，”我妈妈说。"That is good," my mother says.
我微笑着。I smile.
丹麦是我的新家。Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
我打开了我的房门。I open my door.
我站在楼梯间里。I am standing in the stairwell.
这里很安静。It is quiet.
一扇门开了。A door opens.
一个女人对我微笑。A woman smiles at me.
“你好，”她说。"Hi," she says.
“你叫什么名字？”她问。"What is your name?" she asks.
“我叫玛雅，”我回答。"My name is Maya," I answer.
“你来自哪里？”她问。"Where do you come from?" she asks.
“我来自墨西哥，”我说。"I come from Mexico," I say.
“欢迎来到这栋楼，”那个女人说。"Welcome to the building," the woman says.
“我叫妮娜，”她说。"My name is Nina," she says.
“我是你的邻居。”"I am your neighbour."
妮娜非常友好。Nina is very friendly.
她说话又慢又清楚。She speaks slowly and clearly.
“这是我的门，”她说。"Here is my door," she says.
“我很乐意帮忙。”"I am happy to help."
“你直接敲门就行，”妮娜说。"You just knock," Nina says.
“非常感谢，妮娜，”我回答。"Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
我很高兴。I am very happy.
现在我在哥本哈根有一个朋友了。Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
现在是晚上了。Now it is night.
这是我在哥本哈根的第一晚。It is my first night in Copenhagen.
我在洗手间里。I am in the bathroom.
它非常小。It is very small.
这里有一个洗手池。There is a sink.
我的牙刷是红色的。My toothbrush is red.
我把牙膏挤在牙刷上。I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
我刷牙。I brush my teeth.
水很冷。The water is cold.
我洗脸。I wash my face.
我擦干脸。I dry my face.
我走到我的床边。I go over to my bed.
床很窄。The bed is narrow.
但它又暖和又柔软。But it is warm and soft.
我现在躺在被子里。I am lying under the duvet now.
我的手机放在桌子上。My phone is lying on the table.
我设置了闹钟。I set my alarm.
它明天八点响。It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
我很累，但我准备好了。I am tired, but I am ready.
晚安，哥本哈根。Goodnight, Copenhagen.
