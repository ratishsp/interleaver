Week 25: A Sunday in the city

Spanish with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning calendar
Storyboard: On Sunday morning, Maya makes a fresh pot of coffee and flips her wall calendar to July. She realizes that she moved to Copenhagen exactly six months ago, and decides to celebrate by spending this sunny day exploring the city at her own pace.

Salgo a la cocina.I go out into the kitchen.
Preparo una jarra de café recién hecho.I make a pot of fresh coffee.
El aroma a café es fantástico esta mañana.The smell of coffee is fantastic this morning.
Tengo un calendario en la pared.I have a calendar on the wall.
Paso la página al mes siguiente.I turn the page to the next month.
Ahora es julio.It is July now.
Me mudé a Copenhague en enero.I moved to Copenhagen in January.
Vine aquí hace exactamente seis meses.I came here exactly six months ago.
El tiempo realmente pasa rápido.Time really goes fast.
Mi primer día en esta ciudad fue muy frío.My first day in this city was very cold.
Pero hoy el clima es cálido y hermoso.But today the weather is warm and beautiful.
Quiero celebrar este pequeño aniversario.I want to celebrate this little anniversary.
No tengo planes fijos hoy.I have no fixed plans today.
Tomo mi taza de café y miro hacia la calle.I take my coffee cup and look out over the street.
Quiero salir y explorar la ciudad a mi propio ritmo.I want to go out and explore the city at my own pace.
Espero con ansias disfrutar de estas horas luminosas.I look forward to enjoying these bright hours.
ceramic plates
Storyboard: Maya wanders into a lively second-hand market in a nearby square. She spots two blue ceramic plates on a table, decides these plates are perfect for her kitchen, and buys them from the vendor for forty kroner.
Doy un paseo por una gran plaza de la ciudad.I go for a walk across a large square in the city.
Aquí hay un gran mercado de pulgas todos los domingos.Here there is a large flea market every Sunday.
El sol brilla y mucha gente camina entre las mesas.The sun is shining, and many people are walking around between the tables.
Me detengo junto a una mesa pequeña con muchas cosas diferentes.I stop by a small table with many different things.
Detrás de la mesa hay un vendedor amable.Behind the table stands a friendly vendor.
De repente veo dos platos de cerámica azules.Suddenly I see two blue ceramic plates.
Los tomo y los miro detenidamente.I pick them up and look closely at them.
"¿Cuánto cuestan estos platos?", le pregunto al vendedor."How much do these plates cost?" I ask the vendor.
El vendedor me sonríe.The vendor smiles at me.
"Cuestan solo cuarenta coronas", responde."They cost only forty kroner," he replies.
Es un precio realmente bueno.That is a really good price.
Compré una mesa pequeña para mi cocina hace un mes.I bought a small table for my kitchen a month ago.
Estos platos combinarán perfectamente con esta mesa.These plates will fit perfectly with this table.
"Me gustaría mucho comprarlos", digo."I would very much like to buy them," I say.
Le doy cuarenta coronas al vendedor y pongo los platos con cuidado en mi bolsa.I give the vendor forty kroner and carefully put the plates in my bag.
Siempre es agradable encontrar algo especial un domingo tranquilo.It is always nice to find something special on a quiet Sunday.
winter coat
Storyboard: At a clothing stall, Maya tries on a heavy wool winter coat. She immediately takes it off because this coat is far too warm for summer, laughing with the vendor that she desperately needed something exactly like it five months ago.
Sigo caminando por el mercado y me detengo en un puesto de ropa.I walk further into the market and stop at a clothing stall.
Veo un pesado abrigo de invierno hecho de lana.I see a heavy winter coat made of wool.
Este abrigo es hermoso, pero muy grueso.This coat is beautiful, but very thick.
Me pruebo el abrigo de todos modos.I try the coat on anyway.
Es increíblemente abrigador.It is incredibly warm.
Me lo quito de nuevo inmediatamente.I take it off again immediately.
Es julio, y el sol brilla hoy.It is July, and the sun is shining today.
Una mujer amable está de pie detrás de la mesa y sonríe.A friendly woman stands behind the table and smiles.
"¿Es demasiado abrigador?", pregunta la mujer."Is it too warm?" the woman asks.
"Sí, es un poco demasiado abrigador para el verano", respondo y me río."Yes, it is a bit too warm for the summer," I reply and laugh.
Toco la tela gruesa.I touch the thick fabric.
"¡Pero realmente necesitaba este abrigo de invierno hace cinco meses!", digo."But I really needed this winter coat five months ago!" I say.
La mujer se ríe fuerte.The woman laughs loudly.
"Sí, febrero fue muy frío", dice ella."Yes, February was very cold," she says.
Recuerdo mis primeras semanas en Copenhague.I remember my first weeks in Copenhagen.
En aquel entonces me congelaba constantemente.I was constantly freezing back then.
Afortunadamente, no necesito ropa tan abrigadora en este momento.Fortunately, I do not need such warm clothes right now.
Vuelvo a colgar el abrigo en el perchero y me despido.I hang the coat back on the rack and say goodbye.
museum ticket
Storyboard: Moving on, Maya enters a local history museum and goes to the front desk. She asks the clerk if this entrance ticket includes the special upstairs exhibition, and the clerk tells her to hurry because the guided tour started ten minutes ago.
Me alejo del mercado y encuentro un pequeño museo local.I walk away from the market and find a small, local museum.
Entro en el edificio tranquilo.I walk into the quiet building.
Me acerco al mostrador para comprar un boleto de entrada.I walk over to the counter to buy an entrance ticket.
Un joven está de pie detrás de la caja.A young man is standing behind the register.
"Hola, un boleto de adulto, por favor", digo."Hi, one adult ticket, please," I say.
"Serán cien coronas", dice él y lo imprime."That will be one hundred kroner," he says and prints it.
Pago y miro estos coloridos folletos en el mostrador.I pay and look at these colorful brochures on the counter.
Anuncian una exposición especial sobre la historia de la ciudad.They advertise a special exhibition about the history of the city.
"¿Este boleto también da acceso a la exposición de arriba?", pregunto."Does this ticket also give access to the exhibition upstairs?" I ask.
"Sí, este museo solo tiene un boleto combinado", responde."Yes, this museum only has one combined ticket," he answers.
"La exposición está arriba, en el primer piso", dice."The exhibition is upstairs on the first floor," he says.
"Puedes tomarte tu tiempo allá arriba.""You can take your time up there."
"Tiene muchas fotografías antiguas de la ciudad", explica."It has many old photographs of the city," he explains.
"Algunas de ellas tienen más de cien años.""Some of them are over a hundred years old."
"Eso suena realmente emocionante", digo."That sounds really exciting," I say.
"Muchas gracias", sonrío."Thank you very much," I smile.
Tomo mi boleto y subo tranquilamente las escaleras.I take my ticket and walk calmly up the stairs.
harbor photograph

Hace cien años, el área era una zona industrial.A hundred years ago, the area was an industrial zone.

El contraste con hoy en día es enorme.The contrast to today is enormous.
Ahora, esta zona del puerto es tranquila y limpia.Now, this waterfront is quiet and clean.

seagull encounter
Storyboard: Walking home through a large park, Maya stops at a kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream. As she strolls along the pond, a bold seagull suddenly swoops down, forcing her to quickly duck and shield this dessert with her hand to prevent the bird from stealing it.
Camino a casa por un parque grande.I walk home through a large park.
Hace una hora estaba en un museo.An hour ago I was at a museum.
Ahora solo quiero disfrutar del buen clima.Now I just want to enjoy the good weather.
Me detengo en un pequeño quiosco para comprar un cono de helado.I stop at a small kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream.
"Aquí tienes, aquí está tu helado", dice el hombre del quiosco."Here you go, here is your ice cream," the man in the kiosk says.
"Muchas gracias", respondo y tomo el helado."Thank you very much," I reply and take the ice cream.
Camino lentamente junto al lago en el parque.I walk slowly along the lake in the park.
Este cono de helado se ve realmente bueno.This waffle ice cream looks really good.
De repente escucho un ruido fuerte sobre mí.Suddenly I hear a loud noise above me.
Una gaviota grande se lanza en picada directo hacia mí.A large seagull dives right down towards me.
¡Quiere robar mi helado!It wants to steal my ice cream!
Me agacho rápidamente y protejo mi helado con la mano.I duck quickly and shield my ice cream with my hand.
El pájaro no me va a robar la comida.The bird is not going to steal my food.
La gaviota pasa volando y aterriza en el pasto.The seagull flies past and lands on the grass.

"Son muy descaradas hoy", dice ella."They are very cheeky today," she says.
"Sí, definitivamente lo son", respondo riendo."Yes, they certainly are," I laugh back.

unpacking plates
