Week 20: Maya starts volunteering at the café

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding the volunteering
Storyboard: Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. One evening she browses a volunteering website, reads a few listings, and finds the little café she already knows, which needs weekend helpers, so she fills in the short sign-up form.
Počula som, že mnohí Dáni vo svojom voľnom čase pracujú ako dobrovoľníci.I have heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time.
Páči sa mi ten nápad.I like the idea.
Chcem sa naučiť lepšie hovoriť po dánsky a spoznať nových ľudí.I want to learn to speak better Danish and meet new people.
Jeden večer sedím pri svojom počítači.One evening, I am sitting with my computer.
Nájdem si webovú stránku pre dobrovoľnícku prácu.I find a website for voluntary work.
Prečítam si prvé tri inzeráty na stránke.I read the first three listings on the site.
Prvý inzerát je zo športového klubu.The first listing is from a sports club.
Ale potrebujú pomoc vo štvrtok večer a vtedy hrám bedminton.But they need help on Thursday evenings, and I play badminton then.
Druhý inzerát je z múzea.The second listing is from a museum.
Potrebujú človeka v stredu večer, ale vtedy cvičím.They need a person on Wednesday evenings, but I work out then.
Potom sa pozriem na tretí inzerát.Then I look at the third listing.
Je z tej malej kaviarne, ktorú už poznám.It is from the little café that I already know.
Potrebujú ľudí na sobotu dopoludnia.They need people for Saturday mornings.
To mi úplne perfektne vyhovuje.That suits me absolutely perfectly.
Vyplním krátky formulár so svojím menom a e-mailovou adresou.I fill in a short form with my name and my email address.
Napíšem aj svoje telefónne číslo: dvanásť, tridsaťštyri, päťdesiatšesť, sedemdesiatosem.I also write my phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight.
Nakoniec stlačím odoslať.Finally, I press send.
Ešte nikdy predtým som v kaviarni nepracovala, ale som pripravená sa to naučiť.I have never worked in a café before, but I am ready to learn it.
first shift arrival
Storyboard: On her very first Saturday shift, Maya arrives early at the little café. She ties her new apron in the back room and carries the chairs out to the pavement to prepare for opening.
Je to moja prvá sobotná zmena v malej kaviarni.It is my first Saturday shift at the little café.
Prišla som skoro.I have arrived early.
Slnko svieti na ulici v Kodani.The sun is shining on the street in Copenhagen.
Teším sa, ale som aj trochu nervózna.I am looking forward to it, but I am also a little nervous.
Je to prvýkrát, čo pracujem v kaviarni.It is the first time I am working in a café.
Išla som do zadnej miestnosti, aby som sa pripravila.I went into the back room to get ready.
Na stole pre mňa ležala nová zástera.On a table lay a new apron for me.
Obliekla som si ju a zaviazala na chrbte.I put it on and tied it in the back.
Bol to dobrý pocit, mať ju na sebe.It felt good to wear it.
Teraz som pripravená začať.Now I am ready to begin.
Musím sa naučiť správne otvárať kaviareň.I have to learn to open the café correctly.
Najprv vedúci odomkol ťažké vchodové dvere.First, the manager unlocked the heavy front door.
Potom som vyšla na chodník, aby som všetko pripravila.Afterwards, I went out onto the pavement to get ready.
Vyniesla som von štyri stoličky.I carried four chairs out.
Rozostavila som ich okolo malého stola.I have placed them around a little table.
O chvíľu prídu ostatní dobrovoľníci.In a little while, the other volunteers will arrive.
Som veľmi nadšená zo svojho prvého dňa.I am very excited about my first day.
learning the machine
Storyboard: Before the doors open, an experienced volunteer brings Maya over to the large espresso machine. The volunteer shows her how to grind the coffee beans and how to steam the milk. Maya watches every step closely, because it is her first time behind the counter.
Je to môj prvý raz za pultom.It is my first time behind the counter.
Kaviareň ešte nie je otvorená.The café is not open yet.
Jeden zo skúsených dobrovoľníkov ma volá.One of the experienced volunteers calls for me.
Stojíme pred veľkým kávovarom na espresso.We stand in front of the large espresso machine.
„Dnes sa musíš naučiť pripravovať kávu,“ hovorí."Today you must learn to make coffee," he says.
Nikdy predtým som na takomto prístroji kávu nepripravovala.I have never made coffee on such a machine before.
Prvým krokom je pomlieť kávové zrná.The first step is to grind the coffee beans.
Stlačí malé tlačidlo.He presses a small button.
Prístroj s hlasným zvukom melie hnedé zrná.The machine grinds the brown beans with a loud sound.
Druhým krokom je napeniť mlieko.The second step is to steam the milk.
Nalieva studené mlieko do kovovej kanvičky.He pours cold milk into a metal jug.
Potom zapne horúcu paru.Afterwards he turns on the hot steam.
Medzitým pozorne sledujem jeho ruky.I look closely at his hands meanwhile.
Mlieko v kanvičke sa rýchlo zohrieva a hustne.The milk in the jug quickly becomes warm and thick.
Očividne už predtým veľakrát pripravoval kávu.He has clearly made coffee many times before.
Keď som dnes ráno prišla, bola som veľmi nervózna.When I arrived this morning, I was very nervous.
Ale vysvetlil to dobre, takže som už väčšinu z toho pochopila.But he explained it well, so I have already understood most of it.
the first coffee
Storyboard: When the morning rush hits, Maya makes a cappuccino for a waiting customer. She has held the steam wand a bit too deep in the milk, so it does not get a thick foam. It still looks acceptable, though, so she pours the milk over the coffee and hands it over with a polite smile.
Dnes dopoludnia sme mali neuveriteľne veľa práce.This morning we have been incredibly busy.
Práve som pripravila svoje úplne prvé cappuccino.I have just made my very first cappuccino.
Chvíľu pred desiatou prišiel k pultu nejaký muž.A little before ten, a man came over to the counter.
„Jedno cappuccino, prosím,“ povedal."A cappuccino, please," he said.
Práve som sa naučila používať kávovar na espresso.I have just learned to use the espresso machine.
Takže som hneď podišla k prístroju.So I went over to the machine right away.
Uvarila som kávu a ponorila parnú trysku do mlieka.I brewed the coffee and put the steam wand down into the milk.
Ale držala som ju trochu príliš hlboko.But I held it a little too deep down.
Asi som bola trochu príliš nervózna.I have probably been a little too nervous.
Bohužiaľ, na mlieku sa nevytvorila žiadna hustá pena.Unfortunately, the milk did not get any thick foam.
Opatrne som sa pozrela dole do kanvičky.I looked carefully down into the pitcher.
Pena nebola úplne dokonalá.The foam was not completely perfect.
Ale aj tak vyzerala prijateľne.But it still looked acceptable.
Naliala som horúce mlieko na kávu v šálke.I poured the hot milk over the coffee in the cup.
Potom som kávu podala mužovi so zdvorilým úsmevom.Then I gave the coffee to the man with a polite smile.
„Nech sa páči, bude to päťdesiatpäť korún,“ povedala som."Here you go, that will be fifty-five kroner," I said.
Vzal si šálku a sadol si k prvému stolu.He took the cup and sat down at the first table.
bun with cheese
Storyboard: A moment later, she slices a warm sourdough bun and adds a thick piece of cheese. She places it on a small plate and navigates through the standing customers to deliver it safely to a corner booth.
Krátko nato prišla ďalšia objednávka.Shortly after, the next order came.
Dnes som už pripravila svoju prvú kávu.I have already made my first coffee today.
Ale tento zákazník chcel iba jedlo.But this customer just wanted food.
Objednal si žemľu so syrom.He ordered a bun with cheese.
Z košíka som vzala teplú kváskovú žemľu.I took a warm sourdough bun from the basket.
Kváskový chlieb má často tvrdú kôrku.Sourdough bread often has a hard crust.
Ale naučila som sa chlieb rýchlo prekrojiť na polovicu.But I have learned to cut the bread in half quickly.
Potom som na žemľu položila hrubý plátok syra.Afterwards, I put a thick slice of cheese on the bun.
Jedlo som pekne naservírovala na malý tanier.I arranged the food nicely on a small plate.
Teraz som ho musela zaniesť zákazníkovi.Now I had to deliver it to the customer.
Uprostred kaviarne stálo veľa ľudí.There were many people standing in the middle of the café.
Balansovala som s tanierom a opatrne som kráčala dopredu.I balanced the plate and walked carefully forward.
Pomaly som prešla okolo ostatných hostí.I walked slowly past the other guests.
Zákazník našťastie sedel úplne vzadu v rohu.Fortunately, the customer was sitting right down in the corner.
Čakal pri štvrtom stole.He was waiting at the fourth table.
Položila som tanier na stôl pred neho.I placed the plate on the table in front of him.
"Nech sa páči, teplá žemľa so syrom," povedala som."Here you go, a warm bun with cheese," I said.
"Veľmi pekne ďakujem," odpovedal muž."Thank you very much," the man replied.
Je pekné vidieť spokojného hosťa.It is lovely to see a satisfied guest.
the ginger beer
Storyboard: During her second Saturday shift, a woman asks for a ginger beer. Maya finds the ginger beer in the glass fridge, inverts it so that the ginger mixes. Then she hands it to the customer.
Bola to moja druhá sobotná zmena v kaviarni.It was my second Saturday shift in the café.
Už som sa naučila robiť veľkú časť práce.I have already learned to do a lot of the work.
V to dopoludnie bolo vonku nádherne teplo.That morning it was wonderfully warm outside.
K pultu pristúpila žena.A woman came over to the counter.
"Poprosila by som si zázvorové pivo," povedala."I would like to ask for a ginger beer," she said.
"Samozrejme, moment," odpovedala som."Of course, just a moment," I answered.
Podišla som k veľkej presklenej chladničke za pultom.I went over to the large glass fridge behind the counter.
Pozrela som sa na tretiu policu.I looked on the third shelf.
Stáli tam studené fľaše zázvorového piva.The cold bottles of ginger beer were standing there.
Našla som jednu fľašu a vybrala ju.I found a bottle and took it out.
Zázvor ležal dole na dne ako hustý oblak.The ginger lay like a thick cloud down at the bottom.
Naučila som sa to premiešať predtým, než fľašu otvorím.I have learned to mix it before I open the bottle.
Tak som fľašu veľmi opatrne otočila hore dnom.So I turned the bottle very carefully upside down.
Držala som ju takto pár sekúnd.I held it like that for a couple of seconds.
Zázvor sa pomaly zmiešal so zvyškom tekutiny.The ginger mixed slowly with the rest of the liquid.
Teraz nápoj vyzeral presne tak, ako mal.Now the drink looked exactly right.
Znova som fľašu otočila a položila ju na pult.I turned the bottle around again and placed it on the counter.
"Nech sa páči, bude to tridsaťpäť korún," povedala som jej."Here you go, that will be thirty-five kroner," I said to her.
Zaplatila a svoj studený nápoj si pravdepodobne vychutnala.She paid, and she has probably enjoyed her cold drink.
second coffee progress
Storyboard: Later that morning, Maya tries foaming milk again for a large latte. This second attempt turns out much better, and she realizes that she has finally learned to control the pressure of the hot steam.
Neskôr v tú istú sobotu sme mali opäť veľmi veľa práce.Later the same Saturday, we got very busy again.
Mladá žena prišla k pultu a objednala si veľké cappuccino.A young woman came up to the counter and ordered a large cappuccino.
Moja prvá šálka nebola úplne dokonalá.My first cup was not quite perfect.
Ale tentoraz som bola pripravená.But this time I was ready.
Vzala som kovovú kanvičku a naliala do nej studené mlieko.I took the metal pitcher and poured cold milk into it.
Položila som kanvičku pod kávovar a zapla horúcu paru.I placed the pitcher under the machine and turned on the hot steam.
Nezvýšila som tlak hneď.I did not turn up the pressure immediately.
Naučila som sa totiž počúvať kávovar.I have actually learned to listen to the machine.
Hlasný zvuk pary bol tentoraz úplne správny.The loud sound from the steam was exactly right this time.
Mlieko sa pomaly zohrievalo.The milk slowly became warm.
A pena navrchu sa stala veľmi hustou a jemnou.And the foam on top became very thick and soft.
Bol to veľký krok vpred.It was a big step forward.
Opatrne som naliala mlieko na kávu vo veľkej šálke.I carefully poured the milk over the coffee in a large cup.
Naozaj to vyzeralo ako skutočné profesionálne cappuccino.It actually looked like a real professional cappuccino.
„Nech sa páči,“ povedala som a položila šálku na pult."Here you go," I said and placed the cup on the counter.
Žena sa usmiala a vzala si kávu.The woman smiled and took her coffee.
Konečne som sa naučila ovládať tlak.I have finally learned to control the pressure.
third saturday regular
Storyboard: By her third Saturday, Maya spots a familiar older man walking towards the front door. She has memorized his usual order, so when he reaches the counter she greets him and offers to make his usual cortado.
V moju tretiu sobotu som stála pripravená za pultom.On my third Saturday, I stood ready behind the counter.
Konečne som sa naučila v kaviarni trochu uvoľniť.I have finally learned to relax a bit in the café.
Cez okno som videla na ulici staršieho muža.Through the window, I saw an older man on the street.
Pomaly kráčal k vchodovým dverám.He walked slowly towards the front door.
Bol tu aj na mojej prvej a druhej zmene.He was also here on my first and my second shift.
Je jedným zo stálych zákazníkov kaviarne.He is one of the café's regular customers.
Naučila som sa jeho objednávku naspamäť.I have learned to remember his order by heart.
Vždy si kupuje presne to isté.He always buys exactly the same thing.
Otvoril dvere a prišiel k pokladni.He opened the door and came up to the register.
Pozrela som sa na neho a usmiala sa.I looked at him and smiled.
„Dobré ráno,“ povedala som."Good morning," I said.
„Mám vám pripraviť vaše zvyčajné cortado?“"Should I make your usual cortado?"
Muž sa na mňa prekvapene pozrel.The man looked at me surprised.
Potom sa radostne usmial.Then he smiled happily.
„Áno, prosím, budem veľmi rád,“ odpovedal."Yes please, I would very much like that," he answered.
Podišla som ku kávovaru na espresso.I went over to the espresso machine.
Kávu som pripravovala už mnohokrát, takže to išlo rýchlo.I have made coffee many times now, so it went quickly.
Bol to dobrý pocit, poznať našich hostí.It was a good feeling to know our guests.
hanging up apron
Storyboard: After the busy shift ends, Maya scrubs the wooden counter clean. She hangs her apron on the hook in the staff room, locks the front door, and steps out into the bright afternoon street, happy with what she has learned so far.

Bola to veľmi rušná sobota.It has been a very busy Saturday.

Dala som si dole zásteru.I took off my apron.
Zavesila som ju na prvý háčik na stene.I hung it on the first hook on the wall.

Cestou k autobusu som sa sama pre seba usmiala.I smiled to myself on the way to the bus.
Mám veľkú radosť zo svojej novej dobrovoľníckej práce.I am very happy with my new volunteer work.
Už teraz sa teším na svoju štvrtú smenu.I am already looking forward to my fourth shift.
