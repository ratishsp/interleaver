# Week 14 — Storyboard (Maya crosses the city by train)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** **einsteigen / aussteigen / umsteigen**; **mit + dative** (*mit der U-Bahn*); the modal **müssen** (*ich muss … fahren*)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_planning_the_route | Maya comes out of the office after work and walks to the station. Today she has an errand at a small coffee roaster by the river, right across the city. The route app shows her the way: first with the U-Bahn, then she must change onto the S-Bahn. Her monthly pass is lying at home on the kitchen table, so today she must buy her tickets one by one — one in the morning, and now another one. The shop closes at six, so she must hurry. |
| 2 | 02_the_ticket_machine | At the ticket machine Maya picks her ticket quickly — one ticket, three Euro fifty, valid for the whole journey including the change. Then she sees the problem: this machine is out of order. A woman beside her points her to the other machine at the end of the platform, keeping to the formal Sie. Maya thanks her, hurries over and buys her ticket there. |
| 3 | 03_stamping_the_ticket | On the platform Maya goes straight to the small yellow stamping box on the pillar — one must stamp a paper ticket. She pushes the ticket into the slot and hears a loud click. Now the ticket is valid. |
| 4 | 04_boarding_the_u_bahn | The bright yellow U-Bahn arrives at the platform. The passengers get out first, then Maya gets in. The doors close with a warning beep, and the train rolls into the dark tunnel. Maya finds a free seat by the window and holds her Rucksack on her lap. |
| 5 | 05_the_fellow_passenger | At the next station a lot of people get in, and the train is suddenly quite full. An older passenger stops beside Maya and asks her a direct question: is the seat free? They keep to the formal Sie. Maya says yes and moves over a little, so that he can sit down comfortably. They exchange a few friendly words about the weather. |
| 6 | 06_changing_trains | A voice over the loudspeaker announces the big connection station. Maya must change trains here. She gets out of the U-Bahn and follows the signs through the busy hall. Up on the platform above ground her S-Bahn is already standing with its doors open, and she has to hurry the last few metres. |
| 7 | 07_the_s_bahn_ride | The doors close behind her and the S-Bahn pulls out. This train runs above the streets, and she looks out of the window at the city going past. She rarely takes the S-Bahn. After a few stops she sees the wide water of the river. She must get out soon. |
| 8 | 08_arriving_at_the_river | Maya gets out at the next station and walks down the stairs. She follows the riverbank between the old brick buildings. Then she finds the small coffee roaster, and the smell of roasted beans reaches her out on the street. |
| 9 | 09_the_coffee_roaster | It is five minutes before six when Maya opens the door. Inside the shop it is warm and dark and full of sacks. Maya greets the man behind the counter, keeping to the formal Sie, and asks him a direct question: which coffee does he recommend? He lets her smell two kinds and she chooses a dark coffee. He weighs out the beans and she pays. Outside the sun is on the water, and she walks a little along the river with the packet in her Rucksack. |
