Week 2: First Day & Registration

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning commute
Storyboard: Maya walks briskly through the chilly morning air, looking for her new workplace. She checks the large numbers on the buildings along the street until she spots the software company's logo next to number forty-four.
Ni asubuhi Kopenhagen.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Ni mwezi wa Januari, na kuna baridi sana.It is January, and it is very cold.
Nina wasiwasi kidogo.I am a little nervous.
Leo ni siku kubwa.Today is a big day.
Ni mwanzo mpya kwangu.It is a new start for me.
Ni siku yangu ya kwanza kwenye kazi yangu mpya.It is my first day at my new job.
Ninapanda basi kwenda kazini.I take the bus to work.
Ninashuka kwenye kituo sahihi.I get off at the right stop.
Sasa ninatembea sehemu ya mwisho.Now I walk the last part.
Ofisi iko wapi?Where is the office?
Ninatazama majengo makubwa.I look at the large buildings.
Kila jengo lina namba.Each building has a number.
Hapa ni namba thelathini na nane.Here is number thirty-eight.
Na hapa ni namba arobaini.And here is number forty.
Ninatembea mbele kidogo.I walk a little further.
Hapa ni namba arobaini na mbili.Here is number forty-two.
Na hiyo hapo!And there it is!
Hapa ni namba arobaini na nne.Here is number forty-four.
Ninaona nembo kubwa.I see a large logo.
Kampuni yangu mpya ya programu iko hapa hapa.My new software company is right here.
Nina hisia nzuri.I have a good feeling.
Niko tayari kabisa.I am completely ready.
meeting peter
Storyboard: Inside the bright office, a smiling colleague introduces himself as Peter. He shakes her hand, hands her a new laptop, and leads her through the open-plan workspace to her assigned desk.
Sasa niko ndani ya ofisi mpya.I am now inside the new office.
Ni chumba kikubwa na chenye mwangaza.It is a large and bright room.
Mwanamume mkarimu amesimama kando ya mlango.A friendly man stands by the door.
Ananitabasamia.He smiles at me.
"Karibu!" anasema."Welcome!" he says.
"Ninaitwa Peter.""My name is Peter."
Ninampa mkono.I shake his hand.
"Hujambo Peter, ninaitwa Maya," ninasema."Hi Peter, my name is Maya," I say.
Peter ni mfanyakazi mwenza.Peter is a colleague.
Tunatembea pamoja ndani ya ofisi.We walk together through the office.
"Una dawati hapa," anasema Peter."You have a desk here," says Peter.
Dawati lina nambari kumi na sita.The desk has number sixteen.
"Na una kompyuta," anasema."And you have a computer," he says.
Ananipa kompyuta mpya ya mkononi.He gives me a new laptop.
Ninatazama nafasi yangu mpya.I look at my new spot.
Nina kiti na skrini kubwa.I have a chair and a large monitor.
"Asante sana, Peter," ninasema."Thank you very much, Peter," I say.
Niko tayari kwa siku yangu ya kwanza ya kazi.I am ready for my first workday.
the phone shop
Storyboard: On her lunch break, Maya ducks into a telecom shop to buy a local SIM card. She hands the clerk her phone, and he activates it, reading her brand-new local number aloud as she saves it.
Ni mapumziko yangu ya chakula cha mchana.It is my lunch break.
Niko kwenye duka la simu.I am in a phone shop.
Nina simu kutoka Mexico.I have a phone from Mexico.
Mhudumu ni kijana wa kiume.The clerk is a young man.
"Una simkadi ya Denmark?" ninauliza."Do you have a Danish SIM card?" I ask.
"Ndiyo, bila shaka," anajibu."Yes, of course," he answers.
Ninampa simu yangu.I give him my phone.
Anaweka simkadi kwenye simu.He puts the SIM card in the phone.
Simu iko tayari sasa.The phone is ready now.
"Nambari yako mpya ni kumi na mbili, thelathini na nne, hamsini na sita, sabini na nane," anasema."Your new number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," he says.
Nina daftari kwenye mkoba wangu.I have a notebook in my bag.
Ninaandika nambari.I write the number.
"Kumi na mbili, thelathini na nne, hamsini na sita, sabini na nane," ninarudia."Twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I repeat.
"Ndiyo, haswa," anasema mhudumu."Yes, exactly," says the clerk.
"Asante kwa msaada," ninasema."Thank you for the help," I say.
Nina nambari ya Denmark sasa!I have a Danish number now!
Ninaweka simu kwenye mfuko wangu.I put the phone in my pocket.
queue ticket
Storyboard: The next morning, Maya enters the crowded public registration office. She presses a button on a red terminal, pulls a paper ticket, and checks her high number before finding a seat to wait.
Ni asubuhi inayofuata.It is the next morning.
Nipo kwenye ofisi kubwa ya usajili leo.I am at the large registration office today.
Kuna watu wengi humu ndani.There are many people in here.
Nina pasipoti yangu na karatasi zangu kwenye begi langu.I have my passport and my papers in my bag.
Kila kitu kipo tayari kwa usajili wangu.Everything is ready for my registration.
Nimesimama karibu na mashine nyekundu.I stand by a red machine.
Ninabonyeza kitufe.I press a button.
Mashine inanipa kikaratasi kidogo cha nambari.The machine gives me a small number slip.
Nambari yangu ni ngapi?What is my number?
Ninaangalia karatasi.I look at the paper.
Nina nambari hamsini na tisa.I have number fifty-nine.
Ni nambari kubwa kiasi.It is a rather high number.
Skrini kubwa ukutani inaonyesha nambari.A large screen on the wall shows a number.
Skrini inaonyesha nambari ishirini na moja tu.The screen only shows number twenty-one.
Nina muda wa kutosha.I have plenty of time.
Ninapata kiti kitupu.I find an empty chair.
Ninaketi chini na karatasi zangu.I sit down with my papers.
Sasa ninasubiri zamu yangu.Now I wait for my turn.
registration
Storyboard: The screen chimes with her number, and Maya steps up to the counter. She volunteers her name and age to the clerk, hands over her passport, and he processes the paperwork for her private national ID number.
Skrini inaonyesha namba yangu.A screen shows my number.
Namba hiyo ni hamsini na tisa.The number is fifty-nine.
Ninaenda kwenye kaunta.I walk up to the counter.
Mwanamume amekaa nyuma ya kaunta.A man sits behind the counter.
"Habari ya asubuhi," anasema."Good morning," he says.
"Habari ya asubuhi," ninajibu."Good morning," I reply.
"Ninaitwa Maya, na nina umri wa miaka thelathini na moja," ninasema."My name is Maya, and I am thirty-one years old," I say.
"Karibu, Maya," anasema."Welcome, Maya," he says.
"Una pasipoti yako?" anauliza."Do you have your passport?" he asks.
"Ndiyo, nina pasipoti yangu na nyaraka zangu," ninajibu."Yes, I have my passport and my papers," I answer.
"Hizi hapa," ninasema."Here you go," I say.
Anatazama nyaraka hizo.He looks at the papers.
Anachapa kwenye kompyuta yake.He types on his computer.
Ninapata namba ya kitambulisho binafsi ya Kideni.I get a Danish personal identification number.
Namba hiyo ni ya siri na muhimu sana.The number is private and very important.
Ananipa kadi mpya.He gives me a new card.
"Chukua, hii hapa kadi yako," anasema."Here you go, here is your card," he says.
"Sasa umesajiliwa nchini Denmaki.""Now you are registered in Denmark."
Ninatabasamu na kuchukua kadi hiyo.I smile and take the card.
Sasa mimi ni sehemu ya Denmaki.Now I am a part of Denmark.
bakery celebration
Storyboard: Heading home from the registration office, Maya stops at a neighbourhood bakery to celebrate being officially registered at last. She points to a cinnamon pastry in the glass display, asks the price, and taps her card to pay.
Niko nje barabarani tena.I am out on the street again.
Hatimaye kila kitu kiko mahali pake.Everything is finally in place.
Ni wakati wa sherehe ndogo.It is time for a little celebration.
Ninaona duka dogo la mikate kwenye kona.I see a little bakery on the corner.
Ninaingia ndani.I go inside.
Kunanukia vizuri.It smells lovely.
Mwokaji amesimama nyuma ya kaunta.A baker stands behind the counter.
"Hujambo," mwokaji anasema."Hello," the baker says.
"Sijambo," ninajibu."Hello," I reply.
Ninaonyesha keki nyuma ya kioo.I point to a pastry behind the glass.
"Ningependa keki ya mdalasini," ninasema."I would like a cinnamon pastry," I say.
"Ndiyo, bila shaka," anasema."Yes, of course," he says.
"Inagharimu kiasi gani?" ninauliza."What does it cost?" I ask.
"Inagharimu kroner ishirini na tano," mwokaji anajibu."It costs twenty-five kroner," the baker replies.
Nina pochi yangu kwenye begi langu.I have my wallet in my bag.
Ninatoa kadi yangu ya malipo.I take out my debit card.
Ninalipia keki yangu ya mdalasini.I pay for my cinnamon pastry.
Mwokaji ananipa mfuko mdogo.The baker gives me a small bag.
Sasa nina keki ya mdalasini kwa ajili ya sherehe yangu ndogo.Now I have a cinnamon pastry for my little celebration.
swapping numbers
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her apartment building with her pastry bag and bumps into her neighbour Nina by the front door. They chat warmly and swap phone numbers so they can stay in touch; Nina reads out hers for Maya to save.
Nimesimama karibu na fleti yangu.I am standing by my apartment.
Nina mfuko wenye keki ya mdalasini.I have a bag with a cinnamon pastry.
Jirani yangu Nina yuko mlangoni.My neighbour Nina is at the door.
Anatabasamu kwa upana na kusema hujambo.She smiles broadly and says hello.
"Hujambo Maya, una nambari ya Kidenmark sasa?" Nina anauliza."Hello Maya, do you have a Danish number now?" Nina asks.
"Ndiyo, nina nambari mpya ya simu," ninajibu."Yes, I have a new phone number," I reply.
"Hiyo ni vizuri," anasema."That is good," she says.
Nina simu yangu mkononi.I have my phone in my hand.
"Nambari yako ni ngapi?" ninauliza."What is your number?" I ask.
"Nambari yangu ni themanini na saba, sitini na tano, arobaini na tatu, ishirini na moja," Nina anasema."My number is eighty-seven, sixty-five, forty-three, twenty-one," Nina says.
Ninaandika nambari yake kwenye simu yangu.I write her number on my phone.
"Na nambari yangu ni kumi na mbili, thelathini na nne, hamsini na sita, sabini na nane," ninasema."And my number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
Nina anahifadhi nambari yangu kwenye simu yake.Nina saves my number on her phone.
Sasa tuna mawasiliano.Now we are in touch.
Tunaingia ndani pamoja.We go inside together.
Nina furaha sana kuhusu rafiki yangu mpya.I am very happy about my new friend.
