# Week 15 — Storyboard (Maya's everyday life in the city)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Mixed present-tense narration; consolidation

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_weather | Maya wakes up early and looks out the window. The sun shines, but the wind is cold, so she puts on a warm sweater for the day. |
| 2 | 02_the_bus_commute | She walks to the bus stop and waits for her daily transport. The yellow bus arrives, she steps inside, scans her travel card, and rides through the busy streets. |
| 3 | 03_working_with_peter | Maya arrives at the software company and says good morning to her friendly colleague Peter. She sits at her desk, drinks black coffee, and types code on her computer. |
| 4 | 04_canteen_lunch | At noon, Maya and Peter go down to the bright canteen together. She chooses a classic open sandwich with fish and pays fyrre kroner at the register. |
| 5 | 05_giving_directions | After work, Maya steps out into a large square. A tourist asks for the way to a local museum, and she politely explains that they must walk straight ahead and then turn left. |
| 6 | 06_buying_a_cake | On the way home, she stops at a small bakery on the corner. She orders a chocolate cake for the evening and pays femogtres kroner to the smiling baker. |
| 7 | 07_arriving_home | Maya takes the bus back to her familiar street. She unlocks the front door of her building and feels happy and completely at home in her new neighborhood. |
| 8 | 08_evening_with_nina | She knocks on her neighbor Nina's door and holds up the sweet cake. They sit in the cozy living room, drink tea, and chat about their day in the city. |
