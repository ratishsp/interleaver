# Week 19 — Storyboard (Maya joins a new project team)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Perfect **-ിട്ടുണ്ട്** (ചെയ്തിട്ടുണ്ട് — what she has done); professions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_bus_to_infopark | Maya takes her usual morning bus to Infopark through steady monsoon rain, feeling a mix of nerves and excitement. Today marks a milestone: she is officially joining a new software project, and she spends the ride reviewing the general notes she has prepared over the weekend for the kickoff. |
| 2 | 02_meeting_the_manager | At the office, the team boss, Suresh, calls Maya into his cabin to formally welcome her onto the core project team. As they talk, he asks what similar work she has done before, and Maya describes the projects she has worked on — glad to find her experience fits the new role. |
| 3 | 03_morning_stand_up | The team — Prakash beside her — gathers for their short morning stand-up meeting. Maya feels a flutter of nerves when her turn comes, but she presents her simple work plan, and the nods around the table leave her quietly relieved. |
| 4 | 04_lunch_with_deepa | At their usual canteen table, Maya and Prakash are joined by Deepa, the team's designer, whom Maya has known only by face until now. Over the meal, Deepa shows some simple early sketches for the new project on her tablet, and when Maya asks, she mentions how many years she has worked as a designer — then the talk drifts happily to food and home towns. |
| 5 | 05_at_the_testing_corner | In the afternoon, Maya walks over to the testing corner with a question: how will her part of the work be checked? Arun, the team's tester, explains his role and walks her through his simple plan for testing her work when it is ready. |
| 6 | 06_pantry_tea_break | During the late afternoon tea break in the office pantry, the team chats informally to decompress. Moving entirely away from work, they ask Maya which places in Kerala she has visited so far and whether she has tried specific local snacks from the Kochi bakeries. |
| 7 | 07_telling_anitha | Back at her building that evening, Maya runs into Anitha on the stairs. Anitha asks about the big day, and Maya — tired but glowing — says it has gone well: the team is friendly, and the new project feels like hers now. She climbs up to her flat light with relief. |
