# Week 3 — Storyboard (A slow Sunday and the call home)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Genitive possessives **मम / भवत्याः / तस्य / तस्याः**; **एकः/एका/एकम्** agreeing in gender; **कः/का** questions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_sunday_sweets | On a quiet Sunday morning, Maya walks to the neighborhood corner shop. She addresses the shopkeeper as 'bhaiya' and buys one box of bright, colorful Indian sweets, planning to show them to her family. |
| 2 | 02_connecting_home | Back in her room, Maya sits at her desk and initiates a video call on her laptop. She waits for the connection, thinking about the time difference—it is morning in Mumbai, but still Saturday evening in Mexico. |
| 3 | 03_parents_greeting | Her parents appear on the screen, looking happy to see her. Maya addresses them respectfully using 'aap' and excitedly turns her laptop around to give them a virtual tour, announcing, 'This is my room!' |
| 4 | 04_showing_the_treats | Maya opens the box from the corner shop in front of the camera. Her father asks what is in it, and she proudly shows off the local sweets, explaining what they are and wishing she could share them. |
| 5 | 05_sister_sofia | Her younger sister, Sofia, grabs the phone. Maya switches to the familiar register as they joke around. Maya holds her phone up to the camera to show a photo of herself with her new neighbour; Sofia asks, 'Who is that?', and Maya explains it is Asha, from her building. |
| 6 | 06_brother_diego | Her elder brother, Diego, leans into the frame next to Sofia. He asks about her job, and Maya happily tells him about the office, the people, and her helpful colleague Rohan. |
| 7 | 07_lola_the_dog | A loud bark interrupts the conversation as Lola, the family dog, jumps into view. Maya laughs and talks affectionately to the dog using 'tum', telling Lola she is a good girl. |
| 8 | 08_saying_goodbye | The family sends their love and they finally say their goodbyes. Maya closes the laptop, and the screen goes dark, leaving her sitting alone in the sudden quiet of her room. |
| 9 | 09_quiet_street_walk | Feeling a touch homesick but comforted by the call, Maya steps outside for a walk. She strolls down her street in the Sunday calm, the voices of her family still echoing happily in her mind. |
