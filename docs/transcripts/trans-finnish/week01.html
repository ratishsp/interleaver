Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Finnish with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
Lentokone on nyt Kööpenhaminassa.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
On tammikuu.It is January.
Olen todella innoissani.I am very excited.
Pakkaan kirjani laukkuuni.I pack my book into my bag.
Vieressäni on matkustaja.There is a passenger next to me.
Hän katsoo minua ja hymyilee.He looks at me and smiles.
"Hei", hän sanoo."Hello," he says.
"Hei", sanon."Hello," I say.
"Mikä sinun nimesi on?" hän kysyy."What is your name?" he asks.
"Nimeni on Maya", sanon."My name is Maya," I say.
"Mistä olet kotoisin, Maya?" hän kysyy."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
Hymyilen iloisesti.I smile happily.
"Olen kotoisin Meksikosta", vastaan."I come from Mexico," I answer.
"Oletko Tanskasta?" kysyn."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
"Kyllä, olen Tanskasta", hän sanoo."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
"Tervetuloa Tanskaan", hän sanoo."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
"Kiitos paljon", sanon."Thank you very much," I say.
Nyt olemme täällä.Now we are here.
Olen valmis.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
Olen nyt ulkona.I am outside now.
Matkalaukkuni on iso ja painava.My suitcase is big and heavy.
Tuolla on taksi.There is a taxi.
Nostan matkalaukkuni taksiin.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
Kuljettaja on ystävällinen mies.The driver is a friendly man.
"Mikä on osoite?" hän kysyy."What is the address?" he asks.
"Se on Parkvej numero kaksitoista", sanon."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
Hän nyökkää ja ajaa.He nods and drives.
"Oletko turisti?" kuljettaja kysyy."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
"En, en ole turisti", vastaan."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
"Mistä tulet?" hän kysyy."Where do you come from?" he asks.
"Tulen Meksikosta", sanon."I come from Mexico," I say.
Katson ulos ikkunasta.I look out the window.
Olen hyvin ylpeä.I am very proud.
"Olen ohjelmistoinsinööri", sanon."I am a software engineer," I say.
"Uusi työpaikkani on täällä Kööpenhaminassa.""My new job is here in Copenhagen."
Hän hymyilee.He smiles.
"Onpa jännittävää.""How exciting."
"Onnea Tanskassa", hän sanoo."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
"Kiitos paljon", sanon."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
Ovi on auki.The door is open.
Kynnys on korkea.The threshold is high.
Matkalaukku on erittäin painava!The suitcase is very heavy!
Mutta nyt matkalaukku on eteisessä.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
Täällä on täysin pimeää.It is completely dark here.
Missä katkaisija on?Where is the switch?
Seinä on kylmä.The wall is cold.
Onko katkaisija täällä?Is the switch here?
Kyllä, siinä on katkaisija!Yes, there is the switch!
Nyt on valoa.Now there is light.
Olen asunnossa.I am in the apartment.
Se on pieni, mutta se on kiva.It is small, but it is nice.
Tässä on olohuone.Here is a living room.
Siellä on sohva ja pöytä.There is a sofa and a table.
Se on kalustettu koti.It is a furnished home.
Matkalaukku on lattialla.The suitcase is on the floor.
Olen todella väsynyt.I am very tired.
Mutta olen myös iloinen.But I am also happy.
Olen Kööpenhaminassa!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
Seison ikkunan luona.I am standing by the window.
Lasi on kylmä.The glass is cold.
On tammikuu.It is January.
Katson ulos.I look out.
Kööpenhamina on tänään harmaa.Copenhagen is grey today.
Ulkona on kylmä.It is cold outside.
Kadulla on lunta.There is snow on the street.
Mutta täällä on lämmintä.But it is warm here.
Asunto on hyvä.The apartment is good.
Ajattelen Meksikoa.I think about Mexico.
Meksikossa paistaa aurinko.In Mexico, there is sun.
Siellä on myös lämmintä.It is also warm there.
Kaipaan hieman aurinkoa.I miss the sun a little.
Mutta hymyilen.But I smile.
Tämä on uusi kaupunki.It is a new city.
Olen nyt Tanskassa.I am in Denmark now.
Tämä on hyvä alku.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
Olen nyt olohuoneessa.I am in the living room now.
Täällä on pieni sohva.There is a small sofa.
Soitan äidilleni Meksikoon.I call my mother in Mexico.
"Hei äiti", sanon."Hi Mom," I say.
"Hei Maya", äitini sanoo."Hi Maya," my mother says.
"Missä sinä olet?" hän kysyy."Where are you?" she asks.
"Olen Kööpenhaminassa", vastaan."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
"Olen nyt asunnossa.""I am in the apartment now."
"Onpa hyvä", äitini sanoo."That is so good," my mother says.
"Onko se iso?" hän kysyy."Is it big?" she asks.
"Ei, se on pieni", vastaan."No, it is small," I answer.
"Mutta se on kodikas, ja olen onnellinen.""But it is cozy, and I am happy."
"Onko siellä kylmä?" hän kysyy."Is it cold?" she asks.
"Kyllä, ulkona on kylmä", sanon."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
"Mutta täällä on lämmintä.""But it is warm in here."
"Olen turvassa.""I am safe."
"Se on hyvä", äitini sanoo."That is good," my mother says.
Hymyilen.I smile.
Tanska on uusi kotini.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
Avaan oveni.I open my door.
Seison porraskäytävässä.I am standing in the stairwell.
On hiljaista.It is quiet.
Ovi avautuu.A door opens.
Nainen hymyilee minulle.A woman smiles at me.
"Hei", hän sanoo."Hi," she says.
"Mikä nimesi on?" hän kysyy."What is your name?" she asks.
"Nimeni on Maya", vastaan."My name is Maya," I answer.
"Mistä olet kotoisin?" hän kysyy."Where do you come from?" she asks.
"Olen kotoisin Meksikosta", sanon."I come from Mexico," I say.
"Tervetuloa taloon", nainen sanoo."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
"Nimeni on Nina", hän sanoo."My name is Nina," she says.
"Olen naapurisi.""I am your neighbour."
Nina on erittäin ystävällinen.Nina is very friendly.
Hän puhuu hitaasti ja selkeästi.She speaks slowly and clearly.
"Tässä on oveni", hän sanoo."Here is my door," she says.
"Autan mielelläni.""I am happy to help."
"Koputa vain", Nina sanoo."You just knock," Nina says.
"Kiitos paljon, Nina", vastaan."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
Olen todella iloinen.I am very happy.
Nyt minulla on ystävä Kööpenhaminassa.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
Nyt on yö.Now it is night.
Tämä on ensimmäinen yöni Kööpenhaminassa.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
Olen kylpyhuoneessa.I am in the bathroom.
Se on todella pieni.It is very small.
Siellä on pesuallas.There is a sink.
Hammasharjani on punainen.My toothbrush is red.
Laitan hammastahnaa hammasharjalleni.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
Harjaan hampaani.I brush my teeth.
Vesi on kylmää.The water is cold.
Pesen kasvoni.I wash my face.
Kuivaan kasvoni.I dry my face.
Menen sänkyni luo.I go over to my bed.
Sänky on kapea.The bed is narrow.
Mutta se on lämmin ja pehmeä.But it is warm and soft.
Makaan nyt peiton alla.I am lying under the duvet now.
Puhelimeni on pöydällä.My phone is lying on the table.
Asetan herätykseni.I set my alarm.
Se soi huomenna kello kahdeksan.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
Olen väsynyt, mutta olen valmis.I am tired, but I am ready.
Hyvää yötä, Kööpenhamina.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
