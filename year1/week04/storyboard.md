# Week 4 — Storyboard (Maya moves into her own flat)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** *der ligger/står* (review *der er*); place prepositions *i, på, under, ved*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_final_sweep | Maya stands in her temporary room in the building. She zips up her large suitcases and checks the space one last time, making sure nothing lies under the small bed or stands forgotten in the closet. |
| 2 | 02_unlocking_the_door | Carrying her first few bags up the communal stairs, Maya arrives at the door of her new, permanent flat. She turns the key, pushes the door open, and steps inside, thrilled to finally see the small furnished space waiting for her. |
| 3 | 03_ninas_help | Maya steps back out to the shared stairwell to grab her heaviest suitcase. Nina comes up the stairs, spots her struggling, and cheerfully helps carry the bag inside, setting it carefully on the floor by the large living room window. |
| 4 | 04_kitchen_unpacking | Maya opens a travel bag in the small kitchen and begins organizing. She stacks her favorite mugs in the cupboard and makes sure her red kettle stands on the counter, right by the sink. |
| 5 | 05_the_workspace | Knowing she needs a good spot for her software engineering work, Maya sets up her equipment at the provided desk. Her laptop stands on the table with a warm lamp by it, while a bag of cables lies out of the way under the desk. |
| 6 | 06_bedroom_comforts | Moving into the bedroom, Maya unpacks her clothes from the main suitcase and folds them in the wardrobe. A thick blanket from Mexico lies on the bed, and she drops a pair of soft slippers on the floor under a small chair. |
| 7 | 07_the_plant | Maya fetches her new potted plant. She carries it into the living room and sets it on the windowsill, deciding it stands perfectly in the pale winter sunlight. |
| 8 | 08_celebratory_pizza | With the furnished flat finally feeling like a real home, Nina returns with two warm pizzas. They sit comfortably on the sofa together, chatting as the cardboard pizza boxes lie on the coffee table between them. |
