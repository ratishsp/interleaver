Week 19: Maya starts a new project with the team

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

monday bus excitement
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya takes the bus to work. She feels excited and a little nervous because a large new software project starts today, and she will finally work closely with the entire team.
依家係星期一朝早，我坐喺巴士上面。It is Monday morning, and I am sitting on the bus.
我喺返工嘅途中。I am on my way to work.
我望出窗外。I look out of the window.
今日我有啲緊張。I am a little nervous today.
不過我都好期待。But I am also looking forward to it a lot.
一個大型嘅新軟件項目今日開始。A large, new software project starts today.
由一月開始，我就一直喺公司做軟件工程師。I have worked as a software engineer at the company since January.
我同同事 Peter 好熟。I know my colleague Peter well.
由我返工第一日開始，我哋就一齊做嘢。We have worked together since my first day.
但係而家我要同成個團隊緊密合作。But now I must work closely with the whole team.
我要同我哋嘅老闆、設計師，仲有測試員一齊做嘢。I will work with our boss, our designer, and our tester.
佢哋喺公司已經做咗好耐。They have worked at the company for a long time.
巴士停站，有更多人上車。The bus stops, and more people get on.
我望吓隻錶。I look at my watch.
依家係八點鐘。It is eight o'clock.
早會喺半個鐘之後開始。The morning meeting begins in half an hour.
我深呼吸一吓。I take a deep breath.
我為新項目準備好喇。I am ready for the new project.
first morning presentation
Storyboard: At the short morning meeting, Maya stands in front of her colleagues. She presents her part of the technical plan for the first time, explaining what she has worked on to prepare for the week's tasks.
我哋坐喺會議室度。We are sitting in the meeting room.
係時候開我哋嘅簡短早會喇。It is time for our short morning meeting.
我望下周圍嘅同事。I look around at my colleagues.
今日對我嚟講係一個特別嘅日子。Today is a special day for me.
我企起身。I stand up.
我係軟件工程師，準備匯報我喺項目入面負責嘅部分。I am a software engineer, and I am going to present my part of the project.
我對手有啲震。My hands are shaking a little.
但我已經準備得好好。But I have prepared well.
「早晨，」我大聲咁講。"Good morning," I say loudly.
「我負責處理咗技術計劃，」我同佢哋講。"I have worked on the technical plan," I tell them.
「我已經睇晒所有文件。」"I have read all the documents."
「而且我為今個禮拜嘅任務寫低咗筆記。」"And I have written notes for the week's tasks."
我向成個團隊解釋呢個計劃。I explain the plan to the whole team.
其實講技術方面嘅嘢並唔難。It is actually not difficult to talk about the technology.
彼得坐喺我對面。Peter is sitting opposite me.
我覺得自己放鬆咗啲。I feel myself relaxing more.
佢一邊聽一邊點頭。He listens and nods.
我做完匯報，其他人都笑笑口。I finish my presentation, and the others smile.
我喺公司嘅第一次匯報進行得好順利。My first presentation at work has gone well.
boss project manager
Storyboard: After the morning meeting, her boss Thomas stops by Maya's desk with a part of the new project for her. He is warm and welcoming: he compliments her on her presentation and says the whole team has looked forward to having her on board. Maya feels genuinely welcomed onto the team.
早會之後，我坐喺我張辦公枱前。After the morning meeting, I sit at my desk.
我睇緊簡報嘅筆記。I look at my notes from the presentation.
我老闆 Thomas 走過嚟我度。My boss, Thomas, comes over to me.
佢係我哋嘅項目經理。He is our project manager.
佢喺度做咗好多年喇。He has worked here for many years.
「早晨，Maya，」佢微笑住咁講。"Good morning, Maya," he says with a smile.
「頭先個簡報做得好好。」"That was a really good presentation."
「多謝你，Thomas，」我答。"Thank you, Thomas," I answer.
「我哋好開心有你加入團隊，」Thomas 繼續講。"We are very happy to have you on the team," Thomas continues.
「成個團隊都好期待同你合作。」"The whole team has looked forward to working with you."
「我都好期待，」我好開心咁講。"I have also looked forward to it," I say happily.
「呢個係一個好令人興奮嘅項目。」"It is an exciting project."
Thomas 再次微笑，然後行返去佢間辦公室。Thomas smiles again and walks back to his office.
我望住部電腦螢幕。I look at my computer screen.
我真係覺得團隊好歡迎我。I feel genuinely welcome on the team.
peter software engineer
Storyboard: Maya sits down with Peter to write code. As software engineers, they solve complex problems together, and Peter jokes that he has worked on one specific error for three hours until Maya easily spots his mistake.
我坐喺Peter嘅辦公枱前。I sit down at Peter's desk.
我哋望住佢個大熒幕。We look at his large screen.
Peter都係一個軟件工程師。Peter is also a software engineer.
佢係一個好同事，我哋一齊寫好多程式碼。He is a good colleague, and we write a lot of code together.
今日佢望落好攰。Today he looks very tired.
「你做緊乜呀，Peter？」我問。"What are you doing, Peter?" I ask.
「我遇到個大問題，」佢答。"I have a big problem," he answers.
「我為咗呢個錯誤搞咗三個鐘。」"I have worked on this error for three hours."
我笑一笑，仔細噉睇吓啲程式碼。I smile and look closely at the code.
我哋做嘢嗰陣，成日都會一齊解決複雜嘅問題。We often solve complex problems together in our work.
突然間，我見到熒幕上面有啲奇怪嘅嘢。Suddenly I see something strange on the screen.
係一個好細嘅錯誤。It is a very small mistake.
「你試吓睇呢度，」我指住個熒幕講。"Try to look here," I say and point at the screen.
「你喺呢度有個小錯字。」"You have made a small typo here."
Peter靜靜噉望住個熒幕兩秒。Peter looks quietly at the screen for two seconds.
然後佢大聲笑咗出嚟。Then he laughs loudly.
「你講得完全啱，」Peter話。"You are absolutely right," Peter says.
「我搞咗三個鐘，結果只係一個咁細嘅錯誤！」"I have worked for three hours, and then it is just one small error!"
我都跟住笑，因為所有軟件工程師都明白呢種情況。I laugh too, because all software engineers know that situation.
我哋修正好個錯誤，個程式終於完美噉運作喇。We fix the error, and the program finally works perfectly.
eva digital designer
Storyboard: Later, Maya walks over to meet Eva, the team's designer. Eva shows Maya her beautiful sketches and explains that she has worked as a digital designer since she finished university, focusing on how users experience the product.
晏晝遲啲嗰陣，我行過去伊娃張寫字枱度。Later in the afternoon, I walk over to Eva's desk.
佢係團隊嘅數碼設計師。She is the team's digital designer.
喺今日之前，我未同佢合作過。I have not worked with her before today.
佢話：「哈囉，瑪雅，過嚟睇下。」"Hi Maya, come and look," she says.
佢指住佢個大螢幕。She points at her large screen.
我見到我哋新軟件嘅一啲好靚嘅草圖。I see some beautiful sketches of our new software.
啲顏色好柔和，設計好簡潔。The colors are calm, and the design is very clean.
我話：「睇落真係好正。」"That looks really good," I say.
伊娃笑住答：「多謝。」"Thank you," Eva answers with a smile.
佢同我講解佢嘅工作。She explains her work to me.
佢話：「我由讀大學嗰陣開始就已經做數碼設計師。」"I have worked as a digital designer since university," she says.
「我一直都好注重用戶體驗。」"I always focus on the user's experience."
佢示範畀我睇用戶會點樣喺個程式入面撳嚟撳去。She shows me how the user clicks around the program.
佢解釋話：「用起上嚟一定要簡單，而且合乎邏輯。」"It must be easy and logical to use," she explains.
我好多時都淨係會睇啲代碼本身。I often only look at the code itself.
但係佢嘅設計對呢個項目嚟講極之重要。But her design is incredibly important for the project.
我哋再傾多咗一陣大家一齊負責嘅工作。We talk a little more about our shared tasks.
我好期待為佢咁靚嘅設計寫程式。I look forward to programming her beautiful design.
meeting the tester
Storyboard: The next day, Maya sits with Mads, the software tester, as he clicks quickly through the new screens and spots a small mistake at once. He laughs that his job is to break things before the users do — he has done it for five years and finds problems fast.
第二日，喺辦公室。It is the next day at the office.
我坐喺我哋嘅軟件測試員麥斯隔離。I am sitting next to Mads, our software tester.
佢望住啲新畫面。He looks at the new screens.
佢撳滑鼠撳得好快。He clicks very fast with the mouse.
突然間佢停咗落嚟。Suddenly he stops.
佢已經發現咗一個小錯誤。He has already found a small mistake.
佢望住我，笑咗一下。He looks at me and laughs a little.
佢話：「我嘅工作就係整壞啲嘢。」"My job is to break things," he says.
「我要喺我哋嘅用戶之前搵到啲錯誤。」"I have to find the mistakes before our users."
佢做測試員做咗五年。He has worked as a tester for five years.
佢測試過好多唔同嘅項目。He has tested many different projects.
所以佢先可以咁快搵到啲問題。That is why he finds the problems so fast.
我望住我電腦上面嗰個錯誤。I look at the mistake on my computer.
好彩，呢個只係一個小問題。Fortunately, it is only a small problem.
我即刻改返好啲代碼。I fix the code right away.
佢再測試多次個程式。He tests the program one more time.
依家所有嘢都運作得非常完美。Now everything works absolutely perfectly.
我好開心有佢幫手。I am very glad for his help.
wednesday gym reflection
Storyboard: On Wednesday evening, Maya attends her usual gym class. While lifting weights, she thinks about her colleagues and realizes how much she has learned about their different professions in just a few days.
依家係星期三夜晚。It is Wednesday evening.
我喺健身室上我平時上開嘅堂。I am at the gym for my regular class.
我今日舉重。I am lifting weights today.
我對手臂好攰，但係感覺幾好。My arms are tired, but it is nice.
我諗起過去呢幾日。I think about the last few days.
呢個禮拜過得好忙。It has been a very busy week.
我同成個團隊合作得好緊密。I have worked closely with the whole team.
我學到好多關於佢哋唔同專業嘅嘢。I have learned a lot about their different professions.
我對住部電腦坐咗好多個鐘。I have sat many hours at my computer.
呢個禮拜我寫咗好多新嘅程式碼。I have written a lot of new code this week.
我向團隊做咗第一次匯報。I have given my first presentation to the team.
過程有啲難，但係都幾好玩。It has been a little hard, but also fun.
我加深咗對新同事嘅了解。I have gotten to know my new colleagues better.
我喺辦公室嘅感覺越來越好。I feel better and better at the office.
漫長嘅一日之後，做運動真係好正。The workout is lovely after a long day.
郁動下身體感覺好好。It is good to move my body.
我真係值得好好享受呢次運動。I have really earned this workout.
我將啲啞鈴放低喺地下，飲少少水。I put the weights on the floor and drink a little water.
我對住塊鏡入面嘅自己微笑。I smile to myself in the mirror.
我終於真正成為團隊嘅一份子。I am finally a real part of the team.
friday canteen lunch
Storyboard: The busy week ends with a shared lunch in the bright canteen. Maya sits with Peter, Eva, and the tester Mads, discussing how hard everyone has worked, and she finally feels like a true part of the team.
終於到星期五喇，忙碌嘅一星期就快完喇。It is finally Friday, and the busy week is almost over.
我哋喺光猛嘅飯堂度一齊食晏。We are eating lunch together in the bright canteen.
我同我嘅團隊一齊坐喺一張大枱度。I am sitting at a large table together with my team.
Peter、我哋嘅設計師 Eva，同埋測試員 Mads 都喺度。Peter, our designer Eva, and our tester Mads are here too.
我哋全部人都好攰，不過就好開心。We are all tired, but we are very happy.
呢個新項目真係好令人興奮。The new project has been exciting.
「我哋真係好努力做嘢，」Peter 笑住講。"We have worked really hard," Peter says and smiles.
「係呀，我哋解決咗好多複雜嘅問題，」我答。"Yes, we have solved many complex problems," I reply.
作為軟件工程師，我哋今個星期寫咗好多程式碼。As software engineers, we have written a lot of code this week.
「而我就測試晒全部，」Mads 笑住講。"And I have tested it all," Mads laughs.
「好彩你冇搵到好多錯處啫，」Peter 講笑咁話。"Luckily, you have not found many mistakes," Peter teases.
Eva 喺佢部手機度畀我哋睇佢好靚嘅設計。Eva shows us her beautiful design on her phone.
佢做設計師已經做咗好多年。She has worked as a designer for many years.
我哋一邊食，一邊傾大家唔同嘅工作任務。We talk about our different tasks while we eat.
可以更加深入認識我嘅同事真係好。It is lovely to get to know my colleagues better.
我哋已經成為咗一個好好嘅團隊。We have become a really good team.
我食咗一啖嘢，然後望住佢哋。I take a bite of my food and look at them.
我好期待同佢哋繼續做呢個項目。I look forward to continuing the project with them.
