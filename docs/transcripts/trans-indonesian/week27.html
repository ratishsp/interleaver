tuesday admin
Storyboard: Maya clears her small kitchen table on a quiet Tuesday evening. She realizes she needs to update her payment details on her electricity provider's website, a boring digital chore she usually puts off until the last minute.

Aku biasanya menunda hal semacam itu sampai menit terakhir.I usually put off that kind of thing until the last minute.
Sebenarnya, hal itu sering kali benar-benar terlupakan sampai aku mendapat peringatan.Actually, it often gets completely forgotten until I get a warning.

Aku menatap meja yang kosong sejenak.I look at the empty table for a moment.

Aku mengeluarkan ponsel dari saku dengan helaan napas berat.I take the phone out of my pocket with a heavy sigh.
Sekarang ini hanya perlu segera diselesaikan.Now it just needs to be over and done with.
digital form
