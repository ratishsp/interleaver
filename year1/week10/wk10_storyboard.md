# Week 10 — Storyboard (Maya buys winter clothes)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Adjective agreement *en/et/*plural; *har på*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_a_cold_morning | It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter. |
| 2 | 02_entering_the_shop | After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things. |
| 3 | 03_the_red_sweater | Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it. |
| 4 | 04_the_tight_green_coat | Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a large size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. |
| 5 | 05_the_blue_scarf | Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases. |
| 6 | 06_the_black_gloves | Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well. |
| 7 | 07_paying_for_the_clothes | Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is six hundred and fifty kroner, and Maya pays with her card. |
| 8 | 08_stepping_into_the_cold | Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She wears the red sweater under her jacket, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm. |
