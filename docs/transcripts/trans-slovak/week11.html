dark winter morning

Rozvidnieva sa veľmi neskoro.It becomes light very late.

Ale tu slnko spí.But here the sun is sleeping.
Je ťažké zobudiť sa v tme.It is hard to wake up in the dark.
Pozerám sa na hodiny.I look at the clock.
Vstávam z postele.I get out of bed.
Deň sa teraz začína.The day begins now.
checking the forecast

bundling up

strong wind
