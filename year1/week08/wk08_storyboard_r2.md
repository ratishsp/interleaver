# Week 8 — Storyboard (Maya explores the city)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Imperatives *gå/drej*; direction prepositions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_nina_gives_directions | On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn right at the park." |
| 2 | 02_the_first_leg | Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns right at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door. |
| 3 | 03_asking_an_older_man | Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn left." |
| 4 | 04_getting_lost | Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician. She wanders down a narrow side street, takes two random turns, and suddenly stands in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum or find her way back to the square. |
| 5 | 05_the_helpful_dog_walker | Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for the correct way to the harbour. The woman smiles, points down the quiet street, and gives her a new route: "Go straight to the end of this street, and then turn right." |
| 6 | 06_arriving_at_the_harbour | Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She walks straight to the end of the street, turns right, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats. |
| 7 | 07_the_little_mermaid | Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico. |
| 8 | 08_walking_home | Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. She does not ask anyone for directions this time. She confidently goes straight, turns the right corners, and feels happy that she can finally picture the city map in her head. |
