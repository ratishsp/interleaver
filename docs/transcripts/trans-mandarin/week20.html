Week 20: Maya starts volunteering at the café

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding the volunteering
Storyboard: Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. One evening she browses a volunteering website, reads a few listings, and finds the little café she already knows, which needs weekend helpers, so she fills in the short sign-up form.
我听说很多丹麦人在空闲时间做志愿工作。I have heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time.
我很喜欢这个主意。I like the idea.
我想学着把丹麦语说得更好，并结识新朋友。I want to learn to speak better Danish and meet new people.
一天晚上，我坐在电脑前。One evening, I am sitting with my computer.
我找到了一个关于志愿工作的网站。I find a website for voluntary work.
我读了网站上的前三条招募信息。I read the first three listings on the site.
第一条招募信息来自一家体育俱乐部。The first listing is from a sports club.
但他们星期四晚上需要人帮忙，而那时我要打羽毛球。But they need help on Thursday evenings, and I play badminton then.
第二条招募信息来自一家博物馆。The second listing is from a museum.
他们星期三晚上需要一个人，但我那时要健身。They need a person on Wednesday evenings, but I work out then.
然后我看了第三条招募信息。Then I look at the third listing.
它来自我已经知道的那家小咖啡馆。It is from the little café that I already know.
他们星期六上午需要人手。They need people for Saturday mornings.
这对我来说再合适不过了。That suits me absolutely perfectly.
我填了一张简短的表格，写上了我的名字和电子邮件地址。I fill in a short form with my name and my email address.
我还写下了我的电话号码：十二，三十四，五十六，七十八。I also write my phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight.
最后，我点击发送。Finally, I press send.
我以前从未在咖啡馆工作过，但我已经准备好去学了。I have never worked in a café before, but I am ready to learn it.
first shift arrival
Storyboard: On her very first Saturday shift, Maya arrives early at the little café. She ties her new apron in the back room and carries the chairs out to the pavement to prepare for opening.
这是我在小咖啡馆的第一个周六班。It is my first Saturday shift at the little café.
我提前到了。I have arrived early.
阳光照在哥本哈根的街道上。The sun is shining on the street in Copenhagen.
我很期待，但也有一点紧张。I am looking forward to it, but I am also a little nervous.
这是我第一次在咖啡馆工作。It is the first time I am working in a café.
我走进后面的房间做准备。I went into the back room to get ready.
桌子上放着一条给我的新围裙。On a table lay a new apron for me.
我把它穿上，并在背后系好。I put it on and tied it in the back.
穿上它的感觉很好。It felt good to wear it.
现在我准备好开始了。Now I am ready to begin.
我需要学习如何正确地进行咖啡馆的开门工作。I have to learn to open the café correctly.
首先，经理打开了那扇沉重的正门。First, the manager unlocked the heavy front door.
之后，我走到人行道上做准备。Afterwards, I went out onto the pavement to get ready.
我搬了四把椅子出去。I carried four chairs out.
我把它们摆在了一张小桌子周围。I have placed them around a little table.
过一会儿，其他志愿者就会来。In a little while, the other volunteers will arrive.
我对我的第一天感到非常激动。I am very excited about my first day.
learning the machine
Storyboard: Before the doors open, an experienced volunteer brings Maya over to the large espresso machine. The volunteer shows her how to grind the coffee beans and how to steam the milk. Maya watches every step closely, because it is her first time behind the counter.
这是我第一次站在吧台后面。It is my first time behind the counter.
咖啡馆还没有开门。The café is not open yet.
其中一位经验丰富的志愿者叫了我。One of the experienced volunteers calls for me.
我们站在那台大型浓缩咖啡机前面。We stand in front of the large espresso machine.
“今天你必须学习做咖啡，”他说。"Today you must learn to make coffee," he says.
我以前从来没有用过这种机器做咖啡。I have never made coffee on such a machine before.
第一步是研磨咖啡豆。The first step is to grind the coffee beans.
他按下一个小按钮。He presses a small button.
机器发出很大的声音，研磨着棕色的豆子。The machine grinds the brown beans with a loud sound.
第二步是用蒸汽打发牛奶。The second step is to steam the milk.
他把冷牛奶倒进一个金属壶里。He pours cold milk into a metal jug.
然后他打开了热蒸汽。Afterwards he turns on the hot steam.
与此同时，我仔细看着他的双手。I look closely at his hands meanwhile.
壶里的牛奶很快就变得温热而浓稠。The milk in the jug quickly becomes warm and thick.
很明显，他以前做过很多次咖啡。He has clearly made coffee many times before.
今天早上我刚到的时候，我非常紧张。When I arrived this morning, I was very nervous.
但他解释得很好，所以我已经明白了大部分。But he explained it well, so I have already understood most of it.
the first coffee
Storyboard: When the morning rush hits, Maya makes a cappuccino for a waiting customer. She has held the steam wand a bit too deep in the milk, so it does not get a thick foam. It still looks acceptable, though, so she pours the milk over the coffee and hands it over with a polite smile.
今天上午我们真是太忙了。This morning we have been incredibly busy.
我刚刚做了我的第一杯卡布奇诺。I have just made my very first cappuccino.
十点差一点的时候，一个男人走到了柜台前。A little before ten, a man came over to the counter.
“请给我一杯卡布奇诺，”他说。"A cappuccino, please," he said.
我才刚学会使用意式浓缩咖啡机。I have just learned to use the espresso machine.
所以我马上走到了机器前。So I went over to the machine right away.
我煮了咖啡，把蒸汽棒放进牛奶里。I brewed the coffee and put the steam wand down into the milk.
但我把它放得有点太深了。But I held it a little too deep down.
我可能是有点太紧张了。I have probably been a little too nervous.
遗憾的是，牛奶没有打出厚厚的奶泡。Unfortunately, the milk did not get any thick foam.
我小心翼翼地往奶泡壶里看了看。I looked carefully down into the pitcher.
奶泡不是十分完美。The foam was not completely perfect.
但它看起来还算可以接受。But it still looked acceptable.
我把热牛奶倒在杯子里的咖啡上。I poured the hot milk over the coffee in the cup.
然后我带着礼貌的微笑把咖啡递给了那个男人。Then I gave the coffee to the man with a polite smile.
“给您，一共五十五克朗，”我说。"Here you go, that will be fifty-five kroner," I said.
他接过杯子，在第一张桌子旁坐了下来。He took the cup and sat down at the first table.
bun with cheese
Storyboard: A moment later, she slices a warm sourdough bun and adds a thick piece of cheese. She places it on a small plate and navigates through the standing customers to deliver it safely to a corner booth.
没过多久，下一个订单来了。Shortly after, the next order came.
今天我已经做好了我的第一杯咖啡。I have already made my first coffee today.
但是这位顾客只想要食物。But this customer just wanted food.
他点了一个奶酪小圆面包。He ordered a bun with cheese.
我从篮子里拿出一个温热的酸种小圆面包。I took a warm sourdough bun from the basket.
酸种面包通常有一层硬外壳。Sourdough bread often has a hard crust.
但我已经学会了快速把面包切成两半。But I have learned to cut the bread in half quickly.
之后，我在小圆面包上放了一厚片奶酪。Afterwards, I put a thick slice of cheese on the bun.
我把食物漂亮地摆放在一个小盘子上。I arranged the food nicely on a small plate.
现在我得把它端给顾客。Now I had to deliver it to the customer.
咖啡馆中间站着很多人。There were many people standing in the middle of the café.
我端平盘子，小心翼翼地往前走。I balanced the plate and walked carefully forward.
我慢慢地从其他客人身边走过。I walked slowly past the other guests.
幸好那位顾客就坐在角落里。Fortunately, the customer was sitting right down in the corner.
他在第四张桌子旁等着。He was waiting at the fourth table.
我把盘子放在他面前的桌子上。I placed the plate on the table in front of him.
“给您，一个温热的奶酪小圆面包，”我说。"Here you go, a warm bun with cheese," I said.
“非常感谢，”那位男士回答道。"Thank you very much," the man replied.
看到满意的客人真是一件愉快的事。It is lovely to see a satisfied guest.
the ginger beer
Storyboard: During her second Saturday shift, a woman asks for a ginger beer. Maya finds the ginger beer in the glass fridge, inverts it so that the ginger mixes. Then she hands it to the customer.
那是我在咖啡馆的第二个周六班。It was my second Saturday shift in the café.
我已经学会了做很多工作。I have already learned to do a lot of the work.
那天上午外面非常暖和。That morning it was wonderfully warm outside.
一位女士走到了柜台前。A woman came over to the counter.
“我想点一瓶姜汁啤酒，”她说。"I would like to ask for a ginger beer," she said.
“当然，请稍等，”我回答。"Of course, just a moment," I answered.
我走到柜台后面的大玻璃冰箱前。I went over to the large glass fridge behind the counter.
我看向第三层架子。I looked on the third shelf.
那些冰镇的姜汁啤酒就放在那里。The cold bottles of ginger beer were standing there.
我找到一瓶，把它拿了出来。I found a bottle and took it out.
姜沉在底部，就像一团厚厚的云。The ginger lay like a thick cloud down at the bottom.
我学会了在打开瓶子之前把它混匀。I have learned to mix it before I open the bottle.
所以我非常小心地把瓶子倒过来。So I turned the bottle very carefully upside down.
我就这样拿了几秒钟。I held it like that for a couple of seconds.
姜慢慢地与其余的液体混合在了一起。The ginger mixed slowly with the rest of the liquid.
现在这饮料看起来刚刚好。Now the drink looked exactly right.
我又把瓶子转回来，放在了柜台上。I turned the bottle around again and placed it on the counter.
“给你，一共三十五克朗，”我对她说。"Here you go, that will be thirty-five kroner," I said to her.
她付了钱，想必她很享受她的冷饮。She paid, and she has probably enjoyed her cold drink.
second coffee progress
Storyboard: Later that morning, Maya tries foaming milk again for a large latte. This second attempt turns out much better, and she realizes that she has finally learned to control the pressure of the hot steam.
同一个星期六的晚些时候，我们又变得非常忙碌了。Later the same Saturday, we got very busy again.
一位年轻女士走到柜台前，点了一杯大杯卡布奇诺。A young woman came up to the counter and ordered a large cappuccino.
我做的第一杯不太完美。My first cup was not quite perfect.
但这次我准备好了。But this time I was ready.
我拿起金属壶，往里面倒了冷牛奶。I took the metal pitcher and poured cold milk into it.
我把壶放在机器下面，打开了热蒸汽。I placed the pitcher under the machine and turned on the hot steam.
我没有立刻调高压力。I did not turn up the pressure immediately.
我其实已经学会了去倾听机器的声音。I have actually learned to listen to the machine.
这次蒸汽发出的巨大声音刚刚好。The loud sound from the steam was exactly right this time.
牛奶慢慢变热了。The milk slowly became warm.
顶部的奶泡变得非常厚实绵软。And the foam on top became very thick and soft.
这是一个很大的进步。It was a big step forward.
我小心翼翼地把牛奶倒在大杯里的咖啡上。I carefully poured the milk over the coffee in a large cup.
它看起来确实像是一杯真正专业的卡布奇诺。It actually looked like a real professional cappuccino.
“给您，”我说着，把杯子放在了柜台上。"Here you go," I said and placed the cup on the counter.
女士笑了笑，端走了她的咖啡。The woman smiled and took her coffee.
我终于学会了控制压力。I have finally learned to control the pressure.
third saturday regular
