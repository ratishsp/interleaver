Week 14: Maya takes public transport across the city

Latvian with English gloss, line-aligned.

checking the route
Storyboard: Maya looks at a travel app on her phone. She needs to go across the city to pick up a package, so she plans a route with three different types of public transport.
Man šodien jāizņem paka.I need to pick up a package today.
Tā atrodas pilsētas otrā pusē.It is on the other side of the city.
Es skatos savā telefonā.I look at my phone.
Es atveru sabiedriskā transporta lietotni.I open a public transport app.
Es plānoju savu maršrutu.I plan my route.
Maršruts ir garš.The route is long.
Tam ir trīs daļas.It has three parts.
Vispirms man jābrauc ar autobusu.First, I need to take a bus.
Autobuss brauc līdz metro.The bus drives to the metro.
Tad man jābrauc ar metro.Then I need to ride the metro.
Visbeidzot man jādodas uz ostu.Finally, I need to go to the harbour.
Šeit man jābrauc ar ostas autobusu.Here, I need to take the harbour bus.
Ostas autobuss ir liela laiva.The harbour bus is a large boat.
Tas kuģo pa ūdeni.It sails on the water.
Paka ir mazā veikalā.The package is in a small shop.
Veikals atrodas tuvu ūdenim.The shop is located close to the water.
Tas ir labs plāns.It is a good plan.
Es paņemu savu zilo braukšanas karti.I take my blue travel card.
Es esmu gatava braucienam.I am ready for the trip.
topping up the card
Storyboard: She walks to a nearby ticket machine. She taps her blue travel card on the screen and pays two hundred kroner to top up her balance for the long trip.
Es pieeju pie biļešu automāta uz ielas.I walk over to a ticket machine on the street.
Vispirms manai braukšanas kartei ir nepieciešams vairāk naudas.First my travel card needs more money.
Es izņemu savu braukšanas karti no somas.I take my travel card out of my bag.
Tā ir maza, zila karte.It is a small, blue card.
Es uzlieku karti uz automāta ekrāna.I place the card on the machine's screen.
Ekrāns parāda manu atlikumu.The screen shows my balance.
Atlikums ir pārāk mazs garam braucienam.The balance is too low for a long trip.
Es pieskaros ekrānam.I tap on the screen.
Es izvēlos summu.I choose an amount.
Es samaksāju divsimt kronu ar savu norēķinu karti.I pay two hundred kroner with my payment card.
Automāts uz ekrāna uzraksta "Apstiprināts".The machine writes "Approved" on the screen.
Tagad man kartē ir nauda.Now I have money on the card.
Es ielieku karti atpakaļ somā.I put the card back in the bag.
Es esmu gatava garajam braucienam.I am ready for the long trip.
Tagad man ir jābrauc ar autobusu.Now I need to take the bus.
Es dodos uz autobusa pieturu.I walk over to the bus stop.
taking the bus
Storyboard: Maya waits at the bus stop until a long yellow bus arrives. She steps inside, scans her card at the door, and finds an empty seat by the window.
Es stāvu autobusa pieturā.I stand at the bus stop.
Drīz es braucu ar autobusu.Soon I travel by bus.
Es skatos lejup pa ielu.I look down the street.
Pēc piecām minūtēm atbrauc autobuss.After five minutes, the bus arrives.
Tas ir garš un dzeltens autobuss.It is a long and yellow bus.
Autobuss apstājas tieši manā priekšā.The bus stops right in front of me.
Durvis atveras.The doors open.
Es iekāpju autobusā.I step up into the bus.
Man rokā ir mana braukšanas karte.I have my travel card in my hand.
Es skenēju karti pie durvīm.I scan the card by the door.
Šodien ir daudz pasažieru.There are many passengers today.
Es meklēju brīvu vietu.I look for an empty seat.
Es atrodu labu vietu pie loga.I find a good seat by the window.
Es apsēžos.I sit down.
Es skatos ārā uz pilsētu.I look out at the city.
Tagad es braucu ar autobusu uz metro staciju.Now I take the bus to the metro station.
asking a passenger
Storyboard: She turns to an older woman sitting nearby. Maya politely asks if she travels with this bus to the metro station, and the woman nods with a friendly smile.
Es sēžu autobusā.I am sitting in the bus.
Es skatos ārā pa logu.I look out the window.
Es neesmu pilnīgi pārliecināta par maršrutu.I am not completely sure about the route.
Kāda vecāka sieviete sēž tuvu man.An older woman is sitting close to me.
Es paskatos uz viņu.I look over at her.
"Atvainojiet," es pieklājīgi saku."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Vai šis autobuss brauc uz metro staciju?""Does this bus go to the metro station?"
Sieviete paskatās uz mani.The woman looks at me.
Viņa pamāj ar draudzīgu smaidu.She nods with a friendly smile.
"Jā, tas brauc uz metro staciju," viņa atbild."Yes, it goes to the metro station," she replies.
"Vai jūs brauksiet ar metro?" sieviete jautā."Are you taking the metro?" the woman asks.
"Jā, es braukšu ar metro," es saku."Yes, I am taking the metro," I say.
"Es bieži braucu ar šo autobusu," sieviete saka."I often ride with this bus," the woman says.
"Tas ir vienkāršs maršruts," viņa piebilst."It is an easy route," she adds.
"Liels paldies par palīdzību," es saku."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Šis ir mans pirmais brauciens šajā maršrutā.""It is my first trip on this route."
Sieviete vēlreiz pamāj.The woman nods again.
Es atslābinos savā sēdvietā.I relax in my seat.
Mēs braucam tālāk cauri pilsētai.We drive on through the city.
transferring to metro
Storyboard: The bus stops, and Maya gets off at a busy square. She walks down the wide stairs into a bright, underground metro station and taps her card again.
Autobuss apstājas rosīgā laukumā.The bus stops at a busy square.
Es izkāpju no autobusa.I get off the bus.
Es atvados no vecākās sievietes.I say goodbye to the older woman.
Tagad es dodos uz metro.Now I am going to the metro.
Es eju pāri laukumam.I walk across the square.
Šeit ir daudz cilvēku.There are many people here.
Es kāpju lejā pa platajām kāpnēm.I walk down the wide stairs.
Stacija atrodas pazemē.The station is underground.
Tā ir ļoti liela un gaiša.It is very big and bright.
Man atkal jāreģistrējas.I have to check in again.
Es izņemu savu zilo karti.I take out my blue card.
Es noskenēju karti ierīcē.I scan the card on the machine.
Ierīce izdod skaļu pīkstienu.The machine makes a loud beep.
Tagad es varu braukt ar metro.Now I can ride with the metro.
Es eju lejā uz peronu.I walk down to the platform.
Es braukšu ar nākamo vilcienu.I am taking the next train.
Tas brauc tieši ostas virzienā.It goes straight towards the harbour.
Vilciens pienāks pēc trim minūtēm.The train arrives in three minutes.
riding the metro
Storyboard: Maya stands right at the front of the driverless train. She rides with the metro through the dark tunnels and watches the lights blur past as they speed forward.
Metro pienāk, un durvis atveras.The metro arrives, and the doors open.
Es iekāpju vilcienā.I go into the train.
Vilciens brauc bez vadītāja.The train drives without a driver.
Es aizeju līdz pašai priekšai.I walk all the way to the front.
Es stāvu pie lielā loga.I stand by the large window.
Es braucu ar metro cauri tumsai.I ride with the metro through the dark.
Mēs esam garā tunelī.We are in a long tunnel.
Mēs braucam ļoti ātri.We drive very fast.
Gaisma lido garām logam.The light flies past the window.
Tas izskatās fantastiski.It looks fantastic.
Mazs zēns stāv man blakus.A little boy stands next to me.
Viņš arī skatās ārā tumsā.He also looks out into the dark.
Es bieži braucu ar metro.I often take the metro.
Tas ir ātrs un viegls brauciens.It is a fast and easy trip.
Tagad mēs tuvojamies manai stacijai.Now we are approaching my station.
Es dodos uz ostu.I am going to the harbour.
Mans nākamais brauciens ir ar ostas autobusu.My next trip is with the harbour bus.
Es ar nepacietību gaidu, kad redzēšu ūdeni.I am looking forward to seeing the water.
finding the pier
Storyboard: She takes the escalator up to the street level and walks toward the water. She finds a small wooden pier by the edge of the canal where the harbour bus stops.
Brauciens ar metro ir beidzies.The trip with the metro is over.
Tagad es dodos augšā uz ielu.Now I am going up to the street.
Es braucu ar eskalatoru.I take the escalator.
Augšā uz ielas spīd saule.The sun is shining up on the street.
Tagad es dodos pie ūdens.Now I am going to the water.
Es braukšu ar ostas autobusu.I am going by the harbour bus.
Es eju pa platu ielu.I walk down a wide street.
Tad es redzu kanālu.Then I see the canal.
Ūdens ir ļoti zils.The water is very blue.
Es meklēju savu pieturu.I look for my stop.
Kur apstājas kuģītis?Where does the boat stop?
Es eju tuvāk malai.I walk closer to the edge.
Tad es redzu mazu koka piestātni.Then I see a small wooden pier.
Tā atrodas pašā lejā pie ūdens.It is located right down by the water.
Pie piestātnes ir dzeltena zīme.There is a yellow sign by the pier.
Uz zīmes ir rakstīts "Ostas autobuss".It says "Harbour bus" on the sign.
Šī ir īstā vieta.This is the right place.
Es izeju uz piestātnes.I walk out onto the pier.
Tagad es tikai gaidu kuģīti.Now I am just waiting for the boat.
the harbour bus
Storyboard: A wide boat arrives, and Maya boards the harbour bus. She stands on the open deck and enjoys the fresh air as she sails across the blue water.
Plata laiva brauc pāri ūdenim.A wide boat comes across the water.
Tas ir ostas autobuss.It is the harbour bus.
Laiva piepeld pie koka piestātnes.The boat sails in to the wooden pier.
Tā apstājas pie malas.It stops at the edge.
Es tagad braucu ar ostas autobusu.I am going with the harbour bus now.
Es uzkāpju uz kuģa.I step on board the ship.
Es atkal noskenēju savu zilo braukšanas karti.I scan my blue travel card again.
Iekšā sēž daudz pasažieru.Many passengers sit inside.
Bet es izeju uz atvērtā klāja.But I walk out onto the open deck.
Es stāvu pašā priekšgalā.I stand at the very front.
Mēs ar laivu braucam pāri zilajam ūdenim.We sail with the boat across the blue water.
Pilsēta no ūdens izskatās skaista.The city looks beautiful from the water.
Saule spīd uz mums.The sun shines down on us.
Es dziļi ieelpoju.I take a deep breath.
Gaiss ir ļoti svaigs un auksts.The air is very fresh and cold.
Es izbaudu īso braucienu.I enjoy the short trip.
Mēs drīz būsim otrā pusē.We are soon over at the other side.
arriving for the errand
Storyboard: The boat docks at the opposite shore. Maya steps off the vessel and walks toward a small shop on the corner to finally collect her package.
Laiva ierodas ūdens otrā pusē.The boat arrives at the other side of the water.
Ostas autobuss apstājas pie nelielas piestātnes.The harbour bus stops at a small pier.
Es izkāpju no laivas.I get off the boat.
Brauciens ar laivu tagad ir beidzies.The trip by boat is now over.
Es apskatu karti savā telefonā.I check the map on my phone.
Es dodos uz nelielu kiosku.I am going to a small kiosk.
Kiosks atrodas tur, uz stūra.The kiosk is located over on a corner.
Es eju pa ielu uz augšu.I walk up the street.
Es redzu veikalu ceļa galā.I see the shop at the end of the road.
Es atveru durvis un ieeju iekšā.I open the door and walk in.
Jauns vīrietis stāv aiz kases.A young man stands behind the register.
"Sveiki," viņš laipni saka."Hi," he says kindly.
"Sveiki, es vēlos saņemt paku," es saku."Hi, I am going to pick up a package," I say.
Vīrietis pamāj ar galvu un pasmaida.The man nods and smiles.
Viņš jautā manu vārdu.He asks for my name.
"Mans vārds ir Maya," es atbildu."My name is Maya," I answer.
Viņš atrod lielu kasti uz plaukta.He finds a large box on a shelf.
Viņš iedod man paku.He gives the package to me.
"Liels paldies par palīdzību," es saku."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
Tagad es ar savu paku atkal dodos garajā mājupceļā.Now I take the long trip home again with my package.
