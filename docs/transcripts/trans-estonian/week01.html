Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Estonian with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
Lennuk on nüüd Kopenhaagenis.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
On jaanuar.It is January.
Ma olen väga põnevil.I am very excited.
Ma pakin oma raamatu kotti.I pack my book into my bag.
Minu kõrval on reisija.There is a passenger next to me.
Ta vaatab mind ja naeratab.He looks at me and smiles.
"Tere," ütleb ta."Hello," he says.
"Tere," ütlen ma."Hello," I say.
"Mis su nimi on?" küsib ta."What is your name?" he asks.
"Minu nimi on Maya," ütlen ma."My name is Maya," I say.
"Kust sa pärit oled, Maya?" küsib ta."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
Ma naeratan rõõmsalt.I smile happily.
"Ma olen pärit Mehhikost," vastan ma."I come from Mexico," I answer.
"Kas sa oled Taanist?" küsin ma."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
"Jah, ma olen Taanist," ütleb ta."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
"Tere tulemast Taani," ütleb ta."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
"Suur tänu," ütlen ma."Thank you very much," I say.
Nüüd me oleme siin.Now we are here.
Ma olen valmis.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
Ma olen nüüd väljas.I am outside now.
Minu kohver on suur ja raske.My suitcase is big and heavy.
Seal on takso.There is a taxi.
Ma tõstan oma kohvri taksosse.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
Juht on sõbralik mees.The driver is a friendly man.
"Mis on aadress?" küsib ta."What is the address?" he asks.
"See on Parkvej number kaksteist," ütlen ma."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
Ta noogutab ja sõidab.He nods and drives.
"Kas te olete turist?" küsib juht."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
"Ei, ma ei ole turist," vastan ma."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
"Kust te pärit olete?" küsib ta."Where do you come from?" he asks.
"Ma olen pärit Mehhikost," ütlen ma."I come from Mexico," I say.
Ma vaatan aknast välja.I look out the window.
Ma olen väga uhke.I am very proud.
"Ma olen tarkvarainsener," ütlen ma."I am a software engineer," I say.
"Minu uus töökoht on siin Kopenhaagenis.""My new job is here in Copenhagen."
Ta naeratab.He smiles.
"Kui põnev.""How exciting."
"Edu Taanis," ütleb ta."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
"Suur tänu," ütlen ma."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
Uks on lahti.The door is open.
Lävepakk on kõrge.The threshold is high.
Kohver on väga raske!The suitcase is very heavy!
Aga nüüd on kohver esikus.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
Siin on täiesti pime.It is completely dark here.
Kus on lüliti?Where is the switch?
Sein on külm.The wall is cold.
Kas lüliti on siin?Is the switch here?
Jah, seal on lüliti!Yes, there is the switch!
Nüüd on valge.Now there is light.
Ma olen korteris.I am in the apartment.
See on väike, aga see on kena.It is small, but it is nice.
Siin on elutuba.Here is a living room.
Seal on diivan ja laud.There is a sofa and a table.
See on möbleeritud kodu.It is a furnished home.
Kohver on põrandal.The suitcase is on the floor.
Ma olen väga väsinud.I am very tired.
Aga ma olen ka rõõmus.But I am also happy.
Ma olen Kopenhaagenis!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
Ma seisan akna juures.I am standing by the window.
Klaas on külm.The glass is cold.
On jaanuar.It is January.
Ma vaatan välja.I look out.
Kopenhaagen on täna hall.Copenhagen is grey today.
Väljas on külm.It is cold outside.
Tänaval on lumi.There is snow on the street.
Aga siin on soe.But it is warm here.
Korter on hea.The apartment is good.
Ma mõtlen Mehhikole.I think about Mexico.
Mehhikos on päike.In Mexico, there is sun.
Seal on ka soe.It is also warm there.
Ma igatsen natuke päikest.I miss the sun a little.
Aga ma naeratan.But I smile.
See on uus linn.It is a new city.
Ma olen nüüd Taanis.I am in Denmark now.
See on hea algus.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
Ma olen nüüd elutoas.I am in the living room now.
Seal on väike diivan.There is a small sofa.
Ma helistan oma emale Mehhikosse.I call my mother in Mexico.
"Tere, ema," ütlen ma."Hi Mom," I say.
"Tere, Maya," ütleb mu ema."Hi Maya," my mother says.
"Kus sa oled?" küsib ta."Where are you?" she asks.
"Ma olen Kopenhaagenis," vastan ma."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
"Ma olen praegu korteris.""I am in the apartment now."
"See on väga tore," ütleb mu ema."That is so good," my mother says.
"Kas see on suur?" küsib ta."Is it big?" she asks.
"Ei, see on väike," vastan ma."No, it is small," I answer.
"Aga see on hubane ja ma olen õnnelik.""But it is cozy, and I am happy."
"Kas seal on külm?" küsib ta."Is it cold?" she asks.
"Jah, väljas on külm," ütlen ma."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
"Aga siin on soe.""But it is warm in here."
"Tunnen end turvaliselt.""I am safe."
"See on hea," ütleb mu ema."That is good," my mother says.
Ma naeratan.I smile.
Taani on minu uus kodu.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
Ma avan oma ukse.I open my door.
Ma seisan trepikojas.I am standing in the stairwell.
On vaikne.It is quiet.
Üks uks avaneb.A door opens.
Üks naine naeratab mulle.A woman smiles at me.
"Tere," ütleb ta."Hi," she says.
"Mis su nimi on?" küsib ta."What is your name?" she asks.
"Minu nimi on Maya," vastan ma."My name is Maya," I answer.
"Kust sa pärit oled?" küsib ta."Where do you come from?" she asks.
"Ma olen pärit Mehhikost," ütlen ma."I come from Mexico," I say.
"Tere tulemast majja," ütleb naine."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
"Minu nimi on Nina," ütleb ta."My name is Nina," she says.
"Ma olen su naaber.""I am your neighbour."
Nina on väga sõbralik.Nina is very friendly.
Ta räägib aeglaselt ja selgelt.She speaks slowly and clearly.
"Siin on minu uks," ütleb ta."Here is my door," she says.
"Ma aitan meeleldi.""I am happy to help."
"Lihtsalt koputa," ütleb Nina."You just knock," Nina says.
"Suur aitäh, Nina," vastan ma."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
Ma olen väga rõõmus.I am very happy.
Nüüd on mul Kopenhaagenis sõber.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
Nüüd on öö.Now it is night.
See on mu esimene öö Kopenhaagenis.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
Ma olen vannitoas.I am in the bathroom.
See on väga väike.It is very small.
Seal on kraanikauss.There is a sink.
Minu hambahari on punane.My toothbrush is red.
Ma panen hambaharjale hambapastat.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
Ma pesen hambaid.I brush my teeth.
Vesi on külm.The water is cold.
Ma pesen nägu.I wash my face.
Ma kuivatan nägu.I dry my face.
Ma lähen oma voodi juurde.I go over to my bed.
Voodi on kitsas.The bed is narrow.
Aga see on soe ja pehme.But it is warm and soft.
Ma leban nüüd teki all.I am lying under the duvet now.
Mu telefon on laual.My phone is lying on the table.
Ma panen äratuse.I set my alarm.
See heliseb homme kell kaheksa.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
Ma olen väsinud, aga ma olen valmis.I am tired, but I am ready.
Head ööd, Kopenhaagen.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
