# Week 6 — Storyboard (A typical workday routine in Kochi)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Present continuous **-ുകയാണ്** for actions underway, woven naturally through a simple-present **-ഉന്നു** routine

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_coffee | Maya wakes up to her ringing alarm. She boils water on the stove and makes a cup of hot coffee. She turns off the stove, walks onto the balcony, and drinks her coffee in the pleasant morning breeze. |
| 2 | 02_getting_ready | Maya takes a quick shower and brushes her teeth. She chooses a clean cotton shirt and comfortable trousers for the office. She steps out of her flat and locks the front door securely. |
| 3 | 03_green_bus_commute | Maya waits at the local bus stop. A green private city bus arrives. She boards the crowded vehicle and holds the overhead rail tightly. The bus is moving quickly through the heavy Kochi traffic. |
| 4 | 04_office_arrival | Maya walks into the cool, air-conditioned software company. Several colleagues are already sitting at their desks. She greets them politely and settles into her own chair for the day. |
| 5 | 05_morning_focus | Maya looks closely at her bright computer screen. She is typing a long document for her project. She finishes the difficult task and feels very relieved. |
| 6 | 06_lunch_with_prakash | At midday, Maya takes a break in the bright office canteen. Her colleague Prakash sits at her table and tells a funny story. Maya is eating her spicy fish curry and laughs out loud. |
| 7 | 07_evening_return | The workday ends, and Maya rides the green bus back home. The sun is setting over the city. She watches the colorful shop awnings outside the window and relaxes completely. |
| 8 | 08_boiling_rice | Back in her peaceful flat, Maya stands in the kitchen. A pot of local red rice is boiling on the stove. She chops fresh vegetables for dinner and hums an upbeat song. |
| 9 | 09_sofia_and_lola | Maya sits comfortably on her sofa with a full stomach. She starts a video call with her sister Sofia. On the screen, the family dog, Lola, is sleeping heavily on the floor back in Mexico. |
| 10 | 10_falling_asleep | Maya turns off the main lights and lies under her lightweight blanket. She is reading a new book. Her eyes feel heavy, so she puts the novel down and quickly falls asleep. |
