# Week 15 — Storyboard (A Day in the Life)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Mixed present-tense narration; consolidation. BOUNDARY: no past tense anywhere — the Perfekt opens week 16

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_routine | Maya wakes up early. The spring sun shines brightly into the flat. She gets up, goes to the kitchen and makes herself coffee. She drinks it slowly at the table, enjoying the quiet morning, then packs her laptop into her backpack and leaves the flat. |
| 2 | 02_the_lost_tourist | Maya waits on the platform for the U-Bahn. A man with a map approaches her and asks for directions to a famous museum. Maya smiles, points to the map, and explains the route: he must take the next train, ride for three stops, and get out. The man thanks her and boards his train. |
| 3 | 03_office_morning | Maya arrives at the software company. Her colleague Jonas is already sitting at his desk. They greet each other and talk briefly about the sunny weather. Maya sits down, takes her laptop out of her backpack, and starts her work for the day. |
| 4 | 04_lunch_at_the_bakery | At lunchtime, Maya walks to the bakery on the corner. The shop is busy, but she soon orders a cheese Brötchen and a black tea. Maya pays with a ten Euro note, takes her change and the receipt, and goes outside to eat in the sun. |
| 5 | 05_afternoon_with_jonas | Back in the office, Maya focuses on her code. Jonas comes over to her screen because he has a question about the software. They look at the problem together, discuss a few ideas, and find a good solution. |
| 6 | 06_the_supermarket_run | After work, Maya stops at the supermarket near her home. She needs ingredients for dinner. She walks through the aisles, picks up vegetables, pasta, and apples, and carries them to the checkout. She pays for her groceries and packs everything carefully into die Einkaufstasche, so the food stays safely separated from her laptop in her Rucksack. |
| 7 | 07_stairs_with_sabine | Maya enters her building and climbs the stairs. On the second floor, she meets her neighbour Sabine, who is going out for a walk with her small dog, Bruno. They chat about their day, Maya pets Bruno, and they wish each other a pleasant evening. |
| 8 | 08_message_from_home | Maya is finally back in the flat. She cooks her pasta, sits at the kitchen table, and eats her dinner. She picks up her phone and smiles: there is a long, funny message from her sister Sofia in Mexico. She reads the message, replies, and relaxes after a long, successful day. |
