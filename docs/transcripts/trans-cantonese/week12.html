Week 12: Maya joins a badminton club

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding a club
Storyboard: Maya works out at the gym on Wednesdays but wants to meet people, so she looks for a social sport. She loves badminton — she played it a lot back home in Mexico — so she finds a local club online and books a trial session for Thursday evening.
我逢星期三晚都會去健身室。I go to the gym every Wednesday evening.
我鍾意做運動。I like to work out.
但係我都想參加一項體育運動。But I also want to play a sport.
我好鍾意打羽毛球。I love to play badminton.
以前喺墨西哥屋企嗰陣，我成日打。I played it a lot back home in Mexico.
而家我想搵一間本地嘅球會。Now I am looking for a local club.
我坐喺梳化度用緊電腦。I am sitting on my couch with my computer.
我上網搜尋。I search online.
我搵到一間幾好嘅羽毛球會。I find a good badminton club.
我睇吓球會個網頁。I look at the club's website.
佢哋有好多隊。They have many teams.
佢哋星期四晚打波。They play on Thursday evening.
我想預約一堂試堂。I want to book a trial session.
我喺網頁度搵到份表格。I find a form on the website.
我寫低我個名同電郵。I write my name and my email.
我揀星期四晚。I choose Thursday evening.
練習夜晚七點開始。The training starts at seven in the evening.
啱晒。It is perfect.
我好期待可以再打波。I look forward to playing again.
arriving at hall
Storyboard: On Thursday evening Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sound of shoes squeaking on the many wooden courts.
星期四夜晚，我喺屋企。It is Thursday evening, and I am at home.
我執緊我個運動袋。I pack my sports bag.
我將啲鞋同啲衫擺落個袋度。I put my shoes and my clothes in the bag.
我好期待今晚去打波。I look forward to playing tonight.
我搭巴士去一間好大嘅體育館。I take the bus to a large sports hall.
今晚係一個好凍嘅冬天夜晚。It is a cold winter evening.
我坐喺巴士度望出窗外。I sit in the bus and look out.
巴士停低，跟住我落車。The bus stops, and I get out.
我行入去嗰間大體育館。I walk into the large sports hall.
入面好光猛。It is very bright.
我見到體育館入面有好多人。I see many people in the hall.
佢哋好鍾意做運動。They love to play sports.
我聽到一陣好大嘅聲。I hear a loud sound.
啲鞋喺好多個木地板球場上面發出吱吱聲。The shoes squeak on the many wooden courts.
啲人跑得好快，一齊打波。People run fast and play together.
睇落去好好玩。It looks very fun.
我準備好打波喇。I am ready to play.
不過首先我要租塊球拍。But first I need to rent a racket.
renting the racket
Storyboard: Maya goes to the small hall kiosk. She tells the clerk she is there to try out the club, and pays forty kroner to rent a racket for the evening.
我行過去體育館入面嘅一間細小賣部。I walk over to a small kiosk in the sports hall.
有個後生仔企喺櫃檯後面。A young man stands behind the counter.
佢笑住講哈囉。He smiles and says hello.
「你好，我今日想打羽毛球，」我話。"Hello, I want to play badminton today," I say.
「你係咪新嚟呢個球會㗎？」佢問。"Are you new here in the club?" he asks.
「係呀，我想試下玩，」我答。"Yes, I would like to try to join in," I answer.
「歡迎，」個男人話。"Welcome," the man says.
「你需唔需要租球拍呀？」佢問。"Do you need to rent a racket?" he asks.
「要呀唔該，我想租塊球拍今晚用，」我話。"Yes please, I want to rent a racket for the evening," I say.
佢搵咗塊好嘅球拍畀我。He finds a good racket for me.
「四十克朗，」佢話。"It costs forty kroner," he says.
我攞張銀行卡出嚟。I take out my payment card.
我畀咗嗰四十克朗。I pay the forty kroner.
佢隔住個櫃檯將塊球拍遞畀我。He gives me the racket over the counter.
「我覺得呢項運動好好玩，」我開心咁話。"I think it is a fun sport," I say happily.
「祝你打得開心！」"Have a really good game."
我講完多謝，就行過去球場嗰邊。I say thank you and walk over to the courts.
playing doubles
Storyboard: In the busy hall, Maya joins a doubles game with two friendly players, Kasper and Sara, and a fourth person. She has played for years, so she smashes the shuttlecock hard across the net.
我行過去一個羽毛球場。I walk over to a badminton court.
場上面企咗三個人。Three people are standing on the court.
「你好，我叫 Kasper，」一個男人話。"Hi, I am Kasper," a man says.
「而我叫 Sara，」一個女人話。"And I am Sara," a woman says.
第三個球員叫 Emil。The third player is named Emil.
「我叫 Maya，」我話。"I am Maya," I say.
「我哋通常打雙打，」Kasper 話。"We usually play doubles," Kasper says.
「你想唔想同我哋一齊打？」佢問。"Do you want to play with us?" he asks.
「好呀，非常樂意，」我答。"Yes, I would love to," I reply.
我好鍾意打羽毛球。I really like to play badminton.
Kasper 同我對戰 Sara 同 Emil。Kasper and I play against Sara and Emil.
我哋即刻開始打。We start to play immediately.
Sara 開始呢場比賽。Sara starts the match.
個羽毛球好快咁飛嚟飛去。The shuttlecock flies quickly back and forth.
Emil 將個羽毛球打上半空。Emil hits the shuttlecock high up in the air.
我好鍾意扣殺。I love to smash.
我跳起，用力將個波扣殺過網。I jump and smash it hard over the net.
個波落喺佢哋嗰邊半場。It lands on their side of the court.
「好波，Maya！」Kasper 大叫。"Good shot, Maya!" Kasper shouts.
post match chat
Storyboard: The fast match finishes and the tired players walk off the court. Sara says Maya plays really well, and Maya explains that she plays a lot back home in Mexico.
呢場節奏咁快嘅比賽完喇。The fast match is over.
我哋一齊行出球場。We walk off the court together.
我好攰。I am very tired.
卡斯柏同莎拉都好攰。Kasper and Sara are also tired.
我哋飲咗啲水。We drink some water.
莎拉話：「你打得好好呀。」"You play very well," Sara says.
我話：「多謝晒。」"Thank you very much," I say.
「我覺得好好玩。」"I think it is so much fun."
卡斯柏問：「你係咪成日打㗎？」"Do you play often?" Kasper asks.
我答：「係呀，我喺墨西哥老家成日打。」"Yes, I play a lot back home in Mexico," I answer.
「我平時會同我阿妹一齊打。」"I usually play with my sister."
莎拉話：「呢項運動好好玩。」"It is a fun sport," Sara says.
「我哋好鍾意喺呢間球會打波。」"We love to play here in the club."
我話：「我想喺丹麥打多啲波。」"I want to play more here in Denmark," I say.
卡斯柏笑住話：「你真係好快。」"You are definitely fast," Kasper laughs.
我話：「多謝，不過我而家想坐低。」"Thanks, but now I want to sit down," I say.
卡斯柏問：「不如我哋入去餐廳？」"Shall we go into the cafeteria?" Kasper asks.
我答：「好呀，我都好想。」"Yes, I would very much like that," I answer.
hall cafeteria
Storyboard: The group sits in the small hall cafeteria to drink water and chat. Kasper asks if Maya plans to come back, and she happily promises to make it a weekly Thursday routine.
我哋行入體育館間細餐廳。We walk into the small cafeteria in the hall.
我哋搵到張枱。We find a table.
我好口渴。I am very thirsty.
我哋飲住凍水傾偈。We drink cold water and talk.
我話：「呢個球會真係好好。」"This is a really good club," I say.
Kasper話：「呢個係一項好好嘅運動。」"It is a good sport to do," Kasper says.
Kasper望住我。Kasper looks at me.
佢問：「你想唔想再一齊玩啊？」"Do you want to join again?" he asks.
我答：「想啊，非常樂意。」"Yes, very much," I answer.
我解釋：「我想每個星期四都嚟呢度打波。」"I want to play here every Thursday," I explain.
Sara話：「聽落真係好。」"That sounds lovely," Sara says.
Kasper話：「我哋逢星期四都會喺度打波。」"We always play here on Thursdays," Kasper says.
我好開心咁話：「咁我哋下個禮拜見啦。」"Then we'll see each other next week," I say happily.
我哋飲埋淨低嗰啲水。We drink the rest of our water.
識到新朋友真係開心。It is lovely to meet new people.
walking to bus
Storyboard: Maya leaves the sports hall with Sara. They walk together to the bus stop in the cold winter evening, and Maya feels glad to have met fun, active locals.
莎拉同我行出體育館。Sara and I walk out of the sports hall.
時間好夜，而且係一個寒冷嘅冬天夜晚。It is late, and it is a cold winter evening.
不過打完比賽之後我仲覺得好熱。But I am still warm after the match.
我哋一齊行過去巴士站。We walk over to the bus stop together.
吹緊風，條街好黑。The wind is blowing, and the street is dark.
我問：「你週末鍾唔鍾意打羽毛球㗎？」"Do you also like to play badminton at the weekend?" I ask.
莎拉答：「唔會，我淨係星期四打羽毛球。」"No, I only play badminton on Thursdays," Sara answers.
佢話：「但我鍾意做運動。」"But I like to do sports," she says.
佢繼續講：「我平時會去公園跑步。」"I usually go for a run in the park," she continues.
佢係一個好活躍同埋好有趣嘅人。She is a very active and fun person.
我哋喺寒風中等巴士。We wait for the bus in the cold.
我話：「我架巴士十分鐘之後到。」"My bus comes in ten minutes," I say.
莎拉笑住話：「我哋搭同一架巴士喎。」"We take the same bus," Sara smiles.
呢樣嘢令我好開心。I am very happy about that.
我好鍾意同佢傾偈。I like to talk with her.
卡斯帕同莎拉都係有趣又友善嘅人。Kasper and Sara are fun and nice people.
依家我逢星期四都有個好好嘅規律。Now I have a good routine on Thursdays.
我好攰，但係心情好好。I am tired, but I am in a good mood.
巴士終於到喇。The bus finally arrives.
我已經好期待下個星期四喇。I am already looking forward to next Thursday.
