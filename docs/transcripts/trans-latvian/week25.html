Week 25: A Sunday in the city

morning calendar
Storyboard: On Sunday morning, Maya makes a fresh pot of coffee and flips her wall calendar to July. She realizes that she moved to Copenhagen exactly six months ago, and decides to celebrate by spending this sunny day exploring the city at her own pace.

Saule spīd iekšā pa mana dzīvokļa logu.The sun is shining in through the window in my apartment.

Kafijas smarža šorīt ir fantastiska.The smell of coffee is fantastic this morning.
Man pie sienas ir kalendārs.I have a calendar on the wall.
Es pāršķiru lapu uz nākamo mēnesi.I turn the page to the next month.
Tagad ir jūlijs.It is July now.
Es pārvācos uz Kopenhāgenu janvārī.I moved to Copenhagen in January.
Es ierados šeit tieši pirms sešiem mēnešiem.I came here exactly six months ago.
Laiks tiešām rit ātri.Time really goes fast.
Mana pirmā diena šajā pilsētā bija ļoti auksta.My first day in this city was very cold.
Bet šodien laiks ir silts un skaists.But today the weather is warm and beautiful.
Es gribu nosvinēt šo mazo gadadienu.I want to celebrate this little anniversary.
Man šodien nav noteiktu plānu.I have no fixed plans today.
Es paņemu savu kafijas tasi un skatos ārā uz ielu.I take my coffee cup and look out over the street.
Es gribu iet ārā un izpētīt pilsētu savā tempā.I want to go out and explore the city at my own pace.
Es ar nepacietību gaidu, kad varēšu izbaudīt šīs gaišās stundas.I look forward to enjoying these bright hours.
ceramic plates
Storyboard: Maya wanders into a lively second-hand market in a nearby square. She spots two blue ceramic plates on a table, decides these plates are perfect for her kitchen, and buys them from the vendor for forty kroner.

Šeit katru svētdienu ir liels krāmu tirgus.Here there is a large flea market every Sunday.
Spīd saule, un daudzi cilvēki pastaigājas starp galdiem.The sun is shining, and many people are walking around between the tables.
Es apstājos pie neliela galda ar daudzām dažādām lietām.I stop by a small table with many different things.
Aiz galda stāv draudzīgs pārdevējs.Behind the table stands a friendly vendor.
Pēkšņi es ieraugu divus zilus keramikas šķīvjus.Suddenly I see two blue ceramic plates.
Es tos paceļu un rūpīgi aplūkoju.I pick them up and look closely at them.
"Cik maksā šie šķīvji?" es jautāju pārdevējam."How much do these plates cost?" I ask the vendor.
Pārdevējs man uzsmaida.The vendor smiles at me.
"Tie maksā tikai četrdesmit kronas," viņš atbild."They cost only forty kroner," he replies.
Tā ir patiešām laba cena.That is a really good price.
Pirms mēneša es nopirku nelielu galdu savai virtuvei.I bought a small table for my kitchen a month ago.
Šie šķīvji lieliski piestāvēs šim galdam.These plates will fit perfectly with this table.
"Es tos ļoti vēlētos nopirkt," es saku."I would very much like to buy them," I say.
Es iedodu pārdevējam četrdesmit kronas un uzmanīgi ielieku šķīvjus savā somā.I give the vendor forty kroner and carefully put the plates in my bag.
Vienmēr ir jauki atrast kaut ko īpašu klusā svētdienā.It is always nice to find something special on a quiet Sunday.
winter coat
Storyboard: At a clothing stall, Maya tries on a heavy wool winter coat. She immediately takes it off because this coat is far too warm for summer, laughing with the vendor that she desperately needed something exactly like it five months ago.
Es eju tālāk tirgū un apstājos pie apģērbu stenda.I walk further into the market and stop at a clothing stall.
Es redzu smagu ziemas mēteli no vilnas.I see a heavy winter coat made of wool.
Šis mētelis ir skaists, bet ļoti biezs.This coat is beautiful, but very thick.
Es tomēr pielaikoju mēteli.I try the coat on anyway.
Tas ir neticami silts.It is incredibly warm.
Es to uzreiz atkal novelku.I take it off again immediately.
Ir jūlijs, un šodien spīd saule.It is July, and the sun is shining today.
Aiz galda stāv draudzīga sieviete un smaida.A friendly woman stands behind the table and smiles.
"Vai tas ir pārāk silts?" jautā sieviete."Is it too warm?" the woman asks.
"Jā, tas ir nedaudz par siltu vasarai," es atbildu un smejos."Yes, it is a bit too warm for the summer," I reply and laugh.
Es pieskaros biezajam audumam.I touch the thick fabric.
"Bet man tiešām vajadzēja šo ziemas mēteli pirms pieciem mēnešiem!" es saku."But I really needed this winter coat five months ago!" I say.
Sieviete skaļi smejas.The woman laughs loudly.
"Jā, februāris bija ļoti auksts," viņa saka."Yes, February was very cold," she says.
Es atceros savas pirmās nedēļas Kopenhāgenā.I remember my first weeks in Copenhagen.
Toreiz man pastāvīgi sala.I was constantly freezing back then.
Par laimi, man šobrīd nevajag tik siltas drēbes.Fortunately, I do not need such warm clothes right now.

museum ticket
Storyboard: Moving on, Maya enters a local history museum and goes to the front desk. She asks the clerk if this entrance ticket includes the special upstairs exhibition, and the clerk tells her to hurry because the guided tour started ten minutes ago.
Es dodos prom no tirgus un atrodu mazu, vietējo muzeju.I walk away from the market and find a small, local museum.
Es ieeju klusajā ēkā.I walk into the quiet building.
Es pieeju pie letes, lai nopirktu ieejas biļeti.I walk over to the counter to buy an entrance ticket.
Aiz kases stāv jauns vīrietis.A young man is standing behind the register.
"Sveiki, vienu pieaugušo biļeti, lūdzu," es saku."Hi, one adult ticket, please," I say.
"Tas būs simts kronu," viņš saka un to izdrukā."That will be one hundred kroner," he says and prints it.
Es samaksāju un apskatu šīs krāsainās brošūras uz letes.I pay and look at these colorful brochures on the counter.
Tās reklamē īpašu izstādi par pilsētas vēsturi.They advertise a special exhibition about the history of the city.
"Vai šī biļete dod piekļuvi arī izstādei augšstāvā?" es jautāju."Does this ticket also give access to the exhibition upstairs?" I ask.
"Jā, šajā muzejā ir tikai viena kopējā biļete," viņš atbild."Yes, this museum only has one combined ticket," he answers.
"Izstāde atrodas augšā, otrajā stāvā," viņš saka."The exhibition is upstairs on the first floor," he says.
"Jūs tur augšā varat droši nesteigties.""You can take your time up there."
"Tajā ir daudz vecu pilsētas fotogrāfiju," viņš paskaidro."It has many old photographs of the city," he explains.
"Dažas no tām ir vairāk nekā simts gadus vecas.""Some of them are over a hundred years old."
"Tas izklausās patiešām aizraujoši," es saku."That sounds really exciting," I say.
"Liels paldies," es pasmaidu."Thank you very much," I smile.
Es paņemu savu biļeti un mierīgi kāpju augšā pa kāpnēm.I take my ticket and walk calmly up the stairs.
harbor photograph

Šeit ir daudz vecu bilžu.There are many old pictures here.
Es apstājos ļoti lielas melnbaltas fotogrāfijas priekšā.I stop in front of a very large black-and-white photograph.
Šis attēls ir neticami aizraujošs.This picture is incredibly fascinating.

Šeit ir grūti iedomāties vecās rūpnīcas.It is hard to imagine the old factories here.

texting sofia

seagull encounter
Storyboard: Walking home through a large park, Maya stops at a kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream. As she strolls along the pond, a bold seagull suddenly swoops down, forcing her to quickly duck and shield this dessert with her hand to prevent the bird from stealing it.

Pirms stundas es biju muzejā.An hour ago I was at a museum.
