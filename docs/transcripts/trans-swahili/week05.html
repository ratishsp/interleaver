Week 5: Maya stocks her new kitchen

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

empty cupboards
Storyboard: Maya stands in the kitchen, noticing that the fridge and the cupboard are completely empty. She grabs a bag and heads out, ready to buy food.
Ni saa kumi na mbili.It is six o'clock.
Nimesimama jikoni.I am standing in the kitchen.
Nina njaa.I am hungry.
Ninafungua friji.I open the fridge.
Iko tupu kabisa.It is completely empty.
Ninaangalia kabatini.I look in the cupboard.
Hakuna chakula kabatini.There is no food in the cupboard.
Sina chakula.I have no food.
Nina maji kidogo tu.I only have a little water.
Ningependa kupata chakula sasa.I would like to have food now.
Ninapenda mbogamboga.I like vegetables.
Pia ninapenda mkate.I also like bread.
Lakini sina chochote hapa.But I have nothing here.
Ninapata mfuko.I find a bag.
Mfuko ni mwekundu.The bag is red.
Ni mzuri kwa manunuzi.It is good for shopping.
Ninatoka kwenye fleti.I walk out of the flat.
Ninafunga mlango.I close the door.
Niko tayari.I am ready.
Sasa ninatembea kwenda kwenye supamaketi.Now I am walking to the supermarket.
produce section
Storyboard: Maya enters a supermarket and pulls a basket from a stack by the door. Because she likes carrots, she grabs a large bunch from a green crate and drops it into the basket.
Ninasimama mbele ya supamaketi.I stand in front of the supermarket.
Supamaketi ni kubwa na ina mwangaza.The supermarket is large and bright.
Mlango unafunguka wenyewe.The door opens automatically.
Ninaingia ndani ya duka.I go into the shop.
Duka lina bidhaa nyingi.The shop has many goods.
Kuna marundo ya vikapu kwenye kiingilio.There are stacks of baskets by the entrance.
Ninachukua kikapu.I take a basket.
Kikapu ni chekundu na chepesi.The basket is red and light.
Ninatafuta mbogamboga.I look for vegetables.
Sehemu hiyo iko hapa hapa.The section is right here.
Ninaona kreti sakafuni.I see a crate on the floor.
Kreti hilo ni la kijani.The crate is green.
Kuna karoti nyingi ndani ya kreti.There are many carrots in the crate.
Ninapenda karoti.I like carrots.
Ninachagua fungu kubwa.I choose a large bunch.
Fungu hilo linaonekana bichi sana.The bunch looks very fresh.
Ninaweka fungu hilo ndani ya kikapu.I put the bunch down into the basket.
Sasa kikapu si kitupu.Now the basket is not empty.
Ninaendelea kutembea ndani zaidi ya duka.I walk further into the shop.
the parsnip
Storyboard: Looking around, Maya spots a pale, unusual root. She reads a sign and learns it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she decides to buy it anyway out of curiosity.
Ninaona mboga ya ajabu.I see a strange vegetable.
Mboga hiyo ni mzizi mrefu.The vegetable is a long root.
Mzizi huo una rangi nyepesi sana.The root is very pale.
Unafanana na karoti nyeupe.It looks like a white carrot.
Ninatazama chini kwenye kibao.I look down at the sign.
Imeandikwa "pasnipu" kwenye kibao.It says "parsnip" on the sign.
Siifahamu mboga hiyo.I do not know the vegetable.
Je, ni mboga ya Kideni?Is it a Danish vegetable?
Ninapenda mbogamboga.I like vegetables.
Ninapenda karoti.I like carrots.
Labda pasnipu ina ladha nzuri.Maybe the parsnip tastes good.
Ninachukua pasnipu mkononi mwangu.I take the parsnip in my hand.
Mzizi huo ni mkubwa kiasi.The root is quite big.
Ningependa kuipata.I would like to have it.
Ninatazama kikapu.I look at the basket.
Ninaweka mzizi huo kwenye kikapu.I put the root in the basket.
Ningependa kujaribu kitu kipya.I would like to try something new.
the rye bread
Storyboard: At a shelf with baked goods, Maya looks for a local staple. She lifts a loaf of rye bread, surprised by how heavy it feels, and lays the bread carefully in the basket.
Ninasogea kwenye rafu yenye mikate.I walk over to a shelf with bread.
Ninatafuta kitu maarufu cha hapa.I am looking for a local classic.
Ningependa kupata mkate wa rai wa Kideni.I would like to have a Danish rye bread.
Ninatazama rafu.I look at the shelf.
Ninapata mkate wa rai.I find a rye bread.
Upo ndani ya mfuko.It is in a bag.
Ninautazama mfuko.I look at the bag.
Ninachukua mkate wa rai.I take the rye bread.
Ninauinua mkate.I lift the bread.
Nimeshangaa.I am surprised.
Mkate huo ni mzito sana.The bread is very heavy.
Unahisika kama jiwe!It feels like a stone!
Ninapenda mkate mweupe.I like light bread.
Lakini mkate wa rai ni mweusi na mzito.But the rye bread is dark and heavy.
Ninauweka mkate kwenye kikapu kwa uangalifu.I carefully lay the bread in the basket.
Kikapu pia ni kizito sasa.The basket is also heavy now.
bakery counter
Storyboard: The bakery counter has a member of staff waiting. Maya catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry.
Ninaelekea kwenye kaunta ya duka la mikate.I walk over to the bakery counter.
Harufu ni nzuri sana.The smell is very good.
Ninapenda keki.I like cakes.
Na ninapenda sana keki za mdalasini.And I really like cinnamon pastries.
Mwokaji amesimama nyuma ya kaunta.A baker stands behind the counter.
Mwokaji ananiangalia.The baker looks at me.
Anatabasamu.He smiles.
"Ungependa kupata nini?" mwokaji anauliza."What would you like to have?" the baker asks.
Ninanyooshea kidole keki ya mdalasini.I point at a cinnamon pastry.
Keki hiyo inaonekana tamu sana.The pastry looks delicious.
"Ningependa kupata keki ya mdalasini, tafadhali," ninasema."I would like to have a cinnamon pastry, please," I say.
"Ndiyo, bila shaka," mwokaji anasema."Yes, of course," the baker says.
Anachukua keki hiyo.He takes the pastry.
Anaweka keki hiyo kwenye mfuko.He puts the pastry in a bag.
Anapitisha mfuko juu ya kaunta.He hands the bag over the counter.
"Huu hapa," anasema."Here you go," he says.
Ninachukua mfuko huo.I take the bag.
"Asante sana," ninasema."Thank you very much," I say.
Ninaweka mfuko huo ndani ya kikapu.I put the bag down into the basket.
finding the milk
Storyboard: Maya searches for the milk without luck. She stops an employee to ask for help, and he points her toward the dairy section at the back.
Sasa ninahitaji maziwa.Now I need milk.
Ninatembea kwenye njia ndefu.I walk down a long aisle.
Ninaangalia kwenye rafu.I look at the shelf.
Naweza kuona maji na juisi.I can see the water and the juice.
Lakini siwezi kupata maziwa.But I cannot find the milk.
Ninaangalia kila mahali.I look everywhere.
Ninamwona mwanaume.I see a man.
Anafanya kazi katika supamaketi.He works in the supermarket.
Ninamsimamisha mfanyakazi huyo na kutabasamu.I stop the employee and smile.
"Samahani," ninasema."Excuse me," I say.
"Ningependa kupata maziwa.""I would like to have milk."
"Maziwa yako wapi?""Where is the milk?"
Mwanaume huyo anaonyesha upande wa nyuma wa duka.The man points to the back of the shop.
"Sehemu ya bidhaa za maziwa iko kule chini," anasema."The dairy section is down there," he says.
"Asante sana," ninajibu."Thank you very much," I reply.
Ninatembea kuelekea kule chini.I walk down there.
Hatimaye ninapata friji na maziwa.I finally find the fridge and the milk.
Ninachukua katoni na kuiweka kwenye kikapu.I take a carton and lay it in the basket.
taste of home
Storyboard: Exploring another section, Maya hunts for a taste of Mexico because she really likes beans. She recognizes a bright can of black beans on a low shelf and reaches all the way to the back to pull it out.
Ninasogea kwenye rafu nyingine.I walk on to another set of shelves.
Ninaangalia chakula.I look at the food.
Supamaketi ina vitu vingi kutoka nchi nyingine.The supermarket has many things from other countries.
Ninatafuta ladha ya Mexico.I look for a taste of Mexico.
Ninakumbuka chakula cha nyumbani.I miss the food from home.
Ninapenda sana maharagwe.I really like beans.
Ninakula maharagwe meusi mara nyingi.I often eat black beans.
Ninaangalia rafu ya chini.I look at a low shelf.
Rafu ina giza kidogo.The shelf is a bit dark.
Lakini ninaona kopo lenye rangi nyingi.But I see a colourful can.
Kopo liko nyuma kabisa.The can stands all the way at the back.
Ni kopo la maharagwe meusi.It is a can of black beans.
Ninatabasamu.I smile.
Ninanyoosha mkono wangu mpaka ndani kabisa.I reach my arm all the way in.
Ninalivuta kopo nje.I pull the can out.
Ninasoma lebo kwenye kopo.I read the label on the can.
Inafaa sana kwa chakula changu cha jioni.It is perfect for my dinner.
Ninaweka kopo kwenye kikapu.I put the can in the basket.
Nina chakula cha kutosha.I have enough food.
the checkout
Storyboard: Maya brings the basket to the checkout. She places the food on the belt, holds her card over the machine, and packs the bag quickly. No price is spoken or shown.
Ninaenda kwenye kaunta ya kulipia.I walk up to the checkout.
Nina kikapu mkononi mwangu.I have the basket in my hand.
Ninaweka chakula kwenye mkanda.I put the food on the belt.
Kwanza ninachukua mkate wa rai kutoka kwenye kikapu.First I take the rye bread from the basket.
Kisha ninachukua maziwa na pasnipu.Then I take the milk and the parsnip.
Pia ninachukua karoti na maharage meusi.I also take carrots and black beans.
Mwishowe ninaweka andazi kwenye mkanda.Finally I put the pastry on the belt.
Mhudumu amekaa nyuma ya kaunta ya kulipia.The cashier sits behind the checkout.
Ananitabasamu.He smiles at me.
"Hujambo," anasema."Hello," he says.
"Sijambo," ninajibu."Hello," I reply.
Anaskani chakula haraka.He scans the food quickly.
Ninatoa kadi yangu.I take my card out.
Ninashikilia kadi juu ya mashine.I hold the card over the machine.
Mashine inatoa mlio.The machine beeps.
"Ungependa kupata risiti?" anauliza."Would you like to have the receipt?" he asks.
"Ndiyo tafadhali, ningependa kupata risiti," ninasema."Yes please, I would like to have the receipt," I say.
Ninafungua mfuko wangu.I open my bag.
Ninapakia mfuko haraka.I pack the bag quickly.
"Uwe na siku njema," anasema."Have a good day," he says.
putting away
Storyboard: Back in the flat, Maya empties the bag on the counter. She puts the milk and the parsnip in the fridge, and she places the bread and the can in the cupboard.
Niko nyumbani kwenye fleti tena.I am home in the flat again.
Nimesimama jikoni.I am standing in the kitchen.
Ninatoa vitu vyote kwenye mfuko juu ya kaunta ya jikoni.I empty the bag on the kitchen counter.
Ninafungua chakula.I unpack the food.
Ninachukua maziwa.I take the milk.
Ninaweka maziwa kwenye friji.I put the milk in the fridge.
Kisha ninatazama parsnipu.Then I look at the parsnip.
Ni mzizi wa kuchekesha.It is a funny root.
Nitautumia kwenye supu yangu.I will use it in my soup.
Ninaweka parsnipu kwenye friji.I put the parsnip in the fridge.
Sasa friji sio tupu.Now the fridge is not empty.
Ninachukua mkate.I take the bread.
Mkate wa rai ni mzito sana.The rye bread is very heavy.
Ninaweka mkate kwenye kabati.I put the bread in the cupboard.
Kisha ninachukua kopo la maharage.Then I take the can of beans.
Maharage hudumu kwa muda mrefu.Beans last a long time.
Ninaweka kopo kwenye kabati.I put the can in the cupboard.
Sasa kuna chakula kwenye kabati.Now there is food in the cupboard.
Nina njaa kiasi.I am quite hungry.
Ningependa kula chakula cha jioni sasa.I would like to have dinner now.
first home cooked meal
Storyboard: Ready to eat, Maya chops some vegetables and opens the can. She cooks dinner on the stove, finally making a proper meal in the flat — and afterwards she has the pastry from the bakery counter for dessert.
Jiko ni dogo.The kitchen is small.
Lakini jiko la kupikia ni zuri.But the stove is good.
Ninachukua parsnipu na karoti.I take the parsnip and a carrot.
Pia ninachukua kisu.I also take a knife.
Kisu ni kikali.The knife is sharp.
Ninakata parsnipu na karoti.I cut the parsnip and the carrot.
Ninafungua kopo.I open the can.
Ninaweka chakula kwenye sufuria.I put the food in a pot.
Sufuria iko kwenye jiko.The pot is on the stove.
Sasa chakula ni cha moto.Now the food is warm.
Kinanukia vizuri.It smells lovely.
Ninachukua mkate wa rai.I take the rye bread.
Ninakata kipande.I cut a slice.
Sahani iko mezani.The plate is on the table.
Hatimaye, ninakula mlo kamili katika fleti.Finally, I am eating a proper meal in the flat.
Baadaye ninakula keki ya mdalasini.Afterwards I eat the cinnamon pastry.
Ina ladha tamu.It tastes sweet.
