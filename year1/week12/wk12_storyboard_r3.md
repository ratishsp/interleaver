# Week 12 — Storyboard (Maya joins a local badminton club)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Verb + infinitive; *godt kan lide at*; *spille/dyrke*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_seeking_social_sport | Maya sits in her apartment on a Tuesday. She likes to work out at the gym on Wednesdays, but she wants to meet new people. She decides to find a social sport to play in her free time. |
| 2 | 02_booking_trial_session | Maya really loves to play badminton, a sport she plays a lot in Mexico. She searches online and books a trial session at a local sports club for Thursday evening. |
| 3 | 03_arriving_at_hall | On Thursday evening, Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sounds of shoes squeaking on the wooden courts. |
| 4 | 04_renting_the_racket | Maya goes to the small hall kiosk. She tells the clerk that she is there to try out the club, and she pays forty kroner to rent a racket for the evening. |
| 5 | 05_the_locker_room | Maya steps into the women's locker room to change her clothes. She puts on her indoor sports shoes and feels excited to finally practice her favorite sport in Denmark. |
| 6 | 06_playing_doubles | Inside the busy hall, Maya joins a doubles game with two friendly players, Sara and Kasper, and a fourth person. Maya really likes to smash the shuttlecock hard across the net. |
| 7 | 07_post_match_chat | The fast match finishes, and the tired players walk off the court. Sara notes that Maya plays very well, and Maya explains that she loves to practice badminton back home. |
| 8 | 08_hall_cafeteria | The group sits in the small hall cafeteria to drink water and chat. Kasper asks if Maya plans to come back, and she happily promises to make it a weekly Thursday routine. |
| 9 | 09_walking_to_bus | Maya leaves the sports hall with Sara. They walk together to the bus stop in the cold winter evening, and Maya feels very glad to meet fun, active locals. |
