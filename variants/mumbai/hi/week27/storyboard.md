# Week 27 — Storyboard (Maya tackles online admin)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Habitual **अक्सर/आमतौर पर** + present; instructions; the formal passive **किया जाता है** of official language

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_tuesday_evening_routine | Maya returns to her apartment from her Tuesday evening gym session. She reflects that she usually relaxes on the sofa with a book after working out, but tonight she has a tedious online admin task she has been putting off: updating her local utility account details. |
| 2 | 02_portal_instructions | She opens the public self-service website on her phone. Navigating to the profile section, she reads the portal's instructions, noting the stiff, official language in which user details 'are updated' and forms 'are submitted'. |
| 3 | 03_filling_the_form | Working through the digital form, Maya checks that her mobile number and flat number are correct and fills in the empty fields. She observes that she frequently encounters glitches on government websites, but tonight the pages seem to be loading smoothly. |
| 4 | 04_the_missing_code | Maya taps 'Submit', and the screen asks for a one-time code sent via SMS. The countdown runs out with no message; she requests a fresh code while rain streaks the window, reflecting that these servers are usually slower at night — and the second code never comes either. |
| 5 | 05_the_email_workaround | Maya rereads the formal instructions at the bottom of the screen: if a message is not received, an alternative code 'is sent via email' upon request. She taps the button, switches to her email app, and finds the message waiting instantly. She types the code in, and a green checkmark declares her profile 'has been successfully updated'. |
| 6 | 06_calling_sofia | Relieved that the boring administrative chore is finally over, Maya makes a cup of chai, takes her phone to the balcony, and video calls her sister Sofia in Mexico. They chat about how they both usually procrastinate endlessly on official paperwork, sharing a laugh over a very small but satisfying victory. |
