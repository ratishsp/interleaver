Week 19: Maya starts a new project with the team

Lithuanian with English gloss, line-aligned.

monday bus excitement
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya takes the bus to work. She feels excited and a little nervous because a large new software project starts today, and she will finally work closely with the entire team.
Pirmadienio rytas, ir aš sėdžiu autobuse.It is Monday morning, and I am sitting on the bus.
Esu pakeliui į darbą.I am on my way to work.
Žiūriu pro langą.I look out of the window.
Šiandien šiek tiek jaudinuosi.I am a little nervous today.
Bet taip pat labai to laukiu.But I am also looking forward to it a lot.
Šiandien prasideda didelis, naujas programinės įrangos projektas.A large, new software project starts today.
Nuo sausio įmonėje dirbu programinės įrangos inžiniere.I have worked as a software engineer at the company since January.
Gerai pažįstu savo kolegą Peterį.I know my colleague Peter well.
Kartu dirbame nuo mano pirmosios dienos.We have worked together since my first day.
Bet dabar turiu glaudžiai dirbti su visa komanda.But now I must work closely with the whole team.
Dirbsiu su mūsų vadovu, mūsų dizaineriu ir mūsų testuotoju.I will work with our boss, our designer, and our tester.
Jie įmonėje dirba jau ilgą laiką.They have worked at the company for a long time.
Autobusas sustoja, ir įlipa daugiau žmonių.The bus stops, and more people get on.
Žiūriu į savo laikrodį.I look at my watch.
Aštunta valanda.It is eight o'clock.
Rytinis susitikimas prasideda po pusvalandžio.The morning meeting begins in half an hour.
Giliai įkvepiu.I take a deep breath.
Esu pasiruošusi naujam projektui.I am ready for the new project.
first morning presentation
Storyboard: At the short morning meeting, Maya stands in front of her colleagues. She presents her part of the technical plan for the first time, explaining what she has worked on to prepare for the week's tasks.
Mes sėdime posėdžių salėje.We are sitting in the meeting room.
Laikas mūsų trumpam rytiniam susitikimui.It is time for our short morning meeting.
Apžvelgiu savo kolegas.I look around at my colleagues.
Šiandien man ypatinga diena.Today is a special day for me.
Aš atsistoju.I stand up.
Esu programinės įrangos inžinierė ir ketinu pristatyti savo projekto dalį.I am a software engineer, and I am going to present my part of the project.
Mano rankos šiek tiek dreba.My hands are shaking a little.
Bet esu gerai pasiruošusi.But I have prepared well.
„Labas rytas“, – pasakau garsiai."Good morning," I say loudly.
„Dirbau prie techninio plano“, – sakau jiems."I have worked on the technical plan," I tell them.
„Perskaičiau visus dokumentus.“"I have read all the documents."
„Ir pasidariau užrašus savaitės užduotims.“"And I have written notes for the week's tasks."
Paaiškinu planą visai komandai.I explain the plan to the whole team.
Iš tikrųjų kalbėti apie technologijas nėra sunku.It is actually not difficult to talk about the technology.
Peteris sėdi priešais mane.Peter is sitting opposite me.
Jaučiu, kaip labiau atsipalaiduoju.I feel myself relaxing more.
Jis klausosi ir linksi.He listens and nods.
Baigiu savo pristatymą, ir kiti šypsosi.I finish my presentation, and the others smile.
Mano pirmasis pristatymas darbe praėjo gerai.My first presentation at work has gone well.
boss project manager
Storyboard: After the morning meeting, her boss Thomas stops by Maya's desk with a part of the new project for her. He is warm and welcoming: he compliments her on her presentation and says the whole team has looked forward to having her on board. Maya feels genuinely welcomed onto the team.
Po rytinio susirinkimo sėdžiu prie savo rašomojo stalo.After the morning meeting, I sit at my desk.
Žiūriu į savo užrašus iš pristatymo.I look at my notes from the presentation.
Mano viršininkas Thomas prieina prie manęs.My boss, Thomas, comes over to me.
Jis yra mūsų projektų vadovas.He is our project manager.
Jis čia dirba jau daug metų.He has worked here for many years.
„Labas rytas, Maya“, – sako jis šypsodamasis."Good morning, Maya," he says with a smile.
„Tai buvo tikrai geras pristatymas.“"That was a really good presentation."
„Ačiū, Thomai“, – atsakau."Thank you, Thomas," I answer.
„Mes labai džiaugiamės turėdami tave komandoje“, – tęsia Thomas."We are very happy to have you on the team," Thomas continues.
„Visa komanda labai laukė galimybės dirbti su tavimi.“"The whole team has looked forward to working with you."
„Aš taip pat to laukiau“, – džiugiai sakau."I have also looked forward to it," I say happily.
„Tai įdomus projektas.“"It is an exciting project."
Thomas vėl nusišypso ir grįžta į savo kabinetą.Thomas smiles again and walks back to his office.
Žiūriu į savo kompiuterio ekraną.I look at my computer screen.
Jaučiuosi tikrai laukiama komandoje.I feel genuinely welcome on the team.
peter software engineer
Storyboard: Maya sits down with Peter to write code. As software engineers, they solve complex problems together, and Peter jokes that he has worked on one specific error for three hours until Maya easily spots his mistake.
Atsisėdu prie Piterio stalo.I sit down at Peter's desk.
Mes žiūrime į jo didelį ekraną.We look at his large screen.
Piteris taip pat yra programinės įrangos inžinierius.Peter is also a software engineer.
Jis yra geras kolega, ir mes kartu rašome daug kodo.He is a good colleague, and we write a lot of code together.
Šiandien jis atrodo labai pavargęs.Today he looks very tired.
„Ką tu darai, Piteri?“ – klausiu aš."What are you doing, Peter?" I ask.
„Turiu didelę problemą“, – atsako jis."I have a big problem," he answers.
„Prie šios klaidos dirbau tris valandas.“"I have worked on this error for three hours."
Nusišypsau ir atidžiai pažiūriu į kodą.I smile and look closely at the code.
Darbe mes dažnai kartu sprendžiame sudėtingas problemas.We often solve complex problems together in our work.
Staiga ekrane pamatau kažką keisto.Suddenly I see something strange on the screen.
Tai labai maža klaida.It is a very small mistake.
„Pabandyk pažiūrėti čia“, – sakau ir parodau į ekraną."Try to look here," I say and point at the screen.
„Čia padarei mažą spausdinimo klaidą.“"You have made a small typo here."
Piteris dvi sekundes tyliai žiūri į ekraną.Peter looks quietly at the screen for two seconds.
Tada jis garsiai nusijuokia.Then he laughs loudly.
„Tu visiškai teisi“, – sako Piteris."You are absolutely right," Peter says.
„Dirbau tris valandas, o čia tik viena maža klaida!“"I have worked for three hours, and then it is just one small error!"
Aš irgi juokiuosi, nes visi programinės įrangos inžinieriai žino šią situaciją.I laugh too, because all software engineers know that situation.
Mes ištaisome klaidą, ir programa pagaliau veikia puikiai.We fix the error, and the program finally works perfectly.
eva digital designer
Storyboard: Later, Maya walks over to meet Eva, the team's designer. Eva shows Maya her beautiful sketches and explains that she has worked as a digital designer since she finished university, focusing on how users experience the product.
Vėliau po pietų prieinu prie Evos rašomojo stalo.Later in the afternoon, I walk over to Eva's desk.
Ji yra komandos skaitmeninė dizainerė.She is the team's digital designer.
Iki šiandien su ja dar nesu dirbusi.I have not worked with her before today.
„Labas, Maja, ateik ir pažiūrėk“, – sako ji."Hi Maya, come and look," she says.
Ji rodo į savo didelį ekraną.She points at her large screen.
Matau keletą gražių mūsų naujos programinės įrangos eskizų.I see some beautiful sketches of our new software.
Spalvos ramios, o dizainas labai švarus.The colors are calm, and the design is very clean.
„Atrodo tikrai gerai“, – sakau."That looks really good," I say.
„Ačiū“, – su šypsena atsako Eva."Thank you," Eva answers with a smile.
Ji man paaiškina savo darbą.She explains her work to me.
„Dirbu skaitmenine dizainere nuo universiteto laikų“, – sako ji."I have worked as a digital designer since university," she says.
„Visada sutelkiu dėmesį į vartotojo patirtį.“"I always focus on the user's experience."
Ji man rodo, kaip vartotojas naršo po programą.She shows me how the user clicks around the program.
„Turi būti lengva ir logiška naudotis“, – paaiškina ji."It must be easy and logical to use," she explains.
Aš dažnai žiūriu tik į patį kodą.I often only look at the code itself.
Tačiau jos dizainas yra be galo svarbus projektui.But her design is incredibly important for the project.
Mes dar šiek tiek pasikalbame apie mūsų bendras užduotis.We talk a little more about our shared tasks.
Nekantrauju programuoti jos gražų dizainą.I look forward to programming her beautiful design.
meeting the tester
Storyboard: The next day, Maya sits with Mads, the software tester, as he clicks quickly through the new screens and spots a small mistake at once. He laughs that his job is to break things before the users do — he has done it for five years and finds problems fast.
Tai kita diena biure.It is the next day at the office.
Sėdžiu šalia Madso, mūsų programinės įrangos testuotojo.I am sitting next to Mads, our software tester.
Jis žiūri į naujus ekranus.He looks at the new screens.
Jis labai greitai spragsi pele.He clicks very fast with the mouse.
Staiga jis sustoja.Suddenly he stops.
Jis jau rado nedidelę klaidą.He has already found a small mistake.
Jis pažiūri į mane ir truputį nusijuokia.He looks at me and laughs a little.
„Mano darbas – laužyti dalykus“, – sako jis."My job is to break things," he says.
„Turiu rasti klaidas anksčiau nei mūsų vartotojai.“"I have to find the mistakes before our users."
Jis jau penkerius metus dirba testuotoju.He has worked as a tester for five years.
Jis yra testavęs daug skirtingų projektų.He has tested many different projects.
Štai kodėl jis taip greitai randa problemas.That is why he finds the problems so fast.
Žiūriu į klaidą savo kompiuteryje.I look at the mistake on my computer.
Laimei, tai tik nedidelė problema.Fortunately, it is only a small problem.
Iš karto ištaisau kodą.I fix the code right away.
Jis testuoja programą dar kartą.He tests the program one more time.
Dabar viskas veikia visiškai tobulai.Now everything works absolutely perfectly.
Labai džiaugiuosi jo pagalba.I am very glad for his help.
wednesday gym reflection

friday canteen lunch
Storyboard: The busy week ends with a shared lunch in the bright canteen. Maya sits with Peter, Eva, and the tester Mads, discussing how hard everyone has worked, and she finally feels like a true part of the team.
Pagaliau penktadienis, ir užimta savaitė beveik baigėsi.It is finally Friday, and the busy week is almost over.
Mes kartu valgome pietus šviesioje valgykloje.We are eating lunch together in the bright canteen.
Sėdiu prie didelio stalo kartu su savo komanda.I am sitting at a large table together with my team.
Peteris, mūsų dizainerė Eva ir mūsų testuotojas Madsas taip pat čia.Peter, our designer Eva, and our tester Mads are here too.

Eva rodo mums savo gražų dizainą telefone.Eva shows us her beautiful design on her phone.
