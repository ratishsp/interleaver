Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Latvian with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
Lidmašīna tagad ir Kopenhāgenā.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
Ir janvāris.It is January.
Es esmu ļoti satraukta.I am very excited.
Es ielieku savu grāmatu savā somā.I pack my book into my bag.
Blakus man ir pasažieris.There is a passenger next to me.
Viņš skatās uz mani un smaida.He looks at me and smiles.
"Sveiki," viņš saka."Hello," he says.
"Sveiki," es saku."Hello," I say.
"Kā tevi sauc?" viņš jautā."What is your name?" he asks.
"Mani sauc Maija," es saku."My name is Maya," I say.
"No kurienes tu nāc, Maija?" viņš jautā."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
Es priecīgi smaidu.I smile happily.
"Es nāku no Meksikas," es atbildu."I come from Mexico," I answer.
"Vai tu esi no Dānijas?" es jautāju."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
"Jā, es esmu no Dānijas," viņš saka."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
"Laipni lūgta Dānijā," viņš saka."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
"Liels paldies," es saku."Thank you very much," I say.
Tagad mēs esam šeit.Now we are here.
Es esmu gatava.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
Es tagad esmu ārā.I am outside now.
Mans čemodāns ir liels un smags.My suitcase is big and heavy.
Tur ir taksometrs.There is a taxi.
Es ieceļu savu čemodānu taksometrā.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
Šoferis ir draudzīgs vīrietis.The driver is a friendly man.
"Kāda ir adrese?" viņš jautā."What is the address?" he asks.
"Tā ir Parkvej numur divpadsmit," es saku."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
Viņš pamāj un brauc.He nods and drives.
"Vai jūs esat tūriste?" šoferis jautā."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
"Nē, es neesmu tūriste," es atbildu."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
"No kurienes jūs esat?" viņš jautā."Where do you come from?" he asks.
"Es esmu no Meksikas," es saku."I come from Mexico," I say.
Es skatos ārā pa logu.I look out the window.
Es esmu ļoti lepna.I am very proud.
"Es esmu programmatūras inženiere," es saku."I am a software engineer," I say.
"Mans jaunais darbs ir šeit, Kopenhāgenā.""My new job is here in Copenhagen."
Viņš pasmaida.He smiles.
"Cik aizraujoši.""How exciting."
"Veiksmi Dānijā," viņš saka."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
"Liels paldies," es saku."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
Durvis ir atvērtas.The door is open.
Slieksnis ir augsts.The threshold is high.
Čemodāns ir ļoti smags!The suitcase is very heavy!
Bet tagad čemodāns ir priekšnamā.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
Šeit ir pilnīgi tumšs.It is completely dark here.
Kur ir slēdzis?Where is the switch?
Siena ir auksta.The wall is cold.
Vai slēdzis ir šeit?Is the switch here?
Jā, tur ir slēdzis!Yes, there is the switch!
Tagad ir gaisma.Now there is light.
Es esmu dzīvoklī.I am in the apartment.
Tas ir mazs, bet tas ir jauks.It is small, but it is nice.
Šeit ir viesistaba.Here is a living room.
Tur ir dīvāns un galds.There is a sofa and a table.
Tas ir mēbelēts mājoklis.It is a furnished home.
Čemodāns ir uz grīdas.The suitcase is on the floor.
Es esmu ļoti nogurusi.I am very tired.
Bet es esmu arī priecīga.But I am also happy.
Es esmu Kopenhāgenā!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
Es stāvu pie loga.I am standing by the window.
Stikls ir auksts.The glass is cold.
Ir janvāris.It is January.
Es skatos ārā.I look out.
Kopenhāgena šodien ir pelēka.Copenhagen is grey today.
Ārā ir auksts.It is cold outside.
Uz ielas ir sniegs.There is snow on the street.
Bet šeit ir silti.But it is warm here.
Dzīvoklis ir labs.The apartment is good.
Es domāju par Meksiku.I think about Mexico.
Meksikā ir saule.In Mexico, there is sun.
Arī tur ir silti.It is also warm there.
Man mazliet pietrūkst saules.I miss the sun a little.
Bet es smaidu.But I smile.
Tā ir jauna pilsēta.It is a new city.
Es tagad esmu Dānijā.I am in Denmark now.
Tas ir labs sākums.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
Es tagad esmu viesistabā.I am in the living room now.
Tur ir mazs dīvāns.There is a small sofa.
Es zvanu savai mammai Meksikā.I call my mother in Mexico.
"Sveika, mamm," es saku."Hi Mom," I say.
"Sveika, Maija," saka mana mamma."Hi Maya," my mother says.
"Kur tu esi?" viņa jautā."Where are you?" she asks.
"Es esmu Kopenhāgenā," es atbildu."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
"Es tagad esmu dzīvoklī.""I am in the apartment now."
"Cik labi," saka mana mamma."That is so good," my mother says.
"Vai tas ir liels?" viņa jautā."Is it big?" she asks.
"Nē, tas ir mazs," es atbildu."No, it is small," I answer.
"Bet tas ir mājīgs, un es esmu priecīga.""But it is cozy, and I am happy."
"Vai ir auksti?" viņa jautā."Is it cold?" she asks.
"Jā, ārā ir auksti," es saku."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
"Bet šeit ir silts.""But it is warm in here."
"Es esmu drošībā.""I am safe."
"Tas ir labi," saka mana mamma."That is good," my mother says.
Es smaidu.I smile.
Dānija ir manas jaunās mājas.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
Es atveru savas durvis.I open my door.
Es stāvu kāpņutelpā.I am standing in the stairwell.
Ir kluss.It is quiet.
Kādas durvis atveras.A door opens.
Kāda sieviete man uzsmaida.A woman smiles at me.
"Sveika," viņa saka."Hi," she says.
"Kā tevi sauc?" viņa jautā."What is your name?" she asks.
"Mani sauc Maija," es atbildu."My name is Maya," I answer.
"No kurienes tu esi?" viņa jautā."Where do you come from?" she asks.
"Es esmu no Meksikas," es saku."I come from Mexico," I say.
"Laipni lūgta šajā mājā," sieviete saka."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
"Mani sauc Nīna," viņa saka."My name is Nina," she says.
"Es esmu tava kaimiņiene.""I am your neighbour."
Nīna ir ļoti draudzīga.Nina is very friendly.
Viņa runā lēni un skaidri.She speaks slowly and clearly.
"Šeit ir manas durvis," viņa saka."Here is my door," she says.
"Es labprāt palīdzēšu.""I am happy to help."
"Vienkārši pieklauvē," Nīna saka."You just knock," Nina says.
"Liels paldies, Nīna," es atbildu."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
Es esmu ļoti priecīga.I am very happy.
Tagad man ir draugs Kopenhāgenā.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
Tagad ir nakts.Now it is night.
Tā ir mana pirmā nakts Kopenhāgenā.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
Es esmu vannas istabā.I am in the bathroom.
Tā ir ļoti maza.It is very small.
Tur ir izlietne.There is a sink.
Mana zobu birste ir sarkana.My toothbrush is red.
Es uzlieku zobu pastu uz savas zobu birstes.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
Es tīru zobus.I brush my teeth.
Ūdens ir auksts.The water is cold.
Es mazgāju seju.I wash my face.
Es noslauku seju.I dry my face.
Es eju pie savas gultas.I go over to my bed.
Gulta ir šaura.The bed is narrow.
Bet tā ir silta un mīksta.But it is warm and soft.
Tagad es guļu zem segas.I am lying under the duvet now.
Mans telefons atrodas uz galda.My phone is lying on the table.
Es uzlieku modinātāju.I set my alarm.
Tas zvanīs rīt pulksten astoņos.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
Es esmu nogurusi, bet es esmu gatava.I am tired, but I am ready.
Ar labu nakti, Kopenhāgena.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
