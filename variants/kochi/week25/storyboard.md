# Week 25 — Storyboard (A Slow Sunday in Fort Kochi)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Demonstratives **ഈ/ആ + ഇത്/അത്** as a system; **മുമ്പ്** (ago — രണ്ട് മാസം മുമ്പ്); time adverbs

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_sunday_morning_rain | Maya wakes up to a dark, wet Sunday morning, the heavy June monsoon rain already beating steadily against her window. As she drinks her morning coffee, she glances at the calendar — the month is almost over — and takes her time over her coffee. |
| 2 | 02_ferry_to_fort_kochi | With nowhere to be, Maya takes the ferry across the water to Fort Kochi for a slow Sunday outing. She checks the time, noting that the heavy rain stopped just twenty minutes ago, and enjoys the cool, breezy ride across the harbor. |
| 3 | 03_sunday_market | Near the ferry landing, a small Sunday market is underway despite the wet streets. Maya drifts from stall to stall — fruit here, woven baskets there — asking the seller about this basket in her hand and that bigger one hanging behind him, and she picks up a packet of the cardamom tea her mother asked about a few weeks ago. |
| 4 | 04_escaping_the_downpour | Suddenly, the skies open up and a heavy monsoon downpour begins. Maya hurries along the street to find cover and decides to duck into the nearby Indo-Portuguese Museum to wait out the weather. |
| 5 | 05_museum_ticket | Shaking off her umbrella in the dry lobby, Maya buys a museum ticket, takes a brochure from the friendly clerk, and heads into the main exhibit hall on the ground floor. |
| 6 | 06_history_exhibit | Maya spends a peaceful hour exploring the exhibits while the rain hammers the roof. She studies some old artifacts, reading a plaque about a Portuguese trade ship that sank hundreds of years ago, fascinated by the deep history of the port. |
| 7 | 07_beach_promenade_stroll | Leaving the museum after the rain finally stops, Maya takes a late afternoon walk along the Fort Kochi promenade by the Chinese fishing nets. She looks out over the water, feeling deeply settled and at home compared to the anxious newcomer she was five months ago. |
