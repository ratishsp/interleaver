# Week 8 — Storyboard (Maya navigates the city to Marine Drive)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Polite imperatives **जा/वळा**; directions **सरळ, डावीकडे/उजवीकडे**; **-कडे** (towards)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_asha_transfer_advice | On Saturday morning, Maya plans her first big outing to Marine Drive in South Mumbai. She speaks with her neighbour Asha, who tells her to take her usual daily bus but get down at a major junction, using polite imperatives to explain how to walk towards the connecting bus stop. |
| 2 | 02_vendor_transfer_directions | Maya rides her regular bus and gets off at the busy transfer junction. Unsure exactly where the southbound bus stop is located, she asks a fruit vendor, politely addressing him as bhaiya, and he tells her to go straight and turn left towards the big intersection. |
| 3 | 03_bus_conductor | Maya successfully boards the connecting BEST bus. During the ride, she asks the bus conductor where she should get down for Marine Drive; he tells her the name of the stop and politely instructs her to walk straight towards the sea after getting off. |
| 4 | 04_getting_lost | Maya gets off the bus in a bustling South Mumbai district. She tries to follow the conductor's instructions but accidentally takes a wrong turn down a crowded commercial street, walking away from the water. |
| 5 | 05_asking_chacha | Realizing she is lost and cannot see the ocean, Maya stops at a small store. She respectfully addresses the elderly shopkeeper as chacha and asks him for the correct way; he smiles and uses polite imperatives, instructing her to turn right at the next corner and keep walking straight towards the sea face. |
| 6 | 06_reaching_marine_drive | Maya follows the new directions carefully, turning right and walking straight. The dense buildings suddenly clear, and she finally steps out onto the wide, breezy promenade of Marine Drive. |
| 7 | 07_watching_water | Maya finds a spot to sit on the sea face. She watches the water, the people walking by, and the famous long curve of the road, mentally connecting the route she just took to map her part of the city. |
| 8 | 08_confident_return | After enjoying the view for a while, Maya easily finds the return bus stop on her own. She boards the bus heading home, feeling proud of her new sense of direction. |
