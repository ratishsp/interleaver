Week 26: Maya's first Danish summer and thirty-second birthday

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

birthday messages
Storyboard: Maya wakes up on the morning she turns thirty-two, feeling a bit nostalgic as it is her first birthday away from Mexico. She smiles as she reads the flood of loving messages that her family has sent overnight while she slept.
我今朝好早就醒咗。I woke up early this morning.
陽光好燦爛咁照入我睡房。The sun shone brightly into my bedroom.
我瞓喺張床度，諗起墨西哥嘅屋企。I lay in bed and thought about my home in Mexico.
今日其實係我生日。Today is actually my birthday.
我今日滿三十二歲喇。I turn thirty-two years old today.
呢個係我第一次離開屋企人過生日。It is my first birthday away from my family.
我為此覺得有少少感觸。I felt a little nostalgic about it.
不過之後我喺床頭櫃拎起部電話。But then I picked up my phone from the nightstand.
我屋企人傳咗好多溫馨嘅信息畀我。My family has sent many loving messages.
佢哋係尋晚趁我瞓緊覺嗰陣寫嘅。They wrote them last night while I slept.
我阿爸阿媽同我哋隻狗Lola拍咗段好搞笑嘅片。My parents have recorded a funny video with Lola, our dog.
我阿妹Sofía寫咗段好長、好窩心嘅信息。My sister Sofía has written a long and sweet text.
而我細佬Diego就傳咗一張我最鍾意嘅蛋糕相。And my brother Diego sent a picture of my favorite cake.
我見到張相嗰陣，笑得好開心。I smiled broadly when I saw the picture.
佢哋完全冇忘記我嘅大日子。They have not forgotten my big day at all.
我即刻覆晒所有信息。I replied to all the messages immediately.
我覆佢哋話，我好愛佢哋，亦都好掛住佢哋。I wrote to them that I love and miss them.
我將啲信息睇完又睇。I read the messages again and again.
呢個真係我生日嘅一個美好開始。It is a fantastic start to my birthday.
office pastry
Storyboard: At the software company, Maya is surprised when Peter and Eva place a small cake with a single candle on her desk. They wish her a happy birthday.
我坐喺辦公室自己張寫字檯度。I am sitting at my desk in the office.
我今日好早就返到公司。I arrived early today.
我喺度已經做咗半年嘢喇。I have already worked here for half a year.
突然間，彼得同伊娃行咗過嚟我個位度。Suddenly Peter and Eva come over to my desk.
彼得攞住一個插咗一支蠟燭嘅細蛋糕。Peter is carrying a small cake with a single candle.
佢哋齊聲話：「生日快樂呀，瑪雅！」"Happy birthday, Maya!" they say in unison.
我覺得好開心，而且好驚喜。I become really happy and very surprised.
「真係好多謝你哋，」我同佢哋講。"Thank you very much," I say to them.
伊娃笑得好燦爛。Eva smiles broadly.
佢解釋話：「我哋今朝喺麵包舖買呢個蛋糕㗎。」"We bought the cake at the bakery this morning," she explains.
彼得補充話：「係呀，我仲沖咗咖啡畀大家添。」"Yes, and I have made coffee for us," Peter adds.
佢將一大杯咖啡放喺我張檯上面。He places a large cup of coffee on my table.
我話：「你哋真係好窩心呀。」"You are simply so sweet," I say.
「我以前喺墨西哥做嘢嗰陣，朝早好少有蛋糕食。」"When I worked in Mexico, we rarely got cake in the morning."
彼得同伊娃拉咗兩張凳過嚟我張檯度。Peter and Eva pull two chairs over to the desk.
我哋一齊食蛋糕，呢個係我今日一個好正嘅開始。We eat the cake together, and it is a fantastic start to my day.
family call
Storyboard: That evening, Maya has a lively video call with her parents, Sofía, Diego, and Lola the dog. Sofía eagerly asks if Maya has opened the package they sent, and Maya happily holds up the colorful scarf that arrived in the mail yesterday.
夜晚，我攞住部電腦坐喺梳化度。In the evening, I sit on my sofa with my computer.
我打畀喺墨西哥嘅屋企人。I call my family in Mexico.
螢幕見到我阿爸阿媽、阿妹蘇菲亞同細佬迪亞哥。The screen shows my parents, my sister Sofía, and my brother Diego.
我哋隻狗蘿拉都喺度。Our dog, Lola, is there too.
「生日快樂！」佢哋一齊大叫。"Happy birthday!" they all shout.
蘇菲亞問。Sofía asks.
「你收唔收到我哋個包裹呀？」"Have you received our package?"
「收到啦，尋日郵寄送到喇，」我答。"Yes, it arrived in the mail yesterday," I answer.
「你拆咗未呀？」佢好興奮咁問。"Have you opened it?" she asks excitedly.
我微笑，喺張枱度攞起一條色彩繽紛嘅頸巾。I smile and take a colorful scarf from the table.
「我一返到屋企就拆咗喇，」我一邊講，一邊舉起條頸巾。"I opened it when I came home," I say and hold the scarf up.
「我真係好鍾意啲顏色。」"I really love the colors."
我將條頸巾圍喺頸度。I put the scarf around my neck.
「好襯你呀，」我阿媽講。"It suits you well," my mother says.
「你今日過得好唔好呀？」我阿爸問。"Have you had a good day today?" my father asks.
「好好呀，我啲同事頭先買咗個蛋糕畀我，」我解釋。"Yes, my colleagues bought a cake for me earlier," I explain.
迪亞哥抱起蘿拉對住個鏡頭。Diego lifts Lola up to the camera.
「蘿拉都食咗件細蛋糕呀，」佢笑住講。"Lola has also eaten a little cake," he laughs.
望住隻小狗，我哈哈大笑。I laugh loudly at the little dog.
呢個係我第一次離開屋企過生日，但係我覺得佢哋離我好近。It is my first birthday away from home, but they feel very close.
cafe summer
Storyboard: A few days after her birthday, Maya finishes her Saturday morning café shift, unties her apron and wipes down the last counter. Eager for the big summer bonfire by the sea that evening, she texts Eva to confirm they are still meeting, noting she got off early today unlike last weekend when she stayed late.
今日係星期六上晝，我喺咖啡舖做完嘢啦。It is Saturday morning, and I am finished at the café.
我啱啱抹乾淨最後一張枱。I have just wiped down the last table.
我解開條圍裙，將佢擺喺張櫈上面。I untie my apron and put it on a chair.
今日我返咗短更。Today I have had a short shift.
上個禮拜我做到三點。Last week I worked until three o'clock.
嗰日我哋全日都好忙。That day we were very busy all day.
不過今日我哋得幾個客。But today we have only had a few guests.
出面出緊太陽，係一個好靚嘅夏日。The sun is shining outside, and it is a beautiful summer day.
今晚海邊嗰度會有一個好大嘅營火。Tonight there is a big bonfire down by the sea.
我非常期待去睇。I am looking forward to seeing it very much.
我攞起部電話，打個短訊畀伊娃。I take my phone and write a message to Eva.
我問佢：「我哋今晚係咪照見啊？」I ask her, "Are we still meeting tonight?"
我亦都寫低我已經收咗工啦。I also write that I am already off work.
我寫住：「我今日提早收工，所以已經準備好啦。」"I finished early today, so I am ready," I write.
伊娃幾乎即刻就覆。Eva replies almost immediately.
佢覆話：「係呀，當然啦！」"Yes, of course!" she replies.
「我啱啱買咗新鮮嘅士多啤梨。」"I have just bought fresh strawberries."
佢寫住：「一陣沙灘見啦。」"See you at the beach later," she writes.
我笑一笑，攞起我個袋。I smile and take my bag.
跟住我就行出去，走入溫暖嘅夏日天氣之中。Then I walk out into the warm summer weather.
waterfront
Storyboard: Back home that evening, Maya gets ready to head down to the waterfront.
我喺屋企，喺我嘅公寓入面。I am home in my apartment.
而家係星期六傍晚，但係太陽仲喺度曬緊。It is Saturday evening, but the sun is still shining.
我準備緊去海灘嘅營火會。I am getting ready for the bonfire by the beach.
我已經執好我個袋。I have packed my bag.
我擺咗件保暖嘅冷衫落個袋度。I put a warm sweater in the bag.
件冷衫我係喺二月買嘅。I bought the sweater in February.
而家嘅夜晚仲係好光，但係晏啲通常會變凍。The evenings are bright now, but it often gets cold later.
我仲整咗啲小食。I have also made some snacks.
我今朝喺咖啡店返工之前去咗買嘢。I shopped this morning before my shift at the café.
我部電話響，跟住我接咗。My phone rings, and I answer it.
係 Eva。It is Eva.
佢問：「Maya，你準備好未呀？」"Are you ready, Maya?" she asks.
我答：「係呀，我差唔多準備好喇。」"Yes, I am almost ready," I answer.
我話：「我啱啱先搵到我副太陽眼鏡。」"I have just found my sunglasses," I say.
我笑住話：「我搵咗成二十分鐘呀。」"I looked for them for twenty minutes," I laugh.
Eva 話：「太好喇，半個鐘之後海邊見啦。」"Perfect, see you at the water in half an hour," Eva says.
我著好鞋，將個袋孭上膊頭。I put on my shoes and put the bag over my shoulder.
我鎖好門，然後行樓梯落去。I lock the door and walk down the stairs.
我真係好期待我第一個丹麥嘅夏天夜晚。I am really looking forward to my first Danish summer evening.
bus stop contrast
Storyboard: Walking down toward the beach alone, Maya marvels at how bright the evening sky still is. She thinks back to January, when she stood at the bus stop after work in pitch darkness at four o'clock, freezing — a sharp contrast with tonight, when the sun has not even started to set.
我自己一個人向住海灘行落去。I walk down towards the beach alone.
已經係夜晚，但個天仲係好光。It is already evening, but the sky is still very bright.
太陽仲未開始落山。The sun has not started to set yet.
我抬起頭，回想返一月嗰陣。I look up and think back to January.
嗰時我放工之後經常要等巴士。Back then I often waited for the bus after work.
當時下晝只係得四點。It was only four o'clock in the afternoon.
但係周圍已經黑鼆鼆。But it was already pitch dark everywhere.
我企喺巴士站度凍到震。I stood at the bus stop and froze.
依家我喺度住咗半年喇。Now I have lived here for half a year.
所有嘢都唔同晒。Everything has changed.
喺墨西哥，太陽總係喺差唔多同一時間落山。In Mexico, the sun always went down at almost the same time.
全年都係咁。It was like that all year.
喺呢度，我從來未經歷過咁長嘅日頭。Here I have never experienced such long days before.
光線為呢座城市帶嚟全新嘅生氣。The light has given the city a whole new life.
我著起我件保暖冷衫。I put on my warm sweater.
雖然個天好光，但海面吹過嚟嘅風有啲凍。Even though the sky is bright, the wind blows a bit cold from the sea.
我好期待去睇營火。I am looking forward to seeing the bonfire.
呢個將會係一個同朋友一齊嘅美好夜晚。It will be a lovely evening with my friends.
beach bonfire
Storyboard: At the beach Maya meets Eva and Peter just as the crowd lights the towering summer bonfire. Eva shares fresh strawberries she bought at the market, and the glowing fire chases away the coastal chill as they watch the flames together.
我行落去沙灘。I walk down to the beach.
已經有好多人嚟到參加今晚嘅派對。Many people have already arrived for the evening's party.
我喺人群入面搵伊娃同埋彼得。I look for Eva and Peter in the crowd.
突然間，我見到佢哋喺水邊，就即刻趕過去。Suddenly I see them down by the water, and I hurry over there.
「你生日呢個禮拜過得開心嗎？」彼得微笑住問。"Have you had a good birthday week?" Peter asks with a smile.
「係呀，過得非常之好，」我好開心咁答。"Yes, it has been absolutely fantastic," I answer happily.
伊娃打開一個細籃。Eva opens a small basket.
「我朝早喺街市買咗啲新鮮士多啤梨，」佢話。"I bought fresh strawberries at the market this morning," she says.
「你有冇試過丹麥嘅夏天士多啤梨呀？」"Have you ever tasted Danish summer strawberries?"
「冇呀，我從來未試過，」我一邊講，一邊攞起一粒。"No, I have never tried that," I say and take a berry.
味道超級甜，令我諗起夏天。It tastes incredibly sweet and reminds me of summer.
就喺呢個時候，有個男人點着咗個大營火。Right at that moment, a man lights the big bonfire.
火焰好快就變大，一直燒向高空。The flames grow quickly and stretch high up toward the sky.
頭先仲好凍，但係而家啲火已經將空氣變暖。It was cold earlier, but now the fire has warmed up the air.
火光嘅熱力好快就趕走咗海邊嘅寒意。The glowing heat quickly chases away the cold from the coast.
我哋企埋一齊，望住啲火焰望咗好耐。We stand close together and look into the flames for a long time.
我諗起自己喺呢個城市已經住咗六個月喇。I think about how I have now lived here in the city for six months.
我喺度嘅第一個月好黑暗，但係呢個明亮嘅夏日夜晚就非常完美。My first month here was dark, but this bright summer evening is perfect.
midnight blue
Storyboard: Strolling back near midnight under a sky that stays soft blue, Maya feels a deep peace. Walking with Eva and Peter, she looks at her friends and realizes how much has changed — she has found real friends and built a life she loves in her new city.
依家差唔多夜晚十二點，但係個天仲係好光。It is almost twelve o'clock at night, but the sky is still light.
佢有一種柔和嘅藍色。It has a soft, blue color.
我同 Eva 同埋 Peter 一齊喺街度行。I am walking on the street with Eva and Peter.
我哋冇講太多嘢。We do not talk much.
夜晚只係聽到我哋嘅腳步聲。Only our footsteps sound in the night.
我望住我啲朋友。I look at my friends.
Eva 喺度微笑，而 Peter 望落有啲攰，但係好開心。Eva is smiling, and Peter looks tired but happy.
我初初嚟到呢度嗰陣，一個人都唔識。When I came here, I knew no one.
但係之後好多嘢都變咗。But a lot has changed since.
我有咗好好嘅日常生活，仲識咗好多人。I have gotten a good everyday life, and I have met many people.
其實我搵到真正嘅朋友。I have actually found real friends.
Peter 係我第一個傾過偈嘅同事。Peter was the first colleague that I talked to.
而 Eva 最近請咗我去佢屋企。And Eva invited me to her home recently.
依家我哋一齊喺明亮嘅夏日天空下面行。Now we are walking here together under the bright summer sky.
我啱啱滿三十二歲。I have just turned thirty-two years old.
我建立咗自己鍾意嘅生活。I have built a life that I love.
我終於喺我嘅新城市完全有返屋企嘅感覺。I finally feel completely at home in my new city.
