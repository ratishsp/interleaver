planning the week
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya makes a cup of tea and opens her calendar at the kitchen table — she always plans the week here. She works through it: the bike goes to the repair shop, the salon needs a phone call, the gym is on Wednesday. She writes each one in, and February fills up.
Je pondelok ráno.It is Monday morning.
Robím si šálku horúceho čaju.I make a cup of hot tea.
Sedím za stolom v kuchyni.I sit at the table in the kitchen.
Otváram svoj kalendár.I open my calendar.
Vždy si tu plánujem svoj týždeň.I always plan my week here.
Teraz je február.It is February now.
Mesiac je už veľmi plný.The month is already very full.
Zajtra je utorok.Tomorrow is Tuesday.
Môj bicykel je bohužiaľ pokazený.My bicycle is unfortunately broken.
Zajtra ráno idem pešo k opravárovi bicyklov.I walk to the bicycle mechanic tomorrow morning.
Otvára o ôsmej.He opens at eight o'clock.
Zapisujem si to do kalendára.I write it in the calendar.
Dnes volám aj kaderníkovi.I also call the hairdresser today.
Moje vlasy sú až príliš dlhé.My hair is way too long.
V stredu idem do posilňovne.Wednesday I go to the gym.
V stredy často trénujem.I often train on Wednesdays.
Zvyčajne je to o siedmej večer.It is usually at seven o'clock in the evening.
Znova sa pozerám na svoj plán.I look at my plan again.
Pijem svoj čaj.I drink my tea.
Teraz som pripravená na nový týždeň.Now I am ready for a new week.
dropping off the bike

booking a haircut
Storyboard: During her lunch break, Maya calls a hair salon on her mobile phone. She speaks to the hairdresser and books an appointment for Thursday at half past four.
Mám obednú prestávku.It is my lunch break.
Sedím v jedálni v práci.I am sitting in the canteen at my work.
Včera som mala veľa práce.I was busy yesterday.
Preto teraz volám kaderníčke.Therefore I am calling the hairdresser now.
Beriem si svoj mobilný telefón.I take my mobile phone.
Volám do kaderníckeho salónu v meste.I call a hair salon in the city.
Telefón zdvihne žena.A woman answers the phone.
„Dobrý deň, tu je kadernícky salón,“ hovorí žena."Hello, this is the hair salon," says the woman.
„Dobrý deň, volám sa Maya,“ hovorím."Hello, my name is Maya," I say.
„Chcela by som si rezervovať termín na štvrtok.“"I would like to book an appointment on Thursday."
„Okamih,“ hovorí kaderníčka."One moment," says the hairdresser.
„Máte voľný termín popoludní?“ pýtam sa."Do you have an appointment in the afternoon?" I ask.
„Áno, máme voľný termín o pol piatej,“ hovorí."Yes, we have an available appointment at half past four," she says.
„To mi veľmi vyhovuje,“ hovorím."That suits me very well," I say.
„Aké je vaše telefónne číslo?“ pýta sa kaderníčka."What is your phone number?" asks the hairdresser.

„Veľmi pekne ďakujem, uvidíme sa vo štvrtok o pol piatej,“ hovorí."Thank you very much, see you on Thursday at half past four," she says.
„Uvidíme sa,“ odpovedám."See you," I reply.

Teraz konečne jem svoje jedlo.Now I finally eat my food.

hallway chat
