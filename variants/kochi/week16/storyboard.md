# Week 16 — Storyboard (Maya Remembers Her Arrival)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Simple past **-ി/-ു** (വന്നു, പോയി, കണ്ടു); time phrases **ഇന്നലെ / കഴിഞ്ഞ ആഴ്ച / ജനുവരിയിൽ** (the perfect **-ിട്ടുണ്ട്** is out of scope — week 19 owns it)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_finishing_badminton | On Thursday evening, just after half past six, Maya finishes a lively game of badminton with Kiran and Sara. They pack their rackets into their bags, wipe their faces, and sit on the benches to rest and drink water. |
| 2 | 02_asking_about_the_move | While they rest, they chat about small recent things — yesterday's sudden rain, last week's close game that Kiran lost. Then Sara asks Maya exactly when she moved to Kochi, and Maya smiles, realizing it is already time to look back. |
| 3 | 03_the_january_taxi | Maya explains how she arrived in January for her new software job. She describes landing at the airport, taking a taxi from the prepaid counter, and showing the driver the paper with the address of her first temporary room. |
| 4 | 04_meeting_anitha_first | Maya tells Kiran and Sara about reaching the building that first day — the heavy bags, the stairs — and Kiran interrupts with questions. She recalls meeting her neighbour Anitha right outside the door, and Sara laughs — a kind neighbour on day one is good luck. |
| 5 | 05_the_first_office_day | Maya then recounts her first day at the Infopark office the following week. She explains how she walked in nervously, sat at her new desk, and met Prakash, who kindly helped her with her computer. Sara nods and says her own first day at work felt exactly the same. |
| 6 | 06_cycling_home_afterward | The storytelling finishes, and the friends laugh together about those early days. Maya says goodbye to Kiran and Sara, unlocks her bicycle, and rides back to her apartment through the warm April evening, quiet after all the remembering. |
| 7 | 07_calling_mexico | Later that night, Maya sits in her comfortable flat and starts a video call to Mexico. The screen connects, and her mother smiles warmly, with Lola the dog sleeping in the background. |
| 8 | 08_retelling_the_story | Maya mentions that she told Kiran and Sara her arrival story today. Her mother smiles and asks to hear it the way Maya told them — so the story comes out once more — shorter this time — and her mother adds what that January morning felt like from her side of the Mexico airport. |
| 9 | 09_a_warm_reflection | Maya and her mother fondly discuss that busy January. Maya tells her mother how fast the time passed, feeling happy about how quickly the city, her friends, and her apartment became home. |
