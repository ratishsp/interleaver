Week 17: Maya explores Odense in the light spring

spring breeze diary
Storyboard: Maya opens her living room window to feel the warm spring breeze. She sits at her table in the bright evening sunlight and begins to write in her travel diary about her weekend trip.
哥本哈根終於到咗春天喇。It is finally spring in Copenhagen.
我行到去客廳個窗前面。I walk over to the window in my living room.
我打開個窗，感受到和暖嘅微風。I open it and feel the warm breeze.
今晚嘅空氣好清新同溫和。The air is fresh and mild this evening.
而家夜晚突然間光猛咗好多。The evenings are suddenly much lighter now.
太陽仲照緊入嚟我張枱上面。The sun is still shining onto my table.
我喺張凳度坐低。I sit down on my chair.
我攞出我本旅遊日記同一支藍色原子筆。I take out my travel diary and a blue pen.
我好鍾意寫低我喺丹麥嘅經歷。I love to write about my experiences in Denmark.
我打開本日記去到空白嘅一頁。I open to a blank page in the book.
我諗起我嗰個好精彩嘅週末。I think about my fantastic weekend.
尋日我去咗一趟長途旅行。I was on a long trip yesterday.
我自己一個人去咗歐登塞。I travelled all alone to Odense.
呢個係我第一次去哥本哈根以外嘅地方旅行。It was my first trip outside Copenhagen.
我微笑，因為我過咗非常好嘅一日。I smile, because I had a really good day.
而家我想將所有嘢都寫低。Now I want to write it all down.
我將支筆擺落張紙上面，開始寫我嘅故事。I put the pen on the paper and begin my story.
sunday train trip
Storyboard: She writes about her Saturday morning excursion. She packed a backpack, bought a warm coffee, and took the fast train out of Copenhagen to visit the city of Odense.
我喺日記寫低咗去奧登塞嘅旅程。I wrote in my diary about my trip to Odense.
嗰日係一個美好嘅星期六朝早。It was a lovely Saturday morning.
我七點鐘就一早醒咗。I woke up early at seven o'clock.
出面陽光普照，天空好藍。The sun was shining, and the sky was blue.
我執好我個綠色細背囊。I packed my small, green backpack.
我擺咗一本筆記簿同一部相機落個袋度。I put a notebook and a camera in the bag.
我仲帶咗件厚冷衫。I also brought a thick sweater.
丹麥嘅春天天氣可以有啲凍。The spring weather in Denmark can be a bit cold.
跟住我搭巴士去哥本哈根個大火車站。Then I took the bus into the large station in Copenhagen.
入面有好多匆匆忙忙嘅人。There were many busy people inside.
我行咗去一間細小賣部。I walked over to a small kiosk.
我買咗杯大嘅熱咖啡準備喺車上飲。I bought a large, warm coffee for the trip.
價錢係四十五克朗。It cost forty-five kroner.
我抬高頭望住個大螢幕。I looked up at the large screen.
我好快就喺螢幕上搵到我班火車。I quickly found my train on the screen.
我準備搭快車去奧登塞。I was taking the fast train to Odense.
火車正正喺八點鐘到月台。The train arrived at the platform exactly at eight o'clock.
我上咗車，搵到我喺大窗隔籬個位。I went into the train and found my seat by a large window.
我飲住熱咖啡，好期待呢次嘅短途旅行。I drank my warm coffee and looked forward to the excursion.
long sea bridge
Storyboard: She describes the spectacular journey as the train crossed the very long bridge over the sea. She looked out the large window and admired the blue water and the small ships.
架火車快速駛過沿途嘅風景。The train drove quickly through the landscape.
我安靜噉坐喺自己個位度。I sat quietly in my seat.
我坐咗一個好好嘅窗口位。I had a really good window seat.
過咗一陣，我哋終於到咗海邊。After a little while, we finally reached the coast.
架火車駛上咗一條好長嘅跨海大橋。The train drove out onto a very long bridge over the sea.
突然之間，我哋周圍全部都係水。Suddenly there was only water all around us.
條橋離水面好高。The bridge lay high above the water.
我由個大窗望出去。I looked out of the large window.
下面嘅海水好藍。The water below us was very blue.
我欣賞住呢個又靚又開揚嘅風景。I admired the beautiful, open view.
喺好遠嘅下面，我見到幾隻小船。Far below, I saw some small ships.
佢哋望落幾乎好似玩具咁。They almost looked like toys.
個天係淺藍色嘅，一啲雲都冇。The sky was light blue and completely without clouds.
感覺就好似喺海面上飛緊一樣。It was almost like flying over the sea.
我喺袋入面攞部相機出嚟。I took my camera out of my bag.
我影咗幾張小船嘅相。I took a couple of pictures of the small ships.
我從來都未見過咁嘅景象。I had never seen anything like it.
之後，我收埋部相機，好好享受呢趟旅程。Afterwards, I put my camera away and just enjoyed the journey.
stressed father train
Storyboard: She watched a stressed father sitting across the aisle. He desperately tried to read his book, but his two young children constantly threw their toys on the floor.
我坐喺火車嘅座位上四圍望。I sat in my seat on the train and looked around.
有個爸爸坐喺我對面。A father sat opposite me.
佢帶住佢兩個小朋友。He brought his two small children.
呢個爸爸有一本好大嘅書。The father had a big book.
佢好想睇佢本書。He really wanted to read his book.
但係佢都冇咩時間靜下。But he did not get much peace.
佢個女有個紅色嘅波。His daughter had a red ball.
佢個仔有架綠色嘅玩具車。His son had a green toy car.
啲小朋友完全唔肯坐定定。The children did not sit still at all.
佢哋不停將啲玩具掟落地下。They constantly threw their toys on the floor.
個爸爸大聲嘆咗口氣，然後合埋本書。The father sighed loudly and closed his book.
佢喺地下執返起啲嘢。He picked the things up from the floor.
佢將啲玩具畀返啲小朋友。He gave the children their toys back.
「你哋要坐定定，」佢好攰咁講。"You must sit quietly," he said tiredly.
佢又打開本書。He opened his book again.
兩分鐘之後，個波跌咗落地下。Two minutes later, the ball fell on the floor.
打中咗爸爸隻左邊鞋。It hit the father's left shoe.
個男人又執返起個波。The man picked up the ball again.
我對住個好攰嘅爸爸笑咗笑。I smiled at the tired father.
佢只係搖搖頭，低頭望住自己本書。He just shook his head and looked down into his book.
naughty little dog
Storyboard: The family also brought a little dog. The dog suddenly jumped out of its basket and ran away with the father's left shoe, making the children laugh loudly at him.
嗰家人仲帶咗隻狗仔上火車。The family also brought a little dog on the train.
佢好得意，但係唔安靜。It was very cute, but it was not quiet.
隻狗伏喺地下佢個籃仔度。The dog lay in its little basket on the floor.
個爸爸又望落去自己本書度。The father looked down at his book again.
佢淨係想睇陣書。He just wanted to read a little.
不過隻狗突然由個籃度跳咗出嚟。But suddenly the dog jumped out of its basket.
佢快步跑過去個爸爸度。It ran quickly over to the father.
佢坐喺度，有一隻腳放咗喺個袋上面。He sat with one foot up on a bag.
隻狗搶走咗佢左腳隻鞋。The dog grabbed his left shoe.
佢用個口咬住隻鞋。It took the shoe in its mouth.
之後好快咁跑走咗。Then it ran away quickly.
「唔好呀，攞番我隻鞋返嚟！」個爸爸大叫。"No, come back with my shoe!" the father shouted.
佢淨係著住對襪企起身。He stood up in his socks.
佢喺走廊追住隻狗跑。He ran after the dog down the aisle.
嗰兩個小朋友指住佢。The two children pointed at him.
佢哋望住大家，笑得好大聲。They looked at each other and laughed loudly.
我喺座位度望住佢哋。I looked at them from my seat.
我都喺本書後面笑咗一下。I also laughed a little behind my book.
好彩，佢最後都捉得番隻狗。Fortunately, he finally caught his dog.
佢亦都攞番自己隻鞋。He also got his shoe back.
crooked colorful houses
Storyboard: Maya arrived in Odense and walked straight into the old quarter. She loved the historic atmosphere and took pictures of the small, crooked houses painted in bright yellow and red.
我九點多啲到咗歐登塞。I arrived in Odense a little past nine.
我離開火車站，直接向舊城區行過去。I left the station and walked directly toward the old quarter.
陽光好好，我享受住清新嘅春天空氣。The sun was shining, and I enjoyed the fresh spring air.
行咗一陣，個城市就變咗樣。After a short walk, the city changed.
突然間，我企咗喺一條鋪滿鵝卵石嘅窄街度。Suddenly I stood on a narrow street with cobblestones.
啲鵝卵石好舊，表面都磨損晒。The cobblestones were worn and old.
我望住好多間細細嘅屋。I looked at the many small houses.
佢哋沿住條街排成一排，挨得好近。They lay close together in a row along the street.
幾乎所有屋都好得意，仲歪歪斜斜咁。Almost all the houses were funny and very crooked.
每間屋都展現出自己獨特嘅個性。Each house showed its very own personality.
好多間屋都油上咗好靚、好光鮮嘅顏色。Many of them were painted in beautiful, bright colors.
我特別留意到黃色同紅色嘅屋。I especially noticed the yellow and red houses.
色彩繽紛嘅牆壁喺春天嘅陽光下顯得特別耀眼。The colorful walls lit up in the spring sun.
我簡直愛上咗呢個舊城區。I simply loved the old part of town.
我企喺度，望住啲歪歪斜斜嘅屋望咗好耐。I stood and looked at the crooked houses for a long time.
每間屋都有自己令人開心嘅顏色。Each house had its own cheerful colour.
我想永遠記住呢條街。I wanted to remember this street forever.
之後，我慢慢咁沿住條街繼續向前行。After that, I slowly walked further down the street.
painting the house
Storyboard: She stopped to watch an older local couple. The man stood in the garden and painted his house, while his wife sat on a wooden bench and drank her warm tea.
我繼續沿住條狹窄嘅街行。I continued down the narrow street.
我停喺一間好舊嘅屋前面。I stopped in front of a very old house.
一對年長嘅夫婦喺外面曬緊春日嘅太陽。An older couple was out in the spring sun.
個男人企喺佢個花園度。The man stood in his garden.
佢用油掃髹緊佢個舊籬笆。He painted his old fence with a brush.
新嘅顏色係淺黃色。The new color was light yellow.
佢太太坐喺附近一張木凳上面。His wife sat on a wooden bench nearby.
佢好悠閒咁飲住熱茶。She relaxed and drank her warm tea.
個男人見到我，停低咗一陣。The man saw me and paused for a moment.
佢拎住把油掃好友善咁揮手。He waved kindly with his brush.
「早晨！」佢同我講。"Good morning!" he said to me.
「你哋個花園好靚啊，」我話。"Your garden looks lovely," I said.
佢太太喺木凳上面對住我微笑。His wife smiled at me from her bench.
「多謝，我哋享受緊呢個春天，」佢答。"Thank you, we are enjoying the spring," she answered.
個男人好平靜咁繼續髹佢個籬笆。The man calmly kept painting his fence.
啲雀仔喺佢哋個花園度唱歌。The birds sang in their garden.

fairy tale museum

而家我覺得我對丹麥文化了解多咗少少。Now I feel that I know Danish culture a little better.
river boat trip
