# Week 23 — Storyboard (Maya visits Deepa for dinner)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Future **-ും** and intentional **-ാം**; thinking/feeling with the quotative **എന്ന്** (ഞാൻ കരുതുന്നു … എന്ന്)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_the_invitation | At the Infopark office early in the week, Deepa approaches Maya's desk and invites her over for dinner on Friday evening. Deepa says that she will prepare a special traditional meal, and Maya happily accepts, promising that she will definitely come. |
| 2 | 02_consulting_prakash | During a tea break, Maya talks through her Friday plans with Prakash. Since it is her first time visiting Deepa's home, she asks for his advice, and Prakash suggests that bringing some local sweets from a nearby bakery is always a good idea. |
| 3 | 03_buying_a_gift | During her lunch break on Friday, Maya stops at the bakery Prakash recommended. She looks at the colourful displays and decides that a mixed box of halwa will be the perfect dessert to share. |
| 4 | 04_planning_the_commute | Late Friday afternoon, Maya checks a map app on her phone to figure out how to get to Deepa's neighbourhood. Seeing the heavy monsoon clouds, she realises that taking the bus will take too long, so she plans to hire an auto-rickshaw instead. |
| 5 | 05_the_auto_ride | Leaving the office, Maya flags down an auto-rickshaw in the light drizzle. She tells the driver the destination and confidently gives the main junction as a landmark before they speed off. |
| 6 | 06_arriving_at_deepas | Maya arrives safely, pays the driver, and walks up to the house. Deepa opens the door with a warm smile, and Maya hands over the box of sweets, mentioning that Prakash highly recommended this particular bakery. |
| 7 | 07_the_dinner | They sit down at the dining table, and Deepa serves steaming hot appam and fragrant vegetable stew. Maya eagerly tastes the food and immediately declares that it is the most delicious stew she has eaten since moving to Kochi. |
| 8 | 08_becoming_friends | After the meal, the two women relax in the living room, sharing the sweet halwa and drinking glasses of warm jeera water while the rain falls outside. They share stories about their lives beyond the software company, finally connecting as real friends rather than just colleagues. |
