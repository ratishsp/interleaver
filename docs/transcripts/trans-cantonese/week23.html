Week 23: Maya visits Eva for dinner and makes future plans

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

office invitation
Storyboard: At the software company, Eva warmly invites Maya to her home for dinner this Friday. Maya gladly accepts, smiling, and says that she will definitely be there.
星期三朝早，我喺辦公室坐喺自己張寫字檯前面。I am sitting at my desk in the office on a Wednesday morning.
伊娃攞住杯咖啡行過嚟我張寫字檯。Eva walks over to my desk with a cup of coffee.
「嗨，瑪雅，你星期五得唔得閒呀？」佢滿面笑容咁問。"Hi Maya, do you have time on Friday?" she asks with a big smile.
我諗我嗰晚的確係得閒嘅。I think that I actually have a free evening.
「係呀，我好得閒，」我答佢。"Yes, I have plenty of time," I answer her.
「我好想請你嚟我屋企食晚飯，」佢話。"I would really like to invite you home for dinner," she says.
收到呢個邀請，我好開心。I become very happy about the invitation.
「多謝，我好樂意嚟，」我話。"Thank you, I will really gladly come," I say.
「我打算煮素菜，」伊娃解釋。"I intend to make a vegetarian dish," Eva explains.
「聽落非常完美，」我笑住話。"That sounds absolutely perfect," I smile.
「我幾點要到呀？」我問。"What time shall I be there?" I ask.
「你六點鐘過嚟就得喇，」佢答。"You can just come at six o'clock," she replies.
我覺得拜訪新朋友總係好開心。I think that it always is nice to visit new friends.
「我一定會到，」我肯定咁話。"I will definitely be there," I confirm.
伊娃將佢嘅地址寫喺一張黃色嘅細便條紙上面。Eva writes her address on a small yellow note.
佢將張便條紙遞畀我。She hands the note to me.
「我非常之期待星期五呀，」我同佢講。"I will look forward very much to Friday," I say to her.
伊娃再笑咗一下，然後行返去佢部電腦度。Eva smiles again and walks back to her computer.
planning the route
Storyboard: During a break, Maya looks up directions to the apartment on her phone. She sees it is quite far across town, so she thinks that she will probably take the bus rather than ride her bicycle.
我暫時放低工作，休息一陣。I am taking a little break from work.
我攞部電話出嚟睇地圖。I take out my phone to look at a map.
我想搵伊娃嘅地址。I want to find Eva's address.
佢住喺成個城市嘅另一邊。She lives completely on the other side of town.
我仔細咁睇螢幕上面嘅路線。I look closely at the route on the screen.
其實都幾遠吓。It is actually quite far away.
彼得行入茶水間攞杯咖啡。Peter comes into the break room to get a cup of coffee.
佢喺我膊頭上面望過嚟，問我做緊咩。He looks over my shoulder and asks what I am doing.
「我星期五會去探伊娃，」我解釋。"I am going to visit Eva on Friday," I explain.
「你會唔會踩單車去呀？」佢問。"Will you cycle there?" he asks.
我諗咗一陣。I think about it for a moment.
我覺得我應該會搭巴士。I think that I probably will take the bus.
我覺得踩單車根本太遠喇。I feel that it simply is too far by bike.
「唔會，我打算搭巴士，」我答佢。"No, I intend to take the bus," I answer him.
「咁你搭車嗰陣都可以放鬆吓啦，」彼得笑住講。"Then you can also relax on the trip," Peter says with a smile.
佢講得好啱。He is absolutely right.
我閂咗個應用程式，將部電話擺返落袋。I close the app and put the phone back in my pocket.
依家我嘅路線已經計劃好喇。Now my route is planned.
我好期待一個溫馨嘅夜晚。I am looking forward to a cozy evening.
buying a gift
Storyboard: On Friday afternoon, Maya stops at a local florist after work. She intends to bring a bright bouquet of yellow tulips, believing that Eva, being a designer, will always appreciate colorful things.
星期五下晝，我收咗工。It is Friday afternoon, and I am off from work.
我沿住條街行，停喺一間花店門口。I walk down the street and stop at a florist.
我打算買份禮物畀 Eva。I intend to buy a gift for Eva.
我推開門，行入呢間小店。I open the door and walk into the small shop.
店員笑容滿面咁話：「午安。」"Good afternoon," the clerk says with a big smile.
我答：「午安。」"Good afternoon," I reply.
我望住咁多靚花。I look at the many beautiful flowers.
我諗 Eva 一向都鍾意色彩。I think that Eva always likes colours.
佢係設計師，所以應該會好鍾意啲色彩繽紛嘅嘢。She is a designer, so she will probably love something colourful.
我搵到一大束黃色鬱金香。I find a large bouquet with yellow tulips.
「我想買呢束花，」我指住啲鬱金香話。"I would like to buy this bouquet," I say and point at the tulips.
店員話：「呢個選擇真係唔錯。」"That is a really good choice," the clerk says.
佢用啲好靚嘅紙包好啲花。He wraps the flowers in some nice paper.
佢話：「總共一百五十克朗。」"That will be one hundred and fifty kroner," he says.
我攞張卡出嚟畀錢買花。I take out my card and pay for the flowers.
我同佢講：「我今晚要去食晚飯。」"I am going to a dinner tonight," I tell him.
佢答：「咁你朋友一定會好開心。」"Then your friend will definitely be happy," he replies.
我攞住束花行出舖頭。I take the bouquet and walk out of the shop.
依家我淨係要搵我個巴士站。Now I just have to find my bus stop.
taking the bus
Storyboard: Waiting at the busy bus stop, Maya checks the timetable. She feels that the evening traffic is often slow on Fridays, but she expects a smooth ride today because the roads look surprisingly clear.
我拎住啲黃花企喺巴士站度。I stand at the bus stop with the yellow flowers.
嗰度有好多人喺度等緊。There are many people who are waiting.
我望住個螢幕睇時間表。I look at the screen to see the timetable.
架巴士應該五分鐘之後就到。The bus is supposed to be here in five minutes.
我覺得星期五啲車通常都行得好慢。I think that the Friday traffic is often very slow.
但係今日條街睇落空蕩蕩。But the streets look completely empty today.
所以我估架巴士應該會準時到。So I think that the bus will probably arrive on time.
有個上咗年紀嘅男人企喺我隔離。An older man stands next to me.
佢望住隻錶，嘆咗口氣。He looks at his watch and sighs.
佢問我：「你知唔知架巴士係咪遲咗？」"Do you know if the bus is late?" he asks me.
我再睇吓我部手機。I check my phone again.
我答：「唔係，我估冇遲。」"No, I don't think so," I answer.
「我都係搭同一架巴士。」"I am also taking the same bus."
「我估其實佢轉個彎就到。」"I think that it is actually just around the corner."
佢對住我笑，點咗點頭。He smiles at me and nods.
突然，我哋見到街尾有架紅色嘅大巴士。Suddenly we see the big red bus down the street.
佢好快咁向我哋駛過嚟。It drives quickly towards us.
個男人鬆咗口氣咁講：「你講得啱。」"You were right," the man says, relieved.
我哋一齊上咗巴士。We step onto the bus together.
我肯定可以準時去到 Eva 屋企。I will definitely be at Eva's place on time.
我好期待睇吓佢間屋。I am excited to see her apartment.
arriving at evas
Storyboard: Maya rings the doorbell at Eva's apartment building. Eva welcomes her warmly into a highly creative, cozy space, and Maya gives her the flowers, hoping that she does not already have a full vase on the table.
我企喺十二號大廈前面。I am standing in front of building number twelve.
我搵到 Eva 個名，然後撳門鐘。I find Eva's name and ring the doorbell.
大門開咗，我行上樓梯。The door opens, and I walk up the stairs.
Eva 企喺門口，笑容滿面。Eva is standing in the doorway and smiles broadly.
「嗨 Maya，歡迎！」佢話，仲攬咗我一下。"Hi Maya, welcome!" she says and gives me a hug.
「多謝你嘅邀請，」我答。"Thanks for the invitation," I answer.
我行入佢間屋。I step into her apartment.
呢度好溫馨，充滿色彩繽紛嘅藝術品。It is very cozy and full of colorful art.
我將啲黃色鬱金香送畀佢。I give her the yellow tulips.
我希望佢張檯上面唔係已經擺咗樽花啦。I hope that she does not already have a vase with flowers on the table.
Eva 望住啲花，睇落好開心。Eva looks at the flowers and looks very happy.
「真係好靚呀！」佢驚嘆噉講。"How beautiful they are!" she exclaims.
「我好鍾意黃色嘅花。」"I love yellow flowers."

飯檯佈置得好靚，放咗啲精緻嘅玻璃杯。The dining table is beautifully set with fine glasses.
我打算放鬆下，好好享受呢個夜晚。I intend to relax and enjoy the evening.
