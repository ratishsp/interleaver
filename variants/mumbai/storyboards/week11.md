# Week 11 — Storyboard (Maya experiences the Mumbai heat and a sudden shower)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Weather expressions **गर्मी है / धूप है / बारिश हो रही है**; simple future **-एगा/-एगी** for the forecast (बारिश होगी)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_sun | Maya wakes up in her flat and pulls back the curtains to a bright, glaring morning. She immediately feels the intense warmth in the room and realizes it is a very hot and sunny day. |
| 2 | 02_weather_forecast | While drinking her morning coffee, Maya checks the weather app on her phone. She sees the high temperatures for the day but is surprised to read the forecast predicting that it will rain in the evening. |
| 3 | 03_dressing_light | Getting ready for the office, Maya knows she must dress for the hot weather. She puts on her new light white cotton shirt, knowing it will keep her comfortable. |
| 4 | 04_hot_commute | Maya walks to the BEST bus stop. The morning sun is already harsh on the street, and she waits in a small patch of shade, feeling the heavy, sticky heat of the city. |
| 5 | 05_office_window | Inside the cool software office, Maya chats with her colleague Rohan by a large window. They look out at the blazing afternoon sun; Rohan complains about the heat, but Maya happily tells him the forecast says it will rain later. |
| 6 | 06_dark_clouds | Maya finishes her work and steps out of the office building. The weather suddenly shifts—the bright sunshine vanishes as thick, dark clouds roll in, and a cool wind begins to blow. |
| 7 | 07_sudden_shower | Large drops start falling, and a sudden, heavy shower begins. It is Maya's first experience of rain in Mumbai; she smiles at the sudden change, quickly steps under a shop awning with other commuters to stay dry, and then catches her BEST bus home once the heavy downpour lightens. |
| 8 | 08_cool_evening | Later that night, Maya stands by the open window in her flat. The rain stops, leaving the air completely fresh and cool, and she happily watches the wet street below. |
