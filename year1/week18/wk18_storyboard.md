# Week 18 — Storyboard (Maya gets a spring allergy)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** *gør ondt*; body parts; *har det godt/dårligt*; *burde*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_waking_up_unwell | On Thursday morning, Maya wakes up and feels terrible. Her head is heavy, her eyes itch, and her throat is dry. She decides she ought to stay home from work. |
| 2 | 02_calling_the_clinic | She calls her local medical clinic. She explains her unfamiliar symptoms to the receptionist, who agrees she ought to see a doctor today and gives her an appointment for the early afternoon. |
| 3 | 03_scanning_health_card | Maya walks slowly to the clinic. In the reception area, she scans the yellow health card she received when she moved to Denmark to check in for her appointment. |
| 4 | 04_waiting_room_boy | She sits in the quiet waiting room near a little boy. The boy complains loudly that his ear hurts, while his mother gently tries to comfort him. |
| 5 | 05_explaining_the_pain | The friendly doctor calls her in. Maya sits in his bright office and explains exactly where it hurts, pointing to her scratchy throat, her heavy head, and her red eyes. |
| 6 | 06_spring_allergy_diagnosis | The doctor listens to her breathing and looks at her throat. He explains it is a common spring allergy she has never experienced before, and says she ought to buy some mild medicine. |
| 7 | 07_pharmacy_visit | Maya stops at the pharmacy on her way back. The pharmacist hands her a small box of allergy pills and suggests some sweet drops because her throat still hurts. |
| 8 | 08_skipping_badminton | That evening, Maya has to cancel her usual Thursday badminton game. She rests on her sofa, takes her new medicine, and drinks a large cup of hot tea with honey. |
| 9 | 09_healthy_saturday_morning | By Saturday morning, the mild medicine has worked perfectly. Maya feels completely well again — clear eyes, no headache. She opens the window to the mild spring air, makes a proper breakfast, and takes a slow walk in the park, glad to have her energy back. |
