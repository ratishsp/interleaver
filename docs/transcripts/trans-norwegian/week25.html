Week 25: A Sunday in the city

Norwegian with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning calendar
Storyboard: On Sunday morning, Maya makes a fresh pot of coffee and flips her wall calendar to July. She realizes that she moved to Copenhagen exactly six months ago, and decides to celebrate by spending this sunny day exploring the city at her own pace.

Solen skinner inn gjennom vinduet i leiligheten min.The sun is shining in through the window in my apartment.

Jeg lager en kanne fersk kaffe.I make a pot of fresh coffee.
Duften av kaffe er fantastisk denne morgenen.The smell of coffee is fantastic this morning.
Jeg har en kalender på veggen.I have a calendar on the wall.
Jeg blar om til neste måned.I turn the page to the next month.
Det er juli nå.It is July now.
Jeg flyttet til København i januar.I moved to Copenhagen in January.
Jeg kom hit for akkurat seks måneder siden.I came here exactly six months ago.
Tiden går virkelig fort.Time really goes fast.
Min første dag i denne byen var veldig kald.My first day in this city was very cold.
Men i dag er været varmt og vakkert.But today the weather is warm and beautiful.
Jeg vil feire dette lille jubileet.I want to celebrate this little anniversary.
Jeg har ingen faste planer i dag.I have no fixed plans today.
Jeg tar kaffekoppen min og ser ut over gaten.I take my coffee cup and look out over the street.
Jeg vil gå ut og utforske byen i mitt eget tempo.I want to go out and explore the city at my own pace.
Jeg gleder meg til å nyte disse lyse timene.I look forward to enjoying these bright hours.
ceramic plates
Storyboard: Maya wanders into a lively second-hand market in a nearby square. She spots two blue ceramic plates on a table, decides these plates are perfect for her kitchen, and buys them from the vendor for forty kroner.
Jeg går en tur over en stor plass i byen.I go for a walk across a large square in the city.
Her er det et stort loppemarked hver søndag.Here there is a large flea market every Sunday.
Solen skinner, og mange mennesker går rundt mellom bordene.The sun is shining, and many people are walking around between the tables.
Jeg stopper ved et lite bord med mange forskjellige ting.I stop by a small table with many different things.
Bak bordet står det en vennlig selger.Behind the table stands a friendly vendor.
Plutselig ser jeg to blå keramikktallerkener.Suddenly I see two blue ceramic plates.
Jeg tar dem opp og ser nøye på dem.I pick them up and look closely at them.
"Hvor mye koster disse tallerkenene?" spør jeg selgeren."How much do these plates cost?" I ask the vendor.
Selgeren smiler til meg.The vendor smiles at me.
"De koster bare førti kroner," svarer han."They cost only forty kroner," he replies.
Det er en veldig god pris.That is a really good price.
Jeg kjøpte et lite bord til kjøkkenet mitt for en måned siden.I bought a small table for my kitchen a month ago.
Disse tallerkenene vil passe perfekt til dette bordet.These plates will fit perfectly with this table.
"Dem vil jeg veldig gjerne kjøpe," sier jeg."I would very much like to buy them," I say.
Jeg gir selgeren førti kroner og legger tallerkenene forsiktig i vesken min.I give the vendor forty kroner and carefully put the plates in my bag.
Det er alltid fint å finne noe spesielt på en rolig søndag.It is always nice to find something special on a quiet Sunday.
winter coat
Storyboard: At a clothing stall, Maya tries on a heavy wool winter coat. She immediately takes it off because this coat is far too warm for summer, laughing with the vendor that she desperately needed something exactly like it five months ago.
Jeg går videre på markedet og stopper ved en klesbod.I walk further into the market and stop at a clothing stall.
Jeg ser en tung vinterfrakk av ull.I see a heavy winter coat made of wool.
Denne frakken er flott, men veldig tykk.This coat is beautiful, but very thick.
Jeg prøver frakken likevel.I try the coat on anyway.
Den er utrolig varm.It is incredibly warm.
Jeg tar den av igjen med en gang.I take it off again immediately.
Det er juli, og solen skinner i dag.It is July, and the sun is shining today.
En vennlig kvinne står bak bordet og smiler.A friendly woman stands behind the table and smiles.
"Er den for varm?" spør kvinnen."Is it too warm?" the woman asks.
"Ja, den er litt for varm til sommeren," svarer jeg og ler."Yes, it is a bit too warm for the summer," I reply and laugh.
Jeg tar på det tykke stoffet.I touch the thick fabric.
"Men jeg trengte virkelig denne vinterfrakken for fem måneder siden!" sier jeg."But I really needed this winter coat five months ago!" I say.
Kvinnen ler høyt.The woman laughs loudly.
"Ja, februar var veldig kald," sier hun."Yes, February was very cold," she says.
Jeg husker mine første uker i København.I remember my first weeks in Copenhagen.
Jeg frøs konstant den gangen.I was constantly freezing back then.
Heldigvis trenger jeg ikke så varmt tøy akkurat nå.Fortunately, I do not need such warm clothes right now.
Jeg henger frakken tilbake på stativet og sier farvel.I hang the coat back on the rack and say goodbye.
museum ticket
Storyboard: Moving on, Maya enters a local history museum and goes to the front desk. She asks the clerk if this entrance ticket includes the special upstairs exhibition, and the clerk tells her to hurry because the guided tour started ten minutes ago.
Jeg går vekk fra markedet og finner et lite, lokalt museum.I walk away from the market and find a small, local museum.
Jeg går inn i den stille bygningen.I walk into the quiet building.
Jeg går bort til skranken for å kjøpe en inngangsbillett.I walk over to the counter to buy an entrance ticket.
Det står en ung mann bak kassen.A young man is standing behind the register.
"Hei, en voksenbillett, takk," sier jeg."Hi, one adult ticket, please," I say.
"Det blir hundre kroner," sier han og printer den."That will be one hundred kroner," he says and prints it.
Jeg betaler og ser på disse fargerike brosjyrene på disken.I pay and look at these colorful brochures on the counter.
De reklamerer for en spesiell utstilling om byens historie.They advertise a special exhibition about the history of the city.
"Gir denne billetten også adgang til utstillingen ovenpå?" spør jeg."Does this ticket also give access to the exhibition upstairs?" I ask.
"Ja, dette museet har kun én samlet billett," svarer han."Yes, this museum only has one combined ticket," he answers.
"Utstillingen ligger ovenpå i andre etasje," sier han."The exhibition is upstairs on the first floor," he says.
"Du kan ta deg god tid der oppe.""You can take your time up there."
"Den har mange gamle fotografier av byen," forklarer han."It has many old photographs of the city," he explains.
"Noen av dem er over hundre år gamle.""Some of them are over a hundred years old."
"Det høres veldig spennende ut," sier jeg."That sounds really exciting," I say.
"Tusen takk," smiler jeg."Thank you very much," I smile.
Jeg tar billetten min og går rolig opp trappen.I take my ticket and walk calmly up the stairs.
harbor photograph

Jeg går ofte en tur langs vannet i helgene.I often go for a walk along the water on the weekends.

texting sofia

Disse tallerkenene passer perfekt til kjøkkenet mitt.These plates fit perfectly in my kitchen.

seagull encounter
Storyboard: Walking home through a large park, Maya stops at a kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream. As she strolls along the pond, a bold seagull suddenly swoops down, forcing her to quickly duck and shield this dessert with her hand to prevent the bird from stealing it.
Jeg går hjem gjennom en stor park.I walk home through a large park.
For en time siden var jeg på museum.An hour ago I was at a museum.
Nå vil jeg bare nyte det gode været.Now I just want to enjoy the good weather.
Jeg stopper ved en liten kiosk for å kjøpe en isvaffel.I stop at a small kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream.
"Vær så god, her er isen din," sier mannen i kiosken."Here you go, here is your ice cream," the man in the kiosk says.
"Tusen takk," svarer jeg og tar isen."Thank you very much," I reply and take the ice cream.
Jeg går sakte langs vannet i parken.I walk slowly along the lake in the park.

Plutselig hører jeg en høy lyd over meg.Suddenly I hear a loud noise above me.

Jeg dukker meg raskt og skjermer isen min med hånden.I duck quickly and shield my ice cream with my hand.

Jeg spiser raskt ferdig isen min mens jeg går videre.I quickly finish eating my ice cream while I walk on.
unpacking plates
