# Week 22 — Storyboard (Maya cooks a Berlin dinner for her friends)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Imperative sequences; quantities (**ein halbes Kilo, zwei Esslöffel**); **zuerst / dann / danach**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_planning_the_menu | After her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the café, Maya relaxes in the flat. She has invited her badminton friends, Lena and Timo, over for dinner this evening. The video chef addresses his viewers with **Sie** — *Kochen Sie, mischen Sie* — and keeps that register in every scene he appears in; never *ihr*, never *du*. Wanting to cook something authentically local, she decides on Kartoffelsalat mit Bouletten. She finds an energetic cooking video on her phone by a local Berlin chef to guide her. |
| 2 | 02_writing_the_list | Maya checks her kitchen cupboards. She sees she already has onions, eggs and a day-old Brötchen, so she writes a short list of what she still needs — the quantities are named at the counter and the shelves, not here: minced meat, potatoes, mayonnaise, and mustard. She grabs her backpack and heads out to the local supermarket. |
| 3 | 03_at_the_meat_counter | Inside the supermarket, Maya goes directly to the fresh meat counter. She politely addresses the butcher using the formal 'Sie' and asks for exactly half a kilo (ein halbes Kilo) of mixed minced meat. The butcher weighs it out, wraps it in paper, and hands it to her over the glass counter. |
| 4 | 04_gathering_and_checkout | She quickly gathers the remaining items from the aisles: a bag of potatoes, a small jar of mustard, and a jar of mayonnaise. At the till, she greets the cashier with 'Sie', pays for her groceries in Euro, and makes sure to take the receipt before packing everything neatly into her backpack. |
| 5 | 05_step_one_potatoes | Back in the flat, Maya props up her phone on the counter and hits play. The chef delivers the first command — do not re-describe him, scene 1 already did: 'First (zuerst), boil the potatoes!' Maya smiles at the dramatic delivery, puts a pot of salted water on the stove, and gets to work. |
| 6 | 06_mixing_the_bouletten | As the water bubbles, the video moves to the next step. Vary how the sequence words land — not every instruction needs to open with zuerst/dann/danach in the same rigid order. The chef says: 'Then (dann), mix the minced meat with an egg, diced onions, a soaked bread roll and two tablespoons (zwei Esslöffel) of mustard!' Maya happily follows along with the video, getting her hands messy as she kneads the mixture and forms flat, round patties. |
| 7 | 07_frying_and_dressing | Maya washes her hands and fries the patties in a pan until they are dark and crispy. While they cook, the potatoes are ready: she drains them, lets them cool a little, peels them and slices them into a bowl. Then she plays the final part of the video. The chef points at the camera: 'Afterwards (danach), mix the cooled potatoes with mayonnaise!' She follows the final instruction, tossing the sliced potatoes in the rich dressing to complete the true Berlin meal. |
| 8 | 08_friends_arrive | The doorbell rings. Timo and Lena arrive, bringing a few bottles of chilled local beer with them. Maya greets them enthusiastically — informal, and **ihr** rather than *du*, because she is speaking to two people, takes their jackets, and invites them to sit down at her small, neatly set dining table. |
| 9 | 09_sharing_the_meal | Maya brings out the large bowl of creamy Kartoffelsalat and the platter of warm Bouletten. Timo and Lena serve themselves and are highly impressed by the authentic local meal. The conversation flows easily as they enjoy the food, and Maya simply toasts with her friends, thrilled that her first attempt at Berlin cuisine is such a success. |
