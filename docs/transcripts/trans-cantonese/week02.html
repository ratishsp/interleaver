Week 2: First Day & Registration

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning commute
Storyboard: Maya walks briskly through the chilly morning air, looking for her new workplace. She checks the large numbers on the buildings along the street until she spots the software company's logo next to number forty-four.
哥本哈根而家係朝早。It is morning in Copenhagen.
而家係一月，天氣好凍。It is January, and it is very cold.
我有啲緊張。I am a little nervous.
今日係一個大日子。Today is a big day.
對我嚟講，呢個係一個新開始。It is a new start for me.
今日係我返新工嘅第一日。It is my first day at my new job.
我搭巴士返工。I take the bus to work.
我喺啱嘅車站落車。I get off at the right stop.
而家我行埋淨低嗰段路。Now I walk the last part.
辦公室喺邊呢？Where is the office?
我望住嗰啲大廈。I look at the large buildings.
每棟大廈都有一個號碼。Each building has a number.
呢度係三十八號。Here is number thirty-eight.
跟住呢度係四十號。And here is number forty.
我再行前少少。I walk a little further.
呢度係四十二號。Here is number forty-two.
就喺嗰度喇！And there it is!
呢度係四十四號。Here is number forty-four.
我見到一個好大嘅標誌。I see a large logo.
我間新軟件公司就喺呢度。My new software company is right here.
我有種好好嘅感覺。I have a good feeling.
我已經準備好晒喇。I am completely ready.
meeting peter
Storyboard: Inside the bright office, a smiling colleague introduces himself as Peter. He shakes her hand, hands her a new laptop, and leads her through the open-plan workspace to her assigned desk.
我依家喺新寫字樓入面。I am now inside the new office.
呢度係一間又大又光猛嘅房。It is a large and bright room.
有個好友善嘅男人企喺門口。A friendly man stands by the door.
佢對住我笑。He smiles at me.
佢話：「歡迎！」"Welcome!" he says.
「我叫彼得。」"My name is Peter."
我同佢握手。I shake his hand.
我話：「你好彼得，我叫瑪雅。」"Hi Peter, my name is Maya," I say.
彼得係一位同事。Peter is a colleague.
我哋一齊穿過寫字樓。We walk together through the office.
彼得話：「你喺呢度有張寫字檯。」"You have a desk here," says Peter.
張寫字檯嘅號碼係十六號。The desk has number sixteen.
佢話：「同埋你有一部電腦。」"And you have a computer," he says.
佢畀咗一部新嘅手提電腦我。He gives me a new laptop.
我望住我個新位。I look at my new spot.
我有一張凳同一個大螢幕。I have a chair and a large monitor.
我話：「多謝晒，彼得。」"Thank you very much, Peter," I say.
我準備好開始第一日嘅工作喇。I am ready for my first workday.
the phone shop
Storyboard: On her lunch break, Maya ducks into a telecom shop to buy a local SIM card. She hands the clerk her phone, and he activates it, reading her brand-new local number aloud as she saves it.
而家係我嘅午飯時間。It is my lunch break.
我喺一間電話舖。I am in a phone shop.
我有一部嚟自墨西哥嘅電話。I have a phone from Mexico.
個店員係一個後生仔。The clerk is a young man.
「你有冇丹麥嘅 SIM 咭？」我問。"Do you have a Danish SIM card?" I ask.
「有，當然有，」佢答。"Yes, of course," he answers.
我將部電話畀佢。I give him my phone.
佢將張 SIM 咭插落部電話度。He puts the SIM card in the phone.
部電話而家用得喇。The phone is ready now.
「你嘅新號碼係十二、三十四、五十六、七十八，」佢話。"Your new number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," he says.
我個袋入面有一本筆記簿。I have a notebook in my bag.
我寫低個號碼。I write the number.
「十二、三十四、五十六、七十八，」我重複。"Twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I repeat.
「係，冇錯，」店員話。"Yes, exactly," says the clerk.
「唔該晒你幫手，」我話。"Thank you for the help," I say.
我而家有一個丹麥號碼喇！I have a Danish number now!
我將部電話放落口袋。I put the phone in my pocket.
queue ticket
Storyboard: The next morning, Maya enters the crowded public registration office. She presses a button on a red terminal, pulls a paper ticket, and checks her high number before finding a seat to wait.
依家係第二日朝早。It is the next morning.
我今日去咗嗰間好大嘅登記處。I am at the large registration office today.
呢度入面有好多人。There are many people in here.
我嘅護照同文件都喺個袋入面。I have my passport and my papers in my bag.
登記需要嘅嘢全部都準備好喇。Everything is ready for my registration.
我企喺一部紅色機旁邊。I stand by a red machine.
我撳咗個掣。I press a button.
部機出咗張細細張嘅號碼飛畀我。The machine gives me a small number slip.
我個號碼係幾多呢？What is my number?
我望吓張紙。I look at the paper.
我係五十九號。I have number fifty-nine.
呢個號碼都幾後吓。It is a rather high number.
牆上面有個大螢幕顯示緊一個號碼。A large screen on the wall shows a number.
個螢幕只係顯示緊二十一號。The screen only shows number twenty-one.
我仲有大把時間。I have plenty of time.
我搵咗張吉凳。I find an empty chair.
我拎住啲文件坐低。I sit down with my papers.
依家我就等輪到我。Now I wait for my turn.
registration
Storyboard: The screen chimes with her number, and Maya steps up to the counter. She volunteers her name and age to the clerk, hands over her passport, and he processes the paperwork for her private national ID number.
螢幕顯示我個號碼。A screen shows my number.
個號碼係五十九。The number is fifty-nine.
我行埋去櫃枱。I walk up to the counter.
有個男人坐喺櫃枱後面。A man sits behind the counter.
佢話：「早晨。」"Good morning," he says.
我答：「早晨。」"Good morning," I reply.
我話：「我叫瑪雅，今年三十一歲。」"My name is Maya, and I am thirty-one years old," I say.
佢話：「歡迎你，瑪雅。」"Welcome, Maya," he says.
佢問：「你有冇帶護照呀？」"Do you have your passport?" he asks.
我答：「有，我有帶護照同埋我啲文件。」"Yes, I have my passport and my papers," I answer.
我話：「畀你。」"Here you go," I say.
佢睇吓啲文件。He looks at the papers.
佢喺電腦度打字。He types on his computer.
我攞到一個丹麥嘅個人身分號碼。I get a Danish personal identification number.
個號碼係私人嘅，而且好重要。The number is private and very important.
佢畀一張新卡我。He gives me a new card.
佢話：「畀你，呢張係你嘅卡。」"Here you go, here is your card," he says.
「你而家已經喺丹麥登記好喇。」"Now you are registered in Denmark."
我微笑住接過張卡。I smile and take the card.
而家我係丹麥嘅一部分喇。Now I am a part of Denmark.
bakery celebration
Storyboard: Heading home from the registration office, Maya stops at a neighbourhood bakery to celebrate being officially registered at last. She points to a cinnamon pastry in the glass display, asks the price, and taps her card to pay.
我又出返去街上面。I am out on the street again.
所有嘢終於安頓好。Everything is finally in place.
係時候要有個小慶祝喇。It is time for a little celebration.
我見到街角有間細細嘅麵包舖。I see a little bakery on the corner.
我行入去。I go inside.
陣味好香。It smells lovely.
有個麵包師傅企喺櫃檯後面。A baker stands behind the counter.
「你好，」麵包師傅話。"Hello," the baker says.
「你好，」我答。"Hello," I reply.
我指住玻璃後面嘅一件糕點。I point to a pastry behind the glass.
「我想要一個肉桂卷，」我話。"I would like a cinnamon pastry," I say.
「好，當然可以，」佢話。"Yes, of course," he says.
「幾多錢？」我問。"What does it cost?" I ask.
「要二十五克朗，」麵包師傅答。"It costs twenty-five kroner," the baker replies.
我個銀包喺我個袋度。I have my wallet in my bag.
我攞我張扣帳卡出嚟。I take out my debit card.
我畀我個肉桂卷嘅錢。I pay for my cinnamon pastry.
麵包師傅畀咗個細袋我。The baker gives me a small bag.
而家我有個肉桂卷嚟做我嘅小慶祝。Now I have a cinnamon pastry for my little celebration.
swapping numbers
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her apartment building with her pastry bag and bumps into her neighbour Nina by the front door. They chat warmly and swap phone numbers so they can stay in touch; Nina reads out hers for Maya to save.
我企喺我間公寓出面。I am standing by my apartment.
我攞住一個裝住肉桂卷嘅袋。I have a bag with a cinnamon pastry.
我個鄰居 Nina 喺門口。My neighbour Nina is at the door.
佢笑得好燦爛咁同我講哈囉。She smiles broadly and says hello.
「哈囉 Maya，你而家有冇丹麥號碼呀？」Nina 問。"Hello Maya, do you have a Danish number now?" Nina asks.
「有呀，我有個新嘅電話號碼，」我答。"Yes, I have a new phone number," I reply.
「咁就好啦，」佢話。"That is good," she says.
我手上面攞住部電話。I have my phone in my hand.
「你個號碼係幾多呀？」我問。"What is your number?" I ask.
「我個號碼係八十七、六十五、四十三、二十一，」Nina 話。"My number is eighty-seven, sixty-five, forty-three, twenty-one," Nina says.
我喺部電話度輸入佢個號碼。I write her number on my phone.
「而我個號碼係十二、三十四、五十六、七十八，」我話。"And my number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
Nina 將我個號碼儲存喺佢部電話度。Nina saves my number on her phone.
而家我哋有大家嘅聯絡方法啦。Now we are in touch.
我哋一齊行入去。We go inside together.
我好開心識到呢個新朋友。I am very happy about my new friend.
