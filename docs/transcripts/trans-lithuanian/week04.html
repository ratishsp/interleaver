Week 4: Maya moves into her own flat

Lithuanian with English gloss, line-aligned.

final sweep
Storyboard: Maya stands in her temporary room in the building. She zips up her large suitcases and checks the space one last time, making sure nothing lies under the small bed or stands forgotten in the closet.
Kopenhagoje sausis.It is January in Copenhagen.
Stoviu savo laikiname kambaryje.I am standing in my temporary room.
Šiandien kraustausi į naują butą.Today I am moving to a new apartment.
Jis yra tame pačiame pastate.It is in the same building.
Mano didelis lagaminas stovi ant grindų.My large suitcase stands on the floor.
Uždarau jį.I close it.
Paskutinį kartą apsidairau po kambarį.I look around the room one last time.
Pažiūriu po maža lova.I look under the small bed.
Po lova guli knyga.There is a book lying under the bed.
Ji iš Meksikos.It is from Mexico.
Įsidedu knygą į krepšį.I put the book in my bag.
Atidarau spintą.I open the closet.
Spintoje stovi pora batų.There is a pair of shoes standing in the closet.
Paimu batus.I take the shoes.
Dabar spintoje nieko nėra.Now there is nothing in the closet.
Prieinu prie rašomojo stalo šalia lango.I walk over to the desk by the window.
Ant stalo guli laidas.There is a cable lying on the desk.
Paimu laidą.I take the cable.
Įsidedu laidą į krepšį.I put the cable in my bag.
Paimu savo du krepšius.I take my two bags.
Išjungiu šviesą ir uždarau duris.I turn off the light and close the door.
unlocking the door
Storyboard: Carrying her first few bags up the communal stairs, Maya arrives at the door of her new, permanent flat. She turns the key, pushes the door open, and steps inside, thrilled to finally see the small furnished space waiting for her.
Lipu laiptais.I walk up the stairs.
Rankose turiu du krepšius.I have two bags in my hands.
Pagaliau esu prie savo naujų durų.I am finally at my new door.
Kišenėje turiu raktą.I have a key in my pocket.
Padedu krepšius ant grindų.I put the bags down on the floor.
Išsitraukiu raktą.I take out the key.
Atrakinu duris.I unlock the door.
Pastumiu duris ir įeinu į vidų.I push the door open and go inside.
Butas yra mažas, bet jis tobulas.The apartment is small, but it is perfect.
Jame yra gražūs, šviesūs baldai.It has nice, light furniture.
Žiūriu į svetainę.I look into the living room.
Prie lango stovi sofa.There is a sofa standing by the window.
Po sofa yra kilimas.There is a rug under the sofa.
Ant sofos guli maža pagalvėlė.There is a small cushion lying on the sofa.
Kampe stovi lempa.There is a lamp standing in the corner.
Uždarau duris už savęs.I close the door behind me.
Padedu savo raktą ant stalo.I put my key on the table.
ninas help
Storyboard: Maya steps back out to the shared stairwell to grab her heaviest suitcase. Nina comes up the stairs, spots her struggling, and cheerfully helps carry the bag inside, setting it carefully on the floor by the large living room window.
Aš vėl išeinu į laiptinę.I walk out into the stairwell again.
Mano didžiausias lagaminas stovi ant grindų.My biggest suitcase stands on the floor.
Jis yra labai sunkus.It is very heavy.
Aš traukiu lagaminą.I pull on the suitcase.
Staiga išgirstu žingsnius ant laiptų.Suddenly I hear footsteps on the stairs.
Tai mano kaimynė danė, Nina.It is my Danish neighbor, Nina.
Ji man šiltai šypsosi.She smiles warmly at me.
„Ar šiandien įsikraustai, Maja?“ – klausia ji."Are you moving in today, Maya?" she asks.
„Taip, pagaliau esu savo naujame bute“, – sakau aš."Yes, I am finally in my new apartment," I say.
„Aš tau padėsiu“, – maloniai sako Nina."I will help you," Nina says kindly.
Ji suima lagaminą.She grabs hold of the suitcase.
Kartu nešame lagaminą į svetainę.Together we carry the suitcase into the living room.
Nina atsargiai pastato jį ant grindų.Nina sets it carefully on the floor.
Dabar lagaminas stovi prie didelio lango.Now the suitcase stands by the large window.
Po langu jau guli krepšys.There already lies a bag under the window.
„Labai ačiū už pagalbą, Nina“, – džiugiai sakau aš."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say happily.
„Nėra už ką“, – šypsosi ji."You are welcome," she smiles.
Nina išeina į laiptinę.Nina walks out into the stairwell.
Uždarau duris ir žiūriu į lagaminą prie lango.I close the door and look at the suitcase by the window.
kitchen unpacking
Storyboard: Maya opens a travel bag in the small kitchen and begins organizing. She stacks her favorite mugs in the cupboard and makes sure her red kettle stands on the counter, right by the sink.
Išeinu į mažą virtuvę.I walk out into the small kitchen.
Mano kelioninis krepšys stovi ant grindų.My travel bag stands on the floor.
Atidarau krepšį.I open the bag.
Krepšyje yra daug daiktų.There are many things in the bag.
Mano mėgstamiausi puodeliai guli krepšyje.My favorite mugs lie in the bag.
Išimu puodelius.I take the mugs out.
Virš stalviršio yra spintelė.There is a cupboard above the counter.
Sustatau puodelius į spintelę.I put the mugs in the cupboard.
Dabar jie stovi ant lentynos.Now they stand on the shelf.
Taip pat turiu raudoną elektrinį virdulį.I also have a red electric kettle.
Jis guli krepšyje po storu megztiniu.It lies in the bag under a thick sweater.
Išimu elektrinį virdulį.I take the electric kettle out.
Pastatau jį ant virtuvinio stalviršio.I put it on the kitchen counter.
Dabar raudonas elektrinis virdulys stovi prie pat kriauklės.Now there stands a red electric kettle right by the sink.
Krepšyje taip pat guli arbatos dėžutė.There also lies a box of tea in the bag.
Paimu dėžutę ir padedu ją prie elektrinio virdulio.I take the box and place it by the electric kettle.
Dabar virtuvė beveik paruošta.Now the kitchen is almost ready.
Nekantrauju vėliau išgerti puodelį arbatos.I look forward to a cup of tea later.
the workspace
Storyboard: Knowing she needs a good spot for her software engineering work, Maya sets up her equipment at the provided desk. Her laptop stands on the table with a warm lamp by it, while a bag of cables lies out of the way under the desk.
Einu į svetainę.I go into the living room.
Prie lango stovi mažas rašomasis stalas.There is a small desk by the window.
Stalas skirtas mano darbui.The table is for my work.
Čia galiu ramiai dirbti.Here I can work in peace.
Tai puiki maža vieta.It is a lovely little spot.
Atidarau savo krepšį.I open my bag.
Išimu savo kompiuterį.I take my computer out.
Dabar jis stovi ant stalo.It now sits on the table.
Ant stalo taip pat stovi lempa.There is also a lamp on the table.
Lempa stovi visai šalia kompiuterio.The lamp stands right by the computer.
Jos šviesa šilta.It has a warm light.
Ant stalo taip pat guli maža knyga.There is also a small book lying on the table.
Turiu mažą juodą krepšį su laidais.I have a small black bag with cables.
Jis guli po rašomuoju stalu.It lies under the desk.
Taip krepšys netrukdo.That way, the bag is not in the way.
Prie rašomojo stalo stovi gera kėdė.There is a good chair by the desk.
Sėdžiu kėdėje ir žiūriu.I sit in the chair and look.
Atidarau kompiuterį ir pradedu rašyti.I open the computer and begin to type.
bedroom comforts

the plant

celebratory pizza
