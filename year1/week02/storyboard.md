# Week 2 — Storyboard (First Day & Registration)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Numbers 0–100; *har*; *være* + age

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_commute | Maya walks briskly through the chilly morning air, looking for her new workplace. She checks the large numbers on the buildings along the street until she spots the software company's logo next to number forty-four. |
| 2 | 02_meeting_peter | Inside the bright office, a smiling colleague introduces himself as Peter. He shakes her hand, hands her a new laptop, and leads her through the open-plan workspace to her assigned desk. |
| 3 | 03_the_phone_shop | On her lunch break, Maya ducks into a telecom shop to buy a local SIM card. She hands the clerk her phone, and he activates it, reading her brand-new local number aloud as she saves it. |
| 4 | 04_queue_ticket | The next morning, Maya enters the crowded public registration office. She presses a button on a red terminal, pulls a paper ticket, and checks her high number before finding a seat to wait. |
| 5 | 05_registration | The screen chimes with her number, and Maya steps up to the counter. She volunteers her name and age to the clerk, hands over her passport, and he processes the paperwork for her private national ID number. |
| 6 | 06_bakery_celebration | Heading home from the registration office, Maya stops at a neighbourhood bakery to celebrate being officially registered at last. She points to a cinnamon pastry in the glass display, asks the price, and taps her card to pay. |
| 7 | 07_swapping_numbers | Maya arrives at her apartment building with her pastry bag and bumps into her neighbour Nina by the front door. They chat warmly and swap phone numbers so they can stay in touch; Nina reads out hers for Maya to save. |
