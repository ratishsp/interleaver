# Week 25 — Storyboard (A Sunday in the city)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Demonstratives *denne/dette/disse*; *for … siden* (ago); time adverbs

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_calendar | On Sunday morning, Maya makes a fresh pot of coffee and flips her wall calendar to July. She realizes that she moved to Copenhagen exactly six months ago, and decides to celebrate by spending this sunny day exploring the city at her own pace. |
| 2 | 02_ceramic_plates | Maya wanders into a lively second-hand market in a nearby square. She spots two blue ceramic plates on a table, decides these plates are perfect for her kitchen, and buys them from the vendor for forty kroner. |
| 3 | 03_winter_coat | At a clothing stall, Maya tries on a heavy wool winter coat. She immediately takes it off because this coat is far too warm for summer, laughing with the vendor that she desperately needed something exactly like it five months ago. |
| 4 | 04_museum_ticket | Moving on, Maya enters a local history museum and goes to the front desk. She asks the clerk if this entrance ticket includes the upstairs exhibition, but the clerk advises her to explore the ground-floor gallery first, explaining that a massive tourist group went upstairs ten minutes ago. |
| 5 | 05_harbor_photograph | Enjoying the quiet ground-floor gallery, Maya steps close to the wall to read the tiny text beneath a massive photograph. The caption explains that this harbor area was a loud industrial zone a hundred years ago, so she walks over to a nearby window to compare the historical image with the peaceful modern waterfront outside. |
| 6 | 06_texting_sofia | Taking a break in the museum café, Maya takes a picture of the plates she bought and texts it to her sister Sofía. Sofía replies immediately, sending back an old photo to remind Maya that they explored a similar flea market in Mexico exactly two years ago. |
| 7 | 07_seagull_encounter | Walking home through a large park, Maya stops at a kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream. As she strolls along the pond, a bold seagull suddenly swoops down, forcing her to quickly duck and shield this ice cream with her hand to prevent the bird from stealing it. |
| 8 | 08_unpacking_plates | Back in her apartment that evening, Maya washes these newly bought plates in the sink and places them in the cupboard. She looks out the window at the lingering summer sunset, entirely content with this quiet Sunday and deeply settled in the life she started half a year ago. |
