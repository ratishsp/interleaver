# Week 9 — Storyboard (Week 9 — Storyboard (Maya visits the bustling local market))

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Prices — **വില എത്ര? / എത്ര രൂപ ആയി?**; amounts as words; **തരൂ/വേണം** at the stall

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_arriving_at_the_market | Maya walks into the busy Ernakulam market on a warm morning. Vendors call out their fresh goods over the noise of the crowd, and she takes out her cloth shopping bags, ready to buy groceries and supplies for the week. |
| 2 | 02_weighing_the_vegetables | She stops at a large vegetable stall and asks the price of tomatoes per kilo. The vendor replies with the amount; she agrees and asks for two kilos, and he weighs them out on a metal scale. |
| 3 | 03_the_sweet_mango | At a nearby fruit stand, a woman sells fresh mangoes. The vendor cuts a small, bright slice and offers it to Maya to taste. It is perfectly sweet. The vendor names the price per kilo unprompted, and Maya happily asks her to pack one kilo of the fruit. |
| 4 | 04_too_expensive | Maya wanders past the food stalls and sees a merchant selling beautiful, handmade wooden kitchen ladles. She picks one up and asks the price. The vendor names a very high amount in rupees; Maya smiles, politely decides it is too expensive for now, and puts it back. |
| 5 | 05_price_on_the_board | She spots a wholesale banana stall with several varieties hanging in heavy bunches. A handwritten slate clearly shows the price of each size of bunch. Maya reads the board, tells the vendor she wants a small bunch, and hands him the exact amount in small notes without needing to ask the price. |
| 6 | 06_snack_stall_and_change | Maya stops at a brightly lit snack stall to buy treats for her colleagues at the software company. She asks for a packet of banana chips and a packet of jaggery-coated bananas, then asks what the total comes to. The vendor calculates the amount, takes her five-hundred rupee note, and carefully counts out her change. |
| 7 | 07_buying_spices | Remembering she needs spices for her cooking, Maya visits a fragrant, narrow shop packed with open sacks. She asks the merchant for a hundred grams of black pepper and cardamom; he weighs the spices, names the total, then waves a hand and rounds it down a little for her, handing over the neatly folded paper packets. |
| 8 | 08_heading_home_with_groceries | Her cloth bags are now completely full and heavy. Maya checks her watch, feels satisfied with her successful market trip, and walks out of the crowds to take an auto home with her heavy bags. |
