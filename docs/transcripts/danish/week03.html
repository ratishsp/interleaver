Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
Det er januar, og det er meget koldt.It is January, and it is very cold.
Jeg går hjem fra busstoppestedet i København.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Jeg ser en blomsterbutik på gaden.I see a flower shop on the street.
Der er lyst og varmt derinde.It is bright and warm inside.
Jeg går ind i den varme butik.I go into the warm shop.
Jeg kigger på en lille, grøn plante.I look at a small, green plant.
En ekspedient smiler til mig.A clerk smiles at me.
"Hej," siger han."Hello," he says.
"Hej," siger jeg."Hello," I say.
"Jeg leder efter en plante.""I am looking for a plant."
"Er det en gave?" spørger han."Is it a gift?" he asks.
"Nej, det er til mit værelse," svarer jeg."No, it is for my room," I answer.
"Det er en rigtig god plante," siger han."It is a really good plant," he says.
"Hvad koster den?" spørger jeg."How much does it cost?" I ask.
"Den koster femoghalvtreds kroner," svarer han."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
Jeg betaler for min nye plante.I pay for my new plant.
Han lægger planten ned i en papirpose.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
"Værsgo, her er din pose," siger han."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
"Mange tak," siger jeg glad."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Jeg går ud i den kolde aften.I go out into the cold evening.
Min lille plante gør mig meget glad.My little plant makes me very happy.

parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.

Der er sol, og stuen er meget lys.There is sun, and the living room is very bright.

"Hej, Maya," siger min far."Hello, Maya," my father says.

Det er dejligt at se dem alle.It is lovely to see them all.
