Week 8: Maya explores the city

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
今日係星期六朝早。It is Saturday morning.
太陽照住成個城市。The sun is shining over the city.
我行落街。I go down to the street.
我鄰居 Nina 企喺我哋大廈外面。My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
「早晨，Maya，」佢笑住講。"Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
「早晨，Nina，」我答。"Good morning, Nina," I answer.
「我今日放假，」我同佢講。"I have the day off today," I tell her.
「我準備出市區。」"I am heading out into the city."
「你去邊呀？」Nina 問。"Where are you going?" Nina asks.
「我行落去海邊，」我講。"I am walking down to the water," I say.
「但我唔識路。」"But I do not know the way."
Nina 指住前面條街。Nina points down the street.
「好彩，好容易去，」佢話。"Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
「喺呢度直行。」"Go straight ahead here."
「去到公園就轉左。」"And turn left at the park."
「咁你就會直接落到海港。」"Then you come right down to the harbour."
「多謝你幫手呀，Nina，」我講。"Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
「唔使客氣，玩得開心啲啦，」佢答。"You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
我同佢揮手。I wave to her.
我開始向住海邊行去。I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
我跟住Nina嘅路線。I follow Nina's route.
空氣又清新又凍。The air is fresh and cold.
依家我沿住大街行。Now I walk down the main street.
首先我一直向前行。First I just walk straight ahead.
街上面有好多車。There is a lot of traffic on the street.
途中我見到三架黃色單車。On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
啲單車好快咁喺我身邊踩過。The bicycles ride quickly past me.
之後我繼續向前行。After that I walk further ahead.
跟住我嚟到一個大公園。Then I come to a large park.
依家我醒起Nina講過嘅說話。Now I remember Nina's words.
佢話：「喺公園轉左。」"Turn left at the park," she says.
我喺度轉左。Here I turn left.
冇幾耐我見到有道門開住。A little later I see an open door.
係一間細麵包舖。It is a small bakery.
有陣好香嘅新鮮咖啡味。There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
我好鍾意熱辣辣嘅麵包。I love warm bread.
今日個城市好靚。Today the city is lovely.
之後我繼續向海邊行。Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
我嚟到一個大廣場。I arrive at a large square.
嗰度有好多人。There are many people.
但係我見唔到啲水。But I do not see the water.
我搵緊個海港。I am looking for the harbour.
我見到行人路上面有個老伯。I see an older man on the pavement.
我截停佢。I stop him.
「唔好意思，」我話。"Excuse me," I say.
「請問海港喺邊度？」"Where is the harbour?"
個老伯點點頭。The older man nods.
佢指住前面。He points ahead.
「直行穿過個廣場，」佢話。"Walk straight across the square," he says.
「行過間博物館。」"Go past the museum."
「然後轉右。」"And turn right."
我望過去廣場對面。I look across the square.
我見到一棟好大嘅建築物。I see a large building.
嗰間就係博物館。It is the museum.
「多謝晒，」我話。"Thank you very much," I say.
「唔使客氣，」佢答。"You are welcome," he replies.
我繼續行。I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
我穿過嗰個大廣場。I walk across the large square.
突然間，我聽到音樂。Suddenly, I hear music.
街角處企住一個街頭音樂人。On the corner stands a street musician.
佢喺度彈結他。He plays the guitar.
我聽住呢啲好聽嘅音樂。I listen to the good music.
我望住佢望咗好耐。I look at him for a long time.
然後我繼續行。Then I walk on.
我唔記得咗間博物館。I forget the museum.
我亦都唔記得咗我嘅路線。I also forget my route.
我反而行咗入一條好窄嘅橫街。Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
呢度好靜。It is very quiet here.
四圍我都只係見到一啲細屋。All around I only see some small houses.
然後我停低。Then I stop.
間博物館喺邊度？Where is the museum?
依家我四圍望下。Now I look around.
我已經見唔到佢喇。I cannot see it anymore.
我好快咁回頭望返條街。I quickly look back up the street.
我亦都見唔到個廣場。I cannot see the square either.
我完全迷路喇。I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
我企喺一條寧靜嘅街度。I am standing on a quiet street.
我迷路喇。I am lost.
我喺前面見到一個年輕女士。Ahead of me I see a young woman.
佢放緊一隻狗仔。She is walking a small dog.
隻狗望落好似我屋企隻狗 Lola。The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
我行過去嗰位女士度。I walk over to the woman.
我話：「唔好意思。」"Excuse me," I say.
「我搵緊海港。」"I am looking for the harbour."
個女士停低聽我講。The woman stops and listens.
佢向住條街嘅後面指。She points back up the street.
佢話：「直行返去廣場。」"Go straight back to the square," she says.
「然後喺博物館轉右。」"And turn right at the museum."
我話：「非常多謝你幫手。」"Thank you very much for the help," I say.
個女士答：「唔使客氣。」"You are welcome," the woman replies.
我低頭望住隻狗仔。I look down at the little dog.
佢望住我。It looks at me.
我行返去廣場嗰邊。I walk back toward the square.
依家我知道自己喺邊喇。Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
我沿住嗰條寧靜嘅街道行返轉頭。I walk back along the quiet street.
我一直向前行。I walk straight ahead.
好快我又見返嗰個大廣場。Soon I see the large square again.
然後我穿過廣場。Then I walk across the square.
而家我企喺博物館旁邊。Now I am standing by the museum.
我記得嗰個女人講嘅話：「轉右。」I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
喺博物館嗰度，我轉右。At the museum I turn right.
我沿住一條寬闊嘅街道行。I walk down a wide street.
我望向前面。I look ahead.
終於我見到啲藍色嘅嘢。Finally I see something blue.
係水呀！It is the water!
途中，我行過一間細嘅咖啡店。On the way I walk past a little café.
最後，我一直行落去水邊。At last I walk all the way down to the water.
而家我喺海港。Now I am at the harbour.
個海港好大。The harbour is very large.
啲風好凍，好清新。The wind is cold and fresh.
水面上有好多船。There are many boats on the water.
我喺呢度停低。Here I stop.
我企喺度望住啲船。I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
我沿住海港一直向前行。I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
我行過啲小船。I walk past the small boats.
突然間我見到好多人。Suddenly I see many people.
佢哋企喺水邊。They stand down by the water.
我行過去。I walk over there.
終於，我見到佢喇。Finally, I see it.
小美人魚坐喺水入面嘅一塊石頭上面。The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
佢係哥本哈根一個好出名嘅雕像。She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.
個雕像其實唔係好大。The figure is actually not very big.
但係個銅像喺陽光底下好靚。But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
我企喺其他遊人中間。I stand among the other visitors.
好多遊客幫個雕像影相。Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
我都攞部電話出嚟。I also take out my phone.
我幫美人魚影咗張好靚嘅相。I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
我將張相傳畀喺墨西哥嘅屋企人。I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
我阿妹索菲亞好快就覆我。My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
「佢好靚，」佢打字話。"She is very pretty," she writes.
我再次望向水面。I look at the water again.
walking home
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.
依家係傍晚。It is late in the afternoon.
依家我行返去我嘅公寓。Now I walk home to my apartment.
我行一條好易行嘅路。I walk an easy way.
我淨係沿住條長路行。I just follow the long road.
條路係完全直嘅。The road is completely straight.
我沿住海傍行。I walk along the water.
最後我轉彎離開海傍。Finally I turn away from the water.
依家我又行返入市區。Now I walk into the city again.
然後我行過嗰間麵包舖。Then I walk past the bakery.
嗰度仲聞到咖啡嘅香味。It still smells of coffee.
過咗一陣，我見到個公園。A little later I see the park.
喺公園嗰度我轉右。At the park I turn right.
我冇問任何人點樣行。I do not ask anyone for directions.
我完全靠自己搵到條路。I find the way all by myself.
過咗一陣，我見到自己住嗰條街。A little later I see my own street.
最後，我企喺自己嘅大門口。Finally I stand at my front door.
我由大門口望返落去條街度。From the door I look back down the street.
依家我認得返屋企條路喇。Now I know my way home.
