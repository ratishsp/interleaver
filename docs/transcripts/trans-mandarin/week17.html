sunday train trip
Storyboard: She writes about her Saturday morning excursion. She packed a backpack, bought a warm coffee, and took the fast train out of Copenhagen to visit the city of Odense.
我在日记里写了去欧登塞的旅行。I wrote in my diary about my trip to Odense.
那是一个美好的星期六早晨。It was a lovely Saturday morning.
我七点钟就早早地醒了。I woke up early at seven o'clock.
阳光明媚，天空蔚蓝。The sun was shining, and the sky was blue.
我收拾好我的绿色小背包。I packed my small, green backpack.
我在包里放了一本笔记本和一台相机。I put a notebook and a camera in the bag.

丹麦春天的天气可能会有点冷。The spring weather in Denmark can be a bit cold.
然后我坐公交车去了哥本哈根的大车站。Then I took the bus into the large station in Copenhagen.
里面有很多忙碌的人。There were many busy people inside.
我走向一个小售货亭。I walked over to a small kiosk.

我抬头看了看大屏幕。I looked up at the large screen.
我很快在屏幕上找到了我的火车。I quickly found my train on the screen.
我要乘快车去欧登塞。I was taking the fast train to Odense.

我上了火车，在一扇大窗户旁找到了我的座位。I went into the train and found my seat by a large window.
我喝着热咖啡，期待着这次出游。I drank my warm coffee and looked forward to the excursion.
long sea bridge
Storyboard: She describes the spectacular journey as the train crossed the very long bridge over the sea. She looked out the large window and admired the blue water and the small ships.
火车在风景中飞速穿行。The train drove quickly through the landscape.
我安静地坐在座位上。I sat quietly in my seat.
我有一个非常好的靠窗座位。I had a really good window seat.
过了一会儿，我们终于到达了海岸。After a little while, we finally reached the coast.
火车驶上了一座很长的跨海大桥。The train drove out onto a very long bridge over the sea.
突然间，我们周围全都是水。Suddenly there was only water all around us.
桥高高地悬在水面上。The bridge lay high above the water.
我向大窗外望去。I looked out of the large window.
我们下方的水非常蓝。The water below us was very blue.
我欣赏着这美丽而开阔的景色。I admired the beautiful, open view.
在远远的下方，我看到了几艘小船。Far below, I saw some small ships.

感觉简直就像在海面上飞行。It was almost like flying over the sea.

stressed father train

他带着他的两个小孩子。He brought his two small children.
这位父亲有一本大书。The father had a big book.
他很想看他的书。He really wanted to read his book.

他的儿子有一辆绿色的玩具车。His son had a green toy car.

crooked colorful houses
Storyboard: Maya arrived in Odense and walked straight into the old quarter. She loved the historic atmosphere and took pictures of the small, crooked houses painted in bright yellow and red.

我离开车站，径直朝老城区走去。I left the station and walked directly toward the old quarter.
阳光明媚，我享受着春天清新的空气。The sun was shining, and I enjoyed the fresh spring air.

每栋房子都有属于自己的欢快色彩。Each house had its own cheerful colour.

之后，我沿着街道慢慢地继续往下走。After that, I slowly walked further down the street.

fairy tale museum

river boat trip

我在小船里向后靠去。I leaned back in the little boat.
