Week 26: Maya's first Danish summer and thirty-second birthday

birthday messages
Storyboard: Maya wakes up on the morning she turns thirty-two, feeling a bit nostalgic as it is her first birthday away from Mexico. She smiles as she reads the flood of loving messages that her family has sent overnight while she slept.

Leo kwa kweli ni siku yangu ya kuzaliwa.Today is actually my birthday.
Ninatimiza miaka thelathini na miwili leo.I turn thirty-two years old today.
Ni siku yangu ya kuzaliwa ya kwanza nikiwa mbali na familia yangu.It is my first birthday away from my family.
Nilihisi hamu ya nyumbani kidogo kuhusu hilo.I felt a little nostalgic about it.
Lakini kisha nikachukua simu yangu kutoka kwenye meza ya kando ya kitanda.But then I picked up my phone from the nightstand.
Familia yangu imetuma jumbe nyingi za upendo.My family has sent many loving messages.
Waliziandika jana usiku nilipokuwa nimelala.They wrote them last night while I slept.

Dada yangu Sofía ameandika ujumbe mrefu na mtamu.My sister Sofía has written a long and sweet text.
Na kaka yangu Diego alituma picha ya keki niipendayo zaidi.And my brother Diego sent a picture of my favorite cake.
Nilitabasamu sana nilipoona picha hiyo.I smiled broadly when I saw the picture.
Hawajasahau kabisa siku yangu kubwa.They have not forgotten my big day at all.
Nilijibu jumbe zote mara moja.I replied to all the messages immediately.
Niliwaandikia kwamba ninawapenda na kuwakumbuka.I wrote to them that I love and miss them.
Ninasoma jumbe hizo tena na tena.I read the messages again and again.
Ni mwanzo mzuri sana wa siku yangu ya kuzaliwa.It is a fantastic start to my birthday.
office pastry

Tunakula keki pamoja, na ni mwanzo mzuri sana wa siku yangu.We eat the cake together, and it is a fantastic start to my day.
family call

"Umepokea kifurushi chetu?""Have you received our package?"
"Ndiyo, kilifika kwa njia ya posta jana," ninajibu."Yes, it arrived in the mail yesterday," I answer.

"Ndiyo, wafanyakazi wenzangu walininunulia keki mapema," ninaeleza."Yes, my colleagues bought a cake for me earlier," I explain.

"Lola pia amekula keki kidogo," anacheka."Lola has also eaten a little cake," he laughs.
Ninamchekea kwa sauti yule mbwa mdogo.I laugh loudly at the little dog.
Ni siku yangu ya kwanza ya kuzaliwa nikiwa mbali na nyumbani, lakini ninawahisi wako karibu sana.It is my first birthday away from home, but they feel very close.
cafe summer

Leo usiku kuna moto mkubwa kule chini karibu na bahari.Tonight there is a big bonfire down by the sea.

Ninatabasamu na kuchukua mkoba wangu.I smile and take my bag.

bus stop contrast

beach bonfire
