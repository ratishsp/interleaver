Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Norwegian with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
Flyet er i København nå.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
Det er januar.It is January.
Jeg er veldig spent.I am very excited.
Jeg pakker boken min ned i vesken min.I pack my book into my bag.
Det er en passasjer ved siden av meg.There is a passenger next to me.
Han ser på meg og smiler.He looks at me and smiles.
"Hei," sier han."Hello," he says.
"Hei," sier jeg."Hello," I say.
"Hva heter du?" spør han."What is your name?" he asks.
"Jeg heter Maya," sier jeg."My name is Maya," I say.
"Hvor kommer du fra, Maya?" spør han."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
Jeg smiler glad.I smile happily.
"Jeg kommer fra Mexico," svarer jeg."I come from Mexico," I answer.
"Er du fra Danmark?" spør jeg."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
"Ja, jeg er fra Danmark," sier han."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
"Velkommen til Danmark," sier han."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
"Tusen takk," sier jeg."Thank you very much," I say.
Nå er vi her.Now we are here.
Jeg er klar.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
Jeg er utenfor nå.I am outside now.
Kofferten min er stor og tung.My suitcase is big and heavy.
Der er en taxi.There is a taxi.
Jeg løfter kofferten min inn i taxien.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
Sjåføren er en vennlig mann.The driver is a friendly man.
"Hva er adressen?" spør han."What is the address?" he asks.
"Det er Parkvej nummer tolv," sier jeg."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
Han nikker og kjører.He nods and drives.
"Er du turist?" spør sjåføren."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
"Nei, jeg er ikke turist," svarer jeg."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
"Hvor kommer du fra?" spør han."Where do you come from?" he asks.
"Jeg kommer fra Mexico," sier jeg."I come from Mexico," I say.
Jeg ser ut av vinduet.I look out the window.
Jeg er veldig stolt.I am very proud.
"Jeg er programvareingeniør," sier jeg."I am a software engineer," I say.
"Min nye jobb er her i København.""My new job is here in Copenhagen."
Han smiler.He smiles.
"Så spennende.""How exciting."
"Lykke til i Danmark," sier han."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
"Tusen takk," sier jeg."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
Døren er åpen.The door is open.
Dørterskelen er høy.The threshold is high.
Kofferten er veldig tung!The suitcase is very heavy!
Men nå er kofferten i gangen.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
Det er helt mørkt her.It is completely dark here.
Hvor er bryteren?Where is the switch?
Veggen er kald.The wall is cold.
Er bryteren her?Is the switch here?
Ja, der er bryteren!Yes, there is the switch!
Nå er det lys.Now there is light.
Jeg er i leiligheten.I am in the apartment.
Den er liten, men den er fin.It is small, but it is nice.
Her er en stue.Here is a living room.
Det er en sofa og et bord.There is a sofa and a table.
Det er et møblert hjem.It is a furnished home.
Kofferten er på gulvet.The suitcase is on the floor.
Jeg er veldig trøtt.I am very tired.
Men jeg er også glad.But I am also happy.
Jeg er i København!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
Jeg står ved vinduet.I am standing by the window.
Glasset er kaldt.The glass is cold.
Det er januar.It is January.
Jeg kikker ut.I look out.
København er grå i dag.Copenhagen is grey today.
Det er kaldt ute.It is cold outside.
Det er snø på gaten.There is snow on the street.
Men her er det varmt.But it is warm here.
Leiligheten er bra.The apartment is good.
Jeg tenker på Mexico.I think about Mexico.
I Mexico er det sol.In Mexico, there is sun.
Det er også varmt der.It is also warm there.
Jeg savner solen litt.I miss the sun a little.
Men jeg smiler.But I smile.
Det er en ny by.It is a new city.
Jeg er i Danmark nå.I am in Denmark now.
Det er en god start.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
Jeg er i stuen nå.I am in the living room now.
Det er en liten sofa.There is a small sofa.
Jeg ringer til min mor i Mexico.I call my mother in Mexico.
"Hei mor," sier jeg."Hi Mom," I say.
"Hei Maya," sier min mor."Hi Maya," my mother says.
"Hvor er du?" spør hun."Where are you?" she asks.
"Jeg er i København," svarer jeg."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
"Jeg er i leiligheten nå.""I am in the apartment now."
"Det er så bra," sier min mor."That is so good," my mother says.
"Er den stor?" spør hun."Is it big?" she asks.
"Nei, den er liten," svarer jeg."No, it is small," I answer.
"Men den er koselig, og jeg er glad.""But it is cozy, and I am happy."
"Er det kaldt?" spør hun."Is it cold?" she asks.
"Ja, det er kaldt ute," sier jeg."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
"Men det er varmt her inne.""But it is warm in here."
"Jeg er trygg.""I am safe."
"Det er bra," sier min mor."That is good," my mother says.
Jeg smiler.I smile.
Danmark er mitt nye hjem.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
Jeg åpner døren min.I open my door.
Jeg står i oppgangen.I am standing in the stairwell.
Det er stille.It is quiet.
En dør åpnes.A door opens.
En kvinne smiler til meg.A woman smiles at me.
"Hei," sier hun."Hi," she says.
"Hva heter du?" spør hun."What is your name?" she asks.
"Jeg heter Maya," svarer jeg."My name is Maya," I answer.
"Hvor kommer du fra?" spør hun."Where do you come from?" she asks.
"Jeg kommer fra Mexico," sier jeg."I come from Mexico," I say.
"Velkommen til bygningen," sier kvinnen."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
"Jeg heter Nina," sier hun."My name is Nina," she says.
"Jeg er naboen din.""I am your neighbour."
Nina er veldig vennlig.Nina is very friendly.
Hun snakker sakte og tydelig.She speaks slowly and clearly.
"Her er døren min," sier hun."Here is my door," she says.
"Jeg hjelper gjerne.""I am happy to help."
"Du banker bare på," sier Nina."You just knock," Nina says.
"Tusen takk, Nina," svarer jeg."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
Jeg er veldig glad.I am very happy.
Nå har jeg en venn i København.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
Nå er det natt.Now it is night.
Det er min første natt i København.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
Jeg er på badet.I am in the bathroom.
Det er veldig lite.It is very small.
Det er en vask.There is a sink.
Tannbørsten min er rød.My toothbrush is red.
Jeg tar tannkrem på tannbørsten min.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
Jeg pusser tennene mine.I brush my teeth.
Vannet er kaldt.The water is cold.
Jeg vasker ansiktet mitt.I wash my face.
Jeg tørker ansiktet mitt.I dry my face.
Jeg går bort til sengen min.I go over to my bed.
Sengen er smal.The bed is narrow.
Men den er varm og myk.But it is warm and soft.
Jeg ligger under dynen nå.I am lying under the duvet now.
Telefonen min ligger på bordet.My phone is lying on the table.
Jeg setter alarmen min.I set my alarm.
Den ringer klokken åtte i morgen.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
Jeg er trøtt, men jeg er klar.I am tired, but I am ready.
God natt, København.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
