# Week 13 — Storyboard (Maya goes to a café)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Modal *vil gerne*; politeness; *må jeg bede om*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_escaping_the_wind | Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft. |
| 2 | 02_finding_a_seat | She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders. |
| 3 | 03_the_pastry_case | Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll. |
| 4 | 04_ordering_at_the_counter | Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants. |
| 5 | 05_carrying_the_tray | The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window. |
| 6 | 06_observing_the_room | Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw. |
| 7 | 07_one_more_thing | Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water. |
| 8 | 08_asking_for_the_bill | Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab. |
| 9 | 09_paying_and_leaving | Maya taps her card on the terminal to pay eighty-five kroner. She puts on her heavy coat, thanks the staff for a nice time, and pushes the front door open to face the cold again. |
