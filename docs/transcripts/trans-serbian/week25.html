Week 25: A Sunday in the city

Serbian with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning calendar
Storyboard: On Sunday morning, Maya makes a fresh pot of coffee and flips her wall calendar to July. She realizes that she moved to Copenhagen exactly six months ago, and decides to celebrate by spending this sunny day exploring the city at her own pace.
Nedeljno je jutro.It is Sunday morning.
Sunce sija kroz prozor u mom stanu.The sun is shining in through the window in my apartment.
Odlazim u kuhinju.I go out into the kitchen.
Pravim bokal sveže kafe.I make a pot of fresh coffee.
Miris kafe je fantastičan ovog jutra.The smell of coffee is fantastic this morning.
Imam kalendar na zidu.I have a calendar on the wall.
Okrećem stranicu na sledeći mesec.I turn the page to the next month.
Sada je jul.It is July now.
Preselila sam se u Kopenhagen u januaru.I moved to Copenhagen in January.
Došla sam ovde pre tačno šest meseci.I came here exactly six months ago.
Vreme zaista brzo prolazi.Time really goes fast.
Moj prvi dan u ovom gradu bio je veoma hladan.My first day in this city was very cold.
Ali danas je vreme toplo i prelepo.But today the weather is warm and beautiful.
Želim da proslavim ovu malu godišnjicu.I want to celebrate this little anniversary.
Danas nemam određene planove.I have no fixed plans today.
Uzimam svoju šolju za kafu i gledam na ulicu.I take my coffee cup and look out over the street.
Želim da izađem i istražim grad svojim tempom.I want to go out and explore the city at my own pace.
Radujem se što ću uživati u ovim svetlim satima.I look forward to enjoying these bright hours.
ceramic plates
Storyboard: Maya wanders into a lively second-hand market in a nearby square. She spots two blue ceramic plates on a table, decides these plates are perfect for her kitchen, and buys them from the vendor for forty kroner.
Idem u šetnju preko velikog trga u gradu.I go for a walk across a large square in the city.
Ovde se svake nedelje održava veliki buvljak.Here there is a large flea market every Sunday.
Sunce sija, a mnogo ljudi šeta između stolova.The sun is shining, and many people are walking around between the tables.
Zaustavljam se pored malog stola sa mnogo različitih stvari.I stop by a small table with many different things.
Iza stola stoji ljubazan prodavac.Behind the table stands a friendly vendor.
Odjednom vidim dva plava keramička tanjira.Suddenly I see two blue ceramic plates.
Uzimam ih i pažljivo ih posmatram.I pick them up and look closely at them.
„Koliko koštaju ovi tanjiri?” pitam prodavca."How much do these plates cost?" I ask the vendor.
Prodavac mi se smeši.The vendor smiles at me.
„Koštaju samo četrdeset kruna”, odgovara on."They cost only forty kroner," he replies.
To je zaista dobra cena.That is a really good price.
Kupila sam mali sto za svoju kuhinju pre mesec dana.I bought a small table for my kitchen a month ago.
Ovi tanjiri će se savršeno slagati uz ovaj sto.These plates will fit perfectly with this table.
„Veoma bih volela da ih kupim”, kažem."I would very much like to buy them," I say.
Dajem prodavcu četrdeset kruna i pažljivo stavljam tanjire u torbu.I give the vendor forty kroner and carefully put the plates in my bag.
Uvek je lepo pronaći nešto posebno tokom mirne nedelje.It is always nice to find something special on a quiet Sunday.
winter coat
Storyboard: At a clothing stall, Maya tries on a heavy wool winter coat. She immediately takes it off because this coat is far too warm for summer, laughing with the vendor that she desperately needed something exactly like it five months ago.
Nastavljam dalje kroz pijacu i zaustavljam se kod tezge sa odećom.I walk further into the market and stop at a clothing stall.
Vidim teški zimski kaput od vune.I see a heavy winter coat made of wool.
Ovaj kaput je prelep, ali veoma debeo.This coat is beautiful, but very thick.
Ipak isprobavam kaput.I try the coat on anyway.
Neverovatno je topao.It is incredibly warm.
Odmah ga ponovo skidam.I take it off again immediately.
Jul je i sunce danas sija.It is July, and the sun is shining today.
Ljubazna žena stoji iza stola i smeši se.A friendly woman stands behind the table and smiles.
„Da li je previše topao?“ pita žena."Is it too warm?" the woman asks.
„Da, malo je pretopao za leto“, odgovaram i smejem se."Yes, it is a bit too warm for the summer," I reply and laugh.
Dodirujem debelu tkaninu.I touch the thick fabric.
„Ali ovaj zimski kaput mi je zaista trebao pre pet meseci!“ kažem."But I really needed this winter coat five months ago!" I say.
Žena se glasno smeje.The woman laughs loudly.
„Da, februar je bio veoma hladan“, kaže ona."Yes, February was very cold," she says.
Sećam se svojih prvih nedelja u Kopenhagenu.I remember my first weeks in Copenhagen.
Tada sam se stalno smrzavala.I was constantly freezing back then.
Srećom, trenutno mi ne treba tako topla odeća.Fortunately, I do not need such warm clothes right now.
Kačim kaput nazad na stalak i govorim doviđenja.I hang the coat back on the rack and say goodbye.
museum ticket
Storyboard: Moving on, Maya enters a local history museum and goes to the front desk. She asks the clerk if this entrance ticket includes the special upstairs exhibition, and the clerk tells her to hurry because the guided tour started ten minutes ago.
Udaljavam se od pijace i pronalazim mali, lokalni muzej.I walk away from the market and find a small, local museum.
Ulazim u tihu zgradu.I walk into the quiet building.
Prilazim pultu da kupim ulaznicu.I walk over to the counter to buy an entrance ticket.
Iza kase stoji jedan mladić.A young man is standing behind the register.
„Zdravo, jednu ulaznicu za odrasle, molim vas”, kažem."Hi, one adult ticket, please," I say.
„To će biti sto kruna”, kaže on i štampa je."That will be one hundred kroner," he says and prints it.
Plaćam i gledam ove šarene brošure na pultu.I pay and look at these colorful brochures on the counter.
One reklamiraju posebnu izložbu o istoriji grada.They advertise a special exhibition about the history of the city.
„Da li ova ulaznica takođe omogućava pristup izložbi na spratu?”, pitam."Does this ticket also give access to the exhibition upstairs?" I ask.
„Da, ovaj muzej ima samo jednu kombinovanu ulaznicu”, odgovara on."Yes, this museum only has one combined ticket," he answers.
„Izložba je gore, na prvom spratu”, kaže on."The exhibition is upstairs on the first floor," he says.
„Možete tamo gore da odvojite vremena koliko vam treba.”"You can take your time up there."
„Tamo ima mnogo starih fotografija grada”, objašnjava on."It has many old photographs of the city," he explains.
„Neke od njih su stare preko sto godina.”"Some of them are over a hundred years old."
„To zvuči zaista uzbudljivo”, kažem."That sounds really exciting," I say.
„Hvala vam puno”, osmehujem se."Thank you very much," I smile.
Uzimam svoju ulaznicu i mirno se penjem uz stepenice.I take my ticket and walk calmly up the stairs.
harbor photograph
Storyboard: Upstairs in the gallery, Maya studies a massive black-and-white photograph of ships and factories. The caption explains that this harbor area was a loud, dirty industrial zone a hundred years ago, contrasting sharply with the modern waterfront she knows.
Idem gore na izložbu.I walk upstairs to the exhibition.
Ovde ima mnogo starih slika.There are many old pictures here.
Zaustavljam se ispred veoma velike crno-bele fotografije.I stop in front of a very large black-and-white photograph.
Ova slika je neverovatno fascinantna.This picture is incredibly fascinating.
Prikazuje prometnu luku sa velikim fabrikama.It shows a busy harbor with large factories.
Takođe mogu da vidim mnogo visokih dimnjaka i tamnih brodova.I can also see many tall chimneys and dark ships.
Ovi brodovi izgledaju veoma teški.These ships look very heavy.
Čitam mali tekst pored fotografije.I read the small text next to the photograph.
Tekst objašnjava istoriju ove luke.The text explains the history of this harbor.
Pre sto godina, ovo područje je bilo industrijska zona.A hundred years ago, the area was an industrial zone.
Tada je to bilo bučno i prljavo mesto.It was a loud and dirty place back then.
Kontrast u odnosu na danas je ogroman.The contrast to today is enormous.
Sada je ova obala tiha i čista.Now, this waterfront is quiet and clean.
Često idem u šetnju uz vodu vikendom.I often go for a walk along the water on the weekends.
Teško je zamisliti stare fabrike ovde.It is hard to imagine the old factories here.
Dugo gledam ovu fotografiju.I look at this photograph for a long time.
Zatim polako idem dalje u sledeću sobu.Then I slowly walk on into the next room.
texting sofia
Storyboard: Taking a break in the museum café, Maya takes a picture of the plates she bought and texts it to her sister Sofía. Sofía replies immediately, sending back an old photo to remind Maya that they explored a similar flea market in Mexico exactly two years ago.
Sedim u muzejskom kafiću i pravim pauzu.I am sitting in the museum's café and taking a break.
Pijem šolju kafe i opuštam se.I drink a cup of coffee and relax.
Vadim plave tanjire iz torbe.I take the blue plates out of my bag.
Ovi tanjiri se savršeno uklapaju u moju kuhinju.These plates fit perfectly in my kitchen.
Slikam ih telefonom.I take a picture of them with my phone.
Šaljem sliku svojoj sestri, Sofiji.I send the picture to my sister, Sofía.
"Samo pogledaj ove prelepe tanjire", pišem."Just look at these beautiful plates," I write.
"Našla sam ih na pijaci pre sat vremena.""I found them at a market an hour ago."
Sofija mi odgovara skoro odmah.Sofía answers me almost immediately.
Šalje mi nazad staru sliku.She sends an old picture back to me.
Ova poruka me čini veoma srećnom.This message makes me very happy.
Slika prikazuje nas dve na velikom buvljaku u Meksiku.The picture shows the two of us at a large flea market in Mexico.
"Da li se sećaš ove pijace?""Do you remember this market?"
Sofija pita u sledećoj poruci.Sofía asks in the next message.
"Bile smo tamo pre tačno dve godine!", piše ona."We were there exactly two years ago!" she writes.
Gledam naša srećna lica na slici.I look at our happy faces in the picture.
Čini se da je to bilo veoma davno.It feels like a very long time ago.
Pre dve godine, još uvek sam živela u Meksiku.Two years ago, I still lived in Mexico.
Smešim se i pišem srećnu poruku nazad.I smile and write a happy message back.
seagull encounter
Storyboard: Walking home through a large park, Maya stops at a kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream. As she strolls along the pond, a bold seagull suddenly swoops down, forcing her to quickly duck and shield this dessert with her hand to prevent the bird from stealing it.
Idem kući kroz veliki park.I walk home through a large park.
Pre sat vremena sam bila u muzeju.An hour ago I was at a museum.
Sada samo želim da uživam u lepom vremenu.Now I just want to enjoy the good weather.
Zaustavljam se kod malog kioska da kupim sladoled u kornetu.I stop at a small kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream.
"Izvolite, evo vašeg sladoleda", kaže čovek u kiosku."Here you go, here is your ice cream," the man in the kiosk says.
"Mnogo hvala", odgovaram i uzimam sladoled."Thank you very much," I reply and take the ice cream.
Hodam polako duž jezera u parku.I walk slowly along the lake in the park.
Ovaj sladoled u kornetu izgleda zaista dobro.This waffle ice cream looks really good.
Odjednom čujem glasan zvuk iznad sebe.Suddenly I hear a loud noise above me.
Veliki galeb se obrušava pravo ka meni.A large seagull dives right down towards me.
Hoće da mi ukrade sladoled!It wants to steal my ice cream!
Brzo se saginjem i štitim sladoled rukom.I duck quickly and shield my ice cream with my hand.
Ptica mi neće ukrasti hranu.The bird is not going to steal my food.
Galeb proleće i sleće na travu.The seagull flies past and lands on the grass.
Žena na klupi se smeje.A woman on a bench laughs.
"Baš su bezobrazni danas", kaže ona."They are very cheeky today," she says.
"Da, i te kako jesu", uzvraćam kroz smeh."Yes, they certainly are," I laugh back.
Brzo dovršavam sladoled dok hodam dalje.I quickly finish eating my ice cream while I walk on.
unpacking plates
Storyboard: Back in her apartment that evening, Maya washes these newly bought plates in the sink and places them in the cupboard. She looks out the window at the lingering summer sunset, entirely content with this quiet Sunday and deeply settled in the life she started half a year ago.
Ponovo sam u svom stanu.I am back in my apartment.
Nedelja je uveče i ovde je veoma tiho.It is Sunday evening, and it is very quiet here.
Stojim u kuhinji.I am standing out in the kitchen.
Perem svoje nove tanjire u sudoperi.I wash my new plates in the sink.
Ovi tanjiri su zaista prelepi.These plates are really beautiful.
Plavi su i malo teški.They are blue and a little heavy.
Brišem ih kuhinjskom krpom.I dry them with a tea towel.
Nakon toga, stavljam ih u ormarić.Afterwards, I put them in the cupboard.
Ulazim u dnevnu sobu i gledam kroz prozor.I walk into the living room and look out the window.
Sunce polako zalazi iznad gradskih krovova.The sun is slowly going down over the city's roofs.
Ovo letnje veče je prelepo i svetlo.This summer evening is beautiful and bright.
Zaista mi se sviđa ovaj kraj.I really like this neighborhood.
Živim ovde već pola godine.I have lived here for half a year now.
Toliko toga se desilo od januara.So much has happened since January.
Tada nisam poznavala nikoga u gradu.Back then I knew no one in the city.
Sada mnogo bolje poznajem ulice i svakodnevni život.Now I know the streets and everyday life much better.
Osećam se potpuno kao kod kuće u ovom gradu.I feel completely at home in this city.
Sutra je ponedeljak i idem autobusom na posao.Tomorrow is Monday, and I take the bus to work.
Ali trenutno se samo opuštam.But right now I am just relaxing.
Potpuno sam zadovoljna svojim životom ovde.I am completely content with my life here.
