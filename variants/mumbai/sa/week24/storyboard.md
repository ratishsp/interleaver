# Week 24 — Storyboard (Maya looks after Asha's dog)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Imperatives + object pronouns **तम् / एनम्**; rules with **-तव्यम् / न कर्तव्यम्** (must / must not)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_asha_arrives_with_moti | On Friday evening, Asha arrives at Maya's door with her dog Moti, a heavy bag of dog food, and his leash. She thanks Maya profusely for agreeing to dog-sit for the weekend while she goes out of town. |
| 2 | 02_ashas_strict_rules | Before leaving, Asha explains Moti's care routine, setting clear rules: he must be fed at exactly eight o'clock in the morning and evening, he must stay on his leash in the park, and giving him human snacks is strictly forbidden. Maya listens carefully and reassures her that she understands. |
| 3 | 03_calming_moti | After Asha leaves, Moti paces nervously around the flat, whining for his owner. Maya pats her rug, using gentle imperatives to tell him to come and sit, then hands him his favourite chew toy, telling him to take it. |
| 4 | 04_the_morning_walk | Early Saturday morning, Maya takes Moti for a walk in the local park. When he spots a squirrel and pulls hard to chase it, Maya holds tight, enforcing Asha's rule that keeping him leashed in the park is mandatory. |
| 5 | 05_serving_breakfast | Back in the flat, Maya checks the clock to ensure it is exactly eight, following the strict feeding schedule. When Moti jumps up excitedly, she firmly commands him to wait, places the metal bowl on the floor, and uses an object pronoun to tell him to eat it. |
| 6 | 06_the_hallway_escape | Maya safely leaves Moti resting inside her flat while she attends her regular Saturday morning volunteering shift at the neighbourhood café. When she returns and opens the door, Moti excitedly slips past her and wanders off down the apartment hallway; Maya urgently uses imperative verbs to call him back to her. |
| 7 | 07_the_shoe_thief | Back at the flat on Saturday afternoon, Maya catches Moti happily gnawing on one of her sandals. She claps her hands, using imperatives to command him to drop it and give it to her immediately, before swapping the shoe for his toy. |
| 8 | 08_enforcing_the_snack_rule | On Sunday afternoon, Maya is sitting on a park bench eating a warm bun maska when Moti begs with big, sad eyes. She firmly tells him no, reminding him that eating human food is forbidden, and instead gives him one of his specific dog treats. |
| 9 | 09_the_weekend_ends | Sunday evening, Asha returns to collect Moti, who wags his tail furiously at the door. Maya hands back his leash and bowls, reporting warmly that he was a lovely guest, though she laughingly admits the sandal incident and shows Asha the slightly chewed shoe. |
