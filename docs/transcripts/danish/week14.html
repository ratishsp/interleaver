Week 14: Maya takes public transport across the city

Danish with English gloss, line-aligned.

checking the route
Storyboard: Maya looks at a travel app on her phone. She needs to go across the city to pick up a package, so she plans a route with three different types of public transport.
Jeg skal hente en pakke i dag.I need to pick up a package today.
Den er på den anden side af byen.It is on the other side of the city.
Jeg kigger på min telefon.I look at my phone.
Jeg åbner en app til offentlig transport.I open a public transport app.
Jeg planlægger min rute.I plan my route.
Ruten er lang.The route is long.
Den har tre dele.It has three parts.
Først skal jeg med en bus.First, I need to take a bus.
Bussen kører til metroen.The bus drives to the metro.
Så skal jeg køre med metroen.Then I need to ride the metro.
Til sidst skal jeg til havnen.Finally, I need to go to the harbour.
Her skal jeg med havnebussen.Here, I need to take the harbour bus.
Havnebussen er en stor båd.The harbour bus is a large boat.
Den sejler på vandet.It sails on the water.
Pakken er i en lille butik.The package is in a small shop.
Butikken ligger tæt på vandet.The shop is located close to the water.
Det er en god plan.It is a good plan.
Jeg tager mit blå rejsekort.I take my blue travel card.
Jeg er klar til turen.I am ready for the trip.
topping up the card
Storyboard: She walks to a nearby ticket machine. She taps her blue travel card on the screen and pays two hundred kroner to top up her balance for the long trip.
Jeg går hen til en billetautomat på gaden.I walk over to a ticket machine on the street.
Først skal mit rejsekort have flere penge.First my travel card needs more money.
Jeg tager mit rejsekort ud af min taske.I take my travel card out of my bag.
Det er et lille, blåt kort.It is a small, blue card.
Jeg lægger kortet på automatens skærm.I place the card on the machine's screen.
Skærmen viser min saldo.The screen shows my balance.
Saldoen er for lav til en lang tur.The balance is too low for a long trip.
Jeg trykker på skærmen.I tap on the screen.
Jeg vælger et beløb.I choose an amount.
Jeg betaler to hundrede kroner med mit betalingskort.I pay two hundred kroner with my payment card.
Automaten skriver "Godkendt" på skærmen.The machine writes "Approved" on the screen.
Nu har jeg penge på kortet.Now I have money on the card.
Jeg lægger kortet tilbage i tasken.I put the card back in the bag.
Jeg er klar til den lange tur.I am ready for the long trip.
Nu skal jeg med bussen.Now I need to take the bus.
Jeg går over til busstoppestedet.I walk over to the bus stop.
taking the bus
Storyboard: Maya waits at the bus stop until a long yellow bus arrives. She steps inside, scans her card at the door, and finds an empty seat by the window.
Jeg står ved busstoppestedet.I stand at the bus stop.
Snart kører jeg med bussen.Soon I travel by bus.
Jeg kigger ned ad gaden.I look down the street.
Efter fem minutter kommer bussen.After five minutes, the bus arrives.
Det er en lang og gul bus.It is a long and yellow bus.
Bussen stopper lige foran mig.The bus stops right in front of me.
Dørene åbner.The doors open.
Jeg går op i bussen.I step up into the bus.
Jeg har mit rejsekort i hånden.I have my travel card in my hand.
Jeg scanner kortet ved døren.I scan the card by the door.
Der er mange passagerer i dag.There are many passengers today.
Jeg kigger efter en ledig plads.I look for an empty seat.
Jeg finder en god plads ved vinduet.I find a good seat by the window.
Jeg sætter mig ned.I sit down.
Jeg kigger ud på byen.I look out at the city.
Nu tager jeg bussen til metrostationen.Now I take the bus to the metro station.
asking a passenger
Storyboard: She turns to an older woman sitting nearby. Maya politely asks if she travels with this bus to the metro station, and the woman nods with a friendly smile.
Jeg sidder i bussen.I am sitting in the bus.
Jeg kigger ud ad vinduet.I look out the window.
Jeg er ikke helt sikker på ruten.I am not completely sure about the route.
En ældre kvinde sidder tæt på mig.An older woman is sitting close to me.
Jeg kigger over på hende.I look over at her.
"Undskyld," siger jeg høfligt."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Kører denne bus til metrostationen?""Does this bus go to the metro station?"
Kvinden kigger på mig.The woman looks at me.
Hun nikker med et venligt smil.She nods with a friendly smile.
"Ja, den kører til metrostationen," svarer hun."Yes, it goes to the metro station," she replies.
"Skal du med metroen?" spørger kvinden."Are you taking the metro?" the woman asks.
"Ja, jeg skal med metroen," siger jeg."Yes, I am taking the metro," I say.
"Jeg kører tit med denne bus," siger kvinden."I often ride with this bus," the woman says.
"Det er en nem rute," tilføjer hun."It is an easy route," she adds.
"Mange tak for hjælpen," siger jeg."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Det er min første tur på denne rute.""It is my first trip on this route."
Kvinden nikker igen.The woman nods again.
Jeg slapper af på sædet.I relax in my seat.
Vi kører videre gennem byen.We drive on through the city.
transferring to metro
Storyboard: The bus stops, and Maya gets off at a busy square. She walks down the wide stairs into a bright, underground metro station and taps her card again.
Bussen stopper på en travl plads.The bus stops at a busy square.
Jeg står af bussen.I get off the bus.
Jeg siger farvel til den ældre dame.I say goodbye to the older woman.
Nu skal jeg til metroen.Now I am going to the metro.
Jeg går over pladsen.I walk across the square.
Der er mange mennesker her.There are many people here.
Jeg går ned ad de brede trapper.I walk down the wide stairs.
Stationen er under jorden.The station is underground.
Den er meget stor og lys.It is very big and bright.
Jeg skal tjekke ind igen.I have to check in again.
Jeg tager mit blå kort frem.I take out my blue card.
Jeg scanner kortet på maskinen.I scan the card on the machine.
Maskinen siger et højt bip.The machine makes a loud beep.
Nu kan jeg køre med metroen.Now I can ride with the metro.
Jeg går ned til perronen.I walk down to the platform.
Jeg skal med det næste tog.I am taking the next train.
Det kører direkte mod havnen.It goes straight towards the harbour.
Toget kommer om tre minutter.The train arrives in three minutes.
riding the metro
Storyboard: Maya stands right at the front of the driverless train. She rides with the metro through the dark tunnels and watches the lights blur past as they speed forward.
Metroen kommer, og dørene åbner.The metro arrives, and the doors open.
Jeg går ind i toget.I go into the train.
Toget kører uden en fører.The train drives without a driver.
Jeg går helt op foran.I walk all the way to the front.
Jeg står ved det store vindue.I stand by the large window.
Jeg kører med metroen gennem mørket.I ride with the metro through the dark.
Vi er i en lang tunnel.We are in a long tunnel.
Vi kører meget hurtigt.We drive very fast.
Lyset flyver forbi vinduet.The light flies past the window.
Det ser fantastisk ud.It looks fantastic.
En lille dreng står ved siden af mig.A little boy stands next to me.
Han kigger også ud i mørket.He also looks out into the dark.
Jeg tager ofte med metroen.I often take the metro.
Det er en hurtig og nem tur.It is a fast and easy trip.
Nu nærmer vi os min station.Now we are approaching my station.
Jeg skal til havnen.I am going to the harbour.
Min næste tur er med havnebussen.My next trip is with the harbour bus.
Jeg glæder mig til at se vandet.I am looking forward to seeing the water.
finding the pier
Storyboard: She takes the escalator up to the street level and walks toward the water. She finds a small wooden pier by the edge of the canal where the harbour bus stops.
Turen med metroen er slut.The trip with the metro is over.
Nu skal jeg op på gaden.Now I am going up to the street.
Jeg tager rulletrappen.I take the escalator.
Solen skinner oppe på gaden.The sun is shining up on the street.
Nu skal jeg til vandet.Now I am going to the water.
Jeg skal med havnebussen.I am going by the harbour bus.
Jeg går ned ad en bred gade.I walk down a wide street.
Så ser jeg kanalen.Then I see the canal.
Vandet er meget blåt.The water is very blue.
Jeg kigger efter mit stoppested.I look for my stop.
Hvor stopper båden?Where does the boat stop?
Jeg går tættere på kanten.I walk closer to the edge.
Så ser jeg en lille træbro.Then I see a small wooden pier.
Den ligger helt nede ved vandet.It is located right down by the water.
Der er et gult skilt ved broen.There is a yellow sign by the pier.
Der står "Havnebus" på skiltet.It says "Harbour bus" on the sign.
Det er det rigtige sted.This is the right place.
Jeg går ud på broen.I walk out onto the pier.
Nu venter jeg bare på båden.Now I am just waiting for the boat.
the harbour bus
Storyboard: A wide boat arrives, and Maya boards the harbour bus. She stands on the open deck and enjoys the fresh air as she sails across the blue water.
En bred båd kommer over vandet.A wide boat comes across the water.
Det er havnebussen.It is the harbour bus.
Båden sejler ind til træmolen.The boat sails in to the wooden pier.
Den stopper ved kanten.It stops at the edge.
Jeg skal med havnebussen nu.I am going with the harbour bus now.
Jeg går om bord på skibet.I step on board the ship.
Jeg scanner mit blå rejsekort igen.I scan my blue travel card again.
Der sidder mange passagerer indenfor.Many passengers sit inside.

Jeg står helt fremme.I stand at the very front.

arriving for the errand

Jeg tjekker kortet på min telefon.I check the map on my phone.
Jeg skal til en lille kiosk.I am going to a small kiosk.
