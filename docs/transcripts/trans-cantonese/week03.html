Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
依家係一月，天氣好凍。It is January, and it is very cold.
我喺哥本哈根由巴士站行緊返屋企。I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
我喺街度見到一間花店。I see a flower shop on the street.
入面又光猛又溫暖。It is bright and warm inside.
我行入去呢間溫暖嘅舖頭。I go into the warm shop.
我望住一盆細小嘅綠色植物。I look at a small, green plant.
有個店員對住我笑。A clerk smiles at me.
「你好，」佢話。"Hello," he says.
「你好，」我話。"Hello," I say.
「我搵緊一盆植物。」"I am looking for a plant."
「係禮物嚟㗎？」佢問。"Is it a gift?" he asks.
「唔係，係放喺我間房嘅，」我答。"No, it is for my room," I answer.
「呢盆植物好好㗎，」佢話。"It is a really good plant," he says.
「幾多錢啊？」我問。"How much does it cost?" I ask.
「呢盆五十五克朗，」佢答。"It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
我畀錢買我嘅新植物。I pay for my new plant.
佢將盆植物放入一個紙袋度。He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
「畀你，呢個係你個袋，」佢話。"Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
「唔該晒，」我好開心咁話。"Thank you very much," I say happily.
我行出去寒冷嘅夜晚。I go out into the cold evening.
我呢盆細小嘅植物令我好開心。My little plant makes me very happy.
evening prep
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her temporary room and sets the new plant on a small desk. With it already being pitch black outside, she turns on a warm lamp to reflect against the dark window, makes a cup of tea, and flips open her laptop to dial home.
我而家喺屋企。I am home now.
呢度係我間房。Here is my room.
呢間係一間細房。It is a small room.
我有盆新盆栽。I have a new plant.
佢放咗喺我張書檯上面。It stands on my desk.
而家係夜晚，而且係一月。It is evening, and it is January.
我隻窗好暗。My window is very dark.
出面完全黑晒。It is completely black outside.
我有一盞燈同一杯茶。I have a lamp and a cup of tea.
我開著我盞燈。I turn on my lamp.
而家我間房又溫暖又光猛。Now my room is warm and bright.
我杯茶都係暖嘅。My tea is also warm.
我坐喺我張書檯前面。I sit at my desk.
我部電腦喺呢度。I have my computer here.
我打開部電腦。I open my computer.
而家我打畀我喺墨西哥嘅屋企人。Now I call my family in Mexico.
我好期待見到佢哋。I look forward to seeing them.
parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
電話響咗幾聲。It rings a couple of times.
個螢幕著咗。The screen lights up.
我見到一個客廳。I see a living room.
有陽光，個客廳好光猛。There is sun, and the living room is very bright.
我見到兩個人。I see two people.
係我阿媽同阿爸。It is my mother and my father.
佢哋笑得好開心，同我揮手。They smile a lot and wave to me.
我大聲講：「哈囉，阿媽、阿爸！」"Hello mom and dad!" I say loudly.
阿爸話：「哈囉，Maya。」"Hello, Maya," my father says.
「最近點呀？」"How is it going?"
我好開心咁答：「幾好呀。」"It is going well," I answer happily.
突然間我聽到有聲。Suddenly I hear a sound.
係一下細細嘅狗吠聲。It is a little bark.
我笑住講：「噢，我聽到有狗叫呀！」"Oh, I hear a dog!" I say and laugh.
一隻狗仔跳咗上梳化。A little dog jumps up onto a sofa.
佢望住個鏡頭。It looks into the camera.
「我隻狗喺度呀！」"There is my dog!"
我話：「哈囉，Lola。」"Hello, Lola," I say.
Lola 喺度搖尾。Lola wags her tail.
阿媽笑住話：「係呀，係你隻狗呀。」"Yes, it is your dog," my mother says with a smile.
我好愛我嘅屋企人。I love my family.
可以見到佢哋全部人真係好開心。It is lovely to see them all.
the siblings
Storyboard: Her younger sister Sofía and older brother Diego squeeze into the frame, playfully shoving each other. Sofía proudly holds up a thick university textbook, while Diego jokingly asks Maya if she is surviving the freezing Danish winter.
依家我個螢幕上面出現咗兩張新面孔。Now there are two new faces on my screen.
係我細妹索菲亞同埋我阿哥迪亞哥。It is my little sister, Sofía, and my big brother, Diego.
佢哋笑住互相推咗對方幾吓。They laugh and push each other a little.
「細妹、阿哥，你哋好！」我好開心咁講。"Hello little sister and big brother!" I say happily.
「瑪雅，哈囉，」迪亞哥講。"Hi Maya," says Diego.
我阿哥笑得好燦爛。My brother has a big smile.
「丹麥凍唔凍呀？」佢問。"Is it cold in Denmark?" he asks.
「冬天係咪好凍㗎？」"Is it a cold winter?"
「係呀，呢個冬天好凍呀，」我笑住講。"Yes, it is a very cold winter," I laugh.
「不過我間房好暖，仲有一杯茶。」"But I have a warm room and a cup of tea."
依家索菲亞喺螢幕前舉起一件好大嘅嘢。Now Sofía lifts a big thing up in front of the screen.
係一本好大嘅書。It is a large book.
「索菲亞，你嗰本係咪新書呀？」我問。"Do you have a new book there, Sofía?" I ask.
「係呀，呢本係我嘅新書，」阿妹好自豪咁講。"Yes, it is my new book," my sister says proudly.
「係我大學嘅書嚟㗎。」"It is a book from my university."
「呢本係一本好厚嘅書！」"It is a very thick book!"
我對住阿哥同細妹微笑。I smile at my brother and my sister.
我好掛住喺墨西哥嘅屋企人。I miss my family in Mexico.
不過佢哋依家就喺我個細細嘅螢幕上面。But they are right here on my little screen.
temporary room tour
Storyboard: Her mother asks for a tour of her current living space. Maya pans the camera around to show her new plant and the small bed, explaining that it is just a temporary room and pointing out the black, sunless window.
我媽媽望住個螢幕。My mother looks at the screen.
「嗰間係咪你間房呀，瑪雅？」佢問。"Is that your room, Maya?" she asks.
「係呀，呢間係我間房，」我答。"Yes, it is my room," I answer.
「不過只係一間臨時房。」"But it is a temporary room."
我舉起部電腦。I lift my computer.
我俾屋企人睇我間細房。I show my small room to my family.
「呢度有張床，」我話。"Here is a bed," I say.
「同埋呢度有我樖新植物。」"And here is my new plant."
「呢樖植物好靚，」我媽媽話。"It is a very nice plant," my mother says.
我轉一轉部電腦。I turn my computer.
「同埋呢度有隻窗，」我話。"And here is a window," I say.
我妹妹蘇菲亞望住我隻窗。My sister Sofía looks at my window.
「但係好暗喎，」佢話。"But it is very dark," she says.
「係呀，出面完全黑晒，」我答。"Yes, it is completely black out there," I answer.
「不過夏天會有好多陽光。」"But in summer there is plenty of sun."
依家係夜晚，天氣好凍。It is evening, and it is cold.
「但我有盞燈，」我笑住話。"But I have my lamp," I smile.
「所以呢間係一間好房。」"So it is a good room."
mexico garden
Storyboard: Feeling a bit homesick, Maya asks her father to take the phone outside. He walks out to their sunny garden, showing a large, familiar leafy tree. Maya smiles deeply, feeling warm and comforted by the glimpse of home before they all wave goodbye.
我望住部電腦。I look at my computer.
我有啲掛住喺墨西哥嘅屋企。I miss my home in Mexico a little bit.
我話：「阿爸，你個花園點啊？」"Dad, how is your garden?" I say.
我阿爸對住我笑。My dad smiles at me.
佢攞住部電話行出去。He goes outside with the phone.
嗰度有個大花園。There is a large garden.
嗰度好好太陽。The sun is shining a lot there.
我阿爸話：「Maya，你睇。」"Look, Maya," my dad says.
「呢度係個花園，仲有一樖樹。」"Here is the garden and a tree."
係一樖好大嘅樹。It is a very big tree.
係一樖好靚嘅樹。It is a beautiful tree.
我望住個熒幕笑。I smile at my screen.
我覺得心入面好舒服、好溫暖。I feel good and warm inside.
我阿媽、家姐同阿哥都行埋出嚟。My mother, my sister, and my brother come outside too.
我隻狗喺樹底度跑。My dog runs under the tree.
我成家人同我揮手。My whole family waves at me.
我話：「大家拜拜！」"Goodbye, everyone!" I say.
佢哋話：「拜拜，Maya。」"Goodbye, Maya," they say.
我閂埋部電腦。I close my computer.
