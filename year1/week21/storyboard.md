# Week 21 — Storyboard (Maya buys spring clothes and makes an exchange)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Comparatives *større/mindre*; *prøve*; sizes

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_spring_afternoon_shopping | After finishing her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the café, Maya enjoys the mild spring weather. She walks down the bustling main street to look for lighter clothes for the changing season. |
| 2 | 02_trying_spring_jacket | Inside a large clothing store, she finds a light green spring jacket. She decides to try it on in front of a mirror on the shop floor, and since it fits perfectly, she decides to buy it. |
| 3 | 03_grabbing_a_shirt | On her way to the cash register, Maya spots a nice blue cotton shirt. The line for the fitting rooms is very long, so she simply picks her usual size and decides not to try it on first. |
| 4 | 04_keeping_the_receipt | At the checkout, she pays for both the jacket and the shirt. The friendly clerk hands her the paper receipt, and Maya folds it into her wallet. |
| 5 | 05_shirt_too_small | Later that afternoon, back in her apartment, Maya unpacks her shopping bags. She puts on the new blue shirt, but quickly realizes it is much too small across the shoulders. |
| 6 | 06_returning_with_receipt | On Monday afternoon, after taking the bus home from the software company, Maya stops by the same clothing store. She walks directly to the front counter holding the unworn shirt and her receipt. |
| 7 | 07_asking_for_exchange | She explains to a helpful shop assistant that the shirt is too small. She asks if she can exchange it for a bigger size, and the assistant kindly hands her the next size up to try. |
| 8 | 08_fitting_room_comparison | Maya takes the shirt into the fitting room so she can try it. Looking in the mirror, she sees that the bigger size is much more comfortable and fits better than the smaller one she bought earlier. |
| 9 | 09_successful_exchange | She confirms that she wants to keep the larger size. The clerk scans the old receipt to complete the exchange, and Maya leaves the store satisfied. |
