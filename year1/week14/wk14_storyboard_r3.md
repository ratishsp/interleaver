# Week 14 — Storyboard (Maya takes public transport across the city)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** *tage/køre med*; *til/med*; *skal til*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_checking_the_route | Maya checks a travel app on her phone. She wants to go across the city to buy a vintage desk lamp from a private seller, so she plans a route with three different types of public transport. |
| 2 | 02_topping_up | She walks to a ticket machine at the station. She inserts her blue travel card and pays two hundred kroner to top up her balance for the long journey. |
| 3 | 03_boarding_the_bus | Maya waits at the stop until a long yellow bus arrives. She boards the vehicle, ready to ride with the bus toward the city center, and looks for an empty spot. |
| 4 | 04_asking_for_a_seat | She notices an older woman with a shopping bag resting on the seat next to her. Maya politely asks if the seat is free, and the woman smiles and moves her bag so Maya can sit. |
| 5 | 05_entering_the_metro | The bus stops at a busy square, and Maya steps out. She walks down the wide stairs into a bright, underground metro station to continue her trip. |
| 6 | 06_riding_the_metro | Maya stands right at the front window of the driverless train. She rides with the metro through the dark tunnels and watches the lights blur past as the train speeds forward. |
| 7 | 07_finding_the_pier | She takes the escalator up to the street level and walks toward the canal. She spots a small wooden pier by the water where passengers wait for the harbour bus. |
| 8 | 08_the_harbour_bus | A wide boat arrives, and Maya steps aboard. She stands on the open deck and enjoys the fresh breeze as she sails with the boat across the blue water. |
| 9 | 09_checking_out_onboard | The boat approaches the opposite shore. Just before she steps off the vessel, Maya taps her travel card on the blue reader located right by the boat's doors to finish her journey. |
| 10 | 10_collecting_the_lamp | Maya walks down a quiet cobblestone street to a brick apartment building. She meets the friendly seller at the door and happily collects her beautiful second-hand lamp. |
