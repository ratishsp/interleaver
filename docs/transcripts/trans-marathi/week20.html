finding the volunteering
Storyboard: Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. मी ऐकले आहे की अनेक डेनिश लोक त्यांच्या मोकळ्या वेळेत स्वयंसेवा करतात.I have heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time.

learning the machine

यादरम्यान मी त्याच्या हातांकडे बारकाईने पाहते.I look closely at his hands meanwhile.

the first coffee

the ginger beer
