# Week 22 — Storyboard (Maya cooks a Kerala meal for her friends)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Imperative sequences; quantities (**അര കിലോ, രണ്ട് സ്പൂൺ**); **ആദ്യം/പിന്നെ/അതിനുശേഷം**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_deciding_the_menu | On Sunday morning, Maya wakes up with guests coming: at Thursday's badminton game she invited Kiran and Sara over for lunch today. She browses the internet on her phone and finds a straightforward recipe for a traditional Kerala chicken curry to serve with rice. |
| 2 | 02_writing_the_shopping_list | Maya carefully reads the recipe and writes down a shopping list for three people. She scales the amounts for three people, checks her spice shelf for what she already has, and notes down only what is missing. |
| 3 | 03_buying_the_vegetables | Carrying her umbrella against the steady monsoon drizzle, Maya walks to the local neighborhood market. She visits her usual vegetable vendor and asks for exactly two large onions, three tomatoes, some ginger, and green chilies. |
| 4 | 04_buying_the_chicken | Next, she stops at the nearby butcher shop before the busy Sunday morning rush depletes their stock. She asks the butcher for half a kilo of chicken and specifically requests that he cut it into small pieces suitable for a curry. |
| 5 | 05_kitchen_prep_work | Back in her own kitchen late that morning, Maya begins the prep work for lunch, reading the recipe's imperative instructions out loud to herself from her phone to follow along. 'First, wash the chicken thoroughly,' she reads. 'Then, finely chop the onions and tomatoes.' |
| 6 | 06_cooking_step_by_step | Maya cooks the curry step by step. First, she sautés the onions in hot oil until they turn golden. After that, she adds the chopped tomatoes and exactly two spoons of masala powder, stirs it well, and finally drops in the chicken pieces to simmer. |
| 7 | 07_the_guests_arrive | Just as the kitchen fills with the rich aroma of roasted spices, Kiran and Sara arrive at the door. They shake off their wet umbrellas in the hallway and compliment the wonderful smell coming from the stove. |
| 8 | 08_setting_the_table | Maya warmly welcomes them inside. While the curry finishes its final simmer, she sets her small dining table herself, arranging the plates, bringing out a jug of water, and carrying over a large bowl of hot rice. |
| 9 | 09_eating_together | The three friends sit down and serve themselves generous portions of rice and curry. Kiran and Sara are highly impressed by the authentic taste, and they enjoy a warm, lively Sunday lunch together while the rain falls outside. |
