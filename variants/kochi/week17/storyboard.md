# Week 17 — Storyboard (Maya's Backwater Diary)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Past-tense narration; **സ്വന്തം** vs **അവന്റെ/അവളുടെ** (own vs another's); time expressions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_a_hot_sunday_morning | On a very hot Sunday morning, Maya decides to escape the city heat. She takes an auto-rickshaw to the Ernakulam railway station, buys a ticket for a town down the coast, and waits on the crowded platform. |
| 2 | 02_boarding_the_train | When the train arrives, Maya finds a window seat. She watches a family settle in across from her, smiling as a young boy insists on carrying his own heavy bag rather than letting his mother carry it. |
| 3 | 03_coastal_train_views | Maya recounts the beautiful train journey south. She describes how the train left the noisy city behind, crossed long bridges, and how bright glimpses of water flashed between the dense green trees along the tracks. |
| 4 | 04_arriving_at_the_dock | Getting off at the coastal town, Maya walks from the station to the ferry dock. The midday heat is intense, so she stops at a small roadside stall to buy a cold bottle of water and asks the vendor for directions to the boat. |
| 5 | 05_the_slow_ferry | Maya boards a slow public ferry. As the boat pulls away from the dock, she describes how it glided smoothly between thick palm trees, the gentle breeze off the water finally breaking the heavy summer heat. |
| 6 | 06_watching_the_passengers | While the boat moves slowly, Maya people-watches. She sees an older man borrow his friend's newspaper to read. Maya then takes out her own camera to photograph the small houses sitting right on the water's edge. |
| 7 | 07_lunch_on_an_island | The ferry stops at a small island village in the afternoon. Maya gets off, finds a quiet local restaurant by the water, and eats a simple, delicious lunch of rice and fish while watching the small canoes paddle past. |
| 8 | 08_the_evening_return | In the late afternoon, Maya catches the returning ferry, and by evening she is on the train back to Kochi. The sun begins to set over the water, and she rests her head against the window, feeling physically tired but completely happy. |
| 9 | 09_writing_the_diary | Back in her comfortable apartment that night, Maya sits at her table with her travel diary. She finishes writing down the times and memories of the day, happy that she successfully navigated her first solo trip out of the city. |
