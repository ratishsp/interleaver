Week 8: Maya explores the city

Norwegian with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
Det er lørdag morgen.It is Saturday morning.
Solen skinner over byen.The sun is shining over the city.
Jeg går ned på gaten.I go down to the street.
Min nabo, Nina, står utenfor bygningen vår.My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
"God morgen, Maya," sier hun og smiler."Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
"God morgen, Nina," svarer jeg."Good morning, Nina," I answer.
"Jeg har fri i dag," forteller jeg henne."I have the day off today," I tell her.
"Jeg drar ut i byen.""I am heading out into the city."
"Hvor skal du?" spør Nina."Where are you going?" Nina asks.
"Jeg går ned til vannet," sier jeg."I am walking down to the water," I say.
"Men jeg vet ikke veien.""But I do not know the way."
Nina peker nedover gaten.Nina points down the street.
"Det er heldigvis veldig enkelt," sier hun."Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
"Gå rett frem her.""Go straight ahead here."
"Og sving til venstre ved parken.""And turn left at the park."
"Så kommer du rett ned til havnen.""Then you come right down to the harbour."
"Tusen takk for hjelpen, Nina," sier jeg."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
"Selv takk, og god tur," svarer hun."You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
Jeg vinker til henne.I wave to her.
Jeg begynner min gåtur mot vannet.I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
Jeg følger Ninas rute.I follow Nina's route.
Luften er frisk og kald.The air is fresh and cold.
Nå går jeg nedover hovedgaten.Now I walk down the main street.
Først går jeg bare rett frem.First I just walk straight ahead.
Det er mye trafikk i gaten.There is a lot of traffic on the street.
På veien ser jeg tre gule sykler.On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
Syklene kjører raskt forbi meg.The bicycles ride quickly past me.
Deretter går jeg lenger frem.After that I walk further ahead.
Så kommer jeg til en stor park.Then I come to a large park.
Nå husker jeg Ninas ord.Now I remember Nina's words.
"Ta til venstre ved parken," sier hun."Turn left at the park," she says.
Her tar jeg til venstre.Here I turn left.
Litt etter ser jeg en åpen dør.A little later I see an open door.
Det er et lite bakeri.It is a small bakery.
Det er en deilig duft av fersk kaffe.There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
Jeg elsker varmt brød.I love warm bread.
I dag er byen nydelig.Today the city is lovely.
Etterpå går jeg videre mot vannet.Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
Jeg kommer til en stor plass.I arrive at a large square.
Det er mange mennesker.There are many people.
Men jeg ser ikke vannet.But I do not see the water.
Jeg leter etter havnen.I am looking for the harbour.
Jeg ser en eldre mann på fortauet.I see an older man on the pavement.
Jeg stopper ham.I stop him.
"Unnskyld," sier jeg."Excuse me," I say.
"Hvor er havnen?""Where is the harbour?"
Den eldre mannen nikker.The older man nods.
Han peker fremover.He points ahead.
"Gå rett over plassen," sier han."Walk straight across the square," he says.
"Gå forbi museet.""Go past the museum."
"Og ta til høyre.""And turn right."
Jeg ser over plassen.I look across the square.
Jeg ser en stor bygning.I see a large building.
Det er museet.It is the museum.
"Tusen takk," sier jeg."Thank you very much," I say.
"Bare hyggelig," svarer han."You are welcome," he replies.
Jeg går videre.I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
Jeg går over den store plassen.I walk across the large square.
Plutselig hører jeg musikk.Suddenly, I hear music.
På hjørnet står en gatemusikant.On the corner stands a street musician.
Han spiller gitar.He plays the guitar.
Jeg lytter til den gode musikken.I listen to the good music.
Jeg ser lenge på ham.I look at him for a long time.
Så går jeg videre.Then I walk on.
Jeg glemmer museet.I forget the museum.
Jeg glemmer også ruten min.I also forget my route.
I stedet går jeg ned en smal sidegate.Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
Her er det veldig stille.It is very quiet here.
Rundt omkring ser jeg bare noen små hus.All around I only see some small houses.
Så stopper jeg.Then I stop.
Hvor er museet?Where is the museum?
Nå ser jeg meg omkring.Now I look around.
Jeg kan ikke se det lenger.I cannot see it anymore.
Jeg ser raskt tilbake oppover gaten.I quickly look back up the street.
Jeg kan heller ikke se plassen.I cannot see the square either.
Jeg har gått meg helt bort.I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
Jeg står i en stille gate.I am standing on a quiet street.
Jeg har gått meg vill.I am lost.
Foran meg ser jeg en ung kvinne.Ahead of me I see a young woman.
Hun går tur med en liten hund.She is walking a small dog.
Hunden ligner på min families hund, Lola.The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
Jeg går bort til kvinnen.I walk over to the woman.
"Unnskyld," sier jeg."Excuse me," I say.
"Jeg leter etter havnen.""I am looking for the harbour."
Kvinnen stopper og lytter.The woman stops and listens.
Hun peker tilbake opp gaten.She points back up the street.
"Gå rett frem tilbake til plassen," sier hun."Go straight back to the square," she says.
"Og ta til høyre ved museet.""And turn right at the museum."
"Tusen takk for hjelpen," sier jeg."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Bare hyggelig," svarer kvinnen."You are welcome," the woman replies.
Jeg ser ned på den lille hunden.I look down at the little dog.
Den ser på meg.It looks at me.
Jeg går tilbake mot plassen.I walk back toward the square.
Nå vet jeg hvor jeg er.Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
Jeg går tilbake langs den stille gaten.I walk back along the quiet street.
Jeg går rett frem.I walk straight ahead.
Snart ser jeg den store plassen igjen.Soon I see the large square again.
Så går jeg over plassen.Then I walk across the square.
Nå står jeg ved museet.Now I am standing by the museum.
Jeg husker kvinnens ord: "Sving til høyre."I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
Ved museet svinger jeg til høyre.At the museum I turn right.
Jeg går nedover en bred gate.I walk down a wide street.
Jeg ser fremover.I look ahead.
Endelig ser jeg noe blått.Finally I see something blue.
Det er vannet!It is the water!
På veien går jeg forbi en liten kafé.On the way I walk past a little café.
Til slutt går jeg helt ned til vannet.At last I walk all the way down to the water.

Havnen er veldig stor.The harbour is very large.

Det er mange båter på vannet.There are many boats on the water.
Her stopper jeg.Here I stop.
Jeg står og ser på båtene.I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
Jeg går rett frem langs havnen.I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
Jeg går forbi de små båtene.I walk past the small boats.
Plutselig ser jeg mange mennesker.Suddenly I see many people.
De står nede ved vannet.They stand down by the water.
Jeg går bort dit.I walk over there.
Endelig ser jeg den.Finally, I see it.
Den lille havfruen sitter på en stein ute i vannet.The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
Hun er en veldig berømt statue i København.She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.

Men bronsefiguren er vakker i solen.But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
Jeg står blant de andre besøkende.I stand among the other visitors.
Mange turister tar bilder av statuen.Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
Jeg tar også frem telefonen min.I also take out my phone.
Jeg tar et vakkert bilde av havfruen.I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
Jeg sender bildet til familien min i Mexico.I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
Søsteren min, Sofía, svarer raskt.My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
"Hun er veldig fin," skriver hun."She is very pretty," she writes.
Jeg ser på vannet igjen.I look at the water again.
walking home

To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.
