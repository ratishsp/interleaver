Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
Det er januar, og det er veldig kaldt.It is January, and it is very cold.
Jeg går hjem fra busstoppet i København.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Jeg ser en blomsterbutikk på gaten.I see a flower shop on the street.
Det er lyst og varmt der inne.It is bright and warm inside.
Jeg går inn i den varme butikken.I go into the warm shop.
Jeg ser på en liten, grønn plante.I look at a small, green plant.

"Hei," sier han."Hello," he says.
"Hei," sier jeg."Hello," I say.
"Jeg leter etter en plante.""I am looking for a plant."
"Er det en gave?" spør han."Is it a gift?" he asks.
"Nei, det er til rommet mitt," svarer jeg."No, it is for my room," I answer.
"Det er en veldig god plante," sier han."It is a really good plant," he says.
"Hva koster den?" spør jeg."How much does it cost?" I ask.
"Den koster femtifem kroner," svarer han."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
Jeg betaler for den nye planten min.I pay for my new plant.
Han legger planten ned i en papirpose.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
"Vær så god, her er posen din," sier han."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
"Tusen takk," sier jeg glad."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Jeg går ut i den kalde kvelden.I go out into the cold evening.
Den lille planten min gjør meg veldig glad.My little plant makes me very happy.

parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.

Jeg ser en stue.I see a living room.
Det er sol, og stuen er veldig lys.There is sun, and the living room is very bright.

"Hei, Maya," sier min far."Hello, Maya," my father says.
"Hvordan går det?""How is it going?"

"Der er min hund!""There is my dog!"

Det er herlig å se dem alle.It is lovely to see them all.
