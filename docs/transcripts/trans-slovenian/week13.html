Week 13: Maya goes to a café

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.
V Københavnu je zima.It is winter in Copenhagen.
Hodim po ulici.I am walking down the street.
Veter je zelo hladen.The wind is very cold.
Danes močno piha.It is blowing hard today.
Nagnem se proti hladnemu vetru.I lean against the cold wind.
Šal si potegnem gor.I pull my scarf up.
V obraz me zebe.My face is freezing.
Ozrem se naokoli.I look around.
Rada bi šla na toplo.I would like to get into the warmth.
Na vogalu vidim majhno kavarno.I see a small café on the corner.
Okna so zarošena.The windows are fogged up.
Tam notri je videti toplo.It looks warm in there.
Grem do kavarne.I walk over to the café.
Odprem težka vrata.I open the heavy door.
Stopim noter.I step inside.
Tukaj diši po kavi.It smells like coffee here.
Zrak je prijetno topel.The air is nice and warm.
Zaprem vrata za seboj.I close the door behind me.
Hladen veter ostane zunaj.The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
Zdaj sem v kavarni.I am inside the café now.
Tukaj je zelo toplo.It is very warm here.
Slečem svojo debelo zimsko jakno.I take off my thick winter coat.
Težka je.It is heavy.
Na steni najdem kavelj.I find a hook on the wall.
Svojo jakno obesim na kavelj.I hang my coat on the hook.
Snemem si tudi šal in rokavice.I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
Kavarna je majhna in prijetna.The café is small and cozy.
Iščem dobro mesto.I look for a good seat.
Ob oknu je majhna miza.There is a small table by the window.
Miza stoji zraven radiatorja.The table is next to a radiator.
Stopim do mize.I walk over to the table.
Usedem se na stol.I sit down on the chair.
Moji roki sta še vedno mrzli.My hands are still cold.
Podrgnem ju eno ob drugo.I rub them together.
Moji roki postajata toplejši.My hands get warmer.
Občutek je fantastičen.It feels fantastic.
Zdaj mi je skoraj toplo.Now I am almost warm.
Lačna in žejna sem.I am hungry and thirsty.
Zdaj bi rada nekaj naročila.I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
Stopim do steklene vitrine.I walk over to the glass counter.
Polna je tort in kruha.It is full of cakes and bread.
Gledam številne stvari.I look at the many things.
Videti so zelo okusne.They look very delicious.
Zraven tort so majhne tablice.There are small signs next to the cakes.
Tablice so napisane na roko.The signs are handwritten.
Berem imena na tablicah.I read the names on the signs.
Na voljo je veliko vrst peciva.There are many kinds of pastry.
Težko je izbrati.It is hard to choose.
Tam je velika čokoladna torta.There is a large chocolate cake.
Toda gledam drug kos peciva.But I look at another piece of pastry.
To je velika cimetova rolica.It is a large cinnamon roll.
Na vrhu ima veliko sladkorja.It has a lot of sugar on top.
Obožujem okus cimeta.I love the taste of cinnamon.
Popolna je za hladen zimski dan.It is perfect for a cold winter day.
Zelo rada bi cimetovo rolico.I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
Zdaj sem pripravljena naročiti.Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
Stopim do blagajne.I walk over to the register.
Mlad moški stoji za pultom.A young man stands behind the counter.
On je barista v kavarni.He is a barista in the café.
Nasmehnem se mu.I smile at him.
Prijazno pokima.He nods warmly.
"Živijo," reče."Hi," he says.
"Kaj bi želeli?" vpraša."What would you like?" he asks.
"Živijo," odgovorim."Hi," I answer.
"Želela bi eno črno kavo, prosim.""I would like a black coffee, please."
"In ali lahko prosim še za en cimetov polžek?""And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
"Seveda," reče."Of course," he says.
"Ena črna kava in en cimetov polžek.""A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
"Boste sedeli tukaj?" vpraša."Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
"Da, prosim, usedla se bom tja," rečem."Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
"V redu," reče barista."Alright," the barista says.
"Zdaj vam bom pripravil kavo.""I will make your coffee now."
"Najlepša hvala," rečem."Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
Čakam pri pultu.I wait at the counter.
Pripravlja mojo kavo.He makes my coffee.
Vročo skodelico kave postavi na lesen pladenj.He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
Na pladenj postavi tudi majhen krožnik.He also puts a small plate on the tray.
Moj cimetov polžek je na krožniku.My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
Čudovito diši.It smells lovely.
"Izvolite, vaše naročilo," reče."Here you go, your order," he says.
"Najlepša hvala," odgovorim."Thank you very much," I reply.
"Hvala za dobro postrežbo.""Thank you for the good service."
"Ni za kaj," prijazno reče."You are welcome," he says kindly.
Pladenj vzamem z obema rokama.I take the tray with both hands.
Zelo previdno stopam po tleh.I walk very carefully across the floor.
Grem nazaj k svoji mizi ob oknu.I walk back to my table by the window.
Pladenj odložim na mizo.I put the tray down on the table.
Zdaj bi se rada usedla.Now I would like to sit down.
Pripravljena sem na dober odmor.I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
Zdaj sedim za svojo majhno mizo.I now sit at my small table.
Tukaj je prijetno toplo.It is lovely and warm here.
Gledam svojega cimetovega polžka.I look at my cinnamon roll.
Diši sladko in dobro.It smells sweet and good.
Naredim majhen grižljaj.I take a small bite.
Pecivo je popolnoma sveže in toplo.The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
Res je dobrega okusa.It tastes really good.
Popijem malo svoje črne kave.I drink a little of my black coffee.
Končno so moji prsti spet topli.Finally, my fingers are warm again.
Sproščam se v mehkem stolu.I relax in the soft chair.
Kavarna je skoraj polna ljudi.The café is almost full of people.
Ljudje sedijo v majhnih skupinah.People sit in small groups.
Skupaj se pogovarjajo in smejejo.They talk and laugh together.
Poslušam tihe glasove v prostoru.I listen to the low voices in the room.
Zvok je zelo prijeten.The sound is very cozy.
Gledam skozi veliko okno.I look out of the large window.
Vreme zunaj je sivo in vetrovno.The weather outside is gray and windy.
Zdaj dejansko spet sneži.Now it is actually snowing again.
Bele snežinke počasi padajo na ulico.White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
Samo vesela sem, da sem notri.I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
Jem zadnji kos svojega cimetovega polžka.I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
Res je okusen.It tastes really good.
Ampak je tudi zelo sladek.But it is also very sweet.
Moja skodelica za kavo je popolnoma prazna.My coffee cup is completely empty.
Nenadoma sem zelo žejna.I am suddenly very thirsty.
Rada bi malo hladne vode.I would like to have some cold water.
Mimo moje mize gre mlad moški.A young man walks past my table.
On dela tukaj v kavarni.He works here in the café.
Pobira prazne skodelice in krožnike.He collects empty cups and plates.
Ujamem njegov pogled.I catch his eye.
Ustavi se pri moji mizi in se nasmehne.He stops at my table and smiles.
"Oprostite," rečem vljudno."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Prosim?" odgovori mlad moški."Yes?" answers the young man.
"Lahko prosim za kozarec vode iz pipe?" vprašam."May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
"Da, seveda," reče."Yes, of course," he says.
"Najlepša hvala," rečem."Thank you very much," I say.
Vzame moj prazen krožnik in skodelico.He takes my empty plate and cup.
Gre nazaj k pultu.He walks back to the counter.
Malo kasneje pride s hladnim kozarcem vode.A little later he comes with a cold glass of water.
asking for the bill
Storyboard: Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab.
Pijem svojo vodo.I drink my water.
Voda je hladna in prijetna.The water is cold and nice.
Vzamem svojo torbo s tal.I take my bag from the floor.
Končala sem s premorom.I am finished with my break.
Stopim do pulta.I walk up to the counter.
Prijazni barista še vedno stoji tam.The friendly barista is still standing there.
Pogleda me z nasmehom.He looks at me with a smile.
"Bi želeli še kaj drugega?" vpraša."Would you like anything else?" he asks.
"Ne, hvala," odgovorim."No thank you," I answer.
"Rada bi plačala.""I would like to pay."
"Ja, seveda," reče."Yes, of course," he says.
Pogleda na svoj mali zaslon.He looks at his little screen.
"Ena črna kava in en cimetov polžek," reče."One black coffee and one cinnamon roll," he says.
"Ja, drži," rečem."Yes, that is correct," I say.
"Želite plačati s kartico ali z gotovino?" vpraša."Do you want to pay with card or cash?" he asks.

"V redu.""Fine."
"To bo petinosemdeset kron," reče."That will be eighty-five kroner," he says.

paying and leaving
Storyboard: Maya taps her card on the terminal to pay eighty-five kroner. She puts on her heavy coat, thanks the staff for a nice time, and pushes the front door open to face the cold again.
Barista se mi nasmehne.The barista smiles at me.
Svojo kartico podržim nad terminalom.I hold my card over the terminal.
Naprava zapiska.The machine beeps.
"Želite račun?" vpraša."Do you want a receipt?" he asks.
"Ne, hvala," odgovorim."No thank you," I answer.

"Najlepša hvala za kavo," rečem."Thank you very much for the coffee," I say.
"Ni za kaj," reče z nasmehom."You are welcome," he says with a smile.

"Enako," rečem."Likewise," I say.

Veter je hladen, a se počutim dobro.The wind is cold, but I feel good.
