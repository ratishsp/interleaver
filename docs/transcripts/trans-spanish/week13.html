Week 13: Maya goes to a café

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.

Camino por la calle.I am walking down the street.
El viento está muy frío.The wind is very cold.
Hoy sopla fuerte.It is blowing hard today.
Me inclino contra el viento frío.I lean against the cold wind.
Me subo la bufanda.I pull my scarf up.
Se me congela la cara.My face is freezing.
Miro a mi alrededor.I look around.
Me gustaría entrar al calor.I would like to get into the warmth.
Veo un pequeño café en la esquina.I see a small café on the corner.
Las ventanas están empañadas.The windows are fogged up.
Se ve cálido ahí dentro.It looks warm in there.
Camino hacia el café.I walk over to the café.
Abro la puerta pesada.I open the heavy door.
Paso adentro.I step inside.
Aquí huele a café.It smells like coffee here.
El aire está agradable y cálido.The air is nice and warm.
Cierro la puerta detrás de mí.I close the door behind me.
El viento frío se queda afuera.The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
Ahora estoy dentro de la cafetería.I am inside the café now.
Hace mucho calor aquí.It is very warm here.
Me quito mi abrigo grueso de invierno.I take off my thick winter coat.
Es pesado.It is heavy.
Encuentro un gancho en la pared.I find a hook on the wall.
Cuelgo mi abrigo en el gancho.I hang my coat on the hook.
También me quito la bufanda y los guantes.I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
La cafetería es pequeña y acogedora.The café is small and cozy.
Busco un buen lugar.I look for a good seat.
Hay una mesa pequeña junto a la ventana.There is a small table by the window.
La mesa está al lado de un radiador.The table is next to a radiator.
Camino hacia la mesa.I walk over to the table.
Me siento en la silla.I sit down on the chair.
Mis manos aún están frías.My hands are still cold.
Las froto entre sí.I rub them together.
Mis manos se calientan.My hands get warmer.
Se siente fantástico.It feels fantastic.
Ahora casi he entrado en calor.Now I am almost warm.
Tengo hambre y sed.I am hungry and thirsty.
Me gustaría pedir algo ahora.I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
Me acerco al mostrador de cristal.I walk over to the glass counter.
Está lleno de pasteles y pan.It is full of cakes and bread.
Miro la gran cantidad de cosas.I look at the many things.
Se ven muy deliciosas.They look very delicious.
Hay pequeños letreros junto a los pasteles.There are small signs next to the cakes.
Los letreros están escritos a mano.The signs are handwritten.
Leo los nombres en los letreros.I read the names on the signs.
Hay muchos tipos de pan dulce.There are many kinds of pastry.
Es difícil elegir.It is hard to choose.
Hay un pastel de chocolate grande.There is a large chocolate cake.
Pero miro otra pieza de pan dulce.But I look at another piece of pastry.
Es un rollo de canela grande.It is a large cinnamon roll.
Tiene mucha azúcar encima.It has a lot of sugar on top.
Me encanta el sabor de la canela.I love the taste of cinnamon.
Es perfecto para un día frío de invierno.It is perfect for a cold winter day.
Me gustaría mucho pedir el rollo de canela.I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
Ahora estoy lista para ordenar.Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
Me acerco a la caja.I walk over to the register.
Un joven está detrás del mostrador.A young man stands behind the counter.
Es barista en la cafetería.He is a barista in the café.
Le sonrío.I smile at him.
Él asiente amablemente.He nods warmly.
"Hola", dice."Hi," he says.
"¿Qué te gustaría?", pregunta."What would you like?" he asks.
"Hola", respondo."Hi," I answer.
"Quisiera un café negro, por favor"."I would like a black coffee, please."
"¿Y podría pedir también un rol de canela?"."And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
"Por supuesto", dice."Of course," he says.
"Un café negro y un rol de canela"."A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
"¿Te gustaría sentarte aquí?", pregunta."Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
"Sí, por favor, me sentaré allá", digo."Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
"Muy bien", dice el barista."Alright," the barista says.
"Voy a preparar tu café ahora"."I will make your coffee now."
"Muchas gracias", digo."Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
Espero en el mostrador.I wait at the counter.
Él prepara mi café.He makes my coffee.
Pone una taza de café caliente en una bandeja de madera.He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
También pone un plato pequeño en la bandeja.He also puts a small plate on the tray.
Mi rollo de canela está en el plato.My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
Huele delicioso.It smells lovely.
"Aquí tienes, tu pedido", dice él."Here you go, your order," he says.
"Muchas gracias", respondo."Thank you very much," I reply.
"Gracias por el buen servicio"."Thank you for the good service."
"De nada", dice amablemente."You are welcome," he says kindly.
Tomo la bandeja con ambas manos.I take the tray with both hands.
Camino con mucho cuidado por el piso.I walk very carefully across the floor.
Regreso a mi mesa junto a la ventana.I walk back to my table by the window.
Pongo la bandeja en la mesa.I put the tray down on the table.
Ahora me gustaría sentarme.Now I would like to sit down.
Estoy lista para un buen descanso.I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
Ahora me siento a mi pequeña mesa.I now sit at my small table.
Aquí hace un calor agradable.It is lovely and warm here.
Miro mi rollo de canela.I look at my cinnamon roll.
Huele dulce y rico.It smells sweet and good.
Doy un pequeño bocado.I take a small bite.
El panecillo está totalmente fresco y caliente.The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
Sabe realmente bien.It tastes really good.
Tomo un poco de mi café negro.I drink a little of my black coffee.
Por fin, mis dedos están calientes de nuevo.Finally, my fingers are warm again.
Me relajo en la silla mullida.I relax in the soft chair.
La cafetería está casi llena de gente.The café is almost full of people.
Las personas están sentadas en pequeños grupos.People sit in small groups.
Platican y ríen juntos.They talk and laugh together.
Escucho las voces bajas en el lugar.I listen to the low voices in the room.
El sonido es muy acogedor.The sound is very cozy.
Miro por la gran ventana.I look out of the large window.
El clima afuera está gris y ventoso.The weather outside is gray and windy.
De hecho, ahora está nevando de nuevo.Now it is actually snowing again.
Copos de nieve blancos caen lentamente sobre la calle.White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
Simplemente estoy feliz de estar adentro.I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
Me como el último pedazo de mi rollo de canela.I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
Sabe muy bien.It tastes really good.
Pero también es muy dulce.But it is also very sweet.
Mi taza de café está completamente vacía.My coffee cup is completely empty.
De repente tengo mucha sed.I am suddenly very thirsty.
Me gustaría un poco de agua fría.I would like to have some cold water.
Un joven pasa por mi mesa.A young man walks past my table.
Él trabaja aquí en la cafetería.He works here in the café.
Recoge tazas y platos vacíos.He collects empty cups and plates.
Capto su mirada.I catch his eye.
Se detiene en mi mesa y sonríe.He stops at my table and smiles.
—Disculpe —digo cortésmente."Excuse me," I say politely.
—¿Sí? —responde el joven."Yes?" answers the young man.
—¿Puedo pedir un vaso de agua del grifo? —pregunto."May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
—Sí, por supuesto —dice él."Yes, of course," he says.
—Muchas gracias —digo."Thank you very much," I say.
Toma mi plato vacío y mi taza.He takes my empty plate and cup.
Regresa al mostrador.He walks back to the counter.
Poco después, llega con un vaso de agua fría.A little later he comes with a cold glass of water.
asking for the bill
Storyboard: Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab.
Tomo mi agua.I drink my water.
El agua está fría y rica.The water is cold and nice.

Ya terminé mi descanso.I am finished with my break.

El amable barista sigue ahí parado.The friendly barista is still standing there.

"¿Te gustaría algo más?", pregunta."Would you like anything else?" he asks.
"No, gracias", respondo."No thank you," I answer.
"Quisiera pagar.""I would like to pay."
