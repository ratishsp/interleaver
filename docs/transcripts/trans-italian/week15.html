Week 15: Maya's everyday life in the city

morning weather
Storyboard: Maya wakes up early and looks out the window. The sun shines, but the wind is cold, so she puts on a warm sweater for the day.

Guardo fuori verso la città.I look out at the city.

Vado in bagno.I go into the bathroom.
Mi lavo il viso.I wash my face.
Mi lavo i denti.I brush my teeth.
Poi mi vesto.Then I get dressed.
Indosso un maglione spesso e caldo.I put on a thick, warm sweater.

Ora sono completamente sveglia.Now I am completely awake.
Sono pronta per il nuovo giorno.I am ready for the new day.
the bus commute

Anche loro aspettano l'autobus.They are also waiting for the bus.

Arriva un grande autobus rosso.A big red bus arrives.

working with peter

"Buongiorno, Maya," dice Peter."Good morning, Maya," Peter says.

Mi siedo alla mia scrivania.I sit at my desk.

canteen lunch

È ora di pranzo.It is lunch time.

giving directions

Non è lontano.It is not far away.

Proseguo verso la panetteria.I walk on towards the bakery.
buying a cake
Storyboard: On the way home, she stops at a small bakery on the corner. She orders a chocolate cake for the evening and pays sixty-five kroner to the smiling baker.

Entro nel negozio caldo.I go into the warm shop.
