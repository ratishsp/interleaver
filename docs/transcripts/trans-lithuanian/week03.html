Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

Lithuanian with English gloss, line-aligned.

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
Sausis, ir labai šalta.It is January, and it is very cold.
Einu namo iš autobusų stotelės Kopenhagoje.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Gatvėje matau gėlių parduotuvę.I see a flower shop on the street.
Viduje šviesu ir šilta.It is bright and warm inside.
Įeinu į šiltą parduotuvę.I go into the warm shop.
Žiūriu į mažą žalią augalą.I look at a small, green plant.
Pardavėjas man nusišypso.A clerk smiles at me.
„Sveiki,“ – sako jis."Hello," he says.
„Sveiki,“ – sakau."Hello," I say.
„Ieškau augalo.“"I am looking for a plant."
„Ar tai dovana?“ – klausia jis."Is it a gift?" he asks.
„Ne, tai mano kambariui,“ – atsakau."No, it is for my room," I answer.
„Tai tikrai geras augalas,“ – sako jis."It is a really good plant," he says.
„Kiek jis kainuoja?“ – klausiu."How much does it cost?" I ask.
„Jis kainuoja penkiasdešimt penkias kronas,“ – atsako jis."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
Sumoku už savo naują augalą.I pay for my new plant.
Jis įdeda augalą į popierinį maišelį.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
„Prašau, štai jūsų maišelis,“ – sako jis."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
„Labai ačiū,“ – sakau laiminga."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Išeinu į šaltą vakarą.I go out into the cold evening.
Mano mažas augalas mane labai džiugina.My little plant makes me very happy.
evening prep
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her temporary room and sets the new plant on a small desk. With it already being pitch black outside, she turns on a warm lamp to reflect against the dark window, makes a cup of tea, and flips open her laptop to dial home.
Aš dabar namuose.I am home now.
Štai mano kambarys.Here is my room.
Tai mažas kambarys.It is a small room.
Turiu naują augalą.I have a new plant.
Jis stovi ant mano rašomojo stalo.It stands on my desk.
Dabar vakaras ir sausis.It is evening, and it is January.
Mano langas labai tamsus.My window is very dark.
Lauke visiškai juoda.It is completely black outside.
Turiu lempą ir puodelį arbatos.I have a lamp and a cup of tea.
Įjungiu savo lempą.I turn on my lamp.
Dabar mano kambarys šiltas ir šviesus.Now my room is warm and bright.
Mano arbata taip pat šilta.My tea is also warm.
Sėdžiu prie savo rašomojo stalo.I sit at my desk.
Čia turiu savo kompiuterį.I have my computer here.
Atidarau savo kompiuterį.I open my computer.
Dabar skambinu savo šeimai į Meksiką.Now I call my family in Mexico.
Nekantrauju juos pamatyti.I look forward to seeing them.
parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
Suskamba porą kartų.It rings a couple of times.
Ekranas įsižiebia.The screen lights up.
Matau svetainę.I see a living room.
Šviečia saulė, ir svetainė labai šviesi.There is sun, and the living room is very bright.
Matau du žmones.I see two people.
Tai mano mama ir tėtis.It is my mother and my father.
Jie daug šypsosi ir man moja.They smile a lot and wave to me.
„Labas, mama ir tėti!“ – garsiai sakau."Hello mom and dad!" I say loudly.
„Labas, Maya“, – sako mano tėtis."Hello, Maya," my father says.
„Kaip sekasi?“"How is it going?"
„Sekasi gerai“, – džiugiai atsakau."It is going well," I answer happily.
Staiga išgirstu garsą.Suddenly I hear a sound.
Tai nedidelis amtelėjimas.It is a little bark.
„O, girdiu šunį!“ – sakau ir juokiuosi."Oh, I hear a dog!" I say and laugh.
Mažas šuo užšoka ant sofos.A little dog jumps up onto a sofa.
Jis žiūri į kamerą.It looks into the camera.
„Štai mano šuo!“"There is my dog!"
„Labas, Lola“, – sakau."Hello, Lola," I say.
Lola vizgina uodegą.Lola wags her tail.
„Taip, tai tavo šuo“, – su šypsena sako mano mama."Yes, it is your dog," my mother says with a smile.
Aš myliu savo šeimą.I love my family.
Gera juos visus matyti.It is lovely to see them all.
the siblings
Storyboard: Her younger sister Sofía and older brother Diego squeeze into the frame, playfully shoving each other. Sofía proudly holds up a thick university textbook, while Diego jokingly asks Maya if she is surviving the freezing Danish winter.
Dabar mano ekrane yra du nauji veidai.Now there are two new faces on my screen.
Tai mano jaunesnioji sesuo Sofija ir vyresnysis brolis Diegas.It is my little sister, Sofía, and my big brother, Diego.
Jie juokiasi ir šiek tiek stumdosi.They laugh and push each other a little.
„Labas, mažoji sese ir vyresnysis broli!“ – linksmai sakau."Hello little sister and big brother!" I say happily.
„Labas, Maja“, – sako Diegas."Hi Maya," says Diego.
Mano brolis plačiai šypsosi.My brother has a big smile.
„Ar Danijoje šalta?“ – klausia jis."Is it cold in Denmark?" he asks.
„Ar tai šalta žiema?“"Is it a cold winter?"
„Taip, tai labai šalta žiema“, – juokiuosi aš."Yes, it is a very cold winter," I laugh.
„Bet aš turiu šiltą kambarį ir puodelį arbatos.“"But I have a warm room and a cup of tea."
Dabar Sofija priešais ekraną pakelia didelį daiktą.Now Sofía lifts a big thing up in front of the screen.
Tai didelė knyga.It is a large book.
„Ar ten turi naują knygą, Sofija?“ – klausiu."Do you have a new book there, Sofía?" I ask.
„Taip, tai mano nauja knyga“, – išdidžiai sako mano sesuo."Yes, it is my new book," my sister says proudly.
„Tai knyga iš mano universiteto.“"It is a book from my university."
„Tai labai stora knyga!“"It is a very thick book!"
Šypsausi savo broliui ir seseriai.I smile at my brother and my sister.
Pasiilgau savo šeimos Meksikoje.I miss my family in Mexico.
Bet jie yra čia pat, mano mažame ekrane.But they are right here on my little screen.
temporary room tour
Storyboard: Her mother asks for a tour of her current living space. Maya pans the camera around to show her new plant and the small bed, explaining that it is just a temporary room and pointing out the black, sunless window.
Mano mama žiūri į ekraną.My mother looks at the screen.
„Ar tai tavo kambarys, Maya?“ – klausia ji."Is that your room, Maya?" she asks.
„Taip, tai mano kambarys“, – atsakau."Yes, it is my room," I answer.
„Bet tai laikinas kambarys.“"But it is a temporary room."
Pakeliu savo kompiuterį.I lift my computer.
Parodau savo mažą kambarį šeimai.I show my small room to my family.
„Čia yra lova“, – sakau."Here is a bed," I say.
„O čia mano naujas augalas.“"And here is my new plant."
„Tai labai gražus augalas“, – sako mano mama."It is a very nice plant," my mother says.
Pasuku savo kompiuterį.I turn my computer.
„O čia yra langas“, – sakau."And here is a window," I say.
Mano sesuo Sofija žiūri į mano langą.My sister Sofía looks at my window.
„Bet labai tamsu“, – sako ji."But it is very dark," she says.
„Taip, ten lauke visiškai juoda“, – atsakau."Yes, it is completely black out there," I answer.
„Bet vasarą būna daug saulės.“"But in summer there is plenty of sun."
Vakaras, ir šalta.It is evening, and it is cold.
„Bet aš turiu savo lempą“, – nusišypsau."But I have my lamp," I smile.
„Taigi tai geras kambarys.“"So it is a good room."
mexico garden
Storyboard: Feeling a bit homesick, Maya asks her father to take the phone outside. He walks out to their sunny garden, showing a large, familiar leafy tree. Maya smiles deeply, feeling warm and comforted by the glimpse of home before they all wave goodbye.
Žiūriu į savo kompiuterį.I look at my computer.
Šiek tiek pasiilgau savo namų Meksikoje.I miss my home in Mexico a little bit.
„Tėti, kaip tavo sodas?“ – sakau aš."Dad, how is your garden?" I say.
Mano tėtis man šypsosi.My dad smiles at me.
Jis išeina į lauką su telefonu.He goes outside with the phone.
Ten yra didelis sodas.There is a large garden.
Ten labai šviečia saulė.The sun is shining a lot there.
„Žiūrėk, Maya“, – sako mano tėtis."Look, Maya," my dad says.
„Čia yra sodas ir medis.“"Here is the garden and a tree."
Tai labai didelis medis.It is a very big tree.
Tai gražus medis.It is a beautiful tree.
Šypsausi į savo ekraną.I smile at my screen.
Viduje man gera ir šilta.I feel good and warm inside.
Mano mama, sesuo ir brolis taip pat išeina į lauką.My mother, my sister, and my brother come outside too.
Mano šuo bėgioja po medžiu.My dog runs under the tree.
Visa mano šeima man moja.My whole family waves at me.
„Viso gero visiems!“ – sakau aš."Goodbye, everyone!" I say.
„Viso gero, Maya“, – sako jie."Goodbye, Maya," they say.
Uždarau savo kompiuterį.I close my computer.
