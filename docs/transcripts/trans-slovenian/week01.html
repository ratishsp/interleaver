Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
Letalo je zdaj v Københavnu.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
Januar je.It is January.
Zelo sem vznemirjena.I am very excited.
Knjigo spravim v torbo.I pack my book into my bag.
Zraven mene je potnik.There is a passenger next to me.
Pogleda me in se nasmehne.He looks at me and smiles.
"Živijo," reče."Hello," he says.
"Živijo," rečem."Hello," I say.
"Kako ti je ime?" vpraša."What is your name?" he asks.
"Ime mi je Maya," rečem."My name is Maya," I say.
"Od kod prihajaš, Maya?" vpraša."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
Veselo se nasmehnem.I smile happily.
"Prihajam iz Mehike," odgovorim."I come from Mexico," I answer.
"Si iz Danske?" vprašam."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
"Ja, iz Danske sem," reče."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
"Dobrodošla na Danskem," reče."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
"Najlepša hvala," rečem."Thank you very much," I say.
Zdaj smo tukaj.Now we are here.
Pripravljena sem.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
Zdaj sem zunaj.I am outside now.
Moj kovček je velik in težek.My suitcase is big and heavy.
Tam je taksi.There is a taxi.
Svoj kovček dvignem v taksi.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
Voznik je prijazen moški.The driver is a friendly man.
"Kakšen je naslov?" vpraša."What is the address?" he asks.
"To je Parkvej številka dvanajst," rečem."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
Pokima in odpelje.He nods and drives.
"Ste turistka?" vpraša voznik."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
"Ne, nisem turistka," odgovorim."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
"Od kod prihajate?" vpraša."Where do you come from?" he asks.
"Prihajam iz Mehike," rečem."I come from Mexico," I say.
Gledam skozi okno.I look out the window.
Zelo sem ponosna.I am very proud.
"Sem inženirka programske opreme," rečem."I am a software engineer," I say.
"Moja nova služba je tukaj v Københavnu.""My new job is here in Copenhagen."
Nasmehne se.He smiles.
"Kako zanimivo.""How exciting."
"Veliko sreče na Danskem," reče."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
"Najlepša hvala," rečem."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
Vrata so odprta.The door is open.
Prag je visok.The threshold is high.
Kovček je zelo težek!The suitcase is very heavy!
Ampak zdaj je kovček na hodniku.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
Tukaj je čisto temno.It is completely dark here.
Kje je stikalo?Where is the switch?
Stena je hladna.The wall is cold.
Je stikalo tukaj?Is the switch here?
Ja, tam je stikalo!Yes, there is the switch!
Zdaj je svetlo.Now there is light.
Sem v stanovanju.I am in the apartment.
Je majhno, ampak je lepo.It is small, but it is nice.
Tukaj je dnevna soba.Here is a living room.
Tam sta kavč in miza.There is a sofa and a table.
To je opremljen dom.It is a furnished home.
Kovček je na tleh.The suitcase is on the floor.
Zelo sem utrujena.I am very tired.
Ampak sem tudi vesela.But I am also happy.
Sem v Københavnu!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
Stojim ob oknu.I am standing by the window.
Steklo je mrzlo.The glass is cold.
Januar je.It is January.
Gledam ven.I look out.
Kopenhagen je danes siv.Copenhagen is grey today.
Zunaj je mrzlo.It is cold outside.
Na ulici je sneg.There is snow on the street.
Ampak tukaj je toplo.But it is warm here.
Stanovanje je dobro.The apartment is good.
Mislim na Mehiko.I think about Mexico.
V Mehiki je sonce.In Mexico, there is sun.
Tudi tam je toplo.It is also warm there.
Malo pogrešam sonce.I miss the sun a little.
Ampak se nasmehnem.But I smile.
To je novo mesto.It is a new city.
Zdaj sem na Danskem.I am in Denmark now.
To je dober začetek.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
Zdaj sem v dnevni sobi.I am in the living room now.
Tukaj je majhen kavč.There is a small sofa.
Pokličem mamo v Mehiko.I call my mother in Mexico.
"Živjo, mami," rečem."Hi Mom," I say.
"Živjo, Maya," reče moja mama."Hi Maya," my mother says.
"Kje si?" vpraša."Where are you?" she asks.
"V Kopenhagnu sem," odgovorim."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
"Zdaj sem v stanovanju.""I am in the apartment now."
"To je tako dobro," reče moja mama."That is so good," my mother says.
"Je veliko?" vpraša."Is it big?" she asks.
"Ne, majhno je," odgovorim."No, it is small," I answer.
"Ampak je prijetno in srečna sem.""But it is cozy, and I am happy."
"Je mrzlo?" vpraša."Is it cold?" she asks.
"Ja, zunaj je mrzlo," rečem."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
"Ampak tukaj je toplo.""But it is warm in here."
"Na varnem sem.""I am safe."
"To je dobro," reče moja mama."That is good," my mother says.
Nasmehnem se.I smile.
Danska je moj novi dom.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
Odprem svoja vrata.I open my door.
Stojim na stopnišču.I am standing in the stairwell.
Tiho je.It is quiet.
Vrata se odprejo.A door opens.
Ženska se mi nasmehne.A woman smiles at me.
"Živjo," reče."Hi," she says.
"Kako ti je ime?" vpraša."What is your name?" she asks.
"Ime mi je Maya," odgovorim."My name is Maya," I answer.
"Od kod prihajaš?" vpraša."Where do you come from?" she asks.
"Prihajam iz Mehike," rečem."I come from Mexico," I say.
"Dobrodošla v stavbi," reče ženska."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
"Ime mi je Nina," reče."My name is Nina," she says.
"Sem tvoja soseda.""I am your neighbour."
Nina je zelo prijazna.Nina is very friendly.
Govori počasi in razločno.She speaks slowly and clearly.
"Tukaj so moja vrata," reče."Here is my door," she says.
"Z veseljem pomagam.""I am happy to help."
"Kar potrkaj," reče Nina."You just knock," Nina says.
"Najlepša hvala, Nina," odgovorim."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
Zelo sem vesela.I am very happy.
Zdaj imam prijateljico v Kopenhagnu.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
Zdaj je noč.Now it is night.
To je moja prva noč v Københavnu.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
Sem v kopalnici.I am in the bathroom.
Zelo je majhna.It is very small.
Tu je umivalnik.There is a sink.
Moja zobna ščetka je rdeča.My toothbrush is red.
Na zobno ščetko dam zobno pasto.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
Ščetkam si zobe.I brush my teeth.
Voda je mrzla.The water is cold.
Umijem si obraz.I wash my face.
Obrišem si obraz.I dry my face.
Grem do svoje postelje.I go over to my bed.
Postelja je ozka.The bed is narrow.
Ampak je topla in mehka.But it is warm and soft.
Zdaj ležim pod odejo.I am lying under the duvet now.
Moj telefon leži na mizi.My phone is lying on the table.
Nastavim budilko.I set my alarm.
Jutri zvoni ob osmih.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
Utrujena sem, ampak sem pripravljena.I am tired, but I am ready.
Lahko noč, København.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
