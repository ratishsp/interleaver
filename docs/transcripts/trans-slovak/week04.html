Week 4: Maya moves into her own flat

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

final sweep
Storyboard: Maya stands in her temporary room in the building. She zips up her large suitcases and checks the space one last time, making sure nothing lies under the small bed or stands forgotten in the closet.
Je január v Kodani.It is January in Copenhagen.
Stojím vo svojej dočasnej izbe.I am standing in my temporary room.
Dnes sa sťahujem do nového bytu.Today I am moving to a new apartment.
Je v tej istej budove.It is in the same building.
Môj veľký kufor stojí na podlahe.My large suitcase stands on the floor.
Zatváram ho.I close it.
Poslednýkrát sa rozhliadam po izbe.I look around the room one last time.
Pozerám sa pod malú posteľ.I look under the small bed.
Pod posteľou leží kniha.There is a book lying under the bed.
Je z Mexika.It is from Mexico.
Dávam knihu do tašky.I put the book in my bag.
Otváram skriňu.I open the closet.
V skrini je pár topánok.There is a pair of shoes standing in the closet.
Beriem topánky.I take the shoes.
Teraz v skrini nie je nič.Now there is nothing in the closet.
Idem k písaciemu stolu pri okne.I walk over to the desk by the window.
Na stole leží kábel.There is a cable lying on the desk.
Beriem kábel.I take the cable.
Dávam kábel do tašky.I put the cable in my bag.
Beriem svoje dve tašky.I take my two bags.
Zhasínam svetlo a zatváram dvere.I turn off the light and close the door.
unlocking the door
Storyboard: Carrying her first few bags up the communal stairs, Maya arrives at the door of her new, permanent flat. She turns the key, pushes the door open, and steps inside, thrilled to finally see the small furnished space waiting for her.
Idem hore schodmi.I walk up the stairs.
V rukách mám dve tašky.I have two bags in my hands.
Konečne som pri svojich nových dverách.I am finally at my new door.
Vo vrecku mám kľúč.I have a key in my pocket.
Položím tašky na podlahu.I put the bags down on the floor.
Vyberiem kľúč.I take out the key.
Odomknem dvere.I unlock the door.
Potlačím dvere a vojdem dnu.I push the door open and go inside.
Byt je malý, ale je dokonalý.The apartment is small, but it is perfect.
Má pekný, svetlý nábytok.It has nice, light furniture.
Pozriem sa do obývačky.I look into the living room.
Pri okne stojí pohovka.There is a sofa standing by the window.
Pod pohovkou je koberec.There is a rug under the sofa.
Na pohovke leží malý vankúš.There is a small cushion lying on the sofa.
V rohu stojí lampa.There is a lamp standing in the corner.
Zatvorím za sebou dvere.I close the door behind me.
Položím svoj kľúč na stôl.I put my key on the table.
ninas help
Storyboard: Maya steps back out to the shared stairwell to grab her heaviest suitcase. Nina comes up the stairs, spots her struggling, and cheerfully helps carry the bag inside, setting it carefully on the floor by the large living room window.
Znova vychádzam na schodisko.I walk out into the stairwell again.
Môj najväčší kufor stojí na podlahe.My biggest suitcase stands on the floor.
Je veľmi ťažký.It is very heavy.
Ťahám za kufor.I pull on the suitcase.
Zrazu počujem na schodoch kroky.Suddenly I hear footsteps on the stairs.
Je to moja dánska susedka Nina.It is my Danish neighbor, Nina.
Vrúcne sa na mňa usmieva.She smiles warmly at me.
„Sťahuješ sa dnes, Maya?“ pýta sa."Are you moving in today, Maya?" she asks.
„Áno, konečne som vo svojom novom byte,“ hovorím."Yes, I am finally in my new apartment," I say.
„Pomôžem ti,“ hovorí Nina milo."I will help you," Nina says kindly.
Chytí kufor.She grabs hold of the suitcase.
Spolu nesieme kufor do obývačky.Together we carry the suitcase into the living room.
Nina ho opatrne položí na podlahu.Nina sets it carefully on the floor.
Teraz kufor stojí pri veľkom okne.Now the suitcase stands by the large window.
Pod oknom už leží taška.There already lies a bag under the window.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem za pomoc, Nina,“ hovorím radostne."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say happily.
„Niet za čo,“ usmieva sa."You are welcome," she smiles.
Nina vychádza na schodisko.Nina walks out into the stairwell.
Zatváram dvere a pozerám sa na kufor pri okne.I close the door and look at the suitcase by the window.
kitchen unpacking
Storyboard: Maya opens a travel bag in the small kitchen and begins organizing. She stacks her favorite mugs in the cupboard and makes sure her red kettle stands on the counter, right by the sink.
Vyjdem do malej kuchyne.I walk out into the small kitchen.
Moja cestovná taška stojí na podlahe.My travel bag stands on the floor.
Otvorím tašku.I open the bag.
V taške je veľa vecí.There are many things in the bag.
Moje obľúbené hrnčeky ležia v taške.My favorite mugs lie in the bag.
Vyberiem hrnčeky.I take the mugs out.
Nad kuchynskou linkou je skrinka.There is a cupboard above the counter.
Vložím hrnčeky do skrinky.I put the mugs in the cupboard.
Teraz stoja na polici.Now they stand on the shelf.
Mám aj červenú rýchlovarnú kanvicu.I also have a red electric kettle.
Leží v taške pod hrubým svetrom.It lies in the bag under a thick sweater.
Vyberiem rýchlovarnú kanvicu.I take the electric kettle out.
Položím ju na kuchynskú linku.I put it on the kitchen counter.
Teraz stojí červená rýchlovarná kanvica hneď pri dreze.Now there stands a red electric kettle right by the sink.
V taške leží aj škatuľka čaju.There also lies a box of tea in the bag.
Vezmem škatuľku a položím ju k rýchlovarnej kanvici.I take the box and place it by the electric kettle.
Teraz je kuchyňa takmer pripravená.Now the kitchen is almost ready.
Teším sa, že si neskôr dám šálku čaju.I look forward to a cup of tea later.
the workspace

Pri okne je malý písací stôl.There is a small desk by the window.
Ten stôl je na moju prácu.The table is for my work.

Je to pekné malé miesto.It is a lovely little spot.

Na stole je aj lampa.There is also a lamp on the table.
Lampa stojí hneď pri počítači.The lamp stands right by the computer.
Má teplé svetlo.It has a warm light.
Na stole leží aj malá kniha.There is also a small book lying on the table.
Mám malú čiernu tašku s káblami.I have a small black bag with cables.
Leží pod písacím stolom.It lies under the desk.
Takto tá taška nezavadzia.That way, the bag is not in the way.
Pri písacom stole je dobrá stolička.There is a good chair by the desk.
Sedím na stoličke a pozerám sa.I sit in the chair and look.
Otváram počítač a začínam písať.I open the computer and begin to type.

Deka je z Mexika.The blanket is from Mexico.

Sú veľmi mäkké.They are very soft.
Pri stene stojí malá stolička.There stands a small chair by the wall.
Položím svoje papuče na podlahu.I put my slippers on the floor.
Teraz ležia pod stoličkou.Now they lie under the chair.

Zatvorím ho a dám ho pod posteľ.I close it and put it under the bed.
the plant

celebratory pizza
