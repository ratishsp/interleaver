# Week 6 — Storyboard (Maya's daily routine)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Present continuous **-ുകയാണ്** for actions underway, woven naturally through a simple-present **-ഉന്നു** routine

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_coffee | Maya wakes up early in her peaceful Kochi flat. The February morning is pleasant. She brushes her teeth, gets dressed, and makes a hot cup of coffee, drinking it while she prepares for the day. |
| 2 | 02_bus_to_infopark | Maya leaves her apartment building and walks to the local bus stop. She boards a crowded bus going to her office. The conductor comes through the crowd and gives her a ticket, and she stands holding the rail while the bus is moving through the busy city. |
| 3 | 03_settling_at_the_desk | Maya enters her software company building and greets the security guard. She walks to her desk, turns on her computer, and is soon reading her morning emails. |
| 4 | 04_morning_meeting | Maya attends a morning team meeting in a small conference room. She sits with her colleagues and listens carefully while the team leader is explaining the plan for a new software project. |
| 5 | 05_lunch_with_prakash | At midday, Maya and her colleague Prakash walk to the office cafeteria. Prakash is eating a traditional Kerala meal, while Maya opens her lunchbox. She eats a simple meal of rice and vegetables. |
| 6 | 06_afternoon_tea_break | In the late afternoon, Maya leaves her desk and goes to the office pantry for a short break. She makes a cup of tea and relaxes for a few quiet minutes in the pantry. |
| 7 | 07_evening_commute | Maya leaves the office and waits for the return bus. The sun is setting. She boards the bus and looks out the window, watching the familiar streets as she travels home. |
| 8 | 08_cooking_dinner | Back inside her flat, Maya changes into comfortable clothes and goes to the kitchen. She is chopping onions and tomatoes, cooking a simple dinner using the fresh vegetables she buys locally. |
| 9 | 09_calling_mexico | After eating, Maya sits on her sofa and starts a video call with her family in Mexico. She smiles as she watches her younger sister Sofia, who is playing with their dog Lola on the screen. |
| 10 | 10_falling_asleep | Maya finishes her call and washes her dinner plate. She turns off the lights in the flat, gets into bed, and goes to sleep, ready to start her routine again the next day. |
