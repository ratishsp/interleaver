Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
Ni mwezi wa Januari, na kuna baridi sana.It is January, and it is very cold.
Ninatembea kuelekea nyumbani kutoka kwenye kituo cha basi huko Copenhagen.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Ninaona duka la maua barabarani.I see a flower shop on the street.
Kuna mwanga na joto ndani.It is bright and warm inside.
Ninaingia ndani ya duka lenye joto.I go into the warm shop.
Ninatazama mmea mdogo wa kijani.I look at a small, green plant.
Mhudumu ananitabasamu.A clerk smiles at me.
"Hujambo," anasema."Hello," he says.
"Sijambo," ninasema."Hello," I say.
"Ninatafuta mmea.""I am looking for a plant."
"Ni zawadi?" anauliza."Is it a gift?" he asks.
"Hapana, ni kwa ajili ya chumba changu," ninajibu."No, it is for my room," I answer.
"Ni mmea mzuri sana," anasema."It is a really good plant," he says.
"Unagharimu kiasi gani?" ninauliza."How much does it cost?" I ask.
"Unagharimu kroner hamsini na tano," anajibu."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
Ninalipia mmea wangu mpya.I pay for my new plant.
Anaweka mmea kwenye mfuko wa karatasi.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
"Karibu, huu hapa mfuko wako," anasema."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
"Asante sana," ninasema kwa furaha."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Ninatoka nje kwenye jioni yenye baridi.I go out into the cold evening.
Mmea wangu mdogo unanifurahisha sana.My little plant makes me very happy.
evening prep
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her temporary room and sets the new plant on a small desk. With it already being pitch black outside, she turns on a warm lamp to reflect against the dark window, makes a cup of tea, and flips open her laptop to dial home.
Niko nyumbani sasa.I am home now.
Hiki hapa chumba changu.Here is my room.
Ni chumba kidogo.It is a small room.
Nina mmea mpya.I have a new plant.
Upo kwenye dawati langu.It stands on my desk.
Ni jioni, na ni Januari.It is evening, and it is January.
Dirisha langu lina giza sana.My window is very dark.
Nje kuna giza kabisa.It is completely black outside.
Nina taa na kikombe cha chai.I have a lamp and a cup of tea.
Ninawasha taa yangu.I turn on my lamp.
Sasa chumba changu kina joto na mwanga.Now my room is warm and bright.
Chai yangu pia ni ya moto.My tea is also warm.
Nimekaa kwenye dawati langu.I sit at my desk.
Nina kompyuta yangu hapa.I have my computer here.
Ninafungua kompyuta yangu.I open my computer.
Sasa ninaipigia simu familia yangu kule Mexico.Now I call my family in Mexico.
Ninatazamia kwa hamu kuwaona.I look forward to seeing them.
parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
Inaita mara kadhaa.It rings a couple of times.
Skrini inawaka.The screen lights up.
Ninaona sebule.I see a living room.
Kuna jua, na sebule ina mwanga mwingi.There is sun, and the living room is very bright.
Ninaona watu wawili.I see two people.
Ni mama yangu na baba yangu.It is my mother and my father.
Wanatabasamu sana na kunipungia mkono.They smile a lot and wave to me.
"Habari mama na baba!" ninasema kwa sauti kubwa."Hello mom and dad!" I say loudly.
"Habari, Maya," baba yangu anasema."Hello, Maya," my father says.
"Unaendeleaje?""How is it going?"
"Ninaendelea vizuri," ninajibu kwa furaha."It is going well," I answer happily.
Ghafla ninasikia sauti.Suddenly I hear a sound.
Ni mbweko mdogo.It is a little bark.
"Oh, ninasikia mbwa!" ninasema na kucheka."Oh, I hear a dog!" I say and laugh.
Mbwa mdogo anaruka juu ya sofa.A little dog jumps up onto a sofa.
Anatazama kwenye kamera.It looks into the camera.
"Huyo pale mbwa wangu!""There is my dog!"
"Habari, Lola," ninasema."Hello, Lola," I say.
Lola anatikisa mkia wake.Lola wags her tail.
"Ndiyo, ni mbwa wako," mama yangu anasema kwa tabasamu."Yes, it is your dog," my mother says with a smile.
Ninaipenda familia yangu.I love my family.
Inapendeza kuwaona wote.It is lovely to see them all.
the siblings
Storyboard: Her younger sister Sofía and older brother Diego squeeze into the frame, playfully shoving each other. Sofía proudly holds up a thick university textbook, while Diego jokingly asks Maya if she is surviving the freezing Danish winter.
Sasa kuna nyuso mbili mpya kwenye skrini yangu.Now there are two new faces on my screen.
Ni dada yangu mdogo, Sofía, na kaka yangu mkubwa, Diego.It is my little sister, Sofía, and my big brother, Diego.
Wanacheka na kusukumana kidogo.They laugh and push each other a little.
"Habari dada mdogo na kaka mkubwa!" ninasema kwa furaha."Hello little sister and big brother!" I say happily.
"Habari Maya," anasema Diego."Hi Maya," says Diego.
Kaka yangu ana tabasamu kubwa.My brother has a big smile.
"Je, kuna baridi Denmaki?" anauliza."Is it cold in Denmark?" he asks.
"Je, ni majira ya baridi kali?""Is it a cold winter?"
"Ndiyo, ni majira ya baridi kali sana," ninacheka."Yes, it is a very cold winter," I laugh.
"Lakini nina chumba chenye joto na kikombe cha chai.""But I have a warm room and a cup of tea."
Sasa Sofía anainua kitu kikubwa mbele ya skrini.Now Sofía lifts a big thing up in front of the screen.
Ni kitabu kikubwa.It is a large book.
"Je, una kitabu kipya hapo, Sofía?" ninauliza."Do you have a new book there, Sofía?" I ask.
"Ndiyo, ni kitabu changu kipya," dada yangu anasema kwa fahari."Yes, it is my new book," my sister says proudly.
"Ni kitabu kutoka chuo kikuu changu.""It is a book from my university."
"Ni kitabu kinene sana!""It is a very thick book!"
Ninawatabasamia kaka yangu na dada yangu.I smile at my brother and my sister.
Ninaikumbuka familia yangu huko Meksiko.I miss my family in Mexico.
Lakini wako hapa hapa kwenye skrini yangu ndogo.But they are right here on my little screen.
temporary room tour
Storyboard: Her mother asks for a tour of her current living space. Maya pans the camera around to show her new plant and the small bed, explaining that it is just a temporary room and pointing out the black, sunless window.
Mama yangu anatazama skrini.My mother looks at the screen.
"Hicho ni chumba chako, Maya?" anauliza."Is that your room, Maya?" she asks.
"Ndiyo, ni chumba changu," ninajibu."Yes, it is my room," I answer.
"Lakini ni chumba cha muda.""But it is a temporary room."
Ninainua kompyuta yangu.I lift my computer.
Ninaonyesha familia yangu chumba changu kidogo.I show my small room to my family.
"Hapa kuna kitanda," ninasema."Here is a bed," I say.
"Na hapa kuna mmea wangu mpya.""And here is my new plant."
"Ni mmea mzuri sana," mama yangu anasema."It is a very nice plant," my mother says.
Ninageuza kompyuta yangu.I turn my computer.
"Na hapa kuna dirisha," ninasema."And here is a window," I say.
Dada yangu Sofía anatazama dirisha langu.My sister Sofía looks at my window.
"Lakini kuna giza sana," anasema."But it is very dark," she says.
"Ndiyo, kuna giza kabisa huko nje," ninajibu."Yes, it is completely black out there," I answer.
"Lakini majira ya joto kuna jua jingi.""But in summer there is plenty of sun."
Ni jioni, na kuna baridi.It is evening, and it is cold.
"Lakini nina taa yangu," ninatabasamu."But I have my lamp," I smile.
"Kwa hivyo ni chumba kizuri.""So it is a good room."
mexico garden
Storyboard: Feeling a bit homesick, Maya asks her father to take the phone outside. He walks out to their sunny garden, showing a large, familiar leafy tree. Maya smiles deeply, feeling warm and comforted by the glimpse of home before they all wave goodbye.
Ninaangalia kompyuta yangu.I look at my computer.
Ninakumbuka nyumbani kwangu Mexico kidogo.I miss my home in Mexico a little bit.
"Baba, bustani yako ikoje?" ninasema."Dad, how is your garden?" I say.
Baba yangu ananitabasamia.My dad smiles at me.
Anaenda nje na simu.He goes outside with the phone.
Kuna bustani kubwa.There is a large garden.
Jua linawaka sana huko.The sun is shining a lot there.
"Angalia, Maya," baba yangu anasema."Look, Maya," my dad says.
"Hapa kuna bustani na mti.""Here is the garden and a tree."
Ni mti mkubwa sana.It is a very big tree.
Ni mti mzuri.It is a beautiful tree.
Ninatabasamia skrini yangu.I smile at my screen.
Ninajisikia vizuri na joto kwa ndani.I feel good and warm inside.
Mama yangu, dada yangu, na kaka yangu wanakuja nje pia.My mother, my sister, and my brother come outside too.
Mbwa wangu anakimbia chini ya mti.My dog runs under the tree.
Familia yangu yote inanipungia mkono.My whole family waves at me.
"Kwaherini nyote!" ninasema."Goodbye, everyone!" I say.
"Kwaheri, Maya," wanasema."Goodbye, Maya," they say.
Ninafunga kompyuta yangu.I close my computer.
