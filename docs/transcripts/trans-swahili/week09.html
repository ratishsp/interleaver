Week 9: Maya visits the winter market

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

arriving at the market
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a busy outdoor market in a large square. The winter morning is cold, but the stalls look inviting. She carries a large empty bag and looks for fresh food for the weekend.
Ni asubuhi ya Jumamosi.It is Saturday morning.
Ni majira ya baridi nchini Denmark.It is winter in Denmark.
Hali ya hewa ni baridi sana leo.The weather is very cold today.
Nimesimama kwenye uwanja mkubwa mjini.I am standing in a large square in the city.
Kuna soko la wazi hapa.There is an outdoor market here.
Ninatazama vibanda vingi.I look at the many stalls.
Vyote vinaonekana kuvutia.They all look inviting.
Kuna watu wengi kwenye uwanja.There are many people in the square.
Watu wamevaa nguo za joto za majira ya baridi.People are wearing warm winter clothes.
Nimevaa jaketi jembamba tu.I only have a thin jacket on.
Nimebeba begi kubwa.I have a large bag with me.
Begi ni tupu kabisa sasa hivi.The bag is completely empty right now.
Ninatafuta chakula safi kwa ajili ya wikendi.I am looking for fresh food for the weekend.
Soko lina bidhaa nyingi safi.The market has many fresh goods.
Ninaona mboga, matunda, na mkate.I see vegetables, fruit, and bread.
Ninafikiria kuhusu bei.I think about the prices.
Vitu vinagharimu kiasi gani hapa?How much do things cost here?
Ninatembea hadi kwenye kibanda cha kwanza.I walk over to the first stall.
carrots for soup
Storyboard: Maya stops at a vegetable stall to buy ingredients for a hot soup. She points at some winter carrots and asks how much they cost. The vendor says they cost fifteen kroner per kilo. Maya asks for one kilo and pays.
Ninatembea kuelekea kwenye kibanda cha mboga.I walk over to a vegetable stall.
Ninapika supu ya moto leo.I am making a hot soup today.
Ninatazama mboga nyingi zilizo mezani.I look at the many vegetables on the table.
Ninaonyesha baadhi ya karoti kubwa za msimu wa baridi.I point at some large winter carrots.
Ninahitaji karoti kwa supu yangu.I need carrots for my soup.
Kuna muuzaji amesimama nyuma ya meza.There is a vendor standing behind the table.
"Habari," anasema."Hello," he says.
"Habari," ninajibu."Hello," I reply.
"Karoti zinagharimu kiasi gani?" ninauliza."How much do the carrots cost?" I ask.
"Zinagharimu kroner kumi na tano kwa kilo," muuzaji anajibu."They cost fifteen kroner per kilo," the vendor answers.
Kroner kumi na tano ni bei nzuri.Fifteen kroner is a good price.
"Nitachukua kilo moja, tafadhali," ninasema."I will take one kilo, please," I say.
Muuzaji anapima karoti.The vendor weighs the carrots.
Anaziweka kwenye mfuko wa karatasi.He puts them in a paper bag.
Ananipa mfuko huo.He gives me the bag.
Ninalipa zile kroner kumi na tano.I pay the fifteen kroner.
Ninaweka mfuko huo ndani ya begi langu kubwa.I put the bag down in my large bag.
Ninasubiri kwa hamu supu hiyo ya moto.I look forward to the hot soup.
a taste of cheese
Storyboard: At the next stall, a friendly vendor sells large cheeses. He offers Maya a small piece on a wooden stick to taste. She eats it, likes it, and asks for the price of a small block. It costs fifty-five kroner, so she buys it.
Mbele yangu kuna kibanda kikubwa cha jibini.In front of me is a large cheese stall.
Kibanda kina jibini nyingi kubwa.The stall has many large cheeses.
Muuzaji mkarimu ananitabasamia.A friendly vendor smiles at me.
Ana kipande kidogo cha jibini kwenye kijiti cha mbao.He has a small piece of cheese on a wooden stick.
"Karibu, kionjo kidogo," anasema."Here you go, a small taste," he says.
Ananipa kijiti.He gives the stick to me.
Ninakula jibini ya njano.I eat the yellow cheese.
Ina ladha nzuri sana.It tastes really good.
Ninatazama jibini ndogo.I look at a small cheese.
"Jibini hii inagharimu kiasi gani?" ninauliza."How much does this cheese cost?" I ask.
"Inagharimu kroner hamsini na tano," muuzaji anajibu."It costs fifty-five kroner," the vendor answers.
Hiyo ni bei nzuri.That is a good price.
Ninainyooshea kidole jibini ndogo.I point at the small cheese.
"Nitainunua," ninasema."I will buy it," I say.
Anaifunga jibini kwenye karatasi.He wraps the cheese in paper.
Ninampa pesa.I give him the money.
"Asante sana," anasema."Thank you very much," he says.
Jibini ina harufu kali na nzuri.The cheese smells strong and good.
the expensive gloves
Storyboard: The wind blows cold, so Maya looks at a stall with warm clothes. She picks up a pair of thick wool gloves and asks how much the gloves cost. The vendor answers that the price is two hundred kroner. Maya thinks that is too dear, so she politely says no thank you and walks on.
Upepo unavuma kwa ubaridi leo.The wind blows cold today.
Mikono yangu ina baridi sana.My hands are very cold.
Ninaona kibanda kikubwa chenye nguo za joto.I see a large stall with warm clothes.
Ninatembea kuelekea kwa muuzaji.I walk over to the vendor.
Ana vitu vingi vya joto mezani.He has many warm things on the table.
Ninachukua pea ya glavu nene za sufu.I pick up a pair of thick wool gloves.
Ni laini sana.They are very soft.
"Glavu zinagharimu kiasi gani?" ninauliza."How much do the gloves cost?" I ask.
"Zinagharimu kroner mia mbili," muuzaji anajibu."They cost two hundred kroner," the vendor answers.
Ninazitazama glavu tena.I look at the gloves again.
Kroner mia mbili ni bei ya juu.Two hundred kroner is a high price.
Ni ghali sana kwangu.It is too expensive for me.
Ninarudisha glavu mezani.I put the gloves back on the table.
"Hapana asante," ninasema kwa heshima."No thank you," I say politely.
"Uwe na siku njema.""Have a good day."
Muuzaji anaitikia kwa kichwa na kutabasamu.The vendor nods and smiles.
"Na wewe pia," anasema."You too," he says.
Ninaendelea kutembea sokoni.I walk on around the market.
buying winter apples
Storyboard: Maya finds a fruit stall with baskets of dark red winter apples. She asks the vendor how much a small bag costs. The vendor smiles and says the price is twenty kroner. Maya hands him a paper note for fifty kroner. The vendor hands her the bag of apples and gives her exactly thirty kroner back in coins. Maya puts the heavy coins into her coat pocket.
Kuna watu wengi sokoni leo.There are many people at the market today.
Ninapata kibanda kidogo cha matunda.I find a small fruit stall.
Kuna vikapu vingi kibandani.There are many baskets in the stall.
Vikapu vimejaa matufaha mekundu yaliyokolea ya msimu wa baridi.The baskets are full of dark red winter apples.
Ninaangalia mfuko mdogo wa matufaha.I look at a small bag of apples.
"Mfuko mdogo unagharimu kiasi gani?" ninamuuliza muuzaji."How much does a small bag cost?" I ask the vendor.
Anaangalia kwenye kikapu.He looks in the basket.
"Bei yake ni kroner ishirini," anajibu."The price is twenty kroner," he answers.
Ninaangalia kwenye mkoba wangu kutafuta pesa.I look in my bag for money.
Ninampa noti ya kroner hamsini.I give him a fifty-krone note.
Muuzaji ananikabidhi mfuko wa matufaha.The vendor hands me the bag of apples.
Kisha ananirudishia pesa.Then he gives me money back.
Ninapata kurudishiwa kroner thelathini kamili kwa sarafu.I get exactly thirty kroner back in coins.
Sarafu hizo ni nene na nzito.The coins are thick and heavy.
Ninaweka sarafu nzito ndani ya mfuko wa koti langu.I put the heavy coins down into my coat pocket.
Matufaha yana harufu safi sana.The apples smell very fresh.
Ninaweka mfuko huo ndani ya mfuko wangu mkubwa wa manunuzi.I put the bag down into my large shopping bag.
fresh rye bread
Storyboard: Maya smells baked goods and walks over to a baker's stall. She points to a fresh, dark rye bread and asks how much it costs. The baker tells her it costs thirty-five kroner. She pays with her card and places the warm bread in her bag.
Ninapita karibu na kibanda kidogo.I walk past a small stall.
Kunanukia mkate mpya.It smells of fresh bread.
Ninaelekea kwenye kile kibanda.I walk over to the stall.
Mwokaji amesimama nyuma ya meza kubwa.The baker is standing behind a large table.
Meza imejaa bidhaa zilizookwa.The table is full of baked goods.
Ninaona mkate mweusi wa rai.I see a dark rye bread.
Unaonekana mpya sana.It looks very fresh.
Ninanyooshea kidole ule mkate wa rai.I point at the rye bread.
"Mkate mweusi wa rai unagharimu kiasi gani?" ninauliza."How much does the dark rye bread cost?" I ask.
Mwokaji anafuta mikono yake.The baker wipes his hands.
"Unagharimu kroner thelathini na tano," anajibu."It costs thirty-five kroner," he replies.
"Ninauunua," ninasema."I am buying it," I say.
Ninatoa kadi yangu ya malipo.I take out my payment card.
Ninalipa kwa kadi.I pay with the card.
Mwokaji ananipa mkate ndani ya mfuko wa karatasi.The baker gives me the bread in a paper bag.
Ninaugusa mfuko.I touch the bag.
Mkate bado ni moto.The bread is still warm.
Ninasubiri kwa hamu kuula.I look forward to eating it.
exact change for coffee
Storyboard: Maya feels cold and spots a small coffee cart. She approaches and asks the barista how much a hot black coffee costs. The barista replies that it costs thirty kroner. Maya takes the new coins from her coat pocket and pays for the coffee with exact change.
Ninasikia baridi sana sasa.I am very cold now.
Mikono yangu ina baridi.My hands are cold.
Ninaangalia huku na kule sokoni.I look around the market.
Ninaona kigari kidogo cha kahawa.I see a small coffee cart.
Kijana mwanamume amesimama nyuma ya kigari.A young man stands behind the cart.
Ninatembea kuelekea kwake.I walk over to him.
Ninamsalimia barista.I say hello to the barista.
"Hujambo, kahawa nyeusi inagharimu kiasi gani?" ninauliza."Hello, how much does a black coffee cost?" I ask.
Barista anatabasamu.The barista smiles.
"Kahawa nyeusi ya moto inagharimu kroner thelathini," anajibu."A hot, black coffee costs thirty kroner," he answers.
Nina sarafu kwenye mfuko wa koti langu.I have coins in my coat pocket.
Ni chenji yangu ya yale matufaha.It is my change from the apples.
Ninazitoa zile sarafu.I take the coins out.
Ninazihesabu.I count them.
Nina kroner thelathini kamili.I have exactly thirty kroner.
"Tafadhali, hizi hapa kroner thelathini," ninasema."Here you go, here are thirty kroner," I say.
Anachukua pesa hizo.He takes the money.
Ananipa ile kahawa ya moto.He gives me the hot coffee.
Kahawa inanukia vizuri sana.The coffee smells wonderful.
Ninapasha mikono yangu joto kwenye kikombe.I warm my hands on the cup.
winter flowers
Storyboard: Maya walks toward the market exit and stops at a flower stall. She points at a bundle of bright winter tulips and asks for the price. The vendor says they cost forty kroner. Maya buys the flowers and walks back to her apartment, drinking her hot coffee and holding the colorful tulips.
Ninatembea kuelekea njia ya kutokea sokoni.I walk towards the exit of the market.
Ninaona kibanda kikubwa cha maua.I see a large flower stall.
Kibanda kina maua mengi mazuri.The stall has many beautiful flowers.
Ninasimama mbele ya kibanda.I stop in front of the stall.
Ninatazama fungu la maua ya tulipi ya msimu wa baridi.I look at a bundle of winter tulips.
Yana rangi nyingi sana.They are very colourful.
Ninanyooshea kidole maua ya tulipi.I point at the tulips.
"Maua ya tulipi yanagharimu kiasi gani?" ninamuuliza muuzaji."How much do the tulips cost?" I ask the vendor.
Muuzaji anayainua juu maua ya tulipi.The vendor holds up the tulips.
"Yanagharimu kroner arobaini," anajibu."They cost forty kroner," he answers.
"Nitayanunua," ninasema."I will buy them," I say.
Ninalipia maua hayo.I pay for the flowers.
Muuzaji ananipa maua ya tulipi.The vendor gives me the tulips.
Sasa nimemaliza shughuli zangu sokoni.Now I am finished at the market.
Ninatembea kurudi kwenye fleti yangu.I walk back to my apartment.
Nimeshika maua ya tulipi yenye rangi nyingi mkononi mwangu.I hold the colourful tulips in my hand.
Ninakunywa kahawa yangu ya moto.I drink my hot coffee.
Jua linawaka juu ya soko lenye shughuli nyingi.The sun shines over the busy market.
