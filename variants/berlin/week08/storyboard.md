# Week 8 — Storyboard (Maya explores the city center)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Polite imperatives (**Gehen Sie, biegen Sie ab**); directions **geradeaus, links/rechts**; **zu + dative**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_sabines_route | It is Saturday morning. Maya meets Sabine on the stairs. Maya wants to explore the city today. Sabine suggests Museum Island, and the tram rather than the U-Bahn — from the tram Maya sees much more of the city. The tram goes almost the whole way, she says, and she names the stop where Maya gets off. From there it is a short walk to the museums. |
| 2 | 02_tram_ride | Maya walks to the tram stop on the corner and gets on the tram. She enjoys the ride above ground and watches the unfamiliar streets and buildings go past the window. |
| 3 | 03_asking_the_way | Maya gets off the tram in the city centre. The streets are busy with weekend crowds. Outside a bakery she stops a woman and asks the way to the museums. The woman points down the boulevard and directs her: straight ahead, then right at the end. |
| 4 | 04_getting_lost | Maya sets off down the boulevard. From a side street she hears a street musician playing. She has time, and she goes to look. She follows the music through a couple of quiet streets. The crowds fade away behind her, the music stops, and the quiet street is completely unfamiliar. She realises she is lost. |
| 5 | 05_stranger_helps | Maya sees a man walking his dog and asks him for help. He smiles and gives her clear directions back: to the crossing, then left, and straight on to the water. |
| 6 | 06_museum_island | Maya follows his directions and finally sees the river Spree in front of her. She crosses a bridge to Museum Island. She stands and looks up at the huge museums and at the Berliner Dom. |
| 7 | 07_by_the_water | Maya finds a place at the edge of the water and sits down. She watches the river a while. Tourist boats go past, and on the far bank people walk along the water. She has no errand today and nowhere to be. |
| 8 | 08_finding_the_station | It is getting late and Maya wants to go home. This time she asks nobody. She finds a city map on an information board and then follows the green signs with the white S: straight ahead and past a small park by the river to the S-Bahn station. |
| 9 | 09_s_bahn_home | Maya sits in the S-Bahn on the way home and looks out of the window at the passing streets. She sees the city map again in her head. Now she can picture her part of Berlin. |
