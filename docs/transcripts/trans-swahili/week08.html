Week 8: Maya explores the city

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
Ni asubuhi ya Jumamosi.It is Saturday morning.
Jua linaangaza juu ya mji.The sun is shining over the city.
Ninashuka hadi barabarani.I go down to the street.
Jirani yangu, Nina, amesimama nje ya jengo letu.My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
"Habari ya asubuhi, Maya," anasema na kutabasamu."Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
"Habari ya asubuhi, Nina," ninajibu."Good morning, Nina," I answer.
"Nina mapumziko leo," ninamwambia."I have the day off today," I tell her.
"Ninaenda mjini.""I am heading out into the city."
"Unaenda wapi?" Nina anauliza."Where are you going?" Nina asks.
"Ninatembea kuelekea kwenye maji," ninasema."I am walking down to the water," I say.
"Lakini sijui njia.""But I do not know the way."
Nina ananyoosha kidole kuelekea barabarani.Nina points down the street.
"Kwa bahati nzuri, ni rahisi sana," anasema."Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
"Nenda moja kwa moja hapa.""Go straight ahead here."
"Na ugeuke kushoto kwenye bustani.""And turn left at the park."
"Kisha utafika moja kwa moja hadi bandarini.""Then you come right down to the harbour."
"Asante sana kwa msaada, Nina," ninasema."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
"Karibu, na uwe na safari njema," anajibu."You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
Ninampungia mkono.I wave to her.
Ninaanza matembezi yangu kuelekea kwenye maji.I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
Ninafuata njia ya Nina.I follow Nina's route.
Hewa ni safi na baridi.The air is fresh and cold.
Sasa ninatembea kwenye barabara kuu.Now I walk down the main street.
Kwanza ninatembea tu moja kwa moja.First I just walk straight ahead.
Kuna magari mengi barabarani.There is a lot of traffic on the street.
Njiani ninaona baiskeli tatu za njano.On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
Baiskeli hizo zinanipita kwa haraka.The bicycles ride quickly past me.
Baada ya hapo ninatembea mbele zaidi.After that I walk further ahead.
Kisha ninafika kwenye bustani kubwa.Then I come to a large park.
Sasa ninakumbuka maneno ya Nina.Now I remember Nina's words.
"Pinda kushoto kwenye bustani," anasema."Turn left at the park," she says.
Hapa ninapinda kushoto.Here I turn left.
Baadaye kidogo ninaona mlango ulio wazi.A little later I see an open door.
Ni duka dogo la mikate.It is a small bakery.
Kuna harufu nzuri ya kahawa safi.There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
Ninapenda mkate wa moto.I love warm bread.
Leo mji ni mzuri.Today the city is lovely.
Baadaye ninaendelea kutembea kuelekea kwenye maji.Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
Ninafika kwenye uwanja mkubwa.I arrive at a large square.
Kuna watu wengi.There are many people.
Lakini sioni maji.But I do not see the water.
Ninatafuta bandari.I am looking for the harbour.
Ninamwona mwanaume mzee kwenye njia ya waenda kwa miguu.I see an older man on the pavement.
Ninamsimamisha.I stop him.
"Samahani," ninasema."Excuse me," I say.
"Bandari iko wapi?""Where is the harbour?"
Yule mwanaume mzee anaitikia kwa kichwa.The older man nods.
Anaonyesha mbele.He points ahead.
"Tembea moja kwa moja kuvuka uwanja," anasema."Walk straight across the square," he says.
"Pita jumba la makumbusho.""Go past the museum."
"Na ugeuke kulia.""And turn right."
Ninatazama ng'ambo ya uwanja.I look across the square.
Ninaona jengo kubwa.I see a large building.
Ni jumba la makumbusho.It is the museum.
"Asante sana," ninasema."Thank you very much," I say.
"Karibu," anajibu."You are welcome," he replies.
Ninaendelea kutembea.I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
Ninatembea kuvuka uwanja mkubwa.I walk across the large square.
Ghafla, ninasikia muziki.Suddenly, I hear music.
Kwenye kona amesimama mwanamuziki wa mitaani.On the corner stands a street musician.
Anapiga gitaa.He plays the guitar.
Ninasikiliza muziki mzuri.I listen to the good music.
Ninamtazama kwa muda mrefu.I look at him for a long time.
Kisha ninaendelea kutembea.Then I walk on.
Ninasahau jumba la makumbusho.I forget the museum.
Pia ninasahau njia yangu.I also forget my route.
Badala yake, ninatembea kwenye mtaa mwembamba wa kando.Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
Hapa ni kimya sana.It is very quiet here.
Pande zote ninaona tu nyumba kadhaa ndogo.All around I only see some small houses.
Kisha ninasimama.Then I stop.
Jumba la makumbusho liko wapi?Where is the museum?
Sasa ninatazama huku na huko.Now I look around.
Siwezi kuliona tena.I cannot see it anymore.
Ninatazama nyuma haraka kwenye mtaa.I quickly look back up the street.
Siwezi kuuona uwanja pia.I cannot see the square either.
Nimepotea kabisa.I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
Nimesimama katika barabara tulivu.I am standing on a quiet street.
Nimepotea.I am lost.
Mbele yangu ninamwona mwanamke kijana.Ahead of me I see a young woman.
Anatembeza mbwa mdogo.She is walking a small dog.
Mbwa huyo anafanana na mbwa wa familia yangu, Lola.The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
Ninatembea kuelekea kwa mwanamke huyo.I walk over to the woman.
"Samahani," ninasema."Excuse me," I say.
"Ninatafuta bandari.""I am looking for the harbour."
Mwanamke huyo anasimama na kusikiliza.The woman stops and listens.
Anaelekeza nyuma barabarani.She points back up the street.
"Nenda moja kwa moja kurudi kwenye uwanja," anasema."Go straight back to the square," she says.
"Na ugeuke kulia kwenye jumba la makumbusho.""And turn right at the museum."
"Asante sana kwa msaada," ninasema."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Karibu," mwanamke huyo anajibu."You are welcome," the woman replies.
Ninamtazama chini yule mbwa mdogo.I look down at the little dog.
Ananiangalia.It looks at me.
Ninatembea kurudi kuelekea kwenye uwanja.I walk back toward the square.
Sasa ninajua mahali nilipo.Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
Ninatembea kurudi kando ya barabara tulivu.I walk back along the quiet street.
Ninatembea moja kwa moja.I walk straight ahead.
Hivi karibuni ninaona uwanja mkubwa tena.Soon I see the large square again.
Kisha ninavuka uwanja.Then I walk across the square.
Sasa nimesimama karibu na jumba la makumbusho.Now I am standing by the museum.
Ninakumbuka maneno ya yule mwanamke: "Geuka kulia."I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
Kwenye jumba la makumbusho ninageuka kulia.At the museum I turn right.
Ninatembea kufuata barabara pana.I walk down a wide street.
Ninaangalia mbele.I look ahead.
Hatimaye ninaona kitu cha bluu.Finally I see something blue.
Ni maji!It is the water!
Njiani ninapita mkahawa mdogo.On the way I walk past a little café.
Mwishowe ninatembea hadi chini kwenye maji.At last I walk all the way down to the water.
Sasa niko bandarini.Now I am at the harbour.
Bandari ni kubwa sana.The harbour is very large.
Upepo ni baridi na safi.The wind is cold and fresh.
Kuna boti nyingi kwenye maji.There are many boats on the water.
Hapa ninasimama.Here I stop.
Ninasimama na kuangalia boti.I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
Ninatembea moja kwa moja kando ya bandari.I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
Ninapita boti ndogo.I walk past the small boats.
Ghafla ninaona watu wengi.Suddenly I see many people.
Wamesimama chini kando ya maji.They stand down by the water.
Ninatembea kuelekea huko.I walk over there.
Hatimaye, ninaiona.Finally, I see it.
Nguva Mdogo amekaa juu ya jiwe kule kwenye maji.The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
Yeye ni sanamu maarufu sana huko Copenhagen.She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.
Sanamu hiyo kwa kweli si kubwa sana.The figure is actually not very big.
Lakini sanamu ya shaba ni nzuri kwenye jua.But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
Ninasimama miongoni mwa wageni wengine.I stand among the other visitors.
Watalii wengi wanapiga picha za sanamu.Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
Mimi pia ninatoa simu yangu.I also take out my phone.
Ninapiga picha nzuri ya nguva.I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
Ninatuma picha kwa familia yangu huko Mexico.I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
Dada yangu, Sofía, anajibu haraka.My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
"Yeye ni mrembo sana," anaandika."She is very pretty," she writes.
Ninatazama maji tena.I look at the water again.
walking home
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.
Ni alasiri sana.It is late in the afternoon.
Sasa ninatembea kwenda nyumbani kwenye fleti yangu.Now I walk home to my apartment.
Ninapita njia rahisi.I walk an easy way.
Ninafuata tu barabara ndefu.I just follow the long road.
Barabara imenyooka kabisa.The road is completely straight.
Ninatembea kando ya maji.I walk along the water.
Mwishowe ninageuka mbali na maji.Finally I turn away from the water.
Sasa ninatembea kuingia mjini tena.Now I walk into the city again.
Kisha ninapita duka la mikate.Then I walk past the bakery.
Bado kunanukia kahawa.It still smells of coffee.
Muda mfupi baadaye ninaiona bustani.A little later I see the park.
Kwenye bustani ninageuka kulia.At the park I turn right.
Simuulizi mtu yeyote njia.I do not ask anyone for directions.
Ninaipata njia mimi mwenyewe kabisa.I find the way all by myself.
Muda mfupi baadaye ninauona mtaa wangu mwenyewe.A little later I see my own street.
Mwishowe ninasimama kwenye mlango wangu mkuu.Finally I stand at my front door.
Kutoka mlangoni ninatazama nyuma kwenye mtaa.From the door I look back down the street.
Sasa ninajua njia yangu ya kwenda nyumbani.Now I know my way home.
