# Week 21 — Storyboard (Maya buys spring clothes and makes an exchange)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Comparatives *større/mindre*; *prøve*; sizes

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_after_the_shift | After finishing her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the café, Maya takes off her apron and steps out into the mild spring sun. She realizes her heavy winter coat is now much too warm and heads toward the main pedestrian street to look for lighter clothes. |
| 2 | 02_trying_spring_jacket | Inside a bustling clothing store, she finds a light green spring jacket on a display table. She tries it on in front of a large floor mirror, zips it all the way up, and decides to buy it because the lighter fabric feels perfect. |
| 3 | 03_skipping_the_queue | She also spots a nice blue cotton shirt and pulls a size thirty-eight from the rack. Seeing an incredibly long queue for the fitting rooms, she decides to skip trying it on, pays for both items at the checkout, and safely tucks the paper receipt into her wallet. |
| 4 | 04_tight_shoulders | Back in her apartment, Maya unpacks her shopping bags and puts on the new blue shirt. She tries to button it up, but when she lifts her arms, she realizes immediately that size thirty-eight is too small and uncomfortably tight across her shoulders. |
| 5 | 05_asking_for_bigger | Heading straight back to town, Maya approaches the clothing store counter with the unworn shirt and her receipt. She explains the problem to a helpful shop assistant and asks if she can try the same shirt in a bigger size. |
| 6 | 06_comparing_size_forty | The assistant brings her a size forty, and Maya takes it into an empty fitting room. She puts on the new shirt, rolls her shoulders to test the fabric, and clearly sees in the mirror that the bigger size fits much better than the smaller one. |
| 7 | 07_scanning_the_receipt | She brings both shirts back to the main counter and hands the smaller one to the clerk. The clerk quickly scans the paper receipt to process the exchange, folding the size forty neatly into a fresh paper bag. |
| 8 | 08_wearing_it_home | Instead of packing her other purchase away, Maya leaves her heavy winter coat in her tote bag and puts on her new light green jacket. She walks out of the store into the late afternoon sun, feeling comfortable and happy with her successful exchange. |
