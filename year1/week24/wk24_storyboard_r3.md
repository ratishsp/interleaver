# Week 24 — Storyboard (Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Imperatives + object pronouns *den/ham/hende*; *skal/må* (rules)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_ninas_handover | On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig to Maya's apartment for the weekend. Nina hands over the leash and explains the rules: Maya must feed him his special dry food, he definitely may not eat any human snacks, and he must stay on the lead near traffic. |
| 2 | 02_sofa_cuddles | Later that night, Maya relaxes in her living room. She pats the sofa cushion next to her and invitingly commands Ludwig to jump up and join her. She strokes his fur, softly telling him to settle down, and gives him a dog chew, saying that he may have it. |
| 3 | 03_saturday_breakfast | Saturday morning. Having taken the morning off from her usual volunteer café shift to stay with her guest, Maya happily prepares his breakfast. She places the bowl on the floor and cheerfully tells him to go ahead and eat it, watching him wag his tail. |
| 4 | 04_the_hundeskov | Saturday afternoon, Maya walks Ludwig to a local hundeskov (dog forest). Once inside the safe, gated area, she unclips the lead and excitedly tells him to run and play, throwing a tennis ball and happily commanding him to fetch it. |
| 5 | 05_gentle_recall | Ludwig spots a bird and wanders off toward the trees. When he does not immediately come back, Maya avoids yelling; she crouches down with a treat, softly calling him to come over to her. He quickly trots to her, and she warmly tells him to stand still while she clips the leash back on. |
| 6 | 06_family_video_call | That evening, Maya video calls her family in Mexico to introduce her furry weekend roommate. She points the camera at the sleeping dog, eagerly telling Sofía and Diego to look at him, noting that he is just as sweet as their dog Lola back home. |
| 7 | 07_sunday_grooming | On Sunday morning, Maya gently brushes Ludwig's coat in the living room. She playfully tells him to give her his paw, laughing out loud when he licks her hand instead, and cheerfully commands him to take it easy while she finishes the grooming. |
| 8 | 08_ninas_return | On Sunday evening, Nina knocks on the door to pick him up. Ludwig greets her joyfully, and Maya hands back the leash, warmly telling Nina that she must definitely let her look after him again next time she travels. |
