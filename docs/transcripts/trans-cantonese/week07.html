Week 7: Maya plans a busy week

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

planning the week
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya makes a cup of tea and opens her calendar at the kitchen table — she always plans the week here. She works through it: the bike goes to the repair shop, the salon needs a phone call, the gym is on Wednesday. She writes each one in, and February fills up.
今日係星期一朝早。It is Monday morning.
我沖一杯熱茶。I make a cup of hot tea.
我坐喺廚房張枱度。I sit at the table in the kitchen.
我打開我本行事曆。I open my calendar.
我總係喺度計劃我成個禮拜嘅行程。I always plan my week here.
依家係二月。It is February now.
呢個月已經排得好滿。The month is already very full.
聽日係星期二。Tomorrow is Tuesday.
唔好彩，我架單車壞咗。My bicycle is unfortunately broken.
我聽朝會行去搵單車師傅。I walk to the bicycle mechanic tomorrow morning.
佢八點鐘開門。He opens at eight o'clock.
我將佢寫落行事曆度。I write it in the calendar.
我今日仲會打電話畀髮型師。I also call the hairdresser today.
我啲頭髮真係太長喇。My hair is way too long.
星期三我會去健身室。Wednesday I go to the gym.
我經常喺星期三做運動。I often train on Wednesdays.
通常係夜晚七點鐘。It is usually at seven o'clock in the evening.
我再望下我個行程表。I look at my plan again.
我飲我杯茶。I drink my tea.
依家我準備好開始新嘅一個禮拜喇。Now I am ready for a new week.
dropping off the bike
Storyboard: On Tuesday morning at a quarter past eight, Maya wheels her bicycle to the local repair shop. She hands the bike to the mechanic, leaves the shop, and walks the rest of the way to the office.
今日係星期二朝早。It is Tuesday morning.
依家係八點三個字。The time is a quarter past eight.
我推住我架單車。I wheel my bicycle.
架車壞咗。It is broken.
我行入去搵單車師傅。I go in to the bicycle mechanic.
佢經常好早就開工。He often works early.
維修舖入面擺滿晒單車。The workshop is full of bicycles.
單車師傅抬起頭。The bicycle mechanic looks up.
「早晨，」佢話。"Good morning," he says.
「早晨，」我話。"Good morning," I say.
「我架單車壞咗。」"My bicycle is broken."
佢接過我架單車。He takes my bicycle.
「我今日會整好佢，」佢話。"I am repairing it today," he says.
「唔該晒，」我話。"Thank you very much," I say.
我行出維修舖。I walk out of the workshop.
依家我行去巴士站。Now I walk to the bus stop.
我通常搭巴士返工。I normally take the bus to work.
我多數喺週末踩單車。I mostly cycle at the weekend.
我班巴士好快就到。Soon my bus comes.
呢個係一個好靚嘅二月朝早。It is a beautiful morning in February.
我八點九就喺公司。I am at work at a quarter to nine.
booking a haircut
Storyboard: During her lunch break, Maya calls a hair salon on her mobile phone. She speaks to the hairdresser and books an appointment for Thursday at half past four.
依家係我嘅食晏時間。It is my lunch break.
我坐喺公司嘅飯堂度。I am sitting in the canteen at my work.
我尋日好忙。I was busy yesterday.
所以我依家打電話畀髮型師。Therefore I am calling the hairdresser now.
我拎起我部手機。I take my mobile phone.
我打畀市內嘅一間髮型屋。I call a hair salon in the city.
有個女人聽電話。A woman answers the phone.
個女人話：「你好，呢度係髮型屋。」"Hello, this is the hair salon," says the woman.
我話：「你好，我叫瑪雅。」"Hello, my name is Maya," I say.
「我想預約星期四嘅時間。」"I would like to book an appointment on Thursday."
髮型師話：「等一陣。」"One moment," says the hairdresser.
我問：「你哋晏晝有冇時間呀？」"Do you have an appointment in the afternoon?" I ask.
佢話：「有呀，我哋四點半有空檔。」"Yes, we have an available appointment at half past four," she says.
我話：「呢個時間好啱我。」"That suits me very well," I say.
髮型師問：「你嘅電話號碼係幾多呀？」"What is your phone number?" asks the hairdresser.
我話：「我嘅號碼係十二，三十四，五十六，七十八。」"My number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
佢話：「唔該晒，星期四四點半見。」"Thank you very much, see you on Thursday at half past four," she says.
我答：「到時見。」"See you," I reply.
我將部電話放喺枱面。I put the phone on the table.
依家我終於可以食嘢喇。Now I finally eat my food.
evening gym
Storyboard: On Wednesday evening, Maya visits the local gym. She usually trains there twice a week, so she lifts weights and runs on the treadmill for an hour before heading home.
依家係星期三夜晚。It is Wednesday evening.
依家係七點。It is seven o'clock.
我喺附近嘅健身室。I am at the local gym.
我好鍾意健身。I really like to work out.
逢星期三我都會喺度。I am always here on Wednesdays.
我換衫。I change clothes.
首先，我舉鐵。First, I lift weights.
啲鐵好重。The weights are very heavy.
我休息一陣。I take a short break.
之後，我跑跑步機。Afterwards, I run on the treadmill.
我成日都跑得好快。I often run fast.
我一定會聽音樂。I always listen to music.
我總共練咗一個鐘。I train for one hour in total.
依家係八點。Now it is eight o'clock.
我好攰，但係好開心。I am tired, but I am happy.
我拎起我個袋。I take my bag.
我行返屋企。I walk home.
hallway chat
Storyboard: After the gym, Maya bumps into Nina in the stairwell. They stand and chat cheerfully, and they agree to drink coffee together on Thursday afternoon at half past four.
我行上樓梯。I walk up the stairs.
去完健身室之後，我好攰。I am tired after the gym.
喺二樓，我撞到Nina。On the second floor, I meet Nina.
佢對住我笑。She smiles at me.
佢話：「嗨，Maya。」"Hi Maya," she says.
我問：「嗨，Nina，最近點呀？」"Hi Nina, how is it going?" I ask.
Nina答：「好好呀。」"It is going very well," Nina answers.
「你睇落有啲攰喎。」"You look a little tired."
我解釋話：「係呀，我逢星期三都會去做gym。」"Yes, I always work out on Wednesdays," I explain.
Nina話：「我哋最近好少一齊飲咖啡。」"We rarely drink coffee together lately," Nina says.
佢問：「你聽日得唔得閒呀？」"Do you have time tomorrow?" she asks.
我諗緊我星期四有咩做。I think about what I am doing on Thursday.
我話：「得呀，星期四幾好。」"Yes, Thursday is a really good day," I say.
我問：「幾點呀？」"What time?" I ask.
「不如四點半？」"Maybe at half past four?"
我開心咁答：「四點半啱曬。」"Half past four sounds perfect," I answer happily.
Nina話：「聽日下晝見啦。」"See you tomorrow afternoon," Nina says.
我哋喺樓梯口講再見。We say goodbye out in the stairwell.
我行入自己間屋。I go into my apartment.
我好期待我哋嘅咖啡約會。I am looking forward to our coffee date.
scheduling conflict
Storyboard: Inside her apartment, Maya opens her calendar to add the coffee date and suddenly sees a problem: the haircut and the coffee are both scheduled at half past four. She immediately sends Nina a text message to move the coffee to six o'clock, and Nina happily agrees.
我攞起部電話。I take my phone.
我打開日曆。I open my calendar.
我睇吓星期四嘅日程。I look at Thursday.
我突然發現有問題。I suddenly see a problem.
我四點半約咗髮型師。I have an appointment at the hairdresser at half past four.
我同 Nina 飲咖啡嘅時間都係四點半！My coffee with Nina is also at half past four!
我即刻傳個短訊畀 Nina。I immediately send a text message to Nina.
我寫：「嗨 Nina，唔好意思。」I write: "Hi Nina, sorry."
「我星期四四點半約咗髮型師。」"I have a hairdresser appointment on Thursday at half past four."
「改做六點你有冇時間呀？」"Do you have time at six o'clock instead?"
Nina 好快就覆我。Nina replies very quickly.
佢寫：「冇問題！」She writes: "No problem!"
「六點見啦。」"See you at six o'clock."
我笑一笑，更改日曆上面嘅時間。I smile and change the time in the calendar.
依家我星期四嘅行程就啱啱好喇。Now my Thursday fits perfectly.
picking up the bike
Storyboard: On Thursday afternoon, Maya leaves work and returns to the mechanic at a quarter to four. She collects her freshly repaired bicycle and rides happily towards the hair salon.
依家係星期四晏晝。It is Thursday afternoon.
我放咗工。I am off from work.
我行過去單車師傅嗰度。I walk over to the bicycle mechanic.
時間係三點九。The time is a quarter to four.
個師傅企喺舖頭入面。The mechanic is standing in the shop.
我話：「你好。」"Hi," I say.
佢將架單車推出嚟畀我。He rolls the bicycle out to me.
佢話：「你架單車整好喇。」"Your bike is ready," he says.
我話：「唔該晒。」"Thank you very much," I say.
架單車睇落真係好靚。The bike looks really nice.
啲車胎依家好實。The tires are hard now.
我將架單車推出去街度。I wheel the bike out onto the street.
今日陽光好好。The sun is shining today.
我坐上架單車度。I get on the bike.
我成日喺哥本哈根踩單車。I often cycle in Copenhagen.
真係好容易。It is very easy.
依家我開開心心咁踩去髮型屋。Now I happily cycle towards the hairdresser.
我約咗四點半。My appointment is at half past four.
我好期待。I am looking forward to it.
appointments kept
Storyboard: Maya arrives at the salon on time and gets her hair cut. Afterwards, she cycles through the city to a cozy cafe, where she finally sits down to enjoy coffee with Nina at six o'clock.
我四點半去到髮型屋。I arrive at the hairdresser at half past four.
我一向都好準時。I am always punctual.
髮型師同我打招呼。The hairdresser greets me.
佢幫我洗頭同剪頭髮。She washes and cuts my hair.
大約用咗一個鐘。It takes about an hour.
我個新髮型好靚。My new hair is very nice.
我多謝髮型師，然後離開髮型屋。I thank the hairdresser and leave the salon.
我架單車就喺出面。My bike is out front.
我踩單車穿過市區。I cycle through the city.
今日係星期四夜晚。It is Thursday evening.
我踩單車去一間好舒適嘅咖啡店。I cycle to a cozy cafe.
我泊好單車然後行入去。I park my bike and go inside.
妮娜已經坐咗喺一張枱嗰度。Nina is already sitting at a table.
我望吓隻錶。I look at my watch.
啱啱好六點鐘。It is exactly six o'clock.
「嗨，瑪雅，你把頭髮真係好靚，」妮娜話。"Hi Maya, your hair is really nice," Nina says.
「多謝晒，」我話。"Thank you very much," I say.
我哋叫咗兩杯咖啡。We order two cups of coffee.
我哋一邊飲咖啡一邊傾偈。We drink the coffee and chat together.
我一向都好享受我哋嘅咖啡約會。I always enjoy our coffee dates.
