Week 12: Maya joins a badminton club

office departure

She wants to play badminton instead. She puts her sports clothes into her backpack and leaves the office at half past four.

She really wants to meet new people in the city. After a short ride, she arrives at a large local sports hall and parks her bike.

renting the racket
Storyboard: Maya goes inside to the reception desk. She explains to the staff member that she wants to participate as a guest for the evening. She pays the guest fee of seven Euro, and because she does not own a racket, she rents one for three Euro. She pays with a ten Euro note and declines the Kassenbon.
Ich gehe in die Sporthalle.I walk into the sports hall.
Ich laufe direkt zur Rezeption.I walk directly to the reception desk.
Ein Mitarbeiter steht hinter dem Tresen.A staff member stands behind the counter.
„Guten Abend“, sagt der Mann."Good evening," the man says.
„Guten Abend“, antworte ich."Good evening," I answer.
„Ich spiele sehr gern Badminton“, erkläre ich."I like playing badminton very much," I explain.
„Ich will heute als Gast mitspielen.“"I want to play along as a guest today."

„Ja, natürlich“, antwortet der Mitarbeiter."Yes, of course," the staff member answers.
„Das kostet drei Euro extra.“"That costs three Euro extra."

She can see that the players are very fast. She stands near an empty court, hoping she can join a match. Her keenness shows in how she watches — she never announces that she likes badminton.
