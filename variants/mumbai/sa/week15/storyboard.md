# Week 15 — Storyboard (A full weekday in Mumbai)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Mixed present-tense narration; consolidation. BOUNDARY: no past tense anywhere — the past opens week 16

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_routine | Maya wakes up in her quiet Mumbai apartment, brews a fresh cup of coffee, and prepares for a typical weekday. She packs her laptop bag, makes sure she has her keys and wallet, and steps out, locking the door behind her. |
| 2 | 02_morning_bus_commute | Maya walks to the nearby bus stop and boards a familiar red BEST bus. She hands a few rupees to the conductor for her ticket, holding the handrail securely as she watches the bustling morning street life on her way to the office. |
| 3 | 03_coding_with_rohan | Arriving at the software company, Maya settles at her desk and boots up her computer. Her colleague Rohan comes over to her workstation, and they casually chat using their first names while collaborating on a tricky piece of code. |
| 4 | 04_lunch_break_directions | During her lunch break, Maya steps out onto the busy pavement. A lost tourist approaches her; using polite terms, Maya confidently gives them directions, pointing out how to reach a large nearby post office down the road. |
| 5 | 05_street_fruit_errand | Before heading back inside, Maya stops by a vibrant fruit cart. She addresses the seller affectionately as bhaiya, asks the price of some fresh bananas, pays him in rupees, and safely tucks the fruit into her bag. |
| 6 | 06_wrapping_up_work | The afternoon flies by as Maya focuses on typing and testing her software code. As evening sets in, she saves her progress, waves goodbye to Rohan and the rest of the team, and heads out of the office building. |
| 7 | 07_evening_bus_ride | Maya walks back to the bus stop and boards her regular BEST bus for the journey home. She finds a window seat, enjoying a moment of rest as the city lights blur past and the cool evening breeze blows through the open window. |
| 8 | 08_catching_up_with_asha | Maya arrives back at her apartment building and runs into her kind neighbor, Asha, who is walking her dog, Moti. They stand in the corridor chatting warmly about the pleasant evening weather, Moti's playful energy, and what they are currently preparing for dinner, making Maya feel truly at home in her new city. |
