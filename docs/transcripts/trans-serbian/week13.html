Week 13: Maya goes to a café

Serbian with English gloss, line-aligned.

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.
Zima je u Kopenhagenu.It is winter in Copenhagen.
Hodam niz ulicu.I am walking down the street.
Vetar je veoma hladan.The wind is very cold.
Danas jako duva.It is blowing hard today.
Naginjem se nasuprot hladnom vetru.I lean against the cold wind.
Povlačim šal nagore.I pull my scarf up.
Lice mi se smrzava.My face is freezing.
Gledam oko sebe.I look around.
Volela bih da uđem na toplo.I would like to get into the warmth.
Vidim mali kafić na uglu.I see a small café on the corner.
Prozori su zamagljeni.The windows are fogged up.
Izgleda da je unutra toplo.It looks warm in there.
Idem do kafića.I walk over to the café.
Otvaram teška vrata.I open the heavy door.
Ulazim unutra.I step inside.
Ovde miriše na kafu.It smells like coffee here.
Vazduh je prijatno topao.The air is nice and warm.
Zatvaram vrata za sobom.I close the door behind me.
Hladni vetar ostaje napolju.The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
Sada sam u kafiću.I am inside the café now.
Ovde je veoma toplo.It is very warm here.
Skidam svoju debelu zimsku jaknu.I take off my thick winter coat.
Teška je.It is heavy.
Pronalazim kuku na zidu.I find a hook on the wall.
Kačim jaknu na kuku.I hang my coat on the hook.
Takođe skidam šal i rukavice.I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
Kafić je mali i ušuškan.The café is small and cozy.
Tražim dobro mesto.I look for a good seat.
Pored prozora je mali sto.There is a small table by the window.
Sto se nalazi pored radijatora.The table is next to a radiator.
Idem do stola.I walk over to the table.
Sedam na stolicu.I sit down on the chair.
Ruke su mi i dalje hladne.My hands are still cold.
Trljam ih jednu o drugu.I rub them together.
Ruke mi postaju toplije.My hands get warmer.
Osećaj je fantastičan.It feels fantastic.
Sada mi je skoro toplo.Now I am almost warm.
Gladna sam i žedna.I am hungry and thirsty.
Želela bih sada nešto da naručim.I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
Prilazim staklenoj vitrini.I walk over to the glass counter.
Puna je kolača i hleba.It is full of cakes and bread.
Gledam te mnogobrojne stvari.I look at the many things.
Izgledaju veoma ukusno.They look very delicious.
Pored kolača su mali natpisi.There are small signs next to the cakes.
Natpisi su rukom pisani.The signs are handwritten.
Čitam imena na natpisima.I read the names on the signs.
Ima mnogo vrsta peciva.There are many kinds of pastry.
Teško je izabrati.It is hard to choose.
Tu je velika čokoladna torta.There is a large chocolate cake.
Ali gledam jedno drugo pecivo.But I look at another piece of pastry.
To je velika rolnica sa cimetom.It is a large cinnamon roll.
Ima mnogo šećera na vrhu.It has a lot of sugar on top.
Volim ukus cimeta.I love the taste of cinnamon.
Savršeno je za hladan zimski dan.It is perfect for a cold winter day.
Veoma bih volela rolnicu sa cimetom.I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
Sada sam spremna da naručim.Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
Prilazim kasi.I walk over to the register.
Mlad muškarac stoji iza pulta.A young man stands behind the counter.
On je barista u kafiću.He is a barista in the café.
Smešim mu se.I smile at him.
On ljubazno klima glavom.He nods warmly.
"Zdravo," kaže on."Hi," he says.
"Šta želite?" pita on."What would you like?" he asks.
"Zdravo," odgovaram."Hi," I answer.
"Želela bih crnu kafu, molim vas.""I would like a black coffee, please."
"I mogu li da dobijem rolnicu sa cimetom?""And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
"Naravno," kaže on."Of course," he says.
"Crna kafa i rolnica sa cimetom.""A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
"Da li želite da sednete ovde?" pita on."Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
"Da, hvala, sešću tamo," kažem."Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
"U redu," kaže barista."Alright," the barista says.
"Sada ću vam napraviti kafu.""I will make your coffee now."
"Hvala puno," kažem."Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
Čekam za pultom.I wait at the counter.
On pravi moju kafu.He makes my coffee.
Stavlja toplu šolju kafe na drveni poslužavnik.He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
Takođe stavlja mali tanjir na poslužavnik.He also puts a small plate on the tray.
Moja rolnica sa cimetom je na tanjiru.My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
Divno miriše.It smells lovely.
„Izvolite, vaša porudžbina”, kaže on."Here you go, your order," he says.
„Hvala puno”, odgovaram."Thank you very much," I reply.
„Hvala na dobroj usluzi.”"Thank you for the good service."
„Nema na čemu”, kaže on ljubazno."You are welcome," he says kindly.
Uzimam poslužavnik obema rukama.I take the tray with both hands.
Hodam veoma pažljivo preko poda.I walk very carefully across the floor.
Vraćam se do svog stola pored prozora.I walk back to my table by the window.
Spuštam poslužavnik na sto.I put the tray down on the table.
Sada bih volela da sednem.Now I would like to sit down.
Spremna sam za dobru pauzu.I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
Sada sedim za svojim malim stolom.I now sit at my small table.
Ovde je divno i toplo.It is lovely and warm here.
Gledam svoju rolnicu sa cimetom.I look at my cinnamon roll.
Miriše slatko i lepo.It smells sweet and good.
Uzimam mali zalogaj.I take a small bite.
Pecivo je potpuno sveže i toplo.The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
Zaista je ukusno.It tastes really good.
Pijem malo svoje crne kafe.I drink a little of my black coffee.
Konačno su mi prsti ponovo topli.Finally, my fingers are warm again.
Opuštam se u mekanoj stolici.I relax in the soft chair.
Kafić je skoro pun ljudi.The café is almost full of people.
Ljudi sede u malim grupama.People sit in small groups.
Pričaju i smeju se zajedno.They talk and laugh together.
Slušam tihe glasove u prostoriji.I listen to the low voices in the room.
Zvuk je veoma prijatan.The sound is very cozy.
Gledam kroz veliki prozor.I look out of the large window.
Vreme napolju je sivo i vetrovito.The weather outside is gray and windy.
Sada zapravo ponovo pada sneg.Now it is actually snowing again.
Bele pahulje polako padaju na ulicu.White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
Samo sam srećna što sam unutra.I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
Jedem poslednji komad svoje rolnice sa cimetom.I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
Stvarno je ukusna.It tastes really good.
Ali je i veoma slatka.But it is also very sweet.
Moja šolja za kafu je potpuno prazna.My coffee cup is completely empty.
Odjednom sam veoma žedna.I am suddenly very thirsty.
Želela bih malo hladne vode.I would like to have some cold water.
Jedan mladić prolazi pored mog stola.A young man walks past my table.
On radi ovde u kafiću.He works here in the café.
Skuplja prazne šolje i tanjire.He collects empty cups and plates.
Uhvatim njegov pogled.I catch his eye.
Zaustavlja se pored mog stola i smeši se.He stops at my table and smiles.
„Izvinite”, kažem ljubazno."Excuse me," I say politely.
„Da?”, odgovara mladić."Yes?" answers the young man.
„Smem li da zamolim za čašu vode sa česme?”, pitam."May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
„Da, naravno”, kaže on."Yes, of course," he says.
„Hvala vam puno”, kažem."Thank you very much," I say.
On uzima moj prazan tanjir i šolju.He takes my empty plate and cup.
Vraća se do pulta.He walks back to the counter.
Malo kasnije dolazi sa čašom hladne vode.A little later he comes with a cold glass of water.
asking for the bill
Storyboard: Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab.
Pijem svoju vodu.I drink my water.
Voda je hladna i prijatna.The water is cold and nice.
Uzimam svoju torbu sa poda.I take my bag from the floor.
Završila sam sa pauzom.I am finished with my break.
Prilazim pultu.I walk up to the counter.
Ljubazni barista i dalje stoji tamo.The friendly barista is still standing there.
Gleda me sa osmehom.He looks at me with a smile.
"Da li želite još nešto?" pita on."Would you like anything else?" he asks.
"Ne, hvala," odgovaram."No thank you," I answer.
"Želela bih da platim.""I would like to pay."
"Da, naravno," kaže on."Yes, of course," he says.
Gleda u svoj mali ekran.He looks at his little screen.
"Jedna crna kafa i jedna rolnica sa cimetom," kaže on."One black coffee and one cinnamon roll," he says.
"Da, to je tačno," kažem."Yes, that is correct," I say.
"Da li želite da platite karticom ili gotovinom?" pita on."Do you want to pay with card or cash?" he asks.
"Želela bih da platim karticom," odgovaram."I would like to pay with a card," I answer.
"U redu.""Fine."
"To će biti osamdeset pet kruna," kaže on."That will be eighty-five kroner," he says.
Vadim svoju platnu karticu.I take out my payment card.
paying and leaving
Storyboard: Maya taps her card on the terminal to pay eighty-five kroner. She puts on her heavy coat, thanks the staff for a nice time, and pushes the front door open to face the cold again.
Barista mi se smeši.The barista smiles at me.
Držim svoju karticu iznad terminala.I hold my card over the terminal.
Aparat zapišti.The machine beeps.
„Želite li račun?“ pita on."Do you want a receipt?" he asks.
„Ne, hvala“, odgovaram."No thank you," I answer.
Prilazim zidu.I walk over to the wall.
Uzimam svoju debelu zimsku jaknu sa kuke.I take my thick winter coat from a hook.
Oblačim jaknu.I put the coat on.
Takođe stavljam svoj šal.I also put my scarf on.
Takođe navlačim svoje rukavice.I also put my gloves on.
Sada sam spremna za hladnoću.Now I am ready for the cold.
Ponovo gledam u baristu.I look at the barista again.
„Hvala vam puno na kafi“, kažem."Thank you very much for the coffee," I say.
„Nema na čemu“, kaže on uz osmeh."You are welcome," he says with a smile.
„Prijatan dan.“"Have a good day."
„Takođe“, kažem."Likewise," I say.
Prilazim ulaznim vratima.I walk over to the front door.
Guram teška vrata i otvaram ih.I push the heavy door open.
Vetar je hladan, ali se osećam dobro.The wind is cold, but I feel good.
