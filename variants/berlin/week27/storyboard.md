# Week 27 — Storyboard (Maya tackles online admin)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Habitual **normalerweise / meistens**; instructions; the passive of official language (*wird bearbeitet, wird geschickt*)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_sofa_setup | Wednesday evening in the flat. Maya has been putting off one small piece of admin: her email address on the transit portal is still the old one from Mexico. Normalerweise she does this kind of thing on her phone in the evening, and tonight is no different — her laptop stays shut on the table and she works from the sofa. This scene ends with the site open and her profile on screen — she does not fill anything in yet; the form belongs to scene 2. |
| 2 | 02_portal_instructions | Now she works through the form, starting from the profile page she already has open. The site is full of flat, impersonal instructions — *Bitte geben Sie Ihre neue E-Mail-Adresse ein*, *Klicken Sie hier* — and after she submits, the screen turns to the passive of official German: *Ihre Daten werden überprüft*, *Ein Code wird an Ihr Handy geschickt*. |
| 3 | 03_missing_sms | The code does not come. Maya checks her signal. Normalerweise these texts arrive instantly. The screen times out. Rather than sit and wait, she opens the help section, finds the alternative route, has the code sent to her old email instead, and finishes the form. It takes four minutes in total. |
| 4 | 04_thursday_badminton | Thursday evening at half past five at the badminton club. Maya rents her racket at the desk as always — she owns none — and plays doubles and does *not* retell last night's admin story — it was four minutes of her life. Instead the talk is about the game and about the summer heat in the hall. She uses **ihr** with the group. |
| 5 | 05_the_group_chat | Friday evening. The badminton group has a chat on her phone, and Maya reads it on the U-Bahn home: who can play next week, who is away, a photo from the hall. She types a short reply with one thumb, standing up, and normalerweise she would miss her stop doing this. |
| 6 | 06_cafe_wifi_guide | Saturday morning at the café, volunteering alongside Elif ('du'). A customer ('Sie') orders coffee and asks how to get onto the guest WiFi. Maya gives the instructions out loud — the network name, the password on the card by the till, what to do if it does not connect the first time. |
| 7 | 07_the_parcel_notice | Saturday afternoon. A delivery notice is stuck to Maya's door, in the same official passive as the portal: *Ihre Sendung wird im Paketshop hinterlegt*. She reads it twice to be sure which shop and until when, and puts it in her pocket. |
| 8 | 08_video_call_home | Saturday evening. A video call home. It is early afternoon in Mexico, so nothing there has happened yet today. Her mother's picture keeps freezing, so Maya talks her through it: close the other apps, move nearer the router, try again. Meistens that is all it takes, and it is — her mother appears sharp and delighted, mid-sentence. |
| 9 | 09_sunday_flea_market | Sunday morning, a slow breakfast with Brötchen, then the U-Bahn to a flea market. She pays the seller in cash, like everyone else there. On the way home her phone buzzes: the transit portal, confirming in its flat official passive that her new address *wurde gespeichert*. She reads it once and puts the phone away. |
