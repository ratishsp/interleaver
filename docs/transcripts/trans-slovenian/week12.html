Week 12: Maya joins a badminton club

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding a club
Storyboard: Maya works out at the gym on Wednesdays but wants to meet people, so she looks for a social sport. She loves badminton — she played it a lot back home in Mexico — so she finds a local club online and books a trial session for Thursday evening.
Vsako sredo zvečer grem v fitnes.I go to the gym every Wednesday evening.
Rada treniram.I like to work out.
Ampak želim si igrati tudi kakšen šport.But I also want to play a sport.
Obožujem igrati badminton.I love to play badminton.
Doma v Mehiki sem ga veliko igrala.I played it a lot back home in Mexico.
Zdaj iščem lokalni klub.Now I am looking for a local club.
Sedim na kavču s svojim računalnikom.I am sitting on my couch with my computer.
Iščem po spletu.I search online.
Najdem dober badmintonski klub.I find a good badminton club.
Gledam spletno stran kluba.I look at the club's website.
Imajo veliko ekip.They have many teams.
Igrajo v četrtek zvečer.They play on Thursday evening.
Rada bi rezervirala poskusni trening.I want to book a trial session.
Najdem obrazec na spletni strani.I find a form on the website.
Napišem svoje ime in svoj e-mail.I write my name and my email.
Izberem četrtek zvečer.I choose Thursday evening.
Trening se začne ob sedmih zvečer.The training starts at seven in the evening.
To je popolno.It is perfect.
Veselim se, da bom spet igrala.I look forward to playing again.
arriving at hall
Storyboard: On Thursday evening Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sound of shoes squeaking on the many wooden courts.
Četrtek zvečer je in sem doma.It is Thursday evening, and I am at home.
Pakiram svojo športno torbo.I pack my sports bag.
V torbo dam svoje čevlje in oblačila.I put my shoes and my clothes in the bag.
Veselim se, da bom nocoj igrala.I look forward to playing tonight.
Z avtobusom grem do velike športne dvorane.I take the bus to a large sports hall.
Je hladen zimski večer.It is a cold winter evening.
Sedim na avtobusu in gledam ven.I sit in the bus and look out.
Avtobus se ustavi in izstopim.The bus stops, and I get out.
Stopim v veliko športno dvorano.I walk into the large sports hall.
Zelo je svetla.It is very bright.
V dvorani vidim veliko ljudi.I see many people in the hall.
Zelo radi se ukvarjajo s športom.They love to play sports.
Slišim glasen zvok.I hear a loud sound.
Čevlji škripajo na številnih lesenih igriščih.The shoes squeak on the many wooden courts.
Ljudje hitro tečejo in igrajo skupaj.People run fast and play together.
Videti je zelo zabavno.It looks very fun.
Pripravljena sem igrati.I am ready to play.
Ampak najprej si moram izposoditi lopar.But first I need to rent a racket.
renting the racket
Storyboard: Maya goes to the small hall kiosk. She tells the clerk she is there to try out the club, and pays forty kroner to rent a racket for the evening.
Stopim do majhnega kioska v športni dvorani.I walk over to a small kiosk in the sports hall.
Za pultom stoji mlad moški.A young man stands behind the counter.
Nasmehne se in reče živijo.He smiles and says hello.
"Živijo, danes bi rada igrala badminton," rečem."Hello, I want to play badminton today," I say.
"Si nova tukaj v klubu?" vpraša."Are you new here in the club?" he asks.
"Ja, rada bi se poskusila pridružiti," odgovorim."Yes, I would like to try to join in," I answer.
"Dobrodošla," reče moški."Welcome," the man says.
"Si moraš izposoditi lopar?" vpraša."Do you need to rent a racket?" he asks.
"Ja, prosim, rada bi si izposodila lopar za nocoj," rečem."Yes please, I want to rent a racket for the evening," I say.
Zame poišče dober lopar.He finds a good racket for me.
"Stane štirideset kron," reče."It costs forty kroner," he says.
Vzamem svojo plačilno kartico.I take out my payment card.
Plačam štirideset kron.I pay the forty kroner.
Prek pulta mi poda lopar.He gives me the racket over the counter.
"Zdi se mi zabaven šport," rečem veselo."I think it is a fun sport," I say happily.
"Želim ti res dobro igro.""Have a really good game."
Zahvalim se in grem do igrišč.I say thank you and walk over to the courts.
playing doubles
Storyboard: In the busy hall, Maya joins a doubles game with two friendly players, Kasper and Sara, and a fourth person. She has played for years, so she smashes the shuttlecock hard across the net.
Stopim do igrišča za badminton.I walk over to a badminton court.
Na igrišču stojijo tri osebe.Three people are standing on the court.
"Živijo, ime mi je Kasper," reče moški."Hi, I am Kasper," a man says.
"In meni je ime Sara," reče ženska."And I am Sara," a woman says.
Tretjemu igralcu je ime Emil.The third player is named Emil.
"Ime mi je Maya," rečem."I am Maya," I say.
"Običajno igramo dvojice," reče Kasper."We usually play doubles," Kasper says.
"Bi rada igrala z nami?" vpraša."Do you want to play with us?" he asks.
"Ja, z veseljem," odgovorim."Yes, I would love to," I reply.
Zelo rada igram badminton.I really like to play badminton.
Kasper in jaz igrava proti Sari in Emilu.Kasper and I play against Sara and Emil.
Takoj začnemo igrati.We start to play immediately.
Sara začne tekmo.Sara starts the match.
Žogica hitro leti sem in tja.The shuttlecock flies quickly back and forth.
Emil udari žogico visoko v zrak.Emil hits the shuttlecock high up in the air.
Obožujem zabijati.I love to smash.
Skočim in jo močno zabijem čez mrežo.I jump and smash it hard over the net.
Pristane na njuni strani igrišča.It lands on their side of the court.
"Dober udarec, Maya!" zakliče Kasper."Good shot, Maya!" Kasper shouts.
post match chat
Storyboard: The fast match finishes and the tired players walk off the court. Sara says Maya plays really well, and Maya explains that she plays a lot back home in Mexico.
Hitra tekma je končana.The fast match is over.
Skupaj gremo z igrišča.We walk off the court together.
Zelo sem utrujena.I am very tired.
Kasper in Sara sta tudi utrujena.Kasper and Sara are also tired.
Spijemo malo vode.We drink some water.
"Zelo dobro igraš," reče Sara."You play very well," Sara says.
"Najlepša hvala," rečem."Thank you very much," I say.
"Tako zabavno se mi zdi.""I think it is so much fun."
"Ali pogosto igraš?" vpraša Kasper."Do you play often?" Kasper asks.
"Da, veliko igram doma v Mehiki," odgovorim."Yes, I play a lot back home in Mexico," I answer.
"Običajno igram s sestro.""I usually play with my sister."
"To je zabaven šport," reče Sara."It is a fun sport," Sara says.
"Zelo radi igramo tukaj v klubu.""We love to play here in the club."
"Želim si več igrati tukaj na Danskem," rečem."I want to play more here in Denmark," I say.
"Vsekakor si hitra," se nasmeje Kasper."You are definitely fast," Kasper laughs.
"Hvala, ampak zdaj bi se rada usedla," rečem."Thanks, but now I want to sit down," I say.
"Gremo v kafeterijo?" vpraša Kasper."Shall we go into the cafeteria?" Kasper asks.
"Da, to bi zelo rada," odgovorim."Yes, I would very much like that," I answer.
hall cafeteria
Storyboard: The group sits in the small hall cafeteria to drink water and chat. Kasper asks if Maya plans to come back, and she happily promises to make it a weekly Thursday routine.
Gremo v majhno kavarno v dvorani.We walk into the small cafeteria in the hall.
Najdemo mizo.We find a table.
Zelo sem žejna.I am very thirsty.
Pijemo hladno vodo in se pogovarjamo.We drink cold water and talk.
"To je res dober klub," rečem."This is a really good club," I say.
"To je dober šport za rekreacijo," reče Kasper."It is a good sport to do," Kasper says.
Kasper me pogleda.Kasper looks at me.
"Se nam želiš spet pridružiti?" vpraša."Do you want to join again?" he asks.
"Ja, zelo rada," odgovorim."Yes, very much," I answer.
"Tukaj bi rada igrala vsak četrtek," razložim."I want to play here every Thursday," I explain.
"To zveni čudovito," reče Sara."That sounds lovely," Sara says.
"Tukaj vedno igramo ob četrtkih," reče Kasper."We always play here on Thursdays," Kasper says.
"Potem se pa vidimo naslednji teden," veselo rečem."Then we'll see each other next week," I say happily.
Spijemo preostanek vode.We drink the rest of our water.
Lepo je spoznavati nove ljudi.It is lovely to meet new people.
walking to bus
Storyboard: Maya leaves the sports hall with Sara. They walk together to the bus stop in the cold winter evening, and Maya feels glad to have met fun, active locals.
Sara in jaz greva iz športne dvorane.Sara and I walk out of the sports hall.
Pozno je in je hladen zimski večer.It is late, and it is a cold winter evening.
Ampak meni je po tekmi še vedno toplo.But I am still warm after the match.
Skupaj greva do avtobusne postaje.We walk over to the bus stop together.
Veter piha in ulica je temna.The wind is blowing, and the street is dark.
"Ali tudi ob vikendih rada igraš badminton?" vprašam."Do you also like to play badminton at the weekend?" I ask.
"Ne, badminton igram samo ob četrtkih," odgovori Sara."No, I only play badminton on Thursdays," Sara answers.
"Ampak rada se ukvarjam s športom," reče."But I like to do sports," she says.
"Ponavadi grem teč v park," nadaljuje."I usually go for a run in the park," she continues.
Je zelo aktivna in zabavna oseba.She is a very active and fun person.
Na mrazu čakava na avtobus.We wait for the bus in the cold.
"Moj avtobus pride čez deset minut," rečem."My bus comes in ten minutes," I say.
"Greva z istim avtobusom," se nasmehne Sara."We take the same bus," Sara smiles.
Tega sem zelo vesela.I am very happy about that.
Rada se pogovarjam z njo.I like to talk with her.
Kasper in Sara sta zabavna in prijazna človeka.Kasper and Sara are fun and nice people.
Zdaj imam ob četrtkih dobro rutino.Now I have a good routine on Thursdays.
Utrujena sem, ampak sem dobre volje.I am tired, but I am in a good mood.
Avtobus končno pride.The bus finally arrives.
Že zdaj se veselim naslednjega četrtka.I am already looking forward to next Thursday.
