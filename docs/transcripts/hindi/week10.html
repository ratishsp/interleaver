Week 10: Maya buys cotton clothes for the summer

Hindi with English gloss, line-aligned.

march heat
Storyboard: Maya feels the March heat arriving in Mumbai. She puts on her usual thick jeans for the office, but she immediately feels too hot. She realizes she needs light cotton clothes to wear for the coming summer.

मैं ऑफिस के लिए तैयार होती हूँ।I get ready for the office.
मैं अपनी अलमारी खोलती हूँ।I open my wardrobe.
मैं अपनी नीली जींस निकालती हूँ।I take out my blue jeans.
यह जींस बहुत मोटी है।These jeans are very thick.
मैं यह मोटी जींस पहनती हूँ।I put on these thick jeans.
मुझे तुरंत बहुत गर्मी लगती है।I immediately feel very hot.
मैं यह जींस उतारती हूँ।I take off these jeans.
मैं पतली पैंट पहनती हूँ।I put on thin pants.
अब मुंबई में गर्मियाँ हैं।It is summer in Mumbai now.
मेरे भारी कपड़े अब अच्छे नहीं हैं।My heavy clothes are not good now.
मेरे पास सूती कपड़े नहीं हैं।I do not have cotton clothes.
मुझे हल्के सूती कपड़े चाहिए।I need light cotton clothes.
मुझे ऑफिस के लिए नए कपड़े चाहिए।I need new clothes for the office.
आज शाम मैं बाजार जाती हूँ।I go to the market this evening.
cotton shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya visits a bright neighbourhood clothing store. She greets the young salesman as bhaiya and explains that she wants light cotton clothes to wear in the Mumbai heat.

मैं कपड़े देखना शुरू करती हूँ।I start looking at the clothes.
blue kurta
