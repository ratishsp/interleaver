# Week 12 — Storyboard (Maya joins a badminton club)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Verb + infinitive; *godt kan lide at*; *spille/dyrke*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_online_search | Maya sits in her apartment and browses the internet. She wants to do sports during the dark winter to stay active. She really likes to play badminton, so she decides to find a local club. |
| 2 | 02_sports_hall_arrival | Maya takes the bus to a large community sports center. She walks through the main entrance and listens to the echoing sounds. She can hear people shouting and rackets hitting shuttlecocks across the busy courts. |
| 3 | 03_renting_a_racket | Maya approaches the front desk and speaks to a volunteer. She wants to join the club today, but she needs a racket. The volunteer explains that she can rent a good racket for fifty kroner. |
| 4 | 04_meeting_new_people | Maya takes her rented racket and walks over to court number three. She introduces herself to two friendly members, Kasper and Sara. They welcome her warmly and ask if she wants to play a game with them. |
| 5 | 05_warm_up_drills | Maya steps onto the green floor and begins to warm up. Sara hits the shuttlecock high into the air. Maya tries to hit it back perfectly, realizing she needs to practice her timing after a long break. |
| 6 | 06_fast_paced_game | The friendly match officially starts. Maya has to run incredibly fast from side to side to keep the rally going. She breathes heavily but notices that she still loves to play this fast-paced game. |
| 7 | 07_water_break_chat | The players decide to take a short break. Maya sits on a wooden bench with Kasper and Sara to drink some water. They chat about their lives, and Maya explains that she works as a software engineer. |
| 8 | 08_returning_next_week | The evening session ends, and Maya packs her things into her bag. Kasper and Sara ask if she plans to practice with them again. Maya agrees immediately and promises to come back to play next week. |
