# Week 13 — Storyboard (Maya takes a café break from the heat)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Polite requests — **തരുമോ? / കിട്ടുമോ? / വേണം** ladder; thanking

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_escaping_the_heat | The afternoon sun shines brightly, and the March heat feels very intense. Maya walks down a busy street in Kochi and decides to step inside a small local café to cool down. |
| 2 | 02_finding_a_table | Inside, the cold air conditioning feels wonderful against her skin. The café is quiet and relaxing, and Maya easily finds a comfortable corner table near the front window. |
| 3 | 03_ordering_at_the_counter | Maya walks up to the main counter to look at the menu. The friendly worker at the counter greets her, and Maya politely asks if she can get a big glass of iced tea and a slice of banana cake. |
| 4 | 04_enjoying_the_break | A server brings a small tray to her table, and Maya warmly thanks him. She slowly sips the refreshing iced tea and eats the sweet cake while happily watching the busy street outside. |
| 5 | 05_asking_for_water | After finishing her sweet cake, Maya feels thirsty for plain water. She catches the attention of a passing server and politely asks if he can give her a glass of cold water. |
| 6 | 06_walking_to_the_counter | The server brings the water, and Maya drinks it quickly. Knowing she needs to head back outside, she gathers her bag and walks back to the front counter to ask for her total. |
| 7 | 07_paying_and_thanking | The worker at the counter tells her the amount, and Maya politely pays two hundred and fifty rupees. She warmly thanks him for the delicious iced tea before stepping back out into the bright sun. |
