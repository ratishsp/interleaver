# Week 1 — Storyboard (Maya arrives in Kochi)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Equational **ആണ്** (X is Y) and existential **ഉണ്ട്** (there is); subject pronouns *ഞാൻ/നിങ്ങൾ/അവൻ/അവൾ*; **എന്ത്/എവിടെ** questions and the yes/no particle **-ഓ** (ആണോ?); *എന്റെ പേര് … ആണ്*. (Malayalam verbs do not change for person.)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_airport_arrival | Maya walks out of the airport into the warm, humid January air. A friendly fellow passenger notices her excitement and asks what her name is and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile. |
| 2 | 02_the_taxi | Maya puts her bag into a taxi and gives the driver her new address. The driver asks if she is a tourist visiting Kochi. She shakes her head and proudly explains that she is a software engineer. |
| 3 | 03_suitcase_struggle | Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new apartment building. She finds her door, inserts the key, and pushes her luggage inside the dark room. |
| 4 | 04_ceiling_fan | Maya flicks the switch on the wall to start the overhead ceiling fan. She walks around the room, relieved to see that there is a comfortable bed and a sturdy table for her computer. |
| 5 | 05_green_city | Maya leans out the window to look at the lush green trees. She feels the pleasant January sea breeze against her face and misses the familiar streets of Mexico for a brief moment. |
| 6 | 06_calling_mom | Maya sits on the edge of the bed and makes an audio call to her mother back in Mexico. She tells her family that she is safely in her flat and that she is happy. |
| 7 | 07_meeting_anitha | Maya steps out into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens. A kind woman smiles, introduces herself as Anitha, and asks Maya where she is from. |
| 8 | 08_family_photo | Maya returns to her room and opens her suitcase. She unpacks her clothes and places a cheerful photo of her sister Sofía, her brother Diego, and their dog Lola on the table. |
| 9 | 09_bedtime_routine | Maya brushes her teeth in the small bathroom and washes her face. She crawls into bed, listens to the whir of the ceiling fan, and sets her alarm for the morning. |
