# Week 9 — Storyboard (Maya visits the winter market)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Prices; *koster*; *hvor meget*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_arriving_at_the_market | Maya arrives at a busy outdoor market in a large square. The winter morning is cold, but the stalls look inviting. She carries a large empty bag and wants to buy fresh food for the weekend. |
| 2 | 02_carrots_for_soup | Maya stops at a vegetable stall to buy ingredients for a hot soup. She points at some winter carrots and asks how much they cost. The vendor says they cost "femten kroner" per kilo. Maya asks for one kilo and pays. |
| 3 | 03_a_taste_of_cheese | At the next stall, a friendly vendor sells large cheeses. He offers Maya a small piece on a wooden stick to taste. She eats it, likes it, and asks for the price of a small block. It costs "femoghalvtreds kroner", so she buys it. |
| 4 | 04_the_expensive_gloves | The wind blows cold, so Maya looks at a stall with warm clothes. She picks up a pair of thick wool gloves and asks: "How much do the gloves cost?" The vendor answers: "Tohundrede kroner." Maya thinks that is too dear, so she politely says no thank you and walks on. |
| 5 | 05_buying_winter_apples | Maya finds a fruit stall with baskets of dark red winter apples. She asks the vendor how much five apples cost. The vendor smiles and says the price is "femogtyve kroner". Maya takes out a paper note for "halvtreds kroner" and hands it to him. |
| 6 | 06_the_change | The fruit vendor takes the note and hands Maya a paper bag with the apples. He gives her the change and counts out the coins: "Femogtyve kroner back." Maya puts the heavy coins into her coat pocket. |
| 7 | 07_exact_change_for_coffee | Maya feels cold and spots a small coffee cart. She approaches and asks the barista how much a hot black coffee costs. The barista replies that it costs "tredive kroner". Maya takes the new coins from her pocket and pays with exact change. |
| 8 | 08_walking_home_with_groceries | Maya drinks her warm coffee and watches the busy market for a few minutes. Her bag is heavy with fresh winter food, and she knows how to ask for prices now. She leaves the square and walks back to her apartment. |
