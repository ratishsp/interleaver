# Week 20 — Storyboard (Maya starts volunteering at a café)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Past + perfect mixed; *lære at*; ordinals

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_first_saturday_morning | On her very first Saturday morning, Maya arrives early at the cozy local café. She has decided to volunteer on weekends because she wants to meet new people in the neighborhood and speak more Danish. |
| 2 | 02_learning_the_machine | Before the café opens, an experienced volunteer shows her how the large espresso machine works. Maya watches carefully because she must learn to steam the milk properly and pull a good shot of espresso. |
| 3 | 03_the_first_coffee | When the doors open, a rush of people comes in, and Maya makes her first coffee. She poured the milk too fast, so the drink comes out completely wrong and flat, but she hands it over with an apologetic smile. |
| 4 | 04_second_coffee_and_bun | Her second coffee turns out much better, topped with a thick layer of soft foam. She carefully balances the cup and carries it out to the third table, along with a warm bun with cheese. |
| 5 | 05_the_ginger_beer | A little later, a young woman sitting at the fourth table asks for a ginger beer instead of a hot drink. Maya has not served one before, but she searches the fridge and finally finds the cold bottles in the back. |
| 6 | 06_second_shift_progress | When her second Saturday shift rolls around, the café routines already feel much easier. Maya realizes that she has learned to operate the digital cash register quickly, and she no longer needs to ask where the spoons are kept. |
| 7 | 07_third_saturday_regular | By her third Saturday morning, Maya spots a familiar older man walking through the front door. She already knows his exact order by heart, and she has prepared his black coffee before he even reaches the counter. |
| 8 | 08_taking_stock | After finishing her shift, Maya wipes down the counter and takes stock of the past few weeks. She has made several mistakes, but she has learned to handle the busy periods, and she feels proud of her new skills. |
