# Week 24 — Storyboard (Maya looks after Anitha's dog)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Imperatives + accusative pronouns **അതിനെ/അവനെ**; rules with **വേണം/പാടില്ല** (must / must not)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_the_favor | Late Thursday evening, just as Maya returns to her flat from her weekly badminton game, Anitha knocks on her door and explains that she has to travel for the weekend. She asks if Maya would be willing to look after her dog, Tommy, and Maya happily agrees. |
| 2 | 02_learning_the_rules | On Friday evening, Anitha drops Tommy off with his food and leash. She clearly lays out the rules, explaining when he must be fed and warning Maya that he absolutely must not eat any sweet snacks. |
| 3 | 03_setting_boundaries | Later that night in the flat, Tommy attempts to jump onto Maya's sofa. Maya uses a firm imperative command to tell him no, pointing him toward his own cozy dog bed instead. |
| 4 | 04_early_breakfast | Very early on Saturday morning, before her scheduled café volunteering shift, Maya prepares Tommy's breakfast. She carefully measures out his specific dry food, strictly remembering Anitha's rule that she must not give him any human food from her own plate. |
| 5 | 05_saturday_walk | After breakfast, Maya takes Tommy for a walk in the light monsoon drizzle before her café shift. She securely attaches his leash — near the busy road he must stay on the lead — and when he suddenly pulls toward a stray cat, she firmly commands him to stop and wait until he calms down. Back at the flat, Tommy curls up contentedly on his dog bed, and Maya leaves for the café. |
| 6 | 06_video_call_home | On Sunday morning, Maya starts a video call with her family in Mexico. She points the camera at Tommy to show her sister Sofia and their own dog Lola, explaining how she has been taking care of him. |
| 7 | 07_sunday_walk | On Sunday afternoon, Maya takes Tommy on a longer, easier walk with his little rubber ball. On a quiet patch of grass she plays fetch with him — throw it, catch it, bring it back — and Tommy is delighted. Only once does she have to call him back sharply, when he wanders toward a deep, muddy puddle: he must not go into the water. |
| 8 | 08_anithas_return | Anitha returns on Sunday evening to pick up Tommy. Maya hands him back with a smile, reporting that he was a very good boy who perfectly followed all of the weekend's rules. |
