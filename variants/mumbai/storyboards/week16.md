# Week 16 — Storyboard (Maya recounts her arrival in Mumbai)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Simple past — intransitives **गया/गई, आया/आई** agreeing with the subject; transitives with **ने** (मैंने देखा); time phrases **कल / पिछले हफ़्ते / जनवरी में** (the perfect *-आ है* is out of scope — week 19 owns it)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_returning_the_racket | It is Thursday evening, well after Maya's weekly half-past-five badminton session. She walks to the club counter and returns her rented racket. Her friends Amit, Neha, and Sanjay are waiting for her near the courts. |
| 2 | 02_cutting_chai_conversation | Stepping outside the club, the friends grab cutting chai from a local vendor. Now comfortable friends, Amit, Neha, and Sanjay slip into informal address with Maya for the first time, and ask her how she originally ended up moving to Mumbai. |
| 3 | 03_the_january_arrival | Maya begins her story, recalling the past. She recounts landing in January for her software job, stepping out of the airport with her heavy bags, and nervously handing a taxi driver a slip of paper with her new apartment address. |
| 4 | 04_meeting_asha | Continuing her tale, Maya describes her very first day at the apartment building. She explains how she arrived at her door and met her kind neighbour Asha, who immediately made her feel welcome in the unfamiliar city. |
| 5 | 05_first_day_at_office | Maya finishes her timeline by recounting her first day at the software company. She describes how she went to the office, logged into her computer, and met her helpful colleague Rohan. Amit, Neha, and Sanjay listen warmly to the story. |
| 6 | 06_evening_bus_ride_home | Bidding Amit, Neha, and Sanjay a friendly goodbye, Maya walks to the nearby bus stop. She boards her regular BEST bus, looking out the window and reflecting happily on those busy first few days in the city. |
| 7 | 07_calling_mexico | Back in her quiet apartment, Maya opens her laptop and initiates a video call home to Mexico. Her mother answers the call with a bright smile, and Maya respectfully addresses her using 'आप'. |
| 8 | 08_a_mothers_request | Maya mentions to her mother that she spent the evening telling her new friends the story of her arrival in Mumbai. Feeling nostalgic, her mother asks to hear the story of that day again, specifically the morning they said goodbye. |
| 9 | 09_the_airport_memory | Maya recounts the morning she left Mexico, recalling how she packed her final bags. Her mother affectionately chimes in, adding her own memories of how they stood at the airport gates and shared a tearful, loving goodbye before Maya flew away. |
