Week 13: Maya goes to a café

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.
哥本哈根而家係冬天。It is winter in Copenhagen.
我喺街度行緊。I am walking down the street.
啲風好凍。The wind is very cold.
今日好大風。It is blowing hard today.
我頂住啲寒風向前行。I lean against the cold wind.
我將條頸巾拉高。I pull my scarf up.
我塊面覺得好凍。My face is freezing.
我望吓四周圍。I look around.
我好想入去啲暖笠笠嘅地方。I would like to get into the warmth.
我見到轉角有間細細哋嘅咖啡店。I see a small café on the corner.
啲窗起晒霧。The windows are fogged up.
入面睇落好暖。It looks warm in there.
我行過去間咖啡店度。I walk over to the café.
我打開度好重嘅門。I open the heavy door.
我行入去。I step inside.
呢度有陣好香嘅咖啡味。It smells like coffee here.
啲空氣好暖好舒服。The air is nice and warm.
我閂埋身後度門。I close the door behind me.
啲凍風留晒喺出面。The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
我而家喺咖啡店入面。I am inside the café now.
呢度好暖。It is very warm here.
我除低件厚厚嘅冬天大褸。I take off my thick winter coat.
件褸好重。It is heavy.
我喺牆上面搵到個掛勾。I find a hook on the wall.
我將件褸掛喺個勾上面。I hang my coat on the hook.
我仲除低埋條頸巾同手套。I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
間咖啡店細細間，好溫馨。The café is small and cozy.
我想搵個好位坐。I look for a good seat.
窗邊有張細枱。There is a small table by the window.
張枱旁邊有部暖氣機。The table is next to a radiator.
我行過去張枱度。I walk over to the table.
我坐低喺張凳度。I sit down on the chair.
我對手仲係好凍。My hands are still cold.
我搓吓對手。I rub them together.
對手變得暖返啲。My hands get warmer.
感覺好正。It feels fantastic.
而家我差唔多暖晒喇。Now I am almost warm.
我又肚餓又口渴。I am hungry and thirsty.
我而家想叫啲嘢食。I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
我行埋去玻璃櫃嗰度。I walk over to the glass counter.
裏面擺滿晒蛋糕同麵包。It is full of cakes and bread.
我望住咁多樣嘢。I look at the many things.
佢哋望落好好味。They look very delicious.
蛋糕旁邊有啲細細嘅牌。There are small signs next to the cakes.
啲牌係手寫嘅。The signs are handwritten.
我睇緊牌上面嘅名。I read the names on the signs.
有好多種糕點。There are many kinds of pastry.
好難揀。It is hard to choose.
有一個好大嘅朱古力蛋糕。There is a large chocolate cake.
但係我望住另一件糕點。But I look at another piece of pastry.
係一個好大嘅肉桂卷。It is a large cinnamon roll.
面頭有好多糖。It has a lot of sugar on top.
我好鍾意肉桂嘅味道。I love the taste of cinnamon.
最啱凍冰冰嘅冬天。It is perfect for a cold winter day.
我好想要嗰個肉桂卷。I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
而家我準備好落單喇。Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
我行過去收銀台。I walk over to the register.
一個年輕男人企喺櫃檯後面。A young man stands behind the counter.
佢係咖啡店嘅咖啡師。He is a barista in the café.
我對佢微笑。I smile at him.
佢友善咁點頭。He nods warmly.
「你好，」佢話。"Hi," he says.
「你想叫啲咩？」佢問。"What would you like?" he asks.
「你好，」我答。"Hi," I answer.
「我要一杯黑咖啡，唔該。」"I would like a black coffee, please."
「同埋我可唔可以要一件肉桂卷？」"And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
「當然可以，」佢話。"Of course," he says.
「一杯黑咖啡同一件肉桂卷。」"A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
「你想喺度坐低嗎？」佢問。"Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
「好呀，唔該，我會去嗰邊坐低，」我話。"Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
「冇問題，」咖啡師話。"Alright," the barista says.
「我依家幫你沖咖啡。」"I will make your coffee now."
「多謝晒，」我話。"Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
我喺櫃枱等。I wait at the counter.
佢沖我杯咖啡。He makes my coffee.
佢將一杯熱咖啡放喺木盤上面。He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
佢又放咗隻細碟喺個盤上面。He also puts a small plate on the tray.
我個肉桂卷就喺隻碟上面。My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
聞落好香。It smells lovely.
佢話：「呢度係你叫嘅嘢。」"Here you go, your order," he says.
我答：「唔該晒。」"Thank you very much," I reply.
「多謝你咁好嘅服務。」"Thank you for the good service."
佢好友善咁話：「唔使客氣。」"You are welcome," he says kindly.
我用雙手捧起個盤。I take the tray with both hands.
我好小心咁行過地板。I walk very carefully across the floor.
我行返去窗邊嗰張枱。I walk back to my table by the window.
我將個盤放低喺枱面。I put the tray down on the table.
依家我想坐低。Now I would like to sit down.
我準備好要好好休息吓喇。I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
我而家坐喺我張細枱度。I now sit at my small table.
呢度好暖好舒服。It is lovely and warm here.
我望住我個玉桂卷。I look at my cinnamon roll.
佢聞落又甜又香。It smells sweet and good.
我咬咗一細啖。I take a small bite.
呢件包非常新鮮，仲好暖。The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
食落真係好好味。It tastes really good.
我飲咗少少我杯黑咖啡。I drink a little of my black coffee.
終於，我啲手指又暖返喇。Finally, my fingers are warm again.
我坐喺張軟綿綿嘅櫈度放鬆落嚟。I relax in the soft chair.
咖啡店差唔多坐滿晒人。The café is almost full of people.
大家幾個人一組咁坐。People sit in small groups.
佢哋一齊有傾有笑。They talk and laugh together.
我聽住室內細細聲嘅說話聲。I listen to the low voices in the room.
呢啲聲聽落好舒服。The sound is very cozy.
我望向大窗外面。I look out of the large window.
外面嘅天氣灰沉沉，仲吹緊大風。The weather outside is gray and windy.
而家其實又落緊雪喇。Now it is actually snowing again.
白色嘅雪花慢慢飄落條街度。White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
我淨係好開心可以留喺室內。I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
我食埋我個肉桂卷最後一嚿。I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
味道真係好好。It tastes really good.
但係都好甜。But it is also very sweet.
我隻咖啡杯已經空晒。My coffee cup is completely empty.
我突然間好口渴。I am suddenly very thirsty.
我想飲啲凍水。I would like to have some cold water.
有個後生仔行過我張枱。A young man walks past my table.
佢喺呢間咖啡店度做嘢。He works here in the café.
佢收緊啲吉杯同吉碟。He collects empty cups and plates.
我同佢對上眼。I catch his eye.
佢喺我張枱前面停低微笑。He stops at my table and smiles.
「唔該，」我有禮貌噉講。"Excuse me," I say politely.
「係？」個後生仔答。"Yes?" answers the young man.
「請問可唔可以要杯自來水呀？」我問。"May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
「可以，當然冇問題，」佢話。"Yes, of course," he says.
「唔該晒，」我講。"Thank you very much," I say.
佢收走我隻吉碟同吉杯。He takes my empty plate and cup.
佢行返去櫃枱。He walks back to the counter.
過咗一陣，佢攞住一杯凍水過嚟。A little later he comes with a cold glass of water.
asking for the bill
Storyboard: Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab.
我飲我杯水。I drink my water.
啲水好凍，好正。The water is cold and nice.
我喺地下拎起我個袋。I take my bag from the floor.
我休息完喇。I am finished with my break.
我行埋去櫃枱。I walk up to the counter.
嗰個友善嘅咖啡師仲企喺度。The friendly barista is still standing there.
佢笑住咁望住我。He looks at me with a smile.
「仲要唔要其他嘢呀？」佢問。"Would you like anything else?" he asks.
「唔使喇，唔該，」我答。"No thank you," I answer.
「我想埋單。」"I would like to pay."
「好，當然可以，」佢話。"Yes, of course," he says.
佢望住佢個細熒幕。He looks at his little screen.
「一杯黑咖啡同一個肉桂卷，」佢話。"One black coffee and one cinnamon roll," he says.
「係，無錯，」我話。"Yes, that is correct," I say.
「你想畀卡定現金？」佢問。"Do you want to pay with card or cash?" he asks.
「我想畀卡，」我答。"I would like to pay with a card," I answer.
「好。」"Fine."
「總共八十五克朗，」佢話。"That will be eighty-five kroner," he says.
我攞我張銀行卡出嚟。I take out my payment card.
paying and leaving
Storyboard: Maya taps her card on the terminal to pay eighty-five kroner. She puts on her heavy coat, thanks the staff for a nice time, and pushes the front door open to face the cold again.
咖啡師對住我笑。The barista smiles at me.
我將張卡放喺刷卡機上面。I hold my card over the terminal.
部機「嗶」咗一聲。The machine beeps.
「你要唔要收據呀？」佢問。"Do you want a receipt?" he asks.
「唔使喇，唔該，」我答。"No thank you," I answer.
我行過去牆邊。I walk over to the wall.
我喺掛鈎度攞我件厚嘅冬天外套。I take my thick winter coat from a hook.
我著起件外套。I put the coat on.
我亦都戴上頸巾。I also put my scarf on.
我亦都戴上手套。I also put my gloves on.
依家我準備好面對寒冷喇。Now I am ready for the cold.
我再望向咖啡師。I look at the barista again.
「多謝晒你杯咖啡，」我講。"Thank you very much for the coffee," I say.
「唔使客氣，」佢微笑住咁講。"You are welcome," he says with a smile.
「祝你有美好嘅一日。」"Have a good day."
「你都係，」我講。"Likewise," I say.
我行去大門。I walk over to the front door.
我推開嗰道好重嘅門。I push the heavy door open.
啲風好凍，但我感覺好好。The wind is cold, but I feel good.
