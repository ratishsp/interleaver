Week 5: Maya stocks her new kitchen

Croatian with English gloss, line-aligned.

empty cupboards
Storyboard: Maya stands in the kitchen, noticing that the fridge and the cupboard are completely empty. She grabs a bag and heads out, ready to buy food.
Šest je sati.It is six o'clock.
Stojim u kuhinji.I am standing in the kitchen.
Gladna sam.I am hungry.
Otvaram hladnjak.I open the fridge.
Potpuno je prazan.It is completely empty.
Gledam u ormarić.I look in the cupboard.
U ormariću nema hrane.There is no food in the cupboard.
Nemam hrane.I have no food.
Imam samo malo vode.I only have a little water.
Sada bih htjela hranu.I would like to have food now.
Volim povrće.I like vegetables.
Volim i kruh.I also like bread.
Ali ovdje nemam ništa.But I have nothing here.
Pronalazim vrećicu.I find a bag.
Vrećica je crvena.The bag is red.
Dobra je za kupovinu.It is good for shopping.
Izlazim iz stana.I walk out of the flat.
Zatvaram vrata.I close the door.
Spremna sam.I am ready.
Sada idem u supermarket.Now I am walking to the supermarket.
produce section
Storyboard: Maya enters a supermarket and pulls a basket from a stack by the door. Because she likes carrots, she grabs a large bunch from a green crate and drops it into the basket.
Stojim ispred supermarketa.I stand in front of the supermarket.
Supermarket je velik i svijetao.The supermarket is large and bright.
Vrata se automatski otvaraju.The door opens automatically.
Ulazim u trgovinu.I go into the shop.
Trgovina ima mnogo proizvoda.The shop has many goods.
Pored ulaza su hrpe košara.There are stacks of baskets by the entrance.
Uzimam košaru.I take a basket.
Košara je crvena i lagana.The basket is red and light.
Tražim povrće.I look for vegetables.
Odjel je odmah ovdje.The section is right here.
Vidim gajbu na podu.I see a crate on the floor.
Gajba je zelena.The crate is green.
U gajbi ima mnogo mrkvi.There are many carrots in the crate.
Volim mrkve.I like carrots.
Biram veliki svežanj.I choose a large bunch.
Svežanj izgleda vrlo svježe.The bunch looks very fresh.
Stavljam svežanj u košaru.I put the bunch down into the basket.
Sada košara nije prazna.Now the basket is not empty.
Idem dalje u trgovinu.I walk further into the shop.
the parsnip
Storyboard: Looking around, Maya spots a pale, unusual root. She reads a sign and learns it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she decides to buy it anyway out of curiosity.
Vidim čudno povrće.I see a strange vegetable.
To povrće je dugačak korijen.The vegetable is a long root.
Korijen je vrlo blijed.The root is very pale.
Izgleda kao bijela mrkva.It looks like a white carrot.
Gledam dolje na natpis.I look down at the sign.
Na natpisu piše "pastrnjak".It says "parsnip" on the sign.
Ne poznajem to povrće.I do not know the vegetable.
Je li to dansko povrće?Is it a Danish vegetable?
Volim povrće.I like vegetables.
Volim mrkve.I like carrots.
Možda pastrnjak ima dobar okus.Maybe the parsnip tastes good.
Uzimam pastrnjak u ruku.I take the parsnip in my hand.
Korijen je prilično velik.The root is quite big.
Voljela bih ga uzeti.I would like to have it.
Gledam u košaru.I look at the basket.
Stavljam korijen u košaru.I put the root in the basket.
Voljela bih probati nešto novo.I would like to try something new.
the rye bread
Storyboard: At a shelf with baked goods, Maya looks for a local staple. She lifts a loaf of rye bread, surprised by how heavy it feels, and lays the bread carefully in the basket.
Prilazim polici s kruhom.I walk over to a shelf with bread.
Tražim lokalni klasik.I am looking for a local classic.
Htjela bih danski raženi kruh.I would like to have a Danish rye bread.
Gledam policu.I look at the shelf.
Nalazim raženi kruh.I find a rye bread.
U vrećici je.It is in a bag.
Gledam vrećicu.I look at the bag.
Uzimam raženi kruh.I take the rye bread.
Podižem kruh.I lift the bread.
Iznenađena sam.I am surprised.
Kruh je jako težak.The bread is very heavy.
Djeluje kao kamen!It feels like a stone!
Volim svijetli kruh.I like light bread.
Ali raženi kruh je taman i težak.But the rye bread is dark and heavy.
Pažljivo stavljam kruh u košaru.I carefully lay the bread in the basket.
Košara je sada također teška.The basket is also heavy now.
bakery counter
Storyboard: The bakery counter has a member of staff waiting. Maya catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry.
Prilazim pekarskom pultu.I walk over to the bakery counter.
Miris je jako dobar.The smell is very good.
Volim kolače.I like cakes.
I jako volim peciva s cimetom.And I really like cinnamon pastries.
Pekar stoji iza pulta.A baker stands behind the counter.
Pekar me gleda.The baker looks at me.
Smiješi se.He smiles.
"Što biste željeli?" pita pekar."What would you like to have?" the baker asks.
Pokazujem na pecivo s cimetom.I point at a cinnamon pastry.
Pecivo izgleda ukusno.The pastry looks delicious.
"Željela bih pecivo s cimetom, molim vas," kažem."I would like to have a cinnamon pastry, please," I say.
"Da, naravno," kaže pekar."Yes, of course," the baker says.
On uzima pecivo.He takes the pastry.
Stavlja pecivo u vrećicu.He puts the pastry in a bag.
Pruža vrećicu preko pulta.He hands the bag over the counter.
"Izvolite," kaže on."Here you go," he says.
Uzimam vrećicu.I take the bag.
"Hvala vam puno," kažem."Thank you very much," I say.
Stavljam vrećicu u košaru.I put the bag down into the basket.
finding the milk
Storyboard: Maya searches for the milk without luck. She stops an employee to ask for help, and he points her toward the dairy section at the back.
Sada mi treba mlijeko.Now I need milk.
Hodam niz dugi prolaz.I walk down a long aisle.
Gledam policu.I look at the shelf.
Mogu vidjeti vodu i sok.I can see the water and the juice.
Ali ne mogu pronaći mlijeko.But I cannot find the milk.
Gledam posvuda.I look everywhere.
Vidim muškarca.I see a man.
On radi u supermarketu.He works in the supermarket.
Zaustavljam zaposlenika i smiješim se.I stop the employee and smile.
"Oprostite," kažem."Excuse me," I say.
"Htjela bih mlijeko.""I would like to have milk."
"Gdje je mlijeko?""Where is the milk?"
Muškarac pokazuje prema stražnjem dijelu trgovine.The man points to the back of the shop.
"Mliječni odjel je tamo dolje," kaže on."The dairy section is down there," he says.
"Puno hvala," odgovaram."Thank you very much," I reply.
Idem tamo dolje.I walk down there.
Konačno pronalazim hladnjak i mlijeko.I finally find the fridge and the milk.
Uzimam tetrapak i stavljam ga u košaru.I take a carton and lay it in the basket.
taste of home
Storyboard: Exploring another section, Maya hunts for a taste of Mexico because she really likes beans. She recognizes a bright can of black beans on a low shelf and reaches all the way to the back to pull it out.
Idem dalje do druge police.I walk on to another set of shelves.
Gledam hranu.I look at the food.
Supermarket ima puno stvari iz drugih zemalja.The supermarket has many things from other countries.
Tražim okus Meksika.I look for a taste of Mexico.
Nedostaje mi hrana od kuće.I miss the food from home.
Jako volim grah.I really like beans.
Često jedem crni grah.I often eat black beans.
Gledam nisku policu.I look at a low shelf.
Polica je malo tamna.The shelf is a bit dark.
Ali vidim šarenu konzervu.But I see a colourful can.
Konzerva stoji skroz straga.The can stands all the way at the back.
To je konzerva crnog graha.It is a can of black beans.
Smiješim se.I smile.
Pružam ruku skroz unutra.I reach my arm all the way in.
Izvlačim konzervu.I pull the can out.
Čitam etiketu na konzervi.I read the label on the can.
Savršena je za moju večeru.It is perfect for my dinner.
Stavljam konzervu u košaru.I put the can in the basket.
Imam dovoljno hrane.I have enough food.
the checkout
Storyboard: Maya brings the basket to the checkout. She places the food on the belt, holds her card over the machine, and packs the bag quickly. No price is spoken or shown.
Prilazim blagajni.I walk up to the checkout.
Imam košaru u ruci.I have the basket in my hand.
Stavljam hranu na traku.I put the food on the belt.
Prvo uzimam raženi kruh iz košare.First I take the rye bread from the basket.
Zatim uzimam mlijeko i pastrnjak.Then I take the milk and the parsnip.
Također uzimam mrkve i crni grah.I also take carrots and black beans.
Na kraju stavljam pecivo na traku.Finally I put the pastry on the belt.
Blagajnik sjedi iza blagajne.The cashier sits behind the checkout.
Smiješi mi se.He smiles at me.
"Bok", kaže on."Hello," he says.
"Bok", odgovaram."Hello," I reply.
Brzo skenira hranu.He scans the food quickly.
Vadim svoju karticu.I take my card out.
Držim karticu iznad aparata.I hold the card over the machine.
Aparat zapišti.The machine beeps.
"Želite li račun?" pita on."Would you like to have the receipt?" he asks.
"Da, molim, željela bih račun", kažem."Yes please, I would like to have the receipt," I say.
Otvaram svoju vrećicu.I open my bag.
Brzo pakiram vrećicu.I pack the bag quickly.
"Ugodan dan", kaže on."Have a good day," he says.
putting away
Storyboard: Back in the flat, Maya empties the bag on the counter. She puts the milk and the parsnip in the fridge, and she places the bread and the can in the cupboard.
Opet sam kod kuće u stanu.I am home in the flat again.
Stojim u kuhinji.I am standing in the kitchen.
Praznim vrećicu na kuhinjski pult.I empty the bag on the kitchen counter.
Raspakiravam hranu.I unpack the food.
Uzimam mlijeko.I take the milk.
Stavljam mlijeko u hladnjak.I put the milk in the fridge.
Zatim gledam pastrnjak.Then I look at the parsnip.
To je smiješan korijen.It is a funny root.
Koristit ću ga u svojoj juhi.I will use it in my soup.
Stavljam pastrnjak u hladnjak.I put the parsnip in the fridge.
Sada hladnjak nije prazan.Now the fridge is not empty.
Uzimam kruh.I take the bread.
Raženi kruh je jako težak.The rye bread is very heavy.
Stavljam kruh u ormarić.I put the bread in the cupboard.
Zatim uzimam konzervu graha.Then I take the can of beans.
Grah dugo traje.Beans last a long time.
Stavljam konzervu u ormarić.I put the can in the cupboard.
Sada u ormariću ima hrane.Now there is food in the cupboard.
Prilično sam gladna.I am quite hungry.
Htjela bih sada večerati.I would like to have dinner now.
first home cooked meal
Storyboard: Ready to eat, Maya chops some vegetables and opens the can. She cooks dinner on the stove, finally making a proper meal in the flat — and afterwards she has the pastry from the bakery counter for dessert.
Kuhinja je mala.The kitchen is small.
Ali štednjak je dobar.But the stove is good.
Uzimam pastrnjak i mrkvu.I take the parsnip and a carrot.
Uzimam i nož.I also take a knife.
Nož je oštar.The knife is sharp.
Režem pastrnjak i mrkvu.I cut the parsnip and the carrot.
Otvaram konzervu.I open the can.
Stavljam hranu u lonac.I put the food in a pot.
Lonac je na štednjaku.The pot is on the stove.
Sada je hrana topla.Now the food is warm.
Divno miriše.It smells lovely.
Uzimam raženi kruh.I take the rye bread.
Režem krišku.I cut a slice.
Tanjur je na stolu.The plate is on the table.
Konačno jedem pravi obrok u stanu.Finally, I am eating a proper meal in the flat.
Nakon toga jedem pecivo s cimetom.Afterwards I eat the cinnamon pastry.
Ima sladak okus.It tastes sweet.
