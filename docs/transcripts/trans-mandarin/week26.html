Week 26: Maya's first Danish summer and thirty-second birthday

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

birthday messages
Storyboard: Maya wakes up on the morning she turns thirty-two, feeling a bit nostalgic as it is her first birthday away from Mexico. She smiles as she reads the flood of loving messages that her family has sent overnight while she slept.
我今天早上很早就醒了。I woke up early this morning.
阳光明媚地照进了我的卧室。The sun shone brightly into my bedroom.
我躺在床上，想着我在墨西哥的家。I lay in bed and thought about my home in Mexico.
今天其实是我的生日。Today is actually my birthday.
我今天满三十二岁。I turn thirty-two years old today.
这是我第一次离开家人过生日。It is my first birthday away from my family.
我对此感到有些怀旧。I felt a little nostalgic about it.
但随后我从床头柜上拿起了手机。But then I picked up my phone from the nightstand.
我的家人发来了许多充满爱意的信息。My family has sent many loving messages.
他们是在昨晚我睡觉时写的。They wrote them last night while I slept.
我的父母录了一段我们的狗萝拉的搞笑视频。My parents have recorded a funny video with Lola, our dog.
我的妹妹索菲娅写了一条很长很温馨的信息。My sister Sofía has written a long and sweet text.
而我的弟弟迭戈发来了一张我最喜欢的蛋糕的照片。And my brother Diego sent a picture of my favorite cake.
看到照片时，我露出了大大的笑容。I smiled broadly when I saw the picture.
他们根本没有忘记我的大日子。They have not forgotten my big day at all.
我立刻回复了所有的信息。I replied to all the messages immediately.
我给他们写道，我爱他们，也很想念他们。I wrote to them that I love and miss them.
我一遍又一遍地读着这些信息。I read the messages again and again.
这是我生日的美妙开端。It is a fantastic start to my birthday.
office pastry
Storyboard: At the software company, Maya is surprised when Peter and Eva place a small cake with a single candle on her desk. They wish her a happy birthday.
我正坐在办公室的办公桌前。I am sitting at my desk in the office.
我今天很早就来上班了。I arrived early today.
我已经在这里工作半年了。I have already worked here for half a year.
突然，彼得和伊娃走到我的办公桌旁。Suddenly Peter and Eva come over to my desk.
彼得端着一个小蛋糕，上面插着一根蜡烛。Peter is carrying a small cake with a single candle.
“生日快乐，玛雅！”他们异口同声地说。"Happy birthday, Maya!" they say in unison.
我感到非常开心，也非常惊讶。I become really happy and very surprised.
“非常感谢，”我对他们说。"Thank you very much," I say to them.
伊娃笑得很灿烂。Eva smiles broadly.
“我们今天早上在面包店买了这块蛋糕，”她解释道。"We bought the cake at the bakery this morning," she explains.
“对，我还给我们泡了咖啡，”彼得补充道。"Yes, and I have made coffee for us," Peter adds.
他在我的桌上放了一大杯咖啡。He places a large cup of coffee on my table.
“你们真是太贴心了，”我说。"You are simply so sweet," I say.
“以前我在墨西哥工作的时候，早上很少有蛋糕吃。”"When I worked in Mexico, we rarely got cake in the morning."
彼得和伊娃拉了两把椅子到办公桌旁。Peter and Eva pull two chairs over to the desk.
我们一起吃蛋糕，这是我这一天极好的开始。We eat the cake together, and it is a fantastic start to my day.
family call
Storyboard: That evening, Maya has a lively video call with her parents, Sofía, Diego, and Lola the dog. Sofía eagerly asks if Maya has opened the package they sent, and Maya happily holds up the colorful scarf that arrived in the mail yesterday.
晚上，我带着电脑坐在我的沙发上。In the evening, I sit on my sofa with my computer.
我给在墨西哥的家人打电话。I call my family in Mexico.
屏幕上显示出我的父母、妹妹索菲亚和弟弟迭戈。The screen shows my parents, my sister Sofía, and my brother Diego.
我们的狗，萝拉，也在那里。Our dog, Lola, is there too.
“生日快乐！”他们一起大喊。"Happy birthday!" they all shout.
索菲亚问。Sofía asks.
“你收到我们的包裹了吗？”"Have you received our package?"
“是的，昨天邮递到的，”我回答。"Yes, it arrived in the mail yesterday," I answer.
“你打开了吗？”她兴奋地问。"Have you opened it?" she asks excitedly.
我微笑着从桌子上拿起一条色彩鲜艳的围巾。I smile and take a colorful scarf from the table.
“我回家的时候就打开了，”我说着，把围巾举了起来。"I opened it when I came home," I say and hold the scarf up.
“我真的很喜欢这些颜色。”"I really love the colors."
我把围巾绕在脖子上。I put the scarf around my neck.
“你戴着真好看，”我妈妈说。"It suits you well," my mother says.
“你今天过得好吗？”我爸爸问。"Have you had a good day today?" my father asks.
“是的，我的同事之前给我买了一个蛋糕，”我解释道。"Yes, my colleagues bought a cake for me earlier," I explain.
迭戈把萝拉举到了镜头前。Diego lifts Lola up to the camera.
“萝拉也吃了一个小蛋糕，”他笑着说。"Lola has also eaten a little cake," he laughs.
我对着这只小狗大声笑了起来。I laugh loudly at the little dog.
这是我第一次离家在外过生日，但感觉他们离我很近。It is my first birthday away from home, but they feel very close.
cafe summer
Storyboard: A few days after her birthday, Maya finishes her Saturday morning café shift, unties her apron and wipes down the last counter. Eager for the big summer bonfire by the sea that evening, she texts Eva to confirm they are still meeting, noting she got off early today unlike last weekend when she stayed late.
今天是星期六上午，我在咖啡馆的工作结束了。It is Saturday morning, and I am finished at the café.
我刚擦完最后一张桌子。I have just wiped down the last table.
我解开围裙，把它放在椅子上。I untie my apron and put it on a chair.
今天我的班次很短。Today I have had a short shift.
上周我一直工作到三点。Last week I worked until three o'clock.
那天我们整天都很忙。That day we were very busy all day.
但今天我们只有几位客人。But today we have only had a few guests.
外面阳光明媚，这是一个美丽的夏日。The sun is shining outside, and it is a beautiful summer day.
今晚海边有一场大篝火。Tonight there is a big bonfire down by the sea.
我非常期待去看。I am looking forward to seeing it very much.
我拿起手机，给伊娃写了一条信息。I take my phone and write a message to Eva.
我问她：“我们今晚还见面吗？”I ask her, "Are we still meeting tonight?"
我还写道，我已经下班了。I also write that I am already off work.
“今天我下班早，所以我准备好了，”我写道。"I finished early today, so I am ready," I write.
伊娃几乎立刻就回复了。Eva replies almost immediately.
“是的，当然！”她回复道。"Yes, of course!" she replies.
“我刚买了新鲜的草莓。”"I have just bought fresh strawberries."
“晚点在海滩见，”她写道。"See you at the beach later," she writes.
我微笑着拿起包。I smile and take my bag.
然后我走到外面温暖的夏日天气中。Then I walk out into the warm summer weather.
waterfront
Storyboard: Back home that evening, Maya gets ready to head down to the waterfront.
我在我的公寓里。I am home in my apartment.
现在是星期六晚上，但太阳依然照耀着。It is Saturday evening, but the sun is still shining.
我正在为海滩边的篝火晚会做准备。I am getting ready for the bonfire by the beach.
我已经收拾好包了。I have packed my bag.
我在包里放了一件保暖的毛衣。I put a warm sweater in the bag.
这件毛衣是我二月份买的。I bought the sweater in February.
现在的傍晚天还很亮，但晚些时候通常会变冷。The evenings are bright now, but it often gets cold later.
我还做了一些零食。I have also made some snacks.
我今天早上在去咖啡馆值班前买了东西。I shopped this morning before my shift at the café.
我的手机响了，我接了起来。My phone rings, and I answer it.
是伊娃。It is Eva.
“你准备好了吗，玛雅？”她问。"Are you ready, Maya?" she asks.
“是的，我差不多准备好了，”我回答。"Yes, I am almost ready," I answer.
“我刚找到我的墨镜，”我说。"I have just found my sunglasses," I say.
“我找了它们二十分钟，”我笑着说。"I looked for them for twenty minutes," I laugh.
“完美，半小时后我们在水边见，”伊娃说。"Perfect, see you at the water in half an hour," Eva says.
我穿上鞋，把包背在肩上。I put on my shoes and put the bag over my shoulder.
我锁上门，走下楼梯。I lock the door and walk down the stairs.
我真的很期待我在丹麦的第一个夏日夜晚。I am really looking forward to my first Danish summer evening.
bus stop contrast
Storyboard: Walking down toward the beach alone, Maya marvels at how bright the evening sky still is. She thinks back to January, when she stood at the bus stop after work in pitch darkness at four o'clock, freezing — a sharp contrast with tonight, when the sun has not even started to set.
我独自向海滩走去。I walk down towards the beach alone.
已经是傍晚了，但天空依然很亮。It is already evening, but the sky is still very bright.
太阳还没有开始下山。The sun has not started to set yet.
我抬起头，回想起一月份。I look up and think back to January.
那时我下班后经常等公交车。Back then I often waited for the bus after work.
当时才下午四点。It was only four o'clock in the afternoon.
但到处都已经漆黑一片了。But it was already pitch dark everywhere.
我站在公交车站，冻得瑟瑟发抖。I stood at the bus stop and froze.
现在我在这里已经住了半年了。Now I have lived here for half a year.
一切都改变了。Everything has changed.
在墨西哥，太阳总是几乎在同一时间下山。In Mexico, the sun always went down at almost the same time.
一年到头都是如此。It was like that all year.
在这里，我以前从未经历过这么长的白天。Here I have never experienced such long days before.
阳光给这座城市带来了全新的生机。The light has given the city a whole new life.
我穿上保暖的毛衣。I put on my warm sweater.
尽管天空很亮，但海面上吹来的风还是有点冷。Even though the sky is bright, the wind blows a bit cold from the sea.
我很期待去看篝火。I am looking forward to seeing the bonfire.
这将是和朋友们度过的一个美好的夜晚。It will be a lovely evening with my friends.
beach bonfire
Storyboard: At the beach Maya meets Eva and Peter just as the crowd lights the towering summer bonfire. Eva shares fresh strawberries she bought at the market, and the glowing fire chases away the coastal chill as they watch the flames together.
我朝海滩走去。I walk down to the beach.
已经有很多人来参加今晚的派对了。Many people have already arrived for the evening's party.
我在人群中寻找伊娃和彼得。I look for Eva and Peter in the crowd.
突然，我看到他们在水边，便赶紧走了过去。Suddenly I see them down by the water, and I hurry over there.
“你的生日周过得开心吗？”彼得微笑着问。"Have you had a good birthday week?" Peter asks with a smile.
“是的，简直太棒了，”我高兴地回答。"Yes, it has been absolutely fantastic," I answer happily.
伊娃打开一个小篮子。Eva opens a small basket.
“我今天早上在市场上买了新鲜的草莓，”她说。"I bought fresh strawberries at the market this morning," she says.
“你尝过丹麦的夏日草莓吗？”"Have you ever tasted Danish summer strawberries?"
“没有，我从来没吃过，”我一边说一边拿了一颗。"No, I have never tried that," I say and take a berry.
它尝起来非常甜，让我想起了夏天。It tastes incredibly sweet and reminds me of summer.
就在那时，一名男子点燃了巨大的篝火。Right at that moment, a man lights the big bonfire.
火焰迅速变大，高高地窜向天空。The flames grow quickly and stretch high up toward the sky.
之前还有点冷，但现在火已经让空气变暖了。It was cold earlier, but now the fire has warmed up the air.
炽热的温度迅速驱散了海岸边的寒意。The glowing heat quickly chases away the cold from the coast.
我们紧挨着站在一起，久久地注视着火焰。We stand close together and look into the flames for a long time.
我想到自己在这座城市已经住了六个月了。I think about how I have now lived here in the city for six months.
我在这里的第一个月十分昏暗，但这个明亮的夏夜堪称完美。My first month here was dark, but this bright summer evening is perfect.
midnight blue
Storyboard: Strolling back near midnight under a sky that stays soft blue, Maya feels a deep peace. Walking with Eva and Peter, she looks at her friends and realizes how much has changed — she has found real friends and built a life she loves in her new city.
夜里快十二点了，但天空还是亮的。It is almost twelve o'clock at night, but the sky is still light.
它呈现出一种柔和的蓝色。It has a soft, blue color.
我和伊娃、彼得一起走在街上。I am walking on the street with Eva and Peter.
我们没怎么说话。We do not talk much.

现在，我们一起漫步在明亮的夏日天空下。Now we are walking here together under the bright summer sky.
我刚刚满三十二岁。I have just turned thirty-two years old.
我建立起了自己热爱的生活。I have built a life that I love.
在这座新城市里，我终于完全有了家的感觉。I finally feel completely at home in my new city.
