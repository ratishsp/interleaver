# Week 5 — Storyboard (Maya stocks her new kitchen)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Definite nouns *-en/-et* — singular and bare (an adjective or a plural takes a different definite form, out of scope); *kan lide* — a general preference takes the indefinite plural; *jeg vil gerne have*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_empty_cupboards | Maya stands in her flat, noticing that the fridge and the cupboard are completely empty. She grabs a large canvas bag and heads out, eager to buy food for the week. |
| 2 | 02_produce_section | Maya enters a large supermarket and picks up a basket. She walks straight to the produce section, as she really likes fresh vegetables and wants a healthy base for her dinners. |
| 3 | 03_the_parsnip | Among the familiar produce, Maya spots a strange, pale root vegetable. She realizes it is a parsnip, something she has never eaten before, but she drops it into the basket out of pure curiosity. |
| 4 | 04_the_rye_bread | Moving to the bread aisle, Maya searches for a local staple. She likes dark, hearty bread, so she selects a heavy loaf of traditional Danish rye bread to try. |
| 5 | 05_bakery_counter | Maya stops at the staffed bakery counter for a sweet treat. She catches the eye of the baker behind the glass and politely asks that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry. |
| 6 | 06_finding_the_milk | Realizing she still needs something for her morning coffee, Maya wanders around looking for the milk. Unable to find it, she stops an employee stocking shelves, who helpfully points her toward the cold dairy section at the back. |
| 7 | 07_taste_of_home | In the international aisle, Maya hunts for a comforting taste of Mexico. She smiles when she spots a familiar can of black beans, a food she truly likes, and happily adds it to her basket. |
| 8 | 08_the_checkout | Maya brings her basket to the checkout line. She places her items on the moving belt, waves her card over the machine to pay, and quickly packs everything into her canvas bag. |
| 9 | 09_putting_groceries_away | Back in her flat, Maya unpacks the groceries on the kitchen counter. She organizes her space, placing the milk and the parsnip in the fridge, and setting the bread and the beans neatly in the cupboard. |
| 10 | 10_first_home_cooked_meal | With a stocked kitchen, Maya chops her vegetables and opens the beans. She cooks a warm, simple dinner on the stove, thrilled to finally eat a real meal in her own home. |
