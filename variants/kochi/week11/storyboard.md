# Week 11 — Storyboard (Maya experiences her first Kochi rain)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Weather expressions **ചൂടാണ് / വെയിലാണ് / മഴ പെയ്യുന്നു**; simple future **-ും** for the forecast (മഴ പെയ്യും)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_waking_to_the_heat | Maya wakes up on a Wednesday morning. She steps out onto her small balcony to stretch. The sun shines brightly, and she instantly feels the heavy Kochi summer heat. |
| 2 | 02_the_weather_forecast | She checks the weather app on her mobile phone while drinking coffee. The app shows a bright sun symbol for the morning, but it predicts that rain will fall in the evening. |
| 3 | 03_doubting_the_forecast | Preparing for a hot day, Maya wears her new light blue cotton kurta. She looks at her umbrella but decides the morning sun is too bright to believe the rain forecast. She leaves the umbrella behind and packs her white cotton scarf instead. |
| 4 | 04_the_harsh_sunlight | Maya walks to the bus stop. The morning sun is extremely harsh on the street. She takes out the white scarf and wears it over her head to block the strong sunlight while waiting. |
| 5 | 05_afternoon_heat | At lunchtime, Maya and her colleague Prakash step out of the air-conditioned office building. A sudden blast of hot air hits them instantly, and Prakash remarks on how hot and sunny the day is. |
| 6 | 06_dark_clouds_gather | At five o'clock, Maya leaves the office. The sky is suddenly dark with heavy black clouds. The hot sun is completely gone, and a strong, cool wind blows across the campus. |
| 7 | 07_the_first_rain | Suddenly, a loud thunderclap sounds and heavy rain falls. Maya regrets leaving her umbrella at home! She runs to the bus stop shelter to stay dry, watching her first sudden Kochi downpour. |
| 8 | 08_rainy_bus_ride | Maya boards the bus home. As the heavy rain blows sideways, she helps the other passengers pull down the metal window shutters to keep the water out. The air inside the bus feels wonderfully cool. |
| 9 | 09_cool_evening_breeze | Maya arrives back at her apartment. She opens the window and stands there a while, feeling the newly cool evening air after the rain. With the hot weather finally broken, she closes the day quietly at the window. |
