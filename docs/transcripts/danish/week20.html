Week 20: Maya starts volunteering at the café

Danish with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding the volunteering
Storyboard: Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. One evening she browses a volunteering website, reads a few listings, and finds the little café she already knows, which needs weekend helpers, so she fills in the short sign-up form.
Jeg har hørt, at mange danskere arbejder frivilligt i deres fritid.I have heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time.
Jeg kan godt lide idéen.I like the idea.
Jeg vil gerne lære at tale bedre dansk og møde nye mennesker.I want to learn to speak better Danish and meet new people.
En aften sidder jeg med min computer.One evening, I am sitting with my computer.
Jeg finder en hjemmeside for frivilligt arbejde.I find a website for voluntary work.
Jeg læser de første tre annoncer på siden.I read the first three listings on the site.
Den første annonce er fra en sportsklub.The first listing is from a sports club.
Men de mangler hjælp torsdag aften, og der spiller jeg badminton.But they need help on Thursday evenings, and I play badminton then.
Den anden annonce er fra et museum.The second listing is from a museum.
De har brug for en person onsdag aften, men der træner jeg.They need a person on Wednesday evenings, but I work out then.
Så kigger jeg på den tredje annonce.Then I look at the third listing.
Den er fra den lille café, som jeg allerede kender.It is from the little café that I already know.
De mangler folk til lørdag formiddag.They need people for Saturday mornings.
Det passer mig helt perfekt.That suits me absolutely perfectly.
Jeg udfylder en kort formular med mit navn og min e-mailadresse.I fill in a short form with my name and my email address.
Jeg skriver også mit telefonnummer: tolv, fireogtredive, seksoghalvtreds, otteoghalvfjerds.I also write my phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight.
Til sidst trykker jeg på send.Finally, I press send.
Jeg har aldrig arbejdet på en café før, men jeg er klar til at lære det.I have never worked in a café before, but I am ready to learn it.
first shift arrival
Storyboard: On her very first Saturday shift, Maya arrives early at the little café. She ties her new apron in the back room and carries the chairs out to the pavement to prepare for opening.
Det er min første lørdagsvagt på den lille café.It is my first Saturday shift at the little café.
Jeg er ankommet tidligt.I have arrived early.

Jeg glæder mig, men jeg er også lidt nervøs.I am looking forward to it, but I am also a little nervous.
Det er første gang, jeg arbejder på en café.It is the first time I am working in a café.
Jeg gik ind i baglokalet for at gøre mig klar.I went into the back room to get ready.
På et bord lå der et nyt forklæde til mig.On a table lay a new apron for me.
Jeg tog det på og bandt det på ryggen.I put it on and tied it in the back.
Det føltes godt at have det på.It felt good to wear it.

Jeg bar fire stole ud.I carried four chairs out.
Jeg har stillet dem rundt om et lille bord.I have placed them around a little table.
Om lidt kommer de andre frivillige.In a little while, the other volunteers will arrive.
Jeg er meget spændt på min første dag.I am very excited about my first day.
learning the machine

Det er min første gang bag disken.It is my first time behind the counter.

En af de erfarne frivillige kalder på mig.One of the experienced volunteers calls for me.
Vi står foran den store espressomaskine.We stand in front of the large espresso machine.
"I dag skal du lære at lave kaffe," siger han."Today you must learn to make coffee," he says.
Jeg har aldrig lavet kaffe på sådan en maskine før.I have never made coffee on such a machine before.
Det første skridt er at male kaffebønnerne.The first step is to grind the coffee beans.
Han trykker på en lille knap.He presses a small button.
Maskinen maler de brune bønner med en høj lyd.The machine grinds the brown beans with a loud sound.
Det andet skridt er at dampe mælken.The second step is to steam the milk.
Han hælder kold mælk op i en metalkande.He pours cold milk into a metal jug.
Bagefter tænder han for den varme damp.Afterwards he turns on the hot steam.
Jeg kigger nøje på hans hænder imens.I look closely at his hands meanwhile.
Mælken i kanden bliver hurtigt varm og tyk.The milk in the jug quickly becomes warm and thick.
Han har tydeligvis lavet kaffe mange gange før.He has clearly made coffee many times before.
Da jeg ankom i morges, var jeg meget nervøs.When I arrived this morning, I was very nervous.
Men han forklarede det godt, så jeg har allerede forstået det meste.But he explained it well, so I have already understood most of it.
the first coffee
Storyboard: When the morning rush hits, Maya makes a cappuccino for a waiting customer. She has held the steam wand a bit too deep in the milk, so it does not get a thick foam. It still looks acceptable, though, so she pours the milk over the coffee and hands it over with a polite smile.

Jeg har lige lavet min allerførste cappuccino.I have just made my very first cappuccino.
Lidt i ti kom en mand hen til disken.A little before ten, a man came over to the counter.
"En cappuccino, tak," sagde han."A cappuccino, please," he said.
Jeg har netop lært at bruge espressomaskinen.I have just learned to use the espresso machine.
Så jeg gik hen til maskinen med det samme.So I went over to the machine right away.
Jeg bryggede kaffen og satte damprøret ned i mælken.I brewed the coffee and put the steam wand down into the milk.
Men jeg holdt det lidt for dybt nede.But I held it a little too deep down.
Jeg har nok været lidt for nervøs.I have probably been a little too nervous.
Der kom desværre ikke noget tykt skum på mælken.Unfortunately, the milk did not get any thick foam.
Jeg kiggede forsigtigt ned i kanden.I looked carefully down into the pitcher.
Skummet var ikke helt perfekt.The foam was not completely perfect.
Men det så stadig acceptabelt ud.But it still looked acceptable.
Jeg hældte den varme mælk over kaffen i koppen.I poured the hot milk over the coffee in the cup.
Derefter gav jeg kaffen til manden med et høfligt smil.Then I gave the coffee to the man with a polite smile.
"Værsgo, det bliver femoghalvtreds kroner," sagde jeg."Here you go, that will be fifty-five kroner," I said.

bun with cheese
Storyboard: A moment later, she slices a warm sourdough bun and adds a thick piece of cheese. She places it on a small plate and navigates through the standing customers to deliver it safely to a corner booth.
Lidt efter kom den næste bestilling.Shortly after, the next order came.
Jeg har allerede lavet min første kaffe i dag.I have already made my first coffee today.

Nu skulle jeg aflevere den til kunden.Now I had to deliver it to the customer.

Han ventede ved det fjerde bord.He was waiting at the fourth table.

the ginger beer

Den formiddag var det dejligt varmt udenfor.That morning it was wonderfully warm outside.
