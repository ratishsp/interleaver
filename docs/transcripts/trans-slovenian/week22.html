Week 22: Maya cooks a local dinner for her friends

planning the menu
Storyboard: Maya decides to host her Thursday badminton friends, Kasper and Sara, for dinner on Friday evening. She wants to cook a traditional local dish, so she finds a recipe for Danish meatballs with potatoes and writes down a shopping list with the exact quantities.

Želim skuhati tradicionalno dansko jed.I want to cook a traditional Danish dish.
Zato se odločim, da bom pripravila mesne kroglice s krompirjem.So I decide to make meatballs with potatoes.
Na spletu najdem dober recept.I find a good recipe on the internet.
Zdaj moram napisati nakupovalni seznam.Now I need to write a shopping list.
Najprej potrebujem meso za mesne kroglice.First, I need meat for the meatballs.
V receptu piše petsto gramov mletega svinjskega mesa.The recipe says five hundred grams of minced pork.
Potem potrebujem tudi dve veliki rumeni čebuli.Then I also need two large yellow onions.
Zapišem si tudi malo mleka in eno jajce.I also note down a little milk and an egg.
Vse to si zapišem v svojo beležnico.I write it all down on my notepad.
Nato pogledam količino krompirja.Afterwards, I look at the amount of potatoes.
Kupiti moram natanko en kilo majhnega krompirja.I must buy exactly one kilo of small potatoes.
Na koncu na seznam dodam še maslo in sol.Finally, I add butter and salt to the list.
Za peko mesa je treba vedno uporabiti veliko masla.You must always use plenty of butter to fry the meat.
Seznam je končno končan in jaz sem pripravljena.The list is finally complete, and I am ready.
Jutri po službi grem naravnost v supermarket.Tomorrow after work, I will go straight to the supermarket.

Meso dam v svojo veliko modro skledo.I put the meat into my large, blue bowl.

then the potatoes

afterwards frying
