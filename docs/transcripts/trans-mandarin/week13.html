Week 13: Maya goes to a café

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.
哥本哈根现在是冬天。It is winter in Copenhagen.
我沿着街道走着。I am walking down the street.
风非常冷。The wind is very cold.
今天风刮得很大。It is blowing hard today.
我顶着冷风倾着身子。I lean against the cold wind.
我把围巾拉高。I pull my scarf up.
我的脸快冻僵了。My face is freezing.
我环顾四周。I look around.
我想去暖和的地方。I would like to get into the warmth.
我看到街角有一家小咖啡馆。I see a small café on the corner.
窗户上起了雾。The windows are fogged up.
里面看起来很暖和。It looks warm in there.
我走向那家咖啡馆。I walk over to the café.
我打开沉重的门。I open the heavy door.
我走了进去。I step inside.
这里闻起来有咖啡的香味。It smells like coffee here.
空气温暖又舒服。The air is nice and warm.
我在身后关上门。I close the door behind me.
冷风留在了外面。The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
我现在在咖啡馆里面。I am inside the café now.
这里很暖和。It is very warm here.
我脱下我厚厚的冬装外套。I take off my thick winter coat.
它很重。It is heavy.
我在墙上找到一个挂钩。I find a hook on the wall.
我把外套挂在挂钩上。I hang my coat on the hook.
我也摘下我的围巾和手套。I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
咖啡馆又小又温馨。The café is small and cozy.
我在找一个好座位。I look for a good seat.
窗边有一张小桌子。There is a small table by the window.
桌子在暖气片旁边。The table is next to a radiator.
我走到桌子旁。I walk over to the table.
我在椅子上坐下。I sit down on the chair.
我的手还是很冷。My hands are still cold.
我搓了搓双手。I rub them together.
我的手变暖和了。My hands get warmer.
感觉棒极了。It feels fantastic.
现在我差不多暖和了。Now I am almost warm.
我又饿又渴。I am hungry and thirsty.
我现在想点些东西。I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
我走向玻璃柜台。I walk over to the glass counter.
里面摆满了蛋糕和面包。It is full of cakes and bread.
我看着这许多东西。I look at the many things.
它们看起来非常美味。They look very delicious.
蛋糕旁边有小标牌。There are small signs next to the cakes.
标牌是手写的。The signs are handwritten.
我读着标牌上的名字。I read the names on the signs.
有很多种糕点。There are many kinds of pastry.
很难做出选择。It is hard to choose.
有一个很大的巧克力蛋糕。There is a large chocolate cake.
但我看着另一块糕点。But I look at another piece of pastry.
这是一个很大的肉桂卷。It is a large cinnamon roll.
它上面有很多糖。It has a lot of sugar on top.
我非常喜欢肉桂的味道。I love the taste of cinnamon.
它非常适合寒冷的冬日。It is perfect for a cold winter day.
我非常想要这个肉桂卷。I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
现在我准备好点单了。Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
我走到收银台前。I walk over to the register.
一个年轻男子站在柜台后面。A young man stands behind the counter.
他是这家咖啡馆的咖啡师。He is a barista in the café.
我对他微笑。I smile at him.
他友善地点点头。He nods warmly.
“你好，”他说。"Hi," he says.
“你想要点什么？”他问。"What would you like?" he asks.
“你好，”我回答。"Hi," I answer.
“我想要一杯黑咖啡，谢谢。”"I would like a black coffee, please."
“我还可以再要一个肉桂卷吗？”"And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
“当然，”他说。"Of course," he says.
“一杯黑咖啡和一个肉桂卷。”"A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
“你要坐在这里吗？”他问。"Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
“是的，谢谢，我要坐那边，”我说。"Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
“没问题，”咖啡师说。"Alright," the barista says.
“我现在就给你做咖啡。”"I will make your coffee now."
“非常感谢，”我说。"Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
我在柜台前等待。I wait at the counter.
他在做我的咖啡。He makes my coffee.
他把一杯热咖啡放在木托盘上。He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
他还把一个小盘子放在托盘上。He also puts a small plate on the tray.
我的肉桂卷在盘子上。My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
闻起来很香。It smells lovely.
“给您，您点的东西，”他说。"Here you go, your order," he says.
“非常感谢，”我回答。"Thank you very much," I reply.
“谢谢您的优质服务。”"Thank you for the good service."
“不客气，”他亲切地说。"You are welcome," he says kindly.
我用双手端起托盘。I take the tray with both hands.
我非常小心地走过地板。I walk very carefully across the floor.
我走回靠窗的桌子。I walk back to my table by the window.
我把托盘放在桌子上。I put the tray down on the table.
现在我想坐下来。Now I would like to sit down.
我准备好好休息一下了。I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
我现在坐在我的小桌旁。I now sit at my small table.
这里既暖和又舒适。It is lovely and warm here.
我看着我的肉桂卷。I look at my cinnamon roll.
它闻起来香甜可口。It smells sweet and good.
我咬了一小口。I take a small bite.
糕点非常新鲜，而且还是热的。The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
味道真的很好。It tastes really good.
我喝了一点我的黑咖啡。I drink a little of my black coffee.
我的手指终于又暖和起来了。Finally, my fingers are warm again.
我在柔软的椅子上放松下来。I relax in the soft chair.
咖啡馆里几乎坐满了人。The café is almost full of people.
人们三五成群地坐着。People sit in small groups.
他们在一起聊天说笑。They talk and laugh together.
我听着房间里低低的说话声。I listen to the low voices in the room.
这声音让人感觉很温馨。The sound is very cozy.
我透过大窗户向外看。I look out of the large window.
外面的天气灰蒙蒙的，还刮着风。The weather outside is gray and windy.
现在竟然又下雪了。Now it is actually snowing again.
白色的雪花缓缓飘落在街道上。White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
我只是很高兴能待在室内。I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
我吃掉了最后一块肉桂卷。I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
味道真的很好。It tastes really good.
但它也非常甜。But it is also very sweet.
我的咖啡杯完全空了。My coffee cup is completely empty.
我突然觉得很渴。I am suddenly very thirsty.
我想喝点冷水。I would like to have some cold water.
一个年轻男子从我的桌旁走过。A young man walks past my table.
他在这家咖啡馆工作。He works here in the café.
他在收拾空杯子和盘子。He collects empty cups and plates.
我和他对上了视线。I catch his eye.
他停在我的桌旁，笑了笑。He stops at my table and smiles.
“打扰一下，”我礼貌地说。"Excuse me," I say politely.
“有什么事吗？”年轻男子回答。"Yes?" answers the young man.
“能给我一杯自来水吗？”我问。"May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
“当然可以，”他说。"Yes, of course," he says.
“非常感谢，”我说。"Thank you very much," I say.
他拿走了我的空盘子和杯子。He takes my empty plate and cup.
他走回柜台。He walks back to the counter.
过了一会儿，他端来一杯冷水。A little later he comes with a cold glass of water.
asking for the bill
Storyboard: Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab.
我喝了我的水。I drink my water.
水很冰凉，很好喝。The water is cold and nice.
我从地板上拿起我的包。I take my bag from the floor.
我的休息时间结束了。I am finished with my break.
我走到柜台前。I walk up to the counter.
那个友好的咖啡师还站在那里。The friendly barista is still standing there.
他微笑着看着我。He looks at me with a smile.
“您还需要点别的吗？”他问。"Would you like anything else?" he asks.
“不需要了，谢谢，”我回答。"No thank you," I answer.
“我想结账。”"I would like to pay."
“好的，当然可以，”他说。"Yes, of course," he says.
他看着他的小屏幕。He looks at his little screen.
“一杯黑咖啡和一个肉桂卷，”他说。"One black coffee and one cinnamon roll," he says.
“对，没错，”我说。"Yes, that is correct," I say.
“您想刷卡还是付现金？”他问。"Do you want to pay with card or cash?" he asks.
“我想刷卡，”我回答。"I would like to pay with a card," I answer.
“好的。”"Fine."
“一共是八十五克朗，”他说。"That will be eighty-five kroner," he says.
我拿出了我的银行卡。I take out my payment card.
paying and leaving
Storyboard: Maya taps her card on the terminal to pay eighty-five kroner. She puts on her heavy coat, thanks the staff for a nice time, and pushes the front door open to face the cold again.
咖啡师对我微笑。The barista smiles at me.
我把卡放在刷卡机上方。I hold my card over the terminal.
机器哔了一声。The machine beeps.
“你需要收据吗？”他问。"Do you want a receipt?" he asks.
“不用了，谢谢，”我回答。"No thank you," I answer.
我走到墙边。I walk over to the wall.
我从挂钩上取下我的厚冬装外套。I take my thick winter coat from a hook.
我穿上外套。I put the coat on.
我也戴上我的围巾。I also put my scarf on.
我也戴上我的手套。I also put my gloves on.
现在我准备好面对寒冷了。Now I am ready for the cold.
我再次看向咖啡师。I look at the barista again.
“非常感谢你的咖啡，”我说。"Thank you very much for the coffee," I say.
“不客气，”他微笑着说。"You are welcome," he says with a smile.
“祝你今天愉快。”"Have a good day."
“你也是，”我说。"Likewise," I say.
我走到大门前。I walk over to the front door.
我推开那扇沉重的门。I push the heavy door open.
风很冷，但我感觉很好。The wind is cold, but I feel good.
