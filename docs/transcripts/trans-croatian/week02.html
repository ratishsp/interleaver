Week 2: First Day & Registration

Croatian with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning commute
Storyboard: Maya walks briskly through the chilly morning air, looking for her new workplace. She checks the large numbers on the buildings along the street until she spots the software company's logo next to number forty-four.
Jutro je u Kopenhagenu.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Siječanj je i vrlo je hladno.It is January, and it is very cold.
Malo sam nervozna.I am a little nervous.
Danas je veliki dan.Today is a big day.
To je novi početak za mene.It is a new start for me.
Ovo je moj prvi dan na novom poslu.It is my first day at my new job.
Idem autobusom na posao.I take the bus to work.
Silazim na pravoj stanici.I get off at the right stop.
Sada zadnji dio idem pješice.Now I walk the last part.
Gdje je ured?Where is the office?
Gledam velike zgrade.I look at the large buildings.
Svaka zgrada ima broj.Each building has a number.
Ovdje je broj trideset osam.Here is number thirty-eight.
A ovdje je broj četrdeset.And here is number forty.
Hodam malo dalje.I walk a little further.
Ovdje je broj četrdeset dva.Here is number forty-two.
I evo ga!And there it is!
Ovdje je broj četrdeset četiri.Here is number forty-four.
Vidim veliki logo.I see a large logo.
Moja nova softverska tvrtka je upravo ovdje.My new software company is right here.
Imam dobar osjećaj.I have a good feeling.
Potpuno sam spremna.I am completely ready.
meeting peter
Storyboard: Inside the bright office, a smiling colleague introduces himself as Peter. He shakes her hand, hands her a new laptop, and leads her through the open-plan workspace to her assigned desk.
Sada sam u novom uredu.I am now inside the new office.
To je velika i svijetla prostorija.It is a large and bright room.
Ljubazan muškarac stoji pored vrata.A friendly man stands by the door.
Smiješi mi se.He smiles at me.
"Dobrodošla!" kaže on."Welcome!" he says.
"Zovem se Peter.""My name is Peter."
Rukujem se s njim.I shake his hand.
"Bok, Peter, zovem se Maya," kažem."Hi Peter, my name is Maya," I say.
Peter je kolega.Peter is a colleague.
Hodamo zajedno kroz ured.We walk together through the office.
"Ovdje imaš radni stol," kaže Peter."You have a desk here," says Peter.
Radni stol ima broj šesnaest.The desk has number sixteen.
"I imaš računalo," kaže on."And you have a computer," he says.
Daje mi novi laptop.He gives me a new laptop.
Gledam svoje novo mjesto.I look at my new spot.
Imam stolicu i veliki monitor.I have a chair and a large monitor.
"Puno ti hvala, Peter," kažem."Thank you very much, Peter," I say.
Spremna sam za svoj prvi radni dan.I am ready for my first workday.
the phone shop
Storyboard: On her lunch break, Maya ducks into a telecom shop to buy a local SIM card. She hands the clerk her phone, and he activates it, reading her brand-new local number aloud as she saves it.
Moja je pauza za ručak.It is my lunch break.
U trgovini sam mobitelima.I am in a phone shop.
Imam mobitel iz Meksika.I have a phone from Mexico.
Prodavač je mladić.The clerk is a young man.
"Imate li dansku SIM karticu?" pitam."Do you have a Danish SIM card?" I ask.
"Da, naravno," odgovara on."Yes, of course," he answers.
Dajem mu svoj mobitel.I give him my phone.
On stavlja SIM karticu u mobitel.He puts the SIM card in the phone.
Mobitel je sada spreman.The phone is ready now.
"Vaš novi broj je dvanaest, trideset četiri, pedeset šest, sedamdeset osam," kaže on."Your new number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," he says.
U torbi imam bilježnicu.I have a notebook in my bag.
Zapisujem broj.I write the number.
"Dvanaest, trideset četiri, pedeset šest, sedamdeset osam," ponavljam."Twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I repeat.
"Da, točno," kaže prodavač."Yes, exactly," says the clerk.
"Hvala na pomoći," kažem."Thank you for the help," I say.
Sada imam danski broj!I have a Danish number now!
Stavljam mobitel u džep.I put the phone in my pocket.
queue ticket
Storyboard: The next morning, Maya enters the crowded public registration office. She presses a button on a red terminal, pulls a paper ticket, and checks her high number before finding a seat to wait.
Sljedeće je jutro.It is the next morning.
Danas sam u velikom uredu za registraciju.I am at the large registration office today.
Ovdje unutra ima mnogo ljudi.There are many people in here.
Imam svoju putovnicu i svoje papire u torbi.I have my passport and my papers in my bag.
Sve je spremno za moju registraciju.Everything is ready for my registration.
Stojim pored crvenog aparata.I stand by a red machine.
Pritišćem gumb.I press a button.
Aparat mi daje mali listić s brojem.The machine gives me a small number slip.
Koji je moj broj?What is my number?
Gledam u papir.I look at the paper.
Imam broj pedeset devet.I have number fifty-nine.
To je prilično visok broj.It is a rather high number.
Veliki ekran na zidu pokazuje broj.A large screen on the wall shows a number.
Ekran pokazuje samo broj dvadeset jedan.The screen only shows number twenty-one.
Imam puno vremena.I have plenty of time.
Nalazim slobodan stolac.I find an empty chair.
Sjedam sa svojim papirima.I sit down with my papers.
Sada čekam svoj red.Now I wait for my turn.
registration
Storyboard: The screen chimes with her number, and Maya steps up to the counter. She volunteers her name and age to the clerk, hands over her passport, and he processes the paperwork for her private national ID number.
Ekran prikazuje moj broj.A screen shows my number.
Broj je pedeset devet.The number is fifty-nine.
Prilazim šalteru.I walk up to the counter.
Muškarac sjedi iza šaltera.A man sits behind the counter.
"Dobro jutro", kaže on."Good morning," he says.
"Dobro jutro", odgovaram."Good morning," I reply.
"Zovem se Maya i imam trideset jednu godinu", kažem."My name is Maya, and I am thirty-one years old," I say.
"Dobro došli, Maya", kaže on."Welcome, Maya," he says.
"Imate li svoju putovnicu?", pita on."Do you have your passport?" he asks.
"Da, imam svoju putovnicu i svoje papire", odgovaram."Yes, I have my passport and my papers," I answer.
"Izvolite", kažem."Here you go," I say.
On gleda papire.He looks at the papers.
On tipka na svom računalu.He types on his computer.
Dobivam danski osobni identifikacijski broj.I get a Danish personal identification number.
Broj je privatan i vrlo važan.The number is private and very important.
On mi daje novu karticu.He gives me a new card.
"Izvolite, evo vaše kartice", kaže on."Here you go, here is your card," he says.
"Sada ste registrirani u Danskoj.""Now you are registered in Denmark."
Smiješim se i uzimam karticu.I smile and take the card.
Sada sam dio Danske.Now I am a part of Denmark.
bakery celebration
Storyboard: Heading home from the registration office, Maya stops at a neighbourhood bakery to celebrate being officially registered at last. She points to a cinnamon pastry in the glass display, asks the price, and taps her card to pay.
Opet sam vani na ulici.I am out on the street again.
Sve je konačno na svom mjestu.Everything is finally in place.
Vrijeme je za malo slavlje.It is time for a little celebration.
Vidim malu pekarnicu na uglu.I see a little bakery on the corner.
Ulazim unutra.I go inside.
Predivno miriše.It smells lovely.
Pekar stoji iza pulta.A baker stands behind the counter.
"Dobar dan", kaže pekar."Hello," the baker says.
"Dobar dan", odgovaram."Hello," I reply.
Pokazujem na pecivo iza stakla.I point to a pastry behind the glass.
"Htjela bih pecivo s cimetom", kažem."I would like a cinnamon pastry," I say.
"Da, naravno", kaže on."Yes, of course," he says.
"Koliko košta?", pitam."What does it cost?" I ask.
"Košta dvadeset pet kruna", odgovara pekar."It costs twenty-five kroner," the baker replies.
Imam novčanik u torbi.I have my wallet in my bag.
Vadim debitnu karticu.I take out my debit card.
Plaćam svoje pecivo s cimetom.I pay for my cinnamon pastry.
Pekar mi daje malu vrećicu.The baker gives me a small bag.
Sada imam pecivo s cimetom za svoje malo slavlje.Now I have a cinnamon pastry for my little celebration.
swapping numbers
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her apartment building with her pastry bag and bumps into her neighbour Nina by the front door. They chat warmly and swap phone numbers so they can stay in touch; Nina reads out hers for Maya to save.
Stojim pored svog stana.I am standing by my apartment.
Imam vrećicu s pecivom od cimeta.I have a bag with a cinnamon pastry.
Moja susjeda Nina je na vratima.My neighbour Nina is at the door.
Široko se smiješi i kaže bok.She smiles broadly and says hello.
"Bok Maya, imaš li sada danski broj?" pita Nina."Hello Maya, do you have a Danish number now?" Nina asks.
"Da, imam novi telefonski broj," odgovaram."Yes, I have a new phone number," I reply.
"To je dobro," kaže ona."That is good," she says.
Imam mobitel u ruci.I have my phone in my hand.
"Koji je tvoj broj?" pitam."What is your number?" I ask.
"Moj broj je osamdeset sedam, šezdeset pet, četrdeset tri, dvadeset jedan," kaže Nina."My number is eighty-seven, sixty-five, forty-three, twenty-one," Nina says.
Zapisujem njezin broj u svoj mobitel.I write her number on my phone.
"A moj broj je dvanaest, trideset četiri, pedeset šest, sedamdeset osam," kažem."And my number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
Nina sprema moj broj u svoj mobitel.Nina saves my number on her phone.
Sada smo u kontaktu.Now we are in touch.
Zajedno ulazimo unutra.We go inside together.
Jako sam sretna zbog svoje nove prijateljice.I am very happy about my new friend.
