Week 8: Maya explores the city

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
今天是星期六早上。It is Saturday morning.
阳光照耀着这座城市。The sun is shining over the city.
我走到街上。I go down to the street.
我的邻居妮娜站在我们楼外。My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
“早上好，玛雅，”她笑着说。"Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
“早上好，妮娜，”我回答。"Good morning, Nina," I answer.
“我今天休息，”我告诉她。"I have the day off today," I tell her.
“我要去城里逛逛。”"I am heading out into the city."
“你要去哪里？”妮娜问。"Where are you going?" Nina asks.
“我要走到海边去，”我说。"I am walking down to the water," I say.
“但我不知道怎么走。”"But I do not know the way."
妮娜指着街道前方。Nina points down the street.
“幸好，路很好走，”她说。"Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
“从这里一直往前走。”"Go straight ahead here."
“在公园那里左转。”"And turn left at the park."
“然后你就能直接走到港口了。”"Then you come right down to the harbour."
“非常感谢你的帮忙，妮娜，”我说。"Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
“不客气，祝你玩得开心，”她回答。"You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
我向她挥挥手。I wave to her.
我开始朝着海边走去。I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
我沿着尼娜的路线走。I follow Nina's route.
空气清新而寒冷。The air is fresh and cold.
现在我沿着主街走。Now I walk down the main street.
首先我只是一直往前走。First I just walk straight ahead.
街上交通很繁忙。There is a lot of traffic on the street.
路上我看到三辆黄色的自行车。On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
自行车飞快地从我身边骑过。The bicycles ride quickly past me.
之后我继续往前走。After that I walk further ahead.
然后我来到一个大公园。Then I come to a large park.
现在我想起了尼娜的话。Now I remember Nina's words.
“在公园左转，”她说。"Turn left at the park," she says.
在这里我向左转。Here I turn left.
过了一会儿，我看到一扇开着的门。A little later I see an open door.
那是一家小面包店。It is a small bakery.
有一股新鲜咖啡的迷人香味。There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
我喜欢热腾腾的面包。I love warm bread.
今天这座城市真美。Today the city is lovely.
随后我继续朝水边走去。Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
我来到一个大广场。I arrive at a large square.
这里有很多人。There are many people.
但我没有看到水。But I do not see the water.
我在找港口。I am looking for the harbour.
我看到人行道上有一位老人。I see an older man on the pavement.
我拦住他。I stop him.
“打扰一下，”我说。"Excuse me," I say.
“港口在哪里？”"Where is the harbour?"
老人点点头。The older man nods.
他指向前方。He points ahead.
“直走穿过广场，”他说。"Walk straight across the square," he says.
“经过博物馆。”"Go past the museum."
“然后右转。”"And turn right."
我望向广场对面。I look across the square.
我看到一座很大的建筑。I see a large building.
那是博物馆。It is the museum.
“非常感谢，”我说。"Thank you very much," I say.
“不客气，”他回答。"You are welcome," he replies.
我继续往前走。I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
我穿过那个大广场。I walk across the large square.
突然，我听到了音乐。Suddenly, I hear music.
拐角处站着一位街头艺人。On the corner stands a street musician.
他在弹吉他。He plays the guitar.
我听着这美妙的音乐。I listen to the good music.
我看了他很久。I look at him for a long time.
然后我继续往前走。Then I walk on.
我忘了博物馆。I forget the museum.
我也忘了我的路线。I also forget my route.
我转而走进了一条狭窄的小街。Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
这里非常安静。It is very quiet here.
周围我只看到一些小房子。All around I only see some small houses.
然后我停了下来。Then I stop.
博物馆在哪儿？Where is the museum?
现在我环顾四周。Now I look around.
我再也看不到它了。I cannot see it anymore.
我快速回头沿着街道望去。I quickly look back up the street.
我也看不到广场了。I cannot see the square either.
我完全迷路了。I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
我站在一条安静的街道上。I am standing on a quiet street.
我迷路了。I am lost.
在我前面，我看到一位年轻女子。Ahead of me I see a young woman.
她正在遛一只小狗。She is walking a small dog.
这只狗看起来像我家的狗，萝拉。The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
我走向那位女子。I walk over to the woman.
“打扰一下，”我说。"Excuse me," I say.
“我在找港口。”"I am looking for the harbour."
那位女子停下来听我说。The woman stops and listens.
她沿着街道往回指去。She points back up the street.
“一直往回走到广场，”她说。"Go straight back to the square," she says.
“然后在博物馆处右转。”"And turn right at the museum."
“非常感谢你的帮助，”我说。"Thank you very much for the help," I say.
“不客气，”那位女子回答。"You are welcome," the woman replies.
我低头看着那只小狗。I look down at the little dog.
它看着我。It looks at me.
我往回朝广场走去。I walk back toward the square.
现在我知道自己在哪里了。Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
我沿着安静的街道往回走。I walk back along the quiet street.
我一直往前走。I walk straight ahead.
很快我又看到了那个大广场。Soon I see the large square again.
然后我穿过广场。Then I walk across the square.
现在我站在博物馆旁边。Now I am standing by the museum.
我想起了那个女人的话：“向右转。”I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
在博物馆那里我向右转。At the museum I turn right.
我顺着一条宽阔的街道往下走。I walk down a wide street.
我望向前方。I look ahead.
终于，我看到了一片蓝色。Finally I see something blue.
那是水！It is the water!
路上我经过了一家小咖啡馆。On the way I walk past a little café.
最后我一直走到了水边。At last I walk all the way down to the water.
现在我在港口了。Now I am at the harbour.
港口非常大。The harbour is very large.
风又冷又清新。The wind is cold and fresh.
水面上有许多船。There are many boats on the water.
我在这里停了下来。Here I stop.
我站着看那些船。I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
我沿着港口一直往前走。I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
我走过那些小船。I walk past the small boats.
突然，我看到很多人。Suddenly I see many people.
他们站在水边。They stand down by the water.
我朝那边走过去。I walk over there.
终于，我看到了它。Finally, I see it.
小美人鱼坐在水中的一块石头上。The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
她是哥本哈根一座非常著名的雕像。She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.
这座雕像其实不是很大。The figure is actually not very big.
但这座铜像在阳光下很美。But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
我站在其他参观者中间。I stand among the other visitors.
许多游客在给雕像拍照。Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
我也拿出了我的手机。I also take out my phone.
我给美人鱼拍了一张美丽的照片。I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
我把照片发给了在墨西哥的家人。I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
我的妹妹索菲娅很快回复了。My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
“她非常漂亮，”她写道。"She is very pretty," she writes.
我再次看向水面。I look at the water again.
walking home
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.
已经是傍晚时分了。It is late in the afternoon.
现在我走路回我的公寓。Now I walk home to my apartment.
我走了一条很好走的路。I walk an easy way.
我只是沿着这条长长的路走。I just follow the long road.
这条路完全是笔直的。The road is completely straight.
我沿着水边走。I walk along the water.
最后我转弯离开了水边。Finally I turn away from the water.
现在我又走进了市区。Now I walk into the city again.
然后我经过了那家面包店。Then I walk past the bakery.
那里依然散发着咖啡的香味。It still smells of coffee.
过了一会儿，我看到了公园。A little later I see the park.
在公园那里我向右转。At the park I turn right.
我没有向任何人问路。I do not ask anyone for directions.
我完全靠自己找到了路。I find the way all by myself.
过了一会儿，我看到了我自己的街道。A little later I see my own street.
最后我站在了我的大门前。Finally I stand at my front door.
从门口，我顺着街道回头望去。From the door I look back down the street.
现在我认识回家的路了。Now I know my way home.
