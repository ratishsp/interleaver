Week 19: Maya starts a new project with the team

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

monday bus excitement
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya takes the bus to work. She feels excited and a little nervous because a large new software project starts today, and she will finally work closely with the entire team.
Ni Jumatatu asubuhi, na nimekaa kwenye basi.It is Monday morning, and I am sitting on the bus.
Niko njiani kwenda kazini.I am on my way to work.
Naangalia nje ya dirisha.I look out of the window.
Nina wasiwasi kidogo leo.I am a little nervous today.
Lakini pia ninatazamia kwa hamu sana.But I am also looking forward to it a lot.
Mradi mkubwa, mpya wa programu unaanza leo.A large, new software project starts today.
Nimefanya kazi kama mhandisi wa programu katika kampuni tangu Januari.I have worked as a software engineer at the company since January.
Ninamfahamu vizuri mfanyakazi mwenzangu Peter.I know my colleague Peter well.
Tumefanya kazi pamoja tangu siku yangu ya kwanza.We have worked together since my first day.
Lakini sasa lazima nifanye kazi kwa karibu na timu nzima.But now I must work closely with the whole team.
Nitafanya kazi na bosi wetu, mbunifu wetu, na mpimaji wetu.I will work with our boss, our designer, and our tester.
Wamefanya kazi katika kampuni kwa muda mrefu.They have worked at the company for a long time.
Basi linasimama, na watu wengi zaidi wanapanda.The bus stops, and more people get on.
Naangalia saa yangu.I look at my watch.
Ni saa mbili.It is eight o'clock.
Mkutano wa asubuhi unaanza baada ya nusu saa.The morning meeting begins in half an hour.
Ninavuta pumzi ndefu.I take a deep breath.
Niko tayari kwa mradi mpya.I am ready for the new project.
first morning presentation
Storyboard: At the short morning meeting, Maya stands in front of her colleagues. She presents her part of the technical plan for the first time, explaining what she has worked on to prepare for the week's tasks.
Tumekaa katika chumba cha mikutano.We are sitting in the meeting room.
Ni wakati wa mkutano wetu mfupi wa asubuhi.It is time for our short morning meeting.
Ninawatazama wafanyakazi wenzangu huku na huko.I look around at my colleagues.
Leo ni siku maalum kwangu.Today is a special day for me.
Ninasimama.I stand up.
Mimi ni mhandisi wa programu, na ninaenda kuwasilisha sehemu yangu ya mradi.I am a software engineer, and I am going to present my part of the project.
Mikono yangu inatetemeka kidogo.My hands are shaking a little.
Lakini nimejiandaa vizuri.But I have prepared well.
"Habari za asubuhi," ninasema kwa sauti."Good morning," I say loudly.
"Nimefanyia kazi mpango wa kiufundi," ninawaambia."I have worked on the technical plan," I tell them.
"Nimesoma nyaraka zote.""I have read all the documents."
"Na nimeandika vidokezo kwa ajili ya majukumu ya wiki.""And I have written notes for the week's tasks."
Ninaeleza mpango kwa timu nzima.I explain the plan to the whole team.
Kwa kweli si vigumu kuzungumzia teknolojia.It is actually not difficult to talk about the technology.
Peter amekaa mkabala nami.Peter is sitting opposite me.
Ninahisi nikitulia zaidi.I feel myself relaxing more.
Anasikiliza na kuitikia kwa kichwa.He listens and nods.
Ninamaliza wasilisho langu, na wengine wanatabasamu.I finish my presentation, and the others smile.
Wasilisho langu la kwanza kazini limeenda vizuri.My first presentation at work has gone well.
boss project manager
Storyboard: After the morning meeting, her boss Thomas stops by Maya's desk with a part of the new project for her. He is warm and welcoming: he compliments her on her presentation and says the whole team has looked forward to having her on board. Maya feels genuinely welcomed onto the team.
Baada ya mkutano wa asubuhi, ninakaa kwenye dawati langu.After the morning meeting, I sit at my desk.
Ninatazama noti zangu kutoka kwenye wasilisho.I look at my notes from the presentation.
Bosi wangu, Thomas, anakuja kwangu.My boss, Thomas, comes over to me.
Yeye ni meneja wetu wa mradi.He is our project manager.
Amefanya kazi hapa kwa miaka mingi.He has worked here for many years.
"Habari ya asubuhi, Maya," anasema huku akitabasamu."Good morning, Maya," he says with a smile.
"Lilikuwa wasilisho zuri sana.""That was a really good presentation."
"Asante, Thomas," ninajibu."Thank you, Thomas," I answer.
"Tuna furaha sana kuwa nawe kwenye timu," Thomas anaendelea."We are very happy to have you on the team," Thomas continues.
"Timu nzima imekuwa ikisubiri kwa hamu kufanya kazi na wewe.""The whole team has looked forward to working with you."
"Mimi pia nimekuwa nikisubiri kwa hamu," ninasema kwa furaha."I have also looked forward to it," I say happily.
"Ni mradi wa kusisimua.""It is an exciting project."
Thomas anatabasamu tena na kurudi kwenye ofisi yake.Thomas smiles again and walks back to his office.
Ninatazama skrini ya kompyuta yangu.I look at my computer screen.
Ninajihisi nimekaribishwa kweli kwenye timu.I feel genuinely welcome on the team.
peter software engineer
Storyboard: Maya sits down with Peter to write code. As software engineers, they solve complex problems together, and Peter jokes that he has worked on one specific error for three hours until Maya easily spots his mistake.
Ninakaa kwenye dawati la Peter.I sit down at Peter's desk.
Tunaangalia skrini yake kubwa.We look at his large screen.
Peter pia ni mhandisi wa programu.Peter is also a software engineer.
Yeye ni mfanyakazi mwenzangu mzuri, na tunaandika msimbo mwingi pamoja.He is a good colleague, and we write a lot of code together.
Leo anaonekana mchovu sana.Today he looks very tired.
"Unafanya nini, Peter?" ninauliza."What are you doing, Peter?" I ask.
"Nina tatizo kubwa," anajibu."I have a big problem," he answers.
"Nimefanyia kazi kosa hili kwa saa tatu.""I have worked on this error for three hours."
Ninatabasamu na kuangalia msimbo kwa makini.I smile and look closely at the code.
Mara nyingi tunatatua matatizo magumu pamoja katika kazi yetu.We often solve complex problems together in our work.
Ghafla ninaona kitu cha ajabu kwenye skrini.Suddenly I see something strange on the screen.
Ni kosa dogo sana.It is a very small mistake.
"Jaribu kuangalia hapa," ninasema na kuonyesha kwenye skrini."Try to look here," I say and point at the screen.
"Umefanya kosa dogo la uchapaji hapa.""You have made a small typo here."
Peter anaangalia skrini kimya kimya kwa sekunde mbili.Peter looks quietly at the screen for two seconds.
Kisha anacheka kwa sauti.Then he laughs loudly.
"Upo sahihi kabisa," Peter anasema."You are absolutely right," Peter says.
"Nimefanya kazi kwa saa tatu, na kumbe ni kosa moja dogo tu!""I have worked for three hours, and then it is just one small error!"
Ninacheka pia, kwa sababu wahandisi wote wa programu wanaijua hali hiyo.I laugh too, because all software engineers know that situation.
Tunarekebisha kosa hilo, na hatimaye programu inafanya kazi kikamilifu.We fix the error, and the program finally works perfectly.
eva digital designer
Storyboard: Later, Maya walks over to meet Eva, the team's designer. Eva shows Maya her beautiful sketches and explains that she has worked as a digital designer since she finished university, focusing on how users experience the product.
Baadaye alasiri, naenda kwenye dawati la Eva.Later in the afternoon, I walk over to Eva's desk.
Yeye ni mbunifu wa kidijitali wa timu.She is the team's digital designer.
Sijawahi kufanya kazi naye kabla ya leo.I have not worked with her before today.
"Hujambo Maya, njoo uone," anasema."Hi Maya, come and look," she says.
Anaelekeza kwenye skrini yake kubwa.She points at her large screen.
Ninaona michoro mizuri ya programu yetu mpya.I see some beautiful sketches of our new software.
Rangi ni tulivu, na muundo ni safi sana.The colors are calm, and the design is very clean.
"Inaonekana vizuri sana," nasema."That looks really good," I say.
"Asante," Eva anajibu kwa tabasamu."Thank you," Eva answers with a smile.
Ananieleza kuhusu kazi yake.She explains her work to me.
"Nimefanya kazi kama mbunifu wa kidijitali tangu chuo kikuu," anasema."I have worked as a digital designer since university," she says.
"Mimi hulenga uzoefu wa mtumiaji kila wakati.""I always focus on the user's experience."
Ananionyesha jinsi mtumiaji anavyobofya huku na huko kwenye programu.She shows me how the user clicks around the program.
"Lazima iwe rahisi na yenye mantiki kuitumia," anaeleza."It must be easy and logical to use," she explains.
Mara nyingi mimi hutazama tu msimbo wenyewe.I often only look at the code itself.
Lakini muundo wake ni muhimu mno kwa mradi.But her design is incredibly important for the project.
Tunazungumza kidogo zaidi kuhusu majukumu yetu ya pamoja.We talk a little more about our shared tasks.
Ninatarajia kwa hamu kuprogramu muundo wake mzuri.I look forward to programming her beautiful design.
meeting the tester
Storyboard: The next day, Maya sits with Mads, the software tester, as he clicks quickly through the new screens and spots a small mistake at once. He laughs that his job is to break things before the users do — he has done it for five years and finds problems fast.
Ni siku inayofuata ofisini.It is the next day at the office.
Nimekaa karibu na Mads, mjaribu wetu wa programu.I am sitting next to Mads, our software tester.
Anaangalia skrini mpya.He looks at the new screens.
Anabofya haraka sana kwa mausi.He clicks very fast with the mouse.
Ghafla anasimama.Suddenly he stops.
Tayari ameshapata kosa dogo.He has already found a small mistake.
Ananiangalia na anacheka kidogo.He looks at me and laughs a little.
"Kazi yangu ni kuharibu vitu," anasema."My job is to break things," he says.
"Inabidi nipate makosa kabla ya watumiaji wetu.""I have to find the mistakes before our users."
Amefanya kazi kama mjaribu kwa miaka mitano.He has worked as a tester for five years.
Amejaribu miradi mingi tofauti.He has tested many different projects.
Ndiyo maana anapata matatizo haraka sana.That is why he finds the problems so fast.
Ninaangalia kosa kwenye kompyuta yangu.I look at the mistake on my computer.
Kwa bahati nzuri, ni tatizo dogo tu.Fortunately, it is only a small problem.
Ninarekebisha kodi mara moja.I fix the code right away.
Anajaribu programu mara moja tena.He tests the program one more time.
Sasa kila kitu kinafanya kazi kikamilifu kabisa.Now everything works absolutely perfectly.
Nina furaha sana kwa msaada wake.I am very glad for his help.
