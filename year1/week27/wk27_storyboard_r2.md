# Week 27 — Storyboard (Maya handles some digital admin)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** *pleje at*; instructions; passive *-s* + *blive* + participle

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_evening_snack | Maya makes some toast in her kitchen after a long day at the software company. She remembers she needs to update her billing details for her electricity provider, an annoying digital chore she usually puts off until the last minute. |
| 2 | 02_entering_details | Settling on the sofa, she opens the utility company's website on her phone. The instructions state that all mandatory fields must be filled out, so she carefully types her phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight. |
| 3 | 03_missing_code | A prompt notes that a one-time code is being sent via SMS to verify her identity. Maya takes a bite of toast and waits; she usually receives these messages immediately, but her screen remains stubbornly blank. |
| 4 | 04_successful_update | Following the on-screen help text that says a new code can be requested, she taps the link and her phone finally buzzes. She enters the digits, and a green banner immediately declares that her profile is successfully updated. |
| 5 | 05_packing_gear | Glad the tedious task is finally handled, Maya tosses her phone onto the cushion and heads out to the hallway. She pulls her sports bag from the closet, packing her racket and shoes for her regular Thursday evening badminton match. |
| 6 | 06_nina_delivery | Nina knocks on the door to ask if a package was delivered to Maya's apartment by mistake. Maya happily hands over the small cardboard box she accepted from the mail carrier earlier, mentioning her relief at finishing her online admin. |
| 7 | 07_shared_frustration | Nina laughs, agreeing that self-service portals usually are incredibly frustrating when systems delay the security codes. They chat comfortably in the doorway about their busy work weeks before Nina heads back across the hall. |
| 8 | 08_offline_evening | Enjoying the quiet of her apartment, Maya turns off the bright hallway lamp and settles into her armchair with a good book. Having completed her chores and prepared her gear, she looks forward to spending the rest of her night completely offline. |
