# Week 24 — Storyboard (Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Imperatives + object pronouns *den/ham/hende*; *skal/må* (rules)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_ninas_handover | On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend, not as a stern list of orders: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig. |
| 2 | 02_friday_evening_stroll | Before bed, Maya takes Ludwig for a short evening walk around the neighborhood. When he suddenly sniffs at a discarded food wrapper on the pavement, she gently commands him to leave it and pulls him away, reminding him that he must not eat trash. |
| 3 | 03_saturday_breakfast | Early Saturday morning, Ludwig nudges Maya awake. Maya prepares his breakfast, gently telling him to sit and wait while she fills the bowl, before finally petting him and telling him to eat it. |
| 4 | 04_a_morning_together | Saturday morning, with the café shift she arranged off in advance so Ludwig is not left alone, Maya has the morning free. she and Ludwig enjoy it together — she throws his ball and calls him back, gives him a good fuss — happy she chose to stay with him. |
| 5 | 05_the_hundeskov | Saturday afternoon, Maya walks with Ludwig to a local hundeskov (dog forest). Once inside the safe, gated area, she unclips the lead, happily telling him that he may run free now, and throws a stick for him to fetch. |
| 6 | 06_gentle_recall | Ludwig spots a bird and wanders off toward the trees, completely ignoring Maya's loud commands to stop. Realizing yelling does not work, Maya crouches down with a treat, softly calling him over; he quickly runs to her, and she tells him to sit before clipping the leash back on him. |
| 7 | 07_family_video_call | That evening, Maya video calls her family in Mexico to show them her furry guest. She points the camera at Ludwig, telling Sofía and Diego to look at him, noting that he is just as calm as their dog Lola back home. |
| 8 | 08_ninas_return | On Sunday evening, Nina returns from her trip and knocks on the door. Ludwig greets her joyfully, and Maya warmly tells Nina that she must definitely let her look after him again next time she travels. |
