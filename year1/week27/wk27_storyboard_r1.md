# Week 27 — Storyboard (Maya handles some digital admin)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** *pleje at*; instructions; passive *-s* + *blive* + participle

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_sofa_admin | Maya relaxes on her sofa on a quiet evening. She remembers she needs to update her contact details on the municipal self-service portal. She usually puts off this kind of boring digital admin, but she decides to quickly handle it on her smartphone. |
| 2 | 02_login_screen | She opens the website and reads the instructions on the front page: "Enter your email and press log in." She carefully types her address and waits while her account is located by the system. |
| 3 | 03_updating_details | Navigating to the settings menu, a small text box states that all mandatory fields must be filled out before continuing. She updates her profile by typing her phone number, twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight. |
| 4 | 04_missing_code | A prompt informs her that a one-time code is being sent to her mobile phone. Maya waits patiently, noting that she usually receives these security texts immediately, but her notification screen stays completely empty. |
| 5 | 05_troubleshooting_step | After a few minutes of waiting, she reads the helpful instruction at the bottom of the screen: "Click here if a message is not received." She taps the link to request a fresh code from the portal. |
| 6 | 06_successful_entry | Her phone finally buzzes with the new text message. She quickly memorizes the short number and types it into the form, watching the loading wheel spin as her identity is verified. |
| 7 | 07_profile_saved | A large green checkmark appears with a final system message declaring that her changes are saved and her profile is successfully updated. Maya takes a quick screenshot of the confirmation page just to be safe. |
| 8 | 08_digital_relief | Relieved that the annoying task is finally completed, Maya closes the browser app. She opens her calendar to check the time for her regular badminton match on Thursday evening, ready to enjoy the rest of her night offline. |
