Week 21: Maya shops for the monsoon and exchanges a shirt

Malayalam with English gloss, line-aligned.

after the cafe shift
Storyboard: After finishing her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the community café, Maya steps out into the steady monsoon drizzle. Three weeks of daily rain have shown her that an umbrella is not enough on the bicycle, so she decides to stop at a large local clothing store in town for a proper raincoat — and a fresh shirt.

മൂന്ന് ആഴ്ചയായി ഇവിടെ മഴയാണ്.It has been raining here for three weeks.

buying without trying
