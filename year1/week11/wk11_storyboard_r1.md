# Week 11 — Storyboard (Maya experiences the Danish winter weather)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Impersonal *det er / det bliver*; seasons

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_dark_winter_morning | Maya wakes up to the sound of her alarm. She looks towards the window, but it is completely pitch-black outside. It is midwinter in Denmark, and she realizes the mornings are incredibly dark. |
| 2 | 02_checking_the_forecast | Maya stands by the window and checks the weather app on her phone. The sky slowly turns gray. The app shows that it is cold now, and that it becomes very wet and windy later in the day. |
| 3 | 03_dressing_warmly | Maya remembers the freezing cold from last week. She puts on her new thick red sweater, her blue scarf, and her warm black gloves. It is chilly in her hallway, but she feels perfectly prepared. |
| 4 | 04_rain_and_the_bus | Maya steps out of her building to commute to work. It is raining heavily now. She opens her umbrella and walks quickly through the rain to the bus stop. It is very wet, and she is glad she is wearing her warm clothes. |
| 5 | 05_a_strong_wind | Maya gets off the bus near her software company. Suddenly, a hard wind blows through the street. It becomes too windy to hold the umbrella, so she folds it up and hurries the rest of the way to the office. |
| 6 | 06_office_window | Maya sits at her desk and writes code. She looks out the office window. It is stormy and gray outside, but inside it is comfortably warm. She thinks about how different the weather is in the summer. |
| 7 | 07_early_dusk | Maya finishes her work and steps outside the office building. She is surprised to see that dusk is already falling. It becomes dark very early in this season, and she walks back to the bus in the fading light. |
| 8 | 08_cozy_lamplight | Maya arrives back at her apartment. It is completely night outside now. She turns on a small lamp in the corner of her living room, and the room instantly becomes cozy and inviting. |
| 9 | 09_a_hot_drink | Maya brews a large mug of hot tea and sits down on the sofa. She listens to the wind and the rain hitting the window glass. It is a dark and harsh season outside, but she feels completely safe and warm indoors. |
