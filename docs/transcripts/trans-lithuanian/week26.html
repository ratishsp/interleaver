birthday messages

Tai mano pirmasis gimtadienis toli nuo šeimos.It is my first birthday away from my family.

Jie visiškai nepamiršo mano didžiosios dienos.They have not forgotten my big day at all.

Tai nuostabi mano gimtadienio pradžia.It is a fantastic start to my birthday.
office pastry

family call

cafe summer
