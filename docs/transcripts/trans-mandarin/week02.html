morning commute

这对我来说是一个新的开始。It is a new start for me.

meeting peter
Storyboard: Inside the bright office, a smiling colleague introduces himself as Peter. He shakes her hand, hands her a new laptop, and leads her through the open-plan workspace to her assigned desk.
我现在在新的办公室里。I am now inside the new office.
这是一个宽敞明亮的房间。It is a large and bright room.

“欢迎！”他说。"Welcome!" he says.

“你还有一台电脑，”他说。"And you have a computer," he says.
他给我一台新的笔记本电脑。He gives me a new laptop.
我看着我的新工位。I look at my new spot.
我有一把椅子和一个大显示器。I have a chair and a large monitor.
“非常感谢，彼得，”我说。"Thank you very much, Peter," I say.
我准备好迎接我的第一个工作日了。I am ready for my first workday.
the phone shop
Storyboard: On her lunch break, Maya ducks into a telecom shop to buy a local SIM card. She hands the clerk her phone, and he activates it, reading her brand-new local number aloud as she saves it.
这是我的午休时间。It is my lunch break.
我在一家手机店里。I am in a phone shop.
我有一部从墨西哥带来的手机。I have a phone from Mexico.
店员是一位年轻男子。The clerk is a young man.
“你们有丹麦的 SIM 卡吗？”我问。"Do you have a Danish SIM card?" I ask.
“有，当然有了，”他回答。"Yes, of course," he answers.
我把我的手机递给他。I give him my phone.
他把 SIM 卡装进手机里。He puts the SIM card in the phone.
手机现在可以用了。The phone is ready now.
“你的新号码是十二、三十四、五十六、七十八，”他说。"Your new number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," he says.
我的包里有一本笔记本。I have a notebook in my bag.
我写下号码。I write the number.
“十二、三十四、五十六、七十八，”我重复道。"Twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I repeat.
“对，没错，”店员说。"Yes, exactly," says the clerk.
“谢谢你的帮忙，”我说。"Thank you for the help," I say.
我现在有一个丹麦号码了！I have a Danish number now!
我把手机放进口袋里。I put the phone in my pocket.
queue ticket
Storyboard: The next morning, Maya enters the crowded public registration office. She presses a button on a red terminal, pulls a paper ticket, and checks her high number before finding a seat to wait.
现在是第二天早上。It is the next morning.
今天我在大型登记处。I am at the large registration office today.
这里面有很多人。There are many people in here.
我的包里有我的护照和文件。I have my passport and my papers in my bag.
登记所需的一切都准备好了。Everything is ready for my registration.
我站在一台红色的机器旁。I stand by a red machine.
我按下一个按钮。I press a button.
机器给了我一张小小的号码条。The machine gives me a small number slip.

我看了看纸条。I look at the paper.
我拿到了五十九号。I have number fifty-nine.
这是一个挺靠后的号码。It is a rather high number.
墙上的大屏幕显示着一个号码。A large screen on the wall shows a number.
屏幕上只显示着二十一号。The screen only shows number twenty-one.
我的时间很充裕。I have plenty of time.
我找到一把空椅子。I find an empty chair.
我拿着文件坐下来。I sit down with my papers.
现在我等着轮到我。Now I wait for my turn.
registration
Storyboard: The screen chimes with her number, and Maya steps up to the counter. She volunteers her name and age to the clerk, hands over her passport, and he processes the paperwork for her private national ID number.
屏幕上显示着我的号码。A screen shows my number.
号码是五十九。The number is fifty-nine.
我走到柜台前。I walk up to the counter.
一个男人坐在柜台后面。A man sits behind the counter.
“早上好，”他说。"Good morning," he says.
“早上好，”我回答。"Good morning," I reply.
“我叫玛雅，今年三十一岁，”我说。"My name is Maya, and I am thirty-one years old," I say.
“欢迎你，玛雅，”他说。"Welcome, Maya," he says.
“你带护照了吗？”他问。"Do you have your passport?" he asks.
“是的，我带了护照和我的文件，”我回答。"Yes, I have my passport and my papers," I answer.
“给你，”我说。"Here you go," I say.
他看了看那些文件。He looks at the papers.
他在电脑上打字。He types on his computer.
我得到了一个丹麦个人身份号码。I get a Danish personal identification number.
这个号码是私人的，非常重要。The number is private and very important.
他给了我一张新卡。He gives me a new card.
“给你，这是你的卡，”他说。"Here you go, here is your card," he says.
“现在你已经在丹麦注册了。”"Now you are registered in Denmark."
我微笑着接过卡。I smile and take the card.
现在我是丹麦的一部分了。Now I am a part of Denmark.
bakery celebration

swapping numbers
