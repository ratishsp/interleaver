Week 10: Maya buys winter clothes

Serbian with English gloss, line-aligned.

a cold morning
Storyboard: It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to the bus stop on her way to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter.
Jutro je u Kopenhagenu.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Hodam do autobuske stanice.I walk to the bus stop.
Vreme je danas veoma hladno.The weather is very cold today.
Vetrovito je.It is windy.
Vetar je leden.The wind is ice-cold.
Smrzavam se.I am freezing.
Nosim tanku jaknu.I am wearing a thin jacket.
Moja jakna uopšte nije topla.My jacket is not warm at all.
Takođe nosim tanke cipele.I am also wearing thin shoes.
Ne nosim topao šal.I am not wearing a warm scarf.
Moje ruke su veoma hladne.My hands are very cold.
Drhtim.I am shivering.
Zima u Danskoj je teška.The winter in Denmark is hard.
Treba mi topla odeća.I need warm clothes.
Treba mi debeo džemper.I need a thick sweater.
Takođe mi treba veliki šal.I also need a large scarf.
I trebaju mi tople rukavice.And I need warm gloves.
Hodam veoma brzo.I walk very fast.
Kupujem zimsku odeću posle posla.I am buying winter clothes after work.
entering the shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things.
Završila sam s poslom.I am finished at work.
Idem niz pešačku ulicu.I walk down the pedestrian street.
Vetar je i dalje veoma hladan.The wind is still very cold.
Vidim veliku prodavnicu odeće.I see a large clothing shop.
Prodavnica je veoma svetla.The shop is very bright.
Ulazim u prodavnicu.I go into the shop.
Ovde unutra je predivno toplo.It is wonderfully warm in here.
Prodavačica stoji za kasom.A shop assistant stands behind the register.
Gleda me i smeši se.She looks at me and smiles.
"Zdravo," kaže ona."Hello," she says.
"Zdravo," odgovaram."Hello," I answer.
"Mnogo mi je hladno," kažem."I am freezing a lot," I say.
"Na sebi imam samo tanku jaknu.""I only have a thin jacket on."
"Volela bih da nađem neke tople stvari.""I would like to find some warm things."
Prodavačica se ponovo smeši.The shop assistant smiles again.
"Danas je takođe veoma hladno," kaže ona."It is also very cold today," she says.
"Imamo mnogo debelih džempera.""We have many thick sweaters."
"Imamo i toplu zimsku odeću.""We also have warm winter clothes."
"Slobodno razgledajte," kaže ona ljubazno."You just take a look," she says kindly.
"Mnogo hvala," odgovaram."Many thanks," I answer.
the red sweater
Storyboard: Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it.
Gledam odeću na policama.I look at the clothes on the shelves.
Vidim debeo džemper.I see a thick sweater.
Crven je.It is red.
Treba mi topao džemper.I need a warm sweater.
Prodavnica ima mnogo veličina.The shop has many sizes.
Nalazim veličinu medijum.I find a size medium.
Ulazim u kabinu za presvlačenje.I walk into the fitting room.
Oblačim crveni džemper.I put the red sweater on.
Imam ga na sebi i gledam se u velikom ogledalu.I have it on and look in the big mirror.
Džemper mi savršeno stoji.The sweater fits perfectly.
Neverovatno je topao.It is incredibly warm.
Volim crvenu boju.I love the red colour.
Gledam etiketu sa cenom.I look at the price tag.
Džemper košta trista kruna.The sweater costs three hundred kroner.
To je dobra cena za debeo džemper.It is a good price for a thick sweater.
Ponovo skidam džemper.I take the sweater off again.
Kupujem ga.I buy it.
the tight green coat
Storyboard: Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a larger size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. The assistant brings her a warm dark-blue winter jacket in her size instead — it fits perfectly, and Maya decides to take it.
Takođe mi treba i zimska jakna.I also need a winter jacket.
Gledam još odeće u prodavnici.I look at more clothes in the shop.
Nalazim prelep, zeleni kaput.I find a beautiful, green coat.
To je veoma lep dizajn.It is a very beautiful design.
Oblačim zeleni kaput.I put the green coat on.
Ali je premali.But it is too small.
Veoma je uzak preko ramena.It is very tight across the shoulders.
"Imate li ga u drugoj veličini?" pitam."Do you have it in another size?" I ask.
Prodavačica gleda.The shop assistant looks.
"Ne, nažalost," odgovara ona."No, unfortunately," she answers.
Zato kačim zeleni kaput nazad.So I hang the green coat back.
Prodavačica mi nalazi drugu jaknu.The shop assistant finds another jacket for me.
To je topla, tamnoplava zimska jakna.It is a warm, dark-blue winter jacket.
Oblačim tamnoplavu jaknu.I put the dark-blue jacket on.
Savršeno stoji.It fits perfectly.
Fino je velika i nije uska.It is nice and big and not tight.
Gledam se u velikom ogledalu.I look in a large mirror.
Sviđa mi se.I like it.
"Uzeću je," kažem."I will take it," I say.
the blue scarf
Storyboard: Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases.
Prilazim stolu u prodavnici.I walk over to a table in the shop.
Na stolu ima mnogo stvari.There are many things on the table.
Danas ne nosim šal.I am not wearing a scarf today.
Veoma mi je hladno oko vrata.I am very cold around my neck.
Vidim plavi šal.I see a blue scarf.
To je veoma dug šal.It is a very long scarf.
Napravljen je od vune.It is made of wool.
Uzimam plavi šal.I take the blue scarf.
Stavljam ga oko vrata.I put it around my neck.
Divno je topao.It feels wonderfully warm.
"To je lepa boja", kaže prodavačica."It is a nice colour," the shop assistant says.
"Da, i veoma je mekan", kažem."Yes, and it is very soft," I say.
Gledam etiketu sa cenom.I look at the price tag.
Košta sto pedeset kruna.It costs one hundred and fifty kroner.
To je dobra cena za topao šal.It is a good price for a warm scarf.
Ponovo ga skidam.I take it off again.
"Kupujem i njega", kažem joj."I am buying it too," I say to her.
Stavljam plavi šal zajedno sa crvenim džemperom.I put the blue scarf together with the red sweater.
the black gloves
Storyboard: Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well.
Još uvek nosim svoju tanku jaknu.I am still wearing my thin jacket.
Razmišljam o svojim hladnim rukama od jutros.I think about my cold hands from this morning.
Trebaju mi debele rukavice.I need thick gloves.
Osvrćem se po svetloj prodavnici.I look around the bright shop.
Vidim veliku korpu.I see a large basket.
Korpa je puna rukavica.The basket is full of gloves.
Nalazim par crnih zimskih rukavica.I find a pair of black winter gloves.
Veoma su debele i mekane.They are very thick and soft.
Navlačim rukavice.I put the gloves on.
Savršeno mi pristaju.They fit me perfectly.
Pitam nasmejanu prodavačicu.I ask the smiling shop assistant.
„Koliko koštaju crne rukavice?” pitam."What do the black gloves cost?" I ask.
„Koštaju sto dvadeset kruna,” odgovara ona."They cost one hundred and twenty kroner," she answers.
Sto dvadeset kruna je dobra cena.One hundred and twenty kroner is a good price.
Ruke su mi već tople.My hands are already warm.
„Uzeću ih,” kažem radosno."I will take them," I say happily.
Ponovo skidam rukavice.I take the gloves off again.
Stavljam crne rukavice zajedno sa svojim crvenim džemperom i plavim šalom.I put the black gloves together with my red sweater and my blue scarf.
Sada sam spremna da platim.Now I am ready to pay.
paying for the clothes
Storyboard: Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the dark-blue winter jacket, the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is one thousand five hundred kroner, and Maya pays with her card.
Prilazim kasi sa svojom novom odećom.I walk over to the counter with my new clothes.
Prodavačica mi se ljubazno osmehuje.The shop assistant smiles kindly at me.
„Da li je to sve?“ pita ona."Is that everything?" she asks.
„Da, to je sve“, odgovaram."Yes, that is everything," I answer.
Ona skenira sve moje nove stvari.She scans all my new things.
Tu su džemper, jakna, šal i rukavice.There is a sweater, a jacket, a scarf and gloves.
„Sada se nećete smrzavati“, kaže ona radosno."Now you will not freeze," she says happily.
„To će iznositi hiljadu pet stotina kruna, molim vas.“"That will be one thousand five hundred kroner, please."
Odeća je skupa, ali je veoma topla.The clothes are expensive, but they are very warm.
Vadim svoju platnu karticu.I take out my payment card.
„Plaćam karticom“, kažem."I am paying by card," I say.
„Možete samo ovde da platite“, kaže ona."You can just pay here," she says.
Pokazuje na mali, crni aparat.She points at a small, black machine.
Držim svoju karticu iznad aparata.I hold my card over the machine.
Zapišti, i plaćanje je odobreno.It beeps, and the payment is approved.
„Da li želite račun?“ pita prodavačica."Do you want the receipt?" the shop assistant asks.
„Ne, hvala“, odgovaram."No thank you," I answer.
Sada posedujem mnogo lepe zimske odeće.Now I own a lot of nice winter clothes.
stepping into the cold
Storyboard: Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She pulls on the red sweater, zips her new dark-blue winter jacket over it, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm.
"Kesu?" pita prodavac."A bag?" the shop assistant asks.
"Ne, hvala", kažem."No thank you," I say.
Ne treba mi kesa.I do not need a bag.
Odmah oblačim novu odeću.I put the new clothes on right away.
Prvo oblačim debeli, crveni džemper.First, I put on the thick, red sweater.
Zatim oblačim tamnoplavu zimsku jaknu.Then, I put on the dark-blue winter jacket.
Zakopčavam toplu jaknu.I zip the warm jacket.
Nakon toga uzimam dugi, plavi šal.Afterwards, I take the long, blue scarf.
Omotavam ga oko vrata.I wrap it around my neck.
Na kraju navlačim nove, crne rukavice.Finally, I put on the new, black gloves.
Sada imam svu svoju toplu odeću na sebi.Now I have all my warm clothes on.
Nasmešim se i kažem doviđenja.I smile and say goodbye.
Izlazim iz prodavnice.I walk out of the shop.
Opet stojim napolju na hladnoj ulici.I stand out on the cold street again.
Ledeno je.It is freezing cold.
Vetar jako duva.The wind blows hard.
Ali meni uopšte nije hladno.But I am not freezing at all.
Sada sam spremna za zimu.Now I am ready for winter.
