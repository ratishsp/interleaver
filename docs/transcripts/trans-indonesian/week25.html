Week 25: A Sunday in the city

Indonesian with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning calendar
Storyboard: On Sunday morning, Maya makes a fresh pot of coffee and flips her wall calendar to July. She realizes that she moved to Copenhagen exactly six months ago, and decides to celebrate by spending this sunny day exploring the city at her own pace.
Ini hari Minggu pagi.It is Sunday morning.
Matahari bersinar masuk melalui jendela di apartemenku.The sun is shining in through the window in my apartment.
Aku pergi ke dapur.I go out into the kitchen.
Aku membuat seteko kopi segar.I make a pot of fresh coffee.
Aroma kopi sangat fantastis pagi ini.The smell of coffee is fantastic this morning.
Aku punya kalender di dinding.I have a calendar on the wall.
Aku membalik halamannya ke bulan berikutnya.I turn the page to the next month.
Sekarang bulan Juli.It is July now.
Aku pindah ke Kopenhagen pada bulan Januari.I moved to Copenhagen in January.
Aku datang ke sini tepat enam bulan yang lalu.I came here exactly six months ago.
Waktu benar-benar berjalan cepat.Time really goes fast.
Hari pertamaku di kota ini sangat dingin.My first day in this city was very cold.
Namun, hari ini cuacanya hangat dan indah.But today the weather is warm and beautiful.
Aku ingin merayakan hari jadi kecil ini.I want to celebrate this little anniversary.
Aku tidak punya rencana pasti hari ini.I have no fixed plans today.
Aku mengambil cangkir kopiku dan memandang ke luar ke arah jalan.I take my coffee cup and look out over the street.
Aku ingin pergi keluar dan menjelajahi kota dengan tempoku sendiri.I want to go out and explore the city at my own pace.
Aku menantikan untuk menikmati jam-jam cerah ini.I look forward to enjoying these bright hours.
ceramic plates
Storyboard: Maya wanders into a lively second-hand market in a nearby square. She spots two blue ceramic plates on a table, decides these plates are perfect for her kitchen, and buys them from the vendor for forty kroner.
Saya berjalan-jalan melintasi sebuah alun-alun besar di kota.I go for a walk across a large square in the city.
Di sini ada pasar loak yang besar setiap hari Minggu.Here there is a large flea market every Sunday.
Matahari bersinar, dan banyak orang berjalan berkeliling di antara meja-meja.The sun is shining, and many people are walking around between the tables.
Saya berhenti di dekat sebuah meja kecil dengan banyak barang yang berbeda.I stop by a small table with many different things.
Di belakang meja berdiri seorang penjual yang ramah.Behind the table stands a friendly vendor.
Tiba-tiba saya melihat dua piring keramik biru.Suddenly I see two blue ceramic plates.
Saya mengambilnya dan memperhatikannya dengan saksama.I pick them up and look closely at them.
"Berapa harga piring-piring ini?" tanya saya kepada penjual itu."How much do these plates cost?" I ask the vendor.
Penjual itu tersenyum kepada saya.The vendor smiles at me.
"Harganya hanya empat puluh kroner," jawabnya."They cost only forty kroner," he replies.
Itu harga yang sangat bagus.That is a really good price.
Saya membeli sebuah meja kecil untuk dapur saya sebulan yang lalu.I bought a small table for my kitchen a month ago.
Piring-piring ini akan sangat cocok dengan meja ini.These plates will fit perfectly with this table.
"Saya sangat ingin membelinya," kata saya."I would very much like to buy them," I say.
Saya memberi penjual itu empat puluh kroner dan dengan hati-hati memasukkan piring-piring itu ke dalam tas saya.I give the vendor forty kroner and carefully put the plates in my bag.
Selalu menyenangkan menemukan sesuatu yang spesial di hari Minggu yang tenang.It is always nice to find something special on a quiet Sunday.
winter coat
Storyboard: At a clothing stall, Maya tries on a heavy wool winter coat. She immediately takes it off because this coat is far too warm for summer, laughing with the vendor that she desperately needed something exactly like it five months ago.
Saya berjalan lebih jauh ke dalam pasar dan berhenti di sebuah kios pakaian.I walk further into the market and stop at a clothing stall.
Saya melihat mantel musim dingin berat yang terbuat dari wol.I see a heavy winter coat made of wool.
Mantel ini indah, tetapi sangat tebal.This coat is beautiful, but very thick.
Saya tetap mencoba mantel itu.I try the coat on anyway.
Mantel ini luar biasa hangat.It is incredibly warm.
Saya segera melepaskannya lagi.I take it off again immediately.
Sekarang bulan Juli, dan matahari bersinar hari ini.It is July, and the sun is shining today.
Seorang wanita yang ramah berdiri di belakang meja dan tersenyum.A friendly woman stands behind the table and smiles.
"Apakah terlalu hangat?" tanya wanita itu."Is it too warm?" the woman asks.
"Ya, ini sedikit terlalu hangat untuk musim panas," jawab saya dan tertawa."Yes, it is a bit too warm for the summer," I reply and laugh.
Saya menyentuh kain tebal itu.I touch the thick fabric.
"Tapi saya benar-benar membutuhkan mantel musim dingin ini lima bulan yang lalu!" kata saya."But I really needed this winter coat five months ago!" I say.
Wanita itu tertawa keras.The woman laughs loudly.
"Ya, bulan Februari sangat dingin," katanya."Yes, February was very cold," she says.
Saya teringat minggu-minggu pertama saya di København.I remember my first weeks in Copenhagen.
Saat itu saya terus-menerus kedinginan.I was constantly freezing back then.
Untungnya, saya tidak membutuhkan pakaian sehangat itu saat ini.Fortunately, I do not need such warm clothes right now.
Saya menggantung mantel itu kembali ke rak dan berpamitan.I hang the coat back on the rack and say goodbye.
museum ticket
Storyboard: Moving on, Maya enters a local history museum and goes to the front desk. She asks the clerk if this entrance ticket includes the special upstairs exhibition, and the clerk tells her to hurry because the guided tour started ten minutes ago.
Saya berjalan menjauh dari pasar dan menemukan sebuah museum lokal kecil.I walk away from the market and find a small, local museum.
Saya berjalan masuk ke bangunan yang tenang itu.I walk into the quiet building.
Saya berjalan menghampiri loket untuk membeli tiket masuk.I walk over to the counter to buy an entrance ticket.
Seorang pria muda berdiri di belakang kasir.A young man is standing behind the register.
"Halo, satu tiket dewasa, tolong," kata saya."Hi, one adult ticket, please," I say.
"Harganya seratus kroner," katanya lalu mencetaknya."That will be one hundred kroner," he says and prints it.
Saya membayar dan melihat brosur-brosur berwarna-warni ini di atas meja kasir.I pay and look at these colorful brochures on the counter.
Brosur-brosur itu mempromosikan pameran khusus tentang sejarah kota ini.They advertise a special exhibition about the history of the city.
"Apakah tiket ini juga memberikan akses ke pameran di lantai atas?" tanya saya."Does this ticket also give access to the exhibition upstairs?" I ask.
"Ya, museum ini hanya memiliki satu tiket gabungan," jawabnya."Yes, this museum only has one combined ticket," he answers.
"Pameran itu ada di atas di lantai satu," katanya."The exhibition is upstairs on the first floor," he says.
"Anda bisa meluangkan waktu dengan santai di atas sana.""You can take your time up there."
"Ada banyak foto lama kota ini di sana," jelasnya."It has many old photographs of the city," he explains.
"Beberapa di antaranya berusia lebih dari seratus tahun.""Some of them are over a hundred years old."
"Kedengarannya sangat menarik," kata saya."That sounds really exciting," I say.
"Terima kasih banyak," saya tersenyum."Thank you very much," I smile.
Saya mengambil tiket saya dan berjalan dengan tenang menaiki tangga.I take my ticket and walk calmly up the stairs.
harbor photograph
Storyboard: Upstairs in the gallery, Maya studies a massive black-and-white photograph of ships and factories. The caption explains that this harbor area was a loud, dirty industrial zone a hundred years ago, contrasting sharply with the modern waterfront she knows.
Saya berjalan ke atas menuju pameran.I walk upstairs to the exhibition.
Ada banyak gambar lama di sini.There are many old pictures here.
Saya berhenti di depan sebuah foto hitam-putih yang sangat besar.I stop in front of a very large black-and-white photograph.
Gambar ini luar biasa menarik.This picture is incredibly fascinating.
Ini menunjukkan sebuah pelabuhan yang sibuk dengan pabrik-pabrik besar.It shows a busy harbor with large factories.
Saya juga bisa melihat banyak cerobong asap yang tinggi dan kapal-kapal gelap.I can also see many tall chimneys and dark ships.
Kapal-kapal ini terlihat sangat berat.These ships look very heavy.
Saya membaca teks kecil di sebelah foto itu.I read the small text next to the photograph.
Teks tersebut menjelaskan sejarah pelabuhan ini.The text explains the history of this harbor.
Seratus tahun yang lalu, area ini adalah kawasan industri.A hundred years ago, the area was an industrial zone.
Itu adalah tempat yang bising dan kotor pada masa itu.It was a loud and dirty place back then.
Kontrasnya dengan saat ini sangat besar.The contrast to today is enormous.
Sekarang, kawasan tepi laut ini tenang dan bersih.Now, this waterfront is quiet and clean.
Saya sering berjalan-jalan di sepanjang tepi air pada akhir pekan.I often go for a walk along the water on the weekends.
Sulit untuk membayangkan pabrik-pabrik tua ada di sini.It is hard to imagine the old factories here.
Saya memandangi foto ini untuk waktu yang lama.I look at this photograph for a long time.
Kemudian, saya berjalan perlahan menuju ruangan berikutnya.Then I slowly walk on into the next room.
texting sofia
Storyboard: Taking a break in the museum café, Maya takes a picture of the plates she bought and texts it to her sister Sofía. Sofía replies immediately, sending back an old photo to remind Maya that they explored a similar flea market in Mexico exactly two years ago.
Saya sedang duduk di kafe museum dan beristirahat.I am sitting in the museum's café and taking a break.
Saya minum secangkir kopi dan bersantai.I drink a cup of coffee and relax.
Saya mengeluarkan piring-piring biru itu dari tas saya.I take the blue plates out of my bag.
Piring-piring ini sangat cocok untuk dapur saya.These plates fit perfectly in my kitchen.
Saya memotretnya dengan ponsel saya.I take a picture of them with my phone.
Saya mengirimkan foto itu kepada saudara perempuan saya, Sofía.I send the picture to my sister, Sofía.
"Coba lihat piring-piring cantik ini," tulis saya."Just look at these beautiful plates," I write.
"Saya menemukannya di sebuah pasar satu jam yang lalu.""I found them at a market an hour ago."
Sofía membalas saya hampir seketika.Sofía answers me almost immediately.
Dia mengirimkan kembali sebuah foto lama kepada saya.She sends an old picture back to me.
Pesan ini membuat saya sangat senang.This message makes me very happy.
Foto itu memperlihatkan kami berdua di sebuah pasar loak besar di Meksiko.The picture shows the two of us at a large flea market in Mexico.
"Apakah kamu ingat pasar ini?""Do you remember this market?"
Sofía bertanya di pesan berikutnya.Sofía asks in the next message.
"Kita berada di sana tepat dua tahun yang lalu!" tulisnya."We were there exactly two years ago!" she writes.
Saya melihat wajah bahagia kami di foto itu.I look at our happy faces in the picture.
Rasanya seperti sudah sangat lama.It feels like a very long time ago.
Dua tahun yang lalu, saya masih tinggal di Meksiko.Two years ago, I still lived in Mexico.
Saya tersenyum dan menulis pesan balasan yang bahagia.I smile and write a happy message back.
seagull encounter
Storyboard: Walking home through a large park, Maya stops at a kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream. As she strolls along the pond, a bold seagull suddenly swoops down, forcing her to quickly duck and shield this dessert with her hand to prevent the bird from stealing it.
Saya berjalan pulang melewati taman besar.I walk home through a large park.
Satu jam yang lalu saya berada di museum.An hour ago I was at a museum.
Sekarang saya hanya ingin menikmati cuaca yang bagus.Now I just want to enjoy the good weather.
Saya berhenti di sebuah kios kecil untuk membeli es krim wafel.I stop at a small kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream.
"Silakan, ini es krim Anda," kata pria di kios itu."Here you go, here is your ice cream," the man in the kiosk says.
"Terima kasih banyak," jawab saya dan mengambil es krim itu."Thank you very much," I reply and take the ice cream.
Saya berjalan perlahan di sepanjang danau di taman.I walk slowly along the lake in the park.
Es krim wafel ini kelihatannya sangat enak.This waffle ice cream looks really good.
Tiba-tiba saya mendengar suara keras di atas saya.Suddenly I hear a loud noise above me.
Seekor burung camar besar menukik tepat ke arah saya.A large seagull dives right down towards me.
Ia ingin mencuri es krim saya!It wants to steal my ice cream!
Saya menunduk dengan cepat dan melindungi es krim saya dengan tangan.I duck quickly and shield my ice cream with my hand.
Burung itu tidak akan mencuri makanan saya.The bird is not going to steal my food.
Burung camar itu terbang melintas dan mendarat di rumput.The seagull flies past and lands on the grass.
Seorang wanita di bangku tertawa.A woman on a bench laughs.
"Mereka sangat nakal hari ini," katanya."They are very cheeky today," she says.
"Ya, mereka memang begitu," balas saya sambil tertawa."Yes, they certainly are," I laugh back.
Saya cepat-cepat menghabiskan es krim saya sambil terus berjalan.I quickly finish eating my ice cream while I walk on.
unpacking plates
Storyboard: Back in her apartment that evening, Maya washes these newly bought plates in the sink and places them in the cupboard. She looks out the window at the lingering summer sunset, entirely content with this quiet Sunday and deeply settled in the life she started half a year ago.
Saya sudah kembali di apartemen saya.I am back in my apartment.
Sekarang hari Minggu malam, dan di sini sangat sepi.It is Sunday evening, and it is very quiet here.
Saya sedang berdiri di dapur.I am standing out in the kitchen.
Saya mencuci piring-piring baru saya di tempat cuci piring.I wash my new plates in the sink.
Piring-piring ini benar-benar indah.These plates are really beautiful.
Warnanya biru dan sedikit berat.They are blue and a little heavy.
Saya mengeringkannya dengan lap dapur.I dry them with a tea towel.
Setelah itu, saya menyimpannya di dalam lemari.Afterwards, I put them in the cupboard.
Saya berjalan ke ruang tamu dan melihat ke luar jendela.I walk into the living room and look out the window.
Matahari perlahan terbenam di atas atap-atap kota.The sun is slowly going down over the city's roofs.
Malam musim panas ini indah dan terang.This summer evening is beautiful and bright.
Saya sangat menyukai lingkungan ini.I really like this neighborhood.
Sekarang saya sudah tinggal di sini selama setengah tahun.I have lived here for half a year now.
Begitu banyak hal telah terjadi sejak bulan Januari.So much has happened since January.
Saat itu saya tidak mengenal siapa pun di kota ini.Back then I knew no one in the city.
Sekarang saya mengenal jalan-jalan dan kehidupan sehari-hari jauh lebih baik.Now I know the streets and everyday life much better.
Saya benar-benar merasa seperti di rumah sendiri di kota ini.I feel completely at home in this city.
Besok adalah hari Senin, dan saya naik bus ke tempat kerja.Tomorrow is Monday, and I take the bus to work.
Namun saat ini saya hanya bersantai.But right now I am just relaxing.
Saya sepenuhnya puas dengan kehidupan saya di sini.I am completely content with my life here.
