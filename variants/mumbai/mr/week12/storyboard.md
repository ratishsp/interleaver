# Week 12 — Storyboard (Maya joins a badminton club)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** The **-ायला** form (खेळायला) with **आवडतं / जाते**; ability with **येणे** (मला खेळता येतं) — landing inside varied actions and exchanges, not as declarations

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_thursday_afternoon | At the software office, Maya chats with her colleague Rohan about free time. She tells him she likes to play badminton and wants to meet new people in the city, so she decides to permanently replace her Thursday gym sessions with visits to a local badminton club. |
| 2 | 02_arriving_at_club | Maya arrives at the local badminton club at half past five in the evening. She watches the fast rallies on the brightly lit indoor courts and feels excited to get active. |
| 3 | 03_guest_registration | Maya speaks to the staff at the reception desk to enter as a guest for the evening. Since she does not own any sports gear in Mumbai, she pays the daily entry fee and rents a racket for the session, testing its weight in her hand. |
| 4 | 04_meeting_the_group | Maya walks towards a court where three friendly players—Amit, Neha, and Sanjay—are warming up. She introduces herself, explains that she likes to play doubles, and asks if she can join their game. |
| 5 | 05_doubles_match | The group warmly welcomes her, and they start a doubles match. Maya immediately shows that she can play very well, moving quickly across the court thanks to her years of practice back in Mexico. |
| 6 | 06_post_game_chat | After a lively and competitive game, the four players take a break and drink water by the side of the court. Amit, Neha, and Sanjay ask if she can play with them regularly, and Maya happily agrees to join them every Thursday evening. |
| 7 | 07_returning_gear | At the end of the evening, Maya returns the rented racket to the reception desk. She leaves the club and heads home, thrilled to have a new Thursday routine and new friends. |
