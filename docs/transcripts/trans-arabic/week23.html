office invitation
Storyboard: At the software company, Eva warmly invites Maya to her home for dinner this Friday. Maya gladly accepts, smiling, and says that she will definitely be there.

تناولني الورقة.She hands the note to me.
"أتطلع بشدة إلى يوم الجمعة،" أقول لها."I will look forward very much to Friday," I say to her.

buying a gift

taking the bus
