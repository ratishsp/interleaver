Week 20: Maya starts volunteering at the café

Estonian with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding the volunteering
Storyboard: Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. One evening she browses a volunteering website, reads a few listings, and finds the little café she already knows, which needs weekend helpers, so she fills in the short sign-up form.
Ma olen kuulnud, et paljud taanlased teevad vabal ajal vabatahtlikku tööd.I have heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time.
Mulle meeldib see mõte.I like the idea.
Ma tahan õppida paremini taani keelt rääkima ja kohtuda uute inimestega.I want to learn to speak better Danish and meet new people.
Ühel õhtul istun ma oma arvuti taga.One evening, I am sitting with my computer.
Ma leian vabatahtliku töö veebilehe.I find a website for voluntary work.
Ma loen lehel kolme esimest kuulutust.I read the first three listings on the site.
Esimene kuulutus on spordiklubilt.The first listing is from a sports club.
Aga nad vajavad abi neljapäeva õhtuti ja siis mängin ma sulgpalli.But they need help on Thursday evenings, and I play badminton then.
Teine kuulutus on muuseumilt.The second listing is from a museum.
Nad vajavad inimest kolmapäeva õhtuti, aga siis ma teen trenni.They need a person on Wednesday evenings, but I work out then.
Siis ma vaatan kolmandat kuulutust.Then I look at the third listing.
See on väikeselt kohvikult, mida ma juba tean.It is from the little café that I already know.
Nad vajavad inimesi laupäeva hommikuteks.They need people for Saturday mornings.
See sobib mulle täiesti ideaalselt.That suits me absolutely perfectly.
Ma täidan lühikese ankeedi oma nime ja e-posti aadressiga.I fill in a short form with my name and my email address.
Ma kirjutan ka oma telefoninumbri: kaksteist, kolmkümmend neli, viiskümmend kuus, seitsekümmend kaheksa.I also write my phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight.
Lõpuks vajutan saada.Finally, I press send.
Ma ei ole kunagi varem kohvikus töötanud, aga olen valmis seda õppima.I have never worked in a café before, but I am ready to learn it.
first shift arrival

Ma ootan seda põnevusega, aga olen ka natuke närvis.I am looking forward to it, but I am also a little nervous.

learning the machine
Storyboard: Before the doors open, an experienced volunteer brings Maya over to the large espresso machine. The volunteer shows her how to grind the coffee beans and how to steam the milk. Maya watches every step closely, because it is her first time behind the counter.
See on minu esimene kord leti taga.It is my first time behind the counter.
Kohvik ei ole veel avatud.The café is not open yet.
Üks kogenud vabatahtlikest kutsub mind.One of the experienced volunteers calls for me.

Samal ajal vaatan ma hoolega tema käsi.I look closely at his hands meanwhile.

the first coffee

Ma tegin just oma päris esimese cappuccino.I have just made my very first cappuccino.

Kahjuks ei tekkinud piimale paksu vahtu.Unfortunately, the milk did not get any thick foam.
Ma vaatasin ettevaatlikult kannu sisse.I looked carefully down into the pitcher.

bun with cheese

the ginger beer
