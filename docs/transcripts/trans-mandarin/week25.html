Week 25: A Sunday in the city

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning calendar
Storyboard: On Sunday morning, Maya makes a fresh pot of coffee and flips her wall calendar to July. She realizes that she moved to Copenhagen exactly six months ago, and decides to celebrate by spending this sunny day exploring the city at her own pace.
今天是星期天早上。It is Sunday morning.
阳光透过我公寓的窗户照了进来。The sun is shining in through the window in my apartment.
我走进厨房。I go out into the kitchen.
我煮了一壶新鲜的咖啡。I make a pot of fresh coffee.
今天早晨咖啡的香味太棒了。The smell of coffee is fantastic this morning.
我墙上挂着一本日历。I have a calendar on the wall.
我把日历翻到了下个月。I turn the page to the next month.
现在是七月了。It is July now.
我是一月份搬到哥本哈根的。I moved to Copenhagen in January.
我正好是六个月前来到这里的。I came here exactly six months ago.
时间过得真快。Time really goes fast.
我在这座城市的第一天非常冷。My first day in this city was very cold.
但是今天天气温暖而美好。But today the weather is warm and beautiful.
我想庆祝一下这个小小的纪念日。I want to celebrate this little anniversary.
我今天没有固定的安排。I have no fixed plans today.
我端起咖啡杯，望向外面的街道。I take my coffee cup and look out over the street.
我想按自己的节奏出去探索这座城市。I want to go out and explore the city at my own pace.
我很期待享受这段明媚的时光。I look forward to enjoying these bright hours.
ceramic plates
Storyboard: Maya wanders into a lively second-hand market in a nearby square. She spots two blue ceramic plates on a table, decides these plates are perfect for her kitchen, and buys them from the vendor for forty kroner.
我散步穿过城里的一个大广场。I go for a walk across a large square in the city.
这里每个星期天都有一个大型跳蚤市场。Here there is a large flea market every Sunday.
阳光明媚，许多人在桌子之间走来走去。The sun is shining, and many people are walking around between the tables.
我在一张放着许多不同物品的小桌子旁停了下来。I stop by a small table with many different things.
桌子后面站着一位友好的摊主。Behind the table stands a friendly vendor.
突然，我看到了两个蓝色的陶瓷盘子。Suddenly I see two blue ceramic plates.
我把它们拿起来，仔细看了看。I pick them up and look closely at them.
“这些盘子多少钱？”我问摊主。"How much do these plates cost?" I ask the vendor.
摊主对我笑了笑。The vendor smiles at me.
“它们只要四十克朗，”他回答道。"They cost only forty kroner," he replies.
这个价格真的很划算。That is a really good price.
一个月前，我给厨房买了一张小桌子。I bought a small table for my kitchen a month ago.
这些盘子和这张桌子会非常相配。These plates will fit perfectly with this table.
“我很想买下它们，”我说。"I would very much like to buy them," I say.
我给了摊主四十克朗，小心地把盘子放进包里。I give the vendor forty kroner and carefully put the plates in my bag.
在一个宁静的星期天发现一些特别的东西，总是一件美好的事。It is always nice to find something special on a quiet Sunday.
winter coat
Storyboard: At a clothing stall, Maya tries on a heavy wool winter coat. She immediately takes it off because this coat is far too warm for summer, laughing with the vendor that she desperately needed something exactly like it five months ago.
我继续往市场里面走，停在一个服装摊位前。I walk further into the market and stop at a clothing stall.
我看到一件厚重的羊毛冬装大衣。I see a heavy winter coat made of wool.
这件大衣很漂亮，但非常厚。This coat is beautiful, but very thick.
不过我还是试穿了一下这件大衣。I try the coat on anyway.
它暖和极了。It is incredibly warm.
我马上又把它脱了下来。I take it off again immediately.
现在是七月，今天阳光明媚。It is July, and the sun is shining today.
一位友善的女人站在桌子后面微笑着。A friendly woman stands behind the table and smiles.
“太热了吗？”女人问。"Is it too warm?" the woman asks.
“是的，夏天穿有点太热了。”我笑着回答。"Yes, it is a bit too warm for the summer," I reply and laugh.
我摸了摸那厚实的布料。I touch the thick fabric.
“但是五个月前，我真的非常需要这件冬装大衣！”我说。"But I really needed this winter coat five months ago!" I say.
女人哈哈大笑。The woman laughs loudly.
“是的，二月份非常冷。”她说。"Yes, February was very cold," she says.
我想起了我刚来哥本哈根的那几个星期。I remember my first weeks in Copenhagen.
那时候我总是觉得很冷。I was constantly freezing back then.
幸运的是，我现在不需要这么保暖的衣服。Fortunately, I do not need such warm clothes right now.
我把大衣挂回衣架上，说了声再见。I hang the coat back on the rack and say goodbye.
museum ticket
Storyboard: Moving on, Maya enters a local history museum and goes to the front desk. She asks the clerk if this entrance ticket includes the special upstairs exhibition, and the clerk tells her to hurry because the guided tour started ten minutes ago.
我离开市场，找到了一家当地的小博物馆。I walk away from the market and find a small, local museum.
我走进这座安静的建筑。I walk into the quiet building.
我走到柜台前买门票。I walk over to the counter to buy an entrance ticket.
收银台后面站着一位年轻人。A young man is standing behind the register.
“你好，请给我一张成人票，”我说。"Hi, one adult ticket, please," I say.
“一共是一百克朗，”他说着，把门票打印出来。"That will be one hundred kroner," he says and prints it.
我付了钱，看着柜台上这些彩色的宣传册。I pay and look at these colorful brochures on the counter.
它们在宣传一个关于这座城市历史的特别展览。They advertise a special exhibition about the history of the city.
“这张票也可以参观楼上的展览吗？”我问。"Does this ticket also give access to the exhibition upstairs?" I ask.
“是的，这家博物馆只有这一种通票，”他回答。"Yes, this museum only has one combined ticket," he answers.
“展览在楼上的二层，”他说。"The exhibition is upstairs on the first floor," he says.
“您可以在上面慢慢参观。”"You can take your time up there."
“那里有很多这座城市的老照片，”他解释道。"It has many old photographs of the city," he explains.
“其中一些有一百多年的历史了。”"Some of them are over a hundred years old."
“听起来真的很有意思，”我说。"That sounds really exciting," I say.
“非常感谢，”我微笑着说。"Thank you very much," I smile.
我拿着门票，不紧不慢地走上楼梯。I take my ticket and walk calmly up the stairs.
harbor photograph
Storyboard: Upstairs in the gallery, Maya studies a massive black-and-white photograph of ships and factories. The caption explains that this harbor area was a loud, dirty industrial zone a hundred years ago, contrasting sharply with the modern waterfront she knows.
我上楼去看展览。I walk upstairs to the exhibition.
这里有很多老照片。There are many old pictures here.
我停在一张非常大的黑白照片前。I stop in front of a very large black-and-white photograph.
这张照片非常吸引人。This picture is incredibly fascinating.
它展示了一个繁忙的港口和大型工厂。It shows a busy harbor with large factories.
我还能看到许多高耸的烟囱和深色的船只。I can also see many tall chimneys and dark ships.
这些船看起来非常沉重。These ships look very heavy.
我读了照片旁边的一小段文字。I read the small text next to the photograph.
这段文字介绍了这个港口的历史。The text explains the history of this harbor.
一百年前，这个地区是一个工业区。A hundred years ago, the area was an industrial zone.
那时候，这里是一个嘈杂又肮脏的地方。It was a loud and dirty place back then.
它与现在的反差巨大。The contrast to today is enormous.
如今，这片滨水区安静又干净。Now, this waterfront is quiet and clean.
周末我经常沿着水边散步。I often go for a walk along the water on the weekends.
很难想象这里曾经有那些老工厂。It is hard to imagine the old factories here.
我盯着这张照片看了很久。I look at this photograph for a long time.
然后，我慢慢地走进下一个房间。Then I slowly walk on into the next room.
texting sofia

我坐在博物馆的咖啡馆里休息。I am sitting in the museum's café and taking a break.
我喝着一杯咖啡，放松一下。I drink a cup of coffee and relax.
我从包里拿出那些蓝色的盘子。I take the blue plates out of my bag.
这些盘子非常适合我的厨房。These plates fit perfectly in my kitchen.
我用手机给它们拍了张照。I take a picture of them with my phone.
我把照片发给我的妹妹索菲娅。I send the picture to my sister, Sofía.
“快看这些漂亮的盘子，”我写道。"Just look at these beautiful plates," I write.
“我一个小时前在一个市场发现它们的。”"I found them at a market an hour ago."
索菲娅几乎立刻回复了我。Sofía answers me almost immediately.
她给我发回了一张旧照片。She sends an old picture back to me.
这条消息让我非常高兴。This message makes me very happy.
照片上是我们俩在墨西哥的一个大型跳蚤市场。The picture shows the two of us at a large flea market in Mexico.
“你还记得这个市场吗？”"Do you remember this market?"
索菲娅在下一条消息里问道。Sofía asks in the next message.

我看着照片上我们开心的面庞。I look at our happy faces in the picture.

两年前，我还住在墨西哥。Two years ago, I still lived in Mexico.
我笑着回了一条开心的消息。I smile and write a happy message back.
seagull encounter
Storyboard: Walking home through a large park, Maya stops at a kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream. As she strolls along the pond, a bold seagull suddenly swoops down, forcing her to quickly duck and shield this dessert with her hand to prevent the bird from stealing it.
我穿过一个大公园走回家。I walk home through a large park.
一小时前我在一家博物馆。An hour ago I was at a museum.
现在我只想享受这好天气。Now I just want to enjoy the good weather.
我在一个小售货亭停下来，买了一个蛋筒冰淇淋。I stop at a small kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream.
“给你，你的冰淇淋，”售货亭里的男人说。"Here you go, here is your ice cream," the man in the kiosk says.
“非常感谢，”我回答着，接过了冰淇淋。"Thank you very much," I reply and take the ice cream.
我沿着公园里的湖慢慢走着。I walk slowly along the lake in the park.
这个蛋筒冰淇淋看起来真不错。This waffle ice cream looks really good.
突然，我听到头顶上方传来很大的声音。Suddenly I hear a loud noise above me.
一只大海鸥直冲我俯冲下来。A large seagull dives right down towards me.
它想偷我的冰淇淋！It wants to steal my ice cream!
我迅速低下头，用手护住我的冰淇淋。I duck quickly and shield my ice cream with my hand.
这只鸟休想偷走我的食物。The bird is not going to steal my food.
海鸥飞了过去，落在草地上。The seagull flies past and lands on the grass.
长椅上的一个女人笑了起来。A woman on a bench laughs.
“它们今天可真调皮，”她说。"They are very cheeky today," she says.
“是啊，确实如此，”我笑着回应。"Yes, they certainly are," I laugh back.
我一边继续往前走，一边迅速吃完了冰淇淋。I quickly finish eating my ice cream while I walk on.
unpacking plates
