# Week 21 — Storyboard (Maya buys spring clothes and makes an exchange)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Comparatives *større/mindre*; *prøve*; sizes

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_spring_saturday_shopping | After finishing her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the café, Maya enjoys the mild spring weather. She walks down the bustling pedestrian street to look for lighter clothes for the changing season. |
| 2 | 02_trying_the_jacket | Inside a large clothing store, she finds a light green spring jacket. She tries it on in front of a mirror on the shop floor, sees that it fits perfectly, and decides to buy it. |
| 3 | 03_grabbing_size_medium | On her way to the cash register, Maya spots a nice blue cotton shirt. The line to try clothes on is very long, so she just picks a size medium off the rack and decides to skip the fitting room. |
| 4 | 04_saving_the_receipt | At the checkout, she pays for both the jacket and the shirt. The friendly clerk hands her the paper receipt, and Maya carefully folds it into her wallet in case she needs it later. |
| 5 | 05_shirt_too_small | Back in her apartment shortly after, Maya unpacks her shopping bags. She puts on the new blue shirt, but quickly realizes the size medium is much too small across her shoulders. |
| 6 | 06_returning_to_store | Wanting to fix the problem immediately, Maya heads right back out into the Saturday afternoon sun. She walks back to the same clothing store holding the unworn shirt and her paper receipt. |
| 7 | 07_asking_for_size_forty | She finds a helpful shop assistant and explains that the shirt she bought is too small. She asks to try a bigger size, specifically requesting a size forty, and the assistant hands one to her. |
| 8 | 08_comparing_in_mirror | Maya takes the clothes into an empty fitting room so she can properly evaluate the fit. Looking in the mirror, she sees that the bigger size is much more comfortable and fits better than the smaller one. |
| 9 | 09_completing_exchange | She brings both items to the counter, handing the smaller shirt back to the clerk. The clerk scans the receipt to process the exchange for the size forty, and Maya leaves the store fully satisfied. |
