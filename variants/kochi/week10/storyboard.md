# Week 10 — Storyboard (Maya shops for summer clothes)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Adjectives as a system — attributive **നല്ല/വലിയ/ചെറിയ** and predicative with **ആണ്** (Malayalam adjectives never change form); **ധരിക്കുക/ഇടുക** (wear)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_saturday_morning_heat | It is a hot Saturday morning. Maya meets Anitha outside their apartment building. Maya explains that her thick clothes are too warm for the arriving March heat. Anitha recommends a large textile shop in the city for thin, light cotton clothes. |
| 2 | 02_the_blue_kurta | Maya travels by bus to the shop and browses the women's section. She picks up a beautiful blue cotton kurta and asks a shop assistant the price — six hundred rupees. In the fitting room the medium size fits well, and she decides to buy it. |
| 3 | 03_the_bright_yellow_top | On a rack right beside the fitting rooms, a bright yellow top catches her eye, and she tries it as well. It fits her well, but the price is much too high, so she leaves it behind. |
| 4 | 04_the_red_gym_shirt | In the activewear section, Maya finds a large, breathable red t-shirt for her gym evenings — three hundred rupees. She tries it on, it feels light and comfortable, and she decides to buy it. |
| 5 | 05_the_white_scarf | While walking toward the front of the shop, Maya picks up a large white cotton scarf. A nearby clerk tells her it costs one hundred rupees. She decides to buy it to block the strong sun during her daily commute. |
| 6 | 06_paying_the_bill | Maya takes the blue kurta, the red t-shirt, and the white scarf to the billing counter. The cashier names the total — one thousand rupees exactly. Maya pays with cash and takes her shopping bags. |
| 7 | 07_wearing_the_new_scarf | Maya steps out of the shop into the extremely hot afternoon sun. She immediately takes the new white scarf out of her bag and wears it over her head and shoulders, feeling comfortable as she walks to the bus stop. |
