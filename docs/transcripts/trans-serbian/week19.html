Week 19: Maya starts a new project with the team

Serbian with English gloss, line-aligned.

monday bus excitement
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya takes the bus to work. She feels excited and a little nervous because a large new software project starts today, and she will finally work closely with the entire team.
Ponedeljak je ujutru i sedim u autobusu.It is Monday morning, and I am sitting on the bus.
Na putu sam na posao.I am on my way to work.
Gledam kroz prozor.I look out of the window.
Danas sam malo nervozna.I am a little nervous today.
Ali se i veoma radujem tome.But I am also looking forward to it a lot.
Danas počinje veliki, novi softverski projekat.A large, new software project starts today.
Radim kao softverski inženjer u kompaniji od januara.I have worked as a software engineer at the company since January.
Dobro poznajem svog kolegu Petera.I know my colleague Peter well.
Radimo zajedno od mog prvog dana.We have worked together since my first day.
Ali sada moram blisko da sarađujem sa celim timom.But now I must work closely with the whole team.
Radiću sa našim šefom, našim dizajnerom i našim testerom.I will work with our boss, our designer, and our tester.
Oni već dugo rade u kompaniji.They have worked at the company for a long time.
Autobus staje i još ljudi ulazi.The bus stops, and more people get on.
Gledam na sat.I look at my watch.
Osam je sati.It is eight o'clock.
Jutarnji sastanak počinje za pola sata.The morning meeting begins in half an hour.
Duboko udišem.I take a deep breath.
Spremna sam za novi projekat.I am ready for the new project.
first morning presentation
Storyboard: At the short morning meeting, Maya stands in front of her colleagues. She presents her part of the technical plan for the first time, explaining what she has worked on to prepare for the week's tasks.
Sedimo u sali za sastanke.We are sitting in the meeting room.
Vreme je za naš kratki jutarnji sastanak.It is time for our short morning meeting.
Gledam svoje kolege oko sebe.I look around at my colleagues.
Danas je poseban dan za mene.Today is a special day for me.
Ustajem.I stand up.
Ja sam softverska inženjerka i predstaviću svoj deo projekta.I am a software engineer, and I am going to present my part of the project.
Ruke mi se malo tresu.My hands are shaking a little.
Ali dobro sam se pripremila.But I have prepared well.
"Dobro jutro", kažem glasno."Good morning," I say loudly.
"Radila sam na tehničkom planu", kažem im."I have worked on the technical plan," I tell them.
"Pročitala sam sve dokumente.""I have read all the documents."
"I napisala sam beleške za ovonedeljne zadatke.""And I have written notes for the week's tasks."
Objašnjavam plan celom timu.I explain the plan to the whole team.
Zapravo nije teško govoriti o tehnologiji.It is actually not difficult to talk about the technology.
Peter sedi prekoputa mene.Peter is sitting opposite me.
Osećam kako se više opuštam.I feel myself relaxing more.
On sluša i klima glavom.He listens and nods.
Završavam svoju prezentaciju, a ostali se smeše.I finish my presentation, and the others smile.
Moja prva prezentacija na poslu je prošla dobro.My first presentation at work has gone well.
boss project manager
Storyboard: After the morning meeting, her boss Thomas stops by Maya's desk with a part of the new project for her. He is warm and welcoming: he compliments her on her presentation and says the whole team has looked forward to having her on board. Maya feels genuinely welcomed onto the team.
Posle jutarnjeg sastanka, sedim za svojim radnim stolom.After the morning meeting, I sit at my desk.
Gledam svoje beleške sa prezentacije.I look at my notes from the presentation.
Moj šef, Tomas, prilazi mi.My boss, Thomas, comes over to me.
On je naš menadžer projekta.He is our project manager.
On ovde radi mnogo godina.He has worked here for many years.
"Dobro jutro, Majo", kaže on uz osmeh."Good morning, Maya," he says with a smile.
"To je bila stvarno dobra prezentacija.""That was a really good presentation."
"Hvala, Tomase", odgovaram."Thank you, Thomas," I answer.
"Veoma smo srećni što te imamo u timu", nastavlja Tomas."We are very happy to have you on the team," Thomas continues.
"Ceo tim se radovao saradnji sa tobom.""The whole team has looked forward to working with you."
"I ja sam se radovala tome", kažem radosno."I have also looked forward to it," I say happily.
"To je uzbudljiv projekat.""It is an exciting project."
Tomas se ponovo osmehuje i vraća u svoju kancelariju.Thomas smiles again and walks back to his office.
Gledam u ekran svog računara.I look at my computer screen.
Osećam se zaista dobrodošlo u timu.I feel genuinely welcome on the team.
peter software engineer
Storyboard: Maya sits down with Peter to write code. As software engineers, they solve complex problems together, and Peter jokes that he has worked on one specific error for three hours until Maya easily spots his mistake.
Sedam za Peterov radni sto.I sit down at Peter's desk.
Gledamo u njegov veliki ekran.We look at his large screen.
Peter je takođe softverski inženjer.Peter is also a software engineer.
On je dobar kolega i zajedno pišemo mnogo koda.He is a good colleague, and we write a lot of code together.
Danas izgleda veoma umorno.Today he looks very tired.
"Šta radiš, Petere?" pitam."What are you doing, Peter?" I ask.
"Imam veliki problem", odgovara on."I have a big problem," he answers.
"Radio sam na ovoj grešci tri sata.""I have worked on this error for three hours."
Smešim se i pažljivo gledam kod.I smile and look closely at the code.
Često zajedno rešavamo složene probleme na poslu.We often solve complex problems together in our work.
Odjednom vidim nešto čudno na ekranu.Suddenly I see something strange on the screen.
To je veoma mala greška.It is a very small mistake.
"Pokušaj da pogledaš ovde", kažem i pokazujem na ekran."Try to look here," I say and point at the screen.
"Napravio si malu grešku u kucanju ovde.""You have made a small typo here."
Peter tiho gleda u ekran dve sekunde.Peter looks quietly at the screen for two seconds.
Zatim se glasno nasmeje.Then he laughs loudly.
"Potpuno si u pravu", kaže Peter."You are absolutely right," Peter says.
"Radio sam tri sata, i onda je to samo jedna mala greška!""I have worked for three hours, and then it is just one small error!"
I ja se smejem, jer svi softverski inženjeri znaju tu situaciju.I laugh too, because all software engineers know that situation.
Ispravljamo grešku i program konačno radi savršeno.We fix the error, and the program finally works perfectly.
eva digital designer
Storyboard: Later, Maya walks over to meet Eva, the team's designer. Eva shows Maya her beautiful sketches and explains that she has worked as a digital designer since she finished university, focusing on how users experience the product.
Kasnije popodne, odlazim do Evinog radnog stola.Later in the afternoon, I walk over to Eva's desk.
Ona je digitalna dizajnerka tima.She is the team's digital designer.
Do danas nisam radila sa njom.I have not worked with her before today.
"Zdravo Majo, dođi i pogledaj", kaže ona."Hi Maya, come and look," she says.
Pokazuje na svoj veliki ekran.She points at her large screen.
Vidim neke prelepe skice našeg novog softvera.I see some beautiful sketches of our new software.
Boje su mirne, a dizajn je veoma čist.The colors are calm, and the design is very clean.
"To izgleda stvarno dobro", kažem."That looks really good," I say.
"Hvala", odgovara Eva uz osmeh."Thank you," Eva answers with a smile.
Objašnjava mi svoj rad.She explains her work to me.
"Radim kao digitalna dizajnerka još od fakulteta", kaže ona."I have worked as a digital designer since university," she says.
"Uvek se fokusiram na korisničko iskustvo.""I always focus on the user's experience."
Pokazuje mi kako korisnik klikće po programu.She shows me how the user clicks around the program.
"Mora biti lako i logično za korišćenje", objašnjava ona."It must be easy and logical to use," she explains.
Često gledam samo sâm kôd.I often only look at the code itself.
Ali njen dizajn je neverovatno važan za projekat.But her design is incredibly important for the project.
Razgovaramo još malo o našim zajedničkim zadacima.We talk a little more about our shared tasks.
Radujem se programiranju njenog prelepog dizajna.I look forward to programming her beautiful design.
meeting the tester
Storyboard: The next day, Maya sits with Mads, the software tester, as he clicks quickly through the new screens and spots a small mistake at once. He laughs that his job is to break things before the users do — he has done it for five years and finds problems fast.
Sledeći je dan u kancelariji.It is the next day at the office.
Sedim pored Madsa, našeg softverskog testera.I am sitting next to Mads, our software tester.
On gleda u nove ekrane.He looks at the new screens.
Veoma brzo klikće mišem.He clicks very fast with the mouse.
Odjednom se zaustavlja.Suddenly he stops.
Već je našao malu grešku.He has already found a small mistake.
Gleda me i malo se smeje.He looks at me and laughs a little.
"Moj posao je da kvarim stvari", kaže on."My job is to break things," he says.
"Moram da nađem greške pre naših korisnika.""I have to find the mistakes before our users."
Radi kao tester pet godina.He has worked as a tester for five years.
Testirao je mnogo različitih projekata.He has tested many different projects.
Zato tako brzo nalazi probleme.That is why he finds the problems so fast.
Gledam grešku na svom računaru.I look at the mistake on my computer.
Srećom, to je samo mali problem.Fortunately, it is only a small problem.
Odmah ispravljam kod.I fix the code right away.
Testira program još jednom.He tests the program one more time.
Sada sve radi apsolutno savršeno.Now everything works absolutely perfectly.
Veoma sam zahvalna na njegovoj pomoći.I am very glad for his help.

friday canteen lunch
