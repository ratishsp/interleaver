# Week 23 — Storyboard (Maya visits Eva for dinner and makes future plans)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Future *skal/vil*; *har tænkt mig at*; *synes/tror/føler at* + subordinate *at*-clause word order (needs a sentence adverb — *ikke/altid/nok* — in the clause to show)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_office_invitation | At the software company, Eva asks Maya if she will come over to her place for dinner on Friday evening. Maya gladly accepts the invitation, smiling, and says that she will definitely be there. |
| 2 | 02_flowers_and_bus | On Friday afternoon, Maya buys a bright bouquet of yellow tulips after work. She intends to take the bus directly to Eva's neighborhood, because she believes that Eva will always appreciate colorful things. |
| 3 | 03_arriving_at_evas | Maya rings the doorbell, and Eva warmly welcomes her into a highly creative, cozy apartment. Maya cheerfully hands over the tulips, saying she hopes that Eva will actually like them. |
| 4 | 04_mushroom_tart_dinner | They sit down at a wooden dining table lit by tall candles. Eva serves a warm mushroom tart and fresh crusty bread, and Maya tells her host that she thinks that the food is simply wonderful. |
| 5 | 05_finding_a_rhythm | As they eat, they discuss their current daily routines. Maya explains that she feels that she is finally settling into her life in the city, and she announces that she intends to stay for a very long time. |
| 6 | 06_sister_visiting | Eva asks about Maya's family back in Mexico. Maya answers that she misses them, but she hopes that her sister Sofía will perhaps travel to Europe next summer so they can explore the city together. |
| 7 | 07_art_museum_plan | Over a cup of coffee, Eva suggests a weekend trip to an art museum next month. Maya loves the idea, stating she believes that they will certainly have a fantastic time. |
| 8 | 08_heading_home | Before Maya puts on her coat to leave, she thanks Eva for a wonderful evening. They cheerfully agree that they will text each other tomorrow to finalize the museum plans. |
