# Week 9 — Storyboard (Maya at the weekly market)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Prices — **Was kostet das? / Das macht …**; amounts as words; **plural noun forms** (*ein Apfel / drei Äpfel*, *eine Karte / zwei Karten*); **ich hätte gern / ich nehme** at the stall

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_arrival_at_market | It is Saturday morning. Maya rides her bicycle to a large weekly market near her home. She parks her Fahrrad, takes out her cloth bags, and walks into the bustling crowds. |
| 2 | 02_fruit_stall | Maya stops at a large fruit and vegetable stall. She sees a sign showing the per-kilo price for apples. She points and says „Ich hätte gern fünf Äpfel“. The trader weighs them and tells her the exact price. |
| 3 | 03_cheese_tasting | At the next stall, a trader cuts large wheels of cheese. He offers Maya a small piece on a wooden stick to taste. It is spicy and delicious. Maya says „Ich nehme das“ and the trader wraps up a generous piece for her. |
| 4 | 04_expensive_cup | Maya browses a stall selling handmade ceramics. She picks up a beautiful blue cup and asks the stallholder „Was kostet das?“. The trader names the price. It is too expensive for her today. Maya puts the cup down, declines politely, and walks on. |
| 5 | 05_bakery_chalkboard | Maya smells fresh bread and finds a bakery stall. A large chalkboard lists the prices for all the baked goods. She reads the board, steps up to the counter, and orders vier Brötchen. The trader confirms the total: „Das macht...“ before taking the money. Maya pays the exact amount in small coins. |
| 6 | 06_flower_stall | Before she leaves, Maya wants some colour for her Wohnung. She picks out a large bunch of yellow flowers. The trader names the price unprompted. Maya pays with a twenty-euro note, and the trader counts the change out into her hand. |
| 7 | 07_coffee_break | Her bags are heavy and full. Maya goes to a small cart and buys a Kaffee. She stands at a high table at the edge of the market, sips her warm drink, and watches the people walking past the colourful stalls. |
| 8 | 08_ride_home | Maya returns to her bicycle. She carefully packs the flowers, the Brötchen, and the groceries into her bike basket. She unlocks her Fahrrad and rides home, ready for a delicious weekend breakfast. |
