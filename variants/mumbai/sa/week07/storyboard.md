# Week 7 — Storyboard (Maya schedules her week)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Time — **कति वादनम्?**; weekdays (**सोमवासरः** …) & months; frequency adverbs **सर्वदा / कदाचित् / प्रतिदिनम्**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_weekly_calendar | Maya sits with her morning tea, reviewing her calendar for the month of February. She notes her daily office hours and highlights her new, regular Tuesday and Thursday evening gym sessions at half past five. |
| 2 | 02_bicycle_service | Maya walks her bicycle to the neighbourhood mechanic to get it serviced. She politely calls the mechanic bhaiya, explains what she needs, and asks what time she should return to collect it later in the week. |
| 3 | 03_salon_phone_call | Maya calls a local salon to book a haircut. She asks the receptionist what times are available and successfully schedules an appointment for Saturday morning at exactly eleven o'clock. |
| 4 | 04_tuesday_gym | On Tuesday evening at half past five, Maya arrives at the local gym for her workout. She completes her exercises, happy to finally have a healthy routine that she can follow always. |
| 5 | 05_asha_invitation | Midweek, Maya bumps into her neighbour Asha in the building lobby. Asha warmly suggests they go out for coffee together this Saturday at eleven o'clock. |
| 6 | 06_calendar_conflict | Back in her flat, Maya checks her phone and realizes she has a double booking. She sees that both her haircut and the coffee with Asha land at the exact same hour on Saturday. |
| 7 | 07_rescheduling_salon | Maya quickly calls the salon back to fix the schedule. She explains the situation and moves her haircut appointment to half past two in the afternoon, keeping her morning free. |
| 8 | 08_picking_up_bicycle | On Thursday, Maya returns to the mechanic at the agreed time to collect her freshly serviced bicycle. She thanks the mechanic for having it ready right on schedule. |
| 9 | 09_saturday_coffee | Saturday morning arrives, and Maya meets Asha for coffee at eleven o'clock. They relax and chat about the places they sometimes visit on weekends, with Maya's calendar finally perfectly organized. |
