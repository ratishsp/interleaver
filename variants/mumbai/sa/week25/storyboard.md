# Week 25 — Storyboard (A slow Sunday and the monsoon's arrival)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Demonstratives **एषः/सः + एतत्/तत्** as a system; **पूर्वम्** (ago — मासद्वयात् पूर्वम्); time adverbs

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_monsoon | Maya stands by her apartment window watching a sudden, heavy burst of June monsoon rain. Reflecting with the week's 'ago' word, she thinks about on how mild and dry Mumbai felt when she arrived five months ago in January, contrasting it with the intense weather now. |
| 2 | 02_the_fruit_stall | The rain pauses, and Maya heads out for a slow walk, finding herself at a bustling Sunday street market. At a fruit stall, she asks the vendor about these local mangoes in front of her versus those larger ones stacked at the back, and buys a few of the local ones. |
| 3 | 03_browsing_antiques | Maya stops to browse a cluttered stall of old trinkets. She points out this small brass bell nearby and that carved wooden box on the high shelf, asking the elderly vendor what each costs — and walks on smiling, buying nothing. |
| 4 | 04_sudden_downpour | The sky darkens and the rain returns in a torrential burst. Narrating how fast the weather changes today, Maya hurries through the gates of a museum compound and takes shelter under the grand entrance portico, shaking out her umbrella before stepping in. |
| 5 | 05_museum_ticket | At the quiet ticket counter, Maya buys her entry pass and asks the clerk whether the upstairs galleries are open right now. The clerk points her toward the main hall, and the hush of the building settles around her. |
| 6 | 06_ancient_exhibits | Wandering the dry, echoing halls, Maya studies displays of ancient pottery and coins. She reads the plaques, marveling at how many centuries ago these everyday items were used by people with days not so unlike hers. |
| 7 | 07_museum_cafe_chai | At the museum's small cafeteria, Maya orders a hot cutting chai and takes a window seat, watching the rain sweep across the courtyard. A fellow visitor at the next table remarks on the weather, and they trade a few easy words about the monsoon. |
| 8 | 08_evening_bus_ride | The rain clears into a breezy Sunday evening, and Maya boards a BEST bus home. Looking out at the wet, shining streets, she contrasts this settled feeling she has today with that overwhelming nervousness of her very first bus ride five months ago. |
