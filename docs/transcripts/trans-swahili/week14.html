Week 14: Maya takes public transport across the city

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

checking the route
Storyboard: Maya looks at a travel app on her phone. She needs to go across the city to pick up a package, so she plans a route with three different types of public transport.
Ninahitaji kuchukua kifurushi leo.I need to pick up a package today.
Kipo upande mwingine wa jiji.It is on the other side of the city.
Ninaangalia simu yangu.I look at my phone.
Ninafungua programu ya usafiri wa umma.I open a public transport app.
Ninapanga njia yangu.I plan my route.
Njia ni ndefu.The route is long.
Ina sehemu tatu.It has three parts.
Kwanza, ninahitaji kupanda basi.First, I need to take a bus.
Basi linaenda kwenye metro.The bus drives to the metro.
Kisha ninahitaji kupanda metro.Then I need to ride the metro.
Mwishowe, ninahitaji kwenda bandarini.Finally, I need to go to the harbour.
Hapa, ninahitaji kupanda basi la bandarini.Here, I need to take the harbour bus.
Basi la bandarini ni boti kubwa.The harbour bus is a large boat.
Linasafiri kwenye maji.It sails on the water.
Kifurushi kiko kwenye duka dogo.The package is in a small shop.
Duka liko karibu na maji.The shop is located close to the water.
Ni mpango mzuri.It is a good plan.
Ninachukua kadi yangu ya usafiri ya bluu.I take my blue travel card.
Niko tayari kwa safari.I am ready for the trip.
topping up the card
Storyboard: She walks to a nearby ticket machine. She taps her blue travel card on the screen and pays two hundred kroner to top up her balance for the long trip.
Ninatembea hadi kwenye mashine ya tiketi barabarani.I walk over to a ticket machine on the street.
Kwanza kadi yangu ya usafiri inahitaji pesa zaidi.First my travel card needs more money.
Ninatoa kadi yangu ya usafiri kutoka kwenye begi langu.I take my travel card out of my bag.
Ni kadi ndogo ya bluu.It is a small, blue card.
Ninaweka kadi kwenye skrini ya mashine.I place the card on the machine's screen.
Skrini inaonyesha salio langu.The screen shows my balance.
Salio ni dogo mno kwa safari ndefu.The balance is too low for a long trip.
Ninagusa kwenye skrini.I tap on the screen.
Ninachagua kiasi.I choose an amount.
Ninalipa kroner mia mbili kwa kadi yangu ya malipo.I pay two hundred kroner with my payment card.
Mashine inaandika "Imekubaliwa" kwenye skrini.The machine writes "Approved" on the screen.
Sasa nina pesa kwenye kadi.Now I have money on the card.
Ninarudisha kadi kwenye begi.I put the card back in the bag.
Niko tayari kwa safari ndefu.I am ready for the long trip.
Sasa ninahitaji kupanda basi.Now I need to take the bus.
Ninatembea kwenda kwenye kituo cha basi.I walk over to the bus stop.
taking the bus
Storyboard: Maya waits at the bus stop until a long yellow bus arrives. She steps inside, scans her card at the door, and finds an empty seat by the window.
Ninasimama kwenye kituo cha basi.I stand at the bus stop.
Hivi karibuni nitasafiri kwa basi.Soon I travel by bus.
Ninatazama mbele barabarani.I look down the street.
Baada ya dakika tano, basi linafika.After five minutes, the bus arrives.
Ni basi refu na la njano.It is a long and yellow bus.
Basi linasimama mbele yangu kabisa.The bus stops right in front of me.
Milango inafunguka.The doors open.
Ninapanda ndani ya basi.I step up into the bus.
Nina kadi yangu ya kusafiria mkononi.I have my travel card in my hand.
Ninaskani kadi mlangoni.I scan the card by the door.
Kuna abiria wengi leo.There are many passengers today.
Ninatafuta kiti kilicho wazi.I look for an empty seat.
Ninapata kiti kizuri karibu na dirisha.I find a good seat by the window.
Ninakaa chini.I sit down.
Ninatazama nje kuangalia jiji.I look out at the city.
Sasa ninachukua basi hadi kituo cha metro.Now I take the bus to the metro station.
asking a passenger
Storyboard: She turns to an older woman sitting nearby. Maya politely asks if she travels with this bus to the metro station, and the woman nods with a friendly smile.
Nimekaa kwenye basi.I am sitting in the bus.
Ninaangalia nje ya dirisha.I look out the window.
Sina uhakika kabisa kuhusu njia.I am not completely sure about the route.
Mwanamke mzee amekaa karibu nami.An older woman is sitting close to me.
Ninamtazama.I look over at her.
"Samahani," ninasema kwa heshima."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Je, basi hili linaenda kwenye kituo cha metro?""Does this bus go to the metro station?"
Mwanamke ananiangalia.The woman looks at me.
Anaitikia kwa kichwa na tabasamu la kirafiki.She nods with a friendly smile.
"Ndiyo, linaenda kwenye kituo cha metro," anajibu."Yes, it goes to the metro station," she replies.
"Je, utapanda metro?" mwanamke anauliza."Are you taking the metro?" the woman asks.
"Ndiyo, nitapanda metro," ninasema."Yes, I am taking the metro," I say.
"Mimi hupanda basi hili mara kwa mara," mwanamke anasema."I often ride with this bus," the woman says.
"Ni njia rahisi," anaongeza."It is an easy route," she adds.
"Asante sana kwa msaada," ninasema."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Ni safari yangu ya kwanza kwenye njia hii.""It is my first trip on this route."
Mwanamke anaitikia kwa kichwa tena.The woman nods again.
Ninapumzika kwenye kiti changu.I relax in my seat.
Tunaendelea na safari kupitia mjini.We drive on through the city.
transferring to metro
Storyboard: The bus stops, and Maya gets off at a busy square. She walks down the wide stairs into a bright, underground metro station and taps her card again.
Basi linasimama kwenye uwanja wenye shughuli nyingi.The bus stops at a busy square.
Ninashuka kwenye basi.I get off the bus.
Ninamuaga yule mwanamke mzee.I say goodbye to the older woman.
Sasa ninaenda kwenye metro.Now I am going to the metro.
Ninatembea kuvuka uwanja.I walk across the square.
Kuna watu wengi hapa.There are many people here.
Ninashuka kwenye ngazi pana.I walk down the wide stairs.
Kituo kiko chini ya ardhi.The station is underground.
Ni kikubwa sana na kina mwangaza.It is very big and bright.
Inabidi nijisajili tena.I have to check in again.
Ninatoa kadi yangu ya bluu.I take out my blue card.
Ninaskani kadi kwenye mashine.I scan the card on the machine.
Mashine inatoa mlio mkubwa.The machine makes a loud beep.
Sasa ninaweza kusafiri na metro.Now I can ride with the metro.
Ninatembea chini hadi kwenye jukwaa la treni.I walk down to the platform.
Ninapanda treni inayofuata.I am taking the next train.
Inaenda moja kwa moja kuelekea bandarini.It goes straight towards the harbour.
Treni inafika baada ya dakika tatu.The train arrives in three minutes.
riding the metro
Storyboard: Maya stands right at the front of the driverless train. She rides with the metro through the dark tunnels and watches the lights blur past as they speed forward.
Metro inafika, na milango inafunguka.The metro arrives, and the doors open.
Ninaingia ndani ya treni.I go into the train.
Treni inaenda bila dereva.The train drives without a driver.
Ninatembea mpaka mbele kabisa.I walk all the way to the front.
Ninasimama karibu na dirisha kubwa.I stand by the large window.
Ninasafiri kwa metro kupitia gizani.I ride with the metro through the dark.
Tupo ndani ya handaki refu.We are in a long tunnel.
Tunaenda kwa kasi sana.We drive very fast.
Mwanga unapita haraka dirishani.The light flies past the window.
Inaonekana ya kupendeza sana.It looks fantastic.
Mvulana mdogo amesimama kando yangu.A little boy stands next to me.
Yeye pia anatazama nje gizani.He also looks out into the dark.
Mara nyingi mimi hupanda metro.I often take the metro.
Ni safari ya haraka na rahisi.It is a fast and easy trip.
Sasa tunakaribia kituo changu.Now we are approaching my station.
Ninaenda bandarini.I am going to the harbour.
Safari yangu inayofuata ni kwa basi la bandarini.My next trip is with the harbour bus.
Ninatazamia kwa hamu kuyaona maji.I am looking forward to seeing the water.
finding the pier
Storyboard: She takes the escalator up to the street level and walks toward the water. She finds a small wooden pier by the edge of the canal where the harbour bus stops.
Safari ya metro imekwisha.The trip with the metro is over.
Sasa ninapanda juu barabarani.Now I am going up to the street.
Ninatumia ngazi za umeme.I take the escalator.
Jua linawaka huko juu barabarani.The sun is shining up on the street.
Sasa ninaenda kwenye maji.Now I am going to the water.
Ninaenda kwa basi la bandari.I am going by the harbour bus.
Ninatembea kwenye barabara pana.I walk down a wide street.
Kisha ninaona mfereji.Then I see the canal.
Maji ni ya bluu sana.The water is very blue.
Ninatafuta kituo changu.I look for my stop.
Boti inasimama wapi?Where does the boat stop?
Ninasogea karibu zaidi na ukingo.I walk closer to the edge.
Kisha ninaona gati dogo la mbao.Then I see a small wooden pier.
Liko hapo chini kabisa karibu na maji.It is located right down by the water.
Kuna kibao cha njano karibu na gati.There is a yellow sign by the pier.
Imeandikwa "Basi la bandari" kwenye kibao hicho.It says "Harbour bus" on the sign.
Hapa ndipo mahali sahihi.This is the right place.
Ninatembea mpaka juu ya gati.I walk out onto the pier.
Sasa ninasubiri tu boti.Now I am just waiting for the boat.
the harbour bus
Storyboard: A wide boat arrives, and Maya boards the harbour bus. She stands on the open deck and enjoys the fresh air as she sails across the blue water.
Boti pana inakuja ikivuka maji.A wide boat comes across the water.
Ni basi la bandarini.It is the harbour bus.
Boti inasafiri kuelekea kwenye gati la mbao.The boat sails in to the wooden pier.
Inasimama kwenye ukingo.It stops at the edge.
Ninaenda na basi la bandarini sasa.I am going with the harbour bus now.
Ninapanda kwenye meli.I step on board the ship.
Ninaskani kadi yangu ya bluu ya kusafiria tena.I scan my blue travel card again.
Abiria wengi wamekaa ndani.Many passengers sit inside.
Lakini ninatoka nje kwenye deki iliyo wazi.But I walk out onto the open deck.
Ninasimama mbele kabisa.I stand at the very front.
Tunasafiri na boti kuvuka maji ya bluu.We sail with the boat across the blue water.
Jiji linaonekana zuri kutoka kwenye maji.The city looks beautiful from the water.
Jua linatuangazia.The sun shines down on us.
Ninavuta pumzi ndefu.I take a deep breath.
Hewa ni safi sana na ya baridi.The air is very fresh and cold.
Ninafurahia safari fupi.I enjoy the short trip.
Muda si mrefu tutakuwa upande wa pili.We are soon over at the other side.
arriving for the errand
Storyboard: The boat docks at the opposite shore. Maya steps off the vessel and walks toward a small shop on the corner to finally collect her package.
Boti inafika upande wa pili wa maji.The boat arrives at the other side of the water.
Basi la bandarini linasimama kwenye gati dogo.The harbour bus stops at a small pier.
Ninashuka kwenye boti.I get off the boat.
Safari ya boti sasa imekwisha.The trip by boat is now over.
Ninaangalia ramani kwenye simu yangu.I check the map on my phone.
Ninaenda kwenye kioski kidogo.I am going to a small kiosk.
Kioski kiko pale kwenye kona.The kiosk is located over on a corner.
Ninatembea mtaani.I walk up the street.
Ninaona duka mwishoni mwa barabara.I see the shop at the end of the road.
Ninafungua mlango na kuingia ndani.I open the door and walk in.
Mwanamume kijana amesimama nyuma ya kaunta.A young man stands behind the register.
"Habari," anasema kwa fadhili."Hi," he says kindly.
"Habari, ninaenda kuchukua kifurushi," ninasema."Hi, I am going to pick up a package," I say.
Mwanamume huyo anaitikia kwa kichwa na kutabasamu.The man nods and smiles.
Anauliza jina langu.He asks for my name.
"Jina langu ni Maya," ninajibu."My name is Maya," I answer.
Anapata sanduku kubwa kwenye rafu.He finds a large box on a shelf.
Ananipa kifurushi hicho.He gives the package to me.
"Asante sana kwa msaada," ninasema."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
Sasa ninaanza safari ndefu ya kurudi nyumbani tena nikiwa na kifurushi changu.Now I take the long trip home again with my package.
