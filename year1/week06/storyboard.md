# Week 6 — Storyboard (Maya builds a daily rhythm)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Present-tense regular verbs; reflexives (vasker mig, sætter mig); V2 review (inversion after time/place); recurring wk 1-5 grammar · **Target:** ~30 min

The curriculum beat 'Building a daily rhythm' is realized as a 'day in the life' sequence. Walking through Maya's typical Tuesday is the perfect narrative engine for present-tense regular verbs (spiser, arbejder, tager) and V2 word order, as she naturally frames her routine with time and place expressions ('Om morgenen spiser jeg...', 'I metroen læser jeg...'). Reflexive verbs are seamlessly woven into the morning and commute ('vasker mig', 'skynder mig', 'sætter mig'). Continuity anchors the routine: she eats the rugbrød bought in week 5, goes to the software job she moved to Copenhagen for, wears her heavy winter coat, and interacts with her neighbour Nina and family dog Lola. The scenes move cleanly from waking to sleeping, providing a warm, grounded picture of her new settled life.

| # | stem | beat |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_alarm | Maya's phone alarm rings at seven o'clock. She opens her eyes and gets out of bed. It is still dark outside in the Danish winter. She goes to the bathroom, washes her face, and brushes her teeth. (Present tense regular verbs *vågner, står op*; reflexive *vasker mig*) |
| 2 | 02_coffee_and_rugbrod | In her kitchen, she makes coffee. She takes out the dark rye bread and cheese she bought at the supermarket. 'Every morning, I eat rye bread,' she thinks. She sits at the table and eats her breakfast. (V2 review with time/place: *I køkkenet laver jeg...*, *Om morgenen spiser jeg...*; continuity from wk5) |
| 3 | 03_the_metro_commute | Maya puts on her thick winter coat and locks her door. She walks to the Metro station quickly because it is cold. On the train, she finds a free seat, sits down, and listens to a podcast while the city goes by outside the window. (Reflexive verbs *skynder mig*, *sætter mig*; V2 *I metroen lytter jeg...*) |
| 4 | 04_the_software_office | Maya arrives at the tech company. She greets her colleagues, walks to her desk, and turns on her computer. She works as a software engineer, so she writes code and tests programs all morning. She focuses on her tasks. (Present verbs *ankommer, hilser, arbejder, skriver*; V2 *På arbejdet skriver jeg...*; no manufactured "likes her work" tag) |
| 5 | 05_canteen_lunch | At noon, Maya goes to the company canteen with her colleague Peter. They take plates of warm food and sit at a table by the window. They eat, talk, and laugh together about the dark winter weather. (V2 with time *Klokken tolv spiser vi...*; present plural *vi taler, vi griner*; the colleague is named Peter) |
| 6 | 06_home_from_work | At four, Maya finishes work and takes the Metro home. She hurries from the station to her flat and lets herself in, into the warmth. (a brief commute-home transition — no Nina; she isn't needed every week; clock *klokken seksten*; reflexive *skynder mig*; present *får fri, tager*) |
| 7 | 07_dinner_and_music | Inside her warm flat, Maya cooks dinner. She puts on some lively Mexican music while she chops vegetables and fries them in a pan. The smell of home fills her Danish kitchen. (Action-heavy present tense verbs *laver mad, lytter, skærer, steger*; V2 *Nu laver jeg mad*) |
| 8 | 08_calling_home | After dinner, Maya video calls her family in Mexico. Her sister Sofía answers, and Maya can see her brother Diego in the background. Then the family dog, Lola, barks at the screen. Maya smiles and tells them about her day. (Recurring cast; present tense verbs *ringer, taler, ser, smiler*) |
| 9 | 09_reading_in_bed | It is late. Maya makes a cup of tea, turns off the main lights, and gets into bed. She reads a book for half an hour. Her eyes get heavy, she puts down the book, and she sleeps. Her routine is set. (Present verbs *læser, sover*; peaceful close) |
