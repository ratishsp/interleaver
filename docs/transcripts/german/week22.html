Week 22: Maya cooks a Berlin dinner for her friends

planning the menu
Storyboard: After her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the café, Maya relaxes in the flat. She has invited her badminton friends, Lena and Timo, over for dinner this evening. She follows a cooking video by one loud, cheerful chef — the same man in every scene — who addresses his viewers formally. Wanting to cook something authentically local, she decides on Kartoffelsalat mit Bouletten. She finds an energetic cooking video on her phone by a local Berlin chef to guide her.
