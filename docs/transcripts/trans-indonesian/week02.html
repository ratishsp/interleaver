morning commute
Storyboard: Maya walks briskly through the chilly morning air, looking for her new workplace. She checks the large numbers on the buildings along the street until she spots the software company's logo next to number forty-four.

Dan ini nomor empat puluh.And here is number forty.
Aku berjalan sedikit lebih jauh.I walk a little further.
Ini nomor empat puluh dua.Here is number forty-two.
Dan itu dia!And there it is!
Ini nomor empat puluh empat.Here is number forty-four.
Aku melihat sebuah logo besar.I see a large logo.

Aku punya perasaan yang baik.I have a good feeling.
Aku benar-benar siap.I am completely ready.
meeting peter
Storyboard: Inside the bright office, a smiling colleague introduces himself as Peter. He shakes her hand, hands her a new laptop, and leads her through the open-plan workspace to her assigned desk.

Ini adalah ruangan yang besar dan terang.It is a large and bright room.
Seorang pria yang ramah berdiri di dekat pintu.A friendly man stands by the door.

"Nama saya Peter.""My name is Peter."

Saya melihat tempat baru saya.I look at my new spot.
Saya memiliki sebuah kursi dan sebuah monitor besar.I have a chair and a large monitor.
"Terima kasih banyak, Peter," kata saya."Thank you very much, Peter," I say.
Saya siap untuk hari kerja pertama saya.I am ready for my first workday.
the phone shop
Storyboard: On her lunch break, Maya ducks into a telecom shop to buy a local SIM card. She hands the clerk her phone, and he activates it, reading her brand-new local number aloud as she saves it.
Ini jam istirahat makan siangku.It is my lunch break.
Aku sedang berada di sebuah toko ponsel.I am in a phone shop.
Aku punya ponsel dari Meksiko.I have a phone from Mexico.
Pegawai toko itu adalah seorang pria muda.The clerk is a young man.
"Apa Anda punya kartu SIM Denmark?" tanyaku."Do you have a Danish SIM card?" I ask.
"Ya, tentu saja," jawabnya."Yes, of course," he answers.
Aku memberikan ponselku kepadanya.I give him my phone.
Dia memasukkan kartu SIM itu ke dalam ponsel.He puts the SIM card in the phone.
Ponselnya sudah siap sekarang.The phone is ready now.
"Nomor baru Anda adalah dua belas, tiga puluh empat, lima puluh enam, tujuh puluh delapan," katanya."Your new number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," he says.
Aku punya buku catatan di dalam tasku.I have a notebook in my bag.
Aku menulis nomor itu.I write the number.
"Dua belas, tiga puluh empat, lima puluh enam, tujuh puluh delapan," ulangku."Twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I repeat.
"Ya, tepat sekali," kata pegawai toko itu."Yes, exactly," says the clerk.
"Terima kasih atas bantuannya," kataku."Thank you for the help," I say.
Aku punya nomor Denmark sekarang!I have a Danish number now!
Aku memasukkan ponsel itu ke dalam sakuku.I put the phone in my pocket.
queue ticket

registration
Storyboard: The screen chimes with her number, and Maya steps up to the counter. She volunteers her name and age to the clerk, hands over her passport, and he processes the paperwork for her private national ID number.
Sebuah layar menampilkan nomor saya.A screen shows my number.
Nomornya adalah lima puluh sembilan.The number is fifty-nine.
Saya berjalan ke loket.I walk up to the counter.
Seorang pria duduk di belakang loket.A man sits behind the counter.
"Selamat pagi," katanya."Good morning," he says.
"Selamat pagi," jawab saya."Good morning," I reply.
"Nama saya Maya, dan saya berumur tiga puluh satu tahun," kata saya."My name is Maya, and I am thirty-one years old," I say.
"Selamat datang, Maya," katanya."Welcome, Maya," he says.
"Apakah Anda membawa paspor Anda?" tanyanya."Do you have your passport?" he asks.
"Ya, saya membawa paspor dan dokumen-dokumen saya," jawab saya."Yes, I have my passport and my papers," I answer.
"Ini dia," kata saya."Here you go," I say.
Dia melihat dokumen-dokumen itu.He looks at the papers.
Dia mengetik di komputernya.He types on his computer.
Saya mendapatkan nomor identitas pribadi Denmark.I get a Danish personal identification number.
Nomor itu bersifat pribadi dan sangat penting.The number is private and very important.
Dia memberi saya kartu baru.He gives me a new card.
"Ini dia, ini kartu Anda," katanya."Here you go, here is your card," he says.
"Sekarang Anda sudah terdaftar di Denmark.""Now you are registered in Denmark."
Saya tersenyum dan mengambil kartu itu.I smile and take the card.
Sekarang saya adalah bagian dari Denmark.Now I am a part of Denmark.
bakery celebration
