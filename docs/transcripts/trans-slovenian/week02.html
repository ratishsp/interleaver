Week 2: First Day & Registration

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning commute
Storyboard: Maya walks briskly through the chilly morning air, looking for her new workplace. She checks the large numbers on the buildings along the street until she spots the software company's logo next to number forty-four.
Jutro je v Kopenhagnu.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Januar je in zelo je mrzlo.It is January, and it is very cold.
Malo sem živčna.I am a little nervous.
Danes je velik dan.Today is a big day.
To je nov začetek zame.It is a new start for me.
To je moj prvi dan v novi službi.It is my first day at my new job.
V službo grem z avtobusom.I take the bus to work.
Izstopim na pravi postaji.I get off at the right stop.
Zdaj grem zadnji del peš.Now I walk the last part.
Kje je pisarna?Where is the office?
Gledam velike stavbe.I look at the large buildings.
Vsaka stavba ima številko.Each building has a number.
Tukaj je številka osemintrideset.Here is number thirty-eight.
In tukaj je številka štirideset.And here is number forty.
Grem še malo naprej.I walk a little further.
Tukaj je številka dvainštirideset.Here is number forty-two.
In tam je!And there it is!
Tukaj je številka štiriinštirideset.Here is number forty-four.
Vidim velik logotip.I see a large logo.
Moje novo podjetje za programsko opremo je prav tukaj.My new software company is right here.
Imam dober občutek.I have a good feeling.
Popolnoma sem pripravljena.I am completely ready.
meeting peter
Storyboard: Inside the bright office, a smiling colleague introduces himself as Peter. He shakes her hand, hands her a new laptop, and leads her through the open-plan workspace to her assigned desk.
Zdaj sem v novi pisarni.I am now inside the new office.
To je velik in svetel prostor.It is a large and bright room.
Pri vratih stoji prijazen moški.A friendly man stands by the door.
Nasmehne se mi.He smiles at me.
"Dobrodošla!" reče."Welcome!" he says.
"Ime mi je Peter.""My name is Peter."
Segnem mu v roko.I shake his hand.
"Živijo, Peter, ime mi je Maya," rečem."Hi Peter, my name is Maya," I say.
Peter je sodelavec.Peter is a colleague.
Skupaj greva skozi pisarno.We walk together through the office.
"Tukaj imaš pisalno mizo," reče Peter."You have a desk here," says Peter.
Pisalna miza ima številko šestnajst.The desk has number sixteen.
"In imaš računalnik," reče."And you have a computer," he says.
Da mi nov prenosni računalnik.He gives me a new laptop.
Pogledam svoje novo delovno mesto.I look at my new spot.
Imam stol in velik zaslon.I have a chair and a large monitor.
"Najlepša hvala, Peter," rečem."Thank you very much, Peter," I say.
Pripravljena sem na svoj prvi delovni dan.I am ready for my first workday.
the phone shop
Storyboard: On her lunch break, Maya ducks into a telecom shop to buy a local SIM card. She hands the clerk her phone, and he activates it, reading her brand-new local number aloud as she saves it.
To je moj odmor za kosilo.It is my lunch break.
Sem v trgovini s telefoni.I am in a phone shop.
Imam telefon iz Mehike.I have a phone from Mexico.
Prodajalec je mlad moški.The clerk is a young man.
"Imate dansko SIM-kartico?" vprašam."Do you have a Danish SIM card?" I ask.
"Ja, seveda," odgovori."Yes, of course," he answers.
Dam mu svoj telefon.I give him my phone.
Vstavi SIM-kartico v telefon.He puts the SIM card in the phone.
Telefon je zdaj pripravljen.The phone is ready now.
"Vaša nova številka je dvanajst, štiriintrideset, šestinpetdeset, oseminsedemdeset," reče."Your new number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," he says.
V torbi imam beležnico.I have a notebook in my bag.
Zapišem številko.I write the number.
"Dvanajst, štiriintrideset, šestinpetdeset, oseminsedemdeset," ponovim."Twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I repeat.
"Ja, točno tako," reče prodajalec."Yes, exactly," says the clerk.
"Hvala za pomoč," rečem."Thank you for the help," I say.
Zdaj imam dansko številko!I have a Danish number now!
Telefon dam v žep.I put the phone in my pocket.
queue ticket
Storyboard: The next morning, Maya enters the crowded public registration office. She presses a button on a red terminal, pulls a paper ticket, and checks her high number before finding a seat to wait.
Naslednje jutro je.It is the next morning.
Danes sem na velikem prijavnem uradu.I am at the large registration office today.
Tukaj noter je veliko ljudi.There are many people in here.
Svoj potni list in papirje imam v torbi.I have my passport and my papers in my bag.
Vse je pripravljeno za mojo prijavo.Everything is ready for my registration.
Stojim pri rdečem avtomatu.I stand by a red machine.
Pritisnem na gumb.I press a button.
Avtomat mi da majhen listek s številko.The machine gives me a small number slip.
Katera je moja številka?What is my number?
Pogledam papir.I look at the paper.
Imam številko devetinpetdeset.I have number fifty-nine.
To je precej visoka številka.It is a rather high number.
Velik zaslon na steni kaže številko.A large screen on the wall shows a number.
Zaslon kaže samo številko enaindvajset.The screen only shows number twenty-one.
Imam veliko časa.I have plenty of time.
Najdem prost stol.I find an empty chair.
Usedem se s svojimi papirji.I sit down with my papers.
Zdaj čakam na svojo vrsto.Now I wait for my turn.
registration
Storyboard: The screen chimes with her number, and Maya steps up to the counter. She volunteers her name and age to the clerk, hands over her passport, and he processes the paperwork for her private national ID number.
Zaslon prikazuje mojo številko.A screen shows my number.
Številka je devetinpetdeset.The number is fifty-nine.
Stopim k okencu.I walk up to the counter.
Za okencem sedi moški.A man sits behind the counter.
"Dobro jutro," reče."Good morning," he says.
"Dobro jutro," odgovorim."Good morning," I reply.
"Ime mi je Maya in stara sem enaintrideset let," rečem."My name is Maya, and I am thirty-one years old," I say.
"Dobrodošla, Maya," reče."Welcome, Maya," he says.
"Imaš svoj potni list?" vpraša."Do you have your passport?" he asks.
"Da, imam svoj potni list in papirje," odgovorim."Yes, I have my passport and my papers," I answer.
"Izvoli," rečem."Here you go," I say.
Pogleda papirje.He looks at the papers.
Tipka po svojem računalniku.He types on his computer.
Dobim dansko osebno identifikacijsko številko.I get a Danish personal identification number.
Številka je zasebna in zelo pomembna.The number is private and very important.
Da mi novo kartico.He gives me a new card.
"Izvoli, tukaj je tvoja kartica," reče."Here you go, here is your card," he says.
"Zdaj si registrirana na Danskem.""Now you are registered in Denmark."
Nasmehnem se in vzamem kartico.I smile and take the card.
Zdaj sem del Danske.Now I am a part of Denmark.
bakery celebration
Storyboard: Heading home from the registration office, Maya stops at a neighbourhood bakery to celebrate being officially registered at last. She points to a cinnamon pastry in the glass display, asks the price, and taps her card to pay.
Spet sem zunaj na ulici.I am out on the street again.
Vse je končno na svojem mestu.Everything is finally in place.
Čas je za majhno praznovanje.It is time for a little celebration.
Na vogalu vidim majhno pekarno.I see a little bakery on the corner.
Vstopim.I go inside.
Čudovito diši.It smells lovely.
Za pultom stoji pek.A baker stands behind the counter.
"Živijo," reče pek."Hello," the baker says.
"Živijo," odgovorim."Hello," I reply.
Pokažem na pecivo za steklom.I point to a pastry behind the glass.
"Rada bi cimetovega polžka," rečem."I would like a cinnamon pastry," I say.
"Ja, seveda," reče."Yes, of course," he says.
"Koliko stane?" vprašam."What does it cost?" I ask.
"Stane petindvajset kron," odgovori pek."It costs twenty-five kroner," the baker replies.
Denarnico imam v torbi.I have my wallet in my bag.
Vzamem svojo plačilno kartico.I take out my debit card.
Plačam za svojega cimetovega polžka.I pay for my cinnamon pastry.
Pek mi da majhno vrečko.The baker gives me a small bag.
Zdaj imam cimetovega polžka za svoje majhno praznovanje.Now I have a cinnamon pastry for my little celebration.
swapping numbers
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her apartment building with her pastry bag and bumps into her neighbour Nina by the front door. They chat warmly and swap phone numbers so they can stay in touch; Nina reads out hers for Maya to save.
Stojim ob svojem stanovanju.I am standing by my apartment.
Imam vrečko s cimetovim polžkom.I have a bag with a cinnamon pastry.
Moja soseda Nina je pri vratih.My neighbour Nina is at the door.
Široko se nasmehne in reče živijo.She smiles broadly and says hello.
"Živijo, Maya, imaš zdaj dansko številko?" vpraša Nina."Hello Maya, do you have a Danish number now?" Nina asks.
"Ja, imam novo telefonsko številko," odgovorim."Yes, I have a new phone number," I reply.
"To je dobro," reče."That is good," she says.
Svoj telefon imam v roki.I have my phone in my hand.
"Katera je tvoja številka?" vprašam."What is your number?" I ask.
"Moja številka je sedeminosemdeset, petinšestdeset, triinštirideset, enaindvajset," reče Nina."My number is eighty-seven, sixty-five, forty-three, twenty-one," Nina says.
Njeno številko vpišem v svoj telefon.I write her number on my phone.
"In moja številka je dvanajst, štiriintrideset, šestinpetdeset, oseminsedemdeset," rečem."And my number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
Nina shrani mojo številko v svoj telefon.Nina saves my number on her phone.
Zdaj sva v stiku.Now we are in touch.
Skupaj greva notri.We go inside together.
Zelo sem vesela svoje nove prijateljice.I am very happy about my new friend.
