final sweep
Storyboard: Maya stands in her temporary room in the building. She zips up her large suitcases and checks the space one last time, making sure nothing lies under the small bed or stands forgotten in the closet.

我站在我的临时房间里。I am standing in my temporary room.

它在同一栋楼里。It is in the same building.

我拿出这双鞋。I take the shoes.
现在衣柜里什么都没有了。Now there is nothing in the closet.
我走到窗边的书桌前。I walk over to the desk by the window.

我拿上我的两个包。I take my two bags.
我关掉灯，关上门。I turn off the light and close the door.

ninas help
Storyboard: Maya steps back out to the shared stairwell to grab her heaviest suitcase. Nina comes up the stairs, spots her struggling, and cheerfully helps carry the bag inside, setting it carefully on the floor by the large living room window.

我最大的行李箱放在地板上。My biggest suitcase stands on the floor.
它非常重。It is very heavy.
我拉了拉行李箱。I pull on the suitcase.
突然，我听见楼梯上传来脚步声。Suddenly I hear footsteps on the stairs.
是我的丹麦邻居妮娜。It is my Danish neighbor, Nina.
她热情地对我微笑。She smiles warmly at me.
“你今天搬进来吗，玛雅？”她问。"Are you moving in today, Maya?" she asks.
“是的，我终于来到了我的新公寓，”我说。"Yes, I am finally in my new apartment," I say.
“我来帮你，”妮娜友好地说。"I will help you," Nina says kindly.

妮娜小心翼翼地把它放在地板上。Nina sets it carefully on the floor.
现在行李箱就放在大窗户旁边。Now the suitcase stands by the large window.
窗户下面已经放着一个包。There already lies a bag under the window.
“非常感谢你的帮忙，妮娜，”我高兴地说。"Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say happily.
“不客气，”她微笑着说。"You are welcome," she smiles.
