# Week 2 — Storyboard (First day and getting connected)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Present habitual **-ता/-ती हूँ** (मैं काम करती हूँ); numbers 0–100 as words; possession **मेरे पास … है** (I have) and its negation **नहीं**; age — **मैं … साल की हूँ**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_prep | Maya gets ready for her first day at the software company. She packs her bag, verifying what she has: she notes that she has her laptop and her ID documents, but realizes she still does not have a local phone number. |
| 2 | 02_meeting_rohan | Maya arrives at the bright, bustling office. A friendly colleague introduces himself as Rohan; following Indian IT culture, they use first names. He asks what her specific team is, and she explains that she works on the mobile app development team. |
| 3 | 03_the_desk | Rohan shows Maya to her new desk. She inspects the workspace, noting happily that she has a large monitor and a comfortable chair, and settles in to start her daily routine. |
| 4 | 04_mobile_shop | During her lunch break, Maya visits a nearby mobile shop to buy a SIM card. She addresses the shopkeeper respectfully as 'bhaiya' and explains that she currently does not have an Indian SIM. |
| 5 | 05_sim_form | The shopkeeper fills out the registration form on his tablet and asks for her personal details. Maya tells him that she is from Mexico and states her age, confirming she is thirty-one years old. |
| 6 | 06_memorizing_number | The shopkeeper hands over the new SIM card. Maya carefully reads her new phone number aloud to herself—98765 43210—to make sure she remembers it. |
| 7 | 07_frro_registration | The next day, Maya visits the FRRO to complete her mandatory expat registration. She addresses the official formally as 'sir', hands over her documents, and confidently provides her newly acquired phone number. |
| 8 | 08_celebratory_snack | With her errands successfully finished, Maya stops at a busy street-side stall. She orders a cutting chai and a vada pav from the vendor, enjoying the hot, spicy food as she celebrates finally being connected in Mumbai. |
