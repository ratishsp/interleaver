# Week 10 — Storyboard (Maya buys winter clothes)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Adjective agreement (en/et/plural); har på (wearing); sizes, colours, and prices as words · **Target:** ~18 min

A biting cold snap forces Maya to upgrade her wardrobe from thin layers to true Scandinavian winter gear. The sequence moves naturally through a single shopping trip: realizing the need, asking for help, using the fitting room, browsing accessories, trying on footwear, and checking out. The grammar (adjective agreement: en/et/plural, and the verb 'har på') drives the action of choosing, sizing, and assessing warmth. The sequence is kept strictly in the present tense (A1 constraint) and features just Maya and a helpful shop assistant. Prices and sizes are written as Danish words.

| # | stem | beat |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_cold_morning | Maya stands in her apartment and looks out the window at the frost. She is wearing three of her thin sweaters from Mexico, but she is still freezing. She decides she urgently needs real winter clothes today. (Grammar: 'har på' for wearing clothes; thin/cold adjectives: tynde trøjer, et koldt værelse) |
| 2 | 02_asking_for_help | Maya enters a large clothing shop in the city and shakes off the cold. She approaches a friendly shop assistant and asks for help finding a thick sweater and a heavy winter jacket. (Grammar: en/et agreement: en varm jakke, en tyk trøje, et stort udvalg) |
| 3 | 03_the_fitting_room | Maya stands in the fitting room in front of a tall mirror. She tries on a blue jacket, but it is too small. She opens the curtain and asks the assistant for a large size. The assistant brings a green jacket that fits perfectly. (Grammar: et stort spejl, en lille jakke, en stor jakke, farver: grøn/blå) |
| 4 | 04_winter_accessories | Leaving the fitting room, Maya browses the accessory tables. She needs to protect her ears and hands. She picks out a soft woolen scarf, a red beanie, and thick black gloves. (Grammar: et blødt halstørklæde, en rød hue, plural agreement: tykke sorte handsker) |
| 5 | 05_warm_boots | Maya sits on a bench in the shoe department. She asks the assistant for brown winter boots in size thirty-nine. She tries them on—they are heavy but incredibly warm. She also grabs two pairs of thick woolen socks. (Grammar: plural agreement: varme brune støvler, tunge sko, tykke sokker; numbers as words: størrelse niogtredive) |
| 6 | 06_paying_at_the_register | Maya brings her pile of clothes to the checkout. The assistant scans the items and announces the total: one thousand five hundred kroner. Maya pays, removes her old thin sweater, and puts the new thick jacket and scarf on immediately. She steps outside, finally warm. (Grammar: et dyrt køb, tager på/har på; price as words: tusind fem hundrede kroner) |
