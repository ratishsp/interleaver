Week 9: Maya visits the winter market

Mandarin with English gloss, line-aligned.

arriving at the market
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a busy outdoor market in a large square. The winter morning is cold, but the stalls look inviting. She carries a large empty bag and looks for fresh food for the weekend.
现在是星期六早上。It is Saturday morning.
丹麦现在是冬天。It is winter in Denmark.
今天天气非常冷。The weather is very cold today.
我正站在城里的一个大广场上。I am standing in a large square in the city.
这里有一个露天市场。There is an outdoor market here.
我看着许多摊位。I look at the many stalls.
它们看起来都很吸引人。They all look inviting.
广场上有很多人。There are many people in the square.
人们穿着保暖的冬装。People are wearing warm winter clothes.
我只穿了一件薄外套。I only have a thin jacket on.
我随身带了一个大包。I have a large bag with me.
包现在完全是空的。The bag is completely empty right now.
我正在为周末寻找新鲜的食物。I am looking for fresh food for the weekend.
市场里有许多新鲜的商品。The market has many fresh goods.
我看到了蔬菜、水果和面包。I see vegetables, fruit, and bread.
我心里盘算着价格。I think about the prices.
这里的东西要多少钱？How much do things cost here?
我走向第一个摊位。I walk over to the first stall.
carrots for soup
Storyboard: Maya stops at a vegetable stall to buy ingredients for a hot soup. She points at some winter carrots and asks how much they cost. The vendor says they cost fifteen kroner per kilo. Maya asks for one kilo and pays.
我走向一个蔬菜摊。I walk over to a vegetable stall.
我今天要做热汤。I am making a hot soup today.
我看着桌上的许多蔬菜。I look at the many vegetables on the table.
我指着一些大个的冬胡萝卜。I point at some large winter carrots.
我做汤需要胡萝卜。I need carrots for my soup.
桌子后面站着一位摊主。There is a vendor standing behind the table.
“你好，”他说。"Hello," he says.
“你好，”我回答。"Hello," I reply.
“胡萝卜多少钱？”我问。"How much do the carrots cost?" I ask.
“每公斤十五克朗，”摊主回答。"They cost fifteen kroner per kilo," the vendor answers.
十五克朗是个好价钱。Fifteen kroner is a good price.
“请给我来一公斤，”我说。"I will take one kilo, please," I say.
摊主给胡萝卜称重。The vendor weighs the carrots.
他把它们装进一个纸袋里。He puts them in a paper bag.
他把袋子递给我。He gives me the bag.
我付了那十五克朗。I pay the fifteen kroner.
我把袋子放进我的大包里。I put the bag down in my large bag.
我很期待那热汤。I look forward to the hot soup.
a taste of cheese
Storyboard: At the next stall, a friendly vendor sells large cheeses. He offers Maya a small piece on a wooden stick to taste. She eats it, likes it, and asks for the price of a small block. It costs fifty-five kroner, so she buys it.
我面前有一个大奶酪摊。In front of me is a large cheese stall.
摊位上有很多大块的奶酪。The stall has many large cheeses.
一位友善的摊贩对我微笑。A friendly vendor smiles at me.
他手里的木签上插着一小块奶酪。He has a small piece of cheese on a wooden stick.
“给你，尝一小块吧，”他说。"Here you go, a small taste," he says.
他把木签递给我。He gives the stick to me.
我吃下那块黄色的奶酪。I eat the yellow cheese.
味道非常好。It tastes really good.
我看着一块小奶酪。I look at a small cheese.
“这块奶酪多少钱？”我问。"How much does this cheese cost?" I ask.
“五十五克朗，”摊贩回答。"It costs fifty-five kroner," the vendor answers.
这个价格很不错。That is a good price.
我指着那块小奶酪。I point at the small cheese.
“我买了，”我说。"I will buy it," I say.
他用纸把奶酪包起来。He wraps the cheese in paper.
我把钱递给他。I give him the money.
“非常感谢，”他说。"Thank you very much," he says.
奶酪闻起来又浓又香。The cheese smells strong and good.
the expensive gloves
Storyboard: The wind blows cold, so Maya looks at a stall with warm clothes. She picks up a pair of thick wool gloves and asks how much the gloves cost. The vendor answers that the price is two hundred kroner. Maya thinks that is too dear, so she politely says no thank you and walks on.
今天风刮得很冷。The wind blows cold today.
我的手非常冷。My hands are very cold.
我看到一个卖保暖衣服的大摊位。I see a large stall with warm clothes.
我走向摊主。I walk over to the vendor.
他桌上有许多保暖的东西。He has many warm things on the table.
我拿起一双厚厚的羊毛手套。I pick up a pair of thick wool gloves.
它们非常柔软。They are very soft.
“这副手套多少钱？”我问。"How much do the gloves cost?" I ask.
“两百克朗，”摊主回答。"They cost two hundred kroner," the vendor answers.
我又看了看手套。I look at the gloves again.
两百克朗是个很高的价格。Two hundred kroner is a high price.
这对我来说太贵了。It is too expensive for me.
我把手套放回桌子上。I put the gloves back on the table.
“不用了，谢谢，”我礼貌地说。"No thank you," I say politely.
“祝你今天愉快。”"Have a good day."
摊主点头微笑。The vendor nods and smiles.
“你也是，”他说。"You too," he says.
我继续在市场里逛。I walk on around the market.
buying winter apples
Storyboard: Maya finds a fruit stall with baskets of dark red winter apples. She asks the vendor how much a small bag costs. The vendor smiles and says the price is twenty kroner. Maya hands him a paper note for fifty kroner. The vendor hands her the bag of apples and gives her exactly thirty kroner back in coins. Maya puts the heavy coins into her coat pocket.
今天市场上人很多。There are many people at the market today.
我找到一个小水果摊。I find a small fruit stall.
摊位上有很多篮子。There are many baskets in the stall.
篮子里装满了深红色的冬苹果。The baskets are full of dark red winter apples.
我看着一小袋苹果。I look at a small bag of apples.
“一小袋多少钱？”我问摊主。"How much does a small bag cost?" I ask the vendor.
他往篮子里看了看。He looks in the basket.
“价格是二十克朗，”他回答。"The price is twenty kroner," he answers.
我在包里找钱。I look in my bag for money.
我给了他一张五十克朗的纸币。I give him a fifty-krone note.
摊主把那袋苹果递给我。The vendor hands me the bag of apples.
然后他找钱给我。Then he gives me money back.
我找回了正好三十克朗的硬币。I get exactly thirty kroner back in coins.
硬币又厚又重。The coins are thick and heavy.
我把沉重的硬币放进我的大衣口袋里。I put the heavy coins down into my coat pocket.
苹果闻起来很新鲜。The apples smell very fresh.
我把袋子放进我的大购物袋里。I put the bag down into my large shopping bag.
fresh rye bread
Storyboard: Maya smells baked goods and walks over to a baker's stall. She points to a fresh, dark rye bread and asks how much it costs. The baker tells her it costs thirty-five kroner. She pays with her card and places the warm bread in her bag.
我走过一个小摊。I walk past a small stall.
闻起来有新鲜面包的香味。It smells of fresh bread.
我走到摊位前。I walk over to the stall.
面包师站在一张大桌子后面。The baker is standing behind a large table.
桌上摆满了烘焙食品。The table is full of baked goods.
我看到一个深色的黑麦面包。I see a dark rye bread.
它看起来非常新鲜。It looks very fresh.
我指着那个黑麦面包。I point at the rye bread.
“这个深色黑麦面包多少钱？”我问。"How much does the dark rye bread cost?" I ask.
面包师擦了擦手。The baker wipes his hands.
“三十五克朗，”他回答。"It costs thirty-five kroner," he replies.
“我买了，”我说。"I am buying it," I say.
我拿出我的银行卡。I take out my payment card.
我用卡付款。I pay with the card.
面包师把装在纸袋里的面包递给我。The baker gives me the bread in a paper bag.
我摸了摸袋子。I touch the bag.
面包还是热的。The bread is still warm.
我很期待吃它。I look forward to eating it.
exact change for coffee
Storyboard: Maya feels cold and spots a small coffee cart. She approaches and asks the barista how much a hot black coffee costs. The barista replies that it costs thirty kroner. Maya takes the new coins from her coat pocket and pays for the coffee with exact change.
我现在非常冷。I am very cold now.
我的手很冷。My hands are cold.
我在市场里四处看看。I look around the market.
我看到一辆小咖啡推车。I see a small coffee cart.
一个年轻男子站在推车后面。A young man stands behind the cart.
我朝他走过去。I walk over to him.
我向咖啡师问好。I say hello to the barista.
“你好，一杯黑咖啡多少钱？”我问。"Hello, how much does a black coffee cost?" I ask.
咖啡师笑了笑。The barista smiles.
“一杯热黑咖啡三十克朗，”他回答。"A hot, black coffee costs thirty kroner," he answers.
我的大衣口袋里有硬币。I have coins in my coat pocket.
这是我买苹果找回的零钱。It is my change from the apples.
我把硬币拿出来。I take the coins out.
我数了数。I count them.
我刚好有三十克朗。I have exactly thirty kroner.
“给你，这是三十克朗，”我说。"Here you go, here are thirty kroner," I say.
他接过钱。He takes the money.
他把热咖啡递给我。He gives me the hot coffee.
咖啡闻起来香极了。The coffee smells wonderful.
我用杯子暖手。I warm my hands on the cup.
winter flowers
Storyboard: Maya walks toward the market exit and stops at a flower stall. She points at a bundle of bright winter tulips and asks for the price. The vendor says they cost forty kroner. Maya buys the flowers and walks back to her apartment, drinking her hot coffee and holding the colorful tulips.
我朝市场的出口走去。I walk towards the exit of the market.
我看到一个很大的花摊。I see a large flower stall.
摊位上有许多漂亮的花。The stall has many beautiful flowers.
我停在摊位前。I stop in front of the stall.
我看着一束冬季郁金香。I look at a bundle of winter tulips.
它们色彩非常鲜艳。They are very colourful.
我指了指那些郁金香。I point at the tulips.
“郁金香多少钱？”我问摊主。"How much do the tulips cost?" I ask the vendor.
摊主举起郁金香。The vendor holds up the tulips.
“四十克朗，”他回答。"They cost forty kroner," he answers.
“我买了，”我说。"I will buy them," I say.
我为这些花付了钱。I pay for the flowers.
摊主把郁金香递给我。The vendor gives me the tulips.
现在我逛完市场了。Now I am finished at the market.
我走回我的公寓。I walk back to my apartment.
我手里拿着那些色彩鲜艳的郁金香。I hold the colourful tulips in my hand.
我喝着我的热咖啡。I drink my hot coffee.
阳光照耀着熙熙攘攘的市场。The sun shines over the busy market.
