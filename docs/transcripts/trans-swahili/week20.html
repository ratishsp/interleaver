Week 20: Maya starts volunteering at the café

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding the volunteering
Storyboard: Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. One evening she browses a volunteering website, reads a few listings, and finds the little café she already knows, which needs weekend helpers, so she fills in the short sign-up form.
Nimesikia kwamba Wadenmark wengi hufanya kazi za kujitolea katika muda wao wa ziada.I have heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time.
Ninapenda wazo hilo.I like the idea.
Nataka kujifunza kuongea Kidenmark vizuri zaidi na kukutana na watu wapya.I want to learn to speak better Danish and meet new people.
Jioni moja, nimekaa na kompyuta yangu.One evening, I am sitting with my computer.
Ninapata tovuti ya kazi za kujitolea.I find a website for voluntary work.
Ninasoma matangazo matatu ya kwanza kwenye tovuti.I read the first three listings on the site.
Tangazo la kwanza linatoka kwa klabu ya michezo.The first listing is from a sports club.
Lakini wanahitaji msaada Alhamisi jioni, na mimi hucheza badminton wakati huo.But they need help on Thursday evenings, and I play badminton then.
Tangazo la pili linatoka kwenye makumbusho.The second listing is from a museum.
Wanahitaji mtu Jumatano jioni, lakini mimi hufanya mazoezi wakati huo.They need a person on Wednesday evenings, but I work out then.
Kisha ninaangalia tangazo la tatu.Then I look at the third listing.
Linatoka kwenye mkahawa mdogo ambao tayari ninaufahamu.It is from the little café that I already know.
Wanahitaji watu Jumamosi asubuhi.They need people for Saturday mornings.
Hiyo inanifaa kabisa.That suits me absolutely perfectly.
Ninajaza fomu fupi nikiweka jina langu na anwani yangu ya barua pepe.I fill in a short form with my name and my email address.
Pia ninaandika nambari yangu ya simu: kumi na mbili, thelathini na nne, hamsini na sita, sabini na nane.I also write my phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight.
Mwishowe, ninabonyeza tuma.Finally, I press send.
Sijawahi kufanya kazi kwenye mkahawa hapo awali, lakini niko tayari kujifunza.I have never worked in a café before, but I am ready to learn it.
first shift arrival
Storyboard: On her very first Saturday shift, Maya arrives early at the little café. She ties her new apron in the back room and carries the chairs out to the pavement to prepare for opening.
Ni zamu yangu ya kwanza ya Jumamosi kwenye mkahawa mdogo.It is my first Saturday shift at the little café.
Nimefika mapema.I have arrived early.
Jua linawaka mtaani Kopenhagen.The sun is shining on the street in Copenhagen.
Ninasubiri kwa hamu, lakini pia nina wasiwasi kidogo.I am looking forward to it, but I am also a little nervous.
Ni mara yangu ya kwanza kufanya kazi kwenye mkahawa.It is the first time I am working in a café.
Niliingia chumba cha nyuma ili kujiandaa.I went into the back room to get ready.
Kwenye meza kulikuwa na eproni mpya kwa ajili yangu.On a table lay a new apron for me.
Niliivaa na kuifunga kwa nyuma.I put it on and tied it in the back.
Nilijisikia vizuri kuivaa.It felt good to wear it.
Sasa niko tayari kuanza.Now I am ready to begin.
Inabidi nijifunze kufungua mkahawa kwa usahihi.I have to learn to open the café correctly.
Kwanza, meneja alifungua mlango mzito wa mbele.First, the manager unlocked the heavy front door.
Baadaye, nilitoka nje kwenye njia ya waenda kwa miguu kufanya maandalizi.Afterwards, I went out onto the pavement to get ready.
Nilibeba viti vinne nje.I carried four chairs out.
Nimeviweka kuzunguka meza ndogo.I have placed them around a little table.
Baada ya muda mfupi, watu wengine wa kujitolea watafika.In a little while, the other volunteers will arrive.
Nina msisimko mkubwa kuhusu siku yangu ya kwanza.I am very excited about my first day.
learning the machine
Storyboard: Before the doors open, an experienced volunteer brings Maya over to the large espresso machine. The volunteer shows her how to grind the coffee beans and how to steam the milk. Maya watches every step closely, because it is her first time behind the counter.
Ni mara yangu ya kwanza nyuma ya kaunta.It is my first time behind the counter.
Mkahawa haujafunguliwa bado.The café is not open yet.
Mmoja wa watu wa kujitolea wenye uzoefu ananiita.One of the experienced volunteers calls for me.
Tunasimama mbele ya mashine kubwa ya espreso.We stand in front of the large espresso machine.
"Leo lazima ujifunze kutengeneza kahawa," anasema."Today you must learn to make coffee," he says.
Sijawahi kutengeneza kahawa kwenye mashine kama hii kabla.I have never made coffee on such a machine before.
Hatua ya kwanza ni kusaga mbegu za kahawa.The first step is to grind the coffee beans.
Anabonyeza kitufe kidogo.He presses a small button.
Mashine inasaga mbegu za kahawia kwa sauti kubwa.The machine grinds the brown beans with a loud sound.
Hatua ya pili ni kupasha maziwa kwa mvuke.The second step is to steam the milk.
Anamimina maziwa baridi kwenye jagi la chuma.He pours cold milk into a metal jug.
Baadaye anawasha mvuke wa moto.Afterwards he turns on the hot steam.
Ninatazama mikono yake kwa makini wakati huo.I look closely at his hands meanwhile.
Maziwa kwenye jagi yanakuwa ya moto na mazito haraka.The milk in the jug quickly becomes warm and thick.
Ni wazi kwamba ametengeneza kahawa mara nyingi kabla.He has clearly made coffee many times before.
Nilipofika asubuhi ya leo, nilikuwa na wasiwasi sana.When I arrived this morning, I was very nervous.
Lakini alieleza vizuri, hivyo tayari nimeelewa sehemu kubwa.But he explained it well, so I have already understood most of it.
the first coffee
Storyboard: When the morning rush hits, Maya makes a cappuccino for a waiting customer. She has held the steam wand a bit too deep in the milk, so it does not get a thick foam. It still looks acceptable, though, so she pours the milk over the coffee and hands it over with a polite smile.
Asubuhi hii tumekuwa na shughuli nyingi mno.This morning we have been incredibly busy.
Ndiyo kwanza nimetengeneza cappuccino yangu ya kwanza kabisa.I have just made my very first cappuccino.
Muda mfupi kabla ya saa nne, mwanaume mmoja alikuja kwenye kaunta.A little before ten, a man came over to the counter.
"Cappuccino moja, tafadhali," alisema."A cappuccino, please," he said.
Nimejifunza hivi punde kutumia mashine ya espresso.I have just learned to use the espresso machine.
Hivyo nilienda kwenye mashine mara moja.So I went over to the machine right away.
Nilitengeneza kahawa na kuweka bomba la mvuke ndani ya maziwa.I brewed the coffee and put the steam wand down into the milk.
Lakini nililishikilia chini kidogo kupita kiasi.But I held it a little too deep down.
Huenda nilikuwa na wasiwasi kidogo kupita kiasi.I have probably been a little too nervous.
Kwa bahati mbaya, maziwa hayakupata povu zito.Unfortunately, the milk did not get any thick foam.
Nilichungulia kwa uangalifu ndani ya jagi.I looked carefully down into the pitcher.
Povu halikuwa kamilifu kabisa.The foam was not completely perfect.
Lakini bado lilionekana kukubalika.But it still looked acceptable.
Nilimimina maziwa moto juu ya kahawa ndani ya kikombe.I poured the hot milk over the coffee in the cup.
Kisha nilimpa mwanaume yule kahawa nikiwa na tabasamu la heshima.Then I gave the coffee to the man with a polite smile.
"Karibu, itakuwa kroner hamsini na tano," nilisema."Here you go, that will be fifty-five kroner," I said.
Alichukua kikombe na kuketi kwenye meza ya kwanza.He took the cup and sat down at the first table.
bun with cheese
Storyboard: A moment later, she slices a warm sourdough bun and adds a thick piece of cheese. She places it on a small plate and navigates through the standing customers to deliver it safely to a corner booth.
Muda mfupi baadaye, oda iliyofuata ilikuja.Shortly after, the next order came.
Tayari nimetengeneza kahawa yangu ya kwanza leo.I have already made my first coffee today.
Lakini mteja huyu alitaka chakula tu.But this customer just wanted food.
Aliagiza mkate mdogo na jibini.He ordered a bun with cheese.
Nilichukua mkate mdogo wa moto wa unga uliochacha kutoka kwenye kikapu.I took a warm sourdough bun from the basket.
Mkate wa unga uliochacha mara nyingi huwa na ganda gumu.Sourdough bread often has a hard crust.
Lakini nimejifunza kukata mkate huo katikati haraka.But I have learned to cut the bread in half quickly.
Baadaye, niliweka kipande kinene cha jibini kwenye mkate huo mdogo.Afterwards, I put a thick slice of cheese on the bun.
Nilipanga chakula hicho vizuri kwenye sahani ndogo.I arranged the food nicely on a small plate.
Sasa ilinibidi nimpelekee mteja.Now I had to deliver it to the customer.
Kulikuwa na watu wengi wamesimama katikati ya mkahawa.There were many people standing in the middle of the café.
Niliiweka sahani sawa na kutembea mbele kwa uangalifu.I balanced the plate and walked carefully forward.
Nilitembea polepole kuwapita wageni wengine.I walked slowly past the other guests.
Kwa bahati nzuri, mteja alikuwa amekaa kule mwishoni kwenye kona.Fortunately, the customer was sitting right down in the corner.
Alikuwa akisubiri kwenye meza ya nne.He was waiting at the fourth table.
Niliweka sahani kwenye meza mbele yake.I placed the plate on the table in front of him.
"Huu hapa, mkate mdogo wa moto na jibini," nilisema."Here you go, a warm bun with cheese," I said.
"Asante sana," mwanaume huyo alijibu."Thank you very much," the man replied.
Inapendeza kuona mgeni aliyeridhika.It is lovely to see a satisfied guest.
the ginger beer
Storyboard: During her second Saturday shift, a woman asks for a ginger beer. Maya finds the ginger beer in the glass fridge, inverts it so that the ginger mixes. Then she hands it to the customer.
Ilikuwa zamu yangu ya pili ya Jumamosi kwenye mkahawa.It was my second Saturday shift in the café.
Tayari nimejifunza kufanya sehemu kubwa ya kazi.I have already learned to do a lot of the work.
Asubuhi hiyo kulikuwa na joto zuri nje.That morning it was wonderfully warm outside.
Mwanamke mmoja alikuja kwenye kaunta.A woman came over to the counter.
"Ningependa kuomba bia ya tangawizi," alisema."I would like to ask for a ginger beer," she said.
"Bila shaka, subiri kidogo," nilijibu."Of course, just a moment," I answered.
Nilienda kwenye jokofu kubwa la kioo nyuma ya kaunta.I went over to the large glass fridge behind the counter.
Nilitazama kwenye rafu ya tatu.I looked on the third shelf.
Chupa baridi za bia ya tangawizi zilikuwa zimesimama hapo.The cold bottles of ginger beer were standing there.
Nilipata chupa na kuitoa.I found a bottle and took it out.
Tangawizi ilituama kama wingu zito chini kabisa.The ginger lay like a thick cloud down at the bottom.
Nimejifunza kuichanganya kabla ya kufungua chupa.I have learned to mix it before I open the bottle.
Hivyo niligeuza chupa juu chini kwa uangalifu sana.So I turned the bottle very carefully upside down.
Niliishikilia hivyo kwa sekunde kadhaa.I held it like that for a couple of seconds.
Tangawizi ilichanganyikana polepole na kimiminika kilichobaki.The ginger mixed slowly with the rest of the liquid.
Sasa kinywaji kilionekana kuwa sawa kabisa.Now the drink looked exactly right.
Niligeuza chupa tena na kuiweka kwenye kaunta.I turned the bottle around again and placed it on the counter.
"Karibu, itakuwa kroner thelathini na tano," nilimwambia."Here you go, that will be thirty-five kroner," I said to her.
Alilipa, na bila shaka amefurahia kinywaji chake baridi.She paid, and she has probably enjoyed her cold drink.
second coffee progress
Storyboard: Later that morning, Maya tries foaming milk again for a large latte. This second attempt turns out much better, and she realizes that she has finally learned to control the pressure of the hot steam.
Baadaye Jumamosi hiyo hiyo, tulikuwa na kazi nyingi sana tena.Later the same Saturday, we got very busy again.
Mwanamke kijana alikuja kwenye kaunta na kuagiza kapuchino kubwa.A young woman came up to the counter and ordered a large cappuccino.
Kikombe changu cha kwanza hakikuwa kamilifu kabisa.My first cup was not quite perfect.
Lakini wakati huu nilikuwa tayari.But this time I was ready.
Nilichukua jagi la chuma na kumimina maziwa baridi ndani yake.I took the metal pitcher and poured cold milk into it.
Niliweka jagi chini ya mashine na kuwasha mvuke wa moto.I placed the pitcher under the machine and turned on the hot steam.
Sikuongeza shinikizo mara moja.I did not turn up the pressure immediately.
Kwa kweli nimejifunza kuisikiliza mashine.I have actually learned to listen to the machine.
Sauti kubwa kutoka kwenye mvuke ilikuwa sahihi kabisa wakati huu.The loud sound from the steam was exactly right this time.
Maziwa yalipata joto polepole.The milk slowly became warm.
Na povu kwa juu likawa zito sana na laini.And the foam on top became very thick and soft.
Ilikuwa hatua kubwa mbele.It was a big step forward.
Nilimimina maziwa kwa uangalifu juu ya kahawa kwenye kikombe kikubwa.I carefully poured the milk over the coffee in a large cup.
Kwa kweli ilionekana kama kapuchino halisi ya kitaalamu.It actually looked like a real professional cappuccino.
"Karibu," nilisema na kuweka kikombe kwenye kaunta."Here you go," I said and placed the cup on the counter.
Mwanamke huyo alitabasamu na kuchukua kahawa yake.The woman smiled and took her coffee.
Hatimaye nimejifunza kudhibiti shinikizo.I have finally learned to control the pressure.
third saturday regular
