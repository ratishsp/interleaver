# Week 12 — Storyboard (Maya joins a local badminton club)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Infinitive **-ാൻ** (കളിക്കാൻ, പഠിക്കാൻ) with **ഇഷ്ടമാണ്/പോകുന്നു**; ability **കഴിയും** — landing inside varied actions and exchanges, not as declarations

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_thursday_afternoon_chat | At the software company, Maya talks with her colleague Prakash during a tea break. She tells him she is changing her weekly routine: instead of the gym, she plans to go play badminton on Thursdays, in her usual half-past-five slot. She explains that she likes to play the sport back home in Mexico. |
| 2 | 02_arriving_at_the_club | Maya arrives at the local indoor sports club. She hears the loud squeak of sports shoes and the sharp pop of rackets hitting shuttlecocks from the courts. She watches the active games and feels excited to play again. |
| 3 | 03_a_trial_session | She goes to the front desk to ask about playing. To see if she likes the club, she pays a guest fee for a single trial evening. Since she does not own a racket in Kochi, she asks the clerk to rent one for the night. |
| 4 | 04_meeting_new_players | Carrying her rented racket, Maya walks towards the courts. Three friendly local players—Kiran, Sara, and Vishnu—wave at her. They introduce themselves and ask if she wants to join them for a doubles game. |
| 5 | 05_the_doubles_match | Maya steps onto the court, partnering with Kiran against Sara and Vishnu. The game moves quickly as they run, jump, and laugh while hitting the shuttlecock. Seeing her strong returns, Sara remarks that Maya can play very well. |
| 6 | 06_a_quick_water_break | After a fast-paced game, the players step off the court for a short water break. Vishnu asks Maya if she likes living in Kochi. She replies that she loves the city and happily mentions that she likes to meet new friends here. |
| 7 | 07_returning_the_racket | The evening finishes, and the players pack up their bags. Maya returns to the front desk, hands over the rented racket to the clerk, and tells him the courts are very good. |
| 8 | 08_a_new_routine | Outside the club, Kiran, Sara, and Vishnu ask Maya if she can come back next week. Maya happily agrees to join them every Thursday, thrilled to make the badminton club her permanent new social routine. |
