Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Lithuanian with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
Lėktuvas dabar Kopenhagoje.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
Dabar sausis.It is January.
Aš labai susijaudinusi.I am very excited.
Įsidedu savo knygą į krepšį.I pack my book into my bag.
Šalia manęs yra keleivis.There is a passenger next to me.
Jis žiūri į mane ir šypsosi.He looks at me and smiles.
„Labas“, – sako jis."Hello," he says.
„Labas“, – sakau."Hello," I say.
„Kuo tu vardu?“ – klausia jis."What is your name?" he asks.
„Mano vardas Maya“, – sakau."My name is Maya," I say.
„Iš kur tu atvykai, Maya?“ – klausia jis."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
Aš laimingai nusišypsau.I smile happily.
„Aš iš Meksikos“, – atsakau."I come from Mexico," I answer.
„Ar tu iš Danijos?“ – klausiu."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
„Taip, aš iš Danijos“, – sako jis."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
„Sveika atvykusi į Daniją“, – sako jis."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
„Labai ačiū“, – sakau."Thank you very much," I say.
Dabar mes čia.Now we are here.
Aš pasiruošusi.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
Dabar aš lauke.I am outside now.
Mano lagaminas didelis ir sunkus.My suitcase is big and heavy.
Štai taksi.There is a taxi.
Įkeliu savo lagaminą į taksi.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
Vairuotojas yra draugiškas vyras.The driver is a friendly man.
„Koks adresas?“ – klausia jis."What is the address?" he asks.
„Tai Parkvej numeris dvylika“, – sakau."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
Jis linkteli ir važiuoja.He nods and drives.
„Ar jūs turistė?“ – klausia vairuotojas."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
„Ne, aš ne turistė“, – atsakau."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
„Iš kur jūs esate?“ – klausia jis."Where do you come from?" he asks.
„Aš iš Meksikos“, – sakau."I come from Mexico," I say.
Žiūriu pro langą.I look out the window.
Aš labai didžiuojuosi.I am very proud.
„Aš programinės įrangos inžinierė“, – sakau."I am a software engineer," I say.
„Mano naujas darbas yra čia, Kopenhagoje.“"My new job is here in Copenhagen."
Jis šypsosi.He smiles.
„Kaip įdomu.“"How exciting."
„Sėkmės Danijoje“, – sako jis."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
„Labai ačiū“, – sakau."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
Durys atviros.The door is open.
Slenkstis aukštas.The threshold is high.
Lagaminas labai sunkus!The suitcase is very heavy!
Bet dabar lagaminas yra prieškambaryje.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
Čia visiškai tamsu.It is completely dark here.
Kur jungiklis?Where is the switch?
Siena šalta.The wall is cold.
Ar jungiklis čia?Is the switch here?
Taip, štai jungiklis!Yes, there is the switch!
Dabar šviesu.Now there is light.
Aš esu bute.I am in the apartment.
Jis mažas, bet gražus.It is small, but it is nice.
Čia yra svetainė.Here is a living room.
Yra sofa ir stalas.There is a sofa and a table.
Tai apstatyti namai.It is a furnished home.
Lagaminas ant grindų.The suitcase is on the floor.
Aš labai pavargusi.I am very tired.
Bet aš taip pat laiminga.But I am also happy.
Aš esu Kopenhagoje!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
Stoviu prie lango.I am standing by the window.
Stiklas šaltas.The glass is cold.
Dabar sausis.It is January.
Žiūriu į lauką.I look out.
Kopenhaga šiandien pilka.Copenhagen is grey today.
Lauke šalta.It is cold outside.
Gatvėje yra sniego.There is snow on the street.
Bet čia šilta.But it is warm here.
Butas geras.The apartment is good.
Galvoju apie Meksiką.I think about Mexico.
Meksikoje šviečia saulė.In Mexico, there is sun.
Ten taip pat šilta.It is also warm there.
Šiek tiek pasiilgau saulės.I miss the sun a little.
Bet šypsausi.But I smile.
Tai naujas miestas.It is a new city.
Dabar esu Danijoje.I am in Denmark now.
Tai gera pradžia.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
Dabar esu svetainėje.I am in the living room now.
Čia yra maža sofa.There is a small sofa.
Skambinu mamai į Meksiką.I call my mother in Mexico.
„Labas, mama“, – sakau."Hi Mom," I say.
„Labas, Maja“, – sako mano mama."Hi Maya," my mother says.
„Kur tu esi?“ – klausia ji."Where are you?" she asks.
„Esu Kopenhagoje“, – atsakau."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
„Dabar esu bute.“"I am in the apartment now."
„Tai labai gerai“, – sako mano mama."That is so good," my mother says.
„Ar jis didelis?“ – klausia ji."Is it big?" she asks.
„Ne, jis mažas“, – atsakau."No, it is small," I answer.
„Bet jis jaukus, ir aš esu laiminga.“"But it is cozy, and I am happy."
„Ar šalta?“ – klausia ji."Is it cold?" she asks.
„Taip, lauke šalta“, – sakau."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
„Bet čia šilta.“"But it is warm in here."
„Aš saugi.“"I am safe."
„Tai gerai“, – sako mano mama."That is good," my mother says.
Aš šypsausi.I smile.
Danija yra mano naujieji namai.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
Atidarau savo duris.I open my door.
Stoviu laiptinėje.I am standing in the stairwell.
Tylu.It is quiet.
Atsidaro durys.A door opens.
Moteris man šypsosi.A woman smiles at me.
„Labas“, – sako ji."Hi," she says.
„Kuo tu vardu?“ – klausia ji."What is your name?" she asks.
„Mano vardas Maya“, – atsakau."My name is Maya," I answer.
„Iš kur tu atvykai?“ – klausia ji."Where do you come from?" she asks.
„Aš iš Meksikos“, – sakau."I come from Mexico," I say.
„Sveika atvykusi į šį namą“, – sako moteris."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
„Mano vardas Nina“, – sako ji."My name is Nina," she says.
„Aš esu tavo kaimynė.“"I am your neighbour."
Nina labai draugiška.Nina is very friendly.
Ji kalba lėtai ir aiškiai.She speaks slowly and clearly.
„Štai mano durys“, – sako ji."Here is my door," she says.
„Mielai padėsiu.“"I am happy to help."
„Tiesiog pasibelsk“, – sako Nina."You just knock," Nina says.
„Labai ačiū, Nina“, – atsakau."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
Aš labai laiminga.I am very happy.
Dabar turiu draugę Kopenhagoje.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
Dabar naktis.Now it is night.
Tai mano pirmoji naktis Kopenhagoje.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
Aš esu vonios kambaryje.I am in the bathroom.
Jis labai mažas.It is very small.
Čia yra kriauklė.There is a sink.
Mano dantų šepetėlis raudonas.My toothbrush is red.
Aš užtepu dantų pastos ant savo dantų šepetėlio.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
Aš valausi dantis.I brush my teeth.
Vanduo yra šaltas.The water is cold.
Aš prausiuosi veidą.I wash my face.
Aš šluostausi veidą.I dry my face.
Aš einu prie savo lovos.I go over to my bed.
Lova yra siaura.The bed is narrow.
Bet ji šilta ir minkšta.But it is warm and soft.
Dabar aš guliu po antklode.I am lying under the duvet now.
Mano telefonas guli ant stalo.My phone is lying on the table.
Aš nustatau žadintuvą.I set my alarm.
Jis skambės rytoj aštuntą valandą.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
Aš pavargusi, bet esu pasiruošusi.I am tired, but I am ready.
Labanakt, Kopenhaga.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
