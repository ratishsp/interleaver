Week 10: Maya buys winter clothes

Spanish with English gloss, line-aligned.

a cold morning
Storyboard: It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to the bus stop on her way to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter.
Es de mañana en Copenhague.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Camino a la parada del autobús.I walk to the bus stop.
El clima está muy frío hoy.The weather is very cold today.
Hace viento.It is windy.
El viento está helado.The wind is ice-cold.
Me estoy congelando.I am freezing.
Llevo puesta una chamarra delgada.I am wearing a thin jacket.
Mi chamarra no es nada abrigadora.My jacket is not warm at all.
También llevo zapatos delgados.I am also wearing thin shoes.
No llevo puesta una bufanda abrigadora.I am not wearing a warm scarf.
Mis manos están muy frías.My hands are very cold.
Estoy temblando.I am shivering.
El invierno en Dinamarca es duro.The winter in Denmark is hard.
Necesito ropa abrigadora.I need warm clothes.
Necesito un suéter grueso.I need a thick sweater.
También necesito una bufanda grande.I also need a large scarf.
Y necesito guantes abrigadores.And I need warm gloves.
Camino muy rápido.I walk very fast.
Voy a comprar ropa de invierno después del trabajo.I am buying winter clothes after work.
entering the shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things.
Terminé de trabajar.I am finished at work.
Camino por la calle peatonal.I walk down the pedestrian street.
El viento sigue muy frío.The wind is still very cold.
Veo una tienda de ropa grande.I see a large clothing shop.
La tienda es muy luminosa.The shop is very bright.
Entro a la tienda.I go into the shop.
Hace un calor maravilloso aquí adentro.It is wonderfully warm in here.
Una vendedora está detrás de la caja.A shop assistant stands behind the register.
Me mira y sonríe.She looks at me and smiles.
"Hola", dice ella."Hello," she says.
"Hola", respondo."Hello," I answer.
"Me estoy congelando", digo."I am freezing a lot," I say.
"Solo llevo puesta una chamarra delgada.""I only have a thin jacket on."
"Me gustaría encontrar algunas cosas abrigadoras.""I would like to find some warm things."
La vendedora sonríe de nuevo.The shop assistant smiles again.
"Hoy también hace mucho frío", dice ella."It is also very cold today," she says.
"Tenemos muchos suéteres gruesos.""We have many thick sweaters."
"También tenemos ropa de invierno abrigadora.""We also have warm winter clothes."
"Solo eche un vistazo", dice amablemente."You just take a look," she says kindly.
"Muchas gracias", respondo."Many thanks," I answer.
the red sweater
Storyboard: Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it.
Miro la ropa en los estantes.I look at the clothes on the shelves.
Veo un suéter grueso.I see a thick sweater.
Es rojo.It is red.
Necesito un suéter cálido.I need a warm sweater.
La tienda tiene muchas tallas.The shop has many sizes.
Encuentro una talla mediana.I find a size medium.
Entro al probador.I walk into the fitting room.
Me pongo el suéter rojo.I put the red sweater on.
Lo tengo puesto y me miro en el espejo grande.I have it on and look in the big mirror.
El suéter me queda perfecto.The sweater fits perfectly.
Es increíblemente cálido.It is incredibly warm.
Me encanta el color rojo.I love the red colour.
Miro la etiqueta del precio.I look at the price tag.
El suéter cuesta trescientas coronas.The sweater costs three hundred kroner.
Es un buen precio para un suéter grueso.It is a good price for a thick sweater.
Me quito el suéter de nuevo.I take the sweater off again.
Lo compro.I buy it.
the tight green coat
Storyboard: Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a larger size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. The assistant brings her a warm dark-blue winter jacket in her size instead — it fits perfectly, and Maya decides to take it.
También necesito una chamarra de invierno.I also need a winter jacket.
Miro más ropa en la tienda.I look at more clothes in the shop.
Encuentro un hermoso abrigo verde.I find a beautiful, green coat.
Es un diseño muy hermoso.It is a very beautiful design.
Me pongo el abrigo verde.I put the green coat on.
Pero es demasiado pequeño.But it is too small.
Me queda muy apretado de los hombros.It is very tight across the shoulders.
"¿Lo tienen en otra talla?", pregunto."Do you have it in another size?" I ask.
La vendedora revisa.The shop assistant looks.
"No, desafortunadamente", responde ella."No, unfortunately," she answers.
Así que vuelvo a colgar el abrigo verde.So I hang the green coat back.
La vendedora encuentra otra chamarra para mí.The shop assistant finds another jacket for me.
Es una chamarra de invierno cálida y azul oscuro.It is a warm, dark-blue winter jacket.
Me pongo la chamarra azul oscuro.I put the dark-blue jacket on.
Me queda perfecta.It fits perfectly.
Es muy amplia y no me queda apretada.It is nice and big and not tight.
Me miro en un espejo grande.I look in a large mirror.
Me gusta.I like it.
"Me la llevo", digo."I will take it," I say.
the blue scarf
Storyboard: Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases.
Me acerco a una mesa en la tienda.I walk over to a table in the shop.
Hay muchas cosas en la mesa.There are many things on the table.
Hoy no llevo bufanda.I am not wearing a scarf today.
Tengo mucho frío en el cuello.I am very cold around my neck.
Veo una bufanda azul.I see a blue scarf.
Es una bufanda muy larga.It is a very long scarf.
Está hecha de lana.It is made of wool.
Tomo la bufanda azul.I take the blue scarf.
Me la pongo alrededor del cuello.I put it around my neck.
Se siente maravillosamente cálida.It feels wonderfully warm.
"Es un color bonito", dice la vendedora."It is a nice colour," the shop assistant says.
"Sí, y es muy suave", digo."Yes, and it is very soft," I say.
Miro la etiqueta de precio.I look at the price tag.
Cuesta ciento cincuenta coronas.It costs one hundred and fifty kroner.
Es un buen precio para una bufanda cálida.It is a good price for a warm scarf.
Me la quito de nuevo.I take it off again.
"También la compro", le digo."I am buying it too," I say to her.
Pongo la bufanda azul junto con el suéter rojo.I put the blue scarf together with the red sweater.
the black gloves
Storyboard: Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well.
Todavía llevo puesta mi chaqueta fina.I am still wearing my thin jacket.
Pienso en mis manos frías de esta mañana.I think about my cold hands from this morning.
Necesito guantes gruesos.I need thick gloves.
Miro alrededor de la tienda luminosa.I look around the bright shop.
Veo una canasta grande.I see a large basket.
La canasta está llena de guantes.The basket is full of gloves.
Encuentro un par de guantes negros de invierno.I find a pair of black winter gloves.
Son muy gruesos y suaves.They are very thick and soft.
Me pongo los guantes.I put the gloves on.
Me quedan perfectos.They fit me perfectly.
Le pregunto a la vendedora sonriente.I ask the smiling shop assistant.
"¿Cuánto cuestan los guantes negros?", pregunto."What do the black gloves cost?" I ask.
"Cuestan ciento veinte coronas", responde ella."They cost one hundred and twenty kroner," she answers.
Ciento veinte coronas es un buen precio.One hundred and twenty kroner is a good price.
Mis manos ya están calientes.My hands are already warm.
"Me los llevo", digo feliz."I will take them," I say happily.
Me quito los guantes de nuevo.I take the gloves off again.
Pongo los guantes negros junto con mi suéter rojo y mi bufanda azul.I put the black gloves together with my red sweater and my blue scarf.
Ahora estoy lista para pagar.Now I am ready to pay.
paying for the clothes
Storyboard: Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the dark-blue winter jacket, the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is one thousand five hundred kroner, and Maya pays with her card.
Me acerco a la caja con mi ropa nueva.I walk over to the counter with my new clothes.
La dependienta me sonríe amablemente.The shop assistant smiles kindly at me.
"¿Eso es todo?", pregunta ella."Is that everything?" she asks.
"Sí, eso es todo", respondo."Yes, that is everything," I answer.
Ella escanea todas mis cosas nuevas.She scans all my new things.
Hay un suéter, una chamarra, una bufanda y guantes.There is a sweater, a jacket, a scarf and gloves.
"Ahora no pasarás frío", dice ella feliz."Now you will not freeze," she says happily.
"Serán mil quinientas coronas, por favor"."That will be one thousand five hundred kroner, please."
La ropa es cara, pero es muy abrigadora.The clothes are expensive, but they are very warm.
Saco mi tarjeta de pago.I take out my payment card.
"Pago con tarjeta", digo."I am paying by card," I say.
"Puedes pagar aquí", dice ella."You can just pay here," she says.
Ella señala una pequeña máquina negra.She points at a small, black machine.
Sostengo mi tarjeta sobre la máquina.I hold my card over the machine.
Emite un pitido y el pago es aprobado.It beeps, and the payment is approved.
"¿Quieres el recibo?", pregunta la dependienta."Do you want the receipt?" the shop assistant asks.
"No, gracias", respondo."No thank you," I answer.
Ahora tengo mucha ropa de invierno bonita.Now I own a lot of nice winter clothes.
stepping into the cold
Storyboard: Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She pulls on the red sweater, zips her new dark-blue winter jacket over it, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm.
¿Una bolsa?, pregunta el dependiente."A bag?" the shop assistant asks.
"No, gracias", digo."No thank you," I say.
No necesito una bolsa.I do not need a bag.
Me pongo la ropa nueva de inmediato.I put the new clothes on right away.
Primero, me pongo el suéter grueso y rojo.First, I put on the thick, red sweater.
Luego, me pongo la chamarra de invierno azul oscuro.Then, I put on the dark-blue winter jacket.
Me subo el cierre de la cálida chamarra.I zip the warm jacket.
Después, tomo la bufanda larga y azul.Afterwards, I take the long, blue scarf.
Me la envuelvo alrededor del cuello.I wrap it around my neck.
Finalmente, me pongo los nuevos guantes negros.Finally, I put on the new, black gloves.
Ahora tengo puesta toda mi ropa abrigada.Now I have all my warm clothes on.
Sonrío y digo adiós.I smile and say goodbye.
Salgo de la tienda.I walk out of the shop.
Estoy parada afuera en la calle fría otra vez.I stand out on the cold street again.
Hace un frío helado.It is freezing cold.
El viento sopla fuerte.The wind blows hard.
Pero no tengo nada de frío.But I am not freezing at all.
Ahora estoy lista para el invierno.Now I am ready for winter.
