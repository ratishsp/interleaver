# Week 13 — Storyboard (Maya escapes the cold in a café)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Polite requests — **Ich hätte gern … / Könnte ich … haben?**; **bitte** and **danke**; asking to pay

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_escape_the_cold | Maya walks through the streets of Berlin. It is very cold today, and the wind blows hard. She wants to warm up, so she looks for a cozy place. She sees a small café on a corner and decides to go inside. |
| 2 | 02_finding_a_seat | She opens the door. It is warm inside and the air smells of fresh coffee. The café is busy, but she spots an empty table near a large window. She walks over, takes off her green winter coat, and leaves it on the wooden chair to hold her spot, keeping her Rucksack safely on her shoulder. |
| 3 | 03_choosing_at_the_counter | Maya goes to the large glass counter. There are many delicious cakes and pastries on display. A waiter stands behind the counter. Maya waits for a moment and looks at the selection. She decides on a piece of chocolate cake. |
| 4 | 04_the_polite_order | The waiter greets her and Maya orders. She politely asks for a large coffee and the chocolate cake, adding a clear please. The waiter nods, smiles, and says he brings everything to her table. Maya thanks him. |
| 5 | 05_waiting_and_messaging | Maya returns to her seat. She looks out the window at the freezing people on the street. She takes her phone from her backpack and reads a few messages from her sister Sofia in Mexico. A few minutes later, the waiter brings her order. Maya thanks him again. |
| 6 | 06_enjoying_the_break | The coffee is hot and strong, exactly how she likes it. The chocolate cake tastes wonderful. She takes her time, enjoys the warm atmosphere, and puts her phone away. |
| 7 | 07_ordering_water | After eating the rich, sweet cake, Maya is a bit thirsty. The waiter walks past her table. Maya politely catches his attention. She asks if she can have a bottle of sparkling water. The waiter agrees and quickly brings the water. |
| 8 | 08_asking_to_pay | Maya finishes her water and decides it is time to leave. She stays comfortably seated at her table, signals the waiter once more, and asks politely if she can pay. The waiter comes over to her table with his small leather wallet. |
| 9 | 09_paying_the_bill | The waiter says that her total is eleven Euro and eighty cents. Maya gives him a twenty Euro note. She says please and tells him that thirteen Euro is fine, leaving a small tip. He gives her the change and asks if she needs the Kassenbon, but she declines. Maya then packs her things, puts on her winter coat, says goodbye, and steps back out into the cold Berlin air. |
