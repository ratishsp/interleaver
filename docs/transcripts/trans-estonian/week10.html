Week 10: Maya buys winter clothes

Estonian with English gloss, line-aligned.

a cold morning
Storyboard: It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to the bus stop on her way to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter.
Kopenhaagenis on hommik.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Ma kõnnin bussipeatusesse.I walk to the bus stop.
Ilm on täna väga külm.The weather is very cold today.
On tuuline.It is windy.
Tuul on jääkülm.The wind is ice-cold.
Ma külmetan.I am freezing.
Mul on seljas õhuke jope.I am wearing a thin jacket.
Mu jope pole üldse soe.My jacket is not warm at all.
Mul on jalas ka õhukesed kingad.I am also wearing thin shoes.
Mul ei ole kaelas sooja salli.I am not wearing a warm scarf.
Mu käed on väga külmad.My hands are very cold.
Ma värisen.I am shivering.
Talv Taanis on karm.The winter in Denmark is hard.
Mul on vaja sooje riideid.I need warm clothes.
Mul on vaja paksu kampsunit.I need a thick sweater.
Mul on vaja ka suurt salli.I also need a large scarf.
Ja mul on vaja sooje kindaid.And I need warm gloves.
Ma kõnnin väga kiiresti.I walk very fast.
Ma ostan pärast tööd talveriideid.I am buying winter clothes after work.
entering the shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things.
Ma lõpetasin tööl.I am finished at work.
Ma kõnnin mööda jalakäijate tänavat.I walk down the pedestrian street.
Tuul on ikka veel väga külm.The wind is still very cold.
Ma näen suurt riidepoodi.I see a large clothing shop.
Pood on väga valgusküllane.The shop is very bright.
Ma lähen poodi sisse.I go into the shop.
Siin on mõnusalt soe.It is wonderfully warm in here.
Müüja seisab kassa taga.A shop assistant stands behind the register.
Ta vaatab mind ja naeratab.She looks at me and smiles.
"Tere," ütleb ta."Hello," she says.
"Tere," vastan ma."Hello," I answer.
"Mul on väga külm," ütlen ma."I am freezing a lot," I say.
"Mul on ainult õhuke jope seljas.""I only have a thin jacket on."
"Ma tahaksin leida mõned soojad asjad.""I would like to find some warm things."
Müüja naeratab uuesti.The shop assistant smiles again.
"Täna on ka väga külm," ütleb ta."It is also very cold today," she says.
"Meil on palju pakse kampsuneid.""We have many thick sweaters."
"Meil on ka sooje talveriideid.""We also have warm winter clothes."
"Vaadake rahulikult ringi," ütleb ta lahkelt."You just take a look," she says kindly.
"Suur tänu," vastan ma."Many thanks," I answer.
the red sweater
Storyboard: Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it.
Ma vaatan riideid riiulitel.I look at the clothes on the shelves.
Ma näen paksu kampsunit.I see a thick sweater.
See on punane.It is red.
Mul on vaja sooja kampsunit.I need a warm sweater.
Poes on palju suurusi.The shop has many sizes.
Ma leian M-suuruse.I find a size medium.
Ma lähen proovikabiini.I walk into the fitting room.
Ma panen punase kampsuni selga.I put the red sweater on.
Mul on see seljas ja ma vaatan suurde peeglisse.I have it on and look in the big mirror.
Kampsun istub ideaalselt.The sweater fits perfectly.
See on uskumatult soe.It is incredibly warm.
Ma armastan seda punast värvi.I love the red colour.
Ma vaatan hinnasilti.I look at the price tag.
Kampsun maksab kolmsada krooni.The sweater costs three hundred kroner.
See on hea hind paksu kampsuni eest.It is a good price for a thick sweater.
Ma võtan kampsuni uuesti seljast.I take the sweater off again.
Ma ostan selle.I buy it.
the tight green coat
Storyboard: Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a larger size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. The assistant brings her a warm dark-blue winter jacket in her size instead — it fits perfectly, and Maya decides to take it.
Mul on ka talvejopet vaja.I also need a winter jacket.
Ma vaatan poes veel riideid.I look at more clothes in the shop.
Ma leian ilusa rohelise mantli.I find a beautiful, green coat.
See on väga ilus disain.It is a very beautiful design.
Ma panen rohelise mantli selga.I put the green coat on.
Aga see on liiga väike.But it is too small.
See on õlgadest väga kitsas.It is very tight across the shoulders.
"Kas teil on seda teises suuruses?" küsin ma."Do you have it in another size?" I ask.
Müüja vaatab.The shop assistant looks.
"Ei, kahjuks mitte," vastab ta."No, unfortunately," she answers.
Nii et ma riputan rohelise mantli tagasi.So I hang the green coat back.
Müüja leiab mulle teise jope.The shop assistant finds another jacket for me.
See on soe tumesinine talvejope.It is a warm, dark-blue winter jacket.
Ma panen tumesinise jope selga.I put the dark-blue jacket on.
See istub ideaalselt.It fits perfectly.
See on mõnusalt suur ja ei ole kitsas.It is nice and big and not tight.
Ma vaatan suurde peeglisse.I look in a large mirror.
See meeldib mulle.I like it.
"Ma võtan selle," ütlen ma."I will take it," I say.
the blue scarf
Storyboard: Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases.
Ma lähen poes ühe laua juurde.I walk over to a table in the shop.
Laual on palju asju.There are many things on the table.
Mul ei ole täna salli kaelas.I am not wearing a scarf today.
Mul on kaela ümbert väga külm.I am very cold around my neck.
Ma näen üht sinist salli.I see a blue scarf.
See on väga pikk sall.It is a very long scarf.
See on villast tehtud.It is made of wool.
Ma võtan sinise salli.I take the blue scarf.
Ma panen selle ümber kaela.I put it around my neck.
See tundub mõnusalt soe.It feels wonderfully warm.
"See on ilus värv," ütleb müüja."It is a nice colour," the shop assistant says.
"Jah, ja see on väga pehme," ütlen ma."Yes, and it is very soft," I say.
Ma vaatan hinnasilti.I look at the price tag.
See maksab sada viiskümmend krooni.It costs one hundred and fifty kroner.
See on hea hind sooja salli eest.It is a good price for a warm scarf.
Ma võtan selle uuesti ära.I take it off again.
"Ma ostan selle ka," ütlen ma talle."I am buying it too," I say to her.
Ma panen sinise salli punase kampsuniga kokku.I put the blue scarf together with the red sweater.
the black gloves
Storyboard: Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well.
Mul on ikka veel oma õhuke jope seljas.I am still wearing my thin jacket.
Ma mõtlen oma tänahommikustele külmadele kätele.I think about my cold hands from this morning.
Mul on vaja pakse kindaid.I need thick gloves.
Ma vaatan valgusküllases poes ringi.I look around the bright shop.
Ma näen suurt korvi.I see a large basket.
Korv on kindaid täis.The basket is full of gloves.
Ma leian ühe paari musti talvekindaid.I find a pair of black winter gloves.
Need on väga paksud ja pehmed.They are very thick and soft.
Ma panen kindad kätte.I put the gloves on.
Need sobivad mulle ideaalselt.They fit me perfectly.
Ma küsin naeratavalt müüjalt.I ask the smiling shop assistant.
"Kui palju need mustad kindad maksavad?" küsin ma."What do the black gloves cost?" I ask.
"Need maksavad sada kakskümmend krooni," vastab ta."They cost one hundred and twenty kroner," she answers.
Sada kakskümmend krooni on hea hind.One hundred and twenty kroner is a good price.
Mu käed on juba soojad.My hands are already warm.
"Ma võtan need," ütlen ma rõõmsalt."I will take them," I say happily.
Ma võtan kindad uuesti käest ära.I take the gloves off again.
Ma panen mustad kindad oma punase kampsuni ja sinise salli juurde.I put the black gloves together with my red sweater and my blue scarf.
Nüüd olen ma valmis maksma.Now I am ready to pay.
paying for the clothes
Storyboard: Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the dark-blue winter jacket, the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is one thousand five hundred kroner, and Maya pays with her card.
Ma lähen oma uute riietega kassa juurde.I walk over to the counter with my new clothes.
Müüja naeratab mulle lahkelt.The shop assistant smiles kindly at me.
"Kas see on kõik?" küsib ta."Is that everything?" she asks.
"Jah, see on kõik," vastan ma."Yes, that is everything," I answer.
Ta skannib kõik mu uued asjad.She scans all my new things.
Seal on kampsun, jope, sall ja kindad.There is a sweater, a jacket, a scarf and gloves.
"Nüüd te ei külmeta," ütleb ta rõõmsalt."Now you will not freeze," she says happily.
"See teeb tuhat viissada krooni, palun.""That will be one thousand five hundred kroner, please."
Riided on kallid, aga need on väga soojad.The clothes are expensive, but they are very warm.
Ma võtan välja oma maksekaardi.I take out my payment card.
"Ma maksan kaardiga," ütlen ma."I am paying by card," I say.
"Te saate lihtsalt siin maksta," ütleb ta."You can just pay here," she says.
Ta osutab väikesele mustale masinale.She points at a small, black machine.
Ma hoian oma kaarti masina kohal.I hold my card over the machine.
See piiksub ja makse on heaks kiidetud.It beeps, and the payment is approved.
"Kas te soovite tšekki?" küsib müüja."Do you want the receipt?" the shop assistant asks.
"Ei, tänan," vastan ma."No thank you," I answer.
Nüüd oman ma palju ilusaid talveriideid.Now I own a lot of nice winter clothes.
stepping into the cold
Storyboard: Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She pulls on the red sweater, zips her new dark-blue winter jacket over it, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm.
"Kotti?" küsib müüja."A bag?" the shop assistant asks.
"Ei, tänan," ütlen ma."No thank you," I say.
Mul ei ole kotti vaja.I do not need a bag.
Ma panen uued riided kohe selga.I put the new clothes on right away.
Kõigepealt panen ma selga paksu punase kampsuni.First, I put on the thick, red sweater.
Siis panen ma selga tumesinise talvejope.Then, I put on the dark-blue winter jacket.
Ma tõmban sooja jope luku kinni.I zip the warm jacket.
Seejärel võtan ma pika sinise salli.Afterwards, I take the long, blue scarf.
Ma mässin selle ümber kaela.I wrap it around my neck.
Lõpuks panen ma kätte uued mustad kindad.Finally, I put on the new, black gloves.
Nüüd on mul kõik soojad riided seljas.Now I have all my warm clothes on.
Ma naeratan ja ütlen head aega.I smile and say goodbye.
Ma lähen poest välja.I walk out of the shop.
Ma seisan jälle väljas külmal tänaval.I stand out on the cold street again.
On jäiselt külm.It is freezing cold.
Tuul puhub tugevalt.The wind blows hard.
Aga mul ei ole üldse külm.But I am not freezing at all.
Nüüd olen ma talveks valmis.Now I am ready for winter.
