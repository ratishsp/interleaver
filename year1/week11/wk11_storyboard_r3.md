# Week 11 — Storyboard (Maya experiences the Danish winter weather)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Impersonal *det er / det bliver*; seasons

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_dark_winter_morning | Maya wakes up to the sound of her alarm. She looks towards the window, but it is completely pitch-black outside. It is midwinter in Denmark, and she realizes the mornings are incredibly dark. |
| 2 | 02_checking_the_forecast | Maya stands by the window and checks the weather app on her phone. The app shows that it is very cold right now, and that it becomes wet and windy very soon. |
| 3 | 03_bundling_up | Maya prepares to face the chilly morning. She pulls her thick red sweater over her head and wraps her long blue winter scarf tightly around her neck. She zips her coat all the way up to block out the cold hallway draft. |
| 4 | 04_the_rain_begins | Maya steps out of her building to commute to work. It is gray but dry at first, until she reaches the end of the street. Suddenly, it becomes wet as heavy rain starts falling, so she quickly opens her umbrella and navigates around puddles on her way to the bus stop. |
| 5 | 05_fighting_the_wind | Maya gets off the bus near her software company. A hard wind blows through the street. It becomes impossible to hold the umbrella straight, so she wrestles to close it and hurries through the fierce gusts to the office entrance. |
| 6 | 06_early_dusk | Maya finishes her work and steps outside the office building. She is surprised to see that dusk is already falling and the streetlights are on. It becomes dark very early in this season, and she walks back to the bus in the fading light. |
| 7 | 07_wet_layers | Maya arrives back at her apartment. She peels off her wet coat and leaves her dripping umbrella on the bathroom tile. She walks into the dark living room and switches on a small lamp in the corner to brighten the space. |
| 8 | 08_steaming_tea | Maya boils water and brews a large mug of herbal tea. She sits down on the sofa, holding the steaming mug in her hands. She listens to the heavy wind outside, letting the hot drink warm her up as it becomes a cold winter night. |
