Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
Je piatok večer a Nina klope na moje dvere.It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
Má so sebou svojho psa Ludwiga.She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.
Cez víkend zostane u mňa.He is going to stay with me for the weekend.
Nina sa usmieva a podáva mi vôdzku.Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
"Veľmi pekne ti ďakujem za pomoc, Maya," hovorí."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
"To je úplne v poriadku," odpovedám veselo."It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
Vysvetľuje mi o ňom zopár vecí.She explains a few things to me about him.
"Dávaj mu jedlo o siedmej ráno a o šiestej večer," hovorí Nina."Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
"A pamätaj, nesmie dostať ľudské jedlo.""And remember, he must not have human food."
Smejem sa a pozerám sa dole na malého psa.I laugh and look down at the little dog.
"Nie, Ludwig, moja večera nie je pre teba," hovorím mu."No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
Nina sa tiež smeje a ukazuje na dvere.Nina laughs too and points at the door.
"Vždy ho maj na vôdzke, keď pôjdete blízko veľkej cesty," hovorí."Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
"V parku môže behať voľne.""In the park he is allowed to run freely."
Prikývnem.I nod.
"Neboj sa, dobre sa o neho postarám.""Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
Nina ma objíma a lúči sa s nami.Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
Zatvára dvere a ja stojím sama s Ludwigom.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
Pozerá sa na mňa a vrtí chvostom.He looks up at me and wags his tail.
Už teraz ho mám veľmi rada.I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll
Storyboard: Before bed, Maya takes Ludwig for a short evening walk around the neighborhood. When he suddenly sniffs at a discarded food wrapper on the pavement, she gently commands him to leave it and pulls him away, reminding him that he must not eat trash.
Je neskorý piatkový večer.It is late Friday evening.

Ideme na krátku prechádzku po okolí.We go for a short walk in the neighborhood.

Zrazu sa zastaví.Suddenly he stops.
Dychtivo oňucháva niečo na chodníku.He sniffs eagerly at something on the pavement.

„Nie, pusť to!“ poviem mu rázne."No, drop it!" I say firmly to him.
Jemne ho odtiahnem od tašky.I gently pull him away from the bag.
„Odpadky nie sú jedlo, Ludwig,“ vysvetľujem."The trash is not food, Ludwig," I explain.

Rýchlo začne vrteť chvostom.He quickly begins to wag his tail.
„Dobrý psík,“ poviem a pohladkám ho."Good boy," I say and pet him.
Pokračujeme v prechádzke po ulici.We continue our walk down the street.
Teraz už len musíme ísť domov a spať.Now we just need to go home and sleep.
saturday breakfast
Storyboard: Early Saturday morning, Ludwig nudges Maya awake. Maya prepares his breakfast, gently telling him to sit and wait while she fills the bowl, before finally petting him and telling him to eat it.

Pozrie na mňa a sadne si na podlahu.He looks at me and sits down on the floor.

„Nie, čakaj,“ poviem."No, wait," I say.

a morning together
Storyboard: Saturday morning, with the café shift she arranged off in advance so Ludwig is not left alone, Maya has the morning free. she and Ludwig enjoy it together — she throws his ball and calls him back, gives him a good fuss — happy she chose to stay with him.
Po raňajkách mám veľa času.After breakfast I have plenty of time.

„Prines ju!“ zakričím."Fetch it!" I shout.

the hundeskov
Storyboard: Saturday afternoon, Maya walks with Ludwig to a local hundeskov (dog forest). Once inside the safe, gated area, she unclips the lead, happily telling him that he may run free now, and throws a stick for him to fetch.

„Poď, ideme pekne.“"Come, let us walk nicely."
