# Week 19 — Storyboard (Maya starts a new project with the team)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Present perfect *har arbejdet*; professions

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_monday_bus_excitement | On Monday morning, Maya takes the bus to work. She feels excited and a little nervous because a large new software project starts today, and she will finally work closely with the entire team. |
| 2 | 02_first_morning_presentation | At the short morning meeting, Maya stands in front of her colleagues. She presents her part of the technical plan for the first time, explaining what she has worked on to prepare for the week's tasks. |
| 3 | 03_boss_project_manager | After the meeting, the boss stops by Maya's desk to hand her a part of the new project. He has led teams here for ten years, and it shows — he explains exactly what he needs, calm and clear, and asks if she has any questions. |
| 4 | 04_peter_software_engineer | Maya sits down with Peter to write code. As software engineers, they solve complex problems together, and Peter jokes that he has worked on one specific error for three hours until Maya easily spots his mistake. |
| 5 | 05_eva_digital_designer | Later, Maya walks over to meet Eva, the team's designer. Eva shows Maya her beautiful sketches and explains that she has worked as a digital designer since she finished university, focusing on how users experience the product. |
| 6 | 06_meeting_the_tester | The next day, Maya sits with the software tester as he clicks quickly through the new screens and spots a small mistake at once. He laughs that his job is to break things before the users do — he has done it for five years and finds problems fast. |
| 7 | 07_wednesday_gym_reflection | On Wednesday evening, Maya attends her usual gym class. While lifting weights, she thinks about her colleagues and realizes how much she has learned about their different professions in just a few days. |
| 8 | 08_friday_canteen_lunch | The busy week ends with a shared lunch in the bright canteen. Maya sits with Peter, Eva, and the tester, discussing how hard everyone has worked, and she finally feels like a true part of the team. |
