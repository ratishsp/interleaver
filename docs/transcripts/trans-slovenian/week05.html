Week 5: Maya stocks her new kitchen

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

empty cupboards
Storyboard: Maya stands in the kitchen, noticing that the fridge and the cupboard are completely empty. She grabs a bag and heads out, ready to buy food.
Ura je šest.It is six o'clock.
Stojim v kuhinji.I am standing in the kitchen.
Lačna sem.I am hungry.
Odprem hladilnik.I open the fridge.
Popolnoma prazen je.It is completely empty.
Pogledam v omaro.I look in the cupboard.
V omari ni hrane.There is no food in the cupboard.
Nimam hrane.I have no food.
Imam samo malo vode.I only have a little water.
Zdaj bi rada hrano.I would like to have food now.
Rada imam zelenjavo.I like vegetables.
Rada imam tudi kruh.I also like bread.
Ampak tukaj nimam ničesar.But I have nothing here.
Najdem vrečko.I find a bag.
Vrečka je rdeča.The bag is red.
Dobra je za nakupovanje.It is good for shopping.
Grem iz stanovanja.I walk out of the flat.
Zaprem vrata.I close the door.
Pripravljena sem.I am ready.
Zdaj grem v supermarket.Now I am walking to the supermarket.
produce section
Storyboard: Maya enters a supermarket and pulls a basket from a stack by the door. Because she likes carrots, she grabs a large bunch from a green crate and drops it into the basket.
Stojim pred supermarketom.I stand in front of the supermarket.
Supermarket je velik in svetel.The supermarket is large and bright.
Vrata se odprejo samodejno.The door opens automatically.
Grem v trgovino.I go into the shop.
Trgovina ima veliko izdelkov.The shop has many goods.
Pri vhodu so kupi košar.There are stacks of baskets by the entrance.
Vzamem košaro.I take a basket.
Košara je rdeča in lahka.The basket is red and light.
Iščem zelenjavo.I look for vegetables.
Oddelek je prav tukaj.The section is right here.
Vidim zaboj na tleh.I see a crate on the floor.
Zaboj je zelen.The crate is green.
V zaboju je veliko korenja.There are many carrots in the crate.
Rada imam korenje.I like carrots.
Izberem velik šop.I choose a large bunch.
Šop je videti zelo svež.The bunch looks very fresh.
Šop položim v košaro.I put the bunch down into the basket.
Zdaj košara ni prazna.Now the basket is not empty.
Grem naprej v trgovino.I walk further into the shop.
the parsnip
Storyboard: Looking around, Maya spots a pale, unusual root. She reads a sign and learns it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she decides to buy it anyway out of curiosity.
Vidim čudno zelenjavo.I see a strange vegetable.
Zelenjava je dolga korenina.The vegetable is a long root.
Korenina je zelo bleda.The root is very pale.
Videti je kot bel korenček.It looks like a white carrot.
Pogledam dol na napis.I look down at the sign.
Na napisu piše "pastinak".It says "parsnip" on the sign.
Ne poznam te zelenjave.I do not know the vegetable.
Je to danska zelenjava?Is it a Danish vegetable?
Rada imam zelenjavo.I like vegetables.
Rada imam korenje.I like carrots.
Mogoče ima pastinak dober okus.Maybe the parsnip tastes good.
Pastinak vzamem v roko.I take the parsnip in my hand.
Korenina je precej velika.The root is quite big.
Rada bi jo imela.I would like to have it.
Pogledam košaro.I look at the basket.
Korenino položim v košaro.I put the root in the basket.
Rada bi poskusila nekaj novega.I would like to try something new.
the rye bread
Storyboard: At a shelf with baked goods, Maya looks for a local staple. She lifts a loaf of rye bread, surprised by how heavy it feels, and lays the bread carefully in the basket.
Stopim k polici s kruhom.I walk over to a shelf with bread.
Iščem lokalno klasiko.I am looking for a local classic.
Rada bi danski rženi kruh.I would like to have a Danish rye bread.
Pogledam polico.I look at the shelf.
Najdem rženi kruh.I find a rye bread.
Je v vrečki.It is in a bag.
Pogledam vrečko.I look at the bag.
Vzamem rženi kruh.I take the rye bread.
Dvignem kruh.I lift the bread.
Presenečena sem.I am surprised.
Kruh je zelo težek.The bread is very heavy.
Na otip je kot kamen!It feels like a stone!
Rada imam svetel kruh.I like light bread.
Toda rženi kruh je temen in težek.But the rye bread is dark and heavy.
Kruh previdno položim v košaro.I carefully lay the bread in the basket.
Tudi košara je zdaj težka.The basket is also heavy now.
bakery counter
Storyboard: The bakery counter has a member of staff waiting. Maya catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry.
Stopim do pekovskega pulta.I walk over to the bakery counter.
Vonj je zelo dober.The smell is very good.
Rada imam pecivo.I like cakes.
In res imam rada cimetove polže.And I really like cinnamon pastries.
Za pultom stoji pek.A baker stands behind the counter.
Pek me pogleda.The baker looks at me.
Nasmehne se.He smiles.
"Kaj bi želeli?" vpraša pek."What would you like to have?" the baker asks.
Pokažem na cimetovega polža.I point at a cinnamon pastry.
Pecivo izgleda slastno.The pastry looks delicious.
"Rada bi cimetovega polža, prosim," rečem."I would like to have a cinnamon pastry, please," I say.
"Ja, seveda," reče pek."Yes, of course," the baker says.
Vzame pecivo.He takes the pastry.
Pecivo položi v vrečko.He puts the pastry in a bag.
Poda vrečko čez pult.He hands the bag over the counter.
"Izvolite," reče."Here you go," he says.
Vzamem vrečko.I take the bag.
"Najlepša hvala," rečem."Thank you very much," I say.
Vrečko položim v košaro.I put the bag down into the basket.
finding the milk
Storyboard: Maya searches for the milk without luck. She stops an employee to ask for help, and he points her toward the dairy section at the back.
Zdaj potrebujem mleko.Now I need milk.
Hodim po dolgem hodniku.I walk down a long aisle.
Pogledam na polico.I look at the shelf.
Vidim vodo in sok.I can see the water and the juice.
Ampak ne morem najti mleka.But I cannot find the milk.
Gledam vsepovsod.I look everywhere.
Vidim moškega.I see a man.
Dela v supermarketu.He works in the supermarket.
Ustavim zaposlenega in se nasmehnem.I stop the employee and smile.
"Oprostite," rečem."Excuse me," I say.
"Rada bi mleko.""I would like to have milk."
"Kje je mleko?""Where is the milk?"
Moški pokaže v zadnji del trgovine.The man points to the back of the shop.
"Mlečni oddelek je tam spodaj," reče."The dairy section is down there," he says.
"Najlepša hvala," odgovorim."Thank you very much," I reply.
Grem tja dol.I walk down there.
Končno najdem hladilnik in mleko.I finally find the fridge and the milk.
Vzamem tetrapak in ga položim v košaro.I take a carton and lay it in the basket.
taste of home
Storyboard: Exploring another section, Maya hunts for a taste of Mexico because she really likes beans. She recognizes a bright can of black beans on a low shelf and reaches all the way to the back to pull it out.
Grem naprej do drugega regala.I walk on to another set of shelves.
Gledam hrano.I look at the food.
Supermarket ima veliko stvari iz drugih držav.The supermarket has many things from other countries.
Iščem okus Mehike.I look for a taste of Mexico.
Pogrešam hrano od doma.I miss the food from home.
Zelo imam rada fižol.I really like beans.
Pogosto jem črni fižol.I often eat black beans.
Pogledam nizko polico.I look at a low shelf.
Polica je nekoliko temna.The shelf is a bit dark.
Ampak vidim pisano pločevinko.But I see a colourful can.
Pločevinka stoji čisto zadaj.The can stands all the way at the back.
To je pločevinka s črnim fižolom.It is a can of black beans.
Nasmehnem se.I smile.
Sežem z roko čisto notri.I reach my arm all the way in.
Potegnem pločevinko ven.I pull the can out.
Preberem nalepko na pločevinki.I read the label on the can.
Popolna je za mojo večerjo.It is perfect for my dinner.
Pločevinko položim v košaro.I put the can in the basket.
Imam dovolj hrane.I have enough food.
the checkout
Storyboard: Maya brings the basket to the checkout. She places the food on the belt, holds her card over the machine, and packs the bag quickly. No price is spoken or shown.
Stopim do blagajne.I walk up to the checkout.
V roki imam košaro.I have the basket in my hand.
Hrano položim na trak.I put the food on the belt.
Najprej iz košare vzamem rženi kruh.First I take the rye bread from the basket.
Potem vzamem mleko in pastinak.Then I take the milk and the parsnip.
Vzamem tudi korenje in črni fižol.I also take carrots and black beans.
Na koncu položim pecivo na trak.Finally I put the pastry on the belt.
Blagajničar sedi za blagajno.The cashier sits behind the checkout.
Nasmehne se mi.He smiles at me.
"Živijo," reče."Hello," he says.
"Živijo," odgovorim."Hello," I reply.
Hitro skenira hrano.He scans the food quickly.
Vzamem svojo kartico.I take my card out.
Kartico držim nad aparatom.I hold the card over the machine.
Aparat zapiska.The machine beeps.
"Bi želeli račun?" vpraša."Would you like to have the receipt?" he asks.
"Ja, prosim, rada bi račun," rečem."Yes please, I would like to have the receipt," I say.
Odprem svojo vrečko.I open my bag.
Hitro zložim stvari v vrečko.I pack the bag quickly.
"Imejte lep dan," reče."Have a good day," he says.
putting away
Storyboard: Back in the flat, Maya empties the bag on the counter. She puts the milk and the parsnip in the fridge, and she places the bread and the can in the cupboard.
Spet sem doma v stanovanju.I am home in the flat again.
Stojim v kuhinji.I am standing in the kitchen.
Vrečko izpraznim na kuhinjski pult.I empty the bag on the kitchen counter.
Razpakiram hrano.I unpack the food.
Vzamem mleko.I take the milk.
Mleko postavim v hladilnik.I put the milk in the fridge.
Potem pogledam pastinak.Then I look at the parsnip.
To je smešna korenina.It is a funny root.
Uporabila ga bom v svoji juhi.I will use it in my soup.
Pastinak položim v hladilnik.I put the parsnip in the fridge.
Zdaj hladilnik ni prazen.Now the fridge is not empty.
Vzamem kruh.I take the bread.
Rženi kruh je zelo težek.The rye bread is very heavy.
Kruh položim v omarico.I put the bread in the cupboard.
Potem vzamem pločevinko s fižolom.Then I take the can of beans.
Fižol zdrži dolgo.Beans last a long time.
Pločevinko postavim v omarico.I put the can in the cupboard.
Zdaj je v omarici hrana.Now there is food in the cupboard.
Precej sem lačna.I am quite hungry.
Zdaj bi rada večerjala.I would like to have dinner now.
first home cooked meal
Storyboard: Ready to eat, Maya chops some vegetables and opens the can. She cooks dinner on the stove, finally making a proper meal in the flat — and afterwards she has the pastry from the bakery counter for dessert.
Kuhinja je majhna.The kitchen is small.
Ampak štedilnik je dober.But the stove is good.
Vzamem pastinak in korenček.I take the parsnip and a carrot.
Vzamem tudi nož.I also take a knife.
Nož je oster.The knife is sharp.
Narežem pastinak in korenček.I cut the parsnip and the carrot.
Odprem pločevinko.I open the can.
Hrano dam v lonec.I put the food in a pot.
Lonec je na štedilniku.The pot is on the stove.
Zdaj je hrana topla.Now the food is warm.
Čudovito diši.It smells lovely.
Vzamem rženi kruh.I take the rye bread.
Odrežem rezino.I cut a slice.
Krožnik je na mizi.The plate is on the table.
Končno jem pravi obrok v stanovanju.Finally, I am eating a proper meal in the flat.
Potem pojem cimetovega polža.Afterwards I eat the cinnamon pastry.
Ima sladek okus.It tastes sweet.
