Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Korean with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
비행기가 지금 코펜하겐에 있어요.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
1월이에요.It is January.
저는 아주 설레요.I am very excited.
저는 가방에 책을 챙겨 넣어요.I pack my book into my bag.
제 옆자리에 승객이 한 분 있어요.There is a passenger next to me.
그가 저를 보며 미소 지어요.He looks at me and smiles.
"안녕하세요," 그가 말해요."Hello," he says.
"안녕하세요," 제가 말해요."Hello," I say.
"이름이 어떻게 되세요?" 그가 물어요."What is your name?" he asks.
"제 이름은 마야예요," 제가 말해요."My name is Maya," I say.
"어디서 오셨어요, 마야 씨?" 그가 물어요."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
저는 기쁘게 미소 지어요.I smile happily.
"멕시코에서 왔어요," 제가 대답해요."I come from Mexico," I answer.
"덴마크 분이세요?" 제가 물어요."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
"네, 저는 덴마크 사람이에요," 그가 말해요."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
"덴마크에 오신 것을 환영합니다," 그가 말해요."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
"정말 감사합니다," 제가 말해요."Thank you very much," I say.
이제 우리는 이곳에 있어요.Now we are here.
저는 준비됐어요.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
저는 지금 밖에 있어요.I am outside now.
제 여행 가방은 크고 무거워요.My suitcase is big and heavy.
택시가 한 대 있어요.There is a taxi.
저는 여행 가방을 들어 택시 안에 넣어요.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
기사님은 친절한 분이에요.The driver is a friendly man.
"주소가 어떻게 되시죠?" 그가 물어요."What is the address?" he asks.
"파르크베이 12번지예요," 제가 말해요."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
그는 고개를 끄덕이고 차를 몰아요.He nods and drives.
"관광객이신가요?" 기사님이 물어요."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
"아니요, 관광객이 아니에요," 제가 대답해요."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
"어디서 오셨어요?" 그가 물어요."Where do you come from?" he asks.
"멕시코에서 왔어요," 제가 말해요."I come from Mexico," I say.
저는 창밖을 내다봐요.I look out the window.
저는 아주 자랑스러워요.I am very proud.
"저는 소프트웨어 엔지니어예요," 제가 말해요."I am a software engineer," I say.
"제 새로운 직장이 여기 코펜하겐에 있거든요.""My new job is here in Copenhagen."
그가 미소를 지어요.He smiles.
"정말 멋진 일이네요.""How exciting."
"덴마크에서 행운을 빕니다," 그가 말해요."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
"정말 감사합니다," 제가 말해요."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
문이 열려 있어요.The door is open.
문지방이 높아요.The threshold is high.
여행 가방이 너무 무거워요!The suitcase is very heavy!
하지만 이제 여행 가방은 복도에 있어요.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
여기는 완전히 깜깜해요.It is completely dark here.
스위치가 어디에 있죠?Where is the switch?
벽이 차가워요.The wall is cold.
스위치가 여기에 있나요?Is the switch here?
네, 저기 스위치가 있어요!Yes, there is the switch!
이제 불이 켜졌어요.Now there is light.
저는 아파트에 있어요.I am in the apartment.
작지만 좋아요.It is small, but it is nice.
여기에 거실이 있어요.Here is a living room.
소파와 탁자가 있어요.There is a sofa and a table.
가구가 갖춰진 집이에요.It is a furnished home.
여행 가방은 바닥에 있어요.The suitcase is on the floor.
저는 너무 피곤해요.I am very tired.
하지만 기쁘기도 해요.But I am also happy.
저는 코펜하겐에 있어요!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
저는 창가에 서 있어요.I am standing by the window.
유리가 차가워요.The glass is cold.
일월이에요.It is January.
밖을 내다봐요.I look out.
오늘 코펜하겐은 회색빛이에요.Copenhagen is grey today.
밖은 추워요.It is cold outside.
거리에 눈이 있어요.There is snow on the street.
하지만 여기는 따뜻해요.But it is warm here.
아파트가 좋아요.The apartment is good.
멕시코를 생각해요.I think about Mexico.
멕시코에는 햇빛이 있어요.In Mexico, there is sun.
거기도 따뜻해요.It is also warm there.
햇빛이 조금 그리워요.I miss the sun a little.
하지만 미소를 지어요.But I smile.
새로운 도시예요.It is a new city.
저는 지금 덴마크에 있어요.I am in Denmark now.
좋은 시작이에요.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
나는 지금 거실에 있다.I am in the living room now.
작은 소파가 있다.There is a small sofa.
나는 멕시코에 계신 엄마에게 전화를 건다.I call my mother in Mexico.
"안녕, 엄마," 내가 말한다."Hi Mom," I say.
"안녕, 마야," 엄마가 말씀하신다."Hi Maya," my mother says.
"어디니?" 엄마가 물으신다."Where are you?" she asks.
"코펜하겐이에요," 내가 대답한다."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
"지금 아파트에 있어요.""I am in the apartment now."
"정말 다행이구나," 엄마가 말씀하신다."That is so good," my mother says.
"집은 크니?" 엄마가 물으신다."Is it big?" she asks.
"아니요, 작아요," 내가 대답한다."No, it is small," I answer.
"하지만 아늑하고, 저는 행복해요.""But it is cozy, and I am happy."
"춥니?" 엄마가 물으신다."Is it cold?" she asks.
"네, 밖은 추워요," 내가 말한다."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
"하지만 여긴 따뜻해요.""But it is warm in here."
"저는 안전해요.""I am safe."
"다행이구나," 엄마가 말씀하신다."That is good," my mother says.
나는 미소 짓는다.I smile.
덴마크는 나의 새로운 집이다.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
나는 문을 연다.I open my door.
나는 계단 복도에 서 있다.I am standing in the stairwell.
조용하다.It is quiet.
문 하나가 열린다.A door opens.
한 여자가 나에게 미소를 짓는다.A woman smiles at me.
"안녕하세요," 그녀가 말한다."Hi," she says.
"이름이 어떻게 되세요?" 그녀가 묻는다."What is your name?" she asks.
"제 이름은 마야예요," 나는 대답한다."My name is Maya," I answer.
"어디서 오셨어요?" 그녀가 묻는다."Where do you come from?" she asks.
"멕시코에서 왔어요," 나는 말한다."I come from Mexico," I say.
"이 건물에 오신 것을 환영해요," 여자가 말한다."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
"제 이름은 니나예요," 그녀가 말한다."My name is Nina," she says.
"제가 이웃이에요.""I am your neighbour."
니나는 매우 친절하다.Nina is very friendly.
그녀는 천천히 그리고 또렷하게 말한다.She speaks slowly and clearly.
"여기가 제 집 문이에요," 그녀가 말한다."Here is my door," she says.
"기꺼이 도와드릴게요.""I am happy to help."
"그냥 노크하세요," 니나가 말한다."You just knock," Nina says.
"정말 감사합니다, 니나," 나는 대답한다."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
나는 매우 기쁘다.I am very happy.
이제 나는 코펜하겐에 친구가 있다.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
이제 밤이에요.Now it is night.
코펜하겐에서의 첫날 밤이에요.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
저는 욕실에 있어요.I am in the bathroom.
아주 작아요.It is very small.
세면대가 있어요.There is a sink.
제 칫솔은 빨간색이에요.My toothbrush is red.
칫솔에 치약을 짜요.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
이를 닦아요.I brush my teeth.
물이 차가워요.The water is cold.
세수를 해요.I wash my face.
얼굴을 닦아요.I dry my face.
침대로 가요.I go over to my bed.
침대는 좁아요.The bed is narrow.
하지만 따뜻하고 푹신해요.But it is warm and soft.
지금 이불 속에 누워 있어요.I am lying under the duvet now.
제 핸드폰은 탁자 위에 놓여 있어요.My phone is lying on the table.
알람을 맞춰요.I set my alarm.
내일 여덟 시에 울려요.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
피곤하지만, 준비됐어요.I am tired, but I am ready.
잘 자요, 코펜하겐.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
