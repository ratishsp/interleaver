Week 10: Maya buys winter clothes

Norwegian with English gloss, line-aligned.

a cold morning
Storyboard: It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to the bus stop on her way to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter.
Det er morgen i København.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Jeg går til busstoppet.I walk to the bus stop.
Været er veldig kaldt i dag.The weather is very cold today.
Det blåser.It is windy.
Vinden er iskald.The wind is ice-cold.
Jeg fryser.I am freezing.
Jeg har på meg en tynn jakke.I am wearing a thin jacket.
Jakken min er ikke varm i det hele tatt.My jacket is not warm at all.
Jeg har også på meg tynne sko.I am also wearing thin shoes.
Jeg har ikke på meg et varmt skjerf.I am not wearing a warm scarf.
Hendene mine er veldig kalde.My hands are very cold.
Jeg skjelver.I am shivering.
Vinteren i Danmark er hard.The winter in Denmark is hard.
Jeg trenger varme klær.I need warm clothes.
Jeg trenger en tykk genser.I need a thick sweater.
Jeg trenger også et stort skjerf.I also need a large scarf.
Og jeg trenger varme hansker.And I need warm gloves.
Jeg går veldig fort.I walk very fast.
Jeg kjøper vinterklær etter jobb.I am buying winter clothes after work.
entering the shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things.
Jeg er ferdig på jobb.I am finished at work.
Jeg går nedover gågaten.I walk down the pedestrian street.
Vinden er fortsatt veldig kald.The wind is still very cold.
Jeg ser en stor klesbutikk.I see a large clothing shop.
Butikken er veldig lys.The shop is very bright.
Jeg går inn i butikken.I go into the shop.
Det er deilig varmt her inne.It is wonderfully warm in here.
En ekspeditør står bak kassen.A shop assistant stands behind the register.
Hun ser på meg og smiler.She looks at me and smiles.
"Hei," sier hun."Hello," she says.
"Hei," svarer jeg."Hello," I answer.
"Jeg fryser veldig," sier jeg."I am freezing a lot," I say.
"Jeg har bare en tynn jakke på.""I only have a thin jacket on."
"Jeg vil gjerne finne noen varme ting.""I would like to find some warm things."
Ekspeditøren smiler igjen.The shop assistant smiles again.
"Det er også veldig kaldt i dag," sier hun."It is also very cold today," she says.
"Vi har mange tykke gensere.""We have many thick sweaters."
"Vi har også varme vinterklær.""We also have warm winter clothes."
"Du kan bare se deg rundt," sier hun vennlig."You just take a look," she says kindly.
"Tusen takk," svarer jeg."Many thanks," I answer.
the red sweater
Storyboard: Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it.
Jeg ser på klærne på hyllene.I look at the clothes on the shelves.
Jeg ser en tykk genser.I see a thick sweater.
Den er rød.It is red.
Jeg trenger en varm genser.I need a warm sweater.
Butikken har mange størrelser.The shop has many sizes.
Jeg finner en størrelse medium.I find a size medium.
Jeg går inn i prøverommet.I walk into the fitting room.
Jeg tar på meg den røde genseren.I put the red sweater on.
Jeg har den på og ser i det store speilet.I have it on and look in the big mirror.
Genseren passer perfekt.The sweater fits perfectly.
Den er utrolig varm.It is incredibly warm.
Jeg elsker den røde fargen.I love the red colour.
Jeg ser på prislappen.I look at the price tag.
Genseren koster tre hundre kroner.The sweater costs three hundred kroner.
Det er en god pris for en tykk genser.It is a good price for a thick sweater.
Jeg tar av meg genseren igjen.I take the sweater off again.
Jeg kjøper den.I buy it.
the tight green coat
Storyboard: Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a larger size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. The assistant brings her a warm dark-blue winter jacket in her size instead — it fits perfectly, and Maya decides to take it.
Jeg trenger også en vinterjakke.I also need a winter jacket.
Jeg ser på mer klær i butikken.I look at more clothes in the shop.
Jeg finner en flott, grønn kåpe.I find a beautiful, green coat.
Det er et veldig vakkert design.It is a very beautiful design.
Jeg tar på meg den grønne kåpen.I put the green coat on.
Men den er for liten.But it is too small.
Den er veldig stram over skuldrene.It is very tight across the shoulders.
"Har dere den i en annen størrelse?" spør jeg."Do you have it in another size?" I ask.
Ekspeditøren kikker.The shop assistant looks.
"Nei, dessverre," svarer hun."No, unfortunately," she answers.
Så jeg henger den grønne kåpen tilbake.So I hang the green coat back.
Ekspeditøren finner en annen jakke til meg.The shop assistant finds another jacket for me.
Det er en varm, mørkeblå vinterjakke.It is a warm, dark-blue winter jacket.
Jeg tar på meg den mørkeblå jakken.I put the dark-blue jacket on.
Den passer perfekt.It fits perfectly.
Den er deilig stor og ikke stram.It is nice and big and not tight.
Jeg ser i et stort speil.I look in a large mirror.
Jeg liker den godt.I like it.
"Jeg tar den," sier jeg."I will take it," I say.
the blue scarf
Storyboard: Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases.
Jeg går bort til et bord i butikken.I walk over to a table in the shop.
Det er mange ting på bordet.There are many things on the table.
Jeg har ikke på meg et skjerf i dag.I am not wearing a scarf today.
Jeg fryser veldig om halsen.I am very cold around my neck.
Jeg ser et blått skjerf.I see a blue scarf.
Det er et veldig langt skjerf.It is a very long scarf.
Det er laget av ull.It is made of wool.
Jeg tar det blå skjerfet.I take the blue scarf.
Jeg tar det rundt halsen.I put it around my neck.
Det føles deilig varmt.It feels wonderfully warm.
"Det er en flott farge," sier ekspeditøren."It is a nice colour," the shop assistant says.
"Ja, og det er veldig mykt," sier jeg."Yes, and it is very soft," I say.
Jeg ser på prislappen.I look at the price tag.
Det koster hundre og femti kroner.It costs one hundred and fifty kroner.
Det er en god pris for et varmt skjerf.It is a good price for a warm scarf.
Jeg tar det av igjen.I take it off again.
"Jeg kjøper det også," sier jeg til henne."I am buying it too," I say to her.
Jeg legger det blå skjerfet sammen med den røde genseren.I put the blue scarf together with the red sweater.
the black gloves
Storyboard: Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well.
Jeg har fortsatt på meg den tynne jakken min.I am still wearing my thin jacket.
Jeg tenker på de kalde hendene mine fra i morges.I think about my cold hands from this morning.
Jeg trenger tykke hansker.I need thick gloves.
Jeg ser meg rundt i den lyse butikken.I look around the bright shop.
Jeg ser en stor kurv.I see a large basket.
Kurven er full av hansker.The basket is full of gloves.
Jeg finner et par svarte vinterhansker.I find a pair of black winter gloves.
De er veldig tykke og myke.They are very thick and soft.
Jeg tar på meg hanskene.I put the gloves on.
De passer meg perfekt.They fit me perfectly.
Jeg spør den smilende ekspeditøren.I ask the smiling shop assistant.
"Hva koster de svarte hanskene?" spør jeg."What do the black gloves cost?" I ask.
"De koster hundre og tjue kroner," svarer hun."They cost one hundred and twenty kroner," she answers.
Hundre og tjue kroner er en god pris.One hundred and twenty kroner is a good price.
Hendene mine er allerede varme.My hands are already warm.
"Jeg tar dem," sier jeg glad."I will take them," I say happily.
Jeg tar av meg hanskene igjen.I take the gloves off again.
Jeg legger de svarte hanskene sammen med den røde genseren min og det blå skjerfet mitt.I put the black gloves together with my red sweater and my blue scarf.
Nå er jeg klar til å betale.Now I am ready to pay.
paying for the clothes
Storyboard: Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the dark-blue winter jacket, the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is one thousand five hundred kroner, and Maya pays with her card.
Jeg går bort til kassen med de nye klærne mine.I walk over to the counter with my new clothes.
Ekspeditøren smiler vennlig til meg.The shop assistant smiles kindly at me.
"Er det alt?" spør hun."Is that everything?" she asks.
"Ja, det er alt," svarer jeg."Yes, that is everything," I answer.
Hun skanner alle de nye tingene mine.She scans all my new things.
Det er en genser, en jakke, et skjerf og hansker.There is a sweater, a jacket, a scarf and gloves.
"Nå fryser du ikke," sier hun glad."Now you will not freeze," she says happily.
"Det blir ett tusen fem hundre kroner, takk.""That will be one thousand five hundred kroner, please."
Klærne er dyre, men de er veldig varme.The clothes are expensive, but they are very warm.
Jeg finner fram betalingskortet mitt.I take out my payment card.
"Jeg betaler med kort," sier jeg."I am paying by card," I say.
"Du kan bare betale her," sier hun."You can just pay here," she says.
Hun peker på en liten, svart maskin.She points at a small, black machine.
Jeg holder kortet mitt over maskinen.I hold my card over the machine.
Den bipper, og betalingen er godkjent.It beeps, and the payment is approved.
"Vil du ha kvitteringen?" spør ekspeditøren."Do you want the receipt?" the shop assistant asks.
"Nei takk," svarer jeg."No thank you," I answer.
Nå eier jeg masse fine vinterklær.Now I own a lot of nice winter clothes.
stepping into the cold
Storyboard: Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She pulls on the red sweater, zips her new dark-blue winter jacket over it, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm.
"En pose?" spør ekspeditøren."A bag?" the shop assistant asks.
"Nei takk," sier jeg."No thank you," I say.
Jeg har ikke bruk for en pose.I do not need a bag.
Jeg tar på meg de nye klærne med en gang.I put the new clothes on right away.
Først tar jeg på meg den tykke, røde genseren.First, I put on the thick, red sweater.
Så tar jeg på meg den mørkeblå vinterjakken.Then, I put on the dark-blue winter jacket.
Jeg drar opp glidelåsen på den varme jakken.I zip the warm jacket.
Etterpå tar jeg det lange, blå skjerfet.Afterwards, I take the long, blue scarf.
Jeg vikler det rundt halsen.I wrap it around my neck.
Til slutt tar jeg på meg de nye, svarte hanskene.Finally, I put on the new, black gloves.
Nå har jeg på meg alle de varme klærne mine.Now I have all my warm clothes on.
Jeg smiler og sier ha det.I smile and say goodbye.
Jeg går ut av butikken.I walk out of the shop.
Jeg står ute på den kalde gaten igjen.I stand out on the cold street again.
Det er isnende kaldt.It is freezing cold.
Vinden blåser hardt.The wind blows hard.
Men jeg fryser slett ikke.But I am not freezing at all.
Nå er jeg klar for vinteren.Now I am ready for winter.
