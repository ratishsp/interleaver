Week 13: Maya goes to a café

Indonesian with English gloss, line-aligned.

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.
Saat ini musim dingin di Kopenhagen.It is winter in Copenhagen.
Aku sedang berjalan menyusuri jalan.I am walking down the street.
Anginnya sangat dingin.The wind is very cold.
Angin bertiup kencang hari ini.It is blowing hard today.
Aku mencondongkan tubuh melawan angin dingin.I lean against the cold wind.
Aku menarik syalku ke atas.I pull my scarf up.
Wajahku membeku.My face is freezing.
Aku melihat ke sekeliling.I look around.
Aku ingin masuk ke tempat yang hangat.I would like to get into the warmth.
Aku melihat sebuah kafe kecil di sudut jalan.I see a small café on the corner.
Jendela-jendelanya berembun.The windows are fogged up.
Kelihatannya hangat di dalam sana.It looks warm in there.
Aku berjalan menghampiri kafe itu.I walk over to the café.
Aku membuka pintu yang berat itu.I open the heavy door.
Aku melangkah masuk.I step inside.
Di sini tercium aroma kopi.It smells like coffee here.
Udaranya hangat dan nyaman.The air is nice and warm.
Aku menutup pintu di belakangku.I close the door behind me.
Angin dingin tetap berada di luar.The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
Saya ada di dalam kafe sekarang.I am inside the café now.
Di sini sangat hangat.It is very warm here.
Saya melepas jaket musim dingin saya yang tebal.I take off my thick winter coat.
Jaket itu berat.It is heavy.
Saya menemukan gantungan di dinding.I find a hook on the wall.
Saya menggantung jaket saya di gantungan itu.I hang my coat on the hook.
Saya juga melepas syal dan sarung tangan saya.I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
Kafe ini kecil dan nyaman.The café is small and cozy.
Saya mencari tempat duduk yang bagus.I look for a good seat.
Ada meja kecil di dekat jendela.There is a small table by the window.
Meja itu berada di sebelah radiator.The table is next to a radiator.
Saya berjalan ke meja itu.I walk over to the table.
Saya duduk di kursi.I sit down on the chair.
Tangan saya masih dingin.My hands are still cold.
Saya saling menggosokkan kedua tangan saya.I rub them together.
Tangan saya menjadi lebih hangat.My hands get warmer.
Rasanya luar biasa.It feels fantastic.
Sekarang saya hampir merasa hangat.Now I am almost warm.
Saya lapar dan haus.I am hungry and thirsty.
Saya ingin memesan sesuatu sekarang.I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
Saya berjalan ke etalase kaca.I walk over to the glass counter.
Etalase itu penuh dengan kue dan roti.It is full of cakes and bread.
Saya melihat berbagai macam makanan itu.I look at the many things.
Semuanya terlihat sangat lezat.They look very delicious.
Ada label-label kecil di sebelah kue-kue tersebut.There are small signs next to the cakes.
Label-label itu ditulis tangan.The signs are handwritten.
Saya membaca nama-nama pada label itu.I read the names on the signs.
Ada banyak jenis pastri.There are many kinds of pastry.
Sulit untuk memilih.It is hard to choose.
Ada sebuah kue cokelat besar.There is a large chocolate cake.
Tapi saya melihat sepotong pastri yang lain.But I look at another piece of pastry.
Itu adalah roti gulung kayu manis yang besar.It is a large cinnamon roll.
Ada banyak gula di atasnya.It has a lot of sugar on top.
Saya sangat menyukai rasa kayu manis.I love the taste of cinnamon.
Itu sempurna untuk hari yang dingin di musim dingin.It is perfect for a cold winter day.
Saya sangat menginginkan roti gulung kayu manis itu.I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
Sekarang saya siap untuk memesan.Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
Saya berjalan ke kasir.I walk over to the register.
Seorang pria muda berdiri di belakang konter.A young man stands behind the counter.
Dia adalah barista di kafe ini.He is a barista in the café.
Saya tersenyum kepadanya.I smile at him.
Dia mengangguk dengan ramah.He nods warmly.
"Hai," katanya."Hi," he says.
"Anda ingin pesan apa?" tanyanya."What would you like?" he asks.
"Hai," jawab saya."Hi," I answer.
"Saya ingin pesan satu kopi hitam, terima kasih.""I would like a black coffee, please."
"Dan bolehkah saya juga minta satu roti kayu manis?""And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
"Tentu saja," katanya."Of course," he says.
"Satu kopi hitam dan satu roti kayu manis.""A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
"Apakah Anda mau duduk di sini?" tanyanya."Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
"Ya, terima kasih, saya akan duduk di sebelah sana," kata saya."Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
"Baiklah," kata barista itu."Alright," the barista says.
"Saya akan membuatkan kopi Anda sekarang.""I will make your coffee now."
"Terima kasih banyak," kata saya."Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
Saya menunggu di konter.I wait at the counter.
Dia membuat kopi saya.He makes my coffee.
Dia meletakkan secangkir kopi panas di atas nampan kayu.He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
Dia juga meletakkan piring kecil di atas nampan itu.He also puts a small plate on the tray.
Roti kayu manis saya ada di atas piring itu.My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
Aromanya sangat harum.It smells lovely.
"Silakan, ini pesanan Anda," katanya."Here you go, your order," he says.
"Terima kasih banyak," jawab saya."Thank you very much," I reply.
"Terima kasih atas pelayanan yang baik.""Thank you for the good service."
"Sama-sama," katanya dengan ramah."You are welcome," he says kindly.
Saya mengambil nampan itu dengan kedua tangan.I take the tray with both hands.
Saya berjalan melintasi lantai dengan sangat hati-hati.I walk very carefully across the floor.
Saya berjalan kembali ke meja saya di dekat jendela.I walk back to my table by the window.
Saya meletakkan nampan itu di atas meja.I put the tray down on the table.
Sekarang saya ingin duduk.Now I would like to sit down.
Saya siap untuk waktu istirahat yang menyenangkan.I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
Saya sekarang duduk di meja kecil saya.I now sit at my small table.
Di sini terasa menyenangkan dan hangat.It is lovely and warm here.
Saya melihat roti kayu manis saya.I look at my cinnamon roll.
Aromanya manis dan enak.It smells sweet and good.
Saya mengambil gigitan kecil.I take a small bite.
Rotinya benar-benar segar dan hangat.The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
Rasanya sangat enak.It tastes really good.
Saya meminum sedikit kopi hitam saya.I drink a little of my black coffee.
Akhirnya, jari-jari saya kembali hangat.Finally, my fingers are warm again.
Saya bersantai di kursi yang empuk.I relax in the soft chair.
Kafe ini hampir penuh dengan orang.The café is almost full of people.
Orang-orang duduk dalam kelompok-kelompok kecil.People sit in small groups.
Mereka mengobrol dan tertawa bersama.They talk and laugh together.
Saya mendengarkan suara-suara pelan di ruangan ini.I listen to the low voices in the room.
Suaranya terdengar sangat nyaman.The sound is very cozy.
Saya melihat ke luar jendela besar itu.I look out of the large window.
Cuaca di luar kelabu dan berangin.The weather outside is gray and windy.
Sekarang salju benar-benar turun lagi.Now it is actually snowing again.
Kepingan salju putih turun perlahan ke jalanan.White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
Saya hanya merasa senang bisa berada di dalam.I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
Saya makan potongan terakhir roti kayu manis saya.I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
Rasanya sangat enak.It tastes really good.
Tapi ini juga sangat manis.But it is also very sweet.
Cangkir kopi saya benar-benar kosong.My coffee cup is completely empty.
Tiba-tiba saya merasa sangat haus.I am suddenly very thirsty.
Saya ingin sedikit air dingin.I would like to have some cold water.
Seorang pria muda berjalan melewati meja saya.A young man walks past my table.
Dia bekerja di kafe ini.He works here in the café.
Dia mengumpulkan cangkir dan piring kosong.He collects empty cups and plates.
Saya menangkap pandangannya.I catch his eye.
Dia berhenti di meja saya dan tersenyum.He stops at my table and smiles.
"Permisi," kata saya dengan sopan."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Ya?" jawab pria muda itu."Yes?" answers the young man.
"Boleh saya minta segelas air keran?" tanya saya."May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
"Ya, tentu saja," katanya."Yes, of course," he says.
"Terima kasih banyak," kata saya."Thank you very much," I say.
Dia mengambil piring dan cangkir kosong saya.He takes my empty plate and cup.
Dia berjalan kembali ke konter.He walks back to the counter.
Tidak lama kemudian dia datang membawa segelas air dingin.A little later he comes with a cold glass of water.
asking for the bill
Storyboard: Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab.
Saya meminum air saya.I drink my water.
Airnya dingin dan segar.The water is cold and nice.
Saya mengambil tas saya dari lantai.I take my bag from the floor.
Saya sudah selesai dengan istirahat saya.I am finished with my break.
Saya berjalan ke meja kasir.I walk up to the counter.
Barista yang ramah itu masih berdiri di sana.The friendly barista is still standing there.
Dia menatap saya sambil tersenyum.He looks at me with a smile.
"Apakah Anda menginginkan yang lain?" tanyanya."Would you like anything else?" he asks.
"Tidak, terima kasih," jawab saya."No thank you," I answer.
"Saya ingin membayar.""I would like to pay."
"Ya, tentu saja," katanya."Yes, of course," he says.
Dia melihat ke layar kecilnya.He looks at his little screen.
"Satu kopi hitam dan satu roti kayu manis," katanya."One black coffee and one cinnamon roll," he says.
"Ya, itu benar," kata saya."Yes, that is correct," I say.
"Apakah Anda ingin membayar dengan kartu atau uang tunai?" tanyanya."Do you want to pay with card or cash?" he asks.
"Saya ingin membayar dengan kartu," jawab saya."I would like to pay with a card," I answer.
"Baik.""Fine."
"Semuanya jadi delapan puluh lima kroner," katanya."That will be eighty-five kroner," he says.
Saya mengeluarkan kartu pembayaran saya.I take out my payment card.
paying and leaving
Storyboard: Maya taps her card on the terminal to pay eighty-five kroner. She puts on her heavy coat, thanks the staff for a nice time, and pushes the front door open to face the cold again.
Barista itu tersenyum kepada saya.The barista smiles at me.
Saya menahan kartu saya di atas terminal.I hold my card over the terminal.
Mesin itu berbunyi bip.The machine beeps.
"Apakah Anda mau struk?" tanyanya."Do you want a receipt?" he asks.
"Tidak, terima kasih," jawab saya."No thank you," I answer.
Saya berjalan ke arah dinding.I walk over to the wall.
Saya mengambil jaket musim dingin saya yang tebal dari sebuah gantungan.I take my thick winter coat from a hook.
Saya memakai jaket itu.I put the coat on.
Saya juga memakai syal saya.I also put my scarf on.
Saya juga memakai sarung tangan saya.I also put my gloves on.
Sekarang saya siap menghadapi udara dingin.Now I am ready for the cold.
Saya melihat ke arah barista itu lagi.I look at the barista again.
"Terima kasih banyak atas kopinya," kata saya."Thank you very much for the coffee," I say.
"Sama-sama," katanya sambil tersenyum."You are welcome," he says with a smile.
"Semoga hari Anda menyenangkan.""Have a good day."
"Anda juga," kata saya."Likewise," I say.
Saya berjalan menuju pintu depan.I walk over to the front door.
Saya mendorong pintu yang berat itu hingga terbuka.I push the heavy door open.
Anginnya dingin, tapi saya merasa nyaman.The wind is cold, but I feel good.
