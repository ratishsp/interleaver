# Week 13 — Storyboard (Maya goes to a café)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Modal *vil gerne*; politeness; *må jeg bede om*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_escaping_the_cold | Maya walks down the street and feels the freezing winter wind. She sees a cozy café with warm lights and decides to go inside to escape the cold. |
| 2 | 02_finding_a_seat | She opens the door and immediately smells fresh coffee. She takes off her thick winter coat, hangs it on a chair, and sits at a small table by the window. |
| 3 | 03_the_pastry_case | Maya walks up to the counter to look at the food. She studies the delicious cakes and fresh sandwiches behind the glass to see what she wants. |
| 4 | 04_polite_ordering | She speaks to the friendly barista and politely states what she would like. She asks for a hot cup of coffee and requests a warm cheese sandwich. |
| 5 | 05_taking_the_tray | The barista places her plate and a blue cup on a wooden tray. She thanks him politely, carefully carries the items back to her table, and sits down. |
| 6 | 06_enjoying_the_warmth | Maya eats the tasty sandwich and slowly drinks her hot coffee. She watches the busy people outside in the cold and feels completely warm again. |
| 7 | 07_one_more_thing | She finishes her lunch but really wants something sweet. She returns to the counter and politely asks if she may have a piece of chocolate cake. |
| 8 | 08_asking_for_the_bill | Maya finishes the soft dessert and decides it is time to go. She walks back to the counter, politely asks for the bill, and takes out her card. |
| 9 | 09_paying_and_leaving | The barista says the total is one hundred and fifty kroner. Maya pays, thanks the staff for a nice visit, and steps back out into the chilly afternoon. |
