Week 19: Maya starts a new project with the team

monday bus excitement

Ma tunnen oma kolleegi Peterit hästi.I know my colleague Peter well.

boss project manager

peter software engineer

"Ma olen selle vea kallal töötanud kolm tundi.""I have worked on this error for three hours."
Ma naeratan ja vaatan koodi hoolikalt.I smile and look closely at the code.

See on väga väike viga.It is a very small mistake.
"Proovi siia vaadata," ütlen ma ja osutan ekraanile."Try to look here," I say and point at the screen.
"Sa oled siin teinud väikese trükivea.""You have made a small typo here."

Siis naerab ta valjusti.Then he laughs loudly.

eva digital designer

meeting the tester
