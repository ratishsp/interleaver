# Week 26 — Storyboard (A Monsoon Birthday)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Perfect **-ിട്ടുണ്ട്** vs past **-ി** contrast

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_endless_monsoon_rain | Maya wakes up to the deep, steady drum of rain on her roof. The monsoon is at its July peak, raining continuously for days, and as she makes her morning coffee, she appreciates how the constant wet weather has completely transformed the city into a lush, green landscape. |
| 2 | 02_veranda_downpour | During an afternoon break at the Infopark office, Maya stands on the covered cafeteria veranda with Prakash and Deepa. The sky turns pitch black, and they watch in awe as the heaviest downpour of the season so far crashes down around them while they sip hot tea. |
| 3 | 03_birthday_eve_call | On the eve of her birthday, Maya video calls her family in Mexico. Talking over the sound of Lola barking in the background, she chats with her parents, Sofia, and Diego, laughing about old family birthday traditions and feeling a warm, sudden wave of longing for home. |
| 4 | 04_thirty_second_birthday | Maya wakes up the next morning, officially thirty-two years old. As she steps out into the hallway, she is greeted by Anitha and Tommy; Anitha warmly wishes her a happy birthday and hands her a small container of homemade sweet payasam to start her special day. |
| 5 | 05_rainy_commute_reflection | Riding the bus to work through the waterlogged streets, Maya looks out the wet window. She allows herself a quiet moment of reflection on this being her first birthday away from home, comparing the nervous newcomer who arrived in January to the independent, settled woman she has become. |
| 6 | 06_office_celebration | At the afternoon tea break, Maya is called to the meeting room — where a small, beautifully decorated cake is waiting for her. Prakash and Deepa gather the team for a quick, cheerful birthday song before everyone drifts back to work. |
| 7 | 07_birthday_dinner | That evening, Maya treats Anitha, Prakash, and Deepa — all invited earlier in the week — to a celebratory dinner at a cozy, well-lit restaurant in Panampilly Nagar, ordering plates of warm, spicy food to share while the heavy monsoon rain continues to wash the streets outside. |
| 8 | 08_into_the_monsoon_night | As the dinner winds down, the four of them step out under the restaurant awning into the rain-washed night. Watching Anitha laugh with Prakash and Deepa while they wait for their autos, Maya realizes that bringing her neighbor and her coworkers together was the best gift she could have asked for. She and Anitha share an auto home through the glistening wet streets — thirty-two, and quietly at home in her life. |
