Week 8: Maya explores the city

Spanish with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
Es sábado por la mañana.It is Saturday morning.
El sol brilla sobre la ciudad.The sun is shining over the city.
Bajo a la calle.I go down to the street.
Mi vecina, Nina, está fuera de nuestro edificio.My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
"Buenos días, Maya", dice ella y sonríe."Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
"Buenos días, Nina", respondo."Good morning, Nina," I answer.
"Hoy tengo el día libre", le digo."I have the day off today," I tell her.
"Voy a dar una vuelta por la ciudad.""I am heading out into the city."
"¿Adónde vas?", pregunta Nina."Where are you going?" Nina asks.
"Voy caminando hacia el agua", digo."I am walking down to the water," I say.
"Pero no conozco el camino.""But I do not know the way."
Nina señala calle abajo.Nina points down the street.
"Afortunadamente, es muy fácil", dice ella."Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
"Sigue derecho por aquí.""Go straight ahead here."
"Y gira a la izquierda en el parque.""And turn left at the park."
"Entonces bajas directo al puerto.""Then you come right down to the harbour."
"Muchas gracias por la ayuda, Nina", digo."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
"De nada, y que tengas un buen paseo", responde ella."You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
Me despido de ella con la mano.I wave to her.
Comienzo mi caminata hacia el agua.I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
Sigo la ruta de Nina.I follow Nina's route.
El aire está fresco y frío.The air is fresh and cold.
Ahora camino por la calle principal.Now I walk down the main street.
Primero solo camino todo derecho.First I just walk straight ahead.
Hay mucho tráfico en la calle.There is a lot of traffic on the street.
En el camino veo tres bicicletas amarillas.On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
Las bicicletas pasan rápidamente junto a mí.The bicycles ride quickly past me.
Después de eso camino más adelante.After that I walk further ahead.
Luego llego a un parque grande.Then I come to a large park.
Ahora recuerdo las palabras de Nina.Now I remember Nina's words.
"Gira a la izquierda en el parque", dice ella."Turn left at the park," she says.
Aquí giro a la izquierda.Here I turn left.
Un poco después veo una puerta abierta.A little later I see an open door.
Es una pequeña panadería.It is a small bakery.
Hay un olor delicioso a café fresco.There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
Me encanta el pan caliente.I love warm bread.
Hoy la ciudad está encantadora.Today the city is lovely.
Después sigo caminando hacia el agua.Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
Llego a una plaza grande.I arrive at a large square.
Hay mucha gente.There are many people.
Pero no veo el agua.But I do not see the water.
Busco el puerto.I am looking for the harbour.
Veo a un hombre mayor en la acera.I see an older man on the pavement.
Lo detengo.I stop him.
"Disculpe", digo."Excuse me," I say.
"¿Dónde está el puerto?""Where is the harbour?"
El hombre mayor asiente.The older man nods.
Señala hacia adelante.He points ahead.
"Cruce la plaza todo derecho", dice."Walk straight across the square," he says.
"Pase el museo.""Go past the museum."
"Y gire a la derecha.""And turn right."
Miro al otro lado de la plaza.I look across the square.
Veo un edificio grande.I see a large building.
Es el museo.It is the museum.
"Muchas gracias", digo."Thank you very much," I say.
"De nada", responde."You are welcome," he replies.
Sigo caminando.I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
Cruzo la gran plaza.I walk across the large square.
De repente, escucho música.Suddenly, I hear music.
En la esquina hay un músico callejero.On the corner stands a street musician.
Él toca la guitarra.He plays the guitar.
Escucho la buena música.I listen to the good music.
Lo miro por un largo rato.I look at him for a long time.
Luego sigo caminando.Then I walk on.
Olvido el museo.I forget the museum.
También olvido mi ruta.I also forget my route.
En lugar de eso, camino por una calle secundaria estrecha.Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
Aquí hay mucho silencio.It is very quiet here.
A mi alrededor solo veo unas casas pequeñas.All around I only see some small houses.
Entonces me detengo.Then I stop.
¿Dónde está el museo?Where is the museum?
Ahora miro a mi alrededor.Now I look around.
Ya no puedo verlo.I cannot see it anymore.
Miro rápidamente hacia atrás por la calle.I quickly look back up the street.
Tampoco puedo ver la plaza.I cannot see the square either.
Estoy completamente perdida.I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
Estoy parada en una calle tranquila.I am standing on a quiet street.
Estoy perdida.I am lost.
Delante de mí veo a una mujer joven.Ahead of me I see a young woman.
Está paseando a un perro pequeño.She is walking a small dog.
El perro se parece al perro de mi familia, Lola.The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
Me acerco a la mujer.I walk over to the woman.
"Disculpe", digo."Excuse me," I say.
"Estoy buscando el puerto"."I am looking for the harbour."
La mujer se detiene y escucha.The woman stops and listens.
Señala hacia atrás, calle arriba.She points back up the street.
"Vaya todo derecho de regreso a la plaza", dice."Go straight back to the square," she says.
"Y gire a la derecha en el museo"."And turn right at the museum."
"Muchas gracias por la ayuda", digo."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"De nada", responde la mujer."You are welcome," the woman replies.
Bajo la mirada hacia el perro pequeño.I look down at the little dog.
Me mira.It looks at me.
Camino de regreso hacia la plaza.I walk back toward the square.
Ahora sé dónde estoy.Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
Camino de regreso por la calle tranquila.I walk back along the quiet street.
Sigo derecho.I walk straight ahead.
Pronto vuelvo a ver la gran plaza.Soon I see the large square again.
Luego cruzo la plaza.Then I walk across the square.
Ahora estoy de pie junto al museo.Now I am standing by the museum.
Recuerdo las palabras de la mujer: "Gire a la derecha".I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
En el museo giro a la derecha.At the museum I turn right.
Bajo por una calle ancha.I walk down a wide street.
Miro hacia adelante.I look ahead.
Finalmente veo algo azul.Finally I see something blue.
¡Es el agua!It is the water!
En el camino paso junto a un pequeño café.On the way I walk past a little café.
Al final, camino hasta el agua.At last I walk all the way down to the water.
Ahora estoy en el puerto.Now I am at the harbour.
El puerto es muy grande.The harbour is very large.
El viento es frío y fresco.The wind is cold and fresh.
Hay muchos barcos en el agua.There are many boats on the water.
Aquí me detengo.Here I stop.
Me quedo de pie mirando los barcos.I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
Camino recto a lo largo del puerto.I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
Paso junto a los barcos pequeños.I walk past the small boats.
De repente veo a mucha gente.Suddenly I see many people.
Están de pie abajo, junto al agua.They stand down by the water.
Voy hacia allá.I walk over there.
Por fin la veo.Finally, I see it.
La Sirenita está sentada sobre una piedra en el agua.The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
Es una estatua muy famosa en Copenhague.She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.
En realidad, la figura no es muy grande.The figure is actually not very big.
Pero la figura de bronce es hermosa bajo el sol.But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
Estoy de pie entre los otros visitantes.I stand among the other visitors.
Muchos turistas toman fotos de la estatua.Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
Yo también saco mi teléfono.I also take out my phone.
Tomo una hermosa foto de la sirena.I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
Le envío la foto a mi familia en México.I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
Mi hermana, Sofía, responde rápido.My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
"Es muy bonita", escribe."She is very pretty," she writes.
Vuelvo a mirar el agua.I look at the water again.
walking home
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.
Es muy entrada la tarde.It is late in the afternoon.
Ahora camino a casa, a mi departamento.Now I walk home to my apartment.
Voy por un camino fácil.I walk an easy way.
Solo sigo el camino largo.I just follow the long road.
El camino es completamente recto.The road is completely straight.
Camino junto al agua.I walk along the water.
Finalmente me desvío del agua.Finally I turn away from the water.
Ahora entro de nuevo a la ciudad.Now I walk into the city again.
Luego paso por la panadería.Then I walk past the bakery.
Todavía huele a café.It still smells of coffee.
Un poco después veo el parque.A little later I see the park.
En el parque giro a la derecha.At the park I turn right.
No le pido indicaciones a nadie.I do not ask anyone for directions.
Encuentro el camino yo sola.I find the way all by myself.
Un poco después veo mi propia calle.A little later I see my own street.
Finalmente estoy en mi puerta principal.Finally I stand at my front door.
Desde la puerta miro hacia atrás, calle abajo.From the door I look back down the street.
Ahora conozco mi camino a casa.Now I know my way home.
