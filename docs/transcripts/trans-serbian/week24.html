Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Serbian with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.

„Mnogo ti hvala na pomoći, Majo“, kaže ona."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.

Objašnjava mi nekoliko stvari o njemu.She explains a few things to me about him.
„Daj mu hranu u sedam ujutru i u šest uveče“, kaže Nina."Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
„I zapamti, on ne sme da jede ljudsku hranu.“"And remember, he must not have human food."
Smejem se i gledam dole u malog psa.I laugh and look down at the little dog.
„Ne, Ludvige, moja večera nije za tebe“, kažem mu."No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
Nina se takođe smeje i pokazuje na vrata.Nina laughs too and points at the door.
„Uvek ga drži na povocu kada šetate blizu velikog puta“, kaže ona."Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
„U parku sme slobodno da trči.“"In the park he is allowed to run freely."

„Ne brini, dobro ću paziti na njega.“"Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
Nina me grli i oprašta se od nas.Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
Ona zatvara vrata, a ja stojim sama sa Ludvigom.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
Podiže pogled ka meni i maše repom.He looks up at me and wags his tail.
Već mi je veoma drag.I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll

a morning together
