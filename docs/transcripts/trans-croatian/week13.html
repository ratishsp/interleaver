Week 13: Maya goes to a café

Croatian with English gloss, line-aligned.

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.
Zima je u Kopenhagenu.It is winter in Copenhagen.
Hodam niz ulicu.I am walking down the street.
Vjetar je jako hladan.The wind is very cold.
Danas jako puše.It is blowing hard today.
Naginjem se ususret hladnom vjetru.I lean against the cold wind.
Podižem šal.I pull my scarf up.
Lice mi se smrzava.My face is freezing.
Gledam oko sebe.I look around.
Htjela bih ući na toplo.I would like to get into the warmth.
Vidim mali kafić na uglu.I see a small café on the corner.
Prozori su zamagljeni.The windows are fogged up.
Unutra izgleda toplo.It looks warm in there.
Idem do kafića.I walk over to the café.
Otvaram teška vrata.I open the heavy door.
Ulazim unutra.I step inside.
Ovdje miriše na kavu.It smells like coffee here.
Zrak je ugodno topao.The air is nice and warm.
Zatvaram vrata za sobom.I close the door behind me.
Hladan vjetar ostaje vani.The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
Sada sam u kafiću.I am inside the café now.
Ovdje je jako toplo.It is very warm here.
Skidam svoju debelu zimsku jaknu.I take off my thick winter coat.
Teška je.It is heavy.
Nalazim kuku na zidu.I find a hook on the wall.
Vješam jaknu na kuku.I hang my coat on the hook.
Također skidam šal i rukavice.I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
Kafić je mali i ugodan.The café is small and cozy.
Tražim dobro mjesto.I look for a good seat.
Pored prozora je mali stol.There is a small table by the window.
Stol je pored radijatora.The table is next to a radiator.
Odlazim do stola.I walk over to the table.
Sjedam na stolicu.I sit down on the chair.
Ruke su mi još uvijek hladne.My hands are still cold.
Trljam ih jednu o drugu.I rub them together.
Ruke mi postaju toplije.My hands get warmer.
Osjećaj je fantastičan.It feels fantastic.
Sada mi je gotovo toplo.Now I am almost warm.
Gladna sam i žedna.I am hungry and thirsty.
Htjela bih sada nešto naručiti.I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
Prilazim staklenoj vitrini.I walk over to the glass counter.
Puna je kolača i kruha.It is full of cakes and bread.
Gledam to mnoštvo stvari.I look at the many things.
Izgledaju jako ukusno.They look very delicious.
Pored kolača su mali natpisi.There are small signs next to the cakes.
Natpisi su napisani rukom.The signs are handwritten.
Čitam nazive na natpisima.I read the names on the signs.
Ima mnogo vrsta peciva.There are many kinds of pastry.
Teško je izabrati.It is hard to choose.
Tu je velika čokoladna torta.There is a large chocolate cake.
Ali gledam jedno drugo pecivo.But I look at another piece of pastry.
To je velika rolica s cimetom.It is a large cinnamon roll.
Na vrhu ima puno šećera.It has a lot of sugar on top.
Volim okus cimeta.I love the taste of cinnamon.
Savršeno je za hladan zimski dan.It is perfect for a cold winter day.
Jako bih željela tu rolicu s cimetom.I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
Sada sam spremna naručiti.Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
Idem do blagajne.I walk over to the register.
Mladić stoji iza pulta.A young man stands behind the counter.
On je barista u kafiću.He is a barista in the café.
Nasmiješim mu se.I smile at him.
Prijateljski kimne.He nods warmly.
"Bok," kaže on."Hi," he says.
"Što želite?" pita on."What would you like?" he asks.
"Bok," odgovaram."Hi," I answer.
"Htjela bih crnu kavu, molim.""I would like a black coffee, please."
"A mogu li dobiti i rolicu s cimetom?""And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
"Naravno," kaže on."Of course," he says.
"Crna kava i rolica s cimetom.""A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
"Želite li sjesti ovdje?" pita on."Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
"Da, molim vas, sjest ću tamo," kažem."Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
"U redu," kaže barista."Alright," the barista says.
"Sada ću vam napraviti kavu.""I will make your coffee now."
"Puno hvala," kažem."Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
Čekam za pultom.I wait at the counter.
On priprema moju kavu.He makes my coffee.
Stavlja vruću šalicu kave na drveni pladanj.He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
Također stavlja mali tanjur na pladanj.He also puts a small plate on the tray.
Moja rolica s cimetom je na tanjuru.My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
Divno miriše.It smells lovely.
"Izvolite, vaša narudžba", kaže on."Here you go, your order," he says.
"Puno hvala", odgovaram."Thank you very much," I reply.
"Hvala na dobroj usluzi.""Thank you for the good service."
"Nema na čemu", kaže on ljubazno."You are welcome," he says kindly.
Uzimam pladanj objema rukama.I take the tray with both hands.
Vrlo oprezno hodam preko poda.I walk very carefully across the floor.
Vraćam se do svog stola pored prozora.I walk back to my table by the window.
Spuštam pladanj na stol.I put the tray down on the table.
Sada bih htjela sjesti.Now I would like to sit down.
Spremna sam za dobru pauzu.I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
Sada sjedim za svojim malim stolom.I now sit at my small table.
Ovdje je lijepo i toplo.It is lovely and warm here.
Gledam svoju rolicu s cimetom.I look at my cinnamon roll.
Miriše slatko i dobro.It smells sweet and good.
Uzimam mali zalogaj.I take a small bite.
Pecivo je potpuno svježe i toplo.The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
Stvarno je ukusno.It tastes really good.
Pijem malo svoje crne kave.I drink a little of my black coffee.
Napokon su mi prsti opet topli.Finally, my fingers are warm again.
Opuštam se u mekanoj stolici.I relax in the soft chair.
Kafić je gotovo pun ljudi.The café is almost full of people.
Ljudi sjede u malim grupama.People sit in small groups.
Razgovaraju i smiju se zajedno.They talk and laugh together.
Slušam tihe glasove u prostoriji.I listen to the low voices in the room.
Zvuk je vrlo ugodan.The sound is very cozy.
Gledam kroz veliki prozor.I look out of the large window.
Vrijeme vani je sivo i vjetrovito.The weather outside is gray and windy.
Sad zapravo opet pada snijeg.Now it is actually snowing again.
Bijele pahulje polako padaju na ulicu.White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
Samo sam sretna što sam unutra.I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
Jedem zadnji komad svoje rolice s cimetom.I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
Stvarno je jako ukusna.It tastes really good.
Ali je također jako slatka.But it is also very sweet.
Moja šalica za kavu je potpuno prazna.My coffee cup is completely empty.
Odjednom sam jako žedna.I am suddenly very thirsty.
Htjela bih malo hladne vode.I would like to have some cold water.
Mladić prolazi pored mog stola.A young man walks past my table.
On radi ovdje u kafiću.He works here in the café.
Skuplja prazne šalice i tanjure.He collects empty cups and plates.
Uhvatim njegov pogled.I catch his eye.
Zastaje pored mog stola i nasmiješi se.He stops at my table and smiles.
"Oprostite," kažem pristojno."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Da?" odgovara mladić."Yes?" answers the young man.
"Mogu li zamoliti za čašu vode iz slavine?" pitam."May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
"Da, naravno," kaže on."Yes, of course," he says.
"Puno hvala," kažem."Thank you very much," I say.
Uzima moj prazan tanjur i šalicu.He takes my empty plate and cup.
Odlazi natrag do pulta.He walks back to the counter.
Malo kasnije dolazi s čašom hladne vode.A little later he comes with a cold glass of water.
asking for the bill
Storyboard: Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab.
Pijem svoju vodu.I drink my water.
Voda je hladna i ugodna.The water is cold and nice.
Uzimam svoju torbu s poda.I take my bag from the floor.
Završila sam s pauzom.I am finished with my break.
Idem do pulta.I walk up to the counter.
Ljubazni barist još uvijek stoji tamo.The friendly barista is still standing there.
Gleda me s osmijehom.He looks at me with a smile.
"Želite li još nešto?" pita on."Would you like anything else?" he asks.
"Ne, hvala," odgovaram."No thank you," I answer.
"Željela bih platiti.""I would like to pay."
"Da, naravno," kaže on."Yes, of course," he says.
Gleda u svoj mali ekran.He looks at his little screen.
"Jedna crna kava i jedna rolica s cimetom," kaže on."One black coffee and one cinnamon roll," he says.
"Da, tako je," kažem."Yes, that is correct," I say.
"Želite li platiti karticom ili gotovinom?" pita on."Do you want to pay with card or cash?" he asks.
"Željela bih platiti karticom," odgovaram."I would like to pay with a card," I answer.
"U redu.""Fine."
"To će biti osamdeset pet kruna," kaže on."That will be eighty-five kroner," he says.
Vadim svoju platnu karticu.I take out my payment card.
paying and leaving
Storyboard: Maya taps her card on the terminal to pay eighty-five kroner. She puts on her heavy coat, thanks the staff for a nice time, and pushes the front door open to face the cold again.
Barist mi se smiješi.The barista smiles at me.
Držim svoju karticu iznad terminala.I hold my card over the terminal.
Aparat zapišti.The machine beeps.
"Želite li račun?" pita on."Do you want a receipt?" he asks.
"Ne, hvala," odgovaram."No thank you," I answer.
Prilazim zidu.I walk over to the wall.
Uzimam svoju debelu zimsku jaknu s kuke.I take my thick winter coat from a hook.
Oblačim jaknu.I put the coat on.
Također stavljam svoj šal.I also put my scarf on.
Također stavljam svoje rukavice.I also put my gloves on.
Sada sam spremna za hladnoću.Now I am ready for the cold.
Ponovno pogledam baristu.I look at the barista again.
"Puno hvala na kavi," kažem."Thank you very much for the coffee," I say.
"Nema na čemu," kaže on s osmijehom."You are welcome," he says with a smile.
"Ugodan dan.""Have a good day."
"Također," kažem."Likewise," I say.
Odlazim do ulaznih vrata.I walk over to the front door.
Guram teška vrata da ih otvorim.I push the heavy door open.
Vjetar je hladan, ali osjećam se dobro.The wind is cold, but I feel good.
