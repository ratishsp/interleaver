# Week 6 — Storyboard (Maya settles into a daily routine)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Present continuous **रहा/रही है** (कर रही हूँ) for actions underway, woven naturally through a habitual **-ती हूँ** routine

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_coffee | Maya wakes up early in her Mumbai flat to begin her usual morning routine. She stands by the window and watches the neighbourhood wake up while enjoying a hot cup of coffee. |
| 2 | 02_getting_ready | Maya gets dressed for the office. She gathers her things for the day, packing her laptop and lunchbox into her bag before doing a final check to make sure she has everything. |
| 3 | 03_bus_stop | Maya walks out to the nearby stop to start her commute. She takes the bus to work every day and waits patiently in a short line for her regular BEST bus to arrive. |
| 4 | 04_buying_ticket | On the crowded bus, Maya finds a seat by the window. The conductor approaches, and she politely calls him bhaiya while buying her ticket, watching the heavy morning traffic outside. |
| 5 | 05_office_morning | Maya arrives at the software company and greets her colleague Rohan cheerfully. She settles at her desk, turns on her computer, and begins typing her morning emails. |
| 6 | 06_canteen_lunch | At midday, Maya and Rohan take a break from their screens to eat together in the office canteen. Maya enjoys her packed lunch while Rohan brings a hot meal from the counter. |
| 7 | 07_evening_groceries | Maya returns to her neighbourhood in the evening, walking home from the bus stop. She pauses at a local street vendor to buy some fresh milk and bread, politely calling him bhaiya. |
| 8 | 08_cooking_dinner | Back in her flat, Maya changes into comfortable clothes and prepares a simple dinner. She opens her new stainless steel masala dabba to add spices, feeling at ease in her quiet kitchen. |
| 9 | 09_video_call_home | After dinner, Maya relaxes on her sofa to wind down for the night. She video-calls her parents and her younger sister Sofia back in Mexico, describing her new daily routine before finally going to bed. |
