Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
星期五夜晚，妮娜敲我嘅門。It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
佢帶埋佢隻狗，魯德維希。She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.
佢今個週末會喺我度住。He is going to stay with me for the weekend.
妮娜笑住將條狗帶交畀我。Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
「瑪雅，真係好多謝你幫手，」佢話。"Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
「完全冇問題，」我好開心咁答。"It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
佢同我解釋咗幾樣關於佢嘅事。She explains a few things to me about him.
「朝早七點同夜晚六點畀嘢佢食，」妮娜話。"Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
「同埋記住，唔可以畀人類嘅食物佢食。」"And remember, he must not have human food."
我笑住低頭望住隻小狗。I laugh and look down at the little dog.
「唔得呀，魯德維希，我嘅晚餐唔係畀你㗎，」我同佢講。"No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
妮娜都笑咗，指住大門。Nina laughs too and points at the door.
「行近大馬路嗰陣，一定要用狗帶拖住佢，」佢話。"Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
「喺公園入面，佢可以隨便跑。」"In the park he is allowed to run freely."
我點點頭。I nod.
「放心啦，我會好好照顧佢。」"Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
妮娜攬咗我一下，同我哋講拜拜。Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
佢閂埋門，得番我一個人同魯德維希企喺度。She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
佢岳高頭望住我，不停咁搖尾巴。He looks up at me and wags his tail.
我已經好鍾意佢啦。I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll
Storyboard: Before bed, Maya takes Ludwig for a short evening walk around the neighborhood. When he suddenly sniffs at a discarded food wrapper on the pavement, she gently commands him to leave it and pulls him away, reminding him that he must not eat trash.
星期五夜晚已經好夜喇。It is late Friday evening.
臨瞓前，我帶路德維希出街。Before we go to bed, I take Ludwig out.
我哋喺附近散一陣步。We go for a short walk in the neighborhood.
依家條街好靜。The streets are very quiet now.
路德維希好乖咁行喺我隔籬。Ludwig walks nicely next to me.
突然間，佢停低咗。Suddenly he stops.
佢喺行人路上好心急咁索緊啲嘢。He sniffs eagerly at something on the pavement.
嗰個係一個裝住食物殘渣嘅污糟袋。It is a dirty bag with food scraps.
佢擘大個口想擔起佢。He opens his mouth to take it.
「唔准，放低！」我好堅決咁同佢講。"No, drop it!" I say firmly to him.
我輕輕將佢拉開，離開個袋。I gently pull him away from the bag.
「垃圾唔係嘢食嚟㗎，路德維希，」我同佢解釋。"The trash is not food, Ludwig," I explain.
「過嚟呢度，路德維希，」我叫佢。"Come here, Ludwig," I call to him.
佢放低個袋，抬頭望住我。He drops the bag and looks up at me.
佢好快就開始搖尾。He quickly begins to wag his tail.
「乖仔，」我同佢講，然後摸吓佢。"Good boy," I say and pet him.
我哋沿住條街繼續散步。We continue our walk down the street.
依家我哋只要返屋企瞓覺就得喇。Now we just need to go home and sleep.
saturday breakfast
Storyboard: Early Saturday morning, Ludwig nudges Maya awake. Maya prepares his breakfast, gently telling him to sit and wait while she fills the bowl, before finally petting him and telling him to eat it.
依家係星期六朝早七點鐘。It is Saturday morning at seven o'clock.
我仲瞓得好冧。I am still sleeping heavily.
突然間，我感覺到手臂有啲凍冰冰嘅嘢。Suddenly I feel something cold on my arm.
我擘大眼，見到Ludwig。I open my eyes and see Ludwig.
佢用個鼻推吓我。He nudges me with his snout.
「早晨呀，Ludwig，」我講完就起身。"Good morning, Ludwig," I say and get up.
我哋一齊行出廚房。We go out into the kitchen together.
佢好肚餓，撲上我度。He is very hungry and jumps up on me.
「定啲嚟，Ludwig，」我同佢講。"Easy now, Ludwig," I say to him.
「坐低，」我平靜噉講。"Sit," I say calmly.
佢望住我，然後坐低喺地下。He looks at me and sits down on the floor.
我攞起包狗糧，然後打開。I take the bag of dog food and open it.
我將啲狗糧倒落佢個碗度。I pour the food into his bowl.
佢企起身，因為佢想即刻要。He gets up because he wants it immediately.
「唔得，等陣，」我話。"No, wait," I say.
佢又坐返低，乖乖哋等。He sits down again and waits nicely.
我將個碗放低喺佢面前。I put the bowl down in front of him.
我輕輕噉摸吓佢個頭。I gently pet him on the head.
「食得喇，」我笑住講。"Here you go, eat," I say with a smile.
佢搖住條尾，開始食嘢。He wags his tail and begins to eat.
a morning together
Storyboard: Saturday morning, with the café shift she arranged off in advance so Ludwig is not left alone, Maya has the morning free. she and Ludwig enjoy it together — she throws his ball and calls him back, gives him a good fuss — happy she chose to stay with him.
食完早餐之後，我有好多時間。After breakfast I have plenty of time.
我平時星期六會喺一間咖啡店做義工。I normally volunteer at a café on Saturdays.
但係路德維希唔應該自己一個。But Ludwig should not be alone.
好彩我今日咖啡店有假放。Fortunately I have the day off from the café today.
我上個星期已經同佢哋講好咗。I arranged it with them last week.
依家路德維希同我成個朝頭早都可以一齊。Now Ludwig and I have the whole morning together.
我哋行出去我大廈後面個後園。We go out into the courtyard behind my building.
我攞佢個細波出嚟。I take out his little ball.
我同佢講：「望住個波呀，路德維希！」"Look at the ball, Ludwig!" I say to him.
我將個波掟過去草地。I throw it across the grass.
我大叫：「執返嚟！」"Fetch it!" I shout.
佢跑得好快，接住咗個波。He runs fast and catches the ball.
我叫佢：「過嚟！」"Come here!" I call.
佢好開心咁跑返過嚟我度。He runs happily back to me.
我好溫柔咁講：「畀我啦。」"Give it to me," I say gently.
佢放開個波，然後我摸吓佢。He lets go of the ball, and I pet him.
我笑住講：「你真係一隻乖狗。」"You are a good dog," I say and smile.
我再掟個波，佢又執返嚟。I throw it again, and he fetches it.
成個週末我都要好好照顧佢。I must take good care of him all weekend.
我好開心可以留喺屋企陪佢。I am very happy that I stayed at home with him.
the hundeskov
Storyboard: Saturday afternoon, Maya walks with Ludwig to a local hundeskov (dog forest). Once inside the safe, gated area, she unclips the lead, happily telling him that he may run free now, and throws a stick for him to fetch.
Ludwig喺我側邊行得好快。Ludwig walks quickly next to me.
我哋去緊附近一個狗公園。We are on our way to a local dog forest.
今日晏晝陽光好好。The sun is shining nicely this afternoon.
佢有啲心急噉扯住條狗帶。He pulls a little eagerly on the leash.
「等陣，」我好堅決噉同佢講。"Wait," I say firmly to him.
「嚟，我哋乖乖哋行。」"Come, let us walk nicely."
我哋終於嚟到嗰道大閘。We finally arrive at the large gate.
狗公園圍咗欄，非常安全。The dog forest is fenced and completely safe.
我哋行入去，然後我喺後面閂返道閘。We go in, and I close the gate behind us.
我踎低望住佢。I crouch down and look at him.
我慢慢噉喺佢個頸圈度解開條狗帶。I slowly unclip the leash from his collar.
「而家你可以自由噉跑喇，」我笑住講。"Now you may run freely," I say with a smile.
佢好開心噉望住我，然後跑走咗。He looks happily at me and runs off quickly.
我喺地下搵到一條好嘅樹枝。I find a good stick on the ground.
「望下呢度，Ludwig！」我大聲叫，攞畀佢睇。"Look here, Ludwig!" I shout and show it to him.
我將條樹枝遠遠噉掉入去啲樹中間。I throw the stick far away in among the trees.
「執返嚟！」我大聲講。"Fetch it!" I say loudly.
佢跑過去條樹枝度，用口擔住。He runs over to the stick and takes it in his mouth.
「返嚟啦，」我拍住手掌噉叫。"Come back," I call and clap my hands.
佢真係一隻乖狗。He is really a good dog.
gentle recall
Storyboard: Ludwig spots a bird and wanders off toward the trees, completely ignoring Maya's loud commands to stop. Realizing yelling does not work, Maya crouches down with a treat, softly calling him over; he quickly runs to her, and she tells him to sit before clipping the leash back on him.
Ludwig 喺樹林度好開心咁跑嚟跑去。Ludwig runs happily around in the forest.
突然之間，佢見到一隻好大嘅雀仔。Suddenly he sees a big bird.
佢好快咁追住佢。He runs quickly after it.
佢一直跑到過去啲樹嗰邊。He runs all the way over towards the trees.
「停呀，Ludwig！」我大聲嗌。"Stop, Ludwig!" I shout loudly.
「過嚟呢邊！」"Come over here!"
我喺袋度攞一粒細嘅零食出嚟。I take a small treat out of my pocket.
我喺草地度踎低。I crouch down in the grass.
「Ludwig，望吓呢度，」我好溫柔咁叫。"Ludwig, look here," I call softly.
佢停低望住我。He stops and looks at me.
佢好想食嗰粒零食。He really wants the treat.
佢好快咁跑返嚟我度。He runs quickly back to me.
「坐低，」我好平靜咁講。"Sit," I say calmly.
佢坐低，我就將粒零食畀佢。He sits down, and I give it to him.
「乖狗狗，」我講，然後摸吓佢。"Good dog," I say and pet him.
我幫佢扣返條狗帶。I clip the leash onto him again.
依家我哋要返屋企休息吓啦。Now we must go home and relax.
family video call
Storyboard: That evening, Maya video calls her family in Mexico to show them her furry guest. She points the camera at Ludwig, telling Sofía and Diego to look at him, noting that he is just as calm as their dog Lola back home.
星期六夜晚，Ludwig 喺梳化度瞓咗喺我隔離。Saturday evening, Ludwig is lying asleep next to me on the sofa.
我哋去樹林散步之後，佢好攰。He is very tired after our walk in the forest.
我打開電腦，打視像電話返墨西哥畀屋企人。I open my computer and video call home to my family in Mexico.
我阿妹 Sofía 同我細佬 Diego 已經坐定定喺螢光幕前。My sister Sofía and my brother Diego are sitting ready in front of the screen.
「你哋一定要睇吓我呢位小客人，」我同佢哋講。"You simply must see my little guest," I say to them.
我攞起部電腦，將個鏡頭向下對住隻狗。I take the computer and point the camera down at the dog.
Sofía 好開心咁笑。Sofía smiles happily.
「你睇吓佢！」"Just look at him!"
「佢真係好得意，」Diego 話。"He sure is cute," Diego says.
Ludwig 擘大一隻眼，但係就繼續瞓喺度。Ludwig opens one eye, but he just stays lying down.
「睇吓佢。」"Look at him."
「佢同我哋屋企隻狗 Lola 一樣咁定，」我話。"He is just as calm as our dog Lola back home," I say.
Diego 問我佢係咪一隻好易照顧嘅狗。Diego asks me if he is an easy dog to look after.
「係呀，我真係好鍾意照顧佢，」我答。"Yes, I really like looking after him," I answer.
「出到街嗰陣，佢淨係需要綁住狗帶嚟行。」"He just has to walk on a leash out on the street."
「而且絕對唔可以食人食嘅嘢。」"And he definitely must not have human food."
「所以我淨係畀佢自己嘅零食佢食。」"So I only give him his own treats."
突然間，我哋聽到 Lola 喺視像通話嘅背景度大聲吠。Suddenly we hear Lola bark loudly in the background on the video.
Ludwig 抬起頭，好疑惑咁望住個螢光幕。Ludwig lifts his head and looks very confused at the screen.
「瞓返低啦，朋友，」我溫柔咁講，再摸吓佢。"Just lie down again, my friend," I say gently and pet him.
我同屋企人講早抖，等佢可以靜吓。I say goodnight to my family so he can get some peace.
佢合埋對眼，好快又瞓著返。He closes his eyes and quickly falls back asleep.
ninas return
Storyboard: On Sunday evening, Nina returns from her trip and knocks on the door. Ludwig greets her joyfully, and Maya warmly tells Nina that she must definitely let her look after him again next time she travels.
星期日下晝，有人敲門。It is Sunday afternoon, and there is a knock on the door.
Ludwig 跑出走廊，開心咁吠。Ludwig runs out into the hallway and barks happily.
我打開門，Nina 拎住佢個週末旅行袋企喺出面。I open the door, and Nina stands outside with her weekend bag.
「嗨 Maya，好多謝你幫手，」佢笑住講。"Hi Maya, thank you so much for the help," she says and smiles.
佢搖住尾，撲向佢。He wags his tail and jumps up at her.
「落嚟啦，Ludwig，」Nina 邊講邊拍拍佢。"Down, Ludwig," Nina says and pats him.
「佢真係一隻好乖嘅狗，」我同佢講。"He has been a really good dog," I tell her.
Nina 笑咗笑，喺張細檯度拎返佢條狗帶。Nina laughs and takes his dog leash from the small table.
「你有冇帶佢去狗狗森林散步呀？」佢問。"Did you walk him in the dog forest?" she asks.
「有呀，佢好鍾意，」我答。"Yes, and he loved it," I reply.
我將淨低嘅狗糧畀佢。I give her the rest of the dog food.
「畀你，呢啲係淨低嘅。」"Here you go, this is the rest."
「我好樂意下次再幫手照顧佢。」"I would love to look after him again sometime."
「好呀，」Nina 講。"Sure," Nina says.
「嚟啦，Ludwig，我哋要返屋企喇，」Nina 同隻狗講。"Come, Ludwig, we must go home," Nina says to the dog.
我同佢哋兩個揮手講拜拜。I wave goodbye to them both.
冇咗佢，間屋突然變得好靜。The apartment is suddenly very quiet without him.
