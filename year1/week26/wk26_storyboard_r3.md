# Week 26 — Storyboard (Maya's first Danish summer and thirty-second birthday)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Perfect vs preteritum contrast

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_birthday_messages | Maya wakes up on the morning she turns thirty-two, feeling a bit nostalgic as it is her first birthday away from Mexico. She smiles as she reads the flood of loving messages that her family has sent overnight while she slept. |
| 2 | 02_office_pastry | At the software company, Maya is surprised when Peter and Eva place a small pastry with a single candle on her desk. Peter explains that they bought the treat from the corner bakery earlier because she has worked incredibly hard on their latest project. |
| 3 | 03_family_call | That evening, Maya has a lively video call with her parents, Sofía, Diego, and Lola the dog. Sofía eagerly asks if Maya has opened the package they sent, and Maya happily holds up the colorful scarf that arrived in the mail yesterday. |
| 4 | 04_cafe_midsummer | Finishing her Saturday morning café shift, Maya unties her apron and wipes down the final counter. Eager for her first Danish Midsummer, she texts Nina to confirm their plans, noting that she has finished work early today unlike last weekend when she stayed late. |
| 5 | 05_packing_sweaters | Later that evening, Nina knocks on Maya's door so they can head down to the waterfront. Nina asks if Maya has packed a warm jacket for the night; Maya nods, explaining she grabbed her thickest sweater because the wind picked up earlier in the afternoon. |
| 6 | 06_bus_stop_contrast | Walking toward the beach, Maya marvels at the vibrant evening sky. She tells Nina how she stood waiting for her commute bus in pitch black at four o'clock back in January, contrasting it with tonight where the sun has not even started to set. |
| 7 | 07_beach_bonfire | They meet Eva and Peter on the sand just as a large crowd lights the towering Midsummer bonfire. Eva shares fresh strawberries she bought at the market, noting that the glowing fire has already chased away the coastal chill. |
| 8 | 08_midnight_blue | Strolling back to the apartment near midnight under a sky that remains soft blue, Maya feels a deep sense of peace. She looks at her friends and realizes she has built a wonderful life here since she stepped off the plane six months ago. |
