Week 10: Maya buys winter clothes

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

a cold morning
Storyboard: It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to the bus stop on her way to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter.
Je ráno v Kodani.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Idem na autobusovú zastávku.I walk to the bus stop.
Počasie je dnes veľmi chladné.The weather is very cold today.
Fúka.It is windy.
Vietor je ľadový.The wind is ice-cold.
Mrznem.I am freezing.
Mám na sebe tenkú bundu.I am wearing a thin jacket.
Moja bunda vôbec nie je teplá.My jacket is not warm at all.
Mám obuté aj tenké topánky.I am also wearing thin shoes.
Nemám na sebe teplý šál.I am not wearing a warm scarf.
Moje ruky sú veľmi studené.My hands are very cold.
Trasiem sa.I am shivering.
Zima v Dánsku je tvrdá.The winter in Denmark is hard.
Potrebujem teplé oblečenie.I need warm clothes.
Potrebujem hrubý sveter.I need a thick sweater.
Potrebujem aj veľký šál.I also need a large scarf.
A potrebujem teplé rukavice.And I need warm gloves.
Kráčam veľmi rýchlo.I walk very fast.
Po práci si kúpim zimné oblečenie.I am buying winter clothes after work.
entering the shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things.
Skončila som v práci.I am finished at work.
Kráčam po pešej zóne.I walk down the pedestrian street.
Vietor je stále veľmi studený.The wind is still very cold.
Vidím veľký obchod s oblečením.I see a large clothing shop.
Obchod je veľmi svetlý.The shop is very bright.
Idem do obchodu.I go into the shop.
Je tu úžasne teplo.It is wonderfully warm in here.
Za pokladňou stojí predavačka.A shop assistant stands behind the register.
Pozerá sa na mňa a usmieva sa.She looks at me and smiles.
"Dobrý deň," hovorí."Hello," she says.
"Dobrý deň," odpovedám."Hello," I answer.
"Je mi veľmi zima," hovorím."I am freezing a lot," I say.
"Mám na sebe len tenkú bundu.""I only have a thin jacket on."
"Chcela by som nájsť nejaké teplé veci.""I would like to find some warm things."
Predavačka sa opäť usmeje.The shop assistant smiles again.
"Dnes je tiež veľmi chladno," hovorí."It is also very cold today," she says.
"Máme veľa hrubých svetrov.""We have many thick sweaters."
"Máme aj teplé zimné oblečenie.""We also have warm winter clothes."
"Len sa pokojne poobzerajte," hovorí milo."You just take a look," she says kindly.
"Veľmi pekne ďakujem," odpovedám."Many thanks," I answer.
the red sweater
Storyboard: Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it.
Pozerám sa na oblečenie na policiach.I look at the clothes on the shelves.
Vidím hrubý sveter.I see a thick sweater.
Je červený.It is red.
Potrebujem teplý sveter.I need a warm sweater.
Obchod má veľa veľkostí.The shop has many sizes.
Nachádzam veľkosť medium.I find a size medium.
Vchádzam do skúšobnej kabínky.I walk into the fitting room.
Obliekam si červený sveter.I put the red sweater on.
Mám ho na sebe a pozerám sa do veľkého zrkadla.I have it on and look in the big mirror.
Sveter perfektne sedí.The sweater fits perfectly.
Je neuveriteľne teplý.It is incredibly warm.
Milujem tú červenú farbu.I love the red colour.
Pozerám sa na cenovku.I look at the price tag.
Sveter stojí tristo korún.The sweater costs three hundred kroner.
Je to dobrá cena za hrubý sveter.It is a good price for a thick sweater.
Znovu si sveter vyzliekam.I take the sweater off again.
Kupujem si ho.I buy it.
the tight green coat
Storyboard: Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a larger size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. The assistant brings her a warm dark-blue winter jacket in her size instead — it fits perfectly, and Maya decides to take it.
Potrebujem aj zimnú bundu.I also need a winter jacket.
Pozerám si v obchode ďalšie oblečenie.I look at more clothes in the shop.
Nájdem krásny zelený kabát.I find a beautiful, green coat.
Je to veľmi krásny dizajn.It is a very beautiful design.
Oblečiem si ten zelený kabát.I put the green coat on.
Ale je príliš malý.But it is too small.
Cez ramená je veľmi tesný.It is very tight across the shoulders.
"Máte ho v inej veľkosti?" pýtam sa."Do you have it in another size?" I ask.
Predavačka sa pozrie.The shop assistant looks.
"Nie, bohužiaľ," odpovedá."No, unfortunately," she answers.
Takže zelený kabát zavesím naspäť.So I hang the green coat back.
Predavačka mi nájde inú bundu.The shop assistant finds another jacket for me.
Je to teplá, tmavomodrá zimná bunda.It is a warm, dark-blue winter jacket.
Oblečiem si tú tmavomodrú bundu.I put the dark-blue jacket on.
Sedí perfektne.It fits perfectly.
Je príjemne veľká a nie je tesná.It is nice and big and not tight.
Pozriem sa do veľkého zrkadla.I look in a large mirror.
Páči sa mi.I like it.
"Vezmem si ju," hovorím."I will take it," I say.
the blue scarf
Storyboard: Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases.
Idem k stolu v obchode.I walk over to a table in the shop.
Na stole je veľa vecí.There are many things on the table.
Dnes nemám na sebe šál.I am not wearing a scarf today.
Je mi veľmi zima na krk.I am very cold around my neck.
Vidím modrý šál.I see a blue scarf.
Je to veľmi dlhý šál.It is a very long scarf.
Je vyrobený z vlny.It is made of wool.
Vezmem ten modrý šál.I take the blue scarf.
Dám si ho okolo krku.I put it around my neck.
Je úžasne teplý.It feels wonderfully warm.
„To je pekná farba,“ hovorí predavačka."It is a nice colour," the shop assistant says.
„Áno, a je veľmi mäkký,“ hovorím."Yes, and it is very soft," I say.
Pozerám sa na cenovku.I look at the price tag.
Stojí stopäťdesiat korún.It costs one hundred and fifty kroner.
To je dobrá cena za teplý šál.It is a good price for a warm scarf.
Znova si ho dám dole.I take it off again.
„Kupujem si ho tiež,“ hovorím jej."I am buying it too," I say to her.
Položím modrý šál k červenému svetru.I put the blue scarf together with the red sweater.
the black gloves
Storyboard: Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well.
Stále mám na sebe svoju tenkú bundu.I am still wearing my thin jacket.
Myslím na svoje studené ruky z dnešného rána.I think about my cold hands from this morning.
Potrebujem hrubé rukavice.I need thick gloves.
Rozhliadam sa po svetlom obchode.I look around the bright shop.
Vidím veľký kôš.I see a large basket.
Kôš je plný rukavíc.The basket is full of gloves.
Nachádzam pár čiernych zimných rukavíc.I find a pair of black winter gloves.
Sú veľmi hrubé a mäkké.They are very thick and soft.
Nasadzujem si rukavice.I put the gloves on.
Perfektne mi sedia.They fit me perfectly.
Pýtam sa usmievajúcej sa predavačky.I ask the smiling shop assistant.
„Koľko stoja čierne rukavice?“ pýtam sa."What do the black gloves cost?" I ask.
„Stoja stodvadsať korún,“ odpovedá."They cost one hundred and twenty kroner," she answers.
Stodvadsať korún je dobrá cena.One hundred and twenty kroner is a good price.
Moje ruky sú už teplé.My hands are already warm.
„Vezmem si ich,“ hovorím radostne."I will take them," I say happily.
Znovu si dávam rukavice dole.I take the gloves off again.
Dávam čierne rukavice spolu so svojím červeným svetrom a modrým šálom.I put the black gloves together with my red sweater and my blue scarf.
Teraz som pripravená platiť.Now I am ready to pay.
paying for the clothes
Storyboard: Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the dark-blue winter jacket, the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is one thousand five hundred kroner, and Maya pays with her card.
Idem k pokladni so svojím novým oblečením.I walk over to the counter with my new clothes.
Predavačka sa na mňa milo usmeje.The shop assistant smiles kindly at me.
„To je všetko?“ pýta sa."Is that everything?" she asks.
„Áno, to je všetko,“ odpovedám."Yes, that is everything," I answer.
Naskenuje všetky moje nové veci.She scans all my new things.
Je tam sveter, bunda, šál a rukavice.There is a sweater, a jacket, a scarf and gloves.
„Teraz vám už nebude zima,“ hovorí veselo."Now you will not freeze," she says happily.
„Bude to tisíc päťsto kroner, prosím.“"That will be one thousand five hundred kroner, please."
Oblečenie je drahé, ale je veľmi teplé.The clothes are expensive, but they are very warm.
Vyberám svoju platobnú kartu.I take out my payment card.
„Platím kartou,“ hovorím."I am paying by card," I say.
„Môžete zaplatiť tu,“ hovorí."You can just pay here," she says.
Ukazuje na malý čierny prístroj.She points at a small, black machine.
Podržím kartu nad prístrojom.I hold my card over the machine.
Pípne to a platba je schválená.It beeps, and the payment is approved.
„Chcete účtenku?“ pýta sa predavačka."Do you want the receipt?" the shop assistant asks.
„Nie, ďakujem,“ odpovedám."No thank you," I answer.
Teraz vlastním veľa pekného zimného oblečenia.Now I own a lot of nice winter clothes.
stepping into the cold
Storyboard: Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She pulls on the red sweater, zips her new dark-blue winter jacket over it, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm.
„Tašku?“ pýta sa predavač."A bag?" the shop assistant asks.
„Nie, ďakujem,“ hovorím."No thank you," I say.
Nepotrebujem tašku.I do not need a bag.
Hneď si obliekam nové oblečenie.I put the new clothes on right away.
Najprv si obliekam hrubý červený sveter.First, I put on the thick, red sweater.
Potom si obliekam tmavomodrú zimnú bundu.Then, I put on the dark-blue winter jacket.
Zapínam si teplú bundu.I zip the warm jacket.
Následne si beriem dlhý modrý šál.Afterwards, I take the long, blue scarf.
Omotávam si ho okolo krku.I wrap it around my neck.
Nakoniec si dávam nové čierne rukavice.Finally, I put on the new, black gloves.
Teraz mám na sebe všetko svoje teplé oblečenie.Now I have all my warm clothes on.
Usmievam sa a hovorím dovidenia.I smile and say goodbye.
Vychádzam z obchodu.I walk out of the shop.
Opäť stojím vonku na chladnej ulici.I stand out on the cold street again.
Je mrazivá zima.It is freezing cold.
Vietor silno fúka.The wind blows hard.
Ale vôbec mi nie je zima.But I am not freezing at all.
Teraz som pripravená na zimu.Now I am ready for winter.
