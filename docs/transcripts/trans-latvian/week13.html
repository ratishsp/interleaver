Week 13: Maya goes to a café

Latvian with English gloss, line-aligned.

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.
Kopenhāgenā ir ziema.It is winter in Copenhagen.
Es eju pa ielu.I am walking down the street.
Vējš ir ļoti auksts.The wind is very cold.
Šodien stipri pūš.It is blowing hard today.
Es liecos pret auksto vēju.I lean against the cold wind.
Es pavelku šalli uz augšu.I pull my scarf up.
Man salst seja.My face is freezing.
Es skatos apkārt.I look around.
Es vēlos tikt siltumā.I would like to get into the warmth.
Es redzu mazu kafejnīcu uz stūra.I see a small café on the corner.
Logi ir aizsvīduši.The windows are fogged up.
Tur iekšā izskatās silti.It looks warm in there.
Es pieeju pie kafejnīcas.I walk over to the café.
Es atveru smagās durvis.I open the heavy door.
Es ieeju iekšā.I step inside.
Šeit smaržo pēc kafijas.It smells like coffee here.
Gaiss ir patīkami silts.The air is nice and warm.
Es aizveru durvis aiz sevis.I close the door behind me.
Aukstais vējš paliek ārā.The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
Es tagad esmu iekšā kafejnīcā.I am inside the café now.
Šeit ir ļoti silti.It is very warm here.
Es novelku savu biezo ziemas jaku.I take off my thick winter coat.
Tā ir smaga.It is heavy.
Es atrodu āķi pie sienas.I find a hook on the wall.
Es uzkarinu savu jaku uz āķa.I hang my coat on the hook.
Es novelku arī savu šalli un cimdus.I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
Kafejnīca ir maza un mājīga.The café is small and cozy.
Es meklēju labu vietu.I look for a good seat.
Tur ir mazs galds pie loga.There is a small table by the window.
Galds atrodas blakus radiatoram.The table is next to a radiator.
Es pieeju pie galda.I walk over to the table.
Es apsēžos uz krēsla.I sit down on the chair.
Manas rokas joprojām ir aukstas.My hands are still cold.
Es berzēju tās vienu pret otru.I rub them together.
Manas rokas kļūst siltākas.My hands get warmer.
Tā ir fantastiska sajūta.It feels fantastic.
Tagad man ir gandrīz silti.Now I am almost warm.
Es esmu izsalkusi un izslāpusi.I am hungry and thirsty.
Es tagad vēlētos kaut ko pasūtīt.I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
Es pieeju pie stikla vitrīnas.I walk over to the glass counter.
Tā ir pilna ar kūkām un maizi.It is full of cakes and bread.
Es aplūkoju daudzās lietas.I look at the many things.
Tās izskatās ļoti garšīgas.They look very delicious.
Blakus kūkām ir mazas zīmītes.There are small signs next to the cakes.
Zīmītes ir rakstītas ar roku.The signs are handwritten.
Es lasu nosaukumus uz zīmītēm.I read the names on the signs.
Ir daudz smalkmaizīšu veidu.There are many kinds of pastry.
Ir grūti izvēlēties.It is hard to choose.
Tur ir liela šokolādes kūka.There is a large chocolate cake.
Bet es skatos uz citu smalkmaizīti.But I look at another piece of pastry.
Tā ir liela kanēļmaizīte.It is a large cinnamon roll.
Tai virsū ir daudz cukura.It has a lot of sugar on top.
Man ļoti patīk kanēļa garša.I love the taste of cinnamon.
Tā ir ideāli piemērota aukstai ziemas dienai.It is perfect for a cold winter day.
Es ļoti vēlētos šo kanēļmaizīti.I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
Tagad esmu gatava pasūtīt.Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
Es pieeju pie kases.I walk over to the register.
Jauns vīrietis stāv aiz letes.A young man stands behind the counter.
Viņš ir barista kafejnīcā.He is a barista in the café.
Es viņam uzsmaidu.I smile at him.
Viņš laipni pamāj ar galvu.He nods warmly.
"Sveiki," viņš saka."Hi," he says.
"Ko tu vēlētos?" viņš jautā."What would you like?" he asks.
"Sveiki," es atbildu."Hi," I answer.
"Es vēlētos melnu kafiju, lūdzu.""I would like a black coffee, please."
"Un vai es varētu palūgt arī kanēļmaizīti?""And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
"Protams," viņš saka."Of course," he says.
"Melna kafija un kanēļmaizīte.""A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
"Vai vēlies sēdēt šeit?" viņš jautā."Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
"Jā, lūdzu, es apsēdīšos tur," es saku."Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
"Labi," barista saka."Alright," the barista says.
"Es tagad pagatavošu tavu kafiju.""I will make your coffee now."
"Liels paldies," es saku."Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
Es gaidu pie letes.I wait at the counter.
Viņš gatavo manu kafiju.He makes my coffee.
Viņš noliek karstu kafijas tasi uz koka paplātes.He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
Viņš noliek uz paplātes arī mazu šķīvi.He also puts a small plate on the tray.
Mana kanēļmaizīte ir uz šķīvja.My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
Tā smaržo brīnišķīgi.It smells lovely.
"Lūdzu, jūsu pasūtījums," viņš saka."Here you go, your order," he says.
"Liels paldies," es atbildu."Thank you very much," I reply.
"Paldies par labo apkalpošanu.""Thank you for the good service."
"Lūdzu," viņš laipni saka."You are welcome," he says kindly.
Es paņemu paplāti ar abām rokām.I take the tray with both hands.
Es ļoti uzmanīgi eju pāri grīdai.I walk very carefully across the floor.
Es eju atpakaļ pie sava galda pie loga.I walk back to my table by the window.
Es nolieku paplāti uz galda.I put the tray down on the table.
Tagad es gribētu apsēsties.Now I would like to sit down.
Es esmu gatava labai pauzei.I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
Es tagad sēžu pie sava mazā galdiņa.I now sit at my small table.
Šeit ir jauki un silti.It is lovely and warm here.
Es skatos uz savu kanēļmaizīti.I look at my cinnamon roll.
Tā smaržo saldi un labi.It smells sweet and good.
Es nokožu mazu kumosu.I take a small bite.
Bulciņa ir pilnīgi svaiga un silta.The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
Tā garšo patiešām labi.It tastes really good.
Es iedzeru mazliet no savas melnās kafijas.I drink a little of my black coffee.
Beidzot mani pirksti atkal ir silti.Finally, my fingers are warm again.
Es atpūšos mīkstajā krēslā.I relax in the soft chair.
Kafejnīca ir gandrīz pilna ar cilvēkiem.The café is almost full of people.
Cilvēki sēž mazās grupās.People sit in small groups.
Viņi kopā sarunājas un smejas.They talk and laugh together.
Es klausos klusajās balsīs telpā.I listen to the low voices in the room.
Skaņa ir ļoti mājīga.The sound is very cozy.
Es skatos ārā pa lielo logu.I look out of the large window.
Laiks ārā ir pelēks un vējains.The weather outside is gray and windy.
Tagad patiesībā atkal snieg.Now it is actually snowing again.
Baltas sniegpārslas lēnām krīt lejā uz ielas.White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
Es esmu vienkārši priecīga būt iekšā.I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
Es ēdu savas kanēļmaizītes pēdējo gabaliņu.I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
Tā garšo patiešām labi.It tastes really good.
Bet tā ir arī ļoti salda.But it is also very sweet.
Mana kafijas krūze ir pilnīgi tukša.My coffee cup is completely empty.
Es pēkšņi esmu ļoti izslāpusi.I am suddenly very thirsty.
Es vēlētos nedaudz auksta ūdens.I would like to have some cold water.
Jauns vīrietis iet garām manam galdiņam.A young man walks past my table.
Viņš strādā šeit kafejnīcā.He works here in the café.
Viņš savāc tukšās krūzes un šķīvjus.He collects empty cups and plates.

"Vai drīkstu palūgt glāzi krāna ūdens?" es jautāju."May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
"Jā, protams," viņš saka."Yes, of course," he says.
"Liels paldies," es saku."Thank you very much," I say.
Viņš paņem manu tukšo šķīvi un krūzi.He takes my empty plate and cup.

asking for the bill

paying and leaving
