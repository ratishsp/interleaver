Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Italian with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
È venerdì sera e Nina bussa alla mia porta.It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
Ha con sé il suo cane, Ludwig.She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.
Starà con me per il fine settimana.He is going to stay with me for the weekend.
Nina sorride e mi dà il guinzaglio.Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
"Grazie mille per l'aiuto, Maya", dice."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
"Nessun problema", rispondo felice."It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
Mi spiega un paio di cose su di lui.She explains a few things to me about him.
"Dagli da mangiare alle sette del mattino e alle sei di sera", dice Nina."Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
"E ricorda, non deve mangiare cibo per umani.""And remember, he must not have human food."
Rido e guardo giù verso il cagnolino.I laugh and look down at the little dog.
"No, Ludwig, la mia cena non è per te", gli dico."No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
Anche Nina ride e indica la porta.Nina laughs too and points at the door.
"Tienilo sempre al guinzaglio quando camminate vicino alla strada principale", dice."Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
"Nel parco può correre libero.""In the park he is allowed to run freely."
Annuisco.I nod.
"Non preoccuparti, mi prenderò bene cura di lui.""Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
Nina mi abbraccia e ci saluta.Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
Chiude la porta e rimango sola con Ludwig.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
Alza lo sguardo verso di me e scodinzola.He looks up at me and wags his tail.
Gli sono già molto affezionata.I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll
Storyboard: Before bed, Maya takes Ludwig for a short evening walk around the neighborhood. When he suddenly sniffs at a discarded food wrapper on the pavement, she gently commands him to leave it and pulls him away, reminding him that he must not eat trash.
È venerdì sera tardi.It is late Friday evening.
Prima di andare a letto, porto fuori Ludwig.Before we go to bed, I take Ludwig out.
Facciamo una breve passeggiata nel quartiere.We go for a short walk in the neighborhood.
Le strade sono molto tranquille ora.The streets are very quiet now.
Ludwig cammina bene accanto a me.Ludwig walks nicely next to me.
All'improvviso si ferma.Suddenly he stops.
Annusa avidamente qualcosa sul marciapiede.He sniffs eagerly at something on the pavement.
È un sacchetto sporco con degli avanzi di cibo.It is a dirty bag with food scraps.

"No, lascialo!" gli dico con fermezza."No, drop it!" I say firmly to him.
Lo tiro via delicatamente dal sacchetto.I gently pull him away from the bag.
"La spazzatura non è cibo, Ludwig," spiego."The trash is not food, Ludwig," I explain.
"Vieni qui, Ludwig," lo chiamo."Come here, Ludwig," I call to him.
Lascia il sacchetto e alza lo sguardo verso di me.He drops the bag and looks up at me.
Inizia rapidamente a scodinzolare.He quickly begins to wag his tail.
"Bravo cane," dico e lo accarezzo."Good boy," I say and pet him.
Continuiamo la nostra passeggiata lungo la strada.We continue our walk down the street.
Ora dobbiamo solo tornare a casa a dormire.Now we just need to go home and sleep.
saturday breakfast
Storyboard: Early Saturday morning, Ludwig nudges Maya awake. Maya prepares his breakfast, gently telling him to sit and wait while she fills the bowl, before finally petting him and telling him to eat it.
È sabato mattina alle sette.It is Saturday morning at seven o'clock.
Sto ancora dormendo profondamente.I am still sleeping heavily.
All'improvviso sento qualcosa di freddo sul braccio.Suddenly I feel something cold on my arm.
Apro gli occhi e vedo Ludwig.I open my eyes and see Ludwig.
Mi dà un colpetto con il muso.He nudges me with his snout.
"Buongiorno, Ludwig," dico e mi alzo."Good morning, Ludwig," I say and get up.
Andiamo insieme in cucina.We go out into the kitchen together.
È molto affamato e mi salta addosso.He is very hungry and jumps up on me.
"Calmo, Ludwig," gli dico."Easy now, Ludwig," I say to him.
"Seduto," dico con calma."Sit," I say calmly.
Mi guarda e si siede sul pavimento.He looks at me and sits down on the floor.
Prendo il sacchetto del cibo per cani e lo apro.I take the bag of dog food and open it.

"No, aspetta," dico."No, wait," I say.
Si siede di nuovo e aspetta buono.He sits down again and waits nicely.
Metto giù la ciotola davanti a lui.I put the bowl down in front of him.
Lo accarezzo dolcemente sulla testa.I gently pet him on the head.
"Tieni, mangia," dico con un sorriso."Here you go, eat," I say with a smile.
Scodinzola e inizia a mangiare.He wags his tail and begins to eat.
a morning together
Storyboard: Saturday morning, with the café shift she arranged off in advance so Ludwig is not left alone, Maya has the morning free. she and Ludwig enjoy it together — she throws his ball and calls him back, gives him a good fuss — happy she chose to stay with him.
Dopo colazione ho molto tempo.After breakfast I have plenty of time.
Normalmente faccio volontariato in un caffè il sabato.I normally volunteer at a café on Saturdays.
Ma Ludwig non deve stare da solo.But Ludwig should not be alone.
Per fortuna oggi ho il giorno libero dal caffè.Fortunately I have the day off from the café today.
Mi sono messa d'accordo con loro la settimana scorsa.I arranged it with them last week.
Ora io e Ludwig abbiamo tutta la mattina insieme.Now Ludwig and I have the whole morning together.
Usciamo nel cortile dietro il mio palazzo.We go out into the courtyard behind my building.
Tiro fuori la sua pallina.I take out his little ball.
"Guarda la palla, Ludwig!" gli dico."Look at the ball, Ludwig!" I say to him.
La lancio sull'erba.I throw it across the grass.
"Vai a prenderla!" grido."Fetch it!" I shout.
Corre veloce e afferra la palla.He runs fast and catches the ball.
"Vieni qui!" chiamo."Come here!" I call.
Torna da me correndo felice.He runs happily back to me.
"Dammela," dico dolcemente."Give it to me," I say gently.
Lascia andare la palla e io lo accarezzo.He lets go of the ball, and I pet him.
"Sei un bravo cane," dico e sorrido."You are a good dog," I say and smile.
La lancio di nuovo e lui la riporta.I throw it again, and he fetches it.
Devo prendermi cura di lui per tutto il fine settimana.I must take good care of him all weekend.
Sono molto felice di essere rimasta a casa con lui.I am very happy that I stayed at home with him.
the hundeskov
Storyboard: Saturday afternoon, Maya walks with Ludwig to a local hundeskov (dog forest). Once inside the safe, gated area, she unclips the lead, happily telling him that he may run free now, and throws a stick for him to fetch.
Ludwig cammina velocemente accanto a me.Ludwig walks quickly next to me.

"Vieni, camminiamo per bene.""Come, let us walk nicely."
Finalmente arriviamo al grande cancello.We finally arrive at the large gate.

gentle recall
Storyboard: Ludwig spots a bird and wanders off toward the trees, completely ignoring Maya's loud commands to stop. Realizing yelling does not work, Maya crouches down with a treat, softly calling him over; he quickly runs to her, and she tells him to sit before clipping the leash back on him.

family video call

ninas return
