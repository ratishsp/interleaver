# Week 5 — Storyboard (Maya stocks her new kitchen)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Definite nouns *-en/-et* — singular and bare (an adjective or a plural takes a different definite form, out of scope); *kan lide* — a general preference takes the indefinite plural; *jeg vil gerne have*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_empty_cupboards | Maya stands in the kitchen, noticing that the fridge and the cupboard are completely empty. She grabs a bag and heads out, ready to buy food. |
| 2 | 02_produce_section | Maya enters a supermarket and pulls a basket from a stack by the door. Because she likes carrots, she grabs a large bunch from a green crate and drops it into the basket. |
| 3 | 03_the_parsnip | Looking around, Maya spots a pale, unusual root. She reads a sign and learns it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she decides to buy it anyway out of curiosity. |
| 4 | 04_the_rye_bread | At a shelf with baked goods, Maya looks for a local staple. She lifts a loaf of rye bread, surprised by how heavy it feels, and lays the bread carefully in the basket. |
| 5 | 05_bakery_counter | The bakery counter has a member of staff waiting. Maya catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry. |
| 6 | 06_finding_the_milk | Maya searches for the milk without luck. She stops an employee to ask for help, and he points her toward the dairy section at the back. |
| 7 | 07_taste_of_home | Exploring another section, Maya hunts for a taste of Mexico because she really likes beans. She recognizes a bright tin of black beans on a low shelf and reaches all the way to the back to pull it out. |
| 8 | 08_the_checkout | Maya brings the basket to the checkout. She places the food on the belt, holds her card over the machine, and packs the bag quickly. |
| 9 | 09_putting_away | Back in the flat, Maya empties the bag on the counter. She puts the milk and the parsnip in the fridge, and she places the bread and the tin in the cupboard. |
| 10 | 10_first_home_cooked_meal | Ready to eat, Maya chops some vegetables and opens the tin. She cooks dinner on the stove, finally making a proper meal in the flat. |
