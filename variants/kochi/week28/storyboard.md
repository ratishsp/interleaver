# Week 28 — Storyboard (A week in the new life)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Consolidation across all A2 grammar

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_monday_monsoon_commute | Maya takes her usual bus to the Infopark office on a heavily raining Monday morning. Watching the familiar flooded streets through the window, she reflects on how bewildering this commute felt during her first dry, sunny week back in January; now, she automatically knows exactly where to step to avoid the deep puddles. |
| 2 | 02_office_routine_with_deepa | At the office, Maya and Deepa sit side-by-side to review the user interface implementation on Maya's screen. The massive software release that seemed so intimidating at the kickoff meeting is now just comfortable, daily work; Deepa points out a minor spacing issue in the design layout, Maya adjusts it on the spot, and they head to the breakroom for tea. |
| 3 | 03_thursday_badminton | On Thursday evening at half past five, Maya arrives at the indoor sports club and collects her usual rented racket — the front-desk staff greet her by name now. On the court she plays a tough, energetic match against Sara: the rallies are long and fast, and though Sara's smashes win most of the points, they both leave the court laughing, sweating, and completely exhausted. |
| 4 | 04_friday_vegetable_errand | After work on Friday, Maya walks to the small local market for a quiet errand. The vendor knows her usual order by now — tomatoes, onions, green chilies — and drops a small bunch of curry leaves into her bag for free while they chat about the endless rain; she pays the total, thanks him, and heads home. |
| 5 | 05_saturday_cafe_shift | Saturday morning finds Maya volunteering at the café. The weekend rush is heavy as people take shelter from the monsoon showers, but she and Jessy work in a smooth, unspoken rhythm, calling out orders and wiping down tables as a team. |
| 6 | 06_cooking_a_taste_of_home | Back in her flat on Saturday afternoon, Maya spends a quiet hour cooking a taste of home: a Mexican salsa and a spiced bean dish made from the fresh local ingredients she bought the day before, the familiar smells mixing with the steady sound of the rain outside. |
| 7 | 07_dinner_with_anitha | That evening, Anitha comes over to Maya's flat for dinner, bringing Tommy the dog along. As they eat the homemade meal and chat comfortably about their week, Maya realizes she is no longer just settling in — Kochi has truly become her home. |
