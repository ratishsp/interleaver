# Week 12 — Storyboard (Maya joins a badminton club)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** The modal verbs **können** and **wollen**; **gern** + verb — landing inside varied actions and exchanges, not as declarations

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_office_departure | It is Thursday afternoon at the software company. Maya finishes her work on her Laptop and chats with her colleague Jonas. Today she does not want to go to the gym. She wants to play badminton instead. She puts her sports clothes into her Rucksack and leaves the office at half past four. |
| 2 | 02_ride_to_sports_hall | Maya unlocks her Fahrrad and rides through the busy Berlin streets. It is almost five o'clock. She rides this route gladly — the evening is cool and the streets are busy. She really wants to meet new people in the city. After a short ride, she arrives at a large local sports hall and parks her bike. |
| 3 | 03_renting_the_racket | Maya goes inside to the reception desk. She speaks politely to the staff member, keeping to the formal Sie. She explains that she wants to participate as a guest for the evening. She pays the guest fee of seven Euro, and because she does not own a racket, she rents one for three Euro. She pays with a ten Euro note and declines the Kassenbon. |
| 4 | 04_entering_the_court | Maya walks into the brightly lit sports hall. It is loud, and many people are already running and jumping on the indoor courts. She holds her rented racket and watches a few games. She can see that the players are very fast. She stands near an empty court, hoping she can join a match. Her keenness shows in how she watches — she never announces that she likes badminton. |
| 5 | 05_meeting_the_players | A young woman and two men walk over to the empty court. The woman smiles and speaks to Maya. German sports clubs use the informal du from the first day, so she introduces herself as Lena, and points to Timo and Max. Lena asks Maya if she wants to play a doubles match with them. Maya gladly accepts. The *wollen* here sits in Lena's question and Maya's answer, not in a statement about herself. |
| 6 | 06_the_doubles_match | They start the game. Maya plays with Timo against Lena and Max. Maya plays badminton very often back home in Mexico, so she can hit the shuttlecock hard and accurately. Timo is surprised but very happy with his new partner. They run across the court, score points, and laugh a lot. What she can do is shown by the rally, never claimed out loud. |
| 7 | 07_water_break | After a long, energetic game, the four players take a short break. They stand by the court and drink water. Max asks Maya what she likes to do in Berlin. She replies that she likes to ride her Fahrrad, explore the city, and drink good coffee. Timo asks if she can play tennis too, but she laughs and says she can only play badminton. |
| 8 | 08_planning_next_week | It is eight o'clock, and the players are exhausted. They pack their things, and Maya puts her water bottle into her Rucksack. Lena asks Maya if she wants to come back next Thursday at half past five. Maya definitely wants to return. They all say goodbye, and Maya gives her rented racket back at the reception desk. |
| 9 | 09_happy_ride_home | Maya leaves the sports hall and steps out into the cool evening air. She gets on her Fahrrad and rides back to her Wohnung. Her legs are tired, but she is very cheerful. She can play badminton every Thursday now, and she already knows three friendly people at the club. She does not restate that she enjoys the game. |
