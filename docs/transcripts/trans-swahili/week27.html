tuesday admin
Storyboard: Maya clears her small kitchen table on a quiet Tuesday evening. She realizes she needs to update her payment details on her electricity provider's website, a boring digital chore she usually puts off until the last minute.

Hiyo tayari ni kesho!That is already tomorrow!
Ni jukumu la kidijitali linalochosha sana.It is a really boring digital chore.

Lakini jioni ya leo ninataka kuyashughulikia mambo mapema.But tonight I want to be ahead of things.
Ninaiangalia meza tupu kwa kitambo kidogo.I look at the empty table for a moment.

Sina nguvu ya kuichukua na kuiwasha sasa hivi.I do not have the energy to fetch and start it now.
Kwa bahati nzuri, kwa kawaida mimi hutumia tu simu yangu kwa kazi ndogo ndogo.Fortunately, I usually just use my phone for the small tasks.

Sasa inabidi tu imalizwe na kupita.Now it just needs to be over and done with.
digital form

successful refresh

nina delivery
