# Week 1 — Storyboard (Maya arrives in Berlin)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** **sein** (ich bin / Sie sind), **heißen**, **kommen aus**; **es gibt**; subject pronouns **ich/Sie/er/sie**; **wie/wo/was** questions and yes-no questions with the verb first (*Sind Sie …?*)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_airport_arrival | Maya pulls her heavy suitcase through the sliding doors of the airport into the freezing, dark January evening. She shivers in the sudden cold and asks a passing airport worker where the taxi rank is. |
| 2 | 02_the_taxi_ride | Maya climbs into the back of a warm taxi and gives the driver the address of her new building. The driver asks where she is from and what she does, so she introduces herself politely, explaining that she is a software engineer from Mexico. |
| 3 | 03_finding_the_building | The taxi drops Maya on a quiet street in front of an old building. She reads the names on the bell panel by the heavy front door, finds the right one, and asks herself aloud which staircase and which floor she needs. |
| 4 | 04_meeting_frau_krueger | Hauling her suitcase up the stairs, Maya meets a brisk, friendly woman coming down with a small dog on a lead. They introduce themselves politely by surname — the woman is her neighbour, and the dog is called Bruno — and she asks whether Maya is the new tenant and where she is from. |
| 5 | 05_entering_the_flat | Maya turns the key to her temporary room, flicks on the light, and drops her bags with relief. She looks around at what is there: a bed, a table, a chair, a lamp, and a tall window. |
| 6 | 06_the_window_view | Maya walks to the window and presses her hand against the cold glass, looking out over the unfamiliar, icy city. The winter darkness hits her, and she feels a sudden, sharp pang of homesickness for the warmth of Mexico. |
| 7 | 07_calling_home | Needing a familiar voice, Maya sits on the edge of the bed, takes out her phone, and makes a quick audio call to her parents. She cheerfully reassures them that she is safe, the room is warm, and she is doing fine. |
| 8 | 08_settling_in | Maya unzips her suitcase and unpacks what she needs for the night. She puts her laptop on the small table, hangs up her heavy winter coat, and takes a deep breath, ready for her new life. |
