# Week 3 — Storyboard (A Video Call Home)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Possessives *min/din*; indefinite *en/et*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_evening_supermarket | Maya hurries through the biting wind in her thin jacket and ducks into a local supermarket. She picks out a pastry and a box of tea, actively preparing for her big evening video call. (Det er en kold aften, og min jakke er for tynd. Jeg køber en kage og en te.) |
| 2 | 02_hallway_chat | Entering her apartment building, Maya bumps into her neighbor Nina in the hallway. Maya admits she feels a bit homesick today, but brightens up as she explains she is rushing inside to call her parents. (Hej Nina! Jeg savner min familie i dag. Jeg ringer til min mor og min far nu.) |
| 3 | 03_setting_up_call | Inside her flat, Maya prepares her space for the call. She places a warm steaming mug on a small table, opens a laptop, and clicks the call button, waiting anxiously for a connection. (Jeg stiller en varm kop på et bord. Jeg åbner en computer og ringer hjem.) |
| 4 | 04_parents_and_garden | The screen brightens, and her parents wave from their sunny garden in Mexico. They ask to see her new place, so Maya lifts the laptop and carries it around to show them her flat. (Her er min mor og min far. De har et hus og en have. Hvordan er dit værelse? Se, her er mit værelse.) |
| 5 | 05_lola_the_dog | A loud bark comes through the laptop speakers. Her father lifts the family dog, Lola, up to the webcam, and Maya laughs, pressing her hand to the screen to greet her pet. (Hører jeg en hund? Ja, det er Lola! Hun er min hund.) |
| 6 | 06_siblings_window | Her brother Diego and sister Sofía take the camera from their parents. When they ask about Denmark, Maya walks to her window and points the webcam at the dark, freezing street outside. (Her er min bror og min søster. Er din by kold? Se mit vindue, derude er en mørk gade.) |
| 7 | 07_fridge_photo | After a warm round of blown kisses, Maya closes the laptop, feeling deeply comforted. She pulls a printed photograph from her bag and uses a magnet to proudly pin it to her fridge. (Jeg tager et billede fra min taske. Det er et billede af min familie. Nu sidder min familie på mit køleskab.) |
