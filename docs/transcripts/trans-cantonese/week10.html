Week 10: Maya buys winter clothes

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

a cold morning
Storyboard: It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to the bus stop on her way to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter.
哥本哈根而家係朝早。It is morning in Copenhagen.
我行去巴士站。I walk to the bus stop.
今日天氣好凍。The weather is very cold today.
好大風。It is windy.
啲風凍冰冰。The wind is ice-cold.
我凍僵喇。I am freezing.
我着住一件好薄嘅外套。I am wearing a thin jacket.
我件外套一啲都唔暖。My jacket is not warm at all.
我仲着住一對好薄嘅鞋。I am also wearing thin shoes.
我冇戴保暖嘅頸巾。I am not wearing a warm scarf.
我對手好凍。My hands are very cold.
我凍到震。I am shivering.
丹麥嘅冬天好難捱。The winter in Denmark is hard.
我需要啲保暖嘅衫。I need warm clothes.
我需要一件厚冷衫。I need a thick sweater.
我仲需要一條大頸巾。I also need a large scarf.
同埋我需要保暖嘅手套。And I need warm gloves.
我行得好快。I walk very fast.
我放工之後會去買冬天衫。I am buying winter clothes after work.
entering the shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things.
我放工喇。I am finished at work.
我沿住步行街行。I walk down the pedestrian street.
啲風仲係好凍。The wind is still very cold.
我見到一間好大嘅服裝店。I see a large clothing shop.
間舖頭好光猛。The shop is very bright.
我行入舖頭。I go into the shop.
呢度入面暖笠笠，好舒服。It is wonderfully warm in here.
有個售貨員企喺收銀機後面。A shop assistant stands behind the register.
佢望住我笑。She looks at me and smiles.
「你好，」佢話。"Hello," she says.
「你好，」我答。"Hello," I answer.
「我好凍，」我話。"I am freezing a lot," I say.
「我淨係着咗件薄外套。」"I only have a thin jacket on."
「我想搵啲保暖嘅嘢。」"I would like to find some warm things."
售貨員又笑一笑。The shop assistant smiles again.
「今日都好凍，」佢話。"It is also very cold today," she says.
「我哋有好多厚冷衫。」"We have many thick sweaters."
「我哋仲有保暖嘅冬天衫。」"We also have warm winter clothes."
「你隨便睇吓啦，」佢好親切咁話。"You just take a look," she says kindly.
「唔該晒，」我答。"Many thanks," I answer.
the red sweater
Storyboard: Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it.
我望住貨架上面嘅衫。I look at the clothes on the shelves.
我見到一件厚冷衫。I see a thick sweater.
佢係紅色嘅。It is red.
我需要一件保暖嘅冷衫。I need a warm sweater.
間舖頭有好多尺碼。The shop has many sizes.
我搵到件中碼。I find a size medium.
我行入試身室。I walk into the fitting room.
我著起件紅色冷衫。I put the red sweater on.
我著住佢，望住塊大鏡。I have it on and look in the big mirror.
件冷衫非常啱身。The sweater fits perfectly.
佢非常之暖。It is incredibly warm.
我好鍾意呢隻紅色。I love the red colour.
我睇吓個價錢牌。I look at the price tag.
件冷衫賣三百克朗。The sweater costs three hundred kroner.
對於一件厚冷衫嚟講，呢個價錢好抵。It is a good price for a thick sweater.
我除返件冷衫落嚟。I take the sweater off again.
我買佢。I buy it.
the tight green coat
Storyboard: Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a larger size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. The assistant brings her a warm dark-blue winter jacket in her size instead — it fits perfectly, and Maya decides to take it.
我都需要一件冬天外套。I also need a winter jacket.
我喺舖頭度睇多啲衫。I look at more clothes in the shop.
我搵到一件好靚嘅綠色大褸。I find a beautiful, green coat.
呢款設計非常之靚。It is a very beautiful design.
我着起呢件綠色大褸。I put the green coat on.
但係佢太細件喇。But it is too small.
膊頭位好緊。It is very tight across the shoulders.
「有冇其他碼呀？」我問。"Do you have it in another size?" I ask.
售貨員睇一睇。The shop assistant looks.
「唔好意思，冇喇，」佢答。"No, unfortunately," she answers.
所以我將件綠色大褸掛返上去。So I hang the green coat back.
售貨員搵咗另一件外套畀我。The shop assistant finds another jacket for me.
係一件好暖嘅深藍色冬天外套。It is a warm, dark-blue winter jacket.
我着起呢件深藍色外套。I put the dark-blue jacket on.
啱晒身。It fits perfectly.
佢夠晒大件，又唔緊。It is nice and big and not tight.
我照吓塊大鏡。I look in a large mirror.
我好鍾意。I like it.
「我要呢件，」我話。"I will take it," I say.
the blue scarf
Storyboard: Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases.
我行埋去舖頭入面嘅一張枱度。I walk over to a table in the shop.
枱面有好多嘢。There are many things on the table.
我今日無戴頸巾。I am not wearing a scarf today.
我條頸覺得好凍。I am very cold around my neck.
我見到一條藍色嘅頸巾。I see a blue scarf.
佢係一條好長嘅頸巾。It is a very long scarf.
佢係羊毛造嘅。It is made of wool.
我拎起嗰條藍色頸巾。I take the blue scarf.
我將佢圍喺條頸度。I put it around my neck.
感覺好舒服，好溫暖。It feels wonderfully warm.
店員話：「呢隻色好靚。」"It is a nice colour," the shop assistant says.
我話：「係呀，而且好柔軟。」"Yes, and it is very soft," I say.
我望吓個價錢牌。I look at the price tag.
佢要一百五十克朗。It costs one hundred and fifty kroner.
以一條咁暖嘅頸巾嚟講，呢個價錢真係好抵。It is a good price for a warm scarf.
我將佢除返落嚟。I take it off again.
我同佢講：「我都買埋呢條。」"I am buying it too," I say to her.
我將條藍色頸巾同件紅色冷衫擺埋一齊。I put the blue scarf together with the red sweater.
the black gloves
Storyboard: Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well.
我仲係着住我件薄外套。I am still wearing my thin jacket.
我諗起今朝凍冰冰嘅雙手。I think about my cold hands from this morning.
我需要厚手套。I need thick gloves.
我喺光猛嘅舖頭四圍望。I look around the bright shop.
我見到一個大籃。I see a large basket.
個籃裝滿晒手套。The basket is full of gloves.
我搵到一對黑色嘅冬天手套。I find a pair of black winter gloves.
佢哋好厚、好柔軟。They are very thick and soft.
我戴上對手套。I put the gloves on.
佢哋好啱我戴。They fit me perfectly.
我問面帶笑容嘅店員。I ask the smiling shop assistant.
「呢對黑色手套幾多錢？」我問。"What do the black gloves cost?" I ask.
「賣一百二十克朗，」佢答。"They cost one hundred and twenty kroner," she answers.
一百二十克朗係個好價錢。One hundred and twenty kroner is a good price.
我對手已經好暖。My hands are already warm.
「我要呢對，」我好開心咁講。"I will take them," I say happily.
我除返對手套落嚟。I take the gloves off again.
我將對黑色手套同我件紅色冷衫以及藍色頸巾放埋一齊。I put the black gloves together with my red sweater and my blue scarf.
依家我準備好畀錢喇。Now I am ready to pay.
paying for the clothes
Storyboard: Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the dark-blue winter jacket, the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is one thousand five hundred kroner, and Maya pays with her card.
我攞住啲新衫行過去收銀台。I walk over to the counter with my new clothes.
店員友善咁對住我笑。The shop assistant smiles kindly at me.
「係咪就係咁多？」佢問。"Is that everything?" she asks.
「係呀，就係咁多。」我答。"Yes, that is everything," I answer.
佢掃描晒我所有嘅新嘢。She scans all my new things.
有一件冷衫、一件外套、一條頸巾同一對手套。There is a sweater, a jacket, a scarf and gloves.
「而家你唔會覺得凍喇。」佢好開心咁講。"Now you will not freeze," she says happily.
「總共係一千五百克朗，唔該。」"That will be one thousand five hundred kroner, please."
啲衫好貴，但係好暖。The clothes are expensive, but they are very warm.
我攞張付款卡出嚟。I take out my payment card.
「我用卡畀錢。」我講。"I am paying by card," I say.
「你喺度畀就得喇。」佢講。"You can just pay here," she says.
佢指住一部黑色嘅細機。She points at a small, black machine.
我將張卡放喺部機上面。I hold my card over the machine.
部機「嘟」咗一聲，付款成功。It beeps, and the payment is approved.
「要唔要收據呀？」店員問。"Do you want the receipt?" the shop assistant asks.
「唔使喇，唔該。」我答。"No thank you," I answer.
而家我有一大堆好靚嘅冬天衫喇。Now I own a lot of nice winter clothes.
stepping into the cold
Storyboard: Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She pulls on the red sweater, zips her new dark-blue winter jacket over it, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm.
「要個袋嗎？」店員問。"A bag?" the shop assistant asks.
「唔使喇，唔該，」我話。"No thank you," I say.
我唔需要袋。I do not need a bag.
我即刻著起啲新衫。I put the new clothes on right away.
首先，我著上嗰件厚嘅紅色冷衫。First, I put on the thick, red sweater.
然後，我著上嗰件深藍色嘅冬季外套。Then, I put on the dark-blue winter jacket.
我拉上呢件保暖外套嘅拉鍊。I zip the warm jacket.
之後，我攞起嗰條長嘅藍色頸巾。Afterwards, I take the long, blue scarf.
我將佢圍喺頸度。I wrap it around my neck.
最後，我戴上嗰對新嘅黑色手套。Finally, I put on the new, black gloves.
依家我著晒所有保暖嘅衫。Now I have all my warm clothes on.
我笑住講再見。I smile and say goodbye.
我行出舖頭。I walk out of the shop.
我又再企喺寒冷嘅街道上。I stand out on the cold street again.
天氣凍到結冰。It is freezing cold.
風吹得好大。The wind blows hard.
但係我完全唔覺得凍。But I am not freezing at all.
依家我準備好迎接冬天喇。Now I am ready for winter.
