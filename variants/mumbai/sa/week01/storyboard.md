# Week 1 — Storyboard (Maya arrives in Mumbai)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Equational **अस्ति** (X is Y) and *there is*; pronouns **अहम् / भवान् / भवती / सः / सा**; **किम् / कुत्र** questions and yes-no questions with **किम्**; *मम नाम … अस्ति*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_airport_arrival | Maya steps out of the airport into the mild January night, immediately noticing the sea in the air. Bags around her, she takes in the lights and the crowd, and asks an airport attendant where the prepaid taxi counter is; he points the way. |
| 2 | 02_kaali_peeli_taxi | Maya puts her bags into a classic kaali-peeli taxi and greets the driver respectfully. She shows him her new address written on her phone; he reads it, nods, and sets off. The driver asks if she is a tourist visiting the city, and she explains that she is a software engineer here for work. |
| 3 | 03_building_gate | The taxi stops at her building, and the elderly night watchman comes to the gate. He asks who she is; she explains she is the new resident and gives her name, and he helps her in with the heavy suitcase, waving her warmly toward the stairs. |
| 4 | 04_temporary_room | Maya looks around the quiet temporary room. She finds the switch for the ceiling fan, turns it on, and notes that there is a bed and a sturdy table. At the window, the enormous unfamiliar city stretches into the night, and for a moment she feels a sharp pang for Mexico. |
| 5 | 05_family_photo | Maya opens her suitcase and settles in. She unpacks her clothes and places a framed family photograph — her parents, her sister Sofia, her brother Diego, and their dog Lola — on the table. The room already feels a little more like hers, and she smiles. |
| 6 | 06_calling_mexico | Maya sits on the edge of her bed, connects to the room's wifi, and makes a quick audio call to her parents back in Mexico. She assures them that she is safely in her room and answers their worried questions by confirming that everything is fine. |
| 7 | 07_meeting_asha | As Maya steps into the hallway, another door on the landing opens: a kind woman is heading out with her small dog for its late-night walk. She smiles, introduces herself as Asha, and asks whether Maya is new here and whether she needs anything; Maya says where she is from, and they part warmly. |
| 8 | 08_first_night | Maya returns to her room, washes her face in the small bathroom, and crawls into bed. She listens to the whir of the fan and the hum of the city, excited and nervous that her new life in India is finally beginning. |
