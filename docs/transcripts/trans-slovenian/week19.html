Week 19: Maya starts a new project with the team

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

monday bus excitement
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya takes the bus to work. She feels excited and a little nervous because a large new software project starts today, and she will finally work closely with the entire team.
Ponedeljek zjutraj je in sedim na avtobusu.It is Monday morning, and I am sitting on the bus.
Na poti sem v službo.I am on my way to work.
Gledam skozi okno.I look out of the window.
Danes sem malo živčna.I am a little nervous today.
Ampak se tudi zelo veselim.But I am also looking forward to it a lot.
Danes se začne velik, nov programski projekt.A large, new software project starts today.
Od januarja v podjetju delam kot programska inženirka.I have worked as a software engineer at the company since January.
Dobro poznam svojega sodelavca Petra.I know my colleague Peter well.
Skupaj delava že od mojega prvega dne.We have worked together since my first day.
Ampak zdaj moram tesno sodelovati s celotno ekipo.But now I must work closely with the whole team.
Delala bom z našim šefom, našim oblikovalcem in našim preizkuševalcem.I will work with our boss, our designer, and our tester.
V podjetju delajo že dolgo.They have worked at the company for a long time.
Avtobus se ustavi in vstopi še več ljudi.The bus stops, and more people get on.
Pogledam na uro.I look at my watch.
Ura je osem.It is eight o'clock.
Jutranji sestanek se začne čez pol ure.The morning meeting begins in half an hour.
Globoko vdihnem.I take a deep breath.
Pripravljena sem na nov projekt.I am ready for the new project.
first morning presentation
Storyboard: At the short morning meeting, Maya stands in front of her colleagues. She presents her part of the technical plan for the first time, explaining what she has worked on to prepare for the week's tasks.
Sedimo v sejni sobi.We are sitting in the meeting room.
Čas je za naš kratek jutranji sestanek.It is time for our short morning meeting.
Ozrem se po svojih sodelavcih.I look around at my colleagues.
Danes je zame poseben dan.Today is a special day for me.
Vstanem.I stand up.
Sem programska inženirka in predstavila bom svoj del projekta.I am a software engineer, and I am going to present my part of the project.
Roke se mi malo tresejo.My hands are shaking a little.
Ampak sem se dobro pripravila.But I have prepared well.
"Dobro jutro," rečem naglas."Good morning," I say loudly.
"Delala sem na tehničnem načrtu," jim povem."I have worked on the technical plan," I tell them.
"Prebrala sem vse dokumente.""I have read all the documents."
"In napisala sem zapiske za naloge tega tedna.""And I have written notes for the week's tasks."
Celotni ekipi razložim načrt.I explain the plan to the whole team.
Pravzaprav ni težko govoriti o tehnologiji.It is actually not difficult to talk about the technology.
Peter sedi nasproti mene.Peter is sitting opposite me.
Čutim, da se bolj sproščam.I feel myself relaxing more.
Posluša in prikimava.He listens and nods.
Končam svojo predstavitev in ostali se nasmehnejo.I finish my presentation, and the others smile.
Moja prva predstavitev v službi je šla dobro.My first presentation at work has gone well.
boss project manager
Storyboard: After the morning meeting, her boss Thomas stops by Maya's desk with a part of the new project for her. He is warm and welcoming: he compliments her on her presentation and says the whole team has looked forward to having her on board. Maya feels genuinely welcomed onto the team.
Po jutranjem sestanku sedim za svojo pisalno mizo.After the morning meeting, I sit at my desk.
Gledam svoje zapiske s predstavitve.I look at my notes from the presentation.
Moj šef, Thomas, pride do mene.My boss, Thomas, comes over to me.
On je naš vodja projekta.He is our project manager.
Tukaj dela že vrsto let.He has worked here for many years.
"Dobro jutro, Maya," reče z nasmeškom."Good morning, Maya," he says with a smile.
"To je bila res dobra predstavitev.""That was a really good presentation."
"Hvala, Thomas," odgovorim."Thank you, Thomas," I answer.
"Zelo smo veseli, da te imamo v ekipi," nadaljuje Thomas."We are very happy to have you on the team," Thomas continues.
"Cela ekipa se je veselila sodelovanja s tabo.""The whole team has looked forward to working with you."
"Tudi jaz sem se ga veselila," rečem veselo."I have also looked forward to it," I say happily.
"To je vznemirljiv projekt.""It is an exciting project."
Thomas se znova nasmehne in odide nazaj v svojo pisarno.Thomas smiles again and walks back to his office.
Gledam v zaslon svojega računalnika.I look at my computer screen.
V ekipi se počutim resnično dobrodošlo.I feel genuinely welcome on the team.
peter software engineer
Storyboard: Maya sits down with Peter to write code. As software engineers, they solve complex problems together, and Peter jokes that he has worked on one specific error for three hours until Maya easily spots his mistake.
Usedem se k Petrovi pisalni mizi.I sit down at Peter's desk.
Gledava njegov velik zaslon.We look at his large screen.
Tudi Peter je programski inženir.Peter is also a software engineer.
Je dober sodelavec in skupaj piševa veliko kode.He is a good colleague, and we write a lot of code together.
Danes je videti zelo utrujen.Today he looks very tired.
"Kaj delaš, Peter?" vprašam."What are you doing, Peter?" I ask.
"Imam velik problem," odgovori."I have a big problem," he answers.
"Na tej napaki sem delal tri ure.""I have worked on this error for three hours."
Nasmehnem se in si natančno ogledam kodo.I smile and look closely at the code.
Pri delu pogosto skupaj rešujeva zapletene probleme.We often solve complex problems together in our work.
Nenadoma na zaslonu zagledam nekaj čudnega.Suddenly I see something strange on the screen.
To je zelo majhna napaka.It is a very small mistake.
"Poskusi pogledati sem," rečem in pokažem na zaslon."Try to look here," I say and point at the screen.
"Tukaj si naredil majhno tipkarsko napako.""You have made a small typo here."
Peter dve sekundi tiho gleda v zaslon.Peter looks quietly at the screen for two seconds.
Nato se na glas zasmeje.Then he laughs loudly.
"Imaš popolnoma prav," reče Peter."You are absolutely right," Peter says.
"Delal sem tri ure, in potem je to le ena majhna napaka!""I have worked for three hours, and then it is just one small error!"
Tudi jaz se smejim, saj vsi programski inženirji poznajo to situacijo.I laugh too, because all software engineers know that situation.
Popraviva napako in program končno deluje brezhibno.We fix the error, and the program finally works perfectly.
eva digital designer
Storyboard: Later, Maya walks over to meet Eva, the team's designer. Eva shows Maya her beautiful sketches and explains that she has worked as a digital designer since she finished university, focusing on how users experience the product.
Pozneje popoldne grem do Evine pisalne mize.Later in the afternoon, I walk over to Eva's desk.
Ona je digitalna oblikovalka ekipe.She is the team's digital designer.
Pred današnjim dnem še nisem delala z njo.I have not worked with her before today.
"Živijo, Maya, pridi in poglej," reče."Hi Maya, come and look," she says.
Pokaže na svoj velik zaslon.She points at her large screen.
Vidim nekaj čudovitih skic naše nove programske opreme.I see some beautiful sketches of our new software.
Barve so mirne in oblikovanje je zelo čisto.The colors are calm, and the design is very clean.
"To je videti res dobro," rečem."That looks really good," I say.
"Hvala," odgovori Eva z nasmeškom."Thank you," Eva answers with a smile.
Razloži mi svoje delo.She explains her work to me.
"Kot digitalna oblikovalka delam že vse od univerze," reče."I have worked as a digital designer since university," she says.
"Vedno se osredotočam na uporabniško izkušnjo.""I always focus on the user's experience."
Pokaže mi, kako uporabnik klika po programu.She shows me how the user clicks around the program.
"Mora biti preprosto in logično za uporabo," pojasni."It must be easy and logical to use," she explains.
Pogosto gledam zgolj samo kodo.I often only look at the code itself.
Toda njeno oblikovanje je izjemno pomembno za projekt.But her design is incredibly important for the project.
Še malo se pogovarjava o najinih skupnih nalogah.We talk a little more about our shared tasks.
Veselim se, da bom sprogramirala njeno čudovito oblikovanje.I look forward to programming her beautiful design.
meeting the tester
Storyboard: The next day, Maya sits with Mads, the software tester, as he clicks quickly through the new screens and spots a small mistake at once. He laughs that his job is to break things before the users do — he has done it for five years and finds problems fast.
Je naslednji dan v pisarni.It is the next day at the office.
Sedim poleg Madsa, našega testerja programske opreme.I am sitting next to Mads, our software tester.
Gleda nove zaslone.He looks at the new screens.
Zelo hitro klika z miško.He clicks very fast with the mouse.
Nenadoma se ustavi.Suddenly he stops.
Že je našel majhno napako.He has already found a small mistake.
Pogleda me in se malo zasmeje.He looks at me and laughs a little.
"Moje delo je, da uničujem stvari," pravi."My job is to break things," he says.
"Napake moram najti pred našimi uporabniki.""I have to find the mistakes before our users."
Kot tester dela že pet let.He has worked as a tester for five years.
Testiral je veliko različnih projektov.He has tested many different projects.
Zato tako hitro najde težave.That is why he finds the problems so fast.
Pogledam napako na svojem računalniku.I look at the mistake on my computer.
Na srečo je to le majhna težava.Fortunately, it is only a small problem.
Kodo takoj popravim.I fix the code right away.
Program testira še enkrat.He tests the program one more time.
Zdaj vse deluje popolnoma brezhibno.Now everything works absolutely perfectly.
Zelo sem vesela njegove pomoči.I am very glad for his help.

friday canteen lunch
