Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Spanish with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
El avión está en Copenhague ahora.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
Es enero.It is January.
Estoy muy emocionada.I am very excited.
Guardo mi libro en mi bolso.I pack my book into my bag.
Hay un pasajero a mi lado.There is a passenger next to me.
Él me mira y sonríe.He looks at me and smiles.
"Hola", dice."Hello," he says.
"Hola", digo."Hello," I say.
"¿Cómo te llamas?", pregunta."What is your name?" he asks.
"Me llamo Maya", digo."My name is Maya," I say.
"¿De dónde vienes, Maya?", pregunta."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
Sonrío feliz.I smile happily.
"Vengo de México", respondo."I come from Mexico," I answer.
"¿Eres de Dinamarca?", pregunto."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
"Sí, soy de Dinamarca", dice."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
"Bienvenida a Dinamarca", dice."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
"Muchas gracias", digo."Thank you very much," I say.
Ahora estamos aquí.Now we are here.
Estoy lista.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
Ahora estoy afuera.I am outside now.
Mi maleta es grande y pesada.My suitcase is big and heavy.
Hay un taxi.There is a taxi.
Subo mi maleta al taxi.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
El conductor es un hombre amable.The driver is a friendly man.
"¿Cuál es la dirección?", pregunta él."What is the address?" he asks.
"Es Parkvej número doce", digo."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
Él asiente y conduce.He nods and drives.
"¿Eres turista?", pregunta el conductor."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
"No, no soy turista", respondo."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
"¿De dónde vienes?", pregunta él."Where do you come from?" he asks.
"Vengo de México", digo."I come from Mexico," I say.
Miro por la ventana.I look out the window.
Estoy muy orgullosa.I am very proud.
"Soy ingeniera de software", digo."I am a software engineer," I say.
"Mi nuevo trabajo es aquí en Copenhague"."My new job is here in Copenhagen."
Él sonríe.He smiles.
"Qué emocionante"."How exciting."
"Buena suerte en Dinamarca", dice él."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
"Muchas gracias", digo."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
La puerta está abierta.The door is open.
El umbral es alto.The threshold is high.
¡La maleta está muy pesada!The suitcase is very heavy!
Pero ahora la maleta está en el pasillo.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
Aquí está completamente oscuro.It is completely dark here.
¿Dónde está el interruptor?Where is the switch?
La pared está fría.The wall is cold.
¿Está aquí el interruptor?Is the switch here?
¡Sí, ahí está el interruptor!Yes, there is the switch!
Ahora hay luz.Now there is light.
Estoy en el departamento.I am in the apartment.
Es pequeño, pero es bonito.It is small, but it is nice.
Aquí hay una sala.Here is a living room.
Hay un sofá y una mesa.There is a sofa and a table.
Es un hogar amueblado.It is a furnished home.
La maleta está en el piso.The suitcase is on the floor.
Estoy muy cansada.I am very tired.
Pero también estoy feliz.But I am also happy.
¡Estoy en Copenhague!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
Estoy de pie junto a la ventana.I am standing by the window.
El vidrio está frío.The glass is cold.
Es enero.It is January.
Miro hacia afuera.I look out.
Copenhague está gris hoy.Copenhagen is grey today.
Hace frío afuera.It is cold outside.
Hay nieve en la calle.There is snow on the street.
Pero aquí hace calor.But it is warm here.
El departamento está bien.The apartment is good.
Pienso en México.I think about Mexico.
En México hay sol.In Mexico, there is sun.
Allá también hace calor.It is also warm there.
Extraño un poco el sol.I miss the sun a little.
Pero sonrío.But I smile.
Es una ciudad nueva.It is a new city.
Estoy en Dinamarca ahora.I am in Denmark now.
Es un buen comienzo.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
Estoy en la sala ahora.I am in the living room now.
Hay un sofá pequeño.There is a small sofa.
Llamo a mi madre en México.I call my mother in Mexico.
"Hola, mamá", digo."Hi Mom," I say.
"Hola, Maya", dice mi madre."Hi Maya," my mother says.
"¿Dónde estás?", pregunta ella."Where are you?" she asks.
"Estoy en Copenhague", respondo."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
"Estoy en el departamento ahora.""I am in the apartment now."
"Eso es muy bueno", dice mi madre."That is so good," my mother says.
"¿Es grande?", pregunta ella."Is it big?" she asks.
"No, es pequeño", respondo."No, it is small," I answer.
"Pero es acogedor, y estoy contenta.""But it is cozy, and I am happy."
"¿Hace frío?", pregunta ella."Is it cold?" she asks.
"Sí, hace frío afuera", digo."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
"Pero aquí adentro está cálido.""But it is warm in here."
"Estoy segura.""I am safe."
"Eso es bueno", dice mi madre."That is good," my mother says.
Sonrío.I smile.
Dinamarca es mi nuevo hogar.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
Abro mi puerta.I open my door.
Estoy parada en las escaleras.I am standing in the stairwell.
Hay silencio.It is quiet.
Una puerta se abre.A door opens.
Una mujer me sonríe.A woman smiles at me.
"Hola", dice ella."Hi," she says.
"¿Cómo te llamas?", pregunta ella."What is your name?" she asks.
"Me llamo Maya", respondo."My name is Maya," I answer.
"¿De dónde eres?", pregunta ella."Where do you come from?" she asks.
"Soy de México", digo."I come from Mexico," I say.
"Bienvenida al edificio", dice la mujer."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
"Me llamo Nina", dice ella."My name is Nina," she says.
"Soy tu vecina"."I am your neighbour."
Nina es muy amable.Nina is very friendly.
Habla despacio y claro.She speaks slowly and clearly.
"Aquí está mi puerta", dice ella."Here is my door," she says.
"Con gusto te ayudo"."I am happy to help."
"Solo tienes que tocar", dice Nina."You just knock," Nina says.
"Muchas gracias, Nina", respondo."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
Estoy muy contenta.I am very happy.
Ahora tengo una amiga en Copenhague.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
Ahora es de noche.Now it is night.
Es mi primera noche en Copenhague.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
Estoy en el baño.I am in the bathroom.
Es muy pequeño.It is very small.
Hay un lavabo.There is a sink.
Mi cepillo de dientes es rojo.My toothbrush is red.
Pongo pasta de dientes en mi cepillo.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
Me cepillo los dientes.I brush my teeth.
El agua está fría.The water is cold.
Me lavo la cara.I wash my face.
Me seco la cara.I dry my face.
Voy hacia mi cama.I go over to my bed.
La cama es angosta.The bed is narrow.
Pero es cálida y suave.But it is warm and soft.
Ahora estoy acostada bajo el edredón.I am lying under the duvet now.
Mi teléfono está sobre la mesa.My phone is lying on the table.
Pongo mi alarma.I set my alarm.
Suena mañana a las ocho.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
Estoy cansada, pero estoy lista.I am tired, but I am ready.
Buenas noches, Copenhague.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
