# Week 5 — Storyboard (Maya stocks her new kitchen)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Wanting and liking with the dative experiencer — **मला हवं** and **मला आवडतं**; the object marker **-ला** on people

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_empty_cupboards | Maya opens her kitchen cabinets in the evening, hoping to make dinner. She sees only empty shelves and realizes she immediately needs groceries for her new flat. |
| 2 | 02_grocery_list | Maya sits at her desk with a notebook to plan her shopping. She writes down the basic staples she wants for the week and makes sure to include the fresh fruits she likes. |
| 3 | 03_supermarket_aisles | Maya walks into a brightly lit supermarket in her neighbourhood. She pushes a small shopping cart down the long aisles, carefully selecting a heavy bag of rice and some yellow dal. |
| 4 | 04_finding_coffee | Maya looks around for coffee but cannot find the right shelf. She approaches a store worker, addressing him politely as bhaiya, and asks him where the hot drinks are kept. |
| 5 | 05_street_vegetable_cart | Leaving the supermarket, Maya stops at a busy street vegetable cart. She tells the vendor she wants a kilo of fresh tomatoes and some spinach, picking out the ones she likes best. |
| 6 | 06_curious_green_vegetable | Maya points to a bumpy, dark green vegetable on the cart that she does not recognize. The vendor explains it is karela, and after hearing it is bitter, Maya decides she does not want it today. |
| 7 | 07_kirana_shop_spices | Maya visits the small corner kirana shop for her essential cooking supplies. She reads her list to the older shopkeeper across the counter, asking him for turmeric, cumin, and salt. |
| 8 | 08_asha_helps_out | Maya walks into her building compound carrying three heavy, bulging cloth bags. Her neighbour Asha sees her struggling and quickly steps forward, kindly taking the heaviest bag to help her upstairs. |
| 9 | 09_a_full_kitchen | Maya stands in her kitchen, happily unpacking the groceries onto the counter. She empties the simple plastic packets of spices from the kirana shop into her new stainless steel masala dabba, finally ready to cook her first real dinner. |
