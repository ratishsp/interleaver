# Week 3 — Storyboard (A video call home to Mexico)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Possessives *min/din*; indefinite *en/et*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_the_flower_shop | On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag. |
| 2 | 02_evening_prep | Maya arrives at her temporary room and sets the new plant on a small desk. With it already being pitch black outside, she turns on a warm lamp to reflect against the dark window, makes a cup of tea, and flips open her laptop to dial home. |
| 3 | 03_parents_and_lola | The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen. |
| 4 | 04_the_siblings | Her younger sister Sofía and older brother Diego squeeze into the frame, playfully shoving each other. Sofía proudly holds up a thick university textbook, while Diego jokingly asks Maya if she is surviving the freezing Danish winter. |
| 5 | 05_temporary_room_tour | Her mother asks for a tour of her current living space. Maya pans the camera around to show her new plant and the small bed, explaining that it is just a temporary room and pointing out the black, sunless window. |
| 6 | 06_mexico_garden | Feeling a bit homesick, Maya asks her father to take the phone outside. He walks out to their sunny garden, showing a large, familiar leafy tree. Maya smiles deeply, feeling warm and comforted by the glimpse of home before they all wave goodbye. |
