Week 8: Maya explores the city

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
Je sobota ráno.It is Saturday morning.
Nad mestom svieti slnko.The sun is shining over the city.
Idem dole na ulicu.I go down to the street.
Moja suseda, Nina, stojí vonku pred našou budovou.My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
„Dobré ráno, Maya,“ hovorí a usmieva sa."Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
„Dobré ráno, Nina,“ odpovedám."Good morning, Nina," I answer.
„Dnes mám voľno,“ hovorím jej."I have the day off today," I tell her.
„Vyrážam do mesta.“"I am heading out into the city."
„Kam ideš?“ pýta sa Nina."Where are you going?" Nina asks.
„Idem dole k vode,“ hovorím."I am walking down to the water," I say.
„Ale nepoznám cestu.“"But I do not know the way."
Nina ukazuje dole ulicou.Nina points down the street.
„Našťastie je to veľmi jednoduché,“ hovorí."Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
„Tu choď rovno.“"Go straight ahead here."
„A pri parku zaboč doľava.“"And turn left at the park."
„Potom prídeš priamo dole k prístavu.“"Then you come right down to the harbour."
„Veľmi pekne ti ďakujem za pomoc, Nina,“ hovorím."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
„Niet za čo a šťastnú cestu,“ odpovedá."You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
Zamávam jej.I wave to her.
Začnem svoju prechádzku smerom k vode.I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
Sledujem Nininu trasu.I follow Nina's route.
Vzduch je svieži a chladný.The air is fresh and cold.
Teraz kráčam po hlavnej ulici.Now I walk down the main street.
Najprv kráčam len rovno.First I just walk straight ahead.
Na ulici je veľká premávka.There is a lot of traffic on the street.
Cestou vidím tri žlté bicykle.On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
Bicykle rýchlo prechádzajú okolo mňa.The bicycles ride quickly past me.
Následne kráčam ďalej vpred.After that I walk further ahead.
Potom prídem k veľkému parku.Then I come to a large park.
Teraz si spomínam na Ninine slová.Now I remember Nina's words.
"Pri parku zaboč doľava," hovorí."Turn left at the park," she says.
Tu zabočím doľava.Here I turn left.
O chvíľu neskôr vidím otvorené dvere.A little later I see an open door.
Je to malá pekáreň.It is a small bakery.
Je tu krásna vôňa čerstvej kávy.There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
Milujem teplý chlieb.I love warm bread.
Dnes je mesto krásne.Today the city is lovely.
Potom kráčam ďalej smerom k vode.Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
Prichádzam na veľké námestie.I arrive at a large square.
Je tu veľa ľudí.There are many people.
Ale nevidím vodu.But I do not see the water.
Hľadám prístav.I am looking for the harbour.
Vidím staršieho muža na chodníku.I see an older man on the pavement.
Zastavím ho.I stop him.
„Prepáčte,“ hovorím."Excuse me," I say.
„Kde je prístav?“"Where is the harbour?"
Starší muž prikývne.The older man nods.
Ukáže dopredu.He points ahead.
„Choďte rovno cez námestie,“ hovorí."Walk straight across the square," he says.
„Prejdite okolo múzea.“"Go past the museum."
„A zabočte doprava.“"And turn right."
Pozriem sa cez námestie.I look across the square.
Vidím veľkú budovu.I see a large building.
Je to múzeum.It is the museum.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem,“ hovorím."Thank you very much," I say.
„Niet za čo,“ odpovedá."You are welcome," he replies.
Kráčam ďalej.I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
Prechádzam cez veľké námestie.I walk across the large square.
Zrazu počujem hudbu.Suddenly, I hear music.
Na rohu stojí pouličný hudobník.On the corner stands a street musician.
Hrá na gitare.He plays the guitar.
Počúvam dobrú hudbu.I listen to the good music.
Dlho sa na neho pozerám.I look at him for a long time.
Potom idem ďalej.Then I walk on.
Zabúdam na múzeum.I forget the museum.
Zabúdam aj na svoju trasu.I also forget my route.
Namiesto toho kráčam úzkou bočnou uličkou.Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
Je tu veľmi ticho.It is very quiet here.
Naokolo vidím len nejaké malé domy.All around I only see some small houses.
Potom zastanem.Then I stop.
Kde je múzeum?Where is the museum?
Teraz sa pozerám okolo seba.Now I look around.
Už ho nevidím.I cannot see it anymore.
Rýchlo sa pozriem späť hore ulicou.I quickly look back up the street.
Nevidím ani námestie.I cannot see the square either.
Som úplne stratená.I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
Stojím na tichej ulici.I am standing on a quiet street.
Stratila som sa.I am lost.
Pred sebou vidím mladú ženu.Ahead of me I see a young woman.
Venčí malého psa.She is walking a small dog.
Ten pes vyzerá ako pes mojej rodiny, Lola.The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
Podídem k tej žene.I walk over to the woman.
"Prepáčte," hovorím."Excuse me," I say.
"Hľadám prístav.""I am looking for the harbour."
Žena zastane a počúva.The woman stops and listens.
Ukáže naspäť hore ulicou.She points back up the street.
"Choďte rovno späť na námestie," hovorí."Go straight back to the square," she says.
"A pri múzeu odbočte doprava.""And turn right at the museum."
"Veľmi pekne ďakujem za pomoc," hovorím."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Niet za čo," odpovedá žena."You are welcome," the woman replies.
Pozriem sa dole na malého psa.I look down at the little dog.
Pozerá sa na mňa.It looks at me.
Idem späť smerom k námestiu.I walk back toward the square.
Teraz viem, kde som.Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
Kráčam späť po tichej ulici.I walk back along the quiet street.
Idem rovno.I walk straight ahead.
Čoskoro opäť vidím to veľké námestie.Soon I see the large square again.
Potom prechádzam cez námestie.Then I walk across the square.
Teraz stojím pri múzeu.Now I am standing by the museum.
Pamätám si slová tej ženy: „Odbočte doprava.“I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
Pri múzeu odbočím doprava.At the museum I turn right.
Kráčam dolu širokou ulicou.I walk down a wide street.
Pozerám sa dopredu.I look ahead.
Konečne vidím niečo modré.Finally I see something blue.
Je to voda!It is the water!
Cestou idem okolo malej kaviarne.On the way I walk past a little café.
Nakoniec idem až úplne dole k vode.At last I walk all the way down to the water.
Teraz som v prístave.Now I am at the harbour.
Prístav je veľmi veľký.The harbour is very large.
Vietor je chladný a svieži.The wind is cold and fresh.
Na vode je veľa lodí.There are many boats on the water.
Tu sa zastavím.Here I stop.
Stojím a pozerám sa na lode.I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
Kráčam rovno popri prístave.I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
Prechádzam okolo malých člnov.I walk past the small boats.
Zrazu vidím veľa ľudí.Suddenly I see many people.
Stoja dole pri vode.They stand down by the water.
Idem tam.I walk over there.
Konečne ju vidím.Finally, I see it.
Malá morská panna sedí na kameni vo vode.The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
Je to veľmi slávna socha v Kodani.She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.
Tá postava v skutočnosti nie je veľmi veľká.The figure is actually not very big.
Ale bronzová postava je na slnku krásna.But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
Stojím medzi ostatnými návštevníkmi.I stand among the other visitors.
Veľa turistov si fotí sochu.Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
Aj ja si vyťahujem telefón.I also take out my phone.
Robím krásnu fotku morskej panny.I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
Posielam fotku svojej rodine do Mexika.I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
Moja sestra Sofía rýchlo odpovedá.My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
„Je veľmi pekná,“ píše."She is very pretty," she writes.
Znova sa pozerám na vodu.I look at the water again.
walking home
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.
Je neskoro popoludní.It is late in the afternoon.
Teraz idem domov do svojho bytu.Now I walk home to my apartment.
Idem jednoduchou cestou.I walk an easy way.
Len idem po tej dlhej ceste.I just follow the long road.
Cesta je úplne rovná.The road is completely straight.
Idem popri vode.I walk along the water.
Nakoniec odbočím od vody.Finally I turn away from the water.
Teraz idem opäť do mesta.Now I walk into the city again.
Potom idem okolo pekárne.Then I walk past the bakery.
Stále to vonia kávou.It still smells of coffee.
O niečo neskôr vidím park.A little later I see the park.
Pri parku odbočím doprava.At the park I turn right.
Nikoho sa nepýtam na cestu.I do not ask anyone for directions.
Cestu nájdem úplne sama.I find the way all by myself.
O niečo neskôr vidím svoju vlastnú ulicu.A little later I see my own street.
Nakoniec stojím pri svojich vchodových dverách.Finally I stand at my front door.
Od dverí sa pozerám späť dole ulicou.From the door I look back down the street.
Teraz poznám svoju cestu domov.Now I know my way home.
