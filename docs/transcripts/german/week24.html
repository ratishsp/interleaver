Week 24: Maya looks after Sabine's dog

sabines instructions
Storyboard: Sabine arrives one Friday evening at Maya's flat with her small dog, Bruno, his basket, and a bag containing his food, lead and favourite toy. Sabine is going away for the weekend. Standing in the hallway, Sabine quickly lists the rules for Bruno's care. She emphasizes what Maya must do and what Bruno is not allowed to do, such as sleeping on the human bed. Maya takes the bag and reassures her friend that she will take great care of the dog. She smiles and says, 'I will feed him'. Sabine says a warm goodbye and leaves for her trip.

early routine
Storyboard: Maya wakes up early on Saturday before her café shift. Knowing Bruno cannot be left alone all morning without a bathroom break, she first takes him for a quick walk around the block, keeping him strictly on the lead as Sabine instructed. Back inside, Bruno is whining for breakfast. Maya opens his dog food. As Bruno jumps up excitedly, Maya calms him down: 'Sit!'. Once he sits, she places the bowl on the floor for him.
