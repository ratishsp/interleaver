Week 6: Maya settles into a daily routine

morning routine
Storyboard: In the morning, Maya wakes up and gets out of bed. She steps into the bathroom, where she takes a warm shower and washes herself to wake up fully.

Топлата вода ме събужда.The warm water wakes me up.
Накрая спирам душа.Finally, I turn off the shower.

Напълно съм будна и готова за деня.I am completely awake and ready for the day.
getting ready

Накрая взимам чантата и якето си.Finally, I take my bag and my jacket.
Заключвам вратата и излизам.I lock the door and go out.
the bus ride

lunch with peter
