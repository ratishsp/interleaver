Week 12: Maya joins a badminton club

finding a club
Storyboard: Maya works out at the gym on Wednesdays but wants to meet people, so she looks for a social sport. She loves badminton — she played it a lot back home in Mexico — so she finds a local club online and books a trial session for Thursday evening.

Aga ma tahan ka mõne spordialaga tegeleda.But I also want to play a sport.
Ma armastan sulgpalli mängida.I love to play badminton.
Ma mängisin seda kodus Mehhikos palju.I played it a lot back home in Mexico.
Nüüd otsin ma kohalikku klubi.Now I am looking for a local club.
Ma istun oma diivanil arvutiga.I am sitting on my couch with my computer.

Ma leian hea sulgpalliklubi.I find a good badminton club.
Ma vaatan klubi kodulehte.I look at the club's website.
Neil on palju meeskondi.They have many teams.
Nad mängivad neljapäeva õhtul.They play on Thursday evening.
Ma tahan broneerida proovitrenni.I want to book a trial session.
Ma leian kodulehelt ankeedi.I find a form on the website.
Ma kirjutan oma nime ja e-maili.I write my name and my email.
Ma valin neljapäeva õhtu.I choose Thursday evening.
Trenn algab kell seitse õhtul.The training starts at seven in the evening.
See on ideaalne.It is perfect.
Ma ootan väga, et saaksin jälle mängida.I look forward to playing again.
arriving at hall
Storyboard: On Thursday evening Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sound of shoes squeaking on the many wooden courts.

renting the racket

Noor mees seisab leti taga.A young man stands behind the counter.
Ta naeratab ja ütleb tere.He smiles and says hello.
"Tere, ma tahan täna sulgpalli mängida," ütlen ma."Hello, I want to play badminton today," I say.
"Kas sa oled siin klubis uus?" küsib ta."Are you new here in the club?" he asks.
"Jah, ma tahaksin proovida kaasa lüüa," vastan ma."Yes, I would like to try to join in," I answer.
"Tere tulemast," ütleb mees."Welcome," the man says.

Ta leiab mulle hea reketi.He finds a good racket for me.
"See maksab nelikümmend krooni," ütleb ta."It costs forty kroner," he says.

Ta annab mulle reketi üle leti.He gives me the racket over the counter.

playing doubles
