# Week 20 — Storyboard (Maya starts volunteering at the café)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Perfekt and present mixed; **lernen** + zu-infinitive; ordinals **erste/zweite**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_saturday_morning_ride | On a pleasant Saturday morning, Maya leaves the flat and hops on her bicycle. She has decided to volunteer on Saturday mornings at a non-profit neighbourhood café to meet people outside of work. She pedals through the streets, excited and slightly nervous for her very first shift. |
| 2 | 02_meeting_elif | Maya arrives at the Kiezcafé and heads around the counter, where she is greeted by Elif, the experienced hand who organizes the weekend volunteer shifts. Elif shows Maya where to leave her jacket and hands her an apron, welcoming her to the team. |
| 3 | 03_learning_the_machine | Elif introduces Maya to the gleaming, donated espresso machine. She explains that it takes patience to master. Maya watches Elif pull a perfect shot, noting that she has to learn to operate the machine properly. They practice tamping the coffee grounds together while the community space is still quiet. |
| 4 | 04_the_first_coffee | The doors open, and a local resident orders a cappuccino. Maya steps up to make her first coffee. She fumbles the milk jug and the espresso poured far too fast. It is a minor disaster, and Elif kindly remakes it. |
| 5 | 05_the_second_coffee | Ten minutes later, another patron approaches. Maya takes a deep breath and tries again. She focuses on everything Elif taught her. This time, her second coffee turns out much better. The milk is smooth, the customer smiles, and Maya feels a rush of relief. |
| 6 | 06_till_and_pastries | To give Elif a break from the register, Maya takes over the till. An older gentleman orders a black coffee and a Brötchen. Maya carefully bags the Brötchen, rings up the price in Euro, and successfully navigates the formal exchange, asking politely if he would like his receipt. |
| 7 | 07_shift_break | After the initial morning rush, there is a lull. Elif and Maya lean against the back counter and share coffee. Elif praises Maya's quick progress, saying it is perfectly normal to need time to learn the machine (lernen, guten Espresso zu machen). They chat comfortably about the upcoming neighbourhood events the Verein is planning. |
| 8 | 08_video_call_home | The next day, Sunday, Maya is relaxing at home. She opens her laptop to video call her younger sister Sofia back in Mexico. Maya describes the chaos of her first shift and the challenge of mastering the espresso machine. She explains that it feels incredible to step away from tech and learn to do something completely new and physical (lernen, etwas Neues zu tun), making her feel truly rooted in Berlin. |
