# Week 13 — Storyboard (Maya goes to a café)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Modal *vil gerne*; politeness; *må jeg bede om*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_escaping_the_wind | Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the draft. |
| 2 | 02_finding_a_seat | She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm. |
| 3 | 03_the_pastry_case | Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry, settles on a cinnamon roll, and returns to her seat. |
| 4 | 04_ordering_the_food | A waiter approaches Maya's table with a notepad. She practices her new Danish phrases to request a cup of black coffee and a cheese sandwich. |
| 5 | 05_observing_the_room | Maya sits alone and listens to the low hum of voices in the room. She watches the snow start to fall outside the window and finally feels her fingers thaw. |
| 6 | 06_receiving_the_food | The waiter returns and sets a steaming mug and a plate in front of Maya. She smiles, thanks him for the service, and takes her first sip of the hot coffee. |
| 7 | 07_one_more_thing | Maya eats her sandwich but realizes she is very thirsty. When the waiter walks past her table, she stops him and asks if she may have a glass of tap water. |
| 8 | 08_asking_for_the_bill | Maya finishes her food and gathers her bag from the floor. She catches the waiter's eye across the room and asks him to bring the bill to her table. |
| 9 | 09_paying_and_leaving | The waiter brings a wireless terminal, and Maya pays eighty-five kroner. She puts on her heavy coat, says goodbye to the staff, and pushes the door open to face the cold again. |
