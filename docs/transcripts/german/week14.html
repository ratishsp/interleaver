the fellow passenger
Storyboard: At the next station a lot of people get in, and the train is suddenly quite full. An older passenger stops beside Maya and asks: is the seat free? Maya says yes and moves over a little, so that he can sit down comfortably. They exchange a few friendly words about the mild spring day.

„Ja, natürlich“, sage ich.“Yes, of course,” I say.

„Das Wetter ist heute wirklich schön.“"The weather is really nice today."

the coffee roaster

He lets her smell two kinds and she chooses a dark coffee. He weighs out the beans and she pays. Outside the sun is on the water, and she walks a little along the river with the packet in her backpack.
