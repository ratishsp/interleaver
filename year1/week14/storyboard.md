# Week 14 — Storyboard (Maya takes public transport across the city)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** *tage/køre med*; *til/med*; *skal til*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_checking_the_route | Maya looks at a travel app on her phone. She needs to go across the city to pick up a package, so she plans a route with three different types of public transport. |
| 2 | 02_topping_up_the_card | She walks to a nearby ticket machine. She taps her blue travel card on the screen and pays two hundred kroner to top up her balance for the long trip. |
| 3 | 03_taking_the_bus | Maya waits at the bus stop until a long yellow bus arrives. She steps inside, scans her card at the door, and finds an empty seat by the window. |
| 4 | 04_asking_a_passenger | She turns to an older woman sitting nearby. Maya politely asks if she travels with this bus to the metro station, and the woman nods with a friendly smile. |
| 5 | 05_transferring_to_metro | The bus stops, and Maya gets off at a busy square. She walks down the wide stairs into a bright, underground metro station and taps her card again. |
| 6 | 06_riding_the_metro | Maya stands right at the front of the driverless train. She rides with the metro through the dark tunnels and watches the lights blur past as they speed forward. |
| 7 | 07_finding_the_pier | She takes the escalator up to the street level and walks toward the water. She finds a small wooden pier by the edge of the canal where the harbour bus stops. |
| 8 | 08_the_harbour_bus | A wide boat arrives, and Maya boards the harbour bus. She stands on the open deck and enjoys the fresh air as she sails across the blue water. |
| 9 | 09_arriving_for_the_errand | The boat docks at the opposite shore. Maya steps off the vessel and walks toward a small shop on the corner to finally collect her package. |
