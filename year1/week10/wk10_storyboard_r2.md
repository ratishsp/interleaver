# Week 10 — Storyboard (Maya buys winter clothes)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Adjective agreement *en/et/*plural; *har på*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_the_cold_commute | Maya walks to work in the freezing morning. She is wearing a thin jacket from Mexico and feels the biting cold. She realizes she needs proper winter clothes to survive the midwinter and decides to go shopping immediately after work. |
| 2 | 02_escaping_the_cold | After work, Maya hurries into a brightly lit clothing shop to escape the wind. A friendly shop assistant greets her at the door. Maya explains that she is freezing and desperately needs thick, warm clothes for the Danish winter. |
| 3 | 03_the_fitting_room | Maya takes a pile of clothes into the fitting room. She tries on a thick red sweater, but it is too tight. The assistant quickly passes a larger size under the curtain, which fits perfectly and feels wonderfully warm. |
| 4 | 04_the_expensive_coat | Maya steps out to look for outerwear and finds a long, elegant black coat. She tries it on in front of a tall mirror. It looks beautiful, but she checks the price tag and sees it costs two thousand kroner. She decides it is too expensive and leaves it on the rack. |
| 5 | 05_the_practical_jacket | The assistant notices her hesitation and suggests a short, practical green jacket instead. Maya puts it on over her new sweater. The green jacket is comfortable, it fits well, and it only costs eight hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it. |
| 6 | 06_the_winter_accessories | On her way to the register, Maya stops by a colorful display of wool accessories. She picks out a long grey scarf and a pair of thick black gloves. She tests the gloves on her hands and likes how soft and warm they feel. |
| 7 | 07_paying_for_warmth | Maya places the green jacket, the red sweater, and the accessories on the counter. The shop assistant smiles and says the total is one thousand, three hundred kroner. Maya gladly pays the amount with her card. |
| 8 | 08_ready_for_the_wind | Maya leaves the shop and steps back out into the dark street. This time, she is wearing her new green jacket, the long scarf, and the thick gloves. The winter wind still blows, but she smiles because she is finally warm. |
