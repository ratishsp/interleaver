# Week 10 — Storyboard (Maya buys cotton clothes for the heat)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Adjectives as a system — attributive **നല്ല/വലിയ/ചെറിയ** and predicative with **ആണ്** (Malayalam adjectives never change form); **ധരിക്കുക/ഇടുക** (wear)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_heat | Maya walks to her software office. The March sun is very hot. She notes that her heavy clothes are uncomfortable. She decides she needs light cotton clothes for work and plans to visit a textile shop after her shift. |
| 2 | 02_big_textile_shop | After work, Maya enters a large textile shop on MG Road. She tells the saleswoman she wants cotton clothes. Maya looks around and remarks that the shop is big, and the cotton section has a good selection. |
| 3 | 03_yellow_kurti | The saleswoman shows Maya a yellow kurti. Maya feels the fabric, asks the price—which is six hundred rupees—and takes it to the fitting room. She puts it on, sees that it is a good fit, and decides to keep it. |
| 4 | 04_blue_top_too_small | Back in the aisle, Maya picks up a blue cotton top. She takes it to the fitting room and puts it on, but she realizes it is a small top. She tells the saleswoman the top is too small, so she leaves it behind. |
| 5 | 05_white_shawl | Maya wants something to cover her shoulders from the hot sun. The saleswoman brings a white cotton shawl. Maya drapes it over her shoulders and remarks that it is a very big shawl. The price is two hundred rupees, so she adds it to her pile. |
| 6 | 06_red_cotton_pants | Maya finds bright red cotton pants in the trouser section. She wears them in the fitting room and finds they are wide, light, and excellent for the heat. The saleswoman says they are eight hundred rupees, and Maya agrees to take them. |
| 7 | 07_paying_at_counter | Maya takes the yellow kurti, the large white shawl, and the red pants to the billing counter. The cashier calculates the items and tells her the total is one thousand six hundred rupees. Maya pays and walks out with her bags. |
| 8 | 08_wearing_cotton_at_work | The next morning, Maya wears her new yellow kurti to the software office. Her colleague Prakash notices and tells her it is a nice kurti. Maya smiles and tells him that light cotton is perfect to wear in the Kochi heat. |
