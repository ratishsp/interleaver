# Week 18 — Storyboard (Maya catches a monsoon cold)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** **വേദനിക്കുന്നു / വേദന ഉണ്ട്**; body parts; **സുഖമാണ് / സുഖമില്ല**; advice with **-ണം**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_waking_up_sick | Heavy rains finally arrive in Kochi, breaking the intense heat. Maya wakes up on a weekday morning feeling terrible; she realizes she is not feeling well at all, noting that her head hurts and she has a very sore throat. |
| 2 | 02_calling_in_sick | Knowing she cannot take the bus to Infopark today, Maya calls her manager to say she has a fever, will not be at work, and is going to see a doctor. |
| 3 | 03_booking_the_appointment | Maya looks up a local clinic on her phone and calls the reception desk. She explains that she is unwell, gives her mobile number for the patient file, and books an appointment for later that morning. |
| 4 | 04_waiting_at_the_clinic | Maya takes her umbrella and walks carefully through the rain to the nearby clinic. Inside, the nurse checks her temperature and asks her to take a seat in the busy waiting room until the doctor is ready. |
| 5 | 05_the_consultation | Maya goes into the doctor's room. The doctor asks how she is feeling, and Maya details her symptoms, explaining exactly where she has pain, how much her throat hurts when she swallows, and that she has a heavy cough. |
| 6 | 06_the_doctors_advice | After checking her breathing, the doctor writes a prescription and gives Maya strict advice on what she must do to recover quickly. He emphasizes that she must drink only hot water, must take her medicine after meals, and must rest. |
| 7 | 07_the_pharmacy | Before leaving the clinic building, Maya stops at the pharmacy attached to it. She hands over her prescription, pays for her tablets and cough syrup, and confirms with the pharmacist how often she needs to take them. |
| 8 | 08_anithas_visit | When Maya arrives home, her neighbor Anitha sees her in the hallway. Realizing Maya is sick, Anitha insists that Maya must go straight to bed, and kindly promises to bring over some hot kanji (rice gruel) for her lunch. |
| 9 | 09_resting_at_home | Later that afternoon, Maya eats the comforting kanji Anitha brought, takes her new medicine, and gets warmly into bed. Listening to the steady monsoon rain outside her window, she closes her eyes to get the rest she needs. |
