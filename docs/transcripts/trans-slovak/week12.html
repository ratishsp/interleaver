Week 12: Maya joins a badminton club

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding a club
Storyboard: Maya works out at the gym on Wednesdays but wants to meet people, so she looks for a social sport. She loves badminton — she played it a lot back home in Mexico — so she finds a local club online and books a trial session for Thursday evening.
Každú stredu večer chodím do fitnescentra.I go to the gym every Wednesday evening.
Rada cvičím.I like to work out.
Ale chcem sa venovať aj nejakému športu.But I also want to play a sport.
Milujem hrať bedminton.I love to play badminton.
Hrala som ho veľa doma v Mexiku.I played it a lot back home in Mexico.
Teraz hľadám nejaký miestny klub.Now I am looking for a local club.
Sedím na gauči so svojím počítačom.I am sitting on my couch with my computer.
Hľadám na internete.I search online.
Nájdem dobrý bedmintonový klub.I find a good badminton club.
Pozerám si webovú stránku klubu.I look at the club's website.
Majú veľa tímov.They have many teams.
Hrajú vo štvrtok večer.They play on Thursday evening.
Chcem si rezervovať skúšobný tréning.I want to book a trial session.
Na webovej stránke nájdem formulár.I find a form on the website.
Napíšem svoje meno a svoj e-mail.I write my name and my email.
Vyberiem si štvrtok večer.I choose Thursday evening.
Tréning sa začína o siedmej večer.The training starts at seven in the evening.
Je to perfektné.It is perfect.
Teším sa, že si znova zahrám.I look forward to playing again.
arriving at hall
Storyboard: On Thursday evening Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sound of shoes squeaking on the many wooden courts.
Je štvrtok večer a som doma.It is Thursday evening, and I am at home.
Balím si športovú tašku.I pack my sports bag.
Dávam si topánky a oblečenie do tašky.I put my shoes and my clothes in the bag.
Teším sa, že si dnes večer zahrám.I look forward to playing tonight.
Idem autobusom do veľkej športovej haly.I take the bus to a large sports hall.
Je chladný zimný večer.It is a cold winter evening.
Sedím v autobuse a pozerám sa von.I sit in the bus and look out.
Autobus zastavuje a ja vystupujem.The bus stops, and I get out.
Vchádzam do veľkej športovej haly.I walk into the large sports hall.
Je veľmi svetlá.It is very bright.
Vidím v hale veľa ľudí.I see many people in the hall.
Milujú športovať.They love to play sports.
Počujem hlasný zvuk.I hear a loud sound.
Topánky pískajú na mnohých drevených kurtoch.The shoes squeak on the many wooden courts.
Ľudia rýchlo behajú a hrajú spolu.People run fast and play together.
Vyzerá to veľmi zábavne.It looks very fun.
Som pripravená hrať.I am ready to play.
Ale najprv si musím požičať raketu.But first I need to rent a racket.
renting the racket
Storyboard: Maya goes to the small hall kiosk. She tells the clerk she is there to try out the club, and pays forty kroner to rent a racket for the evening.
Idem k malému stánku v športovej hale.I walk over to a small kiosk in the sports hall.
Za pultom stojí mladý muž.A young man stands behind the counter.
Usmieva sa a povie ahoj.He smiles and says hello.
„Ahoj, chcela by som si dnes zahrať bedminton,“ hovorím."Hello, I want to play badminton today," I say.
„Si tu v klube nová?“ pýta sa."Are you new here in the club?" he asks.
„Áno, chcela by som sa skúsiť pridať,“ odpovedám."Yes, I would like to try to join in," I answer.
„Vitaj,“ hovorí muž."Welcome," the man says.
„Potrebuješ si požičať raketu?“ pýta sa."Do you need to rent a racket?" he asks.
„Áno, prosím, chcela by som si požičať raketu na večer,“ hovorím."Yes please, I want to rent a racket for the evening," I say.
Nájde pre mňa dobrú raketu.He finds a good racket for me.
„Stojí to štyridsať korún,“ hovorí."It costs forty kroner," he says.
Vytiahnem svoju platobnú kartu.I take out my payment card.
Zaplatím tých štyridsať korún.I pay the forty kroner.
Podá mi raketu cez pult.He gives me the racket over the counter.
„Podľa mňa je to zábavný šport,“ hovorím veselo."I think it is a fun sport," I say happily.
„Prajem naozaj dobrú hru.“"Have a really good game."
Poďakujem a idem ku kurtom.I say thank you and walk over to the courts.
playing doubles
Storyboard: In the busy hall, Maya joins a doubles game with two friendly players, Kasper and Sara, and a fourth person. She has played for years, so she smashes the shuttlecock hard across the net.
Idem k bedmintonovému kurtu.I walk over to a badminton court.
Na kurte stoja traja ľudia.Three people are standing on the court.
„Ahoj, volám sa Kasper,“ hovorí muž."Hi, I am Kasper," a man says.
„A ja sa volám Sara,“ hovorí žena."And I am Sara," a woman says.
Tretí hráč sa volá Emil.The third player is named Emil.
„Ja sa volám Maya,“ hovorím."I am Maya," I say.
„Zvyčajne hráme štvorhru,“ hovorí Kasper."We usually play doubles," Kasper says.
„Chceš hrať s nami?“ pýta sa."Do you want to play with us?" he asks.
„Áno, veľmi rada,“ odpovedám."Yes, I would love to," I reply.
Veľmi rada hrám bedminton.I really like to play badminton.
Kasper a ja hráme proti Sare a Emilovi.Kasper and I play against Sara and Emil.
Ihneď začíname hrať.We start to play immediately.
Sara začína zápas.Sara starts the match.
Košík lieta rýchlo tam a späť.The shuttlecock flies quickly back and forth.
Emil odbije košík vysoko do vzduchu.Emil hits the shuttlecock high up in the air.
Milujem smečovať.I love to smash.
Vyskočím a silno ho zasmečujem ponad sieť.I jump and smash it hard over the net.
Dopadne na ich stranu kurtu.It lands on their side of the court.
„Dobrý úder, Maya!“ kričí Kasper."Good shot, Maya!" Kasper shouts.
post match chat
Storyboard: The fast match finishes and the tired players walk off the court. Sara says Maya plays really well, and Maya explains that she plays a lot back home in Mexico.
Rýchly zápas sa skončil.The fast match is over.
Spoločne schádzame z kurtu.We walk off the court together.
Som veľmi unavená.I am very tired.
Kasper a Sara sú tiež unavení.Kasper and Sara are also tired.
Pijeme trochu vody.We drink some water.
„Hráš veľmi dobre,“ hovorí Sara."You play very well," Sara says.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem,“ hovorím."Thank you very much," I say.
„Myslím si, že je to veľká zábava.“"I think it is so much fun."
„Hrávaš často?“ pýta sa Kasper."Do you play often?" Kasper asks.
„Áno, doma v Mexiku hrám veľa,“ odpovedám."Yes, I play a lot back home in Mexico," I answer.
„Zvyčajne hrám so svojou sestrou.“"I usually play with my sister."
„Je to zábavný šport,“ hovorí Sara."It is a fun sport," Sara says.
„Veľmi radi hráme tu v klube.“"We love to play here in the club."
„Chcem hrať viac tu v Dánsku,“ hovorím."I want to play more here in Denmark," I say.
„Rozhodne si rýchla,“ smeje sa Kasper."You are definitely fast," Kasper laughs.
„Vďaka, ale teraz si chcem sadnúť,“ hovorím."Thanks, but now I want to sit down," I say.
„Pôjdeme do bufetu?“ pýta sa Kasper."Shall we go into the cafeteria?" Kasper asks.
„Áno, veľmi rada,“ odpovedám."Yes, I would very much like that," I answer.
hall cafeteria
Storyboard: The group sits in the small hall cafeteria to drink water and chat. Kasper asks if Maya plans to come back, and she happily promises to make it a weekly Thursday routine.
Vojdeme do malého bufetu v hale.We walk into the small cafeteria in the hall.
Nájdeme si stôl.We find a table.
Som veľmi smädná.I am very thirsty.
Pijeme studenú vodu a rozprávame sa.We drink cold water and talk.
„Toto je naozaj dobrý klub,“ hovorím."This is a really good club," I say.
„Je to dobrý šport,“ hovorí Kasper."It is a good sport to do," Kasper says.
Kasper sa na mňa pozrie.Kasper looks at me.
„Chceš sa znova pridať?“ pýta sa."Do you want to join again?" he asks.
„Áno, veľmi rada,“ odpovedám."Yes, very much," I answer.
„Chcem tu hrať každý štvrtok,“ vysvetľujem."I want to play here every Thursday," I explain.
„To znie skvelo,“ hovorí Sara."That sounds lovely," Sara says.
„Vždy tu hráme vo štvrtok,“ hovorí Kasper."We always play here on Thursdays," Kasper says.
„Tak sa uvidíme budúci týždeň,“ hovorím radostne."Then we'll see each other next week," I say happily.
Vypijeme zvyšok našej vody.We drink the rest of our water.
Je príjemné spoznávať nových ľudí.It is lovely to meet new people.
walking to bus
Storyboard: Maya leaves the sports hall with Sara. They walk together to the bus stop in the cold winter evening, and Maya feels glad to have met fun, active locals.
Sara a ja vychádzame zo športovej haly.Sara and I walk out of the sports hall.
Je neskoro a je chladný zimný večer.It is late, and it is a cold winter evening.
Ale po zápase mi je stále teplo.But I am still warm after the match.
Spolu kráčame k autobusovej zastávke.We walk over to the bus stop together.
Fúka vietor a ulica je tmavá.The wind is blowing, and the street is dark.
„Tiež rada hráš bedminton cez víkend?“ pýtam sa."Do you also like to play badminton at the weekend?" I ask.
„Nie, bedminton hrám len vo štvrtok,“ odpovedá Sara."No, I only play badminton on Thursdays," Sara answers.
„Ale rada športujem,“ hovorí."But I like to do sports," she says.
„Zvyčajne si chodím zabehať do parku,“ pokračuje."I usually go for a run in the park," she continues.
Je to veľmi aktívna a zábavná osoba.She is a very active and fun person.
Čakáme na autobus v chlade.We wait for the bus in the cold.
„Môj autobus príde o desať minút,“ hovorím."My bus comes in ten minutes," I say.
„Ideme rovnakým autobusom,“ usmieva sa Sara."We take the same bus," Sara smiles.
Veľmi sa z toho teším.I am very happy about that.
Rada sa s ňou rozprávam.I like to talk with her.
Kasper a Sara sú zábavní a milí ľudia.Kasper and Sara are fun and nice people.
Teraz mám vo štvrtok dobrú rutinu.Now I have a good routine on Thursdays.
Som unavená, ale mám dobrú náladu.I am tired, but I am in a good mood.
Autobus konečne prichádza.The bus finally arrives.
Už teraz sa teším na ďalší štvrtok.I am already looking forward to next Thursday.
