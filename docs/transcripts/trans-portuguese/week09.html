Week 9: Maya visits the winter market

Portuguese with English gloss, line-aligned.

arriving at the market
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a busy outdoor market in a large square. The winter morning is cold, but the stalls look inviting. She carries a large empty bag and looks for fresh food for the weekend.
É sábado de manhã.It is Saturday morning.
É inverno na Dinamarca.It is winter in Denmark.
O tempo está muito frio hoje.The weather is very cold today.
Estou numa grande praça na cidade.I am standing in a large square in the city.
Há um mercado ao ar livre aqui.There is an outdoor market here.
Olho para as muitas bancas.I look at the many stalls.
Todas parecem convidativas.They all look inviting.
Há muitas pessoas na praça.There are many people in the square.
As pessoas vestem roupas quentes de inverno.People are wearing warm winter clothes.
Só estou com um casaco fino.I only have a thin jacket on.
Tenho uma sacola grande comigo.I have a large bag with me.
A sacola está completamente vazia agora.The bag is completely empty right now.
Procuro comida fresca para o fim de semana.I am looking for fresh food for the weekend.
O mercado tem muitos produtos frescos.The market has many fresh goods.
Vejo legumes, frutas e pão.I see vegetables, fruit, and bread.
Penso nos preços.I think about the prices.
Quanto custam as coisas aqui?How much do things cost here?
Vou até a primeira banca.I walk over to the first stall.
carrots for soup
Storyboard: Maya stops at a vegetable stall to buy ingredients for a hot soup. She points at some winter carrots and asks how much they cost. The vendor says they cost fifteen kroner per kilo. Maya asks for one kilo and pays.
Eu vou até uma barraca de legumes.I walk over to a vegetable stall.
Eu vou fazer uma sopa quente hoje.I am making a hot soup today.
Eu olho para os muitos legumes na mesa.I look at the many vegetables on the table.
Eu aponto para algumas cenouras grandes de inverno.I point at some large winter carrots.
Eu preciso de cenouras para a minha sopa.I need carrots for my soup.
Há um vendedor em pé atrás da mesa.There is a vendor standing behind the table.
"Olá," ele diz."Hello," he says.
"Olá," eu respondo."Hello," I reply.
"Quanto custam as cenouras?" eu pergunto."How much do the carrots cost?" I ask.
"Custam quinze kroner por quilo," o vendedor responde."They cost fifteen kroner per kilo," the vendor answers.
Quinze kroner é um bom preço.Fifteen kroner is a good price.
"Eu vou levar um quilo, por favor," eu digo."I will take one kilo, please," I say.
O vendedor pesa as cenouras.The vendor weighs the carrots.
Ele as coloca em um saco de papel.He puts them in a paper bag.
Ele me dá o saco.He gives me the bag.
Eu pago os quinze kroner.I pay the fifteen kroner.
Eu coloco o saco dentro da minha bolsa grande.I put the bag down in my large bag.
Estou ansiosa pela sopa quente.I look forward to the hot soup.
a taste of cheese
Storyboard: At the next stall, a friendly vendor sells large cheeses. He offers Maya a small piece on a wooden stick to taste. She eats it, likes it, and asks for the price of a small block. It costs fifty-five kroner, so she buys it.
À minha frente há uma grande banca de queijos.In front of me is a large cheese stall.
A banca tem muitos queijos grandes.The stall has many large cheeses.
Um vendedor simpático sorri para mim.A friendly vendor smiles at me.
Ele tem um pequeno pedaço de queijo em um palito de madeira.He has a small piece of cheese on a wooden stick.
"Aqui está, uma pequena prova," ele diz."Here you go, a small taste," he says.
Ele me dá o palito.He gives the stick to me.
Eu como o queijo amarelo.I eat the yellow cheese.
Tem um sabor muito bom.It tastes really good.
Eu olho para um queijo pequeno.I look at a small cheese.
"Quanto custa este queijo?" eu pergunto."How much does this cheese cost?" I ask.
"Custa cinquenta e cinco kroner," o vendedor responde."It costs fifty-five kroner," the vendor answers.
É um bom preço.That is a good price.
Eu aponto para o queijo pequeno.I point at the small cheese.
"Eu vou comprá-lo," eu digo."I will buy it," I say.
Ele embrulha o queijo em papel.He wraps the cheese in paper.
Eu dou o dinheiro a ele.I give him the money.
"Muito obrigado," ele diz."Thank you very much," he says.
O queijo tem um cheiro forte e bom.The cheese smells strong and good.
the expensive gloves
Storyboard: The wind blows cold, so Maya looks at a stall with warm clothes. She picks up a pair of thick wool gloves and asks how much the gloves cost. The vendor answers that the price is two hundred kroner. Maya thinks that is too dear, so she politely says no thank you and walks on.
O vento sopra frio hoje.The wind blows cold today.
Minhas mãos estão muito frias.My hands are very cold.
Vejo uma barraca grande com roupas quentes.I see a large stall with warm clothes.
Vou até o vendedor.I walk over to the vendor.
Ele tem muitas coisas quentes na mesa.He has many warm things on the table.
Pego um par de luvas grossas de lã.I pick up a pair of thick wool gloves.
Elas são muito macias.They are very soft.
"Quanto custam as luvas?", pergunto."How much do the gloves cost?" I ask.
"Custam duzentas kroner", responde o vendedor."They cost two hundred kroner," the vendor answers.
Olho para as luvas novamente.I look at the gloves again.
Duzentas kroner é um preço alto.Two hundred kroner is a high price.
É caro demais para mim.It is too expensive for me.
Coloco as luvas de volta na mesa.I put the gloves back on the table.
"Não, obrigada", digo educadamente."No thank you," I say politely.
"Tenha um bom dia.""Have a good day."
O vendedor acena com a cabeça e sorri.The vendor nods and smiles.
"Igualmente", diz ele."You too," he says.
Sigo caminhando pelo mercado.I walk on around the market.
buying winter apples
Storyboard: Maya finds a fruit stall with baskets of dark red winter apples. She asks the vendor how much a small bag costs. The vendor smiles and says the price is twenty kroner. Maya hands him a paper note for fifty kroner. The vendor hands her the bag of apples and gives her exactly thirty kroner back in coins. Maya puts the heavy coins into her coat pocket.
Há muitas pessoas no mercado hoje.There are many people at the market today.
Encontro uma pequena barraca de frutas.I find a small fruit stall.
Há muitas cestas na barraca.There are many baskets in the stall.
As cestas estão cheias de maçãs de inverno vermelho-escuras.The baskets are full of dark red winter apples.
Olho para um pequeno saco de maçãs.I look at a small bag of apples.
"Quanto custa um saco pequeno?" pergunto ao vendedor."How much does a small bag cost?" I ask the vendor.
Ele olha na cesta.He looks in the basket.
"O preço é vinte kroner," ele responde."The price is twenty kroner," he answers.
Procuro dinheiro na minha bolsa.I look in my bag for money.
Dou a ele uma nota de cinquenta kroner.I give him a fifty-krone note.
O vendedor me entrega o saco de maçãs.The vendor hands me the bag of apples.
Então ele me dá o dinheiro de volta.Then he gives me money back.
Recebo exatamente trinta kroner de volta em moedas.I get exactly thirty kroner back in coins.
As moedas são grossas e pesadas.The coins are thick and heavy.
Coloco as moedas pesadas no bolso do meu casaco.I put the heavy coins down into my coat pocket.
As maçãs têm um cheiro muito fresco.The apples smell very fresh.
Coloco o saco dentro da minha sacola grande de compras.I put the bag down into my large shopping bag.
fresh rye bread
Storyboard: Maya smells baked goods and walks over to a baker's stall. She points to a fresh, dark rye bread and asks how much it costs. The baker tells her it costs thirty-five kroner. She pays with her card and places the warm bread in her bag.
Passo por uma pequena banca.I walk past a small stall.
Cheira a pão fresco.It smells of fresh bread.
Vou até a banca.I walk over to the stall.
O padeiro está em pé atrás de uma mesa grande.The baker is standing behind a large table.
A mesa está cheia de produtos de padaria.The table is full of baked goods.
Vejo um pão de centeio escuro.I see a dark rye bread.
Parece muito fresco.It looks very fresh.
Aponto para o pão de centeio.I point at the rye bread.
"Quanto custa o pão de centeio escuro?", pergunto."How much does the dark rye bread cost?" I ask.
O padeiro limpa as mãos.The baker wipes his hands.
"Custa trinta e cinco kroner", ele responde."It costs thirty-five kroner," he replies.
"Vou levar", digo."I am buying it," I say.
Tiro meu cartão de pagamento.I take out my payment card.
Pago com o cartão.I pay with the card.
O padeiro me dá o pão em um saco de papel.The baker gives me the bread in a paper bag.
Toco no saco.I touch the bag.
O pão ainda está quente.The bread is still warm.
Estou ansiosa para comê-lo.I look forward to eating it.
exact change for coffee
Storyboard: Maya feels cold and spots a small coffee cart. She approaches and asks the barista how much a hot black coffee costs. The barista replies that it costs thirty kroner. Maya takes the new coins from her coat pocket and pays for the coffee with exact change.
Estou com muito frio agora.I am very cold now.
Minhas mãos estão frias.My hands are cold.
Olho ao redor do mercado.I look around the market.
Vejo um pequeno carrinho de café.I see a small coffee cart.
Um jovem está atrás do carrinho.A young man stands behind the cart.
Vou até ele.I walk over to him.
Digo olá ao barista.I say hello to the barista.
"Olá, quanto custa um café preto?" pergunto."Hello, how much does a black coffee cost?" I ask.
O barista sorri.The barista smiles.
"Um café preto quente custa trinta kroner", ele responde."A hot, black coffee costs thirty kroner," he answers.
Tenho moedas no bolso do meu casaco.I have coins in my coat pocket.
É o meu troco das maçãs.It is my change from the apples.
Tiro as moedas.I take the coins out.
Eu as conto.I count them.
Tenho exatamente trinta kroner.I have exactly thirty kroner.
"Aqui está, aqui estão trinta kroner", eu digo."Here you go, here are thirty kroner," I say.
Ele pega o dinheiro.He takes the money.
Ele me dá o café quente.He gives me the hot coffee.
O café tem um cheiro maravilhoso.The coffee smells wonderful.
Aqueço minhas mãos no copo.I warm my hands on the cup.
winter flowers
Storyboard: Maya walks toward the market exit and stops at a flower stall. She points at a bundle of bright winter tulips and asks for the price. The vendor says they cost forty kroner. Maya buys the flowers and walks back to her apartment, drinking her hot coffee and holding the colorful tulips.
Caminho em direção à saída do mercado.I walk towards the exit of the market.
Vejo uma grande barraca de flores.I see a large flower stall.
A barraca tem muitas flores bonitas.The stall has many beautiful flowers.
Paro em frente à barraca.I stop in front of the stall.
Olho para um maço de tulipas de inverno.I look at a bundle of winter tulips.
Elas são muito coloridas.They are very colourful.
Aponto para as tulipas.I point at the tulips.
"Quanto custam as tulipas?", pergunto ao vendedor."How much do the tulips cost?" I ask the vendor.
O vendedor levanta as tulipas.The vendor holds up the tulips.
"Elas custam quarenta kroner", responde ele."They cost forty kroner," he answers.
"Vou comprá-las", digo."I will buy them," I say.
Pago pelas flores.I pay for the flowers.
O vendedor me dá as tulipas.The vendor gives me the tulips.
Agora terminei no mercado.Now I am finished at the market.
Caminho de volta para o meu apartamento.I walk back to my apartment.
Seguro as tulipas coloridas na minha mão.I hold the colourful tulips in my hand.
Bebo meu café quente.I drink my hot coffee.
O sol brilha sobre o mercado movimentado.The sun shines over the busy market.
