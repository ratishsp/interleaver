# Week 1 — Storyboard (Maya's arrival day)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** present tense of være / hedde / komme fra; existential der er (there
is/are); subject pronouns; hvad/hvor questions; greetings (hej, goddag, tak, velkommen, farvel,
undskyld) · **Target:** ~25 min.

The curriculum gives one beat per week ("Maya lands in Copenhagen, knowing no one"); this decomposes
it into 10 scene-level beats — the arrival arc from the plane to her first night. Maya = 31, from
Mexico, a software engineer who moved for a new job; recurring cast = Nina (Danish neighbour, her
first friend) and family back home in Mexico. The flat she lands in is a *temporary* place; her real
home comes in week 4 (a flat opens up in Nina's building, so she stays next door to Nina).

Two design notes from a review of the earlier 12-scene cut: (1) **warmth shows through what she does
and notices, not a declared mood** — she is by turns nervous, curious, tired, and settling, never
"happy" scene after scene. (2) The greeting vocabulary is practised twice *on purpose* but the two
must not be the same script: a **light, incidental** hello (sc3), then the **real** meeting with Nina
(sc6, the friendship). The phone call home (sc9) is a brief **audio** call — the full family **video**
call is its own week (wk3). Weather contrast and window-gazing each happen once.

| # | stem | beat |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_landing | The plane lands in Copenhagen. Maya looks out the window at the grey winter sky. She is a little nervous, but very curious about her new city. She says who she is — her name, that she is 31, that she comes from Mexico, that she is a software engineer with a new job here. Now she is in Denmark. (*jeg hedder, jeg er enogtredive, jeg kommer fra Mexico, jeg har et nyt job*) |
| 2 | 02_baggage | The airport is big and busy — there are many people. Maya walks to the baggage. There are many suitcases; hers is big and red. She takes it. She is tired after the long flight. (*der er mange mennesker / mange kufferter*; *min er stor og rød*) |
| 3 | 03_a_friendly_face | A friendly woman helps Maya with a heavy door. They share a quick hello and their names. "Hej, jeg hedder Maya." "Hej, jeg hedder Sofie." A short, warm first exchange — then each goes her own way. (a LIGHT, incidental greeting; just *hej / jeg hedder / tak* — not the full where-from-and-welcome script; that belongs to Nina in sc6) |
| 4 | 04_the_taxi | Maya takes a taxi. The driver says hello and asks where she is going. Maya gives her new address. They talk a little — Copenhagen is big. From the car she sees the streets and the tall houses. (*hvor skal du hen?*; an address; *byen er stor*) |
| 5 | 05_the_street | The taxi stops on Maya's new street. The street is quiet. There are old houses, and they are tall. Everything here is new to her. She is curious. (*der er gamle huse; de er høje*; *alt er nyt*) |
| 6 | 06_the_neighbour | At the door Maya meets Nina, who lives next door. Nina says hello warmly and tells Maya her name; Maya does the same. "Hvor kommer du fra?" — Nina is from Denmark, Maya from Mexico. Nina is kind, and just like that, Maya knows someone in Copenhagen. (the REAL meeting — fuller and warmer than sc3; *hvor kommer du fra*, *velkommen*, a friendship begins) |
| 7 | 07_the_flat | Maya goes into the small flat where she is staying for now. It is small but nice — there is a little kitchen, a table, two chairs, a big window. She sets down her red suitcase. A good start in Denmark. (*der er et lille køkken, et bord, to stole, et stort vindue*) |
| 8 | 08_the_window | Maya stands at the window and looks out at Copenhagen. It is a big city — there are many people and many bikes. The sky is grey, and it is cold. At home in Mexico it is warm. She thinks of her family far away. (the ONE weather contrast; *det er koldt her / der er varmt i Mexico*) |
| 9 | 09_a_call_home | Maya takes her phone and calls home — a quick call, just to say she has arrived. "Hej, mor." She hears their voices. She tells them she is in Copenhagen, she is well, and her new job starts Monday. It is short and warm. (a brief AUDIO call — she HEARS them, not a video call; the proper video call is wk3) |
| 10 | 10_first_night | It is late — Maya's first night in Copenhagen. The flat is quiet, and she is tired. She misses her family a little, but she is settling in. Copenhagen is her home now, and Nina is her first friend here. Tomorrow is a new day. (a calm close — settling, NOT dwelling on homesickness) |
