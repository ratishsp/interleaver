# Week 6 — Storyboard (Maya settles into a weekday routine)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Separable verbs (**aufstehen, anziehen, einkaufen**) and their split word order; reflexive **sich waschen**; verb-second word order when a time phrase comes first

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_morning_alarm | At half past six, the alarm on Maya's mobile phone rings. She wakes up. She is still a little tired, but she gets up right away and walks into the bathroom. |
| 2 | 02_wash_and_dress | Maya washes and brushes her teeth. Then she goes to her bedroom and gets dressed. She puts on a thick sweater, because the Berlin winter is still cold. |
| 3 | 03_ubahn_mishap | At eight o'clock, Maya leaves the flat and locks the door. She walks quickly to the station. The U-Bahn is already on the platform. She runs, but the doors close. She sighs and waits ten minutes for the next train. |
| 4 | 04_office_start | Maya arrives a little late at the software company and hangs up her coat. She cheerfully greets her colleague Jonas. She sits down at her desk, opens her laptop, and starts her work for the day. |
| 5 | 05_midday_lunch | At midday, Maya and Jonas take their lunch break together. They walk down the street to a small bakery. Maya orders a crusty Brötchen and a coffee. They eat and chat about their current projects. |
| 6 | 06_finishing_work | The afternoon is busy with code and short meetings. At five o'clock, Maya finishes her work. She shuts down her laptop, says goodbye to Jonas, and heads out. |
| 7 | 07_quick_grocery_shop | On the way back, Maya stops at a neighbourhood supermarket. She quickly shops for dinner. She buys fresh vegetables and a packet of pasta, pays at the till, and walks the rest of the way home. |
| 8 | 08_evening_dinner | Back in her flat, Maya goes straight to the kitchen. She cooks the pasta and prepares a simple vegetable sauce. She eats her dinner at the table and enjoys the quiet evening. |
| 9 | 09_family_video_call | After dinner, Maya relaxes on the sofa. She makes a video call to Mexico. She talks with her younger sister Sofia and laughs when the family dog Lola suddenly barks at the camera. |
| 10 | 10_going_to_bed | At half past ten, Maya is finally tired. She goes to the bathroom and washes. She turns off the lights in the flat, goes to bed, and falls asleep quickly. |
