Week 10: Maya buys winter clothes

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

a cold morning
Storyboard: It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to the bus stop on her way to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter.
Ni asubuhi Kopenhagen.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Ninatembea kwenda kwenye kituo cha basi.I walk to the bus stop.
Hali ya hewa ni baridi sana leo.The weather is very cold today.
Kuna upepo.It is windy.
Upepo ni baridi kama barafu.The wind is ice-cold.
Ninaganda kwa baridi.I am freezing.
Nimevaa jaketi jepesi.I am wearing a thin jacket.
Jaketi langu halina joto hata kidogo.My jacket is not warm at all.
Pia nimevaa viatu vyepesi.I am also wearing thin shoes.
Sijavaa skafu ya joto.I am not wearing a warm scarf.
Mikono yangu ina baridi sana.My hands are very cold.
Ninatetemeka.I am shivering.
Msimu wa baridi nchini Denmaki ni mgumu.The winter in Denmark is hard.
Ninahitaji nguo za joto.I need warm clothes.
Ninahitaji sweta nene.I need a thick sweater.
Pia ninahitaji skafu kubwa.I also need a large scarf.
Na ninahitaji glavu za joto.And I need warm gloves.
Ninatembea haraka sana.I walk very fast.
Nitanunua nguo za baridi baada ya kazi.I am buying winter clothes after work.
entering the shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things.
Nimemaliza kazini.I am finished at work.
Ninatembea kwenye barabara ya waenda kwa miguu.I walk down the pedestrian street.
Upepo bado ni baridi sana.The wind is still very cold.
Ninaona duka kubwa la nguo.I see a large clothing shop.
Duka lina mwangaza sana.The shop is very bright.
Ninaingia dukani.I go into the shop.
Kuna joto zuri sana hapa ndani.It is wonderfully warm in here.
Mhudumu wa duka amesimama nyuma ya kaunta.A shop assistant stands behind the register.
Ananiangalia na kutabasamu.She looks at me and smiles.
"Hujambo," anasema."Hello," she says.
"Sijambo," ninajibu."Hello," I answer.
"Ninasikia baridi sana," ninasema."I am freezing a lot," I say.
"Nimevaa tu jaketi jembamba.""I only have a thin jacket on."
"Ningependa kupata vitu vya joto.""I would like to find some warm things."
Mhudumu wa duka anatabasamu tena.The shop assistant smiles again.
"Kuna baridi sana pia leo," anasema."It is also very cold today," she says.
"Tuna sweta nyingi nene.""We have many thick sweaters."
"Pia tuna nguo za joto za msimu wa baridi.""We also have warm winter clothes."
"Unaweza kuangalia tu," anasema kwa upole."You just take a look," she says kindly.
"Asante sana," ninajibu."Many thanks," I answer.
the red sweater
Storyboard: Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it.
Ninaangalia nguo kwenye rafu.I look at the clothes on the shelves.
Ninaona sweta nene.I see a thick sweater.
Ni jekundu.It is red.
Ninahitaji sweta la joto.I need a warm sweater.
Duka lina saizi nyingi.The shop has many sizes.
Ninapata saizi ya kati.I find a size medium.
Ninaingia kwenye chumba cha kujaribia nguo.I walk into the fitting room.
Ninavaa sweta jekundu.I put the red sweater on.
Nimelivaa na ninaangalia kwenye kioo kikubwa.I have it on and look in the big mirror.
Sweta linanitosha kikamilifu.The sweater fits perfectly.
Lina joto la ajabu.It is incredibly warm.
Ninapenda rangi nyekundu.I love the red colour.
Ninaangalia lebo ya bei.I look at the price tag.
Sweta linagharimu kroner mia tatu.The sweater costs three hundred kroner.
Ni bei nzuri kwa sweta nene.It is a good price for a thick sweater.
Ninavua sweta tena.I take the sweater off again.
Ninalinunua.I buy it.
the tight green coat
Storyboard: Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a larger size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. The assistant brings her a warm dark-blue winter jacket in her size instead — it fits perfectly, and Maya decides to take it.
Pia ninahitaji jaketi la baridi.I also need a winter jacket.
Ninaangalia nguo zaidi dukani.I look at more clothes in the shop.
Ninapata koti zuri, la kijani.I find a beautiful, green coat.
Ni muundo mzuri sana.It is a very beautiful design.
Ninavaa lile koti la kijani.I put the green coat on.
Lakini ni dogo sana.But it is too small.
Linabana sana kwenye mabega.It is very tight across the shoulders.
"Mnalo la saizi nyingine?" ninauliza."Do you have it in another size?" I ask.
Mhudumu wa duka anaangalia.The shop assistant looks.
"Hapana, kwa bahati mbaya," anajibu."No, unfortunately," she answers.
Kwa hivyo ninalitundika lile koti la kijani mahali pake.So I hang the green coat back.
Mhudumu wa duka ananipatia jaketi jingine.The shop assistant finds another jacket for me.
Ni jaketi la baridi lenye joto, la bluu iliyokoza.It is a warm, dark-blue winter jacket.
Ninavaa lile jaketi la bluu iliyokoza.I put the dark-blue jacket on.
Linanitosha kikamilifu.It fits perfectly.
Ni zuri na kubwa na halibani.It is nice and big and not tight.
Ninaangalia kwenye kioo kikubwa.I look in a large mirror.
Ninalipenda.I like it.
"Nitalichukua," ninasema."I will take it," I say.
the blue scarf
Storyboard: Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases.
Ninatembea hadi kwenye meza dukani.I walk over to a table in the shop.
Kuna vitu vingi mezani.There are many things on the table.
Sijavaa skafu leo.I am not wearing a scarf today.
Ninahisi baridi sana shingoni.I am very cold around my neck.
Ninaona skafu ya bluu.I see a blue scarf.
Ni skafu ndefu sana.It is a very long scarf.
Imetengenezwa kwa sufu.It is made of wool.
Ninachukua skafu ya bluu.I take the blue scarf.
Ninaiweka shingoni mwangu.I put it around my neck.
Inahisi kuwa na joto la kupendeza.It feels wonderfully warm.
"Ni rangi nzuri," anasema muuzaji."It is a nice colour," the shop assistant says.
"Ndiyo, na ni laini sana," ninasema."Yes, and it is very soft," I say.
Ninatazama lebo ya bei.I look at the price tag.
Inagharimu kroner mia moja na hamsini.It costs one hundred and fifty kroner.
Ni bei nzuri kwa skafu yenye joto.It is a good price for a warm scarf.
Ninaivua tena.I take it off again.
"Ninainunua pia," ninamwambia."I am buying it too," I say to her.
Ninaweka skafu ya bluu pamoja na sweta nyekundu.I put the blue scarf together with the red sweater.
the black gloves
Storyboard: Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well.
Bado nimevaa jaketi langu jembamba.I am still wearing my thin jacket.
Ninafikiria kuhusu mikono yangu baridi ya asubuhi ya leo.I think about my cold hands from this morning.
Ninahitaji glavu nene.I need thick gloves.
Ninatazama huku na huko katika duka lenye mwanga.I look around the bright shop.
Ninaona kikapu kikubwa.I see a large basket.
Kikapu kimejaa glavu.The basket is full of gloves.
Ninapata jozi ya glavu nyeusi za majira ya baridi.I find a pair of black winter gloves.
Ni nene sana na laini.They are very thick and soft.
Ninavaa glavu.I put the gloves on.
Zinanitosha vizuri kabisa.They fit me perfectly.
Ninamuuliza mhudumu wa duka anayetabasamu.I ask the smiling shop assistant.
"Glavu nyeusi zinagharimu kiasi gani?" ninauliza."What do the black gloves cost?" I ask.
"Zinagharimu kroner mia moja na ishirini," anajibu."They cost one hundred and twenty kroner," she answers.
Kroner mia moja na ishirini ni bei nzuri.One hundred and twenty kroner is a good price.
Mikono yangu tayari ina joto.My hands are already warm.
"Nitazichukua," ninasema kwa furaha."I will take them," I say happily.
Ninavua glavu tena.I take the gloves off again.
Ninaweka glavu nyeusi pamoja na sweta langu jekundu na skafu yangu ya bluu.I put the black gloves together with my red sweater and my blue scarf.
Sasa niko tayari kulipa.Now I am ready to pay.
paying for the clothes
Storyboard: Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the dark-blue winter jacket, the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is one thousand five hundred kroner, and Maya pays with her card.
Natembea hadi kwenye kaunta na nguo zangu mpya.I walk over to the counter with my new clothes.
Mhudumu wa duka ananitabasamia kwa wema.The shop assistant smiles kindly at me.
"Ni hayo tu?" anauliza."Is that everything?" she asks.
"Ndiyo, ni hayo tu," ninajibu."Yes, that is everything," I answer.
Anaskani vitu vyangu vyote vipya.She scans all my new things.
Kuna sweta, jaketi, skafu na glavu.There is a sweater, a jacket, a scarf and gloves.
"Sasa hutahisi baridi," anasema kwa furaha."Now you will not freeze," she says happily.
"Hiyo itakuwa kroner elfu moja mia tano, tafadhali.""That will be one thousand five hundred kroner, please."
Nguo hizo ni ghali, lakini zina joto sana.The clothes are expensive, but they are very warm.
Ninatoa kadi yangu ya malipo.I take out my payment card.
"Ninalipa kwa kadi," ninasema."I am paying by card," I say.
"Unaweza kulipa hapa tu," anasema."You can just pay here," she says.
Ananyooshea kidole mashine ndogo, nyeusi.She points at a small, black machine.
Ninashikilia kadi yangu juu ya mashine.I hold my card over the machine.
Inatoa mlio, na malipo yanaidhinishwa.It beeps, and the payment is approved.
"Unataka risiti?" mhudumu wa duka anauliza."Do you want the receipt?" the shop assistant asks.
"Hapana asante," ninajibu."No thank you," I answer.
Sasa ninamiliki nguo nyingi nzuri za msimu wa baridi.Now I own a lot of nice winter clothes.
stepping into the cold
Storyboard: Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She pulls on the red sweater, zips her new dark-blue winter jacket over it, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm.
"Mfuko?" muuzaji anauliza."A bag?" the shop assistant asks.
"Hapana asante," ninasema."No thank you," I say.
Sihitaji mfuko.I do not need a bag.
Ninavaa nguo mpya mara moja.I put the new clothes on right away.
Kwanza, ninavaa sweta nene, nyekundu.First, I put on the thick, red sweater.
Kisha, ninavaa koti la baridi la bluu iliyokolea.Then, I put on the dark-blue winter jacket.
Ninafunga zipu ya koti la joto.I zip the warm jacket.
Baadaye, ninachukua skafu ndefu, ya bluu.Afterwards, I take the long, blue scarf.
Ninaizungushia shingoni.I wrap it around my neck.
Mwishowe, ninavaa glavu mpya, nyeusi.Finally, I put on the new, black gloves.
Sasa nimevaa nguo zangu zote za joto.Now I have all my warm clothes on.
Ninatabasamu na kusema kwa heri.I smile and say goodbye.
Ninatoka nje ya duka.I walk out of the shop.
Ninasimama nje kwenye mtaa wenye baridi tena.I stand out on the cold street again.
Kuna baridi ya kugandisha.It is freezing cold.
Upepo unavuma kwa nguvu.The wind blows hard.
Lakini sisikii baridi hata kidogo.But I am not freezing at all.
Sasa niko tayari kwa msimu wa baridi.Now I am ready for winter.
