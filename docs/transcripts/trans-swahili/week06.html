Week 6: Maya settles into a daily routine

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning routine
Storyboard: In the morning, Maya wakes up and gets out of bed. She steps into the bathroom, where she takes a warm shower and washes herself to wake up fully.
Bado kuna giza nje.It is still dark outside.
Asubuhi, ninaamka polepole.In the morning, I wake up slowly.
Ninalala kitandani kwa muda kidogo.I lie in bed for a bit.
Leo ni siku ya kawaida kabisa ya kazi.Today is a completely ordinary weekday.
Sasa ninatoka kitandani.Now I get out of bed.
Sakafu ni baridi kidogo chini ya miguu yangu.The floor is a little cold under my feet.
Ninaenda moja kwa moja bafuni.I walk straight out to the bathroom.
Bafuni, ninafungulia maji.In the bathroom, I turn on the water.
Maji ni ya joto na mazuri.The water is warm and nice.
Ninaingia kwenye sehemu ya kuogea.I step into the shower cubicle.
Hapa ninaoga maji mazuri ya joto.Here I take a lovely warm shower.
Kwanza ninaosha nywele zangu kwa shampuu.First I wash my hair with shampoo.
Baadaye ninajiosha kwa sabuni.Afterward I wash myself with soap.
Sabuni ina harufu safi ya sitrusi.The soap smells fresh of citrus.
Maji ya joto yananiamsha.The warm water wakes me up.
Hatimaye, ninafunga shawa.Finally, I turn off the shower.
Ninachukua taulo laini.I take a soft towel.
Sasa ninajikausha vizuri.Now I dry myself thoroughly.
Nimeamka kabisa na niko tayari kwa siku hii.I am completely awake and ready for the day.
getting ready
Storyboard: Back in the bedroom, she chooses her clothes and gets dressed for work. In the kitchen, she pours a cup of coffee and eats a quick breakfast before leaving the flat.
Chumbani, ninafungua kabati langu.In the bedroom, I open my closet.
Ninaangalia nguo zangu.I look at my clothes.
Ninachagua shati la bluu na suruali nyeusi.I choose a blue shirt and black trousers.
Kisha ninavaa nguo.Then I get dressed.
Ninachana nywele zangu mbele ya kioo.I comb my hair in front of the mirror.
Sasa ninaenda jikoni.Now I go out into the kitchen.
Nina njaa.I am hungry.
Ninapata sahani na kikombe.I find a plate and a cup.
Ninaandaa kifungua kinywa cha haraka.I make a quick breakfast.
Leo ninakula mkate na jibini.Today I eat bread with cheese.
Ninamimina kahawa kwenye kikombe.I pour coffee into the cup.
Kahawa ni nyeusi na kali.The coffee is black and strong.
Ninaketi mezani.I sit down at the table.
Hapa ninakula chakula changu.Here I eat my food.
Kina ladha nzuri.It tastes good.
Ninakunywa kahawa yangu.I drink my coffee.
Mwishowe, ninachukua begi langu na jaketi langu.Finally, I take my bag and my jacket.
Ninafunga mlango na kutoka nje.I lock the door and go out.
the bus ride
Storyboard: Out on the street, Maya waits at the stop for her bus. She steps on board, finds a seat by the window, and watches the city pass by on her way to work.
Nje barabarani, ninasubiri basi langu.Out on the street, I wait for my bus.
Kuna baridi leo.It is cold today.
Watu wengi wamesimama kwenye kituo cha basi.Many people stand at the bus stop.
Basi jekundu linafika.A red bus arrives.
Milango inafunguka.The doors open.
Ninapanda basi.I get on the bus.
Hapa ninasikani kadi yangu ya usafiri.Here I scan my travel card.
Dereva wa basi ananiangalia.The bus driver looks at me.
"Habari za asubuhi," anasema dereva."Good morning," says the driver.
"Habari za asubuhi," ninajibu."Good morning," I answer.
Ninatembea kupita ndani ya basi.I walk down through the bus.
Ninapata kiti kilicho wazi karibu na dirisha.I find an empty seat by the window.
Ninakaa chini.I sit down.
Basi linapita mjini.The bus drives through the city.
Nje ya dirisha, ninaona watu wengi.Outside the window, I see many people.
Wanaendesha baiskeli kwa kasi kwenye njia ya baiskeli.They cycle fast on the bike lane.
Tunapita kando ya bustani nzuri.We drive past a beautiful park.
Kwenye bustani, watu wanafanya matembezi.In the park, people take a walk.
Sasa basi linasimama mahali pangu pa kazi.Now the bus stops at my workplace.
Ninashuka na kutembea kuingia ofisini.I get off and walk into the office.
at the desk
Storyboard: At the software company, Maya sits down at her desk. She turns on her computer, reads her messages, and begins to write code for her project.
Ninafika kazini kwangu.I arrive at my work.
Ninafanya kazi katika kampuni ya programu za kompyuta.I work in a software company.
Ninaingia kwenye chumba kikubwa.I walk into the large room.
Dawati langu lipo kando ya dirisha.My desk stands by the window.
Hapa ninaketi kwenye kiti changu.Here I sit down on my chair.
Ninawasha kompyuta yangu.I turn on my computer.
Skrini inawaka.The screen lights up.
Kwanza ninasoma jumbe zangu.First I read my messages.
Nina barua pepe kutoka kwa meneja wangu.I have an email from my manager.
Barua pepe inahusu mradi wangu mpya.The email is about my new project.
Ninaandika jibu fupi.I write a short reply.
Kisha ninafungua programu yangu ya kazi.Next I open my work program.
Sasa ninaandika msimbo kwa ajili ya mradi.Now I write code for the project.
Msimbo unajaza skrini nzima.The code fills the whole screen.
Ninachapa haraka kwenye kibodi yangu.I type quickly on my keyboard.
Ninakaza fikira sana kwenye msimbo.I concentrate closely on the code.
Kazi inasisimua.The work is exciting.
Ninakunywa maji kidogo kutoka kwenye chupa yangu.I drink a little water from my bottle.
Nina furaha sana na kazi yangu mpya huko Kopenhagen.I am very happy with my new job in Copenhagen.
lunch with peter
Storyboard: At midday, Maya walks down to the bright canteen. She meets her friendly colleague Peter by the buffet, and they sit together to eat and chat about their work.
Katikati ya mchana, ninashuka kwenda kantini.In the middle of the day, I walk down to the canteen.
Chumba ni changavu na kizuri.The room is bright and nice.
Watu wengi wamesimama kwenye bafa.Many people are standing by the buffet.
Ninamwona mfanyakazi mwenzangu Peter.I see my colleague Peter.
Anachukua sahani yenye chakula.He takes a plate with food.
"Habari Maya," anasema kwa tabasamu."Hello Maya," he says with a smile.
"Habari Peter," ninajibu."Hello Peter," I reply.
Tunachukua mkate kidogo na saladi.We take some bread and salad.
Pamoja tunapata meza namba tisa kando ya dirisha.Together we find table number nine by the window.
Tunaketi kwenye viti.We sit down on the chairs.
Chakula kina ladha nzuri sana.The food tastes really good.
Peter anazungumza kuhusu mradi wake.Peter talks about his project.
Anafanya kazi na programu mpya.He works with new software.
Ninasikiliza na kuitikia kwa kichwa.I listen and nod.
Mwishowe ninazungumza kuhusu msimbo wangu mwenyewe.Finally I talk about my own code.
Tunakunywa glasi ya maji.We drink a glass of water.
Ninafurahia sana wakati wa mapumziko.I enjoy myself a lot during the break.
Baada ya muda mfupi tunatembea kurudi ofisini.Soon we walk back to the office.
finishing work
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya completes her final coding task. She closes her laptop, packs her bag, and says goodbye to Peter before heading out of the building.
Ni alasiri sana ofisini.It is late afternoon in the office.
Ninaangalia skrini yangu.I look at my screen.
Ninaandika msimbo wa mwisho.I write the last code.
Sasa kazi imekamilika.Now the task is finished.
Ninatabasamu.I smile.
Ninafunga kompyuta yangu.I close my computer.
Ninaiweka kwenye begi langu.I put it in my bag.
Kisha ninafunga vitu vyangu.Then I pack my things.
Ninasimama.I stand up.
Mfanyakazi mwenzangu Peter amekaa kwenye dawati lake.My colleague Peter sits at his desk.
Ninatembea kuelekea kwake.I walk over to him.
"Kwaheri, Peter," ninasema."Goodbye, Peter," I say.
"Kwaheri, Maya," Peter anasema."Goodbye, Maya," Peter says.
"Uwe na jioni njema," anasema."Have a good evening," he says.
"Asante, na wewe pia," ninajibu."Thank you, likewise," I answer.
Ninatoka nje ya ofisi.I walk out of the office.
Nje kwenye korido, ninapanda lifti kushuka chini.Out in the hallway, I take the elevator down.
Hatimaye, ninatoka nje ya jengo.Finally, I walk out of the building.
Sasa ninaenda nyumbani.Now I go home.
cooking dinner

Pia ninakata nyanya mbili nyekundu.I also cut two red tomatoes.
Ninachukua kikaango.I take a pan.
Kwenye jiko ninapasha moto mafuta kidogo.On the stove I heat some oil.
Ninaweka mboga kwenye kikaango.I put the vegetables in the pan.
Ninakaanga chakula.I fry the food.
Kinanukia vizuri sana.It smells really good.
Ninaweka chumvi na pilipili kwenye chakula.I put salt and pepper in the food.
Chakula kiko tayari.The food is ready.
Ninachukua sahani.I take a plate.
Ninaweka chakula kwenye sahani.I put the food on the plate.
Ninaleta glasi ya maji.I fetch a glass of water.
Ninakaa mezani.I sit down at the table.
Ninakula chakula changu cha jioni.I eat my dinner.
Chakula kina ladha nzuri.The food tastes lovely.
Ninafurahia mlo mtulivu.I enjoy a quiet meal.
relaxing evening

bedtime
