Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Hungarian with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
Péntek este van, és Nina kopog az ajtómon.It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
Vele van a kutyája, Ludwig.She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.
Nálam fog maradni a hétvégén.He is going to stay with me for the weekend.
Nina mosolyog, és ideadja a pórázt.Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
„Nagyon köszönöm a segítséget, Maya” – mondja."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
„Semmi gond” – válaszolom vidáman."It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
Elmagyaráz nekem néhány dolgot róla.She explains a few things to me about him.
„Reggel hétkor és este hatkor adj neki enni” – mondja Nina."Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
„És ne feledd, nem ehet emberi ételt.”"And remember, he must not have human food."
Nevetek, és lenézek a kiskutyára.I laugh and look down at the little dog.
„Nem, Ludwig, az én vacsorám nem neked való” – mondom neki."No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
Nina is nevet, és rámutat az ajtóra.Nina laughs too and points at the door.
„Mindig tartsd pórázon, amikor a nagy út közelében sétáltok” – mondja."Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
„A parkban szabadon futkározhat.”"In the park he is allowed to run freely."
Bólintok.I nod.
„Ne aggódj, jól fogok rá vigyázni.”"Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
Nina megölel, és elköszön tőlünk.Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
Becsukja az ajtót, én pedig egyedül maradok Ludwiggal.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
Felnéz rám, és csóválja a farkát.He looks up at me and wags his tail.
Már most nagyon kedvelem.I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll
Storyboard: Before bed, Maya takes Ludwig for a short evening walk around the neighborhood. When he suddenly sniffs at a discarded food wrapper on the pavement, she gently commands him to leave it and pulls him away, reminding him that he must not eat trash.
Késő péntek este van.It is late Friday evening.
Mielőtt lefekszünk, kiviszem Ludwigot.Before we go to bed, I take Ludwig out.
Teszünk egy rövid sétát a környéken.We go for a short walk in the neighborhood.
Az utcák nagyon csendesek most.The streets are very quiet now.
Ludwig szépen sétál mellettem.Ludwig walks nicely next to me.
Hirtelen megáll.Suddenly he stops.
Lelkesen szimatol valamit a járdán.He sniffs eagerly at something on the pavement.
Ez egy piszkos zacskó ételmaradékkal.It is a dirty bag with food scraps.
Kinyitja a száját, hogy felvegye.He opens his mouth to take it.
"Nem, ereszd el!" - mondom neki határozottan."No, drop it!" I say firmly to him.
Óvatosan elhúzom a zacskótól.I gently pull him away from the bag.
"A szemét nem étel, Ludwig" - magyarázom."The trash is not food, Ludwig," I explain.
"Gyere ide, Ludwig" - hívom."Come here, Ludwig," I call to him.
Elereszti a zacskót, és felnéz rám.He drops the bag and looks up at me.
Gyorsan csóválni kezdi a farkát.He quickly begins to wag his tail.
"Okos kutya" - mondom, és megsimogatom."Good boy," I say and pet him.
Folytatjuk a sétánkat az utcán.We continue our walk down the street.
Most már csak haza kell mennünk aludni.Now we just need to go home and sleep.
saturday breakfast
Storyboard: Early Saturday morning, Ludwig nudges Maya awake. Maya prepares his breakfast, gently telling him to sit and wait while she fills the bowl, before finally petting him and telling him to eat it.
Szombat reggel hét óra van.It is Saturday morning at seven o'clock.
Még mindig mélyen alszom.I am still sleeping heavily.
Hirtelen valami hideget érzek a karomon.Suddenly I feel something cold on my arm.
Kinyitom a szemem, és meglátom Ludwigot.I open my eyes and see Ludwig.
Megbök az orrával.He nudges me with his snout.
"Jó reggelt, Ludwig" – mondom, és felkelek."Good morning, Ludwig," I say and get up.
Együtt kimegyünk a konyhába.We go out into the kitchen together.
Nagyon éhes, és felugrik rám.He is very hungry and jumps up on me.
"Nyugi, Ludwig" – mondom neki."Easy now, Ludwig," I say to him.
"Ülj" – mondom nyugodtan."Sit," I say calmly.
Rám néz, és leül a földre.He looks at me and sits down on the floor.
Fogom a kutyatápos zacskót, és kinyitom.I take the bag of dog food and open it.
Beleöntöm az ételt a táljába.I pour the food into his bowl.
Feláll, mert azonnal meg akarja kapni.He gets up because he wants it immediately.
"Nem, várj" – mondom."No, wait," I say.
Újra leül, és szépen vár.He sits down again and waits nicely.
Leteszem elé a tálat.I put the bowl down in front of him.
Finoman megsimogatom a fejét.I gently pet him on the head.
"Tessék, egyél" – mondom mosolyogva."Here you go, eat," I say with a smile.
Csóválja a farkát, és enni kezd.He wags his tail and begins to eat.
a morning together
Storyboard: Saturday morning, with the café shift she arranged off in advance so Ludwig is not left alone, Maya has the morning free. she and Ludwig enjoy it together — she throws his ball and calls him back, gives him a good fuss — happy she chose to stay with him.
Reggeli után bőven van időm.After breakfast I have plenty of time.
Szombatonként általában önkéntesként dolgozom egy kávézóban.I normally volunteer at a café on Saturdays.
De Ludwig nem lehet egyedül.But Ludwig should not be alone.
Szerencsére ma szabadnapos vagyok a kávézóban.Fortunately I have the day off from the café today.
Ezt a múlt héten beszéltem meg velük.I arranged it with them last week.
Most Ludwig és én együtt tölthetjük az egész délelőttöt.Now Ludwig and I have the whole morning together.
Kimegyünk az épületem mögötti udvarra.We go out into the courtyard behind my building.
Előveszem a kis labdáját.I take out his little ball.
"Nézd a labdát, Ludwig!" – mondom neki."Look at the ball, Ludwig!" I say to him.
Átdobom a füvön.I throw it across the grass.
"Hozd vissza!" – kiáltom."Fetch it!" I shout.
Gyorsan fut, és elkapja a labdát.He runs fast and catches the ball.
"Gyere ide!" – hívom."Come here!" I call.
Boldogan szalad vissza hozzám.He runs happily back to me.
"Add ide," – mondom gyengéden."Give it to me," I say gently.
Elengedi a labdát, én pedig megsimogatom.He lets go of the ball, and I pet him.
"Okos kutya vagy," – mondom, és mosolygok."You are a good dog," I say and smile.
Újra eldobom, ő pedig visszahozza.I throw it again, and he fetches it.
Egész hétvégén nagyon vigyáznom kell rá.I must take good care of him all weekend.
Nagyon örülök, hogy itthon maradtam vele.I am very happy that I stayed at home with him.
the hundeskov
Storyboard: Saturday afternoon, Maya walks with Ludwig to a local hundeskov (dog forest). Once inside the safe, gated area, she unclips the lead, happily telling him that he may run free now, and throws a stick for him to fetch.
Ludwig gyorsan sétál mellettem.Ludwig walks quickly next to me.
Egy helyi kutyaerdőbe tartunk.We are on our way to a local dog forest.
Ma délután szépen süt a nap.The sun is shining nicely this afternoon.
Kicsit lelkesen húzza a pórázt.He pulls a little eagerly on the leash.
„Várj!” – mondom neki határozottan."Wait," I say firmly to him.
„Gyere, sétáljunk szépen.”"Come, let us walk nicely."
Végre megérkezünk a nagy kapuhoz.We finally arrive at the large gate.
A kutyaerdő be van kerítve és teljesen biztonságos.The dog forest is fenced and completely safe.

„Most már szabadon futhatsz” – mondom mosolyogva."Now you may run freely," I say with a smile.
Boldogan rám néz, és gyorsan elszalad.He looks happily at me and runs off quickly.
Találok egy jó botot a földön.I find a good stick on the ground.
„Nézd csak, Ludwig!” – kiáltom, és megmutatom neki."Look here, Ludwig!" I shout and show it to him.
Messzire eldobom a botot, be a fák közé.I throw the stick far away in among the trees.
„Hozd!” – mondom hangosan."Fetch it!" I say loudly.
Odaszalad a bothoz, és a szájába veszi.He runs over to the stick and takes it in his mouth.
„Gyere vissza!” – hívom, és tapsolok."Come back," I call and clap my hands.
Ő tényleg egy jó kutya.He is really a good dog.
gentle recall
Storyboard: Ludwig spots a bird and wanders off toward the trees, completely ignoring Maya's loud commands to stop. Realizing yelling does not work, Maya crouches down with a treat, softly calling him over; he quickly runs to her, and she tells him to sit before clipping the leash back on him.

family video call

ninas return
