the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.

Saya sedang berjalan pulang dari halte bus di Kopenhagen.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Saya melihat sebuah toko bunga di jalan.I see a flower shop on the street.

Saya masuk ke dalam toko yang hangat itu.I go into the warm shop.
Saya melihat sebuah tanaman hijau kecil.I look at a small, green plant.
Seorang pegawai toko tersenyum kepada saya.A clerk smiles at me.
"Halo," katanya."Hello," he says.
"Halo," kata saya."Hello," I say.
"Saya sedang mencari sebuah tanaman.""I am looking for a plant."
"Apakah itu untuk hadiah?" tanyanya."Is it a gift?" he asks.
"Bukan, ini untuk kamar saya," jawab saya."No, it is for my room," I answer.
"Itu tanaman yang sangat bagus," katanya."It is a really good plant," he says.
"Berapa harganya?" tanya saya."How much does it cost?" I ask.
"Harganya lima puluh lima kroner," jawabnya."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.

Dia memasukkan tanaman itu ke dalam kantong kertas.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
"Ini dia, ini kantong Anda," katanya."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
"Terima kasih banyak," kata saya dengan senang."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Saya berjalan keluar ke malam yang dingin.I go out into the cold evening.
Tanaman kecil saya membuat saya sangat senang.My little plant makes me very happy.

parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
