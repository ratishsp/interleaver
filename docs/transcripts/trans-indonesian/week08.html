Week 8: Maya explores the city

Indonesian with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
Ini Sabtu pagi.It is Saturday morning.
Matahari bersinar di atas kota.The sun is shining over the city.
Aku turun ke jalan.I go down to the street.
Tetanggaku, Nina, berdiri di luar gedung kami.My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
"Selamat pagi, Maya," katanya sambil tersenyum."Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
"Selamat pagi, Nina," jawabku."Good morning, Nina," I answer.
"Aku libur hari ini," kataku padanya."I have the day off today," I tell her.
"Aku akan pergi keluar ke kota.""I am heading out into the city."
"Kamu mau ke mana?" tanya Nina."Where are you going?" Nina asks.
"Aku mau berjalan ke arah perairan," kataku."I am walking down to the water," I say.
"Tapi aku tidak tahu jalannya.""But I do not know the way."
Nina menunjuk ke ujung jalan.Nina points down the street.
"Untungnya, itu sangat mudah," katanya."Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
"Jalan lurus terus dari sini.""Go straight ahead here."
"Dan belok kiri di taman.""And turn left at the park."
"Lalu kamu akan langsung tiba di pelabuhan.""Then you come right down to the harbour."
"Terima kasih banyak atas bantuannya, Nina," kataku."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
"Sama-sama, dan selamat jalan-jalan," jawabnya."You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
Aku melambaikan tangan padanya.I wave to her.
Aku mulai berjalan ke arah perairan.I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
Saya mengikuti rute Nina.I follow Nina's route.
Udaranya segar dan dingin.The air is fresh and cold.
Sekarang saya berjalan menyusuri jalan utama.Now I walk down the main street.
Pertama-tama, saya hanya berjalan lurus ke depan.First I just walk straight ahead.
Ada banyak kendaraan di jalan.There is a lot of traffic on the street.
Di perjalanan, saya melihat tiga sepeda kuning.On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
Sepeda-sepeda itu melaju cepat melewati saya.The bicycles ride quickly past me.
Setelah itu, saya berjalan lebih jauh ke depan.After that I walk further ahead.
Kemudian saya sampai di sebuah taman besar.Then I come to a large park.
Sekarang saya teringat kata-kata Nina.Now I remember Nina's words.
"Belok kiri di taman," katanya."Turn left at the park," she says.
Di sini saya belok kiri.Here I turn left.
Beberapa saat kemudian, saya melihat sebuah pintu terbuka.A little later I see an open door.
Itu adalah sebuah toko roti kecil.It is a small bakery.
Ada aroma harum dari kopi segar.There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
Saya sangat suka roti hangat.I love warm bread.
Hari ini kotanya sangat indah.Today the city is lovely.
Setelah itu, saya terus berjalan menuju ke arah air.Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
Saya tiba di sebuah alun-alun besar.I arrive at a large square.
Ada banyak orang.There are many people.
Tapi saya tidak melihat air.But I do not see the water.
Saya sedang mencari pelabuhan.I am looking for the harbour.
Saya melihat seorang pria tua di trotoar.I see an older man on the pavement.
Saya menghentikannya.I stop him.
"Permisi," kata saya."Excuse me," I say.
"Di mana pelabuhannya?""Where is the harbour?"
Pria tua itu mengangguk.The older man nods.
Dia menunjuk ke depan.He points ahead.
"Jalan lurus melintasi alun-alun," katanya."Walk straight across the square," he says.
"Lewati museumnya.""Go past the museum."
"Dan belok kanan.""And turn right."
Saya melihat ke seberang alun-alun.I look across the square.
Saya melihat sebuah bangunan besar.I see a large building.
Itu museumnya.It is the museum.
"Terima kasih banyak," kata saya."Thank you very much," I say.
"Sama-sama," jawabnya."You are welcome," he replies.
Saya lanjut berjalan.I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
Aku berjalan melintasi alun-alun besar itu.I walk across the large square.
Tiba-tiba, aku mendengar musik.Suddenly, I hear music.
Di sudut jalan berdiri seorang musisi jalanan.On the corner stands a street musician.
Dia bermain gitar.He plays the guitar.
Aku mendengarkan musik yang bagus itu.I listen to the good music.
Aku memandangnya cukup lama.I look at him for a long time.
Lalu aku berjalan terus.Then I walk on.
Aku melupakan museum itu.I forget the museum.
Aku juga melupakan ruteku.I also forget my route.
Sebaliknya, aku berjalan menyusuri jalan kecil yang sempit.Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
Di sini sangat sepi.It is very quiet here.
Di sekeliling, aku hanya melihat beberapa rumah kecil.All around I only see some small houses.
Lalu aku berhenti.Then I stop.
Di mana museum itu?Where is the museum?
Sekarang aku melihat ke sekeliling.Now I look around.
Aku tidak bisa melihatnya lagi.I cannot see it anymore.
Aku dengan cepat melihat kembali ke arah jalan.I quickly look back up the street.
Aku juga tidak bisa melihat alun-alun itu.I cannot see the square either.
Aku benar-benar tersesat.I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
Saya sedang berdiri di sebuah jalan yang sepi.I am standing on a quiet street.
Saya tersesat.I am lost.
Di depan saya, saya melihat seorang wanita muda.Ahead of me I see a young woman.
Dia sedang berjalan-jalan dengan seekor anjing kecil.She is walking a small dog.
Anjing itu mirip dengan anjing keluarga saya, Lola.The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
Saya berjalan menghampiri wanita itu.I walk over to the woman.
"Permisi," kata saya."Excuse me," I say.
"Saya sedang mencari pelabuhan.""I am looking for the harbour."
Wanita itu berhenti dan mendengarkan.The woman stops and listens.
Dia menunjuk ke belakang menyusuri jalan.She points back up the street.
"Jalan lurus kembali ke alun-alun," katanya."Go straight back to the square," she says.
"Dan belok kanan di museum.""And turn right at the museum."
"Terima kasih banyak atas bantuannya," kata saya."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Sama-sama," jawab wanita itu."You are welcome," the woman replies.
Saya menunduk menatap anjing kecil itu.I look down at the little dog.
Ia menatap saya.It looks at me.
Saya berjalan kembali menuju alun-alun.I walk back toward the square.
Sekarang saya tahu di mana saya berada.Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
Saya berjalan kembali menyusuri jalan yang sepi itu.I walk back along the quiet street.
Saya berjalan lurus ke depan.I walk straight ahead.
Tak lama kemudian saya melihat alun-alun besar itu lagi.Soon I see the large square again.
Lalu saya berjalan melintasi alun-alun itu.Then I walk across the square.
Sekarang saya berdiri di dekat museum.Now I am standing by the museum.
Saya ingat kata-kata wanita itu: "Belok kanan."I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
Di museum itu, saya belok ke kanan.At the museum I turn right.
Saya berjalan menyusuri jalan yang lebar.I walk down a wide street.
Saya melihat ke depan.I look ahead.
Akhirnya saya melihat sesuatu yang biru.Finally I see something blue.
Itu adalah air!It is the water!
Di perjalanan, saya berjalan melewati sebuah kafe kecil.On the way I walk past a little café.
Akhirnya saya berjalan terus sampai ke air.At last I walk all the way down to the water.
Sekarang saya berada di pelabuhan.Now I am at the harbour.
Pelabuhan itu sangat besar.The harbour is very large.
Anginnya dingin dan segar.The wind is cold and fresh.
Ada banyak perahu di atas air.There are many boats on the water.
Di sini saya berhenti.Here I stop.
Saya berdiri dan melihat perahu-perahu itu.I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
Saya berjalan lurus ke depan menyusuri pelabuhan.I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
Saya berjalan melewati perahu-perahu kecil.I walk past the small boats.
Tiba-tiba saya melihat banyak orang.Suddenly I see many people.
Mereka berdiri di dekat air.They stand down by the water.
Saya berjalan ke sana.I walk over there.
Akhirnya, saya melihatnya.Finally, I see it.
Putri Duyung Kecil duduk di atas batu di tengah air.The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
Dia adalah patung yang sangat terkenal di Kopenhagen.She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.
Sosok itu sebenarnya tidak terlalu besar.The figure is actually not very big.
Tetapi patung perunggu itu tampak indah di bawah sinar matahari.But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
Saya berdiri di antara para pengunjung lainnya.I stand among the other visitors.
Banyak turis mengambil foto patung itu.Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
Saya juga mengeluarkan ponsel saya.I also take out my phone.
Saya mengambil foto yang indah dari putri duyung itu.I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
Saya mengirim foto itu kepada keluarga saya di Meksiko.I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
Saudara perempuan saya, Sofía, membalas dengan cepat.My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
"Dia sangat cantik," tulisnya."She is very pretty," she writes.
Saya melihat ke arah air lagi.I look at the water again.
walking home
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.
Hari sudah larut sore.It is late in the afternoon.
Sekarang saya berjalan pulang ke apartemen saya.Now I walk home to my apartment.
Saya berjalan melalui jalan yang mudah.I walk an easy way.
Saya hanya mengikuti jalan panjang itu.I just follow the long road.
Jalan itu benar-benar lurus.The road is completely straight.
Saya berjalan di sepanjang tepi air.I walk along the water.
Akhirnya saya berbelok menjauh dari tepi air.Finally I turn away from the water.
Sekarang saya berjalan masuk ke kota lagi.Now I walk into the city again.
Kemudian saya berjalan melewati toko roti.Then I walk past the bakery.
Masih tercium aroma kopi.It still smells of coffee.
Beberapa saat kemudian, saya melihat taman.A little later I see the park.
Di taman itu, saya berbelok ke kanan.At the park I turn right.
Saya tidak menanyakan arah kepada siapa pun.I do not ask anyone for directions.
Saya menemukan jalannya sendiri.I find the way all by myself.
Beberapa saat kemudian, saya melihat jalan saya sendiri.A little later I see my own street.
Akhirnya saya berdiri di depan pintu utama saya.Finally I stand at my front door.
Dari pintu, saya melihat kembali ke arah jalan.From the door I look back down the street.
Sekarang saya tahu jalan pulang saya.Now I know my way home.
