# Malayalam · Kochi — Scope & Sequence (draft: weeks 1–2)

Continuity ground-truth lives in `story_bible_ml.md`. Same story arc as `curriculum_da.md`; grammar
re-derived for Malayalam; scenes are authored in Malayalam.

| Wk | Lvl | Theme | Grammar focus | Brief |
|----|-----|-------|---------------|----------------|
| 1 | A1 | Arrival & introducing yourself | Equational **ആണ്** (X is Y) and existential **ഉണ്ട്** (there is); subject pronouns *ഞാൻ/നിങ്ങൾ/അവൻ/അവൾ*; **എന്ത്/എവിടെ** questions and the yes/no particle **-ഓ** (ആണോ?); *എന്റെ പേര് … ആണ്*. (Malayalam verbs do not change for person.) | Maya's arrival day in Kochi, knowing no one — stepping out of the airport into warm, humid air, a January sea breeze. Across the day: she lands and meets a stranger, takes a taxi (giving her address), settles into her small flat under a ceiling fan, takes in the unfamiliar green city with a pang for Mexico, makes a quick audio call home, and meets her neighbour Anitha. |
| 2 | A1 | First day & getting connected | Present tense **-ഉന്നു**; numbers 0–100 as words; dative possession **എനിക്ക് … ഉണ്ട്** (I have) and its negation **ഇല്ല** | Her first days getting settled — her first day at the software company, where she meets her colleague Prakash and gets a warm team welcome. Next day, the errands that connect her: getting an Indian SIM and registering at a government office. A celebratory chaya and pazhampori when the errands are done. |
