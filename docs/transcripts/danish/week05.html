Week 5: Maya stocks her new kitchen

empty cupboards

Jeg står i køkkenet.I am standing in the kitchen.

Det er helt tomt.It is completely empty.
Jeg kigger i skabet.I look in the cupboard.

Jeg har ingen mad.I have no food.
Jeg har kun lidt vand.I only have a little water.
Jeg vil gerne have mad nu.I would like to have food now.
Jeg kan godt lide grøntsager.I like vegetables.
Jeg kan også godt lide brød.I also like bread.
Men jeg har ingenting her.But I have nothing here.
Jeg finder en pose.I find a bag.

Den er god til indkøb.It is good for shopping.
Jeg går ud af lejligheden.I walk out of the flat.
Jeg lukker døren.I close the door.
Jeg er klar.I am ready.
Nu går jeg i supermarkedet.Now I am walking to the supermarket.
produce section
Storyboard: Maya enters a supermarket and pulls a basket from a stack by the door. Because she likes carrots, she grabs a large bunch from a green crate and drops it into the basket.

Jeg kigger efter grøntsager.I look for vegetables.
Afdelingen er lige her.The section is right here.
Jeg ser en kasse på gulvet.I see a crate on the floor.
Kassen er grøn.The crate is green.
Der er mange gulerødder i kassen.There are many carrots in the crate.
Jeg kan godt lide gulerødder.I like carrots.
Jeg vælger et stort bundt.I choose a large bunch.

Jeg lægger bundtet ned i kurven.I put the bunch down into the basket.
Nu er kurven ikke tom.Now the basket is not empty.

the parsnip
Storyboard: Looking around, Maya spots a pale, unusual root. She reads a sign and learns it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she decides to buy it anyway out of curiosity.
Jeg ser en mærkelig grøntsag.I see a strange vegetable.
Grøntsagen er en lang rod.The vegetable is a long root.
Roden er meget lys.The root is very pale.
Den ligner en hvid gulerod.It looks like a white carrot.
Jeg kigger ned på skiltet.I look down at the sign.
Der står "pastinak" på skiltet.It says "parsnip" on the sign.
Jeg kender ikke grøntsagen.I do not know the vegetable.
Er det en dansk grøntsag?Is it a Danish vegetable?
Jeg kan godt lide grøntsager.I like vegetables.
Jeg kan godt lide gulerødder.I like carrots.
Måske smager pastinakken godt.Maybe the parsnip tastes good.
Jeg tager pastinakken i hånden.I take the parsnip in my hand.
Roden er ret stor.The root is quite big.
Jeg vil gerne have den.I would like to have it.
Jeg kigger på kurven.I look at the basket.
Jeg lægger roden i kurven.I put the root in the basket.
Jeg vil gerne prøve noget nyt.I would like to try something new.
the rye bread
Storyboard: At a shelf with baked goods, Maya looks for a local staple. She lifts a loaf of rye bread, surprised by how heavy it feels, and lays the bread carefully in the basket.
Jeg går over til en hylde med brød.I walk over to a shelf with bread.
Jeg leder efter en lokal klassiker.I am looking for a local classic.
Jeg vil gerne have et dansk rugbrød.I would like to have a Danish rye bread.
Jeg kigger på hylden.I look at the shelf.
Jeg finder et rugbrød.I find a rye bread.
Det er i en pose.It is in a bag.
Jeg kigger på posen.I look at the bag.
Jeg tager rugbrødet.I take the rye bread.
Jeg løfter brødet.I lift the bread.
Jeg er overrasket.I am surprised.
Brødet er meget tungt.The bread is very heavy.
Det føles som en sten!It feels like a stone!
Jeg kan godt lide lyst brød.I like light bread.
Men rugbrødet er mørkt og tungt.But the rye bread is dark and heavy.
Jeg lægger brødet forsigtigt i kurven.I carefully lay the bread in the basket.
Kurven er også tung nu.The basket is also heavy now.
bakery counter
Storyboard: The bakery counter has a member of staff waiting. Maya catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry.
Jeg går hen til bagerdisken.I walk over to the bakery counter.
Duften er meget god.The smell is very good.
Jeg kan godt lide kager.I like cakes.
Og jeg kan rigtig godt lide kanelsnegle.And I really like cinnamon pastries.
En bager står bag disken.A baker stands behind the counter.
Bageren kigger på mig.The baker looks at me.
Han smiler.He smiles.
"Hvad vil du gerne have?" spørger bageren."What would you like to have?" the baker asks.
Jeg peger på en kanelsnegl.I point at a cinnamon pastry.
Kagen ser lækker ud.The pastry looks delicious.
"Jeg vil gerne have en kanelsnegl, tak," siger jeg."I would like to have a cinnamon pastry, please," I say.
"Ja, selvfølgelig," siger bageren."Yes, of course," the baker says.
Han tager kagen.He takes the pastry.
Han lægger kagen i en pose.He puts the pastry in a bag.
Han rækker posen over disken.He hands the bag over the counter.
"Værsgo," siger han."Here you go," he says.
Jeg tager posen.I take the bag.
"Mange tak," siger jeg."Thank you very much," I say.
Jeg lægger posen ned i kurven.I put the bag down into the basket.
finding the milk
Storyboard: Maya searches for the milk without luck. She stops an employee to ask for help, and he points her toward the dairy section at the back.
Nu mangler jeg mælk.Now I need milk.
Jeg går ned ad en lang gang.I walk down a long aisle.
Jeg kigger på hylden.I look at the shelf.
Jeg kan se vandet og juicen.I can see the water and the juice.
Men jeg kan ikke finde mælken.But I cannot find the milk.

Jeg ser en mand.I see a man.
Han arbejder i supermarkedet.He works in the supermarket.
Jeg stopper medarbejderen og smiler.I stop the employee and smile.
"Undskyld," siger jeg."Excuse me," I say.
"Jeg vil gerne have mælk.""I would like to have milk."
"Hvor er mælken?""Where is the milk?"
Manden peger ned bag i butikken.The man points to the back of the shop.
"Mejeriafdelingen er dernede," siger han."The dairy section is down there," he says.
"Mange tak," svarer jeg."Thank you very much," I reply.
Jeg går derned.I walk down there.
Jeg finder endelig køleskabet og mælken.I finally find the fridge and the milk.
Jeg tager en karton og lægger den i kurven.I take a carton and lay it in the basket.
taste of home
Storyboard: Exploring another section, Maya hunts for a taste of Mexico because she really likes beans. She recognizes a bright can of black beans on a low shelf and reaches all the way to the back to pull it out.

Jeg savner maden hjemmefra.I miss the food from home.

Jeg trækker dåsen ud.I pull the can out.

Jeg har nok mad.I have enough food.
