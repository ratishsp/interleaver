Week 13: Maya goes to a café

Estonian with English gloss, line-aligned.

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.
Kopenhaagenis on talv.It is winter in Copenhagen.
Ma kõnnin mööda tänavat.I am walking down the street.
Tuul on väga külm.The wind is very cold.
Täna puhub kõvasti.It is blowing hard today.
Ma nõjatun vastu külma tuult.I lean against the cold wind.
Ma tõmban salli üles.I pull my scarf up.
Mu nägu külmetab.My face is freezing.
Ma vaatan ringi.I look around.
Ma tahaksin sooja minna.I would like to get into the warmth.
Ma näen nurgal väikest kohvikut.I see a small café on the corner.
Aknad on udused.The windows are fogged up.
Seal paistab soe olevat.It looks warm in there.
Ma kõnnin kohviku juurde.I walk over to the café.
Ma avan raske ukse.I open the heavy door.
Ma astun sisse.I step inside.
Siin lõhnab kohvi järele.It smells like coffee here.
Õhk on mõnusalt soe.The air is nice and warm.
Ma sulgen enda järel ukse.I close the door behind me.
Külm tuul jääb välja.The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
Ma olen nüüd kohvikus sees.I am inside the café now.
Siin on väga soe.It is very warm here.
Ma võtan oma paksu talvejope seljast.I take off my thick winter coat.
See on raske.It is heavy.
Ma leian seinalt nagi.I find a hook on the wall.
Ma riputan oma jope nagisse.I hang my coat on the hook.
Ma võtan ära ka oma salli ja kindad.I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
Kohvik on väike ja hubane.The café is small and cozy.
Ma otsin head kohta.I look for a good seat.
Akna ääres on väike laud.There is a small table by the window.
Laud on radiaatori kõrval.The table is next to a radiator.
Ma lähen laua juurde.I walk over to the table.
Ma istun toolile.I sit down on the chair.
Mu käed on ikka veel külmad.My hands are still cold.
Ma hõõrun neid vastamisi.I rub them together.
Mu käed lähevad soojemaks.My hands get warmer.
See tundub fantastiline.It feels fantastic.
Nüüd on mul peaaegu soe.Now I am almost warm.
Ma olen näljane ja janune.I am hungry and thirsty.
Ma tahaksin nüüd midagi tellida.I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
Ma lähen klaasleti juurde.I walk over to the glass counter.
See on täis kooke ja leiba.It is full of cakes and bread.
Ma vaatan neid paljusid asju.I look at the many things.
Need näevad väga maitsvad välja.They look very delicious.
Kookide kõrval on väikesed sildid.There are small signs next to the cakes.
Sildid on käsitsi kirjutatud.The signs are handwritten.
Ma loen siltidelt nimesid.I read the names on the signs.
Seal on mitut sorti küpsetisi.There are many kinds of pastry.
Raske on valida.It is hard to choose.
Seal on suur šokolaadikook.There is a large chocolate cake.
Aga ma vaatan üht teist küpsetist.But I look at another piece of pastry.
See on suur kaneelirull.It is a large cinnamon roll.
Selle peal on palju suhkrut.It has a lot of sugar on top.
Ma armastan kaneeli maitset.I love the taste of cinnamon.
See on täiuslik külmaks talvepäevaks.It is perfect for a cold winter day.
Ma tahaksin väga seda kaneelirulli.I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
Nüüd olen ma valmis tellima.Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
Ma lähen kassa juurde.I walk over to the register.
Noor mees seisab leti taga.A young man stands behind the counter.
Ta on kohviku barista.He is a barista in the café.
Ma naeratan talle.I smile at him.
Ta noogutab sõbralikult.He nods warmly.
"Tere," ütleb ta."Hi," he says.
"Mida sa soovid?" küsib ta."What would you like?" he asks.
"Tere," vastan ma."Hi," I answer.
"Ma sooviksin üht musta kohvi, palun.""I would like a black coffee, please."
"Ja kas ma saaksin ka ühe kaneelirulli?""And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
"Muidugi," ütleb ta."Of course," he says.
"Üks must kohv ja üks kaneelirull.""A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
"Kas sa soovid siin istuda?" küsib ta."Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
"Jah, palun, ma istun sinna," ütlen ma."Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
"Selge," ütleb barista."Alright," the barista says.
"Ma teen su kohvi nüüd valmis.""I will make your coffee now."
"Suur aitäh," ütlen ma."Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
Ma ootan leti ääres.I wait at the counter.
Ta teeb minu kohvi.He makes my coffee.
Ta paneb kuuma tassi kohvi puidust kandikule.He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
Ta paneb kandikule ka väikese taldriku.He also puts a small plate on the tray.
Minu kaneelirull on taldrikul.My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
See lõhnab imeliselt.It smells lovely.
"Palun, sinu tellimus," ütleb ta."Here you go, your order," he says.
"Suur tänu," vastan ma."Thank you very much," I reply.
"Aitäh hea teeninduse eest.""Thank you for the good service."
"Võta heaks," ütleb ta lahkelt."You are welcome," he says kindly.
Ma võtan kandiku mõlema käega.I take the tray with both hands.
Ma kõnnin väga ettevaatlikult üle põranda.I walk very carefully across the floor.
Ma kõnnin tagasi oma laua juurde akna ääres.I walk back to my table by the window.
Ma panen kandiku lauale.I put the tray down on the table.
Nüüd tahaksin ma maha istuda.Now I would like to sit down.
Ma olen valmis heaks pausiks.I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
Ma istun nüüd oma väikese laua taga.I now sit at my small table.
Siin on mõnusalt soe.It is lovely and warm here.
Ma vaatan oma kaneelirulli.I look at my cinnamon roll.
See lõhnab magusalt ja hästi.It smells sweet and good.
Ma võtan väikese ampsu.I take a small bite.
Saiake on täiesti värske ja soe.The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
See maitseb tõeliselt hästi.It tastes really good.
Ma joon natuke oma musta kohvi.I drink a little of my black coffee.
Lõpuks on mu sõrmed jälle soojad.Finally, my fingers are warm again.
Ma lõõgastun pehmes toolis.I relax in the soft chair.
Kohvik on peaaegu inimesi täis.The café is almost full of people.
Inimesed istuvad väikestes gruppides.People sit in small groups.
Nad räägivad ja naeravad koos.They talk and laugh together.
Ma kuulan ruumis vaikseid hääli.I listen to the low voices in the room.
Heli on väga hubane.The sound is very cozy.
Ma vaatan suurest aknast välja.I look out of the large window.
Ilm väljas on hall ja tuuline.The weather outside is gray and windy.
Nüüd sajab tegelikult jälle lund.Now it is actually snowing again.
Valged lumehelbed langevad aeglaselt tänavale.White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
Ma olen lihtsalt rõõmus, et olen sees.I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
Ma söön oma kaneelirulli viimase tüki.I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
See maitseb tõesti hästi.It tastes really good.
Aga see on ka väga magus.But it is also very sweet.
Mu kohvitass on täiesti tühi.My coffee cup is completely empty.
Mul on äkki väga janu.I am suddenly very thirsty.
Ma tahaksin natuke külma vett.I would like to have some cold water.
Üks noormees kõnnib mu lauast mööda.A young man walks past my table.
Ta töötab siin kohvikus.He works here in the café.
Ta korjab kokku tühje tasse ja taldrikuid.He collects empty cups and plates.
Ma püüan ta pilku.I catch his eye.
Ta peatub mu laua juures ja naeratab.He stops at my table and smiles.
"Vabandage," ütlen ma viisakalt."Excuse me," I say politely.
"Jah?" vastab noormees."Yes?" answers the young man.
"Kas ma võiksin paluda klaasi kraanivett?" küsin ma."May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
"Jah, muidugi," ütleb ta."Yes, of course," he says.
"Suur tänu," ütlen ma."Thank you very much," I say.
Ta võtab mu tühja taldriku ja tassi.He takes my empty plate and cup.
Ta kõnnib tagasi leti juurde.He walks back to the counter.
Veidi hiljem tuleb ta klaasi külma veega.A little later he comes with a cold glass of water.
asking for the bill
Storyboard: Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab.
Ma joon oma vett.I drink my water.
Vesi on külm ja mõnus.The water is cold and nice.
Ma võtan oma koti põrandalt.I take my bag from the floor.
Ma olen oma pausiga lõpetanud.I am finished with my break.
Ma lähen leti juurde.I walk up to the counter.
Sõbralik barista seisab endiselt seal.The friendly barista is still standing there.
Ta vaatab mind naeratades.He looks at me with a smile.
"Kas sa soovid veel midagi?" küsib ta."Would you like anything else?" he asks.
"Ei, aitäh," vastan ma."No thank you," I answer.
"Ma tahaksin maksta.""I would like to pay."
"Jah, muidugi," ütleb ta."Yes, of course," he says.
Ta vaatab oma väikest ekraani.He looks at his little screen.
"Üks must kohv ja üks kaneelirull," ütleb ta."One black coffee and one cinnamon roll," he says.
"Jah, see on õige," ütlen ma."Yes, that is correct," I say.
"Kas sa soovid maksta kaardiga või sularahas?" küsib ta."Do you want to pay with card or cash?" he asks.
"Ma soovin maksta kaardiga," vastan ma."I would like to pay with a card," I answer.
"Hästi.""Fine."
"See teeb kaheksakümmend viis krooni," ütleb ta."That will be eighty-five kroner," he says.
Ma võtan oma maksekaardi välja.I take out my payment card.
paying and leaving
Storyboard: Maya taps her card on the terminal to pay eighty-five kroner. She puts on her heavy coat, thanks the staff for a nice time, and pushes the front door open to face the cold again.
Barista naeratab mulle.The barista smiles at me.
Ma hoian oma kaarti terminali kohal.I hold my card over the terminal.
Masin piiksub.The machine beeps.
"Kas sa soovid kviitungit?" küsib ta."Do you want a receipt?" he asks.
"Ei, tänan," vastan ma."No thank you," I answer.
Ma lähen seina juurde.I walk over to the wall.
Ma võtan konksu otsast oma paksu talvejope.I take my thick winter coat from a hook.
Ma panen jope selga.I put the coat on.
Ma panen ka salli kaela.I also put my scarf on.
Ma panen ka kindad kätte.I also put my gloves on.
Nüüd olen ma külmaks valmis.Now I am ready for the cold.
Ma vaatan uuesti baristat.I look at the barista again.
"Suur tänu kohvi eest," ütlen ma."Thank you very much for the coffee," I say.
"Võta heaks," ütleb ta naeratades."You are welcome," he says with a smile.
"Head päeva.""Have a good day."
"Samuti," ütlen ma."Likewise," I say.
Ma lähen välisukse juurde.I walk over to the front door.
Ma lükkan raske ukse lahti.I push the heavy door open.
Tuul on külm, aga mul on hea olla.The wind is cold, but I feel good.
