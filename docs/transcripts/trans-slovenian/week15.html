Week 15: Maya's everyday life in the city

morning weather
Storyboard: Maya wakes up early and looks out the window. The sun shines, but the wind is cold, so she puts on a warm sweater for the day.

Ura je šele šest.It is only six o'clock.
Nov dan je.It is a new day.
Počasi vstanem.I get up slowly.
Stopim k oknu.I walk over to the window.
Pogledam ven na mesto.I look out at the city.
Zunaj je še vedno temno in mrzlo.It is still dark and cold outside.
Zima je še vedno tu.Winter is still here.
Grem v kopalnico.I go into the bathroom.
Umijem si obraz.I wash my face.
Umijem si zobe.I brush my teeth.
Potem se oblečem.Then I get dressed.
Oblečem si debel, topel pulover.I put on a thick, warm sweater.
Popoln je za mrzel dan.It is perfect for a cold day.
Grem v kuhinjo.I go into the kitchen.
Pripravim si skodelico tople kave.I make a cup of warm coffee.
Pojem kos kruha s sirom.I eat a slice of bread with cheese.
Zdaj sem popolnoma budna.Now I am completely awake.
Pripravljena sem na nov dan.I am ready for the new day.
the bus commute
Storyboard: She walks to the bus stop and waits for her daily transport. The yellow bus arrives, she steps inside, scans her travel card, and rides through the busy streets.

Je običajno jutro.It is a normal morning.

Prispe velik rdeč avtobus.A big red bus arrives.

Avtobus se ustavi pred mano.The bus stops in front of me.
Vrata se odprejo.The doors open.

V torbi najdem svojo potovalno kartico.I find my travel card in my bag.
Skeniram modro kartico na napravi.I scan the blue card on the machine.
Naprava zapiska.The machine beeps.
Grem v zadnji del avtobusa.I walk to the back of the bus.
Najdem prost sedež ob oknu.I find an empty seat by the window.
Usedem se.I sit down.
Gledam ljudi na ulici.I look at the people in the street.

Pripravljena sem na nov delovni dan.I am ready for a new workday.
working with peter

Moj sodelavec Peter je že tam.My colleague Peter is already there.
On je zelo prijazen.He is very friendly.
"Dobro jutro, Maya," reče Peter."Good morning, Maya," Peter says.
"Dobro jutro, Peter," rečem."Good morning, Peter," I say.

Zadovoljna sem s svojo službo.I am happy with my job.
canteen lunch
Storyboard: At noon, Maya and Peter go down to the bright canteen together. She chooses a classic open sandwich with fish and pays forty kroner at the register.
Ura je dvanajst.It is twelve o'clock.
Čas je za kosilo.It is lunch time.
Peter in jaz greva dol v menzo.Peter and I go down to the canteen.
Menza je velika in svetla.The canteen is large and bright.
Zelo sva lačna.We are very hungry.

Greva do blagajne.We walk over to the register.

giving directions
Storyboard: After work, Maya steps out into a large square. A tourist asks for the way to a local museum, and she politely explains that they must walk straight ahead and then turn left.
Imam prosto od dela.I am off from work.
Stopim na velik trg.I walk out onto a large square.
Danes je v mestu veliko ljudi.There are many people in the city today.
Do mene stopi moški.A man walks up to me.
Ima velik fotoaparat.He has a large camera.
Turist je.He is a tourist.
Nasmehne se in reče živjo.He smiles and says hi.
"Kje je muzej?" vpraša turist."Where is the museum?" the tourist asks.
Muzej dobro poznam.I know the museum well.
Ni daleč stran.It is not far away.

"Greste naravnost," pojasnim."You walk straight ahead," I explain.
"Potem zavijete levo.""Then you turn to the left."
"Muzej je na desni strani.""The museum is on the right side."
"Najlepša hvala za pomoč," reče moški."Thank you very much for the help," the man says.
"Ni za kaj," veselo odgovorim."You are welcome," I answer happily.
Odpravi se po ulici navzdol.He walks down the street.
Grem naprej proti pekarni.I walk on towards the bakery.
buying a cake
Storyboard: On the way home, she stops at a small bakery on the corner. She orders a chocolate cake for the evening and pays sixty-five kroner to the smiling baker.
Prispem v majhno pekarno na vogalu.I arrive at the small bakery on the corner.
Grem v toplo trgovino.I go into the warm shop.
Čudovito diši po kavi in tortah.It smells lovely of coffee and cake.
Pek v belem predpasniku stoji za pultom.A baker in a white apron stands behind the counter.

"Kako lahko pomagam?" vpraša."How can I help?" he asks.
Gledam številne torte.I look at the many cakes.

Pokažem na lepo čokoladno torto.I point at a nice chocolate cake.

"To bo petinšestdeset kron," reče."That will be sixty-five kroner," he says.

Plačam petinšestdeset kron.I pay the sixty-five kroner.
"Najlepša hvala in lep večer," reče pek."Thank you very much, and have a good evening," the baker says.
"Hvala, enako," odgovorim."Thank you, you too," I answer.
Vzamem belo škatlo.I take the white box.
Previdno grem iz trgovine s torto.I walk carefully out of the shop with the cake.
