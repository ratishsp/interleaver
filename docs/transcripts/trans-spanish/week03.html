Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
Es enero y hace mucho frío.It is January, and it is very cold.
Camino a casa desde la parada de autobús en Copenhague.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Veo una floristería en la calle.I see a flower shop on the street.
Adentro hay luz y hace calor.It is bright and warm inside.
Entro a la cálida tienda.I go into the warm shop.
Miro una pequeña planta verde.I look at a small, green plant.
Un dependiente me sonríe.A clerk smiles at me.
"Hola", dice él."Hello," he says.
"Hola", digo yo."Hello," I say.
"Estoy buscando una planta"."I am looking for a plant."
"¿Es un regalo?", pregunta."Is it a gift?" he asks.
"No, es para mi habitación", respondo."No, it is for my room," I answer.
"Es una planta muy buena", dice."It is a really good plant," he says.
"¿Cuánto cuesta?", pregunto."How much does it cost?" I ask.
"Cuesta cincuenta y cinco coronas", responde."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
Pago mi planta nueva.I pay for my new plant.
Mete la planta en una bolsa de papel.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
"Aquí tienes, aquí está tu bolsa", dice."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
"Muchas gracias", digo contenta."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Salgo a la fría noche.I go out into the cold evening.
Mi pequeña planta me hace muy feliz.My little plant makes me very happy.

parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
Suena un par de veces.It rings a couple of times.
La pantalla se ilumina.The screen lights up.
Veo una sala.I see a living room.
Hay sol y la sala es muy luminosa.There is sun, and the living room is very bright.
Veo a dos personas.I see two people.
Son mi madre y mi padre.It is my mother and my father.
Sonríen mucho y me saludan con la mano.They smile a lot and wave to me.
"¡Hola, mamá y papá!", digo en voz alta."Hello mom and dad!" I say loudly.
"Hola, Maya", dice mi padre."Hello, Maya," my father says.
"¿Cómo te va?""How is it going?"
"Me va bien", respondo feliz."It is going well," I answer happily.
De repente oigo un sonido.Suddenly I hear a sound.
Es un pequeño ladrido.It is a little bark.
"¡Oh, oigo un perro!", digo y me río."Oh, I hear a dog!" I say and laugh.
Un perro pequeño salta a un sofá.A little dog jumps up onto a sofa.
Mira a la cámara.It looks into the camera.
"¡Ahí está mi perro!""There is my dog!"
"Hola, Lola", digo."Hello, Lola," I say.

"Sí, es tu perro", dice mi madre con una sonrisa."Yes, it is your dog," my mother says with a smile.
Amo a mi familia.I love my family.
Es hermoso verlos a todos.It is lovely to see them all.
