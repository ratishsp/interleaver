the parsnip
Storyboard: Looking around, Maya spots a pale, unusual root. She reads a sign and learns it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she decides to buy it anyway out of curiosity.
我看到一种奇怪的蔬菜。I see a strange vegetable.

这根茎的颜色很浅。The root is very pale.
它看起来像一根白色的胡萝卜。It looks like a white carrot.
我低头看牌子。I look down at the sign.
牌子上写着“欧防风”。It says "parsnip" on the sign.
我不认识这种蔬菜。I do not know the vegetable.
这是丹麦的蔬菜吗？Is it a Danish vegetable?
我喜欢蔬菜。I like vegetables.
我喜欢胡萝卜。I like carrots.
也许欧防风味道不错。Maybe the parsnip tastes good.
我把欧防风拿在手里。I take the parsnip in my hand.
这根茎挺大的。The root is quite big.
我想要它。I would like to have it.
我看了看篮子。I look at the basket.
我把根茎放进篮子里。I put the root in the basket.
我想尝试一些新东西。I would like to try something new.
the rye bread
Storyboard: At a shelf with baked goods, Maya looks for a local staple. She lifts a loaf of rye bread, surprised by how heavy it feels, and lays the bread carefully in the basket.
我走到一个放有面包的货架前。I walk over to a shelf with bread.
我在寻找一款当地的经典食物。I am looking for a local classic.
我想要一个丹麦黑麦面包。I would like to have a Danish rye bread.
我看着货架。I look at the shelf.
我找到了一个黑麦面包。I find a rye bread.
它装在一个袋子里。It is in a bag.
我看着袋子。I look at the bag.
我拿过黑麦面包。I take the rye bread.
我把面包拿了起来。I lift the bread.
我很惊讶。I am surprised.
面包非常重。The bread is very heavy.
感觉就像一块石头！It feels like a stone!
我喜欢浅色面包。I like light bread.
但黑麦面包颜色深，而且很重。But the rye bread is dark and heavy.
我小心地把面包放进篮子里。I carefully lay the bread in the basket.
现在篮子也很重了。The basket is also heavy now.
bakery counter
Storyboard: The bakery counter has a member of staff waiting. Maya catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry.
我走到面包店的柜台前。I walk over to the bakery counter.
香气非常好闻。The smell is very good.
我喜欢糕点。I like cakes.
而且我特别喜欢肉桂卷。And I really like cinnamon pastries.

面包师看着我。The baker looks at me.
他微笑着。He smiles.
“请问您需要点什么？”面包师问。"What would you like to have?" the baker asks.
我指着一个肉桂卷。I point at a cinnamon pastry.
这个糕点看起来很美味。The pastry looks delicious.
“我想要一个肉桂卷，谢谢，”我说。"I would like to have a cinnamon pastry, please," I say.
“好的，当然，”面包师说。"Yes, of course," the baker says.
他拿起那个糕点。He takes the pastry.
他把糕点装进一个袋子里。He puts the pastry in a bag.
他隔着柜台递过袋子。He hands the bag over the counter.
“给你，”他说。"Here you go," he says.
我接过袋子。I take the bag.
“非常感谢，”我说。"Thank you very much," I say.
我把袋子放进篮子里。I put the bag down into the basket.
finding the milk

我看着货架。I look at the shelf.
我能看到水和果汁。I can see the water and the juice.
但是我找不到牛奶。But I cannot find the milk.

“我想要牛奶。”"I would like to have milk."

“非常感谢，”我回答。"Thank you very much," I reply.
我向那边走去。I walk down there.
我终于找到了冰箱和牛奶。I finally find the fridge and the milk.
我拿了一盒，把它放进篮子里。I take a carton and lay it in the basket.
taste of home

我继续走向另一个货架。I walk on to another set of shelves.
我看着这些食物。I look at the food.

罐头放在最里面。The can stands all the way at the back.
那是一罐黑豆。It is a can of black beans.
