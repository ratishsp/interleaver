# Week 2 — Storyboard (First day at work and getting connected)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Present tense **-ഉന്നു**; numbers 0–100 as words; dative possession **എനിക്ക് … ഉണ്ട്** (I have) and its negation **ഇല്ല**; age with dative **വയസ്സ്** (എനിക്ക് മുപ്പത്തിയൊന്ന് വയസ്സാണ്)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_office_commute | Maya leaves her apartment building and takes an auto-rickshaw to the software company. She looks out at the busy streets of Kochi, enjoying the pleasant January morning. |
| 2 | 02_meeting_prakash | Maya enters the bright office building. Her colleague Prakash greets her with a warm smile, welcomes her to the team, and asks if she has her entry badge yet. Maya replies that she does not. |
| 3 | 03_setting_up_desk | Prakash leads Maya to her workspace. Maya unpacks her bag, turns on her laptop, and tells Prakash she has all the necessary equipment to start working. |
| 4 | 04_mobile_shop | During her lunch break, Maya walks to a nearby mobile shop to buy an Indian SIM card. The shopkeeper gives her a registration form and asks her age. Maya explains that she is thirty-one years old. |
| 5 | 05_confirming_number | The shopkeeper hands over the activated SIM card and asks Maya to verify the digits. Maya carefully reads her new mobile number aloud in Malayalam words—nine eight seven six five, four three two one zero—to make sure it is correct. |
| 6 | 06_government_office | The following day, Maya visits a busy government office in the city to complete her residence registration. She takes a paper ticket and sits patiently on a wooden bench. |
| 7 | 07_registration_desk | An official calls Maya to the desk and requests her local contact number. Maya recites her new mobile number aloud in Malayalam words again. The official asks if she has a local driving license, and she answers that she does not. |
| 8 | 08_tea_stall_treat | Her errands finally complete, Maya stops at a small roadside thattukada. She orders a steaming glass of chaya and a warm pazhampori, happily paying a few rupees for the snack. |
| 9 | 09_calling_diego | Maya returns to her flat and initiates a video call to her brother Diego in Mexico. She proudly tells him that she now has a working Indian phone number and a wonderful new desk at her job. |
