# Week 18 — Storyboard (Maya catches a monsoon cold)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** **दुखणे** (माझं डोकं दुखतंय); body parts; **बरं आहे / बरं वाटत नाही**; advice with **-वं** (तुम्ही आराम करावा)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_waking_up_sick | The first pre-monsoon rains finally broke the intense Mumbai heat overnight. Maya wakes up feeling terrible, realizing immediately that her health is not fine today, suffering from a heavy head and a blocked nose. |
| 2 | 02_calling_the_clinic | Maya finds the contact number for a nearby neighborhood clinic and makes a phone call. She speaks politely to the receptionist to explain her situation and successfully books a morning appointment to see the doctor. |
| 3 | 03_messaging_rohan | Knowing she cannot take the bus to the office in this condition, Maya messages her colleague Rohan. She explains that she has caught a bad cold and will be taking a sick day to stay home and recover. |
| 4 | 04_the_damp_walk | Maya puts on a light jacket and steps out into the cool, damp morning air. The streets smell of wet earth from the fresh showers as she walks slowly toward the clinic, feeling aches throughout her body. |
| 5 | 05_clinic_waiting_room | Inside the small, busy clinic, Maya takes a seat in the waiting area. She watches an elderly man coughing and a young mother comforting a restless child while she waits patiently for her name to be called. |
| 6 | 06_explaining_the_symptoms | Sitting across from the doctor, Maya explains exactly how she has been feeling since the morning. She points to her head and throat, describing the severe pain and the heavy congestion she is experiencing. |
| 7 | 07_the_doctors_advice | After a quick examination, the doctor reassures her that it is just a seasonal viral infection and writes a prescription. He advises her that she should drink plenty of warm water, eat light food, and rest as much as possible. |
| 8 | 08_the_medical_store | Leaving the clinic, Maya stops at the neighborhood pharmacy on her way back. She addresses the chemist as 'bhaiya', hands over the doctor's prescription, and purchases her prescribed tablets and a bottle of cough syrup. |
| 9 | 09_ashas_check_in | Maya returns to her quiet flat and takes her first dose of medicine. Her kind neighbor Asha knocks on the door briefly with her dog Moti to check on Maya's health, before leaving her to finally get into bed and sleep. |
