Week 4: Maya moves into her own flat

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

final sweep
Storyboard: Maya stands in her temporary room in the building. She zips up her large suitcases and checks the space one last time, making sure nothing lies under the small bed or stands forgotten in the closet.
依家係哥本哈根嘅一月。It is January in Copenhagen.
我企喺我間臨時房入面。I am standing in my temporary room.
今日我要搬去一個新單位。Today I am moving to a new apartment.
佢喺同一棟大廈入面。It is in the same building.
我個大篋擺咗喺地下。My large suitcase stands on the floor.
我閂埋佢。I close it.
我最後望多次間房。I look around the room one last time.
我望吓張細床下面。I look under the small bed.
床下面有一本書。There is a book lying under the bed.
佢係來自墨西哥嘅。It is from Mexico.
我將本書放落我個袋度。I put the book in my bag.
我打開個衣櫃。I open the closet.
衣櫃入面有一對鞋。There is a pair of shoes standing in the closet.
我攞起對鞋。I take the shoes.
依家衣櫃入面咩都冇。Now there is nothing in the closet.
我行過去窗邊嗰張書檯。I walk over to the desk by the window.
檯上面有一條電線。There is a cable lying on the desk.
我攞起條電線。I take the cable.
我將條電線放落我個袋度。I put the cable in my bag.
我拎起我嗰兩個袋。I take my two bags.
我熄燈然後閂門。I turn off the light and close the door.
unlocking the door
Storyboard: Carrying her first few bags up the communal stairs, Maya arrives at the door of her new, permanent flat. She turns the key, pushes the door open, and steps inside, thrilled to finally see the small furnished space waiting for her.
我行上樓梯。I walk up the stairs.
我雙手拎住兩個袋。I have two bags in my hands.
我終於嚟到我嘅新門口。I am finally at my new door.
我褲袋有條鎖匙。I have a key in my pocket.
我將啲袋放喺地下。I put the bags down on the floor.
我拎條鎖匙出嚟。I take out the key.
我打開門鎖。I unlock the door.
我推開道門行入去。I push the door open and go inside.
間屋好細，但係好完美。The apartment is small, but it is perfect.
裡面有靚同淺色嘅傢俬。It has nice, light furniture.
我望入去客廳。I look into the living room.
窗邊有張梳化。There is a sofa standing by the window.
梳化下面有張地毯。There is a rug under the sofa.
梳化上面有個細咕𠱸。There is a small cushion lying on the sofa.
角落度有盞燈。There is a lamp standing in the corner.
我閂埋身後道門。I close the door behind me.
我將條鎖匙擺喺檯面。I put my key on the table.
ninas help
Storyboard: Maya steps back out to the shared stairwell to grab her heaviest suitcase. Nina comes up the stairs, spots her struggling, and cheerfully helps carry the bag inside, setting it carefully on the floor by the large living room window.
我又行出去樓梯間。I walk out into the stairwell again.
我最大嗰個喼放咗喺地下。My biggest suitcase stands on the floor.
佢好重。It is very heavy.
我拉個喼。I pull on the suitcase.
突然間，我聽到樓梯有腳步聲。Suddenly I hear footsteps on the stairs.
係我嘅丹麥鄰居，妮娜。It is my Danish neighbor, Nina.
佢好親切咁對住我笑。She smiles warmly at me.
「瑪雅，你今日搬入嚟呀？」佢問。"Are you moving in today, Maya?" she asks.
「係呀，我終於入到我嘅新屋喇，」我話。"Yes, I am finally in my new apartment," I say.
「等我幫你啦，」妮娜親切咁講。"I will help you," Nina says kindly.
佢捉住個喼。She grabs hold of the suitcase.
我哋一齊將個喼搬入去客廳。Together we carry the suitcase into the living room.
妮娜好小心咁將個喼放喺地下。Nina sets it carefully on the floor.
依家個喼擺咗喺個大窗旁邊。Now the suitcase stands by the large window.
窗下面已經放咗個袋。There already lies a bag under the window.
「唔該晒你幫手呀，妮娜，」我好開心咁講。"Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say happily.
「唔使客氣，」佢笑住講。"You are welcome," she smiles.
妮娜行出去樓梯間。Nina walks out into the stairwell.
我閂埋門，然後望住窗旁邊個喼。I close the door and look at the suitcase by the window.
kitchen unpacking
Storyboard: Maya opens a travel bag in the small kitchen and begins organizing. She stacks her favorite mugs in the cupboard and makes sure her red kettle stands on the counter, right by the sink.
我行出個細廚房。I walk out into the small kitchen.
我個旅行袋擺咗喺地下。My travel bag stands on the floor.
我打開個袋。I open the bag.
個袋入面有好多嘢。There are many things in the bag.
我最鍾意嘅杯喺個袋入面。My favorite mugs lie in the bag.
我將啲杯攞出嚟。I take the mugs out.
枱面上面有個櫃。There is a cupboard above the counter.
我將啲杯放入去個櫃度。I put the mugs in the cupboard.
依家佢哋擺咗喺層板上面。Now they stand on the shelf.
我仲有一個紅色嘅電熱水煲。I also have a red electric kettle.
佢喺個袋入面，一件厚冷衫嘅下面。It lies in the bag under a thick sweater.
我將個電熱水煲攞出嚟。I take the electric kettle out.
我將佢放喺廚房枱面上。I put it on the kitchen counter.
依家洗碗盆側邊就擺咗個紅色嘅電熱水煲。Now there stands a red electric kettle right by the sink.
個袋入面仲有一盒茶。There also lies a box of tea in the bag.
我攞起個盒，放喺電熱水煲側邊。I take the box and place it by the electric kettle.
依家個廚房差唔多搞掂喇。Now the kitchen is almost ready.
我好期待一陣飲杯茶。I look forward to a cup of tea later.
the workspace
Storyboard: Knowing she needs a good spot for her software engineering work, Maya sets up her equipment at the provided desk. Her laptop stands on the table with a warm lamp by it, while a bag of cables lies out of the way under the desk.
我行入客廳。I go into the living room.
窗邊有一張細書檯。There is a small desk by the window.
呢張檯係畀我做嘢用嘅。The table is for my work.
喺度我可以安安靜靜咁做嘢。Here I can work in peace.
呢度係一個好正嘅小角落。It is a lovely little spot.
我打開我個袋。I open my bag.
我攞部電腦出嚟。I take my computer out.
依家佢放咗喺檯面上。It now sits on the table.
檯面仲有一盞燈。There is also a lamp on the table.
盞燈就喺正部電腦旁邊。The lamp stands right by the computer.
佢嘅光線好溫暖。It has a warm light.
檯面仲放咗一本細細嘅書。There is also a small book lying on the table.
我有一個裝住電線嘅黑色細袋。I have a small black bag with cables.
佢放咗喺書檯下面。It lies under the desk.
咁樣個袋就唔會阻住啦。That way, the bag is not in the way.
書檯旁邊有一張好坐嘅凳。There is a good chair by the desk.
我坐喺張凳度望吓。I sit in the chair and look.
我打開部電腦，開始打字。I open the computer and begin to type.
bedroom comforts
Storyboard: Moving into the bedroom, Maya unpacks her clothes from the main suitcase and folds them in the wardrobe. A thick blanket from Mexico lies on the bed, and she drops a pair of soft slippers on the floor under a small chair.
我行入睡房。I go into the bedroom.
我由客廳將個行李箱搬入嚟。I carry the suitcase in from the living room.
我打開個行李箱。I open the suitcase.
我攞啲衫出嚟。I take my clothes out.
我摺好啲衫。I fold the clothes.
我將佢放入衣櫃。I put it in the wardrobe.
依家我啲冷衫放咗喺衣櫃入面。Now my sweaters lie in the wardrobe.
房入面有一張大床。There is a large bed in the room.
床上面有一張厚毛氈。There lies a thick blanket on the bed.
張毛氈係嚟自墨西哥嘅。The blanket is from Mexico.
我仲搵到一對拖鞋。I also find a pair of slippers.
佢哋好軟熟。They are very soft.
牆邊有一張細櫈。There stands a small chair by the wall.
我將我對拖鞋放喺地下。I put my slippers on the floor.
依家佢哋喺張櫈下面。Now they lie under the chair.
依家個行李箱係空嘅。Now the suitcase is empty.
我閂埋佢，然後放喺床下底。I close it and put it under the bed.
the plant
Storyboard: Maya fetches her new potted plant. She carries it into the living room and sets it on the windowsill, deciding it stands perfectly in the pale winter sunlight.
我行出去走廊。I walk out into the hallway.
我棵新植物擺喺地下。My new plant stands on the floor.
佢係一棵細細嘅綠色植物。It is a small, green plant.
我拎起棵植物。I take the plant.
我捧住佢行入客廳。I carry it into the living room.
客廳有隻大窗。There is a large window in the living room.
我將棵植物擺喺窗台。I put the plant on the windowsill.
我退後一步。I take a step back.
依家棵植物擺咗喺窗邊。Now the plant stands in the window.
冬天嘅陽光照耀住。The winter sun is shining.
棵植物擺喺淡淡嘅光線下啱啱好。The plant stands perfectly in the pale light.
我行出去廚房。I go out into the kitchen.
洗碗盆下面放咗一個細嘅淋水壺。There is a small watering can standing under the sink.
我拎起個淋水壺。I take the watering can.
我幫我棵新植物淋水。I water my new plant.
花盆旁邊有兩塊枯葉。There are two dead leaves lying by the flowerpot.
我執走啲葉。I remove the leaves.
我將佢哋掉落垃圾桶。I throw them in the trash can.
我將個淋水壺放喺窗邊嘅地下。I put the watering can on the floor by the window.
陽光照落濕咗嘅綠葉度。The sun shines on the wet, green leaves.
celebratory pizza
Storyboard: With the furnished flat finally feeling like a real home, Nina returns with two warm pizzas. They sit comfortably on the sofa together, chatting as the cardboard pizza boxes lie on the coffee table between them.
間屋終於搞掂喇。The flat is finally ready.
佢依家係一個真正嘅家喇。It is a real home now.
妮娜敲門。Nina knocks on the door.
佢攞住熱辣辣嘅嘢食企喺門口。She stands by the door with warm food.
「我帶咗兩個薄餅過嚟，」佢話。"I have two pizzas with me," she says.
我哋行入客廳。We go into the living room.
窗邊擺咗一張細梳化。There stands a small sofa by the window.
我哋一齊坐喺梳化上面。We sit on the sofa together.
茶几上面放咗兩個薄餅盒。There lie two pizza boxes on the coffee table.
兩個盒放喺我哋中間。The boxes lie between us.
枱面仲擺咗兩隻玻璃杯。There also stand two glasses on the table.
我哋食啲熱辣辣嘅薄餅。We eat the warm pizzas.
「歡迎嚟到你嘅新屋，」妮娜話。"Welcome to your new flat," Nina says.
「多謝晒，」我答。"Thank you very much," I reply.
我哋傾咗好耐偈。We talk together for a long time.
我樖新植物擺咗喺窗台。My new plant stands in the window.
我對拖鞋放咗喺凳底。My slippers lie under the chair.
樣樣嘢都擺好晒。Everything is in its place.
我好滿意我嘅新家。I am very happy with my new home.
