Week 18: Maya gets a spring allergy

scanning health card

explaining the pain

"Tenggorokan saya sangat sakit," jelas saya."My throat hurts a lot," I explain.
"Rasanya sangat kering.""It is very dry."

"Kepala saya juga terasa sangat berat," lanjut saya."My head is also very heavy," I continue.
Saya menyentuh wajah saya.I touch my face.
"Dan mata saya benar-benar merah.""And my eyes are completely red."

"Anda benar-benar harus beristirahat," katanya."You absolutely ought to rest," he says.
Dia menyalakan senter kecil.He turns on a small flashlight.
"Biar saya periksa Anda lebih dekat," katanya."Let me take a closer look at you," he says.
spring allergy diagnosis
