Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Korean with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
금요일 저녁, 니나가 내 현관문을 두드린다.It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
그녀는 반려견 루트비히를 데리고 왔다.She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.
녀석은 주말 동안 나와 함께 지낼 것이다.He is going to stay with me for the weekend.
니나가 미소를 지으며 내게 목줄을 건넨다.Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
"도와줘서 정말 고마워요, 마야," 그녀가 말한다."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
"전혀 문제없어요," 나는 기쁘게 대답한다."It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
그녀는 녀석에 대해 내게 몇 가지를 설명해 준다.She explains a few things to me about him.
"아침 일곱 시와 저녁 여섯 시에 밥을 주세요," 니나가 말한다."Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
"그리고 기억하세요, 사람이 먹는 음식은 주면 안 돼요.""And remember, he must not have human food."
나는 웃으며 그 작은 강아지를 내려다본다.I laugh and look down at the little dog.
"안 돼, 루트비히, 내 저녁은 네가 먹을 게 아니야," 나는 녀석에게 말한다."No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
니나도 웃으며 문을 가리킨다.Nina laughs too and points at the door.
"큰길 근처를 걸을 때는 항상 목줄을 채워야 해요," 그녀가 말한다."Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
"공원에서는 마음껏 뛰어놀게 해도 괜찮아요.""In the park he is allowed to run freely."
나는 고개를 끄덕인다.I nod.
"걱정 마세요, 제가 잘 돌볼게요.""Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
니나는 나를 안아주고 우리에게 작별 인사를 한다.Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
그녀가 문을 닫고, 나는 루트비히와 단둘이 서 있다.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
녀석은 나를 올려다보며 꼬리를 흔든다.He looks up at me and wags his tail.
나는 벌써 이 녀석이 무척 마음에 든다.I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll
Storyboard: Before bed, Maya takes Ludwig for a short evening walk around the neighborhood. When he suddenly sniffs at a discarded food wrapper on the pavement, she gently commands him to leave it and pulls him away, reminding him that he must not eat trash.

루트비히는 봉지를 놓고 저를 올려다봐요.He drops the bag and looks up at me.
재빨리 꼬리를 흔들기 시작해요.He quickly begins to wag his tail.
"옳지, 착하다," 제가 말하며 쓰다듬어요."Good boy," I say and pet him.
우리는 길을 따라 계속 산책해요.We continue our walk down the street.
이제 집에 가서 자기만 하면 돼요.Now we just need to go home and sleep.
saturday breakfast
Storyboard: Early Saturday morning, Ludwig nudges Maya awake. Maya prepares his breakfast, gently telling him to sit and wait while she fills the bowl, before finally petting him and telling him to eat it.

저는 아직 깊이 잠들어 있습니다.I am still sleeping heavily.

"안 돼, 기다려," 제가 말합니다."No, wait," I say.
녀석은 다시 앉아서 얌전하게 기다립니다.He sits down again and waits nicely.
저는 녀석 앞에 그릇을 내려놓습니다.I put the bowl down in front of him.
저는 녀석의 머리를 부드럽게 쓰다듬습니다.I gently pet him on the head.
"자, 먹어," 제가 미소를 지으며 말합니다."Here you go, eat," I say with a smile.
녀석은 꼬리를 흔들며 먹기 시작합니다.He wags his tail and begins to eat.
a morning together

주말 내내 루트비히를 잘 돌봐줘야 해요.I must take good care of him all weekend.
루트비히와 함께 집에 남아서 정말 기뻐요.I am very happy that I stayed at home with him.
the hundeskov
