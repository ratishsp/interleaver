# Week 7 — Storyboard (Maya manages her weekly calendar)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Clock time (**um halb sechs** — German counts *half towards* the next hour); weekdays & months; frequency adverbs **immer/manchmal/jeden Tag**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_monday_planning | It is a Monday morning in February. Maya sits in her flat and looks at her calendar for the month. She has plans for almost every day. She goes to the gym every Tuesday and Thursday at half past five. This week, she also needs a haircut and a repair service for her bicycle. |
| 2 | 02_booking_haircut | Maya calls a local hair salon. A hairdresser — a man, the same one through the week — answers. She asks for an appointment for a haircut. He suggests Wednesday at six o'clock. Maya checks her calendar, agrees, and books the time. |
| 3 | 03_bike_dropoff | On Tuesday morning, Maya pushes her bicycle to a repair shop near her flat. She hands it over to the mechanic. He says the bicycle will be ready on Wednesday afternoon. |
| 4 | 04_tuesday_gym | It is Tuesday evening at half past five. Maya arrives at the gym. She always trains on Tuesdays and Thursdays. She exercises for an hour and then goes home to rest. |
| 5 | 05_coffee_invitation | Wednesday morning on the stairs. Maya meets her neighbour, Frau Krüger. Frau Krüger invites Maya out for a coffee today at six o'clock. Maya happily says yes, but then suddenly realizes this clashes with her haircut. |
| 6 | 06_rescheduling_haircut | From the office, Maya calls the hair salon again. She apologizes and asks to move her appointment. The hairdresser offers Thursday evening at half past seven. Maya accepts, as this fits perfectly after her next gym session. |
| 7 | 07_bike_pickup | Wednesday afternoon after work. Maya arrives back in her neighbourhood and walks to the repair shop to pick up her bicycle. The brakes work perfectly now. She decides she will sometimes ride it to the office instead of taking the U-Bahn. |
| 8 | 08_coffee_with_sabine | Wednesday at six o'clock. Maya meets Frau Krüger at a nearby café. They drink coffee and chat. During the conversation, Frau Krüger warmly suggests they drop the formalities. Maya smiles, accepts, and now calls her Sabine. |
| 9 | 09_thursday_gym | Thursday evening at half past five. Maya goes to the gym for her second workout of the week. She is a little tired after a long day at the office, but she keeps her routine. |
| 10 | 10_evening_haircut | Thursday at half past seven. Maya arrives at the hair salon for her rescheduled appointment. The hairdresser cuts her hair. Maya loves the new style and walks back to her flat, ready for the weekend. |
