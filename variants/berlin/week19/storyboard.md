# Week 19 — Storyboard (Maya joins the core team project)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Perfekt consolidation; professions; **seit + dative** for how long (*seit fünf Jahren*)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_project_kickoff | On a bright May morning, Maya rides her bicycle to the office. Her team lead, Katrin, calls Maya and Jonas into a meeting room to announce that a major new client project has officially landed. Katrin mentions she is glad to have Maya working directly with them on this core project, noting that she has been the team lead here for three years and this is their most important client yet. The atmosphere is focused but friendly. |
| 2 | 02_meeting_nele | Maya walks over to Nele's desk to get the visual assets. Since they are finally collaborating closely on a project, they chat a bit about their roles. Nele, the UI designer, mentions that she has worked at the company for five years. They look at the screens on Nele's monitor and discuss the layouts Nele has already finished and what still needs work. |
| 3 | 03_coffee_with_sven | Maya heads to the office kitchen to pour herself coffee and bumps into Sven. They already know each other by face, but they chat properly now that they are on the same project. Sven, the software tester, mentions that he has lived in Berlin for a year. He asks Maya how she likes the city so far, and they chat briefly about how they will coordinate once Maya's code is ready for him to break. |
| 4 | 04_writing_the_code | Back at her desk, Maya opens her laptop and starts programming. Jonas pulls up a chair to pair-program with her. They discuss the logic they just wrote and troubleshoot a few early errors together as software engineers. |
| 5 | 05_team_lunch | The team grabs lunch together at a nearby spot. The conversation shifts away from the immediate tasks to their backgrounds. Katrin asks Maya about her career in Mexico. Maya explains that she has been a software engineer for many years and talks about what she studied. |
| 6 | 06_the_morning_meeting | The next morning, the whole team gathers for the daily stand-up meeting. Katrin asks everyone for their updates. It is a completely standard tech routine, and Maya briskly explains exactly what she finished yesterday and what she will do today. Katrin nods, notes the progress, and moves on to the rest of the team. |
| 7 | 07_testing_the_feature | Later that afternoon, Sven tests the new feature. He walks over to Maya's desk and explains that he found a small bug, describing what went wrong when he clicked the button. Maya appreciates his sharp eye, finds the missing line of code, and fixes it immediately, proving they are already a solid team. |
| 8 | 08_thursday_badminton | Thursday evening at half past five. Maya is at the badminton club, having traded her work gear for sports clothes. She rents her racket at the desk and heads to the court to play doubles with Timo. Between rallies, she tells him how nice it is to finally be deep in a real project with the wider team. She feels proud, realising she has fully hit her professional stride in Berlin. |
