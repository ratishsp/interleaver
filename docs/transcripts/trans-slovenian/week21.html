Week 21: Maya buys spring clothes and makes an exchange

spring afternoon shopping

trying spring jacket

grabbing a shirt

keeping the receipt

"Upam, da ne bom potrebovala večje.""I hope I won't need a bigger one."

shirt too small

returning with receipt

asking for exchange
