Week 25: A Sunday in the city

Estonian with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning calendar
Storyboard: On Sunday morning, Maya makes a fresh pot of coffee and flips her wall calendar to July. She realizes that she moved to Copenhagen exactly six months ago, and decides to celebrate by spending this sunny day exploring the city at her own pace.

Kohvilõhn on sel hommikul fantastiline.The smell of coffee is fantastic this morning.
Mul on seinal kalender.I have a calendar on the wall.
Ma keeran lehe järgmisele kuule.I turn the page to the next month.
Nüüd on juuli.It is July now.
Ma kolisin Kopenhaagenisse jaanuaris.I moved to Copenhagen in January.
Ma tulin siia täpselt kuus kuud tagasi.I came here exactly six months ago.
Aeg läheb tõesti kiiresti.Time really goes fast.
Minu esimene päev selles linnas oli väga külm.My first day in this city was very cold.
Kuid täna on ilm soe ja ilus.But today the weather is warm and beautiful.
Ma tahan seda väikest juubelit tähistada.I want to celebrate this little anniversary.
Mul ei ole täna kindlaid plaane.I have no fixed plans today.
Ma võtan oma kohvitassi ja vaatan välja tänavale.I take my coffee cup and look out over the street.
Ma tahan minna välja ja avastada linna omas tempos.I want to go out and explore the city at my own pace.
Ootan rõõmuga, et saaksin neid valgeid tunde nautida.I look forward to enjoying these bright hours.
ceramic plates

Ma peatun väikese laua juures, kus on palju erinevaid asju.I stop by a small table with many different things.

Müüja naeratab mulle.The vendor smiles at me.

See on tõesti hea hind.That is a really good price.
Ma ostsin kuu aega tagasi oma kööki väikese laua.I bought a small table for my kitchen a month ago.
Need taldrikud sobivad selle lauaga ideaalselt.These plates will fit perfectly with this table.

Ma annan müüjale nelikümmend krooni ja panen taldrikud ettevaatlikult oma kotti.I give the vendor forty kroner and carefully put the plates in my bag.
Vaiksel pühapäeval on alati tore midagi erilist leida.It is always nice to find something special on a quiet Sunday.
winter coat
Storyboard: At a clothing stall, Maya tries on a heavy wool winter coat. She immediately takes it off because this coat is far too warm for summer, laughing with the vendor that she desperately needed something exactly like it five months ago.

See on uskumatult soe.It is incredibly warm.

Ma katsun paksu kangast.I touch the thick fabric.
"Aga mul oli seda talvemantlit tõesti viis kuud tagasi vaja!" ütlen ma."But I really needed this winter coat five months ago!" I say.
Naine naerab valjult.The woman laughs loudly.
"Jah, veebruar oli väga külm," ütleb ta."Yes, February was very cold," she says.

museum ticket
Storyboard: Moving on, Maya enters a local history museum and goes to the front desk. She asks the clerk if this entrance ticket includes the special upstairs exhibition, and the clerk tells her to hurry because the guided tour started ten minutes ago.

Kassa taga seisab noor mees.A young man is standing behind the register.
"Tere, üks täiskasvanu pilet, palun," ütlen ma."Hi, one adult ticket, please," I say.
"See teeb sada krooni," ütleb ta ja prindib selle välja."That will be one hundred kroner," he says and prints it.

Need reklaamivad erinäitust linna ajaloo kohta.They advertise a special exhibition about the history of the city.
"Kas see pilet tagab sissepääsu ka ülakorruse näitusele?" küsin ma."Does this ticket also give access to the exhibition upstairs?" I ask.
"Jah, sellel muuseumil on ainult üks ühispilet," vastab ta."Yes, this museum only has one combined ticket," he answers.
"Näitus asub üleval teisel korrusel," ütleb ta."The exhibition is upstairs on the first floor," he says.
"Võite seal rahulikult aega võtta.""You can take your time up there."

"Mõned neist on üle saja aasta vanad.""Some of them are over a hundred years old."

harbor photograph

texting sofia

seagull encounter
Storyboard: Walking home through a large park, Maya stops at a kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream. As she strolls along the pond, a bold seagull suddenly swoops down, forcing her to quickly duck and shield this dessert with her hand to prevent the bird from stealing it.

Tund aega tagasi olin ma muuseumis.An hour ago I was at a museum.
Nüüd tahan ma lihtsalt ilusat ilma nautida.Now I just want to enjoy the good weather.
Ma peatun väikese kioski juures, et vahvlijäätist osta.I stop at a small kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream.

unpacking plates
