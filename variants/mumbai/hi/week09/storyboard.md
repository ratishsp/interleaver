# Week 9 — Storyboard (A Morning at the Market)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Prices — **कितने का है? / कितना हुआ?**; amounts as words; **दीजिए/चाहिए** at the stall

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_market_arrival | Maya walks into the lively outdoor neighbourhood market. Vendors call out fresh items, and the street is full of colours and noise. She holds her reusable cloth bags and reviews her shopping list. |
| 2 | 02_banana_board_change | Maya stops at a fruit cart. A small cardboard sign shows the price of bananas per dozen. She addresses the young vendor as bhaiya, says she wants half a dozen, and hands him a large note, waiting while he counts out her change in rupees. |
| 3 | 03_tomatoes_per_kilo | At a busy vegetable stall, Maya points to the fresh tomatoes and asks how much they are per kilo. The vendor weighs out a small batch for her, and she asks him for the total amount before paying. |
| 4 | 04_spice_aroma | Maya approaches a spice stall run by an elderly man. She respectfully calls him chacha, and he proudly offers her a pinch of a special tea masala to smell. She loves the aroma and tells him to give her a small packet. |
| 5 | 05_expensive_mug | Maya browses a small homeware stall and spots a beautifully painted ceramic mug. She asks the vendor the price, but the amount he names is much too high for her budget. She smiles, politely says she does not need it right now, and moves on. |
| 6 | 06_marigold_garland | Maya pauses at a fragrant flower stall where an older woman is stringing flowers. Before Maya even asks, the aunty brightly tells her the price of a fresh marigold garland. Maya happily asks for one to hang on her flat's door. |
| 7 | 07_cutting_chai_break | With her bags feeling heavy, Maya stops at a small roadside tea stall. She asks the tea seller for a cutting chai, pays the exact amount in small coins, and sips the hot tea while watching the bustling market crowd. |
| 8 | 08_heading_home | Maya leaves the busy market lane and walks back towards her apartment building. She feels confident about navigating the bustling market and managing her purchases, looking forward to using her fresh ingredients for dinner. |
