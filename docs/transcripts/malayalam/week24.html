the favor
Storyboard: Late Thursday evening, just as Maya returns to her flat from her weekly badminton game, Anitha knocks on her door and explains that she has to travel for the weekend. She asks if Maya would be willing to look after her dog, Tommy, and Maya happily agrees.

"വളരെ നന്ദി മായേ, ഞാൻ നാളെ വൈകുന്നേരം അവനെ കൊണ്ടുവരാം," അനിത ചിരിച്ചുകൊണ്ട് പറയുന്നു."Thank you very much Maya, I will bring him tomorrow evening," Anitha says with a smile.
