Week 5: Maya stocks her new kitchen

Serbian with English gloss, line-aligned.

empty cupboards
Storyboard: Maya stands in the kitchen, noticing that the fridge and the cupboard are completely empty. She grabs a bag and heads out, ready to buy food.
Šest je sati.It is six o'clock.
Stojim u kuhinji.I am standing in the kitchen.
Gladna sam.I am hungry.
Otvaram frižider.I open the fridge.
Potpuno je prazan.It is completely empty.
Gledam u ormarić.I look in the cupboard.
Nema hrane u ormariću.There is no food in the cupboard.
Nemam hrane.I have no food.
Imam samo malo vode.I only have a little water.
Volela bih sada da jedem.I would like to have food now.
Volim povrće.I like vegetables.
Takođe volim hleb.I also like bread.
Ali ovde nemam ništa.But I have nothing here.
Nalazim kesu.I find a bag.
Kesa je crvena.The bag is red.
Dobra je za kupovinu.It is good for shopping.
Izlazim iz stana.I walk out of the flat.
Zatvaram vrata.I close the door.
Spremna sam.I am ready.
Sada idem u supermarket.Now I am walking to the supermarket.
produce section
Storyboard: Maya enters a supermarket and pulls a basket from a stack by the door. Because she likes carrots, she grabs a large bunch from a green crate and drops it into the basket.
Stojim ispred supermarketa.I stand in front of the supermarket.
Supermarket je velik i svetao.The supermarket is large and bright.
Vrata se automatski otvaraju.The door opens automatically.
Ulazim u prodavnicu.I go into the shop.
Prodavnica ima mnogo proizvoda.The shop has many goods.
Pored ulaza stoje naslagane korpe.There are stacks of baskets by the entrance.
Uzimam korpu.I take a basket.
Korpa je crvena i lagana.The basket is red and light.
Tražim povrće.I look for vegetables.
Odeljenje je baš ovde.The section is right here.
Vidim gajbu na podu.I see a crate on the floor.
Gajba je zelena.The crate is green.
U gajbi ima mnogo šargarepa.There are many carrots in the crate.
Volim šargarepu.I like carrots.
Biram veliku vezu.I choose a large bunch.
Veza izgleda veoma sveže.The bunch looks very fresh.
Stavljam vezu u korpu.I put the bunch down into the basket.
Sada korpa nije prazna.Now the basket is not empty.
Idem dalje u prodavnicu.I walk further into the shop.
the parsnip
Storyboard: Looking around, Maya spots a pale, unusual root. She reads a sign and learns it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she decides to buy it anyway out of curiosity.
Vidim čudno povrće.I see a strange vegetable.
Povrće je dugačak koren.The vegetable is a long root.
Koren je veoma svetao.The root is very pale.
Izgleda kao bela šargarepa.It looks like a white carrot.
Gledam dole u natpis.I look down at the sign.
Na natpisu piše "paškanat".It says "parsnip" on the sign.
Ne poznajem to povrće.I do not know the vegetable.
Da li je to dansko povrće?Is it a Danish vegetable?
Volim povrće.I like vegetables.
Volim šargarepe.I like carrots.
Možda je paškanat ukusan.Maybe the parsnip tastes good.
Uzimam paškanat u ruku.I take the parsnip in my hand.
Koren je prilično velik.The root is quite big.
Volela bih da ga uzmem.I would like to have it.
Gledam u korpu.I look at the basket.
Stavljam koren u korpu.I put the root in the basket.
Volela bih da probam nešto novo.I would like to try something new.
the rye bread
Storyboard: At a shelf with baked goods, Maya looks for a local staple. She lifts a loaf of rye bread, surprised by how heavy it feels, and lays the bread carefully in the basket.
Prilazim polici sa hlebom.I walk over to a shelf with bread.
Tražim lokalni klasik.I am looking for a local classic.
Želela bih danski ražani hleb.I would like to have a Danish rye bread.
Gledam u policu.I look at the shelf.
Nalazim ražani hleb.I find a rye bread.
U kesi je.It is in a bag.
Gledam u kesu.I look at the bag.
Uzimam ražani hleb.I take the rye bread.
Podižem hleb.I lift the bread.
Iznenađena sam.I am surprised.
Hleb je veoma težak.The bread is very heavy.
Deluje kao kamen!It feels like a stone!
Volim svetao hleb.I like light bread.
Ali ražani hleb je taman i težak.But the rye bread is dark and heavy.
Pažljivo spuštam hleb u korpu.I carefully lay the bread in the basket.
Sada je i korpa teška.The basket is also heavy now.
bakery counter
Storyboard: The bakery counter has a member of staff waiting. Maya catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry.
Prilazim pultu u pekari.I walk over to the bakery counter.
Miris je veoma lep.The smell is very good.
Volim kolače.I like cakes.
I zaista volim rolnice sa cimetom.And I really like cinnamon pastries.
Pekar stoji iza pulta.A baker stands behind the counter.
Pekar me gleda.The baker looks at me.
On se smeši.He smiles.
„Šta biste želeli?“ pita pekar."What would you like to have?" the baker asks.
Pokazujem na rolnicu sa cimetom.I point at a cinnamon pastry.
Kolač izgleda ukusno.The pastry looks delicious.
„Želela bih jednu rolnicu sa cimetom, molim vas“, kažem."I would like to have a cinnamon pastry, please," I say.
„Da, naravno“, kaže pekar."Yes, of course," the baker says.
On uzima kolač.He takes the pastry.
On stavlja kolač u kesu.He puts the pastry in a bag.
On pruža kesu preko pulta.He hands the bag over the counter.
„Izvolite“, kaže on."Here you go," he says.
Uzimam kesu.I take the bag.
„Mnogo hvala“, kažem."Thank you very much," I say.
Spuštam kesu u korpu.I put the bag down into the basket.
finding the milk
Storyboard: Maya searches for the milk without luck. She stops an employee to ask for help, and he points her toward the dairy section at the back.
Sada mi treba mleko.Now I need milk.
Idem niz dugačak prolaz.I walk down a long aisle.
Gledam u policu.I look at the shelf.
Vidim vodu i sok.I can see the water and the juice.
Ali ne mogu da nađem mleko.But I cannot find the milk.
Gledam svuda.I look everywhere.
Vidim jednog čoveka.I see a man.
On radi u supermarketu.He works in the supermarket.
Zaustavljam radnika i smešim se.I stop the employee and smile.
"Izvinite," kažem."Excuse me," I say.
"Htela bih mleko.""I would like to have milk."
"Gde je mleko?""Where is the milk?"
Čovek pokazuje ka zadnjem delu prodavnice.The man points to the back of the shop.
"Mlečno odeljenje je tamo dole," kaže on."The dairy section is down there," he says.
"Mnogo vam hvala," odgovaram."Thank you very much," I reply.
Idem tamo dole.I walk down there.
Konačno nalazim frižider i mleko.I finally find the fridge and the milk.
Uzimam tetrapak i stavljam ga u korpu.I take a carton and lay it in the basket.
taste of home
Storyboard: Exploring another section, Maya hunts for a taste of Mexico because she really likes beans. She recognizes a bright can of black beans on a low shelf and reaches all the way to the back to pull it out.
Idem dalje do druge police.I walk on to another set of shelves.
Gledam hranu.I look at the food.
Supermarket ima mnogo stvari iz drugih zemalja.The supermarket has many things from other countries.
Tražim ukus Meksika.I look for a taste of Mexico.
Nedostaje mi hrana od kuće.I miss the food from home.
Stvarno volim pasulj.I really like beans.
Često jedem crni pasulj.I often eat black beans.
Gledam nisku policu.I look at a low shelf.
Polica je malo mračna.The shelf is a bit dark.
Ali vidim šarenu konzervu.But I see a colourful can.
Konzerva stoji skroz pozadi.The can stands all the way at the back.
To je konzerva crnog pasulja.It is a can of black beans.
Smešim se.I smile.
Pružam ruku skroz unutra.I reach my arm all the way in.
Izvlačim konzervu.I pull the can out.
Čitam etiketu na konzervi.I read the label on the can.
Savršena je za moju večeru.It is perfect for my dinner.
Stavljam konzervu u korpu.I put the can in the basket.
Imam dovoljno hrane.I have enough food.
the checkout
Storyboard: Maya brings the basket to the checkout. She places the food on the belt, holds her card over the machine, and packs the bag quickly. No price is spoken or shown.
Prilazim kasi.I walk up to the checkout.
Imam korpu u ruci.I have the basket in my hand.
Stavljam hranu na traku.I put the food on the belt.
Prvo uzimam ražani hleb iz korpe.First I take the rye bread from the basket.
Zatim uzimam mleko i paškanat.Then I take the milk and the parsnip.
Takođe uzimam šargarepe i crni pasulj.I also take carrots and black beans.
Na kraju stavljam pecivo na traku.Finally I put the pastry on the belt.
Kasir sedi za kasom.The cashier sits behind the checkout.
Smeši mi se.He smiles at me.
„Zdravo”, kaže on."Hello," he says.
„Zdravo”, odgovaram."Hello," I reply.
Brzo skenira hranu.He scans the food quickly.
Vadim svoju karticu.I take my card out.
Držim karticu iznad aparata.I hold the card over the machine.
Aparat zapišti.The machine beeps.
„Da li želite račun?” pita on."Would you like to have the receipt?" he asks.
„Da, molim vas, želela bih račun”, kažem."Yes please, I would like to have the receipt," I say.
Otvaram svoju kesu.I open my bag.
Brzo pakujem kesu.I pack the bag quickly.
„Prijatan dan”, kaže on."Have a good day," he says.
putting away
Storyboard: Back in the flat, Maya empties the bag on the counter. She puts the milk and the parsnip in the fridge, and she places the bread and the can in the cupboard.
Opet sam kod kuće u stanu.I am home in the flat again.
Stojim u kuhinji.I am standing in the kitchen.
Praznim kesu na kuhinjski pult.I empty the bag on the kitchen counter.
Raspakujem hranu.I unpack the food.
Uzimam mleko.I take the milk.
Stavljam mleko u frižider.I put the milk in the fridge.
Zatim gledam paškanat.Then I look at the parsnip.
To je smešan koren.It is a funny root.
Koristiću ga u svojoj supi.I will use it in my soup.
Stavljam paškanat u frižider.I put the parsnip in the fridge.
Sada frižider nije prazan.Now the fridge is not empty.
Uzimam hleb.I take the bread.
Ražani hleb je veoma težak.The rye bread is very heavy.
Stavljam hleb u ormarić.I put the bread in the cupboard.
Zatim uzimam konzervu pasulja.Then I take the can of beans.

Stavljam konzervu u ormarić.I put the can in the cupboard.

Prilično sam gladna.I am quite hungry.
Volela bih sada da večeram.I would like to have dinner now.
first home cooked meal
Storyboard: Ready to eat, Maya chops some vegetables and opens the can. She cooks dinner on the stove, finally making a proper meal in the flat — and afterwards she has the pastry from the bakery counter for dessert.
Kuhinja je mala.The kitchen is small.
Ali šporet je dobar.But the stove is good.
Uzimam paškanat i šargarepu.I take the parsnip and a carrot.
Uzimam i nož.I also take a knife.
Nož je oštar.The knife is sharp.
Sečem paškanat i šargarepu.I cut the parsnip and the carrot.
Otvaram konzervu.I open the can.
Stavljam hranu u šerpu.I put the food in a pot.
Šerpa je na šporetu.The pot is on the stove.
Sada je hrana topla.Now the food is warm.
Divno miriše.It smells lovely.
Uzimam ražani hleb.I take the rye bread.
Sečem krišku.I cut a slice.
Tanjir je na stolu.The plate is on the table.
Konačno jedem pravi obrok u stanu.Finally, I am eating a proper meal in the flat.
Posle toga jedem rolnicu sa cimetom.Afterwards I eat the cinnamon pastry.
Ima sladak ukus.It tastes sweet.
