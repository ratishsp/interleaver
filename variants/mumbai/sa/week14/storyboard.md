# Week 14 — Storyboard (Maya navigates the Mumbai metro and BEST bus)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** **आरोहति / अवरोहति** (board/alight); the instrumental case with vehicles (**लोकयानेन, रेलयानेन**); **गन्तव्यम् अस्ति** (need to get to)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_waiting_for_the_bus | Maya waits at her local BEST bus stop on a bright morning. A familiar red bus approaches, and she confidently steps onto it as the doors open, holding the handrail to steady herself. |
| 2 | 02_buying_the_bus_ticket | Maya finds a seat and signals the bus conductor. She tells him that she needs to get to the main metro station, hands him a few rupees, and pockets her small paper ticket. |
| 3 | 03_alighting_at_the_station | The bus navigates through the bustling Mumbai traffic. Upon reaching the large glass-fronted metro station, Maya carefully steps off the bus and joins the crowd walking toward the entrance. |
| 4 | 04_metro_ticket_counter | Inside the spacious station, Maya approaches the ticket counter. She politely addresses the worker as sir, explains that she needs to get to a specific station near the sea, and purchases a permanent metro card for her journey. |
| 5 | 05_boarding_the_metro | Maya rides the escalator up to the platform, tapping her new metro card at the gates, and stands behind the yellow safety line. A sleek train glides in, the doors slide open, and she boards the cool, air-conditioned metro carriage. |
| 6 | 06_asking_for_directions | Looking at the route map above the door, Maya feels unsure about where the lines connect. She politely addresses an older fellow passenger as aunty and asks her at which station she needs to get off to change lines. |
| 7 | 07_changing_metro_lines | Following the passenger's helpful instructions, Maya alights at a busy junction station. She follows the colored signs overhead, walks down a corridor to a new platform, and successfully boards her connecting train. |
| 8 | 08_arriving_near_the_sea | The final train arrives at her destination. Maya steps off the carriage, rides the escalator down to the street level, and instantly feels the humid, salty breeze coming from the nearby sea. |
| 9 | 09_running_the_errand | Maya walks down a narrow lane near the promenade and enters an old local sweets shop to complete her errand. She politely asks the shop worker, calling him bhaiya, to pack a box of fresh Maharashtrian pedhas to share at the office, pays in rupees, and happily concludes her cross-city journey. |
