Week 13: Maya goes to a café

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

escaping the wind
Storyboard: Maya walks down the street and leans against the freezing winter wind. She spots a café with fogged windows and steps inside to escape the cold draft.
V Kodani je zima.It is winter in Copenhagen.
Kráčam po ulici.I am walking down the street.
Vietor je veľmi studený.The wind is very cold.
Dnes silno fúka.It is blowing hard today.
Opieram sa do studeného vetra.I lean against the cold wind.
Vyťahujem si šál vyššie.I pull my scarf up.
Mrzne mi tvár.My face is freezing.
Rozhliadam sa.I look around.
Chcela by som ísť do tepla.I would like to get into the warmth.
Vidím malú kaviareň na rohu.I see a small café on the corner.
Okná sú zahmlené.The windows are fogged up.
Vyzerá, že vo vnútri je teplo.It looks warm in there.
Kráčam ku kaviarni.I walk over to the café.
Otváram ťažké dvere.I open the heavy door.
Vchádzam dnu.I step inside.
Vonia to tu kávou.It smells like coffee here.
Vzduch je príjemne teplý.The air is nice and warm.
Zatváram za sebou dvere.I close the door behind me.
Studený vietor zostáva vonku.The cold wind stays outside.
finding a seat
Storyboard: She takes off her thick coat and hangs it on a wall hook. She chooses a small table near a radiator and rubs her hands together to get warm before she orders.
Teraz som vnútri v kaviarni.I am inside the café now.
Je tu veľmi teplo.It is very warm here.
Vyzliekam si hrubú zimnú bundu.I take off my thick winter coat.
Je ťažká.It is heavy.
Nájdem háčik na stene.I find a hook on the wall.
Zavesím si bundu na háčik.I hang my coat on the hook.
Dávam si dole aj šál a rukavice.I also take off my scarf and my gloves.
Kaviareň je malá a útulná.The café is small and cozy.
Hľadám si dobré miesto.I look for a good seat.
Pri okne je malý stôl.There is a small table by the window.
Stôl stojí vedľa radiátora.The table is next to a radiator.
Idem k stolu.I walk over to the table.
Sadnem si na stoličku.I sit down on the chair.
Moje ruky sú stále studené.My hands are still cold.
Šúcham si ich o seba.I rub them together.
Moje ruky sa zohrievajú.My hands get warmer.
Je to fantastický pocit.It feels fantastic.
Teraz mi je už skoro teplo.Now I am almost warm.
Som hladná a smädná.I am hungry and thirsty.
Rada by som si teraz niečo objednala.I would like to order something now.
the pastry case
Storyboard: Maya walks over to the glass counter to inspect the baked goods. She reads the handwritten signs to choose a pastry and decides she really wants a cinnamon roll.
Idem k presklenej vitríne.I walk over to the glass counter.
Je plná koláčov a chleba.It is full of cakes and bread.
Pozerám sa na to množstvo vecí.I look at the many things.
Vyzerajú veľmi chutne.They look very delicious.
Vedľa koláčov sú malé štítky.There are small signs next to the cakes.
Štítky sú ručne písané.The signs are handwritten.
Čítam názvy na štítkoch.I read the names on the signs.
Je tu veľa druhov pečiva.There are many kinds of pastry.
Je ťažké si vybrať.It is hard to choose.
Je tam veľký čokoládový koláč.There is a large chocolate cake.
Ale pozerám sa na iný kúsok pečiva.But I look at another piece of pastry.
Je to veľké škoricové hniezdo.It is a large cinnamon roll.
Na vrchu má veľa cukru.It has a lot of sugar on top.
Milujem chuť škorice.I love the taste of cinnamon.
Je to perfektné na chladný zimný deň.It is perfect for a cold winter day.
Veľmi by som chcela to škoricové hniezdo.I would very much like to have the cinnamon roll.
Teraz som pripravená si objednať.Now I am ready to order.
ordering at the counter
Storyboard: Maya steps up to the register and smiles at the barista. She politely orders a cup of black coffee and the cinnamon roll she wants.
Idem k pokladni.I walk over to the register.
Za pultom stojí mladý muž.A young man stands behind the counter.
Je barista v kaviarni.He is a barista in the café.
Usmejem sa naňho.I smile at him.
Prívetivo prikývne.He nods warmly.
„Dobrý deň,“ hovorí."Hi," he says.
„Čo si dáte?“ pýta sa."What would you like?" he asks.
„Dobrý deň,“ odpovedám."Hi," I answer.
„Dala by som si čiernu kávu, prosím.“"I would like a black coffee, please."
„A môžem poprosiť aj o škoricového slimáka?“"And may I also ask for a cinnamon roll?"
„Samozrejme,“ hovorí."Of course," he says.
„Jedna čierna káva a škoricový slimák.“"A black coffee and a cinnamon roll."
„Chcete si sadnúť sem?“ pýta sa."Would you like to sit here?" he asks.
„Áno, prosím, sadnem si tamto,“ hovorím."Yes please, I will sit down over there," I say.
„V poriadku,“ hovorí barista."Alright," the barista says.
„Teraz vám pripravím kávu.“"I will make your coffee now."
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem,“ hovorím."Thank you very much," I say.
carrying the tray
Storyboard: The barista sets a steaming mug and a small plate on a wooden tray. Maya thanks him for the polite service and carefully carries the order back to her table by the window.
Čakám pri pulte.I wait at the counter.
Pripravuje moju kávu.He makes my coffee.
Položí horúcu šálku kávy na drevenú tácku.He puts a hot cup of coffee on a wooden tray.
Na tácku položí aj malý tanierik.He also puts a small plate on the tray.
Môj škoricový slimák leží na tanieriku.My cinnamon roll is on the plate.
Vonia nádherne.It smells lovely.
„Nech sa páči, vaša objednávka,“ hovorí."Here you go, your order," he says.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem,“ odpovedám."Thank you very much," I reply.
„Ďakujem za dobrú obsluhu.“"Thank you for the good service."
„Rado sa stalo,“ hovorí milo."You are welcome," he says kindly.
Beriem tácku oboma rukami.I take the tray with both hands.
Kráčam veľmi opatrne po podlahe.I walk very carefully across the floor.
Kráčam späť k svojmu stolu pri okne.I walk back to my table by the window.
Položím tácku na stôl.I put the tray down on the table.
Teraz by som si chcela sadnúť.Now I would like to sit down.
Som pripravená na dobrú prestávku.I am ready for a good break.
observing the room
Storyboard: Maya sits alone and takes a bite of her warm pastry. She listens to the low hum of voices in the room, watches the snow fall outside, and finally feels her fingers thaw.
Teraz sedím za svojím malým stolom.I now sit at my small table.
Je tu príjemne teplo.It is lovely and warm here.
Pozerám sa na svoju škoricovú rolku.I look at my cinnamon roll.
Vonia sladko a dobre.It smells sweet and good.
Odhryznem si malý kúsok.I take a small bite.
Pečivo je úplne čerstvé a teplé.The pastry is completely fresh and warm.
Chutí naozaj dobre.It tastes really good.
Odpijem si trochu zo svojej čiernej kávy.I drink a little of my black coffee.
Konečne sú moje prsty opäť teplé.Finally, my fingers are warm again.
Oddychujem v mäkkom kresle.I relax in the soft chair.
Kaviareň je takmer plná ľudí.The café is almost full of people.
Ľudia sedia v malých skupinkách.People sit in small groups.
Spolu sa rozprávajú a smejú.They talk and laugh together.
Počúvam tiché hlasy v miestnosti.I listen to the low voices in the room.
Ten zvuk je veľmi príjemný.The sound is very cozy.
Pozerám sa von cez veľké okno.I look out of the large window.
Počasie vonku je sivé a veterné.The weather outside is gray and windy.
Teraz vlastne znova sneží.Now it is actually snowing again.
Biele snehové vločky pomaly padajú na ulicu.White snowflakes fall slowly down onto the street.
Som jednoducho rada, že som vnútri.I am just happy to be inside.
one more thing
Storyboard: Maya finishes her sweet roll and realizes she is very thirsty. She catches the eye of a staff member who collects empty cups and politely asks if she may have a glass of tap water.
Jem posledný kúsok svojho škoricového slimáka.I eat the last piece of my cinnamon roll.
Chutí naozaj dobre.It tastes really good.
Ale je aj veľmi sladký.But it is also very sweet.
Moja šálka na kávu je úplne prázdna.My coffee cup is completely empty.
Zrazu som veľmi smädná.I am suddenly very thirsty.
Chcela by som trochu studenej vody.I would like to have some cold water.
Okolo môjho stola prechádza mladý muž.A young man walks past my table.
Pracuje tu v kaviarni.He works here in the café.
Zbiera prázdne šálky a taniere.He collects empty cups and plates.
Zachytím jeho pohľad.I catch his eye.
Zastaví sa pri mojom stole a usmeje sa.He stops at my table and smiles.
„Prepáčte,“ poviem zdvorilo."Excuse me," I say politely.
„Áno?“ odpovie mladý muž."Yes?" answers the young man.
„Môžem poprosiť o pohár vody z vodovodu?“ spýtam sa."May I ask for a glass of tap water?" I ask.
„Áno, samozrejme,“ povie."Yes, of course," he says.
„Ďakujem veľmi pekne,“ poviem."Thank you very much," I say.
Vezme môj prázdny tanier a šálku.He takes my empty plate and cup.
Kráča späť k pultu.He walks back to the counter.
O chvíľu neskôr príde s pohárom studenej vody.A little later he comes with a cold glass of water.
asking for the bill
Storyboard: Maya drinks the refreshing water and gathers her bag from the floor. She walks back to the counter and politely asks the barista for the bill so she can settle her tab.
Pijem svoju vodu.I drink my water.
Voda je studená a príjemná.The water is cold and nice.
Beriem si tašku z podlahy.I take my bag from the floor.
Skončila som s prestávkou.I am finished with my break.
Idem k pultu.I walk up to the counter.
Priateľský barista tam stále stojí.The friendly barista is still standing there.
Pozerá sa na mňa s úsmevom.He looks at me with a smile.
„Dali by ste si ešte niečo?“ pýta sa."Would you like anything else?" he asks.
„Nie, ďakujem,“ odpovedám."No thank you," I answer.
„Chcela by som platiť.“"I would like to pay."
„Áno, samozrejme,“ hovorí."Yes, of course," he says.
Pozerá sa na svoju malú obrazovku.He looks at his little screen.
„Jedna čierna káva a jedna škoricová rolka,“ hovorí."One black coffee and one cinnamon roll," he says.
„Áno, to súhlasí,“ hovorím."Yes, that is correct," I say.
„Chcete platiť kartou alebo v hotovosti?“ pýta sa."Do you want to pay with card or cash?" he asks.
„Chcela by som platiť kartou,“ odpovedám."I would like to pay with a card," I answer.
„V poriadku.“"Fine."
„Bude to osemdesiatpäť korún,“ hovorí."That will be eighty-five kroner," he says.
Vyťahujem svoju platobnú kartu.I take out my payment card.
paying and leaving
Storyboard: Maya taps her card on the terminal to pay eighty-five kroner. She puts on her heavy coat, thanks the staff for a nice time, and pushes the front door open to face the cold again.
Barista sa na mňa usmieva.The barista smiles at me.
Podržím kartu nad terminálom.I hold my card over the terminal.
Prístroj zapípa.The machine beeps.
„Chcete účtenku?“ pýta sa."Do you want a receipt?" he asks.
„Nie, ďakujem,“ odpovedám."No thank you," I answer.
Prejdem k stene.I walk over to the wall.
Vezmem si z háčika svoj hrubý zimný kabát.I take my thick winter coat from a hook.
Oblečiem si kabát.I put the coat on.
Dám si aj šál.I also put my scarf on.
Natiahnem si aj rukavice.I also put my gloves on.
Teraz som pripravená na chlad.Now I am ready for the cold.
Znovu sa pozriem na baristu.I look at the barista again.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem za kávu,“ hovorím."Thank you very much for the coffee," I say.
„Niet za čo,“ hovorí s úsmevom."You are welcome," he says with a smile.
„Majte pekný deň.“"Have a good day."
„Nápodobne,“ hovorím."Likewise," I say.
Prejdem k vchodovým dverám.I walk over to the front door.
Potlačím ťažké dvere a otvorím ich.I push the heavy door open.
Vietor je studený, ale cítim sa dobre.The wind is cold, but I feel good.
