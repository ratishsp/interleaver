Week 8: Maya explores the city

Lithuanian with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
Šeštadienio rytas.It is Saturday morning.
Virš miesto šviečia saulė.The sun is shining over the city.
Nusileidžiu į gatvę.I go down to the street.
Mano kaimynė Nina stovi lauke prie mūsų pastato.My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
„Labas rytas, Maja“, – sako ji ir šypsosi."Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
„Labas rytas, Nina“, – atsakau."Good morning, Nina," I answer.
„Šiandien man laisvadienis“, – pasakau jai."I have the day off today," I tell her.
„Einu į miestą.“"I am heading out into the city."
„Kur eini?“ – klausia Nina."Where are you going?" Nina asks.
„Einu prie vandens“, – sakau."I am walking down to the water," I say.
„Bet nežinau kelio.“"But I do not know the way."
Nina rodo tolyn gatve.Nina points down the street.
„Laimei, tai labai lengva“, – sako ji."Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
„Čia eik tiesiai.“"Go straight ahead here."
„Ir prie parko pasuk į kairę.“"And turn left at the park."
„Tada nueisi tiesiai iki uosto.“"Then you come right down to the harbour."
„Labai ačiū už pagalbą, Nina“, – sakau."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
„Nėra už ką, ir gero pasivaikščiojimo“, – atsako ji."You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
Pamojuoju jai.I wave to her.
Pradedu savo pasivaikščiojimą vandens link.I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
Aš seku Ninos maršrutu.I follow Nina's route.
Oras gaivus ir šaltas.The air is fresh and cold.
Dabar einu pagrindine gatve.Now I walk down the main street.
Pirmiausia tiesiog einu tiesiai.First I just walk straight ahead.
Gatvėje daug eismo.There is a lot of traffic on the street.
Pakeliui matau tris geltonus dviračius.On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
Dviračiai greitai pravažiuoja pro mane.The bicycles ride quickly past me.
Po to einu toliau į priekį.After that I walk further ahead.
Tada prieinu didelį parką.Then I come to a large park.
Dabar prisimenu Ninos žodžius.Now I remember Nina's words.
„Prie parko suk į kairę“, – sako ji."Turn left at the park," she says.
Čia pasuku į kairę.Here I turn left.
Kiek vėliau pamatau atviras duris.A little later I see an open door.
Tai maža kepykla.It is a small bakery.
Sklinda malonus šviežios kavos kvapas.There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
Labai mėgstu šiltą duoną.I love warm bread.
Šiandien miestas nuostabus.Today the city is lovely.
Paskui einu toliau link vandens.Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
Atvykstu į didelę aikštę.I arrive at a large square.
Čia daug žmonių.There are many people.
Bet nematau vandens.But I do not see the water.
Ieškau uosto.I am looking for the harbour.
Ant šaligatvio matau vyresnį vyrą.I see an older man on the pavement.
Sustabdau jį.I stop him.
„Atsiprašau“, – sakau."Excuse me," I say.
„Kur yra uostas?“"Where is the harbour?"
Vyresnis vyras linkteli.The older man nods.
Jis rodo į priekį.He points ahead.
„Eikite tiesiai per aikštę“, – sako jis."Walk straight across the square," he says.
„Praeikite pro muziejų.“"Go past the museum."
„Ir pasukite į dešinę.“"And turn right."
Žvelgiu per aikštę.I look across the square.
Matau didelį pastatą.I see a large building.
Tai muziejus.It is the museum.
„Labai ačiū“, – sakau."Thank you very much," I say.
„Prašom“, – atsako jis."You are welcome," he replies.
Einu toliau.I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
Einu per didelę aikštę.I walk across the large square.
Staiga išgirstu muziką.Suddenly, I hear music.
Ant kampo stovi gatvės muzikantas.On the corner stands a street musician.
Jis groja gitara.He plays the guitar.
Klausausi geros muzikos.I listen to the good music.
Ilgai į jį žiūriu.I look at him for a long time.
Tada einu toliau.Then I walk on.
Pamirštu muziejų.I forget the museum.
Taip pat pamirštu savo maršrutą.I also forget my route.
Vietoj to einu siaura šalutine gatve.Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
Čia labai tylu.It is very quiet here.
Aplinkui matau tik kelis mažus namus.All around I only see some small houses.
Tada sustoju.Then I stop.
Kur yra muziejus?Where is the museum?
Dabar apsidairau.Now I look around.
Daugiau jo nebematau.I cannot see it anymore.
Greitai pažvelgiu atgal gatve.I quickly look back up the street.
Aikštės taip pat nematau.I cannot see the square either.
Aš visiškai pasiklydau.I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
Stoviu tylioje gatvėje.I am standing on a quiet street.
Aš pasiklydau.I am lost.
Priešais save matau jauną moterį.Ahead of me I see a young woman.
Ji vedžioja mažą šunį.She is walking a small dog.
Šuo panašus į mano šeimos šunį Lolą.The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
Prieinu prie moters.I walk over to the woman.
„Atsiprašau“, – sakau."Excuse me," I say.
„Ieškau uosto.“"I am looking for the harbour."
Moteris sustoja ir klausosi.The woman stops and listens.
Ji rodo atgal gatve.She points back up the street.
„Eikite tiesiai atgal į aikštę“, – sako ji."Go straight back to the square," she says.
„Ir prie muziejaus pasukite į dešinę.“"And turn right at the museum."
„Labai ačiū už pagalbą“, – sakau."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
„Nėra už ką“, – atsako moteris."You are welcome," the woman replies.
Pažvelgiu žemyn į mažą šunį.I look down at the little dog.
Jis žiūri į mane.It looks at me.
Einu atgal link aikštės.I walk back toward the square.
Dabar žinau, kur esu.Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
Einu atgal ramia gatve.I walk back along the quiet street.
Einu tiesiai.I walk straight ahead.
Netrukus vėl pamatau didelę aikštę.Soon I see the large square again.
Tada einu per aikštę.Then I walk across the square.
Dabar stoviu prie muziejaus.Now I am standing by the museum.
Prisimenu moters žodžius: „Sukite į dešinę.“I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
Prie muziejaus pasuku į dešinę.At the museum I turn right.
Einu plačia gatve.I walk down a wide street.
Žiūriu į priekį.I look ahead.
Pagaliau matau kažką mėlyno.Finally I see something blue.
Tai vanduo!It is the water!
Pakeliui praeinu pro mažą kavinę.On the way I walk past a little café.
Galiausiai nueinu iki pat vandens.At last I walk all the way down to the water.
Dabar esu prie uosto.Now I am at the harbour.
Uostas yra labai didelis.The harbour is very large.
Vėjas šaltas ir gaivus.The wind is cold and fresh.
Vandenyje yra daug laivų.There are many boats on the water.
Čia aš sustoju.Here I stop.
Stoviu ir žiūriu į laivus.I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
Einu tiesiai palei uostą.I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
Einu pro mažus laivelius.I walk past the small boats.
Staiga pamatau daug žmonių.Suddenly I see many people.
Jie stovi apačioje prie vandens.They stand down by the water.
Nueinu ten.I walk over there.
Pagaliau ją pamatau.Finally, I see it.
Mažoji Undinėlė sėdi ant akmens vandenyje.The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
Ji yra labai garsi statula Kopenhagoje.She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.
Figūra iš tiesų nėra labai didelė.The figure is actually not very big.
Bet bronzinė figūra saulėje yra graži.But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
Stoviu tarp kitų lankytojų.I stand among the other visitors.
Daug turistų fotografuoja statulą.Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
Aš taip pat išsitraukiu telefoną.I also take out my phone.
Padarau gražią undinėlės nuotrauką.I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
Išsiunčiu nuotrauką savo šeimai Meksikoje.I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
Mano sesuo Sofía greitai atsako.My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
„Ji labai graži“, – rašo ji."She is very pretty," she writes.
Vėl žiūriu į vandenį.I look at the water again.
walking home
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.
Vėlyva popietė.It is late in the afternoon.
Dabar einu namo į savo butą.Now I walk home to my apartment.
Einu lengvu keliu.I walk an easy way.
Tiesiog einu ilgu keliu.I just follow the long road.
Kelias yra visiškai tiesus.The road is completely straight.
Einu palei vandenį.I walk along the water.
Galiausiai nusuku nuo vandens.Finally I turn away from the water.
Dabar vėl einu į miestą.Now I walk into the city again.
Tada praeinu pro kepyklą.Then I walk past the bakery.
Vis dar kvepia kava.It still smells of coffee.
Kiek vėliau pamatau parką.A little later I see the park.
Prie parko pasuku į dešinę.At the park I turn right.
Niekieno neklausiu kelio.I do not ask anyone for directions.
Kelią randu visiškai viena.I find the way all by myself.
Kiek vėliau pamatau savo gatvę.A little later I see my own street.
Galiausiai stoviu prie savo lauko durų.Finally I stand at my front door.
Nuo durų pažvelgiu atgal į gatvę.From the door I look back down the street.
Dabar žinau kelią namo.Now I know my way home.
