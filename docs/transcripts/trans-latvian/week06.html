Week 6: Maya settles into a daily routine

morning routine
Storyboard: In the morning, Maya wakes up and gets out of bed. She steps into the bathroom, where she takes a warm shower and washes herself to wake up fully.

Ūdens ir silts un patīkams.The water is warm and nice.

Es esmu pilnībā pamodusies un gatava dienai.I am completely awake and ready for the day.
getting ready

the bus ride

at the desk

lunch with peter
