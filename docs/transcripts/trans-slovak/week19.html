Week 19: Maya starts a new project with the team

monday bus excitement
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya takes the bus to work. She feels excited and a little nervous because a large new software project starts today, and she will finally work closely with the entire team.
Je pondelok ráno a ja sedím v autobuse.It is Monday morning, and I am sitting on the bus.
Som na ceste do práce.I am on my way to work.
Pozerám sa z okna.I look out of the window.
Dnes som trochu nervózna.I am a little nervous today.
Ale tiež sa na to veľmi teším.But I am also looking forward to it a lot.
Dnes sa začína veľký nový softvérový projekt.A large, new software project starts today.
Vo firme pracujem ako softvérová inžinierka od januára.I have worked as a software engineer at the company since January.
Dobre poznám svojho kolegu Petra.I know my colleague Peter well.
Pracujeme spolu od môjho prvého dňa.We have worked together since my first day.
Ale teraz musím úzko spolupracovať s celým tímom.But now I must work closely with the whole team.
Budem spolupracovať s naším šéfom, našou dizajnérkou a naším testerom.I will work with our boss, our designer, and our tester.
Vo firme pracujú už dlho.They have worked at the company for a long time.
Autobus zastavuje a nastupujú ďalší ľudia.The bus stops, and more people get on.
Pozerám sa na hodinky.I look at my watch.
Je osem hodín.It is eight o'clock.
Ranná porada sa začína o pol hodiny.The morning meeting begins in half an hour.
Zhlboka sa nadýchnem.I take a deep breath.
Som pripravená na nový projekt.I am ready for the new project.
first morning presentation
Storyboard: At the short morning meeting, Maya stands in front of her colleagues. She presents her part of the technical plan for the first time, explaining what she has worked on to prepare for the week's tasks.
Sedíme v zasadacej miestnosti.We are sitting in the meeting room.
Je čas na našu krátku rannú poradu.It is time for our short morning meeting.
Rozhliadam sa po svojich kolegoch.I look around at my colleagues.
Dnes je pre mňa výnimočný deň.Today is a special day for me.
Postavím sa.I stand up.
Som softvérová inžinierka a budem prezentovať svoju časť projektu.I am a software engineer, and I am going to present my part of the project.
Trochu sa mi trasú ruky.My hands are shaking a little.
Ale dobre som sa pripravila.But I have prepared well.
"Dobré ráno," poviem nahlas."Good morning," I say loudly.
"Pracovala som na technickom pláne," hovorím im."I have worked on the technical plan," I tell them.
"Prečítala som všetky dokumenty.""I have read all the documents."
"A napísala som si poznámky k úlohám tohto týždňa.""And I have written notes for the week's tasks."
Vysvetľujem plán celému tímu.I explain the plan to the whole team.
Vlastne nie je ťažké hovoriť o technike.It is actually not difficult to talk about the technology.
Peter sedí oproti mne.Peter is sitting opposite me.
Cítim, že sa viac uvoľňujem.I feel myself relaxing more.
Počúva a prikyvuje.He listens and nods.
Končím svoju prezentáciu a ostatní sa usmievajú.I finish my presentation, and the others smile.
Moja prvá prezentácia v práci dopadla dobre.My first presentation at work has gone well.
boss project manager
Storyboard: After the morning meeting, her boss Thomas stops by Maya's desk with a part of the new project for her. He is warm and welcoming: he compliments her on her presentation and says the whole team has looked forward to having her on board. Maya feels genuinely welcomed onto the team.
Po rannom stretnutí sedím za svojím stolom.After the morning meeting, I sit at my desk.
Pozerám sa na svoje poznámky z prezentácie.I look at my notes from the presentation.
Môj šéf, Thomas, prichádza ku mne.My boss, Thomas, comes over to me.
Je to náš projektový manažér.He is our project manager.
Pracuje tu už mnoho rokov.He has worked here for many years.
„Dobré ráno, Maya,“ hovorí s úsmevom."Good morning, Maya," he says with a smile.
„To bola naozaj dobrá prezentácia.“"That was a really good presentation."
„Ďakujem, Thomas,“ odpovedám."Thank you, Thomas," I answer.
„Sme veľmi radi, že ťa máme v tíme,“ pokračuje Thomas."We are very happy to have you on the team," Thomas continues.
„Celý tím sa tešil na spoluprácu s tebou.“"The whole team has looked forward to working with you."
„Aj ja som sa tešila,“ hovorím radostne."I have also looked forward to it," I say happily.
„Je to zaujímavý projekt.“"It is an exciting project."
Thomas sa znova usmeje a kráča späť do svojej kancelárie.Thomas smiles again and walks back to his office.
Pozerám sa na obrazovku svojho počítača.I look at my computer screen.
Cítim sa v tíme naozaj vítaná.I feel genuinely welcome on the team.
peter software engineer
Storyboard: Maya sits down with Peter to write code. As software engineers, they solve complex problems together, and Peter jokes that he has worked on one specific error for three hours until Maya easily spots his mistake.
Sadnem si k Petrovmu stolu.I sit down at Peter's desk.
Pozeráme sa na jeho veľkú obrazovku.We look at his large screen.
Peter je tiež softvérový inžinier.Peter is also a software engineer.
Je to dobrý kolega a spolu píšeme veľa kódu.He is a good colleague, and we write a lot of code together.
Dnes vyzerá veľmi unavene.Today he looks very tired.
„Čo robíš, Peter?“ pýtam sa."What are you doing, Peter?" I ask.
„Mám veľký problém,“ odpovedá."I have a big problem," he answers.
„Pracoval som na tejto chybe tri hodiny.“"I have worked on this error for three hours."
Usmievam sa a pozorne sa pozerám na kód.I smile and look closely at the code.
V práci často spoločne riešime zložité problémy.We often solve complex problems together in our work.
Zrazu vidím na obrazovke niečo zvláštne.Suddenly I see something strange on the screen.
Je to veľmi malá chyba.It is a very small mistake.
„Skús sa pozrieť sem,“ hovorím a ukazujem na obrazovku."Try to look here," I say and point at the screen.
„Urobil si tu malý preklep.“"You have made a small typo here."
Peter sa ticho pozerá na obrazovku dve sekundy.Peter looks quietly at the screen for two seconds.
Potom sa hlasno zasmeje.Then he laughs loudly.
„Máš úplnú pravdu,“ hovorí Peter."You are absolutely right," Peter says.
„Pracoval som tri hodiny a je to len jedna malá chyba!“"I have worked for three hours, and then it is just one small error!"
Tiež sa smejem, pretože tú situáciu poznajú všetci softvéroví inžinieri.I laugh too, because all software engineers know that situation.
Opravíme chybu a program konečne funguje perfektne.We fix the error, and the program finally works perfectly.
eva digital designer
Storyboard: Later, Maya walks over to meet Eva, the team's designer. Eva shows Maya her beautiful sketches and explains that she has worked as a digital designer since she finished university, focusing on how users experience the product.
Neskôr popoludní podídem k Evinmu stolu.Later in the afternoon, I walk over to Eva's desk.
Je digitálna dizajnérka tímu.She is the team's digital designer.
Pred dneškom som s ňou nepracovala.I have not worked with her before today.
„Ahoj Maya, poď sa pozrieť,“ hovorí."Hi Maya, come and look," she says.
Ukazuje na svoju veľkú obrazovku.She points at her large screen.
Vidím niekoľko krásnych skíc nášho nového softvéru.I see some beautiful sketches of our new software.
Farby sú pokojné a dizajn je veľmi čistý.The colors are calm, and the design is very clean.
„Vyzerá to naozaj dobre,“ hovorím."That looks really good," I say.
„Ďakujem,“ odpovedá Eva s úsmevom."Thank you," Eva answers with a smile.
Vysvetľuje mi svoju prácu.She explains her work to me.
„Ako digitálna dizajnérka pracujem už od univerzity,“ hovorí."I have worked as a digital designer since university," she says.
„Vždy sa zameriavam na používateľský zážitok.“"I always focus on the user's experience."
Ukazuje mi, ako používateľ kliká v programe.She shows me how the user clicks around the program.
„Musí to byť jednoduché a logické na používanie,“ vysvetľuje."It must be easy and logical to use," she explains.
Často sa pozerám len na samotný kód.I often only look at the code itself.
Ale jej dizajn je pre projekt nesmierne dôležitý.But her design is incredibly important for the project.
Ešte trochu sa rozprávame o našich spoločných úlohách.We talk a little more about our shared tasks.
Teším sa na programovanie jej krásneho dizajnu.I look forward to programming her beautiful design.
meeting the tester
Storyboard: The next day, Maya sits with Mads, the software tester, as he clicks quickly through the new screens and spots a small mistake at once. He laughs that his job is to break things before the users do — he has done it for five years and finds problems fast.
Je ďalší deň v kancelárii.It is the next day at the office.
Sedím vedľa Madsa, nášho softvérového testera.I am sitting next to Mads, our software tester.
Pozerá na nové obrazovky.He looks at the new screens.
Veľmi rýchlo kliká myšou.He clicks very fast with the mouse.
Zrazu sa zastaví.Suddenly he stops.
Už našiel malú chybu.He has already found a small mistake.
Pozerá sa na mňa a trochu sa smeje.He looks at me and laughs a little.
"Mojou prácou je kaziť veci," hovorí."My job is to break things," he says.
"Musím nájsť chyby pred našimi používateľmi.""I have to find the mistakes before our users."
Pracuje ako tester päť rokov.He has worked as a tester for five years.
Testoval mnoho rôznych projektov.He has tested many different projects.
Preto nachádza problémy tak rýchlo.That is why he finds the problems so fast.
Pozerám sa na chybu na svojom počítači.I look at the mistake on my computer.
Našťastie je to len malý problém.Fortunately, it is only a small problem.
Kód hneď opravím.I fix the code right away.
Ešte raz otestuje program.He tests the program one more time.
Teraz všetko funguje úplne perfektne.Now everything works absolutely perfectly.
Som veľmi rada za jeho pomoc.I am very glad for his help.

friday canteen lunch

Konečne je piatok a rušný týždeň je takmer za nami.It is finally Friday, and the busy week is almost over.
