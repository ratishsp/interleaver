# Week 21 — Storyboard (Week 21 — Storyboard (Maya exchanges a shirt before the monsoon))

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Comparative with **से** (इससे बड़ा); **बड़ा/छोटा**; trying on and exchanging

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_humid_saturday_shopping | After finishing her Saturday morning volunteer shift at the neighbourhood café, Maya takes the bus into town on a sticky, humid afternoon to buy a few essential clothes before the monsoon arrives. |
| 2 | 02_buying_the_raincoat | At a bustling retail garment shop, Maya speaks to a shopkeeper, politely addressing him as 'bhaiya'. She looks at several raincoats, comparing them to find one that is large and sturdy enough to withstand the heavy Mumbai rains. |
| 3 | 03_buying_the_shirt | Before leaving the shop, a bright, patterned cotton shirt catches her eye. Feeling drained from the oppressive humidity, Maya quickly buys it, collecting her paper receipt without trying the shirt on, and heads back to her flat. |
| 4 | 04_too_small_at_home | Back in her quiet flat, Maya unpacks her bags and eagerly tries on the new cotton shirt. She struggles with the shoulders and quickly realizes that the shirt is simply too small for her. |
| 5 | 05_sunday_return_trip | Deciding against a second exhausting bus commute on the same humid afternoon, Maya waits until Sunday morning. She digs the receipt out of her shopping bag and takes the bus back into town, heading straight for the garment shop. |
| 6 | 06_asking_for_exchange | Maya finds the same shopkeeper, shows him the receipt, and explains the issue with the fit. She politely asks him if she can exchange the small shirt for a size that is bigger than this one. |
| 7 | 07_the_right_fit | The shopkeeper nods and hands her the next size up. Maya steps into the shop's small trial room, tries the bigger shirt on, and sees in the mirror that it fits much better than the previous one. She comes out, tells the shopkeeper this size is right, thanks him for the easy exchange, and heads home feeling fully prepared for the changing season. |
