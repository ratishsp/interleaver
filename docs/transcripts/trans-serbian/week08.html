Week 8: Maya explores the city

Serbian with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
Subota je ujutro.It is Saturday morning.
Sunce sija nad gradom.The sun is shining over the city.
Silazim na ulicu.I go down to the street.
Moja komšinica, Nina, stoji ispred naše zgrade.My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
"Dobro jutro, Majo", kaže ona i smeši se."Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
"Dobro jutro, Nino", odgovaram."Good morning, Nina," I answer.
"Danas imam slobodan dan", govorim joj."I have the day off today," I tell her.
"Idem u grad.""I am heading out into the city."
"Kuda ideš?", pita Nina."Where are you going?" Nina asks.
"Idem dole do vode", kažem."I am walking down to the water," I say.
"Ali ne znam put.""But I do not know the way."
Nina pokazuje niz ulicu.Nina points down the street.
"Srećom, vrlo je lako", kaže ona."Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
"Idi pravo ovuda.""Go straight ahead here."
"I skreni levo kod parka.""And turn left at the park."
"Onda stižeš pravo dole do luke.""Then you come right down to the harbour."
"Hvala ti puno na pomoći, Nino", kažem."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
"Nema na čemu, i srećan put", odgovara ona."You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
Mašem joj.I wave to her.
Počinjem svoju šetnju prema vodi.I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
Pratim Nininu rutu.I follow Nina's route.
Vazduh je svež i hladan.The air is fresh and cold.
Sada hodam niz glavnu ulicu.Now I walk down the main street.
Prvo samo hodam pravo.First I just walk straight ahead.
Na ulici ima mnogo saobraćaja.There is a lot of traffic on the street.
Usput vidim tri žuta bicikla.On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
Bicikli brzo prolaze pored mene.The bicycles ride quickly past me.
Nakon toga hodam dalje napred.After that I walk further ahead.
Zatim dolazim do velikog parka.Then I come to a large park.
Sada se sećam Nininih reči.Now I remember Nina's words.
"Skreni levo kod parka", kaže ona."Turn left at the park," she says.
Ovde skrećem levo.Here I turn left.
Malo kasnije vidim otvorena vrata.A little later I see an open door.
To je mala pekara.It is a small bakery.
Oseća se divan miris sveže kafe.There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
Obožavam topao hleb.I love warm bread.
Danas je grad divan.Today the city is lovely.
Posle toga hodam dalje prema vodi.Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
Stižem na veliki trg.I arrive at a large square.
Ima mnogo ljudi.There are many people.
Ali ne vidim vodu.But I do not see the water.
Tražim luku.I am looking for the harbour.
Vidim starijeg čoveka na trotoaru.I see an older man on the pavement.
Zaustavljam ga.I stop him.
"Izvinite", kažem."Excuse me," I say.
"Gde je luka?""Where is the harbour?"
Stariji čovek klima glavom.The older man nods.
Pokazuje napred.He points ahead.
"Idite pravo preko trga", kaže on."Walk straight across the square," he says.
"Prođite pored muzeja.""Go past the museum."
"I skrenite desno.""And turn right."
Gledam preko trga.I look across the square.
Vidim veliku zgradu.I see a large building.
To je muzej.It is the museum.
"Mnogo vam hvala", kažem."Thank you very much," I say.
"Nema na čemu", odgovara on."You are welcome," he replies.
Nastavljam dalje.I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
Hodam preko velikog trga.I walk across the large square.
Odjednom čujem muziku.Suddenly, I hear music.
Na uglu stoji ulični muzičar.On the corner stands a street musician.
On svira gitaru.He plays the guitar.
Slušam dobru muziku.I listen to the good music.
Dugo ga gledam.I look at him for a long time.
Zatim nastavljam dalje.Then I walk on.
Zaboravljam na muzej.I forget the museum.
Takođe zaboravljam na svoju rutu.I also forget my route.
Umesto toga, idem niz usku sporednu ulicu.Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
Ovde je veoma tiho.It is very quiet here.
Svuda unaokolo vidim samo neke male kuće.All around I only see some small houses.
Zatim stajem.Then I stop.
Gde je muzej?Where is the museum?
Sada se osvrćem oko sebe.Now I look around.
Ne mogu više da ga vidim.I cannot see it anymore.
Brzo pogledam unazad uz ulicu.I quickly look back up the street.
Ne mogu da vidim ni trg.I cannot see the square either.
Potpuno sam se izgubila.I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
Stojim u tihoj ulici.I am standing on a quiet street.
Izgubila sam se.I am lost.
Ispred sebe vidim mladu ženu.Ahead of me I see a young woman.
Ona šeta malog psa.She is walking a small dog.
Pas liči na psa moje porodice, Lolu.The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
Prilazim ženi.I walk over to the woman.
"Izvinite," kažem."Excuse me," I say.
"Tražim luku.""I am looking for the harbour."
Žena zastaje i sluša.The woman stops and listens.
Ona pokazuje nazad uz ulicu.She points back up the street.
"Idite pravo nazad do trga," kaže ona."Go straight back to the square," she says.
"I skrenite desno kod muzeja.""And turn right at the museum."
"Hvala vam puno na pomoći," kažem."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Nema na čemu," odgovara žena."You are welcome," the woman replies.
Gledam dole u malog psa.I look down at the little dog.
On me gleda.It looks at me.
Idem nazad prema trgu.I walk back toward the square.
Sada znam gde sam.Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
Vraćam se tihom ulicom.I walk back along the quiet street.
Idem pravo.I walk straight ahead.
Uskoro ponovo vidim veliki trg.Soon I see the large square again.
Zatim prelazim preko trga.Then I walk across the square.
Sada stojim kod muzeja.Now I am standing by the museum.
Sećam se ženinih reči: "Skrenite desno."I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
Kod muzeja skrećem desno.At the museum I turn right.
Idem niz široku ulicu.I walk down a wide street.
Gledam napred.I look ahead.
Konačno vidim nešto plavo.Finally I see something blue.
To je voda!It is the water!
Usput prolazim pored malog kafića.On the way I walk past a little café.
Na kraju idem skroz dole do vode.At last I walk all the way down to the water.
Sada sam u luci.Now I am at the harbour.
Luka je veoma velika.The harbour is very large.
Vetar je hladan i svež.The wind is cold and fresh.
Na vodi ima mnogo čamaca.There are many boats on the water.
Ovde stajem.Here I stop.
Stojim i gledam čamce.I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
Idem pravo duž luke.I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
Prolazim pored malih čamaca.I walk past the small boats.
Odjednom vidim mnogo ljudi.Suddenly I see many people.
Stoje dole pored vode.They stand down by the water.
Idem tamo.I walk over there.
Konačno je vidim.Finally, I see it.
Mala sirena sedi na kamenu u vodi.The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
Ona je veoma poznata statua u Kopenhagenu.She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.
Figura zapravo nije naročito velika.The figure is actually not very big.
Ali bronzana figura je prelepa na suncu.But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
Stojim među ostalim posetiocima.I stand among the other visitors.
Mnogi turisti fotografišu statuu.Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
Takođe vadim svoj telefon.I also take out my phone.
Pravim prelepu sliku sirene.I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
Šaljem sliku svojoj porodici u Meksiku.I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
Moja sestra, Sofija, brzo odgovara.My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
"Veoma je lepa", piše ona."She is very pretty," she writes.
Ponovo gledam u vodu.I look at the water again.
walking home
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.
Kasno je popodne.It is late in the afternoon.
Sada idem kući u svoj stan.Now I walk home to my apartment.
Idem jednostavnim putem.I walk an easy way.
Samo pratim dugački put.I just follow the long road.
Put je potpuno prav.The road is completely straight.
Hodam pored vode.I walk along the water.
Na kraju skrećem od vode.Finally I turn away from the water.
Sada ponovo idem u grad.Now I walk into the city again.
Zatim prolazim pored pekare.Then I walk past the bakery.
Još uvek miriše na kafu.It still smells of coffee.
Malo kasnije vidim park.A little later I see the park.
Kod parka skrećem desno.At the park I turn right.
Ne pitam nikoga za put.I do not ask anyone for directions.
Pronalazim put potpuno sama.I find the way all by myself.
Malo kasnije vidim svoju ulicu.A little later I see my own street.
Na kraju stojim ispred svojih ulaznih vrata.Finally I stand at my front door.
Sa vrata gledam unazad niz ulicu.From the door I look back down the street.
Sada znam svoj put do kuće.Now I know my way home.
