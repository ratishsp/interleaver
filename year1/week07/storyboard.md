# Week 7 — Storyboard (Maya plans her week)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Telling the clock; klokken; weekdays/months; frequency adverbs (altid, ofte, aldrig); V2 review · **Target:** ~30 min

The narrative beat 'Planning her week' provides a natural framework to introduce time and frequency through concrete actions. By following Maya as she maps out her schedule—from a Monday morning work sync to booking a dentist appointment and reserving the shared laundry room—the clock times ('klokken er...', 'kvart i', 'halv') and weekdays are firmly grounded in realistic tasks rather than rote lists. Frequency adverbs ('altid', 'ofte', 'sjældent') are woven into her explanations of her new Danish routines. The week closes warmly with a Sunday family call, cleverly using the time zone difference between Copenhagen and Mexico to practice contrasting clock times while maintaining connection with the recurring cast.

| # | stem | beat |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_calendar_and_morning | Maya drinks her morning tea and looks at the new physical calendar on her kitchen wall. The current month is January, but February is coming soon. The clock on the wall shows half past seven. She often plans her week on Monday mornings. (Months *januar, februar*; clock time *klokken er halv otte*; frequency *ofte*) |
| 2 | 02_monday_work_meeting | Maya sits in a bright meeting room with her software team. They always have a status meeting on Mondays at nine o'clock. She opens her laptop and checks her schedule for Tuesday and Wednesday. She usually has a lot of tasks early in the week. (Weekdays *mandag, tirsdag, onsdag*; frequency *altid, normalt*; clock *klokken ni*) |
| 3 | 03_booking_the_dentist | During her lunch break, Maya calls a local clinic to book a dentist appointment. The receptionist asks if she is free on Thursday at quarter past two. Maya checks her phone, confirms the time, and gives her phone number, 12 34 56 78. (Clock time *kvart over to*; weekday *torsdag*; placeholder phone number woven into dialogue) |
| 4 | 04_the_laundry_room | After work, Maya goes down to the apartment building's shared laundry room to reserve a washing machine. She looks at the paper schedule. She sometimes washes clothes on Fridays, but to keep her weekend free she books Thursday at half past four instead — clean clothes for the weekend. (opens by going home — it's her own building's shared laundry, not the office; clock *halv fem*; weekdays *fredag, torsdag*; frequency *nogle gange*) |
| 5 | 05_coffee_plans_with_nina | Maya meets her neighbor Nina in the hallway and asks if she wants to drink coffee on Friday afternoon. Nina explains she is rarely home on Fridays, but suggests Saturday at three o'clock. Maya smiles and agrees to the weekend plan. (Weekdays *fredag, lørdag*; frequency *sjældent*; clock *klokken tre*; recurring character) |
| 6 | 06_sunday_morning_routine | It is Sunday morning, a day for relaxation. Maya explains her weekend routine: she never sets an alarm, and she always goes for a long walk in her new neighborhood. Today she leaves the flat at quarter to eleven. (Frequency *aldrig, altid*; weekday *søndag*; clock *kvart i elleve*) |
| 7 | 07_time_zones_and_family | Maya video calls her family in Mexico. She explains the time difference: it is eight o'clock in the evening in Denmark, but it is only one o'clock in the afternoon for her parents, Sofía, and Diego. The dog Lola barks at the camera; it is lovely to see them all. (Clock times *klokken otte, klokken et*; recurring cast continuity; the week's one earned warm close — no manufactured "happy" tag) |
