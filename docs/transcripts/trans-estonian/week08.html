Week 8: Maya explores the city

Estonian with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
On laupäeva hommik.It is Saturday morning.
Päike paistab üle linna.The sun is shining over the city.
Ma lähen alla tänavale.I go down to the street.
Mu naaber Nina seisab meie maja ees.My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
"Tere hommikust, Maya," ütleb ta ja naeratab."Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
"Tere hommikust, Nina," vastan ma."Good morning, Nina," I answer.
"Mul on täna vaba päev," ütlen ma talle."I have the day off today," I tell her.
"Ma lähen linna peale.""I am heading out into the city."
"Kuhu sa lähed?" küsib Nina."Where are you going?" Nina asks.
"Ma jalutan alla vee äärde," ütlen ma."I am walking down to the water," I say.
"Aga ma ei tea teed.""But I do not know the way."
Nina osutab mööda tänavat alla.Nina points down the street.
"Õnneks on see väga lihtne," ütleb ta."Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
"Mine siit otse edasi.""Go straight ahead here."
"Ja keera pargi juurest vasakule.""And turn left at the park."
"Siis jõuad otse alla sadamasse.""Then you come right down to the harbour."
"Suur aitäh abi eest, Nina," ütlen ma."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
"Võta heaks ja head teed," vastab ta."You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
Ma lehvitan talle.I wave to her.
Ma alustan oma jalutuskäiku vee poole.I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
Ma järgin Nina marsruuti.I follow Nina's route.
Õhk on värske ja külm.The air is fresh and cold.
Nüüd kõnnin ma mööda peatänavat.Now I walk down the main street.
Kõigepealt kõnnin ma lihtsalt otse edasi.First I just walk straight ahead.
Tänaval on palju liiklust.There is a lot of traffic on the street.
Teel näen ma kolme kollast jalgratast.On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
Jalgrattad sõidavad minust kiiresti mööda.The bicycles ride quickly past me.
Seejärel kõnnin ma veel edasi.After that I walk further ahead.
Siis jõuan ma suure pargini.Then I come to a large park.
Nüüd meenuvad mulle Nina sõnad.Now I remember Nina's words.
"Pööra pargi juures vasakule," ütleb ta."Turn left at the park," she says.
Siin pööran ma vasakule.Here I turn left.
Veidi hiljem näen ma lahtist ust.A little later I see an open door.
See on väike pagariäri.It is a small bakery.
Seal on meeldiv värske kohvi lõhn.There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
Ma armastan sooja leiba.I love warm bread.
Täna on linn imeilus.Today the city is lovely.
Pärast seda kõnnin ma edasi vee poole.Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
Ma jõuan suurele väljakule.I arrive at a large square.
Seal on palju inimesi.There are many people.
Aga ma ei näe vett.But I do not see the water.
Ma otsin sadamat.I am looking for the harbour.
Ma näen kõnniteel vanemat meest.I see an older man on the pavement.
Ma peatan ta.I stop him.
"Vabandage," ütlen ma."Excuse me," I say.
"Kus on sadam?""Where is the harbour?"
Vanem mees noogutab.The older man nods.
Ta osutab ettepoole.He points ahead.
"Minge otse üle väljaku," ütleb ta."Walk straight across the square," he says.
"Minge muuseumist mööda.""Go past the museum."
"Ja pöörake paremale.""And turn right."
Ma vaatan üle väljaku.I look across the square.
Ma näen suurt hoonet.I see a large building.
See on muuseum.It is the museum.
"Suur tänu," ütlen ma."Thank you very much," I say.
"Palun," vastab ta."You are welcome," he replies.
Ma kõnnin edasi.I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
Ma kõnnin üle suure väljaku.I walk across the large square.
Järsku kuulen ma muusikat.Suddenly, I hear music.
Nurgal seisab tänavamuusik.On the corner stands a street musician.
Ta mängib kitarri.He plays the guitar.
Ma kuulan head muusikat.I listen to the good music.
Ma vaatan teda kaua.I look at him for a long time.
Siis kõnnin ma edasi.Then I walk on.
Ma unustan muuseumi.I forget the museum.
Ma unustan ka oma marsruudi.I also forget my route.
Selle asemel kõnnin mööda kitsast kõrvaltänavat.Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
Siin on väga vaikne.It is very quiet here.
Ümberringi näen ma ainult mõningaid väikseid maju.All around I only see some small houses.
Siis jään ma seisma.Then I stop.
Kus on muuseum?Where is the museum?
Nüüd vaatan ma ringi.Now I look around.
Ma ei näe seda enam.I cannot see it anymore.
Ma vaatan kiiresti mööda tänavat tagasi.I quickly look back up the street.
Ma ei näe ka väljakut.I cannot see the square either.
Ma olen täiesti ära eksinud.I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
Ma seisan vaiksel tänaval.I am standing on a quiet street.
Ma olen ära eksinud.I am lost.
Näen enda ees noort naist.Ahead of me I see a young woman.
Ta jalutab väikese koeraga.She is walking a small dog.
Koer näeb välja nagu mu pere koer Lola.The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
Ma kõnnin naise juurde.I walk over to the woman.
"Vabandage," ütlen ma."Excuse me," I say.
"Ma otsin sadamat.""I am looking for the harbour."
Naine peatub ja kuulab.The woman stops and listens.
Ta osutab tagasi mööda tänavat.She points back up the street.
"Minge otse tagasi väljakule," ütleb ta."Go straight back to the square," she says.
"Ja pöörake muuseumi juurest paremale.""And turn right at the museum."
"Suur tänu abi eest," ütlen ma."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Võtke heaks," vastab naine."You are welcome," the woman replies.
Ma vaatan alla väikesele koerale.I look down at the little dog.
Ta vaatab mind.It looks at me.
Ma kõnnin tagasi väljaku poole.I walk back toward the square.
Nüüd ma tean, kus ma olen.Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
Ma kõnnin tagasi mööda vaikset tänavat.I walk back along the quiet street.
Ma kõnnin otse edasi.I walk straight ahead.
Varsti näen ma jälle suurt väljakut.Soon I see the large square again.
Siis kõnnin ma üle väljaku.Then I walk across the square.
Nüüd seisan ma muuseumi juures.Now I am standing by the museum.
Ma mäletan naise sõnu: "Pööra paremale."I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
Muuseumi juures pööran ma paremale.At the museum I turn right.
Ma kõnnin mööda laia tänavat alla.I walk down a wide street.
Ma vaatan ettepoole.I look ahead.
Lõpuks näen ma midagi sinist.Finally I see something blue.
See on vesi!It is the water!
Teel kõnnin ma mööda väikesest kohvikust.On the way I walk past a little café.
Viimaks kõnnin ma päris alla vee äärde.At last I walk all the way down to the water.
Nüüd olen ma sadamas.Now I am at the harbour.
Sadam on väga suur.The harbour is very large.
Tuul on külm ja värske.The wind is cold and fresh.
Vee peal on palju paate.There are many boats on the water.
Siin ma peatun.Here I stop.
Ma seisan ja vaatan paate.I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
Ma kõnnin otse edasi mööda sadamat.I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
Ma kõnnin väikestest paatidest mööda.I walk past the small boats.
Järsku näen ma palju inimesi.Suddenly I see many people.
Nad seisavad all vee ääres.They stand down by the water.
Ma kõnnin sinna.I walk over there.
Lõpuks näen ma seda.Finally, I see it.
Väike merineitsi istub vees kivi peal.The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
Ta on väga kuulus kuju Kopenhaagenis.She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.
Kuju ei ole tegelikult väga suur.The figure is actually not very big.
Aga pronkskuju on päikese käes ilus.But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
Ma seisan teiste külastajate hulgas.I stand among the other visitors.
Paljud turistid teevad kujust pilte.Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
Ma võtan ka oma telefoni välja.I also take out my phone.
Ma teen merineitsist ilusa pildi.I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
Ma saadan pildi oma perele Mehhikos.I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
Minu õde Sofía vastab kiiresti.My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
"Ta on väga kena," kirjutab ta."She is very pretty," she writes.
Ma vaatan uuesti vett.I look at the water again.
walking home
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.
On hiline pärastlõuna.It is late in the afternoon.
Nüüd kõnnin ma koju oma korterisse.Now I walk home to my apartment.
Ma kõnnin lihtsat teed pidi.I walk an easy way.
Ma lihtsalt kõnnin mööda pikka teed.I just follow the long road.
Tee on täiesti sirge.The road is completely straight.
Ma kõnnin mööda veeäärt.I walk along the water.
Lõpuks pööran ma vee äärest eemale.Finally I turn away from the water.
Nüüd kõnnin ma jälle linna.Now I walk into the city again.
Siis kõnnin ma mööda pagariärist.Then I walk past the bakery.
Seal lõhnab ikka veel kohvi järele.It still smells of coffee.
Veidi hiljem näen ma parki.A little later I see the park.
Pargi juures pööran ma paremale.At the park I turn right.
Ma ei küsi kelleltki teed.I do not ask anyone for directions.
Ma leian tee täiesti ise.I find the way all by myself.
Veidi hiljem näen ma oma tänavat.A little later I see my own street.
Lõpuks seisan ma oma välisukse juures.Finally I stand at my front door.
Ukse juurest vaatan ma tagasi piki tänavat.From the door I look back down the street.
Nüüd ma tean oma koduteed.Now I know my way home.
