Week 25: A Sunday in the city

morning calendar
Storyboard: On Sunday morning, Maya makes a fresh pot of coffee and flips her wall calendar to July. She realizes that she moved to Copenhagen exactly six months ago, and decides to celebrate by spending this sunny day exploring the city at her own pace.
Nedjeljno je jutro.It is Sunday morning.
Sunce sja kroz prozor u mom stanu.The sun is shining in through the window in my apartment.
Odlazim u kuhinju.I go out into the kitchen.
Kuham lončić svježe kave.I make a pot of fresh coffee.
Miris kave ovog je jutra fantastičan.The smell of coffee is fantastic this morning.
Imam kalendar na zidu.I have a calendar on the wall.
Okrećem stranicu na sljedeći mjesec.I turn the page to the next month.
Sada je srpanj.It is July now.
Preselila sam se u Kopenhagen u siječnju.I moved to Copenhagen in January.
Došla sam ovamo prije točno šest mjeseci.I came here exactly six months ago.
Vrijeme stvarno brzo prolazi.Time really goes fast.
Moj prvi dan u ovom gradu bio je jako hladan.My first day in this city was very cold.
Ali danas je vrijeme toplo i lijepo.But today the weather is warm and beautiful.
Želim proslaviti ovu malu obljetnicu.I want to celebrate this little anniversary.
Danas nemam fiksnih planova.I have no fixed plans today.
Uzimam svoju šalicu kave i gledam na ulicu.I take my coffee cup and look out over the street.
Želim izaći i istražiti grad svojim tempom.I want to go out and explore the city at my own pace.
Veselim se uživanju u ovim svijetlim satima.I look forward to enjoying these bright hours.
ceramic plates
Storyboard: Maya wanders into a lively second-hand market in a nearby square. She spots two blue ceramic plates on a table, decides these plates are perfect for her kitchen, and buys them from the vendor for forty kroner.
Idem u šetnju preko velikog trga u gradu.I go for a walk across a large square in the city.
Ovdje je svake nedjelje veliki buvljak.Here there is a large flea market every Sunday.
Sunce sja i mnogo ljudi šeta između stolova.The sun is shining, and many people are walking around between the tables.
Zaustavljam se kod malog stola s mnogo različitih stvari.I stop by a small table with many different things.
Iza stola stoji ljubazan prodavač.Behind the table stands a friendly vendor.
Odjednom ugledam dva plava keramička tanjura.Suddenly I see two blue ceramic plates.
Uzimam ih i pažljivo ih razgledavam.I pick them up and look closely at them.
"Koliko koštaju ovi tanjuri?" pitam prodavača."How much do these plates cost?" I ask the vendor.
Prodavač mi se nasmiješi.The vendor smiles at me.
"Koštaju samo četrdeset kruna", odgovara."They cost only forty kroner," he replies.
To je stvarno dobra cijena.That is a really good price.
Kupila sam mali stol za svoju kuhinju prije mjesec dana.I bought a small table for my kitchen a month ago.
Ovi tanjuri će se savršeno uklopiti uz ovaj stol.These plates will fit perfectly with this table.
"Vrlo rado bih ih kupila", kažem."I would very much like to buy them," I say.
Dajem prodavaču četrdeset kruna i pažljivo stavljam tanjure u svoju torbu.I give the vendor forty kroner and carefully put the plates in my bag.
Uvijek je lijepo pronaći nešto posebno u mirnu nedjelju.It is always nice to find something special on a quiet Sunday.
winter coat
Storyboard: At a clothing stall, Maya tries on a heavy wool winter coat. She immediately takes it off because this coat is far too warm for summer, laughing with the vendor that she desperately needed something exactly like it five months ago.
Hodam dalje po tržnici i zaustavljam se kod štanda s odjećom.I walk further into the market and stop at a clothing stall.
Vidim teški zimski kaput od vune.I see a heavy winter coat made of wool.
Ovaj kaput je predivan, ali vrlo debeo.This coat is beautiful, but very thick.
Ipak isprobavam kaput.I try the coat on anyway.
Nevjerojatno je topao.It is incredibly warm.
Odmah ga ponovno skidam.I take it off again immediately.
Srpanj je i danas sja sunce.It is July, and the sun is shining today.
Ljubazna žena stoji iza stola i smiješi se.A friendly woman stands behind the table and smiles.
"Je li pretopao?" pita žena."Is it too warm?" the woman asks.
"Da, malo je pretopao za ljeto", odgovaram i smijem se."Yes, it is a bit too warm for the summer," I reply and laugh.
Dodirujem debelu tkaninu.I touch the thick fabric.
"Ali ovaj zimski kaput mi je stvarno trebao prije pet mjeseci!" kažem."But I really needed this winter coat five months ago!" I say.
Žena se glasno smije.The woman laughs loudly.
"Da, veljača je bila jako hladna", kaže ona."Yes, February was very cold," she says.
Sjećam se svojih prvih tjedana u Kopenhagenu.I remember my first weeks in Copenhagen.
Tada sam se stalno smrzavala.I was constantly freezing back then.
Srećom, trenutno mi ne treba tako topla odjeća.Fortunately, I do not need such warm clothes right now.
Vješam kaput natrag na stalak i kažem doviđenja.I hang the coat back on the rack and say goodbye.
museum ticket
Storyboard: Moving on, Maya enters a local history museum and goes to the front desk. She asks the clerk if this entrance ticket includes the special upstairs exhibition, and the clerk tells her to hurry because the guided tour started ten minutes ago.
Odlazim s tržnice i pronalazim mali, lokalni muzej.I walk away from the market and find a small, local museum.
Ulazim u tihu zgradu.I walk into the quiet building.
Prilazim pultu da kupim ulaznicu.I walk over to the counter to buy an entrance ticket.
Mladić stoji za blagajnom.A young man is standing behind the register.
"Bok, jednu ulaznicu za odrasle, molim vas," kažem."Hi, one adult ticket, please," I say.
"To će biti sto kruna," kaže on i ispisuje je."That will be one hundred kroner," he says and prints it.
Plaćam i gledam ove šarene brošure na pultu.I pay and look at these colorful brochures on the counter.
Reklamiraju posebnu izložbu o povijesti grada.They advertise a special exhibition about the history of the city.
"Omogućuje li ova ulaznica i pristup izložbi na katu?" pitam."Does this ticket also give access to the exhibition upstairs?" I ask.
"Da, ovaj muzej ima samo jednu jedinstvenu ulaznicu," odgovara on."Yes, this museum only has one combined ticket," he answers.
"Izložba je gore na prvom katu," kaže on."The exhibition is upstairs on the first floor," he says.
"Slobodno si tamo gore uzmite vremena.""You can take your time up there."
"Ima mnogo starih fotografija grada," objašnjava on."It has many old photographs of the city," he explains.
"Neke od njih stare su više od sto godina.""Some of them are over a hundred years old."
"To zvuči jako uzbudljivo," kažem."That sounds really exciting," I say.
"Puno vam hvala," nasmiješim se."Thank you very much," I smile.
Uzimam svoju ulaznicu i mirno se penjem uz stepenice.I take my ticket and walk calmly up the stairs.
harbor photograph
Storyboard: Upstairs in the gallery, Maya studies a massive black-and-white photograph of ships and factories. The caption explains that this harbor area was a loud, dirty industrial zone a hundred years ago, contrasting sharply with the modern waterfront she knows.
Idem gore na izložbu.I walk upstairs to the exhibition.
Ovdje ima mnogo starih slika.There are many old pictures here.
Zaustavljam se ispred vrlo velike crno-bijele fotografije.I stop in front of a very large black-and-white photograph.
Ova slika je nevjerojatno fascinantna.This picture is incredibly fascinating.
Prikazuje užurbanu luku s velikim tvornicama.It shows a busy harbor with large factories.
Također mogu vidjeti mnogo visokih dimnjaka i tamnih brodova.I can also see many tall chimneys and dark ships.
Ovi brodovi izgledaju jako teški.These ships look very heavy.
Čitam mali tekst pored fotografije.I read the small text next to the photograph.
Tekst objašnjava povijest ove luke.The text explains the history of this harbor.
Prije sto godina, ovo područje bilo je industrijska zona.A hundred years ago, the area was an industrial zone.
Tada je to bilo bučno i prljavo mjesto.It was a loud and dirty place back then.
Kontrast u odnosu na danas je ogroman.The contrast to today is enormous.
Sada je ova obala tiha i čista.Now, this waterfront is quiet and clean.
Često idem u šetnju uz vodu vikendom.I often go for a walk along the water on the weekends.
Teško je zamisliti stare tvornice ovdje.It is hard to imagine the old factories here.
Dugo gledam ovu fotografiju.I look at this photograph for a long time.
Zatim polako nastavljam dalje u sljedeću sobu.Then I slowly walk on into the next room.
texting sofia
Storyboard: Taking a break in the museum café, Maya takes a picture of the plates she bought and texts it to her sister Sofía. Sofía replies immediately, sending back an old photo to remind Maya that they explored a similar flea market in Mexico exactly two years ago.
Sjedim u muzejskom kafiću i pravim pauzu.I am sitting in the museum's café and taking a break.
Pijem šalicu kave i opuštam se.I drink a cup of coffee and relax.
Vadim plave tanjure iz svoje torbe.I take the blue plates out of my bag.
Ovi se tanjuri savršeno uklapaju u moju kuhinju.These plates fit perfectly in my kitchen.
Fotografiram ih svojim mobitelom.I take a picture of them with my phone.
Šaljem sliku svojoj sestri, Sofíji.I send the picture to my sister, Sofía.
"Samo pogledaj ove predivne tanjure", pišem."Just look at these beautiful plates," I write.
"Našla sam ih na tržnici prije sat vremena.""I found them at a market an hour ago."
Sofía mi odgovara gotovo odmah.Sofía answers me almost immediately.
Šalje mi natrag staru sliku.She sends an old picture back to me.
Ova me poruka jako veseli.This message makes me very happy.
Slika prikazuje nas dvije na velikom buvljaku u Meksiku.The picture shows the two of us at a large flea market in Mexico.
"Sjećaš li se ove tržnice?""Do you remember this market?"
Pita Sofía u sljedećoj poruci.Sofía asks in the next message.
"Bile smo tamo prije točno dvije godine!", piše ona."We were there exactly two years ago!" she writes.
Gledam naša sretna lica na slici.I look at our happy faces in the picture.

Prije dvije godine još uvijek sam živjela u Meksiku.Two years ago, I still lived in Mexico.
Smiješim se i u odgovoru pišem veselu poruku.I smile and write a happy message back.
seagull encounter
Storyboard: Walking home through a large park, Maya stops at a kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream. As she strolls along the pond, a bold seagull suddenly swoops down, forcing her to quickly duck and shield this dessert with her hand to prevent the bird from stealing it.
Idem kući kroz veliki park.I walk home through a large park.
Prije sat vremena bila sam u muzeju.An hour ago I was at a museum.
Sada samo želim uživati u lijepom vremenu.Now I just want to enjoy the good weather.
Zaustavljam se na malom kiosku kako bih kupila sladoled u kornetu.I stop at a small kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream.
"Izvolite, evo vašeg sladoleda," kaže muškarac u kiosku."Here you go, here is your ice cream," the man in the kiosk says.
"Puno vam hvala," odgovaram i uzimam sladoled."Thank you very much," I reply and take the ice cream.
Polako hodam uz jezero u parku.I walk slowly along the lake in the park.
Ovaj sladoled u kornetu izgleda jako dobro.This waffle ice cream looks really good.
Odjednom čujem glasan zvuk iznad sebe.Suddenly I hear a loud noise above me.
Veliki galeb obrušava se ravno prema meni.A large seagull dives right down towards me.
Želi mi ukrasti sladoled!It wants to steal my ice cream!
Brzo se sagnem i zaštitim sladoled rukom.I duck quickly and shield my ice cream with my hand.
Ptica mi neće ukrasti hranu.The bird is not going to steal my food.
Galeb proleti i sleti na travu.The seagull flies past and lands on the grass.
Žena na klupi se smije.A woman on a bench laughs.
"Danas su jako drski," kaže ona."They are very cheeky today," she says.
"Da, itekako jesu," uzvraćam smijehom."Yes, they certainly are," I laugh back.
Brzo dovršavam svoj sladoled dok hodam dalje.I quickly finish eating my ice cream while I walk on.
unpacking plates
Storyboard: Back in her apartment that evening, Maya washes these newly bought plates in the sink and places them in the cupboard. She looks out the window at the lingering summer sunset, entirely content with this quiet Sunday and deeply settled in the life she started half a year ago.
Ponovno sam u svom stanu.I am back in my apartment.
Nedjelja je navečer i ovdje je vrlo tiho.It is Sunday evening, and it is very quiet here.

Ovi su tanjuri stvarno predivni.These plates are really beautiful.

Sutra je ponedjeljak i idem autobusom na posao.Tomorrow is Monday, and I take the bus to work.

Potpuno sam zadovoljna svojim životom ovdje.I am completely content with my life here.
