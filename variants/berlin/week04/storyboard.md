# Week 4 — Storyboard (Moving into the new flat)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Prepositions of place with the dative (**in, auf, unter, neben** + *dem/der*); the placement verbs **stehen/liegen/hängen**; the definite articles **der/die/das**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_temporary_room_departure | Maya packs her last few belongings in her temporary room. She places her laptop carefully into her backpack and zips it up. She pulls her suitcase out into the hallway, ready for the short journey through the building to her permanent home. |
| 2 | 02_hallway_help | Maya drags the suitcase along the corridor. Hearing the noise, her neighbor Sabine Krüger opens her door. Frau Krüger takes the other end and helps her carry it to the door of the new flat. |
| 3 | 03_opening_the_door | Maya turns the key and unlocks the door to her new home—the flat. She steps inside and looks up at the high ceilings and the windows onto the courtyard. Frau Krüger wishes her a successful move and returns to her own flat, leaving Maya to settle in. |
| 4 | 04_hallway_inventory | Maya pulls the suitcase inside and closes the door. She takes stock of the small furnished hallway, noting where things stand and hang. A mirror hangs next to the door, and a shoe rack stands under the coat hooks. |
| 5 | 05_setting_up_the_desk | Moving into the main living space, Maya finishes unpacking her work equipment. Now, her laptop stands on the table. Her books lie next to it. |
| 6 | 06_bedroom_suitcases | Maya tackles her clothes in the small bedroom. After some organizing, her suitcase lies open on the bed, her shirts hang in the wardrobe, and her shoes stand in a row under the bed. |
| 7 | 07_finding_the_charger | Maya wants to play some music while she works, but her phone has a low battery. She searches for her charger, finally finding it lying hidden in her backpack, tucked under a thick winter sweater. |
| 8 | 08_coffee_break | Needing a quick rest, Maya takes a break in her new kitchen. She takes the thermos she filled that morning, pours a cup of coffee and stands at the window looking down into the courtyard. |
| 9 | 09_housewarming_plant | A polite knock interrupts the quiet. Frau Krüger stands at the door with a potted plant as a housewarming gift. Maya thanks her, and they put the plant on the windowsill. |
