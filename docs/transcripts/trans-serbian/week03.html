Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

Serbian with English gloss, line-aligned.

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
Januar je, i veoma je hladno.It is January, and it is very cold.
Idem peške kući sa autobuske stanice u Kopenhagenu.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Vidim cvećaru na ulici.I see a flower shop on the street.
Unutra je svetlo i toplo.It is bright and warm inside.
Ulazim u toplu prodavnicu.I go into the warm shop.
Gledam malu, zelenu biljku.I look at a small, green plant.
Prodavac mi se osmehuje.A clerk smiles at me.
"Zdravo", kaže on."Hello," he says.
"Zdravo", kažem ja."Hello," I say.
"Tražim biljku.""I am looking for a plant."
"Da li je to poklon?", pita on."Is it a gift?" he asks.
"Ne, to je za moju sobu", odgovaram."No, it is for my room," I answer.
"To je stvarno dobra biljka", kaže on."It is a really good plant," he says.
"Koliko košta?", pitam."How much does it cost?" I ask.
"Košta pedeset pet kruna", odgovara on."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
Plaćam svoju novu biljku.I pay for my new plant.
On stavlja biljku u papirnu kesu.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
"Izvolite, evo vaše kese", kaže on."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
"Hvala puno", kažem radosno."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Izlazim u hladno veče.I go out into the cold evening.
Moja mala biljka me čini veoma srećnom.My little plant makes me very happy.
evening prep
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her temporary room and sets the new plant on a small desk. With it already being pitch black outside, she turns on a warm lamp to reflect against the dark window, makes a cup of tea, and flips open her laptop to dial home.
Sada sam kod kuće.I am home now.
Ovde je moja soba.Here is my room.
To je mala soba.It is a small room.
Imam novu biljku.I have a new plant.
Stoji na mom radnom stolu.It stands on my desk.
Veče je, i januar je.It is evening, and it is January.
Moj prozor je veoma mračan.My window is very dark.
Napolju je potpuno crno.It is completely black outside.
Imam lampu i šolju čaja.I have a lamp and a cup of tea.
Palim svoju lampu.I turn on my lamp.
Sada je moja soba topla i svetla.Now my room is warm and bright.
Moj čaj je takođe topao.My tea is also warm.
Sedim za svojim radnim stolom.I sit at my desk.
Imam svoj kompjuter ovde.I have my computer here.
Otvaram svoj kompjuter.I open my computer.
Sada zovem svoju porodicu u Meksiku.Now I call my family in Mexico.
Radujem se što ću ih videti.I look forward to seeing them.
parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
Zvoni par puta.It rings a couple of times.
Ekran zasvetli.The screen lights up.
Vidim dnevnu sobu.I see a living room.
Ima sunca i dnevna soba je veoma svetla.There is sun, and the living room is very bright.
Vidim dve osobe.I see two people.
To su moja majka i moj otac.It is my mother and my father.
Mnogo se smeše i mašu mi.They smile a lot and wave to me.
"Zdravo, mama i tata!" kažem glasno."Hello mom and dad!" I say loudly.
"Zdravo, Majo", kaže moj otac."Hello, Maya," my father says.
"Kako ide?""How is it going?"
"Dobro ide", odgovaram radosno."It is going well," I answer happily.
Odjednom čujem zvuk.Suddenly I hear a sound.
To je mali lavež.It is a little bark.
"Oh, čujem psa!" kažem i smejem se."Oh, I hear a dog!" I say and laugh.
Mali pas skače na sofu.A little dog jumps up onto a sofa.
Gleda u kameru.It looks into the camera.
"Evo mog psa!""There is my dog!"
"Zdravo, Lolo", kažem."Hello, Lola," I say.
Lola maše repom.Lola wags her tail.
"Da, to je tvoj pas", kaže moja majka uz osmeh."Yes, it is your dog," my mother says with a smile.
Volim svoju porodicu.I love my family.
Divno je videti ih sve.It is lovely to see them all.
the siblings
Storyboard: Her younger sister Sofía and older brother Diego squeeze into the frame, playfully shoving each other. Sofía proudly holds up a thick university textbook, while Diego jokingly asks Maya if she is surviving the freezing Danish winter.
Sada su dva nova lica na mom ekranu.Now there are two new faces on my screen.
To su moja mlađa sestra, Sofija, i moj stariji brat, Dijego.It is my little sister, Sofía, and my big brother, Diego.
Smeju se i malo se guraju.They laugh and push each other a little.
„Zdravo, mlađa sestro i stariji brate!“ radosno kažem."Hello little sister and big brother!" I say happily.
„Ćao, Majo“, kaže Dijego."Hi Maya," says Diego.

„Da li je to hladna zima?“"Is it a cold winter?"
„Da, veoma je hladna zima“, smejem se."Yes, it is a very cold winter," I laugh.
„Ali imam toplu sobu i šolju čaja.“"But I have a warm room and a cup of tea."

„Da li to imaš novu knjigu, Sofija?“ pitam."Do you have a new book there, Sofía?" I ask.
„Da, to je moja nova knjiga“, ponosno kaže moja sestra."Yes, it is my new book," my sister says proudly.
„To je knjiga sa mog univerziteta.“"It is a book from my university."
„To je veoma debela knjiga!“"It is a very thick book!"
Smešim se svom bratu i svojoj sestri.I smile at my brother and my sister.

"To je veoma lepa biljka," kaže moja majka."It is a very nice plant," my mother says.

mexico garden
