# Week 23 — Storyboard (Maya visits Priya for dinner)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Future (**लृट्** — करिष्यामि) and "let's" (**कुर्मः / गच्छामः**); reporting thoughts with **इति** (अहं चिन्तयामि … इति)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_the_dinner_invitation | At the office, Priya stops by Maya's desk and invites her over for dinner this Saturday. Priya promises she will cook some special Maharashtrian dishes, and Maya eagerly accepts, saying she will definitely come. |
| 2 | 02_planning_the_visit | During a coffee break, Maya talks through the Saturday plans with Rohan. She mentions that she thinks that taking the metro will be the easiest way to reach Priya's neighbourhood. When Maya wonders aloud what to bring as a guest, Rohan suggests a box of traditional Indian sweets, and she decides she will buy Kaju Katli. |
| 3 | 03_buying_sweets | On Saturday evening, Maya stops at a bright local mithai shop. She addresses the shopkeeper as 'bhaiya' and asks him to pack half a kilo of Kaju Katli in a nice box, paying him five hundred rupees before heading to the station. |
| 4 | 04_the_metro_ride | Maya navigates the metro system effortlessly. Sitting in the cool, air-conditioned coach, she watches the city speed by and thinks to herself that she will have a wonderful time tonight, happy to be making closer friends outside of work hours. |
| 5 | 05_arrival_and_welcome | Maya arrives at Priya's apartment building and rings the doorbell. Priya opens the door with a warm smile and welcomes her inside. Maya hands over the box of sweets, and Priya, delighted, says they will definitely eat them after the meal. |
| 6 | 06_setting_the_table | Maya follows Priya into the kitchen and asks how she can help. Priya hands her some plates and says, 'Let's set the table first.' They chat casually as they arrange the cutlery and water glasses, falling easily into a relaxed, friendly rhythm. |
| 7 | 07_dinner_conversation | Over a delicious, warm meal of traditional Varan Bhaat and spicy Batata Bhaji, the two shift from work talk to personal lives. Maya shares stories about her family back in Mexico—her siblings Diego and Sofia, and her dog Lola—while Priya talks about her own childhood, cementing their new friendship. |
| 8 | 08_eating_dessert | After clearing the plates, Priya serves the Kaju Katli that Maya brought, along with two hot cups of chai. As they enjoy the rich cashew sweets, Maya thinks to herself that she is finally building a true community in Mumbai. |
| 9 | 09_return_invitation | As the evening winds down, Maya gets ready to leave and thanks Priya for the wonderful food. She tells Priya that next time, she will cook Mexican food and Priya must come to her flat, and they agree to plan it soon. |
