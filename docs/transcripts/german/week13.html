Week 13: Maya escapes the cold in a café

escape the cold

finding a seat

choosing at the counter

Ich nehme ein Stück Schokoladenkuchen.I will take a piece of chocolate cake.
Der Kellner kommt nun zu mir.The waiter now comes to me.
the polite order
Storyboard: The waiter greets her and Maya orders. She politely asks for a large coffee and the chocolate cake, adding a clear please. The waiter nods, smiles, and says he brings everything to her table. Maya thanks him.
Der Kellner sieht mich an und lächelt.The waiter looks at me and smiles.
„Guten Tag, was möchten Sie?“, fragt er."Good day, what would you like?" he asks.

„Guten Tag“, antworte ich."Good day," I answer.

„Und könnte ich bitte ein Stück Schokoladenkuchen haben?“"And could I please have a piece of chocolate cake?"

„Natürlich, sehr gern“, sagt er."Of course, very gladly," he says.
Er tippt alles in die Kasse.He types everything into the till.

„Ich bringe den Kaffee und den Kuchen zu Ihrem Tisch.“"I will bring the coffee and the cake to your table."
„Vielen Dank“, sage ich höflich."Thank you very much," I say politely.

Ich drehe mich um und gehe zurück zu meinem Platz.I turn around and walk back to my seat.
waiting and messaging

paying the bill

He gives her the change and asks if she needs the Kassenbon, but she declines. Maya then packs her things, puts on her winter coat, says goodbye, and steps back out into the cold Berlin air.
