# Week 20 — Storyboard (Maya's first volunteer shift at the community café)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Past forms mixed; **शिक्षते + -तुम्** (learning to); ordinals **प्रथम / द्वितीय**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_saturday_morning_arrival | Arriving at the bustling neighbourhood community café on Saturday morning, Maya breathes in the aroma of fresh coffee. Eager to meet more locals and help out at the non-profit space, she pushes open the door, feeling the nervous excitement of her first volunteer shift behind the counter. |
| 2 | 02_meeting_sunita | Maya meets Sunita, an experienced and cheerful worker who has run the community space for years. Sunita warmly greets her and hands Maya an apron to get started. |
| 3 | 03_learning_the_machine | Sunita leads Maya to the large, intimidating espresso machine. Maya watches closely and listens to the instructions, focusing her energy on learning to operate the dials and the steam wand safely. |
| 4 | 04_the_first_coffee | A regular walks in and orders. Maya steps up to make her first coffee, but she struggles with the timing; the milk gets too hot, and the drink comes out completely wrong. Sunita gently steps in to clean up the mess and quickly remakes a perfect cup for the waiting customer. |
| 5 | 05_the_second_coffee | A few minutes later, another order arrives. Taking a deep breath, Maya prepares her second coffee. This time, she carefully follows the steps she has learned, and the drink comes out looking much better, with perfect foam. |
| 6 | 06_serving_the_customer | Maya proudly carries the successful second cup to the counter. She hands it to the waiting customer, politely addressing the woman as 'didi', and feels a surge of accomplishment when the customer smiles in thanks. |
| 7 | 07_bun_maska_prep | During a mid-morning lull, Sunita teaches Maya how to quickly slice the soft bread and apply generous layers of butter for bun maska. Maya enjoys the rhythmic, hands-on task and realizes how much she is learning to do in just one morning. |
| 8 | 08_end_of_shift | As lunchtime approaches, Maya finishes her Saturday shift and unties her apron. She thanks Sunita for her patience and steps out to walk back to her nearby flat, physically tired but delighted by the skills she has learned and the community she has served. |
