Week 8: Maya explores the city

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

nina gives directions
Storyboard: On Saturday morning, Maya meets Nina outside their apartment building. Maya wants to explore the city and see the water, so Nina points down the street and gives her a route: "Go straight ahead, and turn left at the park."
Sobotno jutro je.It is Saturday morning.
Sonce sije nad mestom.The sun is shining over the city.
Grem dol na ulico.I go down to the street.
Moja soseda, Nina, stoji pred našo stavbo.My neighbour, Nina, stands outside our building.
"Dobro jutro, Maya," reče in se nasmehne."Good morning, Maya," she says and smiles.
"Dobro jutro, Nina," odgovorim."Good morning, Nina," I answer.
"Danes sem prosta," ji povem."I have the day off today," I tell her.
"Odpravljam se v mesto.""I am heading out into the city."
"Kam pa greš?" vpraša Nina."Where are you going?" Nina asks.
"Grem dol do vode," rečem."I am walking down to the water," I say.
"Ampak ne poznam poti.""But I do not know the way."
Nina pokaže dol po ulici.Nina points down the street.
"Na srečo je zelo enostavno," reče."Fortunately, it is very easy," she says.
"Tukaj pojdi naravnost.""Go straight ahead here."
"In pri parku zavij levo.""And turn left at the park."
"Potem prideš naravnost dol do pristanišča.""Then you come right down to the harbour."
"Najlepša hvala za pomoč, Nina," rečem."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say.
"Ni za kaj, pa srečno pot," odgovori."You are welcome, and have a good trip," she answers.
Pomaham ji.I wave to her.
Začnem svoj sprehod proti vodi.I begin my walk towards the water.
the first leg
Storyboard: Maya begins her weekend walk and follows Nina's route. She walks straight ahead down the busy main street and turns left at the park. She watches yellow bicycles speed past and smells fresh coffee from an open bakery door.
Sledim Ninini poti.I follow Nina's route.
Zrak je svež in hladen.The air is fresh and cold.
Zdaj hodim po glavni ulici.Now I walk down the main street.
Najprej grem samo naravnost.First I just walk straight ahead.
Na ulici je veliko prometa.There is a lot of traffic on the street.
Na poti vidim tri rumena kolesa.On the way I see three yellow bicycles.
Kolesa hitro peljejo mimo mene.The bicycles ride quickly past me.
Zatem grem še naprej.After that I walk further ahead.
Nato pridem do velikega parka.Then I come to a large park.
Zdaj se spomnim Nininih besed.Now I remember Nina's words.
"Pri parku zavij levo," pravi."Turn left at the park," she says.
Tukaj zavijem levo.Here I turn left.
Malo kasneje vidim odprta vrata.A little later I see an open door.
To je majhna pekarna.It is a small bakery.
V zraku je prijeten vonj po sveži kavi.There is a lovely smell of fresh coffee.
Obožujem topel kruh.I love warm bread.
Danes je mesto čudovito.Today the city is lovely.
Potem grem naprej proti vodi.Afterwards I walk on towards the water.
asking an older man
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a large square, but she does not see the water anywhere. She stops an older man on the pavement and asks for directions to the harbour. He points ahead and says: "Walk straight across the square, go past the museum, and turn right."
Prispem na velik trg.I arrive at a large square.
Tam je veliko ljudi.There are many people.
Ampak ne vidim vode.But I do not see the water.
Iščem pristanišče.I am looking for the harbour.
Na pločniku vidim starejšega moškega.I see an older man on the pavement.
Ustavim ga.I stop him.
"Oprostite," rečem."Excuse me," I say.
"Kje je pristanišče?""Where is the harbour?"
Starejši moški prikima.The older man nods.
Pokaže naprej.He points ahead.
"Pojdite naravnost čez trg," reče."Walk straight across the square," he says.
"Pojdite mimo muzeja.""Go past the museum."
"In zavijte desno.""And turn right."
Pogledam čez trg.I look across the square.
Vidim veliko stavbo.I see a large building.
To je muzej.It is the museum.
"Najlepša hvala," rečem."Thank you very much," I say.
"Ni za kaj," odgovori."You are welcome," he replies.
Grem naprej.I continue walking.
getting lost
Storyboard: Maya crosses the square, but she gets distracted by a street musician and misses her turn at the museum. She walks down a narrow side street and realizes she is completely lost in a quiet residential area. She looks around, but she cannot see the museum anymore.
Hodim čez veliki trg.I walk across the large square.
Nenadoma zaslišim glasbo.Suddenly, I hear music.
Na vogalu stoji ulični glasbenik.On the corner stands a street musician.
Igra kitaro.He plays the guitar.
Poslušam dobro glasbo.I listen to the good music.
Dolgo ga gledam.I look at him for a long time.
Potem grem naprej.Then I walk on.
Pozabim na muzej.I forget the museum.
Pozabim tudi na svojo pot.I also forget my route.
Namesto tega grem po ozki stranski ulici.Instead I walk down a narrow side street.
Tukaj je zelo tiho.It is very quiet here.
Naokoli vidim samo nekaj majhnih hiš.All around I only see some small houses.
Potem se ustavim.Then I stop.
Kje je muzej?Where is the museum?
Zdaj se ozrem naokoli.Now I look around.
Ne vidim ga več.I cannot see it anymore.
Hitro pogledam nazaj po ulici.I quickly look back up the street.
Tudi trga ne vidim.I cannot see the square either.
Popolnoma sem se izgubila.I am completely lost.
the helpful dog walker
Storyboard: Maya spots a young woman walking a small dog. Maya approaches her and asks for help. The woman listens, points back up the street, and gives her the way back to the route: "Go straight back to the square, and turn right at the museum — the turn you missed."
Stojim na mirni ulici.I am standing on a quiet street.
Izgubila sem se.I am lost.
Pred seboj vidim mlado žensko.Ahead of me I see a young woman.
Sprehaja majhnega psa.She is walking a small dog.
Pes je podoben psu moje družine, Loli.The dog looks like my family's dog, Lola.
Stopim do ženske.I walk over to the woman.
"Oprostite," rečem."Excuse me," I say.
"Iščem pristanišče.""I am looking for the harbour."
Ženska se ustavi in posluša.The woman stops and listens.
Pokaže nazaj po ulici.She points back up the street.
"Pojdite naravnost nazaj do trga," reče."Go straight back to the square," she says.
"In pri muzeju zavijte desno.""And turn right at the museum."
"Najlepša hvala za pomoč," rečem."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
"Ni za kaj," odgovori ženska."You are welcome," the woman replies.
Pogledam navzdol k majhnemu psu.I look down at the little dog.
Gleda me.It looks at me.
Grem nazaj proti trgu.I walk back toward the square.
Zdaj vem, kje sem.Now I know where I am.
arriving at the harbour
Storyboard: Maya carefully follows the new instructions. She goes straight back to the square, turns by the museum, and finally spots the blue water. She reaches the wide harbour and stops to watch the boats.
Hodim nazaj po mirni ulici.I walk back along the quiet street.
Hodim naravnost naprej.I walk straight ahead.
Kmalu spet vidim velik trg.Soon I see the large square again.
Nato grem čez trg.Then I walk across the square.
Zdaj stojim pri muzeju.Now I am standing by the museum.
Spomnim se besed ženske: "Zavijte desno."I remember the woman's words: "Turn right."
Pri muzeju zavijem desno.At the museum I turn right.
Hodim po široki ulici.I walk down a wide street.
Gledam naprej.I look ahead.
Končno zagledam nekaj modrega.Finally I see something blue.
To je voda!It is the water!
Na poti grem mimo majhne kavarne.On the way I walk past a little café.
Na koncu grem čisto do vode.At last I walk all the way down to the water.
Zdaj sem v pristanišču.Now I am at the harbour.
Pristanišče je zelo veliko.The harbour is very large.
Veter je hladen in svež.The wind is cold and fresh.
Na vodi je veliko čolnov.There are many boats on the water.
Tukaj se ustavim.Here I stop.
Stojim in gledam čolne.I stand and look at the boats.
the little mermaid
Storyboard: Maya walks along the water until she finds the famous Little Mermaid statue. She stands among the other visitors, admires the bronze figure, and takes a beautiful photo to send to her family in Mexico.
Hodim naravnost ob pristanišču.I walk straight ahead along the harbour.
Hodim mimo majhnih čolnov.I walk past the small boats.
Nenadoma vidim veliko ljudi.Suddenly I see many people.
Stojijo spodaj ob vodi.They stand down by the water.
Grem tja.I walk over there.
Končno jo vidim.Finally, I see it.
Mala morska deklica sedi na kamnu v vodi.The Little Mermaid sits on a stone out in the water.
Je zelo znan kip v Kopenhagnu.She is a very famous statue in Copenhagen.
Figura pravzaprav ni zelo velika.The figure is actually not very big.
Toda bronasta figura je na soncu čudovita.But the bronze figure is beautiful in the sun.
Stojim med drugimi obiskovalci.I stand among the other visitors.
Veliko turistov fotografira kip.Many tourists take pictures of the statue.
Tudi jaz vzamem ven svoj telefon.I also take out my phone.
Naredim lepo sliko morske deklice.I take a beautiful picture of the mermaid.
Sliko pošljem svoji družini v Mehiko.I send the picture to my family in Mexico.
Moja sestra, Sofía, hitro odgovori.My sister, Sofía, answers quickly.
"Zelo je lepa," napiše."She is very pretty," she writes.
Spet pogledam vodo.I look at the water again.
walking home
Storyboard: Later in the afternoon, Maya walks back to her apartment. To avoid getting lost again, she simply follows the long, straight road along the water until she recognizes the park from the morning. She finds her way home without asking anyone, and at her door she looks back down the road she now knows.

Hodim ob vodi.I walk along the water.
Na koncu zavijem stran od vode.Finally I turn away from the water.
Zdaj grem spet v mesto.Now I walk into the city again.

Še vedno diši po kavi.It still smells of coffee.
