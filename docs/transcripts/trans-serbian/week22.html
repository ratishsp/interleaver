Week 22: Maya cooks a local dinner for her friends

Serbian with English gloss, line-aligned.

planning the menu
Storyboard: Maya decides to host her Thursday badminton friends, Kasper and Sara, for dinner on Friday evening. She wants to cook a traditional local dish, so she finds a recipe for Danish meatballs with potatoes and writes down a shopping list with the exact quantities.
Igram badminton svakog četvrtka uveče.I play badminton every Thursday evening.
Kasper i Sara igraju u istom timu kao i ja.Kasper and Sara play on the same team as me.
Sutra je petak i pozvala sam ih na večeru.Tomorrow is Friday, and I have invited them over for dinner.
Želim da napravim tradicionalno dansko jelo.I want to cook a traditional Danish dish.
Zato odlučujem da napravim ćufte sa krompirom.So I decide to make meatballs with potatoes.
Nalazim dobar recept na internetu.I find a good recipe on the internet.
Sada treba da napišem spisak za kupovinu.Now I need to write a shopping list.
Prvo mi treba meso za ćufte.First, I need meat for the meatballs.
U receptu piše petsto grama mlevenog svinjskog mesa.The recipe says five hundred grams of minced pork.
Zatim mi trebaju i dva velika žuta luka.Then I also need two large yellow onions.
Zapisujem i malo mleka i jedno jaje.I also note down a little milk and an egg.
Sve to zapisujem u svoj blok.I write it all down on my notepad.
Nakon toga, gledam količinu krompira.Afterwards, I look at the amount of potatoes.
Moram da kupim tačno jedan kilogram malog krompira.I must buy exactly one kilo of small potatoes.
Na kraju, na spisak dodajem puter i so.Finally, I add butter and salt to the list.
Uvek treba koristiti dosta putera za prženje mesa.You must always use plenty of butter to fry the meat.
Spisak je konačno gotov i spremna sam.The list is finally complete, and I am ready.
Sutra posle posla idem pravo u supermarket.Tomorrow after work, I will go straight to the supermarket.
shopping for amounts
Storyboard: At the supermarket after work, Maya carefully gathers her ingredients. She weighs out exactly one kilo of small potatoes, picks up five hundred grams of minced pork, and grabs two large yellow onions.
Stojim u supermarketu posle posla.I am standing in the supermarket after work.
Danas u prodavnici ima mnogo ljudi.There are many people in the store today.
Imam svoj spisak za kupovinu u ruci.I have my shopping list in my hand.
Trebaju mi tačne količine za sutrašnju večeru.I need exact amounts for my dinner tomorrow.
Prvo odlazim do povrća.First, I go over to the vegetables.
Nalazim neke male, dobre krompire.I find some small, good potatoes.
Stavljam ih u kesu i merim ih.I put them in a bag and weigh them.
Vaga pokazuje tačno jedan kilogram.The scale shows exactly one kilo.
To je prava količina za recept.That is the right amount for the recipe.
Zatim uzimam dva velika, žuta luka.Then, I take two large, yellow onions.
Posle toga odlazim do mesa.After that, I walk over to the meat.
Treba mi mleveno svinjsko meso za ćufte.I need minced pork for the meatballs.
Gledam različita pakovanja u rashladnoj vitrini.I look at the different packages in the cooler.
Biram pakovanje od petsto grama.I choose a package with five hundred grams.
Na kraju, proveravam svoj spisak još jednom.Finally, I check my list one extra time.
Takođe uzimam pakovanje putera i malo mleka.I also take a package of butter and a little milk.
Stavljam sve stvari u svoju korpu.I put all the things down into my basket.
Sada sam spremna da kuvam za Kaspera i Saru.Now I am ready to cook for Kasper and Sara.
first prep step
Storyboard: Back in her apartment kitchen, Maya puts on an apron and reads the recipe's first instructions. The recipe states clearly: first, finely chop the onions, and mix them thoroughly with the meat, an egg, and a little flour.
Kod kuće sam u svojoj kuhinji.I am home in my kitchen.
Oblačim svoju kecelju.I put on my apron.
Čitam recept na svom telefonu.I read the recipe on my phone.
Recept je veoma jasan.The recipe is very clear.
Piše: Prvo veoma sitno iseckati luk.It says: First, chop the onions very finely.
Zatim staviti meso u veliku činiju.Then, put the meat in a large bowl.
Potom dodati jaje, malo brašna i mleka.Afterwards, add an egg, a little flour, and milk.
Na kraju sve to dobro izmešati.Finally, mix it all thoroughly together.
Nalazim dasku za sečenje i oštar nož.I find a cutting board and a sharp knife.
Sečem ta dva crna luka na male komade.I cut the two yellow onions into small pieces.
Oči mi malo suze.My eyes water a little.
Otvaram pakovanje sa pet stotina grama mlevenog svinjskog mesa.I open the package with the five hundred grams of minced pork.
Stavljam meso u svoju veliku, plavu činiju.I put the meat into my large, blue bowl.
Onda stavljam luk kod mesa.Then I put the onions down with the meat.
Razbijam jaje u činiju.I crack the egg into the bowl.
Nakon toga dodajem malo brašna i mleka.Afterwards, I add a little flour and milk.
Uzimam veliku kašiku i sve to mešam.I take a large spoon and stir it all together.
Smesa od mesa izgleda zaista dobro.The meat mixture looks really good.
Prvi korak je bio veoma lak.The first step was very easy.
then the potatoes
Storyboard: Next, Maya prepares the potatoes. She follows the next sequence of commands: scrub the potatoes clean, place them in a pot of water, and then add a large spoonful of coarse salt before turning on the stove.
Sada moram da skuvam krompir.Now I have to boil the potatoes.
Ponovo gledam svoj recept.I look at my recipe again.
Ima neka veoma kratka uputstva.It has some very short instructions.
Prvo piše: „Istrljajte male krompire da budu čisti.“First it says: "Scrub the small potatoes clean."
To radim malom četkom pod hladnom vodom.I do that with a small brush under cold water.
Zatim čitam sledeći red.Then I read the next line.
Nakon toga piše: „Stavite krompir u šerpu sa vodom.“Afterwards it says: "Place the potatoes in a pot of water."
Vadim svoju veliku šerpu iz ormarića.I get out my large pot from the cupboard.
Stavljam ceo kilogram u šerpu.I put the whole kilo down into the pot.
Šerpa je skoro potpuno puna.The pot is almost completely full.
Koji je sledeći korak?What is the next step?
U receptu piše: „Dodajte punu veliku kašiku krupne soli u vodu.“The recipe says: "Add a large spoonful of coarse salt to the water."
Vadim so i kašiku.I take out the salt and a spoon.
Sipam so u kašiku.I pour the salt onto the spoon.
Dodajem tu punu veliku kašiku u krompir.I add the large spoonful down to the potatoes.

Stavljam šerpu na šporet i uključujem ga.I put the pot on the stove and turn it on.
Sada krompir samo treba da se kuva dvadeset minuta.Now the potatoes just have to boil for twenty minutes.
afterwards frying
Storyboard: With the potatoes boiling, Maya heats a large frying pan. The instructions tell her to melt a thick slice of butter, and afterwards, form the meat mixture into round balls using a spoon and fry them until they are crisp and brown.
Krompiri se sada kuvaju u loncu.The potatoes are now boiling in the pot.
Već divno miriše u kuhinji.It already smells lovely in the kitchen.
Vadim veliki tiganj.I take out a large frying pan.
Čitam sledeća uputstva u receptu.I read the next instructions in the recipe.
Sledeći deo je važan.The next part is important.
Piše: „Prvo rastopite debeo komad putera u tiganju.“It says: "First melt a thick piece of butter in the pan."
Sečem malo putera i stavljam ga u tiganj.I cut some butter and put it in the pan.
Puter brzo cvrči na vrelom metalu.The butter quickly sizzles on the hot metal.
Čitam dalje: „Zatim oblikujte meso u okrugle loptice.“I read on: "Afterwards form the meat into round balls."
Koristim kašiku da oblikujem meso.I use a spoon to form the meat.
Malo je teško, ali ispadaju lepe i okrugle.It is a bit difficult, but they turn out nice and round.
Pažljivo ih stavljam u vruć tiganj.I carefully place them in the hot pan.
Recept se nastavlja: „Zatim ih pržite dok ne postanu hrskave i smeđe.“The recipe continues: "Then fry them until they are crisp and brown."
Malo smanjujem vatru.I turn down the heat a little.
Treba da se prže oko petnaest minuta.They need to fry for about fifteen minutes.
Često ih okrećem da ne zagore.I turn them often so they do not burn.
Mirišu fantastično i dobijaju dobru boju.They smell fantastic and get a good color.
Sada su ćufte i krompiri skoro gotovi.Now the meatballs and the potatoes are almost ready.
Radujem se što Kasper i Sara dolaze.I am looking forward to Kasper and Sara arriving.
To je apsolutno savršen tajming.It is absolutely perfect timing.
setting the table

Vadim tri tanjira iz ormarića.I take three plates out of the cupboard.
Stavljam ih na mali sto.I place them on the small table.

guests arrive
