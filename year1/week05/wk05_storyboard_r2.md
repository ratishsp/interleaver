# Week 5 — Storyboard (Maya stocks her new kitchen)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Definite nouns *-en/-et* — singular and bare (an adjective or a plural takes a different definite form, out of scope); *kan lide* — a general preference takes the indefinite plural; *jeg vil gerne have*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_empty_cupboards | Maya stands in the kitchen, noticing that the fridge and the cupboard are completely empty. She grabs a bag and heads out, eager to buy food. |
| 2 | 02_produce_section | Maya enters a supermarket and picks up a basket. She walks to the produce section, and because she likes carrots, she picks up a familiar bunch for her dinners. |
| 3 | 03_the_parsnip | Among the familiar vegetables, Maya spots a strange root. She realizes it is a parsnip, a vegetable she does not know, but she drops the parsnip into the basket out of pure curiosity. |
| 4 | 04_the_rye_bread | Moving to the bread section, Maya looks for a local staple. She selects a loaf of rye bread and places the bread in the basket. |
| 5 | 05_bakery_counter | Maya stops at the bakery counter. She catches the eye of the baker and politely says that she would like to have a cinnamon pastry. |
| 6 | 06_finding_the_milk | Maya wanders around looking for the milk. Unable to find it, she stops an employee. The employee points her toward the dairy aisle at the back. |
| 7 | 07_taste_of_home | In the next aisle, Maya hunts for a taste of Mexico. She smiles when she spots a can of black beans, because she truly likes beans, and happily adds the can to the basket. |
| 8 | 08_the_checkout | Maya brings the basket to the checkout. She places her items on the belt, waves her card over the machine to pay, and quickly packs everything into the bag. |
| 9 | 09_putting_away | Back in her flat, Maya unpacks the bag on the counter. She organizes her space, placing the milk and the parsnip in the fridge, and setting the bread and the can neatly in the cupboard. |
| 10 | 10_first_home_cooked_meal | With a stocked kitchen, Maya chops some vegetables and opens the can. She cooks a dinner on the stove, thrilled to finally eat a real meal in the flat. |
