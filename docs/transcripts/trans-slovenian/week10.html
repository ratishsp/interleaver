Week 10: Maya buys winter clothes

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

a cold morning
Storyboard: It is a freezing morning in Copenhagen. Maya walks to the bus stop on her way to work, but she only wears a thin jacket. She shivers in the cold wind and realizes she desperately needs warm winter clothes to survive the Danish winter.
V Københavnu je jutro.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Grem do avtobusne postaje.I walk to the bus stop.
Vreme je danes zelo hladno.The weather is very cold today.
Piha.It is windy.
Veter je leden.The wind is ice-cold.
Zebe me.I am freezing.
Oblečeno imam tanko jakno.I am wearing a thin jacket.
Moja jakna sploh ni topla.My jacket is not warm at all.
Obute imam tudi tanke čevlje.I am also wearing thin shoes.
Nimam toplega šala.I am not wearing a warm scarf.
Moje roke so zelo mrzle.My hands are very cold.
Tresem se.I am shivering.
Zima na Danskem je huda.The winter in Denmark is hard.
Potrebujem topla oblačila.I need warm clothes.
Potrebujem debel pulover.I need a thick sweater.
Potrebujem tudi velik šal.I also need a large scarf.
In potrebujem tople rokavice.And I need warm gloves.
Hodim zelo hitro.I walk very fast.
Po službi bom kupila zimska oblačila.I am buying winter clothes after work.
entering the shop
Storyboard: After work, Maya enters a brightly lit clothing shop on the main shopping street. She greets the smiling shop assistant and explains that she is very cold and wants to look for some warm things.
Končala sem z delom.I am finished at work.
Hodim po ulici za pešce.I walk down the pedestrian street.
Veter je še vedno zelo hladen.The wind is still very cold.
Vidim veliko trgovino z oblačili.I see a large clothing shop.
Trgovina je zelo svetla.The shop is very bright.
Grem v trgovino.I go into the shop.
Tukaj notri je prijetno toplo.It is wonderfully warm in here.
Prodajalka stoji za blagajno.A shop assistant stands behind the register.
Pogleda me in se nasmehne.She looks at me and smiles.
"Živijo," reče."Hello," she says.
"Živijo," odgovorim."Hello," I answer.
"Zelo me zebe," rečem."I am freezing a lot," I say.
"Na sebi imam samo tanko jakno.""I only have a thin jacket on."
"Rada bi našla nekaj toplih stvari.""I would like to find some warm things."
Prodajalka se spet nasmehne.The shop assistant smiles again.
"Danes je tudi zelo mrzlo," reče."It is also very cold today," she says.
"Imamo veliko debelih puloverjev.""We have many thick sweaters."
"Imamo tudi topla zimska oblačila.""We also have warm winter clothes."
"Kar poglejte si," prijazno reče."You just take a look," she says kindly.
"Najlepša hvala," odgovorim."Many thanks," I answer.
the red sweater
Storyboard: Maya browses the shelves and finds a thick, red sweater. She takes a medium size into the fitting room, puts it on, and looks in the mirror. It is incredibly warm and costs three hundred kroner, so she decides to buy it.
Gledam oblačila na policah.I look at the clothes on the shelves.
Vidim debel pulover.I see a thick sweater.
Je rdeč.It is red.
Potrebujem topel pulover.I need a warm sweater.
Trgovina ima veliko velikosti.The shop has many sizes.
Najdem velikost medium.I find a size medium.
Stopim v kabino za pomerjanje.I walk into the fitting room.
Oblečem rdeč pulover.I put the red sweater on.
Imam ga na sebi in gledam v veliko ogledalo.I have it on and look in the big mirror.
Pulover se popolnoma prilega.The sweater fits perfectly.
Je neverjetno topel.It is incredibly warm.
Obožujem rdečo barvo.I love the red colour.
Pogledam na listek s ceno.I look at the price tag.
Pulover stane tristo kron.The sweater costs three hundred kroner.
To je dobra cena za debel pulover.It is a good price for a thick sweater.
Pulover spet slečem.I take the sweater off again.
Kupim ga.I buy it.
the tight green coat
Storyboard: Next, Maya looks for a new winter coat. She finds a beautiful green coat and tries it on, but it is too small and tight across the shoulders. The shop assistant checks the stock, but they do not have a larger size, so Maya leaves it on the rack. The assistant brings her a warm dark-blue winter jacket in her size instead — it fits perfectly, and Maya decides to take it.
Potrebujem tudi zimsko jakno.I also need a winter jacket.
V trgovini si ogledujem še več oblačil.I look at more clothes in the shop.
Najdem lep, zelen plašč.I find a beautiful, green coat.
To je zelo lep dizajn.It is a very beautiful design.
Oblečem zeleni plašč.I put the green coat on.
Ampak je premajhen.But it is too small.
Zelo je tesen čez ramena.It is very tight across the shoulders.
"Ga imate v drugi velikosti?" vprašam."Do you have it in another size?" I ask.
Prodajalka pogleda.The shop assistant looks.
"Ne, na žalost," odgovori."No, unfortunately," she answers.
Zato zeleni plašč obesim nazaj.So I hang the green coat back.
Prodajalka zame najde drugo jakno.The shop assistant finds another jacket for me.
To je topla, temno modra zimska jakna.It is a warm, dark-blue winter jacket.
Oblečem temno modro jakno.I put the dark-blue jacket on.
Popolnoma mi pristaja.It fits perfectly.
Je prijetno velika in ni tesna.It is nice and big and not tight.
Pogledam se v veliko ogledalo.I look in a large mirror.
Všeč mi je.I like it.
"Vzela jo bom," rečem."I will take it," I say.
the blue scarf
Storyboard: Maya walks over to a table with accessories to find a warm scarf. She picks up a long, blue woolen scarf and wraps it around her neck. It feels very soft, costs one hundred and fifty kroner, and she adds it to her purchases.
Stopim k mizi v trgovini.I walk over to a table in the shop.
Na mizi je veliko stvari.There are many things on the table.
Danes nimam šala.I am not wearing a scarf today.
Okrog vratu me zelo zebe.I am very cold around my neck.
Vidim moder šal.I see a blue scarf.
To je zelo dolg šal.It is a very long scarf.
Narejen je iz volne.It is made of wool.
Vzamem modri šal.I take the blue scarf.
Dam si ga okrog vratu.I put it around my neck.
Na dotik je čudovito topel.It feels wonderfully warm.
"Lepa barva je," reče prodajalka."It is a nice colour," the shop assistant says.
"Ja, in zelo je mehak," rečem."Yes, and it is very soft," I say.
Pogledam listek s ceno.I look at the price tag.
Stane sto petdeset kron.It costs one hundred and fifty kroner.
To je dobra cena za topel šal.It is a good price for a warm scarf.
Spet si ga snamem.I take it off again.
"Tudi tega kupim," ji rečem."I am buying it too," I say to her.
Modri šal položim skupaj z rdečim puloverjem.I put the blue scarf together with the red sweater.
the black gloves
Storyboard: Maya remembers her freezing hands from the morning and searches for thick gloves. She tries on a pair of warm, black winter gloves. They fit her hands perfectly and cost two hundred kroner, so she decides to take them as well.
Še vedno imam na sebi svojo tanko jakno.I am still wearing my thin jacket.
Razmišljam o svojih mrzlih rokah od danes zjutraj.I think about my cold hands from this morning.
Potrebujem debele rokavice.I need thick gloves.
Oziram se po svetli trgovini.I look around the bright shop.
Vidim veliko košaro.I see a large basket.
Košara je polna rokavic.The basket is full of gloves.
Najdem par črnih zimskih rokavic.I find a pair of black winter gloves.
So zelo debele in mehke.They are very thick and soft.
Nadenem si rokavice.I put the gloves on.
Popolnoma so mi prav.They fit me perfectly.
Vprašam nasmejano prodajalko.I ask the smiling shop assistant.
"Koliko stanejo črne rokavice?" vprašam."What do the black gloves cost?" I ask.
"Stanejo sto dvajset kron," odgovori."They cost one hundred and twenty kroner," she answers.
Sto dvajset kron je dobra cena.One hundred and twenty kroner is a good price.
Moje roke so že tople.My hands are already warm.
"Vzela jih bom," rečem veselo."I will take them," I say happily.
Spet si snamem rokavice.I take the gloves off again.
Črne rokavice položim skupaj s svojim rdečim puloverjem in modrim šalom.I put the black gloves together with my red sweater and my blue scarf.
Zdaj sem pripravljena plačati.Now I am ready to pay.
paying for the clothes
Storyboard: Maya carries her new clothes to the counter. The shop assistant scans the dark-blue winter jacket, the thick red sweater, the long blue scarf, and the warm black gloves. The total is one thousand five hundred kroner, and Maya pays with her card.
Grem do blagajne s svojimi novimi oblačili.I walk over to the counter with my new clothes.
Prodajalka se mi prijazno nasmehne.The shop assistant smiles kindly at me.
"Je to vse?" vpraša."Is that everything?" she asks.
"Ja, to je vse," odgovorim."Yes, that is everything," I answer.
Skenira vse moje nove stvari.She scans all my new things.
To so pulover, jakna, šal in rokavice.There is a sweater, a jacket, a scarf and gloves.
"Zdaj vas ne bo zeblo," reče veselo."Now you will not freeze," she says happily.
"To bo tisoč petsto kron, prosim.""That will be one thousand five hundred kroner, please."
Oblačila so draga, ampak so zelo topla.The clothes are expensive, but they are very warm.
Vzamem svojo plačilno kartico.I take out my payment card.
"Plačam s kartico," rečem."I am paying by card," I say.
"Lahko plačate kar tukaj," reče."You can just pay here," she says.
Pokaže na majhno, črno napravo.She points at a small, black machine.
Svojo kartico podržim nad napravo.I hold my card over the machine.
Zapiska in plačilo je odobreno.It beeps, and the payment is approved.
"Želite račun?" vpraša prodajalka."Do you want the receipt?" the shop assistant asks.
"Ne, hvala," odgovorim."No thank you," I answer.
Zdaj imam veliko lepih zimskih oblačil.Now I own a lot of nice winter clothes.
stepping into the cold
Storyboard: Maya does not want a shopping bag. Instead, she puts the new clothes on right away. She pulls on the red sweater, zips her new dark-blue winter jacket over it, wraps the blue scarf around her neck, and pulls on the black gloves. She steps back out into the freezing winter street, but this time she feels completely warm.
"Vrečko?" vpraša prodajalec."A bag?" the shop assistant asks.
"Ne, hvala," rečem."No thank you," I say.
Ne potrebujem vrečke.I do not need a bag.
Takoj si oblečem nova oblačila.I put the new clothes on right away.
Najprej si oblečem debel rdeč pulover.First, I put on the thick, red sweater.
Potem si oblečem temno modro zimsko jakno.Then, I put on the dark-blue winter jacket.
Zapnem toplo jakno.I zip the warm jacket.
Nato vzamem dolg moder šal.Afterwards, I take the long, blue scarf.
Ovijem si ga okoli vratu.I wrap it around my neck.
Nazadnje si nadenem nove črne rokavice.Finally, I put on the new, black gloves.
Zdaj imam na sebi vsa svoja topla oblačila.Now I have all my warm clothes on.
Nasmehnem se in rečem nasvidenje.I smile and say goodbye.
Stopim iz trgovine.I walk out of the shop.
Spet stojim zunaj na mrzli ulici.I stand out on the cold street again.
Ledeno mrzlo je.It is freezing cold.
Veter močno piha.The wind blows hard.
Ampak sploh me ne zebe.But I am not freezing at all.
Zdaj sem pripravljena na zimo.Now I am ready for winter.
