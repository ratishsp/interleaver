Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Czech with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
Je páteční večer a Nina klepe na mé dveře.It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
Má s sebou svého psa, Ludwiga.She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.
Zůstane u mě na víkend.He is going to stay with me for the weekend.
Nina se usmívá a dává mi vodítko.Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
„Moc děkuji za pomoc, Mayo,“ říká."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
„To je naprosto v pořádku,“ odpovídám vesele."It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
Vysvětluje mi o něm pár věcí.She explains a few things to me about him.
„Dávej mu jídlo v sedm ráno a v šest večer,“ říká Nina."Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
„A pamatuj, nesmí dostávat lidské jídlo.“"And remember, he must not have human food."
Směju se a dívám se dolů na malého psa.I laugh and look down at the little dog.
„Ne, Ludwigu, moje večeře není pro tebe,“ říkám mu."No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
Nina se také směje a ukazuje na dveře.Nina laughs too and points at the door.
„Vždy ho měj na vodítku, když půjdete blízko té velké silnice,“ říká."Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
„V parku může běhat volně.“"In the park he is allowed to run freely."
Přikyvuji.I nod.
„Neboj, dobře se o něj postarám.“"Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
Nina mě objímá a loučí se s námi.Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
Zavírá dveře a já stojím sama s Ludwigem.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
Vzhlíží ke mně a vrtí ocasem.He looks up at me and wags his tail.
Už teď ho mám moc ráda.I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll
Storyboard: Before bed, Maya takes Ludwig for a short evening walk around the neighborhood. When he suddenly sniffs at a discarded food wrapper on the pavement, she gently commands him to leave it and pulls him away, reminding him that he must not eat trash.
Je pozdní páteční večer.It is late Friday evening.
Než jdeme spát, beru Ludwiga ven.Before we go to bed, I take Ludwig out.
Jdeme na krátkou procházku po okolí.We go for a short walk in the neighborhood.
Ulice jsou teď velmi tiché.The streets are very quiet now.
Ludwig jde pěkně vedle mě.Ludwig walks nicely next to me.
Najednou se zastaví.Suddenly he stops.
Dychtivě něco očichává na chodníku.He sniffs eagerly at something on the pavement.
Je to špinavý pytlík se zbytky jídla.It is a dirty bag with food scraps.
Otevírá tlamu, aby to vzal.He opens his mouth to take it.
„Ne, pusť to!“ říkám mu rázně."No, drop it!" I say firmly to him.
Jemně ho odtáhnu od pytlíku.I gently pull him away from the bag.

„Pojď sem, Ludwigu,“ volám na něj."Come here, Ludwig," I call to him.
Pustí pytlík a podívá se na mě.He drops the bag and looks up at me.
Rychle začne vrtět ocasem.He quickly begins to wag his tail.
„Hodný kluk,“ říkám a pohladím ho."Good boy," I say and pet him.
Pokračujeme v naší procházce ulicí.We continue our walk down the street.
Teď už musíme jen domů a spát.Now we just need to go home and sleep.
saturday breakfast
Storyboard: Early Saturday morning, Ludwig nudges Maya awake. Maya prepares his breakfast, gently telling him to sit and wait while she fills the bowl, before finally petting him and telling him to eat it.

Ještě tvrdě spím.I am still sleeping heavily.
Najednou na paži ucítím něco studeného.Suddenly I feel something cold on my arm.
Otevřu oči a vidím Ludwiga.I open my eyes and see Ludwig.

„Dobré ráno, Ludwigu,“ řeknu a vstanu."Good morning, Ludwig," I say and get up.
Jdeme spolu do kuchyně.We go out into the kitchen together.
Má velký hlad a skáče na mě.He is very hungry and jumps up on me.
„Klid, Ludwigu,“ řeknu mu."Easy now, Ludwig," I say to him.
„Sedni,“ řeknu klidně."Sit," I say calmly.
Podívá se na mě a sedne si na zem.He looks at me and sits down on the floor.
Vezmu pytel s psím krmivem a otevřu ho.I take the bag of dog food and open it.
Nasypu krmivo do jeho misky.I pour the food into his bowl.
Zvedne se, protože to chce hned.He gets up because he wants it immediately.
„Ne, počkej,“ řeknu."No, wait," I say.
Znovu si sedne a hezky čeká.He sits down again and waits nicely.
Položím misku před něj.I put the bowl down in front of him.
Jemně ho pohladím po hlavě.I gently pet him on the head.
„Tady máš, jez,“ řeknu s úsměvem."Here you go, eat," I say with a smile.

a morning together
Storyboard: Saturday morning, with the café shift she arranged off in advance so Ludwig is not left alone, Maya has the morning free. she and Ludwig enjoy it together — she throws his ball and calls him back, gives him a good fuss — happy she chose to stay with him.
Po snídani mám spoustu času.After breakfast I have plenty of time.
Normálně v sobotu pracuji jako dobrovolnice v kavárně.I normally volunteer at a café on Saturdays.
Ale Ludwig by neměl být sám.But Ludwig should not be alone.
Dnes mám naštěstí v kavárně volno.Fortunately I have the day off from the café today.
Domluvila jsem se s nimi minulý týden.I arranged it with them last week.
Teď máme s Ludwigem celé ráno pro sebe.Now Ludwig and I have the whole morning together.
Jdeme ven na dvůr za mým domem.We go out into the courtyard behind my building.
Vytáhnu jeho malý míček.I take out his little ball.
„Podívej se na ten míček, Ludwigu!“ říkám mu."Look at the ball, Ludwig!" I say to him.
Hodím ho přes trávu.I throw it across the grass.
„Přines!“ křiknu."Fetch it!" I shout.
Rychle běží a chytí míček.He runs fast and catches the ball.
„Pojď sem!“ volám."Come here!" I call.
Vesele běží zpátky ke mně.He runs happily back to me.
„Dej mi ho,“ říkám jemně."Give it to me," I say gently.
Pustí míček a já ho pohladím.He lets go of the ball, and I pet him.
„Jsi šikovný pes,“ říkám a usmívám se."You are a good dog," I say and smile.
Znovu ho hodím a on ho přinese.I throw it again, and he fetches it.
Musím se o něj celý víkend dobře starat.I must take good care of him all weekend.
Jsem moc ráda, že jsem s ním zůstala doma.I am very happy that I stayed at home with him.
the hundeskov
Storyboard: Saturday afternoon, Maya walks with Ludwig to a local hundeskov (dog forest). Once inside the safe, gated area, she unclips the lead, happily telling him that he may run free now, and throws a stick for him to fetch.
Ludwig jde rychle vedle mě.Ludwig walks quickly next to me.
Jsme na cestě do místního psího lesa.We are on our way to a local dog forest.
Dnes odpoledne krásně svítí slunce.The sun is shining nicely this afternoon.
Trochu nedočkavě tahá za vodítko.He pulls a little eagerly on the leash.
„Čekej,“ řeknu mu důrazně."Wait," I say firmly to him.
„Pojď, půjdeme hezky.“"Come, let us walk nicely."

Dřepnu si a podívám se na něj.I crouch down and look at him.

„Podívej sem, Ludwigu!“ zavolám a ukážu mu ho."Look here, Ludwig!" I shout and show it to him.

„Přines!“ řeknu nahlas."Fetch it!" I say loudly.

Je to opravdu šikovný pes.He is really a good dog.
gentle recall
