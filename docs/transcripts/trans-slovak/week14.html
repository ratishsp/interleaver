Week 14: Maya takes public transport across the city

Slovak with English gloss, line-aligned.

checking the route
Storyboard: Maya looks at a travel app on her phone. She needs to go across the city to pick up a package, so she plans a route with three different types of public transport.
Dnes si musím vyzdvihnúť balík.I need to pick up a package today.
Je na druhej strane mesta.It is on the other side of the city.
Pozerám sa na svoj telefón.I look at my phone.
Otváram aplikáciu verejnej dopravy.I open a public transport app.
Plánujem si trasu.I plan my route.
Trasa je dlhá.The route is long.
Má tri časti.It has three parts.
Najprv musím ísť autobusom.First, I need to take a bus.
Autobus ide k metru.The bus drives to the metro.
Potom musím ísť metrom.Then I need to ride the metro.
Nakoniec musím ísť do prístavu.Finally, I need to go to the harbour.
Tu musím ísť prístavným autobusom.Here, I need to take the harbour bus.
Prístavný autobus je veľká loď.The harbour bus is a large boat.
Plaví sa po vode.It sails on the water.
Balík je v malom obchode.The package is in a small shop.
Obchod sa nachádza blízko vody.The shop is located close to the water.
Je to dobrý plán.It is a good plan.
Beriem si svoju modrú cestovnú kartu.I take my blue travel card.
Som pripravená na cestu.I am ready for the trip.
topping up the card
Storyboard: She walks to a nearby ticket machine. She taps her blue travel card on the screen and pays two hundred kroner to top up her balance for the long trip.
Kráčam k automatu na lístky na ulici.I walk over to a ticket machine on the street.
Najprv moja cestovná karta potrebuje viac peňazí.First my travel card needs more money.
Vyberiem svoju cestovnú kartu z tašky.I take my travel card out of my bag.
Je to malá modrá karta.It is a small, blue card.
Položím kartu na obrazovku automatu.I place the card on the machine's screen.
Obrazovka ukazuje môj zostatok.The screen shows my balance.
Zostatok je príliš nízky na dlhú cestu.The balance is too low for a long trip.
Ťuknem na obrazovku.I tap on the screen.
Vyberiem si sumu.I choose an amount.
Zaplatím dvesto korún svojou platobnou kartou.I pay two hundred kroner with my payment card.
Automat na obrazovke vypíše „Schválené“.The machine writes "Approved" on the screen.
Teraz mám na karte peniaze.Now I have money on the card.
Dám kartu späť do tašky.I put the card back in the bag.
Som pripravená na dlhú cestu.I am ready for the long trip.
Teraz musím ísť autobusom.Now I need to take the bus.
Prejdem k autobusovej zastávke.I walk over to the bus stop.
taking the bus
Storyboard: Maya waits at the bus stop until a long yellow bus arrives. She steps inside, scans her card at the door, and finds an empty seat by the window.
Stojím na autobusovej zastávke.I stand at the bus stop.
Čoskoro pôjdem autobusom.Soon I travel by bus.
Pozerám sa dolu ulicou.I look down the street.
Po piatich minútach prichádza autobus.After five minutes, the bus arrives.
Je to dlhý a žltý autobus.It is a long and yellow bus.
Autobus zastavuje priamo predo mnou.The bus stops right in front of me.
Dvere sa otvárajú.The doors open.
Nastupujem do autobusu.I step up into the bus.
V ruke mám svoju cestovnú kartu.I have my travel card in my hand.
Skenujem kartu pri dverách.I scan the card by the door.
Dnes je tu veľa cestujúcich.There are many passengers today.
Hľadám si voľné miesto.I look for an empty seat.
Nachádzam dobré miesto pri okne.I find a good seat by the window.
Sadám si.I sit down.
Pozerám sa von na mesto.I look out at the city.
Teraz idem autobusom na stanicu metra.Now I take the bus to the metro station.
asking a passenger
Storyboard: She turns to an older woman sitting nearby. Maya politely asks if she travels with this bus to the metro station, and the woman nods with a friendly smile.
Sedím v autobuse.I am sitting in the bus.
Pozerám sa z okna.I look out the window.
Nie som si úplne istá trasou.I am not completely sure about the route.
Blízko mňa sedí staršia žena.An older woman is sitting close to me.
Pozriem sa na ňu.I look over at her.
„Prepáčte,“ poviem zdvorilo."Excuse me," I say politely.
„Ide tento autobus na stanicu metra?“"Does this bus go to the metro station?"
Žena sa na mňa pozrie.The woman looks at me.
Prikývne s priateľským úsmevom.She nods with a friendly smile.
„Áno, ide na stanicu metra,“ odpovie."Yes, it goes to the metro station," she replies.
„Idete metrom?“ pýta sa žena."Are you taking the metro?" the woman asks.
„Áno, idem metrom,“ poviem."Yes, I am taking the metro," I say.
„Často cestujem týmto autobusom,“ hovorí žena."I often ride with this bus," the woman says.
„Je to jednoduchá trasa,“ dodáva."It is an easy route," she adds.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem za pomoc,“ poviem."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
„Je to moja prvá cesta na tejto trase.“"It is my first trip on this route."
Žena znova prikývne.The woman nods again.
Uvoľním sa na sedadle.I relax in my seat.
Ideme ďalej cez mesto.We drive on through the city.
transferring to metro
Storyboard: The bus stops, and Maya gets off at a busy square. She walks down the wide stairs into a bright, underground metro station and taps her card again.
Autobus zastavuje na rušnom námestí.The bus stops at a busy square.
Vystupujem z autobusu.I get off the bus.
Lúčim sa so staršou paňou.I say goodbye to the older woman.
Teraz idem na metro.Now I am going to the metro.
Prechádzam cez námestie.I walk across the square.
Je tu veľa ľudí.There are many people here.
Idem dole širokými schodmi.I walk down the wide stairs.
Stanica je v podzemí.The station is underground.
Je veľmi veľká a svetlá.It is very big and bright.
Musím sa znova zaevidovať.I have to check in again.
Vyťahujem svoju modrú kartu.I take out my blue card.
Skenujem kartu na prístroji.I scan the card on the machine.
Prístroj vydá hlasné pípnutie.The machine makes a loud beep.
Teraz môžem cestovať metrom.Now I can ride with the metro.
Idem dole na nástupište.I walk down to the platform.
Idem ďalším vlakom.I am taking the next train.
Ide priamo k prístavu.It goes straight towards the harbour.
Vlak príde o tri minúty.The train arrives in three minutes.
riding the metro
Storyboard: Maya stands right at the front of the driverless train. She rides with the metro through the dark tunnels and watches the lights blur past as they speed forward.
Metro prichádza a dvere sa otvárajú.The metro arrives, and the doors open.
Vchádzam do vlaku.I go into the train.
Vlak jazdí bez vodiča.The train drives without a driver.
Idem úplne dopredu.I walk all the way to the front.
Stojím pri veľkom okne.I stand by the large window.
Cestujem metrom cez tmu.I ride with the metro through the dark.
Sme v dlhom tuneli.We are in a long tunnel.
Ideme veľmi rýchlo.We drive very fast.
Svetlo letí okolo okna.The light flies past the window.
Vyzerá to fantasticky.It looks fantastic.
Vedľa mňa stojí malý chlapec.A little boy stands next to me.
Aj on sa pozerá von do tmy.He also looks out into the dark.
Často cestujem metrom.I often take the metro.
Je to rýchla a jednoduchá cesta.It is a fast and easy trip.
Teraz sa blížime k mojej stanici.Now we are approaching my station.
Idem do prístavu.I am going to the harbour.
Moja ďalšia cesta je prístavným autobusom.My next trip is with the harbour bus.
Teším sa, že uvidím vodu.I am looking forward to seeing the water.
finding the pier
Storyboard: She takes the escalator up to the street level and walks toward the water. She finds a small wooden pier by the edge of the canal where the harbour bus stops.
Cesta metrom sa skončila.The trip with the metro is over.
Teraz idem hore na ulicu.Now I am going up to the street.
Idem eskalátorom.I take the escalator.
Hore na ulici svieti slnko.The sun is shining up on the street.
Teraz idem k vode.Now I am going to the water.
Idem prístavným autobusom.I am going by the harbour bus.
Kráčam dolu širokou ulicou.I walk down a wide street.
Potom uvidím kanál.Then I see the canal.
Voda je veľmi modrá.The water is very blue.
Hľadám svoju zastávku.I look for my stop.
Kde zastavuje loď?Where does the boat stop?
Podídem bližšie k okraju.I walk closer to the edge.
Potom uvidím malé drevené mólo.Then I see a small wooden pier.
Nachádza sa úplne dole pri vode.It is located right down by the water.
Pri móle je žltá tabuľa.There is a yellow sign by the pier.
Na tabuli je napísané „Prístavný autobus“.It says "Harbour bus" on the sign.
Toto je to správne miesto.This is the right place.
Vyjdem na mólo.I walk out onto the pier.
Teraz už len čakám na loď.Now I am just waiting for the boat.
the harbour bus
Storyboard: A wide boat arrives, and Maya boards the harbour bus. She stands on the open deck and enjoys the fresh air as she sails across the blue water.
Široká loď prichádza po vode.A wide boat comes across the water.
Je to prístavný autobus.It is the harbour bus.
Loď pripláva k drevenému mólu.The boat sails in to the wooden pier.
Zastavuje na okraji.It stops at the edge.
Teraz idem prístavným autobusom.I am going with the harbour bus now.
Nastupujem na palubu lode.I step on board the ship.
Znovu si skenujem modrú cestovnú kartu.I scan my blue travel card again.
Vnútri sedí veľa cestujúcich.Many passengers sit inside.
Ale ja vychádzam na otvorenú palubu.But I walk out onto the open deck.
Stojím úplne vpredu.I stand at the very front.
Plavíme sa loďou po modrej vode.We sail with the boat across the blue water.
Mesto vyzerá z vody krásne.The city looks beautiful from the water.
Slnko na nás svieti.The sun shines down on us.
Zhlboka sa nadýchnem.I take a deep breath.
Vzduch je veľmi svieži a studený.The air is very fresh and cold.
Užívam si krátku cestu.I enjoy the short trip.
Čoskoro sme na druhej strane.We are soon over at the other side.
arriving for the errand
Storyboard: The boat docks at the opposite shore. Maya steps off the vessel and walks toward a small shop on the corner to finally collect her package.
Loď prichádza na druhý breh.The boat arrives at the other side of the water.
Prístavný autobus zastavuje pri malom móle.The harbour bus stops at a small pier.
Vystupujem z lode.I get off the boat.
Cesta loďou sa teraz skončila.The trip by boat is now over.
Kontrolujem mapu v telefóne.I check the map on my phone.
Idem do malého kiosku.I am going to a small kiosk.
Kiosk sa nachádza tam na rohu.The kiosk is located over on a corner.
Kráčam hore ulicou.I walk up the street.
Na konci cesty vidím obchod.I see the shop at the end of the road.
Otváram dvere a vchádzam dnu.I open the door and walk in.
Za pokladňou stojí mladý muž.A young man stands behind the register.
„Ahoj,“ hovorí prívetivo."Hi," he says kindly.
„Ahoj, idem si vyzdvihnúť balík,“ hovorím."Hi, I am going to pick up a package," I say.
Muž prikývne a usmeje sa.The man nods and smiles.
Pýta sa na moje meno.He asks for my name.
„Moje meno je Maya,“ odpovedám."My name is Maya," I answer.
Na polici nájde veľkú škatuľu.He finds a large box on a shelf.
Dáva mi balík.He gives the package to me.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem za pomoc,“ hovorím."Thank you very much for the help," I say.
Teraz sa so svojím balíkom opäť vydávam na dlhú cestu domov.Now I take the long trip home again with my package.
