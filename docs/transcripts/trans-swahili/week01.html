Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
Ndege ipo Copenhagen sasa.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
Ni Januari.It is January.
Nimesisimka sana.I am very excited.
Ninaweka kitabu changu kwenye begi langu.I pack my book into my bag.
Kuna abiria kando yangu.There is a passenger next to me.
Ananiangalia na kutabasamu.He looks at me and smiles.
"Halo," anasema."Hello," he says.
"Halo," ninasema."Hello," I say.
"Unaitwa nani?" anauliza."What is your name?" he asks.
"Ninaitwa Maya," ninasema."My name is Maya," I say.
"Unatoka wapi, Maya?" anauliza."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
Ninatabasamu kwa furaha.I smile happily.
"Ninatoka Mexico," ninajibu."I come from Mexico," I answer.
"Je, unatoka Denmark?" ninauliza."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
"Ndiyo, ninatoka Denmark," anasema."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
"Karibu Denmark," anasema."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
"Asante sana," ninasema."Thank you very much," I say.
Sasa tupo hapa.Now we are here.
Niko tayari.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
Niko nje sasa.I am outside now.
Sanduku langu ni kubwa na zito.My suitcase is big and heavy.
Kuna teksi.There is a taxi.
Nainua sanduku langu na kuliweka ndani ya teksi.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
Dereva ni mwanaume mkarimu.The driver is a friendly man.
"Anwani ni gani?" anauliza."What is the address?" he asks.
"Ni Parkvej namba kumi na mbili," nasema."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
Anaitikia kwa kichwa na kuendesha.He nods and drives.
"Wewe ni mtalii?" dereva anauliza."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
"Hapana, mimi si mtalii," najibu."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
"Unatoka wapi?" anauliza."Where do you come from?" he asks.
"Natoka Mexico," nasema."I come from Mexico," I say.
Natazama nje ya dirisha.I look out the window.
Nina fahari sana.I am very proud.
"Mimi ni mhandisi wa programu," nasema."I am a software engineer," I say.
"Kazi yangu mpya iko hapa Copenhagen.""My new job is here in Copenhagen."
Anatabasamu.He smiles.
"Inasisimua sana.""How exciting."
"Kila la heri nchini Denmark," anasema."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
"Asante sana," nasema."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
Mlango uko wazi.The door is open.
Kizingiti kiko juu.The threshold is high.
Sanduku ni zito sana!The suitcase is very heavy!
Lakini sasa sanduku liko kwenye korido.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
Kuna giza kabisa hapa.It is completely dark here.
Swichi iko wapi?Where is the switch?
Ukuta una baridi.The wall is cold.
Je, swichi iko hapa?Is the switch here?
Ndiyo, swichi iko pale!Yes, there is the switch!
Sasa kuna mwanga.Now there is light.
Niko kwenye fleti.I am in the apartment.
Ni ndogo, lakini ni nzuri.It is small, but it is nice.
Hapa kuna sebule.Here is a living room.
Kuna sofa na meza.There is a sofa and a table.
Ni nyumba yenye samani.It is a furnished home.
Sanduku liko sakafuni.The suitcase is on the floor.
Nimechoka sana.I am very tired.
Lakini pia nina furaha.But I am also happy.
Niko Copenhagen!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
Nimesimama kando ya dirisha.I am standing by the window.
Kioo ni baridi.The glass is cold.
Ni Januari.It is January.
Ninatazama nje.I look out.
Copenhagen ina rangi ya kijivu leo.Copenhagen is grey today.
Kuna baridi nje.It is cold outside.
Kuna theluji barabarani.There is snow on the street.
Lakini hapa kuna joto.But it is warm here.
Fleti ni nzuri.The apartment is good.
Ninafikiria kuhusu Meksiko.I think about Mexico.
Kule Meksiko, kuna jua.In Mexico, there is sun.
Kuna joto pia huko.It is also warm there.
Ninalikosa jua kidogo.I miss the sun a little.
Lakini ninatabasamu.But I smile.
Ni mji mpya.It is a new city.
Niko Denmaki sasa.I am in Denmark now.
Ni mwanzo mzuri.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
Niko sebuleni sasa.I am in the living room now.
Kuna kochi dogo.There is a small sofa.
Ninampigia simu mama yangu Mexico.I call my mother in Mexico.
"Halo mama," ninasema."Hi Mom," I say.
"Halo Maya," mama yangu anasema."Hi Maya," my mother says.
"Uko wapi?" anauliza."Where are you?" she asks.
"Niko Copenhagen," ninajibu."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
"Niko kwenye fleti sasa.""I am in the apartment now."
"Ni vizuri sana," mama yangu anasema."That is so good," my mother says.
"Ni kubwa?" anauliza."Is it big?" she asks.
"Hapana, ni ndogo," ninajibu."No, it is small," I answer.
"Lakini inastarehesha, na nina furaha.""But it is cozy, and I am happy."
"Kuna baridi?" anauliza."Is it cold?" she asks.
"Ndiyo, kuna baridi nje," ninasema."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
"Lakini humu ndani kuna joto.""But it is warm in here."
"Niko salama.""I am safe."
"Ni vizuri," mama yangu anasema."That is good," my mother says.
Ninatabasamu.I smile.
Denmark ni makazi yangu mapya.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
Ninafungua mlango wangu.I open my door.
Nimesimama kwenye ngazi.I am standing in the stairwell.
Kuna kimya.It is quiet.
Mlango unafunguka.A door opens.
Mwanamke ananitabasamia.A woman smiles at me.
"Hujambo," anasema."Hi," she says.
"Unaitwa nani?" anauliza."What is your name?" she asks.
"Ninaitwa Maya," ninajibu."My name is Maya," I answer.
"Unatoka wapi?" anauliza."Where do you come from?" she asks.
"Ninatoka Mexico," ninasema."I come from Mexico," I say.
"Karibu kwenye nyumba hii," mwanamke anasema."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
"Ninaitwa Nina," anasema."My name is Nina," she says.
"Mimi ni jirani yako.""I am your neighbour."
Nina ni mkarimu sana.Nina is very friendly.
Anazungumza polepole na wazi.She speaks slowly and clearly.
"Huu ni mlango wangu," anasema."Here is my door," she says.
"Ninafurahi kusaidia.""I am happy to help."
"Bisha tu hodi," Nina anasema."You just knock," Nina says.
"Asante sana, Nina," ninajibu."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
Nimefurahi sana.I am very happy.
Sasa nina rafiki Copenhagen.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
Sasa ni usiku.Now it is night.
Ni usiku wangu wa kwanza Kopenhageni.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
Niko bafuni.I am in the bathroom.
Ni dogo sana.It is very small.
Kuna sinki.There is a sink.
Mswaki wangu ni mwekundu.My toothbrush is red.
Ninaweka dawa ya meno kwenye mswaki wangu.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
Ninasafisha meno yangu.I brush my teeth.
Maji ni baridi.The water is cold.
Ninanawa uso wangu.I wash my face.
Ninakausha uso wangu.I dry my face.
Ninaenda kwenye kitanda changu.I go over to my bed.
Kitanda ni chembamba.The bed is narrow.
Lakini kina joto na ni kilaini.But it is warm and soft.
Nimelala chini ya blanketi sasa.I am lying under the duvet now.
Simu yangu iko mezani.My phone is lying on the table.
Ninatega alamu yangu.I set my alarm.
Italia saa mbili kesho.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
Nimechoka, lakini niko tayari.I am tired, but I am ready.
Usiku mwema, Kopenhageni.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
