Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Dutch with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
Het is vrijdagavond en Nina klopt op mijn deur.It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
Ze heeft haar hond, Ludwig, bij zich.She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.
Hij blijft het weekend bij mij.He is going to stay with me for the weekend.
Nina glimlacht en geeft me de riem.Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
"Heel erg bedankt voor de hulp, Maya," zegt ze."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
"Het is helemaal in orde," antwoord ik blij."It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
Ze legt me een paar dingen over hem uit.She explains a few things to me about him.
"Geef hem eten om zeven uur 's ochtends en om zes uur 's avonds," zegt Nina."Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
"En onthoud, hij mag geen menseneten krijgen.""And remember, he must not have human food."
Ik lach en kijk omlaag naar de kleine hond.I laugh and look down at the little dog.
"Nee, Ludwig, mijn avondeten is niet voor jou," zeg ik tegen hem."No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
Nina lacht ook en wijst naar de deur.Nina laughs too and points at the door.
"Houd hem altijd aan de riem als jullie dicht bij de grote weg lopen," zegt ze."Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
"In het park mag hij vrij rondrennen.""In the park he is allowed to run freely."
Ik knik.I nod.
"Geen zorgen, ik pas goed op hem.""Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
Nina omhelst me en neemt afscheid van ons.Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
Ze sluit de deur en ik sta alleen met Ludwig.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
Hij kijkt naar me op en kwispelt met zijn staart.He looks up at me and wags his tail.
Ik ben nu al erg gesteld op hem.I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll
Storyboard: Before bed, Maya takes Ludwig for a short evening walk around the neighborhood. When he suddenly sniffs at a discarded food wrapper on the pavement, she gently commands him to leave it and pulls him away, reminding him that he must not eat trash.

saturday breakfast

a morning together
Storyboard: Saturday morning, with the café shift she arranged off in advance so Ludwig is not left alone, Maya has the morning free. she and Ludwig enjoy it together — she throws his ball and calls him back, gives him a good fuss — happy she chose to stay with him.

"Haal hem!" roep ik."Fetch it!" I shout.

"Kom hier!" roep ik."Come here!" I call.
Hij rent blij naar me terug.He runs happily back to me.

the hundeskov
