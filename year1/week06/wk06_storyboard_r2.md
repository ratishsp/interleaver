# Week 6 — Storyboard (Maya finds her rhythm in the city)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Present-tense regular verbs; reflexive *vasker mig*; V2 review — the verb comes before the subject whenever a time or place phrase opens the sentence

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_chilly_morning | In the morning, Maya wakes up feeling a bit cold in the new apartment. She steps into the bathroom and washes herself with warm water, finally feeling awake and ready to face the day. |
| 2 | 02_strong_coffee | In the kitchen, she pours a strong cup of coffee to shake off the sleep. She chooses warm clothes because the sky looks grey outside, and she drinks her coffee while looking out the window. |
| 3 | 03_catching_the_bus | Down on the street, Maya spots her bus already approaching the stop. She runs a few quick steps and jumps on board just in time, finding a warm seat at the back to catch her breath. |
| 4 | 04_stubborn_code | At her desk, Maya stares intently at her computer screen. She wrestles with a stubborn line of code for her software project, but she eventually figures out the problem and types the solution. |
| 5 | 05_canteen_lunch | At midday, Maya heads down to the busy canteen for a break. She spots her colleague Peter, and they eat lunch together, laughing warmly as he tries to pronounce the name of her Mexican hometown. |
| 6 | 06_leaving_the_office | Late in the afternoon, Maya closes her laptop with a sigh of relief. She waves goodbye to Peter and heads out of the building, enjoying the crisp evening air after a productive day. |
| 7 | 07_cooking_the_parsnip | At home in the kitchen, Maya decides to cook something comforting for dinner. She experiments with the strange parsnip she bought earlier, roasting it in a pan with familiar spices from home. |
| 8 | 08_reading_on_the_sofa | After dinner, Maya curls up on the sofa under a soft, heavy blanket. She opens a Spanish book, losing herself in the story while the city hums quietly outside her window. |
| 9 | 09_turning_in | In the quiet bathroom, Maya brushes her teeth and prepares for sleep. She turns off the lights and gets into bed, feeling completely at home in her new daily routine. |
