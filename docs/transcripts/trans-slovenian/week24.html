Week 24: Maya looks after Nina's dog for the weekend

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

ninas handover
Storyboard: On Friday evening, Nina brings her dog Ludwig over for the weekend — relaxed and grateful, trusting Maya completely. She talks Maya through his care warmly, friend to friend: when he gets his food, that he's not really allowed table scraps, and to keep him on the lead near the busy road. They laugh, Nina hugs her goodbye, and Maya is already fond of Ludwig.
Petek zvečer je in Nina potrka na moja vrata.It is Friday evening, and Nina knocks on my door.
S seboj ima svojega psa Ludwiga.She has her dog, Ludwig, with her.
Čez vikend bo ostal pri meni.He is going to stay with me for the weekend.
Nina se nasmehne in mi poda povodec.Nina smiles and gives me the leash.
"Najlepša hvala za pomoč, Maya," reče."Thank you so much for the help, Maya," she says.
"Sploh ni problema," veselo odgovorim."It is absolutely fine," I answer happily.
Pojasni mi nekaj stvari o njem.She explains a few things to me about him.
"Daj mu hrano ob sedmih zjutraj in ob šestih zvečer," reče Nina."Give him food at seven in the morning and at six in the evening," Nina says.
"In ne pozabi, ne sme jesti človeške hrane.""And remember, he must not have human food."
Zasmejim se in pogledam navzdol k majhnemu psu.I laugh and look down at the little dog.
"Ne, Ludwig, moja večerja ni zate," mu rečem."No, Ludwig, my dinner is not for you," I say to him.
Tudi Nina se zasmeje in pokaže na vrata.Nina laughs too and points at the door.
"Vedno ga imej na povodcu, ko hodita blizu velike ceste," reče."Always keep him on the leash when you walk close to the big road," she says.
"V parku pa lahko prosto teče.""In the park he is allowed to run freely."
Prikimam.I nod.
"Brez skrbi, dobro bom skrbela zanj.""Don't worry, I will take good care of him."
Nina me objame in se poslovi od naju.Nina hugs me and says goodbye to us.
Zapre vrata in ostanem sama z Ludwigom.She closes the door, and I stand alone with Ludwig.
Pogleda gor vame in zamaha z repom.He looks up at me and wags his tail.
Že zdaj mi je zelo pri srcu.I am already very fond of him.
friday evening stroll
Storyboard: Before bed, Maya takes Ludwig for a short evening walk around the neighborhood. When he suddenly sniffs at a discarded food wrapper on the pavement, she gently commands him to leave it and pulls him away, reminding him that he must not eat trash.
Pozno v petek zvečer je.It is late Friday evening.
Preden greva spat, peljem Ludwiga ven.Before we go to bed, I take Ludwig out.

Ludwig lepo hodi ob meni.Ludwig walks nicely next to me.
Nenadoma se ustavi.Suddenly he stops.
Vneto voha nekaj na pločniku.He sniffs eagerly at something on the pavement.

"Ne, spusti to!" mu odločno rečem."No, drop it!" I say firmly to him.
Nežno ga potegnem stran od vrečke.I gently pull him away from the bag.
"Smeti niso hrana, Ludwig," pojasnim."The trash is not food, Ludwig," I explain.

"Priden fant," rečem in ga pobožam."Good boy," I say and pet him.
Nadaljujeva najin sprehod po ulici.We continue our walk down the street.
Zdaj morava samo še domov in spat.Now we just need to go home and sleep.
saturday breakfast
Storyboard: Early Saturday morning, Ludwig nudges Maya awake. Maya prepares his breakfast, gently telling him to sit and wait while she fills the bowl, before finally petting him and telling him to eat it.

Še vedno trdno spim.I am still sleeping heavily.

"Dobro jutro, Ludwig," rečem in vstanem."Good morning, Ludwig," I say and get up.
Skupaj greva v kuhinjo.We go out into the kitchen together.
Zelo je lačen in skoči name.He is very hungry and jumps up on me.
"Počasi, Ludwig," mu rečem."Easy now, Ludwig," I say to him.
"Sedi," rečem mirno."Sit," I say calmly.
Pogleda me in se usede na tla.He looks at me and sits down on the floor.
Vzamem vrečko s pasjo hrano in jo odprem.I take the bag of dog food and open it.

Vstane, ker jo hoče takoj.He gets up because he wants it immediately.
"Ne, čakaj," rečem."No, wait," I say.
Znova se usede in lepo čaka.He sits down again and waits nicely.

"Izvoli, jej," rečem z nasmeškom."Here you go, eat," I say with a smile.
Maha z repom in začne jesti.He wags his tail and begins to eat.
a morning together
Storyboard: Saturday morning, with the café shift she arranged off in advance so Ludwig is not left alone, Maya has the morning free. she and Ludwig enjoy it together — she throws his ball and calls him back, gives him a good fuss — happy she chose to stay with him.
Po zajtrku imam veliko časa.After breakfast I have plenty of time.
Ob sobotah običajno delam kot prostovoljka v kavarni.I normally volunteer at a café on Saturdays.
Ampak Ludwig ne sme biti sam.But Ludwig should not be alone.
Na srečo sem danes v kavarni prosta.Fortunately I have the day off from the café today.

Greva ven na dvorišče za mojo stavbo.We go out into the courtyard behind my building.
Vzamem njegovo majhno žogo.I take out his little ball.
"Poglej žogo, Ludwig!" mu rečem."Look at the ball, Ludwig!" I say to him.
Vržem jo po travi.I throw it across the grass.
"Prinesi!" zavpijem."Fetch it!" I shout.
Hitro steče in ujame žogo.He runs fast and catches the ball.
"Pridi sem!" pokličem."Come here!" I call.
Veselo priteče nazaj k meni.He runs happily back to me.
"Daj mi jo," rečem nežno."Give it to me," I say gently.
Izpusti žogo, jaz pa ga pobožam.He lets go of the ball, and I pet him.

the hundeskov
Storyboard: Saturday afternoon, Maya walks with Ludwig to a local hundeskov (dog forest). Once inside the safe, gated area, she unclips the lead, happily telling him that he may run free now, and throws a stick for him to fetch.
Ludwig hitro hodi ob meni.Ludwig walks quickly next to me.
Na poti sva v lokalni pasji gozd.We are on our way to a local dog forest.

gentle recall

family video call

ninas return
