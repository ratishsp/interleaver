Week 4: Maya moves into her own flat

final sweep
Storyboard: Maya stands in her temporary room in the building. She zips up her large suitcases and checks the space one last time, making sure nothing lies under the small bed or stands forgotten in the closet.

Uzimam svoje dve torbe.I take my two bags.

unlocking the door
Storyboard: Carrying her first few bags up the communal stairs, Maya arrives at the door of her new, permanent flat. She turns the key, pushes the door open, and steps inside, thrilled to finally see the small furnished space waiting for her.

ninas help
Storyboard: Maya steps back out to the shared stairwell to grab her heaviest suitcase. Nina comes up the stairs, spots her struggling, and cheerfully helps carry the bag inside, setting it carefully on the floor by the large living room window.

Moj najveći kofer stoji na podu.My biggest suitcase stands on the floor.
Veoma je težak.It is very heavy.

To je moja danska komšinica, Nina.It is my Danish neighbor, Nina.
Toplo mi se smeši.She smiles warmly at me.
„Useljavaš li se danas, Majo?” pita ona."Are you moving in today, Maya?" she asks.
„Da, konačno sam u svom novom stanu,” kažem."Yes, I am finally in my new apartment," I say.
„Pomoći ću ti,” kaže Nina ljubazno."I will help you," Nina says kindly.
Ona hvata kofer.She grabs hold of the suitcase.

Ispod prozora se već nalazi jedna torba.There already lies a bag under the window.
„Mnogo hvala na pomoći, Nina,” kažem radosno."Thank you very much for the help, Nina," I say happily.
„Nema na čemu,” smeši se ona."You are welcome," she smiles.

Zatvaram vrata i gledam u kofer pored prozora.I close the door and look at the suitcase by the window.
