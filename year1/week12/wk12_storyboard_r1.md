# Week 12 — Storyboard (Maya joins a local badminton club)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Verb + infinitive; *godt kan lide at*; *spille/dyrke*

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_packing_the_bag | Maya sits in her apartment on a Thursday evening and packs her sports bag. She wants to play badminton tonight. She really likes to play the sport and hopes to join a good local club. |
| 2 | 02_the_busy_hall | Maya enters a large sports hall in her neighborhood. She hears the loud squeak of indoor shoes and sees many people who love to exercise. She walks directly toward the reception area. |
| 3 | 03_renting_a_racket | Maya speaks to the clerk at the desk. She says that she wishes to join the badminton club and needs to rent a racket for the evening. The clerk smiles, registers her, and hands her a racket. |
| 4 | 04_stretching_out | Maya finds an empty spot near the courts to put on her indoor shoes. She begins to stretch her legs and arms. She always prefers to warm up properly before she starts to play. |
| 5 | 05_joining_the_game | Maya walks over to a court where two friendly people, Kasper and Sara, stand. They introduce themselves and ask if Maya wants to join them to play a doubles match. |
| 6 | 06_fast_rallies | The match begins, and Maya immediately shows that she knows how to play well. She likes to hit the shuttlecock hard and fast. She and her partner run quickly around the court to return every shot. |
| 7 | 07_water_break | The players pause the game to drink some water. Kasper and Sara tell Maya that they really like to play against her. They chat happily about their favorite ways to stay active. |
| 8 | 08_next_thursday | The match finishes, and the players shake hands across the net. Sara asks if Maya plans to come back. Maya smiles and promises to return next Thursday to play with them again. |
| 9 | 09_a_good_evening | Maya steps out of the sports hall into the dark winter night. She feels exhausted but very happy. She loves to meet new people and looks forward to her new weekly routine. |
