Week 7: Maya plans a busy week

planning the week
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya makes a cup of tea and opens her calendar at the kitchen table — she always plans the week here. She works through it: the bike goes to the repair shop, the salon needs a phone call, the gym is on Wednesday. She writes each one in, and February fills up.
Ponedjeljak je ujutro.It is Monday morning.
Pravim šalicu vrućeg čaja.I make a cup of hot tea.
Sjedim za stolom u kuhinji.I sit at the table in the kitchen.
Otvaram svoj kalendar.I open my calendar.
Ovdje uvijek planiram svoj tjedan.I always plan my week here.
Sada je veljača.It is February now.
Mjesec je već jako ispunjen.The month is already very full.
Sutra je utorak.Tomorrow is Tuesday.
Moj bicikl je, nažalost, pokvaren.My bicycle is unfortunately broken.
Sutra ujutro idem pješice do servisera za bicikle.I walk to the bicycle mechanic tomorrow morning.
On otvara u osam sati.He opens at eight o'clock.
Zapisujem to u kalendar.I write it in the calendar.
Danas također zovem frizera.I also call the hairdresser today.
Kosa mi je stvarno preduga.My hair is way too long.
U srijedu idem u teretanu.Wednesday I go to the gym.
Često treniram srijedom.I often train on Wednesdays.
To je obično u sedam sati navečer.It is usually at seven o'clock in the evening.
Ponovno gledam svoj plan.I look at my plan again.
Pijem svoj čaj.I drink my tea.
Sada sam spremna za novi tjedan.Now I am ready for a new week.
dropping off the bike
Storyboard: On Tuesday morning at a quarter past eight, Maya wheels her bicycle to the local repair shop. She hands the bike to the mechanic, leaves the shop, and walks the rest of the way to the office.

Na poslu sam u četvrt do devet.I am at work at a quarter to nine.
booking a haircut
Storyboard: During her lunch break, Maya calls a hair salon on her mobile phone. She speaks to the hairdresser and books an appointment for Thursday at half past four.
Pauza mi je za ručak.It is my lunch break.
Sjedim u kantini na poslu.I am sitting in the canteen at my work.
Jučer sam bila zauzeta.I was busy yesterday.
Zato sada zovem frizera.Therefore I am calling the hairdresser now.
Uzimam svoj mobitel.I take my mobile phone.
Zovem frizerski salon u gradu.I call a hair salon in the city.
Žena se javlja na telefon.A woman answers the phone.
"Dobar dan, ovdje frizerski salon", kaže žena."Hello, this is the hair salon," says the woman.
"Dobar dan, zovem se Maya", kažem."Hello, my name is Maya," I say.
"Htjela bih rezervirati termin u četvrtak.""I would like to book an appointment on Thursday."
"Samo trenutak", kaže frizerka."One moment," says the hairdresser.
"Imate li termin poslijepodne?" pitam."Do you have an appointment in the afternoon?" I ask.
"Da, imamo slobodan termin u pola pet", kaže ona."Yes, we have an available appointment at half past four," she says.
"To mi jako dobro odgovara", kažem."That suits me very well," I say.
"Koji je vaš broj telefona?" pita frizerka."What is your phone number?" asks the hairdresser.
"Moj broj je dvanaest, trideset četiri, pedeset šest, sedamdeset osam", kažem."My number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
"Puno hvala, vidimo se u četvrtak u pola pet", kaže ona."Thank you very much, see you on Thursday at half past four," she says.
"Vidimo se", odgovaram."See you," I reply.

hallway chat
Storyboard: After the gym, Maya bumps into Nina in the stairwell. They stand and chat cheerfully, and they agree to drink coffee together on Thursday afternoon at half past four.

Veselim se našem dogovoru za kavu.I am looking forward to our coffee date.
scheduling conflict
