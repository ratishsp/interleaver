# Week 3 — Storyboard (Maya calls her family in Mexico)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** possessives *min/mit, din/dit*; indefinite *en/et*; recurring week 1–2
grammar (present of *være/hedde/komme fra*, *har*, *der er*, subject pronouns, *hvad/hvor*) ·
**Target:** ~34 min.

The curriculum row ("A video call to her family abroad") decomposes into 14 beats. The single-call
narrative fits 14 cleanly (the ramp resumes wk4+). Maya is still in her small **temporary** flat (her
own place comes in week 4); she now has a phone and is registered (wk2). The week's job: possessives
and *en/et* nouns lived through family — *min mor, mit køkken, en hund, et hus* — in a warm evening
call home. Recurring cast: her family in Mexico (mor, far, sister **Sofía**, brother **Diego**, dog
**Lola**) and Nina (neighbour). Season: still early winter (decoupled).

|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_i_miss_them | Evening in Copenhagen. Maya is alone in her flat. She thinks about her family in Mexico. She misses them. Now she calls them on the phone. She is a little excited to call. |
