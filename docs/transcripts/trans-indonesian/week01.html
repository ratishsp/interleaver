Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Indonesian with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
Pesawatnya ada di Kopenhagen sekarang.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
Sekarang bulan Januari.It is January.
Saya sangat bersemangat.I am very excited.
Saya memasukkan buku saya ke dalam tas.I pack my book into my bag.
Ada seorang penumpang di sebelah saya.There is a passenger next to me.
Dia menatap saya dan tersenyum.He looks at me and smiles.
"Halo," katanya."Hello," he says.
"Halo," kata saya."Hello," I say.
"Siapa namamu?" tanyanya."What is your name?" he asks.
"Nama saya Maya," kata saya."My name is Maya," I say.
"Kamu berasal dari mana, Maya?" tanyanya."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
Saya tersenyum gembira.I smile happily.
"Saya berasal dari Meksiko," jawab saya."I come from Mexico," I answer.
"Apakah kamu dari Denmark?" tanya saya."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
"Ya, saya dari Denmark," katanya."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
"Selamat datang di Denmark," katanya."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
"Terima kasih banyak," kata saya."Thank you very much," I say.
Sekarang kita di sini.Now we are here.
Saya siap.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
Saya di luar sekarang.I am outside now.
Koper saya besar dan berat.My suitcase is big and heavy.
Ada sebuah taksi.There is a taxi.
Saya mengangkat koper saya ke dalam taksi.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
Sopir itu adalah pria yang ramah.The driver is a friendly man.
"Apa alamatnya?" tanyanya."What is the address?" he asks.
"Parkvej nomor dua belas," kata saya."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
Dia mengangguk dan melaju.He nods and drives.
"Apakah Anda seorang turis?" tanya sopir itu."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
"Bukan, saya bukan turis," jawab saya."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
"Anda berasal dari mana?" tanyanya."Where do you come from?" he asks.
"Saya berasal dari Meksiko," kata saya."I come from Mexico," I say.
Saya melihat ke luar jendela.I look out the window.
Saya sangat bangga.I am very proud.
"Saya seorang insinyur perangkat lunak," kata saya."I am a software engineer," I say.
"Pekerjaan baru saya di sini, di Kopenhagen.""My new job is here in Copenhagen."
Dia tersenyum.He smiles.
"Menarik sekali.""How exciting."
"Semoga sukses di Denmark," katanya."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
"Terima kasih banyak," kata saya."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
Pintunya terbuka.The door is open.
Ambang pintunya tinggi.The threshold is high.
Kopernya sangat berat!The suitcase is very heavy!
Tapi sekarang kopernya ada di lorong.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
Di sini benar-benar gelap.It is completely dark here.
Di mana sakelarnya?Where is the switch?
Dindingnya dingin.The wall is cold.
Apakah sakelarnya ada di sini?Is the switch here?
Ya, itu sakelarnya!Yes, there is the switch!
Sekarang ada cahaya.Now there is light.
Saya ada di apartemen.I am in the apartment.
Tempatnya kecil, tapi bagus.It is small, but it is nice.
Di sini ada ruang tamu.Here is a living room.
Ada sofa dan meja.There is a sofa and a table.
Ini rumah yang sudah berperabotan.It is a furnished home.
Kopernya ada di lantai.The suitcase is on the floor.
Saya sangat lelah.I am very tired.
Tapi saya juga senang.But I am also happy.
Saya ada di Kopenhagen!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
Aku berdiri di dekat jendela.I am standing by the window.
Kacanya dingin.The glass is cold.
Ini bulan Januari.It is January.
Aku melihat ke luar.I look out.
Kopenhagen kelabu hari ini.Copenhagen is grey today.
Di luar dingin.It is cold outside.
Ada salju di jalanan.There is snow on the street.
Tapi di sini hangat.But it is warm here.
Apartemen ini bagus.The apartment is good.
Aku memikirkan Meksiko.I think about Mexico.
Di Meksiko, ada matahari.In Mexico, there is sun.
Di sana juga hangat.It is also warm there.
Aku sedikit merindukan matahari.I miss the sun a little.
Tapi aku tersenyum.But I smile.
Ini adalah kota baru.It is a new city.
Aku berada di Denmark sekarang.I am in Denmark now.
Ini adalah awal yang baik.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
Aku berada di ruang tamu sekarang.I am in the living room now.
Ada sebuah sofa kecil.There is a small sofa.
Aku menelepon ibuku di Meksiko.I call my mother in Mexico.
"Hai Ibu," kataku."Hi Mom," I say.
"Hai Maya," kata ibuku."Hi Maya," my mother says.
"Kamu di mana?" tanyanya."Where are you?" she asks.
"Aku di Kopenhagen," jawabku."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
"Aku ada di apartemen sekarang.""I am in the apartment now."
"Bagus sekali," kata ibuku."That is so good," my mother says.
"Apakah tempatnya besar?" tanyanya."Is it big?" she asks.
"Tidak, tempatnya kecil," jawabku."No, it is small," I answer.
"Tapi nyaman, dan aku senang.""But it is cozy, and I am happy."
"Apakah di sana dingin?" tanyanya."Is it cold?" she asks.
"Ya, di luar dingin," kataku."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
"Tapi di dalam sini hangat.""But it is warm in here."
"Aku aman.""I am safe."
"Baguslah," kata ibuku."That is good," my mother says.
Aku tersenyum.I smile.
Denmark adalah rumah baruku.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
Saya membuka pintu saya.I open my door.
Saya berdiri di lorong tangga.I am standing in the stairwell.
Suasananya sepi.It is quiet.
Sebuah pintu terbuka.A door opens.
Seorang wanita tersenyum kepada saya.A woman smiles at me.
"Hai," katanya."Hi," she says.
"Siapa namamu?" tanyanya."What is your name?" she asks.
"Nama saya Maya," jawab saya."My name is Maya," I answer.
"Kamu berasal dari mana?" tanyanya."Where do you come from?" she asks.
"Saya berasal dari Meksiko," kata saya."I come from Mexico," I say.
"Selamat datang di gedung ini," kata wanita itu."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
"Nama saya Nina," katanya."My name is Nina," she says.
"Saya tetanggamu.""I am your neighbour."
Nina sangat ramah.Nina is very friendly.
Dia berbicara perlahan dan jelas.She speaks slowly and clearly.
"Ini pintu saya," katanya."Here is my door," she says.
"Saya senang bisa membantu.""I am happy to help."
"Kamu tinggal mengetuk saja," kata Nina."You just knock," Nina says.
"Terima kasih banyak, Nina," jawab saya."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
Saya sangat senang.I am very happy.
Sekarang saya punya teman di Kopenhagen.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
Sekarang sudah malam.Now it is night.
Ini adalah malam pertamaku di Kopenhagen.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
Aku berada di kamar mandi.I am in the bathroom.
Ukurannya sangat kecil.It is very small.
Ada sebuah wastafel.There is a sink.
Sikat gigiku berwarna merah.My toothbrush is red.
Aku menaruh pasta gigi di sikat gigiku.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
Aku menyikat gigiku.I brush my teeth.
Airnya dingin.The water is cold.
Aku mencuci wajahku.I wash my face.
Aku mengeringkan wajahku.I dry my face.
Aku berjalan menuju tempat tidurku.I go over to my bed.
Tempat tidurnya sempit.The bed is narrow.
Tapi terasa hangat dan empuk.But it is warm and soft.
Aku berbaring di bawah selimut sekarang.I am lying under the duvet now.
Ponselku tergeletak di atas meja.My phone is lying on the table.
Aku menyetel alarmku.I set my alarm.
Ini akan berbunyi pada pukul delapan besok.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
Aku lelah, tapi aku siap.I am tired, but I am ready.
Selamat malam, Kopenhagen.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
