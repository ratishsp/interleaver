# Week 23 — Storyboard (Maya visits Eva for dinner and makes future plans)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Future *skal/vil*; *har tænkt mig at*; *synes/tror/føler at* + subordinate *at*-clause word order (needs a sentence adverb — *ikke/altid/nok* — in the clause to show)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_office_invitation | At the software company, Eva warmly invites Maya to her home for dinner this Friday. Maya gladly accepts, smiling, and says that she will definitely be there. |
| 2 | 02_planning_the_route | During a break, Maya looks up directions to the apartment on her phone. She sees it is quite far across town, so she thinks that she will probably take the bus rather than ride her bicycle. |
| 3 | 03_buying_a_gift | On Friday afternoon, Maya stops at a local florist after work. She intends to bring a bright bouquet of yellow tulips, believing that Eva, being a designer, will always appreciate colorful things. |
| 4 | 04_taking_the_bus | Waiting at the busy bus stop, Maya checks the timetable. She feels that the evening traffic is often slow on Fridays, but she expects a smooth ride today because the roads look surprisingly clear. |
| 5 | 05_arriving_at_evas | Maya rings the doorbell at Eva's apartment building. Eva welcomes her warmly into a highly creative, cozy space, and Maya gives her the flowers, hoping that she does not already have a full vase on the table. |
| 6 | 06_dinner_conversation | They sit down at a beautifully set table to enjoy a lovely vegetarian meal. Maya tells Eva that she intends to learn how to cook more local dishes soon, because she thinks that the fresh ingredients are really delicious. |
| 7 | 07_settling_in | Over a cup of coffee, they discuss Maya's new life since January. Maya explains that she feels that she is finally finding her rhythm in the city, and she announces that she intends to stay for a very long time. |
| 8 | 08_future_museum_plan | Before Maya leaves, Eva proposes a weekend trip to an art museum next month. Maya loves the idea, stating she believes that they will certainly have fun, and they promise each other to finalize the plans soon. |
