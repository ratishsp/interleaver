Week 15: Maya's everyday life in the city

Swahili with English gloss, line-aligned.

morning weather
Storyboard: Maya wakes up early and looks out the window. The sun shines, but the wind is cold, so she puts on a warm sweater for the day.
Ninaamka mapema.I wake up early.
Ni saa kumi na mbili asubuhi tu.It is only six o'clock.
Ni siku mpya.It is a new day.
Ninainuka polepole.I get up slowly.
Ninasogea dirishani.I walk over to the window.
Ninatazama nje kwenye mji.I look out at the city.
Bado kuna giza na baridi nje.It is still dark and cold outside.
Majira ya baridi bado yapo hapa.Winter is still here.
Ninaingia bafuni.I go into the bathroom.
Ninanawa uso.I wash my face.
Ninapiga mswaki.I brush my teeth.
Kisha ninavaa nguo.Then I get dressed.
Ninavaa sweta nene, la joto.I put on a thick, warm sweater.
Linafaa kabisa kwa siku ya baridi.It is perfect for a cold day.
Ninaenda jikoni.I go into the kitchen.
Ninatengeneza kikombe cha kahawa ya moto.I make a cup of warm coffee.
Ninakula kipande cha mkate na jibini.I eat a slice of bread with cheese.
Sasa nimeamka kabisa.Now I am completely awake.
Niko tayari kwa siku mpya.I am ready for the new day.
the bus commute
Storyboard: She walks to the bus stop and waits for her daily transport. The yellow bus arrives, she steps inside, scans her travel card, and rides through the busy streets.
Natoka nje barabarani.I walk out onto the street.
Ni asubuhi ya kawaida.It is a normal morning.
Natembea hadi kwenye kituo cha basi.I walk down to the bus stop.
Watu wengi wamesimama hapo.Many people are standing there.
Wao pia wanasubiri basi.They are also waiting for the bus.
Natazama mbele barabarani.I look down the road.
Basi kubwa jekundu linawasili.A big red bus arrives.
Ni basi namba tano.It is bus number five.
Basi linasimama mbele yangu.The bus stops in front of me.
Milango inafunguka.The doors open.
Naingia ndani ya basi.I step inside the bus.
Naipata kadi yangu ya usafiri kwenye begi langu.I find my travel card in my bag.
Naiskani kadi ya bluu kwenye mashine.I scan the blue card on the machine.
Mashine inatoa mlio.The machine beeps.
Natembea hadi nyuma ya basi.I walk to the back of the bus.
Napata kiti kitupu karibu na dirisha.I find an empty seat by the window.
Nakaa chini.I sit down.
Natazama watu walioko barabarani.I look at the people in the street.
Tunapita kwenye barabara zenye shughuli nyingi.We drive through the busy streets.
Niko tayari kwa siku mpya ya kazi.I am ready for a new workday.
working with peter
Storyboard: Maya arrives at the software company and says good morning to her friendly colleague Peter. She sits at her desk, drinks black coffee, and types code on her computer.
Ninafika ofisini.I arrive at the office.
Ni kampuni kubwa ya programu.It is a large software company.
Ninaingia ndani.I walk inside.
Mfanyakazi mwenzangu Peter tayari yuko hapo.My colleague Peter is already there.
Yeye ni mkarimu sana.He is very friendly.
"Habari za asubuhi, Maya," Peter anasema."Good morning, Maya," Peter says.
"Habari za asubuhi, Peter," ninasema."Good morning, Peter," I say.
Ninaenda kwenye nafasi yangu.I walk over to my space.
Ninakaa kwenye dawati langu.I sit at my desk.
Ninawasha kompyuta yangu.I turn on my computer.
Nina kikombe cha kahawa.I have a cup of coffee.
Ni nyeusi na ya moto.It is black and hot.
Ninakunywa kahawa.I drink the coffee.
Ninafanya kazi kama mhandisi wa programu.I work as a software engineer.
Ninatazama skrini.I look at the screen.
Ninaandika kodi kwenye kompyuta yangu.I type code on my computer.
Kazi ni ya kusisimua sana.The work is very exciting.
Nina furaha na kazi yangu.I am happy with my job.
canteen lunch
Storyboard: At noon, Maya and Peter go down to the bright canteen together. She chooses a classic open sandwich with fish and pays forty kroner at the register.
Ni saa sita.It is twelve o'clock.
Ni wakati wa chakula cha mchana.It is lunch time.
Mimi na Peter tunashuka kwenye kantini.Peter and I go down to the canteen.
Kantini ni kubwa na yenye mwanga.The canteen is large and bright.
Tuna njaa sana.We are very hungry.
Tunatazama chakula kizuri.We look at the good food.
Kuna machaguo mengi leo.There are many options today.
Ninachagua sandwichi wazi ya asili.I choose a classic open sandwich.
Ina samaki na limau.It is with fish and lemon.
Peter anachukua saladi kubwa.Peter takes a large salad.
Tunatembea hadi kwenye sehemu ya malipo.We walk over to the register.
Mwanamume mkarimu amesimama kwenye sehemu ya malipo.A friendly man stands at the register.
"Itakuwa kroner arobaini," ananiambia."That will be forty kroner," he says to me.
Ninalipa kwa kadi yangu.I pay with my card.
"Hii hapa," ninasema."Here you go," I say.
"Asante, na ufurahie chakula chako," anajibu."Thank you, and enjoy your meal," he answers.
Tunapata meza iliyo wazi kwenye kona.We find a free table in the corner.
Tunaketi.We sit down.
Chakula kina ladha nzuri sana.The food tastes really good.
Tunazungumza kuhusu siku yetu.We talk about our day.
giving directions
Storyboard: After work, Maya steps out into a large square. A tourist asks for the way to a local museum, and she politely explains that they must walk straight ahead and then turn left.
Nimetoka kazini.I am off from work.
Ninatembea kuelekea kwenye uwanja mkubwa.I walk out onto a large square.
Kuna watu wengi mjini leo.There are many people in the city today.
Mwanamume mmoja ananikaribia.A man walks up to me.
Ana kamera kubwa.He has a large camera.
Yeye ni mtalii.He is a tourist.
Anatabasamu na kusema hujambo.He smiles and says hi.
"Jumba la makumbusho liko wapi?" anauliza mtalii."Where is the museum?" the tourist asks.
Nalijua vizuri jumba la makumbusho.I know the museum well.
Haliko mbali.It is not far away.
Ninaonyesha mbele mtaani.I point down the street.
"Unatembea moja kwa moja," ninaeleza."You walk straight ahead," I explain.
"Kisha unapinda kushoto.""Then you turn to the left."
"Jumba la makumbusho liko upande wa kulia.""The museum is on the right side."
"Asante sana kwa msaada," mwanamume anasema."Thank you very much for the help," the man says.
"Karibu," ninajibu kwa furaha."You are welcome," I answer happily.
Anatembea mbele mtaani.He walks down the street.
Ninaendelea kutembea kuelekea kwenye duka la mikate.I walk on towards the bakery.
buying a cake
Storyboard: On the way home, she stops at a small bakery on the corner. She orders a chocolate cake for the evening and pays sixty-five kroner to the smiling baker.
Ninafika kwenye duka dogo la mikate kwenye kona.I arrive at the small bakery on the corner.
Ninaingia ndani ya duka lenye joto.I go into the warm shop.
Kunanukia vizuri kahawa na keki.It smells lovely of coffee and cake.
Mwokaji aliyevaa eproni nyeupe amesimama nyuma ya kaunta.A baker in a white apron stands behind the counter.
"Mchana mwema," mwokaji anasema."Good afternoon," the baker says.
"Naweza kukusaidia vipi?" anauliza."How can I help?" he asks.
Ninatazama keki nyingi.I look at the many cakes.
Ninanunua keki kwa ajili ya leo usiku.I am buying a cake for tonight.
Ninainyooshea kidole keki nzuri ya chokoleti.I point at a nice chocolate cake.
"Ninaomba keki ya chokoleti, tafadhali," ninasema."I would like the chocolate cake, please," I say.
"Bila shaka," mwokaji anajibu."Of course," the baker answers.
Anaweka keki ndani ya boksi jeupe.He puts the cake in a white box.
"Itakuwa kroner sitini na tano," anasema."That will be sixty-five kroner," he says.
Ninatoa pochi yangu kwenye mkoba wangu.I take my wallet out of my bag.
Ninalipa zile kroner sitini na tano.I pay the sixty-five kroner.
"Asante sana, na uwe na jioni njema," mwokaji anasema."Thank you very much, and have a good evening," the baker says.
"Asante, nawe pia," ninajibu."Thank you, you too," I answer.
Ninachukua lile boksi jeupe.I take the white box.
Ninatoka nje ya duka kwa uangalifu nikiwa na keki.I walk carefully out of the shop with the cake.
arriving home
Storyboard: Maya takes the bus back to her familiar street. She unlocks the front door of her building and feels happy and completely at home in her new neighborhood.
Ninasimama kwenye kituo cha basi na keki yangu.I stand at the bus stop with my cake.
Basi jekundu linakuja haraka.The red bus comes quickly.
Ninapanda na kupata kiti.I get on and find a seat.
Basi linapita katikati ya jiji.The bus drives through the city.
Safari si ndefu.The trip is not long.
Punde tuko kwenye mtaa wangu.Soon we are at my street.
Ninashuka kwenye basi.I get off the bus.
Hewa ya jioni ina baridi kidogo sasa.The evening air is a little cold now.
Lakini nimevaa sweta langu la joto.But I have my warm sweater on.
Ninatembea mtaani.I walk down the street.
Ninayajua majengo vizuri sasa.I know the buildings well now.
Hili hapa jengo langu.Here is my building.
Ninazipata funguo zangu kwenye mkoba wangu.I find my keys in my bag.
Ninafungua mlango mkuu mzito.I unlock the heavy front door.
Ninaingia kwenye eneo la ngazi.I walk into the stairwell.
Hapa kuna utulivu na amani.It is quiet and peaceful here.
Ninafungua mlango wangu mwenyewe na kuingia ndani.I unlock my own door and go in.
Ninaweka mkoba wangu kwenye korido.I put my bag in the hallway.
Sasa nipo nyumbani katika jiji langu mwenyewe.Now I am home in my own city.
evening with nina
Storyboard: She knocks on her neighbor Nina's door and holds up the sweet cake. They sit in the cozy living room, drink tea, and chat about their day in the city.
Ninaenda kwenye mlango wa Nina.I walk over to Nina's door.
Ninagonga mlango.I knock on the door.
Nina anafungua na kutabasamu.Nina opens and smiles.
Ninamuonyesha keki ya chokoleti.I show her the chocolate cake.
"Hujambo Nina," ninasema."Hi Nina," I say.
"Nina keki kutoka duka la mikate.""I have a cake from the bakery."
"Hujambo Maya, inapendeza sana!" Nina anasema."Hi Maya, how lovely!" Nina says.
Tunaingia sebuleni kwake.We go into her living room.
Sebule ina joto na inastarehesha sana.The living room is very warm and cozy.
Tunakaa kwenye sofa laini.We sit on the soft sofa.
Nina anatutengenezea chai ya moto.Nina makes hot tea for us.
Tunakunywa chai na kula keki tamu.We drink tea and eat the sweet cake.
Keki ya chokoleti ina ladha nzuri kweli.The chocolate cake tastes really good.
Tunazungumza kuhusu siku yetu mjini.We talk about our day in the city.
Nina anauliza kuhusu kazi yangu.Nina asks about my work.
Ninaeleza kuhusu ofisi na mfanyakazi mwenzangu Peter.I talk about the office and my colleague Peter.
Nina pia anaeleza kuhusu siku yake.Nina also talks about her day.
Tunacheka na kuzungumza jioni nzima.We laugh and talk all evening.
Ni jioni nzuri kweli.It is a really good evening.
Ninajihisi nyumbani hapa katika jiji langu jipya.I feel at home here in my new city.
