# Week 7 — Storyboard (Maya plans a busy week of appointments)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Clock time **മണി** (എത്ര മണിക്ക്?); weekdays & months; frequency adverbs **എപ്പോഴും/ചിലപ്പോൾ/എല്ലാ ദിവസവും**

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_calendar_planning | Maya drinks a hot cup of tea, a routine she enjoys every day. It is February, and she opens her new calendar to plan her schedule. She always goes to the gym on Mondays and Wednesdays. |
| 2 | 02_dropping_bicycle | On Tuesday morning before work, Maya walks to a small bicycle repair shop near her flat. The mechanic is fixing a wheel and tells Maya to return on Wednesday at six fifteen in the evening. |
| 3 | 03_salon_phone_call | Later on Tuesday, Maya calls a local beauty parlor. A polite woman answers the phone. Maya books a haircut for Thursday at exactly five thirty in the evening. |
| 4 | 04_anithas_invitation | Maya is working at her office desk on Wednesday. Her neighbor Anitha sends a message and suggests a coffee date for Thursday at five thirty in the evening. Maya types a quick yes on her phone. |
| 5 | 05_picking_up_bicycle | After finishing her workday on Wednesday, Maya returns to the repair shop. The time is exactly six fifteen in the evening. She takes her repaired bicycle and rides it happily toward her flat. |
| 6 | 06_the_double_booking | Back inside her flat, Maya writes the coffee date into her calendar and sees the haircut appointment at the exact same time. She immediately calls the beauty parlor again, apologizes, and reschedules her haircut to Friday at six forty-five in the evening. |
| 7 | 07_thursday_coffee | It is Thursday at five thirty. Maya meets Anitha at a popular cafe in Panampilly Nagar. They drink cold coffee and talk about their busy work weeks. |
| 8 | 08_friday_haircut | On Friday evening, Maya arrives at the beauty parlor at six forty-five. The hairdresser washes Maya's hair and cuts it short. Maya looks in the mirror and smiles broadly at her new style. |
| 9 | 09_family_video_call | Sometimes Maya calls her family on Friday nights. She shows her sister Sofia the new haircut through the video screen. The family dog, Lola, is playing happily in the background back in Mexico. |
