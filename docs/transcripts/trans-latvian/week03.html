Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
Ir janvāris, un ir ļoti auksts.It is January, and it is very cold.
Es eju mājās no autobusa pieturas Kopenhāgenā.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Es redzu ziedu veikalu uz ielas.I see a flower shop on the street.
Tur iekšā ir gaišs un silts.It is bright and warm inside.
Es ieeju siltajā veikalā.I go into the warm shop.
Es skatos uz mazu, zaļu augu.I look at a small, green plant.
Pārdevējs man uzsmaida.A clerk smiles at me.
"Sveiki," viņš saka."Hello," he says.
"Sveiki," es saku."Hello," I say.
"Es meklēju augu.""I am looking for a plant."
"Vai tā ir dāvana?" viņš jautā."Is it a gift?" he asks.
"Nē, tas ir manai istabai," es atbildu."No, it is for my room," I answer.
"Tas ir patiešām labs augs," viņš saka."It is a really good plant," he says.
"Cik tas maksā?" es jautāju."How much does it cost?" I ask.
"Tas maksā piecdesmit piecas kronas," viņš atbild."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
Es maksāju par savu jauno augu.I pay for my new plant.
Viņš ieliek augu papīra maisiņā.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
"Lūdzu, šeit ir tavs maisiņš," viņš saka."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
"Liels paldies," es priecīgi saku."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Es izeju ārā aukstajā vakarā.I go out into the cold evening.
Mans mazais augs padara mani ļoti priecīgu.My little plant makes me very happy.
evening prep
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her temporary room and sets the new plant on a small desk. With it already being pitch black outside, she turns on a warm lamp to reflect against the dark window, makes a cup of tea, and flips open her laptop to dial home.

parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
Tas iezvanās pāris reizes.It rings a couple of times.
Ekrāns iedegas.The screen lights up.
Es redzu viesistabu.I see a living room.
Spīd saule, un viesistaba ir ļoti gaiša.There is sun, and the living room is very bright.
Es redzu divus cilvēkus.I see two people.
Tā ir mana mamma un mans tētis.It is my mother and my father.
Viņi daudz smaida un māj man.They smile a lot and wave to me.
"Sveiki, mamma un tēti!" es saku skaļi."Hello mom and dad!" I say loudly.
"Sveika, Maya," saka mans tētis."Hello, Maya," my father says.
"Kā iet?""How is it going?"
"Iet labi," es priecīgi atbildu."It is going well," I answer happily.
Pēkšņi es izdzirdu skaņu.Suddenly I hear a sound.
Tas ir mazs rējiens.It is a little bark.
"Ak, es dzirdu suni!" es saku un smejos."Oh, I hear a dog!" I say and laugh.
Mazs suns uzlec uz dīvāna.A little dog jumps up onto a sofa.
Tas skatās kamerā.It looks into the camera.
"Tur ir mans suns!""There is my dog!"
"Sveika, Lola," es saku."Hello, Lola," I say.
Lola luncina asti.Lola wags her tail.
"Jā, tas ir tavs suns," ar smaidu saka mana mamma."Yes, it is your dog," my mother says with a smile.

Ir jauki viņus visus redzēt.It is lovely to see them all.
the siblings
