Week 16: Maya tells the story of how she came to Berlin

thursday badminton
Storyboard: It is Thursday evening at half past five. Maya is at the badminton club. She rents a racket and plays a lively doubles match with Timo, Lena and Max. They cheer each other on, Afterwards Max says goodbye — he has an early start tomorrow — and the other three stay behind.

Timo, Lena und Max warten schon auf dem Platz.Timo, Lena and Max are already waiting on the court.
„Hallo zusammen!“, rufe ich."Hello everyone!" I call out.
„Habt ihr letzte Woche gut trainiert?“, frage ich."Did you train well last week?" I ask.
„Ja, wir haben viel geübt“, antwortet Timo."Yes, we practised a lot," Timo answers.

Timo und ich spielen gegen Lena und Max.Timo and I play against Lena and Max.
Das Spiel ist sehr schnell und macht viel Spaß.The game is very fast and is a lot of fun.

„Ich sage tschüss“, sagt er."I am saying goodbye," he says.

Timo, Lena und ich bleiben noch ein bisschen.Timo, Lena and I stay a little longer.
the berlin question
Storyboard: After the game they sit down to rest. Timo and Lena tell her about a tournament they played last week, then ask Maya why she chose Berlin and how she found her job. Maya begins her story: she found the advertisement in October and applied straight away. Then she waited a long time — papers, forms, an appointment at the embassy. In January she finally packed her suitcase.
Nach dem Spiel sitzen wir auf der Bank.After the game we sit on the bench.
Wir trinken Wasser und machen eine Pause.We drink water and take a break.
Timo und Lena erzählen von einem Turnier.Timo and Lena talk about a tournament.
Sie haben letzte Woche ein Turnier gespielt.They played a tournament last week.
Dann sieht Lena mich an.Then Lena looks at me.
"Warum eigentlich Berlin, Maya?", fragt sie."Why Berlin anyway, Maya?" she asks.
"Und wie hast du deinen Job gefunden?""And how did you find your job?"
Ich lächle und beginne meine Geschichte.I smile and begin my story.
"Ich habe die Anzeige im Oktober gesehen.""I saw the advertisement in October."
"Ich habe sofort eine Bewerbung geschrieben.""I wrote an application straight away."
"Dann habe ich sehr lange gewartet.""Then I waited a very long time."
"Ich habe viele Papiere gebraucht.""I needed a lot of papers."
"Ich habe Formulare ausgefüllt.""I filled out forms."
"Ich habe einen Termin bei der Botschaft gemacht.""I made an appointment at the embassy."
Das hat alles viel Zeit gekostet.That all took a lot of time.
"Aber im Januar habe ich endlich meinen Koffer gepackt.""But in January I finally packed my suitcase."
Timo lacht und nimmt seinen Schläger.Timo laughs and picks up his racket.
"Dann haben wir ja Glück gehabt", sagt er."Then we got lucky," he says.
taxi and stairs
Storyboard: Maya carries the story on. At the airport she paid for a taxi and gave the driver her new address. She dragged her heavy suitcase up the stairs, and on that very first day she met her neighbour Sabine and the dog Bruno. Timo and Lena laugh at the picture of it.
Ich erzähle Timo und Lena meine Geschichte weiter.I continue telling Timo and Lena my story.
„Im Januar habe ich am Flughafen sofort ein Taxi genommen“, sage ich."In January I immediately took a taxi at the airport," I say.
„Ich habe dem Fahrer meine neue Adresse gegeben.“"I gave the driver my new address."
„Am Haus habe ich dann die Fahrt bezahlt.“"At the house I then paid for the trip."
Timo lächelt und trinkt etwas Wasser.Timo smiles and drinks some water.
„Und dann?“, fragt Lena neugierig."And then?" Lena asks curiously.
„Dann habe ich meine neue Wohnung gesehen“, antworte ich."Then I saw my new flat," I answer.

Lena lacht laut.Lena laughs out loud.
„Das habe ich auch schon oft gemacht“, sagt sie."I have often done that too," she says.
„An diesem ersten Tag habe ich auch meine Nachbarin getroffen“, erzähle ich."On that first day I also met my neighbor," I tell them.
„Ich habe Sabine auf der Treppe gesehen.“"I saw Sabine on the stairs."

first office day recap

„Was hast du an deinem ersten Tag im Büro gemacht?“, fragt sie."What did you do on your first day at the office?" she asks.

calling home

the airport morning
Storyboard: Her mother was there for the goodbye, but she has never heard Maya's own version of that morning, and she asks for it now. So Maya tells her the part she could not see: how she counted the people in the queue to keep herself calm, how she has never told anyone that she cried at the gate, and how she watched the family through the glass for as long as she could.

„Was hast du damals am Flughafen gemacht?“, fragt sie."What did you do at the airport back then?" she asks.

her mothers side
