Week 9: Maya visits the winter market

Serbian with English gloss, line-aligned.

arriving at the market
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a busy outdoor market in a large square. The winter morning is cold, but the stalls look inviting. She carries a large empty bag and looks for fresh food for the weekend.
Subota je ujutru.It is Saturday morning.
Zima je u Danskoj.It is winter in Denmark.
Vreme je danas veoma hladno.The weather is very cold today.
Stojim na velikom trgu u gradu.I am standing in a large square in the city.
Ovde je pijaca na otvorenom.There is an outdoor market here.
Gledam mnoge tezge.I look at the many stalls.
Sve izgledaju primamljivo.They all look inviting.
Na trgu ima mnogo ljudi.There are many people in the square.
Ljudi nose toplu zimsku odeću.People are wearing warm winter clothes.
Imam na sebi samo tanku jaknu.I only have a thin jacket on.
Imam veliku torbu sa sobom.I have a large bag with me.
Torba je trenutno potpuno prazna.The bag is completely empty right now.
Tražim svežu hranu za vikend.I am looking for fresh food for the weekend.
Pijaca ima mnogo svežih namirnica.The market has many fresh goods.
Vidim povrće, voće i hleb.I see vegetables, fruit, and bread.
Razmišljam o cenama.I think about the prices.
Koliko stvari ovde koštaju?How much do things cost here?
Prilazim prvoj tezgi.I walk over to the first stall.
carrots for soup
Storyboard: Maya stops at a vegetable stall to buy ingredients for a hot soup. She points at some winter carrots and asks how much they cost. The vendor says they cost fifteen kroner per kilo. Maya asks for one kilo and pays.
Prilazim tezgi sa povrćem.I walk over to a vegetable stall.
Danas pravim toplu supu.I am making a hot soup today.
Gledam mnoštvo povrća na stolu.I look at the many vegetables on the table.
Pokazujem na nekoliko velikih zimskih šargarepa.I point at some large winter carrots.
Potrebne su mi šargarepe za moju supu.I need carrots for my soup.
Iza stola stoji prodavac.There is a vendor standing behind the table.
„Zdravo”, kaže on."Hello," he says.
„Zdravo”, odgovaram."Hello," I reply.
„Koliko koštaju šargarepe?” pitam."How much do the carrots cost?" I ask.
„Koštaju petnaest kruna po kilogramu”, odgovara prodavac."They cost fifteen kroner per kilo," the vendor answers.
Petnaest kruna je dobra cena.Fifteen kroner is a good price.
„Uzeću jedan kilogram, molim vas”, kažem."I will take one kilo, please," I say.
Prodavac meri šargarepe.The vendor weighs the carrots.
Stavlja ih u papirnu kesu.He puts them in a paper bag.
Daje mi kesu.He gives me the bag.
Plaćam petnaest kruna.I pay the fifteen kroner.
Stavljam kesu u svoju veliku torbu.I put the bag down in my large bag.
Radujem se toploj supi.I look forward to the hot soup.
a taste of cheese
Storyboard: At the next stall, a friendly vendor sells large cheeses. He offers Maya a small piece on a wooden stick to taste. She eats it, likes it, and asks for the price of a small block. It costs fifty-five kroner, so she buys it.
Ispred mene je velika tezga sa sirom.In front of me is a large cheese stall.
Na tezgi ima mnogo velikih sireva.The stall has many large cheeses.
Ljubazni prodavac mi se smeši.A friendly vendor smiles at me.
On ima mali komad sira na drvenom štapiću.He has a small piece of cheese on a wooden stick.
„Izvolite, malo za probu”, kaže on."Here you go, a small taste," he says.
Daje mi štapić.He gives the stick to me.
Jedem žuti sir.I eat the yellow cheese.
Stvarno je ukusan.It tastes really good.
Gledam u jedan mali sir.I look at a small cheese.
„Koliko košta ovaj sir?”, pitam."How much does this cheese cost?" I ask.
„Košta pedeset pet kruna”, odgovara prodavac."It costs fifty-five kroner," the vendor answers.
To je dobra cena.That is a good price.
Pokazujem na mali sir.I point at the small cheese.
„Kupiću ga”, kažem."I will buy it," I say.
On umotava sir u papir.He wraps the cheese in paper.
Dajem mu novac.I give him the money.
„Hvala vam puno”, kaže on."Thank you very much," he says.
Sir miriše jako i lepo.The cheese smells strong and good.
the expensive gloves
Storyboard: The wind blows cold, so Maya looks at a stall with warm clothes. She picks up a pair of thick wool gloves and asks how much the gloves cost. The vendor answers that the price is two hundred kroner. Maya thinks that is too dear, so she politely says no thank you and walks on.
Vetar danas hladno duva.The wind blows cold today.
Ruke su mi veoma hladne.My hands are very cold.
Vidim veliku tezgu sa toplom odećom.I see a large stall with warm clothes.
Prilazim prodavcu.I walk over to the vendor.
On ima mnogo toplih stvari na stolu.He has many warm things on the table.
Uzimam par debelih vunenih rukavica.I pick up a pair of thick wool gloves.
Veoma su mekane.They are very soft.
"Koliko koštaju rukavice?" pitam."How much do the gloves cost?" I ask.
"Koštaju dvesta kruna," odgovara prodavac."They cost two hundred kroner," the vendor answers.
Ponovo gledam rukavice.I look at the gloves again.
Dvesta kruna je visoka cena.Two hundred kroner is a high price.
To je previše skupo za mene.It is too expensive for me.
Vraćam rukavice na sto.I put the gloves back on the table.
"Ne, hvala," kažem ljubazno."No thank you," I say politely.
"Prijatan dan.""Have a good day."
Prodavac klima glavom i smeši se.The vendor nods and smiles.
"I vama," kaže on."You too," he says.
Nastavljam dalje po pijaci.I walk on around the market.
buying winter apples
Storyboard: Maya finds a fruit stall with baskets of dark red winter apples. She asks the vendor how much a small bag costs. The vendor smiles and says the price is twenty kroner. Maya hands him a paper note for fifty kroner. The vendor hands her the bag of apples and gives her exactly thirty kroner back in coins. Maya puts the heavy coins into her coat pocket.
Danas ima mnogo ljudi na pijaci.There are many people at the market today.
Nalazim malu tezgu sa voćem.I find a small fruit stall.
Na tezgi ima mnogo korpi.There are many baskets in the stall.
Korpe su pune tamnocrvenih zimskih jabuka.The baskets are full of dark red winter apples.
Gledam malu kesu sa jabukama.I look at a small bag of apples.
"Koliko košta mala kesa?" pitam prodavca."How much does a small bag cost?" I ask the vendor.
On gleda u korpu.He looks in the basket.
"Cena je dvadeset kruna," odgovara on."The price is twenty kroner," he answers.
Tražim novac u svojoj torbi.I look in my bag for money.
Dajem mu novčanicu od pedeset kruna.I give him a fifty-krone note.
Prodavac mi pruža kesu sa jabukama.The vendor hands me the bag of apples.
Zatim mi vraća novac.Then he gives me money back.
Dobijam nazad tačno trideset kruna u kovanicama.I get exactly thirty kroner back in coins.
Kovanice su debele i teške.The coins are thick and heavy.
Stavljam teške kovanice u džep svog kaputa.I put the heavy coins down into my coat pocket.
Jabuke mirišu veoma sveže.The apples smell very fresh.
Stavljam kesu u svoju veliku torbu za kupovinu.I put the bag down into my large shopping bag.
fresh rye bread
Storyboard: Maya smells baked goods and walks over to a baker's stall. She points to a fresh, dark rye bread and asks how much it costs. The baker tells her it costs thirty-five kroner. She pays with her card and places the warm bread in her bag.
Prolazim pored male tezge.I walk past a small stall.
Miriše na svež hleb.It smells of fresh bread.
Prilazim tezgi.I walk over to the stall.
Pekar stoji iza velikog stola.The baker is standing behind a large table.
Sto je pun peciva.The table is full of baked goods.
Vidim tamni ražani hleb.I see a dark rye bread.
Izgleda veoma sveže.It looks very fresh.
Pokazujem na ražani hleb.I point at the rye bread.
"Koliko košta tamni ražani hleb?" pitam."How much does the dark rye bread cost?" I ask.
Pekar briše ruke.The baker wipes his hands.
"Košta trideset pet kruna," odgovara on."It costs thirty-five kroner," he replies.
"Kupujem ga," kažem."I am buying it," I say.
Vadim svoju platnu karticu.I take out my payment card.
Plaćam karticom.I pay with the card.
Pekar mi daje hleb u papirnoj kesi.The baker gives me the bread in a paper bag.
Dodirujem kesu.I touch the bag.
Hleb je još uvek topao.The bread is still warm.
Jedva čekam da ga pojedem.I look forward to eating it.
exact change for coffee
Storyboard: Maya feels cold and spots a small coffee cart. She approaches and asks the barista how much a hot black coffee costs. The barista replies that it costs thirty kroner. Maya takes the new coins from her coat pocket and pays for the coffee with exact change.
Sada mi je veoma hladno.I am very cold now.
Ruke su mi hladne.My hands are cold.
Razgledam po pijaci.I look around the market.
Vidim mala kolica za kafu.I see a small coffee cart.
Mladić stoji iza kolica.A young man stands behind the cart.
Prilazim mu.I walk over to him.
Kažem zdravo baristi.I say hello to the barista.
„Zdravo, koliko košta crna kafa?“ pitam."Hello, how much does a black coffee cost?" I ask.
Barista se smeši.The barista smiles.
„Topla, crna kafa košta trideset kruna“, odgovara on."A hot, black coffee costs thirty kroner," he answers.
Imam novčiće u džepu kaputa.I have coins in my coat pocket.
To je moj kusur od jabuka.It is my change from the apples.
Vadim novčiće.I take the coins out.
Brojim ih.I count them.
Imam tačno trideset kruna.I have exactly thirty kroner.
„Izvolite, evo trideset kruna“, kažem."Here you go, here are thirty kroner," I say.
On uzima novac.He takes the money.
Daje mi toplu kafu.He gives me the hot coffee.
Kafa divno miriše.The coffee smells wonderful.
Grejem ruke na šolji.I warm my hands on the cup.
winter flowers
Storyboard: Maya walks toward the market exit and stops at a flower stall. She points at a bundle of bright winter tulips and asks for the price. The vendor says they cost forty kroner. Maya buys the flowers and walks back to her apartment, drinking her hot coffee and holding the colorful tulips.
Idem prema izlazu sa pijace.I walk towards the exit of the market.
Vidim veliku tezgu sa cvećem.I see a large flower stall.
Na tezgi ima mnogo lepog cveća.The stall has many beautiful flowers.
Zaustavljam se ispred tezge.I stop in front of the stall.
Gledam u buket zimskih lala.I look at a bundle of winter tulips.
Veoma su šarene.They are very colourful.
Pokazujem na lale.I point at the tulips.
"Koliko koštaju lale?" pitam prodavca."How much do the tulips cost?" I ask the vendor.
Prodavac podiže lale.The vendor holds up the tulips.
"Koštaju četrdeset kruna", odgovara on."They cost forty kroner," he answers.
"Kupiću ih", kažem."I will buy them," I say.
Plaćam cveće.I pay for the flowers.
Prodavac mi daje lale.The vendor gives me the tulips.
Sada sam završila na pijaci.Now I am finished at the market.
Idem nazad u svoj stan.I walk back to my apartment.
Držim šarene lale u ruci.I hold the colourful tulips in my hand.
Pijem svoju toplu kafu.I drink my hot coffee.
Sunce sija iznad užurbane pijace.The sun shines over the busy market.
