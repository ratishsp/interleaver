Week 20: Maya starts volunteering at the café

Spanish with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding the volunteering
Storyboard: Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. One evening she browses a volunteering website, reads a few listings, and finds the little café she already knows, which needs weekend helpers, so she fills in the short sign-up form.
He escuchado que muchos daneses hacen trabajo voluntario en su tiempo libre.I have heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time.
Me gusta la idea.I like the idea.
Quiero aprender a hablar mejor danés y conocer gente nueva.I want to learn to speak better Danish and meet new people.
Una noche, estoy sentada con mi computadora.One evening, I am sitting with my computer.
Encuentro un sitio web para trabajo voluntario.I find a website for voluntary work.
Leo los primeros tres anuncios en la página.I read the first three listings on the site.
El primer anuncio es de un club deportivo.The first listing is from a sports club.
Pero necesitan ayuda los jueves por la noche, y a esa hora juego bádminton.But they need help on Thursday evenings, and I play badminton then.
El segundo anuncio es de un museo.The second listing is from a museum.
Necesitan una persona los miércoles por la noche, pero a esa hora entreno.They need a person on Wednesday evenings, but I work out then.
Luego miro el tercer anuncio.Then I look at the third listing.
Es del pequeño café que ya conozco.It is from the little café that I already know.
Necesitan gente para los sábados por la mañana.They need people for Saturday mornings.
Eso me queda absolutamente perfecto.That suits me absolutely perfectly.
Lleno un formulario corto con mi nombre y mi dirección de correo electrónico.I fill in a short form with my name and my email address.
También escribo mi número de teléfono: doce, treinta y cuatro, cincuenta y seis, setenta y ocho.I also write my phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight.
Finalmente, presiono enviar.Finally, I press send.
Nunca he trabajado en un café antes, pero estoy lista para aprenderlo.I have never worked in a café before, but I am ready to learn it.
first shift arrival
Storyboard: On her very first Saturday shift, Maya arrives early at the little café. She ties her new apron in the back room and carries the chairs out to the pavement to prepare for opening.
Es mi primer turno de sábado en el pequeño café.It is my first Saturday shift at the little café.
He llegado temprano.I have arrived early.
El sol brilla en la calle en Copenhague.The sun is shining on the street in Copenhagen.
Estoy emocionada, pero también estoy un poco nerviosa.I am looking forward to it, but I am also a little nervous.
Es la primera vez que trabajo en un café.It is the first time I am working in a café.
Entré al cuarto de atrás para prepararme.I went into the back room to get ready.
En una mesa había un delantal nuevo para mí.On a table lay a new apron for me.
Me lo puse y lo amarré en la espalda.I put it on and tied it in the back.
Se sentía bien llevarlo puesto.It felt good to wear it.
Ahora estoy lista para empezar.Now I am ready to begin.
Tengo que aprender a abrir el café correctamente.I have to learn to open the café correctly.
Primero, el gerente abrió con llave la pesada puerta principal.First, the manager unlocked the heavy front door.
Después, salí a la acera para preparar todo.Afterwards, I went out onto the pavement to get ready.
Llevé cuatro sillas afuera.I carried four chairs out.
Las he puesto alrededor de una mesa pequeña.I have placed them around a little table.
En poco tiempo, llegarán los otros voluntarios.In a little while, the other volunteers will arrive.
Estoy muy emocionada por mi primer día.I am very excited about my first day.
learning the machine
Storyboard: Before the doors open, an experienced volunteer brings Maya over to the large espresso machine. The volunteer shows her how to grind the coffee beans and how to steam the milk. Maya watches every step closely, because it is her first time behind the counter.
Es mi primera vez detrás del mostrador.It is my first time behind the counter.
La cafetería aún no está abierta.The café is not open yet.
Uno de los voluntarios experimentados me llama.One of the experienced volunteers calls for me.
Estamos frente a la gran máquina de expreso.We stand in front of the large espresso machine.
"Hoy debes aprender a hacer café", dice él."Today you must learn to make coffee," he says.
Nunca antes he preparado café en una máquina así.I have never made coffee on such a machine before.
El primer paso es moler los granos de café.The first step is to grind the coffee beans.
Él presiona un pequeño botón.He presses a small button.
La máquina muele los granos marrones con un fuerte sonido.The machine grinds the brown beans with a loud sound.
El segundo paso es vaporizar la leche.The second step is to steam the milk.
Vierte leche fría en una jarra de metal.He pours cold milk into a metal jug.
Después enciende el vapor caliente.Afterwards he turns on the hot steam.
Mientras tanto, miro atentamente sus manos.I look closely at his hands meanwhile.
La leche en la jarra rápidamente se vuelve caliente y espesa.The milk in the jug quickly becomes warm and thick.
Claramente ha preparado café muchas veces antes.He has clearly made coffee many times before.
Cuando llegué esta mañana, estaba muy nerviosa.When I arrived this morning, I was very nervous.
Pero lo explicó bien, así que ya he entendido la mayor parte.But he explained it well, so I have already understood most of it.
the first coffee
Storyboard: When the morning rush hits, Maya makes a cappuccino for a waiting customer. She has held the steam wand a bit too deep in the milk, so it does not get a thick foam. It still looks acceptable, though, so she pours the milk over the coffee and hands it over with a polite smile.
Esta mañana hemos estado increíblemente ocupados.This morning we have been incredibly busy.
Acabo de hacer mi primerísimo capuchino.I have just made my very first cappuccino.
Un poco antes de las diez, un hombre se acercó al mostrador.A little before ten, a man came over to the counter.
"Un capuchino, por favor", dijo."A cappuccino, please," he said.
Acabo de aprender a usar la máquina de expreso.I have just learned to use the espresso machine.
Así que me acerqué a la máquina de inmediato.So I went over to the machine right away.
Preparé el café y metí el tubo de vapor en la leche.I brewed the coffee and put the steam wand down into the milk.
Pero lo sostuve un poco demasiado abajo.But I held it a little too deep down.
Probablemente he estado un poco demasiado nerviosa.I have probably been a little too nervous.
Desafortunadamente, la leche no formó nada de espuma espesa.Unfortunately, the milk did not get any thick foam.
Miré con cuidado dentro de la jarra.I looked carefully down into the pitcher.
La espuma no era completamente perfecta.The foam was not completely perfect.
Pero aún así se veía aceptable.But it still looked acceptable.
Vertí la leche caliente sobre el café en la taza.I poured the hot milk over the coffee in the cup.
Luego le di el café al hombre con una sonrisa amable.Then I gave the coffee to the man with a polite smile.
"Aquí tiene, serán cincuenta y cinco coronas", dije."Here you go, that will be fifty-five kroner," I said.
Tomó la taza y se sentó en la primera mesa.He took the cup and sat down at the first table.
bun with cheese
Storyboard: A moment later, she slices a warm sourdough bun and adds a thick piece of cheese. She places it on a small plate and navigates through the standing customers to deliver it safely to a corner booth.
Poco después, llegó el siguiente pedido.Shortly after, the next order came.
Ya preparé mi primer café hoy.I have already made my first coffee today.
Pero este cliente solo quería comida.But this customer just wanted food.
Pidió un panecillo con queso.He ordered a bun with cheese.
Tomé un panecillo tibio de masa madre de la canasta.I took a warm sourdough bun from the basket.
El pan de masa madre a menudo tiene una corteza dura.Sourdough bread often has a hard crust.
Pero he aprendido a cortar el pan por la mitad rápidamente.But I have learned to cut the bread in half quickly.
Después, puse una rebanada gruesa de queso en el panecillo.Afterwards, I put a thick slice of cheese on the bun.
Acomodé bien la comida en un plato pequeño.I arranged the food nicely on a small plate.
Ahora tenía que entregárselo al cliente.Now I had to deliver it to the customer.
Había mucha gente de pie en medio de la cafetería.There were many people standing in the middle of the café.
Mantuve el plato en equilibrio y caminé con cuidado hacia adelante.I balanced the plate and walked carefully forward.
Pasé lentamente junto a los demás clientes.I walked slowly past the other guests.
Afortunadamente, el cliente estaba sentado hasta el fondo, en la esquina.Fortunately, the customer was sitting right down in the corner.
Estaba esperando en la cuarta mesa.He was waiting at the fourth table.
Puse el plato en la mesa frente a él.I placed the plate on the table in front of him.
"Aquí tiene, un panecillo tibio con queso", dije."Here you go, a warm bun with cheese," I said.
"Muchas gracias", respondió el hombre."Thank you very much," the man replied.
Es bonito ver a un cliente satisfecho.It is lovely to see a satisfied guest.
the ginger beer
Storyboard: During her second Saturday shift, a woman asks for a ginger beer. Maya finds the ginger beer in the glass fridge, inverts it so that the ginger mixes. Then she hands it to the customer.
Era mi segundo turno de sábado en la cafetería.It was my second Saturday shift in the café.
Ya he aprendido a hacer gran parte del trabajo.I have already learned to do a lot of the work.
Esa mañana hacía un calor maravilloso afuera.That morning it was wonderfully warm outside.
Una mujer se acercó al mostrador.A woman came over to the counter.
"Me gustaría pedir una cerveza de jengibre", dijo."I would like to ask for a ginger beer," she said.
"Por supuesto, un momento", respondí."Of course, just a moment," I answered.
Fui al gran refrigerador de cristal detrás del mostrador.I went over to the large glass fridge behind the counter.
Miré en el tercer estante.I looked on the third shelf.
Allí estaban las botellas frías de cerveza de jengibre.The cold bottles of ginger beer were standing there.
Encontré una botella y la saqué.I found a bottle and took it out.
El jengibre reposaba como una nube espesa en el fondo.The ginger lay like a thick cloud down at the bottom.
He aprendido a mezclarlo antes de abrir la botella.I have learned to mix it before I open the bottle.
Así que volteé la botella boca abajo con mucho cuidado.So I turned the bottle very carefully upside down.
La sostuve así por un par de segundos.I held it like that for a couple of seconds.
El jengibre se mezcló lentamente con el resto del líquido.The ginger mixed slowly with the rest of the liquid.
Ahora la bebida se veía perfecta.Now the drink looked exactly right.
Volteé la botella de nuevo y la puse sobre el mostrador.I turned the bottle around again and placed it on the counter.
"Aquí tiene, son treinta y cinco coronas", le dije."Here you go, that will be thirty-five kroner," I said to her.
Ella pagó, y probablemente ha disfrutado de su bebida fría.She paid, and she has probably enjoyed her cold drink.
second coffee progress
Storyboard: Later that morning, Maya tries foaming milk again for a large latte. This second attempt turns out much better, and she realizes that she has finally learned to control the pressure of the hot steam.
Más tarde ese mismo sábado, volvimos a tener mucho trabajo.Later the same Saturday, we got very busy again.
Una mujer joven se acercó al mostrador y pidió un cappuccino grande.A young woman came up to the counter and ordered a large cappuccino.
Mi primera taza no fue del todo perfecta.My first cup was not quite perfect.
Pero esta vez estaba lista.But this time I was ready.
Tomé la jarra de metal y vertí leche fría en ella.I took the metal pitcher and poured cold milk into it.
Puse la jarra debajo de la máquina y encendí el vapor caliente.I placed the pitcher under the machine and turned on the hot steam.
No subí la presión de inmediato.I did not turn up the pressure immediately.
La verdad es que he aprendido a escuchar la máquina.I have actually learned to listen to the machine.

La leche se calentó lentamente.The milk slowly became warm.

Fue un gran paso adelante.It was a big step forward.
Vertí con cuidado la leche sobre el café en una taza grande.I carefully poured the milk over the coffee in a large cup.
De hecho, parecía un verdadero cappuccino profesional.It actually looked like a real professional cappuccino.
"Aquí tienes", dije, y puse la taza en el mostrador."Here you go," I said and placed the cup on the counter.
La mujer sonrió y tomó su café.The woman smiled and took her coffee.

third saturday regular

En mi tercer sábado, estaba lista detrás del mostrador.On my third Saturday, I stood ready behind the counter.

También estuvo aquí en mi primer y mi segundo turno.He was also here on my first and my second shift.

Siempre compra exactamente lo mismo.He always buys exactly the same thing.
