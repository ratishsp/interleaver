# Week 25 — Storyboard (A Sunday in the city)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** Demonstratives *denne/dette/disse*; *for … siden* (ago); time adverbs

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_sunday_morning | On a peaceful Sunday morning, Maya enjoys her coffee on the sofa. She appreciates that this day is completely open, a sharp contrast to the chaotic weekend when she first arrived in Copenhagen exactly six months ago. |

| 6 | 06_cafe_reflection | Taking a break in the museum café, Maya drinks an apple juice and looks out the window. She reflects on how, just a short time ago, she felt like a tourist, but now this city truly feels familiar. |
