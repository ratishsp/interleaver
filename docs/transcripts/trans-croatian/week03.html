Week 3: A video call home to Mexico

the flower shop
Storyboard: On her way home from work, Maya steps into a brightly lit flower shop to escape the cold January evening. She buys a small potted plant from the clerk to brighten up her temporary living space, carrying it out in a paper bag.
Siječanj je i jako je hladno.It is January, and it is very cold.
Hodam kući s autobusne stanice u Kopenhagenu.I am walking home from the bus stop in Copenhagen.
Vidim cvjećarnicu na ulici.I see a flower shop on the street.
Unutra je svijetlo i toplo.It is bright and warm inside.
Ulazim u toplu trgovinu.I go into the warm shop.
Gledam malu, zelenu biljku.I look at a small, green plant.
Prodavač mi se smiješi.A clerk smiles at me.
"Bok," kaže on."Hello," he says.
"Bok," kažem."Hello," I say.
"Tražim biljku.""I am looking for a plant."
"Je li to poklon?" pita on."Is it a gift?" he asks.
"Ne, to je za moju sobu," odgovaram."No, it is for my room," I answer.
"To je stvarno dobra biljka," kaže on."It is a really good plant," he says.
"Koliko košta?" pitam."How much does it cost?" I ask.
"Košta pedeset pet kruna," odgovara on."It costs fifty-five kroner," he answers.
Plaćam svoju novu biljku.I pay for my new plant.
Stavlja biljku u papirnatu vrećicu.He puts the plant down in a paper bag.
"Izvolite, evo vaše vrećice," kaže on."Here you go, here is your bag," he says.
"Puno hvala," kažem sretno."Thank you very much," I say happily.
Izlazim u hladnu večer.I go out into the cold evening.
Moja mala biljka čini me jako sretnom.My little plant makes me very happy.
evening prep
Storyboard: Maya arrives at her temporary room and sets the new plant on a small desk. With it already being pitch black outside, she turns on a warm lamp to reflect against the dark window, makes a cup of tea, and flips open her laptop to dial home.

Sada je moja soba topla i svijetla.Now my room is warm and bright.

Sjedim za svojim radnim stolom.I sit at my desk.

Sada zovem svoju obitelj u Meksiku.Now I call my family in Mexico.
Veselim se što ću ih vidjeti.I look forward to seeing them.
parents and lola
Storyboard: The video connects to a sunny living room in Mexico, where her mother and father wave enthusiastically. A familiar bark makes Maya laugh out loud as the family dog, Lola, jumps up onto the sofa to investigate the screen.
Zvoni par puta.It rings a couple of times.
Ekran zasvijetli.The screen lights up.
Vidim dnevni boravak.I see a living room.
Ima sunca, a dnevni boravak je vrlo svijetao.There is sun, and the living room is very bright.
Vidim dvije osobe.I see two people.
To su moja majka i moj otac.It is my mother and my father.
Puno se smiješe i mašu mi.They smile a lot and wave to me.
"Bok, mama i tata!" kažem glasno."Hello mom and dad!" I say loudly.
"Bok, Maya", kaže moj otac."Hello, Maya," my father says.
"Kako ide?""How is it going?"
"Dobro ide", odgovaram veselo."It is going well," I answer happily.
Odjednom čujem zvuk.Suddenly I hear a sound.
To je mali lavež.It is a little bark.
"Oh, čujem psa!" kažem i smijem se."Oh, I hear a dog!" I say and laugh.
Mali pas skače na kauč.A little dog jumps up onto a sofa.
Gleda u kameru.It looks into the camera.
"Evo mog psa!""There is my dog!"
"Bok, Lola", kažem."Hello, Lola," I say.

"Da, to je tvoj pas", kaže moja majka s osmijehom."Yes, it is your dog," my mother says with a smile.
Volim svoju obitelj.I love my family.
Lijepo ih je sve vidjeti.It is lovely to see them all.

mexico garden
