# Week 2 — Storyboard (Maya's first day and the Anmeldung)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Present tense of regular verbs (**ich arbeite, Sie arbeiten**); numbers 0–100 as words; **haben**; age with **sein** (*ich bin einunddreißig Jahre alt*)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_office_arrival | Maya arrives at the software company for her first day. She walks in from the January street, takes off her jacket and looks around. Standing there she takes stock: she is thirty-one, and today she starts a new job. |
| 2 | 02_meeting_jonas | A colleague introduces himself as **Jonas**. He asks where she has come from and how long she has been in the city, and tells her a little about the team she is joining. |
| 3 | 03_the_desk_setup | Jonas shows Maya to her new desk. They work out what she has and what is still missing: she has her laptop and a chair, but no monitor and no keyboard yet. Jonas counts up what he needs to order for her. |
| 4 | 04_buergeramt_waiting | The next morning, before the office, Maya sits in the waiting hall of the Bürgeramt for her Anmeldung appointment. She watches the display board and works out how many people are still ahead of her. |
| 5 | 05_the_anmeldung | Maya sits down across from an official. He checks her passport, confirms her name, and registers the flat as her address. He asks whether she has a German telephone number yet; she does not. He stamps her registration and hands it to her. |
| 6 | 06_buying_the_sim | At lunchtime, registration paper in her rucksack, Maya walks to a phone shop for a German SIM card. A clerk comes to serve her. She explains what she needs, and he asks for her passport and her new registration — a German SIM needs both. |
| 7 | 07_the_new_number | The clerk registers the card and hands her the new number written on a slip: **0176 12345678**. Maya puts the SIM in and reads it back to the clerk to check it. She sends Jonas her number so the office has it. |
| 8 | 08_currywurst_after_work | After work that evening, with the registration and the SIM finally behind her, Maya stops at a street stand on the way home. She orders a Currywurst and eats it standing up at the counter. |
