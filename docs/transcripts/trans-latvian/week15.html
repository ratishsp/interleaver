Week 15: Maya's everyday life in the city

morning weather
Storyboard: Maya wakes up early and looks out the window. The sun shines, but the wind is cold, so she puts on a warm sweater for the day.

Pulkstenis ir tikai seši.It is only six o'clock.
Ir jauna diena.It is a new day.
Es lēnām pieceļos.I get up slowly.
Es pieeju pie loga.I walk over to the window.
Es skatos ārā uz pilsētu.I look out at the city.
Ārā joprojām ir tumšs un auksts.It is still dark and cold outside.
Ziema joprojām ir šeit.Winter is still here.
Es eju uz vannas istabu.I go into the bathroom.
Es mazgāju seju.I wash my face.
Es tīru zobus.I brush my teeth.
Tad es apģērbjos.Then I get dressed.
Es uzvelku biezu, siltu džemperi.I put on a thick, warm sweater.
Tas ir ideāls aukstai dienai.It is perfect for a cold day.
Es eju uz virtuvi.I go into the kitchen.
Es pagatavoju tasi karstas kafijas.I make a cup of warm coffee.
Es ēdu maizes šķēli ar sieru.I eat a slice of bread with cheese.
Tagad es esmu pilnīgi nomodā.Now I am completely awake.
Es esmu gatava jaunajai dienai.I am ready for the new day.
the bus commute
Storyboard: She walks to the bus stop and waits for her daily transport. The yellow bus arrives, she steps inside, scans her travel card, and rides through the busy streets.

Autobuss apstājas manā priekšā.The bus stops in front of me.
Durvis atveras.The doors open.
Es iekāpju autobusā.I step inside the bus.
Es atrodu somā savu braukšanas karti.I find my travel card in my bag.
Es noskenēju zilo karti pie aparāta.I scan the blue card on the machine.

Es atrodu brīvu vietu pie loga.I find an empty seat by the window.

Es skatos uz cilvēkiem uz ielas.I look at the people in the street.
Mēs braucam cauri rosīgajām ielām.We drive through the busy streets.
Es esmu gatava jaunai darba dienai.I am ready for a new workday.
working with peter

"Labrīt, Maya," saka Peters."Good morning, Maya," Peter says.

Es rakstu kodu savā datorā.I type code on my computer.
Darbs ir ļoti aizraujošs.The work is very exciting.
Es esmu priecīga par savu darbu.I am happy with my job.
canteen lunch
Storyboard: At noon, Maya and Peter go down to the bright canteen together. She chooses a classic open sandwich with fish and pays forty kroner at the register.
Pulkstenis ir divpadsmit.It is twelve o'clock.
Ir pusdienlaiks.It is lunch time.
Pēters un es ejam lejā uz ēdnīcu.Peter and I go down to the canteen.
Ēdnīca ir liela un gaiša.The canteen is large and bright.
Mēs esam ļoti izsalkuši.We are very hungry.
Mēs aplūkojam labo ēdienu.We look at the good food.
Šodien ir daudz izvēles iespēju.There are many options today.

"Lūdzu," es saku."Here you go," I say.

Mēs atrodam brīvu galdu stūrī.We find a free table in the corner.
Mēs apsēžamies.We sit down.
Ēdiens garšo tiešām labi.The food tastes really good.
Mēs runājam par savu dienu.We talk about our day.
giving directions
Storyboard: After work, Maya steps out into a large square. A tourist asks for the way to a local museum, and she politely explains that they must walk straight ahead and then turn left.
Man ir brīvs no darba.I am off from work.
Es izieju lielā laukumā.I walk out onto a large square.
Šodien pilsētā ir daudz cilvēku.There are many people in the city today.
Kāds vīrietis pienāk man klāt.A man walks up to me.
Viņam ir liela kamera.He has a large camera.
Viņš ir tūrists.He is a tourist.
Viņš smaida un saka sveiki.He smiles and says hi.
"Kur atrodas muzejs?" jautā tūrists."Where is the museum?" the tourist asks.
Es labi zinu šo muzeju.I know the museum well.
Tas nav tālu.It is not far away.
Es norādu tālāk pa ielu.I point down the street.
"Ejiet taisni uz priekšu," es paskaidroju."You walk straight ahead," I explain.
"Tad pagriezieties pa kreisi.""Then you turn to the left."
"Muzejs atrodas labajā pusē.""The museum is on the right side."
"Liels paldies par palīdzību," saka vīrietis."Thank you very much for the help," the man says.
"Lūdzu," es priecīgi atbildu."You are welcome," I answer happily.
Viņš aiziet tālāk pa ielu.He walks down the street.
Es dodos tālāk uz maiznīcu.I walk on towards the bakery.
buying a cake
