# Week 6 — Storyboard (Maya settles into a daily routine)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** Present-tense regular verbs; reflexive *vasker mig*; V2 review — the verb comes before the subject whenever a time or place phrase opens the sentence

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_waking_up | Maya wakes up feeling a bit cold in the new apartment. She steps into the bathroom and washes herself with warm water, finally feeling awake and ready to face the day. |
| 2 | 02_morning_coffee | In the kitchen, she pours a strong cup of coffee to shake off the sleep. She chooses warm clothes because the sky looks grey outside, and she drinks her coffee while looking out the window. |
| 3 | 03_catching_the_bus | The bus is already approaching the stop when Maya runs down the street. She hurries a few quick steps and jumps on board just in time, finding a warm seat at the back. |
| 4 | 04_office_work | Maya sits at her desk and looks closely at the computer screen. She works hard on a difficult problem for her software project, but she eventually types the right solution. |
| 5 | 05_canteen_lunch | At midday, Maya heads down to the busy canteen for a break. She spots her colleague Peter, and they eat lunch together, laughing warmly as he tries to pronounce the name of her hometown. |
| 6 | 06_leaving_the_office | A productive day ends as Maya closes her laptop with a smile. She waves goodbye to Peter and heads out of the building, enjoying the crisp evening air. |
| 7 | 07_cooking_dinner | Back home, Maya decides to cook something comforting for dinner. She takes some fresh vegetables from the fridge and roasts them in a pan with familiar spices from Mexico. |
| 8 | 08_reading_on_sofa | After dinner, Maya curls up on the sofa under a soft blanket. She opens a Spanish book, losing herself in the story while the city hums quietly outside. |
| 9 | 09_turning_in | Maya brushes her teeth in the quiet bathroom and prepares for sleep. She turns off the lights and gets into bed, feeling completely at home in her new daily rhythm. |
