# Week 17 — Storyboard (Maya's Sunday out in Potsdam)

**Level:** A2 · **Grammar:** The **Perfekt** with **sein** (*ich bin gefahren, gegangen*); past-tense narration; time expressions (the Präteritum *war/hatte* is week 26 — keep the telling on **actions**, not on descriptions of states, which would reach for it)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_early_sunday_start | Maya tells the whole day as one long voice note to her sister Sofia, recorded that evening — Sofia is the addressee, not a character in the scene, and never answers. She uses du throughout. On Sunday she woke up early. The sun came out early and reached right into the room, and she decided to leave the city for the day and travel out to Potsdam. |
| 2 | 02_packing_and_leaving | She made herself a lunch and packed it with her water bottle into her backpack. She put on light shoes for the first time this year. Then she left the flat and walked to the station. |
| 3 | 03_station_and_train | At the station bakery she bought a bread roll and ate it later on the train. She went up to the platform on the viaduct, got into the S-Bahn and found a seat by the window. |
| 4 | 04_journey_through_pines | The train rolled out of Berlin. They left the houses behind and drove into thick pine forests. Maya watched the other passengers: hikers with boots and backpacks got in at one station, and a family with three children moved noisily through the carriage. |
| 5 | 05_arriving_in_potsdam | In Potsdam Maya got out and walked into the town. Locals and visitors strolled together through the streets in the spring sunshine. |
| 6 | 06_the_long_park_walk | She went into a huge park and followed the wide paths. She walked for kilometres, past statues and old palaces, in among the tall trees, and went off onto the small side paths too. |
| 7 | 07_rest_at_the_lake | She followed the path down to a big lake. She went down to the shore, walked a little way along the water and came to an empty bench. There she sat down and ate her lunch from the morning, and she stayed a long time and watched the small boats on the calm water. |
| 8 | 08_afternoon_cafe | In the late afternoon she walked back into the old town, crossed the square and went into a busy café. She ordered a slice of cake and took a few photos with her phone. She sat outside and listened to the people around her. |
| 9 | 09_sleepy_ride_home | In the evening she walked back to the station and got on the S-Bahn. Nobody talked in the carriage now; the other day-trippers fell asleep over their backpacks. Maya leaned her head against the window and looked out at the dark pine forests again. She came home late, tired and completely happy. |
