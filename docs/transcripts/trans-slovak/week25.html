Week 25: A Sunday in the city

morning calendar
Storyboard: On Sunday morning, Maya makes a fresh pot of coffee and flips her wall calendar to July. She realizes that she moved to Copenhagen exactly six months ago, and decides to celebrate by spending this sunny day exploring the city at her own pace.
Je nedeľa ráno.It is Sunday morning.
Slnko svieti dovnútra cez okno v mojom byte.The sun is shining in through the window in my apartment.
Idem do kuchyne.I go out into the kitchen.
Uvarím si kanvicu čerstvej kávy.I make a pot of fresh coffee.
Vôňa kávy je toto ráno fantastická.The smell of coffee is fantastic this morning.
Na stene mám kalendár.I have a calendar on the wall.
Obrátim stranu na ďalší mesiac.I turn the page to the next month.
Teraz je júl.It is July now.
Do Kodane som sa presťahovala v januári.I moved to Copenhagen in January.
Prišla som sem presne pred šiestimi mesiacmi.I came here exactly six months ago.
Čas plynie naozaj rýchlo.Time really goes fast.
Môj prvý deň v tomto meste bol veľmi chladný.My first day in this city was very cold.
Ale dnes je počasie teplé a krásne.But today the weather is warm and beautiful.
Chcem osláviť toto malé výročie.I want to celebrate this little anniversary.
Dnes nemám žiadne pevné plány.I have no fixed plans today.
Vezmem si šálku kávy a pozerám sa von na ulicu.I take my coffee cup and look out over the street.
Chcem ísť von a preskúmať mesto vlastným tempom.I want to go out and explore the city at my own pace.
Teším sa na to, ako si vychutnám tieto svetlé hodiny.I look forward to enjoying these bright hours.
ceramic plates
Storyboard: Maya wanders into a lively second-hand market in a nearby square. She spots two blue ceramic plates on a table, decides these plates are perfect for her kitchen, and buys them from the vendor for forty kroner.
Idem sa prejsť cez veľké námestie v meste.I go for a walk across a large square in the city.
Každú nedeľu je tu veľký blší trh.Here there is a large flea market every Sunday.
Slnko svieti a pomedzi stoly sa prechádza veľa ľudí.The sun is shining, and many people are walking around between the tables.
Zastavím sa pri malom stole s množstvom rôznych vecí.I stop by a small table with many different things.
Za stolom stojí priateľský predavač.Behind the table stands a friendly vendor.
Zrazu zbadám dva modré keramické taniere.Suddenly I see two blue ceramic plates.
Zdvihnem ich a pozorne si ich prezerám.I pick them up and look closely at them.
„Koľko stoja tieto taniere?“ pýtam sa predavača."How much do these plates cost?" I ask the vendor.
Predavač sa na mňa usmeje.The vendor smiles at me.
„Stoja len štyridsať korún,“ odpovedá."They cost only forty kroner," he replies.
To je naozaj dobrá cena.That is a really good price.
Pred mesiacom som si kúpila malý stôl do kuchyne.I bought a small table for my kitchen a month ago.
Tieto taniere sa k tomuto stolu budú perfektne hodiť.These plates will fit perfectly with this table.
„Veľmi rada by som si ich kúpila,“ hovorím."I would very much like to buy them," I say.
Dám predavačovi štyridsať korún a opatrne vložím taniere do tašky.I give the vendor forty kroner and carefully put the plates in my bag.
Vždy je príjemné nájsť niečo výnimočné v pokojnú nedeľu.It is always nice to find something special on a quiet Sunday.
winter coat
Storyboard: At a clothing stall, Maya tries on a heavy wool winter coat. She immediately takes it off because this coat is far too warm for summer, laughing with the vendor that she desperately needed something exactly like it five months ago.
Idem ďalej po trhu a zastavujem sa pri stánku s oblečením.I walk further into the market and stop at a clothing stall.
Vidím ťažký zimný kabát z vlny.I see a heavy winter coat made of wool.
Tento kabát je krásny, ale veľmi hrubý.This coat is beautiful, but very thick.
Aj tak si ten kabát skúšam.I try the coat on anyway.
Je neskutočne teplý.It is incredibly warm.
Okamžite si ho znova vyzliekam.I take it off again immediately.
Je júl a dnes svieti slnko.It is July, and the sun is shining today.
Za stolom stojí milá žena a usmieva sa.A friendly woman stands behind the table and smiles.
„Je príliš teplý?“ pýta sa žena."Is it too warm?" the woman asks.
„Áno, na leto je trochu príliš teplý,“ odpovedám a smejem sa."Yes, it is a bit too warm for the summer," I reply and laugh.
Dotýkam sa hrubej látky.I touch the thick fabric.
„Ale pred piatimi mesiacmi som tento zimný kabát naozaj potrebovala!“ hovorím."But I really needed this winter coat five months ago!" I say.
Žena sa nahlas smeje.The woman laughs loudly.
„Áno, február bol veľmi chladný,“ hovorí."Yes, February was very cold," she says.
Spomínam si na svoje prvé týždne v Kodani.I remember my first weeks in Copenhagen.
Vtedy som neustále mrzla.I was constantly freezing back then.
Našťastie práve teraz nepotrebujem také teplé oblečenie.Fortunately, I do not need such warm clothes right now.
Vešiam kabát späť na stojan a hovorím dovidenia.I hang the coat back on the rack and say goodbye.
museum ticket
Storyboard: Moving on, Maya enters a local history museum and goes to the front desk. She asks the clerk if this entrance ticket includes the special upstairs exhibition, and the clerk tells her to hurry because the guided tour started ten minutes ago.
Kráčam preč od trhu a nachádzam malé miestne múzeum.I walk away from the market and find a small, local museum.
Vchádzam do tichej budovy.I walk into the quiet building.
Podídem k pultu, aby som si kúpila vstupenku.I walk over to the counter to buy an entrance ticket.
Za pokladňou stojí mladý muž.A young man is standing behind the register.
„Dobrý deň, jeden lístok pre dospelého, prosím,“ hovorím."Hi, one adult ticket, please," I say.
„Bude to sto korún,“ hovorí a vytlačí ho."That will be one hundred kroner," he says and prints it.
Zaplatím a pozerám sa na tieto pestrofarebné brožúry na pulte.I pay and look at these colorful brochures on the counter.
Propagujú špeciálnu výstavu o histórii mesta.They advertise a special exhibition about the history of the city.
„Platí tento lístok aj na výstavu na poschodí?“ pýtam sa."Does this ticket also give access to the exhibition upstairs?" I ask.
„Áno, toto múzeum má iba jeden spoločný lístok,“ odpovedá."Yes, this museum only has one combined ticket," he answers.
„Výstava je hore na prvom poschodí,“ hovorí."The exhibition is upstairs on the first floor," he says.
„Môžete si tam hore pokojne dopriať dostatok času.“"You can take your time up there."
„Je na nej veľa starých fotografií mesta,“ vysvetľuje."It has many old photographs of the city," he explains.
„Niektoré z nich majú vyše sto rokov.“"Some of them are over a hundred years old."
„To znie naozaj zaujímavo,“ hovorím."That sounds really exciting," I say.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem,“ usmievam sa."Thank you very much," I smile.
Vezmem si lístok a pokojne kráčam hore schodmi.I take my ticket and walk calmly up the stairs.
harbor photograph
Storyboard: Upstairs in the gallery, Maya studies a massive black-and-white photograph of ships and factories. The caption explains that this harbor area was a loud, dirty industrial zone a hundred years ago, contrasting sharply with the modern waterfront she knows.
Idem hore na výstavu.I walk upstairs to the exhibition.
Je tu veľa starých obrázkov.There are many old pictures here.
Zastavím sa pred veľmi veľkou čiernobielou fotografiou.I stop in front of a very large black-and-white photograph.
Tento obrázok je neuveriteľne fascinujúci.This picture is incredibly fascinating.
Zobrazuje rušný prístav s veľkými továrňami.It shows a busy harbor with large factories.
Vidím tiež veľa vysokých komínov a tmavých lodí.I can also see many tall chimneys and dark ships.
Tieto lode vyzerajú veľmi ťažké.These ships look very heavy.
Čítam malý text vedľa fotografie.I read the small text next to the photograph.
Text vysvetľuje históriu tohto prístavu.The text explains the history of this harbor.
Pred sto rokmi bola táto oblasť priemyselnou zónou.A hundred years ago, the area was an industrial zone.
Vtedy to bolo hlučné a špinavé miesto.It was a loud and dirty place back then.
Kontrast s dneškom je obrovský.The contrast to today is enormous.
Teraz je toto nábrežie tiché a čisté.Now, this waterfront is quiet and clean.
Cez víkendy chodím často na prechádzku popri vode.I often go for a walk along the water on the weekends.
Je ťažké si tu predstaviť tie staré továrne.It is hard to imagine the old factories here.
Dlho sa pozerám na túto fotografiu.I look at this photograph for a long time.
Potom pomaly kráčam ďalej do ďalšej miestnosti.Then I slowly walk on into the next room.
texting sofia

Sedím v múzejnej kaviarni a dávam si prestávku.I am sitting in the museum's café and taking a break.
Pijem šálku kávy a oddychujem.I drink a cup of coffee and relax.
Vyberám z tašky modré taniere.I take the blue plates out of my bag.
Tieto taniere sa perfektne hodia do mojej kuchyne.These plates fit perfectly in my kitchen.

„Našla som ich na trhu pred hodinou.“"I found them at a market an hour ago."

„Pamätáš si na tento trh?“"Do you remember this market?"
Sofía sa pýta v ďalšej správe.Sofía asks in the next message.
„Boli sme tam presne pred dvoma rokmi!“ píše."We were there exactly two years ago!" she writes.
Pozerám sa na naše šťastné tváre na fotke.I look at our happy faces in the picture.

seagull encounter
Storyboard: Walking home through a large park, Maya stops at a kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream. As she strolls along the pond, a bold seagull suddenly swoops down, forcing her to quickly duck and shield this dessert with her hand to prevent the bird from stealing it.
Kráčam domov cez veľký park.I walk home through a large park.
Pred hodinou som bola v múzeu.An hour ago I was at a museum.
Teraz si chcem len užiť dobré počasie.Now I just want to enjoy the good weather.
Zastavujem sa pri malom stánku, aby som si kúpila zmrzlinu v kornútku.I stop at a small kiosk to buy a waffle ice cream.
„Nech sa páči, tu je vaša zmrzlina,“ hovorí muž v stánku."Here you go, here is your ice cream," the man in the kiosk says.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem,“ odpovedám a beriem si zmrzlinu."Thank you very much," I reply and take the ice cream.
Pomaly kráčam pozdĺž jazera v parku.I walk slowly along the lake in the park.
Táto zmrzlina v kornútku vyzerá naozaj dobre.This waffle ice cream looks really good.
Zrazu nad sebou počujem hlasný zvuk.Suddenly I hear a loud noise above me.
Veľká čajka letí strmhlav priamo na mňa.A large seagull dives right down towards me.

Rýchlo sa prikrčím a chránim si zmrzlinu rukou.I duck quickly and shield my ice cream with my hand.
Ten vták mi jedlo neukradne.The bird is not going to steal my food.
Čajka preletí okolo a pristane na tráve.The seagull flies past and lands on the grass.
Žena na lavičke sa smeje.A woman on a bench laughs.
„Dnes sú veľmi drzé,“ hovorí."They are very cheeky today," she says.
„Áno, to rozhodne sú,“ odvetím so smiechom."Yes, they certainly are," I laugh back.
Rýchlo dojem zmrzlinu, zatiaľ čo kráčam ďalej.I quickly finish eating my ice cream while I walk on.
unpacking plates
