Week 9: Maya visits the winter market

Slovenian with English gloss, line-aligned.

arriving at the market
Storyboard: Maya arrives at a busy outdoor market in a large square. The winter morning is cold, but the stalls look inviting. She carries a large empty bag and looks for fresh food for the weekend.
Je sobota zjutraj.It is Saturday morning.
Na Danskem je zima.It is winter in Denmark.
Vreme je danes zelo mrzlo.The weather is very cold today.
Stojim na velikem trgu v mestu.I am standing in a large square in the city.
Tukaj je tržnica na prostem.There is an outdoor market here.
Gledam številne stojnice.I look at the many stalls.
Vse so videti vabljive.They all look inviting.
Na trgu je veliko ljudi.There are many people in the square.
Ljudje nosijo topla zimska oblačila.People are wearing warm winter clothes.
Na sebi imam samo tanko jakno.I only have a thin jacket on.
S seboj imam veliko torbo.I have a large bag with me.
Torba je trenutno popolnoma prazna.The bag is completely empty right now.
Iščem svežo hrano za konec tedna.I am looking for fresh food for the weekend.
Tržnica ima veliko svežih izdelkov.The market has many fresh goods.
Vidim zelenjavo, sadje in kruh.I see vegetables, fruit, and bread.
Razmišljam o cenah.I think about the prices.
Koliko stanejo stvari tukaj?How much do things cost here?
Grem do prve stojnice.I walk over to the first stall.
carrots for soup
Storyboard: Maya stops at a vegetable stall to buy ingredients for a hot soup. She points at some winter carrots and asks how much they cost. The vendor says they cost fifteen kroner per kilo. Maya asks for one kilo and pays.
Grem do stojnice z zelenjavo.I walk over to a vegetable stall.
Danes kuham toplo juho.I am making a hot soup today.
Gledam veliko zelenjave na mizi.I look at the many vegetables on the table.
Pokažem na nekaj velikih zimskih korenčkov.I point at some large winter carrots.
Potrebujem korenčke za juho.I need carrots for my soup.
Za mizo stoji prodajalec.There is a vendor standing behind the table.
"Živijo," reče."Hello," he says.
"Živijo," odgovorim."Hello," I reply.
"Koliko stanejo korenčki?" vprašam."How much do the carrots cost?" I ask.
"Stanejo petnajst kron na kilogram," odgovori prodajalec."They cost fifteen kroner per kilo," the vendor answers.
Petnajst kron je dobra cena.Fifteen kroner is a good price.
"Vzela bom en kilogram, prosim," rečem."I will take one kilo, please," I say.
Prodajalec stehta korenčke.The vendor weighs the carrots.
Položi jih v papirnato vrečko.He puts them in a paper bag.
Poda mi vrečko.He gives me the bag.
Plačam petnajst kron.I pay the fifteen kroner.
Vrečko spravim v svojo veliko torbo.I put the bag down in my large bag.
Veselim se tople juhe.I look forward to the hot soup.
a taste of cheese
Storyboard: At the next stall, a friendly vendor sells large cheeses. He offers Maya a small piece on a wooden stick to taste. She eats it, likes it, and asks for the price of a small block. It costs fifty-five kroner, so she buys it.
Pred mano je velika stojnica s sirom.In front of me is a large cheese stall.
Na stojnici je veliko velikih sirov.The stall has many large cheeses.
Prijazen prodajalec se mi nasmehne.A friendly vendor smiles at me.
Na leseni palčki ima majhen kos sira.He has a small piece of cheese on a wooden stick.
"Izvolite, malo za pokušino," reče."Here you go, a small taste," he says.
Poda mi palčko.He gives the stick to me.
Pojem rumeni sir.I eat the yellow cheese.
Ima res dober okus.It tastes really good.
Pogledam majhen sir.I look at a small cheese.
"Koliko stane ta sir?" vprašam."How much does this cheese cost?" I ask.
"Stane petinpetdeset kron," odgovori prodajalec."It costs fifty-five kroner," the vendor answers.
To je dobra cena.That is a good price.
Pokažem na majhen sir.I point at the small cheese.
"Kupila ga bom," rečem."I will buy it," I say.
Sir zavije v papir.He wraps the cheese in paper.
Dam mu denar.I give him the money.
"Najlepša hvala," reče."Thank you very much," he says.
Sir diši močno in dobro.The cheese smells strong and good.
the expensive gloves
Storyboard: The wind blows cold, so Maya looks at a stall with warm clothes. She picks up a pair of thick wool gloves and asks how much the gloves cost. The vendor answers that the price is two hundred kroner. Maya thinks that is too dear, so she politely says no thank you and walks on.
Veter danes hladno piha.The wind blows cold today.
Moje roke so zelo mrzle.My hands are very cold.
Vidim veliko stojnico s toplimi oblačili.I see a large stall with warm clothes.
Grem do prodajalca.I walk over to the vendor.
Na mizi ima veliko toplih stvari.He has many warm things on the table.
Vzamem par debelih volnenih rokavic.I pick up a pair of thick wool gloves.
Zelo so mehke.They are very soft.
"Koliko stanejo rokavice?" vprašam."How much do the gloves cost?" I ask.
"Stanejo dvesto kron," odgovori prodajalec."They cost two hundred kroner," the vendor answers.
Še enkrat pogledam rokavice.I look at the gloves again.
Dvesto kron je visoka cena.Two hundred kroner is a high price.
To je zame predrago.It is too expensive for me.
Rokavice položim nazaj na mizo.I put the gloves back on the table.
"Ne, hvala," rečem vljudno."No thank you," I say politely.
"Imejte lep dan.""Have a good day."
Prodajalec pokima in se nasmehne.The vendor nods and smiles.
"Tudi vam," reče."You too," he says.
Grem naprej po tržnici.I walk on around the market.
buying winter apples
Storyboard: Maya finds a fruit stall with baskets of dark red winter apples. She asks the vendor how much a small bag costs. The vendor smiles and says the price is twenty kroner. Maya hands him a paper note for fifty kroner. The vendor hands her the bag of apples and gives her exactly thirty kroner back in coins. Maya puts the heavy coins into her coat pocket.
Danes je na tržnici veliko ljudi.There are many people at the market today.
Najdem majhno stojnico s sadjem.I find a small fruit stall.
Na stojnici je veliko košar.There are many baskets in the stall.
Košare so polne temno rdečih zimskih jabolk.The baskets are full of dark red winter apples.
Pogledam majhno vrečko jabolk.I look at a small bag of apples.
"Koliko stane majhna vrečka?" vprašam prodajalca."How much does a small bag cost?" I ask the vendor.
Pogleda v košaro.He looks in the basket.
"Cena je dvajset kron," odgovori."The price is twenty kroner," he answers.
V torbi poiščem denar.I look in my bag for money.
Dam mu bankovec za petdeset kron.I give him a fifty-krone note.
Prodajalec mi poda vrečko jabolk.The vendor hands me the bag of apples.
Potem mi vrne denar.Then he gives me money back.
Nazaj dobim točno trideset kron v kovancih.I get exactly thirty kroner back in coins.
Kovanci so debeli in težki.The coins are thick and heavy.
Težke kovance spravim v žep svojega plašča.I put the heavy coins down into my coat pocket.
Jabolka dišijo zelo sveže.The apples smell very fresh.
Vrečko dam v svojo veliko nakupovalno torbo.I put the bag down into my large shopping bag.
fresh rye bread
Storyboard: Maya smells baked goods and walks over to a baker's stall. She points to a fresh, dark rye bread and asks how much it costs. The baker tells her it costs thirty-five kroner. She pays with her card and places the warm bread in her bag.
Grem mimo majhne stojnice.I walk past a small stall.
Diši po svežem kruhu.It smells of fresh bread.
Stopim do stojnice.I walk over to the stall.
Pek stoji za veliko mizo.The baker is standing behind a large table.
Miza je polna pekovskih izdelkov.The table is full of baked goods.
Vidim temen rženi kruh.I see a dark rye bread.
Videti je zelo svež.It looks very fresh.
Pokažem na rženi kruh.I point at the rye bread.
"Koliko stane temni rženi kruh?" vprašam."How much does the dark rye bread cost?" I ask.
Pek si obriše roke.The baker wipes his hands.
"Stane petintrideset kron," odgovori."It costs thirty-five kroner," he replies.
"Kupim ga," rečem."I am buying it," I say.
Izvlečem plačilno kartico.I take out my payment card.
Plačam s kartico.I pay with the card.
Pek mi da kruh v papirnati vrečki.The baker gives me the bread in a paper bag.
Dotaknem se vrečke.I touch the bag.
Kruh je še vedno topel.The bread is still warm.
Veselim se, da ga bom pojedla.I look forward to eating it.
exact change for coffee
Storyboard: Maya feels cold and spots a small coffee cart. She approaches and asks the barista how much a hot black coffee costs. The barista replies that it costs thirty kroner. Maya takes the new coins from her coat pocket and pays for the coffee with exact change.
Zdaj me zelo zebe.I am very cold now.
Moje roke so mrzle.My hands are cold.
Gledam naokrog po tržnici.I look around the market.
Vidim majhen voziček s kavo.I see a small coffee cart.
Mlad moški stoji za vozičkom.A young man stands behind the cart.
Stopim do njega.I walk over to him.
Baristu rečem živijo.I say hello to the barista.
"Živijo, koliko stane črna kava?" vprašam."Hello, how much does a black coffee cost?" I ask.
Barista se nasmehne.The barista smiles.
"Vroča, črna kava stane trideset kron," odgovori."A hot, black coffee costs thirty kroner," he answers.
V žepu plašča imam kovance.I have coins in my coat pocket.
To je moj drobiž od jabolk.It is my change from the apples.
Kovance vzamem ven.I take the coins out.
Preštejem jih.I count them.
Imam točno trideset kron.I have exactly thirty kroner.
"Izvolite, tukaj je trideset kron," rečem."Here you go, here are thirty kroner," I say.
Vzame denar.He takes the money.
Da mi vročo kavo.He gives me the hot coffee.
Kava čudovito diši.The coffee smells wonderful.
Roke si pogrejem na skodelici.I warm my hands on the cup.
winter flowers
Storyboard: Maya walks toward the market exit and stops at a flower stall. She points at a bundle of bright winter tulips and asks for the price. The vendor says they cost forty kroner. Maya buys the flowers and walks back to her apartment, drinking her hot coffee and holding the colorful tulips.
Grem proti izhodu s tržnice.I walk towards the exit of the market.
Vidim veliko cvetlično stojnico.I see a large flower stall.
Stojnica ima veliko lepih rož.The stall has many beautiful flowers.
Ustavim se pred stojnico.I stop in front of the stall.
Gledam šopek zimskih tulipanov.I look at a bundle of winter tulips.
Zelo so barviti.They are very colourful.
Pokažem na tulipane.I point at the tulips.
"Koliko stanejo tulipani?" vprašam prodajalca."How much do the tulips cost?" I ask the vendor.
Prodajalec dvigne tulipane.The vendor holds up the tulips.
"Stanejo štirideset kron," odgovori."They cost forty kroner," he answers.
"Kupila jih bom," rečem."I will buy them," I say.
Plačam rože.I pay for the flowers.
Prodajalec mi da tulipane.The vendor gives me the tulips.
Zdaj sem končala na tržnici.Now I am finished at the market.
Grem nazaj v svoje stanovanje.I walk back to my apartment.
V roki držim barvite tulipane.I hold the colourful tulips in my hand.
Pijem svojo vročo kavo.I drink my hot coffee.
Sonce sije nad živahno tržnico.The sun shines over the busy market.
