birthday messages
Storyboard: Maya wakes up on the morning she turns thirty-two, feeling a bit nostalgic as it is her first birthday away from Mexico. She smiles as she reads the flood of loving messages that her family has sent overnight while she slept.

Dnes mám totiž narodeniny.Today is actually my birthday.
Dnes mám tridsaťdva rokov.I turn thirty-two years old today.
Sú to moje prvé narodeniny ďaleko od rodiny.It is my first birthday away from my family.
Cítila som sa kvôli tomu trochu nostalgicky.I felt a little nostalgic about it.

Moja rodina mi poslala veľa láskyplných správ.My family has sent many loving messages.

Keď som tú fotku uvidela, široko som sa usmiala.I smiled broadly when I saw the picture.

Na všetky správy som okamžite odpovedala.I replied to all the messages immediately.
Napísala som im, že ich ľúbim a chýbajú mi.I wrote to them that I love and miss them.
Čítam si tie správy znova a znova.I read the messages again and again.

office pastry
Storyboard: At the software company, Maya is surprised when Peter and Eva place a small cake with a single candle on her desk. They wish her a happy birthday.

family call

Na obrazovke sú moji rodičia, moja sestra Sofía a môj brat Diego.The screen shows my parents, my sister Sofía, and my brother Diego.

„Lola tiež zjedla malú tortu,“ smeje sa."Lola has also eaten a little cake," he laughs.
Hlasno sa smejem na malom psovi.I laugh loudly at the little dog.
Sú to moje prvé narodeniny mimo domova, ale cítim, že sú mi veľmi blízko.It is my first birthday away from home, but they feel very close.
cafe summer

„Dnes som skončila skoro, takže som pripravená,“ píšem."I finished early today, so I am ready," I write.
Eva odpovedá takmer okamžite.Eva replies almost immediately.

„Práve som kúpila čerstvé jahody.“"I have just bought fresh strawberries."

waterfront

bus stop contrast

beach bonfire
