Week 12: Maya joins a badminton club

Indonesian with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding a club
Storyboard: Maya works out at the gym on Wednesdays but wants to meet people, so she looks for a social sport. She loves badminton — she played it a lot back home in Mexico — so she finds a local club online and books a trial session for Thursday evening.
Saya pergi ke gym setiap hari Rabu malam.I go to the gym every Wednesday evening.
Saya suka berolahraga.I like to work out.
Tapi saya juga ingin menekuni sebuah cabang olahraga.But I also want to play a sport.
Saya sangat suka bermain bulu tangkis.I love to play badminton.
Saya sering memainkannya dulu di Meksiko.I played it a lot back home in Mexico.
Sekarang saya sedang mencari klub lokal.Now I am looking for a local club.
Saya sedang duduk di sofa dengan komputer saya.I am sitting on my couch with my computer.
Saya mencari di internet.I search online.
Saya menemukan klub bulu tangkis yang bagus.I find a good badminton club.
Saya melihat situs web klub itu.I look at the club's website.
Mereka punya banyak tim.They have many teams.
Mereka bermain pada hari Kamis malam.They play on Thursday evening.
Saya ingin memesan sesi latihan percobaan.I want to book a trial session.
Saya menemukan sebuah formulir di situs web itu.I find a form on the website.
Saya menulis nama dan email saya.I write my name and my email.
Saya memilih hari Kamis malam.I choose Thursday evening.
Latihannya dimulai jam tujuh malam.The training starts at seven in the evening.
Itu sempurna.It is perfect.
Saya tidak sabar untuk bermain lagi.I look forward to playing again.
arriving at hall
Storyboard: On Thursday evening Maya packs her sports bag and takes the bus to a large sports hall. She walks inside and hears the loud sound of shoes squeaking on the many wooden courts.
Ini Kamis malam, dan aku ada di rumah.It is Thursday evening, and I am at home.
Aku mengemas tas olahragaku.I pack my sports bag.
Aku memasukkan sepatu dan pakaianku ke dalam tas.I put my shoes and my clothes in the bag.
Aku tidak sabar untuk bermain malam ini.I look forward to playing tonight.
Aku naik bus ke gedung olahraga yang besar.I take the bus to a large sports hall.
Ini adalah malam musim dingin yang dingin.It is a cold winter evening.
Aku duduk di bus dan melihat ke luar.I sit in the bus and look out.
Bus berhenti, dan aku turun.The bus stops, and I get out.
Aku berjalan masuk ke gedung olahraga yang besar.I walk into the large sports hall.
Tempat itu sangat terang.It is very bright.
Aku melihat banyak orang di dalam gedung.I see many people in the hall.
Mereka suka berolahraga.They love to play sports.
Aku mendengar suara yang keras.I hear a loud sound.
Sepatu-sepatu berdecit di atas banyak lapangan kayu.The shoes squeak on the many wooden courts.
Orang-orang berlari cepat dan bermain bersama.People run fast and play together.
Itu terlihat sangat menyenangkan.It looks very fun.
Aku siap untuk bermain.I am ready to play.
Tapi pertama-tama aku harus menyewa raket.But first I need to rent a racket.
renting the racket
Storyboard: Maya goes to the small hall kiosk. She tells the clerk she is there to try out the club, and pays forty kroner to rent a racket for the evening.
Saya berjalan ke sebuah kios kecil di gedung olahraga.I walk over to a small kiosk in the sports hall.
Seorang pria muda berdiri di belakang konter.A young man stands behind the counter.
Dia tersenyum dan mengucapkan halo.He smiles and says hello.
"Halo, saya ingin bermain bulu tangkis hari ini," kata saya."Hello, I want to play badminton today," I say.
"Apakah Anda baru di klub ini?" tanyanya."Are you new here in the club?" he asks.
"Ya, saya ingin mencoba ikut bergabung," jawab saya."Yes, I would like to try to join in," I answer.
"Selamat datang," kata pria itu."Welcome," the man says.
"Apakah Anda perlu menyewa raket?" tanyanya."Do you need to rent a racket?" he asks.
"Ya, terima kasih, saya ingin menyewa raket untuk malam ini," kata saya."Yes please, I want to rent a racket for the evening," I say.
Dia mengambilkan raket yang bagus untuk saya.He finds a good racket for me.
"Harganya empat puluh kroner," katanya."It costs forty kroner," he says.
Saya mengeluarkan kartu pembayaran saya.I take out my payment card.
Saya membayar empat puluh kroner tersebut.I pay the forty kroner.
Dia memberikan raket itu kepada saya melewati konter.He gives me the racket over the counter.
"Menurut saya ini olahraga yang menyenangkan," kata saya dengan gembira."I think it is a fun sport," I say happily.
"Selamat bertanding.""Have a really good game."
Saya mengucapkan terima kasih dan berjalan menuju lapangan.I say thank you and walk over to the courts.
playing doubles
Storyboard: In the busy hall, Maya joins a doubles game with two friendly players, Kasper and Sara, and a fourth person. She has played for years, so she smashes the shuttlecock hard across the net.
Saya berjalan menuju lapangan bulu tangkis.I walk over to a badminton court.
Tiga orang sedang berdiri di lapangan.Three people are standing on the court.
"Hai, saya Kasper," kata seorang pria."Hi, I am Kasper," a man says.
"Dan saya Sara," kata seorang wanita."And I am Sara," a woman says.
Pemain ketiga bernama Emil.The third player is named Emil.
"Saya Maya," kata saya."I am Maya," I say.
"Kami biasanya bermain ganda," kata Kasper."We usually play doubles," Kasper says.
"Apakah kamu mau bermain bersama kami?" tanyanya."Do you want to play with us?" he asks.
"Ya, dengan senang hati," jawab saya."Yes, I would love to," I reply.
Saya sangat suka bermain bulu tangkis.I really like to play badminton.
Kasper dan saya bermain melawan Sara dan Emil.Kasper and I play against Sara and Emil.
Kami segera mulai bermain.We start to play immediately.
Sara memulai pertandingan.Sara starts the match.
Kok terbang bolak-balik dengan cepat.The shuttlecock flies quickly back and forth.
Emil memukul kok tinggi ke udara.Emil hits the shuttlecock high up in the air.
Saya suka sekali melakukan smes.I love to smash.
Saya melompat dan menyemesnya dengan keras melewati net.I jump and smash it hard over the net.
Kok itu mendarat di sisi lapangan mereka.It lands on their side of the court.
"Pukulan yang bagus, Maya!" teriak Kasper."Good shot, Maya!" Kasper shouts.
post match chat
Storyboard: The fast match finishes and the tired players walk off the court. Sara says Maya plays really well, and Maya explains that she plays a lot back home in Mexico.
Pertandingan cepat itu sudah berakhir.The fast match is over.
Kami berjalan keluar lapangan bersama-sama.We walk off the court together.
Saya sangat lelah.I am very tired.
Kasper dan Sara juga lelah.Kasper and Sara are also tired.
Kami minum sedikit air.We drink some water.
"Kamu bermain dengan sangat baik," kata Sara."You play very well," Sara says.
"Terima kasih banyak," kata saya."Thank you very much," I say.
"Menurut saya ini sangat menyenangkan.""I think it is so much fun."
"Apakah kamu sering bermain?" tanya Kasper."Do you play often?" Kasper asks.
"Ya, saya banyak bermain di kampung halaman saya di Meksiko," jawab saya."Yes, I play a lot back home in Mexico," I answer.
"Saya biasanya bermain dengan saudara perempuan saya.""I usually play with my sister."
"Ini olahraga yang menyenangkan," kata Sara."It is a fun sport," Sara says.
"Kami sangat suka bermain di sini di klub.""We love to play here in the club."
"Saya ingin bermain lebih banyak di sini di Denmark," kata saya."I want to play more here in Denmark," I say.
"Kamu benar-benar cepat," Kasper tertawa."You are definitely fast," Kasper laughs.
"Terima kasih, tapi sekarang saya ingin duduk," kata saya."Thanks, but now I want to sit down," I say.
"Bagaimana kalau kita masuk ke kafetaria?" tanya Kasper."Shall we go into the cafeteria?" Kasper asks.
"Ya, saya mau sekali," jawab saya."Yes, I would very much like that," I answer.
hall cafeteria
Storyboard: The group sits in the small hall cafeteria to drink water and chat. Kasper asks if Maya plans to come back, and she happily promises to make it a weekly Thursday routine.
Kami masuk ke kafetaria kecil di aula itu.We walk into the small cafeteria in the hall.
Kami menemukan sebuah meja.We find a table.
Aku sangat haus.I am very thirsty.
Kami minum air dingin dan mengobrol.We drink cold water and talk.
"Klub ini benar-benar bagus," kataku."This is a really good club," I say.
"Ini olahraga yang bagus untuk dilakukan," kata Kasper."It is a good sport to do," Kasper says.
Kasper menatapku.Kasper looks at me.
"Apakah kamu mau bergabung lagi?" tanyanya."Do you want to join again?" he asks.
"Ya, mau sekali," jawabku."Yes, very much," I answer.
"Aku ingin bermain di sini setiap hari Kamis," jelasku."I want to play here every Thursday," I explain.
"Kedengarannya menyenangkan," kata Sara."That sounds lovely," Sara says.
"Kami selalu bermain di sini pada hari Kamis," kata Kasper."We always play here on Thursdays," Kasper says.
"Kalau begitu, kita bertemu lagi minggu depan," kataku dengan senang."Then we'll see each other next week," I say happily.
Kami meminum sisa air kami.We drink the rest of our water.
Senang rasanya bertemu orang-orang baru.It is lovely to meet new people.
walking to bus

"Tapi aku suka berolahraga," katanya."But I like to do sports," she says.
