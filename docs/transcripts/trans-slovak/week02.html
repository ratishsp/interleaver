morning commute
Storyboard: Maya walks briskly through the chilly morning air, looking for her new workplace. She checks the large numbers on the buildings along the street until she spots the software company's logo next to number forty-four.
Je ráno v Kodani.It is morning in Copenhagen.
Je január a je veľmi chladno.It is January, and it is very cold.
Som trochu nervózna.I am a little nervous.
Dnes je veľký deň.Today is a big day.
Je to pre mňa nový začiatok.It is a new start for me.
Je to môj prvý deň v novej práci.It is my first day at my new job.
Idem do práce autobusom.I take the bus to work.
Vystupujem na správnej zastávke.I get off at the right stop.
Teraz idem posledný kúsok pešo.Now I walk the last part.
Kde je kancelária?Where is the office?
Pozerám sa na veľké budovy.I look at the large buildings.
Každá budova má číslo.Each building has a number.
Tu je číslo tridsaťosem.Here is number thirty-eight.
A tu je číslo štyridsať.And here is number forty.
Idem o kúsok ďalej.I walk a little further.
Tu je číslo štyridsaťdva.Here is number forty-two.
A tam to je!And there it is!
Tu je číslo štyridsaťštyri.Here is number forty-four.
Vidím veľké logo.I see a large logo.
Moja nová softvérová firma je priamo tu.My new software company is right here.
Mám dobrý pocit.I have a good feeling.
Som úplne pripravená.I am completely ready.
meeting peter
Storyboard: Inside the bright office, a smiling colleague introduces himself as Peter. He shakes her hand, hands her a new laptop, and leads her through the open-plan workspace to her assigned desk.
Teraz som v novej kancelárii.I am now inside the new office.
Je to veľká a svetlá miestnosť.It is a large and bright room.
Pri dverách stojí priateľský muž.A friendly man stands by the door.
Usmieva sa na mňa.He smiles at me.
„Vitaj!“ hovorí."Welcome!" he says.
„Volám sa Peter.“"My name is Peter."
Podávam mu ruku.I shake his hand.
„Ahoj Peter, volám sa Maya,“ hovorím."Hi Peter, my name is Maya," I say.
Peter je kolega.Peter is a colleague.
Kráčame spolu cez kanceláriu.We walk together through the office.
„Tu máš stôl,“ hovorí Peter."You have a desk here," says Peter.
Stôl má číslo šestnásť.The desk has number sixteen.
„A máš počítač,“ hovorí."And you have a computer," he says.
Dáva mi nový notebook.He gives me a new laptop.
Pozerám sa na svoje nové miesto.I look at my new spot.
Mám stoličku a veľký monitor.I have a chair and a large monitor.
„Veľmi pekne ďakujem, Peter,“ hovorím."Thank you very much, Peter," I say.
Som pripravená na svoj prvý pracovný deň.I am ready for my first workday.
the phone shop
Storyboard: On her lunch break, Maya ducks into a telecom shop to buy a local SIM card. She hands the clerk her phone, and he activates it, reading her brand-new local number aloud as she saves it.
Mám obedňajšiu prestávku.It is my lunch break.
Som v predajni telefónov.I am in a phone shop.
Mám telefón z Mexika.I have a phone from Mexico.
Predavač je mladý muž.The clerk is a young man.
„Máte dánsku SIM kartu?“ pýtam sa."Do you have a Danish SIM card?" I ask.
„Áno, samozrejme,“ odpovedá."Yes, of course," he answers.
Dávam mu svoj telefón.I give him my phone.
Vkladá SIM kartu do telefónu.He puts the SIM card in the phone.
Telefón je teraz pripravený.The phone is ready now.
„Vaše nové číslo je dvanásť, tridsaťštyri, päťdesiatšesť, sedemdesiatosem,“ hovorí."Your new number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," he says.
Mám v taške zápisník.I have a notebook in my bag.
Zapisujem si to číslo.I write the number.
„Dvanásť, tridsaťštyri, päťdesiatšesť, sedemdesiatosem,“ opakujem."Twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I repeat.
„Áno, presne tak,“ hovorí predavač."Yes, exactly," says the clerk.
„Ďakujem za pomoc,“ hovorím."Thank you for the help," I say.
Teraz mám dánske číslo!I have a Danish number now!
Dávam si telefón do vrecka.I put the phone in my pocket.
queue ticket
Storyboard: The next morning, Maya enters the crowded public registration office. She presses a button on a red terminal, pulls a paper ticket, and checks her high number before finding a seat to wait.
Je nasledujúce ráno.It is the next morning.
Dnes som na veľkom registračnom úrade.I am at the large registration office today.
Je tu vnútri veľa ľudí.There are many people in here.
Mám svoj pas a papiere v taške.I have my passport and my papers in my bag.
Všetko je pripravené na moju registráciu.Everything is ready for my registration.
Stojím pri červenom automate.I stand by a red machine.
Stlačím tlačidlo.I press a button.
Automat mi vydá malý lístok s číslom.The machine gives me a small number slip.
Aké je moje číslo?What is my number?
Pozriem sa na papier.I look at the paper.
Mám číslo päťdesiatdeväť.I have number fifty-nine.
Je to dosť vysoké číslo.It is a rather high number.
Veľká obrazovka na stene ukazuje číslo.A large screen on the wall shows a number.
Obrazovka ukazuje len číslo dvadsaťjeden.The screen only shows number twenty-one.
Mám dosť času.I have plenty of time.
Nájdem si voľnú stoličku.I find an empty chair.
Sadnem si so svojimi papiermi.I sit down with my papers.
Teraz čakám na svoj rad.Now I wait for my turn.
registration
Storyboard: The screen chimes with her number, and Maya steps up to the counter. She volunteers her name and age to the clerk, hands over her passport, and he processes the paperwork for her private national ID number.
Obrazovka ukazuje moje číslo.A screen shows my number.
To číslo je päťdesiatdeväť.The number is fifty-nine.
Idem k priehradke.I walk up to the counter.
Za priehradkou sedí muž.A man sits behind the counter.
"Dobré ráno," hovorí."Good morning," he says.
"Dobré ráno," odpovedám."Good morning," I reply.
"Volám sa Maya a mám tridsaťjeden rokov," hovorím."My name is Maya, and I am thirty-one years old," I say.
"Vitajte, Maya," hovorí."Welcome, Maya," he says.
"Máte svoj pas?" pýta sa."Do you have your passport?" he asks.
"Áno, mám svoj pas a svoje papiere," odpovedám."Yes, I have my passport and my papers," I answer.
"Nech sa páči," hovorím."Here you go," I say.
Pozerá sa na papiere.He looks at the papers.
Píše na svojom počítači.He types on his computer.
Dostávam dánske osobné identifikačné číslo.I get a Danish personal identification number.
To číslo je súkromné a veľmi dôležité.The number is private and very important.
Dáva mi novú kartu.He gives me a new card.
"Nech sa páči, tu je vaša karta," hovorí."Here you go, here is your card," he says.
"Teraz ste zaregistrovaná v Dánsku.""Now you are registered in Denmark."
Usmievam sa a beriem si kartu.I smile and take the card.
Teraz som súčasťou Dánska.Now I am a part of Denmark.
bakery celebration
Storyboard: Heading home from the registration office, Maya stops at a neighbourhood bakery to celebrate being officially registered at last. She points to a cinnamon pastry in the glass display, asks the price, and taps her card to pay.
Znova som vonku na ulici.I am out on the street again.
Všetko je konečne na svojom mieste.Everything is finally in place.
Je čas na malú oslavu.It is time for a little celebration.
Na rohu vidím malú pekáreň.I see a little bakery on the corner.
Idem dovnútra.I go inside.
Krásne to tu vonia.It smells lovely.
Za pultom stojí pekár.A baker stands behind the counter.
„Dobrý deň,“ hovorí pekár."Hello," the baker says.
„Dobrý deň,“ odpovedám."Hello," I reply.
Ukážem na koláč za sklom.I point to a pastry behind the glass.
„Prosila by som si škoricové osie hniezdo,“ hovorím."I would like a cinnamon pastry," I say.
„Áno, samozrejme,“ hovorí."Yes, of course," he says.
„Koľko stojí?“ pýtam sa."What does it cost?" I ask.
„Stojí dvadsaťpäť korún,“ odpovedá pekár."It costs twenty-five kroner," the baker replies.
Peňaženku mám v taške.I have my wallet in my bag.
Vyberiem platobnú kartu.I take out my debit card.
Zaplatím za svoje škoricové osie hniezdo.I pay for my cinnamon pastry.
Pekár mi dá malé vrecúško.The baker gives me a small bag.
Teraz mám škoricové osie hniezdo na svoju malú oslavu.Now I have a cinnamon pastry for my little celebration.
swapping numbers

„Áno, mám nové telefónne číslo,“ odpovedám."Yes, I have a new phone number," I reply.
„To je dobre,“ hovorí."That is good," she says.

„Aké je tvoje číslo?“ pýtam sa."What is your number?" I ask.
„Moje číslo je osemdesiat sedem, šesťdesiat päť, štyridsať tri, dvadsať jedna,“ hovorí Nina."My number is eighty-seven, sixty-five, forty-three, twenty-one," Nina says.
Zapisujem si jej číslo do telefónu.I write her number on my phone.
„A moje číslo je dvanásť, tridsať štyri, päťdesiat šesť, sedemdesiat osem,“ hovorím."And my number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
Nina si ukladá moje číslo do svojho telefónu.Nina saves my number on her phone.
Teraz sme v kontakte.Now we are in touch.

Mám veľkú radosť z mojej novej kamarátky.I am very happy about my new friend.
