Week 1: Maya arrives in Copenhagen

Croatian with English gloss, line-aligned.

airplane arrival
Storyboard: Maya packs her book into her bag as the plane touches down in Copenhagen. The passenger next to her notices her excitement and asks her name and where she is from. Maya introduces herself with a smile.
Avion je sada u Kopenhagenu.The airplane is in Copenhagen now.
Siječanj je.It is January.
Jako sam uzbuđena.I am very excited.
Spremam knjigu u torbu.I pack my book into my bag.
Pored mene je putnik.There is a passenger next to me.
Gleda me i smiješi se.He looks at me and smiles.
"Bok", kaže on."Hello," he says.
"Bok", kažem."Hello," I say.
"Kako se zoveš?" pita on."What is your name?" he asks.
"Zovem se Maya", kažem."My name is Maya," I say.
"Odakle dolaziš, Maya?" pita on."Where do you come from, Maya?" he asks.
Veselo se nasmiješim.I smile happily.
"Dolazim iz Meksika", odgovaram."I come from Mexico," I answer.
"Jesi li iz Danske?" pitam."Are you from Denmark?" I ask.
"Da, ja sam iz Danske", kaže on."Yes, I am from Denmark," he says.
"Dobrodošla u Dansku", kaže on."Welcome to Denmark," he says.
"Puno hvala", kažem."Thank you very much," I say.
Sada smo ovdje.Now we are here.
Spremna sam.I am ready.
the taxi
Storyboard: Maya lifts her luggage into the trunk of a taxi. She gives the driver her new address, and he asks if she is a tourist. She proudly corrects him, explaining her new job.
Sada sam vani.I am outside now.
Moj kofer je velik i težak.My suitcase is big and heavy.
Tu je taksi.There is a taxi.
Podižem svoj kofer u taksi.I lift my suitcase into the taxi.
Vozač je ljubazan čovjek.The driver is a friendly man.
"Koja je adresa?" pita on."What is the address?" he asks.
"To je Parkvej broj dvanaest," kažem."It is Park Road number twelve," I say.
On kima i vozi.He nods and drives.
"Jeste li turistica?" pita vozač."Are you a tourist?" the driver asks.
"Ne, nisam turistica," odgovaram."No, I am not a tourist," I answer.
"Odakle dolazite?" pita on."Where do you come from?" he asks.
"Dolazim iz Meksika," kažem."I come from Mexico," I say.
Gledam kroz prozor.I look out the window.
Jako sam ponosna.I am very proud.
"Ja sam softverska inženjerka," kažem."I am a software engineer," I say.
"Moj novi posao je ovdje u Kopenhagenu.""My new job is here in Copenhagen."
On se smiješi.He smiles.
"Kako uzbudljivo.""How exciting."
"Sretno u Danskoj," kaže on."Good luck in Denmark," he says.
"Puno hvala," kažem."Thank you very much," I say.
suitcase struggle
Storyboard: Maya wrestles her heavy suitcase over the threshold of her new flat. The hallway is completely dark, so she runs her hands along the wall, hunting for the light switches, and finally illuminates the small, furnished flat.
Vrata su otvorena.The door is open.
Prag je visok.The threshold is high.
Kofer je vrlo težak!The suitcase is very heavy!
Ali sada je kofer u hodniku.But now the suitcase is in the hallway.
Ovdje je potpuno mračno.It is completely dark here.
Gdje je prekidač?Where is the switch?
Zid je hladan.The wall is cold.
Je li prekidač ovdje?Is the switch here?
Da, tu je prekidač!Yes, there is the switch!
Sada ima svjetla.Now there is light.
U stanu sam.I am in the apartment.
Mali je, ali je lijep.It is small, but it is nice.
Ovdje je dnevni boravak.Here is a living room.
Tu su kauč i stol.There is a sofa and a table.
To je namješten dom.It is a furnished home.
Kofer je na podu.The suitcase is on the floor.
Jako sam umorna.I am very tired.
Ali sam i sretna.But I am also happy.
U Kopenhagenu sam!I am in Copenhagen!
window and home
Storyboard: Maya crosses to the window and rests her hand on the cold glass, looking out at the grey January afternoon. Warm inside now, she watches the icy city and feels a sudden pang for the sunshine back home in Mexico.
Stojim kraj prozora.I am standing by the window.
Staklo je hladno.The glass is cold.
Siječanj je.It is January.
Gledam van.I look out.
Kopenhagen je danas siv.Copenhagen is grey today.
Vani je hladno.It is cold outside.
Na ulici je snijeg.There is snow on the street.
Ali ovdje je toplo.But it is warm here.
Stan je dobar.The apartment is good.
Razmišljam o Meksiku.I think about Mexico.
U Meksiku ima sunca.In Mexico, there is sun.
Tamo je također toplo.It is also warm there.
Malo mi nedostaje sunce.I miss the sun a little.
Ali smiješim se.But I smile.
To je novi grad.It is a new city.
Sada sam u Danskoj.I am in Denmark now.
To je dobar početak.It is a good start.
calling mom
Storyboard: Maya sits down and makes a quick audio call to her mother. She tells her that she has arrived safely and that the flat is small but she is happy.
Sada sam u dnevnom boravku.I am in the living room now.
Tu je mali kauč.There is a small sofa.
Zovem majku u Meksiku.I call my mother in Mexico.
"Bok, mama", kažem."Hi Mom," I say.
"Bok, Maya", kaže moja majka."Hi Maya," my mother says.
"Gdje si?", pita ona."Where are you?" she asks.
"U Kopenhagenu sam", odgovaram."I am in Copenhagen," I answer.
"Sada sam u stanu.""I am in the apartment now."
"To je baš dobro", kaže moja majka."That is so good," my mother says.
"Je li velik?", pita ona."Is it big?" she asks.
"Ne, mali je", odgovaram."No, it is small," I answer.
"Ali je udoban i sretna sam.""But it is cozy, and I am happy."
"Je li hladno?", pita ona."Is it cold?" she asks.
"Da, vani je hladno", kažem."Yes, it is cold outside," I say.
"Ali ovdje je toplo.""But it is warm in here."
"Na sigurnom sam.""I am safe."
"To je dobro", kaže moja majka."That is good," my mother says.
Smiješim se.I smile.
Danska je moj novi dom.Denmark is my new home.
meeting nina
Storyboard: Maya steps into the hallway, and the door next to hers opens: a friendly woman introduces herself as Nina, her neighbour, and welcomes her warmly, telling Maya to knock on her door any time she needs help. Nina does NOT give a phone number yet — numbers arrive in week 2.
Otvaram svoja vrata.I open my door.
Stojim na stubištu.I am standing in the stairwell.
Tiho je.It is quiet.
Vrata se otvaraju.A door opens.
Jedna žena mi se smiješi.A woman smiles at me.
"Bok", kaže ona."Hi," she says.
"Kako se zoveš?", pita ona."What is your name?" she asks.
"Zovem se Maya", odgovaram."My name is Maya," I answer.
"Odakle dolaziš?", pita ona."Where do you come from?" she asks.
"Dolazim iz Meksika", kažem."I come from Mexico," I say.
"Dobrodošla u zgradu", kaže žena."Welcome to the building," the woman says.
"Zovem se Nina", kaže ona."My name is Nina," she says.
"Ja sam tvoja susjeda.""I am your neighbour."
Nina je jako ljubazna.Nina is very friendly.
Govori polako i jasno.She speaks slowly and clearly.
"Ovdje su moja vrata", kaže ona."Here is my door," she says.
"Rado ću pomoći.""I am happy to help."
"Samo pokucaj", kaže Nina."You just knock," Nina says.
"Hvala ti puno, Nina", odgovaram."Thank you very much, Nina," I answer.
Jako sam sretna.I am very happy.
Sada imam prijateljicu u Kopenhagenu.Now I have a friend in Copenhagen.
bedtime routine
Storyboard: Maya squeezes toothpaste onto her toothbrush in the small bathroom. She washes her face, gets into the small bed, and sets her alarm for the morning.
Sada je noć.Now it is night.
Ovo je moja prva noć u Kopenhagenu.It is my first night in Copenhagen.
U kupaonici sam.I am in the bathroom.
Jako je mala.It is very small.
Tu je umivaonik.There is a sink.
Moja četkica za zube je crvena.My toothbrush is red.
Stavljam pastu na svoju četkicu za zube.I put toothpaste on my toothbrush.
Perem zube.I brush my teeth.
Voda je hladna.The water is cold.
Perem lice.I wash my face.
Brišem lice.I dry my face.
Idem do svog kreveta.I go over to my bed.
Krevet je uzak.The bed is narrow.
Ali je topao i mekan.But it is warm and soft.
Sada ležim ispod popluna.I am lying under the duvet now.
Moj telefon je na stolu.My phone is lying on the table.
Postavljam alarm.I set my alarm.
Zvoni sutra u osam sati.It rings at eight o'clock tomorrow.
Umorna sam, ali sam spremna.I am tired, but I am ready.
Laku noć, Kopenhagene.Goodnight, Copenhagen.
