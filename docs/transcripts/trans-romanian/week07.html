Week 7: Maya plans a busy week

planning the week
Storyboard: On Monday morning, Maya makes a cup of tea and opens her calendar at the kitchen table — she always plans the week here. She works through it: the bike goes to the repair shop, the salon needs a phone call, the gym is on Wednesday. She writes each one in, and February fills up.
Este luni dimineața.It is Monday morning.
Fac o ceașcă de ceai fierbinte.I make a cup of hot tea.
Stau la masă în bucătărie.I sit at the table in the kitchen.
Îmi deschid calendarul.I open my calendar.
Îmi planific mereu săptămâna aici.I always plan my week here.
Acum este februarie.It is February now.
Luna este deja foarte plină.The month is already very full.
Mâine este marți.Tomorrow is Tuesday.
Bicicleta mea este, din păcate, stricată.My bicycle is unfortunately broken.
Merg la mecanicul de biciclete mâine dimineață.I walk to the bicycle mechanic tomorrow morning.
El deschide la ora opt.He opens at eight o'clock.
Scriu asta în calendar.I write it in the calendar.
Sun și la coafor astăzi.I also call the hairdresser today.
Părul meu este mult prea lung.My hair is way too long.
Miercuri merg la sala de fitness.Wednesday I go to the gym.
Mă antrenez adesea miercurea.I often train on Wednesdays.
De obicei este la ora șapte seara.It is usually at seven o'clock in the evening.
Mă uit din nou la planul meu.I look at my plan again.
Îmi beau ceaiul.I drink my tea.
Acum sunt pregătită pentru o nouă săptămână.Now I am ready for a new week.
dropping off the bike
Storyboard: On Tuesday morning at a quarter past eight, Maya wheels her bicycle to the local repair shop. She hands the bike to the mechanic, leaves the shop, and walks the rest of the way to the office.

booking a haircut
Storyboard: During her lunch break, Maya calls a hair salon on her mobile phone. She speaks to the hairdresser and books an appointment for Thursday at half past four.
Este pauza mea de prânz.It is my lunch break.
Stau în cantina de la serviciu.I am sitting in the canteen at my work.
Am fost ocupată ieri.I was busy yesterday.
De aceea sun la coafor acum.Therefore I am calling the hairdresser now.
Îmi iau telefonul mobil.I take my mobile phone.
Sun la un salon de coafură din oraș.I call a hair salon in the city.
O femeie răspunde la telefon.A woman answers the phone.
"Bună ziua, aici este salonul de coafură", spune femeia."Hello, this is the hair salon," says the woman.
"Bună ziua, mă numesc Maya", spun eu."Hello, my name is Maya," I say.
"Aș dori să fac o programare pentru joi.""I would like to book an appointment on Thursday."
"Un moment", spune coafeza."One moment," says the hairdresser.
"Aveți o oră disponibilă după-amiaza?" întreb eu."Do you have an appointment in the afternoon?" I ask.
"Da, avem o oră disponibilă la patru și jumătate", spune ea."Yes, we have an available appointment at half past four," she says.
"Îmi convine foarte bine", spun eu."That suits me very well," I say.
"Care este numărul dumneavoastră de telefon?" întreabă coafeza."What is your phone number?" asks the hairdresser.
"Numărul meu este doisprezece, treizeci și patru, cincizeci și șase, șaptezeci și opt", spun eu."My number is twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight," I say.
"Mulțumesc mult, ne vedem joi la patru și jumătate", spune ea."Thank you very much, see you on Thursday at half past four," she says.
"Ne vedem", răspund eu."See you," I reply.
Pun telefonul pe masă.I put the phone on the table.
Acum, în sfârșit, îmi mănânc mâncarea.Now I finally eat my food.

hallway chat
Storyboard: After the gym, Maya bumps into Nina in the stairwell. They stand and chat cheerfully, and they agree to drink coffee together on Thursday afternoon at half past four.
Urc scările.I walk up the stairs.
Sunt obosită după sală.I am tired after the gym.

scheduling conflict
