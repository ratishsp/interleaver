# Week 4 — Storyboard (Maya moves into her permanent flat)

**Level:** A1 · **Grammar:** The locative case (**सप्तमी** — गृहे, प्रकोष्ठे); place words **उपरि / अधः / समीपे**; placement with **स्थापितम् अस्ति** (review *there is*)

| # | stem | scene |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | 01_packing_temporary_room | Maya gathers her final belongings in her temporary room. She checks the space carefully, making sure nothing is left lying on the bed or inside the small cupboard before she zips up her suitcase. |
| 2 | 02_corridor_walk | Maya rolls her heavy suitcase out into the corridor. Her neighbour Asha meets her there, cheerfully taking Maya's smaller bag, and they walk together towards the permanent flat in the same building. |
| 3 | 03_unlocking_the_door | Maya slides her new key into the lock and pushes the door open. She steps inside and looks around the bright, furnished living room, happy to finally see her real home. |
| 4 | 04_dropping_the_bags | Maya leaves her large suitcase on the floor near the door. Asha sets the small bag down right next to it, telling Maya to take her time getting settled. |
| 5 | 05_kitchen_inspection | Maya explores the small kitchen to see what is already there. She finds a few heavy pots kept in the cabinets below the counter, and some cups neatly placed on the shelf above. |
| 6 | 06_arranging_the_bedroom | Moving into the bedroom, Maya unpacks her clothes. She hangs her shirts in the wooden wardrobe and slides the empty suitcase out of the way under the bed. |
| 7 | 07_setting_up_workspace | Maya decides where her home office goes. She takes her laptop out of her backpack and places it carefully on the desk near the window, arranging her notebooks beside it. |
| 8 | 08_asha_brings_chai | Asha knocks lightly on the open front door, bringing two fresh cups of hot chai. She steps inside and places the cups on the small table in the living room so they can take a break. |
| 9 | 09_first_quiet_evening | After Asha says goodnight and leaves, Maya sits alone on her comfortable sofa. She looks around at her neatly arranged flat, her things finally in their proper places, and feels completely at home in Mumbai. |
