Week 20: Maya starts volunteering at the café

Cantonese with English gloss, line-aligned.

finding the volunteering
Storyboard: Maya has heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time, and she likes the idea — a way to meet people and use her Danish. One evening she browses a volunteering website, reads a few listings, and finds the little café she already knows, which needs weekend helpers, so she fills in the short sign-up form.
我聽講好多丹麥人都會喺空餘時間做義工。I have heard that many Danes volunteer in their free time.
我好鍾意呢個主意。I like the idea.
我想學講好啲丹麥文，同埋識多啲新朋友。I want to learn to speak better Danish and meet new people.
有一晚，我坐喺度用電腦。One evening, I am sitting with my computer.
我搵到一個做義工嘅網站。I find a website for voluntary work.
我睇咗網站上面嘅頭三個招募。I read the first three listings on the site.
第一個招募係來自一間體育會嘅。The first listing is from a sports club.
不過佢哋星期四晚需要人幫手，而我嗰晚要打羽毛球。But they need help on Thursday evenings, and I play badminton then.
第二個招募係來自一間博物館嘅。The second listing is from a museum.
佢哋星期三晚需要一個人，但我嗰晚要健身。They need a person on Wednesday evenings, but I work out then.
跟住我睇第三個招募。Then I look at the third listing.
佢係來自一間我已經識得嘅細咖啡店。It is from the little café that I already know.
佢哋星期六上晝需要人。They need people for Saturday mornings.
咁就啱晒我喇。That suits me absolutely perfectly.
我填咗一份簡短嘅表格，寫低我嘅名同電郵地址。I fill in a short form with my name and my email address.
我仲寫埋我嘅電話號碼：十二、三十四、五十六、七十八。I also write my phone number: twelve, thirty-four, fifty-six, seventy-eight.
最後，我撳發送。Finally, I press send.
我以前從來未試過喺咖啡店做嘢，但我準備好去學。I have never worked in a café before, but I am ready to learn it.
first shift arrival
Storyboard: On her very first Saturday shift, Maya arrives early at the little café. She ties her new apron in the back room and carries the chairs out to the pavement to prepare for opening.
呢個係我喺呢間細咖啡店嘅第一個星期六更。It is my first Saturday shift at the little café.
我早咗到。I have arrived early.
陽光照落哥本哈根嘅街道上。The sun is shining on the street in Copenhagen.
我好期待，但亦都有少少緊張。I am looking forward to it, but I am also a little nervous.
呢個係我第一次喺咖啡店做嘢。It is the first time I am working in a café.
我行咗入後房做準備。I went into the back room to get ready.
枱上面放咗一條畀我嘅新圍裙。On a table lay a new apron for me.
我著起佢，喺背後打咗個結。I put it on and tied it in the back.
著住佢感覺幾好。It felt good to wear it.
而家我準備好開始喇。Now I am ready to begin.
我要學識點樣正確咁開舖。I have to learn to open the café correctly.
首先，經理幫嗰度好重嘅大門開咗鎖。First, the manager unlocked the heavy front door.
之後，我行咗出去行人路做準備。Afterwards, I went out onto the pavement to get ready.
我搬咗四張凳出去。I carried four chairs out.
我將佢哋圍住一張細枱擺好。I have placed them around a little table.
一陣間其他義工就會到。In a little while, the other volunteers will arrive.
我對我嘅第一日感到好興奮。I am very excited about my first day.
learning the machine
Storyboard: Before the doors open, an experienced volunteer brings Maya over to the large espresso machine. The volunteer shows her how to grind the coffee beans and how to steam the milk. Maya watches every step closely, because it is her first time behind the counter.
呢個係我第一次喺櫃枱後面。It is my first time behind the counter.
間咖啡室仲未開門。The café is not open yet.
其中一個有經驗嘅義工叫我。One of the experienced volunteers calls for me.
我哋企喺部大型特濃咖啡機前面。We stand in front of the large espresso machine.
佢話：「今日你要學沖咖啡。」"Today you must learn to make coffee," he says.
我以前從來未用過呢種機沖咖啡。I have never made coffee on such a machine before.
第一步係磨咖啡豆。The first step is to grind the coffee beans.
佢撳咗一個細掣。He presses a small button.
部機好大聲咁磨碎啲啡色嘅豆。The machine grinds the brown beans with a loud sound.
第二步係蒸熱啲牛奶。The second step is to steam the milk.
佢將凍牛奶倒入一個金屬壺入面。He pours cold milk into a metal jug.
然後佢開咗啲熱蒸氣。Afterwards he turns on the hot steam.
期間我好留心咁望住佢對手。I look closely at his hands meanwhile.
壺入面嘅牛奶好快就變得又熱又杰。The milk in the jug quickly becomes warm and thick.
好明顯佢以前沖過好多次咖啡。He has clearly made coffee many times before.
今朝我嚟到嘅時候，覺得好緊張。When I arrived this morning, I was very nervous.
不過佢解釋得好好，所以我已經明咗大部份。But he explained it well, so I have already understood most of it.
the first coffee
Storyboard: When the morning rush hits, Maya makes a cappuccino for a waiting customer. She has held the steam wand a bit too deep in the milk, so it does not get a thick foam. It still looks acceptable, though, so she pours the milk over the coffee and hands it over with a polite smile.
今朝我哋超級忙。This morning we have been incredibly busy.
我啱啱沖咗我第一杯卡布奇諾。I have just made my very first cappuccino.
十點前少少，有個男人行咗埋嚟櫃枱。A little before ten, a man came over to the counter.
佢話：「唔該，一杯卡布奇諾。」"A cappuccino, please," he said.
我啱啱先學識用濃縮咖啡機。I have just learned to use the espresso machine.
所以我即刻行咗埋去部機度。So I went over to the machine right away.
我沖好杯咖啡，然後將條蒸氣管放入啲牛奶度。I brewed the coffee and put the steam wand down into the milk.
但係我放得有啲太深。But I held it a little too deep down.
我可能係有啲太緊張。I have probably been a little too nervous.
好可惜，啲牛奶打唔出厚厚嘅奶泡。Unfortunately, the milk did not get any thick foam.
我小心噉望入個奶壺入面。I looked carefully down into the pitcher.
啲奶泡唔算完全完美。The foam was not completely perfect.
但睇落都仲可以接受。But it still looked acceptable.
我將啲熱牛奶倒入裝住咖啡嘅杯度。I poured the hot milk over the coffee in the cup.
然後我帶住有禮貌嘅笑容，將杯咖啡遞畀個男人。Then I gave the coffee to the man with a polite smile.
我話：「呢杯畀你，盛惠五十五克朗。」"Here you go, that will be fifty-five kroner," I said.
佢接過隻杯，然後喺第一張枱坐低。He took the cup and sat down at the first table.
bun with cheese
Storyboard: A moment later, she slices a warm sourdough bun and adds a thick piece of cheese. She places it on a small plate and navigates through the standing customers to deliver it safely to a corner booth.
冇幾耐，下一個柯打就嚟喇。Shortly after, the next order came.
我今日已經沖咗第一杯咖啡。I have already made my first coffee today.
但係呢個客淨係想要嘢食。But this customer just wanted food.
佢叫咗一個芝士餐包。He ordered a bun with cheese.
我喺籃度攞咗一個微溫嘅酸種餐包。I took a warm sourdough bun from the basket.
酸種麵包通常有層硬殼。Sourdough bread often has a hard crust.
但係我已經學識咗好快咁將個麵包切開一半。But I have learned to cut the bread in half quickly.
之後，我喺個餐包上面放咗一塊厚芝士。Afterwards, I put a thick slice of cheese on the bun.
我將啲嘢食靚靚咁擺喺一隻細碟度。I arranged the food nicely on a small plate.
而家我要將佢送過去畀個客。Now I had to deliver it to the customer.
咖啡室中間企咗好多人。There were many people standing in the middle of the café.
我平穩咁捧住隻碟，好小心咁向前行。I balanced the plate and walked carefully forward.
我慢慢咁行過其他客人身邊。I walked slowly past the other guests.
好彩，嗰個客坐咗喺最角落頭。Fortunately, the customer was sitting right down in the corner.
佢喺第四張枱等緊。He was waiting at the fourth table.
我將隻碟放低喺佢前面張枱度。I placed the plate on the table in front of him.
「微溫嘅芝士餐包，請慢用，」我話。"Here you go, a warm bun with cheese," I said.
「多謝晒，」個男人答。"Thank you very much," the man replied.
見到客人咁滿意，真係好開心。It is lovely to see a satisfied guest.
the ginger beer
Storyboard: During her second Saturday shift, a woman asks for a ginger beer. Maya finds the ginger beer in the glass fridge, inverts it so that the ginger mixes. Then she hands it to the customer.
嗰日係我喺咖啡店嘅第二個星期六更。It was my second Saturday shift in the café.
我已經學識咗做呢度好多嘅工作。I have already learned to do a lot of the work.
嗰個朝早出面好溫暖，非常舒服。That morning it was wonderfully warm outside.
有個女人行咗埋嚟櫃枱。A woman came over to the counter.
「我想要一支薑啤，」佢話。"I would like to ask for a ginger beer," she said.
「當然可以，等一陣，」我答。"Of course, just a moment," I answered.
我行咗過去櫃枱後面嗰個大玻璃雪櫃度。I went over to the large glass fridge behind the counter.
我望向第三層架。I looked on the third shelf.
嗰度放咗一支支凍冰冰嘅薑啤。The cold bottles of ginger beer were standing there.
我搵到一支，然後攞咗佢出嚟。I found a bottle and took it out.
啲薑好似一嚿厚厚嘅雲咁沉喺樽底。The ginger lay like a thick cloud down at the bottom.
我學識咗喺開樽之前要溝勻佢。I have learned to mix it before I open the bottle.
所以我好小心咁將個樽倒轉。So I turned the bottle very carefully upside down.
我就咁揸住咗幾秒。I held it like that for a couple of seconds.
啲薑慢慢同其他液體混和。The ginger mixed slowly with the rest of the liquid.
依家支嘢飲望落就啱晒喇。Now the drink looked exactly right.
我將個樽掉返轉頭，然後放喺櫃枱上面。I turned the bottle around again and placed it on the counter.
「呢度，總共三十五克朗，」我同佢講。"Here you go, that will be thirty-five kroner," I said to her.
佢畀咗錢，應該好享受佢嗰支凍飲。She paid, and she has probably enjoyed her cold drink.
second coffee progress
Storyboard: Later that morning, Maya tries foaming milk again for a large latte. This second attempt turns out much better, and she realizes that she has finally learned to control the pressure of the hot steam.
同一個星期六晏啲嘅時候，我哋又變得好忙。Later the same Saturday, we got very busy again.
一個後生女行埋櫃枱，叫咗一杯大嘅卡布奇諾。A young woman came up to the counter and ordered a large cappuccino.
我沖嘅第一杯唔係好完美。My first cup was not quite perfect.
但係今次我準備好喇。But this time I was ready.
我攞起個金屬奶壺，將啲凍牛奶倒落去。I took the metal pitcher and poured cold milk into it.
我將個壺放喺部機下面，然後開咗熱蒸氣。I placed the pitcher under the machine and turned on the hot steam.
我冇即刻加大個壓力。I did not turn up the pressure immediately.
其實我已經學識咗去聽部機嘅聲。I have actually learned to listen to the machine.
今次蒸氣發出嘅響聲啱啱好。The loud sound from the steam was exactly right this time.
啲牛奶慢慢變熱。The milk slowly became warm.
而面頭嘅奶泡變得好厚同埋好綿。And the foam on top became very thick and soft.
呢個係一個好大嘅進步。It was a big step forward.
我小心咁將啲牛奶倒落大杯入面嘅咖啡上面。I carefully poured the milk over the coffee in a large cup.
佢睇落其實好似一杯真正專業嘅卡布奇諾。It actually looked like a real professional cappuccino.
「請慢用，」我話，然後將隻杯放喺櫃枱度。"Here you go," I said and placed the cup on the counter.
個女人笑咗一笑，攞走佢杯咖啡。The woman smiled and took her coffee.
我終於學識咗點樣控制個壓力。I have finally learned to control the pressure.
third saturday regular
Storyboard: By her third Saturday, Maya spots a familiar older man walking towards the front door. She has memorized his usual order, so when he reaches the counter she greets him and offers to make his usual cortado.
喺我第三個星期六，我喺櫃檯後面準備好。On my third Saturday, I stood ready behind the counter.
我終於學識喺咖啡店入面放鬆啲。I have finally learned to relax a bit in the café.
透過隻窗，我見到街上面有個年紀比較大嘅男人。Through the window, I saw an older man on the street.
佢慢慢咁向住正門行過嚟。He walked slowly towards the front door.
我做第一同第二更嗰陣佢都有嚟。He was also here on my first and my second shift.
佢係咖啡店嘅其中一個熟客。He is one of the café's regular customers.
我已經記熟咗佢叫開啲咩。I have learned to remember his order by heart.
佢次次買嘅嘢都係完全一樣。He always buys exactly the same thing.
佢打開門，行到埋收銀機前面。He opened the door and came up to the register.
我望住佢，笑咗笑。I looked at him and smiled.
「早晨，」我話。"Good morning," I said.
「要唔要幫你沖杯你平時飲開嘅哥塔多？」"Should I make your usual cortado?"
個男人好驚訝咁望住我。The man looked at me surprised.
然後佢好開心咁笑咗。Then he smiled happily.
「好呀唔該，麻煩晒，」佢答。"Yes please, I would very much like that," he answered.
我行埋去部濃縮咖啡機度。I went over to the espresso machine.
我而家已經沖過好多次咖啡，所以好快就搞掂。I have made coffee many times now, so it went quickly.
能夠認得我哋嘅客人，感覺真係好好。It was a good feeling to know our guests.
hanging up apron
Storyboard: After the busy shift ends, Maya scrubs the wooden counter clean. She hangs her apron on the hook in the staff room, locks the front door, and steps out into the bright afternoon street, happy with what she has learned so far.
我喺咖啡店嘅第三更終於完啦。My third shift at the café was finally over.
呢個星期六真係好忙。It has been a very busy Saturday.
我將張大木吧枱抹到乾乾淨淨。I washed the large wooden counter clean.
之後，我行咗入員工休息室。Afterwards, I went out into the staff room.
我除低條圍裙。I took off my apron.
我將佢掛喺牆上面第一個掛勾度。I hung it on the first hook on the wall.
喺最初幾更入面，我學到好多嘢。I have learned a whole lot on my first shifts.
我終於學識正確咁打奶泡。I have finally learned to froth milk correctly.
我仲學識記住一個熟客嘅柯打。I have also learned to remember a regular customer's order.
過程好好玩，但我都有啲攰。It has been fun, but I was also a bit tired.
我喺張枱度拎起我個袋。I took my bag from the table.
然後我行出去大門。Then I walked out to the front door.
依家由晏晝更接手。The afternoon shift now took over.
我踏出大街。I stepped out onto the street.
午後嘅陽光燦爛咁照住成個城市。The afternoon sun was shining brightly over the city.
喺行去搭巴士嘅途中，我會心微笑。I smiled to myself on the way to the bus.
我好鍾意呢份新嘅義工工作。I am very happy with my new volunteer work.
我已經好期待我嘅第四更。I am already looking forward to my fourth shift.
